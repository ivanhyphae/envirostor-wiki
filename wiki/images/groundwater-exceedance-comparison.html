A horizontal bar chart comparing the highest detected concentration of eight groundwater constituents to their regulatory limit, showing that only nitrate and manganese exceeded their limits across 2012-2019 monitoring, while barium, lead, chromium, beryllium, arsenic, and strontium stayed well below theirs.

Ratios are each constituent's highest detected concentration across all monitoring wells and events reviewed (2012–2019) divided by its regulatory limit. Most wells went dry and were decommissioned between 2019 and 2023; by February 2023 remaining wells were dry and monitoring discontinuation was recommended.
