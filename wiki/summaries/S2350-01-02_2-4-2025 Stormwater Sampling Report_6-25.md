# Document Identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Stormwater sampling report (February 4, 2025 event) — compliance monitoring for the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles site (Modesto, CA) under DTSC and CVRWQCB oversight. Prepared per the December 13, 2023 Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP).
- **Author/preparer**: Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Rebecca L. Silva, Project Manager Senior Engineer; John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG).
- **Date/period covered**: Sampling event February 4, 2025; report dated June 18, 2025.

# Key Claims
- Stormwater samples (SW-East, SW-West) showed total dissolved solids (TDS) at 210 mg/L and 180 mg/L, respectively; sulfate at 21 mg/L and 14 mg/L. All concentrations below Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for drinking water.
- Barium, copper, and zinc detected in both samples — none exceed respective MCLs.
- Molybdenum, vanadium, and strontium detected but no established MCLs.
- No runoff migration observed beyond Caltrans right-of-way (ROW) during the event.
- Previous sampling events (March 2023, December 2023, February 2024) also reported concentrations below MCLs except for thallium exceedance in December 2023.
- Background location BG-West was dry at time of sampling.

# Regulatory Decisions
- Sampling conducted under the December 2023 SAP approved by DTSC and CVRWQCB. No new formal regulatory decisions documented in this report.

# Analytical Results
- **TDS and Sulfate** (EPA Method 160.1 / 9056/300.0):
  - SW-East: TDS 210 mg/L, sulfate 21 mg/L
  - SW-West: TDS 180 mg/L, sulfate 14 mg/L
  - Secondary MCL: TDS 500 mg/L, sulfate 250 mg/L — no exceedances.
- **Title 22 Dissolved Metals** (EPA Method 6020/7470):
  - Barium: SW-East 18 µg/L, SW-West 14 µg/L (MCL 1000 µg/L — no exceedance)
  - Copper: 2.0 µg/L and 1.7 µg/L (Secondary MCL 1000 µg/L — no exceedance)
  - Zinc: 20 µg/L and <10 µg/L (Secondary MCL 5000 µg/L — no exceedance)
  - Molybdenum: 1.2 µg/L and 0.75 µg/L (no MCL)
  - Vanadium: 1.6 µg/L and 1.2 µg/L (no MCL)
  - Strontium: 83 µg/L and 60 µg/L (no MCL)
  - Thallium: not detected in February 2025 (previous exceedance in December 2023)
- All other Title 22 metals (antimony, arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, chromium, lead, mercury, nickel, selenium, silver) were either non-detect or below MCLs.
- **QA/QC**: Laboratory noted RPD outside acceptance limits for silver in matrix spike duplicate and molybdenum in sample duplicate; LCS/LCSD validated the batch.

# Remedial Actions
- No physical remediation during this reporting period. The site encapsulation of Stockpiles 1 and 2 beneath SR 132 was completed in October 2021; SR 132 opened November 2022.

- **CVRWQCB (Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board)**: Co-regulator.

# Key Terms and Conditions
- Additional stormwater sampling during qualifying rain events in 2025 planned by Caltrans.
- No observed runoff migration from Caltrans ROW — no immediate corrective action required.

- Background location BG-West will be sampled when flowing.
