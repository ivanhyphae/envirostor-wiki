## Document Identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Clarification letter from Caltrans to DTSC and RWQCB regarding disposition of excavated material from ramp safety improvements along SR 99 at Kansas Avenue, Modesto, CA. Addresses whether an Interim Remedial (Removal) Action Workplan is needed for Stockpile No. 3 material.
- **Author or preparer**: Ms. Sam Haack, P.E., Project Manager, Caltrans District 10
- **Date or period covered**: August 24, 2012. References site investigation data from April 24, 2012 (Geocon report).

## Key Claims
- Completion and implementation of an Interim Removal Action Workplan is **not needed** for the ramp project, based on results of additional investigations and proposed material management.
- Approximately 6,000 cubic yards will be excavated; ~2,800 cubic yards from Stockpile No. 3.
- No soil samples in the area to be excavated contained total metal concentrations at or above California hazardous waste TTLC thresholds.
- With the exception of arsenic, no metals exceeded commercial/industrial screening levels. Arsenic concentrations (1.0–3.2 mg/kg) are within site-specific background levels.
- 95% UCL concentrations for barium, strontium, and lead in Stockpile No. 3 excavation area are below residential and commercial/industrial screening levels.
- Material is **not classified as hazardous waste** — WET test results for barium and lead fell below STLC, even though one barium sample and five lead samples exceeded 10× STLC.

## Regulatory Decisions
- **Decision type**: Determination that no Interim Removal Action Workplan is needed for the ramp project.
- **Agency**: Caltrans (self-declared); letter addressed to DTSC (Mr. Steven Becker) and RWQCB (Mr. Steven W. Meeks) for concurrence.
- **Conditions imposed**: 
  - Native soil outside Stockpile No. 3 footprint suitable for reuse as structural backfill or offsite non-hazardous disposal.
  - Excess material not used as backfill to go to Fink Road Landfill (Class III).
  - Material from Stockpile No. 3 to go to Forward Inc., Manteca CA (Class II landfill), per Republic Services Special Waste Department Decision letter.

## Analytical Results
- **Target analytes**: Arsenic, barium, strontium, lead
- **Arsenic**: Range <1.0 to 3.2 mg/kg — within site-specific background; below commercial/industrial screening levels.
- **Barium**: One sample exceeded 10× STLC; 95% UCL below 10× STLC; WET results below STLC.
- **Lead**: Five samples at or above 10× STLC; 95% UCL below 10× STLC; WET results below STLC.
- **Hazardous waste classification**: Not hazardous (below TTLC and STLC thresholds per WET testing).
- **Media**: Soil — Stockpile No. 3 at Modesto ramp rehabilitation site.
- **Standards cited**: California TTLC, STLC (10× threshold), commercial/industrial screening levels, residential screening levels, site-specific background.

## Remedial Actions
- **Activity**: Excavation of ~6,000 cubic yards for widened roadbed, drainage, and retaining wall.
- **Stockpile No. 3**: ~2,800 cubic yards to be excavated and transported offsite to Forward Inc. (Class II landfill).
- **Native soil outside Stockpile No. 3**: Reused as structural backfill or disposed as non-hazardous soil at Fink Road Landfill (Class III).
- **Status**: Ramp safety improvement construction phase; material management plan clarified.

## Responsible Parties
- **Caltrans (District 10)**: Owner/operator; project manager (Ms. Sam Haack); preparer of clarification letter.
- **DTSC**: Mr. Steven Becker, P.G., Chief, Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit; Mr. Randy Adams, CEG — oversight and concurrence.
- **Central Valley RWQCB**: Mr. Steven W. Meeks, P.E. — co-regulator.
- **Geocon**: Prepared site investigation data report (April 24, 2012).
- **Advanced Technology Laboratories**: Performed WET testing for solubility levels.
- **Forward Inc. (Manteca, CA)**: Class II landfill accepting Stockpile No. 3 material.
- **Fink Road Landfill**: Class III landfill for non-Stockpile No. 3 material.
- **Teichert Construction**: Named as recipient copy (contractor).

## Key Terms and Conditions
- All soil analysis information to be provided by Caltrans to regulators.
- Material classified as non-hazardous based on WET results — no need for hazardous waste manifest.
- Use of Forward Inc. due to site-specific limitations of Fink Road Landfill.
- Republic Services Special Waste Department Decision document attached as acceptance.
