## Document Identity
- **Memo subject/question addressed:** Characterization testing of Bent 2 excavation soil stockpiles to determine suitability as clean cap material.
- **Preparer/author organization:** John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon Consultants.
- **Recipient/addressee:** Scott Frenette, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto.
- **Date:** October 30, 2020.
- **Area/stockpile/feature involved:** Bent 2 excavation (west of State Route 99) and Stockpile 2 (south side top) along State Route 132, Modesto, CA.

## Purpose
To determine whether approximately 4,000 cubic yards of native soil excavated from Bent 2 (overcrossing structure under construction) and placed as end dump piles on top of Stockpile 2 meets the clean cap criteria established in the *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan* (April 9, 2020) approved by DTSC. The specific criterion for barium is a maximum site-specific upperbound background concentration of 120 mg/kg.

## Data and Findings
- **Sampling performed:** Ten discrete soil samples (STK1–STK10) collected at ~80-foot intervals along the two rows of end dumps on October 29, 2020. Analyzed for total barium and total lead by EPA Method 6010B (24-hour turnaround) at California Laboratory Services (CLS).
- **Results (all mg/kg; reporting limit 2.5 mg/kg):**
  - **Barium:** STK1=230, STK2=120, STK3=170, STK4=47, STK5=49, STK6=220, STK7=54, STK8=50, STK9=31, STK10=48.
  - **Lead:** STK1=13, STK2=7.6, STK3=5.9, STK4=<2.5, STK5=<2.5, STK6=4.2, STK7=<2.5, STK8=2.9, STK9=<2.5, STK10=2.6.
- **Comparison to criteria:**
  - **Clean cap barium criterion (upperbound background):** 120 mg/kg. Exceeded by STK1 (230), STK3 (170), STK6 (220); STK2 equaled 120 mg/kg.
  - **BCS removal verification thresholds (listed for context):** Barium = 1,000 mg/kg; Lead = 80 mg/kg. No samples exceeded these.
  - **Lead site-specific maximum background:** 3.8 mg/kg; six samples exceeded this, but all are < 13 mg/kg.

## Conclusion and Recommendation
- **Conclusion:** The Bent 2 soil stockpiles do not meet the clean cap criteria (barium ≤ 120 mg/kg) as approved by DTSC.
- **Recommendation (by Geocon):** Pending DTSC review and approval, the material may be suitable for use as **partial clean cap (6 inches or less)** on the upper surface of Stockpile 2 because the planned structural pavement section (base material and asphalt/concrete) will be placed over the entire upper surface, providing additional cover. **Clean fill from previously approved sources** will be required for placement as clean cap on the **northerly slope** of Stockpile 2.

## Follow-on and Approval Status
- Results will be included in the **Removal Action Completion Report** to be submitted following completion of Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities.
- The recommendation is submitted for **DTSC review and approval consideration**; no approval is stated in the memo.
