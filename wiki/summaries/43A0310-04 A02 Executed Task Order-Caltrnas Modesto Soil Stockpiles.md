## Document Identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Interagency Agreement Task Order Contract (Caltrans 43A0310, DTSC 12-T0106), Task Order No. 4, Amendment 2. Purpose is to amend the existing task order for time and funding to reimburse DTSC for oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, and processing of applications/requests related to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project (State Route 132/99, Stanislaus County).
- **Author or preparer:** Not specified; signed by Caltrans (Mauricio Serrano, P.E., Chief, Hazardous Waste, Air, Noise and Paleontology Office, Division of Environmental Analysis; Richard Bailey, Contract Manager) and DTSC (Paul Ruffin, Contract Manager, Site Mitigation and Restoration Program).
- **Date or period covered:** Original term June 7, 2013 – May 31, 2016; amended term June 7, 2013 – May 31, 2018. Amendment execution date: February 9, 2018.

## Key Claims
No assertions about site conditions, compliance, or remedial adequacy are made in this administrative amendment. The document only states the purpose of reimbursing DTSC oversight and confirms that the scope of services remains unchanged from the original task order.
