## Combined Summary of "SR_132_DEIR_EA_Append_A-F.md"

### Document Identity

The document is a compilation of Appendices A through F for the Caltrans State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project Draft Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (DEIR/EA) in Stanislaus County, California. It includes supporting documentation for environmental review (CEQA, NEPA, Section 4(f) and 6(f) compliance), a Title VI policy statement, and comprehensive information on the Caltrans Relocation Assistance Program. The lead author is Caltrans, with support from consultants including ICF International and Jacobs Engineering Group. The documents were published in March 2013, referencing data from 2011-2014.

### Key Claims

- **Historic Properties:** Two properties—3530 Maze Boulevard and 416/418 I Street (Dania Hall)—were determined eligible for the National Register of Historic Places.
- **Parks and Recreational Resources:** Five parks (Charles M. Sharp Park, J.M. Pike Park, Virginia Corridor Trailway, Cesar E. Chavez Park/Maddux Youth Center, and Mellis Park) are located over 0.2 mile from project alternatives, with no identified noise, visual, or access impacts. The Virginia Corridor Trailway and Mellis Park are Section 6(f) resources, and no conversion to non-recreational use will occur.
- **Relocation Protections:** The document emphasizes that displaced persons (businesses, farms, nonprofits, and residents) shall not suffer disproportionately due to the public benefit project. Relocation benefits do not affect eligibility for Social Security, welfare, or income taxes.

### Regulatory Decisions

- **SHPO Concurrence (May 16, 2012):** Confirmed the National Register eligibility of 3530 Maze Boulevard and 416/418 I Street (Dania Hall).
- **Section 106:** Findings of effect will be submitted to the State Historic Preservation Officer (SHPO) after the preferred alternative is selected.
- **Relocation Assistance:** The project is subject to the Uniform Relocation Assistance and Real Property Acquisition Policies Act of 1970 (Uniform Act) and 49 CFR Part 24. A key condition is that persons not legally in the United States are not eligible for relocation payments.
- **Non-Discrimination:** Title VI of the Civil Rights Act of 1964 and Title VIII of the Civil Rights Act of 1968 apply to the relocation program.

### Analytical Results

- **Distance Analysis:** The park/pathway analysis showed all five sites are >0.2 mile from project alternatives, resulting in no use or impact under Section 4(f).
- **Archaeological Survey:** Additional archaeological survey and geoarchaeological investigations are pending and required after the preferred alternative selection.

### Responsible Parties

- **Caltrans:** Lead agency for the EIR/EA, responsible for Section 106 consultation, SHPO submittals, and administering the Relocation Assistance Program (including providing advisory services, determining eligibility, handling appeals, and making payments).
- **City of Modesto:** Owner/operator of the five parks and the Virginia Corridor Trailway.
- **State Historic Preservation Officer (SHPO):** Concurring authority on historic property eligibility determinations.
- **Displacees:** Individuals, families, businesses, farm operations, and nonprofit organizations subject to displacement due to the project.

### Key Terms and Conditions

- **Relocation Program:** Displaced persons shall be offered decent, safe, and sanitary (DS&S) replacement housing before displacement.
- **Housing Payments:** Replacement Housing Payments (RHP) are available, including Price/Mortgage Differentials for homeowners and Rent Differentials for tenants. Rent differentials are based on the difference between base monthly rent and comparable replacement rent, multiplied by 42 months. Payment caps exist ($22,500 for homeowners, $5,250 for tenants).
- **Moving Payments:** Reimbursement is available for actual moving costs (limited to 50 miles), a self-move agreement, or a fixed in-lieu payment ($1,000–$20,000 based on annual earnings).
- **Occupancy Thresholds:** Eligibility for full benefits is tied to occupancy duration (e.g., 90+ days for tenants).
- **Time Limits:** Displaced persons must receive a 90-day written notice before moving. They must purchase or rent a replacement dwelling within one year and file a claim within 18 months of vacating the property or Caltrans taking possession.
- **Last Resort Housing:** Caltrans may use this program if standard payment caps are insufficient or adequate housing is unavailable.
- **Reestablishment Costs:** Reestablishment payments are capped at $10,000 for small businesses, farms, and nonprofits. Searching expenses are capped at $2,500.
- **Right of Appeal:** Displaced persons have the right to appeal any Caltrans decision regarding eligibility or payment determinations, including legal representation and a judicial hearing.
