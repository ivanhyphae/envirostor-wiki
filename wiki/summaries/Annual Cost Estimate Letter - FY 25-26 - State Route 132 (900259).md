## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Annual cost estimate letter from DTSC to Caltrans, providing estimated oversight costs for Fiscal Year 2025-2026 for the State Route 132 West Expressway (SR 132/99 Interchange) site (Site Code 900259). Also serves as a notification of DTSC’s authority to recover costs for Land Use Covenant administration.
- **Author or preparer:** Mr. Abraham Serrato, Acting Unit Chief, Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, Site Mitigation and Restoration Program, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). Project Manager: Arielle McLeskey.
- **Date or period covered:** Letter dated December 24, 2025. Cost estimate covers FY 2025-2026 (July 1, 2025 – June 30, 2026).

## Key claims
- DTSC asserts authority under *California Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5* to provide a cost estimate for regulatory oversight at the site.
- DTSC asserts authority under *California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Section 67391.1(h)* to require Caltrans to pay all costs associated with administration of the Land Use Covenant (LUC) placed on the site.
- Caltrans remains liable for all response costs incurred by DTSC pursuant to *Health and Safety Code Section 79650 et seq.* and *CERCLA (42 U.S.C. § 9601 et seq.)*.
- The cost estimate is based on DTSC’s contracted estimation rates under the interagency agreement; the estimate is not an invoice – actual costs will be invoiced.

## Regulatory decisions
- No new regulatory decisions are documented in this letter. The letter references an existing Land Use Covenant (LUC) that restricts certain uses or activities at the site. No decision date, conditions, or approval details are provided.

## Analytical results
- Not applicable. No analytical data (contaminants, media, concentrations, thresholds) are reported in this document.

## Remedial actions
- Not described. The letter only generically references “cleanup at the Site” and the existence of a LUC. No physical work, quantities, phase status, or verification outcomes are mentioned.

## Responsible parties
- **Caltrans (California Department of Transportation):** Identified as the owner/operator responsible for oversight cost payments. Contact: Mr. Adam Inman, P.G., Engineering Geologist, Caltrans District 6, Office of Environmental Engineering.
- **DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control):** Regulatory agency providing oversight and cost recovery. Key personnel: Abraham Serrato (Acting Unit Chief), Arielle McLeskey (Project Manager), William Martinez (Unit Chief, cc).

## Key terms and conditions
- Caltrans must pay all actual costs incurred by DTSC for oversight activities, including LUC administration, as invoiced.
- The enclosed cost estimate (table) is based on DTSC’s contracted estimation rates and will be updated as necessary to reflect project changes or rate changes.
- DTSC reserves all rights to pursue cost recovery under any and all applicable provisions of state or federal law.
- The letter is not an invoice; actual costs will be billed separately.
- Contact for questions: Arielle McLeskey, (916) 255-3631, Arielle.McLeskey@dtsc.ca.gov.
