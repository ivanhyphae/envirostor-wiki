## Document Identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Task Order No. 4 under Interagency Agreement 43A0310 between Caltrans and DTSC. Purpose is to fund DTSC oversight and consultative services for investigation and remediation of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132/99) prior to construction of the future Route 132 West Expressway. Caltrans agrees to reimburse DTSC for oversight costs.
- **Author or preparer**: Prepared by Caltrans and DTSC jointly. DTSC Task Order Manager: Steven Becker, P.G. Caltrans Task Manager: Richard Stewart, P.G. Caltrans Contract Manager: Scott Nelson. Signatures by G. Scott McGowen, P.E. (Caltrans Chief Environmental Engineer) and Paul Ruffin, P.E. (DTSC Contract Manager).
- **Date or period covered**: Executed June 1, 2013. Term: June 1, 2013 through May 31, 2016. Total Task Order amount: $172,000.00 (FY 13/14: $98,312; FY 14/15: $39,871; FY 15/16: $33,774).

## Key Claims
- **Site characterization**: The Site consists of three soil stockpiles (SP-1, SP-2, SP-3) totaling in excess of 120,000 yd³ of contaminated soil, generated during sub-grade construction of State Route 99 through Modesto, Stanislaus County. Excavation near Kansas Avenue removed tailings from a portion of a Food and Machinery Corporation (FMC) barite (BaSO₄) and celestite (SrSO₄) processing disposal pond.
- **Contamination**: The Site is impacted by hazardous substances (metals), including barium, lead, and strontium (documented in the 2009 Final Preliminary Endangerment Assessment and other data).
- **Regulatory need**: A DTSC letter dated 12/7/2012 identified the need to address soil stockpile material use in the SR 132 West Expressway project and/or its final disposition regarding human health and water quality prior to design and construction.
- **Required deliverables**: DTSC requests update of the Human Health Risk Assessment, collection of technical data for remediation decisions, and preparation/approval of a Remedial Action Plan for inclusion in the environmental document for the SR 132 West Expressway.
- **Compliance assertion**: All work shall be performed in accordance with applicable local, state, and federal statutes, including Health and Safety Code section 25300 et seq. Caltrans does not admit liability by entering the Agreement.

## Regulatory Decisions
- **Interagency Agreement structure**: The work proceeds under preceding 2006 and 2012 Interagency Agreement task orders, with Task Order No. 4 (DTSC #12-T0106) effective June 1, 2013.
- **Co-regulation**: The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) is reviewing existing Site information; additional characterization may be required based on RWQCB review and comments.
- **CEQA role**: Caltrans is addressing CEQA via the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA), which will incorporate the RAP by reference. DTSC serves as a Responsible Agency reviewing hazardous substances information.
- **Remedy selection authority**: DTSC retains authority to approve all work, return documents with required changes, or modify documents after consultation. DTSC may require additions, modifications, or revisions to the RAP and RDIP during implementation.
- **Land Use Covenant**: DTSC may require a Land Use Covenant (LUC) per California Code of Regulations, title 22, section 67391.1 in the Final RAP. Caltrans agrees to sign and record within 10 days of receipt of a fully executed original.
- **Imminent endangerment authority**: DTSC may direct Caltrans to stop implementation or conduct additional activities if any activity poses imminent or substantial endangerment.

## Analytical Results
- **Contaminants identified**: Barium, lead, and strontium (metals) in soil stockpiles. Groundwater monitoring is required but specific analytical results not provided in this Task Order.
- **Media**: Soil (stockpiles), surface water, and groundwater are addressed for characterization.
- **Concentration ranges**: Not specified in this document; the Task Order references the need for additional site characterization and risk evaluation.
- **Applicable thresholds**: Cleanup goals to be identified through risk evaluation consistent with US EPA Risk Assessment Guidance for Superfund (EPA/540/1-89/002) and DTSC Supplemental Guidance for Human Health Multimedia Risk Assessments.
- **Exceedances noted**: Not specified; further characterization is required.

## Remedial Actions
- **Phase status**: Pre-remedial; this Task Order funds oversight for investigation and planning stages. No physical remedial action is described in this document.
- **Planned activities**: Additional Site Characterization (if needed), Risk Evaluation and Cleanup Level Determinations, Feasibility Study, Remedial Action Plan preparation, CEQA review, Public Participation, Remedial Design and Implementation Plan, Implementation of Final RAP, and Implementation Report.
- **Verification outcomes**: Not applicable at this stage; post-remedial sampling and monitoring procedures for air, soil, surface water, and groundwater will be addressed in the RDIP.
- **Ongoing obligations**: Quarterly groundwater monitoring reports required. Operation and Maintenance requirements apply per final RAP or DTSC-approved RDIP or O&M Plan.

## Responsible Parties
- **Caltrans**: Owner and operator of the Site. Responsible for funding all DTSC oversight costs, performing investigation and remediation, preparing all required deliverables, implementing the approved RAP, conducting public participation, entering into O&M Agreement, and recording Land Use Covenant if required.
- **DTSC**: Regulatory oversight agency. Responsible for review/approval of all deliverables, CEQA responsible agency review, public participation support, and regulatory decision-making. Retains all statutory powers and rights to protect public health and environment.
- **Central Valley RWQCB**: Co-regulator. Reviewing existing Site information; may require additional characterization.
- **FMC (Food and Machinery Corporation)**: Identified as the historical source of barite and celestite processing disposal pond tailings that were excavated during SR 99 construction. Not named as a responsible party in this Task Order.
- **Caltrans consultants/contractors**: Must work under direction of a California-licensed professional engineer or professional geologist with hazardous substance cleanup expertise. All contractors and subcontractors must comply with the Health and Safety Plan.

## Key Terms and Conditions
- **Project managers**: Each party to designate a Project Manager within 14 days of effective date. All communications directed through Project Managers.
- **DTSC approval**: All work subject to DTSC review and approval. No informal advice relieves Caltrans of obligation to obtain written approvals.
- **Notification of field activities**: Caltrans shall inform DTSC at least 7 days in advance of all field activities. DTSC may take duplicate samples.
- **Payment**: Caltrans to pay all DTSC oversight costs. Quarterly billing; payment within 60 days. Interest accrues on amounts outstanding more than 60 days. Checks payable to "Department of Toxic Substances Control" with project code #900259 and docket number.
- **Record preservation**: Caltrans shall retain all documents for minimum 6 years after Task Order termination. DTSC may request longer preservation.
- **Reporting obligations**: Caltrans shall notify DTSC immediately of any condition posing immediate threat to public health/safety or environment. Within 7 days, furnish a written report.
- **Access**: DTSC employees, contractors, and consultants granted access to the Site at all reasonable times.
- **Third-party actions**: Caltrans shall notify DTSC within 10 days of service of complaint in any third-party action related to the Site. Caltrans pays all costs.
- **Amendments**: May be made in writing by mutual agreement, effective third business day after last party signs.
- **Termination**: Either party may terminate with 30-day written notice. Caltrans responsible for DTSC costs through effective date. O&M activities may limit termination rights unless O&M Agreement is entered into.
- **Deliverable schedule**: Per Attachment E; modification requires written approval by Caltrans Task Manager but does not require amendment.
