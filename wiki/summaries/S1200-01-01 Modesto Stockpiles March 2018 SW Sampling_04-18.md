Here is the structured summary for the case-file wiki, prepared from the provided source document.

## Document identity
- **Report type:** Surface water sampling event report.
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Sampling date(s):** March 22, 2018.
- **Project number / task order:** Project No. S1200-01-01; Contract No. 06A2184, Task Order No. 1; EA No. 10-0X2700.

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Surface water (stormwater runoff).
- **Locations sampled:** PL1, PL2, PL3, PL4, and BG1.
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled:**
    - PL5: Insufficient water (drainage basin gate valve next to Stockpile 3; not opened during this event).
    - BG2: Insufficient water (background location on North Emerald Avenue).

## Results
- **Analytes measured:**
    - Dissolved metals (Title 22): Antimony, arsenic, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, zinc, beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, thallium.
    - General minerals: Chloride, nitrate as nitrogen, sulfate, sulfide, total alkalinity, bicarbonate alkalinity, carbonate alkalinity, total dissolved solids (TDS), total suspended solids (TSS).
- **Concentration ranges and units:**
    - Dissolved metals reported in micrograms per liter (µg/L).
    - General minerals reported in milligrams per liter (mg/L).
- **Applicable thresholds:** California Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for drinking water (SWRCB, September 23, 2015).
- **Exceedances:**
    - **Lead (Pb):** Detected in sample PL3 at 46 µg/L, exceeding the primary MCL of 15 µg/L.
    - **Manganese (Mn):** Detected in samples PL2 (54 µg/L), PL3 (370 µg/L), PL4 (110 µg/L), and BG1 (110 µg/L), exceeding the secondary MCL of 50 µg/L.
- **Trends versus prior rounds:** The report states that analytical results for most samples (excluding the lead exceedance at PL3) are "generally consistent" with results from previous events dating back to 2013.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Field conditions:** It was lightly raining at the time of collection (total rainfall for event: ~0.53 inch). Samples PL1, PL2, PL3, and BG1 were collected from puddles of water. PL4 was collected from a drainage basin outfall. Locations PL5 and BG2 had insufficient water for sampling. No runoff was observed migrating off Caltrans right-of-way.
- **Sampling / analytical deviations:**
    - Samples were field-filtered (0.45-micron) for dissolved metals analysis.
    - The laboratory (Advanced Technology Laboratories, ATL) noted that samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 were diluted for metals analysis due to high concentrations of target/non-target analytes or failing internal standards.
    - Samples PL1, PL2, PL3, and BG1 were diluted for sulfide analysis due to possible matrix interference.
    - Data qualifiers: "J" flags were used for estimated concentrations reported between the Reporting Limit and Practical Quantitation Limit.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusions (Geocon):** The elevated lead concentration at PL3, which is outside the Caltrans right-of-way, was noted as a deviation from previous results. Otherwise, results were consistent with historical data. Runoff from the stockpiles was observed to be retained within the Caltrans right-of-way.
- **Recommendations (Geocon / Caltrans):** The report notes that Caltrans plans to conduct additional surface water sampling during qualifying rain events in 2018. Surface water management (vegetative cover, straw wattles, interceptor berm) is described as continuing.

## Responsible parties
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans):** Owner/operator of the site and stockpiles; responsible for ongoing management and future sampling.
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC):** Oversight agency (project approval, report recipient).
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB):** Co-regulator (report recipient).
- **Geocon Consultants, Inc.:** Preparer of the report, conducted sampling and analysis.
- **Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL):** California-certified analytical laboratory.
