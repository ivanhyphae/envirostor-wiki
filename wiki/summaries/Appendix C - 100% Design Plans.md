## Document identity
- **Plan type:** 100% design plan set (construction drawings for a highway project incorporating remediation).
- **Preparer / author organization:** California Department of Transportation (Caltrans), District 10; design consultant: Dokken Engineering (Folsom, CA).  
- **Date:** 12/10/2018 (drawings dated); plan approval stamp dated 12/31/19.  
- **Project number or task order:** 10-403504 / 1000000424 (multiple sheets); also referenced as “Total Project No. 424”.

## Objective and scope
- **Purpose of the work:** The design plan set supports the proposed remediation of barium‑contaminated soil (BCS) and aerial‑deposited lead (ADL) from soil stockpiles located within the SR 132 / SR 99 corridor in Modesto, Stanislaus County. The work is part of a larger highway improvement project (Route 99/132 separation and widening) and is intended to address legacy contamination under DTSC oversight, with Caltrans as owner/operator and Central Valley RWQCB as co‑regulator.  
- **Areas, media, and stockpiles/features addressed:**  
  - **Stockpiles:** Stockpile 1, Stockpile 2, and Stockpile 3 (identified on grading and construction detail sheets).  
  - **Media:** Soil (barium‑containing soil and soil with aerial‑deposited lead).  
  - **Features:** Roadway embankment, median, shoulders, sound walls, basins (Basin 6A, Basin 6B, Basin 2, Basin 5), and temporary road access. Also includes existing monitoring wells MW‑1 through MW‑10.  
- **What approval or decision the plan supports or requests:** The plan is a 100% design submittal for agency approval (Caltrans, DTSC, RWQCB). It is explicitly marked “100% SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION” and requires approval before construction can proceed.

## Proposed approach
- **Sampling design: locations/counts, target analytes, methods, depths:**  
  - The plan includes a “BORING LOCATION TABLE” (sheet C‑49) with borehole IDs (N12, N13, N14, N15, N16, S31) and thicknesses of barium‑containing soil (e.g., N12 = 19 ft, N13 = 20.5 ft, N14 = 16.5 ft, N15 = 16 ft, N16 = 13.25 ft, S31 = 10 ft). These are used to delineate excavation limits. No additional sampling is proposed in these design plans; the plan bases excavation volumes on previously collected boring data.  
  - Monitoring wells (MW‑1 to MW‑10) are shown on grading and detail sheets. The plan specifies that MW‑1, MW‑2, MW‑4, MW‑5, MW‑6, MW‑9, MW‑10 are to be protected in place or abandoned (e.g., “TO BE ABANDONED” or “PROTECT IN PLACE”) during construction.  
- **Decision criteria or action levels proposed:**  
  - The plan defines “BCS” (barium‑containing soil) and “ADL” (aerial‑deposited lead) as materials requiring relocation. No specific numeric standards are stated in the drawing descriptions, but the plan proposes a **12‑inch cover of clean soil** over BCS/ADL placement areas (typical cross sections).  
  - Clean fill is defined as “clean roadway embankment material” and “structure backfill” (as shown in cross sections).  
  - Earthwork is governed by a shrinkage factor of 0.85 for roadway excavation.  
- **Sequence, schedule, or phasing of the work:**  
  - The “EARTHWORK QUANTITIES SUMMARY” (sheet 19) indicates staging (“EARTHWORK TOTAL PER STAGE”) and notes that volumes shown are maximum capacity for BCS/ADL material.  
  - The general sequence implied by the drawings:  
    1. Remove BCS and ADL soil from designated stockpile areas (Stockpile 1, 2, 3) to specified depths.  
    2. Place removed material in on‑site “BCS/ADL PLACEMENT” zones (excavated areas within the project limits).  
    3. Backfill with clean roadway embankment and structure backfill, including a 12‑inch clean soil cap.  
  - An **optional temporary road** (sheet 13) is included for construction access, with a temporary barrier rail (Type K) and 2% slope.  
- **For a variance:** Not applicable; this is a design plan set, not a variance request.
