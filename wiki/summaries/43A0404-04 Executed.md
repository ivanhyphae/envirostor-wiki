# Summary: Caltrans/DTSC Task Order 43A0404-04 – Modesto Soil Stockpiles

## Document Identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Interagency Agreement Task Order Contract (Task Order No. 04) between Caltrans and DTSC to reimburse DTSC for regulatory oversight of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles – State Route 132/99 site during construction of the Route 132 West Expressway.
- **Author or preparer**: Caltrans (Hazardous Waste & Paleontology Branch) and DTSC (Site Mitigation and Restoration Program); prepared by Richard Stewart (Caltrans Task Order Manager) and Dean Wright (DTSC Task Order Manager). Contract Managers: Richard Bailey (Caltrans) and Paul Ruffin (DTSC).
- **Date or period covered**: Executed March 18, 2021; term June 1, 2020 to June 30, 2025.

## Key Claims
- DTSC will provide professional staff services for regulatory oversight of activities at the site, including review of plans, reports, and technical documents, field oversight, document review, and participation in meetings.
- The work described is included in the required services under the master agreement (Agreement No. 43A0404 / DTSC No. 19-T4804) and constitutes architectural, engineering, or environmental services as defined in Government Code §4525(d)–(f).

## Regulatory Decisions
- The Task Order is a contractual authorization for DTSC to perform regulatory oversight; it does not itself constitute a formal regulatory decision (e.g., permit, cleanup order, or approval). Oversight is conducted under DTSC’s existing authority as co-regulator (with Central Valley RWQCB) for the site.

## Analytical Results
- None provided. The document is administrative and does not contain monitoring data or laboratory results.

## Remedial Actions
- No remedial actions are described. The document addresses DTSC oversight of *other* Caltrans construction/remediation activities; actual physical remediation work is referenced elsewhere in the corpus.

## Responsible Parties
- **Caltrans** (California Department of Transportation): Owner/operator; responsible for execution of the project and funding DTSC oversight.
  - Contract Manager: Richard Bailey
  - Task Order Manager: Richard Stewart, PG
  - Source District: District 10 (Stanislaus County), EA/EFIS 10/1012050205
- **DTSC** (California Department of Toxic Substances Control): Regulatory oversight agency; responsible for reviewing plans, providing field oversight, and managing the Task Order.
  - Contract Manager: Paul Ruffin, P.E.
  - Task Order Manager: Dean Wright, PG

## Key Terms and Conditions
- **Funding cap**: Total payable by Caltrans shall not exceed $100,000 for the term.
- **Period of performance**: June 1, 2020 – June 30, 2025.
- **Invoicing**: Quarterly billing invoices with daily logs, timesheets, and summary of direct/indirect charges.
- **Reports**: No formal progress reports required; DTSC provides written comments on submittals upon request.
- **Personnel**: Key staff and new classifications require approval of the Caltrans Contract Manager; substitution within approved class codes does not require amendment.
- **Meetings**: Held as necessary or upon request.
- **Conflict of Interest**: Caltrans personnel certify compliance with DD-09-R3 and Government Code §19990.
