## Document identity
- **Report type:** Surface water sampling event report
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Sampling date(s):** October 28, 2016
- **Project number / task order:** Contract No. 06A2184, Task Order No. 1, EA No. 10-0X2700; Project No. S1200-01-01

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Surface water (stormwater runoff)
- **Locations sampled:** PL1, PL2, PL3, PL4, PL5, BG1, BG2
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled:** None reported. All seven planned locations were sampled.

## Results
- **Analytes measured:**
    - **Dissolved metals (Title 22, EPA 6020/7470A):** antimony, arsenic, barium, beryllium, cadmium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, magnesium, manganese, mercury, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, selenium, silver, sodium, strontium, thallium, vanadium, zinc
    - **General minerals:** chloride, nitrate as nitrogen, sulfate, sulfide, total alkalinity, bicarbonate alkalinity, carbonate alkalinity, total dissolved solids (TDS), total suspended solids (TSS)
- **Concentration ranges and units:**
    - Metals: µg/L. Example: barium ranged from non-detect to 2,000 µg/L (historic, 2006 event); for this event, not specifically stated above/below MCLs, but the document states none exceeded MCLs.
    - General minerals: mg/L; TSS ranged from 45 mg/L (PL5) to 330 mg/L (PL4).
- **Applicable thresholds:** California primary and secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for drinking water (SWRCB, September 23, 2015).
- **Locations exceeding thresholds:** None. The manganese concentration at PL3 was reported equal to the secondary MCL. All other results were below respective primary and secondary MCLs.
- **Trends versus prior rounds:** The analytical results for this event are within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported at each sample location. No statistically significant trends were noted.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Field conditions:** Rain was falling during sample collection. The rainfall event began October 27 and ended October 28, 2016, with approximately 1.5 inches of precipitation. Puddles and flowing water were present at sample locations. Site reconnaissance did not identify any area where stormwater runoff appeared to be migrating offsite. The interceptor berm at the base of the south side access ramp to Stockpile 2 appeared to function as designed.
    - Gate valve adjacent to Stockpile 3 was not opened during this event.
- **Field parameters measured:** pH, electrical conductivity, temperature, turbidity, oxygen-reduction potential (ORP), dissolved oxygen (DO). Data provided in Table 1.
- **Sampling method:** Samples collected using disposable bailers and a suction pump, then transferred to laboratory-provided containers. Field-filtration (0.45-micron filter) for dissolved metals.
- **Laboratory QA/QC deviations:**
    - All samples (PL1–PL5, BG1, BG2) were diluted due to failing internal standard in the original run and possible matrix interference.
    - Specific dilutions: PL1–PL5, BG1, BG2 diluted for nitrate and sulfide; PL1, PL3–PL5, BG1, BG2 for chloride; PL1–PL4, BG1, BG2 for sulfate; PL1, PL3, PL5, BG1, BG2 for multiple dissolved metals (barium, beryllium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, magnesium, manganese, nickel, potassium, sodium, thallium, vanadium).
    - Case narrative (ATL): Results were J-flagged (between PQL and MDL) for some analytes, indicating estimated concentrations.
- **Data qualifiers:** J-flag applied to results between the Practical Quantitation Limit (PQL) and Method Detection Limit (MDL). Laboratory QC reports (Appendix A) include additional qualifiers (e.g., R for RPD outside acceptance criteria, M1 for matrix spike recovery outside limits).
- **Detection-limit issues:** None reported beyond the noted dilutions and J-flags.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusions (Geocon):** No runoff was observed migrating offsite. Stormwater falling on the stockpiles appeared to be retained within the Site boundary. The interceptor berm functioned as designed. Analytical results did not exceed applicable MCLs.
- **Recommendations:** Surface water originating from the western slope of Stockpile 2 will continue to be managed by maintaining vegetative/fiber cover and straw wattles. Caltrans plans to conduct additional surface water sampling during qualifying rain events in the future.
