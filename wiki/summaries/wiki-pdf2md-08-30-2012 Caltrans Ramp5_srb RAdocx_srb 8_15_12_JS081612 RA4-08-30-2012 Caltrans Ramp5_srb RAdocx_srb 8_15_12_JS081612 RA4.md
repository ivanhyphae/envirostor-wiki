## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: Letter - DTSC concurrence regarding disposition of excavated material.
- Author or preparer: Steven R. Becker, P.G., Chief, Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)
- Date or period covered: August 30, 2012

## Key claims
- "DTSC finds that the proposed offsite management of soil material from Stockpile No. 3 does not pose a risk to human health or the environment." (DTSC, August 30, 2012)
- "Sampling and analysis conducted by Caltrans' contractor Geocon in the area to be excavated in Stockpile No. 3, reports the concentration of barium, strontium, and lead in samples collected in Stockpile No. 3 material to be below residential and commercial/industrial screening level thresholds, with the exception of one sample having elevated lead which exceeded residential screening levels." (Geocon, prior to August 2012)
- "While arsenic exceeds residential and commercial/industrial screening levels, it is below background." (Geocon, prior to August 2012)
- "The reported 95% upper confidence limit concentrations for barium, strontium, and lead in the area to be excavated in Stockpile No. 3 are below residential and commercial/industrial screening levels." (Geocon, prior to August 2012)

## Regulatory decisions
- Decision type: Concurrence for offsite management of excavated soil material.
- Agency: Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)
- Date: August 30, 2012
- Conditions imposed: DTSC's findings do not absolve Caltrans' obligation to implement Remedial Action Plan activities associated with the SR 132 West Expressway/Freeway at the location of the stockpiles as described in DTSC's letter dated December 17, 2009, and Consultation Agreement No. 43A0260; DTSC No. 08-T3616, dated June 22, 2012.

## Analytical results
- Contaminants: Barium, Strontium, Lead, Arsenic
- Media: Soil (Stockpile No. 3 material)
- Concentration ranges: Not specified, but reported to be below residential and commercial/industrial screening level thresholds for barium and strontium.
- Applicable thresholds: Residential screening levels, Commercial/industrial screening levels, Background levels (for arsenic).
- Exceedances noted: One sample had elevated lead exceeding residential screening levels. Arsenic exceeded residential and commercial/industrial screening levels but was below background.

## Remedial actions
- Activity: Excavation of approximately 6,000 cubic yards of material, of which approximately 2,800 cubic yards is from Stockpile No. 3, for ramp safety improvements.
- Quantities: Approximately 6,000 cubic yards total excavation; approximately 2,800 cubic yards from Stockpile No. 3.
- Phase status: Proposed ramp construction and offsite disposal.
- Verification outcomes: Sampling and analysis indicate contaminants of concern in excavated soil material from Stockpile No. 3 are below respective screening level thresholds (with noted exceptions for lead and arsenic), leading to DTSC concurrence for offsite management.

## Responsible parties
- California Department of Transportation (Caltrans): Project Manager (Sam Haack), owner/operator, responsible for implementing California Environmental Quality Act, dust control, and storm water management.
- Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC): Lead regulatory agency.
- Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB): Co-regulator, consulted by DTSC.
- Geocon Consultants Inc. (Geocon): Caltrans' contractor, conducted site investigation, sampling, and analysis.
- Teichert Construction: Caltrans' contractor, prepared Material Removal Plan.
- Republic Services: Waste profile provider.
- San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution District: Approved Dust Control Plan.
- Stanislaus County Health Agency: Noted as a recipient of the letter.

## Key terms and conditions
- Follow-on actions: Caltrans is obligated to implement Remedial Action Plan activities associated with the SR 132 West Expressway/Freeway at the location of the stockpiles.
- Obligations: Caltrans is responsible for implementing all aspects of the construction activity, including California Environmental Quality Act compliance, dust control, and storm water management.
