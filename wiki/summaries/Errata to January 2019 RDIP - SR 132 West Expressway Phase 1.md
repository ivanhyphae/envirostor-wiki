# Document Identity

- **Deliverable type:** Errata to Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP)
- **Version/Draft/Final status:** Final (post-construction contract award)
- **Preparer/Author organization:** Caltrans (based on content; original RDIP by Geocon Consultants, Inc.)
- **Date:** Not specified in source excerpt (parent RDIP dated January 30, 2019; errata prepared after construction contract award)
- **Project number or task order:** Not stated

# Purpose and Regulatory Context

- **Milestone/requirement:** Corrects the parent RDIP (January 2019) to reflect changes in roles and responsibilities after construction contract award—specifically striking Geocon Consultants, Inc. as Caltrans' agent for ensuring containment of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles via the SR-132 West project.
- **Oversight framework:** DTSC with CVRWQCB as co-regulator; Caltrans is the responsible party.
- **Parent document:** *Remedial Design Implementation Plan – Interim Phase 1* dated January 30, 2019, approved by DTSC on April 3, 2019.
- **What is being corrected:** Sections identifying Geocon as the consultant performing the work; Caltrans is now identified as the responsible party to ensure work is performed per the RDIP, regardless of consultant.

# Site and Contamination Summary

- **Contaminants of concern:** Barium (primary), lead (secondary)
- **Affected media:** Soil (stockpiles designated Stockpile 1, Stockpile 2, Stockpile 3 along SR-132 right-of-way)
- **Extent:** Barium-containing soil is addressed via excavation, consolidation within designated areas (Stockpiles 1 and 2), removal of entire Stockpile 3, and capping with clean fill. Threshold levels: barium 1,000 mg/kg, lead 80 mg/kg.
- **Sources and areas of concern:** Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles (three stockpiles) along SR-132 alignment in Stanislaus County, CA.

# Findings and Risk Characterization

- **Key analytical findings:**
  - Barium and lead present at elevated concentrations in stockpiles (above residential health risk screening levels but below commercial/industrial/construction worker screening levels for most COPCs).
  - Verification sampling uses EPA Method 6010B with reporting limit 1.0 mg/kg; comparison to thresholds of 1,000 mg/kg barium and 80 mg/kg lead.
- **Human health/ecological risk:** Not an HHRA/SSI; document notes that most COPCs are below residential screening levels, but barium and lead require dust control and air monitoring (water spray, perimeter air monitoring) to protect workers and nearby residents.
- **Conclusions:** Stockpile removal is confirmed complete when verification soil sample analytical data (total values or 95% UCLs) are below threshold levels.

# Selected or Recommended Remedy

- **Remedial alternatives evaluated:** Not detailed in this errata (parent RDIP and RAP establish the "containment alternative").
- **Selected/recommended approach:** Containment of barium-containing soil via:
  - Excavation and consolidation of barium-containing soil within designated areas of Stockpiles 1 and 2.
  - Removal of Stockpiles 3 and ends of Stockpile 2 (bridge abutment areas) to original ground.
  - Capping: Minimum 12-inch-thick clean imported fill and/or minimum 4-inch-thick aggregate base (AB) over consolidation zones, plus pavement, hardscape, or additional embankment fill.
  - Placement of vegetated ground cover.
- **Design basis:**
  - Survey data of final containment zones (Stockpiles 1 and 2) for land use covenant (LUC)/deed restriction recording.
  - Clean fill import sources sampled per DTSC's *Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Material* (Appendix E).
- **Performance/cleanup standards:**
  - Barium <1,000 mg/kg; lead <80 mg/kg in verification samples.
  - No visible dust; use of water trucks/spray; stop-work during high winds or inadequate dust control.
  - Stormwater BMPs per SWPPP and Caltrans specifications.
- **Engineered features:** Retaining walls/bridge structures, discing/ripping and recompaction of fill surfaces, grading control, survey monuments.

# Conclusions and Path Forward

- **Closure status:** Not final; construction will proceed under this RDIP. A Remedial Action Completion Report will be prepared by Caltrans after work.
- **Conditions:**
  - Full-time Caltrans representative on-site during barium soil activities.
  - Air monitoring for fugitive dust (Section 5.3 referenced).
  - Groundwater/stormwater monitoring continues during construction per Shaw (2006) work plan.
  - Health and safety plan (HSP) in place (Appendix F).
- **Monitoring obligations:** Annual groundwater monitoring (April), stormwater monitoring, periodic observations.
- **Land use restrictions:** Land use covenant/deed restriction required only for completed barium-containing soil containment zones (Stockpiles 1 and 2) recorded with Stanislaus County Clerk-Recorder; removed areas (Stockpile 3, ends of Stockpile 2) do not require LUC.
- **Follow-on deliverables:** Monthly status reports, Remedial Action Completion Report, final survey data for LUC.
