## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Final Operation and Maintenance Agreement. The purpose is to formalize the O&M procedures for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project.
- **Author or preparer**:
    - Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for the Agreement.
    - GEOCON CONSULTANTS, INC. for the Operation and Maintenance Plan (O&M Plan) submitted to DTSC.
- **Date or period covered**:
    - **Agreement Date**: June 20, 2023 (effective date is DTSC's signature date after Proponent's signature). Proponent signed May 31, 2023.
    - **O&M Plan Date**: December 1, 2022.
    - **Remedial Action Completion Report Approval Date**: January 18, 2023.

## Key claims
- **Remedy has been installed at the Site for the remediation of soil; consists of consolidation and clean fill capping**: The DTSC-approved remedy involves consolidating barium-containing soil (BCS) and clean fill capping it as highway embankment fill for the SR 132 West Expressway. (DTSC, Section 2.0 Installed Remedy)
- **BCS material contains elevated barium and lead concentrations**: The BCS material contains barium concentrations up to 72,000 mg/kg, exceeding regulatory screening levels, and also elevated lead concentrations. (GEOCON, Section 1.3 BCS Containment Zones)
- **Aerially-deposited lead (ADL) impacted soil from SR 99 exceeds California hazardous waste thresholds**: ADL impacted soil from the northbound shoulder of SR 99 was placed within Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone due to lead concentrations exceeding California hazardous waste thresholds. (GEOCON, Section 1.3 BCS Containment Zones)
- **SR 132 was opened to traffic on November 3, 2022**: The highway was opened following the completion of Interim Phase 1 construction. (GEOCON, Section 1.0 Introduction)
- **Proponent is a state government entity and not required to maintain financial assurance mechanism**: As per California Health and Safety Code Section 25355.2(c)(4), Caltrans is exempt from maintaining financial assurance with DTSC for this remedy. (DTSC, Section 9.0 Financial Assurance)
- **BCS material is buried and not in direct contact with stormwater runoff**: The BCS material is located beneath highway pavement and clean fill along the northern fill slopes of the containment zones. (GEOCON, Section 2.6 Stormwater Management)

- **Agency**: Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
- **Date**: June 20, 2023.

    - Proponent shall fully implement the DTSC-approved Operation and Maintenance Plan dated December 1, 2022. (DTSC, Section 4.0)
    - Proponent must submit a written request at least 60 days prior to any modification or discontinuation of the installed remedy. (DTSC, Section 5.0)
    - DTSC may require modification, replacement, or additions to the installed remedy if it fails to achieve remediation objectives or protect human health/environment. (DTSC, Section 6.0)
    - Proponent shall conduct a five-year review of the remedy, with the first review five years after the Remedial Action Completion Report approval (January 18, 2023). (DTSC, Section 7.0)
    - All sampling and analysis must follow DTSC-approved Quality Control/Quality Assurance (QC/QA) procedures. (DTSC, Section 8.0)
    - Proponent is liable for DTSC's costs incurred in implementing the Agreement and overseeing the work. (DTSC, Section 10.1)
    - Proponent must notify DTSC immediately and within seven days furnish a report upon learning of any condition posing an immediate threat to public health, safety, or environment. (DTSC, Section 11.1)
    - Proponent shall provide DTSC employees, contractors, and consultants access to the Site at all reasonable times. (DTSC, Section 12.0)
    - Proponent must make available all data and information concerning contamination at or from the Site upon DTSC's request. (DTSC, Section 13.0)
    - Proponent shall retain all relevant data, reports, and documents for a minimum of six years after agreement termination. (DTSC, Section 14.0)
    - Proponent must inform DTSC at least seven days in advance of all field activities. (DTSC, Section 15.0)
    - Proponent must designate a Project Manager within 14 days of the effective date of the Agreement. (DTSC, Section 16.0)
    - All engineering and geological work must be under the direction of California-licensed professionals. (DTSC, Section 17.0)
    - Compliance with the O&M Plan: Any disturbance of BCS material is subject to health and safety precautions, dust mitigation, proper stockpiling, and DTSC authorization. (GEOCON, Section 2.0 Soil Management Procedures)
    - Caltrans must provide minimum ten working day advance notification to DTSC of any planned activities that may disturb BCS material. No BCS disturbance is permitted without prior written authorization from DTSC. (GEOCON, Section 2.1 Notification of BCS Disturbance)
    - Caltrans is responsible for maintaining stormwater best management practices. (GEOCON, Section 2.6 Stormwater Management)
    - Caltrans will perform minimum annual inspections during the third quarter (July-September), and supplemental inspections after significant events. (GEOCON, Section 3.1 Inspections)
    - Maintenance and repairs will be performed as needed based on inspection findings, typically within 30 days. (GEOCON, Section 3.2 Maintenance and Repairs)

## Analytical results
- **Contaminants**: Barium, lead, aerially-deposited lead (ADL).
- **Media**: Soil.
- **Concentration ranges**: Barium up to 72,000 mg/kg.
- **Applicable thresholds**: Regulatory screening levels for barium, California hazardous waste thresholds for lead.
- **Exceedances noted**: Barium concentrations exceeding regulatory screening levels; lead concentrations exceeding California hazardous waste thresholds in ADL-impacted soil.
- **Required analysis for offsite disposal**: Title 22 metals (EPA Methods 6010B and 7471A for mercury). Soluble metals testing (Waste Extraction Test, Toxicity Characteristic Leaching Procedure) may be required for elevated total metal concentrations. (GEOCON, Section 2.5 BCS Material Reuse/Offsite Disposal)

## Remedial actions
- **Activity**: Consolidation and capping of barium-containing soil (BCS) and aerially-deposited lead (ADL) impacted soil.
- **Quantities**:
    - Average maximum stockpile fill thickness: approximately 20 feet.
    - Total estimated volume of BCS: 160,000 cubic yards.
- **Phase status**: Completed during Interim Phase 1 construction of the SR 132 West Project (2020-2022).
- **Verification outcomes**: The remedy is intended to mitigate exposure to BCS by incorporating the stockpiles as highway embankment fill and covering with clean fill and structural pavement section. A Removal Action Completion Report (2022 RACR) provides a summary of the completed BCS stockpile remedial actions.
- **Installed remedy details**:
    - Consolidation and clean fill capping of barium-containing soil (BCS) as highway embankment fill.
    - BCS Stockpiles 1 through 3 were consolidated, capped, and utilized as highway embankment fill.
    - Stockpile 3 BCS material was entirely removed from the east side of SR 99 and placed within the Stockpile 1 Containment Zone.
    - BCS Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones are located adjacent and west of Emerald Avenue (SR 132 Stations 1125+50 and 1133+50) and between Emerald Avenue and SR 99 (SR 132 Stations 1135+35 and 1148+30).
    - ADL-impacted soil from SR 99 was placed within the Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone.
    - Covered with minimum 12-inch-thick DTSC-approved clean fill material and/or structural pavement section.

## Key terms and conditions
- **Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Agreement**: Formal document governing the ongoing management of the remediated site.
- **Operation and Maintenance Plan (O&M Plan)**: Specific procedures for maintaining the integrity of the containment zones.
- **Land Use Covenant (LUC)**: DTSC will send a draft LUC for review and signature, restricting the use of the remediated area.
- **Five-year review**: Proponent must review and reevaluate the remedy effectiveness every five years, starting from January 18, 2023 (approval of Remedial Action Completion Report).
- **Notification of BCS Disturbance**: Caltrans must provide DTSC with a minimum of ten working days' advance notice for any activities potentially disturbing BCS material. No disturbance is allowed without DTSC's prior written authorization.
- **Health and Safety**: Workers in BCS disturbance areas must implement health and safety precautions, wear appropriate PPE, attend briefings on barium and lead risks, and cleanse hands/face before eating/drinking/smoking. Compliance with Cal/OSHA rules is required.
- **Dust Mitigation**: Implement proper dust mitigation measures during BCS disturbance to minimize fugitive dust emissions, including water spray and covering temporary stockpiles and truck loads. Work cessation during high winds or inadequate control.
- **BCS Material Stockpiling**: Temporary storage (less than 30 days) of excavated BCS material must be on secured, designated areas, covered with minimum 6-mm plastic sheeting, and surrounded by erosion control measures (e.g., straw wattles).
- **BCS Material Reuse/Offsite Disposal**: Removed BCS material can be placed back in Containment Zone if properly compacted and covered with DTSC-approved clean fill (minimum 12-inch) or structural pavement. Excess material requires stockpile sampling and characterization for landfill acceptance. Caltrans authorized representative must sign waste manifests.
- **Stormwater Management**: Caltrans is responsible for maintaining stormwater Best Management Practices (BMPs) and performing periodic stormwater sampling.
- **Site Access and Security**: Caltrans will periodically patrol containment zones for damage or unauthorized access; breaches to security fencing or cap damage must be documented and repaired.
- **Annual Inspections**: Caltrans to conduct minimum annual inspections during Q3 (July-September) for visual damage to the cap, drainage, security fencing, and unauthorized access. Supplemental inspections are also required after significant events (e.g., fires, heavy rainfall, seismic activity, traffic accidents).
- **Maintenance and Repairs**: Based on annual inspection findings, required maintenance to be completed within 30 days unless DTSC grants an extension.
- **Reporting**:
    - **BCS Disturbance Activities**: Written documentation required within 30 days of completion, including scope, dates, depth, volume, final destination, characterization data, manifests, and site photos.
    - **O&M Annual Inspection Summary Report**: Submitted to DTSC by January 31st for the prior calendar year, summarizing BCS disturbance, inspections, maintenance, site condition changes, and effectiveness of the cap.
- **Financial Assurance**: Caltrans, as a state government entity, is exempt from maintaining financial assurance with DTSC.
- **Cost Recovery**: Caltrans is liable for DTSC's costs incurred in implementing the Agreement and overseeing the work.
- **Project Managers**: Each party must designate a Project Manager within 14 days for overseeing implementation and communication.
- **Professional Oversight**: Engineering and geological work must be performed under the direction and supervision of California-licensed professional engineers and geologists.
- **DTSC Approval**: All reports, plans, schedules, and documents are subject to DTSC's review and approval.
- **Record Preservation**: All data, reports, and documents related to the Agreement must be preserved for a minimum of six years after termination.
