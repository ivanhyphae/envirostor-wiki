## Decision or request

- **What is being accepted:** The Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated June 18, 2020, describing Stockpile 2 sampling along the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area and laboratory results.
- **Substance of acceptance:** DTSC and the Central Valley RWQCB agree with the memo’s conclusion that:
  - Soil from 0–3 ft bgs along the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing must be placed in designated containment zones.
  - Soil *below* 3 ft bgs is suitable for use as embankment fill or capping material.
- **Variance Request linkage:** Material below 3 ft bgs is considered part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP); no new VR is required before using this soil.
- **Effective date:** June 22, 2020 (date of acceptance email).

## Open items and next actions

- The correspondence does not establish any required revisions, resubmittals, or pending approvals. The memo is accepted as submitted.
