## Document identity
- **Report type:** Groundwater monitoring round
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Reporting period / sampling date(s):** March 18, 2013 (field activities); report dated May 16, 2013
- **Project number / task order:** Geocon Project No. S9525-01-44B; Task Order No. 44, EA 10-403500; Contract No. 06A1580

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Groundwater
- **Wells, stations, or locations sampled:** MW‑1, MW‑2, MW‑3, MW‑4, MW‑5, MW‑6, MW‑7, MW‑8, MW‑9, MW‑10 (10 wells)
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled, and why:** None; all 10 wells were sampled. However, MW‑5 and MW‑6 went dry during purging and required recovery before sample collection (see Field conditions).

## Results
- **Analytes measured and concentration ranges (units):**
  - **Dissolved metals (primary COCs):** barium (Ba) 59–410 µg/L; lead (Pb) ND–2.5 µg/L (detected in MW‑1 at 2.2 µg/L, MW‑7 at 2.5 µg/L); strontium (Sr) 470–1,400 µg/L.
  - **Other dissolved metals:** arsenic (As) 0.38–2.3 µg/L; chromium (Cr) 0.97–4.9 µg/L; vanadium (V) 0.78–9.5 µg/L; nickel (Ni) ND–5.5 µg/L; molybdenum (Mo) ND–5.7 µg/L; copper (Cu) ND–3.8 µg/L; selenium (Se) ND–1.5 µg/L; cobalt (Co) ND–1.0 µg/L; manganese (Mn) ND–130 µg/L; antimony (Sb) ND–0.78 µg/L; zinc (Zn) ND–100 µg/L; beryllium, cadmium, silver, thallium, mercury all <PQL.
  - **General minerals:** nitrate as N (NO₃-N) 2.7–30 mg/L; total dissolved solids (TDS) 290–700 mg/L; chloride (Cl) 17–100 mg/L; sulfate (SO₄) 36–180 mg/L; sulfide (S²⁻) ND–0.18 mg/L; alkalinity (as CaCO₃) 130–270 mg/L.
- **Applicable thresholds:**
  - California Primary MCLs (CDPH): Ba 1,000 µg/L; Pb 15 µg/L; As 10 µg/L; Cr 50 µg/L; NO₃-N 10 mg/L; Sb 6 µg/L; Ni 100 µg/L; Se 50 µg/L; etc.
  - California Secondary MCLs: TDS 500 mg/L; Mn 50 µg/L; Cu 1,000 µg/L; Zn 5,000 µg/L; etc.
  - EPA Drinking Water Health Advisories: Sr 4,000 µg/L; V 260 µg/L; Mo 40 µg/L.
  - CDPH Notification Level: Co 6 µg/L (not exceeded).
  - CDPH Regulatory Action Level: Mn 50 µg/L (secondary MCL used as threshold).
- **Exceedances:**
  - **Nitrate as N:** exceeded primary MCL of 10 mg/L in MW‑1 (13 mg/L), MW‑5 (30 mg/L), MW‑6 (20 mg/L), MW‑10 (12 mg/L).
  - **TDS:** met or exceeded secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in MW‑1 (500 mg/L), MW‑5 (700 mg/L), MW‑6 (680 mg/L), MW‑10 (570 mg/L).
  - **Manganese:** exceeded secondary MCL of 50 µg/L in MW‑1 (120 µg/L) and MW‑7 (130 µg/L).
  - No other dissolved metals exceeded their respective thresholds.
- **Trends vs. prior rounds:** Barium and strontium concentrations similar to historical levels and remained significantly less than thresholds. Lead detected in only two wells at low concentrations. Other metals stable. Nitrate and TDS elevations consistent with prior events; MW‑1 (upgradient) suggests background nitrate/TDS may be naturally elevated.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Dry wells / low recovery:** Wells MW‑5 and MW‑6 went dry after purging 1 gallon and 1.5 gallons, respectively. Both were allowed to recover; MW‑6 required a second recovery after additional purging. Samples were collected after recovery.
- **Depth-to-water / gradient:** Depth to groundwater ranged from 31.15 ft (MW‑1) to 39.62 ft (MW‑5) below TOC. Groundwater flow direction east-southeast at an average gradient of 0.0006 ft/ft, consistent with historical data.
- **Dissolved oxygen and ORP:** Measured but not reported numerically in the summary; data sheets in Appendix A.
- **Sampling/analytical deviations:**
  - Equipment blank (distilled water over decontaminated pump) contained chromium at 0.91 µg/L. Geocon concluded this did not significantly influence sample results because chromium concentrations were similar to prior sampling events.
  - Several matrix spike/matrix spike duplicate (MS/MSD) recoveries or RPDs were outside laboratory control limits; however, the laboratory control samples validated the batch, and no data qualification was deemed necessary.
- **Other:** Purged groundwater was stored in a 55-gallon drum and transported to Geocon’s Rancho Cordova office, then disposed at Inviro-tec Disposal facility in Lincoln, CA on March 27, 2013.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusions (Geocon):**
  - No dissolved metals (except Mn in MW‑1 and MW‑7) exceeded applicable water quality thresholds.
  - Nitrate and TDS exceedances in MW‑1, MW‑5, MW‑6, and MW‑10 are consistent with prior events; MW‑1 (upgradient) suggests natural background may be elevated for these parameters.
  - Barium and strontium remain at concentrations similar to historical levels and well below thresholds.
  - Stiff diagrams show stable ionic composition: MW‑1, MW‑2, MW‑4, MW‑5, MW‑7, MW‑9, MW‑10 are calcium-dominant; MW‑3, MW‑6, MW‑8 are sodium-dominant.
- **Recommendations (Geocon):**
  - Modify groundwater monitoring schedule from bi-monthly to quarterly, with the next event in June 2013.
  - Redevelop well MW‑5 during the June 2013 event to improve hydraulic communication and sample representativeness (MW‑5 consistently purges dry, has high turbidity, and may have sediment blocking the screen).
