## Document identity
- **Memo subject / question addressed**: BCS Stockpiles Clean Cap Borrow Area Characterization Testing — assessment of soil suitability for use as clean cover fill material over consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) in Stockpiles 1 and 2.
- **Preparer / author organization**: John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon
- **Recipient / addressee**: Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto
- **Date**: February 27, 2020 (fieldwork December 21, 2020; analytical report February 26, 2020)
- **Area, stockpile, or feature involved**: Proposed clean cap borrow area adjacent to and easterly of Carpenter Road within State Route 98 (SR 132) right-of-way, Modesto, California. Approximately 1,250 ft long × 200 ft wide, excavated to ~5 ft depth. Material to be used to cap BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2.

## Purpose
Determine whether soil from the proposed borrow area is suitable as clean fill material for capping the two barium-containing soil stockpiles. The sampling strategy was developed in general accordance with the DTSC *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and was approved by Mr. Dean Wright of DTSC prior to fieldwork.

## Data and findings
- **Sampling performed**: 12 direct-push borings (B1–B12) on a grid pattern within the borrow area. Two depth intervals per boring: 0–2 ft and 2–5 ft. Continuous core samples collected in stainless steel tubes, chilled, and transported to Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL) under chain of custody.
- **Composite preparation**: Each depth interval produced four 3-part composites (e.g., B1+B2+B3, B4+B5+B6, etc.), yielding eight composite samples total.
- **Analytes and methods**:
  - Title 22 metals (arsenic, barium, beryllium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, nickel, silver, vanadium, zinc, antimony, cadmium, selenium, thallium) by EPA Method 6010B (ICP-AES).
  - Mercury by EPA Method 7471A (cold vapor AA).
  - Organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) by EPA Method 8081A on the four 0–2 ft composites.
- **Results (concentration ranges in mg/kg, with site-specific background from 2007 Shaw study in parentheses)**:
  - Arsenic 1.0–1.5 (1.2)
  - Barium 60–110 (72.8)
  - Beryllium 1.3–1.6 (0.2)
  - Chromium 7.5–11 (7.1)
  - Cobalt 3.2–4.9 (4.4)
  - Copper 5.3–9.9 (7.5)
  - Lead 1.7–7.0 (2.0)
  - Nickel 5.6–8.2 (5.3)
  - Silver 1.6–2.5 (0.2)
  - Vanadium 16–25 (31.3)
  - Zinc 21–41 (26.3)
  - Antimony, cadmium, selenium, thallium, mercury, and all OCPs were not detected above laboratory reporting limits in any sample.
- **Comparison to criteria**: Reported metal concentrations are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. No exceedances noted.

## Conclusion and recommendation
**Conclusion**: The proposed clean cap borrow area appears suitable to generate clean cover fill materials for capping BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2.  
**Recommendation**: The characterization data will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) to be submitted after completion of stockpile capping activities.

## Follow-on and approval status
- **Next steps**: Inclusion of results in the RACR; submission of RACR following capping.
- **Approval status**: The sampling strategy was approved by DTSC (Mr. Dean Wright) prior to fieldwork. The memo itself does not indicate that the findings have been formally approved by DTSC or RWQCB; it is presented as a technical memorandum for the project record.
