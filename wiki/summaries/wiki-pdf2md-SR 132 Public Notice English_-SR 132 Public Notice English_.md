## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: Public Notice - Announcement of availability of a Draft Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) and Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP), along with a public hearing and public comment period for both documents.
- Author or preparer: Caltrans
- Date or period covered: Public Comment Period: January 18, 2017 - March 17, 2017; Public Hearing: February 22, 2017

## Key claims
- **Caltrans (as lead agency for CEQA and assigned by FHWA for NEPA) proposes to construct a four-lane freeway/expressway along SR 132 west of Modesto.** (Caltrans)
- **The Caltrans Modesto Stockpiles occupy three areas within state right-of-way south of Kansas Avenue.** (Caltrans)
- **Caltrans proposes to cap the soil stockpiles as part of the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway construction project.** (Caltrans)
- **The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) have reviewed and approved the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for public noticing.** (DTSC, CV RWQCB)
- **The three stockpiles total approximately 160,000 cubic yards of soil containing metals (primarily barium, lead, and strontium).** (Caltrans)
- **If approved, the RAP would allow for the placement of impacted soil beneath roadway pavement, within bridge abutments, and behind retaining walls.** (Caltrans)
- **This action would avoid potential future impacts to human health and and the environment.** (Caltrans)
