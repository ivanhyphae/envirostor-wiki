## Document Identity
- **Memo Subject:** Stockpile 3 BCS Removal Verification Testing
- **Preparer:** John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Recipient:** Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto
- **Date:** March 13, 2020
- **Area/Feature:** Former Stockpile 3, State Route 132 right-of-way, adjacent to SR 99 between Kansas Avenue and the Modesto Irrigation District (MID) water canal, Stanislaus County, California

## Purpose
The memo presents the results of verification soil sampling after excavation and removal of barium‑containing soil (BCS) from Stockpile 3. The excavated material was transported to Stockpile 1 for use as embankment fill. The work was performed to confirm that residual soil in the excavation meets the verification thresholds specified in the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP).

## Data and Findings
- **Excavation details:** Approximately 38,440 cubic yards of BCS (3,075 truck‑loads) were removed. Excavation base elevations ranged from 82.5 to 84 feet above MSL.
- **Verification sampling:** 24 soil samples (S3 1‑0 through S3 24‑0) were collected from the base of the excavation between February 28 and March 12, 2020. Two additional berm samples (S3 SE BERM and S3 SW BERM) were taken from the southern berm adjacent to the MID canal. After initial results showed barium above the threshold at S3 9‑0, S3 10‑0, and S3 11‑0, those areas were deepened about one foot and re‑sampled (S3 9A‑0, S3 10A‑0, S3 11A‑0). Sample S3 16‑0 was collected from storm drain backfill near SR 99.
- **Analytical methods:** All samples were analyzed for barium and lead by EPA Method 6010B at Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL) under chain of custody.
- **Results vs. criteria (RDIP thresholds):**
  - Barium verification threshold: 1,000 mg/kg (groundwater protection)
  - Lead verification threshold: 80 mg/kg (residential screening level)
  - All final excavation samples showed barium concentrations ranging from 46 to 970 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg — all below the respective thresholds.

## Conclusion and Recommendation
The Stockpile 3 BCS removal has met the barium and lead verification thresholds. The excavation area is suitable for placement of clean embankment fill and other highway improvements without further BCS RDIP monitoring requirements. This information will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted after Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities.
