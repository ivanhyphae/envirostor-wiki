This document is a Supplemental Site Investigation (SSI) Report prepared by **Geocon Consultants, Inc.** for **Caltrans District 6**, with a final revised version issued March 1, 2013. The report was prepared to address environmental data gaps identified by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB), in support of a Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project.

The investigation focuses on three soil stockpiles (Stockpiles 1-3) in Modesto, California, containing approximately **160,000 cubic yards of fill** contaminated with **barium** from a former FMC Corporation percolation pond. The primary contaminants of concern (COCs) evaluated include **barium**, **cadmium** (later deemed a false positive), **arsenic, nickel, vanadium, PAHs**, and localized **petroleum hydrocarbons**.

A major component of the supporting data for this report is a laboratory analytical data package for total metals analysis of soil samples from borings FB16 through FB34, analyzed using EPA methods 6020 (ICP-MS) and 6010B (ICP-AES) and dated October 2012. Based on this and other investigations, the report concluded that all barium detections were below California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs) and that a Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA) Update found cumulative cancer risks below thresholds. The report recommends evaluating three alternatives for the RAP: no action, removal and disposal, or using the impacted soil as engineered fill during SR 132 construction. Caltrans must continue current site management (including runoff containment) while regulatory agencies finalize the path forward. The lead parties include **Caltrans District 6** (owner/operator), **DTSC** (lead oversight), **CVRWQCB** (co-regulator), and **Geocon Consultants, Inc.** (preparer of the SSI and HHRA).
