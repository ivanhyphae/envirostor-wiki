## Document Identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Community update fact sheet summarizing the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Site (SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project). Its purpose is to inform the public and invite comment on the proposed cleanup remedy.
- **Author/preparer:** California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC); contact representative Randy Adams (DTSC Project Manager).
- **Date/period covered:** Fact sheet dated for public comment period January 18, 2017 to March 17, 2017; public hearing held February 22, 2017.

## Key Claims
- The primary contaminants in stockpile soil are barium, strontium, and lead (source: site investigations 2004 onward).
- Groundwater beneath stockpiles (depth 30–44 ft) flows consistently southeast; three active City of Modesto water supply wells exist within one mile southeast.
- Contaminants of concern (for groundwater and surface water) are below Water Quality Objectives (source: Caltrans monitoring since 2013).
- The 2007 Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA), confirmed by 2012 soil sampling, concluded that soil and groundwater do not pose unacceptable human risk to off‑site residents, trespassers, or Caltrans workers under existing management (fence, access control, vegetative cover, groundwater monitoring, no soil removal/placement).

## Regulatory Decisions
- **DTSC and Central Valley RWQCB** have reviewed the draft final RAP and approved it for public notice and comment.
- **Proposed remedy:** Alternative 4 (containment as part of the State Route 132 West project) – stockpiles retained behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath pavement; unpaved portions capped with clean soil cover. DTSC and RWQCB concur with this recommendation.
- Following public comment, DTSC may approve the final RAP after completion/certification of the CEQA EIR/EA (Caltrans is lead CEQA agency; DTSC and RWQCB are Responsible Reviewing Agencies).
- If RAP is approved, DTSC and RWQCB will oversee development of a Remedial Design Implementation Plan.

## Analytical Results
- **Media:** Soil stockpiles, groundwater, stormwater runoff.
- **Contaminants of concern:** barium, strontium, lead (primary in stockpile soil).
- **Groundwater:** Depth to water 30–44 ft; flow direction southeast. Monitored bi-monthly or quarterly from 2012; annual monitoring currently. Results evaluated against background concentrations and FMC‑monitored contaminants (2014 analysis).
- **Surface water:** Sampled nine times (annual winter events) since 2013 for metals and general mineral parameters. All contaminants below Water Quality Objectives.
- **Thresholds:** Water Quality Objectives (no specific values given); background concentrations used for groundwater comparison.

## Remedial Actions
- **Proposed action (Alternative 4):** Containment of 160,000 cubic yards of stockpile soil within the structural section of the State Route 132 West Expressway/Freeway between Needham Street and Carpenter Avenue. The stockpiles will be enclosed by retaining walls, bridge abutments, and roadway pavement.
- **Phases:**
  - **Phase 1 (initial construction, 2018–2020):** Build retaining walls and bridge abutments, pave southern half of site; northern half covered with graded engineered soil layer.
  - **Phase 2 (ultimate build‑out, 2028):** Complete the full roadway cross‑section, consolidating stockpile material behind retaining walls and under pavement.
- **Current site management (pre‑construction):** Perimeter fence maintained, vegetative cover, groundwater monitoring, prohibition on soil removal/placement.
- **Alternatives considered:** No Action (Alt 1); Institutional Controls (fences, deed restriction, monitoring – Alt 2); Removal to off‑site disposal (Alt 3); Containment (Alt 4 – selected).

## Responsible Parties
- **Caltrans:** Site owner; prepared the draft final RAP; will construct and maintain the containment remedy; lead agency under CEQA.
- **DTSC:** Co‑regulator; reviews and approves RAP; oversees Remedial Design Implementation Plan.
- **Central Valley RWQCB:** Co‑regulator; reviews and approves RAP; oversees water quality aspects.
- **FMC (Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation):** Former operator of processing plant that discharged to unlined ponds; source of contamination (historical, not current party in this document).

## Key Terms and Conditions
- **Public comment period:** January 18 – March 17, 2017; comments to Randy Adams, DTSC.
- **Public hearing:** February 22, 2017 at Mark Twain J.H.S., Modesto.
- **Deed restriction and land use covenant** required to restrict site use and prevent soil disturbance.
- **Ongoing obligations:** Site inspections, water quality monitoring (groundwater and surface water), maintenance of engineered containment (including cover and drainage).
- **If SR 132 West project is not constructed:** containment would consist of a clean soil cap over stockpiles with the same monitoring and deed restrictions.
- **Contingency:** Modifications to RAP may be required based on public comments on both the draft RAP and the draft EIR/EA.
