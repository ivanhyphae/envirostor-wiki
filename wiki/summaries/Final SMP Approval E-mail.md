## Decision or request
- **What is being approved:** The revised Soil Management Plan (SMP) dated February 8, 2021, which describes excavation, confirmation sampling, handling, and disposal of impacted soil for two proposed retention basins associated with new construction along State Route 132 (SR 132) in Modesto.
- **Conditions / required revisions:** Two minor comments must be addressed in the field (no further SMP revision required):
  1. Soil with Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) above screening levels must be segregated, covered, **and labeled** until transported offsite.
  2. If TPH-impacted soil remains when the maximum depth of the basins is reached, Caltrans must alert and consult with the Water Board and DTSC to determine appropriate next steps.
- **Effective date:** The approval is stated in the February 11, 2021 email; the SMP is accepted as of that date.
- **Other comments:** The approval notes that the revised SMP addresses comments provided by DTSC and the Water Board on January 31, 2021.

## Open items and next actions
- Caltrans / City of Modesto / Rincon Consultants must incorporate the two conditions during field work (labeling TPH soil, and establishing a consultation protocol if TPH persists at maximum basin depth).
- No resubmission of the SMP is required; the conditions are to be implemented in the field.
- The earlier email from Ryan Thacher (Rincon) indicates that if the pending RWQCB discussion on TPH screening levels results in a need for further SMP revisions, those will be addressed later. This does not affect the immediate approval.
