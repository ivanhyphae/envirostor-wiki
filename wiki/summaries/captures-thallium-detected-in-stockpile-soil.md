## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: Summary of analytical findings regarding thallium in soil stockpiles.
- Author or preparer: Not specified.
- Date or period covered: Analysis reported in June 2014; mentions a December 2023 stormwater event and a March 28, 2023 surface water letter.

## Key claims
- Thallium was detected at low concentrations in soil samples from the Modesto Soil Stockpiles.
- Antimony, selenium, and silver were not detected in any of the analyzed soil samples.
- Prior to the December 2023 stormwater event, thallium had not been detected in groundwater or pre-2023 surface water samples.
- The December 2023 stormwater event represented the first reported instance of a Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) exceedance for thallium at the site.

## Analytical results
- Contaminant: Thallium
- Media: Soil (stockpiles)
- Concentration: Low concentrations detected.
- Other detected contaminants: None specified beyond thallium.
- Undetected contaminants: Antimony, Selenium, Silver.
- Applicable thresholds: Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for thallium.
- Exceedances noted: Yes, for thallium in the December 2023 stormwater event.
