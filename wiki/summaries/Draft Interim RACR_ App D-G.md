### Combined Summary

This source document is a technical compilation supporting a Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) for the remediation of Barium-Containing Soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2 along State Route 132 in Modesto, California. Prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for Caltrans under the oversight of the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board, it primarily consists of analytical laboratory data packages from LA Testing (a division of EMSL Analytical, Inc.), produced from March to June 2020.

**Characterization of Clean Cap Material:** The document details a successful investigation to identify suitable clean cover material for capping the BCS stockpiles. Sampling of a primary borrow area and the "Basin 5" area—both historically agricultural land—showed that concentrations of Title 22 metals (including arsenic, barium, chromium, and lead) and organochlorine pesticides were consistent with naturally occurring background levels. Both areas were concluded to be suitable sources for generating clean cover fill. In contrast, material from the Bent 2 Excavation Stockpiles was deemed unsuitable for a full clean cap due to elevated barium and lead levels, though it may be approved for partial use under structural pavement. Carpenter Road Shoofly material met all clean cap criteria and was approved for use.

**Air Monitoring and Compliance:** The document also presents raw industrial hygiene data from air monitoring conducted during the Interim Phase I grading of the stockpiles (February to June 2020). Samples were analyzed for barium, lead, and total dust using NIOSH methods. The data shows that all monitored levels of total dust, barium (detected up to 4.3 µg/m³), and lead (all results below reporting limits) were consistently below designated project action levels. The report concludes that dust control measures were effective and that on-site activities did not cause off-site migration of contaminants above regulatory thresholds.

**Conclusion and Path Forward:** The overarching conclusion is that suitable clean cover material has been identified and characterized, and that interim grading activities were conducted without adverse impacts. This raw analytical data, compiled by Geocon Consultants, Inc. with Caltrans as the owner/operator, is intended to support the final RACR, paving the way for the final capping of the BCS stockpiles and completion of the project.
