The Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles in Stanislaus County, California, were the subject of a stormwater sampling event on January 6, 2016, with results detailed in documents from Caltrans and Geocon Consultants, Inc. The primary focus of the analysis was on Total Suspended Solids (TSS), Barium, Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH), Lead, dissolved metals, and general minerals. Samples were collected from multiple locations, including PL1, PL2, PL4, PL5, SW-1, and SW-2.

Key findings from the sampling event on January 6, 2016, indicate that TSS concentrations ranged from 39 mg/L to 100 mg/L, with one sample (SW-1) meeting the 100 mg/L Action Level. Barium concentrations were significantly below the 2 mg/L Action Level, and TPH (gasoline, diesel, motor oil) and Lead were mostly non-detect or below detection limits. Dissolved metals and general minerals were generally consistent with previous reports and remained below Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs). For instance, Arsenic was measured at 1.0 µg/L (MCL 10 µg/L), Barium at 27 µg/L, and Copper at 2.3 µg/L (MCL 1,300 µg/L). General minerals like Nitrogen (Nitrate as N) (ranging from <0.10 to 9.9 mg/L, MCL 10 mg/L) and Total Dissolved Solids (ranging from <10 to 240 mg/L, MCL 500 mg/L) also remained below their respective MCLs. Field parameters such as pH, conductivity, temperature, turbidity, ORP, and dissolved oxygen were also measured across various sampling dates spanning 2013-2016. Historical data show a single elevated barium concentration in a March 2006 sample, but no similar exceedance was observed in the recent data.

An analytical laboratory report from Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL) further details the January 6, 2016 sampling. TSS concentrations in this report ranged from ND (PQL 10 mg/L) to 430 mg/L (BG1). Dissolved Barium registered between 19 ug/L and 100 ug/L. Dissolved Calcium ranged from 3800 ug/L to 8500 ug/L, and Dissolved Copper from 3.4 ug/L to 15 ug/L. Many other dissolved metals, including Antimony, Arsenic, Beryllium, Cadmium, Chromium, Cobalt, Lead, Magnesium, Manganese, Molybdenum, Nickel, Potassium, Selenium, Silver, Sodium, Thallium, Vanadium, Zinc, and Strontium, were "Not Detected" (ND) at their Practical Quantitation Limits, particularly in sample BG2. The report confirmed that dissolved CAM 17 Metals plus Strontium and Manganese were field-filtered.

Overall, the analytical results for the January 6, 2016 event were largely within expected ranges, with only one sample meeting the TSS Action Level and no other exceedances of MCLs or specific action levels observed. Observations consistently indicated that runoff remained within the Caltrans right-of-way. The documentation stresses the importance of continued stormwater sampling during future storm events at designated locations for ongoing monitoring.
