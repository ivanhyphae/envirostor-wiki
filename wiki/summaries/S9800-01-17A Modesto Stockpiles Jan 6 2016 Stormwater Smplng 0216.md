# Summary: Surface Water Sampling Report – January 6, 2016

## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Surface water sampling report (letter report) documenting results of a stormwater sampling event and site conditions at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, submitted to satisfy DTSC-approved sampling plan requirements.
- **Author or preparer**: Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Rebecca Silva, Project Manager; John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Senior Engineer).
- **Date or period covered**: Report dated February 17, 2016; sampling event conducted January 6, 2016.

## Key claims
- No surface water runoff was observed migrating beyond the Caltrans right-of-way during the January 6, 2016 event, consistent with previous events (April 2013, January 2014, February 2014, December 2014, December 2015).
- All dissolved metals and general minerals reported were below their respective primary and secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs).
- Analytical results were within the same general concentration ranges as previously reported at each sample location.
- The elevated barium concentration (2,000 µg/L) found historically in March 2006 at sample SW03 (northwestern side of Stockpile 3) was characterized as isolated and confined to Caltrans right-of-way; no similar exceedance occurred in the January 2016 event.
- Existing surface water management measures (vegetative/fiber cover on Stockpile 2, straw wattles, interceptor berm, gravel cover on access ramp) are effectively controlling runoff.

## Regulatory decisions
- No formal agency decisions or enforcement actions are documented in this report.
- The sampling plan (Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan, January 2006 by Shaw Environmental, Inc., and Addendum dated February 20, 2013) was approved by DTSC.
- Sample locations PL1–PL5, BG1, and BG2 were approved by DTSC and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB).

## Analytical results
**Dissolved metals (EPA Method 6020/7470A)** – Samples PL1–PL5, BG1, BG2:
- Barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, potassium, sodium, strontium detected in all site samples.
- Nickel, zinc detected in PL1, PL3, PL4, PL5.
- Molybdenum in PL1, PL2, PL4, PL5; antimony in PL1, PL4, PL5; manganese in PL3–PL5; vanadium in PL2 and PL3; lead in PL3.
- Arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, mercury, selenium, silver, thallium not detected above practical quantitation limits (PQLs) in any sample.
- **No exceedances of primary or secondary MCLs** for any dissolved metal.

**General minerals** (alkalinity, chloride, nitrate, sulfate, sulfide, TDS, TSS) – All results below primary/secondary MCLs; concentrations consistent with prior events.

**Historical note**: March 2006 sample SW03 (Stockpile 3) reported barium at 2,000 µg/L; no such exceedance in current event.

**QA/QC observations**:
- Dilution required for several samples due to high target analyte concentrations (calcium, barium, potassium, strontium, zinc).
- Matrix spike recoveries for total sulfide and calcium outside acceptance limits due to possible matrix interference; analytical batch validated by laboratory control sample.

## Remedial actions
- **Vegetative/fiber cover and straw wattles** maintained on western slope of Stockpile 2 to intercept and redirect stormwater within Caltrans right-of-way.
- **Interceptor berm** constructed at base of access ramp (south side of Stockpile 2, north end of Bennett Street) in February 2014; recently regraded.
- **Gravel layer** placed over access ramp to prevent soil erosion (Photos 7 and 8, provided by Caltrans).
- Additional surface water sampling events planned during the 2016 rain season.

## Responsible parties
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) – District 6, Hazardous Waste Branch** – Owner/operator of the stockpile site; responsible for implementing remedial measures and authorizing sampling.
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)** – Lead regulatory oversight; approved sampling plans.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB)** – Co-regulator; approved sampling locations.
- **Geocon Consultants, Inc.** – Consultant performing field sampling, laboratory coordination, and report preparation.
- **Shaw Environmental, Inc.** – Prepared original Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (January 2006).
- **Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL)** – California-certified laboratory performing analyses (ELAP No. 1838).
- **Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC)** – Historical property owner; former evaporation/disposal ponds from barium/strontium processing operations (ca. 1940s–1970s) whose soil was excavated to form stockpiles.

## Key terms and conditions
- Continue surface water monitoring during the 2016 rain season.
- Maintain existing runoff management infrastructure (cover, wattles, berm, gravel) in good condition.
- No surface water from the stockpile area is allowed to migrate off Caltrans right-of-way; berm and drainage measures are to remain functional.
- The gate valve at the Stockpile 3 drainage basin is to remain closed unless emergency capacity concerns for SR 99; has not been opened in recent past.
