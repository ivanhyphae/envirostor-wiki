## Document identity
- **Plan type:** Variance request (Second Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan, RDIP Variance #2)
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Date:** September 2, 2020
- **Project number or task order:** Project No. S1908-01-01

## Objective and scope
- **Purpose of the work and what prompted it:** To request a variance from the approved Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) regarding the clean cover fill material proposed for capping the upper surface of consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) within Stockpiles 1 and 2. The variance was prompted by the highway contractor’s proposal to use Class 3 aggregate subbase as an alternative to the specified aggregate base (AB) material.
- **Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed:** The upper surface of Stockpiles 1 and 2 (consolidated BCS and aerially-deposited lead (ADL) fill containment zones); clean cover fill material (soil/aggregate).
- **What approval or decision the plan supports or requests:** The plan requests DTSC approval for the use of a minimum of 4 inches of Class 3 aggregate subbase, in lieu of 4 inches of aggregate base, as the interim clean cover cap on the stockpiles before the final pavement section or hardscape is placed.

## Proposed approach
- **Decision criteria or action levels proposed:** The proposed variance is based on an equivalency argument. The document asserts that a minimum of 4 inches of Class 3 aggregate subbase provides adequate interim clean cover, equivalent to the 4 inches of aggregate base specified in the RDIP, because:
    - The required structural pavement section for the SR 132 West Project (0.85 ft concrete, 0.25 ft asphalt, 0.70 ft Class 3 aggregate subbase) will ultimately provide the final cap.
    - The gradation of Class 3 aggregate subbase (primarily sand and gravel with lesser fines) is similar to aggregate base materials.
- **Sequence, schedule, or phasing of the work:** The proposed cover is designated as an interim measure until the placement of the final structural pavement section and hardscape. No specific schedule is provided.
- **For a variance: the requirement at issue and the deviation requested, with rationale:**
    - **Requirement at issue:** Section 3.2.2 of the RDIP, which specifies "minimum 4-inch-thick aggregate base (AB) materials may be placed on the upper stockpile surface pending subsequent placement of structural pavement and hardscape."
    - **Deviation requested:** To use a minimum of 4 inches of **Class 3 aggregate subbase** instead of the specified aggregate base.
    - **Rationale:** The Class 3 aggregate subbase has a similar composition (sand and gravel) to aggregate base and will be incorporated into the full structural pavement section. Using it as an interim cover meets the RDIP’s intent of preserving fill capacity while providing adequate clean cover until the final pavement/hardscape cap is installed.

## Deliverables and approvals sought
- **What the plan commits to produce:** This document (RDIP Variance #2) is the deliverable. It presents the proposed material substitution and its justification.
- **Which agency approval it is submitted for:** The document is submitted to the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for approval of the variance.
