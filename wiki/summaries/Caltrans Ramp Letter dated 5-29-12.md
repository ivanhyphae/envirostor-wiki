## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Letter from California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) requesting review and concurrence that excavated material from Stockpile #3 is non-hazardous and may be disposed at an offsite landfill or reused as structural backfill.
- **Author/preparer:** Ms. Sam Haack, P.E., Project Manager, Caltrans District 10.
- **Date:** May 29, 2012.

## Key claims
- Excavation will intersect Stockpile #3, one of several soil stockpiles placed in anticipation of the future State Route 132 Expressway project.
- Final soil analysis by Geocon Consultants, Inc. indicates elevated metal concentrations above site-specific background levels, but below both residential and commercial/industrial California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs).
- Based on the 2009 Preliminary Environmental Assessment and current investigation data, the material is suitable for reuse as structural backfill or for offsite disposal as non-hazardous soil, provided disclosure and review of site characterization data to the accepting facility.
- The ramp safety improvement project (SR 99 at Kansas Avenue, Modesto) will generate approximately 6,000 cy³ of excavated material, with a portion originating from Stockpile #3.

## Regulatory decisions
- No formal DTSC decision is documented in this letter; the letter explicitly requests DTSC concurrence before proceeding.
- Previous DTSC direction and guidance is referenced regarding the Interim Remedial Action Work Plan and soil investigations.

## Analytical results
- **Contaminants:** Elevated metals (specific analytes not named in this letter).
- **Media:** Soil (excavated material from Stockpile #3).
- **Concentration ranges:** Not provided. Document only states levels are above site-specific background but below CHHSLs for residential and commercial/industrial pathways.
- **Thresholds referenced:** Site-specific background levels; California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs) for residential and commercial/industrial.
- **Exceedances noted:** Exceedance of background (not a regulatory threshold), but compliance with CHHSLs.

## Remedial actions
- **Activity:** Excavation for ramp safety improvements (lane extension, curve radius improvement, shoulder widening including retaining wall and embankment slope layback).
- **Phase status:** Contract on hold pending DTSC concurrence.
- **Proposed disposal:** Material will be hauled to Stanislaus County Fink Road Landfill.
- **Controls:** Contractor to implement contract specifications for dust control, storm water management, and erosion control, plus additional measures to limit disturbance.

## Responsible parties
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans):** Owner/operator; project proponent.
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC):** Regulatory oversight (Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program).
- **Geocon Consultants, Inc.:** Performed soil analysis.
- **Teichert Construction:** Contractor for the ramp project.
- **Stanislaus County Fink Road Landfill:** Proposed disposal/receiving facility.

## Key terms and conditions
- DTSC concurrence required for the material to be managed as non-hazardous and for the contract to proceed.
- Disclosure and review of site characterization data to the accepting landfill is required before offsite disposal.
- Excavation must comply with all contract specifications for dust control, storm water plans, and erosion control, with additional disturbance‑limiting measures.
