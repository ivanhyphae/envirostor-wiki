## Document identity
- **Deliverable type**: DTSC Approval Letter (regulatory concurrence) for the Supplemental Site Investigation (SSI) and Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA) Update.
- **Version / draft / final status**: Final concurrence letter.
- **Preparer / author organization**: Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), authored by Randy S. Adams, C.E.G., Senior Engineering Geologist, Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program.
- **Date**: April 4, 2013.
- **Project number or task order**: Referenced as an Interagency Agreement and Task Order with Caltrans (no specific number in the letter).

## Purpose and regulatory context
- **Milestone or requirement**: DTSC review and approval of the revised SSI and HHRA Update, which were prepared under an Interagency Agreement to support a Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the management of soil stockpiles. The approval follows implementation of a Final Supplemental Site Characterization Workplan (Geocon, September 18, 2012) and responses to DTSC comments on earlier draft reports (December 2012).
- **Oversight framework and submitting agency**: DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), provides oversight under the Interagency Agreement. The letter is addressed to Caltrans District 10 as the owner/operator.
- **For an errata**: Not applicable; this is an approval of a stand-alone deliverable.

## Site and contamination summary
- **Contaminants of concern, affected media, and extent**: The letter refers to “hazardous substances in the soil stockpiles” at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (State Route 132/99, Stanislaus County, CA). The additional characterization data collected under the approved workplan (September 2012) evaluated potential lateral and vertical migration of contaminants from the stockpiles. The specific contaminant (barium) is not named in the letter but is the known COC from the broader project context.
- **Sources and areas of concern addressed**: Soil stockpiles located at the proposed State Route 132/99 West Expressway. The SSI and HHRA Update were revised to address DTSC comments issued February 15, 2013.

## Findings and risk characterization
- **Key analytical findings and exceedances**: Not detailed in the letter; the SSI and HHRA Update are the source reports. The letter states DTSC concurrence with the findings in the SSI (March 1, 2013) and HHRA Update (March 1, 2013).
- **Human health / ecological risk results**: The HHRA Update was approved; the letter indicates that the risk assessment was updated using new characterization data, but no specific cancer risks, hazard indices, or exposure pathways are recited.
- **Conclusions about site conditions**: The letter does not recount the reports’ conclusions, only that DTSC concurs.

## Selected or recommended remedy
- Not discussed in the letter. The reports are characterization/risk assessment steps preceding remedy selection (a Remedial Action Plan is anticipated).

## Conclusions and path forward
- **Closure status**: No closure or remedy selected in this document.
- **Conditions, monitoring obligations, land use restrictions**: None specified.
- **Required follow-on deliverables**: The letter implies that the approved SSI and HHRA Update will support preparation of a Remedial Action Plan for management of the soil stockpiles.
