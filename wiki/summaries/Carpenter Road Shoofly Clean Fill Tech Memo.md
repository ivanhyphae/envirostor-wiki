# Document Identity
- **Subject:** Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Characterization Testing (State Route 132 Project, Modesto, CA)
- **Preparer / Author Organization:** John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon
- **Recipient / Addressee:** Rick Demi, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto
- **Date:** April 12, 2021
- **Area / Feature:** Carpenter Road Shoofly; adjacent Stockpiles 1 and 2 (BCS stockpiles)

# Purpose
To characterize native soil excavated from the Carpenter Road Shoofly (approx. 5,000 cubic yards) for use as clean capping material over Barium-Containing Soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2, pending DTSC approval. The characterization was performed to demonstrate compliance with the clean cap criteria established in the *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan* (April 9, 2020) approved by DTSC.

# Data and Findings
- **Sampling:** 24 discrete soil samples collected along six vertical transects (CSF1–CSF6) at depths of 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet from the top of the slope. Samples were composited into eight 3-part composite samples by same elevation along each slope face.
- **Analytes and Methods:** Barium and lead analyzed by EPA Method 6010B (expedited 48-hour turnaround) at California Laboratory Services (CLS) under chain-of-custody.
- **Results:**
  - **Barium:** detected in all 10 soil samples at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 mg/kg. All results below the site-specific maximum background value of 120 mg/kg.
  - **Lead:** detected in 9 of 10 samples at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg. All results less than or equal to the site-specific maximum background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg.
- **Comparison to Criteria:** All barium results < 120 mg/kg (upper-bound background threshold per DTSC-approved variance); all lead results ≤ 3.8 mg/kg (site-specific background maximum).

# Conclusion and Recommendation
- **Conclusion:** Soil from the Carpenter Road Shoofly meets the BCS clean cap criteria established in the DTSC-approved variance (barium ≤ 120 mg/kg).
- **Recommendation:** The material is suitable for use as clean capping material over Stockpiles 1 and 2. The data will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted after capping activities.

# Follow-on and Approval Status
- **Next Steps:** Inclusion of these data in the Removal Action Completion Report following completion of Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities.
- **Approval Status:** The criteria used (barium ≤ 120 mg/kg) were pre-approved by DTSC via the referenced variance (April 9, 2020). This memo itself does not document a separate agency approval action; it presents data for the record.
