## Document identity

- **Document type and purpose:** Storm Water Sampling Report – February 1, 2024, documenting collection and analysis of storm water samples from the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles site in Modesto, California, as part of ongoing monitoring under DTSC and CVRWQCB oversight.
- **Author or preparer:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.; report signed by Rebecca L. Silva (Project Manager) and John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG (Senior Engineer).
- **Date or period covered:** Report dated May 14, 2024; sampling event conducted February 1, 2024; rainfall event began January 31, 2024 and ended February 8, 2024 (total ~3.02 inches).

## Key claims

- The storm water sampling was conducted in general accordance with the *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)* dated December 13, 2023, approved by DTSC and CVRWQCB.
- No discharge/runoff from the Caltrans right-of-way (ROW) was observed during the February 1, 2024 sampling event.
- Total dissolved solids (TDS) and sulfate concentrations in all samples were below secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for drinking water.
- Barium and zinc were detected in all storm water samples at concentrations not exceeding their respective primary or secondary MCLs.
- Copper was detected in sample SW-East at a concentration below its primary MCL.
- Strontium was detected in SW-East and SW-West; no MCL has been established for strontium.
- Laboratory QA/QC data showed acceptable surrogate recoveries, non-detect method blanks, and acceptable LCS/LCSD recoveries.
- Previous storm water sampling events (March 2023 by Stantec; December 2023 by Geocon) reported TDS, sulfate, and metal concentrations below MCLs except for thallium, which exceeded the primary MCL in December 2023 samples.

## Regulatory decisions

- **DTSC and CVRWQCB** approved the December 13, 2023 *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* (SAP) prior to the sampling event.
- **No formal enforcement action** is noted in this report; the sampling is part of ongoing compliance monitoring.

## Remedial actions

- **Stockpile encapsulation:** During Phase I of SR 99/132 expressway construction (January 2020 – October 2021), soil from Stockpile 3 was excavated and placed atop Stockpiles 1 and 2. By October 2021, most of Stockpiles 1 and 2 were completely encapsulated beneath the structural section of SR 132 and covered with clean soil and vegetation on the northern side slopes. SR 132 opened to traffic in November 2022.
- **No active remediation** is described in this report; the sampling is post-encapsulation monitoring.

## Responsible parties

- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans)** – Owner/operator; Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2; responsible for site management and future storm water sampling.
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)** – Co-regulator; approved SAP.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB)** – Co-regulator; approved SAP.
- **Geocon Consultants, Inc.** – Consultant performing sampling and reporting.
- **Stantec** – Previously conducted storm water sampling (March 2023).
- **Barium Products Ltd. / Westvaco / FMC** – Historical operators of the former barium processing facility that generated the barium-contaminated soil.

## Key terms and conditions

- **Future actions:** Caltrans plans to conduct additional storm water sampling during qualifying rain events in 2024.
- **Monitoring obligations:** Follow protocols in the approved SAP; collect samples from designated locations SW-East, SW-West, and BG-West; analyze for dissolved Title 22 metals, sulfate, and TDS.
- **Restrictions:** No runoff observed beyond Caltrans ROW; stockpiles are encapsulated and no longer require surface runoff sampling from original locations.
