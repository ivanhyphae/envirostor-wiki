## Objective and scope

- **Purpose of the work:** To provide additional characterization of soil stockpiles at the proposed State Route 132/99 West Expressway location, in order to evaluate and finalize a remedy for the management of hazardous substances in the stockpiles. The additional data are also intended to evaluate potential lateral and vertical migration of contaminants from the stockpiles and to update the Human Health Risk Assessment.
- **What prompted it:** Prior DTSC comments (dated 9/12/2012) on an earlier version of the workplan; the workplan was revised to address those comments.
- **Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed:** Modesto Soil Stockpiles, located near State Route 99 and 132 in Stanislaus County, California. The stockpiles consist of excess native soils and pond tailings generated in the early 1960s during Caltrans construction of a segment of SR 99 north of Kansas Avenue. The construction traversed a portion of a 4.3-acre parcel formerly owned by Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation Inc. (FMC), which operated an evaporation pond on the southernmost corner of the parcel. Primary chemicals of concern (COCs): barium, lead, and strontium.
- **What approval or decision the plan supports or requests:** The workplan was submitted to DTSC for concurrence; DTSC, in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board (Central Valley Region), approved the Final Workplan.
