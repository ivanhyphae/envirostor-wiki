## Document identity
- Plan type: design plans
- Preparer / author organization: Dokken Engineering, City of Modesto
- Date: December 14, 2018 (for submittal); plans approval date December 31, 2019
- Project number or task order (if stated): STA 99, 132

## Objective and scope
- Purpose of the work and what prompted it: The document provides 100% design plans for earthwork and quantity summaries related to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project. Its purpose is to detail the proposed construction activities for various roadway segments and associated earth movement.
- Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed: The plans address various lines including SR132, SD, D, K, K2, R, C, E, EAST C, SB SR99 OFF, NB SR99 AUX, and STOCKPILE 3. The primary media addressed is soil, specifically roadway excavation, embankment, and excess material.
- What approval or decision the plan supports or requests: The "100% Design Plans" are submitted for approval to proceed with the detailed design and construction aspects of the project.

## Proposed approach
