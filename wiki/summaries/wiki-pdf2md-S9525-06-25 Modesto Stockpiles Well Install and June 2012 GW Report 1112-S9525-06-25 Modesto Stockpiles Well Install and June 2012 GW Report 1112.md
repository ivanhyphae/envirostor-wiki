### Document Summary: June 2012 Groundwater Monitoring Report – Modesto Stockpiles

**Prepared by:** Geocon Consultants, Inc. for Caltrans (Project Nos. S9525-06-44/25, Contract 06A1580, Task Order 44)

This report details the installation of two new monitoring wells, MW-9 and MW-10 (installed May 29-30, 2012, and developed on June 18, 2012), and the subsequent groundwater sampling event conducted on June 20, 2012, at the Modesto Stockpiles site. The report presents the analytical results from these two new wells and a comprehensive data summary from earlier 2012 sampling rounds (March and May) for the pre-existing wells (MW-1 through MW-8). The project was conducted under the regulatory oversight of the DTSC and the Central Valley RWQCB.

**Scope and Methodology**

The monitoring focused on groundwater, with field activities including well installation and development followed by purging and sampling using submersible pumps and disposable bailers. Key field parameters measured during the June 20, 2012, sampling event for MW-9 and MW-10 included turbidity, dissolved oxygen (DO), and oxidation-reduction potential (ORP). All wells were constructed with 2-inch PVC casing and 0.010-inch slotted screens. Pre-field coordination involved obtaining a USA ticket, required permits (Stanislaus County MW12-36, City of Modesto ENC2012-40123), and a health and safety plan.

**Key Results and Exceedances**

Analytical results from the June 2012 sampling of MW-9 and MW-10 were compared against California Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs). Key findings include:

- **Exceedances from MW-9 and MW-10 (June 2012):**
    - **Nitrate (as N):** Exceeded the Primary MCL of 10 mg/L in MW-9 (13 mg/L).
    - **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS):** Exceeded the Secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in both MW-9 (510 mg/L) and MW-10 (710 mg/L).
    - **Manganese:** Exceeded the Secondary MCL of 50 µg/L in MW-10 (290 µg/L).

- **Exceedances from earlier 2012 sampling of existing wells:**
    - **Nitrate (as N):** An earlier exceedance was noted at MW-5 (27 mg/L on 3/12/2012).

- **Analytes not exceeding thresholds:**
    - **Barium:** Detected in MW-9 (67 µg/L) and MW-10 (160 µg/L), but remained below the MCL of 1,000 µg/L. A historical maximum of 410 µg/L was recorded at MW-5 in 2006.
    - **Lead:** Detected at 2.2 µg/L in MW-10, well below the action level of 15 µg/L.
    - **Polycyclic Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAHs):** None were detected above reporting limits.

**Field Conditions and Quality Assurance / Quality Control (QA/QC)**

- The new wells were developed by extracting 8.5-10 gallons of water, with high turbidity noted during the process (up to 950 NTU). This high turbidity persisted and was commented on during sampling.
- **Data Quality Issues:** The laboratory analytical reports contained several flagged QC issues. These included:
    - Matrix spike (MS) recoveries exceeding control limits for barium, chloride, nitrate, and sulfate, attributed to matrix interference from high native concentrations.
    - Duplicate RPDs (Relative Percent Difference) exceeding 20% for magnesium, potassium, antimony, and molybdenum.
    - Multiple results for dissolved/total metals were flagged “E” (estimated) due to required dilutions for concentrations above the calibration range.
    - Despite these issues, the data were deemed usable as no associated results approached regulatory thresholds.

**Data Submission**

All analytical data were uploaded to the Geotracker database in November 2012.
