### Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) Statement of Findings for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remedial Action Plan. Issued by DTSC as a Responsible Agency to document its findings regarding the adequacy of the Lead Agency’s (Caltrans) environmental document (EIR/EA).
- **Author or preparer:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
- **Date or period covered:** Document dated March 15, 2018. EIR/EA certified by Caltrans on March 2, 2018.

### Key claims
- **Site Conditions (Caltrans/DTSC):** Three stockpiles totaling approximately 160,000 cubic yards of soil contain metals, primarily barium, strontium, and lead.
- **Remedy Adequacy (DTSC):** The containment remedy (behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath roadway; unpaved portions covered with clean fill) provides long-term protection by preventing exposure and minimizing infiltration of surface water into groundwater under the stockpiles.
- **Compliance & Impact (Caltrans/DTSC):** Standard best management practices (BMPs) for air quality, noise, dust control, and biological resources are adequate to mitigate environmental impacts.
- **Regulatory Status (Caltrans/DTSC):** Handling of aerially deposited lead (ADL) in soils will be consistent with the July 1, 2016 ADL Agreement between Caltrans and DTSC.

### Regulatory decisions
- **Action:** Issuance of CEQA Statement of Findings for the Remedial Action Plan (RAP).
- **Agency:** DTSC.
- **Date:** March 15, 2018.
- **Conditions/Effect:** DTSC, as a Responsible Agency, determined the EIR/EA is adequate for the RAP CEQA analysis and adopted the findings. The decision enables DTSC to proceed with its regulatory oversight of the RAP implementation.

### Analytical results
- **Contaminants:** Metals (primarily **barium**, strontium, and lead).
- **Media:** Soil (stockpiles).
- **Concentration ranges/exceedances:** Not specified in this document.
- **Applicable thresholds:** Not specified in this document. The document references waste classification and potential to recycle soil onsite based on sampling.

### Remedial actions
- **Activity:** Containment of three soil stockpiles (total ~160,000 cubic yards).
- **Construction details:** Stockpiles contained behind **retaining walls**, **bridge abutments**, and beneath the **roadway**. Unpaved portions to have **clean fill cover**.
- **Phase status:** Remedial action plan approved; implementation pending construction.
- **Construction Oversight:** Requires a Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) submitted to DTSC and Central Valley RWQCB for review and approval prior to construction, including a Health and Safety Plan, Air Monitoring Plan, and Water Quality Monitoring Plan.
- **Verification outcomes:** Not applicable (work not yet performed). Post-remedy requirements include certification of the site by DTSC and recording a land use covenant.

### Responsible parties
- **Caltrans:** Owner/operator of the site; Lead Agency under CEQA; responsible for constructing containment features, preparing and recording the **land use covenant**, and implementing the operation and maintenance plan.
- **DTSC:** Approving agency for the RAP; CEQA Responsible Agency; regulatory oversight of remedial design, air monitoring, groundwater monitoring, and site certification.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Central Valley RWQCB):** Co-regulator; required to review and approve the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP), including water quality monitoring components; sets requirements for dewatering and discharge of non-stormwater.
- **San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District:** Permitting authority; requires a dust control plan and compliance with Regulation VIII.
- **California Department of Fish and Wildlife (CDFW):** Consultation agency for burrowing owl and Swainson's hawk protection.
- **City of Modesto:** Issuing agency for the grading permit.
- **Contractor:** Responsible for implementation, Health and Safety Plan, dust control, air monitoring, debris removal, and maintaining detailed records.

### Key terms and conditions
- **Land Use Covenant (SHAZ-8):** Caltrans must prepare and record a **land use covenant** (per 22 CCR § 67391.1) restricting land use to transportation use. This must be finalized and recorded after remedy implementation and before DTSC certifies the site.
- **Groundwater and Stormwater Monitoring (SHAZ-9):** A monitoring program must be proposed in the RDIP. Until approved, existing monitoring per 2006/2012 Sampling and Analysis Plans will continue.
- **Operation & Maintenance Plan/Agreement (SHAZ-10):** An O&M plan and agreement between Caltrans, DTSC, and RWQCB must be included in the RDIP. It will require annual inspection and evaluation of containment features (pavement, retaining walls, abutments, vegetated soil cover).
- **Dewatering Protocol (WQ-1):** Must follow Central Valley RWQCB and Stanislaus County requirements for dewatering and discharge of non-stormwater.
- **Worker & Public Protection:** Contractor must prepare a Health and Safety Plan and implement an Air Monitoring Plan for dust/PM10, with results submitted to DTSC.
