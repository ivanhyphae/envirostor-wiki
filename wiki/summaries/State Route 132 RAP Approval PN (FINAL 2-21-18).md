## Document identity
- **Form:** Approval letter (public notice of approval)
- **Sender / author organization:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)
- **Recipient(s) / addressee:** California Department of Transportation (Caltrans); also directed to the public and community stakeholders
- **Date:** February 21, 2018 (based on filename “FINAL 2-21-18”)

## Decision or request
- **What is being approved:** DTSC approves the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project.
- **Document acted on:** Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the three soil stockpiles totaling approximately 160,000 cubic yards containing metals (primarily barium, strontium, and lead) located south of Kansans Avenue in Modesto.
- **Selected cleanup alternative:** Containment of the stockpiles during construction of the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway. The stockpiles will be contained behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath the roadway; unpaved portions will have clean fill cover.
- **Conditions / contingencies:** The approval is unconditional. The notice states that land use controls, inspection of pavement and other containment features, and groundwater monitoring will be conducted annually to ensure the integrity of the remedy.
- **Effective date / deadline:** Effective upon issuance (February 21, 2018). No specific deadline for implementation is stated.

## Open items and next actions
- The correspondence does not impose any required revisions, resubmittals, or pending approvals. It implies that Caltrans will proceed with implementing the containment remedy and begin annual groundwater monitoring and inspections.
- DTSC attaches no further deliverables; the notice serves as the final agency action on the RAP.
