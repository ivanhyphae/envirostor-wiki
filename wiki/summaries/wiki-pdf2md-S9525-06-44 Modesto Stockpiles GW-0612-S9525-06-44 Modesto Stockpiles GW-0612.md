A March 2012 groundwater monitoring report for the Modesto Stockpiles site, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for Caltrans District 6, analyzed groundwater samples from multiple monitoring wells (MW-1 through MW-8). The shallow upper aquifer exhibits a southeast flow direction and contains two distinct groundwater types. No active supply wells were identified within a one-mile downgradient radius.

Analytical results from EXCELCHEM Environmental Labs (ELAP Certificate No.: 2119), Advanced Technology Laboratories, and Eurofins Calscience, Inc., revealed several key findings. Polycyclic Aromatic Hydrocarbons (PAHs) were consistently not detected above practical quantitation limits. Dissolved metals such as barium (39-340 µg/L), vanadium (18-37 µg/L), and strontium (390-1,801 µg/L) were detected across all samples but remained below drinking water quality thresholds. Chromium, mercury, and nickel were detected in some samples; however, only manganese in MW-4 exceeded the secondary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for taste and odor. Mercury was also detected in MW-1 (0.41 ug/L) and MW-8 (0.23 ug/L), as well as in the equipment blank (0.31 ug/L), with results noted as estimated and potentially overestimated due to this blank detection.

Significantly, nitrate concentrations surpassed the primary MCL of 10 mg/L in MW-1 (12 mg/L), MW-5 (27 mg/L), and MW-6 (12, 15, 18, 17.7 mg/L). Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) also exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in MW-1 (550 mg/L), MW-4 (530 ug/L), MW-5 (730 mg/L), and MW-6 (700 mg/L). Other detected parameters included various dissolved metals (e.g., calcium, sodium, potassium), anions (chloride, sulfate), and alkalinity, with detailed concentrations provided across individual wells. Notable total and dissolved metal concentrations include Barium, Calcium, Antimony, Arsenic, Lead, Vanadium, and Thallium in specific wells. Quality control data generally indicated compliance with acceptance limits, although some specific issues with surrogate recovery and method blank/matrix spike recoveries were noted.
