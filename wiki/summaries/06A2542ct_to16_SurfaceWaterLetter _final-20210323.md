## Document identity
- **Report type:** Surface water discharge inspection (no sampling performed)
- **Preparer / author organization:** Stantec Consulting Services Inc.
- **Reporting period or sampling date(s):** Inspection conducted December 17, 2020; report dated March 24, 2021
- **Project number or task order:** Contract No. 06A2542, Task Order No. 16; Stantec File: 185733000

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Surface water – no samples collected; inspection only
- **Locations inspected (planned sampling locations):** PL1, PL2, PL3, PL4, PL5 (surface runoff locations); BG1, BG2 (background locations)
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled:** All locations. Reason: No qualifying rain event – only 0.04 in on December 16 and 0.05 in on December 17, 2020 (CIMIS Modesto station); no visible discharge/runoff from Best Management Practices (BMPs) or the site. Additionally, PL1 and PL2 no longer exist due to construction; the gate valve at PL5 has been removed; the stormwater basin (now designated Basin 6B) has been enlarged.

## Results
- **Analytes measured:** None – no surface water samples were collected.
- **Applicable thresholds:** Not applicable.
- **Exceedances:** None.
- **Trends versus prior rounds:** Not reported; document notes prior sampling events (March 2006 by Shaw; April 2013–April 2019 by Geocon; December 4, 2019 and March 17, 2020 by Stantec). No comparison made because no data were generated in this event.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Weather/precipitation:** Minor rain event; 0.04 in on December 16, 0.05 in on December 17 per CIMIS Modesto station. UCANR Modesto station reported 0.0 in on December 17. At inspection time (approximately 0845 h) no rain was falling.
- **Site observations:** Ground surfaces and pavements were wet; no visible discharge/runoff from BMPs (straw waddles) or the site. Isolated puddles observed within Basin 6B.
- **Construction status:** Site under active construction for future roadways and improvements. Concrete abutments for overpass built east and west of Emerald Avenue. Former PL1 and PL2 locations no longer exist. Area near PL3 regraded and sloped; footing for perimeter wall excavated. Stormwater basin enlarged (Basin 6B); former gate valve removed. BMPs (straw waddles) installed at base of slopes and abutments.
- **Sampling deviations:** No samples collected; inspection only. No analytical data qualifiers or detection-limit issues.
- **Interpretation limitations:** Grading and construction changes make the ability to collect future samples at the currently established locations unknown.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusions (Stantec):** No discharge/runoff of surface water beyond the BMPs and/or site was observed during the December 17, 2020 inspection.
- **Recommendations (Stantec):** None explicitly stated; report serves as documentation of a no-discharge event. Future sampling ability is uncertain due to site grading changes.
