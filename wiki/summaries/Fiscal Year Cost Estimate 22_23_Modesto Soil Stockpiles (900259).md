## Document Identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Annual cost estimate for DTSC oversight of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project (Phase 1 Interim SR 132 West Project), provided pursuant to Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5.
- **Author/preparer**: Ms. Lora Jameson, P.G., Chief, Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, Site Mitigation and Restoration Program, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). Contact: Dean Wright, P.G., Project Manager.
- **Date/period covered**: Letter dated October 4, 2022; cost estimate covers the fiscal year July 1, 2022 through June 30, 2023.

## Key Claims
- DTSC is providing an estimate of its oversight costs for project activities scheduled in FY 2022‑2023.
- The estimate is based on planned tasks; actual costs may differ, and Responsible Parties/Project Proponents remain liable for all DTSC costs incurred as required by law.
- The listed tasks are anticipated for the period; no claim of completion or compliance status is made.

## Regulatory Decisions
- None in this document. (The letter is a cost estimate, not a formal regulatory decision.)

## Analytical Results
- None.

## Remedial Actions
- No physical remedial actions described. The letter references planned oversight tasks (see *Key Terms and Conditions*), which may follow prior or concurrent remedial work but are not detailed here.

- **DTSC** – regulatory oversight agency (co-regulator with Central Valley RWQCB as per corpus context; RWQCB not mentioned in this letter).

## Key Terms and Conditions
- **Planned oversight tasks for FY 2022‑2023**:
  1. Review of Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan.
  2. Fiscal Year Cost Estimate (this document).
  3. Review and approval of Interim Phase 1 Remedial Action Completion Report (RACR).
  4. Completion of the O&M Agreement.
  5. Completion of Land Use Covenant (LUC).
  6. Project management.

- **Liability**: Responsible Parties remain liable for all DTSC costs incurred, regardless of the estimate.
- **Contact for questions**: Dean Wright (DTSC Project Manager).
