## Document identity
- Plan type: variance request
- Preparer / author organization: GEOCON Consultants, Inc.
- Date: September 2, 2020
- Project number or task order: S1908-01-01

## Objective and scope
- Purpose of the work and what prompted it: This Second Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP Variance #2) is prepared to address a proposed alternative clean cover fill material by the highway contractor for capping the upper surface of consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) within Stockpiles 1 and 2 during the SR 132 West Project. The variance is prompted by the contractor's proposal to use a material not explicitly detailed in the original RDIP.
- Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed: Upper surface of consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) within Stockpiles 1 and 2.
- What approval or decision the plan supports or requests: Approval from DTSC to use a minimum of 4 inches of Class 3 aggregate subbase materials as clean cover fill on the upper surface of Stockpiles 1 and 2, instead of the 12-inch thick clean fill or 4-inch thick aggregate base materials specified in the original RDIP.

## Proposed approach
- Decision criteria or action levels proposed (clean fill thresholds, comparison standards): The proposed Class 3 aggregate subbase materials are evaluated against Caltrans' gradation summaries and aggregate quality characteristics to demonstrate equivalency to aggregate base materials for the purpose of clean cover.
    - Aggregate Gradation for Class 3 aggregate subbase: Sieve size 3" (100% passing), 2 1/2" (90-100% passing), No. 4 (50-100% passing), No. 200 (0-30% passing).
    - Aggregate Quality Characteristics for Class 3 aggregate subbase: Sand equivalent (min) 21, Resistance (R-value, min) 40.
- For a variance:
    - The requirement at issue: Section 3.2.2 of the RDIP specifies "Placement of minimum 12-inch-thick clean cover fill material over the barium-containing soil and ADL consolidation zones. As an alternative, minimum 4-inch-thick aggregate base (AB) materials may be placed on the upper stockpile surface pending subsequent placement of structural pavement and hardscape."
    - The deviation requested: The use of a minimum of 4 inches of **Class 3 aggregate subbase materials** as clean cover fill on the upper surface of Stockpiles 1 and 2.
    - Rationale: The Class 3 aggregate subbase materials, based on gradation and quality characteristics, are considered equivalent to aggregate base for interim clean cover purposes until the full pavement section or hardscape is placed, thereby meeting the intent of the RDIP. The specified structural pavement section for the Interim Phase I of the SR 132 West Project includes 0.85 feet (10.2 inches) concrete pavement, 0.25 feet (3.0 inches) hot mix asphalt, and 0.70 feet (8.4 inches) Class 3 aggregate subbase.

## Deliverables and approvals sought
The plan commits to using Caltrans' Class 3 aggregate subbase materials meeting specified gradation and quality characteristics. Approval is sought from the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for this deviation.
