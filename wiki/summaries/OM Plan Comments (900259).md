## Decision or request
- **What is being commented on:** The Draft Operation and Maintenance Plan (Draft O&M Plan), March 2022, prepared for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project, Stanislaus County, California (Site Code 900259).
- **Specific deliverable acted on:** Draft O&M Plan (to become Appendix G of the Remedial Action Completion Report), and the accompanying blank O&M Site Inspection Form.
- **Decision:** Conditional — DTSC provides six comments requiring revision before the document is accepted.
- **Required revisions:**
  1. Correct misspelling of "Maintenance" in the subject line.
  2. Section 2.1: Add a minimum 10 working days advance notification to DTSC before any BCS disturbance.
  3. Section 2.4: Add text stating that Health and Safety Code (HSC) 25123.3(b)(4)(A) requirements will be met; correct misspelling of "millimeter."
  4. Section 3.1: Add that inspections will also occur after fires, substantial rainfall (≥1 inch in 24 hours), significant seismic events, and traffic accidents that may damage the cap.
  5. Section 2.7: Periodic patrols should note unauthorized access (e.g., homeless encampments) impacting the cap, especially on the north vegetated slope.
  6. O&M Site Inspection Form: Expand line items under each stockpile with subheadings listing specific elements (e.g., MSE wall bulging, pavement cracks, north slope vegetation/erosion, storm drain conditions).
- **Effective date / deadline:** Not explicitly stated; revisions are to be incorporated and the revised O&M Plan submitted as Appendix G of the Remedial Action Completion Report.

## Open items and next actions
- **Geocon** must incorporate all recommended changes and submit the revised O&M Plan as Appendix G of the Remedial Action Completion Report.
- **DTSC** reviewed in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB); no further action from DTSC noted until resubmittal.
