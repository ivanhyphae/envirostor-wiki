## Document identity
- Plan type: Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)

- Date: December 13, 2023
- Project number or task order: Project No. S2350-01-02, Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2

## Objective and scope
The purpose of the proposed storm water sampling is to evaluate the integrity of the soil stockpile containment system, which was approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and incorporated into the design and construction of the SR 132 West Expressway project. The containment system isolated and encapsulated barium and lead-contaminated soil behind retaining walls and bridge abutments and beneath roadway pavements and clean soil (Stockpiles 1 and 2, which were encapsulated beneath SR 132 between SR 99 and Carpenter Avenue). Analysis of storm water samples will help gauge the effectiveness of the containment system and determine if storm water has been impacted by chemicals of potential concern from the encapsulated stockpiles.

The plan supports ongoing monitoring and evaluation of the containment system's performance.

## Proposed approach
- Sampling design: Up to two samples of storm water runoff will be collected from the drainage system capturing runoff from the SR 132 segment where the containment system is located.
    - Locations:
        - SW-East: storm water discharge point on the north side of SR 132, west of SR 99.
        - SW-West: along the north side of SR 132 at a discharge pipe adjacent and east of Ponding Basin 5.
    - Background samples: At least one background storm water sample will be collected during each sampling event.
        - BG-West: along the east side of North Carpenter Road approximately 700 feet south of the North Carpenter Road and Kansas Avenue intersection.
        - BG-East: near the northeast corner of the intersection of North Emerald Avenue and Loletta Avenue.
    - If runoff is observed leaving the Caltrans ROW during sampling events, additional samples may be collected at those locations.
- Sampling methods: Samples will be collected from impoundments or discharge points using a pre-cleaned or disposable scoop and transferred to laboratory-provided containers. Samples for dissolved metals will be field-filtered through a 0.45-micron filter. Samples will be capped, labeled, chilled, and transported using chain-of-custody procedures. In-situ parameters (pH, electrical conductivity, temperature, turbidity) will be monitored.
- Target analytes:
    - Title 22 dissolved metals (including barium and strontium) by United States Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Test Method 6020/7470.
    - Sulfate by EPA Test Method 9056/300.0.
    - Total dissolved solids (TDS) by EPA Method 160.1.
- Sequence, schedule, or phasing of the work: Samples will be collected during qualifying rain events (visible runoff and 72 hours of prior dry weather). If necessary, samples will be collected during up to four separate rainfall events per year.
- QA/QC: Field QA/QC will include using pre-cleaned or disposable sampling scoops and chain-of-custody documentation. Laboratory QA/QC will include one method blank, one duplicate, and one spiked sample for every ten samples, batch of samples, or type of matrix, whichever is more frequent.

## Deliverables and approvals sought
The plan commits to producing:
- A summary report after each sampling event, detailing field observations, laboratory data, and data evaluation, including:
    - Background summary
    - Scope of services performed
    - Observations during field activities
    - Results of laboratory analysis
    - Conclusions and recommendations
    - Site Location Maps and Site Plans indicating sampling locations
    - Tabular summaries of analytical data
    - Photographs depicting site conditions and sampling locations
    - Appendices including laboratory reports and chain-of-custody documentation
The draft report will be submitted to Caltrans for comments, and a final report will be provided after incorporating comments.

## Responsible parties
- California Department of Transportation (Caltrans): Owner/operator, client, will review draft reports.
- Geocon Consultants, Inc.: Preparer of the SAP, will perform field activities (sampling), manage laboratory analysis, and prepare reports.
- Asset Laboratories (Asset): California-certified laboratory for analytical services.
