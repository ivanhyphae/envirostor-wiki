## Thread / participants
1. **November 2, 2020** – Richard Stewart (Caltrans) to Dean Wright, Perry Myers, Steven Meeks: Submits Technical Memo (Tech Memo) on characterization of Bent 2 excavation soil; notes soil results exceed background criteria for use as clean cap cover material.
2. **November 6, 2020** – Dean Wright (DTSC) to Richard Stewart, Perry Myers, Steven Meeks: Conveys DTSC and Water Board acceptance of the Tech Memo and revised SWPPP Amendment, with conditions on placement of Bent 2 soil.

## Decision or request
- **What is being accepted/approved:** DTSC and Water Board officially accept both the Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020 (Characterization of Excavated Bent 2 Soils for use as Clean Cap Material) and the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020.
- **Condition attached to acceptance:** Soil sample results do not meet the clean cap criteria established in the April 9, 2020 Variance Request. However, both agencies agree this soil may be placed on top of Stockpile 2 at a thickness of 6 inches or less, provided it is covered with clean base material and the asphalt/concrete used to construct the roadway.  
- **Emphasis on SWPPP compliance:** Because Bent 2 soils may be temporarily exposed during the winter, the rainy season surface water sampling effort and erosion control measures described in the Revised SWPPP Amendment must be followed strictly to ensure contaminants remain on site.

## Open items and next actions
- **Implementation by Caltrans:** Adhere to surface water sampling and erosion control protocols in the Revised SWPPP Amendment during the winter/rainy season, as the soil may be exposed before final capping.
- No stated deadlines for resubmittal or further agency review are identified in this correspondence.
