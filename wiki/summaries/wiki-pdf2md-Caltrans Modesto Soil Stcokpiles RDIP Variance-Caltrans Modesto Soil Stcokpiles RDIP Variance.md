## Document identity
- Plan type: Variance request
- Preparer / author organization: GEOCON CONSULTANTS, INC.
- Date: April 9, 2020
- Project number or task order: Project No. S1908-01-01

## Objective and scope
- Purpose of the work and what prompted it: This Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP Variance) was prepared to address the identified sources of clean cover fill materials to properly cap consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) within Stockpiles 1 and 2 during the SR 132 West Project. It was prepared in response to a request from the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
- Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed: Stockpiles 1 and 2, located south of the State Route (SR) 99/Kansas Avenue interchange in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California, specifically regarding clean cover fill material.
- What approval or decision the plan supports or requests: The request seeks DTSC approval to utilize the approved Carpenter Road borrow source area and any DTSC-approved wall footing excavation material as clean cover material on Stockpiles 1 and 2.

## Proposed approach
- Sampling design: Soil samples will be collected from the MSE wall footing excavation along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2 once the southern slope BCS material is excavated. These samples will be analyzed for barium and lead.
- Decision criteria or action levels proposed: For the MSE wall footing material, the samples will be compared to site-specific naturally occurring background concentrations to determine suitability for use as clean cover fill material. Based on previous analysis for Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material, barium concentrations ranging from 42 to 140 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) with a calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) of 113.1 mg/kg were within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background barium concentrations (17 to 120 mg/kg), indicating suitability in that zone. The upper foot of planned wall footing excavation soil in Stockpile 1 was found to contain elevated barium concentrations not suitable for clean cover fill.
- For a variance: The variance addresses Section 4.7 of the original RDIP which states, "The surface of the completed Stockpile 1 and 2 barium-containing soil and ADL consolidation zones (including east- and west-facing temporary slopes) will be capped with minimum 12-inch-thick clean fill material consisting of approved imported fill and/or minimum 4-inch-thick AB materials (pending placement of structural pavement section, hardscape and/or additional clean embankment fill). The proposed source of clean imported fill soil consists of currently vacant and agricultural land within planned excavation areas along the SR-132 ROW between the Carpenter Road and Rosemore Avenue overcrossings as depicted on Figure 1. The specific clean fill source areas (once defined by the highway construction contractor) will be sampled by Geocon for laboratory analysis in general accordance with DTSC's *Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Material* (Appendix E) for pre-approval by DTSC prior to placement."
The deviation requests approval for two specific sources of clean cover fill for Stockpiles 1 and 2:
    1. An area adjacent to and east of Carpenter Road within the SR 132 right-of-way, which has already been evaluated by Geocon and DTSC and determined to generate suitable material.
    2. Material from proposed mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations (approximately five-foot-deep) located along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2, provided that sampled material meets site-specific background concentrations for barium and lead. For Stockpile 1, soil from 1 to 5 feet below existing grade in the MSE wall footing excavation was deemed suitable. The upper foot of soil was not suitable. An evaluation of the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing material is pending excavation of the southern slope BCS material.
The rationale is to utilize additional, contractor-identified sources of clean fill material to ensure proper capping of the consolidated BCS.

## Deliverables and approvals sought
The plan commits to producing analytical results for barium and lead from the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation. Approval is sought from DTSC for the proposed use of the Carpenter Road borrow source area and DTSC-approved wall footing excavation material as clean cover material on Stockpiles 1 and 2.
