## Document identity
- **Plan type:** Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan (includes Sampling and Analysis Plan)
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Date:** September 18, 2012
- **Project number or task order:** Project No. S9525-06-44, Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44, EA No. 10-403500

## Objective and scope
- **Purpose of the work and what prompted it:** Provide additional stockpile and native soil characterization data to address three data gaps identified during a July 26, 2012, meeting between Caltrans, DTSC, and RWQCB: (1) perimeter ROW fenceline sampling to address potential offsite and vertical migration of contaminants; (2) perimeter stockpile sampling to define lateral stockpile limits for consolidation during construction; (3) additional sampling in areas of elevated cadmium in Stockpiles 2 and 3 identified during the 2004 PSI. Data will be used to re-evaluate adjacent resident exposure and potential vertical migration of contaminants, and incorporated into the Remedial Action Plan (RAP).
- **Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed:** Stockpiles 1, 2, and 3 (approximately 121,000 cubic yards of fill embankment) within Caltrans ROW west and east of SR 99, south of Kansas Avenue, Stanislaus County, CA. Media: soil (stockpile fill and native soil). Perimeter/fenceline locations adjacent to residential and commercial/industrial development; cadmium-impacted areas in Stockpiles 2 and 3.
- **What approval or decision the plan supports or requests:** Supports regulatory approval (DTSC and RWQCB) of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the SR132 Project, which will incorporate the stockpile soil as raised roadway fill embankments and bridge abutments.

## Proposed approach
- **Sampling design: locations/counts, target analytes, methods, depths:**
  - **29 Fenceline Borings (5-foot depth):** Stockpile perimeter/fenceline locations adjacent to residential and commercial/industrial development. Depth determined in discussions with RWQCB to allow evaluation of vertical migration. Continuous soil samples collected in 3-foot acetate sleeves from surface (0-6 inches) and at 1-foot intervals to 5 feet. **Analytes:** Title 22 metals (CAM 17) and strontium via EPA Method 6010B; mercury via EPA Method 7471.
  - **22 Perimeter Borings (3-foot depth):** Perimeter and stockpile end locations. Depth to evaluate whether stockpile material is present. **Analytes:** Barium only via EPA Method 6010B (surface samples only).
  - **5 Cadmium Borings (20-foot depth):** Within Stockpiles 2 and 3, in vicinity of elevated cadmium detections from 2004 PSI. Depth based on maximum stockpile fill thickness (~25 ft) and reported maximum depth of elevated cadmium. Samples from 0-6 inches and approximate 5-foot intervals (4-5, 9-10, 14-15, 19-20 ft). **Analytes:** Barium and cadmium via EPA Method 6010B.
  - **Holding and additional analysis:** Remaining soil samples held pending initial results; additional analysis for total and/or soluble metals (using deionized water extractant) assigned after review by Caltrans, DTSC, and RWQCB.
  - **Boring method:** Geoprobe direct-push rig; hand-auger in inaccessible areas. Borings logged by Geocon geologist under direction of California Professional Geologist using USCS. GPS coordinates collected.
  - **Decontamination:** Non-disposable equipment decontaminated prior to use by wash-and-double-rinse with non-phosphate detergent (Alconox®), deionized/distilled water, and tapwater. Decontamination water contained, sampled, analyzed for metals, and disposed of appropriately.
  - **Backfill:** 20-ft borings backfilled with bentonite slurry; 3-ft, 5-ft, and hand-auger borings backfilled with excess soil cuttings.
  - **QA/QC:** One method blank per 10 samples; one duplicate per 10 samples; one spiked sample per 10 samples (spike at 10× detection limit or analyte level). Laboratory to homogenize soil samples for metals per Contract 06A1580. J-flags for estimated values between MDL and PQL. Laboratory reports to include MDLs and PQLs.
  - **Laboratory:** Advanced Technologies Laboratories (ATL), California Department of Health Services-certified, under expedited five-day turnaround.

- **Decision criteria or action levels proposed:** Not explicitly stated in the workplan; data will be used to re-evaluate adjacent resident exposure and vertical migration, and incorporated into the RAP. No quantitative clean fill thresholds or comparison standards are proposed in this document.

- **Sequence, schedule, or phasing of the work:**
  - Pre-field activities (completed): Initial site visit (August 2, 2012); follow-up site visit to stake boring locations (September 13, 2012); site survey with DTSC, Caltrans, and Geocon to review final locations (September 17, 2012).
  - Field activities: Not explicitly scheduled but proposed to commence after workplan approval.
  - Laboratory analysis: Expedited five-day turnaround.
  - Reporting: Draft SSI Report to Caltrans for review; then draft final to DTSC and RWQCB; finalized report uploaded to EnviroStor and Caltrans project websites.
