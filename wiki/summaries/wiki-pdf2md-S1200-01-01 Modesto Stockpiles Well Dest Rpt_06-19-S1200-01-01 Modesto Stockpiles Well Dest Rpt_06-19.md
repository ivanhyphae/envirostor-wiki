Geocon prepared a Groundwater Monitoring Well Destruction Report in June 2019 for Caltrans, detailing the destruction of six groundwater monitoring wells (MW1, MW2, MW3, MW5, MW7, and MW8) at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project in Stanislaus County between April 29-30, 2019. The work, performed by Gregg Drilling under Geocon's supervision, followed California Well Standards Bulletin 74-90 and City of Modesto permit requirements. Pre-field activities included obtaining City of Modesto Encroachment Permit ENC2019-50174, marking well locations, and providing necessary notifications to utility services.

The destruction process involved pressure grouting each well with 13-20 gallons of neat cement using a tremie pipe at 20-25 psi for 5 minutes, followed by the removal and backfilling of the top 5 feet of casing. All well box materials and concrete were disposed of on-site. Well Completion Reports were submitted to the Department of Water Resources and Stanislaus County. The City of Modesto permit stipulated various conditions, including scheduling inspections, adherence to city specifications, utility notifications, detailed well construction reports with GPS coordinates, and the installation of a backflow prevention device. Traffic control measures, peak hour work restrictions, and compliance with the Manual of Uniform Traffic Control Devices were also mandated. Additionally, for projects exceeding one acre, a copy of the Notice of Intent and a Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan were required on-site. Rebecca L. Silva of Geocon affirmed that the services were performed to local standards, noting that while six wells were abandoned, four remain in place.
