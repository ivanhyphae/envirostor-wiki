## Combined Summary: Groundwater Monitoring Report, Modesto Soil Stockpiles (September 2012)

**Document Identity and Scope:** This document is a groundwater monitoring round report for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (Project S9525-06-44A, Task Order 44), prepared by GEOCON Consultants, Inc. for Caltrans (the project owner). Field sampling was conducted on September 20, 2012, by Josh Ewert (Rancho Cordova office). The monitoring scope covered ten groundwater wells (MW-1 through MW-10), located along SR 132 under the regulatory oversight of the DTSC and the Central Valley RWQCB.

**Analytical Results and Groundwater Characterization:**
- **Groundwater Types:** Using Stiff diagrams, seven wells (MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-5, MW-7, MW-9, MW-10) are classified as calcium-dominant, while three wells (MW-3, MW-6, MW-8) are sodium-dominant.
- **Analytes Measured:** The analyses included dissolved CAM 17 metals (field-filtered) plus strontium, sulfide, sulfate, nitrate, PAHs (SIM), alkalinity, chloride, calcium, magnesium, sodium, potassium, and TDS.

**Exceedances and Data Qualifiers:**
- **No exceedances** of applicable thresholds were explicitly identified in the provided excerpts.
- **Data Qualifiers:** Several data qualifiers were applied (e.g., 'M2' for matrix interference, 'D6' for dilution), indicating laboratory or sample-specific issues flagged by Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL).

**Field Conditions and QA/QC:**
- **Sampling Preservation:** Samples were preserved with ice and field-filtered for dissolved metals.
- **Field QC:** One field duplicate and one blank were reported.
- **Chain of Custody (COC):** The COC form, attached to the report, includes detailed codes for sample preservation, container types, and turnaround times. Fields to check sample condition (chilled, headspace, container intact) were present but not marked as checked. No field notes are provided regarding wells going dry or turbidity issues.

**Conclusions and Recommendations:** No explicit conclusions or recommendations were stated in the provided excerpts, although report delivery and GeoTracker submittal were documented.

**Responsible Parties:** Key parties include Caltrans (owner/client), GEOCON Consultants, Inc. (author/preparer), DTSC and Central Valley RWQCB (co-regulators), and Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL, analytical lab).
