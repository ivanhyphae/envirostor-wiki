## Thread / participants
- Richard C. Stewart (Caltrans) to Dean Wright (DTSC), Perry Myers (DTSC), Steven Meeks (CVRWQCB) on June 18, 2020: Transmitted a revised technical memo regarding Stockpile 2 wall footing soil, noting a correction regarding barium value and reiterating conclusions about soil disposition.
- Dean Wright (DTSC) to Richard C. Stewart (Caltrans) on June 22, 2020: Confirmed DTSC and Water Board acceptance of the Technical Memorandum for Stockpile 2 sampling and agreed with its conclusions.

## Decision or request
- What is being approved, accepted, conditionally approved, denied, requested, or commented on: Acceptance of a Technical Memorandum regarding Stockpile 2 sampling and soil disposition.
- The specific document or deliverable it acts on: "Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated June 18, 2020 describing the Stockpile 2 sampling along the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area and the laboratory results of their analysis."
- Conditions, required revisions, or contingencies attached: None stated for the acceptance. Both agencies agree with the memo's determination that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones, and soil below that depth is suitable as embankment fill or capping material.
- Effective date or deadline, if any: The email dated June 22, 2020 serves as DTSC's official acceptance.

## Open items and next actions
- The material below 3 feet bgs along the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing is considered part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, therefore another VR is not necessary before utilizing this soil.
