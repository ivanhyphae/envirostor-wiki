## Decision or request
- **What is being approved**: The *Technical Memorandum for Stockpile 2 Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan* (Tech Memo), dated February 28, 2022.
- **Document acted on**: Technical Memorandum for Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan (Version: February 28, 2022)
- **Substance**: DTSC concurs with the stockpiling and sampling procedures described in the Tech Memo for spoils generated from installation of the Weather Station and Overhead Sign #1 on Stockpile 2 at the State Route 132 West project.
- **Conditions**:
    - If sampling results indicate barium and lead concentrations are **below** established site-specific background, the material may be reused on-site at locations mutually agreed upon by DTSC and Caltrans.
    - If sampling results show concentrations **above** background, the material must be profiled for disposal at the Forward Landfill in Manteca or another acceptable landfill facility.
