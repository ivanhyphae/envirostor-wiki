## Decision or request
- **DTSC acceptance** of the **Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated February 27, 2020**, titled “Clean Cap Borrow Area Tech Memo” (describing backfill area characterization for soil to be used as cap material for SR 132 stockpiles).

- No conditions, required revisions, or contingencies are stated.
- Effective date of acceptance: March 4, 2020 (date of the DTSC email).
