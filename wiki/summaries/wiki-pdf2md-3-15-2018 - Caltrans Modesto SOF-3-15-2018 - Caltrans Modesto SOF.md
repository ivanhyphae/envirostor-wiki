## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: Statement of Findings (SOF) under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA), for the DTSC's approval of the Remedial Action Plan for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project.
- Author or preparer: Randy Adams (Project Manager) and Steven R. Becker (Unit Chief)
- Date or period covered: March 15, 2018

## Key claims
- **DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control)**: asserts that the proposed project will not result in significant and unavoidable effects to the environment.
- **Caltrans (California Department of Transportation)**: certified the Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) on March 2, 2018, asserting that it adequately analyzed project impacts and that the proposed transportation land use is compatible and acceptable with health risk.

## Regulatory decisions
- Decision type: Approval of a Remedial Action Plan (RAP)
- Agency: Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)
- Date: March 15, 2018
- Conditions imposed: The DTSC, acting as a CEQA Responsible Agency, utilizes Caltrans' EIR/EA for the RAP CEQA analysis and adopts the mitigation measures identified in the Lead Agency Final Environmental Document.

## Remedial actions
- Activity: Containment of three soil stockpiles totaling approximately 160,000 cubic yards. The containment will be achieved using retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath roadways. Unpaved portions will receive a clean fill cover.
- Phase status: The DTSC has approved the Remedial Action Plan for the project. Implementation of the containment remedy and subsequent monitoring is planned.
- Verification outcomes: Long-term protection of people and the environment is expected by preventing exposure and minimizing surface water infiltration into groundwater. Annual inspections of pavement and other containment features, along with groundwater monitoring, will ensure remedy integrity.

## Responsible parties
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)**: Project Sponsor; responsible for approving the Remedial Action Plan, providing oversight, and ensuring compliance with CEQA as a Responsible Agency.
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans)**: Owner/Operator; Lead Agency under CEQA; prepared the Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) and Remedial Action Plan (RAP); responsible for implementing construction and remedial activities, including subsequent monitoring and maintenance; will prepare and record a land use covenant.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB)**: Co-regulator; will review and approve the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, groundwater and stormwater quality monitoring program, and operation and maintenance plan.
- **Contractor**: Responsible for implementing various best management practices (BMPs) and mitigation measures, preparing detailed plans (e.g., dust control plan, Health and Safety Plan, Spill Prevention Control and Countermeasures Plan, Lead Compliance Plan, Excavation and Transportation Plan), conducting air quality monitoring, and ensuring proper handling and disposal of materials.
- **San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District**: Approves dust control plans and ensures compliance with Regulation VIII and other applicable standards.
- **California Department of Fish and Wildlife**: Provides consultation for burrowing owl surveys, determines avoidance and minimization measures for biological resources, and coordinates minimization efforts for active Swainson's hawk nests.
- **City of Modesto**: Issues grading permits.
- **Underground Service Alert**: Notified before subsurface excavation.

## Key terms and conditions
- **Land Use Controls**: Caltrans will prepare and record a land use covenant (pursuant to California Code of Regulations, Title 22, section 67391.1) to restrict land uses, identifying the proposed transportation land use as compatible and acceptable regarding health risk. This will be finalized and recorded after remediation and site certification by DTSC.
- **Groundwater and Stormwater Monitoring**: A program for continued monitoring will be proposed in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, submitted to DTSC and Central Valley RWQCB. Until approval, monitoring will continue under existing 2006 and 2012 Sampling and Analysis Plans.
- **Operation and Maintenance (O&M)**: An O&M plan and agreement between Caltrans, DTSC, and Central Valley RWQCB will be included in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan to evaluate the functionality of containment features (pavement, retaining walls, abutments, vegetated soil cover) and ensure their integrity.
- **Air Quality Monitoring**: Perimeter air quality monitoring during earthmoving or construction, as per an Air Monitoring Plan in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan. Monitoring frequency may decrease with DTSC approval if effective dust control is demonstrated.
- **Contractor Submittals/Notifications**:
    - Submit a dust control plan to the San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District at least 30 days prior to earthmoving.
    - Prepare and submit a site-specific Health and Safety Plan (HAZ-6) and a Sampling and Analysis Plan (HAZ-7) prior to construction.
    - Prepare a Spill Prevention Control and Countermeasures Plan (HAZ-8) prior to construction.
    - Prepare a Lead Compliance Plan and an Excavation and Transportation Plan (HAZ-14) for materials containing aerially deposited lead.
    - Notify utility companies and Underground Service Alert before subsurface excavation (HAZ-11).
    - Notify and pay fees to the San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District for demolition/renovation activities (HAZ-12).
    - Secure a grading permit from the City of Modesto prior to earthmoving (SHAZ-1).
    - Submit requests to DTSC for approval prior to modifying procedures for soil excavation, relocation, dust control, air monitoring, or other field activities (SHAZ-6).
- **Construction BMPs**: Implementation of Caltrans Standard Specifications Sections 14-9.02 (Air Pollution Control), 14-9.03 (Dust Control), 17 (water application), and 18 (dust palliative). Minimizing idling time for diesel equipment, using alternative-powered equipment, and using add-on mitigation devices for exhaust emissions. Sound control devices on construction equipment, no un-muffled exhaust, and additional noise control measures where feasible.
- **Biological Resources Mitigation**:
    - AS-1: Burrowing owl surveys per California Department of Fish and Wildlife (CDFW) 2012 guidelines, with potential avoidance, minimization measures (e.g., no-work buffer, passive relocation), and off-site mitigation.
    - AS-2: Shrub and tree trimming/removal outside nesting season (Feb 1 - Aug 31) or with preconstruction surveys if during season.
    - AS-3: General nesting bird surveys prior to construction.
    - AS-4: Establishment of no-work buffers (100 ft for migratory birds, 300 ft for raptors) around active nests, determined by a wildlife biologist in consultation with CDFW.
    - TES-1: Protocol-level Swainson's hawk surveys within a 0.5-mile radius, following 2000 Technical Advisory Committee methodology, with coordination with CDFW for minimization efforts (e.g., 600 ft no-work buffer).
    - IS-1: Use of weed-free erosion control applications (straw, hydro-seed mulch) and native species in revegetation seed mixes.
    - IS-2: Inspection and cleaning of off-road construction equipment to prevent noxious weed introduction.
- **Hazardous Materials Management**:
    - Handling wooden utility poles and associated soils as treated wood waste (HAZ-10).
    - Adherence to Caltrans' Unknown Hazards Procedures for Construction (HAZ-13).
    - Management or disposal of excavated soil exceeding hazardous waste criteria as California hazardous waste, or stockpiling and resampling for recycling (HAZ-14). Adherence to the July 1, 2016, Aerially Deposited Lead Agreement.
    - Disposal of debris from stockpiles according to regulations (SHAZ-2).
- **Hydrology/Water Quality**: Compliance with Central Valley RWQCB and Stanislaus County requirements for dewatering and non-stormwater discharge (WQ-1). Preparation and implementation of construction site best management practices per Caltrans Stormwater Management Plan and NPDES Permit (Order No. 99-06-DWQ NPDES No. CAS000003) (SHAZ-1, WQ-2).
