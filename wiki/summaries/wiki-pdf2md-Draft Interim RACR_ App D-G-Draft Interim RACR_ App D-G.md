Geocon Consultants' series of Technical Memoranda and analytical reports from February 2020 to April 2021 document soil characterization and air monitoring efforts for the SR132 CAP and Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project, adhering to California DTSC guidelines.

Soil characterization included two clean cap borrow areas: the Carpenter Road site and Basin 5. Both locations were sampled for Title 22 metals and organochlorine pesticides, consistently showing non-detects for pesticides and mercury. Barium levels in the Carpenter Road borrow area ranged from 60-110 mg/kg, while Basin 5 showed barium at 66-94 mg/kg, all within expected background ranges and meeting clean cap criteria. Similarly, a 2021 memorandum confirmed that approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil from the Carpenter Road Shoofly, with barium (28-96 mg/kg) and lead (1.4-3.8 mg/kg) concentrations below site-specific background values, also met clean cap specifications for Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2.

In contrast, approximately 4,000 cubic yards of excavated soil from the Bent 2 area, intended for stockpiling, did not fully qualify as "clean cap" material. Barium concentrations (31-230 mg/kg) exceeded the site-specific background of 120 mg/kg in four out of ten samples, and lead (2.6-13 mg/kg) was also elevated above the 3.8 mg/kg background in four samples.

Concurrently, extensive air monitoring was conducted during BCS grading operations, focusing on total dust, barium, and lead around three BCS stockpiles. Analytical reports from various labs confirmed that levels of airborne contaminants largely remained below designated project action levels. Most barium and lead results were below reporting limits, although some perimeter and personal samples occasionally detected total dust (up to 0.43 mg/m³) and isolated instances of barium (e.g., 4.8 µg/m³ at PRM-2AO, 1.2 µg/m³ at PRM-3AR, and 1.7 µg/m³ at PRM-4BE). These findings support the effectiveness of dust control measures and the suitability of the Carpenter Road and Basin 5 sources for clean cap material, while highlighting the unsuitability of bent 2 excavation spoils for such use. All data will be integrated into the final Removal Action Completion Report.
