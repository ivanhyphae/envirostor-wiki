## Document Identity
- **Report type:** Surface water sampling event (stormwater monitoring round)
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Sampling date:** December 15, 2016
- **Project number:** S1200-01-01, Task Order No. 1, Contract No. 06A2184, EA No. 10-0X2700

## Monitoring Scope
- **Media sampled:** Surface water (stormwater runoff)
- **Locations sampled:** PL1, PL2, PL3, PL4, BG1, BG2
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled:** PL5 (dry – gate valve within drainage basin adjacent to Stockpile 3; no water present)

## Results
### Dissolved Metals (EPA 6020/7470A)
- **Barium:** 16–2,000 µg/L range (historical); December 2016 results not explicitly tabulated in narrative but reported as detected in PL1–PL4 and BG1–BG2. No barium exceedance of primary MCL (1,000 µg/L) noted.
- **Manganese:** Exceeded secondary MCL (50 µg/L) in:
  - PL3 – concentration exceeding 50 µg/L
  - PL4 – concentration exceeding 50 µg/L
  - BG1 – concentration exceeding 50 µg/L
- **Antimony, arsenic, cobalt, lead, zinc, etc.:** Detected at various locations but below respective primary/secondary MCLs.
- **Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, thallium:** Not detected above method detection limits (MDLs) in any sample.

### General Minerals (EPA 300.0, SM 2320B, SM 2540C/D, SM 4500-S-D)
- No exceedances of primary or secondary MCLs for chloride, nitrate, sulfate, sulfide, total alkalinity, total dissolved solids (TDS), or total suspended solids (TSS).
- Results reported as within the same general range as previous sampling events at each location.

### Trends vs. Prior Rounds
- Analytical results for all detected constituents were stated to be within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported for each sample location.

## Field Conditions and QA/QC
- **Weather:** Rain event on December 15, 2016; 0.45 inch precipitation.
- **Dry location:** PL5 (within 3 ft of gate valve) was dry; not sampled.
- **Instrument malfunction:** Dissolved oxygen (DO) meter not functioning; DO not measured at any location.
- **Filtration:** Samples for dissolved metals filtered through 0.45‑micron filters in the field.
- **Laboratory QA/QC:** All samples (PL1–PL4, BG1, BG2) required dilution due to high target analyte concentrations:
  - Diluted for calcium and sodium: all samples
  - Diluted for magnesium: PL1, PL3, BG1, BG2
  - Diluted for barium: PL3
  - Diluted for potassium: BG1
- **Chain of custody:** Maintained; laboratory report (Advanced Technology Laboratories, ATL) provided.

## Conclusions and Recommendations
(Attributed to Geocon Consultants, Inc.)
- No stormwater runoff observed migrating off the Caltrans right-of-way during the December 15, 2016 event.
- Surface water management measures (vegetative/fiber cover, straw wattles, interceptor berm) appeared to function as designed.
- Caltrans plans to conduct additional surface water sampling during qualifying rain events in 2017.
