## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Field observations:** No standing water was observed at any of the designated sample locations (BG1, BG3, PL6, PL7). No active runoff or discharge was observed at the stormwater pipes at PL6 or PL7.
- **Precipitation:** 0.09 inches of rain was measured on February 28, 2023. The rain event occurred overnight and had largely stopped by the time of the morning inspection.
- **Site conditions:** Minor soil erosion was noted on the eastern side of stormwater basin 5 and on the northern slopes of Stockpile 1. Some hydroseeded areas on the northern side of Stockpile 2 lacked vegetation. Trash and debris were observed blocking the end of the discharge pipe at PL7. Straw waddles and jute mesh were in place as Best Management Practices (BMPs).
- **Sampling or analytical deviations:** No samples were collected because the qualifying rain event (defined as visible runoff following 72 hours of dry weather) did not produce standing water or runoff. This is a condition of the site, not a deviation from protocol.
- **Anything affecting interpretation of the results:** Not applicable, as no data were generated.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusion:** No discharge or runoff of surface water beyond the BMPs or the site boundary was observed.
- **Recommendations (by Stantec):** None stated explicitly. The report documents the inspection and finding of no surface water to sample.
