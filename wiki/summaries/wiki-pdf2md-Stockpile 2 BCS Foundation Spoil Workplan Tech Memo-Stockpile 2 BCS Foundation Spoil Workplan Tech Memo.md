## Objective and scope
- Purpose of the work and what prompted it: To address proper management and disposal/reuse of barium-containing soil (BCS) spoils generated from foundation excavations within the southern portion of the Stockpile 2 BCS Containment Zone. This includes spoils from a completed Weather Station Foundation Slab/Anchor excavation and a planned Overhead Sign #1 foundation excavation.
- Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed: Barium-containing soil (BCS) from foundation excavations within the southern portion of the Stockpile 2 BCS Containment Zone, specifically associated with the Weather Station Foundation Slab/Anchor (approximate Station 1138+60) and Overhead Sign #1 (approximate Station 1139+30).
- What approval or decision the plan supports or requests: Supports decisions regarding offsite landfill disposal or onsite reuse of the BCS material, dependent on Caltrans and DTSC approval for onsite reuse.

## Proposed approach
- Sampling design:
    - Locations/counts: Eight discrete soil samples will be collected from the stockpile on a grid pattern at various depths.
    - Target analytes: Title 22 metals; potential for soluble metals testing (Waste Extraction Test, Toxicity Characteristic Leaching Procedure) if total metal concentrations are elevated.
    - Methods: Environmental Protection Agency Test Methods 6010B and 7471A (for mercury). Samples transported under chain-of-custody protocol to California Laboratory Services.
    - Depths: Various depths within the stockpile.
- Decision criteria or action levels proposed:
    - Total barium concentration: compared to 120 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) (site-specific background level).
    - Total lead concentration: compared to 3.8 mg/kg (site-specific background level).
    - In the event total barium and lead do not exceed background, BCS may be suitable for onsite reuse, pending Caltrans and DTSC approval.
    - Soluble metals testing to determine if BCS material is a California or Federal Resource Conservation and Recovery Act hazardous waste if total metals are elevated.
- Sequence, schedule, or phasing of the work:
    1. Excavation of BCS material from Weather Station and Overhead Sign #1 foundations (estimated 40 cubic yards total).
    2. Stockpiling of pre-moistened BCS material on and covered with minimum 6-milimeter-thick plastic sheeting, secured with ropes/weights and surrounded by erosion control measures.
    3. Collection of eight discrete soil samples from the stockpile.
    4. Compositing of eight discrete samples into two 4-part composite samples for analysis.
    5. Laboratory analysis for Title 22 metals (48-hour turn-around time).
    6. Comparison of analytical results to site-specific background levels.
    7. Potential soluble metals testing if total metal concentrations are elevated.
    8. Decision for onsite reuse (with Caltrans/DTSC approval) or offsite landfill disposal (profiling for acceptance at Forward Landfill or other facility).
    9. For offsite disposal, an authorized Caltrans representative will sign manifests, and licensed transporters will move covered loads.
    10. Preparation of a letter summary report following disposition of BCS stockpile material.

## Deliverables and approvals sought
- What the plan commits to produce:
    - Stockpiling of BCS material.
    - Collection and analysis of soil samples.
    - A letter summary report following completed disposition of the BCS stockpile material, including:
        - Description of completed BCS disturbance activities.
        - Locations and depth of BCS disturbance.
        - Volume of BCS material removed from the Stockpile 2 Containment Zone.
        - Site plan depicting BCS disturbance areas.
        - Copies of BCS material characterization data (laboratory reports and chain-of-custody documentation).
        - Copies of landfill waste disposal manifests and tonnage receipts.
        - Site photographs.
    - This information will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report.
- Which agency approval it is submitted for: Approval from Caltrans and DTSC for onsite reuse of BCS material, if applicable.
