## Document identity
- Report type: Surface water inspection report
- Preparer / author organization: Stantec Consulting Services Inc. (Stantec)
- Reporting period or sampling date(s): December 17, 2020
- Project number or task order (if stated): Contract No. 06A2542, Task Order No. 16

## Monitoring scope
- Media sampled: Surface water (inspection only; no samples collected)
- Wells, stations, or locations sampled: None
- Locations planned but NOT sampled, and why: Surface water sampling locations PL1, PL2, PL3, PL4, PL5, BG1, and BG2 were inspected, but no samples were collected due to the absence of visible surface water discharge/runoff from the site. Specific conditions at these locations:
    - BG1 and BG2: Area unchanged, no water ponding.
    - PL1 and PL2: No longer exist due to construction of concrete abutments for an overpass.
    - PL3: Area regraded and sloped for a perimeter wall footing; no runoff or ponding observed.
    - PL4 and PL5: Area enlarged into Stormwater Basin 6B; former gate valve removed. Minor isolated puddles observed in Basin 6B, but no flow from the discharge pipe at PL4.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- The inspection was conducted on December 17, 2020. Minimal rain (0.04 inches on December 16th and 0.05 inches on December 17th according to CIMIS Modesto weather station; 0.0 inches on December 17th according to UCANR Modesto weather station) occurred prior to and during the inspection.
- The ground surface and pavements were wet, but no visible discharge/runoff of surface water was occurring from Best Management Practices (BMPs) or the site.
- BMPs (e.g., straw waddles) have been installed around project construction areas.
- Construction and grading activities have significantly altered the landscape, particularly at planned sampling locations PL1, PL2, PL3, PL4, and PL5.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- Stantec concluded that no discharge/runoff of surface water beyond the BMPs and/or Site was observed during the December 17, 2020 surface water inspection.
- Stantec noted that due to the ongoing grading on the site, the ability to collect future samples at the currently established locations is unknown.
