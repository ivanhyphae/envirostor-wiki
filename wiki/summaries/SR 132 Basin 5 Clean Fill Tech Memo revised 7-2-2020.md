## Document Identity
- **Memo subject/question addressed:** Characterization testing of the proposed Basin 5 clean cap borrow area to assess suitability for generating clean cover fill material.
- **Preparer/author organization:** John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Recipient:** Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto
- **Date:** Revised July 2, 2020
- **Area/stockpile/feature involved:** Basin 5 clean cap borrow area, located adjacent and easterly of Carpenter Road within SR 132 right-of-way.

## Purpose
To determine whether soil from the proposed Basin 5 borrow area is suitable for use as clean cover fill material to cap consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) within Stockpiles 1 and 2. The investigation was prompted by the need to identify a source of clean fill and was conducted with a sampling strategy approved by Mr. Dean Wright of DTSC.

## Data and Findings
- **Sampling/testing performed:** On June 16, 2020, six exploratory trenches (BASIN5 1 through 6) were excavated on a grid pattern to a maximum depth of 10 feet. Soil samples were collected at depth intervals of 0–2 ft, 2–5 ft, and 5–10 ft. Three-part field composites were created per depth interval, yielding two composite samples per interval. Samples were analyzed for Title 22 metals via EPA Method 6010B, mercury via EPA Method 7471A, and the 0–2 ft interval composites for organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) via EPA Method 8081A.
- **Results with units (mg/kg):**
    - Arsenic: <1.0 to 1.8 (background: 1.2)
    - Barium: 66 to 94 (background: 72.8)
    - Chromium: 8.5 to 11 (background: 8.6)
    - Cobalt: 3.3 to 3.8 (background: 4.4)
    - Copper: 7.5 to 9.3 (background: 7.5)
    - Lead: 5.8 to 11 (background: 2.0)
    - Nickel: 6.2 to 7.9 (background: 5.3)
    - Vanadium: 33 to 37 (background: 31.3)
    - Zinc: 29 to 36 (background: 26.3)
    - Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, molybdenum, selenium, silver, thallium, mercury, and OCPs: not detected above laboratory reporting limits.
- **Comparison to criteria:** Reported metal concentrations were stated to be consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations (2007 Shaw background data summary) and the DTSC *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* guidance.

## Conclusion and Recommendation
The proposed Basin 5 clean cap borrow area appears suitable to generate clean cover fill materials for capping BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2. (Attributed to John E. Juhrend, Geocon.)
