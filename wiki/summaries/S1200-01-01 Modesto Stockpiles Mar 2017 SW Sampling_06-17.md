## Document identity
- **Report type:** Surface water sampling event
- **Preparer:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Sampling date:** March 24, 2017
- **Project number:** S1200-01-01; Contract No. 06A2184, Task Order No. 1, EA No. 10-0X2700

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Surface water
- **Locations sampled:** PL1, PL2, PL3, PL4, PL5
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled:** BG1 (insufficient water), BG2 (insufficient water)

## Results
- **Analytes measured:**
  - **Dissolved metals (EPA Method 6020/7470A):** antimony, arsenic, barium, cadmium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, selenium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, zinc; also beryllium, mercury, silver, thallium (all non-detect at MDL)
  - **General minerals:** chloride, nitrate as nitrogen, sulfate, sulfide (SM 4500-S-D), total alkalinity/bicarbonate/carbonate alkalinity (SM 2320B), total dissolved solids (SM 2540C), total suspended solids (SM 2540D)
- **Concentration ranges (dissolved metals):** Not fully tabulated in narrative; reported as detected in samples PL1–PL5 with specific exceedances noted.
- **Applicable thresholds:** California primary and secondary MCLs (SWRCB, September 23, 2015)
- **Exceedances:** Manganese exceeded secondary MCL in samples PL2 and PL4. No other analytes exceeded primary or secondary MCLs.
- **Trends versus prior rounds:** Analytical results stated as consistent with previous events (2013–2016). General minerals concentrations within same general range as previously reported at each location.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Rain event:** Rain began March 20, 2017; total 1.21 inches through March 26, 2017. It was raining at time of sample collection.
- **Insufficient water:** BG1 (drop inlet on Loletta Avenue) and BG2 (drop inlet on North Emerald Avenue) had insufficient water; no background samples collected.
- **Sample collection:** PL1 and PL2 from puddles on North Emerald Avenue shoulders; PL3 from puddle at Bennett Street alley (outside Caltrans right-of-way, beyond chainlink fence); PL4 from drainage basin outfall; PL5 within 3 feet of gate valve (not opened). Samples field-filtered (0.45-micron) for dissolved metals.
- **Field parameters measured:** pH, electrical conductivity, temperature, turbidity, ORP, DO (Table 1).
- **Laboratory QA/QC:** ATL (Advanced Technology Laboratories) noted dilutions for:
  - Barium and calcium – high target analyte concentrations
  - Magnesium, manganese, potassium, sodium – high non-target analyte
  - Nitrate as nitrogen – possible matrix interference
  - PL4 and PL5: lead and thallium – failing internal standard in original run
  - PL3: chloride and sulfate – possible matrix interference
- **Data qualifiers:** J-flagged results for values between PQL and MDL (estimated concentrations). No other analytical deviations reported.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusions (Geocon):** Analytical results consistent with previous events. No stormwater runoff observed migrating offsite; stormwater appears retained within Caltrans right-of-way. Interceptor berm at south side access ramp to Stockpile 2 functioned as designed.
- **Recommendations (Geocon):** Surface water management to continue: maintaining vegetative/fiber cover and straw wattles on western slope of Stockpile 2; additional surface water sampling during qualifying rain events in 2017 planned by Caltrans.
- **No recommendations** for well destruction or statistical re-evaluation.
