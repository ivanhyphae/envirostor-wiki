Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Project No. S9800-01-17, Task Order No. 17, EA NO. 10-0X2700) prepared a Groundwater Monitoring Report for Caltrans, detailing groundwater sampling conducted at the Modesto Stockpiles site primarily between February 25-26, 2014, with additional sampling between February 13-14 and February 26-28, 2014. Samples were collected from a total of 22 monitoring wells (MW-1 through MW-22, with some exclusions). Analyzed for Title 22 dissolved metals, general minerals, and other general chemistry parameters, the report references historical data from 2006 to December 2013.

Analyte concentrations varied across the wells. Dissolved metals, such as barium (48-310 µg/L), strontium (260-970 µg/L), arsenic (1.9-5.1 µg/L), and chromium (2.3-8.2 µg/L), were detected, while lead, antimony, beryllium, cadmium, silver, thallium, zinc, and mercury were generally below practical quantitation limits. General minerals like nitrate as nitrogen (2.8-34 mg/L) and total dissolved solids (390-630 mg/L) also showed varied concentrations. Notably, all measured constituent concentrations were found to be below their respective Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs). However, quality control reports indicated matrix interference issues for barium and strontium in matrix spike recoveries, although laboratory control samples validated the analytical batch.

Field observations included inconsistent depth-to-water measurements across different report sections, with some wells reported dry while others showed specific water depths. MW-5 and MW-6 required recharge during purging, and turbidity was noted in MW-1 and MW-2 at the start of purging. Groundwater elevation contours indicated a dominant northwest flow direction. The report also detailed well construction and analytical procedures, including specific QC observations for VOA samples and the need for anion/cation balance calculations and GeoTracker EDF preparation.
