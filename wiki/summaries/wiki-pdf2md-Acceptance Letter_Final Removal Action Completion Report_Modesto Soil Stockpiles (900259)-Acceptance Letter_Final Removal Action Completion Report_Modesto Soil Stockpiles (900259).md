Here is the structured summary of the correspondence for the case-file wiki.

## Document identity
- **Form:** Acceptance letter
- **Sender / author organization:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), signed by Steven Becker, P.G., Chief, Santa Susana Field Laboratory Site Mitigation and Restoration Program
- **Recipient(s) / addressee:** Mr. John E. Juhrend, P.E., CEG, Project Manager, Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Date:** January 18, 2023

## Decision or request
- **What is being accepted:** The *Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project* (RACR), dated December 1, 2022.
- **Description of the action:** DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley RWQCB, accepts the RACR as final. The letter confirms that all soil removal, consolidation, and capping activities for barium-containing soils (BCS) during Interim Phase 1 were completed per the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP), Remedial Design and Implementation Plan (RDIP), and two approved variance requests (April 17, 2020 and September 21, 2020).
- **Conditions/contingencies attached to the acceptance:** None. The letter explicitly states DTSC has no comments on the RACR.
- **Effective date / deadlines stated:**
    - DTSC will prepare a draft Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Agreement and submit it for Caltrans review within 30 days of this letter (i.e., by approximately February 17, 2023).
    - A Land Use Covenant (LUC) will also be completed and sent for review, signature, and recording with the Stanislaus County Assessor's office (no specific deadline given beyond “will be completed”).
    - Final certification of the RAP is deferred until after the Ultimate Phase 2 SR 132 build-out is completed, which is anticipated to begin in 8 to 10 years.

## Open items and next actions
- **DTSC** owes Caltrans: a draft O&M Agreement (within 30 days of the letter date, i.e., by ~Feb 17, 2023) and a completed LUC for review, signature, and recording.
- **Caltrans** (and Geocon, as the representative) are to review the draft O&M Agreement when provided.
- **Ultimate Phase 2** is a future, separate effort. It will require: a supplemental RDIP for DTSC review/approval, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and potential revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement. A separate agreement between DTSC and Caltrans must be executed before that work commences.

## Referenced documents
- *Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project* (RACR), dated December 1, 2022 — the item accepted by this letter.
- Draft RACR (accepted by DTSC in a prior letter dated February 24, 2022) — the initial approval that this final RACR supersedes.
- Remedial Action Plan (RAP) — the overarching plan the work was performed under.

- Supplemental RDIP, Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, revised O&M Plan and O&M Agreement — future documents for the Ultimate Phase 2.

## Responsible parties
- **DTSC:** Regulator; accepting the RACR; will prepare the draft O&M Agreement and LUC; will provide future oversight for Ultimate Phase 2.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB):** Co-regulator; consulted on the review.
- **Caltrans (Department of Transportation):** Owner/operator; will review the draft O&M Agreement; responsible party for the site; must enter a separate agreement with DTSC before Ultimate Phase 2 commences.
- **Geocon Consultants, Inc.:** Project manager/consultant; prepared and submitted the RACR on behalf of Caltrans.
