## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Well Destruction Report (WDR) documenting the decommissioning of four groundwater monitoring wells at the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles site. Prepared to satisfy regulatory requirements and confirm completion of approved well abandonment.
- **Author or preparer:** Geocon Consultants, Inc. – Chris Bates (Senior Staff Geologist/Project Manager) and Rebecca Silva (Project Manager).
- **Date or period covered:** Report dated June 18, 2025. Field activities performed March 18, 2025. Pre‑field activities (permitting, utility marking) conducted March 11–12, 2025.

## Key claims
- *Assertion:* The encapsulated soil stockpiles (barium‑ and lead‑impacted) do not have the potential to impact groundwater and will not impact groundwater in the future.
- *Attribution:* Geocon’s Updated Comparative Evaluation of Groundwater Data (February 26, 2024) concluded this, based on consistency with a 2014 evaluation, a declining water table, and final encapsulation pending Phase II construction.
- *Assertion:* Future monitoring data from the four remaining wells would provide no useful data; therefore, decommissioning was recommended.
- *Agency concurrence:* DTSC and RWQCB agreed in a May 3, 2024 letter that the encapsulated soil is not impacting groundwater and approved well destruction and discontinuation of monitoring.

## Regulatory decisions
- **DTSC and RWQCB:** Approval of the Updated Comparative Evaluation (May 3, 2024) – concurred that groundwater monitoring can be discontinued and the four wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10) decommissioned per the approved Remedial Design and Implementation Plan.
- **Stanislaus County Department of Environmental Resources (SCDER):** Issued well destruction permits MW25‑13 and MW25‑14 (dated March 12, 2025) for the four wells.

## Analytical results
- *No new analytical data were collected or reported in this document.*
- *Previous evaluation* (referenced) addressed barium and lead concentrations in soil stockpiles; groundwater data showed no impact. No concentration ranges or exceedances are presented in this report.

## Remedial actions
- **Activity:** Destruction of four groundwater monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10) on March 18, 2025.
- **Method:** Each well casing was filled to the surface with neat Portland cement grout via tremie pipe, then 20 psi air pressure applied for 5 minutes to force grout through the well screen and filter pack. Well boxes and concrete aprons were removed. Each well was over‑drilled to 5 ft depth using hollow‑stem auger; the over‑drilled annulus was filled with cement grout and capped with native soil.
- **Quantities (grout used vs. anticipated):** Reported in a table on page 5 of the source document (all wells: volumes in gallons – exact values not transcribed here but included in source). No significant anomalies noted.
- **Waste materials:** Well casings, pipes, and concrete debris were transported for disposal by PeneCore Drilling.
- **Previous destruction:** Six wells (MW1, MW2, MW3, MW5, MW7, MW8) were destroyed in 2019 due to highway construction conflicts.
- **Phase status:** Phase II construction for SR‑132, which will fully encapsulate the soil stockpiles, is scheduled to begin in 2026.

## Responsible parties
- **Caltrans (California Department of Transportation) – District 6:** Owner/operator of the site; granted unrestricted access for well destruction; will accompany work.
- **Geocon Consultants, Inc.:** Consultant preparing the report, managing field activities, and submitting DWR Well Completion Reports.
- **PeneCore Drilling (C‑57 license #906899):** Drilling contractor performing the well destruction.
- **DTSC & Central Valley RWQCB:** Co‑regulators; provided approval for monitoring discontinuation and well decommissioning.
- **Stanislaus County Department of Environmental Resources (SCDER):** Issued well destruction permits; requires post‑destruction reporting.

## Key terms and conditions
- **DWR Well Completion Reports:** Must be filed within 60 days of destruction via the Online System for Well Completion Reports (OSWCR). Geocon completed and submitted these on behalf of PeneCore.
- **SCDER permit conditions:** Contractor must remove soil cuttings and purge water within 30 days; submit a photo log (grout consistency, final holes) as a single PDF to HMpermit@envres.org with permit number in subject line.
- **Monitoring discontinuation:** Groundwater monitoring is permanently discontinued for the site.
- **Future construction:** Phase II encapsulation of remaining stockpiles under SR‑132 pavement anticipated in 2026.
- **Access agreement:** Caltrans explicitly granted unrestricted access to Geocon and sub‑consultants for the duration of the project.
