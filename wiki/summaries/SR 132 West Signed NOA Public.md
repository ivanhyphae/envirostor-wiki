## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Notice of Availability (NOA) for public review of the Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) with Finding of No Significant Impact (FONSI) and the attached Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP). Purpose is to announce availability and provide locations for document review.
- **Author or preparer**: Haesun Lim, Acting Senior Environmental Planner, Caltrans District 10.
- **Date or period covered**: NOA dated March 12, 2018. Documents available from that date; original public review period for the Draft EIR/EA with Draft Final RAP was January 18, 2017 through March 17, 2017.

## Key claims
- The Notice states that the Final EIR/EA with FONSI and attached Draft Final RAP are now available for public review.
- Responses to comments received during the public review period are included in Appendix J – Comments and Responses, with responses for the Draft Final RAP prepared by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB).
- The document implicitly asserts that the environmental review and remedial planning process has been completed to the point of issuing a FONSI and a Draft Final RAP.

## Regulatory decisions
- **Finding of No Significant Impact (FONSI)**: Issued by Caltrans for the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project (SCH #2010012010). The FONSI indicates that the Final EIR/EA determined no significant environmental impacts.
- **Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP)**: Prepared by Caltrans (owner/operator) with oversight from DTSC and in consultation with Central Valley RWQCB. This RAP addresses the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles (barium-contaminated fill material). The document is circulated as a draft final for public review; final approval expected after comment period.

## Remedial actions
- The NOA references the attached Draft Final RAP, which presumably describes remedial actions for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles. No specific physical activities, quantities, or verification outcomes are provided in the NOA itself.

## Responsible parties
- **Caltrans (California Department of Transportation)**: Owner/operator of the soil stockpiles; lead agency for the EIR/EA and preparer of the RAP.
- **DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control)**: Co-regulator; responsible for responding to comments on the Draft Final RAP.
- **Central Valley RWQCB (Regional Water Quality Control Board)**: Co-regulator consulted by DTSC for RAP comments.
- **StanCOG (Stanislaus Council of Governments)**: Hosts a copy of the documents at their office.
- **Stanislaus County Library**: Hosts a copy of the documents.

## Key terms and conditions
- **Public review period**: The Final EIR/EA and Draft Final RAP are available for review starting March 12, 2018. The original public comment period (January 18 – March 17, 2017) has closed; responses are compiled.
- **Document locations**: Physical copies at Caltrans District 10 (Stockton), StanCOG (Modesto), Stanislaus County Library, DTSC File Room (Sacramento), and online.
- **Contact**: Haesun Lim, Acting Senior Environmental Planner, Caltrans District 10, (559) 445-6172, haesun.a.lim@dot.ca.gov.
