## Document identity

- **Report type:** Groundwater monitoring round
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Sampling date(s):** January 22–23, 2013
- **Project number / task order:** Geocon Project No. S9525-06-44A; Task Order No. 44; EA 10-403500; Contract No. 06A1580

## Monitoring scope

- **Media sampled:** Groundwater
- **Wells sampled:** MW-1, MW-2, MW-3, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, MW-7, MW-8, MW-9, MW-10
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled:** None; all ten wells were sampled

## Results

### Dissolved metals
- **Barium:** 1.1 µg/L (MW-1) to 380 µg/L (MW-5). Primary MCL = 1,000 µg/L. No exceedances.
- **Lead:** Not detected at or above PQL of 1.0 µg/L in any well.
- **Strontium:** 198 µg/L (MW-1) to 3,900 µg/L (MW-5). EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory = 4,000 µg/L. No exceedances.
- **Arsenic:** 0.28 µg/L (MW-3) to 5.0 µg/L (MW-9). Primary MCL = 10 µg/L. No exceedances.
- **Chromium:** 0.6 µg/L (MW-4) to 2.6 µg/L (MW-10). Primary MCL = 50 µg/L. No exceedances.
- **Vanadium:** 0.4 µg/L (MW-3) to 5.3 µg/L (MW-5). EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory = 21 µg/L. No exceedances.
- **Manganese:** Detected in MW-6 at 62 µg/L. Primary MCL = 50 µg/L. **Exceedance** in MW-6 (12 µg/L above MCL).
- **Cobalt, copper, molybdenum, nickel, selenium, zinc:** Detected in some wells; all concentrations below respective thresholds.
- **Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, silver, thallium, mercury:** Not detected at or above PQLs.

### General minerals
- **Nitrate (as N):** 2.9 mg/L (MW-3) to 30 mg/L (MW-5). Primary MCL = 10 mg/L. **Exceedances** in MW-1 (12 mg/L), MW-5 (30 mg/L), MW-6 (21 mg/L), MW-10 (13 mg/L).
- **Total dissolved solids (TDS):** 310 mg/L (MW-7) to 680 mg/L (MW-5). Secondary MCL = 500 mg/L. **Exceedances** in MW-5 (680 mg/L), MW-6 (640 mg/L), MW-10 (580 mg/L).
- **Sulfide:** 0.014 mg/L (MW-8) to 0.52 mg/L (MW-1).

### Trends versus prior rounds
- Barium and strontium concentrations described as "similar to historical levels" and "significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds."
- Remaining dissolved metals reported at concentrations "similar to historical levels."
- Stiff diagram analysis shows "very slight changes in ionic content" since March 2012. MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-5, MW-7, MW-9 consistently calcium-dominant; MW-3, MW-6, MW-8 consistently sodium-dominant; MW-10 shifted from sodium-dominant (June 2012) to calcium-dominant (July 2012 onward).

## Field conditions and QA/QC

- **Dry wells:** MW-5 went dry after purging 1 gallon; MW-6 went dry after purging 1.5 gallons. Both wells were allowed to recover; MW-6 required a second recovery after an additional 2 gallons were purged before sampling.
- **Depth to groundwater:** 31.04 ft (MW-1) to 40.02 ft (MW-5) below TOC.
- **Groundwater flow direction:** Southeast, average gradient of 0.001, consistent with historical flow.
- **Equipment blank:** Chromium reported at 1.2 µg/L in the equipment blank. Geocon concluded that sample results were not significantly influenced because reported chromium concentrations were similar to historical values.
- **Laboratory QA/QC:** Method blank surrogate recoveries acceptable; no analytes at or above PQLs in method blanks. Some MS/MSD recoveries or RPDs outside control limits, but sample results were validated by laboratory control samples. No data qualification was applied. Data considered sufficient quality.

## Conclusions and recommendations

- **Conclusions (Geocon):** No dissolved metals exceeded their respective numeric water quality thresholds except manganese in MW-6. General minerals (nitrate and TDS) exceeded primary and secondary MCLs in several wells. Barium and strontium remain stable and well below thresholds. Lead not detected.
- **Recommendations (Geocon):** Next monitoring event scheduled for March 2013. Following the March 2013 event, sampling frequency will change to quarterly.
