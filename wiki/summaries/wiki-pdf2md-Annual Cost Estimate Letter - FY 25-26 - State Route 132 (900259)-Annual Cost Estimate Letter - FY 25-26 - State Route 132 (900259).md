## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: This is an Annual Cost Estimate Letter providing an estimate of projected oversight costs for the State Route 132 West Expressway remediation project for Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026.
- Author or preparer: Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), specifically Katherine M. Butler, MPH, Director. The letter is addressed to Mr. Adam Inman, P.G., Engineering Geologist, Caltrans D-6.
- Date or period covered: The letter is dated December 24, 2025. The cost estimate covers the period of FY 2025-2026, which began July 1, 2025, and ends June 30, 2026.

## Key claims
- DTSC has the authority to require Caltrans to pay all costs associated with the administration of the Land Use Covenant (LUC) pursuant to *California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Section 67391.1 (h)*. (DTSC)
- Caltrans remains liable for all response costs incurred by DTSC pursuant to *Health and Safety Code Section 79650 et seq.*, and the *Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA) 42 U.S.C. Section 9601 et seq.* (DTSC)
- The enclosed schedule and cost estimate will be updated as necessary to reflect changes to the project or the rates. (DTSC)

## Key terms and conditions
- **Cost Liability**: Caltrans is liable for all response costs incurred by DTSC, as per *Health and Safety Code Section 79650 et seq.* and *CERCLA 42 U.S.C. Section 9601 et seq.*
- **Cost Estimate Nature**: The document is an estimate, not an invoice. Invoices will be issued based on actual costs.
- **LUC Administration Costs**: DTSC has the authority to require Caltrans to pay costs associated with LUC administration.
- **LUC Inspection Report Due Date**: Annual Land Use Covenant Inspection Report is due to DTSC by January 15, 2026.
- **Stormwater/Surface Water Monitoring Report Due Date**: Surface/Stormwater Monitoring Report (FY25/26) is due to DTSC by February 2026.
