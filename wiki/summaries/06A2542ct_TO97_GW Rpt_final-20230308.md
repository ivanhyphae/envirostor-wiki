## Document identity
- **Report type:** Groundwater monitoring round (semi-annual depth-to-water gauging and annual sampling event)
- **Preparer / author organization:** Stantec Consulting Services Inc.
- **Reporting period / sampling date(s):** Gauging on November 2, 2022 and December 13, 2022; gauging and attempted sampling on February 21, 2023
- **Project number / task order:** Contract No. 06A2542, Task Order No. 97 (TO97)

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Groundwater (none collected – all wells dry)
- **Wells / locations sampled:** MW-4, MW-6, MW-9, MW-10
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled:** All four wells (MW-4, MW-6, MW-9, MW-10) were dry on February 21, 2023; no groundwater samples were collected. On November 2, 2022, well MW-10 could not be located due to wood mulch; it was subsequently located and gauged on December 13, 2022 after the well box was replaced by the onsite contractor.

## Results
- **Analytes measured:** Not applicable – no samples collected. Historical analytical results for Title 22 metals (dissolved) and general minerals/PAHs are summarized in Tables 3 and 4 (attached to report but not discussed in text).
- **Applicable thresholds:** Not applicable for this event.
- **Locations exceeding thresholds:** Not evaluated.
- **Trends versus prior rounds:** Not reported – only historic data provided for reference.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Dry wells:** No measurable water was observed in any of the four monitoring wells during the November 2, 2022, December 13, 2022, or February 21, 2023 gauging events.
- **Well status:** MW-10 well box was destroyed during site work but the PVC casing was undamaged; contractor replaced the box and reburied the well under mulch. No damage or tampering noted on MW-4, MW-6, or MW-9.
- **Gauging method:** Depth to water measured with a GeoTech water level meter to nearest 0.01 ft below top of casing (BTOC) from surveyed reference points. Meter decontaminated with Liquinox/DI wash between wells.
- **Gradient:** Not calculated due to dry conditions. Historic flow direction reported as generally south to southeast with hydraulic gradient 0.0006–0.004.
- **Sampling deviations:** None – samples were not collected because wells were dry.
- **Data qualifiers / detection-limit issues:** None applicable.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusions (Stantec):** All four monitoring wells were dry; therefore no groundwater samples were collected or analyzed by the laboratory.
- **Recommendations (Stantec):** Continue with scheduled monitoring: semi-annual depth-to-water gauging (next event fall 2023) and annual groundwater sampling (next event spring 2024).
