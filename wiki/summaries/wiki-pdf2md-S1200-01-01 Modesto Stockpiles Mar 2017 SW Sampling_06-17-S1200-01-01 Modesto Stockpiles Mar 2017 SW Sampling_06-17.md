### Summary of: S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Mar 2017 SW Sampling_06.17.md

This document is a stormwater sampling report prepared by **Geocon Consultants, Inc.** for the **Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles** project in Stanislaus County, California (Project No. S1200-01-01, Task Order 1). The primary sampling event occurred on **March 24, 2017**, though the document also references a broader monitoring period from April 2013 through March 2017. The report is addressed to **Rebecca Silva** (Caltrans) and involves oversight by **DTSC** and the **CVRWQCB**.

#### Monitoring Scope and Field Conditions
The monitoring targeted surface water (stormwater runoff) at locations PL1, PL2, PL3, PL4, and PL5. Background stations BG1 and BG2 were planned but not sampled on March 24, 2017, due to insufficient water (dry conditions). Prior to this event, other locations (PL2, PL5, BG1, BG2) were also noted as dry on multiple sampling dates.

On March 24, 2017, **1.21 inches of rain** fell, and it was raining at the time of collection. Samples were gathered from puddles (PL1-PL3), a drainage basin outfall (PL4), and near a gate valve (PL5). Field parameters (pH, EC, temperature, turbidity, ORP, DO) were measured, and samples were field-filtered for dissolved metals analysis.

#### Results: Barium and Other Metals

- **Barium**, the primary contaminant of concern, was detected at concentrations ranging from **7.8 µg/L** to **210 µg/L** across the monitoring period. All values were well below the California MCL of **1,000 µg/L**, with no exceedances reported.
- In the March 2017 event, barium was detected at 63 µg/L (PL1), 89 µg/L (PL2), and 130 µg/L (PL3), with a 10x dilution required for PL3. At PL4 and PL5, barium was found at 160 µg/L and 70 µg/L, respectively.
- **Other dissolved metals** detected included antimony, arsenic, copper, lead, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, strontium, vanadium, and zinc. **Manganese exceeded its secondary MCL at PL2 and PL4**. No other MCL exceedances were noted for other metals.
- The analytical results include numerous "J" flags (estimated concentration below the PQL) and data qualifiers for dilutions (D1-D6) due to high concentrations or matrix interference.

#### QA/QC Findings
Quality assurance issues were noted in the analysis of metals (EPA Method 6020). **Matrix spike (M1) recovery exceedances** were observed for several elements, including calcium, magnesium, manganese, potassium, sodium, **barium**, copper, vanadium, and selenium. These failures were attributed to the sample matrix and were not considered indicative of a loss of laboratory control, as the LCS recoveries were acceptable. High RPD values were flagged for cobalt and selenium. **Matthew Tidwell** (Senior Staff Geologist) requested re-runs and explanations for the dilution procedures.

#### General Minerals and Other Parameters
Concentrations of general minerals (chloride, nitrate, sulfate, alkalinity, TDS, TSS, sulfide) were all reported below their respective California MCLs. Notable TSS values reached up to 1,100 mg/L in prior rounds, and TDS ranged from <10 to 420 mg/L. The analytical laboratory was **Advanced Technology Laboratories, Inc. (ATL)**, performing analyses per EPA methods 300, 6020, and 7470A.
