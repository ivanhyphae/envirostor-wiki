## Document identity

- **Form**: Email thread (request email and acceptance reply)
- **Sender / author organization**: Richard Stewart (Caltrans District 10) for the request; Dean Wright (DTSC – Project Manager) for the acceptance
- **Recipient(s) / addressee**: Request sent to Dean Wright (DTSC), Perry Myers (DTSC), Steven Meeks (Central Valley RWQCB); acceptance reply sent to Richard Stewart (Caltrans) with cc to Perry Myers and Steven Meeks
- **Date range**: April 13, 2020 (request) – April 17, 2020 (acceptance)

## Thread / participants

1. **April 13, 2020** – Richard Stewart (Caltrans) to Dean Wright, Perry Myers, Steven Meeks: transmits attached RDIP variance request letter and states expectation that if approved, excavated soil from Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing will only be used as clean cover/embankment fill within stockpile footprints, not elsewhere or off-site.
2. **April 17, 2020** – Dean Wright (DTSC) to Richard Stewart: confirms DTSC and Water Board have reviewed the variance request; accepts the VR for soil along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1; states that approval for Stockpile 2 will be provided once data is submitted and reviewed.

## Decision or request

- **What is being approved/accepted**: DTSC (with Water Board concurrence) accepts the RDIP Variance Request for use of soil excavated from the upper foot of the MSE wall footing along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1 as clean cap soil, because the 95% UCL barium concentration (113.1 mg/kg) is within the site-specific naturally occurring background range.
- **Document acted on**: “Variance Request to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (VR)” dated April 9, 2020 (attached to the April 13 email).
- **Conditions**: The soil may only be used as clean cap soil on the project. Additionally, the request letter specified that soil from Stockpile 1 MSE footing will only be used within the stockpile footprints (not elsewhere or hauled off-site); this condition is noted in the acceptance.
- **Stockpile 2**: Conditional acceptance – if the UCL of MSE wall footing excavation along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2 is under naturally occurring background concentrations, it may also be used as clean cap soil. Formal approval/rejection deferred pending data submission and review.

## Open items and next actions

- **Required action**: Caltrans must submit data for the MSE wall footing excavation along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2 for review by DTSC and Water Board.
- **Pending approval**: DTSC will provide official approval or rejection for the Stockpile 2 material once that data has been submitted and reviewed.
- **No due date stated** for submission of Stockpile 2 data.
