## Document identity
- Form: Approval letter
- Sender / author organization: California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)
- Recipient(s) / addressee: Ms. Grace Magsayo, P.E., Project Manager, California Department of Transportation, District 10
- Date: March 14, 2018

## Decision or request
- What is being approved, accepted, conditionally approved, denied, requested, or commented on: Approval of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP)
- The specific document or deliverable it acts on (name it, and the version/draft if stated): Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project.
- Conditions, required revisions, or contingencies attached:
    - The Draft Final RAP needs to be modified to read "Final RAP" and include updated information that reflects the Final RAP and Final EIR where applicable.
    - The Final RAP must be placed in local repositories for public viewing.
    - A hard copy and an electronic copy of the Final RAP must be sent to DTSC and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board for filing.
- Effective date or deadline, if any: As currently scheduled, RAP will be implemented in two phases: Phase 1 interim project phase, from 2019 to 2020 and Phase 2 ultimate build-out phase, from 2028 to 2029.

## Open items and next actions
- Caltrans will submit a Remedial Design and Implementation Plan (RDIP) for each phase (Phase 1: 2019-2020; Phase 2: 2028-2029) for DTSC's review and approval.
- DTSC will prepare and publish a "Start Work Notice" in English and Spanish in local newspapers for each phase of the project (for the segment associated with the stockpiles) 2 weeks prior to the start of construction.
- Caltrans will prepare a Remedial Action Completion Report for DTSC's review and approval for each phase of the project.
- Following implementation of the interim project phase, Caltrans will enter into an Operation and Maintenance Agreement (OMA) with DTSC and prepare an Operation and Maintenance Plan (OMP) for DTSC's review and approval. The OMP will require annual inspections of pavement and other features of the containment remedy and groundwater monitoring. A land use covenant and soil management plan will be required. The OMA and OMP may need modification following the ultimate build-out phase.
- Certification of the containment remedy will occur following implementation of the ultimate build-out phase.
- DTSC will evaluate the containment remedy every five years, with operation and maintenance activities continuing.
