## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Well installation:** Wells MW-9 and MW-10 installed May 29–30, 2012 via hollow-stem auger to ~40 ft bgs; 2-inch Schedule 40 PVC, 10-ft 0.010-inch slot screens from ~29.5–39.5 ft; #2/12 Monterey silica sand filter pack.
- **Water levels:** Groundwater encountered at 33.5 ft (MW-9) and 35.4 ft (MW-10) during installation.
- **Well development:** June 18, 2012; ~8.5–10 gallons removed per well; hand-bailed then submersible pump.
- **Sampling:** June 20, 2012; low-flow purge; disposable polyethylene bailers.
- **QA/QC deviations:** Matrix spike/matrix spike duplicate (MS/MSD) recoveries outside control limits for barium (MS >130%, MSD <70%) and strontium (MSD <70%). Geocon determined no data qualification necessary since concentrations were far below thresholds.
- **Method blanks and LCS:** Acceptable recoveries reported.
- **Field measurements:** Dissolved oxygen and ORP recorded; pH, EC, temperature during purge.

## Conclusions and recommendations
*Attributed to Geocon Consultants, Inc.*

- No dissolved metals (except manganese in MW-10) exceeded drinking water thresholds.
- No PAHs detected.
- Nitrate exceeded primary MCL in MW-9 (13 mg/L); TDS exceeded secondary MCL in both wells.
- Recommendations: Continue monitoring (implied by ongoing program); no specific remedial recommendations made in this report.
