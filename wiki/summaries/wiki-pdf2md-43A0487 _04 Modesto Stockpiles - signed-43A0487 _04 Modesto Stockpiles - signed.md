## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: Service Request (SR-04) to an existing Interagency Agreement (Agreement No. 43A0487, DTSC Agreement No. 25-T5466) between Caltrans and DTSC. Its purpose is to define the scope of DTSC's regulatory oversight services and associated reimbursement for the environmental work at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site.
- Author or preparer: Not explicitly stated, but prepared on behalf of Caltrans and DTSC.
- Date or period covered:
    - Term of Service Request: August 4, 2025, to June 30, 2030
    - Execution Date: October 17, 2025 (latest signature date)

## Key claims
- The work described in this Service Request is for regulatory oversight and/or concurrence on documents pertaining to remediation of contamination service. (Matthew Farris certification)
- The Service Request and attachments comply with the provisions of Agreement No. 43A0487. (Matthew Farris certification)
- Sufficient funding has been encumbered to pay for this work. (Matthew Farris certification)

## Regulatory decisions
- **Decision Type**: Interagency Agreement Service Request (SR-04)
- **Agency**: California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and California Department of Transportation (Caltrans)
- **Date**: Executed on October 17, 2025 (latest signature date)
- **Conditions imposed**:
    - DTSC will provide regulatory oversight for Caltrans' activities associated with the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project.
    - DTSC's specific tasks include reviewing and commenting on monitoring, inspection, and Remedial Design Implementation Plans, well destruction reports, participating in meetings, and providing field oversight as needed.
    - The total amount payable by Caltrans to DTSC shall not exceed $113,000.00 for the duration of the Service Request.
    - Work shall begin August 4, 2025, or upon execution, and terminate on June 30, 2030.
    - DTSC will provide written comments and recommendations based on submittals.
    - Invoices must include Interagency Agreement and DTSC Agreement Numbers, invoice number, date, billing period, hours by individual classifications, and other direct costs.
    - Key staff and new job classifications must be approved by the Caltrans Contract Manager and listed in the original Contract Estimation Rates.

## Remedial actions
- **Activity**: Regulatory oversight of environmental work and remediation activities at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles.
- **Phase status**: Ongoing regulatory oversight for the project's duration.
- **Verification outcomes**: Not applicable at this stage; this document describes the framework for verification and oversight.

## Key terms and conditions
- **Required follow-on actions**:
    - DTSC to review and comment on routine monitoring/inspection reports, Remedial Design Implementation Plans, and well destruction reports.
    - DTSC to participate in meetings and phone calls regarding the site and construction activities.
    - DTSC to provide field oversight as needed.
    - DTSC to prepare and modify this Service Request as necessary.
    - Work items to be implemented as needed and completed as soon as practical, with a minimum 30-day review period for DTSC.
    - DTSC to provide written comments and recommendations based on submitted data and reports.
    - DTSC to submit invoices with specific details (Interagency Agreement and DTSC Agreement Numbers, invoice number, date, billing period, hours by individual classifications, other direct costs).
    - Caltrans may request copies of daily logs and timesheets from DTSC staff.
    - Meetings between DTSC and Caltrans Service/Contract Managers as necessary, with Caltrans able to request monthly meetings.
- **Deadlines**: Work to terminate on June 30, 2030.
- **Monitoring obligations**: DTSC will review routine monitoring reports.
- **Restrictions**:
    - Service Requests may not modify, amend, or exceed the terms of Agreement Number #43A0487, DTSC Agreement Number 25-T5466.
    - Total amount payable by Caltrans under this Service Request shall not exceed $113,000.00.
    - Substitution of personnel classifications is permitted if it does not exceed the total amount payable and is agreed upon by Caltrans Service Request Manager and Contract Manager.
    - All post-execution requests for new personnel must be submitted to the Caltrans Service Request Manager; those involving key personnel or new classifications require Caltrans Contract Manager approval.
