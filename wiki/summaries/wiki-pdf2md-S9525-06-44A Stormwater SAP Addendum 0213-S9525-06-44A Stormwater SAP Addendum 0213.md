## Document identity
- Plan type: Addendum to Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)
- Preparer / author organization: Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- Date: February 20, 2013
- Project number or task order (if stated): Project No. S9525-06-44A, Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44, EA No. 10-403500

## Objective and scope
- Purpose of the work and what prompted it: The purpose of the proposed surface water sampling is to evaluate whether surface water runoff from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles (Stockpiles 1, 2, and 3) is migrating beyond Caltrans' right-of-way, and if so, whether the stormwater has been impacted by chemicals of potential concern in the stockpiles. This addendum builds upon a previous 2006 SAP and subsequent sampling event that identified an elevated barium concentration in one sample from Stockpile 3.
- Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed: The plan addresses surface water runoff from Stockpiles 1, 2, and 3 located south of Kansas Avenue, between North Emerald Avenue and SR 99, in Stanislaus County, California. The media addressed is surface water, specifically stormwater runoff from the stockpiles and rainwater pooling along the east and west shoulders of Emerald Avenue between Stockpiles 1 and 2.
- What approval or decision the plan supports or requests: The plan supports continued monitoring and evaluation of potential off-site migration of contaminants from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles.

## Proposed approach
- Sampling design: Up to five surface water samples will be collected from locations where water has migrated beyond the right-of-way boundary occupied by the stockpiles. Samples will be collected from puddles of rainwater that pool along the east and west shoulders of Emerald Avenue between Stockpiles 1 and 2. At least one background rainwater sample will be collected along Emerald Avenue, either north or south of Stockpiles 1 and 2. Sampling will occur during up to four separate rainfall events if necessary. During sampling, pH, electrical conductivity, temperature, and turbidity will be monitored. If runoff from the soil stockpiles is observed leaving the Caltrans right-of-way during surface sampling events, additional samples may be collected at those locations. Samples for dissolved metals will be field-filtered through a 0.45-micron filter.
- Target analytes, methods, depths: Samples will be analyzed for:
    - Title 22 total and dissolved metals, including strontium, using EPA Test Method 6020/7470.
    - Nitrate as nitrogen and sulfate using EPA Test Method 300.0.
    - Sulfide using Standard Method (SM) 4500.
    - Total suspended solids (TSS) using SM 2540D.
- Sequence, schedule, or phasing of the work: Field activities will include sample collection. Samples will be delivered to Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL) for analysis. A draft report will be prepared summarizing observations, laboratory data, and data evaluation, which, after incorporating Caltrans' comments, will be finalized.
- For a variance: Not applicable.

## Deliverables and approvals sought
The plan commits to producing:
- A summary report detailing field observations, laboratory data, and data evaluation. The report will include:
    - Background summary
    - Scope of services performed
    - Observations during field activities
    - Results of laboratory analysis
    - Conclusions and recommendations
    - Vicinity Maps and Site Plans indicating sampling locations
    - Tabular summaries of analytical data
    - Photographs depicting site conditions and sampling locations
    - Appendices including laboratory reports and chain-of-custody documentation.
A draft report will be provided to Caltrans, and upon receipt and incorporation of comments, three hard copies and one electronic (CD) copy of the finalized report will be provided. The plan is submitted for review and approval by Caltrans, DTSC, and CVRWQCB.

## Responsible parties
- Caltrans: Owner/operator, client, will review and provide comments on the draft report.
- Geocon Consultants, Inc.: Preparer of the Addendum SAP, will perform field activities, manage laboratory analyses, and prepare the summary report.
- Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL): California-certified laboratory to perform analyses of surface water samples.
- Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC): Approves protocols establishing the project's SAP. (referenced)
- Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB): Co-regulator. (referenced for receipt of documents)
