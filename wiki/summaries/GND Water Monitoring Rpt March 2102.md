## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Preparer's conclusions:** DTSC concurs with the "Groundwater Monitoring Report, dated May 2012" prepared by Geocon Consultants.
- **Recommendations (by DTSC):**
    - Future groundwater monitoring reports should include a discussion comparing results and concentration trends from previous sampling events as more data become available.
    - Graphs and maps should be prepared depicting concentration trends of primary COCs over time.
    - All graphics, including existing "stiff" diagrams, should include a legend defining symbols/colors used.
    - Groundwater monitoring should continue on a quarterly schedule.
