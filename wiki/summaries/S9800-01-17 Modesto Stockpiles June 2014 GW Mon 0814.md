### Summary: June 2014 Groundwater Monitoring Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles

**Document Identity & Scope**
This report, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc., documents the results of a groundwater monitoring event conducted for Caltrans District 6 on June 4–5, 2014 (Project No. S9800-01-17). The monitoring covered all ten wells (MW-1 through MW-10) at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site in Stanislaus County. The analytical data was validated by Geocon (contact: Rebecca Silva), and the final report was prepared and data uploaded to GeoTracker on June 17, 2014.

**Key Analytical Findings & Threshold Exceedances**
- **Metals:** No dissolved metals (including barium, strontium, lead, arsenic, chromium, and vanadium) exceeded their respective Primary or Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), EPA Health Advisories, or other applicable thresholds. Mercury was not detected.
- **Nitrate:** Exceeded the Primary MCL of 10 mg/L in five wells: MW-1 (15 mg/L), MW-5 (29 mg/L), MW-6 (13 mg/L), MW-9 (11 mg/L), and MW-10 (14 mg/L).
- **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS):** Exceeded the Secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in five wells: MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10.
- **Sulfide:** Detected in MW-1 (0.12 mg/L), MW-8 (0.017 mg/L—with a B qualifier indicating presence in the method blank), and MW-10 (0.023 mg/L—with a D1 qualifier for dilution due to matrix interference).

**Trends & Conclusions**
Groundwater chemistry was reported as stable, with ongoing nitrate and TDS exceedances consistent with historical patterns. Stiff diagrams indicated two dominant groundwater types: calcium-dominant (MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-7, MW-9, MW-10) and sodium-dominant (MW-3, MW-5, MW-6, MW-8). Minor ionic changes were noted, with MW-5 shifting from calcium-dominant to sodium-dominant in 2013. The upgradient well MW-1 suggests a natural background contribution to the exceedances.

**Field Conditions & QA/QC**
Depth to groundwater ranged from 35.09 to 42.86 feet below TOC, with a consistent south-southeast flow direction. Laboratory QC was generally acceptable; method blanks, LCS, and surrogate recoveries were within limits. A matrix spike/matrix spike duplicate recovery for total sulfide (68.6%) was slightly below the 70-120% control limit in one batch, but the RPD was acceptable (3.72%), and the data was validated by acceptable LCS results. No formal data qualification was required, though qualifiers B and D1 were noted for specific sulfide samples.

**Responsible Parties & Recommendations**
Caltrans District 6 is the owner/operator, with oversight from DTSC (Randy Adams) and CVRWQCB (Steve Meeks). The report recommends continuing the quarterly groundwater monitoring schedule, with the next event planned for September 2014. No other specific recommendations were made for this event.
