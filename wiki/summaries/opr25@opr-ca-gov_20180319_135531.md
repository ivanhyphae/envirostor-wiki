## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Notice of Determination (NOD) filed under California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) – Public Resources Code §21108/21152. Documents DTSC’s determination that the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project will not have a significant effect on the environment.
- **Author or preparer:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program.
- **Date or period covered:** Certified March 14, 2018 (approval date). EIR/EA certified by Caltrans on March 2, 2018. NOD stamped received by State Clearinghouse March 2018 (year “5” red stamp indicates 2018).

## Key claims
- DTSC, as CEQA Responsible Agency, determined the project **will not have a significant effect on the environment**.
- An Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) was prepared and certified by Caltrans (Lead Agency). DTSC is utilizing that EIR/EA for the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) CEQA analysis.
- Mitigation measures were made a condition of project approval.
- A Statement of Overriding Considerations was **not adopted** by DTSC (though Caltrans prepared one for its own lead‑agency actions).
- The containment remedy provides long‑term protection by preventing exposure and minimizing surface‑water infiltration into groundwater under the stockpiles.

## Regulatory decisions
- **Decision:** Project approval and negative declaration (no significant effect) by DTSC.
- **Agency:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
- **Date:** March 14, 2018.
- **Conditions imposed:** Use of containment remedy (retaining walls, bridge abutments, clean fill cover); annual land‑use controls inspections; annual groundwater monitoring; maintenance of pavement and other remedy features.

## Analytical results
- **Contaminants/media:** Metals in soil (primarily **barium**, **strontium**, and **lead**) in three stockpiles.
- **Quantities/volumes:** Approximately **160,000 cubic yards** of contaminated soil.
- **Exceedances/thresholds:** Not provided in this NOD; document only notes presence of metals.
- **Applicable thresholds:** Not specified in this notice; reference is made to the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and EIR/EA.

## Remedial actions
- **Activity:** Containment of three stockpiles.
- **Quantities/locations:** Three areas within state right‑of‑way south of Kansas Avenue, Modesto: (1) between Carpenter Avenue and East Emerald Avenue, (2) between Emerald Avenue and SR‑99, (3) east of SR‑99.
- **Phase status:** Remedy approved; work described as part of “construction of the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project.” Stockpiles to be contained behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath roadway; unpaved portions receive clean fill cover.
- **Verification outcomes:** Not documented in this NOD. Ongoing integrity verified via annual inspections and groundwater monitoring.

## Responsible parties
- **Caltrans:** Lead Agency under CEQA; owner/operator of the soil stockpiles; prepared EIR/EA and RAP; certified EIR/EA on March 2, 2018.
- **DTSC:** Responsible Agency under CEQA; approved the project on March 14, 2018; oversees remedial action and post‑remedy monitoring/permit requirements.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB):** Implied co‑regulator (groundwater monitoring), but not explicitly named in this NOD.

## Key terms and conditions
- **Annual inspections** of pavement and other containment‑remedy features.
- **Annual groundwater monitoring** to ensure remedy integrity.
- **Land‑use controls** to maintain containment and prevent exposure.
- **Clean fill cover** over unpaved portions of stockpiles.
