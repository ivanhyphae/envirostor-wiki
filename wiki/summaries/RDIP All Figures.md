## Document identity
- **Deliverable type**: Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) – Interim Phase 1  
- **Version / status**: 65% submittal; marked “NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION” (dated 03-05-18 on grading plans); this document is an interim phase design deliverable, not a final construction-ready plan.  
- **Preparer / author organization**: Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Rancho Cordova, CA)  
- **Date**: January 2019  
- **Project number / task order**: Geocon Project No. S1200-01-01; Task Order No. 1; EA 10-0X2700; Contract No. 06A2184  

## Purpose and regulatory context
- **Milestone / requirement**: This RDIP addresses the remedial design and implementation approach for the interim phase of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project. It is a design-level deliverable that translates the selected remedial strategy into engineered plans, including grading, stockpile consolidation, and soil transportation.  
- **Oversight framework**: The document is prepared for the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) – District 6 Hazardous Waste Branch. The project is under DTSC oversight, with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) as co-regulator. The RDIP is submitted to Caltrans and is subject to regulatory review.  
- **Not an errata**.

## Site and contamination summary
- **Contaminants of concern**: Barium (in soil) and aerially deposited lead (ADL).  
- **Affected media**: Soil stockpiles (three identified stockpiles) and surrounding soil. Groundwater is monitored but not directly addressed as a remediation medium in this interim phase.  
- **Sources and areas of concern**:  
  - Three existing barium-containing soil stockpiles (Stockpile 1, Stockpile 2, Stockpile 3) located along State Route 132 (SR-132) in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California.  
  - Stockpile 3 is designated for removal; Stockpiles 1 and 2 are to be incorporated into a consolidation zone.  
  - Additional areas: former FMC facility, former disposal ponds, and ADL removal areas (historical aerially deposited lead contamination).  
- **Extent**: The project area spans the SR-132 West corridor from approximately Kansas Avenue to the SR-99 interchange, including Emerald Avenue and adjacent rights-of-way. Groundwater monitoring wells MW1–MW10 are present; some are to be protected in place, others abandoned.

## Selected or recommended remedy
- **Remedial approach**: Consolidation of barium-containing soil and ADL into a designated “Barium-Containing Soil and ADL Consolidation Zone” located over Stockpiles 1 and 2. Stockpile 3 will be removed and its material hauled to the consolidation zone.  
- **Design basis**:  
  - **Consolidation zone**: Engineered fill placed over existing stockpiles, with a minimum 12-inch-thick cover fill and a structural pavement section (pavement section detail shown in cross-sections).  
  - **Cover system**: Minimum 12-inch cover fill (green in cross-section) on slopes; structural pavement section on top.  
  - **Retaining wall**: A retaining wall is designed for the southern edge of the consolidation zone (Stockpiles 1–2), with imported retaining wall backfill material.  
  - **Cross-sections**: Generalized north-south cross-section (Stockpiles 1 and 2) and west-east cross-section (Emerald Avenue overcrossing) show the vertical configuration of the consolidation zone, cover fill, pavement, and removal/replacement of existing barium soil near the bridge abutment.  
- **Soil transportation plan**: Barium-containing soil from Stockpile 3 and from the Emerald Avenue/Eastern Bridge Abutment removal area will be hauled to the consolidation zone via a designated truck route (Kansas Avenue, Emerald Avenue, SR-99, Franklin Street).  
- **Demolition**: Buildings adjacent to Stockpile 3 are identified for demolition.  
- **Performance/cleanup standards**: Not explicitly stated in this 65% submittal; standards will be established in subsequent design phases. The plan assumes containment and capping of barium soil rather than removal.

## Conclusions and path forward
- **Closure status**: This is an interim design submittal; no closure or remediation completion is claimed. The plan is subject to further refinement (final design).  
- **Conditions**: The RDIP is marked “NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION” – it is a 65% design milestone.  
- **Monitoring obligations**: Groundwater monitoring wells are to be abandoned (MW5, MW7, MW8) or protected in place (MW1, MW2, MW3, MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10) during construction. Ongoing groundwater monitoring is implied but not detailed in this plan.  
- **Land use restrictions**: Not established in this interim phase; future use of the consolidation zone is likely to be transportation-related right-of-way with a paved surface.  
- **Required follow-on deliverables**: Final design, construction plans, and potentially a Remedial Action Completion Report (RACR) after implementation.
