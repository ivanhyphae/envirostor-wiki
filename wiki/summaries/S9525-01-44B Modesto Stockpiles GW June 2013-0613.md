## Document identity
- **Report type:** Groundwater monitoring round (quarterly event)
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Reporting period or sampling date(s):** June 5–6, 2013
- **Project number or task order:** Project No. S9525-01-44B, Task Order No. 44, EA No. 10-0X2700, Contract No. 06A1580

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Groundwater
- **Wells, stations, or locations sampled (list IDs):** MW‑1, MW‑2, MW‑3, MW‑4, MW‑5, MW‑6, MW‑7, MW‑8, MW‑9, MW‑10
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled, and why:** None. All ten wells were sampled. (Note: MW‑5 and MW‑6 went dry during purging but were allowed to recover and were sampled.)

## Results
- **Analytes measured, with concentration ranges and units:**
  - *Dissolved metals (Title 22 + strontium) in µg/L:*
    - Barium: 15–410 µg/L (Primary MCL = 1000 µg/L)
    - Strontium: 170–1200 µg/L (no MCL; compared to EPA Health Advisory = 4000 µg/L)
    - Lead: not detected ≥ PQL (1.0 µg/L) in any sample
    - Arsenic: 0.89–3.5 µg/L (Primary MCL = 10 µg/L)
    - Chromium: 0.50–1.6 µg/L (Primary MCL = 50 µg/L)
    - Vanadium: 0.63–4.3 µg/L (Notification Level = 50 µg/L)
    - Nickel: detected in 9 of 10 wells (max 5.0 µg/L; EPA Health Advisory = 100 µg/L)
    - Selenium: detected in 8 of 10 wells (max 3.5 µg/L; Primary MCL = 50 µg/L)
    - Molybdenum: detected in 7 of 10 wells (max 3.5 µg/L; no MCL)
    - Manganese: detected in 1 of 10 wells (3.5 µg/L; Secondary MCL = 50 µg/L)
    - Copper, antimony, cobalt: detected at low levels; all below thresholds
    - Beryllium, cadmium, silver, thallium, zinc, mercury: not detected ≥ PQL
  - *General minerals in mg/L:*
    - Nitrate as N: 3.3–20 mg/L (Primary MCL = 10 mg/L)
    - Total Dissolved Solids (TDS): 340–620 mg/L (Secondary MCL = 500 mg/L)
    - Chloride, sulfate, calcium, magnesium, sodium, potassium, alkalinity, sulfide reported
- **Applicable thresholds (MCLs, background, action levels, screening levels):**
  - California Department of Public Health (CDPH) Primary MCLs for drinking water
  - CDPH Secondary MCLs (taste/odor)
  - EPA Drinking Water Health Advisories
  - CDPH Regulatory Action Levels and Notification Levels
- **Which locations exceeded thresholds and by how much:**
  - *Nitrate (Primary MCL = 10 mg/L):* MW‑1 (12 mg/L), MW‑5, MW‑6, MW‑9, MW‑10 (20 mg/L each for MW‑6 and MW‑10)
  - *TDS (Secondary MCL = 500 mg/L):* MW‑1 (520 mg/L), MW‑5, MW‑6, MW‑10 (620 mg/L each for MW‑6 and MW‑10)
  - No dissolved metals exceeded any threshold. Barium and strontium remained well below their respective thresholds.
- **Trends versus prior rounds, if reported:**
  - Barium and strontium concentrations were similar to historical levels.
  - Lead was not detected (consistent with prior events).
  - Stiff diagrams show very slight changes in ionic content since March 2012; water types remain consistent (Ca‑dominant: MW‑1, MW‑2, MW‑4, MW‑5, MW‑7, MW‑9; Na‑dominant: MW‑3, MW‑6, MW‑8).
  - Nitrate and TDS exceedances are persistent in the same wells.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Dry wells, low recovery, depth-to-water, gradient/flow observations:**
  - Depth to groundwater: 31.73 ft (MW‑1) to 40.11 ft (MW‑5) below TOC.
  - Groundwater flow direction: east‑southeast; average gradient 0.0005 ft/ft (consistent with historical).
  - *MW‑5:* Went dry after purging 1 gallon; redeveloped with surge block; purged in three cycles, each followed by recovery; sample collected.
  - *MW‑6:* Went dry after purging 1.75 gallons; allowed to recover twice; sample collected.
- **Sampling or analytical deviations, data qualifiers, detection‑limit issues:**
  - Equipment blank contained nickel at 1.8 µg/L; judged not to significantly influence sample results because nickel concentrations in samples were similar to prior events.
  - Several matrix spike / matrix spike duplicate (MS/MSD) recoveries or RPDs were outside laboratory control limits, but laboratory control samples (LCS) were acceptable; no data qualification was applied.
  - Data considered sufficient quality for report purposes.
- **Anything affecting interpretation of the results:** None noted.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusions (by Geocon):**
  - None of the dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality thresholds.
  - Barium and strontium remain similar to historical levels and well below thresholds.
  - Lead not detected ≥ PQL.
  - Nitrate and TDS exceed primary/secondary MCLs in certain wells (MW‑1, MW‑5, MW‑6, MW‑9, MW‑10 for nitrate; MW‑1, MW‑5, MW‑6, MW‑10 for TDS). Upgradient well MW‑1 also shows elevated nitrate and TDS, suggesting possible background influence.
  - Stiff diagrams indicate consistent groundwater types since March 2012.
- **Recommendations (by Geocon):**
  - Continue quarterly groundwater monitoring; next event scheduled for September 2013.
