The Caltrans Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project (S9650-06-03), targeting the northbound SR99 off-ramp to Kansas Avenue, requires the expansion of ramp shoulders, drainage improvements, and construction of a retaining wall. This area, historically impacted by chemical processing from the 1930s-1970s, contains fill embankments (including Stockpile 3) made from native soil and pond tailings contaminated with barium, strontium sulfate, and sodium sulfide. Previous investigations (2003-2009) by Geocon revealed high barium concentrations (up to 44,300 mg/kg, with localized detections exceeding 124,000 mg/kg) in Stockpile 3, though the DTSC deemed it not a significant health risk. Nevertheless, DTSC and CVRWQCB mandated an Interim Remedial Action Workplan (IRAW) due to the elevated contaminants, necessitating the excavation and stabilization of approximately 3,539 cubic yards of soil from Stockpile 3.

To address this, Geocon's April 2012 workplan outlines pre-field and field activities, including a Health and Safety Plan, regulatory notifications, and 19 soil borings in Stockpile 3 for continuous sampling and expedited analysis. Samples will be analyzed for Title 22 metals (including strontium), soluble metals, PAHs, sulfate, nitrate, sulfide, and pH. Historical data shows elevated levels of Total Strontium (105-19,300 mg/kg), Total Chromium (2.9-18 mg/kg), and Total Barium (22-124,000 mg/kg), with some leachable barium exceeding thresholds. Further detailed analyses from 2006-2012, compiled collaboratively by Geocon Consultants and Advanced Technology Laboratories, confirm significant levels of barium, lead, nickel, and sulfate in the stockpiles, often exceeding OEHHA screening levels, though most PAHs were not detected. Recent specific analyses by FGL Environmental in February 2012 of soil samples showed nitrate as N as not detected, sulfate at 14.3 mg/kg, and total sulfide as not detected. Following the investigation, a draft IRAW will be submitted for regulatory approval, including public outreach, by June 22, 2012.
