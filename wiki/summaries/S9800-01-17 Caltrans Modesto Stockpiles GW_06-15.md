## Document identity
- **Report type:** Groundwater monitoring round
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Reporting period or sampling date(s):** April 30, 2015
- **Project number or task order:** Geocon Project No. S9800-01-17, Task Order No. 17, Contract No. 06A1895, EA No. 10-0X2700

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Groundwater
- **Wells, stations, or locations sampled (list IDs):** MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-7
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled, and why:**
  - MW-3 – dry
  - MW-5 – dry
  - MW-6 – insufficient water to purge and sample
  - MW-8 – dry
  - MW-9 – dry
  - MW-10 – dry

## Results
- **Analytes measured, with concentration ranges and units:**
  - **Dissolved metals (Title 22):**
    - Barium (Ba): 80 – 360 µg/L (PQL ~2 µg/L)
    - Lead (Pb): not detected ≥ PQL in any sample
    - Strontium (Sr): 770 – 1,600 µg/L
    - Chromium (Cr): 4.1 – 6.9 µg/L
    - Vanadium (V): 2.2 – 5.8 µg/L
    - Arsenic (As): detected in MW-2 (2.6 µg/L) and MW-4 (3.6 µg/L); not detected in MW-1 or MW-7
    - Manganese (Mn): detected only in MW-4 (27 µg/L)
    - Molybdenum (Mo): detected in MW-1 (15 µg/L), MW-2 (5.8 µg/L), MW-4 (22 µg/L); not detected in MW-7
    - Nickel (Ni): detected in MW-1 (1.6 µg/L), MW-2 (1.9 µg/L), MW-4 (11 µg/L); not detected in MW-7
    - Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, copper, selenium, silver, thallium, zinc, mercury – not detected ≥ PQL in any sample
  - **General minerals:**
    - Nitrate as nitrogen (NO3-N): 7.0 – 17 mg/L
    - Total dissolved solids (TDS): 490 – 660 mg/L
    - Sulfide: 0.015 – 0.13 mg/L
    - Dissolved calcium, magnesium, potassium, sodium, chloride, sulfate, bicarbonate alkalinity – reported on Table 3.
- **Applicable thresholds (MCLs, background, action levels, screening levels):**
  - California Primary MCLs (CDPH, updated February 2015)
  - California Secondary MCLs (CDPH)
  - EPA Drinking Water Health Advisories
  - CDPH Regulatory Action Level (for strontium: 4,000 µg/L — referred to as "Water Quality Threshold" on Figure 6)
  - CDPH Notification Level for Drinking Water
- **Which locations exceeded thresholds and by how much:**
  - **Nitrate (as N):** MW-1 (17 mg/L) and MW-2 (13 mg/L) exceeded the Primary MCL of 10 mg/L.
  - **TDS:** MW-1 (660 mg/L) and MW-4 (540 mg/L) exceeded the Secondary MCL of 500 mg/L.
  - **Barium, chromium, strontium, vanadium, arsenic, manganese, molybdenum, nickel:** No reported concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality thresholds.
- **Trends versus prior rounds, if reported:**
  - Barium and strontium concentrations were similar to historical levels and remained significantly less than thresholds.
  - Other dissolved metals also at concentrations similar to historical levels.
  - Stiff diagrams (2012 through April 2015) show very slight changes in ionic content; samples from MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, and MW-7 have consistently been calcium-dominant.
  - Groundwater flow direction (south, gradient ~0.004) consistent with historical flow.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Dry wells, low recovery, depth-to-water, gradient/flow observations:**
  - Depth to groundwater ranged from 36.85 ft (MW-1) to 41.26 ft (MW-7) below TOC.
  - Groundwater flow direction: toward the south; average gradient: 0.004.
  - Wells MW-3, MW-5, MW-8, MW-9, and MW-10 were dry.
  - MW-6 had insufficient water to purge and sample.
  - No signs of tampering or required maintenance at well boxes.
- **Sampling or analytical deviations, data qualifiers, detection-limit issues:**
  - Samples collected using disposable bailers; dissolved metals field-filtered (0.45 µm).
  - Purge water disposed offsite at Inviro-Tec Disposal, Lincoln, CA.
  - Laboratory QA/QC reviewed: method blank surrogate recoveries acceptable; no method blank contamination; laboratory control sample recoveries appropriate.
  - Several MS/MSD analytes had recoveries or RPDs outside control limits, but sample results were validated by LCS; no data qualification necessary.
  - Data considered of sufficient quality for reporting purposes.
- **Anything affecting interpretation of the results:**
  - Six of ten monitoring wells were dry or had insufficient water, limiting spatial coverage to four wells.
  - MW-1 (upgradient) had the highest nitrate (17 mg/L), potentially representing natural background nitrate in shallow groundwater.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusions drawn by the preparer (Geocon):**
  - No dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality thresholds.
  - Barium and strontium concentrations similar to historical levels and well below thresholds.
  - Nitrate in MW-1 (17 mg/L) and MW-2 (13 mg/L) exceeded the 10 mg/L Primary MCL; TDS in MW-1 (660 mg/L) and MW-4 (540 mg/L) exceeded the 500 mg/L Secondary MCL.
  - Upgradient MW-1 nitrate may reflect natural background.
  - Stiff diagrams show consistent calcium-dominant water type since 2012.
- **Recommendations made by the preparer (Geocon):**
  - Continue annual groundwater monitoring (next event: April 2016).
  - Continue semiannual depth-to-water measurements (next event: October 2015).
