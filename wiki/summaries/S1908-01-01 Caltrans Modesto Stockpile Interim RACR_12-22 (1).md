### Summary of the Interim Removal Action Completion Report for the Caltrans Modesto Stockpile

This document is an **Interim Removal Action Completion Report (RACR)** , including an **Operations and Maintenance (O&M) Plan**, prepared by **Geocon Consultants, Inc.** for **Caltrans** in December 2022 (Project S1908-01-01). It documents the completion of **Interim Phase 1** of remediation at the Modesto Stockpile site, a former **FMC** facility. The site contained three soil stockpiles (totaling ~160,000 yd³) contaminated primarily with **barium** (up to 72,000 mg/kg) and aerially deposited lead (ADL). The report fulfills milestones under the 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) and 2018 Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP), with oversight from the **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)** and the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB)** .

**Selected Remedy and Construction:** The chosen remedy was containment with capping. During Interim Phase 1, Stockpile 3 was excavated and consolidated into Stockpiles 1 and 2, which were then covered with a clean fill cap and a structural pavement section (including an aggregate base layer and geomembrane cover). Verification soil sampling confirmed that residual barium and lead concentrations met the RDIP thresholds (1,000 mg/kg and 80 mg/kg, respectively). The highway embankment (two traffic lanes) was opened to traffic on November 3, 2022.

**Future Work and Site Management:** Site closure is not final. An **Ultimate Phase 2** widening is planned, after which a **Final RACR** will be submitted (scheduled for 2028). A DTSC-required **Land Use Covenant (LUC)** will restrict the site to transportation use, prohibiting residential or sensitive uses and any disturbance of the contained material without prior DTSC authorization. A **Land Use Restriction** specifies that any work disturbing the barium-containing soil (BCS) must be documented and reported to DTSC within 30 days.

**Long-Term Monitoring and Maintenance:** Caltrans is responsible for ongoing stewardship, including:
- **Annual inspections** before the rainy season (reported by January 31st), plus supplemental checks after significant events (fires, earthquakes, etc.).
- **Groundwater and stormwater monitoring.**
- **Five-year reviews** to confirm the cap’s effectiveness.
- Repairs (completed within 30 days unless DTSC approves an extension) under a formal **O&M Agreement** with DTSC.

The document establishes the interim containment system as protective, while defining the long-term obligations for Caltrans to ensure continued protection of human health and the environment.
