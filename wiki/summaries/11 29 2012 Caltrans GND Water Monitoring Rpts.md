Here is a structured summary of the source document for your case-file wiki.

## Document identity
- **Report type:** Regulatory concurrence letter regarding three separate groundwater monitoring reports (May, June, and July 2012) and an additional well installation report (June 2012).
- **Preparer / author organization:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program. Prepared by Randy S. Adams, C.E.G., Senior Engineering Geologist.
- **Reporting period or sampling date(s):** May 2012, June 2012, and July 2012.
- **Project number or task order:** Not stated.

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Groundwater.
- **Wells, stations, or locations sampled:** The reports cover sampling of existing monitoring wells at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles (SR 99 and SR 132). The June 2012 report specifically discusses the installation of two new wells:
    - **MW-9:** Located south of Kansas Avenue and west of SR 99.
    - **MW-10:** Located south of Kansas Avenue and east of SR 99.
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled:** Not stated in this concurrence letter.

## Results
- **Analytes measured:** Barium, lead, and strontium (identified as the primary chemicals of concern, COC).
- **Applicable thresholds:** Not stated in this letter.
- **Which locations exceeded thresholds and by how much:** Not stated in this letter.
- **Trends versus prior rounds:** Not stated in this letter.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- Not discussed in this concurrence letter.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusions:** The DTSC, in consultation with the RWQCB, reviewed the three submitted reports.
- **Recommendations:** The DTSC **concurs** with the subject groundwater monitoring reports. DTSC requests that future groundwater monitoring reports be completed as groundwater data becomes available.
    - *Attributed to:* Randy S. Adams, C.E.G., Senior Engineering Geologist, DTSC, with cc’s to Ms. Sam Haack (Caltrans), Ms. Nicole Damin (Stanislaus County), Mr. Steven Meeks (RWQCB), and Mr. Steven R. Becker (DTSC).

## Responsible parties
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans):** Owner/operator; responsible for preparing and submitting the groundwater monitoring reports (via Geocon Consultants).
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC):** Lead regulatory agency; reviewer and concurring authority for the reports.
- **Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB):** Co-regulator; consulted by DTSC during review.
- **Stanislaus County Health Agency:** Included as a courtesy copy recipient.
- **Geocon Consultants:** Prepared the groundwater monitoring reports for Caltrans.
- **Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC):** Historical operator of the parcel from which the stockpile soils originated; named as the source of the barium, lead, and strontium contamination.
