## Document identity
- **Plan type:** Workplan (Technical Memorandum)
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon (John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager)
- **Date:** February 28, 2022
- **Project number or task order:** Not stated; project is “State Route 132 Project, Modesto, California”

## Objective and scope
- **Purpose of the work and what prompted it:** To address proper management, disposal, and/or reuse of barium-containing soil (BCS) spoils generated from foundation excavations within the southern portion of the Stockpile 2 BCS Containment Zone. The work was prompted by the generation of BCS material from the Weather Station foundation slab/anchor (completed July/August 2021, ~2 yd³) and the planned Overhead Sign #1 foundation excavation (~18.2 yd³, 25 ft deep).
- **Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed:** Southern portion of the Stockpile 2 BCS Containment Zone; two specific features: Weather Station Foundation Slab/Anchor (approximate Station 1138+60) and Overhead Sign #1 (approximate Station 1139+30). Media: soil (BCS foundation spoils). Total estimated volume: ~40 yd³.
- **What approval or decision the plan supports or requests:** Seeks Caltrans and DTSC approval for either onsite reuse (if analytical results meet site-specific background levels) or offsite landfill disposal (if reuse is not approved or if concentrations exceed thresholds).

## Proposed approach
- **Sampling design:**
  - **Locations/Counts:** Eight discrete soil samples collected from the stockpile on a grid pattern at various depths.
  - **Methods:** Samples collected in labelled stainless steel sample tubes, placed in a chilled cooler, transported to California Laboratory Services under chain-of-custody. Laboratory will composite the eight discrete samples into two 4-part composite samples.
  - **Target analytes:** Title 22 metals per EPA Method 6010B (metals) and EPA Method 7471A (mercury).
  - **Depths:** Not specified beyond “various depths.”
  - **Turnaround time:** Expedited 48-hour turnaround.
  - **Contingency analysis:** Soluble metals testing using Waste Extraction Test (WET) and Toxicity Characteristic Leaching Procedure (TCLP) may be required if total metal concentrations are elevated, to determine if material is a California or Federal RCRA hazardous waste.
- **Decision criteria or action levels proposed:**
  - Total barium compared to site-specific background level: 120 mg/kg.
  - Total lead compared to site-specific background level: 3.8 mg/kg.
  - If both barium and lead do not exceed respective background levels, the stockpiled BCS may be suitable for onsite reuse, subject to Caltrans and DTSC approval of the reuse location.
  - If concentrations exceed background levels, or if reuse is not approved, the material will be profiled for offsite landfill disposal.
- **Sequence, schedule, or phasing of the work:**
  - Stockpile pre-moistened BCS material on ≥6-mil plastic sheeting, covered with plastic, secured with ropes/weights, surrounded by erosion control.
  - Collect eight discrete samples → composite into two 4-part composites → analyze for Title 22 metals (48-hour turnaround).
  - If soluble metals testing (WET/TCLP) is needed, it will be performed after total metals results are available.
  - Based on results, determine reuse or disposal pathway.
  - After final disposition, prepare a letter summary report.
- **For a variance:** Not applicable (no variance requested).

## Deliverables and approvals sought
- **Deliverables:**
  - Letter summary report following completed disposition of the BCS stockpile material, including:
    - Description of completed BCS disturbance activities
    - Locations and depth of BCS disturbance
    - Volume of BCS material removed from the Stockpile 2 Containment Zone
    - Site plan depicting BCS disturbance areas
    - Copies of BCS material characterization data (laboratory reports and chain-of-custody documentation)
    - Copies of landfill waste disposal manifests and tonnage receipts
    - Site photographs
  - This information will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report.
- **Approvals sought:** The plan is submitted to Caltrans and DTSC for approval of the proposed sampling, reuse, or disposal approach.

- **DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control):** Co-regulator; must approve reuse location.
- **Central Valley RWQCB:** Not explicitly named in this memo, but referenced as co-regulator in the broader project context.
- **WSP/City of Modesto (Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager):** Recipient of the technical memorandum; oversight role.
- **Geocon (John E. Juhrend):** Preparer of the workplan; project manager.
- **California Laboratory Services:** Analytical laboratory; performs Title 22 metals analysis and compositing.
- **Forward Landfill (Manteca) or other accepting landfill:** Disposal facility for BCS material if not reused.
- **Transporter:** Licensed; maintains appropriate licensing; trucks must have covered loads before leaving the project site.
