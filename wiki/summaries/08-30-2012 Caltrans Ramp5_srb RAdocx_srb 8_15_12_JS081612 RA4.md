## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** DTSC concurrence letter responding to Caltrans’ request for approval of offsite disposal of excavated material from the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project (SR 99 – Kansas Avenue Northbound Off-Ramp). The letter conveys DTSC’s finding that the proposed disposal does not pose a risk to human health or the environment.
- **Author or preparer:** Steven R. Becker, P.G., Chief, Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program, DTSC. Prepared for Ms. Sam Haack, P.E., Caltrans District 10 Project Manager.
- **Date or period covered:** Letter dated August 30, 2012. References actions and documents from April 2012 through August 2012.

## Key claims
- Barium, strontium, and lead in Stockpile No. 3 are below residential and commercial/industrial screening levels, except one sample with elevated lead exceeding residential screening levels.
- Arsenic exceeds both residential and commercial/industrial screening levels but is below background concentrations.
- The 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) concentrations for barium, strontium, and lead in the area to be excavated are below residential and commercial/industrial screening levels.
- The proposed offsite disposal of Stockpile No. 3 material does not pose a risk to human health or the environment, given Caltrans’ implementation of CEQA, dust control, and storm water management.
- DTSC’s concurrence does not absolve Caltrans from implementing existing Remedial Action Plan (RAP) activities for the SR 132 West Expressway/Freeway stockpiles.

## Regulatory decisions
- **Agency:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB).
- **Decision:** DTSC concurs with Caltrans’ proposed disposal of approximately 2,800 cubic yards of material from Stockpile No. 3 to the Forward Inc. Landfill in Manteca, CA.
- **Conditions:** Caltrans must continue to implement CEQA, dust control (approved Dust Control Plan by San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution District), and storm water management requirements. Caltrans remains obligated to implement RAP activities under the December 17, 2009 DTSC letter and Consultation Agreement No. 43A0260 / DTSC No. 08-T3616 (June 22, 2012).
- **Date of decision:** August 30, 2012.

## Analytical results
- **Contaminants of concern (COC):** barium, lead, strontium (primary); arsenic also noted.
- **Media:** Soil from Stockpile No. 3.
- **Concentration ranges:** Not explicitly given; only exceedance status reported.
- **Thresholds:**
  - Barium, strontium, lead: below residential and commercial/industrial screening levels.
  - Lead: one sample exceeded residential screening levels.
  - Arsenic: exceeds residential and commercial/industrial screening levels but below background.
  - 95% UCL for barium, strontium, lead: below both residential and commercial/industrial screening levels.
- **Exceedances:** One sample lead above residential; arsenic above both thresholds (but below background).

## Remedial actions
- **Activity:** Excavation of approximately 6,000 cubic yards total for ramp safety improvements (extending off-ramp lane, improving curve radius, widening shoulder, constructing retaining wall). Of this, approximately 2,800 cubic yards come from Stockpile No. 3.
- **Disposal:** Offsite management at Forward Inc. Landfill, Manteca, Stanislaus County.
- **Phase status:** Proposed; DTSC concurrence granted.
- **Verification outcomes:** Sampling and analysis by Geocon Consultants Inc. (April 2012) confirmed concentrations below screening levels (except as noted).

## Responsible parties
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans):** Owner/operator, project proponent, obligated to implement CEQA, dust control, storm water management, and RAP activities.
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC):** Regulatory oversight, concurrence authority.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB):** Co-regulator, consulted.
- **Geocon Consultants Inc.:** Caltrans contractor – site investigation, sampling.
- **Teichert Construction:** Caltrans contractor – contaminated material removal plan.
- **Forward Inc. Landfill:** Disposal facility.
- **San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution District:** Approved Dust Control Plan.
- **Stanislaus County Health Agency:** Notified.
- **Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC) Inc.:** Historical property owner (purchased parcel with evaporation pond).

## Key terms and conditions
- Caltrans must implement all aspects of construction activity, including CEQA, dust control (per approved Dust Control Plan DCP 826), and storm water management.
- DTSC’s concurrence is specific to the ramp construction and does not relieve Caltrans of obligations under the earlier Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for SR 132 West Expressway/Freeway stockpiles (December 17, 2009 letter; Consultation Agreement No. 43A0260 / DTSC No. 08-T3616, June 22, 2012).
- Offsite disposal must follow Special Waste Profile (Republic Services, July 30, 2012).
- No further action required for the ramp project itself regarding Stockpile No. 3 material.
