Here is a structured summary of the source document for the case-file wiki.

## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Clarification letter from Caltrans to regulators (DTSC and RWQCB) regarding the disposition of excavated material from a ramp safety improvement project. The purpose is to explain why an Interim Remedial (Removal) Action Workplan is unnecessary and to detail the proposed management of the excavated soil.
- **Author or preparer:** Ms. Sam Haack, P.E., Project Manager, California Department of Transportation (Caltrans), District 10.
- **Date or period covered:** Letter dated August 24, 2012. References site investigation data from April 24, 2012.

## Key claims
- The results of additional investigations and the proposed material management plan demonstrate that completion and implementation of an Interim Removal Action Workplan is not needed for the ramp project.
- None of the soil samples from the Stockpile No. 3 excavation area contained total metal concentrations at or above the California hazardous waste Total Threshold Limit Concentration (TTLC).
- With the exception of arsenic, no metals were reported at concentrations at or above commercial/industrial screening levels. Arsenic concentrations (range: <1.0 mg/kg to 3.2 mg/kg) are within site-specific background levels.
- The 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) concentrations for barium, strontium, and lead in the Stockpile No. 3 excavation area are below residential and commercial/industrial screening levels.
- While one soil sample had a barium concentration higher than 10 times the Soluble Threshold Limit Concentration (STLC) and five samples had lead concentrations at or higher than 10 times the STLC, the calculated 95% UCL for barium and lead is well below 10 times the respective STLC. Waste Extraction Test (WET) results for these samples fell below the STLC, confirming the material is not classified as hazardous waste.
- Native soil excavated outside the Stockpile No. 3 footprint is suitable for reuse as structural backfill or for offsite disposal as non-hazardous soil.

## Regulatory decisions
- **Decision:** DTSC and RWQCB are informed that an Interim Removal Action Workplan is not needed for the ramp project.
- **Agency:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC); Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB).
- **Date:** August 24, 2012.
- **Conditions imposed (implicit):** Caltrans will provide a copy of all soil analysis information to regulators.

## Analytical results
- **Contaminants:** Barium, lead, arsenic, strontium.
- **Media:** Soil (from Stockpile No. 3 excavation area).
- **Concentration ranges:**
    - **Arsenic:** <1.0 mg/kg to 3.2 mg/kg.
- **Thresholds and exceedances:**
    - **TTLC (Total Threshold Limit Concentration):** No samples exceeded TTLC for any metal.
    - **Commercial/Industrial Screening Levels:** No metals exceeded these levels, except arsenic, which was within site-specific background.
    - **Residential and Commercial/Industrial Screening Levels:** The 95% UCL for barium, strontium, and lead was below these levels.
    - **10x STLC (Soluble Threshold Limit Concentration):** One soil sample exceeded 10x STLC for barium; five samples exceeded it for lead. However, the 95% UCL for both was below 10x STLC. Subsequent Waste Extraction Test (WET) analysis showed these samples fell below the STLC, confirming non-hazardous classification.

## Remedial actions
- **Activity:** Excavation of material for ramp safety improvements (widened roadbed, drainage, retaining wall).
- **Quantities:** Estimated total excavation of 6,000 cubic yards. Of this, approximately 2,800 cubic yards are from the northwestern end of Stockpile No. 3.
- **Phase status:** Pre-construction; material management planned, not yet executed.
- **Verification outcomes:** WET analysis confirmed material from Stockpile No. 3 is non-hazardous.

## Responsible parties
- **Caltrans (California Department of Transportation):** Owner/operator of the site, lead project manager, and author of the clarification letter.
- **Teichert Construction:** Named as a project contact (contractor).
- **Geocon:** Prepared the site investigation data report ("Transmittal of Site Investigation Data..." dated 4/24/2012).
- **Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL):** Performed the Waste Extraction Test (WET) analysis.

## Key terms and conditions
- **Offsite disposal:** All excavated material from Stockpile No. 3 will be transported offsite.
- **Disposal facilities:**
    - **Fink Road Landfill (Class III):** For excess native soil not used as backfill.
    - **Forward Inc. (Forward) in Manteca, CA (Class II landfill):** For excess soil from Stockpile No. 3, due to site-specific limitations at Fink Road Landfill. Acceptance is documented in an attached Republic Services Special Waste Department Decision.
- **Backfill:** Some of the excavated native soil (outside Stockpile No. 3) will be used as structural backfill for the ramp construction.
- **Documentation:** Caltrans will provide all soil analysis information to the regulators.
