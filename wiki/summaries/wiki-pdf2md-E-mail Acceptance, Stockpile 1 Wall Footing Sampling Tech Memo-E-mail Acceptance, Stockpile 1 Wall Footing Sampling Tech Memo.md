## Decision or request
- **What is being approved/accepted**: DTSC and Water Board acceptance of the Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020, describing the sampling along the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area and the laboratory results of their analysis.
- **Document acted on**: "Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated April 6, 2020" (Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Footing Soil Characterization technical memo)
- **Conditions**: Both agencies agreed with the last paragraph of the Memo **except** for using the MSE wall footing soil as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material. Because the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil exceeds the maximum background of 120 mg/kg and the MSE wall footing falls outside the designated borrow area described in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, this material must be placed within Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill.
- **Effective date**: April 7, 2020 (email date of acceptance)

## Open items and next actions
- Geocon stated: "Once the Stockpile 2 BCS southerly slope material is removed we will complete the wall footing excavation sampling in that area." (Geocon's April 6 email)
- The acceptance is conditioned on field execution of the restriction on using MSE wall footing soil as capping material.
