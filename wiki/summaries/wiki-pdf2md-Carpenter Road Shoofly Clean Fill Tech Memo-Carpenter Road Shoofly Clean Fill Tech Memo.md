This document synthesizes two technical memos and associated Chain of Custody (COC) records regarding the suitability of soil from the Carpenter Road Shoofly alignment for use as clean fill, specifically as capping material over barium-containing soil.

The first evaluation by Golder Associates Inc. (January 22, 2018) analyzed ten soil samples for Title 22 metals, TPH, and VOCs. All constituents, including barium (120 to 1,200 mg/kg total), were below regulatory thresholds, leading to the conclusion that the soil was suitable as clean fill for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remediation Project.

A second, more targeted evaluation by Geocon Consultants, Inc. (April 12, 2021) assessed approximately 5,000 cubic yards of soil for its suitability as clean capping material over barium-containing soil in Stockpiles 1 and 2, contingent on DTSC approval. Twenty-four discrete soil samples, composited into eight, were analyzed for barium (28 to 96 mg/kg) and lead (1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg). All barium concentrations were below the site-specific background of 120 mg/kg, and all lead concentrations were at or below the site-specific background of 3.8 mg/kg. Geocon concluded the soil met the DTSC-approved clean cap criteria.

Supporting Chain of Custody records (April 7-8 & April 21, 2021) detail the transfer of Geocon's samples to ATL Laboratories for various analyses, including organics and Title 22 Metals. These records confirm the requested analyses and provide some specific barium and lead results for composite samples.

The technical memorandum also includes a quality control (QC) assessment for lead and barium testing using EPA 6000/7000 Series Methods. All QC samples (Blank, LCS, MS, MSD) demonstrated acceptable performance: analytes were not detected in the Blank, LCS recoveries for lead (105%) and barium (107%) were within 75-125%, MS/MSD recoveries were also within limits, and the Relative Percent Difference (RPD) for both lead (3%) and barium (0.8%) was well within the 30% limit, indicating good precision. The document further defines key abbreviations and terms for clarity.

In summary, both evaluations, supported by robust QC data, independently concluded that the soil from the Carpenter Road Shoofly alignment is suitable for reuse as clean fill or capping material, meeting applicable regulatory thresholds and site-specific background criteria for contaminants, with Geocon's recommendation requiring DTSC approval.
