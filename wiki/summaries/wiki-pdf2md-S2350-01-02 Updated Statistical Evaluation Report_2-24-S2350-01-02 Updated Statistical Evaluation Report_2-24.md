# Document Summary: Updated Comparative Evaluation of Groundwater Data

## Document identity
- **Report type:** Statistical evaluation / comparative evaluation of groundwater monitoring data
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Reporting period:** Update of 2014 evaluation incorporating data collected through February 2023
- **Project number:** Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2, EA No. 10-1E7003

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Groundwater
- **Wells sampled (remaining):** MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10
- **Wells decommissioned prior to this evaluation (2019):** MW1, MW2, MW3, MW5, MW7, MW8
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled:** All four remaining wells were dry on multiple occasions (see field conditions)

## Results
**Analytes measured with concentration ranges (post-2014 data only):**

| Analyte | MW4 | MW6 | MW9 | MW10 | Units |
|---------|-----|-----|-----|------|-------|
| **TDS** | 450–550 | 570–640 | 560–570 | 630 (single sample) | mg/L |
| **Sulfate (SO4)** | 20–34 | 45–48 | 42–52 | 67 (single sample) | mg/L |
| **Barium (Ba)** | 140–160 | 99–130 | 160–180 | 140 (single sample) | µg/L |
| **Strontium (Sr)** | 760–960 | 700–810 | 1,000–1,100 | 1,100 (single sample) | µg/L |

**Applicable thresholds:**
- **Barium:** US EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory (DWHA) = 700 µg/L; FMC site-specific target level = 6,210 µg/L (approved by Central Valley RWQCB)
- **Strontium:** US EPA DWHA = 4,000 µg/L; FMC site-specific target level = 61,900 µg/L
- **TDS:** Secondary MCL = 500 mg/L
- **Sulfate:** Secondary MCL = 250 mg/L

**Exceedances:**
- TDS exceeded Secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in several samples: MW6 (570–640 mg/L), MW10 (630 mg/L), MW9 (560–570 mg/L), MW4 occasionally (530–550 mg/L)
- Sulfate, barium, and strontium remained below respective thresholds in all samples
- Barium concentrations were comparable to FMC upgradient background concentration of 151 µg/L

**Trends versus prior rounds:**
- TDS, sulfate, barium, and strontium concentrations remained stable and consistent with pre-2014 data
- Sulfate concentrations in remaining wells have been <50 mg/L since 2019
- Barium concentrations well below both DWHA and FMC site-specific target levels
- Strontium concentrations approximately 4× below DWHA and >20× below FMC site-specific target level

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- Due to decreasing regional groundwater levels, the four remaining shallow Caltrans wells were dry on numerous occasions:
  - MW4: dry Sept 2014, May 2016, Mar 2022–Feb 2023 (4 of 11 measurements)
  - MW6: dry Sept 2014–Apr 2017, Mar 2022–Feb 2023 (6 of 11 measurements)
  - MW9: dry Sept 2014–Apr 2017, Apr 2019, Mar 2021–Feb 2023 (8 of 11 measurements)
  - MW10: dry Sept 2014–Apr 2017, Apr 2019–Feb 2023 (9 of 11 measurements)
- Last successful samples: MW4 and MW6 in March 2021; MW9 in February 2022; MW10 in April 2018
- Caltrans wells screened across first-encountered groundwater (41–48 ft bgs, ~10 ft screen); FMC wells screened deeper (45–55 ft bgs, 20–30 ft screen) in more continuous units
- No sampling or analytical deviations reported beyond dry conditions; data qualifiers not noted

## Conclusions and recommendations
**Conclusions (Geocon):**
1. FMC constituents within the Caltrans soil stockpiles have not impacted groundwater, consistent with the 2014 Evaluation
2. Groundwater data from remaining wells confirm constituent concentrations have remained stable
3. Due to declining water table, wells are frequently dry during annual sampling events
4. Encapsulated soil stockpiles do not have the potential to impact groundwater
5. Future monitoring data will not provide useful additional information

**Recommendations (Geocon):**
- Decommission the remaining four Caltrans monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10)
- Discontinue groundwater monitoring related to the soil stockpiles
- Phase II construction (scheduled 2026) will fully encapsulate stockpiles beneath highway pavement and will require removal of MW9 (retaining wall) and MW10 (pump station)
