## Decision or request
- **Request:** DTSC approval of the proposed sampling plan for the MSE wall footing excavations at Stockpiles 1 and 2.
- **Document acted on:** “Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan” (PDF attachment, referenced as “plan sheets”).
- **Plan details:**
  - 10 sampling locations spaced at ~200-ft intervals along the wall footing excavation.
  - Discrete soil samples collected from 1-ft intervals to the planned excavation depth (~4 to 5 ft).
  - Analytes: barium and lead, with 48-hour expedited turnaround.
  - Results compared to:
    - Barium: site-specific background 120 mg/kg; RDIP BCS removal threshold 1,000 mg/kg (groundwater protection); construction worker ESL 3,000 mg/kg; residential ESL 15,000 mg/kg; commercial ESL 220,000 mg/kg.
    - Lead: site-specific background 3.8 mg/kg; DTSC residential 80 mg/kg.
- **No conditions or contingencies stated** in this correspondence; approval is requested without qualification.
- **Effective date/deadline:** Not specified; request is “as soon as possible.”

## Open items and next actions
- DTSC must provide approval before Geocon can begin sampling.
- Geocon will proceed with sampling after the BCS slope zone material is removed (work on Stockpile 1 southern slope zone BCS excavation began March 31, 2020).
- No stated due date for approval or subsequent submittals.
