## Thread / participants
Single correspondence (not an email thread). This letter responds to a DTSC letter dated March 1, 2012.

## Decision or request
Caltrans acknowledges DTSC’s March 1, 2012 request and describes actions taken or planned for each item:

1. **Limit access to authorized personnel**  
   - Fence around stockpile #3: installation of chain‑link fence and locking gates on the west side is underway; completion expected “early next week.” “No Trespassing” signs will be posted.  
   - Perimeter fences for all stockpiles will be inspected every two weeks; an on‑call fence contractor will be authorized to make repairs (with before/after photos).  
   - Trash and debris inside each fenced area will be inspected and removed quarterly.  
   - All inspections and repairs will be recorded in a monthly **Site Report** submitted to DTSC.

2. **Maintain current grade and vegetative cover**  
   - No activities will alter existing grade or drainage.  
   - For bare‑soil areas on stockpile #2 (south side slope and top deck), Caltrans will consult its Maintenance and Landscaping divisions to identify options to reduce soil exposure.  
   - A report describing those options will be provided to DTSC **by April 20, 2012**.  
   - Quarterly inspection of bare‑soil mitigation actions will be included in the monthly Site Report.

3. **Maintain the groundwater monitoring system**  
   - On March 8, 2012, all eight monitoring wells were sounded for total depth and inspected (vaults, locking caps); all were found fully functional.  
   - Quarterly groundwater sampling resumed on March 12, 2012, following the **Groundwater Assessment Work Plan (January 26, 2006)** approved by DTSC; strontium and general minerals were added.  
   - A groundwater monitoring report for the March event will be prepared and submitted to DTSC and the Regional Water Quality Control Board.

In addition, Caltrans states it is verifying oversight agreements with DTSC and the Regional Board that will govern cost reimbursement. All study results will be coordinated with StanCOG.

**What this changes**: The letter accepts DTSC’s requests, commits to specific implementation steps and deadlines, and formalizes ongoing monitoring and reporting obligations.
