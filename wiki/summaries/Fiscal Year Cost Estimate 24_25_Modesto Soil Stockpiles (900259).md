## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Annual cost estimate letter from DTSC to Caltrans, providing projected oversight costs for Fiscal Year July 1, 2024 – June 30, 2025, under Health & Safety Code §25269.5.
- **Author or preparer**: Ms. Lora Jameson, P.G., Chief, Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, Site Mitigation and Restoration Program, DTSC. Letter signed by her.
- **Date or period covered**: Letter dated November 21, 2024; covers FY 2024/25 (July 1, 2024 – June 30, 2025).

## Key claims
- DTSC asserts that Responsible Parties/Project Proponents remain liable for all costs incurred by DTSC as required by law, even though the estimate is provided for fiscal planning.
- The letter claims that the listed tasks (cost estimate preparation, annual inspection review, interagency agreement amendment, project management) are the scheduled oversight activities for the period.

- **Authority cited**: Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5 (obligation to provide cost estimates to responsible parties).
