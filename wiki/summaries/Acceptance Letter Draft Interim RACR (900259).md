## Document identity

- **Form:** Acceptance letter
- **Sender / author organization:** Dean Wright, PG, Project Manager, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), Site Mitigation and Restoration Program
- **Recipient(s) / addressee:** Mr. John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Date:** February 24, 2022

## Decision or request

- What is being approved/accepted: DTSC, in consultation with Central Valley RWQCB, **accepts** the *Draft Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project* (Draft Phase 1 RACR) submitted by Geocon on behalf of Caltrans. The acceptance is as a **technical status report with no comments**.
- The document describes completed consolidation and capping activities for barium-containing soils (BCS) under the Interim Phase 1 Construction effort at the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, California (Site Code: 900259).
- No conditions or revisions are attached to the acceptance itself.

## Open items and next actions

- The following components remain to be completed and will be included in the **Final Phase 1 Removal Action Completion Report (Final Phase 1 RACR)**:
  - As-Built Plan and cross section figures
  - Final survey data
  - Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan
- The O&M Plan will be submitted for review; an approved version will be incorporated into the Final Phase 1 RACR.
- Expected submittal of Final Phase 1 RACR: **July or August 2022**.
- After approval of the Final Phase 1 RACR, DTSC will enter into an **O&M Agreement** with Caltrans (including the O&M Plan) and will complete a **land use covenant** for the containment areas.
- Certification of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) will occur only after completion of the **Ultimate Phase 2 State Route 132 build-out effort**, anticipated to begin in **8 to 10 years**.
- Ultimate Phase 2 will include a supplemental RDIP for DTSC review and approval, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and potential revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement. This phase will be conducted under a separate agreement between DTSC and Caltrans.
