## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: Interagency Agreement Task Order Contract (43A0404-04), to reimburse DTSC for regulatory oversight activities at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site.
- Author or preparer: California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)
- Date or period covered: June 1, 2020, to June 30, 2025; executed March 18, 2021

- DTSC Task Order Manager: Dean Wright, PG (Phone: 916-255-3591, email: dean.wright@dtsc.ca.gov)
- Caltrans Task Order Manager: Richard Stewart, PG (Phone: 559-445-6378, email: richard.c.stewart@dot.ca.gov)
- Caltrans Contract Manager: Richard Bailey, PG (Phone: 916-956-1138, email: rich.bailey@dot.ca.gov)
- DTSC Contract Manager: Paul Ruffin, P.E. (Phone: 916-255-6677, email: paul.ruffin@dtsc.ca.gov)
- Office Chief (Caltrans Hazardous Waste, Air, Noise and Paleontology Office): Scott Fredrickson, P.E.

## Key terms and conditions
- DTSC scope of services: Provide professional staff services to conduct regulatory oversight, including review of plans, reports, and technical documents; field oversight of Caltrans activities; participation in meetings and calls; and task order management.
- Period of performance: Work shall begin on June 1, 2020, and terminate on June 30, 2025.
- Reports and Deliverables: DTSC will provide written comments and recommendations based on submittals. Each billing invoice shall include a summary of activities and daily logs. No formal progress reports are required.
- Meetings: DTSC Task Order Manager shall meet with Caltrans Task Order Manager and Contract Manager as necessary or upon request.
- Cost: Total amount payable by Caltrans under this Task Order shall not exceed $100,000.00. DTSC will be paid in accordance with Agreement No. 43A0404 Exhibit B, Item 3 Payment, and the attached cost estimate. DTSC may utilize suitable personnel classifications as shown in Attachment 3 of Agreement No. 43A0404, provided the total amount payable is not exceeded and Caltrans Task Order Manager and Contract Manager agree to the substitution.
- Invoices: Each quarterly billing invoice will include a brief description of services, Interagency Agreement and Task Order numbers, invoice number, date, billing period, hours by individuals, and other direct costs. Invoices to be sent in duplicate to Caltrans Contract Manager and Caltrans Task Order Manager.
- Personnel: Key staff and new job classifications must be approved by the Caltrans Contract Manager. All post-execution requests for new personnel must be submitted to the Caltrans Task Order Manager.
