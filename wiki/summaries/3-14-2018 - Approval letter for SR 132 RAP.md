## Document identity
- **Form:** Approval letter
- **Sender / author organization:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, San Joaquin Branch, Sacramento Office, Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program (signed by Steven R. Becker, P.G., Chief)
- **Recipient(s) / addressee:** Ms. Grace Magsayo, P.E., Project Manager, California Department of Transportation (Caltrans), District 10
- **Date:** March 14, 2018

## Decision or request
- **What is being approved:** DTSC approves the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County, California.
- **Document acted on:** Remedial Action Plan (RAP) – specific title and scope: addresses the segment south of Kansas Avenue between Carpenter Avenue and just east of State Route 99 in Modesto.
- **Selected remedy:** Alternative 4, Containment – containment of stockpiles behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, beneath roadway; unpaved portions covered with clean fill.
- **Conditions / contingencies:**
  - The Draft Final RAP must be modified to read "Final RAP" and include updated information reflecting the Final RAP and Final EIR/EA where applicable.
  - The Final RAP must be placed in local repositories for public viewing; hard copy and electronic copy must be sent to DTSC and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Board (RWQCB) for filing.
  - Implementation will proceed in two phases: Phase 1 (interim, 2019–2020) and Phase 2 (ultimate build-out, 2028–2029).
  - DTSC will prepare a Statement of Findings and a Notice of Determination under CEQA.
- **Effective date / deadline:** RAP approval effective March 14, 2018; Phase 1 scheduled 2019–2020, Phase 2 2028–2029.

## Open items and next actions
- **Caltrans must submit for DTSC review and approval:**
  - Remedial Design and Implementation Plan (RDIP) for each phase.
  - Remedial Action Completion Report for each phase.
  - Operation and Maintenance Plan (OMP) – required after Phase 1; annual inspection of pavement and containment features plus groundwater monitoring.
  - Operation and Maintenance Agreement (OMA) – entered into with DTSC after Phase 1.
  - Land use covenant and soil management plan – required.
- **DTSC will:**
  - Publish a "Start Work Notice" in English and Spanish in local newspapers for each phase, two weeks before construction start.
  - Evaluate the containment remedy every five years after Phase 2 implementation.
  - Certification of containment remedy will occur following Phase 2 implementation.
- **Who owes what:**
  - Caltrans: modifies Draft Final RAP to Final RAP, submits copies to DTSC and RWQCB, places in public repositories, prepares and submits RDIPs, completion reports, OMP, OMA, land use covenant, soil management plan.
  - DTSC: publishes start work notices, prepares Statement of Findings and Notice of Determination, performs five-year evaluations.
