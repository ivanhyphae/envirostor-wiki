## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Public Notice of Availability for the Draft Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (Draft EIR/EA) and Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project and Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. The notice announces a public hearing and public comment period.
- **Author or preparer**: California Department of Transportation (Caltrans), with input from the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB).
- **Date or period covered**: Notice published February 2017; public comment period January 18, 2017 – March 17, 2017; public hearing held February 22, 2017.

## Key claims
- The three Modesto soil stockpiles (total ~160,000 cubic yards) contain soil impacted with metals, primarily barium, lead, and strontium.
- Caltrans proposes to cap the stockpiles as part of the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway construction project, placing impacted soil beneath roadway pavement, within bridge abutments, and behind retaining walls.
- The capping action is intended to avoid potential future impacts to human health and the environment.

## Regulatory decisions
- **Decision type**: Approval of the Draft Final RAP for public noticing (not a final approval for implementation).
- **Agency**: DTSC and Central Valley RWQCB.
- **Date**: Reviewed and approved prior to public notice issuance (January 18, 2017).
- **Conditions imposed**: The RAP, if approved after public comment, would allow the proposed capping. The public hearing and comment period are required before final decisions by Caltrans, DTSC, and RWQCB.

## Analytical results
- **Contaminants**: Barium, lead, strontium (primary metals reported).
- **Media**: Soil.
- **Concentration ranges**: Not specified in this notice.
- **Applicable thresholds**: Not specified.
- **Exceedances noted**: Not specified.

## Remedial actions
- **Activity**: Capping of soil stockpiles (placement of impacted soil under roadway pavement, bridge abutments, retaining walls).
- **Quantities**: Approximately 160,000 cubic yards of soil.
- **Phase status**: Proposed; Draft Final RAP prepared and under public review; final decision pending.
- **Verification outcomes**: Not addressed.

## Responsible parties
- **Caltrans**: Owner/operator of the stockpiles; lead agency under CEQA and NEPA (assigned by FHWA); preparer of the Draft Final RAP.
- **DTSC**: Oversight agency for the RAP; will make final decision on RAP after public comment.
- **Central Valley RWQCB**: Co-regulator; reviewed and approved the Draft Final RAP for public noticing; will participate in final decision.
- **Stanislaus Council of Governments (StanCOG)**: Cooperating agency on the SR 132 project.

## Key terms and conditions
- **Public comment period**: January 18, 2017 – March 17, 2017.
- **Public hearing**: February 22, 2017, 6:00–8:00 p.m., open house format.
- **Document availability**: Draft EIR/EA and Draft Final RAP available at Caltrans District 10, StanCOG office, Stanislaus County Library, DTSC office, and online.
- **Comment submission**: Written comments to Caltrans (Attn: Phil Vallejo) by March 17, 2017, or via email to phillip.vallejo@dot.ca.gov.
- **Follow-on actions**: After comment period, Caltrans will make a decision on the project; DTSC and RWQCB will make a decision on the RAP.
