## Decision or request
The DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), reviewed the Draft Operation and Maintenance Plan for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles and provided comments.

- What is being approved, accepted, conditionally approved, denied, requested, or commented on: Comments are provided on the Draft Operation and Maintenance Plan and the accompanying O&M Site Inspection Form.
- The specific document or deliverable it acts on: "Draft Operation and Maintenance Plan, Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project, Stanislaus County, California, Dated March, 2022" and the "O&M Site Inspection Form".
- Conditions, required revisions, or contingencies attached: Revisions are required based on the provided comments.
- Effective date or deadline, if any: Not specified, but the revised O&M Plan should be submitted as Appendix G of the Remedial Action Completion Report.

## Open items and next actions
- Geocon Consultants, Inc. is required to incorporate the recommended changes into the Draft O&M Plan.
- The revised O&M Plan must be submitted as Appendix G of the Remedial Action Completion Report.
