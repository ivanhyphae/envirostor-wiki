## Decision or request
- **What is being approved/accepted**: DTSC and the Water Board accept the Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020, regarding use of Bent 2 excavation soil as clean cap material, and the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020.
- **Specific document/deliverable**: “Characterization of Excavated Bent 2 Soils for use as Clean Cap Material” Tech Memo (October 30, 2020) and “Revised SWPPP Amendment” (October 22, 2020).
- **Conditions/limitations**: The soil sample results do not meet the clean cap criteria established in the April 9, 2020 Variance Request approved by DTSC. However, the soil may be placed on top of Stockpile 2 at a thickness of 6 inches or less, provided it is covered with clean base material and the asphalt/concrete roadway. This placement is allowed to efficiently use the Bent 2 soil without jeopardizing the remedy.
- **Additional requirement**: Because the Bent 2 soils may be temporarily exposed to the elements during winter due to the project schedule, the rainy season surface water sampling effort and erosion control measures outlined in the Revised SWPPP Amendment must be strictly followed.
- **Effective date**: November 6, 2020 (date of the acceptance email).

## Open items and next actions
- No revisions or resubmittals are required; the Tech Memo and SWPPP Amendment are accepted as is.
- The next action is to implement the rainy season surface water sampling and erosion control measures per the Revised SWPPP Amendment. No specific due date is stated, but the measures are tied to the winter months and the project schedule.
