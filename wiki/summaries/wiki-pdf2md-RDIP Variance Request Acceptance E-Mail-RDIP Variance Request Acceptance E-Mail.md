## Thread / participants
- On April 13, 2020, Richard C. Stewart (Caltrans) wrote to Dean Wright (DTSC), Perry Myers (DTSC), and Steven Meeks (Waterboards) to request review of an attached RDIP variance request letter regarding the use of Stockpile 1 MSE Wall footing soil as clean cover.
- On April 17, 2020, Dean Wright (DTSC) wrote to Richard C. Stewart (Caltrans) to convey DTSC's official acceptance of the variance request for the soil along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1.

## Decision or request
- What is being approved, accepted, conditionally approved, denied, requested, or commented on: The use of specific excavated soil as clean cap soil.
- The specific document or deliverable it acts on: A "Variance Request to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (VR) dated April 9, 2020."
- Conditions, required revisions, or contingencies attached:
    - The upper foot of planned MSE wall footing excavation soil along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1, due to elevated barium concentrations, will be placed in Stockpile 1 and 2 permanent fill areas.
    - Soil extending from 1 to 5 feet from existing grade in this area (southern boundary of Stockpile 1) can be used as clean cap soil on the project if its 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) for barium concentrations is within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background (113.1 mg/kg was cited as acceptable for Stockpile 1).
    - If the UCL of the MSE wall footing excavation located along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2 is under naturally occurring background concentrations, it may also be used as clean cap soil on the project.
- Effective date or deadline, if any: Official approval or rejection for the soil along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2 will be provided once data is submitted and reviewed.

## Open items and next actions
- Submission and review of data for the MSE wall footing excavation located along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2, to determine its suitability as clean cap soil.

- Variance Request to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (VR) dated April 9, 2020: The document being accepted.
