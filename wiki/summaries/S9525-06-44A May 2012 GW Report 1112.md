## Document identity
- **Report type:** Groundwater monitoring round (May 2012)
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Reporting period or sampling date(s):** May 17, 2012 (field activities); report dated November 28, 2012
- **Project number or task order:** Geocon Project No. S9525-06-44A, Task Order No. 44, EA No. 10-403500, Contract No. 06A1580

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Groundwater
- **Wells, stations, or locations sampled:** MW-1, MW-2, MW-3, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, MW-7, MW-8
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled, and why:** None reported; all eight wells were sampled. However, MW-5 went dry during purging, was allowed to recover, purged again, allowed to recover a second time, and then sampled.

## Results
- **Analytes measured, with concentration ranges and units:**
  - **Dissolved metals (Title 22, including strontium):**
    - Barium: 28 – 910 µg/L (detected in all 8 wells; water quality threshold = 1,000 µg/L)
    - Strontium: 230 – 3,600 µg/L (detected in all 8 wells; threshold = 4,000 µg/L)
    - Lead: All results < 1.0 µg/L (PQL)
    - Arsenic: detected in all wells; range not explicitly stated but all below California Primary MCL of 10 µg/L
    - Chromium: detected in all wells; range not explicitly stated but below threshold
    - Vanadium: detected in all wells; below threshold
    - Manganese: detected at MW-1; concentration not explicitly stated but below threshold except at MW-4 where it exceeded Secondary MCL of 50 µg/L
    - Nitrate as nitrogen: 2.5 – 26 mg/L
    - Total Dissolved Solids (TDS): > 500 mg/L (Secondary MCL) at MW-4, MW-5, MW-6
  - **General minerals:** Calcium, magnesium, chloride, sulfate, sodium, potassium, sulfide, total alkalinity, bicarbonate alkalinity, carbonate alkalinity
  - **PAHs:** No PAHs detected above PQLs in any sample
- **Applicable thresholds:**
  - California Primary MCLs (e.g., nitrate = 10 mg/L)
  - California Secondary MCLs (e.g., TDS = 500 mg/L, manganese = 50 µg/L)
  - Drinking Water Health Advisories (EPA)
  - Notification Levels (California Department of Public Health)
  - Water Quality for Agriculture (Ayers & Westcott)
  - Public Health Goals
  - Integrated Risk Information System Reference Doses
- **Locations exceeding thresholds:**
  - **Nitrate (Primary MCL = 10 mg/L):** MW-1 (12 mg/L), MW-4 (10 mg/L), MW-5 (26 mg/L), MW-6 (18 mg/L)
  - **TDS (Secondary MCL = 500 mg/L):** MW-4, MW-5, MW-6
  - **Manganese (Secondary MCL = 50 µg/L):** MW-4
- **Trends versus prior rounds:** Stated to be "similar to historical levels" and "similar to their March 2012 levels." Stiff diagrams show minimal change in ionic composition since March 2012. Barium, lead, and strontium concentrations "remained significantly below their water quality thresholds."

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Dry wells, low recovery, depth-to-water, gradient/flow observations:**
  - Depth to groundwater ranged from 29.74 ft (MW-1) to 38.25 ft (MW-5) below TOC
  - Groundwater flow direction: southeast, average gradient 0.0006 ft/ft
  - MW-5 went dry after purging 1 gallon; required two recovery periods before sampling
- **Sampling or analytical deviations, data qualifiers, detection-limit issues:**
  - Equipment blank (distilled water over decontaminated pump) contained antimony (1.3 µg/L), barium (1.4 µg/L), calcium (200 µg/L), magnesium (72 µg/L), sodium (610 µg/L) — concentrations for these analytes qualified as estimated with potential to be less than reported
  - Samples MW-1 through MW-4 were received beyond hold time for nitrate analysis; client instructed lab to proceed
  - Matrix spike/MSD recoveries outside control limits for several analytes (arsenic, chromium, thallium, zinc, strontium, barium, nickel) on various dates — data considered sufficient quality since no concentrations were close to exceeding water quality thresholds
- **Anything affecting interpretation of the results:** Equipment blank contamination and hold-time exceedance for nitrate are potential limitations.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusions (by Geocon):**
  - No dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective water quality thresholds for drinking water
  - Nitrate exceeded the Primary MCL at four wells (MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6); TDS exceeded Secondary MCL at three wells (MW-4, MW-5, MW-6)
  - Barium, lead, and strontium concentrations were similar to historical levels and remained significantly below thresholds
  - Groundwater chemistry (Stiff diagrams) showed minimal change since March 2012
  - MW-1 (upgradient) nitrate of 12 mg/L "may be indicative of natural background nitrate concentrations"
- **Recommendations:** None explicitly stated beyond continued monitoring (implied by report purpose). No recommendations for well destruction or statistical re-evaluation.
