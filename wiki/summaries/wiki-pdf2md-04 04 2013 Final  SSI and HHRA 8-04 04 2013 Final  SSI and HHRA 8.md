## Document Identity
- **Deliverable type**: Supplemental Site Investigation (SSI) and Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA) Update
- **Version / status**: Final, approved by DTSC
- **Preparer**: Geocon Consultants Inc. (Caltrans contractor)
- **Date**: March 1, 2013 (reports); DTSC approval letter dated April 4, 2013
- **Project**: Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County, CA

## Purpose and Regulatory Context
- **Milestone**: Additional characterization of soil stockpiles to evaluate lateral and vertical migration of contaminants and to update the HHRA; supports preparation of a Remedial Action Plan
- **Oversight framework**: DTSC (Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program), in consultation with Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), under an Interagency Agreement and Task Order with Caltrans
- **Prior approval**: Follows "Final Supplemental Site Characterization Workplan" (Geocon, September 18, 2012), approved by DTSC September 20, 2012

## Site and Contamination Summary
- **Contaminants of concern**: Hazardous substances in soil stockpiles (barium identified as primary contaminant from prior characterization)
- **Affected media**: Soil stockpiles at the location of the proposed State Route 132/99 West Expressway
- **Areas addressed**: Lateral and vertical migration of contaminants from the soil stockpiles; additional characterization to finalize remedy management

## Findings and Risk Characterization
- **HHRA Update**: Updated human health risk assessment based on additional characterization data from September 2012 fieldwork
- **Risk results**: DTSC concurrence indicates findings support remedial decision-making; specific risk values not detailed in approval letter
- **Site conditions**: DTSC found the SSI and HHRA Update acceptable as basis for moving forward with remedy finalization

## Selected or Recommended Remedy
- Not specified in this approval letter; the documents support preparation of a Remedial Action Plan, but remedy selection is not documented here

## Conclusions and Path Forward
- **Closure status**: DTSC concurred with both the SSI and HHRA Update (March 1, 2013 versions)
- **Conditions**: Reports were revised in response to DTSC comments (February 15, 2013)
- **Follow-on deliverables**: Remedial Action Plan (RAP) to be prepared for management of soil stockpiles
