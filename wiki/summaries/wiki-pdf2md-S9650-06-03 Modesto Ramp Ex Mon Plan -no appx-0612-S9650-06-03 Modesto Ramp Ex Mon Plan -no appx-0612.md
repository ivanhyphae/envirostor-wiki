## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan, prepared to summarize prior investigations, outline health risks from barium in Stockpile 3, and detail air monitoring and excavation verification procedures for the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project.
- Author or preparer: GEOCON CONSULTANTS, INC.
- Date or period covered: June 2012 (specifically, fieldwork conducted April 16, 2012)

## Key claims
- **Stockpile 3 material not hazardous waste:** "Soil materials excavated from the investigation area would not require management or disposal as a hazardous waste." (GEOCON CONSULTANTS, INC.)
- **Elevated metal concentrations exist, but below health screening levels:** "The results of the analytical testing indicated the presence of elevated metal concentrations above site-specific background levels but below residential and commercial/industrial CHHSLs." (GEOCON CONSULTANTS, INC.)
- **Stockpiles do not pose a human health risk under current management:** "DTSC finds that the stockpiles as currently managed by Caltrans on Caltrans property do not pose a risk to human health for: 1) Caltrans workers who access the fenced site ..., 2) trespassers; and 3) residents adjacent to the stockpiles." (DTSC, December 17, 2009 letter)
- **Primary COPCs are certain metals and PAHs:** "Shaw identified metals (notably barium) and PAHs as the primary COPCs in the soil stockpiles." (GEOCON CONSULTANTS, INC.)
- **Elevated barium not always correlated with grayish material:** "The presence of distinctly colored, grayish material layers in the stockpiles has been associated with elevated barium levels but is not an absolute correlation." (GEOCON CONSULTANTS, INC.)

## Regulatory decisions
- Decision type: Response to Preliminary Endangerment Assessment (PEA)
- Agency: California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)
- Date: December 17, 2009
- Conditions imposed: Caltrans to continue managing stockpiles until SR99/Highway 132 Interchange construction and maintain the existing groundwater monitoring system.

## Analytical results
- **Contaminants:** Barium, Lead, Arsenic, PAHs, Nitrate, Sulfate, Sulfide, Aluminum, Iron, Manganese, Strontium, Mercury.
- **Media:** Soil, groundwater (assessment only).
- **Concentration ranges:**
    - Barium (Stockpile 3, Shaw 2004 PSI, borings B16, B17): 31 to 44,300 mg/kg.
    - Barium (Stockpile 3, Shaw 2006, borings S24, S25, N12): 35 to 230 mg/kg.
    - Barium (Stockpile 3, Geocon April 2012): 34 to 1,600 mg/kg.
    - Arsenic (Geocon April 2012): <1.0 mg/kg to 3.2 mg/kg.
    - Lead (Geocon April 2012, maximum): 190 mg/kg (sample HA9-0).
    - Nitrate (Shaw 2006, borings S24, N12): 0.5 to 0.7 mg/kg.
    - Sulfate (Shaw 2006, borings S24, N12): 2.4 to 42 mg/kg.
    - Sulfide (Shaw 2006): Not detected above laboratory method reporting limit.
- **Applicable thresholds:**
    - Commercial/industrial CHHSL for barium: 63,000 mg/kg.
    - Residential CHHSL for barium: 5,200 mg/kg.
    - Site-specific background barium levels: 31 to 150 mg/kg.
    - Site-specific background arsenic (95% UCL): 1.2 mg/kg (mean 0.97 mg/kg).
    - Total Threshold Limit Concentration (TTLC) for hazardous waste: Not exceeded for any metals.
    - Ten times Soluble Threshold Limit Concentration (STLC) for barium: 1,000 mg/kg.
    - Ten times STLC for lead: 50 mg/kg.
    - Cal/OSHA 8-hour time-weighted average Permissible Exposure Limit (PEL-TWA) for barium: 10 mg/m³.
    - Cal/OSHA 8-hour time-weighted average (PEL-TWA) for inorganic lead: 0.05 mg/m³.
    - California Air Resources Board (ARB) 30-day average ambient lead standard: 1.5 µg/m³.
- **Exceedances noted:**
    - Barium in one soil sample (DP1-14.5) at 1,600 mg/kg exceeded ten times the STLC for barium (1,000 mg/kg).
    - Five soil samples contained total lead at concentrations >50 mg/kg (ten times the STLC for lead), with a maximum of 190 mg/kg.
    - Arsenic concentrations (up to 3.2 mg/kg) were within the range of site-specific background but at times above the 95% UCL of 1.2 mg/kg.
    - No exceedances of commercial/industrial CHHSLs for metals, or hazardous waste TTLCs.
    - No exceedances of cancer risk criteria or noncancer hazard quotients in human health risk assessments for various receptors.

## Remedial actions
- Activity: Excavation of approximately 2,800 cubic yards of Stockpile 3 soil embankment.
- Quantities: Estimated 2,800 cubic yards of Stockpile 3 soil.
- Phase status: Planned to occur during daytime off-ramp closures over an estimated five working days.
- Verification outcomes:
    - Oversight by Geocon California Professional Geologist (PG) or supervised field geologist/engineer.
    - Determination of completion of excavation limits within Stockpile 3 fill materials.
    - Daily field logs documenting personnel, activities, air monitoring, visible dust, soil excavation, material quantities, and confirmation of excavation limits (native soil conditions).
    - Collection of soil samples and chain-of-custody documentation as needed.
    - Disposal of excavated Stockpile 3 fill materials as non-hazardous waste to Fink Road Sanitary Landfill.
    - Underlying native soils will not require special handling.

## Key terms and conditions
- **Stockpile management:** Caltrans to continue managing stockpiles, maintaining existing groundwater monitoring system.
- **Pre-construction meeting:** Scoping meeting to discuss excavation activities, dust mitigation, health and safety, and project scheduling.
- **Excavation oversight:** Stockpile 3 excavation activities overseen by a Geocon California Professional Geologist or supervised field geologist/engineer.
- **Air monitoring:**
    - Ambient perimeter air monitoring for total airborne particulate concentrations during excavation and loading.
    - Monitoring daily meteorological forecast.
    - Notification to contractor if downwind real-time particulate counter exceeds preset Fence Line Total Dust Action Level alarm (4 mg/m³ above upwind background).
    - Hourly checks of real-time air monitor instruments and recording of averaged dust readings.
    - Perimeter monitoring includes one upwind and two downwind locations.
    - Monitoring duration: 8 hours or duration of excavation/soil handling/sampling tasks, whichever is shorter.
    - Air sampling and analysis for inorganic lead and barium compounds and total mass using pre-weighed 5µ PVC filters.
    - First day's filters submitted for 24-hour turnaround analysis for inorganic lead and barium.
    - Subsequent filters held for routine 5-7 business day turnaround analysis if lead levels are below ARB-OEHHA unit risk and Fence Line Action Level not exceeded.
- **Equipment calibration:** pDR-1200 real-time monitors calibrated according to manufacturer's instructions, logged every 5 minutes.
- **Audible alarm:** Downwind monitor's audible alarm set to highest total dust level measured on upwind monitor.
- **Reporting:** Draft Excavation Monitoring Report (Completion Report) submitted to Caltrans within ~30 days of completion, to include observed conditions, monitoring results, findings, photos, and disposal manifests. Report finalized upon Caltrans' comments.
- **Limitations:** Plan for Caltrans' use; other parties rely for informational purposes only. Not construction plans. Recommendations based on limited sampling. Valid as of date of Plan, requires updates for additional activities.
