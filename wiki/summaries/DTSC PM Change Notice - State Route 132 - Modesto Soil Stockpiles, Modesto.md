## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** DTSC Project Manager Change Notice (email notification). Purpose: to inform Caltrans that the DTSC Project Manager for the State Route 132 – Modesto Soil Stockpiles site has changed, and to provide contact information for the new PM.
- **Author or preparer:** Dean Wright, P.G., Project Manager, Site Mitigation and Restoration Program, DTSC Sacramento.
- **Date or period covered:** December 11, 2024.

## Key claims
- The DTSC Project Manager role for the State Route 132 – Modesto Soil Stockpiles site has been changed from Dean Wright to Arielle Mcleskey, effective as of the date of the email.
- Future documents and correspondence regarding the Site should be submitted to Arielle Mcleskey.

## Regulatory decisions
None. This document is an administrative change notification; no formal regulatory decisions are recorded.

## Analytical results
None.

## Remedial actions
None.

## Responsible parties
- **Caltrans (California Department of Transportation):** Owner/operator of the Site; recipient of the notice (Adam Inman).
- **DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control):** Regulatory oversight agency; former PM Dean Wright, new PM Arielle Mcleskey, and cc’d Lora Jameson.
- **Geocon Inc.:** Consultant named in cc (John Juhrend).
- **Central Valley RWQCB (Regional Water Quality Control Board):** Co-regulator (referenced in broader corpus context; not mentioned directly in this email).

## Key terms and conditions
- All future submittals to DTSC for the Site must be directed to Arielle Mcleskey at the provided address, email, and phone number.
