## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Regulatory correspondence (letter) from DTSC to Caltrans, directing Caltrans to implement specific ongoing site management and monitoring actions for the SR 132 West Expressway/Freeway soil stockpiles.
- **Author or preparer:** Randy S. Adams, C.E.G., Senior Engineering Geologist, Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
- **Date or period covered:** Letter dated March 1, 2012. Covers actions from January 2005 through the date of the letter, with a site visit conducted on February 15, 2012.

## Key claims
- **DTSC finding (12/17/09 letter):** "DTSC finds soil stockpiles, as currently managed by Caltrans on Caltrans property, do not pose a risk to human health for: 1) Caltrans workers who access the fenced site to conduct mowing operations, conduct fence repairs, or other routine activities; 2) trespassers; and 3) residents adjacent to the stockpiles."
- **Condition for the finding:** The no-risk finding is predicated on Caltrans continuing to manage the stockpiles by limiting access, maintaining the fence, prohibiting excavation/grading/off-site removal/placement of soil, and maintaining grade and vegetative cover, plus maintaining the existing groundwater monitoring system.
- **Basis for the finding:** Based on reports reviewed by DTSC and RWQCB, intended to determine potential for release of hazardous substances presenting risk to human health or the environment.

## Regulatory decisions
- **Action:** DTSC, in consultation with RWQCB, requests Caltrans implement four specific actions going forward.
- **Agency:** DTSC (lead), with RWQCB (co-regulator).
- **Date:** March 1, 2012.
- **Conditions imposed:**
    1. **Limit Access to Caltrans Authorized Personnel:** Install fences where needed, inspect and repair/replace all breaches twice per month. Remove debris quarterly.
    2. **Maintain Current Grade and Vegetative Cover:** Inspect and revegetate all bare surfaces quarterly. Maintain grade and drainage.
    3. **Maintain Groundwater Monitoring System:** Implement quarterly groundwater monitoring in existing wells. Inspect and evaluate all wells for functionality; refurbish or replace non-functional wells. Monitor in accordance with the Groundwater Assessment Workplan (1/26/2006) and include strontium and other analytes to determine sources of groundwater impacts. Submit quarterly groundwater monitoring reports to DTSC and RWQCB, including field data, chain of custody, certified lab analysis, summary tables, and trend discussion.
    4. **Coordinate with StanCOG, DTSC, and RWQCB during preparation of sampling and analysis plan for additional characterization of the Site.** This is a required initial step in DTSC's Draft Voluntary Cleanup Agreement (VCA) with StanCOG (dated 2/7/2012). RWQCB also has a draft Reimbursement Agreement with StanCOG (dated 2/7/2012). Both agreements were unanimously approved by the StanCOG Policy Board on 2/15/2012.

## Analytical results
- **Contaminants of concern:** Barium (from historical FMC Inc. operations) is the primary contaminant. Strontium is also identified as a required analyte for groundwater monitoring.
- **Media:** Soil (stockpiles) and groundwater.
- **Concentration ranges:** Not specified in this document.
- **Applicable thresholds:** Not specified in this document.
- **Exceedances noted:** Not specified in this document. The document focuses on future monitoring requirements.

## Remedial actions
- **Current management activities (to be maintained):** Access control, chain-link fence inspection/maintenance, prohibition of excavation/grading/off-site removal/soil placement, maintenance of grade and vegetative cover, operation of groundwater monitoring system.
- **Requested actions:**
    - Fence repair and debris removal.
    - Vegetation management.
    - Groundwater monitoring (quarterly, including strontium).
    - Coordination on additional characterization (sampling and analysis plan) as part of a future VCA.
- **Phase status:** Ongoing site management and pre-construction characterization. The SR 132 West Expressway/Freeway project is planned but not yet constructed.
- **Verification outcomes:** Not applicable as no active remediation is described.

## Responsible parties
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans):** Owner/operator of the soil stockpiles. Responsible for implementing the actions requested in the letter. Contact: Richard Stewart, P.G., Engineering Geologist.
- **Stanislaus Council of Governments (StanCOG):** Planning the construction of the SR 132 West Expressway/Freeway. Has a Draft VCA with DTSC and a Draft Reimbursement Agreement with RWQCB.
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC):** Lead regulatory oversight agency.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB):** Co-regulator, consulted on decisions.
- **FMC Inc.:** Former owner of a portion of the property (4.3-acre parcel). Historical chemical manufacturing company that processed barium sulfate and strontium sulfate ores, generating the pond tailings that are part of the stockpiles.

## Key terms and conditions
- **Required follow-on actions:**
    - Caltrans must implement the four actions listed in the letter.
    - Caltrans must submit quarterly groundwater monitoring reports to DTSC and RWQCB.
    - Caltrans must coordinate with StanCOG, DTSC, and RWQCB on the sampling and analysis plan for additional characterization.
- **Deadlines/Timeframes:**
    - Fence inspection/repair: two times per month.
    - Debris removal: quarterly.
    - Vegetation inspection and re-vegetation: quarterly.
    - Groundwater monitoring: quarterly.
- **Monitoring obligations:**
    - Quarterly groundwater monitoring in existing wells.
    - Include strontium and other source-identifying analytes in the monitoring program.
    - Reports must include field data, chain of custody, certified lab analysis, summary tables, and trend discussion.
- **Restrictions:**
    - Access limited to Caltrans authorized personnel.
    - No excavation, grading, off-site soil removal, or placement of other soil on the site.
    - Current grade and vegetative cover must be maintained.
