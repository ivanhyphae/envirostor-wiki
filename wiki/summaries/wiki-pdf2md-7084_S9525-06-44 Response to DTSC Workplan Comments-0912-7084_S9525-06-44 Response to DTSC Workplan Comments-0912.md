## Decision or request
- **What is being communicated:** A response letter from Geocon to Caltrans addressing comments from DTSC (issued September 12, 2012, in consultation with the Central Valley RWQCB) on the *Draft Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan* (Draft Workplan) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles.
- **What it acts on:** The *Draft Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan* (August 2012, prepared by Geocon Consultants). Geocon states that the comments have been addressed in the *Final Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan (Final Workplan)* dated September 18, 2012, which is being submitted concurrently.
- **Conditions and required revisions addressed:**
    - **Field Activities:** DTSC requested additional discretionary boring locations for erosion/sediment migration features; Geocon states that Fenceline and Perimeter borings were added or adjusted at each stockpile location based on a September 17, 2012 site survey, and that the new/revised boring locations and depth rationale are presented in the Final Workplan.
    - **Sampling methodology:** DTSC understood the sampling methodology applies to all boring categories. Geocon added details in the Final Workplan clarifying that Fenceline and Perimeter borings will be continuous cored to 5 ft and 3 ft respectively, with specific depth intervals; Cadmium borings will use 0–6 inch and approximate 5-foot intervals thereafter.
    - **Rinse water management:** DTSC and RWQCB requested all equipment decontamination rinse water be contained, analyzed, and managed. Geocon added text to the Final Workplan stating that decontamination water will be contained, sampled and analyzed for metals, and appropriately disposed of.
    - **Laboratory analysis:** DTSC understood the methodology applies to all boring categories, with additional analysis possible after initial review. Geocon added text confirming this and that additional analysis will be performed as directed by DTSC and RWQCB.
    - **Summary Report:** DTSC requested that the report be limited to Supplemental Site Investigation findings only, with nature/extent of contamination and human health risk assessment in separate reports. Geocon added text to confirm this approach.
    - **Appendix A:** DTSC requested laboratory reporting format include analysis for soluble metals with MDL and PQL, detection limits suitable for human health risk assessment and water quality comparison, and "J-flag" for concentrations between MDL and PQL. Geocon added an example laboratory report for soluble metals and text indicating "J-flag" will be noted.
    - **Health and Safety Plan:** DTSC requested a site HSP be included as appendix with dust control measures. Geocon appended the April 2012 HSP for Modesto Stockpiles and added text on additional dust control measures in the Field Activities section.

## Open items and next actions
- **Resubmittal:** The Final Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan (dated September 18, 2012) has been submitted in response to the DTSC comments.
- **Pending approvals:** None explicitly stated; the response letter is addressed to Caltrans for transmittal/acceptance.
- **Future work:** Text in the Final Workplan indicates that additional analysis will be performed after review of initial analysis and as directed by DTSC and RWQCB; separate reports for Updated Site Characterization Summary Report and Updated Human Health Risk Assessment are planned.
