## Decision or request

- **What is being approved/accepted**: DTSC and the Water Board accept the Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated April 6, 2020, describing the sampling along the MSE wall footing excavation area and the laboratory results.
- **Document acted on**: *Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Footing Soil Characterization* technical memo (dated April 6, 2020; referred to as “Stockpile 1 Wall Footing Sampling Tech Memo”).
- **Conditions**: The acceptance is conditional on the following:
  - The MSE wall footing soil **must not** be used as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material.
  - Reason: The 95% Upper Confidence Limit (UCL) of the sampled soil exceeds the maximum background of 120 mg/kg, and the MSE wall footing area falls outside the designated borrow area described in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP).
  - The material **must** be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones **or** used as general bridge embankment fill.
- **Effective date / deadline**: The email itself serves as DTSC’s official acceptance of the memo, contingent on execution of the above condition in the field. No specific deadline stated.

## Open items and next actions

- **Condition to be executed in the field**: The material placement restrictions (no capping use; placement only in fill containment zones or as general bridge embankment fill) must be implemented.
- **Pending sampling**: After removal of the Stockpile 2 BCS southerly slope material, wall footing excavation sampling in that area will be completed (stated by Geocon in the initial message; no separate approval required in this correspondence).
- **No further resubmittals or deadlines** are specified in this acceptance.
