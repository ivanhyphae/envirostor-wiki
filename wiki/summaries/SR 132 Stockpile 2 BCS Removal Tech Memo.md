## Document identity
- **Memo subject / question addressed:** Stockpile 2 BCS Removal Verification Testing – whether barium‑containing soil (BCS) removal at the west and east ends of Stockpile 2 met the barium and lead verification thresholds to allow placement of clean embankment fill.
- **Preparer / author organization:** John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Recipient / addressee:** Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto
- **Date:** March 9, 2020
- **Area, stockpile, or feature involved:** Stockpile 2, State Route 132 right‑of‑way, Modesto, California. West end adjacent to Emerald Avenue; east end adjacent to State Route 99.

## Purpose
The specific question addressed is whether BCS removal at the west and east ends of Stockpile 2 (to facilitate construction of bridge overcrossing structures) has been completed such that the exposed surfaces meet the verification thresholds established in the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). The verification was prompted by the need to confirm that residual barium and lead concentrations are acceptable for clean embankment fill placement.

## Data and findings
- **Sampling performed:**
  - **West end:** Initial excavation to elevation 82 ft MSL; eight soil samples (S2W 1‑0 through 8‑0) collected February 28, 2020. After initial results showed elevated barium at S2W 1‑0 and S2W 3‑0, the western half was deepened to ~81 ft MSL and two additional samples collected March 6, 2020.
  - **East end:** Eastern half excavated to ~83 ft MSL; six samples (S2E 1‑0 through 6‑0) collected February 21, 2020. After elevated barium at S2E 3‑0, the area around that location and the western half were excavated to ~82 ft MSL; seven additional samples (S2E 3A‑0 and S2E 7‑0 through 12‑0) collected February 28, 2020. Sample S2E 8‑0 was elevated, leading to further excavation to ~81 ft MSL and one additional sample (S2E 8A‑0) collected March 6, 2020.
- **Analytes and methods:** Barium and lead by EPA Method 6010B (ICP‑AES). Samples analyzed by Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL) under chain of custody.
- **Verification thresholds (per RDIP):**
  - Barium: 1,000 mg/kg (groundwater protection)
  - Lead: 80 mg/kg (residential screening level)
- **Results:** All final excavation samples from both ends contained barium and lead below the respective thresholds. Barium concentrations ranged from 54 to 760 mg/kg; lead concentrations from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg. No exceedances in the final round of sampling. (Detailed results are presented in Table 1 of the memo and in the attached ATL laboratory reports.)

## Conclusion and recommendation
The memo concludes that BCS removal at the west and east ends of Stockpile 2 has met the barium and lead verification thresholds. The material is suitable for placement of clean embankment fill. This conclusion is attributed to John E. Juhrend of Geocon Consultants, Inc.

## Follow-on and approval status
The information will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted following completion of the stockpile clean fill capping activities. The memo does not explicitly state that it has been submitted for or received agency approval; however, it is prepared for the project team (WSP/City of Modesto) and will support the completion report.
