Here is a structured summary of the document for the case-file wiki.

***

### Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Surface Water Sampling Report documenting the results of a stormwater sampling event conducted to assess the quality of runoff from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site.
- **Author or preparer:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Date or period covered:** Sampling event performed on March 5, 2016. Report dated April 26, 2016.

### Key claims
- Stormwater falling on the stockpiles appeared to be retained within the boundary of the Site (no offsite migration observed).
- The interceptor berm at the base of the south side access ramp to Stockpile 2 appeared to have functioned as designed.
- Surface water originating from the western slope of Stockpile 2 will continue to be managed by maintaining the stockpile's vegetative/fiber cover and straw wattles.
- With the exception of manganese in sample PL2, no reported concentrations of dissolved metals or general minerals exceeded their respective primary or secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs).
- The analytical results for both metals and general minerals were within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported at each sample location.

### Regulatory decisions
- No formal regulatory decisions or actions were documented in this report. The sampling was conducted in general accordance with protocols approved by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), as established in a 2006 Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP) and a 2013 Addendum to the SAP. Sample locations were approved by DTSC and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB).

### Analytical results
- **Dissolved Metals (Title 22):** Samples analyzed for antimony, arsenic, barium, beryllium, cadmium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, magnesium, manganese, mercury, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, selenium, silver, sodium, strontium, thallium, vanadium, and zinc.
    - **Exceedance:** Manganese was detected in sample PL2 at 60 µg/l, exceeding its secondary MCL of 50 µg/l.
    - No other exceedances of primary or secondary MCLs were reported for the seven samples (PL1-PL5, BG1).
    - Beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, lead, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected at or above their PQLs.
- **General Minerals:** Samples analyzed for chloride, nitrate as nitrogen, sulfate, sulfide, total alkalinity, bicarbonate alkalinity, carbonate alkalinity, total dissolved solids (TDS), and total suspended solids (TSS). None of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs.
- **Laboratory QA/QC Issues:**
    - All samples were outside holding time for nitrate analysis.
    - Samples PL1-PL5 and BG1 required dilution for calcium due to high concentration. PL3 also required dilution for barium and potassium.
    - Matrix spike recoveries for calcium, magnesium, nitrate, and sulfate were outside acceptance limits, but the laboratory validated the batch using a laboratory control sample.

### Remedial actions
- No active remedial actions were undertaken during this sampling event. The document describes ongoing management measures:
    - Maintenance of vegetative/fiber cover and straw wattles on the western slope of Stockpile 2.
    - An interceptor berm at the base of the south side access ramp to Stockpile 2.
    - Caltrans plans to conduct additional surface water sampling during future qualifying rain events.

### Responsible parties
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) - District 6:** Owner and operator of the site. Contracted Geocon to perform the work. Manages stormwater and plans future sampling.
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC):** Primary regulatory oversight for the remediation project. Approved the sampling protocols and sample locations.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB):** Co-regulator. Approved sample locations.
- **Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC):** Former owner of the property from which the barium-contaminated soil was excavated.
- **Barium Products Ltd. / Westvaco Chlorine Products Corporation:** Historical operators of the site that generated the barium-contaminated waste.

### Key terms and conditions
- **Future Monitoring:** Caltrans plans to conduct additional surface water sampling during qualifying rain events.
- **Gate Valve (PL5):** The gate valve at the drainage basin near Stockpile 3 is reportedly only opened if the basin nears a capacity that could jeopardize the northbound lanes of SR 99. It was not opened during this event.
- **Background Sample:** Background sample BG1 was collected from a drop inlet on Loletta Avenue, south of Stockpile 2. Sample location BG2 was dry and not sampled.
