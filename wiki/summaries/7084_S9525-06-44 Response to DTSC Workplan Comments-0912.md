## Decision or request
This correspondence transmits and documents the completion of the **Final Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan (Final Workplan)** dated September 18, 2012. It responds to and incorporates revisions requested by DTSC in their September 12, 2012 comment letter on the prior Draft Workplan (dated August 2012). The letter addresses each DTSC/RWQCB comment, detailing how it was resolved in the final document. Key decisions and acceptances include:

- **Field Activities:** The Final Workplan incorporates new/adjusted boring locations (Fenceline, Perimeter, Cadmium) based on a September 17, 2012 site survey with DTSC and Caltrans. The rationale for differing boring depths (5 ft, 3 ft, 20 ft) is explained.
- **Sampling Methodology:** The Final Workplan clarifies that continuous coring and specific depth-interval sampling (0-6 inches, then 1-ft or 5-ft intervals depending on boring type) applies to all boring categories.
- **Decontamination Water:** The Final Workplan now states that all rinse water from equipment decontamination will be contained, sampled for metals, and disposed of appropriately (instead of being discharged to the stockpile surface).
- **Laboratory Analysis:** The Final Workplan confirms that the laboratory methods (EPA 6010B, 7471) apply to all boring categories, and additional analysis will be performed after initial results, as directed by DTSC and RWQCB.
- **Summary Report:** The Final Workplan limits the summary report to findings of the Supplemental Site Investigation. Information on the nature and extent of contamination and the Human Health Risk Assessment will be presented in separate reports.
- **Appendix A:** The Final Workplan includes an example laboratory report for soluble metals with MDL and PQL values and confirms that J-flags will be used for estimated concentrations between those limits.
- **Health and Safety Plan:** The Final Workplan includes the April 2012 Health and Safety Plan as an appendix and adds text on dust control measures.

## Open items and next actions
No specific open items or next actions are defined in this correspondence. The Final Workplan is submitted for Caltrans and DTSC review/acceptance. Subsequent steps (e.g., field work, laboratory analysis, report generation) will proceed per the approved plan.
