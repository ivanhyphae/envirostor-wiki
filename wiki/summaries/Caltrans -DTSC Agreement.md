## Document Identity

- **Document type and purpose**: Task Order No. 6 (TO6) under Interagency Agreement 43A0260 between Caltrans and DTSC. Purpose is to fund DTSC oversight and consultative services for investigation and remediation of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132/99), prior to construction of the future Route 132 West Expressway.
- **Author or preparer**: Caltrans (Task Manager Richard Stewart, Contract Manager Scott Nelson) and DTSC (Task Order Manager Randy Adams, Contract Manager Paul Ruffin). Executed by G. Scott McGowen (Caltrans Chief Environmental Engineer) and Paul Ruffin (DTSC Brownfields Program).
- **Date or period covered**: Execution date June 22, 2012; term June 22, 2012 through May 31, 2013.

## Key Claims

- The site consists of three stockpiles containing in excess of 120,000 yd³ of contaminated soil, generated during sub-grade construction of SR 99 through Modesto. Excavation near Kansas Avenue removed tailings from a former Food and Machinery Corporation (FMC) barite (BaSO₄) and celestite (SrSO₄) processing disposal pond.
- The site is impacted by hazardous substances (metals) including barium, lead, and strontium (based on the 2009 Final Preliminary Endangerment Assessment and other data).
- DTSC letter dated 12/7/2012 stated the need to address use of stockpile material in the SR 132 West Expressway project and/or its final disposition regarding human health and water quality before design and construction.
- RWQCB (Central Valley Region) is reviewing existing site information and may require additional characterization.

## Regulatory Decisions

- **Preceding agreement**: 2006 Interagency Agreement (task order preceding TO6) under which Caltrans submitted a Final Preliminary Endangerment Assessment in 2009.
- **This Task Order**: Formalizes DTSC’s oversight role and cost reimbursement; requires DTSC review and approval of all deliverables (workplans, reports, risk assessments, feasibility study, remedial action plan, etc.). DTSC may direct additional activities or stop work if imminent endangerment exists.
- **CEQA**: Caltrans will address CEQA via the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway EIR/EA, incorporating the Remedial Action Plan by reference; DTSC acts as responsible agency providing comments on hazardous substance information.
- **Public participation**: Required per Health & Safety Code sections 25358.7 and 25356.1(e) and DTSC policy; includes scoping meeting, community profile, fact sheets, public notice, minimum 30-day comment period, public meeting, and response to comments.

## Analytical Results

- **No specific analytical data reported in this document.** The Task Order references earlier data (2009 Final PEA) indicating metals impacts (barium, lead, strontium). Future work includes additional site characterization (soil, surface water, groundwater) and risk evaluation.

## Remedial Actions

- **No physical remedial actions described.** The Task Order is a planning and oversight agreement. Tasks include: submittal of existing data and scoping; additional site characterization (sampling workplan, site characterization report); risk evaluation and cleanup level determination; feasibility study; preparation of a Remedial Action Plan (RAP); public participation; QA/QC plan; quarterly groundwater monitoring reports.

## Responsible Parties

- **Caltrans (State of California, Department of Transportation)**: Owner/operator of the site; responsible for all investigative and remedial work, CEQA documentation, public participation, and reimbursing DTSC oversight costs.
- **DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control)**: Regulatory oversight agency; reviews and approves all deliverables, provides consultative services, conducts field oversight as needed.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB)**: Co-regulator; reviews existing site information and may require additional characterization.
- **Named individuals**:  
  - Richard Stewart, P.G. – Caltrans Task Manager  
  - Scott Nelson – Caltrans Contract Manager  
  - Randy Adams, C.E.G. – DTSC Task Order Manager  
  - Paul Ruffin, P.E. – DTSC Contract Manager  
  - G. Scott McGowen, P.E. – Caltrans Chief Environmental Engineer

## Key Terms and Conditions

- **Advance payment**: Caltrans to pay $25,000 advance to DTSC within 10 days of effective date; condition precedent to DTSC’s obligation to provide oversight.
- **Payment**: Caltrans reimburses DTSC all oversight costs (including interest on past-due invoices) within 60 days of billing; total task order amount capped at $150,000 (may be amended).
- **DTSC review and approval**: All work subject to DTSC written approval; DTSC may return comments with deadlines or modify documents in consultation with Caltrans.
- **Notification of field activities**: Caltrans must notify DTSC at least 7 days in advance of all field activities; DTSC may take duplicate samples.
- **Record preservation**: Caltrans retains all documents for minimum 6 years after termination; longer preservation may be requested.
- **Termination**: Either party may terminate for convenience with 30 days written notice; Caltrans pays DTSC costs through termination date.
- **Endangerment**: Caltrans must immediately report any condition posing immediate threat to public health/safety/environment; DTSC may order work stoppage or interim measures.
- **CEQA**: Caltrans addresses CEQA via the SR 132 Expressway EIR/EA; DTSC reviews as responsible agency.
- **Public participation**: Mandatory activities per statute and DTSC policy; fact sheets, public meetings, comment period, response to comments.
- **QA/QC**: All sampling and analysis must follow a DTSC-approved QA/QC Plan.
- **Groundwater monitoring**: Quarterly monitoring reports required for site wells; must include depth to water, purging/sampling data, laboratory results, QA/QC, comparison to previous events, conclusions.
