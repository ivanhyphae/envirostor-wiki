## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: Summary of analytical results for thallium in soil stockpiles.
- Author or preparer: Not specified.
- Date or period covered: Analysis reported in June 2014; comparison to pre-2023 and December 2023 events.

## Key claims
- Thallium was detected at low concentrations in 278 soil samples from the Modesto Soil Stockpiles, as reported in the June 2014 Final FS Report.
- Antimony, selenium, and silver were not detected in any of the analyzed soil samples.
- Prior to the December 2023 stormwater event, thallium was not detected in groundwater and pre-2023 surface water samples.
- The December 2023 stormwater event is the first reported instance of a Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) exceedance for thallium at the site.

## Analytical results
- Contaminant: Thallium
- Media: Soil (stockpiles)
- Concentration: Low concentrations detected (specific values not provided in this text).
- Contaminants not detected: Antimony, Selenium, Silver
- Exceedances: Thallium MCL exceeded in December 2023 stormwater event.
