## Document identity
- **Form:** Approval letter
- **Sender / author organization:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), Site Mitigation and Restoration Program
- **Recipient(s):** Rebecca L. Silva, Project Manager, Geocon Consultants Inc.
- **Date:** May 3, 2024

## Decision or request
- **What is being approved:** The *Updated Comparative Evaluation of Groundwater Data, Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, Stanislaus County, California* (Evaluation Report), dated February 26, 2024, submitted by Geocon on behalf of Caltrans.
- **Substance:** DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), agrees that the data show the encapsulated soil (barium and lead impacted) is not impacting groundwater and will not impact groundwater in the future. Both agencies concur with the Evaluation Report’s recommendation to discontinue groundwater monitoring beneath the soil stockpiles and to decommission the four remaining monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, and MW10).
- **Conditions:** None stated. The letter is an unconditional approval.

## Open items and next actions
- Decommissioning of monitoring wells MW4, MW6, MW9, and MW10 must be performed “as per the approved Remedial Design and Implementation Plan.” No deadline is specified.
