## Document identity
- **Report type:** Statistical evaluation (updated comparative evaluation of groundwater data)
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Reporting period or sampling date(s):** Data evaluated from 2014 through February 2024; most recent samples collected March 2021 (MW4, MW6), February 2022 (MW9), and April 2018 (MW10)
- **Project number or task order:** Project No. S2350-01-02; Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2, EA No. 10-1E7003

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Groundwater
- **Wells, stations, or locations sampled:** MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10 (four remaining monitoring wells)
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled, and why:** Wells MW4, MW6, MW9, and MW10 were dry on numerous occasions due to declining regional groundwater levels. Specific dry events: MW4 – September 2014, May 2016, March 2022 through February 2023 (4 of 11 measurements dry); MW6 – September 2014 through April 2017, March 2022 through February 2023 (6 of 11); MW9 – September 2014 through April 2017, April 2019, March 2021 through February 2023 (8 of 11); MW10 – September 2014 through April 2017, April 2019 through February 2023 (9 of 11). No samples collected after March 2021 (MW4, MW6), February 2022 (MW9), or April 2018 (MW10). Six wells (MW1, MW2, MW3, MW5, MW7, MW8) were decommissioned in 2019.

## Results
- **Analytes measured, with concentration ranges and units:**
  - Total dissolved solids (TDS): Concentrations in the same range as previously reported (historical range ~200-800 mg/L)
  - Sulfate: Concentrations remained consistent; since 2019, sulfate < 50 mg/L
  - Barium: Consistent with pre-2014 measurements; comparable to FMC upgradient background of 151 µg/L
  - Strontium: Consistent with pre-2014 measurements
- **Applicable thresholds:**
  - Barium: U.S. EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory (DWHA) = 700 µg/L; FMC site-specific target level = 6,210 µg/L
  - Strontium: U.S. EPA DWHA = 4,000 µg/L; FMC site-specific target level = 61,900 µg/L
  - Secondary MCLs referenced in Table 1 notes
- **Which locations exceeded thresholds and by how much:** No thresholds exceeded. Barium concentrations were well below the EPA DWHA (700 µg/L) and approximately 40 times less than the FMC site-specific target level (6,210 µg/L). Strontium concentrations were, on average, about four times less than the EPA DWHA (4,000 µg/L) and more than 20 times less than the FMC site-specific target level (61,900 µg/L).
- **Trends versus prior rounds:** Concentrations remained stable and consistent with pre-2014 data. No new trends or changes identified.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- Dry wells: MW4, MW6, MW9, and MW10 were frequently dry due to declining regional groundwater levels; specific dry periods documented above. No water volume available for sample collection during multiple annual sampling events since 2014.
- Sampling or analytical deviations: Six wells decommissioned in 2019 prior to highway construction; only four wells remain. Data qualifiers noted in Table 1: "S" for split samples submitted by RWQCB; "<" for less than laboratory reporting limit; "---" for not analyzed or not applicable.
- Depth-to-water, gradient/flow observations: Caltrans wells screened across water table at 41-48 feet bgs with 10-foot screens; FMC wells screened 20-30 feet deeper (45-55 ft bgs) providing more continuous saturation.

## Conclusions and recommendations
**Conclusions drawn by Geocon:**
- Groundwater data from the four remaining Caltrans monitoring wells confirms constituent concentrations have remained stable since the 2014 Evaluation.
- The encapsulated soil stockpiles do not have the potential to impact groundwater.
- Future monitoring data from the four remaining wells will not provide useful data for further analysis.

**Recommendations by Geocon:**
- Decommission the remaining four Caltrans monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10).
- Discontinue groundwater monitoring related to the soil stockpiles.
