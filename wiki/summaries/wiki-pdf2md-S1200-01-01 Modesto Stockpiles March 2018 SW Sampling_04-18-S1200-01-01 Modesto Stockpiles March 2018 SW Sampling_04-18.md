Geocon Consultants, Inc. conducted surface water sampling at the Modesto Stockpiles (Project S1200-01-01) in March 2018, with reference to historical data from April 2013 to January 2018. The sampling, which included designated runoff locations (PL1, PL2, PL3, PL4) and background locations (BG1), occurred during a light rain event, though insufficient water prevented sampling at some planned locations (PL5, BG2). Samples were analyzed for dissolved metals (including Title 22 Metals), general minerals (e.g., chloride, nitrate, sulfate), total dissolved solids (TDS), total suspended solids (TSS), and field parameters (e.g., pH, electrical conductivity).

Key findings from the March 2018 event revealed elevated levels of certain metals: Lead was detected at 46 µg/l in sample PL3, exceeding the Primary MCL of 15 µg/l, and Manganese concentrations (54 to 370 µg/l) in multiple samples (PL2, PL3, PL4, BG1) surpassed the Secondary MCL of 50 µg/l. Other metals like Antimony, Arsenic, Copper, and Zinc were also measured, with some historical data (e.g., Barium in 2006) indicating potential past elevations. General mineral analysis remained consistent with earlier sampling.

The report also detailed quality assurance/quality control (QA/QC) measures, noting sample dilutions due to high analyte concentrations or matrix interference, and the use of data qualifiers like "J" (estimated concentration) and "ND" (not detected). Acceptable RPDs and other QA/QC parameters were generally met. Caltrans plans additional sampling during future rain events, and the data will be submitted to the DTSC, CVRWQCB, and relevant state databases.
