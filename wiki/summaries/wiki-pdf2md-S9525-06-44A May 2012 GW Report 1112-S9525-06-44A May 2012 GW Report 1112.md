Based on the eight provided section summaries, the following is a coherent synthesis of the groundwater monitoring round (May 2012) for the Modesto Stockpiles site (Project S9525-06-44).

## Document Identity and Scope

- **Report Type:** Groundwater monitoring round (May 2012) – data tables, field logs, and laboratory reports; narrative text (introduction, discussion, conclusions) was not provided.
- **Preparer:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Sampling Date:** Primary event on May 17, 2012 (with additional sampling through May 19–24, 2012; analysis completed by June 7, 2012).
- **Project Number:** S9525-06-44, Task Order No. 44 (EA 10-403500, Contract No. 06A1580).
- **Media Sampled:** Groundwater (plus one surface water sample).
- **Wells/Stations Sampled:** MW‑1 through MW‑8, plus a “Surface Sample.” All planned locations were sampled; no dry, low‑yield, or inaccessible wells were reported. Historical data from 2006 and March 2012 were also tabulated.

## Key Results

Analytes were measured against California EPA MCLs (May 2009) and health advisories.

- **Metals (dissolved by EPA 6020):**
  - **Barium:** 55–310 µg/L (MCL = 1,000 µg/L; Health Advisory = 700 µg/L). No exceedances.
  - **Arsenic:** 2.1–3.9 µg/L (MCL = 10 µg/L). No exceedances.
  - **Chromium:** 1.6–12 µg/L (MCL = 50 µg/L). No exceedances.
  - **Lead:** Not detected above the PQL of 1.0 µg/L in any well.
  - **Strontium:** 270–1,400 µg/L (Health Advisory = 4,000 µg/L). No exceedances.
  - Other metals (antimony, selenium, vanadium, etc.) were reported at low levels.

- **General Minerals:**
  - **Nitrate as N:** 2.5–26 mg/L. Exceeded the Primary MCL of 10 mg/L in MW‑1 (12 mg/L), MW‑4 (10 mg/L), MW‑5 (26–27 mg/L), and MW‑6 (18 mg/L). MW‑1 is upgradient; the nitrate may reflect natural background.
  - **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS):** Exceeded the Secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in MW‑4, MW‑5, and MW‑6.

- **Organics:**
  - **PAHs (SVOCs by EPA 8270/SIM):** None detected above PQLs (0.20 µg/L) in any sample (MW‑5, MW‑8, and other wells).

- **Trends:** Barium, lead, and strontium concentrations were similar to historical levels and to the prior March 2012 round.

## Field Conditions and QA/QC

- **Hydrogeologic Conditions:**
  - Depth to groundwater: 29.74–38.25 ft below top of casing.
  - Groundwater flow direction: southeast at a gradient of approximately 0.0006.

- **Sampling Issues:**
  - MW‑5 went dry after purging 1 gallon; it was allowed to recover twice before sampling.

- **Laboratory Quality Control Issues (affecting data usability):**
  - **Equipment Blank:** Low-level detections of antimony, barium, calcium, magnesium, and sodium were found in the equipment blank; corresponding sample results were qualified as estimated (potential to be less than reported).
  - **Matrix Spike/Matrix Spike Duplicate (MS/MSD) Outliers:** Several recoveries fell outside control limits for analytes including arsenic, chromium, barium, strontium, calcium, sodium, and zinc. These were flagged with qualifiers such as “M1,” “R” (RPD exceedance), and “E” (exceeded calibration range).
  - **Surrogate Issue (SVOC analysis):** 1,2-Dichlorobenzene‑d4 recovery exceeded the upper acceptance limit (123% vs. limit of 36–107%) in the blank and LCS; a corrective action report was referenced. No target analyte was detected in the sample despite the surrogate exceedance.
  - **Other Qualifiers:** Holding time exceeded for nitrate as N (H3) in one sample; dilution required (D6) for some analyses.
  - **LCS/LCSD:** Recoveries were generally within acceptance limits for metals and SVOCs, and method blanks were non-detect.

## Conclusions

No formal conclusions or recommendations were provided in the extracted data sections. The data indicate that nitrate exceeds the primary MCL in multiple wells, TDS exceeds the secondary MCL in three wells, and that analytical QA/QC flagged a few metals and one surrogate as potentially biased. The overall results for barium, lead, arsenic, chromium, and strontium were below applicable thresholds.
