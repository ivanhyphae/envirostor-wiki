Here is a structured summary of the source document for the case-file wiki.

### Document identity
- **Plan type:** Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Geocon)
- **Date:** December 13, 2023
- **Project number or task order:** Project No. S2350-01-02; Caltrans Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2; EA No. 10-1E7003

### Objective and scope
- **Purpose:** To evaluate the integrity of the stockpile containment system for barium and lead-contaminated soil (Stockpiles 1 and 2) that was encapsulated beneath State Route (SR) 132. The work will determine if storm water runoff has been impacted by chemicals of potential concern (COPCs) from the encapsulated material.
- **Media addressed:** Storm water runoff.
- **Area/features addressed:** Runoff from the drainage system capturing water from the segment of SR 132 overlying the former Stockpiles 1 and 2. Two specific discharge locations are identified: SW-East (north side of SR 132, west of SR 99) and SW-West (along north side of SR 132, adjacent and east of Ponding Basin 5). Two background locations are also proposed: BG-West and BG-East.
- **Approval or decision the plan supports:** The plan supports ongoing compliance with DTSC-approved protocols, as outlined in the referenced 2006 SAP and 2013 SAP Addendum. The analytical results will be used to gauge the long-term effectiveness of the DTSC-approved containment design.

### Proposed approach
- **Sampling design:**
    - **Locations/counts:** Collection of up to two storm water runoff samples per event from the drainage system at locations SW-East and SW-West. At least one background sample will be collected per event (from locations BG-West or BG-East).
    - **Frequency:** Up to four separate qualifying rainfall events per year.
    - **Sampling method:** Collection using a pre-cleaned or disposable scoop, transferred to laboratory-provided containers. Samples for dissolved metals will be field-filtered through a 0.45-micron filter. Field parameters (pH, electrical conductivity, temperature, turbidity) will be measured. Additional samples may be collected if visible runoff is observed leaving the Caltrans right-of-way (ROW).
- **Target analytes:**
    - Title 22 dissolved metals (including barium and strontium).
    - Sulfate.
    - Total dissolved solids (TDS).
- **Analytical methods:**
    - Title 22 metals: EPA Method 6020/7470.
    - Sulfate: EPA Method 9056/300.0.
    - TDS: EPA Method 160.1.
- **Decision criteria or action levels:** Not explicitly proposed in this SAP. The plan references that previous results were compared to Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), but no specific use of these thresholds is defined as a decision criterion for future actions.
- **QA/QC:** Standard procedures are proposed, including pre-cleaned equipment, chain-of-custody, method blanks, duplicate samples, and matrix spike samples at specified frequencies.

### Deliverables and approvals sought
- **Deliverables:** A summary report will be prepared after each sampling event, including a background summary, scope of work, field observations, laboratory data, data evaluation, conclusions and recommendations, maps, tabular data, photographs, and chain-of-custody documentation. A draft report will be provided to Caltrans for review; a final report will be issued after incorporating comments.
- **Agency approval sought:** The plan is submitted to Caltrans (Mr. Richard Stewart, PG) for approval. It follows protocols approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) established in prior SAPs.

### Responsible parties
- **Geocon Consultants, Inc.:** Preparer of the SAP and the entity that will perform field sampling, laboratory coordination, data evaluation, and report preparation.
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) - District 6:** Client and owner/operator of the site (Hazardous Waste Branch).
- **Asset Laboratories:** Proposed laboratory for chemical analysis of water samples.
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC):** Regulatory agency that approved the original containment system and sampling protocols.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB):** Co-regulator.
