### Summary of Groundwater Monitoring Report – November 2012

This document, prepared by **Geocon Consultants, Inc.** (Project No. S9525-06-44A) for **Caltrans**, presents the results of a groundwater monitoring round conducted in November 2012 at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site in Stanislaus County, California. The monitoring event, which took place on November 28–29, 2012, successfully sampled all ten wells in the network (MW-1 through MW-10). Field conditions were generally good, though wells MW-5 and MW-6 went dry during purging, and MW-2 exhibited high turbidity and went dry before sampling could be completed.

Analytical results were compared to California's Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs). Key findings include:

- **Nitrates (as Nitrogen):** Exceeded the Primary MCL of 10 mg/L in five wells: MW-1 (12 mg/L), MW-5 (24 mg/L), MW-6 (20 mg/L), MW-8 (14 mg/L), and MW-10 (18 mg/L).
- **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS):** Exceeded the Secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in four wells: MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10.
- **Metals:** Manganese at MW-8 exceeded the California Notification Level. Barium and strontium remained well below thresholds.
- **PAHs:** No polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) were detected.

The report identifies two distinct groundwater types, as visualized by Stiff diagrams in the appendix. Most wells (MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-5, MW-7, MW-9, MW-10) were **calcium-dominant**, while MW-3, MW-6, and MW-8 were **sodium-dominant**. The report notes that the nitrate and TDS exceedances are persistent trends observed throughout 2012.

In their conclusions, Geocon recommends continuing the every-other-month sampling schedule and discontinuing PAH analysis. The report is submitted for client and regulatory review.
