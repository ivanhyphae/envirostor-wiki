## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Formal directive letter from DTSC to Caltrans, requesting specific actions for ongoing management of barium-impacted soil stockpiles at the SR 132 West Expressway/Freeway project site.
- **Author or preparer:** Randy S. Adams, C.E.G., Senior Engineering Geologist, Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program, DTSC.
- **Date or period covered:** March 1, 2012 (letter date); references site visit of February 15, 2012; actions requested for ongoing implementation.

## Key claims
- DTSC, in consultation with RWQCB, asserts that the soil stockpiles "do not pose a risk to human health for: 1) Caltrans workers who access the fenced site to conduct mowing operations, conduct fence repairs, or other routine activities; 2) trespassers; and 3) residents adjacent to the stockpiles" — *provided* specified management conditions remain in effect (DTSC correspondence dated 12/17/09, reaffirmed in this letter).
- The stockpiles consist of excess native soils and pond tailings generated during 1960s construction of SR 99, originating from a parcel previously occupied by FMC Inc.'s percolation pond (barium sulfate and strontium sulfate ore processing).
- DTSC identifies the site as potentially an "attractive nuisance" requiring quarterly debris removal.

## Regulatory decisions
- **Agency:** DTSC (with RWQCB consultation).
- **Date:** March 1, 2012.
- **Decision type:** Conditional affirmation of prior finding (12/17/09) that stockpiles pose no unacceptable risk under current management, coupled with a directive to implement enhanced maintenance and monitoring actions.
- **Conditions imposed:** See Key terms and conditions below.
- **Related agreements referenced:**
    - DTSC Draft Voluntary Cleanup Agreement (VCA) with StanCOG, dated 2/7/2012.
    - RWQCB Draft Reimbursement Agreement with StanCOG, dated 2/7/2012 (both approved by StanCOG Policy Board on 2/15/2012).

## Analytical results
- **Contaminants/media:** Groundwater (monitoring wells) and soil (stockpiles).
- **Concentration ranges:** Not provided in this letter.
- **Thresholds:** Not specified in this letter (references existing groundwater assessment workplan and required analytes).
- **Exceedances:** Not noted.
- **Required analytes for future monitoring:** Strontium (added to existing analyte list) and others as needed to determine sources of groundwater impacts.

## Remedial actions
- **Activity described:** Continued management and maintenance (no active remediation or removal).
- **Quantity/phase status:** Three separate piles totaling approximately 120,000 cubic yards; generation from 1960s; proposed future beneficial reuse as fill for SR 132 abutments and elevated sections (not yet constructed).
- **Verification outcomes:** None; letter directs actions but does not report completion.

## Responsible parties
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans):** Owner/operator of stockpiles on Caltrans right-of-way property; responsible for implementing all requested actions (fence maintenance, vegetation, groundwater monitoring, coordination).
- **Stanislaus Council of Governments (StanCOG):** Co-sponsor of SR 132 project; entering into agreements with DTSC and RWQCB for additional characterization.
- **FMC Inc. (and predecessors):** Former chemical manufacturer; original source of barium/strontium-impacted material on a 4.3-acre parcel from which stockpile soils were excavated.

## Key terms and conditions
- Caltrans must continue to:
    1. **Limit access** to authorized personnel; install/repair fences; inspect and remove debris quarterly.
    2. **Maintain grade and vegetative cover;** inspect and revegetate quarterly.
    3. **Maintain groundwater monitoring system:** quarterly monitoring in accordance with Groundwater Assessment Workplan dated 1/26/2006; add strontium to analyte list; submit quarterly reports to DTSC and RWQCB including field data, chain of custody, certified lab results, summary tables, and trend discussion.
- Caltrans must coordinate with StanCOG, DTSC, and RWQCB during preparation of a sampling and analysis plan for additional site characterization (required initial step under draft VCA).
