### Combined Summary

This document is a **Public Hearing Summary Report** prepared by Caltrans (in coordination with DTSC, the Central Valley RWQCB, and StanCOG) for the **SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project** in Stanislaus County, CA. It details a public hearing held on **February 22, 2017**, and summarizes comments received during the public comment period from **January 18 to March 17, 2017**. The core of the project links a transportation initiative to a **remedial action plan (RAP)** for the **Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles**.

These three stockpiles contain approximately **160,000 cubic yards** of soil impacted with metals (primarily barium, lead, and strontium) resulting from historical waste discharged by the **FMC Corporation**. A 2007 Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA) concluded the stockpiles posed an unacceptable risk without strict management conditions. The Draft Final RAP proposes **Alternative 4 (Containment)**, where the contaminated soil would be encapsulated within the structural section of the new roadway (beneath pavement, bridge abutments, and behind retaining walls). This is a phased project: Phase 1 (2018–2020) involves constructing retaining walls and capping unpaved areas, while Phase 2 (2028) is the full highway buildout. The plan includes ongoing groundwater monitoring and deed restrictions.

**Public comments** revealed significant opposition to the containment strategy. Several attendees expressed a preference for **Alternative 3 – Removal (excavation and offsite disposal)** due to safety concerns about the long-term efficacy of capping and potential health risks. Commenters cited a list of nearby residents with cancers and diseases they believe are linked to the site, arguing that the stockpiles had been inadequately sampled and that the plan would eventually leak. Other concerns included traffic impacts, the need for soundwalls, and general adverse effects on air and noise pollution.

Following the public comment period, the project team is responsible for reviewing and responding to all comments before finalizing the **Final EIR/EA** and the **Final RAP**, which are necessary steps prior to project approval.
