## Decision or request
- **What is being approved**: DTSC has approved the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project
- **Document acted on**: Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route (SR) 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project
- **Selected cleanup alternative**: Containment of the stockpiles during construction — stockpiles will be contained behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath the roadway; unpaved portions will have clean fill cover
- **Conditions/contingencies**: Land use controls and inspection of pavement and other containment features, and groundwater monitoring will be conducted annually to ensure long-term remedy integrity
- **CEQA/NEPA status**: DTSC determined the selected containment remedy will not have a significant effect on the environment with implementation of project controls (mitigation measures); DTSC finds Caltrans' Final EIR/EA contains all necessary mitigation measures to maintain less than significant impact; DTSC prepared a Statement of Findings and a Notice of Determination (NOD) in compliance with CEQA
