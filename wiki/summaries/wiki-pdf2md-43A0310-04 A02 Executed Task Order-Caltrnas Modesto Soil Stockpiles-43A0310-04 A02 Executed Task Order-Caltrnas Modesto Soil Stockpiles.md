## Document Identity

- **Document type and purpose:** Interagency Agreement Task Order Amendment #2 (Amendment to Task Order No. 4 under Agreement 43A0310 between Caltrans and DTSC). Purpose is to extend the period of performance and provide additional funding for DTSC oversight and regulatory review of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project (State Route 132/99, Stanislaus County).
- **Author/preparer:** Prepared by Caltrans (Contract Manager Richard Bailey, P.G.; Task Manager Richard Stewart) and DTSC (Task Order Manager Steven Becker; Contract Manager Paul Ruffin). Signed by Mauricio Serrano, P.E. (Caltrans) and Paul Ruffin (DTSC).
- **Date/period covered:** Execution date February 9, 2018. Original period June 7, 2013 – May 31, 2016; amended to extend termination to May 31, 2018.

## Key Claims

No assertions about site conditions, compliance, or remedial adequacy are made in this administrative/funding amendment. The document solely addresses funding and schedule.

## Regulatory Decisions

- **Decision type:** Amendment to an existing interagency task order — no new regulatory action (e.g., no cleanup level approval, permit issuance, or enforcement order).
- **Agency:** Caltrans (owner/operator) and DTSC (overseer). The Central Valley RWQCB is a co-regulator per the broader project context but is not named in this document.
- **Date:** February 9, 2018.
- **Conditions imposed:** None beyond the original task order scope; scope of services unchanged.

## Analytical Results

None provided in this document. (No contaminant data, media, or thresholds.)

## Remedial Actions

None described in this document.

## Responsible Parties

- **Caltrans** – Owner/operator; contract manager Richard Bailey, task manager Richard Stewart, approving official Mauricio Serrano (Chief, Hazardous Waste, Air, Noise and Paleontology Office).
- **DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control)** – Regulatory oversight; task order manager Steven Becker, contract manager Paul Ruffin (Site Mitigation and Restoration Program).

## Key Terms and Conditions

- **Total funding cap:** $261,957.00 (FY breakdown: FY13/14 $98,312; FY14/15 $39,871; FY15/16 $20,000; FY17/18 $70,000). Funded 100% federal/state split.
- **Period of performance:** Extended from May 31, 2016 to May 31, 2018. Original start June 7, 2013.
- **Scope of services:** Unchanged from original Task Order No. 4 (DTSC oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, processing of applications/requests).
- **Deliverable schedule:** Per previously approved schedule.
- **Personnel:** Unchanged.
- **Conflict of interest certification required.**
- **Invoicing, meetings, electronic submittal:** Unchanged from original.
