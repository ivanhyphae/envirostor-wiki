## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Annual cost estimate letter from DTSC to Caltrans providing projected oversight costs for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Phase 1 Interim State Route 132 West Project, for Fiscal Year 2023–2024, as required by Health and Safety Code §25269.5.
- **Author or preparer:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), Site Mitigation and Restoration Program, Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit; signed by Ms. Lora Jameson, P.G., Chief. Project manager Mr. Dean Wright, P.G., is identified as the point of contact.
- **Date or period covered:** Letter dated November 7, 2023; cost estimate covers the period July 1, 2023 through June 30, 2024.

## Key claims
- DTSC asserts that the estimate is based on project activities scheduled for the fiscal year and uses DTSC’s 2022-2023 contract estimation rates. The letter notes that this is only an estimate of projected costs; Responsible Parties/Project Proponents remain liable for all actual costs incurred by DTSC as required by law.

## Regulatory decisions
- No formal regulatory decision (approval, enforcement order, or permit) is issued in this letter. The document serves only as a cost estimate notification under HSC §25269.5. Two scheduled regulatory tasks are identified for the FY: completion of a Land Use Covenant (LUC) and review/approval of the Annual Inspection (due by January 31, 2024).
