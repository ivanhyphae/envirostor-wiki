## Document identity
- **Report type:** Groundwater monitoring round (May 2016 event)
- **Preparer/author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Reporting period/sampling date(s):** May 2, 2016
- **Project number/task order:** Project No. S9800-01-17A, Task Order No. 17, EA No. 10-0X2700, Contract No. 06A1895

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Groundwater
- **Locations sampled:** MW-1 and MW-7 (two of ten monitoring wells that contained sufficient groundwater)
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled:**
  - MW-2, MW-3, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, MW-8, MW-9, MW-10 – dry (insufficient water to purge and sample)
  - MW-6 – attempted purge, insufficient water present
  - MW-5 – well box damaged, locking cap missing (integrity intact; casing extended and stove pipe/bollards installed May 12, 2016)

## Results
**Dissolved metals (primary COCs: barium, lead, strontium):**
- Barium: MW-1 = 75 µg/l; MW-7 = 370 µg/l (Primary MCL = 1,000 µg/l; both below threshold)
- Strontium: MW-1 = 2,800 µg/l; MW-7 = 5,100 µg/l (no MCL established; consistently below historical water quality threshold of 4,000 µg/l per figures)
- Lead: not detected above PQL in either well (threshold = 15 µg/l)
- Arsenic, chromium, molybdenum, nickel, vanadium reported in both wells; none exceeded respective thresholds (MCLs or EPA Health Advisories)
- Cobalt and copper reported in MW-1 only; no established thresholds for cobalt; copper below MCL
- Manganese reported in MW-1 (6.9 µg/l; secondary MCL = 50 µg/l – not exceeded)
- Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, selenium, silver, thallium, zinc, mercury – not detected above PQLs

**General minerals:**
- Nitrate as N: MW-1 = 9.2 mg/l; MW-7 = 8.7 mg/l (primary MCL = 10 mg/l; both below)
- TDS: MW-1 = 420 mg/l; MW-7 = 510 mg/l (secondary MCL = 500 mg/l; MW-7 exceeded)
- Sulfide: MW-1 = 0.11 mg/l; MW-7 = 0.036 mg/l
- All other general minerals reported at concentrations below respective MCLs

**Trends vs. prior rounds:**
- Barium and strontium concentrations similar to historical levels and remained "significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds"
- Remaining dissolved metals at concentrations similar to historical levels
- Stiff diagrams show very slight changes in ionic content since March 2012; MW-1 and MW-7 consistently calcium-dominant

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Dry wells/low recovery:** MW-2 through MW-6 and MW-8 through MW-10 were dry or had insufficient water to purge and sample
- **Depth to water (May 2, 2016):** MW-1 = 37.32 ft below TOC; MW-7 = 41.94 ft below TOC
- **Well damage:** MW-5 – well box cover missing, box damaged, locking cap missing; no evidence of tampering with casing; integrity intact; casing extended with stove pipe/bollards installed May 12, 2016
- **Field duplicate (MW-11):** collected from MW-7; sulfide RPD = 106% (threshold 25%), therefore sulfide results qualified as estimated (potentially less than or greater than reported values)
- **Laboratory QA/QC:** Method blanks acceptable; LCS recoveries acceptable; several MS/MSD analytes had recoveries or RPDs outside control limits but validated by LCS; no data qualification necessary; data considered sufficient quality
- **Sample collection:** Dissolved metals field-filtered through 0.45-micron filter; ORP measured in MW-1 and MW-7

## Conclusions and recommendations
- None of the reported dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality thresholds
- None of the reported general minerals exceeded primary MCLs; MW-7 TDS (510 mg/l) exceeded secondary MCL of 500 mg/l
- Barium and strontium concentrations similar to historical levels; remaining dissolved metals also similar
- Groundwater monitoring currently performed annually; next monitoring event scheduled for April 2017
- Depth to water measurements collected semi-annually; next event October 2016
