## Document identity
- **Memo subject:** Stockpile 2 MSE Wall Footing Excavation Characterization Testing – soil characterization following barium‑containing soil (BCS) removal from the Stockpile 2 southerly slope.
- **Preparer:** John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Recipient:** Michael Scott, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto.
- **Date:** June 18, 2020.
- **Area / feature:** Stockpile 2 – mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation; State Route 132, Modesto, California.

## Purpose
To present analytical results and a suitability determination for excavated soil within the planned MSE wall footing zone after removal of BCS material from the Stockpile 2 southerly slope. The work was performed per the *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan* (April 9, 2020) and will support the Removal Action Completion Report.

## Data and findings
- **Sampling and testing:** Twenty‑nine discrete soil samples were collected from seven trenches (STK2‑1 through STK2‑7) at ~200‑ft intervals. Within each trench, 1‑foot‑interval samples were obtained from the surface to the planned footing depth (3 ft except one location to 5 ft). Samples were analyzed by Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL) using EPA Method 6010B for total barium and lead under 48‑hour turnaround.
- **Barium results:** Concentrations ranged from 53 mg/kg to 930 mg/kg. Fourteen of 29 samples exceeded the site‑specific maximum background concentration of 120 mg/kg. Thirteen of those fourteen exceedances occurred at depths between 0 and 3 ft. One sample (STK2‑7‑4, 140 mg/kg) was collected deeper than the planned 3‑ft excavation depth at that location.
- **Lead results:** Concentrations ranged from 3.5 mg/kg to 22 mg/kg. Most samples had lead concentrations on the same order of magnitude as the site‑specific maximum background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg.
- **Comparison to criteria:** No numerical cleanup standard is cited; the site‑specific maximum background values for barium (120 mg/kg) and lead (3.8 mg/kg) were used as thresholds for distinguishing BCS‑impacted material from clean soil. The exceedances are primarily in the upper 3 ft.

## Conclusion and recommendation
The memo concludes that the upper three feet of soil excavated from the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing must be placed within the designated Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones (i.e., managed as BCS). Material excavated from depths greater than 3 ft below the original surface is suitable for placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general embankment fill, or as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material—subject to regulatory agency approval. This recommendation is made by John E. Juhrend (Geocon).

## Follow-on and approval status
The characterization results will be incorporated into the Removal Action Completion Report, to be submitted after the Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities are complete. No separate agency approval of this memo is stated; regulatory approval is referenced for use of deeper material as capping.
