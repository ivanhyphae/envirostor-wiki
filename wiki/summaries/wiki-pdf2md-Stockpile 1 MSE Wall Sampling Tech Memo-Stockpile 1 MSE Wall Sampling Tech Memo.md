This document set, effective April 6, 2020, details Geocon's characterization testing of the Stockpile 1 MSE Wall footing excavation for WSP/City of Modesto, specifically to assess the suitability of excavated material for reuse after barium-containing soil removal. Geocon collected 15 soil samples across three trenches (STK1-1, STK1-2, STK1-3) and analyzed them for barium and lead content using EPA Test Method 6010B.

Barium concentrations ranged from 42 to 420 mg/kg, with a mean of 128.3 mg/kg and a 95% Upper Confidence Limit (UCL) of 171.9 mg/kg. While exceeding the site-specific background of 120 mg/kg, these levels were well below the 1,000 mg/kg removal threshold from the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) and various San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board Environmental Screening Levels (ESLs). Lead concentrations (1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg) were within background levels and significantly below the 80 mg/kg RDIP removal threshold.

Specific test results for samples STKI-3-3 and STKI-3-4 showed Barium at 130 mg/kg and 120 mg/kg, respectively, with Lead at 3.4 mg/kg and 1.1 mg/kg, all above the 1.0 mg/kg PQL. Quality control data for batch B0D0063 met specified limits for Barium and Lead. Additionally, a Chain of Custody record indicated requests for analysis of Gasoline Range Organics (GRO) and Polychlorinated Biphenyls (PCBs) in sample STK1-1-0. ProUCL 5.14 was used to calculate various statistically derived UCLs for Barium, considering lognormal distribution.

Geocon concluded that the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material is suitable for reuse within Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill, or as capping material, pending regulatory approval and inclusion in the Removal Action Completion Report. The memo was prepared by John E. Juhrend (Geocon) for Rick Demi, PE (WSP/City of Modesto).
