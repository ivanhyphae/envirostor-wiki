## Document identity

- **Document type and purpose**: Amendment (Amendment 1) to a Task Order under an Interagency Agreement between Caltrans and DTSC. The amendment extends the period of performance and increases funding for DTSC oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, and/or processing of applications/requests related to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project (State Route 132/99, Stanislaus County, CA).
- **Author or preparer**: Prepared under the authority of Caltrans and DTSC. Signed by Caltrans Contract Manager Richard Bailey, PG; Caltrans Chief Environmental Engineer David Ambuehl, PE; and DTSC Contract Manager Paul Ruffin. Task Order Manager: Steven Becker (DTSC); Caltrans Task Manager: Richard Stewart; Caltrans Contract Manager: Richard Bailey.
- **Date or period covered**: Original term June 7, 2013 – May 31, 2016. Amendment extends termination date to May 31, 2018. Amendment execution date: May 31, 2016 (DTSC signature); June 17, 2016 (Caltrans signature). Amendment effective as of execution.

## Key claims

- No assertions are made in this document about site conditions, compliance status, or remedial adequacy. The document is an administrative/funding amendment only.

## Regulatory decisions

- **Decision type**: Amendment to Interagency Agreement Task Order (43A0310-04, Amendment 1) for continued oversight by DTSC of the Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation.
- **Agency**: California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) – owner/operator; Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) – regulatory oversight under Agreement No. 12-T0106.
- **Date**: Approved and executed May–June 2016.
- **Conditions imposed**: Scope of services unchanged; total amount payable not to exceed $158,183.00; work to be conducted in accordance with previously approved schedule.

## Analytical results

- Not applicable. No analytical data are presented.

## Remedial actions

- Not described in this document. This is a funding/oversight amendment only.

## Responsible parties

- **Caltrans (California Department of Transportation)**: Owner/operator of the Modesto Soil Stockpiles; party to the Interagency Agreement; responsible for funding DTSC oversight and coordinating remediation.
- **DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control)**: Regulatory oversight agency; receives reimbursement for oversight costs under the agreement.
- **Project personnel named**: Steven Becker (DTSC Task Order Manager), Richard Stewart (Caltrans Task Manager), Richard Bailey (Caltrans Contract Manager), David Ambuehl (Caltrans Chief Environmental Engineer), Paul Ruffin (DTSC Contract Manager).

## Key terms and conditions

- **Period of performance**: Extended to May 31, 2018 (from original May 31, 2016).
- **Total funding**: Not to exceed $158,183.00 (prior FY $64,183; FY 16/17 $94,000). Additional amendments possible if necessary.
- **Scope**: Unchanged from original Task Order – DTSC oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, and/or processing applications/requests.
- **Invoicing and meetings**: No changes to original provisions.
- **Conflict of Interest**: Caltrans personnel must comply with Deputy Directive DD-09-R3 and Government Code 19990 regarding incompatible activities.
