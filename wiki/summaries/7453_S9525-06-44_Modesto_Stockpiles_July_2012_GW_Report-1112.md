### Document Identity and Scope
This document is a July 2012 groundwater monitoring report for the "Modesto Stockpiles" project (Project No. S9525-06-44), prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for Caltrans. The sampling event occurred on July 16–17, 2012, with an initial report dated July 27, 2012, and an amended report issued November 13, 2012. The monitoring scope included a network of ten wells: MW-1 through MW-10, all of which were sampled. The report is addressed to Caltrans, DTSC, and the CVRWQCB.

### Analytical Results and Geochemistry
The monitoring program analyzed groundwater for general minerals, major cations and anions, PAHs (by EPA 8270C/SIM), total and dissolved metals (by EPA 6020), and dissolved mercury (by EPA 7470). The primary contaminants of concern were barium, lead, and strontium. Stiff diagrams classified most wells (MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-5, MW-7, MW-9, and MW-10) as calcium-dominant, while three wells (MW-3, MW-6, and MW-8) were sodium-bicarbonate dominant.

**Threshold Exceedances:**
- **Nitrate as N:** Exceeded the Primary MCL of 10 mg/L in MW-1 (12 mg/L), MW-5 (20 mg/L), MW-6, and MW-9, consistent with prior 2012 rounds.
- **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS):** Exceeded the Secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in MW-1 (540 mg/L), MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, and MW-10 (710 mg/L).
- **Vanadium:** Exceeded the 50 µg/L threshold in MW-6 (51 µg/L).
- **Lead, Barium, Strontium:** Lead was not detected above the PQL (1.0 µg/L); barium and strontium were detected in all samples but remained significantly below thresholds, consistent with historical trends.
- **Other Analytes:** PAHs were not reported above PQLs. Arsenic, chromium, molybdenum, nickel, selenium, copper, antimony, and zinc were detected but below thresholds. Beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, manganese, silver, and thallium were not detected above PQLs.

### Field Conditions and Quality Control
Field samples were filtered for dissolved metals analysis. Wells MW-5 and MW-6 went dry during purging but were allowed to recover. Wells MW-1 and MW-2 had turbid water during sampling. Groundwater flow was southeast at a gradient of approximately 0.0006.

**QA/QC Deviations:**
- **Equipment Blank:** Detections of barium (1.1 µg/L), copper (1.0 µg/L), and sodium (100 µg/L) led to J-flags for these analytes.
- **MS/MSD Exceedances:** Recoveries for barium, antimony, and selenium were outside control limits, but no data qualification was deemed necessary.
- **Sulfide QC Failure:** A matrix spike duplicate had a 281% recovery (M2 flag). A "B" qualifier was noted for sulfide in MW-8.
- **External QA:** Split samples were submitted to Excelchem Environmental Labs for independent analysis by the CVRWQCB.

### Conclusions and Recommendations
Geocon concluded that, with the exception of vanadium in MW-6, no dissolved metals exceeded their thresholds. Nitrate and TDS exceedances were noted in multiple wells, consistent with previous samplings. The report offers no explicit recommendations beyond an offer to answer questions.
