### Summary of the Recorded Land Use Covenant for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles

This document is a formally recorded Land Use Covenant (LUC) and associated agreements that impose permanent, enforceable environmental restrictions on property in Stanislaus County, California, known as the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (State Route 132). The primary purpose of the LUC is to ensure the long-term protectiveness of the completed remedial action by controlling future land use and soil disturbance.

**Regulatory and Cleanup Background**
The covenant was imposed by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) to govern property where elevated levels of barium, up to a maximum concentration of **72,000 mg/kg**, remain in the soil. The selected and completed remediation, overseen by DTSC and implemented by the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans), involved the consolidation and clean-fill capping of approximately 160,000 cubic yards of barium-containing soil (BCS) to be used as highway embankment fill. The remedy was finalized in a Remedial Action Completion Report approved on January 18, 2023. The legal boundaries of two main stockpile areas are formally defined in a survey exhibit, with borehole data indicating contamination depths of up to 30 feet below ground surface.

**Key Restrictions, Obligations, and Responsible Parties**
The LUC, which runs with the land indefinitely, establishes several core restrictions and obligations primarily held by **Caltrans** (the owner/covenantor) and all future owners or occupants:

1.  **Prohibited Land Uses:** The property cannot be used for residential housing, a hospital, a school (for persons under 18), or a child day care center without prior written DTSC approval.
2.  **Soil and Groundwater Management:** Any activity that disturbs the soil requires a pre-approved **Soil Management Plan** as detailed in the June 20, 2023, Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Agreement (Exhibit C). Any extraction of groundwater also requires a pre-approved Groundwater Management Plan. DTSC must receive a minimum of 10 working days' advance notice and provide prior written authorization before any BCS disturbance.
3.  **Ongoing Operation and Maintenance:** Caltrans is required to fully implement the attached **O&M Plan** (prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc.), which governs annual inspections, monitoring, and reporting. A formal **Five-Year Review** of the remedy’s protectiveness is required, with the first report due by January 2028. Caltrans is a state entity and is exempt from providing financial assurance for the remedy.
4.  **Compliance and Enforcement:** Failure to comply with the covenant's terms authorizes DTSC to pursue administrative, civil, or criminal actions and may require the removal or modification of improvements. DTSC retains access rights for inspection and enforcement.
5.  **Conveyance and Notification:** The LUC must be referenced in all future deeds and leases. Any new owner must notify DTSC within 30 days of a property transfer. Any violation of the LUC terms must be reported to DTSC within 10 calendar days.
