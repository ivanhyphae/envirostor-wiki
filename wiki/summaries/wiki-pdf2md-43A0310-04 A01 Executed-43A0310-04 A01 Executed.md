## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: Task Order Amendment. Amends Task Order #4 of Interagency Agreement 43A0310 to extend the term and increase funding for DTSC's oversight of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project.
- Author or preparer: California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)
- Date or period covered: Original Term: June 7, 2013 through May 31, 2016. Amended Term: June 7, 2013 through May 31, 2018. Amendment Execution Dates: 5.24.16 (Caltrans Contract Manager), 6/17/16 (Caltrans Chief Environmental Engineer), 5/31/2016 (DTSC Contract Manager).

## Regulatory decisions
- Decision type: Task Order Amendment
- Agency: California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)
- Date: Amendment execution dates range from May 24, 2016, to June 17, 2016.
- Conditions imposed: Extends the period of performance of Task Order #4 from May 31, 2016, to May 31, 2018. Increases the total task order amount to $158,183.00. The scope of services, invoicing, meetings, electronic submittal of documents, and project personnel remain unchanged from the original Task Order.

## Responsible parties
- DTSC Task Order Manager: Steven Becker
- Caltrans Task Manager: Richard Stewart
- Caltrans Contract Manager: Richard Bailey
- Caltrans Chief Environmental Engineer: David Ambuehl, P.E.
- DTSC Contract Manager: Paul Ruffin

## Key terms and conditions
- Required follow-on actions: DTSC will continue to be reimbursed for oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, and/or processing applications and requests made by Caltrans to mitigate impacts from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles.
- Deadlines: Work to be conducted in accordance with the previously approved schedule. The amended termination date is May 31, 2018.
- Monitoring obligations: N/A, refers to oversight reimbursement.
- Restrictions: Total amount payable by Caltrans under this Task Order shall not exceed $158,183.00, unless further amended.
