## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) Notice of Determination (NOD). This document formally notifies the State Clearinghouse that the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), as a Responsible Agency, has approved the project and filed a determination that the project will not have a significant effect on the environment.
- **Author or preparer:** Signed by the Unit Chief (Steven R. Becker) of DTSC’s Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program; contact person is Randy Adams, Senior Engineering Geologist.
- **Date or period covered:** Determination made on March 14, 2018; document filed on March 15, 2018.

## Key claims
- The document asserts, via DTSC’s determination, that the approved remedial project “will not have a significant effect on the environment.”
- It states that DTSC is utilizing the Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) that was prepared and certified by Caltrans (Lead Agency) on March 2, 2018, for its CEQA analysis of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP).
- It specifies that while Caltrans prepared a Statement of Overriding Considerations for the project, that statement is “not applicable to DTSC’s project.”

## Regulatory decisions
- **Decision type (Agency):** CEQA Notice of Determination (DTSC, as Responsible Agency).
- **Date:** March 14, 2018 (approval); March 15, 2018 (filing).
- **Conditions imposed:** The determination notes that “Mitigation measures were made a condition of project approval.”

## Analytical results
- **Contaminants cited:** Metals, primarily barium, strontium, and lead.
- **Media:** Soil (three stockpiles) and groundwater (monitoring mentioned).
- **Concentration ranges/Thresholds/Exceedances:** No specific concentrations, ranges, or threshold values are provided in this document.

## Remedial actions
- **Activity:** Containment of three soil stockpiles totaling approximately 160,000 cubic yards.
- **Phase status:** Approved (via RAP approval on March 14, 2018); construction-phase activity.
- **Description of work:** Stockpiles (located within state right-of-way) will be contained behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath the roadway. Unpaved portions will receive a clean fill cover. This is intended to prevent exposure and minimize surface water infiltration into groundwater.
- **Verification outcomes:** Not described in this document (though annual inspections and groundwater monitoring are cited as future obligations).

## Responsible parties
- **Caltrans:** Named as the owner/operator, Lead Agency under CEQA (prepared EIR/EA and RAP).
- **DTSC:** Named as the CEQA Responsible Agency and the regulatory body approving the RAP.
- **Central Valley RWQCB:** Not explicitly named in this document (co-regulator per corpus context, but not mentioned in this specific NOD).

## Key terms and conditions
- **Land use controls:** Required.
- **Inspection:** Annual inspection of the pavement and other features of the containment remedy.
- **Groundwater monitoring:** Annual monitoring required.
- **Duration:** These obligations will continue “into the future” to ensure remedy integrity.
