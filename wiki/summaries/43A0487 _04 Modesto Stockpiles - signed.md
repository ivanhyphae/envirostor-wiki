## Document Identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Interagency Service Request (Contract Amendment) between Caltrans and DTSC to reimburse DTSC for regulatory oversight of the Modesto Soil Stockpiles (Site) environmental work supporting Phase 2 of the State Route 132 West Expressway construction.
- **Author or preparer**: Prepared by Caltrans (Matthew Farris, Contract Manager; Adam Inman, Service Request Manager) and DTSC (Arielle Mclesky, DTSC Service Request Manager; Katherine Henry, DTSC Contract Manager).
- **Date or period covered**: Executed 8/25/2025; period of performance 8/4/2025 – 6/30/2030.

## Key Claims
- The Site, Modesto Soil Stockpiles, contains environmental contamination requiring regulatory oversight.
- The work supports Phase 2 of the SR 132 West Expressway construction.
- DTSC will perform professional staff services to provide regulatory oversight for Caltrans activities at the Site.
- The scope includes review of monitoring/inspection reports, Remedial Design Implementation Plans, well destruction reports, participation in meetings, and field oversight as needed.

## Regulatory Decisions
- **Decision type**: Interagency service request authorizing DTSC oversight activities; not a standalone regulatory order but formalizes DTSC’s role under the master agreement (Caltrans Agreement #43A0487 / DTSC #25-T5466).
- **Agency**: California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and California Department of Transportation (Caltrans).
- **Date**: Execution 8/25/2025.
- **Conditions imposed**: DTSC must adhere to a 30-day review period for documents; billing must include daily logs and timesheets; cost cap of $113,000; no formal progress reports required but written comments and recommendations will be provided.

## Analytical Results
- **None**: The document contains no sample data, contaminant concentrations, or threshold comparisons.

## Remedial Actions
- **None**: No physical remediation activities are described. DTSC’s role is limited to oversight (review, comment, meetings, field visits).

## Responsible Parties
- **Caltrans**: Owner/operator of the Site; responsible for contracting and funding oversight (via District 10, EA/EFIS 10-1E700/10-1500-0115); named individuals:
  - Matthew Farris, PG – Caltrans Contract Manager (Sacramento)
  - Adam Inman, PG – Caltrans Service Request Manager (Fresno)
- **DTSC**: Regulatory oversight agency; named individuals:
  - Katherine Henry – DTSC Contract Manager (Berkeley)
  - Arielle Mclesky – DTSC Service Request Manager (Sacramento)
- **Other**: Dragomir Bogdanic (Caltrans) signed as additional signatory.

## Key Terms and Conditions
- **Cost**: Total amount payable ≤ $113,000; billed according to Agreement No. 43A0487 Exhibit B; actual direct costs reimbursed; substitutions allowed if both Caltrans managers agree and cost cap not exceeded.
- **Term**: 8/4/2025 – 6/30/2030; work items implemented as needed; 30-day minimum review period for DTSC on submittals.
- **Deliverables**: No formal progress reports; written comments and recommendations provided; invoices must include hours by classification and daily logs.
- **Meetings**: At a minimum, DTSC will meet/confer after report review; monthly meetings can be requested by Caltrans.
- **Personnel**: New key staff or job classifications require approval by Caltrans Contract Manager; principal contacts designated (see Responsible Parties).
- **Conflict of Interest**: Caltrans personnel sign compliance with Deputy Directive DD-09-R3 and Government Code 19990.
