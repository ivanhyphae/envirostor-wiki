### Consolidated Summary: Groundwater Monitoring Report – Caltrans Modesto Stockpiles (December 2013)

This document is a groundwater monitoring report for the **Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site** (Project No. S9800-01-17/S9525-01-44C) in Stanislaus County, California. It was prepared by **Geocon Consultants, Inc.** with laboratory analysis by **Advanced Technology Laboratories (ELAP No. 1838)** for the December 2013 sampling event.

The monitoring event, conducted on December 11-13, 2013, sampled all ten on-site wells (MW-1 through MW-10). Field conditions were standard, though low recharge was noted in wells MW-1, MW-3, and MW-12. Standard purging and sampling protocols were followed, groundwater flow direction was confirmed toward the southeast, and depth-to-groundwater was measured. Key field observations and quality control issues included matrix spike recovery for sulfide outside acceptable limits (192% in one batch) and data qualifiers (D1, D5, D6) applied to several sulfide results due to matrix interference or dilution.

**Analytical Results** covered total sulfide, dissolved metals (via EPA 6020), dissolved mercury (via EPA 7470A), and major ions. Key findings include:

- **Sulfide** was detected in multiple wells: MW-2 (0.033 mg/L), MW-4 (0.040 mg/L), MW-5 (0.011 mg/L), MW-6 (0.11 mg/L), MW-9 (0.021 mg/L), and MW-10 (0.19 mg/L).
- **Nitrate (as N)** exceeded the Primary MCL of 10 mg/L in MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, and MW-10 (range: 2.4 to 25 mg/L).
- **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)** exceeded the Secondary MCL of 500 mg/L at six wells (MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, MW-10).
- **Manganese** exceeded its Secondary MCL at well MW-6.
- Primary contaminants of concern (barium, lead, strontium) remained significantly below thresholds with stable or declining trends.
- Other metals (arsenic, vanadium) were detected but below MCLs; antimony, beryllium, cadmium, silver, thallium, and mercury were not detected.
- PAH analysis had been discontinued prior to this event with regulatory concurrence due to consistent non-detection.
- Cation-anion balance calculations and Stiff diagrams classified groundwater as either calcium-dominant (e.g., MW-1, MW-2, MW-4) or sodium-dominant (e.g., MW-3, MW-5, MW-6).

**Conclusions and Recommendations** note that while dissolved metals (except manganese at MW-6) did not exceed thresholds, nitrate and TDS remain concerns. Elevated levels at upgradient well MW-1 may indicate natural background conditions. Quarterly groundwater monitoring was recommended to continue, with the next event scheduled for March 2014.

**Responsible parties** include **Caltrans** (owner/operator), the **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)** and the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB)** (regulatory oversight), with **Geocon** performing the monitoring and reporting.
