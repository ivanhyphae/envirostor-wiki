Here is the structured summary of the provided source document, formatted for a case-file wiki.

## Document identity
- **Plan type**: Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP); email-formatted proposal for approval.
- **Preparer / author organization**: Geocon Consultants, Inc. (prepared at the request of Caltrans).
- **Date**: Email sent February 4, 2020. Field work tentatively scheduled for February 11, 2020.
- **Project number or task order**: Not stated in the email cover letter or attachment.

## Objective and scope
- **Purpose of the work**: To characterize soil intended for use as clean fill/cover over the BCS stockpile. The work supports a variance or approval to use on-site soil as clean fill cover material.
- **Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed**: Source area along Highway 132 (SR 132) alignment from Carpenter Road extending eastward. Media: surface soil (0–5 ft depth). Target stockpile: BCS stockpile.
- **What approval or decision the plan supports**: Approval from the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) to use this soil as "clean fill" cover on the stockpile. The plan explicitly defers to DTSC approval before field work begins.

## Proposed approach
- **Sampling design**:
    - 12 borings, each 5 feet deep.
    - 6 depth-discrete, 4-point composite samples will be analyzed.
    - Depth intervals:
        - 0–2 ft: analyze for organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) and heavy metals (3 composites).
        - 3–5 ft: analyze for heavy metals only (3 composites).
- **Target analytes**: Organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) and heavy metals (0–2 ft); heavy metals (3–5 ft).
- **Methods**: Proposed use of Geotech logs from 2017 Carpenter Road test borings for lithologic description. Sampling via borings with 4-point compositing and depth-discrete splits.
- **Decision criteria or action levels**: Not explicitly stated in the email. The plan relies on "regulatory screening levels" cited for nearby residential backyards (Rincon 2018 Phase II ESA). The intent is to confirm the material meets clean fill thresholds, but specific numeric levels are not provided.
- **Sequence, schedule, or phasing**:
    - Field work (drilling) tentatively scheduled for February 11, 2020.
    - Results provided after analyses (48-hour analytical turnaround planned).
    - Work contingent on contract authorization, USA notice (or Geocon mark-out), and DTSC sampling plan approval.
    - No further phasing described.
- **For a variance**: Not a variance request. The proposal seeks "acceptance of the sampling plan" and authorization to proceed with characterization.

## Deliverables and approvals sought
- **Deliverables**: Analytical results from the 6 composite samples, plus a written statement of the sampling plan (provided via email and attached exhibit). Geocon logs from Carpenter Road test borings (2017) were included for lithologic context.
- **Approvals sought**:
    - DTSC "quick review and acceptance of the sampling plan" (per Richard Stewart's request).
    - Authorization to proceed with drilling and analyses.
    - Note: The plan states "pending... receipt of DTSC sampling plan approval."
