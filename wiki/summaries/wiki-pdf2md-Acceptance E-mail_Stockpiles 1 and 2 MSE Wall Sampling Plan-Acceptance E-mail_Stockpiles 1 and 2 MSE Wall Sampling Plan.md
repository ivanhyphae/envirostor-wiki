## Decision or request
- **What is being approved:** DTSC (with Water Board concurrence) accepts the "MSE Wall Sampling Plan e-mail and figures received March 31, 2020" – referred to as the "Sampling Plan"
- **The specific document:** Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan, submitted via email from Geocon on March 31, 2020 (including appended plan sheets)
- **Conditions:** None stated – approval is unconditional with no comments
- **Effective date:** April 2, 2020

## Open items and next actions
- **Sampling execution:** Geocon to proceed with sampling of MSE wall footing excavations once BCS slope zone material is removed at Stockpile 1 southern slope zone
- **Analysis:** Soil samples from 10 locations at ~200-foot intervals, discrete one-foot intervals to ~4-5 feet depth, analyzed for barium and lead under 48-hour turnaround
- **Decision framework:** Results to be compared to stated screening levels (site-specific background, RDIP BCS removal threshold, construction worker ESL, residential ESL, commercial ESL for barium; site-specific background and DTSC residential for lead) to determine suitability for reuse as clean capping material, abutment/embankment fill, or BCS containment zone placement
- **Monitoring:** RDIP monitoring including air/dust perimeter sampling ongoing during BCS excavation (Geocon's Dave Watts onsite)
