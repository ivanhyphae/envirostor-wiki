## Document identity
- **Plan type**: Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP Variance)
- **Preparer / author organization**: Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Date**: April 9, 2020
- **Project number or task order**: Project No. S1908-01-01

## Objective and scope
- **Purpose of the work and what prompted it**: To address the identified sources of clean cover fill materials for properly capping consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) within Stockpiles 1 and 2 during the SR 132 West Project. The original RDIP (Section 4.7) required pre-approval of clean fill source areas by DTSC. The highway contractor initially identified a Carpenter Road borrow area, which was approved. An additional source was identified: the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2.
- **Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed**: Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (south of SR 99/Kansas Avenue interchange, Modesto, Stanislaus County, CA). Media: soil from MSE wall footing excavations (approximately 5 ft deep). The approved Carpenter Road borrow source is also referenced.
- **What approval or decision the plan supports or requests**: Request for DTSC approval to use the MSE wall footing excavation soil as clean cover fill material, deviating from the original RDIP’s provision that only the Carpenter Road source would be used.

## Proposed approach
- **Sampling design: locations/counts, target analytes, methods, depths**: For Stockpile 1, the MSE wall footing soil along the southern boundary was evaluated after BCS excavation. Sampling depths: 0–1 ft (not suitable due to elevated barium) and 1–5 ft (suitable). Target analytes: barium and lead. Analytical methods: not specified in detail, but results are compared to site-specific background concentrations. For Stockpile 2, the MSE wall footing excavation will be evaluated once the southern slope BCS material is excavated; soil samples will be analyzed for barium and lead and compared to site-specific background concentrations.
- **Decision criteria or action levels proposed**: The 1–5 ft interval of Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing soil exhibited barium concentrations from 42 to 140 mg/kg, with a calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) of 113.1 mg/kg. This is within the site-specific naturally occurring background barium concentration range of 17–120 mg/kg. Therefore, the material is proposed as suitable for clean cover fill. The same comparison to site-specific background will be applied to Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing soil.
- **Sequence, schedule, or phasing of the work**: For Stockpile 1, the evaluation was completed (Geocon April 6, 2020 Technical Memorandum). For Stockpile 2, the evaluation will occur after the southern slope BCS material is excavated. No specific schedule is provided.
- **For a variance: the requirement at issue and the deviation requested**: The RDIP (Section 4.7) specified that the source of clean imported fill would be “currently vacant and agricultural land within planned excavation areas along the SR-132 ROW between the Carpenter Road and Rosemore Avenue overcrossings.” The variance requests approval to also use soil from the MSE wall footing excavations along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2 as clean cover fill, based on barium concentrations falling within site-specific background levels.

## Deliverables and approvals sought
- **What the plan commits to produce**: The variance letter itself, with attachments: Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Data Summary, 95% UCL Statistical Output, and Site-Specific Background Summary. For Stockpile 2, evaluation results will be provided once available.
- **Which agency approval it is submitted for**: DTSC approval (submitted to Mr. Dean Wright, DTSC). Also cc’d to DTSC Mr. Perry Myers, Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board Mr. Steven Meeks, Caltrans Mr. Richard Stewart, and WSP Mr. Michael Scott.
