## Document Identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Quarterly Groundwater Monitoring Report (March 2012) documenting the fourth quarterly sampling event at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, required under DTSC-approved protocols.
- **Author/preparer:** Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Kevin J. Brown, PG, Senior Geologist; Rebecca L. Silva, REA, Project Manager; John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Principal).
- **Date/period covered:** Field activities March 12, 2012; report issued June 29, 2012. Covers the March 2012 quarterly monitoring event.

## Key Claims
- No dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for drinking water, with one exception: manganese in MW-4 (88 µg/L) exceeded the secondary MCL of 50 µg/L (taste and odor threshold). *(Geocon, Section: Conclusions and Recommendations)*
- Nitrate as nitrogen exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/L in three wells: MW-1 (12 mg/L), MW-5 (27 mg/L), and MW-6 (18 mg/L). The MW-1 exceedance may reflect natural background conditions. *(Geocon, Section: Analytical Results – General Minerals)*
- Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in wells MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, and MW-6. *(Geocon, Section: Conclusions and Recommendations)*
- No polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) were detected above practical quantitation limits in any sample. *(Geocon, Section: Analytical Results – PAHs)*
- Groundwater geochemistry indicates two distinct water types: calcium-dominant water upgradient and beneath Stockpiles 1 and 2, and sodium-dominant water at locations downgradient of the FMC facility (MW-3, MW-6, MW-8). *(Geocon, Section: General Minerals/Stiff Diagram)*
- Mercury results are considered estimated values due to detection in the equipment blank at a similar concentration (0.31 µg/L). *(Geocon, Section: Field and Laboratory QA/QC)*
- Molybdenum results for MW-6 are considered estimated values due to detection in the associated method blank. *(Geocon, Section: Field and Laboratory QA/QC)*

## Regulatory Decisions
- No formal regulatory decisions or actions documented in this report. The monitoring was conducted in accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) as established in the *Final Work Plan, Groundwater Assessment* (Shaw, January 2006).

## Analytical Results
**Groundwater – Dissolved Metals (March 12, 2012):**
- **Barium:** Range 210–750 µg/L across all 8 wells. No exceedances of the primary MCL (1000 µg/L).
- **Vanadium:** Range 6.5–8.5 µg/L. No exceedances of applicable thresholds.
- **Strontium:** Range 520–930 µg/L. No exceedances of applicable thresholds.
- **Chromium:** Detected in 7 of 8 wells (MW-7 not detected). Range 1.1–2.2 µg/L. Primary MCL = 50 µg/L.
- **Mercury:** Detected in 7 of 8 wells (MW-3 not detected). Range 0.21–0.33 µg/L. Primary MCL = 2 µg/L. Results qualified as estimated due to equipment blank contamination.
- **Nickel:** Detected only in MW-4 (3.5 µg/L). Primary MCL = 100 µg/L.
- **Manganese:** Detected only in MW-4 (88 µg/L). Exceeded secondary MCL of 50 µg/L (taste and odor).
- **Molybdenum:** Detected only in MW-6 (3.8 µg/L). Result qualified as estimated due to method blank contamination.
- Antimony, arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, copper, lead, selenium, silver, thallium, zinc: Not detected above PQLs in any well.

**Groundwater – General Minerals (March 12, 2012):**
- **Nitrate as N:** Range 2.9 mg/L (MW-3) to 27 mg/L (MW-5). Exceedances of primary MCL (10 mg/L) in MW-1 (12 mg/L), MW-5 (27 mg/L), and MW-6 (18 mg/L).
- **TDS:** Range 340–840 mg/L. Exceedances of secondary MCL (500 mg/L) in MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, and MW-6.
- **Sulfate:** Range 2.4–11 mg/L. No exceedances.
- **Chloride:** Range 3.7–9.9 mg/L. No exceedances.
- **Sulfide:** Not detected (ND) in all wells.
- **Alkalinity (as CaCO3):** Range 120–360 mg/L.

**Groundwater – PAHs:** Not detected above PQLs in any well.

**Quality Control:**
- Equipment blank contained copper (1.9 µg/L), sodium (95 µg/L), and mercury (0.31 µg/L). Sodium and mercury results qualified as estimated.
- Method blank (March 14, 2012) contained copper, molybdenum, and zinc. MW-6 molybdenum result qualified as estimated.
- Matrix spike/matrix spike duplicate (MS/MSD) recoveries outside limits for nickel, barium, chromium, molybdenum, vanadium, zinc, and strontium; no impact on reported data as samples were not analyzed on that date.

## Remedial Actions
- No remedial actions documented in this report. The event was a monitoring-only activity.

## Key Terms and Conditions
- Quarterly groundwater monitoring required in accordance with DTSC-approved protocols (*Final Work Plan, Groundwater Assessment*, Shaw, January 2006).
- Monitoring network: 8 wells (MW-1 through MW-8).
- Analytical suite: Title 22 dissolved metals (EPA 6020/7470), strontium, calcium, magnesium, potassium, sodium (EPA 6020), chloride, nitrate, sulfate (EPA 300.0), sulfide (SM 4500), TDS (SM 2540C), alkalinity (SM 2320B), and PAHs (EPA 8270-SIM).
- Split samples collected by CVRWQCB and analyzed by Excelchem Environmental Laboratories.
- Electronic data submitted to GeoTracker database on May 11, 2012.
- Purged groundwater stored in a 55-gallon drum near MW-8 pending analytical results; subsequently placed on the ground surface.
- MW-5 went dry during purging; required recovery time before sampling.
