## Document identity
- **Form**: Email thread (with forwarded messages and approval letter embedded)
- **Sender / author organization**: Dean Wright, P.G., Project Manager, Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)
- **Recipient(s) / addressee**: Richard C. Stewart, Caltrans (DOT); cc: Kyle Cockerham, Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB)
- **Date**: April 22, 2021 (final approval email); thread spans April 7–22, 2021

## Thread / participants
Listed in chronological order, most recent last:

1. **April 7, 2021** – Richard Stewart (Caltrans) to Rick Deml (WSP) and others, forwarding an earlier DTSC e-mail approval of the *Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan* (received April 5, 2021). Stewart notes DTSC and the Water Board approved the Plan with no comments.
2. **April 12, 2021** – John Juhrend (Geocon) to Richard Stewart and Rick Deml (cc: Caltrans, WSP, City of Modesto), asking Stewart to forward the *Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum* (Memo) to DTSC for approval.
3. **April 20, 2021** – Richard Stewart (Caltrans) to Dean Wright (DTSC), cc: Kyle Cockerham (RWQCB), forwarding the Memo and requesting review.
4. **April 22, 2021** – Dean Wright (DTSC) to Richard Stewart, cc: Kyle Cockerham, stating that DTSC and the Water Board have reviewed the Memo, agree the composite soil sample results are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and meet the **Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria**, and that this e-mail serves as DTSC’s acceptance of the Memo.

## Decision or request
- **What is being approved/accepted**: DTSC’s acceptance of the *Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum* (Memo) dated April 12, 2021.
- **Document acted on**: Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum, dated April 12, 2021, prepared by Geocon (submitted by John Juhrend).
- **Substance of the decision**: DTSC and the Central Valley RWQCB agree that the composite soil sample results from the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill area are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and meet the **Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria**. No conditions, revisions, or contingencies are attached; the acceptance is unconditional.
- **Effective date**: April 22, 2021 (date of the e-mail).

## Open items and next actions
- No further actions, required revisions, resubmittals, or pending approvals are stated in this correspondence. The Memo has been accepted as submitted.
