## Document identity
- **Form:** Approval letter
- **Sender / author organization:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), Site Mitigation and Restoration Program – Dean Wright, PG, Project Manager
- **Recipient(s) / addressee:** Mr. John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Date:** March 4, 2022

## Decision or request
- **What is being approved:** The *Technical Memorandum for Stockpile 2 Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan* (Tech Memo), dated February 28, 2022.
- **What the Tech Memo covers:** Sampling and management of BCS spoils generated from installation of the Weather Station and Overhead Sign #1 on Stockpile 2 at the State Route 132 West project (Modesto, Stanislaus County).
- **DTSC’s concurrence:**
  - Concurs with the stockpiling and sampling procedures described in the Tech Memo.
  - If sampling results for barium and lead are **below** site-specific background concentrations, the material may be reused on-site at locations mutually agreed upon by DTSC and Caltrans.
  - If results are **above** background concentrations, the material must be profiled for disposal at the Forward Landfill in Manteca (or another acceptable landfill facility).
- **Effective date:** The letter is effective upon issuance (March 4, 2022); no separate deadline stated.

## Open items and next actions
- No explicit follow‑up deliverables or resubmittals are required by the letter.
- The approval sets the stage for Geocon/Caltrans to execute the workplan: perform sampling per the Tech Memo, evaluate results against site‑specific background, and proceed with either on‑site reuse (with joint DTSC/Caltrans agreement on locations) or off‑site disposal.
