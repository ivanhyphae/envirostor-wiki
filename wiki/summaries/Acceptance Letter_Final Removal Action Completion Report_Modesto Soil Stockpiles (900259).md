## Document identity
- **Form:** Acceptance letter
- **Sender / author organization:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) – signed by Steven Becker, P.G., Chief, Santa Susana Field Laboratory, Site Mitigation and Restoration Program
- **Recipient(s) / addressee:** Mr. John E. Juhrend, P.E., CEG, Project Manager, Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Date:** January 18, 2023

## Decision or request
- DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), **accepts as final** the *Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project* (RACR), dated December 1, 2022, submitted by Geocon on behalf of Caltrans. The letter states DTSC has no comments on the RACR.
- The RACR describes consolidation and capping of barium-containing soils (BCS) completed to date under Interim Phase 1.
- Acceptance confirms that the soil removal, consolidation, and capping efforts were completed per the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP), Remedial Design and Implementation Plan (RDIP), and two variance requests (April 17, 2020 and September 21, 2020). The RACR also included As-Built Plans, cross-section figures, final survey data, and the final Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan.
- **Condition:** DTSC will prepare a draft O&M Agreement and submit it for Caltrans review within 30 days of this letter. A Land Use Covenant (LUC) for the remediated area will also be completed and sent for review, signature, and recording with the Stanislaus County Assessor’s office.
- **Effective date / conclusion:** This acceptance and the tasks above mark the conclusion of Interim Phase 1. Final certification of the RAP for the project will not occur until after the Ultimate Phase 2 State Route 132 build-out is completed (anticipated in 8–10 years).

## Open items and next actions
- **DTSC:** Prepare draft O&M Agreement and submit to Caltrans for review within 30 days of this letter.
- **DTSC:** Complete the Land Use Covenant (LUC) and send for review, signature, and recording.
- **Ultimate Phase 2 (future):** Requires a separate agreement between DTSC and Caltrans before commencing; will include a supplemental RDIP for DTSC review/approval, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement if necessary.
