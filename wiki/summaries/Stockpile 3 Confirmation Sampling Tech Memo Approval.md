## Decision or request
- **What is being approved/accepted**: DTSC accepts the *Stockpile 3 BCS Removal Verification Testing Technical Memorandum* dated March 13, 2020.
- **Document acted on**: Technical Memorandum (Memo) – “Stockpile 3 BCS Removal Verification Testing” (March 13, 2020), prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. and submitted by Caltrans.
- **Substance**: The Memo describes removal of barium-containing soil (BCS) from Stockpile 3 and confirmation sampling. DTSC agrees that:
  - BCS removal is complete.
  - Remaining soil meets RDIP verification thresholds.
  - No further RDIP monitoring requirements for this stockpile are necessary.
  - The area is suitable for clean embankment fill.
- **Separate request (not yet decided)**: Caltrans requests DTSC approval to place a minimum 12‑inch clean soil cover and/or a minimum 4‑inch thick layer of aggregate base (AB) on the BCS consolidation zones (including temporary slopes) of Stockpiles 1 and 2. That request is not addressed in this DTSC reply.
- **Effective date**: March 18, 2020 (date of acceptance email).

## Open items and next actions
- **Pending approval**: DTSC has not yet responded to the request to begin clean cover placement on Stockpiles 1 and 2 consolidation zones. That action remains open.
- **Request for document**: Richard Stewart (Caltrans) asked for a copy of DTSC’s approval of the Stockpile 2 BCS Removal (referred to in the original Geocon email). No response to that request is included in this thread.
- **No further actions on Stockpile 3**: With DTSC acceptance, no additional monitoring or remediation is required for that area.
