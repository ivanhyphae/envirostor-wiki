## Decision or request
- **What is being approved:** Final approval of the *Remedial Design Implementation Plan – Interim Phase 1* (RDIP-Interim Phase 1), dated January 30, 2019, submitted by Geocon on behalf of Caltrans.
- **Additional document acted upon:** *City of Modesto Book 2 of 3 100% Special Provisions for The State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Phase 1 Project*. DTSC provided minor comments on this document on March 26, 2019; a revised version was received on March 28, 2019, and DTSC confirmed those comments were addressed.
- **Conditions or contingencies:** None stated. DTSC and the Central Valley RWQCB have “no additional comments on either document” and the RDIP-Interim Phase 1 is approved.
- **Effective date:** April 3, 2019 (date of letter).

- **Required revisions or resubmittals:** None specified; the approval is final and no further actions are requested by DTSC or RWQCB.
- **Pending approvals:** None identified.
- **Who owes what next:** The letter does not impose any follow-up obligations; the approval authorizes the Interim Phase 1 construction activities described in the RDIP-Interim Phase 1.
