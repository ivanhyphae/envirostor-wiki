## Document identity
- **Deliverable type:** Errata (update to Implementation Schedule of Draft Final Remedial Action Plan)
- **Version / draft / final status:** Letter update (errata) to the Draft Final RAP (October 2014)
- **Preparer / author organization:** California Department of Transportation (Caltrans), District 6, Central Region Hazardous Waste Branch
- **Date:** January 10, 2017
- **Project number or task order:** Not stated

## Purpose and regulatory context
- **What milestone or requirement the document fulfills:** Updates the Implementation Schedule (Section 8, page 47) of the October 2014 Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project. The update revises key public comment and approval deadlines originally set in that RAP schedule.
- **Oversight framework and the agency it is submitted to:** Submitted to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) under the Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program. The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) is also a co-regulator, though not explicitly mentioned in this letter.
- **For an errata:** Parent document is the *Draft Final Remedial Action Plan, Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County, California, October 2014*. Only the Implementation Schedule is being corrected/updated; all other dates and sections remain unchanged.

*No updating content in this letter. The underlying Draft Final RAP (October 2014) addresses barium-contaminated fill soils at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles; this errata does not modify those site descriptions.*

*Not addressed in this letter. No changes to human health or ecological risk findings from the parent Draft Final RAP.*

## Selected or recommended remedy
*Not modified by this errata. The parent Draft Final RAP (October 2014) documents the selected remedial approach; this letter does not alter the remedy design or cleanup standards.*

## Conclusions and path forward
The letter revises the near-term schedule for the public review and final approval stages of the Draft Final RAP:

- **January 18, 2017** – Public notice of availability of the Draft Final RAP and the SR-132 Project EIR/EA for 45-day public review.
- **February 22, 2017** – Public meeting.
- **Spring 2017** – DTSC prepares a Responsiveness Summary (response to public comments).
- **Spring 2017** – Revise Draft Final RAP as needed; DTSC approves Final RAP.

All other previously published schedule dates remain unchanged. The letter does not modify closure conditions, monitoring obligations, or land use restrictions specified in the parent RAP.
