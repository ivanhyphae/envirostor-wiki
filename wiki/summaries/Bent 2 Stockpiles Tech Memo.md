### Document Identity
- **Memo Subject:** Bent 2 Excavation Soil Stockpiles Characterization Testing
- **Preparer:** John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Recipient:** Scott Frenette, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto
- **Date:** October 30, 2020
- **Area/Feature:** Bent 2 excavation soil stockpiles (end dumps), placed on the southern portion of Stockpile 2, State Route 132, Modesto, CA.

### Purpose
To determine whether the soil stockpiles generated from the Bent 2 excavation meet the clean cap criteria as established in the DTSC-approved *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan* (April 9, 2020). The material was excavated from the Bent 2 overcrossing structure west of State Route 99.

### Data and Findings
- **Sampling:** Ten discrete soil samples (STK1 through STK10) were collected from the Bent 2 end dump piles at ~80-foot intervals, alternating between two rows of end dumps.
- **Analytes and Methods:** Samples were analyzed for barium and lead using EPA Method 6010B (24-hour turnaround) by California Laboratory Services (CLS).
- **Results:**
    - **Barium:** Detected in all 10 samples at concentrations ranging from 31 to 230 mg/kg. Four of the ten samples had barium concentrations equal to or greater than the site-specific maximum background value of 120 mg/kg.
    - **Lead:** Detected in 6 of 10 samples at concentrations ranging from 2.6 to 13 mg/kg, within the range of the site-specific maximum background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg.
- **Comparison to Criteria:** The data was compared to the clean cap criteria (upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg) from the referenced variance. Four samples exceeded this barium threshold.

### Conclusion and Recommendation
The memo concludes that the Bent 2 soil stockpiles **do not meet the clean cap criteria** for barium. However, the memo recommends that, pending DTSC review and approval, the material may be suitable for use as a **partial clean cap (6 inches or less)** on the upper surface of Stockpile 2, because the planned structural pavement section (base material and asphalt/concrete) will be placed over the entire upper surface. The memo further recommends that fill material from previously approved clean fill sources will be required for the northerly slope of Stockpile 2.

### Follow-On and Approval Status
- The information from this memo will be included in the **Removal Action Completion Report** to be submitted following the completion of Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities.
- The recommendation is explicitly **pending DTSC review and approval consideration**. No agency approval is stated in the document.
