Geocon Consultants, Inc. conducted a groundwater monitoring round at the Modesto Stockpiles in June 2014 (Project S9800-01-17, Task Order No. 17, EA 10-0X2700), submitting results to Rebecca Silva. Groundwater samples were collected from ten monitoring wells (MW-1 through MW-10) between June 4-5, 2014, with additional sampling from MW-3 and MW-9 between June 5-13, 2014. Depth-to-water measurements ranged from 35.09 to 42.86 feet below casing, and groundwater flowed south-southeast with an average gradient of 0.0008. Samples were consistently filtered for dissolved metals.

While most dissolved metals and general minerals were below water quality thresholds, several exceedances were noted. Nitrates (as nitrogen) exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/L in MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, and MW-10 (concentrations 2.6-29 mg/L), with MW-9 specifically reporting 10 mg/L. Total dissolved solids (TDS) also consistently exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 (concentrations 320-680 mg/L), with MW-9 showing 480 mg/L. Manganese sporadically exceeded its secondary MCL. Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) analysis was discontinued due to consistently low concentrations since November 2012. Barium and strontium levels were consistent with historical data and well below thresholds. Stiff diagrams indicated predominantly calcium-dominant water in MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-7, MW-9, and MW-10, and sodium-dominant water in MW-3, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-8, with MW-5 shifting from calcium to sodium dominance in 2013, suggesting potential mixing. Notably, dissolved mercury was consistently undetectable across all analyzed wells when tested by EPA 7470A.

Extensive quality control (QC) data was reported, with several deviations and qualifiers identified, particularly for Sulfide, Total (SM 4500-S=D) where Matrix Spike and Matrix Spike Duplicate recoveries and RPD were outside limits, and for dissolved metals by ICP-MS EPA 6020. A blank for dissolved metals showed elevated Calcium (66.3165 ug/L), and an LCS for Calcium showed 123% recovery. Numerous matrix spike/duplicate analyses for dissolved metals, especially Calcium and Magnesium, exhibited recoveries significantly outside limits and high RPDs, leading to various 'B', 'E', and 'D' qualifiers. Despite detailed monitoring and QC, the report did not provide specific conclusions or recommendations based on these results. All electronic data was uploaded to the GeoTracker database on June 17, 2014.
