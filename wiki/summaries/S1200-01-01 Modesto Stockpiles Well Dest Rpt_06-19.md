### Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Groundwater Monitoring Well Destruction Report (letter report) documenting the abandonment of six monitoring wells at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, prepared for Caltrans District 6 Hazardous Waste Branch.
- **Author or preparer:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.; report prepared by Rebecca L. Silva, Project Manager.
- **Date or period covered:** Report dated June 19, 2019; field activities conducted April 29–30, 2019.

### Key claims
*No claims regarding site conditions, compliance, or remedial adequacy are made in this document.*

### Regulatory decisions
- **City of Modesto Encroachment Permit:** Permit No. ENC2019-50174 issued on April 26, 2019, for well destruction activities. Fees paid: $189.00. Permit conditions include 48‑hour inspection notification, utility mark‑out requirements (USA, MID, PG&E, etc.), traffic control measures, and erosion/sediment control compliance.
- **Notification to City of Modesto:** At least 48 hours prior to field work (satisfied by permit conditions).
- **Underground Service Alert:** 72‑hour notifications provided for six well locations (Ticket Nos. W910200748, W910200753, W910200756, W910200763, W910200770, W910200773).
- **Post‑destruction reporting:** Well Completion Reports (Form DWR 188) submitted to California Department of Water Resources (DWR) and Stanislaus County Department of Environmental Health. Copies also sent to City of Modesto Water Division.

### Analytical results
*No analytical results are presented in this document.*

### Remedial actions
- **Activity:** Destruction of six groundwater monitoring wells (MW1, MW2, MW3, MW5, MW7, MW8) by pressure grouting.
- **Method:** Each well casing was filled with neat cement grout (3 gallons water per 47‑lb sack) via tremie pipe, then pressurized to 20–25 psi for ~5 minutes. Minimum calculated grout volumes: 6.4–7.4 gallons; actual volumes taken: 13–20 gallons per well. After grout stabilization, well boxes were removed and top 5 feet of each casing was drilled out with an 8‑inch‑diameter hollow‑stem auger. The top 5 feet were backfilled with cuttings, native material, and bagged topsoil.
- **Quantity:** Six wells destroyed; four wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10) left in place.
- **Phase status:** Completed.
- **Verification outcomes:** Well Completion Reports filed with DWR and county; City of Modesto permit conditions satisfied.
- **Containment and disposal:** Well box materials and concrete debris deposited in Caltrans’ onsite construction debris pile.
