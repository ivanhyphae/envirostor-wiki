# Document Identity
- **Plan type:** Sampling plan (clean fill source characterization workplan)
- **Preparer/author:** Geocon Consultants, Inc. (John Juhrend, PE, CEG, CEM)
- **Date:** February 4, 2020 (email date)
- **Project number/task order:** Not stated; context: Caltrans District 10 SR 132 Modesto

# Objective and Scope
- **Purpose:** Characterize soil proposed for use as clean fill cover for the BCS (Borrow Source) stockpile on the SR 132 project.
- **Area/media:** The proposed clean fill source area is the upper 5 feet of soil along the Highway 132 alignment from Carpenter Road extending east. This area has been state right-of-way since the 1960s; prior use was agricultural. Media: surface soil.
- **Approval sought:** DTSC review and acceptance of the sampling plan and subsequent analytical results to allow use of the soil as clean fill.

# Proposed Approach
- **Sampling design:**
  - 12 borings, each 5 feet deep, in the clean fill source area.
  - 6 depth-discrete 4-point composite samples:
    - 0–2 ft: organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) and heavy metals (3 composites)
    - 3–5 ft: heavy metals only (3 composites)
- **Target analytes:** OCPs (0–2 ft only) and heavy metals (all depths)
- **Depth intervals:** 0–2 ft and 3–5 ft
- **Decision criteria / action levels:** None explicitly stated in this document; the plan relies on comparison to applicable regulatory screening levels (referenced in earlier investigations: Blackburn 2014 ISA, Rincon 2018 Phase II ESA).
- **Sequence / schedule:** Fieldwork tentatively scheduled for February 11, 2020, pending contract authorization, USA notice, and DTSC sampling plan approval. A 48-hour analytical turnaround is planned.
- **Basis for clean fill classification:** The plan documents that the material is native soil (based on 2017 test boring logs at Carpenter Road) and that no high-risk documented contamination releases are known in the immediate vicinity.

# Deliverables and Approvals Sought
- **Deliverables:** Analytical results to be submitted to DTSC following completion.
- **Approvals sought:** DTSC approval of the sampling plan and subsequent acceptance of the results for use of the soil as clean fill.
