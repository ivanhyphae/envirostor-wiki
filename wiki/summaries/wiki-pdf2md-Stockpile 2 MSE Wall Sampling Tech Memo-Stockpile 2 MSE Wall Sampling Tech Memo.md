- Area, stockpile, or feature involved: Stockpile 2 southerly slope MSE wall footing excavation

## Purpose
This technical memorandum summarizes soil characterization testing conducted following barium containing soil (BCS) removal from the Stockpile 2 southerly slope. The purpose of the testing was to characterize MSE wall footing excavation soil to determine appropriate placement for the excavated material. The underlying prompt was the completed removal of BCS to facilitate the construction of an MSE wall.

## Data and findings
- Sampling or testing performed (locations, analytes, methods): Soil samples were collected from the mid-point of the planned wall footing excavation, from trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7, spaced at approximately 200-foot intervals. Discrete soil samples were collected from one-foot intervals to maximum planned excavation depths of 3 and 5 feet. Samples were analyzed for barium and lead using EPA Test Method 6010B.
- Results with concentrations and units:
    - Barium concentrations ranged from 53 to 930 mg/kg in the 29 soil samples.
    - Lead concentrations ranged from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg in all soil samples.
- Comparison to applicable criteria or thresholds, and any exceedances:
    - Fourteen of the 29 soil samples contained barium concentrations greater than the maximum site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg.
    - Thirteen of these 14 elevated barium samples were from depths between the surface and 3 feet.
    - Sample STK2-7-4, collected deeper than the planned 3-foot footing excavation, had a barium concentration of 140 mg/kg, exceeding background.
    - Most lead concentrations were within the same order of magnitude as the maximum site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg. No lead concentrations exceeded the BCS Removal Verification Threshold of 80 mg/kg. No barium concentrations exceeded the BCS Removal Verification Threshold of 1,000 mg/kg.

## Conclusion and recommendation
Based on the data, Geocon concluded that the upper three feet of soil excavated from the Stockpile 2 southerly slope MSE wall footing will require placement within the designated Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones. Stockpile 2 southerly slope MSE wall footing material excavated from depths greater than 3 feet is deemed suitable for placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general embankment fill, or as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material, pending regulatory agency approval.

## Follow-on and approval status
This information will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report following completion of the Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities. Regulatory agency approval is required for placement of material excavated from depths deeper than 3 feet as general embankment fill or as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material.
