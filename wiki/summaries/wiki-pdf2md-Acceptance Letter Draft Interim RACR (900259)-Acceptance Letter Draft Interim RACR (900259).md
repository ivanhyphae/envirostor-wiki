## Decision or request
- **What is being accepted**: The *Draft Interim Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Phase 1 Interim State Route 132 West Project* (Draft Phase 1 RACR), submitted by Geocon on behalf of Caltrans.
- **Substance of acceptance**: DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), accepts the Draft Phase 1 RACR as a technical status report **with no comments**. The report describes consolidation and capping of barium-containing soils (BCS) completed to date per the approved Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) and two variance requests (April 17, 2020; September 21, 2020).
- **Conditions / contingencies**: The following deliverables remain pending and must be included in the *Final Phase 1 RACR*:
  - As-Built Plans and cross section figures
  - Final survey data
  - Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan (requires soil management plan, annual inspection of pavement/containment features, groundwater monitoring)
  - The O&M Plan must be submitted for review so an approved version can be incorporated.
  - Following Final Phase 1 RACR approval, DTSC will enter into an O&M Agreement with Caltrans and complete a land use covenant for containment areas.
  - Certification of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) will not occur until after the *Ultimate Phase 2* build-out (expected in 8–10 years), which will require a separate agreement, supplemental RDIP, and Ultimate Phase 2 RACR.

## Open items and next actions
- **Pending deliverables for Final Phase 1 RACR**: As-Built Plans, cross sections, final survey data, and an O&M Plan. The Final Phase 1 RACR is anticipated for submittal in **July or August 2022**.
- **O&M Plan**: Must be submitted to DTSC for review; once approved, it will be incorporated into the Final Phase 1 RACR.
- **Ultimate Phase 2**: Expected to begin in 8–10 years; will require a separate DTSC–Caltrans agreement, a supplemental RDIP (for DTSC review/approval, with field oversight), an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and possible revisions to the O&M Plan/Agreement.
- **Who owes what**: Geocon/Caltrans to submit the Final Phase 1 RACR and O&M Plan; DTSC to review and approve O&M Plan, then execute O&M Agreement and land use covenant.
