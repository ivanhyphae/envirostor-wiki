# Summary: Surface Water Report – March 10, 2023

## Document Identity
- **Report type**: Surface water/stormwater sampling event and site inspection
- **Preparer**: Stantec Consulting Services Inc.
- **Sampling date**: March 10, 2023
- **Project number**: Contract No. 06A2542, Task Order No. 97

## Monitoring Scope
- **Media sampled**: Surface water (stormwater runoff)
- **Locations sampled**:
  - Background samples: BG1, BG3
  - Surface runoff samples: PL6, PL7
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled**: None reported; however, sample collection methods were modified at BG3 and PL7 due to field conditions (see below). Previous locations PL1–PL5 and BG2 were terminated due to road construction.

## Results

### Dissolved Metals (Table 2)
- **Analytes detected**: arsenic, barium, chromium, copper, lead, molybdenum, selenium, vanadium, zinc, strontium, mercury
- **Not detected above MDLs**: antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, nickel, silver, thallium
- **Concentration ranges**: Not fully provided in narrative; refer to Table 2. All reported metal detections were below Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs).
- **Exceedances**: None reported. Note: antimony MDL (8.8 µg/L) exceeded the Primary MCL (6.0 µg/L), making compliance assessment inconclusive for that analyte.
- **Trends**: BG1 results consistent with historical data. BG3, PL6, and PL7 have no prior data for comparison.

### General Minerals (Table 3)
- **Analytes**: Nitrate as nitrogen, sulfide, total suspended solids (TSS)
- **Results**: All below Primary and Secondary MCLs. BG1 turbidity higher than previously reported.
- **Data qualifier**: Nitrate-N results qualified as estimated with potential low bias (J-) due to holding time exceedance.

### Thresholds Applied
- Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) as established by California EPA State Water Resources Control Board (dated September 23, 2015 and January 3, 2023)

## Field Conditions and QA/QC

### Field Conditions
- **Weather**: 0.29 inches precipitation on March 10, 2023; rain occurred primarily overnight with minimal rain during sampling
- **BG1**: Collected from minimally flowing water/ponding area east of catch basin inlet on north side of Loletta Avenue
- **BG3**: No visible water flowing into catch basin; ponding water present within catch basin. Per Caltrans direction, Stantec removed grate and collected water from within catch basin
- **PL6**: Surface water actively flowing from stormwater outfall pipe at northeast corner of Stockpile 2
- **PL7**: Discharge pipe blocked by significant trash/debris accumulation. Stantec cleared debris before collecting sample after flow was re-established
- **Site inspection observations**: Soil erosion observed on east side of stormwater basin 5; areas of limited vegetation on northern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 where hydroseeding was previously applied

### Sampling and Analytical Procedures
- Field parameters measured: temperature, pH, electrical conductivity, turbidity, oxidation-reduction potential (ORP), dissolved oxygen (DO) — using Horiba U-50 meter
- Dissolved metal samples field-filtered through 0.45-micron filters
- Samples analyzed by Advanced Technology Laboratories, Inc. (ATL)
- Analyses performed:
  - Dissolved metals (including strontium) by EPA Method 6020/7470A
  - Nitrate as nitrogen by EPA Method 300.0
  - Sulfide by SM 4500-S-D
  - Total suspended solids by SM 2540D

### QA/QC Issues
- **Holding time exceedance**: Nitrate-N analyzed outside 48-hour recommended holding time for all four samples; results qualified as estimated with potential low bias (J-)
- **Method blank contamination**: Nine metals detected in method blank for batch B3C0693. Associated sample results between MDL and PQL qualified as non-detect (U) with non-detect value raised to PQL. Results detected above PQL but below ten times blank value qualified as estimated with potential positive bias (J+)
- All samples analyzed within recommended holding times except nitrate-N
- No field duplicate samples collected
- No data qualified as "rejected"; data considered valid for intended use

## Conclusions and Recommendations

### Conclusions (Stantec)
- No discharge/runoff of surface water beyond BMPs or site boundaries observed during inspection
- All detected metals below MCLs
- BG1 results consistent with historical data
- Data considered "useable" as qualified

### Recommendations
- None explicitly stated; continued monitoring implied under Task Order scope
