## Objective and scope
- **Purpose**: To sample a proposed clean fill source from Basin 5 along SR 132 in Modesto, California, for potential reuse as clean fill. The work supports the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project (SR 132, Stanislaus County).
- **Area, media, and stockpiles/features addressed**: Basin 5 – a proposed cut area approximately 10 feet deep with an estimated volume of 20,000 cubic yards. The media is soil from three depth intervals within the basin. The plan covers six proposed test pit locations.
- **What approval or decision the plan supports**: The sampling results will be used to determine whether the excavated material from Basin 5 meets clean fill criteria and can be used as fill material under DTSC and Central Valley RWQCB oversight.

## Proposed approach
- **Sampling design**:
  - **Locations/counts**: Six test pit locations within Basin 5.
  - **Sampling scheme**: At each test pit, two depth-discrete 3-part composite samples are proposed, yielding a total of six composite soil samples for the entire basin.
  - **Target analytes and methods** – specified per depth interval:
    - 0–2 feet: pesticides (EPA Method 8081A) and metals (EPA Methods 6010B / 7471A)
    - 2–5 feet: metals only (EPA Methods 6010B / 7471A)
    - 5–10 feet: metals only (EPA Methods 6010B / 7471A)
  - **Depths**: Three depth intervals covered: 0–2 ft, 2–5 ft, and 5–10 ft (total 10 ft cut depth).
- **Decision criteria or action levels**: Not explicitly stated in the excerpt; the plan treats the material as “proposed clean fill source,” implying comparison to applicable residential or commercial clean fill standards (to be determined per regulatory oversight).
- **Sequence, schedule, or phasing**:
  - A 48-hour laboratory turnaround time is specified for sample analysis.
  - No further schedule details are given in the source.

## Deliverables and approvals sought
- **Deliverables**: The plan itself is a workplan / sampling plan; the immediate deliverable would be the analytical results from the six composite samples, presumably compiled into a sampling report supporting a clean fill determination.
- **Approvals sought**: The plan was prepared for submission to the regulatory agencies (DTSC and Central Valley RWQCB) for approval of the sampling approach. It is not stated that approval has been obtained.
