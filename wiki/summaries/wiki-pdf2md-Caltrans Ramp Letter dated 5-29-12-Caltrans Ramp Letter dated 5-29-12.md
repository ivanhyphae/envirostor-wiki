## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: Letter requesting review and concurrence on the disposition of excavated material from a ramp safety improvement project.
- Author or preparer: Ms. Sam Haack, PE, Project Manager, California Department of Transportation (Caltrans)
- Date or period covered: May 29, 2012

## Key claims
- The upgrade to the off-ramp at SR 99 and Kansas Avenue is required to improve traffic safety (Caltrans).
- Approximately 6000 cubic yards (cy³) of material will be excavated for the widened roadbed, drainage, and retaining wall (Caltrans).
- A portion of this excavation will intersect Stockpile #3 of soil stockpiles placed for the future State Route 132 Expressway project (Caltrans).
- Final analysis indicates "elevated metal concentrations above site-specific background levels, but below residential and commercial/industrial California Human Health Screening Levels" (Caltrans, based on Geocon Consultants, Inc. analysis).
- Based on current site investigation data and the 2009 Preliminary E A, the soil material proposed for excavation is suitable for reuse as structural backfill or for offsite reuse/disposal as non-hazardous soil to an accepting facility (Caltrans).
- The contract for the ramp project is on hold awaiting DTSC concurrence that the material excavated from Stockpile #3 is considered non-hazardous (Caltrans).

## Regulatory decisions
- Decision type: Request for concurrence on material disposition.
- Agency: Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)
- Conditions imposed: Concurrence from DTSC on the non-hazardous nature of the material from Stockpile #3 is required before proceeding with the contract.

## Remedial actions
- Activity: Excavation of approximately 6000 cy³ of material for ramp safety improvement, including widening the roadbed, drainage, and retaining wall.
- Phase status: Proposed; the contract is on hold pending DTSC concurrence.
- Verification outcomes: Soil analysis by Geocon Consultants, Inc. indicates elevated metal concentrations but below California Human Health Screening Levels.
- Proposed disposition: Hauling material to Stanislaus County Fink Road Landfill; reuse as structural backfill or offsite reuse/disposal as non-hazardous soil.
- Ancillary actions: Implementation of contract specifications for dust control, stormwater plans, and erosion control, as well as additional measures to limit disturbance to the area.
