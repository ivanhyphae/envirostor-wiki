## Decision or request
- **What is being approved:** DTSC and the Water Board (Central Valley RWQCB) approve the *Second Variance to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan* (the “Request”), dated September 2, 2020, for use of Class 3 aggregate subbase material as a clean cap over barium‑contaminated soil / aerially deposited lead (BCS/ADL) stockpiles on the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles project.
- **Conditions attached:**
    1. **Minimum cap thickness:** 6 inches instead of the requested 4 inches, because a visual inspection (September 15, 2020) showed the stockpiled material is primarily fine sand (likely passing a No. 4 sieve). No pavement or hardscape will be in place before the upcoming rainy season, so extra thickness is warranted.
    2. **Increased monitoring:** DTSC and the Water Board agree that additional monitoring may be necessary during the rainy season.
- **Additional request (condition of approval):** One composite sample must be collected from the Class 3 material stockpile adjacent to the Rosemore Overcrossing and analyzed for barium and lead using EPA Method 6010B. This is to verify that the material meets the standards set in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP).

## Open items and next actions

- **Monitoring plan:** DTSC and the Water Board indicate that increased monitoring may be needed; no specific monitoring schedule or resubmittal is required in this email.
- **Effectiveness:** The approval is effective upon receipt of this email.
