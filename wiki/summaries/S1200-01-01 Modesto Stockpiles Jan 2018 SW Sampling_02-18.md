## Document identity
- **Report type:** Surface water sampling event
- **Preparer / author organization:** GEOCON CONSULTANTS, INC.
- **Sampling date(s):** January 8, 2018
- **Project number / task order:** Contract No. 06A2184, Task Order No. 1, EA No. 10-0X2700; Project No. S1200-01-01

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Surface water (stormwater runoff and pooled water)
- **Locations sampled:** PL1, PL2, PL3, PL4, BG1, BG2
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled:**
  - PL5 – insufficient water to sample (gate valve remained closed; basin not near capacity)

## Results
- **Analytes measured:** Dissolved metals (antimony, arsenic, barium, beryllium, cadmium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, selenium, silver, sodium, strontium, thallium, vanadium, zinc; mercury by 7470A); general minerals (chloride, nitrate as N, sulfate, sulfide, alkalinity species, total dissolved solids, total suspended solids)
- **Concentration ranges:**
  - Dissolved metals: barium 6.0–115 µg/L; arsenic <2.0–1.4 µg/L; chromium <1.0–4.3 µg/L; manganese 3.1–710 µg/L; zinc <5.0–260 µg/L (selected analytes; full ranges in laboratory report)
  - General minerals: chloride 2.1–15 mg/L; sulfate <1.0–12 mg/L; total dissolved solids 28–92 mg/L
- **Applicable thresholds:** California primary and secondary maximum contaminant levels (MCLs) for drinking water (SWRCB, September 23, 2015)
- **Exceedances:**
  - Manganese in sample BG2 (710 µg/L) exceeded the secondary MCL (50 µg/L). No other primary or secondary MCL exceedances were reported for any analyte.
- **Trends versus prior rounds:** Analytical results reported as consistent with previous events from 2013 through 2017 (no quantitative trend analysis provided).

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Field conditions:**
  - Rain event began January 4, 2018; total rainfall approximately 2.2 inches; samples collected during active rain on January 8.
  - PL1 and PL2 collected from puddles pooled along shoulders of North Emerald Avenue.
  - PL3 collected from a puddle at the north terminus of Bennett Street, outside the Caltrans right-of-way beyond the chainlink fence around Stockpile 2.
  - PL4 collected at the drainage basin outfall adjacent to Stockpile 3 (northeast of SR 99 off-ramp to Kansas Avenue); basin receives stormwater exclusively from SR 99 depressed section.
  - PL5 not sampled due to insufficient water; gate valve remained closed.
  - No runoff migration beyond Caltrans right-of-way observed during the event.
- **Sampling and analytical deviations / QA/QC:**
  - Samples filtered in field (0.45‑micron) for dissolved metals.
  - Laboratory performed dilutions for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc in samples PL1–PL4, BG1, BG2 due to high target analyte concentrations.
  - Samples BG1 and BG2 were also diluted for sulfide due to possible matrix interference.
  - Data qualifiers: “J” flags applied where analyte detected below Practical Quantitation Limit but ≥ Method Detection Limit.
  - Chain-of-custody procedures followed; laboratory QA/QC reviewed and accepted.

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusions:** Surface water quality results are consistent with those from previous monitoring events (2013–2017). No stormwater runoff from the stockpiles was observed migrating offsite; stormwater falling on stockpiles appeared to be retained within Caltrans right-of-way.
- **Recommendations:** The interceptor berm at the south-side access ramp to Stockpile 2 functioned as designed. Existing vegetative/fiber cover and straw wattles on the western slope of Stockpile 2 will continue to be maintained. Caltrans plans to conduct additional surface water sampling during qualifying rain events in 2018. (Attributed to Geocon Consultants, Inc., per the report.)
