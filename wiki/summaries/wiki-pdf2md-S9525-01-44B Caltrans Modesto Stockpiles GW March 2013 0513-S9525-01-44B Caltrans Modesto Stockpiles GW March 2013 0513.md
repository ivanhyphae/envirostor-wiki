This document summarizes the groundwater monitoring activities conducted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for the California Department of Transportation – District 6 (project S9525-01-44B) at the Caltrans Modesto Stockpiles site in Stanislaus County, California, during March 2013. The objective was to sample and analyze groundwater from ten monitoring wells (MW-1 through MW-10) in accordance with DTSC-approved protocols, with analytical work performed by Advanced Technology Laboratories.

Groundwater depths ranged from 31.15 to 39.62 feet below top of casing, exhibiting an east-southeast flow at an average gradient of 0.0006, consistent with historical trends. While polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) were generally not detected, several contaminants frequently exceeded California Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs). Nitrates (as nitrogen) consistently exceeded the 10 mg/L primary MCL in MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, and MW-6, with concentrations up to 13 mg/L in MW-1 and 30 mg/L in MW-5 during the monitoring period. Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) exceeded the 500 mg/L secondary MCL in MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10, with concentrations ranging from 253 to 730 mg/L. Manganese sporadically exceeded secondary MCLs, notably in MW-1 and MW-7 during this round. Other detected analytes included arsenic (2.0-5.2 ug/L), barium (43-300 ug/L), strontium (250-1,100 ug/L), chloride (7.2-32 mg/L), and sulfate (18-77 mg/L). Most dissolved metals and general minerals, with the exception of manganese and nitrate, were within water quality thresholds and showed trends similar to historical levels. Wells MW-5 and MW-6 dried during purging but recovered for sampling, and turbidity in MW-5 samples was noted.

Quality Control (QC) samples, including blanks, laboratory control samples, duplicates, and matrix spikes, were analyzed. While most QC parameters were within acceptable limits, some deviations were observed: Magnesium, Sodium, and Zinc recoveries were outside the 70-130% range in specific matrix spike samples, indicating potential matrix effects. Relative Percent Differences (RPDs) for Calcium and Magnesium in one duplicate and Beryllium in another were outside control limits or could not be evaluated. Dissolved CAM 17 Metals plus Strontium samples were field filtered, and the report noted MDL/PQL variations due to dilution and observed headspace in VOA containers. The report also includes detailed QC data for dissolved Mercury, showing acceptable recovery percentages and RPDs, and mentions a special instruction for Anion/Cation Balance calculation and EDF preparation for GeoTracker.
