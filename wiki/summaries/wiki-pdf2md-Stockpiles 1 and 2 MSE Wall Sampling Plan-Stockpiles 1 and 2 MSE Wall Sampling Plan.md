## Document identity
- Plan type: Sampling Plan, Workplan
- Preparer / author organization: Caltrans
- Date: February 2022

## Objective and scope
- Purpose of the work and what prompted it: This workplan outlines the sampling and analysis plan for existing mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) walls constructed with impacted soil, confirming proper segregation of clean soil and barium-impacted soil. The plan aims to demonstrate that clean soil used as engineering fill in the MSE walls meets Caltrans specifications and does not pose a risk for off-site disposal.
- Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed: Stockpiles 1 and 2, specifically theMSE walls constructed with stockpiled materials.
- What approval or decision the plan supports or requests: The plan supports the determination of whether the soil in the MSE walls is suitable for continued use as engineering fill or if it needs remediation.

## Proposed approach
- Sampling design:
    - **Sampling Locations:** A total of 30 discrete soil samples are proposed from each of the two MSE walls (MSE Wall 1 and MSE Wall 2), totaling 60 samples. Sampling locations are biased towards areas where potentially impacted soil was observed or where there are breaks in the geomembrane liner.
    - **Sampling Depth:** Samples will be collected from 0 to 1 foot below grade where the soil is exposed.
    - **Sampling Method:** Samples will be collected using hand auger, post-hole digger, or shovel. Sample collection will adhere to Caltrans procedures and DTSC guidance for characterizing contaminated sites.
    - **Target Analytes:** Samples will be analyzed for soluble barium using the California Waste Extraction Test (WET) and total barium by EPA Method 6010B/7010.
    - **Quality Control:** Ten percent of the total samples will be analyzed as duplicates for quality assurance/quality control (QA/QC).
- Decision criteria or action levels proposed:
    - **Clean Fill Threshold for Soluble Barium:** Less than 100 mg/L soluble barium (California WET). This is the action level for barium under Title 22, CCR, for characterization as a hazardous waste.
    - **Clean Fill Threshold for Total Barium:** Greater than background concentration but less than 2,000 mg/kg total barium. The 2,000 mg/kg threshold is based on the average total barium concentration from the Caltrans project-specific background study (Modesto Stockpiles Remediation Project Background Study, January 2020).

## Deliverables and approvals sought
The plan commits to producing the sampling data and analysis results, which will be used to determine the appropriate management and disposal of the soil within the MSE walls. It is submitted for review and approval by relevant regulatory agencies including DTSC and Central Valley RWQCB.

## Responsible parties
- **Caltrans:** Owner/operator, responsible for the implementation of the sampling plan and overall project management.
- **DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control):** Lead regulatory agency providing oversight.
- **Central Valley RWQCB (Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board):** Co-regulator.
