## Decision or request
**Approval granted.** By email dated June 12, 2020, DTSC (Dean Wright) — also speaking for the Water Board (Steven Meeks) — **approved** the "Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan" dated June 11, 2020. The Plan describes sampling and analysis for soil to be used as clean fill to cap the State Route 132 stockpiles. The approval is unconditional: DTSC states the Plan is "consistent with the previous borrow area sampling" and that "there are no comments."

**What was submitted for approval:** Geocon transmitted a sampling plan consisting of a map showing sample locations at proposed Basin 5. No separate technical memorandum with sampling methodology or background metal concentration comparison tables was included.

**Basis for the Plan:** Geocon's submittal letter states that Basin 5 is adjacent to and north of the previously tested SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road that exposed non-impacted native formation, and similar conditions are anticipated at Basin 5.

**Note on completeness:** Richard Stewart's forwarding email notes that a technical memorandum with sampling methodology and comparative background metal concentrations (as had been previously provided for the area east of Carpenter Avenue) was not included; Geocon is assuming the Carpenter technical memo previously submitted is applicable.
