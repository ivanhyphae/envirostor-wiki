## Thread / participants
- **March 13, 2020, John Juhrend (Geocon Consultants, Inc.) to Richard C. Stewart (Caltrans):** Submitted the Stockpile 3 BCS Removal Verification Testing Technical Memorandum for submittal to DTSC and requested a copy of DTSC's approval of Stockpile 2 BCS Removal.
- **March 16, 2020, Richard C. Stewart (Caltrans) to Dean Wright, Perry Myers (DTSC), Steven Meeks (Central Valley Water Board):** Forwarded the Stockpile 3 verification sampling technical memo, stating that all Stockpile 3 Barium Containing Soil (BCS) material has been excavated to depths below established threshold values. Requested approval to begin placement of clean soil cover or aggregate base material on Stockpiles 1 and 2 consolidation zones. Noted that the removal does not include regulated ADL soil along northbound State Route 99.
- **March 18, 2020, Dean Wright (DTSC) to Richard C. Stewart (Caltrans):** Approved the Technical Memorandum for Stockpile 3 BCS removal and confirmation sampling.

## Decision or request
- **What is being approved, accepted, conditionally approved, denied, requested, or commented on:** Acceptance of the Technical Memorandum for Stockpile 3 BCS Removal Verification Testing.
- **The specific document or deliverable it acts on:** Technical Memorandum dated March 13, 2020, describing the removal of Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Stockpile 3 and subsequent confirmation soil sampling.
- **Conditions, required revisions, or contingencies attached:** None stated; DTSC agrees that no further BCS RDIP monitoring requirements are necessary for this area, and it is now suitable for clean embankment fill.
- **Effective date or deadline, if any:** Effective March 18, 2020, with DTSC's email serving as acceptance.

## Open items and next actions
- Caltrans (Richard C. Stewart) had requested approval to begin placement of a minimum 12-inch clean soil cover and/or a minimum 4-inch thick layer of aggregate base material (AB) on the surfaces of the BCS consolidation zones (including temporary slopes) of Stockpiles 1 and 2. The DTSC approval in this correspondence only addresses Stockpile 3.
