## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose:** Annual cost estimate letter (Health and Safety Code §25269.5) providing DTSC’s projected oversight costs for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Phase 1 Interim SR 132 West Project, for FY 2023–2024.
- **Author or preparer:** Ms. Lora Jameson, P.G., Chief, Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, DTSC (letter signed); cost prepared by Dean Wright, P.G., Project Manager.
- **Date or period covered:** Letter dated November 7, 2023; cost estimate covers July 1, 2023 – June 30, 2024.

## Key claims
- DTSC asserts that the listed tasks (cost estimate preparation, completion of Land Use Covenant (LUC), review of Annual Inspection Report, project management) are scheduled for the FY period and that the estimated costs are based on DTSC’s 2022–2023 contract rates.
- DTSC reminds that responsible parties remain liable for all actual costs incurred, regardless of estimate.

## Regulatory decisions
- No formal regulatory decision is documented in this letter. It is a cost estimate notification, not a decision or order.

## Analytical results
- None; no sampling data or analytical results are presented.

## Remedial actions
- No physical remedial work is described. The letter references oversight activities:
  - **Complete Land Use Covenant (LUC)** – scheduled completion by December 15, 2023.
  - **Review Annual Inspection Report** – due by January 31, 2024; DTSC review by March 1, 2024.
  - **Project management** – ongoing through June 30, 2024.

## Responsible parties
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans)** – owner/operator, recipient of cost estimate; contact: Mr. Richard Stewart, P.G., Engineering Geologist.
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)** – regulatory oversight agency providing the cost estimate.
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CV-RWQCB)** – not mentioned in this letter, but is a co-regulator per the corpus.

## Key terms and conditions
- **Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5** – statutory basis for providing annual cost estimates.
- **Land Use Covenant (LUC)** – required to be completed by December 15, 2023.
- **Annual Inspection Report** – due by January 31, 2024; DTSC review deadline March 1, 2024.
- **FY 2023–2024 cost estimate total: $9,679.50** (comprising $1,234.50 for cost estimate preparation, $6,423.00 for LUC completion, $674.00 for annual inspection review, $1,348.00 for project management).
- **DTSC hourly rates** – based on 2022–2023 contract estimation rates; updated rates may apply later.
- **Project site:** Phase 1 Interim, State Route 132 West, Modesto, Stanislaus County, California; Site Code 900259-00.
