This Well Destruction Report, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for Caltrans, details the successful destruction of four groundwater monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10) at the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles site in Modesto, California, following regulatory approval.

The decision to decommission these wells and discontinue groundwater monitoring was made by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) on May 3, 2024. This approval stemmed from Geocon's updated comparative evaluation (2024), which concluded that the encapsulated soil stockpiles do not impact groundwater, rendering further monitoring from these specific wells unnecessary.

The field activities, performed by PeneCore Drilling under Geocon's management, were completed on March 18, 2025. The destruction process involved filling the wells with grout via a tremie pipe, applying air pressure for distribution, removing well boxes and concrete aprons, overdrilling the top 5 feet, backfilling the overdrilled annulus with cement grout, and capping with either native soil or black-dyed concrete. Waste materials generated during the process were transported for appropriate disposal.

Permits for the well destruction were issued by the Stanislaus County Department of Environmental Resources (SCDER), which required adherence to California Department of Water Resources (DWR) Bulletins 74-81 and 74-90, as well as the submission of Well Completion Reports to DWR's online system within 60 days of work completion. Caltrans facilitated access for all necessary work at the site, which is identified by GeoTracker Case ID / Global ID: SL0609924194. The project, including additional planned work for monitoring wells MW25-13 and MW25-14, began on March 11, 2025, and concluded by April 8, 2025.
