The provided documents detail the multi-year process of managing and verifying barium-contaminated soil (BCS) removal from Stockpile 2 along State Route 132 (SR 132) in Modesto, California.

Initial concerns in 2004, documented in a URS Corporation memorandum to Caltrans, recommended the removal of Stockpile 2 soil classified as California hazardous waste due to barium concentrations exceeding the Soluble Threshold Limit Concentration (STLC) of 100 mg/L via the Waste Extraction Test (WET). The proposed solution was transport to a permitted hazardous waste landfill.

Fast forward to 2020, Geocon provided an update to WSP/City of Modesto regarding verification testing for BCS removal to facilitate bridge overcrossing construction. The new cleanup thresholds were 1,000 mg/kg for barium (groundwater protection) and 80 mg/kg for lead (residential screening level), as specified in the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). Verification testing in 2020 revealed that initial excavations at the West End of Stockpile 2 had barium exceedances (3,200 mg/kg and 1,100 mg/kg), requiring further excavation. Subsequent resampling showed barium levels (140 mg/kg and 64 mg/kg) safely below the threshold. However, the East End Stockpile 2 presented a more persistent challenge, with five of six initial samples showing barium concentrations exceeding 1,000 mg/kg (up to 1400 mg/kg), and later samples still showing highs of 4100 mg/kg, even after initial excavation. Although lead levels consistently met the cleanup goals, some areas of the East End continued to exceed barium thresholds. Quality control analyses for the 2020 testing at the East End, while showing acceptable laboratory control sample recoveries, indicated issues with matrix spike recoveries for barium, suggesting potential matrix interference.

Further confirmation and remediation efforts continued into 2023. A Caltrans memo dated October 26, 2023, recommended additional removal and off-site disposal of barium-contaminated soil from Stockpile 2 based on recent sampling. One sample (S2-2-04) out of eleven collected in August 2023 exceeded a 200 mg/kg cleanup goal with a concentration of 340 mg/kg, indicating the ongoing need for management.

A specific soil sample (S2E 8A-0) from the East End of Stockpile 2, collected in March 2020, was analyzed by Advanced Technology Laboratories and found to contain 63 mg/kg of Barium and 1.6 mg/kg of Lead, both well below the East End cleanup thresholds. The associated analytical report highlighted rigorous internal quality control measures, confirming the reliability of these results through acceptable recoveries and relative percent differences for both barium and lead.

In summary, while the initial recommendation in 2004 was for hazardous waste removal, later verification testing in 2020 focused on meeting specific cleanup thresholds for groundwater protection and residential screening levels. Remedial excavation and re-testing successfully brought barium levels below these new thresholds at the West End of Stockpile 2. However, some areas of the East End Stockpile 2 demonstrated persistent barium exceedances during the verification testing period, leading to continued recommendations for removal as late as 2023.
