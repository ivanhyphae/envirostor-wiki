### Summary of Draft Interim Removal Action Completion Report (RACR)

This document is a **Draft Interim Removal Action Completion Report (RACR)** prepared by **Geocon Consultants, Inc.** on December 23, 2021, documenting the completion of Interim Phase 1 construction for the **Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles (SR 132 West Project, Stanislaus County, CA)** . The work was conducted under oversight of the **California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)** and the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB)** , as outlined in the 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP).

**Contaminants and Remedy**

The primary contaminants of concern are **barium** and **aerially deposited lead (ADL)** . The selected remedy consolidated all barium-containing soil (BCS) from Stockpiles 1, 2, and 3 into designated containment zones (Stockpiles 1 and 2) for permanent highway embankment fill, capped with approved clean fill and a structural pavement section. Performance standards required barium below **1,000 mg/kg** and lead below **80 mg/kg** in removal areas.

**Verification and Characterization Findings**

Interim Phase 1 is complete. Verification sampling confirmed that removal areas at Stockpile 3 and edges of Stockpile 2 met all thresholds. Detailed characterization of MSE wall footing excavations at Stockpiles 1 and 2 (analyzed via EPA Method 6010B) found:

- **Stockpile 1:** Barium detected in all samples (42–420 mg/kg), all exceeding site-specific background (120 mg/kg) but below the 1,000 mg/kg removal threshold. Lead was low (1.1–6.7 mg/kg).
- **Stockpile 2:** Barium detected in all samples (53–930 mg/kg), with 14 samples exceeding background, primarily in the upper 3 feet. Lead ranged from 3.5–22 mg/kg.

**Conclusions and Next Steps**

- Upper 3 feet of soil from Stockpile 2 excavations must be placed in designated fill containment zones.
- Soil from deeper than 3 feet (both stockpiles) is suitable for reuse as general embankment fill or capping material, pending regulatory approval.
- While BCS is contained and capped, the site is not yet closed. A **Land Use Covenant (LUC)** will be recorded to restrict future use, with ongoing obligations including annual groundwater sampling and stormwater inspections.
- A final RACR will be submitted after completion of **Ultimate Phase 2 construction**, currently scheduled for **2028**.
