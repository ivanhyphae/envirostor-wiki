## Document identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Engineering exhibit (site map and construction layout plan) for proposed clean fill source testing at the SR 132 West – Freeway/Expressway Project, Modesto, California. The exhibit delineates boring locations, composite sampling methodology, and analytical testing parameters for characterizing soil intended as cover fill material at the remediation site.
- **Author or preparer**: Not explicitly identified; likely Caltrans District 10 or its environmental consultant (referenced “2018 Rincon Phase II ESA” in annotations).
- **Date or period covered**: Base map dated October 2014; handwritten annotations and plan sheets dated February 1, 2019 (sheet L-15). The testing plan appears to have been developed after the 2018 Rincon Phase II ESA.

## Key claims
- **Proposed testing area** (from annotation on sheet L-13): “Proposed clean cover fill source testing” located within the Route 132 right-of-way (ROW) at and east of Carpenter Road, with a maximum cut depth of 5 feet.
- **Boring and composite plan**: Twelve borings to a depth of 5 feet, composited into three 4-point composites. Laboratory analysis specified: for the 0–2 foot interval, test for pesticides and metals; for the 2–5 foot interval, test for metals only.
- **Site conditions map** (page 0): Identifies parcels with recognized environmental conditions (RECs) and risk classifications (High, Medium, Low). A handwritten note references “2018 Rincon Phase II ESA” and “Proposed Clean Fill Source” with arrows pointing to specific areas.
- **Layout plan** (sheet L-15, page 2): Shows a red-dashed polygon surrounding the highway overpass area with multiple blue-circled “X” marks, likely indicating proposed boring locations. Stationing from 1115 to 1130, adjacent features include BASIN 5, Kansas Ave, Comstock Ct, Custer Ct, and existing sound walls.

## Regulatory decisions
- No formal regulatory decisions are recorded in this exhibit. The document is a pre‑remedial testing plan and does not contain approvals or conditions from DTSC, Central Valley RWQCB, or other agencies.

## Analytical results
- **Contaminants and media**: Not provided; this is a *proposed* testing plan. The specified analytes are pesticides and metals (0–2 ft) and metals only (2–5 ft). No concentration ranges, thresholds, or exceedances are reported.

## Remedial actions
- No physical remediation work is described. The exhibit is limited to source characterization and sampling design for clean fill material intended to cover remediated areas (inferred from the “clean cover fill” terminology).

## Responsible parties
- **Caltrans** – Owner/operator of the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project and the SR 132 ROW. Likely responsible for implementing the proposed testing.
- **Rincon Consultants, Inc.** – Referenced in the handwritten note “2018 Rincon Phase II ESA” as preparer of an earlier environmental site assessment.
- **DTSC** (Department of Toxic Substances Control) and **Central Valley RWQCB** (Regional Water Quality Control Board) – Co-regulators for the broader barium-contaminated fill project, though not explicitly named in this document.

## Key terms and conditions
- The proposed clean fill source testing must follow the specified boring pattern (12 borings to 5 ft depth) and composite method (three 4-point composites per site).
- Analytical suites are depth‑dependent:
  - 0–2 ft: pesticides and metals.
  - 2–5 ft: metals only.
- Maximum cut depth in the testing area is 5 feet.
- The testing area is within the existing Route 132 ROW, east of Carpenter Road.
- Reference to “Proposed Clean Fill Source” indicates the material is intended for use as cover soil, subject to meeting applicable reuse criteria (not specified in this exhibit).
