## Document identity
- **Memo subject / question addressed:** Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Footing Excavation Characterization Testing — evaluating suitability of excavated material for reuse as fill.
- **Preparer / author organization:** John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Recipient / addressee:** Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto
- **Date:** April 6, 2020
- **Area, stockpile, or feature involved:** Stockpile 1, southerly slope, MSE wall footing excavation along SR 132 right-of-way, Modesto, California.

## Purpose
To characterize soil from the MSE wall footing excavation after removal of barium-containing soil (BCS) along the Stockpile 1 southerly slope, and to determine whether the excavated material is suitable for placement as fill within the Stockpile 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill, or as capping material.

## Data and findings
- **Sampling performed:** Discrete soil samples collected from the mid-point of the wall footing excavation in three trenches (STK1-1, STK1-2, STK1-3) spaced at approximately 200-foot intervals. Samples collected from one-foot intervals to a maximum depth of 5 feet (total 15 samples).
- **Analytes and methods:** Barium and lead analyzed by EPA Test Method 6010B (ICP-AES) under expedited 24-hour turnaround. Laboratory: Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL), under chain of custody.
- **Barium results:** Detected in all 15 samples at concentrations ranging from 42 to 420 mg/kg. All results below the RDIP removal threshold of 1,000 mg/kg (groundwater quality protection). The 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) of the data set is 171.9 mg/kg; mean is 128.3 mg/kg. Maximum site-specific background value is 120 mg/kg. Comparison to San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board Environmental Screening Levels (ESLs): 3,000 mg/kg (construction workers), 15,000 mg/kg (residential), 220,000 mg/kg (commercial).
- **Lead results:** Detected in 14 of 15 samples at concentrations ranging from 1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg. All results within the range of the maximum site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg and below the RDIP removal threshold of 80 mg/kg (residential land use).
- **No exceedances** of applicable RDIP removal thresholds were identified.

## Conclusion and recommendation
The memo concludes that the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material excavated along the southerly boundary of Stockpile 1 is suitable for placement within the Stockpile 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill at the east end of Stockpile 1 and/or ends of Stockpile 2, or as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material, pending regulatory agency approval. This recommendation is attributed to Geocon Consultants, Inc.

## Follow-on and approval status
The information will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) to be submitted following completion of Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities. No specific agency approval is stated as received in this memo; approval is described as pending.
