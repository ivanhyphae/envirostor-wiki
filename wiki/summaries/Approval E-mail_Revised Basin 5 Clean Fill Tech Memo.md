## Decision or request
- **Substance:** DTSC formally accepts the *Revised Technical Memorandum (Revised Memo)* dated July 2, 2020.
- **Document acted on:** *Revised Technical Memorandum – Basin 5 Clean Fill Borrow Area Soil Characterization*, dated July 2, 2020 (the version that includes a chromium footnote).
- **Conditions:** The acceptance is based on the additional chromium‑justification information provided by e‑mail and attachments received by DTSC on June 29, 2020, and summarized in a footnote in the revised memo. The agencies affirm that the composite soil‑sample results are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and suitable as clean cover material for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2.
- **Effective date:** July 9, 2020 (date of the acceptance e‑mail).

## Open items and next actions
- **None** — The approval is final. (Caltrans had already indicated it would notify the contractor on July 6; no further revisions or deliverables are required by the acceptance e‑mail.)
