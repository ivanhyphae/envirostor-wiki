A technical memorandum from Geocon, dated July 2, 2020, details environmental soil testing at the SR 132 Basin 5 borrow area in Modesto, California, to evaluate its suitability as a clean cover fill for barium-contaminated soil stockpiles. Soil samples collected from six trenches up to 10 feet deep underwent analysis for Title 22 metals and organochlorine pesticides (OCPs). The native soil, described as clayey to sandy silt, showed no visual contamination. Metal concentrations, such as arsenic (1.0-1.8 mg/kg), barium (66-94 mg/kg), and lead (5.8-11 mg/kg), were consistent with 2007 background data. Other metals and all OCPs were non-detect.

A separate Geocon memorandum on June 22, 2020, provided a quality control (QC) review for this testing. Mercury analyses (EPA 7471A) showed all samples as non-detect with acceptable QC batch recoveries. For Title 22 Metals (EPA 6010B), while blanks and laboratory control samples (LCS) were acceptable, matrix spike/matrix spike duplicate (MS/MSD) recoveries for antimony, selenium, and thallium were outside acceptance limits, indicating potential matrix interference. However, relative percent differences (RPDs) remained within 20%, ensuring good precision. Organochlorine pesticide (EPA 8081A) analyses showed acceptable blank results and LCS validation, with surrogate recoveries within limits, despite some MS recoveries being outside. Overall, the Basin 5 borrow area appears suitable for clean fill, with the analytical QC generally acceptable despite some identified matrix interference.
