## Document identity
- **Form:** Email thread culminating in an approval letter (DTSC approval conveyed by email)
- **Sender / author organization:** Dean Wright, PG – DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control)
- **Recipients / addressee:** Richard C. Stewart – Caltrans (California Department of Transportation); cc: Perry Myers (DTSC), Kyle Cockerham (Central Valley RWQCB)
- **Date:** April 7, 2021 (approval date); thread spans February 25 – April 7, 2021

## Thread / participants
Listed in chronological order, most recent last:

1. **Feb 25, 2021 – Adan Corrales (Bay Cities Paving & Grading) to WSP team (Rick Deml, Chad Baker, Inderjit Chadha, Scott Frenette):** Provides plans for clean cap test locations at Modesto 132/99.

2. **Mar 1, 2021 – Scott Frenette (WSP) to Adan Corrales (Bay Cities):** Requests excavation depth, total fill volume, utility clearance, and excavation schedule for Carpenter Shoofly material; notes material from Retaining Wall No. 1110 is already approved but the shoofly material needs a sampling plan reviewed by DTSC before testing.

3. **Mar 30, 2021 – Pablo Bernardino (Bay Cities) to Scott Frenette (WSP):** Provides requested info: excavation depth 20 ft, total fill volume 5,000 cy, USA clearance confirmed, excavation planned week of April 19.

4. **Apr 2, 2021 – Rick Deml (WSP) to John Juhrend (Geocon):** Forwards the above project details and asks if sufficient for the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Cap Test Plan.

5. **Apr 2, 2021 – John Juhrend (Geocon) to Richard Stewart (Caltrans):** Transmits the clean fill sampling plan for the Carpenter Road Shoofly (developed from WSP’s info) and asks Stewart to forward to DTSC for approval. Notes sampling will proceed only after DTSC approval.

6. **Apr 5, 2021 (7:26 AM) – Richard Stewart (Caltrans) to Dean Wright (DTSC):** Submits the proposed soil sampling information (the plan) from Geocon for determination of clean fill material beneath Carpenter Ave. detour; states no use of soil until DTSC approval.

7. **Apr 5, 2021 (9:25 AM) – Dean Wright (DTSC) to Richard Stewart (Caltrans):** Asks for clarification: plan mentions 6 depth-discrete composite samples but appears to show 6 borings with 4 depth-discrete samples each; suggests compositing 3 borings per depth (for 8 composites) as recommended.

8. **Apr 5, 2021 (10:16 AM) – Richard Stewart (Caltrans) to Dean Wright (DTSC):** Concurs with Wright’s interpretation and states he will ask Geocon for clarification.

9. **Apr 5, 2021 (10:26 AM) – Dean Wright (DTSC) to Richard Stewart (Caltrans):** Acknowledges they are on same page; requests update after Geocon clarifies.

10. **Apr 5, 2021 (11:37 AM) – Richard Stewart (Caltrans) to Dean Wright (DTSC):** Transmits Geocon’s revised sampling plan (attached).

11. **Apr 7, 2021 (10:10 AM) – Dean Wright (DTSC) to Richard Stewart (Caltrans):** Approves the revised plan; states DTSC and Waterboard have reviewed it and have no comments.

## Decision or request
- **Action:** DTSC (with concurrence from Central Valley RWQCB) approves the **Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan** (revised version received April 5, 2021).
- **Document acted on:** “Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan” (revised, dated April 5, 2021, prepared by Geocon).
- **Conditions:** None stated. The plan is described as consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts.
- **Effective date:** April 7, 2021 (date of approval email).

## Open items and next actions
- **Next step:** With approval granted, Geocon is to proceed with the sampling as described in the plan. Sampling is to be completed before the material can be used as clean fill.
- **Due dates:** Excavation of material (for which the sampled soil is intended) was previously communicated for the week of April 19, 2021 (per March 30 email from Bay Cities).
- **No required revisions** or resubmittals are identified in the approval.

## Referenced documents
