# Groundwater Monitoring Report – October 2016 and April 2017

## Document identity
- **Report type:** Groundwater monitoring round (semi-annual depth-to-water and annual sampling)
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Reporting period or sampling date(s):** Depth-to-water measured October 28, 2016 and April 12, 2017; groundwater samples collected April 12, 2017
- **Project number or task order:** Geocon Project No. S1200-01-01, Task Order No. 1, EA 10-0X2700, Contract No. 06A2184

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Groundwater
- **Wells, stations, or locations sampled (list IDs):**
  - **October 2016 (depth-to-water only):** MW‑1, MW‑2, MW‑5, MW‑6, MW‑7, MW‑9, MW‑10
  - **April 2017 (sampling):** MW‑1, MW‑2, MW‑3, MW‑4, MW‑7
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled, and why:**
  - **October 2016 – dry:** MW‑3, MW‑4, MW‑8
  - **April 2017 – dry / insufficient water:** MW‑5, MW‑6, MW‑8, MW‑9, MW‑10 (also attempted MW‑6 but insufficient water to purge and sample)

## Results
- **Analytes measured, with concentration ranges and units:**
  - **Dissolved metals (Title 22 plus strontium):** barium (7.6 – 250 µg/L), strontium (370 – 2100 µg/L), antimony, arsenic, chromium, copper, molybdenum, nickel, selenium, vanadium, lead (all < MDL or below thresholds), cadmium, cobalt, manganese, zinc
  - **General minerals:** nitrate‑N (2.6 – 24 mg/L), TDS (230 – 550 mg/L), chloride, sulfate, sulfide, total/bicarbonate/carbonate alkalinity, major cations
- **Applicable thresholds:** California Primary MCLs (e.g., barium 1000 µg/L, nitrate‑N 10 mg/L), Secondary MCLs (e.g., manganese 50 µg/L, TDS 500 mg/L), EPA Drinking Water Health Advisories, Regulatory Action Levels, Notification Levels
- **Locations exceeding thresholds and by how much:**
  - **Nitrate as N** > Primary MCL (10 mg/L): MW‑1 (14 mg/L), MW‑2 (24 mg/L), MW‑4 (15 mg/L)
  - **TDS** > Secondary MCL (500 mg/L): MW‑1 (550 mg/L), MW‑2 (550 mg/L), MW‑4 (540 mg/L)
  - **Manganese** > Secondary MCL (50 µg/L): MW‑1 (71 µg/L)
  - No dissolved metals exceeded Primary MCLs; barium and strontium remained below their thresholds.
- **Trends versus prior rounds:** Barium, strontium, and most dissolved metals reported at concentrations similar to historical levels. Stiff diagrams show very slight changes in ionic content since 2012; MW‑1, MW‑2, MW‑4, MW‑7 remain calcium‑dominant, MW‑3 remains sodium‑dominant.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **Dry / low-yield wells:** October 2016 – MW‑3, MW‑4, MW‑8 dry. April 2017 – MW‑5, MW‑6, MW‑8, MW‑9, MW‑10 dry or with insufficient water; MW‑6 attempted but no groundwater to purge.
- **Depth-to-water / gradient:** October 2016 – range 38.77–47.10 ft below TOC; flow toward south, average gradient 0.007. April 2017 – range 35.52–40.73 ft below TOC; flow toward east‑southeast, average gradient 0.0007.
- **Sampling deviations / data qualifiers:**
  - Duplicate sample (MW‑7 → MW‑11): sulfide RPD = 151%, carbonate RPD = 68% → both qualified as estimated (concentrations may be less or greater than reported).
  - Laboratory narrative: samples diluted due to high target‑analyte concentrations and/or failing internal standard in original run (interferences from matrix).
  - Several MS/MSD recoveries/RPDs outside laboratory control limits; data validated by laboratory control samples; no further qualification needed.
- **Other observations:** No well‑box tampering or damage observed. ORP measured in MW‑1 through MW‑4 and MW‑7 in April 2017.

## Conclusions and recommendations
**Conclusions (Geocon):**
- With the exception of manganese in MW‑1, no dissolved metals exceeded numeric water quality thresholds.
- Barium and strontium concentrations remained similar to historical levels and well below thresholds.
- Nitrate exceeded the Primary MCL in MW‑1, MW‑2, and MW‑4; TDS exceeded the Secondary MCL in those same three wells.
- Groundwater chemistry (Stiff diagrams) has changed very little since 2012.

**Recommendations (Geocon):**
- Continue annual groundwater monitoring, with the next event scheduled for April 2018.
- Continue semi‑annual depth‑to‑water measurements, with the next event scheduled for October 2017.
