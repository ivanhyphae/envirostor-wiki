## Document identity
- Form: Forwarded thread
- Sender / author organization: DTSC (initial message in thread from Rincon Consultants)
- Recipient(s) / addressee: Caltrans (Richard C. Stewart@DOT)
- Date: February 8, 2021 - February 11, 2021

## Thread / participants
- On February 8, 2021, Ryan Thacher (Rincon Consultants) to Richard C. Stewart (Caltrans) with CC to Gary Mohler, John Rawles (City of Modesto), Scott Frenette, Rick Deml (WSP): Submitted a revised Soil Management Plan (SMP) for the SR-132 project, noting revisions to incorporate DTSC requests and a lowered screening level for TPH-d. Requested to proceed with targeted excavation activities in Basin 6A and excavation of soil in Basin 6B, stating these actions should not be impacted by anticipated discussions with the RWQCB regarding groundwater protection screening levels.
- On February 9, 2021, Richard C. Stewart (Caltrans) to Steven Meeks (Waterboards) with CC to Dean Wright (DTSC): Forwarded the revised SMP and expressed concern that the City of Modesto might proceed with excavation without regulatory acceptance of the SMP.
- On February 11, 2021, Dean Wright (DTSC) to Richard C. Stewart (Caltrans) with CC to Chris Flower, Steven Meeks, Kyle Cockerham (Waterboards), Perry Myers (DTSC): Provided official acceptance of the revised SMP, with two minor conditions.

## Decision or request
- What is being approved: Soil Management Plan
- The specific document or deliverable it acts on: Revised Soil Management Plan (SMP) dated February 8, 2021
- Conditions, required revisions, or contingencies attached:
    1. Soil with Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) above screening levels must be segregated, covered, and labeled until transported offsite.
    2. If TPH-impacted soil is still present when the maximum depth of the basins is achieved, Caltrans must be alerted, and consultation with the Water Board and DTSC must take place to determine appropriate next steps.
- Effective date or deadline, if any: This email serves as the official acceptance.

## Open items and next actions
- The two minor comments provided in the February 11, 2021 email may be incorporated in the field to avoid further revision of the SMP.
- In the event TPH-impacted soil is still present at the maximum basin depth, Caltrans must alert the regulators and consult with the Water Board and DTSC for next steps.
