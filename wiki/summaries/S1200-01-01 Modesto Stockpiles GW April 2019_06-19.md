## Document identity
- **Report type:** Groundwater monitoring report covering two events: depth-to-water measurements (October 2018) and groundwater sampling (April 2019).
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Rancho Cordova, CA). Project Manager: Rebecca L. Silva; Senior Engineer: John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG.
- **Reporting period or sampling date(s):** October 18, 2018 (depth-to-water); April 8–9, 2019 (depth-to-water, purging, and sampling).
- **Project number or task order:** Geocon Project No. S1200-01-01, Task Order No. 1, EA 10-0X2700, Contract No. 06A2184.

## Monitoring scope
- **Media sampled:** Groundwater.
- **Wells, stations, or locations sampled:**
    - **October 2018:** Depth-to-water measured at MW-1 through MW-7 and MW-9 (MW-10 could not be located beneath brush/weeds; MW-8 was dry).
    - **April 2019:** Groundwater samples collected from MW-1 through MW-8. Depth-to-water measured at MW-1 through MW-8.
- **Locations planned but NOT sampled, and why:**
    - **October 2018:** MW-10 – inaccessible (obscured by brush/weeds); MW-8 – dry.
    - **April 2019:** MW-9 and MW-10 – both dry. (MW-9 also had a missing well plug/lid noted during inspection.)

## Results
- **Analytes measured and concentration ranges (April 2019):**
    - **Primary COCs (DTSC-identified): barium, lead, strontium.**
        - Barium (dissolved): 32 µg/L (MW-1) to 390 µg/L (MW-5). All < Primary MCL of 1,000 µg/L.
        - Strontium (dissolved): 360 µg/L (MW-5) to 2,300 µg/L (MW-3). All < EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory of 4,000 µg/L.
        - Lead: not detected at or above MDL in any sample.
    - **Other dissolved metals (ranges where detected):**
        - Chromium: 1.1 to 13 µg/L (all < Primary MCL of 50 µg/L).
        - Vanadium: 2.5 to 28 µg/L (all < EPA HA of 21 µg/L? *Note: report cites EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory as threshold for vanadium, but does not state the value in the text; data tables indicate no exceedances.*)
        - Manganese: detected in MW-5 at 59 µg/L, exceeding the Secondary MCL (taste/odor) of 50 µg/L.
        - Copper, selenium, zinc, cobalt, molybdenum, nickel: detected sporadically; none exceeded applicable MCLs or health advisories.
    - **General minerals:**
        - Nitrate as nitrogen: 2.3 to 40 mg/L. Exceeded Primary MCL of 10 mg/L in MW-1, MW-2, MW-5, and MW-6.
        - Total Dissolved Solids (TDS): 300 to 760 mg/L. Exceeded Secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-5, and MW-6.
- **Thresholds applied:** California Primary MCLs (CDPH), California Secondary MCLs (CDPH), EPA Drinking Water Health Advisories.
- **Exceedances:**
    - Nitrate > 10 mg/L: MW-1, MW-2, MW-5, MW-6.
    - TDS ≥ 500 mg/L: MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6.
    - Manganese > 50 µg/L (secondary MCL): MW-5.
- **Trends vs. prior rounds:** The report states that barium, strontium, and other dissolved metals concentrations were "similar to historical levels" and remained significantly less than thresholds. Stiff diagrams from 2012 through April 2019 showed only very slight changes in ionic composition; MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-5, MW-7 remained calcium-dominant; MW-3, MW-6, MW-8 remained sodium-dominant. Nitrate and TDS exceedances have been consistently reported in certain wells (MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-10) in prior events.

## Field conditions and QA/QC
- **October 2018:** Well MW-10 could not be located due to overgrown brush/weeds; depth-to-water not measured. Well MW-8 was dry.
- **April 2019:**
    - Wells MW-9 and MW-10 were dry; no samples collected.
    - Well MW-9 had a missing well plug and lid (observed during inspection; condition noted, not reported as tampering).
    - Well MW-2: filters not working properly during purging; an unfiltered sample was collected in a non-preserved container on 4/8/19, and a filtered sample was collected the following day (4/9/19).
    - Well MW-4: well went dry after purging 0.5 gallons on 4/8/19; returned on 4/9/19 to collect the sample.
    - ORP measured in field: MW-2 = -1 mV; MW-4 = 2 mV; MW-7 = 7 mV.
- **Laboratory QA/QC:**
    - Laboratory (ATL) reported that samples were diluted due to high concentrations of target and/or non-target analytes.
    - Method blank surrogate recoveries were acceptable; no target analytes detected in method blanks.
    - Laboratory control sample recoveries were appropriate.
    - Several matrix spike/matrix spike duplicate analytes had recoveries or RPDs outside laboratory control limits; however, the sample results were validated by the LCS. Data qualified as estimated (J) where appropriate.
    - A duplicate sample was collected from MW-8 (labeled as MW-11); results showed similar concentrations for dissolved metals and general minerals, indicating acceptable field precision.
    - **Data qualifiers issued:** "J" (estimated concentration) for results ≥ MDL but < PQL; "M1" (matrix spike recovery outside limits, batch validated by LCS); "D6" (dilution due to target analyte high concentration); "D2" (dilution due to non-target analyte high concentration).

## Conclusions and recommendations
- **Conclusions (by Geocon):**
    - With the exception of manganese in MW-5, no dissolved metals exceeded their respective numeric water quality thresholds.
    - Barium and strontium concentrations were similar to historical levels and remained well below thresholds.
    - Nitrate exceeded the primary MCL in four wells (MW-1, MW-2, MW-5, MW-6); TDS exceeded the secondary MCL in five wells (MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6).
    - Stiff diagrams showed very slight changes in ionic composition since 2012; calcium-dominant and sodium-dominant groupings remained consistent.
- **Recommendations (by Geocon):**
    - Continue annual groundwater monitoring (next event scheduled for April 2020).
    - Continue semi-annual depth-to-water measurements (next event scheduled for October 2019).
