## Document identity
- Deliverable type: Errata
- Version / draft / final status: Final
- Preparer / author organization: California Department of Transportation (Caltrans)
- Date: February 6, 2020
- Project number or task order (if stated): Not stated

## Purpose and regulatory context
- What milestone or requirement the document fulfills: Updates roles and responsibilities in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) following a change in contractor.
- Oversight framework and the agency it is submitted to: Submitted to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
- For an errata: the parent document and what is being corrected: The parent document is the "January 30, 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan - Interim Phase 1." The errata modifies previously identified roles and responsibilities and removes Geocon, Inc. as Caltrans' agent.

## Selected or recommended remedy
- Design basis, engineered features, and performance/cleanup standards: The prescribed remedy for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remains the "containment alternative" during construction of the SR 132 West Expressway project, as established in the RDIP.

## Responsible parties
- California Department of Transportation (Caltrans): Responsible party required to demonstrate that the remediation work is performed as described in the RDIP.
- Geocon, Inc.: Previously identified as Caltrans' sole agent for ensuring containment of stockpiles but is no longer under contract with the state.
- Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC): Oversight agency.
