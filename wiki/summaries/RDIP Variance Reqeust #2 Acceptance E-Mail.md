## Decision or request
- **What is being approved:** The “Second Variance to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (Request) dated September 2, 2020” (RDIP Variance #2) – the use of Class 3 aggregate subbase as a clean cap over Barium Containing Soil / Aerially Deposited Lead (BCS/ADL) stockpiles.
- **Conditions attached:**
  1. **Minimum cap thickness:** 6 inches of Class 3 subbase (instead of the proposed 4 inches), because the stockpiled Class 3 material is primarily fine sand (grains likely passing a No. 4 sieve) and no pavement/hardscape will be in place before the rainy season.
  2. **Additional monitoring:** Increased monitoring may be necessary, given the fine-grained nature of the cap material.
  3. **Composite sample required:** A single composite sample must be collected from the Class 3 material stockpile adjacent to the Rosemore Overcrossing and analyzed for barium and lead using EPA Method 6010B to confirm it meets RDIP standards.
- **Effective date:** September 21, 2020 (date of email).

## Open items and next actions
- **Caltrans / Geocon:** Ensure the Class 3 subbase cap is placed at a minimum of 6 inches thickness.
- **Caltrans / Geocon:** Obtain a composite sample from the Rosemore Overcrossing stockpile, test for barium and lead per EPA 6010B, and presumably provide results to DTSC and Water Board (not explicitly stated but implied by the request).
- **DTSC / Water Board:** No further action stated; acceptance is contingent on the conditions above. Increased monitoring details are not specified and may need to be proposed by Caltrans.
