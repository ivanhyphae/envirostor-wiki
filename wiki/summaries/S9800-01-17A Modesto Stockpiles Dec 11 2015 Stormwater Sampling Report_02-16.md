# Document Summary: Surface Water Sampling Report – December 11, 2015

## Document Identity
- **Document type and purpose**: Surface Water Sampling Report – summary report detailing surface water sampling activities conducted during a December 11, 2015 rain event at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Site
- **Author/preparer**: Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Rebecca L. Silva, Project Manager; John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Senior Engineer)
- **Date/period covered**: Sampling event December 11, 2015; report dated January 29, 2016
- **Project reference**: Caltrans Contract No. 06A1895, Task Order No. 17, EA No. 10-0X2700, Geocon Project No. S9800-01-17A

## Key Claims
- Surface water samples collected from locations PL1–PL4 contained dissolved metals concentrations within the same general range as previously reported at each sample location (Geocon, p. 2)
- None of the reported dissolved metals concentrations exceed their respective primary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) (Geocon, p. 2)
- Manganese concentrations in samples PL2, PL3, and PL4 exceed secondary (taste/odor/welfare-based) MCLs (Geocon, p. 2)
- Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected at or above practical quantitation limits (PQLs) in any sample (Geocon, p. 2)
- General minerals concentrations were within the same general range as previously reported; none exceed primary or secondary MCLs (Geocon, p. 2)
- Runoff from Stockpile 2 along North Emerald Avenue is managed by vegetative/fiber cover and straw wattles; Caltrans constructed a berm at the base of the access ramp south of Stockpile 2 in February 2014 to intercept runoff (Geocon, p. 3)
- No surface water migration beyond Caltrans right-of-way was observed during this or prior sampling events (Geocon, pp. 1-2)

## Regulatory Decisions
- **Decision type**: Sampling event conducted under DTSC-approved protocols established in the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)* (Shaw Environmental, Inc., January 2006) and *Addendum to Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* (Geocon, February 20, 2013)
- **Agency**: California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) – protocols approved; Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) – sample locations approved
- **Date**: December 11, 2015 (sampling event)
- **Conditions**: Sampling in general accordance with approved SAP and addendum; samples analyzed by California-certified laboratory

## Analytical Results
**Dissolved Metals (EPA Method 6020/7470A):**
- Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, zinc – reported in samples PL1–PL4
- Arsenic – reported only in sample PL3
- Lead – reported only in sample PL4
- **Manganese exceedances**: Samples PL2, PL3, PL4 exceeded secondary MCLs (taste/odor/welfare-based)
- Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, thallium – not detected ≥ PQLs in any sample
- No primary MCL exceedances for any dissolved metal

**General Minerals (SM 2320B, SM 2540C, SM 2540D, EPA 300.0, SM 4500-S-D):**
- Parameters analyzed: chloride, nitrate as nitrogen, sulfate, sulfide, total alkalinity, bicarbonate alkalinity, carbonate alkalinity, total dissolved solids (TDS), total suspended solids (TSS)
- No primary or secondary MCL exceedances reported

**Field Parameters (Table 1):**
- pH, electrical conductivity (μmhos/cm), temperature (°C), turbidity (ntu), oxygen-reduction potential (ORP), dissolved oxygen (DO) – monitored during sampling

**Laboratory QA/QC Notes:**
- Nitrate as nitrogen reanalysis was past holding time (Geocon, p. 2)
- Matrix spike recoveries for several metals in samples PL1–PL4 outside acceptance limits due to possible matrix interference; analytical batch validated by laboratory control sample (Geocon, pp. 2, laboratory report notes)

## Remedial Actions
- **Activity**: Surface water sampling during qualifying rain event (0.18 inch rainfall, December 10-11, 2015)
- **Quantities**: Four surface water samples collected (PL1, PL2, PL3, PL4); three locations dry (PL5, BG1, BG2)
- **Phase status**: Ongoing monitoring – Caltrans plans additional sampling during 2016 rain season
- **Verification outcomes**: No runoff migration beyond Caltrans right-of-way observed; existing management measures (vegetative/fiber cover, straw wattles, berm) appear functional

## Responsible Parties
- **California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) – District 6**: Owner/operator; maintains stockpile covers and constructed berm; responsible for gate valve at drainage basin
- **California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)**: Regulatory oversight; approved sampling protocols
- **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB)**: Co-regulator; sample locations approved
- **Geocon Consultants, Inc.**: Contractor performing sampling and reporting (Caltrans Contract No. 06A1895, Task Order No. 17)
- **Advanced Technology Laboratories, Inc. (ATL)**: California-certified analytical laboratory (ELAP No. 1838)

## Key Terms and Conditions
- Caltrans to conduct additional surface water sampling events during 2016 rain season
- Stockpile vegetative/fiber cover and straw wattles to be maintained for surface water management along North Emerald Avenue (western slope of Stockpile 2)
- Berm constructed in February 2014 at base of access ramp (south side of Stockpile 2) to be maintained
- Gate valve at drainage basin next to Stockpile 3 (PL5) would only be opened if basin neared capacity jeopardizing SR 99 northbound lanes; no record of opening in recent past
- No migration of runoff beyond Caltrans right-of-way observed – condition must continue
- Manganese secondary MCL exceedances in PL2, PL3, PL4 noted but not actionable under primary standards
