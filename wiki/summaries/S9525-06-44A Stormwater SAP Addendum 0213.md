### Document identity
- **Plan type:** Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP) Addendum
- **Preparer / author organization:** Geocon Consultants, Inc.
- **Date:** February 20, 2013
- **Project number or task order:** Project No. S9525-06-44A; Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44; EA No. 10-403500

### Objective and scope
- **Purpose:** To evaluate whether surface water runoff from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles (the Site) is migrating beyond Caltrans’ right-of-way and, if so, whether that stormwater has been impacted by chemicals of potential concern (COPCs) from the stockpiles.
- **What prompted it:** This addendum updates the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* (Shaw Environmental, Inc., January 2006) as requested by Caltrans to address potential runoff from a location (Emerald Avenue shoulder) where soil from Stockpile 2 may have migrated.
- **Area(s), media, and stockpiles/features addressed:**
    - **Area:** Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, located south of the intersection of SR 99 and Kansas Avenue, Stanislaus County, CA. Specifically addresses the area between Stockpiles 1 and 2 along Emerald Avenue.
    - **Media:** Surface water (stormwater runoff and pooled rain water).
    - **Features:** Stockpiles 1, 2, and 3; Emerald Avenue shoulders; right-of-way boundary.
- **What approval or decision the plan supports:** The plan supports obtaining DTSC approval for the proposed surface water sampling approach and subsequent regulatory decisions regarding off-site migration of barium-impacted stormwater.

### Proposed approach
- **Sampling design:**
    - **Locations/counts:** Up to five surface water samples collected from stormwater that has migrated beyond the right-of-way boundary. Samples will be collected from puddles of rainwater pooling along the east and west shoulders of Emerald Avenue between Stockpiles 1 and 2. At least one background sample of rainwater will be taken along Emerald Avenue north or south of the stockpiles. Additional samples may be collected if runoff is observed leaving the right-of-way.
    - **Events:** Collection during up to four separate rainfall events.
    - **Target analytes & methods:**
        - Title 22 total and dissolved metals (including strontium) — EPA Method 6020/7470.
        - Nitrate as nitrogen and sulfate — EPA Method 300.0.
        - Sulfide — Standard Method (SM) 4500.
        - Total suspended solids (TSS) — SM 2540D.
    - **Depths:** Not applicable (surface grab samples of ponded water).
- **Decision criteria or action levels proposed:** Comparison to California Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) and determined site background levels. The specific MCL noted is 1,000 µg/l for barium. The work plan proposes to evaluate whether barium and other target analytes in runoff exceed these thresholds.
- **Sequence, schedule, or phasing:** Field activities (up to four rainfall events) → sample delivery to Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL) → data validation → draft report → final report.

### Deliverables and approvals sought
- **Deliverables:**
    - Draft report summarizing field observations, laboratory data, and data evaluation.
    - Final report (three hard copies and one electronic CD) including:
        - Background summary, scope of services, field observations, results, conclusions, and recommendations.
        - Vicinity maps and site plans showing sampling locations.
        - Tabular summaries of analytical data.
        - Photographs of site conditions and sampling locations.
        - Appendices with laboratory reports and chain-of-custody documentation.
- **Approval sought:** The plan is submitted to the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) for review and to obtain approval from the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
