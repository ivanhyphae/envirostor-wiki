## Document identity
- Document type and purpose: Notice of Availability for the Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment with Finding of No Significant Impact for the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project, and the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. Its purpose is to inform the public of the availability of these documents for review.
- Author or preparer: Haesun Lim, Acting Senior Environmental Planner, Caltrans District 10
- Date or period covered: March 12, 2018 (document date). The Draft EIR/EA with Draft Final RAP was circulated for public review from January 18, 2017, through March 17, 2017.

## Responsible parties
- DEPARTMENT OF TRANSPORTATION (Caltrans District 10): Author/preparer and presumably the project proponent/owner.
- HAESUN LIM: Acting Senior Environmental Planner, Caltrans District 10.
- California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC): Involved in responding to comments for the Draft Final RAP and has an online information repository for Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles.
- Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board: Consulted by DTSC in responding to comments for the Draft Final RAP.

## Key terms and conditions
- The Final EIR/EA with Finding of No Significant Impact and the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Draft Final Remedial Action Plan are available for public review online and at specified physical locations.
- Reviewers requiring arrangements to review documents at the DTSC File Room need to call (916) 255-3758.
- For questions or to request a hardcopy, contact Haesun Lim at (559) 445-6172 or haesun.a.lim@dot.ca.gov.
