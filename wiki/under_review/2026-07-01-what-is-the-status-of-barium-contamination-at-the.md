- **Soil:** Barium‑impacted soil above the site‑specific cleanup goal (10,000 mg/kg residential) was excavated and disposed off‑site during Phase 1 (2019‑2020). Post‑excavation confirmation samples met the required standard (760 mg/kg background threshold). DTSC accepted the Final Removal Action Completion Report, confirming compliance. [[barium#Site record]]

- **Groundwater:** Historical exceedances of the federal MCL (1,000 µg/L) occurred in well MW‑5 (max 1,200 µg/L). However, a 2025 statistical evaluation found all wells below the MCL [[barium-below-mcl#Site Record]]. The 2024 *Updated Comparative Evaluation of Groundwater Data* concluded the encapsulated stockpiles are not and will not impact groundwater, and recommended discontinuation of monitoring and decommissioning wells MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10. This was approved by DTSC and RWQCB. [[updated-comparative-evaluation-of-groundwater-data#Overview]]

- **Current compliance:** No ongoing soil or groundwater corrective actions for barium are pending. The site is in regulatory compliance. [[barium#Regulatory status]]
