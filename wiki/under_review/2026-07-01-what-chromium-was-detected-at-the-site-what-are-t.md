- **Chromium detected**: Total chromium (including hexavalent and trivalent forms) was identified as a chemical of potential concern (COPC) in both soil and groundwater at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site [[chromium]].
- **Soil values**: Up to **760 mg/kg** in stockpile material, reported in the June 2012 groundwater report [[chromium]] (source: S9525‑06‑25).
- **Groundwater values**: Monitoring well **MW‑5** exceeded the California **Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) of 1,000 µg/L** for total chromium. A specific concentration above this MCL is not provided in the source [[chromium]].
- **Regulatory standards**:
  - **Groundwater**: California MCL for total chromium = **1,000 µg/L** (enforced by State Water Board, Division of Drinking Water) [[chromium]].
  - **Soil**: No explicit California Human Health Screening Level (CHHSL) is cited, but the soil value of 760 mg/kg was compared against unspecified screening levels in regulatory correspondence [[chromium]].
- **Health risks**: The Human Health Risk Assessment Update (revised March 2013) evaluated chromium and other COPCs. For current off‑site resident/trespasser exposure scenarios:
  - Cancer risk estimates were **orders of magnitude below 1×10⁻⁶** (e.g., 8×10⁻⁸ for Stockpile 1, 1×10⁻⁷ for Stockpile 2).
  - Noncancer hazard indices were **orders of magnitude below 1** (e.g., 0.04 for Stockpile 1, 0.1 for Stockpile 2).
  - DTSC concluded in December 2009 that the soil stockpiles **do not pose a risk to human health** for Caltrans workers, trespassers, or adjacent residents under current management [[S9525-06-44 HHRA UPDATE Rev.0313]].
- **Note**: The source documents do not provide separate concentration data for hexavalent chromium; chromium is reported as total metal. Ongoing groundwater monitoring is required to confirm trends [[chromium]].
