Based on the provided wiki articles, here are the phases, what has been built, what is planned, and the timeline for the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project.

**Project Overview:**
The project involves constructing a four-lane freeway/expressway along the adopted SR 132 route from near Dakota Avenue to SR 99, plus remediation of three soil stockpiles [[1]].

**Phases and Key Details:**

*   **Phase 1:**
    *   **What is built/planned:** Constructed as described in the project documents. Specific details of what "Phase 1" entails (e.g., which segments) are not broken out in the provided text beyond being part of the two-phase project [[2]].

*   **Phase 2:**
    *   **What is built/planned:** Planned as the second part of the project. Specific details of what "Phase 2" entails are not broken out in the provided text beyond being part of the two-phase project [[2]].

*   **Alternative 2 (Preferred Alternative):**
    *   **What is built/planned:** This is the selected alternative. The project will:
        *   Directly and permanently affect up to 20.8 acres of burrowing owl habitat [[3]].
        *   Result in a total loss of 0.65 acre of seasonal wetlands [[4]].
        *   Remove up to 70 acres of Swainson's hawk foraging habitat and 414 trees [[3]].
        *   Increase impervious surface by 57.5 acres [[5]].
        *   Use the three soil stockpiles as construction materials for the new highway [[4]].
        *   Cause noise levels at 267 receivers to approach or exceed 66 A-weighted decibels [[6]].

**Timeline:**
*   **Document Date:** The Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (FEIR/EA) was finalized and signed on **March 2, 2018** [[2]].
*   **Public Hearing:** A public hearing was held on **Wednesday, February 22, 2017**, from 6:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. at Mark Twain Junior High School in Modesto [[2]].
