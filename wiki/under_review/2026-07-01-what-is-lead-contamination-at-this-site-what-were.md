Based solely on the provided wiki articles:

- **Lead Contamination at Site:** Stockpile 2 soil contains lead at **350 mg/kg**, which exceeds both the site background threshold (80 mg/kg) and the DTSC residential screening level (80 mg/kg) [[wiki/concepts/technical-memorandum-stockpile-2-wall-footing.md]]. In groundwater, lead was **not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than the practical quantitation limit (PQL)** in samples from MW-1 and MW-7 during the May 2016 monitoring event [[wiki/pdf2md/S9800-01-17A Caltrans Modesto Stockpiles GW_06 16/S9800-01-17A Caltrans Modesto Stockpiles GW_06 16.md]].

- **Tested Values:**
    - **Soil (Stockpile 2):** 350 mg/kg [[wiki/concepts/technical-memorandum-stockpile-2-wall-footing.md]].
    - **Groundwater (May 2016):** Lead was below the PQL (not quantified) in both wells MW-1 and MW-7 [[wiki/pdf2md/S9800-01-17A Caltrans Modesto Stockpiles GW_06 16/S9800-01-17A Caltrans Modesto Stockpiles GW_06 16.md]].

- **Regulatory Limits:**
    - **Soil (DTSC CHHSL for residential):** 80 mg/kg [[wiki/concepts/technical-memorandum-stockpile-2-wall-footing.md]].
    - **Groundwater (Safe Drinking Water Act MCL):** 15 µg/L [[wiki/concepts/technical-memorandum-stockpile-2-wall-footing.md]].

- **Human Health Effects:** The provided wiki context does not describe specific human health effects associated with lead exposure.
