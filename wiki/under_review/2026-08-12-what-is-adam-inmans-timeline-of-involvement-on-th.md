Based on the wiki, Adam Inman's involvement timeline on the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project is as follows:

- **February 4, 2025**: Inman informs DTSC that he took over the project "last year" (i.e., sometime in 2024) after the former geologist retired, and states he will conduct an annual inspection in two weeks [[2025 - RE_ Reminder - Annual Inspection Report]].
- **March 19, 2025**: Inman conducts the annual inspection of the Modesto Stockpiles and reports the facility is in good condition with no issues [[2025 - RE_ Reminder - Annual Inspection Report]].
- **June 10, 2025**: Inman reports that slopes are in good condition with no erosion, and notes that re-hydroseeding is planned for the 2025 rainy season [[2025 - RE_ Reminder - Annual Inspection Report]].
- **June 18, 2025**: Inman submits the Well Destruction Report and the Storm Water Sampling Report, both prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. [[adam-inman]].
- **August 25, 2025**: An Interagency Agreement Service Request (SR-04) between Caltrans and DTSC, covering Inman's role as Caltrans Service Request Manager, is executed with a term extending to June 30, 2030 [[adam-inman]].
- **December 11, 2024**: Inman is notified by Dean Wright that Arielle McLeskey would be the new DTSC Project Manager for the project [[dean-wright]].
- **December 24, 2025**: Inman receives a cost estimate letter for FY 2025-2026 regarding DTSC oversight costs [[Annual Cost Estimate Letter - FY 25.26 - State Route 132 (900259)]].
- **January 30, 2026**: DTSC issues a conditional approval letter for the Well Destruction Report and approval for the Storm Water and Surface Water Monitoring Report (FY24/25) [[adam-inman]].
- **March 16, 2026**: Arielle McLeskey inquires if Inman has sent the revised Well Destruction Completion Report, noting a single correction was needed per the January 30, 2026 conditional approval [[adam-inman]].
