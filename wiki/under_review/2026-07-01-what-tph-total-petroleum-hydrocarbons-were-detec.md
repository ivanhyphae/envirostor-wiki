- **TPH detected:**
  - **Diesel-range (TPH-d):** up to 2,300 mg/kg (perimeter boring A‑P2; 2‑4 ft), 1,300 mg/kg (3530 Maze Blvd stockpile), 450 mg/kg (fenceline boring FB‑12).
  - **Motor oil (TPH-mo):** up to 6,700 mg/kg (3530 Maze Blvd stockpile).
  - **Gasoline-range (TPH-g):** up to 2,800 mg/kg (3530 Maze Blvd stockpile).
  - **Other:** up to 1,100 mg/kg TPH‑d at 20‑22 ft (perimeter boring C‑P4).

- **Values compared to limits:**
  - **DTSC low-risk closure level** for TPH‑d in soil: 100 mg/kg – exceeded at FB‑12 (450 mg/kg) and other locations.
  - **California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs):** ~100 mg/kg (residential), ~500 mg/kg (industrial) – site‑specific exceedances occurred for all TPH fractions.
  - **Risk‑based screening levels (RBSLs)** for worker inhalation were exceeded for TPH‑d, TPH‑mo, ethylbenzene, and xylenes at 3530 Maze Blvd.
  - **Groundwater action level** for TPH‑d: 100 µg/L – grab samples from perimeter borings were below this, but no permanent monitoring wells yet installed.

- **Health risks:**
  - TPH‑d and TPH‑mo can cause **cancer and non‑cancer effects** (e.g., neurological, hepatic, renal) via ingestion, inhalation, or dermal contact.
  - **Direct exposure** at concentrations above CHHSLs or RBSLs poses unacceptable risk to residential or construction workers.
  - **Leaching to groundwater** could lead to drinking water exposure, though no groundwater exceedances have been confirmed at the fenceline.

- **Strontium:**
  - **No strontium data** are reported in any of the provided wiki articles. No detections, limits, or health risks for strontium can be cited from the available context.
