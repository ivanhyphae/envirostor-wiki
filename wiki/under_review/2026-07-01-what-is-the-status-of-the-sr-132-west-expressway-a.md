Based on the provided wiki articles (dated 2018 and 2021), the status of the SR 132 West Expressway as of 2025/2026 is not directly updated. However, the documents specify planned completion dates and the scope of each phase:

- **Phase 1 (Interim) Construction**: Was scheduled for completion and opening to traffic in **September 2022** [[Draft Interim RACR, Section 1.0, page 1]].
- **Phase 2 (Ultimate) Construction**: Scheduled for completion in **2028** [[Draft Interim RACR, Section 1.0, page 1]].
- **Sections built in Phase 1**: Two of four planned traffic lanes from **east of SR 99 to North Dakota Avenue**, including related highway bridges and interchanges [[Draft Interim RACR, Section 1.0, page 1]].
- **Overall project alignment**: From near **Dakota Avenue to east of SR 99** (post miles 11.0 to 15.0 in Modesto) [[FEIR/EA, page 1]].
- **Remediation work**: Consolidation and capping of barium-containing soil stockpiles were completed as part of Phase 1 [[Draft Interim RACR, Section 3.0]].

No information is provided on whether Phase 1 was actually completed or if any additional sections have been built past 2022.
