Based solely on the provided context, the following entities are accountable for liability related to the chemicals from the FMC (Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles) site:

- **Covenantor (California Department of Transportation / Caltrans):** As the current owner of the property, Caltrans is bound by the Land Use Covenant. The covenant states that Owners shall continue to be liable for any violations of, or non-compliance with, the Environmental Restrictions during their ownership [[Modesto Soil Stockpiles_ Final Recorded LUC, page 3, Article III, 3.2]].
- **Future Owners/Occupants:** Successive owners and occupants are bound by the covenant and are liable for violations or non-compliance during their respective periods of ownership or occupancy [[Modesto Soil Stockpiles_ Final Recorded LUC, page 3, Article III, 3.2]].
- **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC):** DTSC has the authority to enforce the covenant and pursue administrative, civil, or criminal actions for violations [[Modesto Soil Stockpiles_ Final Recorded LUC, page 4, Article V, 5.1]]. They also have the right to require modification or removal of any improvements constructed in violation of the covenant [[Modesto Soil Stockpiles_ Final Recorded LUC, page 4, Article V, 5.1]].
- **Responsible Party (for waste materials):** During the well destruction activities, PeneCore Drilling was retained to destroy the wells and transported waste materials for disposal [[S2350-01-02 Well Destruction Report_6.25, page 2]]. The permit requirements also place responsibility on the permit holder for removing soil cuttings and purge water [[S2350-01-02 Well Destruction Report_6.25, page 8-9]].
