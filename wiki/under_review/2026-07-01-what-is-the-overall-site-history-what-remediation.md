### Overall Site History

- **Location**: Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles along State Route 132, Stanislaus County, CA. Site code 900259. [[mw-1]]
- **Primary contaminant**: Total mercury (laboratory designation **Mw 1**) in soil and groundwater. [[mw-1]]
- **Stockpile origins**: Soil generated during highway construction, stored in two stockpiles (Stockpile 1 west of Emerald Avenue, Stockpile 2 between Emerald Avenue and SR 99). [[Final O and M Agreement with Cover Letter (900259)]]
- **Phase I soil sampling (2019–2020)**: Total mercury ranged from 0.28 mg/kg to **760 mg/kg** (northwest stockpile, sample SP-NW-02 at 0–1 ft). [[mw-1]]
- **Groundwater monitoring well MW-5 (June 2012)**: Initial total mercury up to **1,200 µg/L** (flagged as outlier). Later quarterly monitoring (2020–2023) after removal of high-mercury soils showed 0.8–4.2 µg/L. [[mw-1]]
- **Regulatory oversight**: DTSC (lead), with RWQCB concurrence on groundwater. [[mw-1]] [[dtsc]]
- **Remedy approved**: Consolidation and clean fill capping of barium-containing soil (BCS) as highway embankment fill for SR 132 West Expressway. [[Final O and M Agreement with Cover Letter (900259)]]

### Remediation Completed

- **Soil excavation and verification**:
  - High-mercury stockpile soils excavated.
  - Phase II verification sampling (2021–2022) showed total mercury **below site-specific RAL of 100 mg/kg** in all but two locations; those were re‑excavated and re‑sampled with results **≤ 15 mg/kg**. [[mw-1]]
- **Remedy installation**:
  - Consolidation and clean fill capping of barium-containing soil as highway embankment fill. [[Final O and M Agreement with Cover Letter (900259)]]
- **Regulatory approvals**:
  - Remedial Action Completion Report approved **January 18, 2023**. [[Final O and M Agreement with Cover Letter (900259)]]
  - DTSC approved soil closure **November 2023**, subject to permanent land use controls. [[mw-1]]
- **Operation and Maintenance Agreement (June 2023)** : Formalizes ongoing inspections, monitoring, and five-year reviews. [[Final O and M Agreement with Cover Letter (900259)]]

### Current Site Status

- **Soil**: Presumptive compliance with DTSC‑approved RAL of 100 mg/kg. [[mw-1]]
- **Groundwater**: Post‑remediation data (2021–2023) show total mercury in MW‑5 mostly **below MCL of 2 µg/L** (except Q2 2022: 3.1 µg/L due to seasonal fluctuation). No MCL exceedances in downgradient wells MW‑6 or MW‑7. [[mw-1]]
- **Regulatory status**:
  - RWQCB has **not issued a formal closure letter** for groundwater. [[mw-1]]
  - DTSC soil closure (Nov 2023) is effective only after deed restriction is recorded with Stanislaus County – **not yet recorded**. [[mw-1]]
- **Open items**:
  - **Statistical outlier evaluation**: The 1,200 µg/L reading from MW‑5 (June 2012) excluded from trend analysis; DTSC/RWQCB acceptance pending. Supplemental groundwater sampling recommended. [[mw-1]]
  - **Deed restriction**: Drafted but not recorded. [[mw-1]]
  - **Monitoring well decommissioning**: MW‑5, MW‑6, MW‑7, MW‑8 not decommissioned pending RWQCB decision on ongoing monitoring. [[mw-1]]
  - **Groundwater arsenic issue** (from separate DTSC context): Arsenic slightly above MCL (8.2–8.9 µg/L) but trending downward; DTSC awaiting Q3 2023 monitoring report. [[dtsc]]
- **Five‑year reviews**: Required by O&M Agreement, first due in 2028 (five years from Jan 2023 completion report approval). [[Final O and M Agreement with Cover Letter (900259)]]
