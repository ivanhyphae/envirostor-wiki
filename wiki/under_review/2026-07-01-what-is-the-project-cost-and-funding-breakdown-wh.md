- **Total project cost**: \$211 million to \$214 million (range reflects Alternative 1 vs Alternative 2; includes \$1.57 million for soil stockpile remediation) [[SR_132_DEIR_EA.md#source]].  
- **Phase 1 funding** (programmed, fiscal years 2018/2019):  
  - Estimated cost: **\$82 million** (escalated to 2018 dollars).  
  - Sources: Regional Improvement Program (RIP), Transportation Congestion Relief Program (TCRP), Federal Demonstration Program (DEMO), Stanislaus County's share of Regional Surface Transportation Program (RSTP), and other local funds from City of Modesto and Stanislaus County [[SR_132_DEIR_EA.md#source]].  
- **Phase 2 funding** (not yet programmed):  
  - Estimated cost: **\$128 million to \$132 million** (based on 2016 dollars).  
  - No specific funding sources identified for Phase 2; only Phase 1 has programmed funding [[SR_132_DEIR_EA.md#source]].  
- **Phase 2 start date**: Construction was planned to begin in **2026** [[SR_132_DEIR_EA.md#source]].
