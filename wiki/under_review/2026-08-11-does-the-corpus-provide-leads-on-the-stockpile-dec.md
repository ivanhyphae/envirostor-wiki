Based on the provided corpus, here are the leads on the stockpile decommission air monitoring dataset archive or availability:

- **2012 Stockpile 3 Excavation:** The corpus explicitly states that air monitoring was completed during the 2012 remedial excavation of Stockpile 3 (part of the SR-99 off-ramp rehabilitation). Results indicated that airborne levels of barium and lead were "well below regulatory thresholds" [[S1200-01-01 Caltrans Modesto Stockpile RDIP_01.19]].
- **Air Monitoring Worksheet:** The "Remedial Design Implementation Plan - Interim Phase 1" document includes an "Air Monitoring Worksheet" as Appendix G, indicating that an air monitoring plan exists for the project activities [[S1200-01-01 Caltrans Modesto Stockpile RDIP_01.19]].
- **Regulatory Oversight:** The work was conducted under the oversight of the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB). The archive of monitoring data would likely be maintained by these agencies or by Caltrans as the responsible party [[S1200-01-01 Caltrans Modesto Stockpile RDIP_01.19]].
- **No Centralized Dataset Mentioned:** The provided documents do not include a specific "air monitoring dataset" or a direct link to an online archive. The information suggests the data exists in project-specific reports (e.g., the 2012 excavation report) and regulatory files.

**Summary:**
- **Data exists** for the 2012 Stockpile 3 removal.
- **Plans exist** for future interim phase air monitoring (Appendix G).
- **Archive location** is not specified but is likely with **DTSC**, the **CVRWQCB**, or **Caltrans District 6** as part of the project record.
