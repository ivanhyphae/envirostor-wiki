Based on the provided wiki articles, here are the findings for manganese, nitrate, and PAH contamination at the site:

**Manganese**
- **Detected Values:** Manganese has been "sporadically reported for various wells at concentrations exceeding the secondary MCL" but has not been consistently elevated for any one well [[S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles GW April 2017_07.17]].
- **Regulatory Limits:** Secondary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for drinking water (specific value not listed in provided text) [[S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles GW April 2017_07.17]].
- **Health Concerns:** Manganese is regulated under a secondary MCL (taste and odor) [[S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles GW April 2017_07.17]].

**Nitrate**
- **Detected Values:** Nitrate (as nitrogen) has been "consistently reported at concentrations that exceed their respective... primary or secondary MCLs" in wells MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 [[S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles GW April 2017_07.17]].
- **Regulatory Limits:** Primary MCL of 10 milligrams per liter (mg/l) [[S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles GW April 2017_07.17]].
- **Health Concerns:** Nitrate is regulated under a primary MCL (drinking water standard for human health).

**PAH (Polycyclic Aromatic Hydrocarbons)**
- **Detected Values:** "No polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) reported for each of the samples analyzed" [[S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles GW April 2017_07.17]].
- **Regulatory Limits:** Not applicable (none detected).
- **Health Concerns:** Based on the consistent lack of detection, PAH analysis was discontinued after the November 2012 sampling event with concurrence from the DTSC [[S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles GW April 2017_07.17]].
