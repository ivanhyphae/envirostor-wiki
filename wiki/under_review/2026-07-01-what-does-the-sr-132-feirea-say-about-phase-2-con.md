Based solely on the provided wiki articles, there is **no mention of "Phase 2" construction** for the SR 132 project.

- The entire FEIR/EA document describes a single project: constructing a four-lane freeway/expressway from Dakota Avenue to east of SR 99. No phasing (Phase 1, Phase 2, etc.) is identified. [[1. SR_132_FEIR_EA_ 20180302 FINAL Main Doc]]
- The Remedial Action Plan (RAP) discusses only the containment of three soil stockpiles (Alternative 4) as part of the overall SR 132 West project, not a separate "Phase 2." [[Response to Comments letter for SR 132 RAP with Appendix J]]
- Since no Phase 2 is identified in the provided context, there is **no information available** on its scope, timeline, funding, or whether it has been funded or started.
