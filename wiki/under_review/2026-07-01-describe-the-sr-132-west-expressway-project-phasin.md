- **Phasing**: The project is identified as having **Phase 1 and Phase 2**, both with projected costs and funding sources [[HearingSummaryReport_SR132 6.21.17 rdcd sz#2.2.7 Project Funding and Costs]]. The specific scope of each phase (e.g., which segments or features) is not defined in the provided documents.

- **Construction timeline**: No explicit construction start or end dates are given in the wiki excerpts. The project schedule display board existed at the 2017 public hearing [[HearingSummaryReport_SR132 6.21.17 rdcd sz#2.2.8 Project Schedule]], but its content is not transcribed. The FEIR/EA and FONSI were finalized in March 2018 [[1. SR_132_FEIR_EA_ 20180302 FINAL Main Doc#FONSI]] – construction had not yet occurred at that time.

- **Completed work**: As of the 2017–2018 documents, **no construction had been completed**. The project was in the environmental review and planning phase (Draft EIR/EA, public hearing, Record of Decision). Only design, environmental studies, and public outreach had been performed.

- **Remaining to be built**: The full project remains to be constructed:
  - A **4‑lane freeway/expressway** along SR 132 from near Dakota Avenue to SR 99 (post miles 11.0–15.0) [[1. SR_132_FEIR_EA_ 20180302 FINAL Main Doc#page1]].
  - **Remediation of three Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles** via containment/encapsulation as construction material, as described in the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) [[1. SR_132_FEIR_EA_ 20180302 FINAL Main Doc#materials management]].
  - Implementation of mitigation measures: noise barriers (e.g., Barrier D), drainage improvements, burrowing owl protection, paleontological monitoring, etc. [[1. SR_132_FEIR_EA_ 20180302 FINAL Main Doc#Findings]].
  - All right-of-way acquisition, drainage structures, and environmental permits.
