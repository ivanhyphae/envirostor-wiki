Based on the provided wiki articles, the available information on beryllium and cadmium contamination at the SR 132 site is limited. The articles do not provide specific detected concentrations or detailed human health risk quantification for either metal, only contextual details.

### Beryllium Contamination
- **Detected Concentrations:** Beryllium was listed as a target analyte in Phase 2 fenceline borings (FB-1 through FB-14), but **no specific detected concentration values** are reported in the provided excerpts.
- **Regulatory Thresholds:** No beryllium-specific screening levels or exceedances are cited in the provided text.
- **Human Health Risks:** No human health risk information for beryllium is given; it is only noted as one of several metals analyzed during site characterization [[fenceline-borings]].

### Cadmium Contamination
- **Detected Concentrations:** Cadmium was analyzed in dedicated borings (CB‑01 through CB‑06) and as part of the metals suite in fenceline borings. However, **specific numerical concentrations (mg/kg) are not reproduced** in the provided excerpts. The report tables (e.g., Table 4‑2) contain the actual data [[cadmium-borings]].
- **Regulatory Thresholds:** 
  - Cadmium concentrations in several borings **exceeded the DTSC residential California Human Health Screening Level (CHHSL)** but were **below or near the industrial screening level** [[cadmium-borings]].
  - Applicable benchmarks include DTSC CHHSLs, Caltrans project-specific screening levels, and a groundwater MCL of 5 µg/L if leaching is a concern [[cadmium-borings]].
- **Human Health Risks:** 
  - A **human health risk assessment (HHRA) using the SSI cadmium data (including bioavailability adjustment) is listed as a required deliverable** and had not been completed at the time of the SSI Report [[cadmium-borings]].
  - An earlier HHRA update (2007, updated in 2014 Feasibility Study) evaluated current and future exposure scenarios for the stockpiles (which contain cadmium), but that document is not provided in detail here; it concluded that remedial action objectives were needed to address risks [[SR_132_DEIR_EA_Append_G-J]].
  - No off‑site migration of contaminants (including cadmium) beyond the stockpile boundaries was confirmed based on fenceline boring data, indicating that human health risks are likely confined to the on‑site stockpile area [[fenceline-borings]].

**Note:** Specific risk numbers (e.g., cancer risk, hazard index) are not available in the provided excerpts; the site is still in the investigation and planning phase, with a Remedial Action Plan pending further data evaluation [[cadmium-borings]].
