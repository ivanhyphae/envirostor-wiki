Based on the provided wiki articles, the dust mitigation process during the decommission of Stockpile 3 consisted of **pre-soaking and water spray** during excavation and loading activities. This was provided by the Caltrans contractor [[removal-remedial-action]].

Air monitoring conducted during this excavation indicated that airborne levels of lead and barium were well below levels of concern [[removal-remedial-action]].
