# thallium-detected-in-stockpile-soil

Analysis of 278 soil samples from the Modesto Soil Stockpiles, as reported in the June 2014 Final FS Report, detected thallium at low concentrations. This contrasts with antimony, selenium, and silver, which were not detected in any of the analyzed soil samples. Existing documentation prior to this finding (e.g., a March 28, 2023 surface water letter) indicated thallium was not detected in groundwater and pre-2023 surface water samples, suggesting the December 2023 stormwater event was the first reported instance of an MCL exceedance for this contaminant at the site.
