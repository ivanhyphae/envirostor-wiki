# thallium-exceeds-mcl-in-stormwater

Stormwater sampling conducted in December 2023 at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles / SR 132 site revealed that thallium concentrations exceeded the Primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL). This exceedance was reported in both the February 2024 and February 2025 stormwater sampling reports. Prior to this event, stormwater sampling in March 2023 did not report thallium above MCLs. No follow-up resampling or investigations related to this specific exceedance have been documented in the provided reports.
