## Hazardous Waste

### Overview
This section details the findings and actions related to hazardous waste as documented in the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remedial Action Plan (RAP). The primary concern at the site has been the presence of barium and lead in soil stockpiles. Previous removal actions and analyses have been conducted to assess and manage these substances.

### Site Record
During the rehabilitation of the off-ramp to Kansas Avenue, a portion of Stockpile 3 was excavated and disposed of offsite. This excavation involved approximately 2,800 yd³ of soil between September 7 and 26, 2012. The excavated material was transported to the Forward Class II landfill facility in Manteca, California, under non-hazardous waste manifests.

Barium was detected in soil samples from Stockpile 3 at concentrations ranging from 34 to 1,600 mg/kg. Soluble barium analysis (WET and DI-WET) of 33 stockpile soil samples showed concentrations ranging from 39 to 2,300 mg/l for WET and 1.8 to 220 mg/l for DI-WET. Twenty-eight of the WET soluble barium results exceeded the Title 22 California Code of Regulations (CCR) Soluble Threshold Limit Concentration (STLC) for barium of 100 mg/l. Nine of the DI-WET soluble barium results exceeded the STLC.

Only two stockpile soil samples contained total lead concentrations exceeding the California hazardous waste threshold of 50 mg/kg, with concentrations of 150 and 1,500 mg/kg. WET soluble lead was detected at 2.9 and 5.7 mg/l, and DI-WET soluble lead at 0.07 and 0.1 mg/l in these samples.

Fenceline boring soil samples showed barium concentrations ranging from 140 to 4,300 mg/kg for surface samples and 42 to 680 mg/kg for the deepest samples. Surface soil samples collected from five borings along the north side of Stockpile 2 adjacent to commercial/industrial development contained the highest barium concentrations greater than 1,000 mg/kg.

Air monitoring during Stockpile 3 excavation and loading activities indicated that airborne levels of lead and barium were well below levels of concern. The fence line total dust action level was 4.0 milligrams per cubic meter (mg/m³).

### Regulatory Status
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) determined that offsite management of soil from Stockpile 3 did not pose a threat to human health or the environment in a letter dated August 30, 2012. Based on this data and review by DTSC, the excavated soil stockpile materials were designated for offsite disposal as non-hazardous soil to an accepting licensed landfill facility.

The Title 22 California Code of Regulations (CCR) Soluble Threshold Limit Concentration (STLC) for barium is 100 mg/l. The California hazardous waste threshold for requiring WET soluble testing for lead is 50 mg/kg.

A "No action" alternative would not meet State or Federal regulations with respect to hazardous waste levels of COPCs in soil on the Site because of the lack of site control and public notification.
