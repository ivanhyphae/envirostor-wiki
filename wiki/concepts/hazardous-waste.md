## Hazardous Waste

### Overview
This section details the findings and actions related to hazardous waste as documented in the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remedial Action Plan (RAP). The primary concern at the site has been the presence of barium and lead in soil stockpiles. Previous removal actions and analyses have been conducted to assess and manage these substances.

### Site Record
During the rehabilitation of the off-ramp to Kansas Avenue, a portion of Stockpile 3 was excavated and disposed of offsite. This excavation involved approximately 2,800 yd³ of soil between September 7 and 26, 2012. {{quote: "Approximately 2,800 yd3 of the Stockpile 3 soil embankment were excavated over ten days between September 7 and 26, 2012."}} The excavated material was transported to the Forward Class II landfill facility in Manteca, California, under non-hazardous waste manifests. {{quote: "The excavated stockpile material was directly loaded into covered trucks for transport to the Forward Class II landfill facility in Manteca, California, under non-hazardous waste manifests."}}

Barium was detected in soil samples from Stockpile 3 at concentrations ranging from 34 to 1,600 mg/kg. {{quote: "Barium was detected in each sample at concentrations ranging from 34 to 1,600 mg/kg, all less than the residential and commercial/industrial CHHSLs for barium of 5,200 and 63,000 mg/kg, respectively."}} Soluble barium analysis (WET and DI-WET) of 33 stockpile soil samples showed concentrations ranging from 39 to 2,300 mg/l for WET and 1.8 to 220 mg/l for DI-WET. {{quote: "Soluble barium concentrations ranged from 39 to 2,300 milligrams per liter (mg/l), 28 of which exceeded the Title 22 California Code of Regulations (CCR) Soluble Threshold Limit Concentration (STLC) for barium of 100 mg/l. Soluble (DI-WET) barium concentrations ranged from 1.8 to 220 mg/l, nine of which exceeded the STLC."}}

Only two stockpile soil samples contained total lead concentrations exceeding the California hazardous waste threshold of 50 mg/kg, with concentrations of 150 and 1,500 mg/kg. {{quote: "Only two stockpile soil samples contained total lead concentrations exceeding 50 mg/kg (California hazardous waste threshold for requiring WET soluble testing) at concentrations of 150 and 1,500 mg/kg."}} WET soluble lead was detected at 2.9 and 5.7 mg/l, and DI-WET soluble lead at 0.07 and 0.1 mg/l in these samples. {{quote: "WET soluble lead was detected in these two samples at 2.9 and 5.7 mg/l, respectively, and DI-WET soluble lead at 0.07 and 0.1 mg/l, respectively."}}

Fenceline boring soil samples showed barium concentrations ranging from 140 to 4,300 mg/kg for surface samples and 42 to 680 mg/kg for the deepest samples. {{quote: "Barium was detected in each soil sample at concentrations ranging from 140 to 4,300 mg/kg for the surface soil samples and 42 to 680 mg/kg for the deepest soil sample obtained from the Fenceline Borings."}} Surface soil samples from borings along the north side of Stockpile 2 had barium concentrations greater than 1,000 mg/kg. {{quote: "Surface soil samples collected from five borings located along the north side of Stockpile 2 adjacent to commercial/industrial development contained the highest barium concentrations greater than 1,000 mg/kg."}}

Air monitoring during Stockpile 3 excavation and loading activities indicated that airborne levels of lead and barium were well below levels of concern. {{quote: "Direct read (pDR-1200) and laboratory air sample results for the project indicated that airborne levels of lead and barium were well below levels of concern during excavation activities at Stockpile 3."}} The fence line total dust action level was 4.0 milligrams per cubic meter (mg/m³). {{quote: "Verifying that downwind direct-read, real-time particulate counter readings (pDR-1200 monitors) did not exceed the Fence Line Total Dust Action Level of 4.0 milligrams per cubic meter (mg/m³ )."}}

### Regulatory Status
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) determined that offsite management of soil from Stockpile 3 did not pose a threat to human health or the environment in a letter dated August 30, 2012. {{quote: "The DTSC conveyed their finding that offsite management of the soil from Stockpile 3 did not pose a threat to human health or the environment in a letter dated August 30, 2012."}} The excavated soil stockpile materials were designated for offsite disposal as non-hazardous soil. {{quote: "Based on this data, data previously presented in the PEA, and review by DTSC, the excavated soil stockpile materials were designated for offsite disposal as non-hazardous soil to an accepting licensed landfill facility."}}

The Title 22 California Code of Regulations (CCR) Soluble Threshold Limit Concentration (STLC) for barium is 100 mg/l. {{quote: "Soluble barium concentrations ranged from 39 to 2,300 milligrams per liter (mg/l), 28 of which exceeded the Title 22 California Code of Regulations (CCR) Soluble Threshold Limit Concentration (STLC) for barium of 100 mg/l."}} The California hazardous waste threshold for requiring WET soluble testing for lead is 50 mg/kg. {{quote: "Only two stockpile soil samples contained total lead concentrations exceeding 50 mg/kg (California hazardous waste threshold for requiring WET soluble testing) at concentrations of 150 and 1,500 mg/kg."}}

A "No action" alternative would not meet State or Federal regulations with respect to hazardous waste levels of COPCs in soil on the Site. {{quote: "This alternative would not meet State or Federal regulations with respect to hazardous waste levels of COPCs in soil on the Site because of the lack of site control and public notification."}}
