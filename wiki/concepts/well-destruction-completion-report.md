## Overview

A **Well Destruction Completion Report** is a formal document required by regulatory agencies (primarily the California Department of Water Resources and local county environmental health departments) to certify the proper abandonment of groundwater monitoring or extraction wells. For the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA), this report confirms that specific monitoring wells were destroyed in compliance with California Well Destruction Standards (California Water Code Sections 13700–13801) and project-specific closure requirements, thereby eliminating potential migration pathways for contaminants—particularly hexavalent chromium and other metals—from on-site stockpiles into deeper aquifers.

## Site record

The DTSC Letter Response - 2025 GW Monitoring Report (dated April 2025) documents that well destruction activities were completed for four monitoring wells within the project boundary: MW-5, MW-6, MW-7, and MW-8. These wells were installed during Phase 1 investigative work (2019–2020) to track hexavalent chromium and total chromium concentrations in shallow groundwater beneath the stockpile staging areas near SR 132 in Stanislaus County. The destruction report, submitted to DTSC on March 15, 2025, contained:

- **Well construction logs** for each well, including casing materials (PVC, 2-inch diameter), screened intervals (ranging from 15 to 30 feet below ground surface), and annular seal details.
- **Destruction methods**: Each well was overdrilled using a hollow-stem auger to a depth of 5 feet below the original screened interval, then grouted with Portland cement-bentonite slurry (18% solids by weight) from total depth to 2 feet below grade. Surface completion used a 6-inch concrete cap.
- **Post-destruction verification**: Soil samples collected from the borehole annulus during overdrilling at MW-5 and MW-7 showed hexavalent chromium concentrations of 0.12 mg/kg and 0.09 mg/kg, respectively—both below the DTSC-approved action level of 0.5 mg/kg for this site. No exceedances were observed.
- **Photographic documentation**: Included in the appendix with time-stamped images of each destruction step.

The report was reviewed by DTSC via the April 2025 letter response, which accepted the well destruction as complete and compliant with Conditions of Approval (COA) #14 for the site’s Remedial Action Plan (RAP).

## Regulatory status

DTSC (California Department of Toxic Substances Control) retains regulatory oversight of all well destruction activities under the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA) corrective action process and the site’s voluntary cleanup agreement. The applicable standards include:

- **California Water Code Section 13801**: Mandates that well destruction must prevent the vertical movement of water or contaminants through the well bore.
- **DTSC-approved Site-Specific Well Destruction Protocol** (dated December 2023): Required abandonment grout with a minimum compressive strength of 150 psi at 28 days.
- **Stanislaus County Environmental Health Department Approval**: Obtained prior to destruction, with the county issuing a no-objection letter on February 20, 2025.

The Well Destruction Completion Report satisfies COA #14, which was a condition for closure of the monitoring well network. No current violations were cited by DTSC in the April 2025 response; the report is deemed **compliant** with all applicable regulatory requirements.

## Open items

- **No unresolved items** related to well destruction were identified by DTSC in the April 2025 letter response. However, the letter notes that DTSC reserves the right to request additional documentation if future groundwater monitoring (per the 2025 GW Monitoring Plan) detects new exceedances at nearby wells MW-2 or MW-3, which were not destroyed.
- The report closed out all obligations under COA #14.
- A subsequent **Well Destruction Completion Report** for three additional wells (MW-1, MW-2, MW-3) is anticipated as part of Phase 2 site closure, with a draft deadline of Q4 2025 per DTSC correspondence.
