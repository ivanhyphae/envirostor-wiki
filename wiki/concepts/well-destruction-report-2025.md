## Overview

This report documents the destruction of four groundwater monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, and MW10) at the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles site in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. Prepared under Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2, EA No. 10-1E7003, the report was submitted to Caltrans District 6 Hazardous Waste Branch on June 18, 2025 (Project No. S2350-01-02). The well destruction was approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) on May 3, 2024, following an updated comparative evaluation demonstrating that the encapsulated soil stockpiles are not impacting groundwater. The Geotracker case number is SL0609924194.

## Site Record

- **Location:** South of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California.
- **Stockpiles:** Three stockpiles: Stockpiles 1 and 2 are encapsulated beneath SR 132; Stockpile 3 was removed and placed at Stockpiles 1 and 2.
- **Previous well destruction (2019):** Six wells (MW1, MW2, MW3, MW5, MW7, MW8) were destroyed in 2019 due to conflicts with Caltrans highway construction.
- **2025 well destruction:** Four wells destroyed: MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10.
- **Basis for destruction:** A 2014 comparative evaluation of groundwater data was updated in 2024. The update showed the encapsulated soil stockpiles do not have the potential to impact groundwater, the water table is declining, and future monitoring data from the four remaining wells would provide no useful data for further analysis. Phase II construction for SR 132 (scheduled to begin in 2026) will fully encapsulate remaining stockpiles beneath highway pavement and perimeter retaining walls.
- **Regulatory approval:** DTSC and Central Valley RWQCB concurred (May 3, 2024) that the encapsulated soil is not impacting groundwater, and approved destruction of MW4, MW6, MW9, and MW10.

## Regulatory Status

- **Applicable agencies:** DTSC (lead for hazardous waste), Central Valley RWQCB (water quality).
- **Decision (May 3, 2024):** Both agencies agreed that the encapsulated soil is not affecting groundwater and approved decommissioning of the four remaining wells.
- **Compliance:** Project is in compliance with agency conditions; no further groundwater monitoring is required for the soil stockpiles.

## Open Items

No open items are documented in this report. Phase II construction of SR 132 is scheduled to begin in 2026, but no further monitoring or well-related actions are identified.
