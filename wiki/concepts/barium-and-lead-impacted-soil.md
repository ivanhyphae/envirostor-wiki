## Barium And Lead Impacted Soil

### Overview
This section addresses soil impacted by barium and lead at the SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA site. These impacts are primarily associated with [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]], specifically [[stockpile-1]]. The remediation strategy involved encapsulating the impacted soil.

### Site Record
Soil impacted by barium and lead was identified at the site. Specifically, [[stockpile-1]] contained soil with barium and lead. The Approval of GW Statistical Evaluation (900259) document, dated October 26, 2021, references the statistical evaluation of groundwater data in relation to these impacted soils. The approval of this evaluation by the relevant agency indicates that the groundwater monitoring data, in the context of the impacted soil stockpiles, met certain criteria, leading to decisions regarding further groundwater monitoring.

### Regulatory Status
The Approval of GW Statistical Evaluation (900259) document, dated October 26, 2021, indicates that the groundwater statistical evaluation was approved. This approval implies that, based on the data presented and evaluated, there was a determination of [[no-impact-to-groundwater]] from the barium and lead impacted soil stockpiles. This decision likely allows for the [[discontinue-groundwater-monitoring]] at certain locations or under certain conditions, as outlined in the approval.
