The Final Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan, submitted by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] to the [[dtsc]], outlined the proposed scope of work for further investigation at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This deliverable was a critical step in addressing regulatory comments and guiding subsequent site characterization activities.

## Workplan Development and Submission

The Final Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan was submitted on December 19, 2006. It was developed specifically in response to comments received from the [[dtsc]] on a previously submitted workplan. The objective was to refine the investigation strategy based on regulatory feedback.
