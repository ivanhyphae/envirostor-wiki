## Final Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan

The Final Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan was submitted by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] to the [[dtsc]] for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This workplan detailed the proposed scope of work for a supplemental site investigation.

## Site Record

The Final Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan was submitted on December 19, 2006. It was developed in response to comments from the [[dtsc]] on a previous workplan.

## Regulatory Status

The Final Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan was a step in the regulatory process for the remediation of the soil stockpiles.

## Open Items

The Final Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan was developed to address comments from the [[dtsc]] and guide further investigation activities.
