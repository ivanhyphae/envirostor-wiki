The [[dtsc-approved-sampling-plan]] refers to the protocols established for surface water sampling activities at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. These protocols were approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).

## Site record

Surface water sampling activities conducted on January 6, 2016, were performed in general accordance with the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)*, prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. and dated January 2006. An addendum to this plan, dated February 20, 2013, was also followed. These documents established the approved sampling and analysis protocols for the project.
