The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) issued a conditional approval for the Well Destruction Report for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. This decision signifies that the well destruction activities are proceeding under regulatory oversight, with final acceptance contingent on Caltrans addressing specific comments.

### DTSC Conditional Approval of Well Destruction Report

On January 30, 2026, DTSC, through Project Manager Arielle McLeskey, issued a conditional approval for the Well Destruction Report, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of Caltrans. This approval was specifically for the report dated June 18, 2025, concerning the decommissioning of four remaining groundwater monitoring wells at the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles site, located south of the intersection of State Route 99 and Kansas Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County. DTSC also approved the Storm Water Sampling Report (FY24/25), also dated June 18, 2025.

The site historically contained soil contaminated with barium, with three stockpiles managed under a Remedial Action Plan. Ten groundwater monitoring wells were installed in 2012 to monitor for potential contamination migration. Six of these wells were decommissioned in 2019 to facilitate highway construction. The Well Destruction Report details the procedures for decommissioning the remaining four wells. The Storm Water Sampling Report evaluated the integrity of the stockpile containment system during qualifying rain events, with results reported within acceptable limits.

### Approval Conditions

DTSC's conditional approval of the Well Destruction Report was subject to one comment from DTSC's Geological Services Branch (GSB). This comment, along with the need to incorporate it into the final version of the report and provide it to DTSC, was a prerequisite for final approval. The DTSC's letter also referenced comments made on December 19, 2024, regarding the 2025 Groundwater Monitoring Report, indicating that satisfactory resolution of these comments was also necessary.
