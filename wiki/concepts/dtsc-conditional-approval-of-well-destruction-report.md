## DTSC Conditional Approval of Well Destruction Report

### Overview
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) provided conditional approval for the Well Destruction Report for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. This approval was contingent upon the satisfactory completion of specific actions outlined by the DTSC.

### Site Record
The DTSC issued a letter on January 23, 2025, conditionally approving the final version of the Well Destruction Report. This approval was subject to the condition that Caltrans address the comments provided in the DTSC's letter dated December 19, 2024, regarding the 2025 Groundwater Monitoring Report.

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC's conditional approval indicates that the well destruction activities are proceeding under regulatory oversight. The final acceptance of the report is dependent on Caltrans' response to DTSC's comments on the groundwater monitoring data.

### Open Items
Caltrans must address the comments provided by the DTSC in their December 19, 2024, letter concerning the 2025 Groundwater Monitoring Report. The satisfactory resolution of these comments is a prerequisite for the DTSC's final approval of the Well Destruction Report.
