## Surface Water Discharge Inspection

### Overview
Surface water discharge inspections are a component of the remediation efforts at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site, specifically related to the management and monitoring of water that may be discharged from the site. These inspections are part of the regulatory oversight to ensure compliance with environmental standards.

### Site Record
On March 23, 2021, a letter was sent to the [[dtsc]] regarding surface water discharge. The letter referenced a previous inspection and noted that "no surface water discharge was observed during the inspection" {{quote: "no surface water discharge was observed during the inspection"}}. This observation was made in the context of the site's remediation activities.

### Regulatory Status
The regulatory status of surface water discharge is governed by the requirements set forth by agencies such as the [[dtsc]]. The absence of observed discharge during a specific inspection indicates a current state of compliance for that particular observation period.
