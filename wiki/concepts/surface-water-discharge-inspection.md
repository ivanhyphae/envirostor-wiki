## Surface Water Discharge Inspection

### Overview
Surface water discharge inspections are a component of the remediation efforts at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. These inspections are conducted to monitor and document any discharge of surface water from the site, ensuring compliance with environmental regulations.

### Site Record
On March 23, 2021, Caltrans sent a letter to the [[dtsc]] regarding surface water discharge. This letter referenced a previous inspection where "no surface water discharge was observed during the inspection." This observation was made in the context of ongoing remediation activities at the site.

### Regulatory Status
The management and monitoring of surface water discharge are subject to regulatory oversight by agencies such as the [[dtsc]]. The absence of observed discharge during the March 23, 2021 inspection indicates a state of compliance for that specific observation period.
