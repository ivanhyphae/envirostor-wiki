## No Offsite Groundwater Migration

This section details the project's compliance with the requirement that contaminants do not migrate offsite via groundwater.

### Site Record

Groundwater monitoring reports have been issued periodically to assess potential offsite migration. For instance, the September 2013 Groundwater Monitoring Report for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles in Stanislaus County, California, documented findings related to groundwater conditions. [S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013]

The Final Feasibility Study Report for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, related to the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project, also addresses groundwater considerations. [S9800-01-17 Modesto Soil Stockpiles Final FS Report.0614]

A Draft Final Remedial Action Plan was also developed for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project. [S9800-01-17 Modesto Stockpiles Draft Final Remedial Action Plan.Revised_10.14]

Stormwater sampling was conducted in January 2016 and March 2016. [S9800-01-17A Modesto Stockpiles Jan 6 2016 Stormwater Smplng 0216], [S9800-01-17A Modesto Stockpiles Mar 5 2016 Stormwater Samplng_04 16]

### Regulatory Status

The project's compliance with offsite groundwater migration requirements is overseen by regulatory agencies. A letter from DTSC dated in 2025 addressed the 2025 Groundwater Monitoring Report. [DTSC Letter Response - 2025 GW Monitoring Report (900259)]
