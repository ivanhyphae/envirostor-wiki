The determination of "No Offsite Groundwater Migration" is a critical finding for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. This conclusion indicates that groundwater affected by the soil stockpiles has not moved beyond the project site boundaries to impact adjacent properties, municipal water sources, or other sensitive receptors. This assessment is based on groundwater monitoring data and analyses performed by [[geocon-consultants]].

## Site record

The assessment of no offsite groundwater migration is primarily supported by the **June 2014 Groundwater Monitoring Report**, prepared by Geocon Consultants for [[caltrans]]. This report analyzed data from six monitoring wells: four onsite (MW-1, MW-2, MW-3, and MW-4) and two offsite (MW-5 and MW-6).

During the second quarter of 2014 monitoring event, **barium** was the only constituent detected above its Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) in an offsite well. However, the report attributes this exceedance to background conditions in the regional groundwater, rather than to impacts originating from the stockpile site. The report states: {{quote: "barium was detected above the MCL in one offsite well, but its presence is consistent with naturally occurring barium in the area's groundwater — not indicative of site-related impacts migrating offsite."}}

The two offsite wells, MW-5 and MW-6, are designated as sentinel wells to detect potential downgradient migration. The consultant's conclusion that no site-related contaminants have migrated offsite above regulatory thresholds is based on the interpretation that the barium detection in an offsite well is unrelated to stockpile activities.

Geocon Consultants also concluded that {{quote: "groundwater underlying the site is not used as a drinking water source by surrounding land uses, and that no municipal supply wells or private domestic wells are located within proximity that would be at risk"}} {{quote: "11 29 2012 Caltrans GND Water Monitoring Rpts/11 29 2012 Caltrans GND Water Monitoring Rpts.md"}}. This further supports the finding that no actionable offsite migration pathway exists under current conditions.

## Regulatory status

The "No Offsite Groundwater Migration" determination is documented in the June 2014 Groundwater Monitoring Report, which was submitted to the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB)** via the **GeoTracker** system. The applicable regulatory threshold for barium is the MCL of 1,000 µg/L, as established by California and federal drinking water standards. The consultant's interpretation that the sole offsite MCL exceedance for barium reflects background conditions, not a site-related release, underpins the ongoing compliance status regarding offsite migration.

The available source material does not document any formal regulatory concurrence or dispute with this conclusion. The finding stands as the consultant's professional determination submitted under Caltrans oversight and CVRWQCB purview.

## Open items

- The available source record does not contain documentation of the CVRWQCB's formal concurrence with, or response to, the "no offsite migration" conclusion.
- While the consultant asserts that the barium exceedance is attributable to background conditions, the source material does not indicate whether the CVRWQCB required or accepted additional geochemical evidence (such as ratio analyses, upgradient comparisons, or trend plots) to support this assertion.
