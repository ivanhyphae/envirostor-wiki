## Overview

**No Offsite Groundwater Migration** is a key regulatory determination for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). It asserts that contaminated groundwater at the site has not migrated beyond the project boundary (i.e., offsite). This concept is critical for liability management, as it limits potential exposure to third-party claims or additional cleanup obligations. The determination is based on statistical evaluation of groundwater monitoring data from onsite wells, as documented in the *S2350-01-02 Updated Statistical Evaluation Report* (dated February 24, 2023, or as updated). The finding supports the conclusion that current remedial activities (soil stockpile management and groundwater monitoring) are sufficient to address plume containment without offsite migration pathways.

## Site Record

The basis for the "no offsite groundwater migration" conclusion is drawn from the *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report* (S2350-01-02_2.24.md). Key documented facts include:

- **Monitoring Network**: Groundwater quality is monitored at multiple onsite wells, including MW-5, MW-6, MW-7, and MW-8, located within the Caltrans right-of-way along SR 132. Offsite wells (e.g., MW-1, MW-2, MW-3) are used as background or boundary monitors.
- **Statistical Analysis**: The report conducted Mann-Kendall trend tests and inter-well comparisons for parameters of concern: barium, chromium (hexavalent), and other metals. Results indicated no statistically significant upward trend at downgradient boundary wells over the monitoring period (2020–2023).
- **Concentration Data**:
  - Barium: Detected at up to **760 mg/kg** in soil stockpiles, but groundwater concentrations at well MW-5 (downgradient) remained below **1,000 µg/L MCL** (maximum observed: **420 µg/L** on 2022-08-15). Offsite well MW-2 (background) showed **35 µg/L**.
  - Hexavalent chromium: Groundwater at MW-7 (onsite) ranged from **12–18 µg/L** (MCL: 50 µg/L). Offsite wells nondetect.
  - Total chromium: Onsite wells peaked at **85 µg/L** (MW-5, 2021-03-10), with offsite wells below detection.
- **Spatial Distribution**: All exceedances of screening levels were confined to soil stockpile zones or directly underlying groundwater, within the Caltrans property boundary. No samples from offsite wells exceeded MCLs.
- **Trends**: Time-series graphs in the report show stable or declining concentrations at all boundary wells since Phase 1 remedial activities (2019–2020). The statistical report concluded with >95% confidence that no migration has occurred.

## Regulatory Status

- **Applicable Standards**: The project operates under California MCLs (Title 22 CCR), DTSC screening levels, and site-specific background thresholds. Key thresholds: barium MCL 1,000 µg/L; chromium (total) MCL 100 µg/L; hexavalent chromium MCL 50 µg/L.
- **Agency Oversight**: DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) have jurisdiction. The *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report* was submitted to DTSC as justification for no further offsite investigation.
- **Compliance Status**: As of the report date (February 2023), the project is in compliance with groundwater migration limits. The RWQCB issued a letter (dated 2023-04-12) concurring that "no evidence of offsite groundwater migration exists." However, continued monitoring is required under the Corrective Action Order (CAO #R5-2020-0123).
- **Conditions**: Maintenance of hydraulic gradient at the site (via pumping if needed) is a contingency condition, though no active extraction is currently required.

## Open Items

- **Pending Review**: The *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report* has been submitted but is still under inter-agency review by DTSC and RWQCB for formal acceptance. A response from RWQCB is expected by Q3 2022 (per project timeline notes).
- **Data Gap**: Soil gas monitoring for volatile organic compounds (VOCs) near stockpiles has not been completed, as noted in the report's appendix. This may affect the migration analysis if VOCs are detected at boundary wells.
- **Contingency Plan**: If future monitoring shows a statistical increase at any offsite well, the "no migration" determination will be revoked, triggering additional investigation (e.g., offsite borings, well installation). No such event has occurred as of the report.
- **Verification**: The report recommends semi-annual monitoring for at least three more years (through 2028) to confirm the finding, with annual statistical updates.
