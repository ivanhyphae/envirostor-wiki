## Stockpile No. 3

### Overview
Stockpile No. 3 is one of several soil stockpiles generated during the [[modesto-ramp-rehabilitation-project]] at SR 132 in Stanislaus County, California. This stockpile was identified as containing contaminants, specifically [[arsenic]] and [[lead]], at concentrations exceeding applicable screening levels. Its management and remediation were part of the project's environmental restoration efforts under the [[brownfields-and-environmental-restoration-program]].

### Site Record
Stockpile No. 3 contained arsenic at a concentration of 15.1 mg/kg {{quote: "Stockpile No. 3: Arsenic 15.1 mg/kg"}} and lead at a concentration of 110 mg/kg {{quote: "Stockpile No. 3: Lead 110 mg/kg"}}. These concentrations were determined to exceed residential and commercial/industrial screening levels. {{quote: "Arsenic and lead concentrations in Stockpile No. 3 exceeded the residential and commercial/industrial screening levels."}} The [[dtsc-approval-letter]] dated August 30, 2012, addressed the management of this stockpile.

### Regulatory Status
The concentrations of [[arsenic]] and [[lead]] in Stockpile No. 3 exceeded the residential and commercial/industrial screening levels. {{quote: "Arsenic and lead concentrations in Stockpile No. 3 exceeded the residential and commercial/industrial screening levels."}} The [[dtsc-approval-letter]] dated August 30, 2012, provided approval for the management of this stockpile.
