# Variance Approval (RDIP Variance #2)

RDIP Variance #2 is a formal deviation from the clean cover fill specification in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] for the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles, submitted by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] to DTSC on September 2, 2020. The variance was requested because the SR 132 West Project's highway contractor proposed a different capping material than the RDIP specified for the upper surfaces of [[stockpile-1]] and [[stockpile-2]], and Geocon's letter sets out the technical basis for treating that substitute material as an acceptable clean cover. The document is addressed to Mr. Dean Wright of DTSC, indicating the variance was prepared at DTSC's request, but the record provided does not include DTSC's written response or a signed approval.

## What the RDIP required and what changed

Section 3.2.2 of the RDIP (January 30, 2019) allowed two capping options for the barium-containing soil (BCS) and aerially-deposited lead (ADL) consolidation zones atop Stockpiles 1 and 2: a minimum 12-inch layer of clean cover fill, or, as an alternative pending final pavement, a minimum 4-inch layer of aggregate base (AB) material. The RDIP's rationale for the thinner AB option was to preserve stockpile fill capacity, on the assumption that the eventual structural pavement section and hardscape would become the permanent clean cap.

The variance request arose because the highway contractor proposed placing Class 3 aggregate subbase (AS) rather than aggregate base (AB) on the stockpile surfaces. Geocon's letter compares Caltrans' gradation and quality specifications for Class 1–4 aggregate subbase against aggregate base, concluding that Class 3 AS is "primarily comprised of sand and gravel with lesser fines content similar to aggregate base materials," and is therefore functionally equivalent to AB for the purpose of interim [[clean-fill-capping]].

## Basis for approval

The variance also documents the permanent pavement section planned for Interim Phase I of the SR 132 West Project, which will ultimately overlie the 4-inch interim layer:

| Layer | Thickness |
|---|---|
| Concrete pavement | 0.85 ft (10.2 in) |
| Hot mix asphalt | 0.25 ft (3.0 in) |
| Class 3 aggregate subbase | 0.70 ft (8.4 in) |

Areas outside the structural pavement footprint on the stockpile surfaces are to be capped with hardscape instead. Geocon's stated conclusion is that a minimum of 4 inches of Class 3 aggregate subbase, used as interim clean cover on Stockpiles 1 and 2 ahead of final pavement/hardscape placement, "meets the intent of the RDIP and would provide adequate clean cover." This finding is the operative basis for treating Class 3 AS as [[clean-fill-suitable-as-cover]] under the RDIP's alternative capping provision, without requiring the full 12-inch clean fill thickness or a switch back to AB.
