## Title 22 Metals

### Overview

Title 22 Metals refers to a category of dissolved metals analyzed in groundwater samples collected at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. These analyses are conducted to assess compliance with regulatory thresholds.

### Site Record

Groundwater samples collected in February 2014 showed that none of the reported dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium were reported at concentrations similar to historical levels and remained significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds. The remaining dissolved metals were also reported at concentrations similar to historical levels.

In April 2015, none of the reported dissolved metals concentrations in groundwater samples exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium concentrations were similar to historical levels and remained significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds. Other dissolved metals were also reported at concentrations similar to historical levels.

Table 2 in the April 2015 report, "Summary of Groundwater Analytical Results – Title 22 Metals (Dissolved)," lists specific metals and their concentrations, along with applicable Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs). For example, the table includes data for various wells, such as MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-5, and MW-6, across different sampling dates.

### Regulatory Status

The analyses for Title 22 Metals are performed to compare against numeric water quality threshold values and California MCLs. The sources indicate that in February 2014 and April 2015, dissolved metals concentrations in groundwater samples did not exceed these thresholds, with the exception of manganese in MW-10 in February 2014. MCLs are established by the California Environmental Protection Agency.
