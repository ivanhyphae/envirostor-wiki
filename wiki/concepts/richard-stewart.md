Richard C. Stewart is an Engineering Geologist with [[caltrans]] who has been instrumental in the characterization and management of soil stockpiles for the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. He has served as a key point of contact for regulatory agencies, including the [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], facilitating the review and approval of technical memorandums and sampling plans related to soil reuse.

## Soil Characterization and Reuse Approvals

Stewart's role involved coordinating the submission of technical documents to regulatory agencies for the approval of various soil sources for reuse on the project.

*   **Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Footing:** On April 6, 2020, Stewart submitted a technical memorandum to the [[dtsc]] and [[central-valley-rwqcb]] detailing the characterization of soil from the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing excavation. This memo proposed the classification of this soil for use at Stockpiles 1 and 2. On April 7, 2020, [[dean-wright]] of DTSC approved the memo, with the condition that MSE wall footing soil could not be used as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2. Instead, it was to be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill, as barium concentrations exceeded background levels and the footing was outside the designated borrow area.
*   **Stockpile 2 MSE Wall Footing:** On June 18, 2020, Stewart submitted a revised technical memorandum from geocon-consultants-inc. concerning Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing sampling. This memo indicated that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) required placement in designated containment zones, while soil below 3 feet bgs was suitable for embankment fill or capping material, and was considered part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request. On June 22, 2020, Dean Wright of DTSC approved this revised memo.
*   **Bent 2 Soils:** On November 2, 2020, Stewart submitted a technical memorandum regarding the characterization of excavated Bent 2 soils for use as clean cap material. The analytical results indicated these soils exceeded background criteria for clean cap cover material. On November 6, 2020, Dean Wright of DTSC and the Water Board accepted the memo, agreeing that the soil could be placed on top of Stockpile 2 at a thickness of 6 inches or less, provided it was covered with clean base material and roadway construction. This approval also emphasized strict adherence to the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment due to potential temporary exposure during winter months.
*   **Basin 5 Clean Fill:** On June 11, 2020, Stewart forwarded the Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan to DTSC and the Water Board for review and approval. On June 12, 2020, Dean Wright of DTSC approved the plan, noting its consistency with previous borrow area sampling. Subsequently, on July 6, 2020, Stewart submitted a revised technical memorandum for Basin 5 clean cap borrow area soil characterization. On July 9, 2020, Dean Wright of DTSC accepted this revised memo, agreeing that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and suitable as clean cover material for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2.
*   **Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill:** On April 20, 2021, Stewart forwarded the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum to DTSC for review. On April 22, 2021, Dean Wright of DTSC accepted the memo, agreeing that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria.
*   **Borrow Area:** On February 28, 2020, Stewart submitted a Technical Memorandum regarding the backfill area characterization for soil intended to cap the SR 132 stockpiles. On March 4, 2020, Dean Wright of DTSC accepted the memo, agreeing that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and suitable as clean cover material.

## Sampling Plan Submittals

Stewart was also involved in the submission and approval of various sampling plans.

*   On March 31, 2020, Stewart forwarded a sampling plan for Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Footing excavations to [[dean-wright]] and [[perry-myers]] at DTSC, and [[steven-meeks]] at the Water Board. This plan proposed sampling for barium and lead with a 48-hour turnaround time, with analytical data to be compared against site-specific background levels, RDIP BCS removal thresholds, and various ESLs. On April 2, 2020, Dean Wright of DTSC accepted this sampling plan via email.
*   On February 12, 2020, Stewart was involved in discussions regarding a clean fill sampling plan, with Dean Wright of DTSC requesting a modification to composite 3 samples instead of 4.

## Project Updates

Stewart has also provided updates on project documentation. On January 10, 2017, he provided an update to the Implementation Schedule of the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles.
