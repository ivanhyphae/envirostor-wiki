# Richard Stewart

## Overview
Richard C. Stewart is an Engineering Geologist (PG 7926) with the [[caltrans]] Central Region Environmental Division, Hazardous Waste & Paleontology Branch, based in Fresno, California. He serves as a [[caltrans]] point of contact for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project (SR 132, Stanislaus County). In the project record from February 2020, Stewart was involved in coordinating with the [[dtsc]] regarding the [[borrow-area-sampling-plan]], work notifications, and a site meeting with the contractor, Bay Cities.

## Site record
Stewart's documented involvement in the project record includes the following:

*   **February 12, 2020, 1:48 p.m.**: [[dean-wright]] (DTSC) forwarded an email to Stewart from his PPS concerning work notice mailing and sick leave notification. The content of the forwarded email was truncated in the available record.
*   **February 12, 2020, 2:48 p.m.**: Stewart provided [[dean-wright]] with a contact list from 2018, which he located in response to Wright's request. Stewart's signature block at the time identified his office as 855 M Street, Ste. 200, Fresno, CA 93721, with a telephone number of 559.445.6378.
*   **February 12, 2020, 3:59 p.m.**: Stewart confirmed a 10 a.m. conference call scheduled for the following day and provided the dial-in details: Conference Number (877) 829-8910, Passcode 8536140. He anticipated that the contractor (Bay Cities) would be interested in the status of the work notification and the clean fill sampling plan. Stewart also stated he would request an example burial report from Kim Christmann at the [[caltrans]] Headquarters office and share it with Wright if available.
*   **February 13, 2020**: Stewart was among the recipients of an email from [[dean-wright]] that conveyed DTSC's approval of the backfill sampling plan. The sole modification requested was to composite 3 samples instead of 4, based on input from Perry Myers (DTSC).
