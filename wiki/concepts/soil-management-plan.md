The Soil Management Plan (SMP) for the State Route 132 (SR 132) Modesto project details the procedures for managing soil excavated during the construction of two retention basins. It covers excavation, confirmation sampling, handling, and disposal of impacted soil.

### Plan Revisions and Approval

The revised SMP, dated February 8, 2021, incorporated DTSC's requests, including lowering the screening level for diesel-range Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH-d) to 500 mg/kg, aligning with the DTSC screening level for commercial/industrial soil. The plan specifically addressed targeted excavation of TPH-d impacted soils at borings RB-10 and RB-11 within Basin 6A, along with confirmation sampling. It also covered the excavation of approximately 500 cubic yards from south of 611 Franklin within Basin 6B, where no known TPH-d impacted soils were identified.

The DTSC and the Central Valley RWQCB officially accepted the revised SMP via an e-mail dated February 11, 2021. This acceptance was subject to two minor comments to be addressed in the field:
*   Soil with Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) exceeding screening levels must be segregated, covered, and labeled prior to offsite transport.
*   If TPH-impacted soil remains at the maximum depth of the basins, Caltrans is to be notified, and consultation with the RWQCB and DTSC is required to determine subsequent actions.

As of February 11, 2021, the RWQCB had not yet responded to requests for a meeting to discuss the appropriate screening level for groundwater protection, which would apply to soils remaining onsite or being reused onsite at finished grade.
