The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) Acceptance signifies the formal approval of the plan detailing how the remediation of [[barium]]-contaminated soil at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site would be carried out. This acceptance is a critical step in the remediation process, authorizing the commencement of the approved remedial actions.

### RDIP Approval

The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA site was approved by the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] on December 18, 2014. This approval was contingent upon the satisfactory resolution of comments provided by the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] in their letter dated October 29, 2014. The RDIP outlined the proposed remedial actions for the site, which involved the excavation and off-site disposal of [[barium]]-containing soil.
