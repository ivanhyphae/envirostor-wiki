The Chemicals of Concern (COC) at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site are barium, lead, and strontium. These were identified as primary COCs due to the historical activities at the site, which involved processing barium sulfate and strontium sulfate ores.

## Site record
The primary Chemicals of Concern (COC) are barium, lead, and strontium. {{quote: "The primary Chemicals of Concern (COC) are barium, lead, and strontium."}} These were identified in relation to soil stockpiles generated during the construction of State Route (SR) 99 north of Kansas Avenue in the early 1960s. {{quote: "The soil stockpiles consist of excess native soils and pond tailings that were generated when Caltrans constructed a segment of SR 99 north of Kansas Avenue in the early 1960's."}} The excavation traversed a parcel previously owned by Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC), a company that processed barium sulfate and strontium sulfate ores. {{quote: "Excavating the segment traversed a portion of a 4.3-acre parcel purchased from Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC). ... FMC (and its predecessors) was a chemical manufacturing company that processed barium sulfate and strontium sulfate ores and other minerals."}}

## Regulatory status
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB), has reviewed groundwater monitoring reports for the site. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB) has reviewed 'Groundwater Monitoring Report, California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) Modesto Soil Stockpiles - State Route (SR) 99 and 132, Modesto, California', prepared by Geocon Consultants and dated May 2012."}} DTSC concurs with the May 2012 Groundwater Monitoring Report. {{quote: "DTSC concurs with the Groundwater Monitoring Report, dated May 2012"}}

Future groundwater monitoring reports are requested to include a discussion comparing groundwater monitoring results and concentration trends from previous sampling events, with graphs and maps depicting concentration trends of primary COCs over time. {{quote: "DTSC concurs with the Groundwater Monitoring Report, dated May 2012 and requests that future groundwater monitoring reports include a discussion comparing groundwater monitoring results/concentration trends from previous sampling events as more data becomes available. Graphs and maps should be prepared depicting concentration trends of primary COC over time."}} Groundwater monitoring is requested to continue on a quarterly schedule. {{quote: "At a minimum, groundwater monitoring should continue on a quartérly schedule."}}
