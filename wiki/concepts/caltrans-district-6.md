# Caltrans District 6

## Overview
California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) District 6 is the contracting entity and project owner for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles investigation, remediation, and planned State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project in Stanislaus County, California. The District 6 Hazardous Waste Branch, located at 855 M Street, Suite 200, Fresno, California 93721, managed the supplemental site investigation (SSI) under Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44, EA No. 10-403500. The investigation was performed to address environmental data gaps identified by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB), as part of regulatory agency approval requirements for the SR 132 project.

## Site record
Caltrans District 6 retained Geocon Consultants, Inc. to perform the Supplemental Site Investigation at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles, located south of the State Route 99 and Kansas Avenue interchange in Modesto. The SSI scope included advancement of 68 borings for soil sampling and analytical testing. The work was directed by DTSC in consultation with CVRWQCB.

Geocon's correspondence was addressed to Mr. Richard Stewart, PG, of Caltrans District 6. The SSI report was originally dated December 14, 2012, and revised March 1, 2013, incorporating revisions based on DTSC's review letter dated February 15, 2013. Project correspondence identifies Caltrans staff including Sam Haack (sam.haack@dot.ca.gov) on the distribution list for the SSI submittal.

The Human Health Risk Assessment Update reviewed by DTSC's Human and Ecological Risk Office (HERO) on January 9, 2013, was also prepared for Caltrans District 6 by Geocon Consultants. HERO's review memorandum references the "CalTrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles" as the project owned by Caltrans District 6.

Prior project documents under Caltrans District 6 include the May 2007 Site Investigation Report, "Characterization of Soil Stockpiles, Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles," and the May 2007 Human Health Risk Assessment, both reviewed by HERO in a memorandum dated July 17, 2007.

## Regulatory status
Caltrans District 6 is operating under regulatory oversight from DTSC, with consultation from CVRWQCB, for the characterization and remediation of the Modesto Soil Stockpiles. The SSI report disclaimer states that the report contents do not necessarily reflect the official views or policies of the State of California or the Federal Highway Administration, and the report does not constitute a standard, specification, or regulation. The District 6 project is subject to DTSC review and approval, with the February 15, 2013, DTSC review letter resulting in revisions incorporated into the revised SSI report.

## Open items
The SSI report was completed to address data gaps required for regulatory agency approval of the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project. The sources do not specify whether final approval has been granted or whether additional submittals are pending.
