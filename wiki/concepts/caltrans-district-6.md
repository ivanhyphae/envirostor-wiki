## Overview
Caltrans District 6 is the contracting entity and project owner for the investigation and remediation of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] located in Stanislaus County, California, associated with the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project. The District 6 Hazardous Waste Branch managed the supplemental site investigation (SSI) under Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44, EA No. 10-403500. This work was conducted to address environmental data gaps identified by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB), to gain regulatory approval for the SR 132 project. Caltrans District 6 retained Geocon Consultants, Inc. to perform the SSI. The project is subject to DTSC oversight.

## Site record
The Supplemental Site Investigation (SSI) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles was conducted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. under Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44, EA No. 10-403500. The SSI involved advancing 68 borings for soil sampling and analysis. The SSI report, originally dated December 14, 2012, was revised on March 1, 2013, following DTSC's review on February 15, 2013. Caltrans staff, including Sam Haack, were on the distribution list for the SSI submittal.

A Human Health Risk Assessment Update, also prepared for Caltrans District 6 by Geocon Consultants, was reviewed by DTSC's Human and Ecological Risk Office (HERO) on January 9, 2013. HERO's review memorandum identified the project as the "CalTrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles." Previous project documents under Caltrans District 6 include the May 2007 Site Investigation Report, "Characterization of Soil Stockpiles, Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles," and the May 2007 Human Health Risk Assessment, both reviewed by HERO in a July 17, 2007, memorandum.

An annual inspection of the Modesto Stockpiles was conducted on March 18, 2025. The facility was noted as being less than two years old and in "great condition" with no issues observed. {{quote: "The facility is less than two years old and is still in great condition. No issues were observed."}} As of June 10, 2025, there had been no significant change in vegetation on the slope, which was in good condition with no signs of erosion or other disturbance, attributed to a lack of rainfall. Re-hydroseeding of the slopes was planned for the 2025 raining season, with construction of the northern retaining wall scheduled to begin in Fall 2026. {{quote: "From my understanding, the slopes will be re-hydroseeded in the raining season 2025. Construction of the northern retaining wall is supposed to begin in the Fall of 2026."}}

Caltrans District 6 entered into a Task Order with DTSC (DTSC #08-T3616) under State Agreement 43A0260 for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles - State Route 132/99. This Task Order was effective from June 22, 2012, through May 31, 2013, with a total amount of $150,000.00. Richard Stewart served as the Caltrans Task Manager for this agreement.

## Regulatory status
Caltrans District 6 operates under DTSC oversight, with CVRWQCB consultation, for the characterization and remediation of the Modesto Soil Stockpiles. The SSI report was completed to address data gaps necessary for regulatory agency approval of the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project. The SSI report includes a disclaimer stating that its contents do not necessarily reflect the official views or policies of the State of California or the Federal Highway Administration and do not constitute a standard, specification, or regulation. The project is subject to DTSC review and approval.
