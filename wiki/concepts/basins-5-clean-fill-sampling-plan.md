## Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan

The [[clean-fill-sampling-plan]] outlines the proposed sampling and analysis for soil intended for use as a cap on the State Route 132 stockpiles. This plan was developed by Geocon Consultants Inc. and submitted to [[caltrans]] for review and approval by the DTSC and the Waterboard.

## Site Record

The [[clean-fill-sampling-plan]], dated June 11, 2020, details sampling locations and methodology for soil at Basin 5, situated adjacent to and north of the previously tested SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road. This area is anticipated to have similar conditions to the non-impacted native formation exposed in the prior section.

The plan specifies a 10-foot cut depth with an estimated volume of 20,000 cubic yards. Six test pit locations are identified. The sampling protocol involves collecting two depth-discrete, 3-part composite samples from each test pit, resulting in a total of six composite soil samples.

Testing parameters are defined for three depth intervals:
*   **0-2 feet:** Pesticides and metals (analyzed using EPA methods 8081A for pesticides and 6010B/7471A for metals).
*   **2-5 feet:** Metals (analyzed using EPA methods 6010B/7471A).
*   **5-10 feet:** Metals (analyzed using EPA methods 6010B/7471A).

A 48-hour laboratory turnaround time is specified for the analyses.

## Regulatory Status

On June 12, 2020, Dean Wright of the DTSC approved the [[clean-fill-sampling-plan]]. This approval email confirmed that the plan was consistent with previous borrow area sampling and that the DTSC and Waterboard had no comments. The soil sampled under this plan is intended to be [[clean-fill-suitable-as-cover]] for the stockpiles, forming a [[clean-soil-cover]].

## Open Items

A technical memorandum detailing sampling methodology and comparative background metal concentrations was not included with the initial submittal for Basin 5. Geocon Consultants Inc. assumed that a previously submitted technical memorandum for the area east of Carpenter Avenue would be applicable.
