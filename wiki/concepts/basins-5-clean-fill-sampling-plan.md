The [[basins-5-clean-fill-sampling-plan]] details the proposed sampling and analysis for soil intended for use as a cap on the State Route 132 stockpiles. Developed by [[geocon-consultants]], this plan was submitted to [[caltrans]] for review and approval by the DTSC and the Waterboard.

## Site Characterization and Sampling Protocol

The plan, dated June 11, 2020, outlines sampling at Basin 5, located adjacent to and north of the previously tested SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road. Similar soil conditions to the non-impacted native formation are anticipated. The plan specifies a 10-foot cut depth with an estimated volume of 20,000 cubic yards. Six test pit locations were identified, with two depth-discrete, 3-part composite samples collected from each, totaling six composite soil samples.

The testing parameters were defined for three depth intervals:
*   **0-2 feet:** Pesticides (EPA Method 8081A) and metals (EPA Methods 6010B/7471A).
*   **2-5 feet:** Metals (EPA Methods 6010B/7471A).
*   **5-10 feet:** Metals (EPA Methods 6010B/7471A).

A 48-hour laboratory turnaround time was specified for the analyses.

## Regulatory Approval

On June 12, 2020, Dean Wright of the DTSC approved the [[basins-5-clean-fill-sampling-plan]]. His approval email stated that the plan was consistent with previous borrow area sampling and that the DTSC and Waterboard had no comments. The soil sampled under this plan is intended to be [[clean-fill-suitable-as-cover]] for the stockpiles.

## Outstanding Information

A technical memorandum detailing sampling methodology and comparative background metal concentrations was not included with the initial submittal for Basin 5. [[geocon-consultants]] assumed that a previously submitted technical memorandum for the area east of Carpenter Avenue would be applicable.
