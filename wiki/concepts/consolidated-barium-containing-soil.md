## Overview

Consolidated Barium Containing Soil (BCS) refers to soil excavated from former Stockpiles 1, 2, and 3 at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, located along the SR 132 West Project alignment in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. This soil was found to contain elevated concentrations of barium, and in some instances, lead, exceeding regulatory screening levels. The remedial strategy involved consolidating this BCS within designated highway embankment fill areas (BCS Containment Zones) and covering it with clean fill and structural pavement as part of the SR 132 West Project's Interim Phase 1 construction.

## Site Record

BCS Stockpiles 1 through 3 were located within the Caltrans SR 132 right-of-way. Stockpile 1, west of Emerald Avenue, had an estimated volume of 34,000 cubic yards (yd³). Stockpile 2, between Emerald Avenue and SR 99, had an estimated volume of 102,000 yd³. Stockpile 3, east of SR 99, had an estimated volume of 24,000 yd³.

During the excavation of Stockpile 3 (February-March 2020), 29 verification soil samples were collected. Initial analysis showed barium concentrations ranging from 2,200 to 7,000 mg/kg. After deepening these areas, final verification samples showed barium concentrations from 46 to 970 mg/kg and lead from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg. Approximately 27,675 yd³ of BCS from Stockpile 3 was transported and placed as embankment fill within the Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone.

Excavation of the western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2 (February-March 2020) involved collecting 10 and 14 verification soil samples, respectively. Initial results indicated four areas with barium concentrations above the verification threshold. After deepening these areas, final samples showed barium ranging from 54 to 760 mg/kg and lead from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg.

Excavation of BCS from the southerly slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 for MSE wall construction (March-April 2020) yielded soil samples with barium concentrations ranging from 42 to 420 mg/kg and lead from 1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg. Due to barium exceeding the site-specific background concentration of 120 mg/kg, all excavated MSE wall footing material was placed in the Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones.

Soil contaminated with aerially deposited lead (ADL), exceeding hazardous waste criteria, from the northbound SR 99 shoulder was placed in the eastern end of the Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone (June-July 2020).

The BCS material contains elevated barium concentrations, up to 72,000 mg/kg, and elevated lead concentrations. The Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones are located between SR 132 Stations 1125+50 and 1133+50 (Stockpile 1) and between SR 132 Stations 1135+35 and 1148+30 (Stockpile 2).

## Regulatory Status

The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP), approved in 2019, established verification thresholds for BCS removal:
*   **Barium:** 1,000 mg/kg (based on groundwater protection).
*   **Lead:** 80 mg/kg (based on DTSC residential screening level).

Site-specific upper-bound background concentrations for clean fill source areas were approved by DTSC in a Variance dated April 9, 2020:
*   **Barium:** 120 mg/kg
*   **Lead:** 3.8 mg/kg

A Land Use Covenant (LUC) is required by DTSC to restrict land use on the contained BCS areas to transportation purposes, prohibiting residential or other sensitive uses. Notification to DTSC is required for any future disturbance of BCS material.

## Open Items

A Final Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) will be submitted following the completion of Ultimate Phase 2 construction of the SR 132 West Project, scheduled for completion in 2028. A supplemental RDIP will be submitted prior to Ultimate Phase 2 construction.
