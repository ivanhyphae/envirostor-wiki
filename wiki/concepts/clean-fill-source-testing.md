## Overview

Clean Fill Source Testing refers to the analytical verification program required for imported soil material used as backfill in the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation at State Route 132 (SR 132) in Stanislaus County, California. The testing ensures that imported fill meets environmental quality standards before placement, preventing introduction of contaminants to the remediated site. The program was established under the oversight of the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) as part of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the project.

## Site Record

The Clean Fill Source Testing protocol is documented in the **Hwy 132 Clean Fill Source Testing Exhibit**, which forms part of the project's administrative record. Key requirements include:

- **Testing frequency**: Imported material must be sampled at a rate of one composite sample per 500 cubic yards of fill, with a minimum of one sample per source.
- **Analytical parameters**: Samples must be analyzed for both organic and inorganic constituents, including:
    - Total petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH)
    - Volatile organic compounds (VOCs) by EPA Method 8260
    - Semi-volatile organic compounds (SVOCs) by EPA Method 8270
    - Metals (arsenic, barium, cadmium, chromium, lead, mercury, selenium, silver) by EPA Method 6010/7471
    - Polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs) by EPA Method 8082
- **Acceptance criteria**: Concentrations must not exceed DTSC’s California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs) for residential land use, unless a site-specific risk assessment demonstrates otherwise. For metals, background concentrations in native Stanislaus County soils (as established by DTSC) were also used as benchmarks.
- **Documentation**: Each load of fill is required to be accompanied by a certificate of compliance and chain-of-custody documentation linking it to source testing results.

The testing program was implemented during the two primary remediation phases: **Phase 1 (2019–2020)** and **Phase 2 (2021–2022)**. During Phase 1, approximately 12,000 cubic yards of fill were imported from approved sources, including local quarries and commercial soil suppliers. All testing results were submitted to DTSC for review prior to placement. No exceedances of acceptance thresholds were reported for imported material during either phase.

## Regulatory Status

The Clean Fill Source Testing program operates under DTSC’s oversight as a condition of the RAP. The applicable standards include:

- **DTSC-modified California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs)** for residential land use (October 2018 version) for organic constituents
- **Background concentrations** for metals, as determined by a site-specific background study approved by DTSC
- **EPA Regional Screening Levels (RSLs)** for chemicals not listed in CHHSLs

Compliance with these standards is verified through the testing protocol described above. DTSC approved all source testing results for both phases, confirming that imported fill met remediation goals. As of the most recent reporting, no compliance violations have been documented for the clean fill program.

## Open Items

- **Final source testing report**: A comprehensive summary of all clean fill source testing conducted during both phases is pending submission to DTSC. This report is expected to include a tabulation of all sample results, certificate of compliance copies, and a quality assurance/quality control review.
- **Post-placement verification**: DTSC has requested post-placement verification sampling of clean fill at a rate of one sample per 2,000 cubic yards to confirm no contamination was introduced during handling; this requirement had not been fulfilled as of the last record update.
