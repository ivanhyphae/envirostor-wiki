## Clean Fill Source Testing

### Overview
Clean Fill Source Testing refers to the sampling and analysis conducted to ensure that soil intended for use as clean fill on the SR 132 West Expressway Project meets specific environmental criteria. This testing is crucial for verifying that the soil is free from contaminants that could pose a risk to human health or the environment. The testing protocols are designed to identify the presence of specific analytes, such as pesticides and metals, within defined depth intervals of the proposed fill material.

### Site Record
On the SR 132 West Expressway Project, a proposed clean fill source was identified within the Route 132 Right-of-Way (ROW) at and east of Carpenter Road. The testing plan for this source involved a maximum excavation depth of 5 feet. A total of 12 borings were to be advanced to this 5-foot depth. From these borings, 3 four-point composite samples were to be created. The analytical testing included pesticides and metals for the 0-2 foot interval and metals for the 2-5 foot interval. This testing was outlined in a handwritten note on Sheet L-13, a detailed engineering layout plan for the intersection of Route 132 and N. Carpenter Road.
