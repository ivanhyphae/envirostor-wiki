## SWPPP Amendment – Bent 2

### Overview
The SWPPP Amendment – Bent 2 is a regulatory revision to the Storm Water Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project (SR 132, Stanislaus County). It specifically addresses soil management activities at the Bent 2 stockpile area. The amendment was proposed in a technical memo and formally accepted by the appropriate reviewing agency via e-mail, as recorded in the project case file. Acceptance of this amendment confirms that the revised stormwater controls and soil handling procedures for Bent 2 comply with the project’s National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) permit requirements.
