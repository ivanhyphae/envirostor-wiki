The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) has the authority to require Caltrans to pay all costs associated with the administration of a [[land-use-covenant]] (LUC) pursuant to [[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]]. This authority is cited in the context of the State Route 132 West Expressway (AKA: State Route 132/99 Interchange) project in Modesto, California {{quote: "Pursuant to California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Section 67391.1 (h), DTSC has the authority to require Caltrans to pay all costs associated with the administration of the LUC."}}.

## Site record

The DTSC's authority under [[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]] is mentioned in relation to the estimated oversight costs for Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026 for the State Route 132 West Expressway project {{quote: "ESTIMATED OVERSIGHT COSTS – FISCAL YEAR 2025-2026, STATE ROUTE 132 WEST EXPRESSWAY (AKA: STATE ROUTE 132/99 INTERCHANGE), INTERSECTION OF STATE ROUTE 132/99 IN MODESTO, CALIFORNIA (SITE CODE: 900259)"}}. The DTSC provided an estimate of costs for regulatory oversight activities for this period, which began July 1, 2025, and ends June 30, 2026 {{quote: "Enclosed are the schedule of expected activities and an estimate of costs for the Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026 which began July 1, 2025, and ends June 30, 2026."}}.

## Regulatory status

As part of the cleanup at the Site, a [[land-use-covenant]] was placed to restrict certain uses or activities {{quote: "As part of the cleanup at the Site, a Land Use Covenant (LUC) was placed to restrict certain uses or activities."}}. DTSC's authority under [[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]] allows them to require Caltrans to cover all administrative costs related to this LUC {{quote: "Pursuant to California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Section 67391.1 (h), DTSC has the authority to require Caltrans to pay all costs associated with the administration of the LUC."}}.

## Open items

The DTSC provided an estimate of projected costs for FY 2025-2026, based on various assumptions regarding activities and deliverables {{quote: "Please note that this is not an invoice, only an estimate of projected costs, prepared with various assumptions regarding activities and deliverables."}}. DTSC will issue invoices based on actual costs {{quote: "DTSC will issue invoices Mr. Adam Inman December 24, 2025 Page 2 based on actual costs."}}. Caltrans remains liable for all response costs incurred by DTSC {{quote: "Caltrans remains liable for all response costs incurred by DTSC pursuant to Health and Safety Code Section 79650 et seq., and the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA) 42 U.S.C. Section 9601 et seq."}}. DTSC reserves all rights to pursue cost recovery under applicable state or federal law {{quote: "DTSC reserves all rights to pursue cost recovery under any, and all applicable provisions of state or federal law."}}.
