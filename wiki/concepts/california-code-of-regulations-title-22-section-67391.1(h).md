The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) has the authority under [[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]] to require Caltrans to pay all costs associated with the administration of a [[land-use-covenant]] (LUC). This authority is cited in the context of the State Route 132 West Expressway project in Modesto, California.

## Site record

The DTSC's authority under [[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]] is mentioned in relation to the estimated oversight costs for Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026 for the State Route 132 West Expressway project, identified by Site Code 900259. The DTSC provided an estimate of costs for regulatory oversight activities for this period, which began July 1, 2025, and ends June 30, 2026.

## Regulatory status

As part of the cleanup at the Site, a [[land-use-covenant]] was placed to restrict certain uses or activities. DTSC's authority under [[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]] allows them to require Caltrans to cover all administrative costs related to this LUC.

## Open items

The DTSC provided an estimate of projected costs for FY 2025-2026, noting that this is not an invoice but an estimate prepared with various assumptions regarding activities and deliverables. DTSC will issue invoices based on actual costs. Caltrans remains liable for all response costs incurred by DTSC pursuant to california-health-and-safety-code-section-79650 et seq., and the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA) 42 U.S.C. Section 9601 et seq. DTSC reserves all rights to pursue cost recovery under any applicable provisions of state or federal law.
