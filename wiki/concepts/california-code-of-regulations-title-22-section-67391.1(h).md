## California Code Of Regulations Title 22 Section 67391.1(h)

[[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]] grants the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) the authority to require Caltrans to cover all costs associated with the administration of a [[land-use-covenant]] (LUC) implemented as part of a cleanup action.

## Site Record

At the State Route 132 West Expressway (AKA: State Route 132/99 Interchange) site in Modesto, California (Site Code: 900259), a Land Use Covenant was established as part of the cleanup. DTSC has asserted its authority under [[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]] to require Caltrans to pay for all costs related to the administration of this LUC. This authority is referenced in the context of the [[annual-cost-estimate-letter]] for [[fiscal-year-2025-2026]].

## Regulatory Status

The DTSC has the authority to require Caltrans to pay all costs associated with the administration of the LUC, pursuant to [[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]].

## Open Items

The DTSC will issue invoices based on actual costs incurred for the administration of the LUC. Caltrans remains liable for all response costs incurred by DTSC.
