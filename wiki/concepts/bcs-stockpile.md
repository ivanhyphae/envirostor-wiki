The [[bcs-stockpile]] refers to soil designated for use as clean fill, specifically for cover material in remediation projects managed by [[caltrans]]. This soil is sourced from areas intended to be free of contamination and is characterized through sampling plans to ensure it meets project requirements.

## Site Record

In February 2020, [[geocon-consultants]] prepared a [[borrow-area-sampling-plan]] for soil proposed for use as clean fill for the Highway 132 project. This soil was to be excavated from the upper five feet of the Highway 132 alignment, extending east from the [[carpenter-road-area]]. This area has been state right-of-way since the 1960s and was previously agricultural property. The sampling plan involved 12 borings, with analysis of six depth-discrete 4-point composite samples. Samples from 0-2 feet were to be analyzed for organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) and heavy metals (3 composite samples). Samples from 3-5 feet were to be analyzed for heavy metals (3 composite samples). Logs of test borings completed at Carpenter Road in 2017 were appended to document the material at this location as native soil. No high-risk documented contamination releases were reported in the immediate vicinity of the proposed clean fill source area based on a review of the Blackburn 2014 ISA and Rincon 2018 Phase II ESA. Testing of residential backyards by Rincon adjacent and west of Carpenter Road indicated pesticides and metals at concentrations less than applicable regulatory screening levels.

A sampling-plan for stockpiles 1 and 2, related to MSE walls, also references "BCS Stockpile" in the context of a grading plan for Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. This plan, dated January 2019, identifies "STOCKPILE 61" as [[barium-containing-soil]].

A Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP Variance) prepared by Geocon on April 9, 2020, addresses the use of clean cover fill materials to cap consolidated [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) within Stockpiles 1 and 2 during the SR 132 West Project. {{quote: "This RDIP Variance has been prepared to address the identified sources of clean cover fill materials to properly cap consolidated bariumcontaining soil (BCS) within Stockpiles 1 and 2 during the SR 132 West Project."}} The RDIP specified that the surface of completed Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS would be capped with a minimum 12-inch-thick clean fill material. {{quote: "The surface of the completed Stockpile 1 and 2 barium-containing soil and ADL consolidation zones (including east- and west-facing temporary slopes) will be capped with minimum 12-inch-thick clean fill material consisting of approved imported fill and/or minimum 4-inch-thick AB materials (pending placement of structural pavement section, hardscape and/or additional clean embankment fill)."}}

The initial proposed source of clean imported fill soil was an area within the SR-132 ROW between the Carpenter Road and Rosemore Avenue overcrossings. {{quote: "The proposed source of clean imported fill soil consists of currently vacant and agricultural land within planned excavation areas along the SR-132 ROW between the Carpenter Road and Rosemore Avenue overcrossings as depicted on Figure 1."}} The highway contractor initially identified an area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road within the SR 132 right-of-way as the clean fill material source. Geocon evaluated this area and determined the planned highway underpass excavation area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road would generate suitable material. {{quote: "Geocon, in coordination with Caltrans and DTSC, evaluated this area and determined the planned highway underpass excavation area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road would generate soil material suitable for use as clean cover fill material (Geocon February 27, 2020 Technical Memorandum)."}} The DTSC subsequently provided approval of the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area. {{quote: "DTSC subsequently provided approval of the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area."}}

An additional source of clean fill material was identified at proposed mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations (approximately five-foot-deep) along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2. Geocon evaluated the MSE wall footing material along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1 following excavation of the southern slope BCS material. {{quote: "Geocon evaluated the MSE wall footing material along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1 following excavation of the southern slope BCS material (Geocon April 6, 2020 Technical Memorandum)."}} In coordination with Caltrans and DTSC, it was determined that the upper foot of planned wall footing excavation soil contained elevated barium concentrations that would not be suitable for use as clean fill. {{quote: "In coordination with Caltrans and DTSC, it was determined that the upper foot of planned wall footing excavation soil contained elevated barium concentrations that would not be suit"}}

## Regulatory Status

The characterization of soil for use as [[clean-fill]] is subject to approval by regulatory agencies such as the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). The [[borrow-area-sampling-plan]] prepared by Geocon was submitted to DTSC for review and acceptance. DTSC provided approval of the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area. {{quote: "DTSC subsequently provided approval of the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area."}}
