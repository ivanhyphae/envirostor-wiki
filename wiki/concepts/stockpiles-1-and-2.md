## Stockpiles 1 and 2

### Overview
Stockpiles 1 and 2 are designated containment zones within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project. These stockpiles were intended for the placement of soil excavated from the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing area.

### Site Record
On April 6, 2020, Geocon Consultants, Inc. submitted a technical memorandum to Caltrans concerning the sampling of soil from the MSE wall footing excavation. The purpose of this sampling was to classify the material for its potential use within Stockpiles 1 and 2 as embankment fill and/or clean cover material.

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board reviewed the memorandum. They concurred with the findings, with the specific exception of using the MSE wall footing soil as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2.

The DTSC determined that the sampled soil, with a 95% Upper Confidence Limit exceeding the maximum background of 120 mg/kg, and originating from an area outside the designated borrow area, was to be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or utilized as general bridge embankment fill.

DTSC's official acceptance of the technical memorandum was communicated via email on April 7, 2020, with the condition that the directive be implemented in the field.

Grading plans dated January 2019 depict Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 as areas designated for Barium-Containing Soil.

### Regulatory Status
The maximum background threshold for the sampled MSE wall footing soil was 120 mg/kg. The DTSC, through Dean Wright, Project Manager, provided regulatory acceptance for the placement of this soil within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones. The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) also reviewed the material.
