## Stockpiles 1 and 2

### Overview
Stockpiles 1 and 2 are designated containment zones within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project. These stockpiles were intended for the placement of soil excavated from the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing area.

### Site Record
On April 6, 2020, Geocon Consultants, Inc. submitted a technical memorandum to Caltrans concerning the sampling of soil from the MSE wall footing excavation. The purpose of this sampling was to classify the material for its potential use within Stockpiles 1 and 2 as embankment fill and/or clean cover material. {{quote: "please submit the appended technical memo to Dean Wright at DTSC and Steve Meeks at the Water Board for approval of use of Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing excavation soil material within Stockpiles 1 and 2 as embankment fill and/or clean cover material."}}

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board reviewed the memorandum. They concurred with the findings, with the specific exception of using the MSE wall footing soil as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "Both agencies agree with the last paragraph of the Memo with the exception of using the MSE wall footing soil as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material."}}

The DTSC determined that the sampled soil, with a 95% Upper Confidence Limit exceeding the maximum background of 120 mg/kg, and originating from an area outside the designated borrow area, was to be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or utilized as general bridge embankment fill. {{quote: "Given that the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil is above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg and the fact that the MSE wall footing falls outside of the designated borrow area described in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, this material must be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill."}}

DTSC's official acceptance of the technical memorandum was communicated via email on April 7, 2020, with the condition that the directive be implemented in the field. {{quote: "With the acceptance of the above comment and it's execution in the field, this e-mail serves as DTSCs official acceptance of the Memo."}}

Grading plans dated January 2019 depict Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 as areas designated for Barium-Containing Soil. {{quote: "This image is a 'Grading Plan' document for Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, dated January 2019. It shows a map indicating site locations and features along 'ROUTE 132', including a highlighted yellow area labeled 'STOCKPILE 61' which is Barium-Containing Soil. The plan includes annotations indicating a 'BCS Removal Sampling Plan' with identified 'Stockpile 1 & 2' and 'Southern Slope Retaining Wall'."}} {{quote: "This image is a grading plan map from January 2019 labeled Figure 3-2, titled 'Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles' for a remedial design implementation plan. The map shows the layout of two large 'Barium-Containing Soil' stockpiles—Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2—located along a roadway corridor, bisected by Emerald Ave."}}

### Regulatory Status
The maximum background threshold for the sampled MSE wall footing soil was 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "Given that the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil is above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg"}} The DTSC, through Dean Wright, Project Manager, provided regulatory acceptance for the placement of this soil within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones. The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) also reviewed the material.
