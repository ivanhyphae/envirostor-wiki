## Stockpiles 1 and 2

### Overview
Stockpiles 1 and 2 are containment zones within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. Soil excavated from the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing area was evaluated for its suitability for placement within these stockpiles.

### Site Record
On April 6, 2020, Geocon Consultants, Inc. submitted a technical memorandum to Caltrans detailing the sampling of MSE wall footing excavation soil. The purpose of this sampling was to classify the material for use within Stockpiles 1 and 2 as embankment fill and/or clean cover material. {{quote: "please submit the appended technical memo to Dean Wright at DTSC and Steve Meeks at the Water Board for approval of use of Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing excavation soil material within Stockpiles 1 and 2 as embankment fill and/or clean cover material."}}

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board reviewed this memorandum. They agreed with the findings, with the exception of using the MSE wall footing soil as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "Both agencies agree with the last paragraph of the Memo with the exception of using the MSE wall footing soil as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material."}}

The DTSC determined that because the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil was above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg, and the MSE wall footing fell outside the designated borrow area, this material must be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill. {{quote: "Given that the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil is above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg and the fact that the MSE wall footing falls outside of the designated borrow area described in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, this material must be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill."}}

DTSC's official acceptance of the technical memorandum was provided via email on April 7, 2020, contingent upon the execution of this directive in the field. {{quote: "With the acceptance of the above comment and it's execution in the field, this e-mail serves as DTSCs official acceptance of the Memo."}}

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC, represented by Dean Wright, Project Manager, provided the regulatory acceptance for the use of the MSE wall footing soil within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones. The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) also reviewed the material. The maximum background threshold for the sampled soil was 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "Given that the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil is above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg"}}
