## Overview
Magnesium is a dissolved metal that has been reported in groundwater samples collected as part of the SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA remediation case file. Its presence and concentration are tracked as part of the site's environmental monitoring program.

## Site record
Magnesium was reported in samples PL1 through PL5 collected in October 2016. It was also reported in samples BG1 and BG2 from the same sampling event. In March 2018, magnesium was reported for some or all of the samples collected.

Laboratory QA/QC notes from October 2016 indicate that samples PL1 through PL5, BG1, and BG2 required dilution for dissolved magnesium due to failing internal standards in the original run and possible matrix interference.

## Regulatory status
The reported concentrations of magnesium in the analyzed samples did not exceed their respective primary or secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) in the October 2016 sampling event. The March 2018 sampling event also indicated that reported magnesium concentrations did not exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs.
