Clean Fill Suitability is the recurring determination — made separately for Basin 5, the SR 132 clean borrow area, and the soil beneath Stockpiles 1 and 2 — of whether excavated or borrowed soil meets the chemical criteria needed to be reused on site as fill, cover, or backfill rather than managed as contaminated material. The determination matters directly to project cost and schedule: soil that qualifies as clean fill can be placed as [[clean-soil-cover]] or used in [[mse-wall-construction]] without offsite disposal, while soil that does not must be handled differently. The record documents this determination through four separate technical memoranda, each addressing a distinct source area.

## Basin 5

The Basin 5 Clean Fill Technical Memo, revised July 2, 2020, addresses the suitability of material associated with Basin 5 for use as clean fill. The source material provided does not include the sampling data, analytes, or conclusions from this memo beyond its title and revision date.

## SR 132 clean borrow area

The SR 132 Clean Borrow Area Technical Memo addresses a separate source of fill material — a borrow area rather than a stockpile or basin — evaluated for suitability as clean fill for the SR 132 project. The source material provided does not include the sampling results or conclusions of this memo.

## Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 MSE wall sampling

Two further memoranda — the Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Sampling Tech Memo and the Stockpile 2 MSE Wall Sampling Tech Memo — evaluate soil from [[stockpile-1]] and Stockpile 2 specifically for use in [[mse-wall-construction]], where the fill functions as reinforced backfill behind a mechanically stabilized earth wall. This use is more sensitive than general grading fill because MSE wall backfill is a structural and long-term component of the roadway, so its chemical suitability bears on both the wall's performance and any residual contamination left in place. The source material provided does not include the analytical results or the suitability conclusions from either memo.

## Significance and limits of the record

Across all four memoranda, the shared question is the same: whether soil already excavated, stockpiled, or available from borrow can be redeployed on the SR 132 project rather than disposed of offsite as contaminated material. This determination is the mechanism by which findings elsewhere in the case file — including detections associated with [[barium]] and other constituents identified during [[bcs-removal]] work — translate into a reuse-versus-disposal decision for specific volumes of soil. However, the source material excerpted here consists only of cover pages and does not carry the sampling data, threshold comparisons, or pass/fail conclusions that would show how that determination was actually made for Basin 5, the borrow area, or the Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 MSE wall backfill. The consultants associated with this body of work in the broader case file include [[geocon-consultants-inc]] and [[wsp]], but the specific authorship, findings, and dates of each memo beyond what is stated above are not established by the material provided.
