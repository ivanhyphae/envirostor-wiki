The Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) documents the outcome of the interim removal action for barium-containing soil (BCS) at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, verifying that excavation, consolidation, and clean-fill capping of Stockpiles 1 through 3 were carried out as embankment fill in accordance with the 2018 Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). Geocon Consultants prepared the RACR in stages — a draft in December 2021, accepted by DTSC in February 2022, followed by a final version dated December 1, 2022, accepted by DTSC (in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board) on January 18, 2023. Because the SR 132 West Project is being built in two construction phases, the RACR itself is split: this document closes out Interim Phase 1 only, and a separate RACR will be required after Ultimate Phase 2 construction, anticipated to begin 8 to 10 years after the January 2023 acceptance letter.

## Draft Interim RACR and DTSC's February 2022 acceptance

Geocon submitted the Draft Interim RACR in December 2021, describing BCS grading and clean-fill capping completed to that point, with remaining work limited to placement of structural pavement section, erosion control, drainage, and median barrier and signage over the capped stockpiles. DTSC's February 24, 2022 acceptance letter confirmed that Stockpile 3 removal (approximately 27,675 yd³ of soil excavated and placed as embankment fill in the Stockpile 1 containment zone) met verification criteria: of 29 confirmation samples, three exceeded the 1,000 mg/kg barium cleanup goal, the affected areas were deepened approximately one foot and resampled, and the resulting samples were below the goal. The letter accepted the draft as documenting work completed to date, but the interim removal action was not yet complete — additional capping, verification of Stockpile 1 and 2 work, and as-built documentation remained outstanding.

## Final RACR and January 2023 acceptance

The final RACR, dated December 1, 2022, added the As-Built Plans, cross-section figures, final survey data, and the final Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan that were missing from the draft. DTSC's January 18, 2023 acceptance letter confirms that soil removal, consolidation, and capping were completed as specified in the RAP, the RDIP, and two variances to the RDIP approved April 17, 2020 and September 21, 2020. DTSC states it has no comments on the RACR and is accepting it as final, and that this acceptance — together with completion of a draft O&M Agreement (to be prepared by DTSC within 30 days of the letter) and a Land Use Covenant (LUC) to be recorded with the Stanislaus County Assessor — marks the conclusion of the Interim Phase 1 effort.

The letter is explicit that this is not final closure of the site: "final certification of the RAP for this project will not occur until after the Ultimate Phase 2 State Route 132 build-out effort is completed." Ultimate Phase 2 will require a supplemental RDIP submitted to DTSC for approval, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and revisions to the O&M Plan/Agreement if necessary, under a separate future agreement between DTSC and Caltrans. As of the January 2023 letter, the LUC and O&M Agreement had not yet been executed or recorded — they are described as forthcoming deliverables, not completed conditions.

## What the RACR documents by stockpile

The body of the Interim RACR (December 2021 draft) compiles the verification sampling and characterization work performed during construction, organized around the removal-verification threshold of 1,000 mg/kg barium (groundwater protection) and 80 mg/kg lead (residential screening level):

- **Stockpile 3**: 24 verification samples (S3 1-0 through S3 24-0) collected February 28–March 12, 2020; three locations (S3 9-0 through S3 11-0) exceeded the barium threshold and were deepened roughly one foot and resampled. Final results ranged from 46 to 970 mg/kg barium and 1.6 to 40 mg/kg lead, all below thresholds.
- **Stockpile 2 west end**: eight samples (S2W 1-0 through 8-0) on February 28, 2020; two locations (S2W 1-0, S2W 3-0) exceeded the barium threshold, requiring the western half to be deepened to elevation 81 feet MSL and resampled March 6, 2020.
- **Stockpile 2 east end**: six samples (S2E 1-0 through 8-0) on February 21, 2020; one exceedance (S2E 3-0) led to re-excavation and seven additional samples on February 28, 2020, which found a further exceedance (SE2 8-0), deepened again and resampled March 6, 2020. Final results across Stockpile 2 ranged from 54 to 760 mg/kg barium and 1.5 to 35 mg/kg lead.
- **Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing** (April 2020 memo): 15 samples from trenches STK1-1 through STK1-3 showed barium from 42 to 420 mg/kg (95% UCL 171.9 mg/kg, mean 128.3 mg/kg) and lead 1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg, both below RDIP removal thresholds, judged suitable for placement within the containment zones or as capping material pending agency approval.
- **Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing** (June 2020 memo): 29 samples from trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7 showed barium 53 to 930 mg/kg and lead 3.5 to 22 mg/kg; material from the upper three feet required placement within the containment zones, while deeper material was suitable as general fill or capping.

Each of these individual technical memoranda states that its findings "will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report," and the RACR functions as the consolidating document for this dispersed verification record.

## Clean fill sourcing folded into the RACR

The RACR also incorporates characterization of borrow sources used to generate the clean cap placed over the BCS containment zones, each screened against site-specific background concentrations (2007 Shaw background data) rather than the BCS removal thresholds:

| Borrow source | Date sampled | Barium range (mg/kg) | Background (mg/kg) | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Carpenter Road borrow area | Feb 21, 2020 | 60–110 | 72.8 | Suitable for clean cap |
| Basin 5 borrow area | Jun 16, 2020 | 66–94 | 72.8 | Suitable for clean cap |
| Bent 2 excavation stockpiles | dated Oct 30, 2020 memo | 31–230 | 120 | Did not meet clean cap criteria; approved only for partial cap (≤6 in.) under DTSC review, since full structural pavement section would overlie it |
| Carpenter Road Shoofly | dated Apr 12, 2021 memo | 28–96 | 120 | Met clean cap criteria |

The Bent 2 result is the one instance in the record where proposed cap material failed the applicable screening criterion (the 120 mg/kg background threshold established in the April 9, 2020 RDIP variance); the RACR records that the material was still usable as a thin partial cap because the full pavement section would be placed above it, rather than being rejected outright.

## Significance

The two acceptance letters are the operative record for liability and land-use purposes: DTSC's January 2023 letter is the regulatory sign-off that Interim Phase 1 containment was built and verified as designed, but it simultaneously establishes that the RAP for the site is not fully certified and will not be until Ultimate Phase 2 is complete. Any reviewer assessing site closure or the scope of continuing Caltrans obligations needs both documents together — the February 2022 letter shows what remained incomplete at the draft stage, and the January 2023 letter shows what was added (as-builts, survey data, O&M Plan) to close it out, while flagging the O&M Agreement and LUC as pending, not yet executed, deliverables at the time of acceptance.
