## Removal Action Completion Report

### Overview
The Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) documents the completion of consolidation and capping activities for barium-containing soils (BCS) at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, as part of the Interim Phase 1 Construction effort for the State Route 132 West Project. The report incorporates findings from soil characterization testing and details the completed remedial actions.

### Site record
The RACR, dated December 1, 2022, was submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of Caltrans. It describes the consolidation and capping activities completed for barium-containing soils (BCS) as part of the Interim Phase 1 Construction effort at the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, California (Site Code: 900259-00). The soil removal, consolidation, and capping efforts were completed as per the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP), Remedial Design and Implementation Plan (RDIP), and two variance requests to the RDIP approved on April 17, 2020, and September 21, 2020. The RACR also includes As-Built Plans, cross-section figures, final survey data, and the final Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan.

A Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020, authored by John E. Juhrend of Geocon, summarized soil characterization testing performed following the removal of BCS along the Stockpile 2 southerly slope to facilitate the construction of a mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall. Excavated BCS material was placed as embankment fill within the designated Stockpile 2 fill containment zone. Soil samples were collected from trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7 at approximate 200-foot intervals and analyzed for barium and lead. Barium was detected in all 29 samples at concentrations ranging from 53 to 930 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). Fourteen samples exceeded the site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg. Thirteen of these fourteen samples were from depths between the surface and 3 feet. Lead was detected in all samples at concentrations ranging from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg, with most concentrations within the order of magnitude of the site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg. Based on this data, the upper three feet of excavated soil from the Stockpile 2 southerly slope MSE wall footing required placement within designated fill containment zones. Material excavated from depths greater than 3 feet was deemed suitable for placement as general embankment fill or capping material, pending regulatory approval. This information was intended for inclusion in the RACR.

### Regulatory status
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), reviewed the RACR. DTSC accepted the document as final on January 18, 2023, stating, "DTSC has no comments on the RACR and is accepting the document as final." This acceptance signifies the conclusion of the Interim Phase 1 effort.

### Open items
Following the acceptance of the RACR, DTSC will prepare a draft Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Agreement for Caltrans review within 30 days of the January 18, 2023 acceptance letter. A Land Use Covenant (LUC) for the remediated area will also be completed and sent for review, signature, and recording with the Stanislaus County Assessor's office. Final certification of the RAP is pending the completion of the Ultimate Phase 2 State Route 132 build-out effort, which is anticipated to occur in 8 to 10 years. This future work will require a supplemental RDIP, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and potential revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement, all conducted under a separate agreement between DTSC and Caltrans.
