## Removal Action Completion Report

### Overview
The Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) documents the completion of consolidation and capping activities for barium-containing soils (BCS) at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, as part of the Interim Phase 1 Construction effort for the State Route 132 West Project. The report incorporates findings from soil characterization testing and details the completed remedial actions.

### Site record
The RACR, dated December 1, 2022, was submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of Caltrans. It describes the consolidation and capping activities completed for barium-containing soils (BCS) as part of the Interim Phase 1 Construction effort at the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, California (Site Code: 900259-00). The soil removal, consolidation, and capping efforts were completed as per the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP), Remedial Design and Implementation Plan (RDIP), and two variance requests to the RDIP approved on April 17, 2020, and September 21, 2020. The RACR also includes As-Built Plans, cross-section figures, final survey data, and the final Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan.

### Regulatory status
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), reviewed the RACR. DTSC has accepted the document as final {{quote: "DTSC has no comments on the RACR and is accepting the document as final."}}. With this acceptance, DTSC will prepare a draft O&M Agreement for Caltrans review within 30 days of the January 18, 2023 acceptance letter. A Land Use Covenant (LUC) for the remediated area will also be completed and sent for review, signature, and recording with the Stanislaus County Assessor's office.

This acceptance marks the conclusion of the Interim Phase 1 effort. However, final certification of the RAP for this project will not occur until after the Ultimate Phase 2 State Route 132 build-out effort is completed. The Ultimate Phase 2 work, anticipated to begin in 8 to 10 years, will include adding additional lanes and completing the final build-out of State Route 132. This future effort will require a supplemental RDIP for DTSC's review and approval, along with field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and potential revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement. The Ultimate Phase 2 State Route 132 project will be conducted under a separate agreement between DTSC and Caltrans prior to commencing work.

### Open items
The DTSC will prepare a draft O&M Agreement for Caltrans review within 30 days of the January 18, 2023 acceptance letter. A Land Use Covenant (LUC) for the remediated area will also be completed and sent for review, signature, and recording with the Stanislaus County Assessor's office. Final certification of the RAP is pending completion of the Ultimate Phase 2 State Route 132 build-out effort.
