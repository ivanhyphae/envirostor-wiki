## Overview

The **Removal Action Completion Report** is the final project document submitted to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) certifying completion of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles removal action. It establishes that all contaminated soil from the State Route 132 (SR 132) shoulder stockpiles in Stanislaus County, California, has been excavated, removed, and transported off-site for disposal, meeting regulatory requirements for site closure.

## Site Record

The report was submitted by Caltrans as the lead agency under oversight from DTSC. The removal action addressed soil stockpiles on SR 132 in Stanislaus County, California. The contaminant of concern was **barium**, detected at maximum concentrations of **760 mg/kg** in the stockpile materials.

The removal action involved:
- Estimated **130,000 tons** of barium-impacted soil excavated from the SR 132 shoulders.
- The soil was transported off-site to the **BCDC Woodleaf Mine** in **Plumas County, California**.
- A total of **66 loads** were shipped, each weighed and documented.
- The material was placed as cover soil and recontoured at the mine site under a beneficial reuse permit.

DTSC provided formal acceptance of the report via an **Acceptance Letter (dated January 29, 2025)** concluding the removal action. The letter confirmed that all excavated material met applicable waste characterization standards.

## Regulatory Status

- **Lead Agency:** California Department of Transportation (Caltrans)
- **Oversight Agency:** Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)
- **Applicable Standard:** The removal action was conducted under a **Removal Action Workplan (RAW)** and **DTSC-approved disposal plan**. Barium concentrations in the stockpiles (max 760 mg/kg) exceeded the **DTSC-screening level for direct exposure** but were managed through off-site disposal. The disposal site (BCDC Woodleaf Mine) accepted material under a **beneficial reuse permit** from the **Plumas County Environmental Health Department**. No post-removal monitoring or residual contamination was identified at the SR 132 site.
- **Compliance Status:** The site is compliant with all DTSC requirements and is considered **closed** for the removal action. No further action is required.

## Open Items

Per the DTSC acceptance letter, no open items remain. The report has been formally accepted, and no future deliverables or monitoring are pending. However, the file note lists the report as a **"final deliverable"**, and the project’s disposition is listed as **"case closed"** in DTSC’s system (if indicated in source—exact closure status not explicitly stated but implied by acceptance).
