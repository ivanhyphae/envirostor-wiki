## Groundwater Monitoring

### Overview
Groundwater monitoring at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, located at State Route (SR) 99 and 132 in Modesto, California, is conducted to assess groundwater quality in the vicinity of three soil stockpiles. These stockpiles consist of excess native soils and pond tailings generated during the construction of SR 99. The primary Chemicals of Concern (COC) identified are barium, lead, and strontium. The monitoring program is overseen by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB).

### Site Record
Groundwater monitoring well sampling was conducted on March 12, 2012, October 5, 2006, and June 14, 2006. The monitoring wells were installed in 2006. The RWQCB collected "split" water samples during the March 12, 2012 sampling event. The DTSC requested that future groundwater monitoring reports include a discussion comparing groundwater monitoring results and concentration trends from previous sampling events, along with graphs and maps depicting concentration trends of primary COCs over time. All graphics, including existing "stiff" diagrams, should include a legend defining symbols and colors. A minimum of quarterly groundwater monitoring is required.

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC has reviewed and concurred with the "Groundwater Monitoring Report, California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) Modesto Soil Stockpiles - State Route (SR) 99 and 132, Modesto, California," prepared by Geocon Consultants and dated May 2012.

### Open Items
Future groundwater monitoring reports are required to include a discussion comparing groundwater monitoring results and concentration trends from previous sampling events. Graphs and maps depicting concentration trends of primary COCs over time are to be prepared. All graphics must include a legend defining symbols and colors. Groundwater monitoring should continue on a quarterly schedule.
