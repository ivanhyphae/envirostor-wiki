Conditional approvals are a regulatory mechanism used by the [[dtsc]] to permit the advancement of remediation activities while awaiting the resolution of specific issues or the provision of further information. For the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, this process has been integral to the review and acceptance of remedial action plans and related documentation.

### DTSC Review of Remedial Action Plan

On October 14, 2014, [[caltrans]] submitted a Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles. This plan was subject to review by the [[dtsc]]. The "Response to Comments letter for SR 132 RAP with Appendix J" indicates that the DTSC provided comments and requested revisions to the RAP. The existence of this response letter signifies that the RAP, as initially submitted, was not fully approved and required modifications to address DTSC concerns.

The DTSC's review process, as evidenced by the response to comments, involves identifying specific areas needing clarification or amendment before a final approval can be issued. This iterative process ensures that the proposed remediation strategies align with regulatory requirements and site-specific conditions.
