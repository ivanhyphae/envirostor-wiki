## Conditional Approvals

Conditional approvals are a mechanism used by the DTSC to allow for the progression of remediation activities while awaiting further information or resolution of specific issues. In the context of the SR 132 West Expressway project, these approvals have been tied to specific aspects of the Remedial Design and Implementation Plan (RDIP) and its subsequent revisions.

## Site Record

On March 1, 2012, the DTSC issued a conditional approval for the SR 132 West Expressway Followup 5 report, prepared by [[caltrans]]. This approval was contingent upon Caltrans addressing specific comments and concerns raised by the DTSC. The report itself detailed findings related to soil stockpiles at the site.

## Regulatory Status

The DTSC, as the regulatory agency overseeing the remediation, has the authority to issue conditional approvals. These approvals signify that while the submitted documentation or proposed actions meet certain criteria, specific outstanding items must be resolved before full acceptance or closure. The conditional approval on March 1, 2012, indicates that the RDIP, or a component thereof, was not fully approved at that time due to outstanding DTSC comments.

## Open Items

The specific conditions attached to the March 1, 2012, conditional approval were detailed in the DTSC's correspondence. Caltrans was required to address these comments to achieve full DTSC acceptance of the RDIP and subsequent remediation phases. The record does not explicitly detail the nature of these comments or their resolution status beyond the issuance of the conditional approval.
