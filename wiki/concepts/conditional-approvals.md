# Conditional Approvals

Two [[dtsc]] acceptance letters, both issued in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], structured the closeout of the Interim Phase 1 remediation at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site: an acceptance of the Draft Interim Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) dated February 24, 2022, and an acceptance of the final RACR dated January 18, 2023. Neither is an unconditional close-out. Each ties DTSC's acceptance to specific prior approvals and leaves specific deliverables or future obligations outstanding — the February 2022 letter by describing work performed against an approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP), and the January 2023 letter by making acceptance of the final RACR contingent on conformance with the RAP, the RDIP, and two RDIP variance requests, while explicitly deferring final certification of the RAP to a future Ultimate Phase 2 project.

## February 24, 2022: acceptance of the Draft Interim RACR

DTSC's first letter accepted the Draft Interim RACR describing consolidation and capping of barium-containing soils (BCS) completed under Interim Phase 1 construction. The acceptance is framed against conformance with the approved RAP and approved Interim Phase 1 RDIP: the excavation of [[stockpile-3]], the western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2, and removal of soil from the southern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 are described as work done "in accordance with" those approved planning documents. The letter also documents a condition generated by the [[stockpile-3-excavation]] itself — three of twenty-nine confirmation samples exceeded the 1,000 mg/kg barium cleanup goal, and those areas were deepened by approximately one foot and resampled before the excavation was treated as complete.

## January 18, 2023: acceptance of the final RACR

The second letter accepted the final RACR, dated December 1, 2022, "as final," with DTSC stating it had no comments. This acceptance is explicitly conditioned on the work having been completed per three prior approvals: the RAP, the RDIP, and two RDIP variance requests approved on April 17, 2020 and September 21, 2020. The final RACR package also added As-Built Plans, cross-section figures, final survey data, and a final Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan that were not part of the draft.

Acceptance of the final RACR did not close out the regulatory file. The letter commits DTSC to two further deliverables tied to this acceptance: a draft O&M Agreement, to be submitted for Caltrans review within 30 days of the letter, and a Land Use Covenant (LUC) for the remediated area, to be completed and sent for review, signature, and recording with the Stanislaus County Assessor's office. The letter does not confirm that either the O&M Agreement or the LUC was executed or recorded; the record as excerpted stops at DTSC's commitment to produce and circulate them.

## The deferred final certification

The January 2023 letter states directly that this acceptance and the associated O&M Agreement/LUC work mark the conclusion of the Interim Phase 1 effort only — final certification of the RAP for the project will not occur until after a future Ultimate Phase 2 State Route 132 build-out, anticipated to begin 8 to 10 years from the letter's date. Ultimate Phase 2 is described as requiring its own supplemental RDIP for DTSC review and approval, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement if necessary, to be conducted under a separate agreement between DTSC and Caltrans before that work commences. This means the "final" RACR accepted in January 2023 is final only with respect to Interim Phase 1; the site's overall remedial certification remains open and contingent on a project phase that had not yet begun as of this letter.

## Significance

The two acceptances function as conditional approvals in a specific, traceable sense: each one closes a discrete phase of work by reference back to earlier approvals (the RAP, the RDIP, the two 2020 variances) rather than by independent re-review, and each leaves at least one obligation open at the moment of acceptance — the confirmation resampling condition in 2022, and the O&M Agreement, LUC, and deferred RAP certification in 2023. For reviewers and counsel, the operative fact is that the site's remedial closure is bifurcated: Interim Phase 1 is accepted as complete, but the RAP itself is not certified, and that certification is expressly tied to a future, separately-agreed Ultimate Phase 2 project rather than to anything resolved in these two letters.
