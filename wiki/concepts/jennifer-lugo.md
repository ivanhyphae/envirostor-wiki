# Jennifer Lugo

## Overview
Jennifer Lugo is a Caltrans employee associated with the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles project in Stanislaus County, California. Her involvement, as documented in the project record, centers on the review and approval process for the borrow area sampling plan in February 2020. While her specific title is not detailed in the provided source, her "@DOT" email address confirms her affiliation with the California Department of Transportation.

## Site record
On February 12, 2020, Jennifer Lugo was copied on an email exchange between [[richard-stewart]] (Caltrans Engineering Geologist) and [[dean-wright]] (DTSC) concerning work notice mailings and the backfill sampling plan for the [[borrow-area-sampling-plan]]. At 3:19 p.m., Lugo responded to a draft document, stating, "Looks good. Thanks!" The specific document she reviewed is not explicitly identified in the email thread but appears to be related to the work notice mailing under discussion.

On February 13, 2020, Lugo was included on an email from Dean Wright to Richard Stewart, confirming DTSC's approval condition for the backfill sampling plan. DTSC requested that samples be composited into 3 samples instead of 4, with no additional comments from Perry Myers of DTSC.
