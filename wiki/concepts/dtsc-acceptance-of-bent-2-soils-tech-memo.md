## DTSC Acceptance of Bent 2 Soils Technical Memorandum

### Overview

On November 6, 2020, the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in concurrence with the Water Board (WB), officially accepted the Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020. This memorandum addressed the characterization of excavated Bent 2 soils for potential use as clean cap material at the [[caltrans]] SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. While the soil sample results did not meet the clean cap criteria established in a prior variance request, DTSC and WB agreed to allow the placement of these soils on top of Stockpile 2, provided specific conditions were met to ensure the integrity of the remediation.

### Site Record

The Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020, submitted by [[richard-stewart]] of [[caltrans]], presented analytical results for excavated Bent 2 soils. These results indicated that the soils exceeded the background criteria for use as clean cap cover material.

Despite the exceedance of clean cap criteria, DTSC and the Water Board agreed to a conditional acceptance. The Bent 2 soils may be placed on top of Stockpile 2 at a maximum thickness of 6 inches. This placement must be followed by covering with clean base material and the subsequent construction of the roadway's asphalt or concrete surface.

It was understood, based on discussions and emails subsequent to the technical memorandum's submission, that the Bent 2 soils might be temporarily exposed to the elements during the winter months due to project scheduling. Consequently, strict adherence to the rainy season surface water sampling plan and the erosion control measures outlined in the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020, was deemed critical. These measures are intended to prevent on-site contaminants from impacting the surrounding area.

This acceptance was communicated via email on November 6, 2020, from [[dean-wright]] of DTSC to [[richard-stewart]] of Caltrans, with copies to other project stakeholders including Perry Myers (DTSC) and Steven Meeks (Waterboards).

### Regulatory Status

The clean cap criteria were established in the April 9, 2020, Variance Request approved by DTSC. The Bent 2 soils, as characterized in the October 30, 2020, Technical Memorandum, did not meet these established criteria. However, DTSC and the Water Board provided conditional acceptance for the use of these soils as a limited layer (6 inches or less) on Stockpile 2, contingent upon specific covering and stormwater management protocols.

### Open Items

The strict adherence to the rainy season surface water sampling effort and the erosion control measures outlined in the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020, is a critical condition for the placement of Bent 2 soils. The effectiveness of these measures in preventing off-site migration of contaminants during potential winter exposure remains a key focus.
