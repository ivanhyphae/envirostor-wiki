The [[dtsc-acceptance-of-bent-2-soils-tech-memo]] represents a regulatory decision by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board to conditionally allow the use of excavated soil from Bent 2 as a limited cover material on Stockpile 2, despite the soil not meeting established clean cap criteria. This decision plays a crucial role in the remediation strategy for the [[caltrans]] SR 132 project by providing a pathway for the reuse of excavated materials while maintaining environmental protection.

### Conditional Acceptance of Bent 2 Soils

On November 6, 2020, DTSC, in concurrence with the Water Board, accepted the Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020, which detailed the characterization of Bent 2 excavation soils. The memorandum, prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] for [[caltrans]], presented analytical results for ten soil samples (STK1 through STK10) collected from the Bent 2 end dump piles. These samples were analyzed for barium and lead.

The analytical results indicated that barium was detected in all ten samples at concentrations ranging from 31 to 230 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). Four of these samples exceeded the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg. Lead was detected in six samples at concentrations ranging from 2.6 to 13 mg/kg, with some exceeding the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg.

Based on these findings, the Bent 2 soil stockpiles did not meet the clean cap criteria established in a prior variance request approved by DTSC on April 9, 2020. However, DTSC and the Water Board conditionally accepted the use of these soils as a partial clean cap, limited to a maximum thickness of 6 inches, on the upper surface of Stockpile 2. This placement is contingent upon subsequent covering with clean base material and the construction of the roadway's structural pavement section (asphalt or concrete). Fill material from previously approved clean fill sources will be required for the northerly slope of Stockpile 2.

### Conditions for Placement and Stormwater Management

The acceptance of the Bent 2 soils was explicitly conditioned on strict adherence to specific protocols to mitigate potential environmental impacts, particularly during potential exposure to winter weather. It was understood that the Bent 2 soils might be temporarily exposed during the winter months due to project scheduling. Therefore, adherence to the rainy season surface water sampling plan and the erosion control measures outlined in the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020, was deemed critical. These measures are designed to prevent on-site contaminants from impacting the surrounding environment.

This conditional acceptance was communicated via email on November 6, 2020, from [[dean-wright]] of DTSC to [[richard-stewart]] of Caltrans, with copies to other project stakeholders, including Perry Myers (DTSC) and Steven Meeks (Waterboards). The information regarding the Bent 2 soil characterization and its conditional use will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report.
