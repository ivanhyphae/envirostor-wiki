# Commercial Industrial Screening Level

## Overview
The Commercial Industrial Screening Level (CHHSL) is a risk-based benchmark used for evaluating soil analytical results at the [[sr-99]] Kansas Avenue Off-Ramp site in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. This screening level is relevant due to the existing commercial and industrial land use adjacent to the project area and is applied to determine appropriate management of excavated soils.

## Site record
In the context of [[stockpile-3-excavation]], the Commercial Industrial Screening Level for [[barium]] was referenced. The highest detected level of barium in sample B17-4.5 was noted as not exceeding the commercial/industrial CHHSL for barium of {{quote: "63,000 mg/kg"}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412/S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412.md"}}. Other barium concentrations in Stockpile 3 samples ranged from {{quote: "31 to 44,300 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg)"}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412/S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412.md"}}, {{quote: "64 to 230 mg/kg"}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412/S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412.md"}}, {{quote: "38 to 93 mg/kg"}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412/S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412.md"}}, and {{quote: "35 to 178 mg/kg"}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412/S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412.md"}}. These latter concentrations were generally within the range of the Stockpile 3 specific background barium levels of {{quote: "between 31 and 150 mg/kg"}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412/S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412.md"}}.

A Caltrans letter dated May 29, 2012, stated that final analysis indicated the presence of elevated metal concentrations above site-specific background levels, but {{quote: "below residential and commercial/industrial California Human Health Screening Levels."}} {{quote: "Caltrans Ramp Letter dated 5.29.12/Caltrans Ramp Letter dated 5.29.12.md"}}. Based on this analysis, Caltrans proposed that the soil material excavated from Stockpile #3 was suitable for reuse as structural backfill or for offsite reuse/disposal as a non-hazardous soil.

During a site investigation by [[geocon-consultants]], barium was reported in each sample at concentrations ranging from 34 to 1,600 mg/kg, which was noted as {{quote: "less than the residential and commercial/industrial CHHSLs for barium of 5,200 and 63,000 mg/kg, respectively."}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 Modesto Ramp Ex Mon Plan -no appx-0612/S9650-06-03 Modesto Ramp Ex Mon Plan -no appx-0612.md"}}. With the exception of arsenic, none of the metals reported in this investigation were at concentrations at or above respective commercial/industrial CHHSLs.

## Regulatory status
The Commercial Industrial Screening Level serves as the project-specific action threshold for soil management decisions during [[stockpile-3-excavation]]. While the [[dtsc-residential-screening-level]] is also considered, the Commercial Industrial Screening Level is the governing benchmark for this site due to its commercial and industrial land uses. The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) concurred with Caltrans' assessment that the stockpiles, as managed, did not pose a risk to human health. In a letter dated December 17, 2009, DTSC stated that {{quote: "the DTSC finds that the stockpiles as currently managed by Caltrans on Caltrans property do not pose a risk to human health for: 1) Caltrans workers who access the fenced site …, 2) trespassers; and 3) residents adjacent to the stockpiles."}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 Modesto Ramp Ex Mon Plan -no appx-0612/S9650-06-03 Modesto Ramp Ex Mon Plan -no appx-0612.md"}}.
