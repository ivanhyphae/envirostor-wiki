# Commercial Industrial Screening Level

## Overview
The Commercial Industrial Screening Level is a risk-based benchmark used in the [[stockpile-3-excavation-monitoring-plan]] for the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project. This screening level is applied to evaluate soil analytical results from the Kansas Avenue Off-Ramp site, reflecting the existing commercial and industrial land use adjacent to [[sr-99]] in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. The plan, prepared by [[geocon-consultants]], uses this threshold to determine appropriate management of excavated soils.

## Site record
The [[stockpile-3-excavation-monitoring-plan]], authored by [[john-e-juhrend]] and reviewed by [[douglas-s-krause]] of Geocon Consultants, Inc., establishes the Commercial Industrial Screening Level as the primary criterion for assessing excavated soil. Soil samples collected during excavation activities are to be analyzed, and the results compared against this screening level. The plan references this benchmark in the context of evaluating detected concentrations of constituents, such as [[barium]], but does not enumerate specific numerical values for the Commercial Industrial Screening Level for individual analytes within the provided text.

## Regulatory status
The Commercial Industrial Screening Level serves as the project-specific action threshold for soil management decisions during the [[stockpile-3-excavation]] work. The DTSC Residential Screening Level is also referenced as a more conservative benchmark, but the Commercial Industrial Screening Level is the governing standard for this site, consistent with its commercial and industrial land uses near the Kansas Avenue Off-Ramp and [[sr-99]] corridor. The available source material does not document specific agency approvals or enforcement actions pertaining to the Commercial Industrial Screening Level.
