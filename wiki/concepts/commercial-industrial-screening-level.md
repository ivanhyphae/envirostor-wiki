# Commercial Industrial Screening Level

## Overview
The Commercial Industrial Screening Level is a regulatory threshold referenced in the Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan for the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project, prepared by geocon-consultants. This screening level serves as the applicable risk-based benchmark for evaluating soil analytical results at the [[kansas-avenue-off-ramp]] site, reflecting the commercial/industrial land use context of the project area adjacent to [[sr-99]] in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. The excavation monitoring plan, dated June 2012 and bearing the project number S9650-06-03, invokes this screening level as the standard against which detected constituents in stockpile soils would be compared.

## Site record
The Commercial Industrial Screening Level is applied in the Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan to determine whether excavated soils from [[stockpile-3-excavation]] require special handling or disposal. The plan document, authored by [[john-e-juhrend]] and reviewed by [[douglas-s-krause]] of Geocon Consultants, Inc., establishes that soil samples collected during excavation activities would be analyzed and results compared to the Commercial Industrial Screening Level. Specific numerical values for the Commercial Industrial Screening Level are not detailed in the available source material. The document references this threshold in the context of evaluating detected concentrations of constituents including [[barium]], but the exact screening level values for individual analytes are not enumerated in the monitoring plan text.

## Regulatory status
The Commercial Industrial Screening Level is applied as the project-specific action threshold for soil management decisions during the [[stockpile-3-excavation]] work. The [[dtsc-residential-screening-level]] is also referenced in the project documentation as a separate, more conservative benchmark, but the Commercial Industrial Screening Level is the primary standard governing the excavation activities at this site, consistent with the existing commercial and industrial land uses surrounding the [[kansas-avenue-off-ramp]] and [[sr-99]] corridor. No specific agency approval or enforcement correspondence regarding the Commercial Industrial Screening Level is documented in the available source material.
