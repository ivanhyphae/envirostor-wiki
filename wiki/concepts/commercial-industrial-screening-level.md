# Commercial Industrial Screening Level

## Overview
The Commercial Industrial Screening Level (CHHSL) is a risk-based benchmark used for evaluating soil analytical results at the [[sr-99]] Kansas Avenue Off-Ramp site in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. This screening level is relevant due to the existing commercial and industrial land use adjacent to the project area and is applied to determine appropriate management of excavated soils.

## Regulatory status
The Commercial Industrial Screening Level serves as the project-specific action threshold for soil management decisions during [[stockpile-3-excavation]]. While the [[dtsc-residential-screening-level]] is also considered, the Commercial Industrial Screening Level is the governing benchmark for this site due to its commercial and industrial land uses.
