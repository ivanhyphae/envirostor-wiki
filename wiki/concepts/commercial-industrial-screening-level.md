# Commercial Industrial Screening Level

## Overview
The Commercial Industrial Screening Level is a risk-based benchmark used for evaluating soil analytical results from the [[sr-99]] Kansas Avenue Off-Ramp site in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. This screening level reflects the existing commercial and industrial land use adjacent to the project area. It is applied to determine appropriate management of excavated soils, particularly those from [[stockpile-3-excavation]].

## Site record
The [[stockpile-3-excavation-monitoring-plan]] references the Commercial Industrial Screening Level as a benchmark for assessing excavated soil. Specifically, for [[barium]], the highest detected level in sample B17-4.5 was noted as not exceeding the commercial/industrial [[california-human-health-screening-levels]] (CHHSL) for barium of {{quote: "63,000 mg/kg"}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412/S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412.md"}}. Other barium concentrations in Stockpile 3 samples ranged from {{quote: "31 to 44,300 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg)"}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412/S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412.md"}}, {{quote: "64 to 230 mg/kg"}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412/S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412.md"}}, {{quote: "38 to 93 mg/kg"}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412/S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412.md"}}, and {{quote: "35 to 178 mg/kg"}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412/S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412.md"}}. These latter concentrations were generally within the range of the Stockpile 3 specific background barium levels of {{quote: "between 31 and 150 mg/kg"}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412/S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412.md"}}.

Caltrans, in a letter dated May 29, 2012, stated that final analysis indicated the presence of elevated metal concentrations above site-specific background levels, but {{quote: "below residential and commercial/industrial California Human Health Screening Levels."}} {{quote: "Caltrans Ramp Letter dated 5.29.12/Caltrans Ramp Letter dated 5.29.12.md"}}. Based on this analysis, Caltrans proposed that the soil material excavated from Stockpile #3 was suitable for reuse as structural backfill or for offsite reuse/disposal as a non-hazardous soil.

## Regulatory status
The Commercial Industrial Screening Level is used as the project-specific action threshold for soil management decisions during the [[stockpile-3-excavation]] work. The [[dtsc-residential-screening-level]] is also referenced as a more conservative benchmark, but the Commercial Industrial Screening Level governs for this site due to its commercial and industrial land uses. The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) concurred with Caltrans' assessment that the stockpiles, as managed, did not pose a risk to human health for workers, trespassers, or adjacent residents, based on a Final PEA and Response to Comments document prepared in 2009. In a letter dated December 17, 2009, DTSC stated that {{quote: "the DTSC finds that the stockpiles as currently managed by Caltrans on Caltrans property do not pose a risk to human health for: 1) Caltrans workers who access the fenced site …, 2) trespassers; and 3) residents adjacent to the stockpiles."}} {{quote: "S9650-06-03 Modesto Ramp Ex Mon Plan -no appx-0612/S9650-06-03 Modesto Ramp Ex Mon Plan -no appx-0612.md"}}.

## Open items
