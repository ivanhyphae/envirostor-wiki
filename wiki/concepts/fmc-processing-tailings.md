## FMC Processing Tailings

### Overview
FMC Processing Tailings are a component of the soil stockpiles at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. These tailings are a source material for certain contaminants of concern at the site.

### Site Record
The [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site includes soil stockpiles that contain FMC Processing Tailings. These tailings are identified as a source material contributing to elevated concentrations of certain constituents. Specifically, the FMC Processing Tailings are noted as a source of [[arsenic]], [[barium]], and [[chromium]].

### Regulatory Status
The presence and potential impact of FMC Processing Tailings are addressed within the context of the overall remediation of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site, which is overseen by the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]].
