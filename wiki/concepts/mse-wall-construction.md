MSE (mechanically stabilized earth) wall construction along the southerly slopes of Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 required excavation of barium-containing soil (BCS) that had been covering the wall footing alignments, generating a distinct sampling and classification problem: the footing soils fell outside the borrow area designated in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP), so their reuse on site required separate characterization and DTSC/Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board sign-off before they could go anywhere but the fill containment zones.

## Sampling approach

Geocon Consultants, Inc. proposed the sampling plan on March 31, 2020, submitted by Richard Stewart of Caltrans to DTSC's Dean Wright and Perry Myers and the Central Valley Water Board's Steven Meeks for approval before excavation of the Stockpile 1 southern slope BCS zone was to begin. As the BCS was removed, discrete soil samples were to be collected at one-foot intervals to the depth of the planned footing excavation (approximately 4 to 5 feet) from trenches spaced at roughly 200-foot intervals, analyzed for barium and lead by EPA Method 6010B under expedited turnaround. The plan set out the comparison values used to judge reuse suitability: barium site-specific background of 120 mg/kg, an RDIP BCS removal threshold of 1,000 mg/kg (groundwater protection), and San Francisco Bay RWQCB Environmental Screening Levels of 3,000 mg/kg (construction worker), 15,000 mg/kg (residential), and 220,000 mg/kg (commercial); lead background of 3.8 mg/kg against a DTSC residential level of 80 mg/kg.

## Stockpile 1 wall footing results

Geocon's April 6, 2020 technical memorandum reported results from trenches STK1-1, STK1-2, and STK1-3 (200-foot spacing, samples to 5 feet). Barium was detected in all 15 samples at 42 to 420 mg/kg — above the 120 mg/kg background value but below the 1,000 mg/kg RDIP removal threshold. The 95% UCL of the data set was 171.9 mg/kg and the mean was 128.3 mg/kg, both above background. Lead was detected in 14 of 15 samples at 1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg, within the range of the 3.8 mg/kg background value and well below the 80 mg/kg residential level.

DTSC and the Water Board accepted the memo on April 7, 2020, but rejected Geocon's proposed use of this material as Stockpile 1/2 capping soil. Because the 95% UCL exceeded the 120 mg/kg background value and the MSE wall footing area was outside the RDIP-designated borrow area, the agencies required that this soil be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill instead.

## Stockpile 2 wall footing results

The June 18, 2020 technical memorandum covered trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7 (200-foot spacing, samples to 3 and 5 feet). Barium was detected in all 29 samples at 53 to 930 mg/kg; 14 of 29 exceeded the 120 mg/kg background value, and 13 of those 14 elevated samples came from the 0–3 foot interval. One deeper sample, STK2-7-4, contained 140 mg/kg barium despite being collected below the planned 3-foot footing depth. Lead ranged from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg, mostly near the 3.8 mg/kg background value.

DTSC and the Water Board accepted the memo on June 22, 2020, adopting a depth-based split: soil in the upper 3 feet along the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing had to go into the designated containment zones, while soil below 3 feet was approved for use as embankment fill or capping material. The agencies further determined that the sub-3-foot material was already covered by the April 9, 2020 Variance Request to the RDIP, so no additional variance was needed to use it.

## Significance for reuse and containment

The two footing investigations produced opposite depth-dependent outcomes that governed how the excavated material could be handled: Stockpile 1's footing soil, despite individual concentrations below the removal threshold, was kept out of capping use because its statistical UCL exceeded background and it lay outside the approved borrow area; Stockpile 2's footing soil was split, with only the top 3 feet restricted and the deeper soil approved for embankment fill or capping without a new variance. Both memoranda note the results would be folded into the Removal Action Completion Report following completion of Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping.
