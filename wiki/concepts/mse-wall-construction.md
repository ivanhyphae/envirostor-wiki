## MSE Wall Construction

### Overview

Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) wall construction at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site involved the excavation and characterization of soils for use as fill and capping material. This process was overseen by [[caltrans]], with regulatory review and approval from the [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. The primary analytes of concern for MSE wall construction materials were [[barium]] and lead.

### Site Record

Sampling plans for MSE wall footing excavations at [[stockpile-1]] and [[stockpile-2]] were developed by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] and submitted for regulatory approval.

*   **Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Footing:**
    *   A Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020, presented characterization testing for MSE wall footing excavation soil.
    *   Barium was detected in 15 soil samples at concentrations ranging from 42 to 420 mg/kg. The maximum site-specific background for barium is 120 mg/kg. The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) removal threshold for barium is 1,000 mg/kg.
    *   The calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) for barium was 171.9 mg/kg, and the mean was 128.3 mg/kg.
    *   Lead was detected in 14 of 15 samples at concentrations ranging from 1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg. The maximum site-specific background for lead is 3.8 mg/kg. The RDIP removal threshold for lead (residential land use) is 80 mg/kg.
    *   Based on these results, the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material was deemed suitable for placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill, or as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material, pending regulatory approval.

*   **Stockpile 2 MSE Wall Footing:**
    *   A Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020, summarized characterization testing for MSE wall footing excavation soil.
    *   Barium was detected in 29 soil samples at concentrations ranging from 53 to 930 mg/kg. Fourteen samples exceeded the maximum site-specific background of 120 mg/kg.
    *   Thirteen of the 14 samples with elevated barium were collected from depths between the surface and 3 feet below ground surface (bgs). One sample (STK2-7-4) at a depth greater than 3 feet had a barium concentration of 140 mg/kg.
    *   Lead was detected in all samples at concentrations ranging from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg, with most within the order of magnitude of the maximum site-specific background of 3.8 mg/kg.
    *   The [[dtsc]] and [[central-valley-rwqcb]] agreed that soil down to 3 feet bgs required placement in designated containment zones, while soil below 3 feet bgs was suitable as embankment fill or capping material.

*   **MSE Wall Sampling Plan Approval:**
    *   A sampling plan for Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE wall footing excavations was submitted on March 31, 2020.
    *   This plan proposed sampling borings or trenches within the MSE wall footing excavation at ten locations, spaced approximately 200 feet apart. Discrete soil samples were to be collected from one-foot intervals to a depth of approximately 4 to 5 feet.
    *   Samples were to be analyzed for barium and lead with a 48-hour turnaround time.
    *   The analytical data would be compared to site-specific background levels and RDIP screening levels.
    *   On April 2, 2020, [[dtsc]] and the Water Board provided acceptance of this sampling plan.

### Regulatory Status

*   **Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Footing Soil:** The [[dtsc]] and [[central-valley-rwqcb]] accepted the Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020, with the condition that MSE wall footing soil exceeding the maximum background of 120 mg/kg for barium, and falling outside the designated borrow area, must be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill. It was not to be used as [[clean-soil-cover]] material for Stockpiles 1 and 2.
*   **Stockpile 2 MSE Wall Footing Soil:** The [[dtsc]] and [[central-valley-rwqcb]] accepted the Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020. Soil down to 3 feet bgs was designated for containment zones, while soil below 3 feet bgs was approved for use as embankment fill or capping material. This material was considered part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan.
