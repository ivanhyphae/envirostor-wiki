## Caltrans Response March 23 2012

### Overview

The Caltrans Response dated March 23, 2012, addresses the Department of Toxic Substances Control's (DTSC) comments on Caltrans's Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the SR 132, Stanislaus County, California site. This response outlines Caltrans's proposed actions and clarifications regarding soil remediation.

### Site Record

Caltrans's response of March 23, 2012, addresses specific comments from the DTSC regarding the Remedial Action Plan (RAP). It confirms that Caltrans will implement a soil management plan that includes dust control measures during excavation and transport of soil. The response also details the proposed excavation depths for specific areas, including:

*   **Stockpile-1:** Excavation to a depth of 2 feet below the existing grade.
*   **Stockpile-3:** Excavation to a depth of 2 feet below the existing grade.

Caltrans also committed to installing [[no-trespassing-signs]] and [[perimeter-fences]] around the stockpiles to restrict access. The response indicates that soil exceeding the DTSC's screening levels will be managed as hazardous waste.

### Regulatory Status

The Caltrans Response March 23, 2012, is a communication between Caltrans and the DTSC regarding the approval of the Remedial Action Plan. The DTSC's comments, to which this response is directed, were issued on March 1, 2012. The response indicates Caltrans's intent to comply with DTSC's requirements for soil remediation.

### Open Items

The Caltrans Response March 23, 2012, addresses comments from the DTSC, indicating a process of review and refinement of the RAP. Specific open items or outstanding issues are not explicitly detailed within this document itself, but it represents a step in the ongoing regulatory review process.
