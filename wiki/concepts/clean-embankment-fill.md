## Clean Embankment Fill

Clean embankment fill is material suitable for use in constructing engineered fills and embankments, meeting specific chemical and physical criteria established for the SR 132 Stockpile 2 BCS Removal Tech Memo. In the context of the SR 132 project, the placement of clean embankment fill was the final step following the remediation of [[barium-containing-soil]] within Stockpile 2.

## Site Record

Following the excavation and verification testing of Stockpile 2, the material was deemed suitable for placement as clean embankment fill.

*   **Stockpile 2 West End Excavation:** Excavated to an approximate elevation of 82 feet above MSL. Eight soil samples (S2W 1-0 through 8-0) were collected on February 28, 2020. After initial analysis indicated elevated [[barium]] concentrations above the verification threshold at sample locations S2W 1-0 and S2W 3-0, the western half of the excavation was deepened to approximately 81 feet above MSL. Two additional verification soil samples were collected on March 6, 2020.
*   **Stockpile 2 East End Excavation:** The eastern half was excavated to approximately 83 feet above MSL. Six soil samples (S2E 1-0 through 8-0) were collected on February 21, 2020. Following elevated [[barium]] at S2E 3-0, that area and the western half of the excavation were deepened to approximately 82 feet above MSL. Seven additional samples (S2E 3A-0 and S2E 7-0 through 12-0) were collected on February 28, 2020. Sample SE2 8-0 still contained elevated [[barium]], leading to a further deepening to approximately 81 feet above MSL. One additional verification sample was collected on March 6, 2020.
*   **Verification Testing:** Soil samples were analyzed for [[barium]] and [[lead]] using EPA Test Method 6010B.
*   **Final Excavation Samples:** All final excavation soil samples contained [[barium]] and [[lead]] below the RDIP verification thresholds.
    *   [[barium]] concentrations ranged from 54 to 760 mg/kg.
    *   [[lead]] concentrations ranged from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg.
*   **Suitability for Clean Embankment Fill:** Based on the verification data, the BCS removal at both the west and east ends of Stockpile 2 met the [[barium]] and [[lead]] verification thresholds and was deemed suitable for placement of clean embankment fill. This conclusion is documented in the SR 132 Stockpile 2 BCS Removal Tech Memo.

## Regulatory Status

The remediation and verification testing were conducted to meet the [[barium]] and [[lead]] verification thresholds established for the project. These thresholds are:

*   [[barium]]: 1,000 mg/kg (groundwater protection)
*   [[lead]]: 80 mg/kg (residential screening level)

The final verification soil samples from the excavation of Stockpile 2 met these thresholds, indicating compliance with the remediation objectives for the placement of clean embankment fill. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) is the regulatory agency overseeing this project.
