## Overview
Chromium is a dissolved metal that has been monitored in groundwater at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. During the June 2014 groundwater monitoring event, chromium was detected in all ten groundwater samples collected. However, no reported concentration exceeded the applicable numeric water quality threshold for drinking water.

## Site record
In the June 2014 groundwater monitoring event, dissolved chromium was detected in all ten samples collected from site monitoring wells MW-1 through MW-10. The detected concentrations were reported within a range summarized in the [[june-2014-groundwater-monitoring-report]]. The report indicates that the aggregate range across the ten samples was provided in tabular form, but specific individual well concentrations for chromium are not itemized in the excerpt.

## Regulatory status
The applicable regulatory threshold for chromium in this investigation is the California Department of Public Health (CDPH) Primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for drinking water. The [[june-2014-groundwater-monitoring-report]] explicitly states that although chromium was detected in samples from each well during the June 2014 event, none of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water.
