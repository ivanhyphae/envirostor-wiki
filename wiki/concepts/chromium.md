## Chromium

Chromium (Cr) has been detected in groundwater and soil at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site.

## Site record

During the June 2014 groundwater monitoring event, dissolved chromium was detected in all ten groundwater samples collected from monitoring wells MW-1 through MW-10. The detected concentrations were reported within a range summarized in the [[june-2014-groundwater-monitoring-report]]. Specific individual well concentrations for chromium are not itemized in the provided excerpt.

In a letter dated May 29, 2012, [[caltrans]] informed the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) that soil analysis of Stockpile #3 indicated the presence of elevated metal concentrations above site-specific background levels. The letter did not specify the concentration of chromium in this soil.

## Regulatory status

The applicable regulatory threshold for chromium in groundwater at this site is the California Department of Public Health (CDPH) Primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for drinking water. The [[june-2014-groundwater-monitoring-report]] states that although chromium was detected in samples from each well during the June 2014 event, none of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water.
