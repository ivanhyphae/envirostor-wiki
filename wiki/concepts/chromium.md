## Overview

Chromium is a metallic element of regulatory concern at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, California). In this project record, chromium contamination exists in both soil and groundwater, primarily associated with historical industrial activities and stockpiled materials. The site’s chromium issues are documented in two primary sources: the *S9525-06-25 Modesto Stockpiles Well Install and June 2012 GW Report 1112.md* and the *S2350-01-02 Updated Statistical Evaluation Report_2.24.md*.

## Site record

### Soil concentrations

- In soil stockpiles, chromium concentrations were reported at up to **760 mg/kg** (source: S9525-06-25 Modesto Stockpiles Well Install and June 2012 GW Report 1112.md). This value exceeds typical background levels for the region and indicates anthropogenic contamination. The specific stockpile identifier and exact location within the site are not provided in the available sources.

### Groundwater monitoring

- Monitoring well **MW-5** has been a key sampling point for chromium in groundwater. The June 2012 groundwater report (S9525-06-25) documents that chromium in MW-5 exceeded the **Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) of 1,000 µg/L** established by the California Department of Public Health (now State Water Resources Control Board Division of Drinking Water). No specific concentration value above this MCL is given in the source for that date.

- The *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report_2.24* (source S2350-01-02) provides statistical evaluation of chromium data from multiple monitoring events. This report indicates that chromium concentrations at certain wells (including MW-5) have shown statistically significant trends but does not specify whether trends are increasing or decreasing. No exact p-values or trend magnitudes are documented in the source excerpt.

## Regulatory status

### Applicable standards

- **MCL for total chromium** in drinking water: **1,000 µg/L** (enforced by State Water Board, Division of Drinking Water). This is the primary regulatory threshold for groundwater at the site.

- **California Human Health Screening Level (CHHSL)** for chromium in soil: not explicitly cited in the provided documents, but the soil value of 760 mg/kg is compared against unspecified screening levels in regulatory correspondence.

- **Background thresholds**: The site’s *Statistical Evaluation Report* (S2350-01-02) references background concentrations for chromium in native soils of Stanislaus County, but specific background values are not stated in the source.

### Agency decisions

- **DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control)** is the lead regulatory agency for this site. In the June 2012 groundwater report, DTSC approved the installation of monitoring well MW-5 but required additional quarterly monitoring for chromium and other constituents. The source states DTSC conditioned approval on completion of four consecutive quarterly sampling events, but does not specify whether this condition was met.

- The current compliance status is **not documented** in the provided sources. No indication is given whether the site meets the chromium MCL or whether DTSC has issued a no-further-action letter.

## Open items

- **Trend evaluation**: The *Statistical Evaluation Report_2.24* (S2350-01-02) indicates ongoing statistical analysis of chromium trends, but no determination of whether chromium concentrations are stable, declining, or increasing is available from the record. A definitive trend conclusion may still be pending.

- **Soil remediation**: The source documents mention soil stockpiles with elevated chromium (760 mg/kg) but do not indicate whether these stockpiles were removed, capped, or left in place. The status of soil chromium remediation is unresolved.

- **Quarterly monitoring condition**: DTSC’s requirement for four consecutive quarterly groundwater sampling events (from the June 2012 GW report) may have been completed, but the record does not confirm this, nor does it provide results of those events.
