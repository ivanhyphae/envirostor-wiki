# Chromium

## Overview
Chromium is one of the dissolved metals monitored in groundwater at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). Under the [[caltrans]] remedial investigation, geocon-consultants included chromium in the analytical suite for the [[june-2014-groundwater-monitoring-report]]. Dissolved chromium was detected in each of the ten groundwater samples collected during that event, but no reported concentration exceeded the applicable numeric water quality threshold for drinking water.

## Site record
In the June 2014 groundwater monitoring event, dissolved chromium was reported for all ten samples collected from site monitoring wells. The detected concentrations fell within a documented range summarized in the monitoring report (GEO-CON 2014, Table summarizing arsenic, chromium, and vanadium results). No individual well-by-well concentrations for chromium are itemized in the available excerpt; only the aggregate range across the ten samples is provided in tabular form.

No chromium concentrations equaled or exceeded the California Department of Public Health (CDPH) Primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for drinking water.

## Regulatory status
The applicable regulatory threshold for chromium in this investigation is the CDPH Primary MCL for drinking water. As of the June 2014 sampling event, all reported dissolved chromium concentrations were below this MCL. The [[june-2014-groundwater-monitoring-report]] explicitly states that although chromium was reported in samples from each well, none of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water.
