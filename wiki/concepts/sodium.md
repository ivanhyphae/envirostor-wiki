## Overview
Sodium is a dissolved metal detected in samples collected as part of the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. Its presence is noted in relation to historical chemical manufacturing activities at the site.

## Site record
Sodium was reported for samples PL1 through PL5 in the October 2016 SW Sampling event. It was also reported for samples BG1 and BG2 during the same sampling event. In the March 2018 SW Sampling event, sodium was reported for some or all of the samples.

During the October 2016 sampling, sodium was among the dissolved metals reported for samples PL1 through PL5 and BG1 and BG2. The analytical laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL5, BG1, and BG2 required dilution for dissolved metals, including sodium, due to failing internal standards or possible matrix interference.

## Regulatory status
The provided sources do not specify any applicable Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) or other regulatory thresholds for sodium in relation to this project. The reported concentrations of sodium did not exceed any identified MCLs in the analyzed samples.
