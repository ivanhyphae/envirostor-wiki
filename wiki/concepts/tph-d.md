## Overview
TPH d (Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons as diesel) is a chemical of concern (COC) at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project along SR 132 in Stanislaus County, California. The project involves excavated soil stockpiles from roadway construction, with TPH d representing diesel-range hydrocarbons (typically C10–C28) that may pose risks to human health and the environment due to potential leaching to groundwater or direct exposure. TPH d is monitored under the Soil Management Plan (SMP) approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
