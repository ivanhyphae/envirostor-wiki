## No Significant Effect On The Environment

### Overview
The determination of "No Significant Effect On The Environment" is a finding made in relation to the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) process for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles Project. This determination signifies that the project, as proposed and implemented, is not expected to have a substantial or potentially significant adverse impact on the environment. This finding is typically documented in a [[notice-of-determination]].

### Site Record
The Modesto Soil Stockpiles Project, located at SR 132 in Stanislaus County, California, underwent environmental review. As part of this process, a determination was made that the project would have no significant effect on the environment. This conclusion was reached following the preparation of an [[environmental-impact-report-environmental-assessment]]. The project is part of the [[brownfields-and-environmental-restoration-program]] and involves remediation efforts overseen by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]]. The project is also referred to as the SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA case file.

### Regulatory Status
The finding of "No Significant Effect On The Environment" is a key outcome of the CEQA review process, as mandated by the [[public-resources-code]]. This determination allows for the issuance of a [[notice-of-determination]], indicating that the project can proceed without the need for a full Environmental Impact Report (EIR) if the initial assessment (Initial Study/Negative Declaration) demonstrates no significant impacts. The specific regulatory thresholds and criteria that led to this determination are detailed within the project's environmental assessment documentation.
