On January 30, 2026, [[dtsc]] approved the Storm Water Sampling Report (dated June 18, 2025, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of [[caltrans]]) for the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles site on State Route 132 in Modesto. The approval was issued in the same letter as DTSC's conditional approval of the separate Well Destruction Report, but the storm water approval itself carried no conditions.

The report evaluated storm water run-off collected during qualifying rain events to confirm that metals in the stockpile soil were not being displaced into surface water — a check on the physical integrity of the stockpile containment system rather than on groundwater. DTSC's letter states that the results were all within reporting limits, with no specific concentrations or sampling dates given in the letter itself.

DTSC's review was conducted by geologists from its Geological Services Branch (GSB) and toxicologists from its Human and Ecological Risk Office (HERO). Both disciplines' sign-off, and the absence of any GSB comment or condition attached to this report (unlike the well destruction report reviewed in the same letter), mark the storm water monitoring for FY24/25 as a closed item in DTSC's review.
