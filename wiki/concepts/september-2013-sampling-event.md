# September 2013 Sampling Event

## Overview
The September 2013 Sampling Event was a groundwater monitoring round conducted at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site under Caltrans Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44, EA No. 10-0X2700. Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Geocon Project No. S9525-01-44C) performed the work in accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), as established in the *Final Work Plan, Groundwater Assessment* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. and dated January 2006. The scope included depth-to-groundwater measurements, groundwater sample collection from ten monitoring wells, laboratory analysis by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of the *Groundwater Monitoring Report – September 2013*, transmitted to Caltrans on October 24, 2013.

## Site record
The September 2013 sampling event covered ten groundwater monitoring wells at the Site, located southerly of the intersection of State Route 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California. The specific wells monitored in this event are not individually enumerated in the transmittal letter constituting the available source material; however, the referenced related wells include [[mw-1]], [[mw-2]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-5]], [[mw-6]], [[mw-7]], and [[mw-9]]. Detailed concentration data, depth-to-groundwater measurements, analytical results, and any exceedances for this specific event are documented in the full report body, which is not included in the available source excerpt.

## Regulatory status
The sampling event was conducted under DTSC-approved protocols per the January 2006 *Final Work Plan, Groundwater Assessment*. The transmittal letter does not contain compliance determinations, regulatory thresholds, or agency correspondence regarding this specific monitoring round.

## Open items
The available source material is limited to the report transmittal letter. The full analytical results, data tables, groundwater elevation data, and any findings or recommendations for further action are contained in the body of the *Groundwater Monitoring Report – September 2013* but are not addressed in the transmittal letter excerpt.
