# September 2013 Sampling Event

## Overview
The September 2013 Sampling Event was a groundwater monitoring round conducted at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. This event was performed by Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Geocon Project No. S9525-01-44C) under Caltrans Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44, EA No. 10-0X2700. The sampling adhered to protocols established in the January 2006 *Final Work Plan, Groundwater Assessment* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc., and approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). The scope of work included measuring depth-to-groundwater, collecting groundwater samples from ten monitoring wells, and submitting these samples for laboratory analysis by a California-certified laboratory. The findings were compiled in the *Groundwater Monitoring Report – September 2013*, which was transmitted to Caltrans on October 24, 2013.

## Site record
During the September 2013 sampling event, groundwater samples were collected from ten monitoring wells located at the site, which is situated south of the intersection of State Route 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California.
