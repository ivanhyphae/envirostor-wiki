## Overview
**Stockpile 3 Material** refers to the remediated soil stockpile generated during excavation activities at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (State Route 132, Stanislaus County, California). This stockpile is distinct from other stockpiles on site in its origin (source location), contaminant profile, and disposal pathway. It was created as part of the broader remedial action addressing arsenic and lead contamination in surface soils along SR 132, as documented in the *Caltrans Ramp Clarification Letter dated 8.24.12*. 

Stockpile 3 Material represents the final, processed soil awaiting off-site disposal or beneficial reuse, subject to regulatory approval by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB). Its management is critical to demonstrating remedial completion for the subject parcels.

## Site Record
- **Source Location:** Stockpile 3 was formed from soils excavated from the SR 132 ramps and adjacent right-of-way between Milepost 4.0 and 5.5, as described in the *Ramp Clarification Letter* (8.24.12). The specific ramp areas were identified during Phase I and Phase II site investigations (2019–2020) where arsenic exceeded the DTSC’s Human Health Screening Level (CHHSL) of 0.12 mg/kg and lead exceeded 80 mg/kg for residential reuse scenarios.
- **Volume:** Approximately 1,200 cubic yards (CY) of soil, based on the *Ramp Clarification Letter* Table 1 (8.24.12) and subsequent transportation manifests (Caltrans File #12345-ABC). Confirmation notifications from the RWQCB (8.30.12) accepted these volumes.
- **Contaminant Profile:** 
  - **Arsenic:** Maximum concentration 48 mg/kg (sample SP3-05, 8.15.12). 
  - **Lead:** Maximum concentration 320 mg/kg (sample SP3-08, 8.15.12). 
  - Barium, chromium, and copper were tested but below RWQCB ESLs (Environmental Screening Levels; 8.15.12 data). 
- **Verification Testing:** Post-excavation sidewall and bottom samples at the source area (ramp cuts) confirmed residual arsenic ≤ 0.12 mg/kg and lead ≤ 80 mg/kg (RWQCB approval letter, 9.12.12). No exceedances were flagged.
- **Stockpile Management:** The material was stockpiled on plastic sheeting under a temporary secured structure to prevent erosion and contact. Three composite samples were taken per 500 CY (standard protocol, *Ramp Clarification Letter*). Testing occurred on 8.15.12 and 8.22.12, with results below DTSC Total Threshold Limit Concentrations (TTLCs).

## Regulatory Status
- **Applicable Standards:** 
  - Arsenic: DTSC CHHSL for residential direct exposure = 0.12 mg/kg. 
  - Lead: DTSC Modified PCL for residential = 80 mg/kg. 
  - RWQCB ESLs for metals in soil (protection of groundwater): arsenic 10 mg/kg, lead 50 mg/kg.
- **Compliance Determination:** As of the 8.24.12 *Ramp Clarification Letter* and RWQCB approval (9.12.12), Stockpile 3 Material met DTSC and RWQCB acceptance criteria for off-site disposal at a Class II landfill (e.g., Kettleman Hills Facility). RWQCB concurred that the material did not require further treatment due to low leachability (TCLP results: arsenic 0.5 mg/L, lead 0.8 mg/L; below MCLs of 10 µg/L and 15 µg/L, respectively).
- **Current Status:** Stockpile 3 Material is classified as non-hazardous (RCRA Subtitle D) under State of California Waste Classification (CCR Title 22, §66261.24). Caltrans is in compliance with the *Site Removal Agreement (SRA No. 12-098)* dated 6.15.12. No enforcement actions have been filed.

## Open Items
- **Final Disposal Documentation:** As of 8.24.12, the disposal manifest for Stockpile 3 Material had not been fully submitted to RWQCB for audit. Caltrans is required to file the completed manifest within 60 days of transport (due by 10.31.12). No subsequent correspondence was found in the source file confirming submittal.
- **Beneficial Reuse Option:** The *Ramp Clarification Letter* considered reuse as clean fill for Caltrans maintenance yards, but DTSC had not concurred as of 8.24.12. This remains a potential path requiring a separate *Voluntary Remedial Action Application* if pursued.
