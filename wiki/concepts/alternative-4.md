## Alternative 4: Containment

Alternative 4, referred to as "Containment," is the selected remedy for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles as described in the Draft Final Remedial-Action-Plan. This alternative involves containing the stockpiles by integrating them into the construction of the SR-132 West Freeway/Expressway Project. The containment is achieved through the construction of retaining walls, bridge abutments, and by placing the roadway pavement over the stockpiles. Unpaved areas will be covered with clean fill. This approach aims to protect human health and the environment by eliminating exposure pathways and minimizing surface water infiltration into groundwater beneath the stockpiles. It is considered cost-effective, technically feasible, and compliant with Applicable or Relevant and Appropriate Requirements (ARARs), meeting criteria for long-term effectiveness, reduction of mobility, short-term effectiveness, and implementability.

### Site Record

Under Alternative 4, the stockpiles are to be contained behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath the roadway pavement of the SR-132 West Freeway/Expressway Project. {{quote: "Unpaved portions will have clean fill cover."}} {{quote: "Draft Final RAP Alternative 4, Containment, which is the recommended alternative in the Draft Final RAP, contains stockpiles behind retaining walls, bridge abutments and beneath the pavement of the State Route 132 West project. Unpaved portions will have clean fill cover."}}

Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 are expected to remain in their current locations, with potential height increases using clean soil to meet the design grade of the elevated section of SR-132. {{quote: "Although Stockpiles 1 and 2 will remain in the present location they now occupy, increasing their height with clean soil will likely be needed to meet the design grade of the elevated section of SR 132."}} As planned, a significant portion of Stockpile 3 will be consolidated within the SR-132 Overcrossing abutment at Needham Avenue. Excess soil from this consolidation will be placed atop Stockpile 2 and covered with clean soil. {{quote: "As currently planned, most of Stockpile 3 will be consolidated within the SR 132 Overcrossing abutment where Needham Avenue meets SR 132. Excess soil from the consolidation of Stockpile 3 will be placed on top of Stockpile 2 and covered with clean soil."}}

During construction, to minimize dust and ensure public safety, all stockpile areas will be thoroughly wetted down before and during work. Air monitoring will also be required. {{quote: "To minimize dust and ensure public safety during construction, DTSC will require that all areas of the stockpiles be thoroughly wetted down before work is started and during work. Air monitoring will also be required."}}

The Department-of-Toxic-Substances-Control (DTSC) has stated that the stockpiles, as currently managed, do not pose an unacceptable risk to human health for Caltrans workers, trespassers, or adjacent residents. Current management includes maintaining perimeter fencing, limiting access to authorized workers, maintaining vegetative cover, conducting surface and groundwater monitoring, and prohibiting soil placement or removal. {{quote: "The stockpiles, as currently managed by Caltrans on Caltrans property, do not pose an unacceptable risk to human health for: 1) Caltrans workers; 2) trespassers; and 3) residents adjacent to the stockpiles. Current management activities consist of maintaining the perimeter fencing, limiting access to authorized Caltrans workers, maintaining the vegetative cover, surface water/groundwater monitoring, and prohibiting placement or removal of soil at the site. These measures are protective of human health."}}

The maximum surface soil concentrations of arsenic, carcinogenic polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), and vanadium are within local soil background ranges. Barium and nickel concentrations are below U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (U.S. EPA) safe levels. Strontium and nitrate are also detected at concentrations lower than safe levels. {{quote: "The maximum surface soil concentrations of arsenic, carcinogenic PAHs, and vanadium in the stockpiles are within the range of local soil background concentrations. The maximum surface soil concentrations of barium and nickel are less than concentrations established by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (U.S. EPA) to be safe. Strontium and nitrate, also identified as chemicals used by FMC, were detected in the stockpiles at concentrations lower than any level considered safe."}} Vibrations from traffic are not expected to cause contaminant migration from the stockpiles into groundwater. {{quote: "Vibrations from traffic will not cause contaminants to migrate from the stockpiles into the groundwater."}}

### Regulatory Status

Alternative 4 requires Caltrans to enter into an Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Agreement with the Department-of-Toxic-Substances-Control (DTSC) and prepare an O&M Plan for DTSC's review and approval. {{quote: "This alternative requires Caltrans to enter into an Operation and Maintenance Agreement with DTSC and prepare an Operation and Maintenance Plan for DTSC's review and approval."}} The O&M Plan will mandate annual inspections of the pavement and other containment features. Groundwater monitoring will continue, and DTSC will conduct evaluations of the containment remedy every five years to ensure it is functioning as designed. {{quote: "The Operation and Maintenance Plan will require an annual inspection of the pavement and other features of the containment remedy. Groundwater monitoring will also continue. DTSC will also evaluate the containment remedy every 5 years to make sure it is operating as designed."}}
