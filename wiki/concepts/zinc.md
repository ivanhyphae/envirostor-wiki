## Overview

Zinc is a metal that has been detected in stormwater samples collected as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. Its presence is noted in relation to regulatory standards for drinking water.

## Site Record

Zinc was detected in each of the storm water samples collected. The concentrations detected did not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs {{quote: "Barium, copper, and zinc were detected in each of the storm water samples at concentrations that do not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs."}}.

## Regulatory Status

Zinc is subject to Primary and Secondary drinking water MCLs {{quote: "Barium, copper, and zinc were detected in each of the storm water samples at concentrations that do not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs."}}. The detected concentrations in stormwater samples did not exceed these established limits {{quote: "Barium, copper, and zinc were detected in each of the storm water samples at concentrations that do not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs."}}.
