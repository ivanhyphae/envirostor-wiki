## Overview

Zinc is a metal detected in stormwater and surface water samples collected at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. Its presence has been evaluated against state and federal drinking water standards.

## Site Record

Zinc has been detected in stormwater and surface water samples collected during multiple sampling events. In the January 2018 sampling event, zinc was reported for some or all of the samples. Dilutions were required for zinc in samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 and BG2 due to high concentrations. In the March 2017 sampling event, zinc was reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5. In the March 10, 2023 surface water sampling event, zinc was detected in one or more of the samples. In the February 4, 2025 stormwater sampling event, zinc was detected in each of the storm water samples. In the October 2016 sampling event, zinc was reported for samples PL1 through PL5 and for samples BG1 and BG2.

## Regulatory Status

Zinc is subject to Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for drinking water. In the January 2018 sampling event, none of the reported concentrations of zinc exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs. In the March 2017 sampling event, none of the reported concentrations of zinc exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs. In the March 10, 2023 sampling event, the reported zinc detections were below their Primary and Secondary MCLs. In the February 4, 2025 sampling event, zinc was detected in each of the storm water samples at concentrations that do not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs. In the October 2016 sampling event, none of the reported concentrations of zinc exceeded their respective primary or secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs).
