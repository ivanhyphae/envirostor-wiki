## Overview

Zinc is a metal detected in stormwater samples collected at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. Its presence has been evaluated against state and federal drinking water standards.

## Site Record

Zinc has been detected in stormwater samples collected during multiple sampling events. In the January 2018 sampling event, zinc was reported for some or all of the samples {{quote: "Antimony, arsenic, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported for some or all of the samples."}}. Dilutions were required for zinc in samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 and BG2 due to high concentrations {{quote: "The laboratory notes state samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 and BG2 were diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc due to the high concentrations of the target analytes."}}. In the March 2017 sampling event, zinc was reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5 {{quote: "Zinc was reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5."}}. In the March 10, 2023 surface water sampling event, zinc was detected in one or more of the samples {{quote: "The following metals were detected in one or more of the surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023: arsenic, barium, chromium, copper, lead, molybdenum, selenium, vanadium, zinc, strontium, and mercury."}}. In the February 4, 2025 stormwater sampling event, zinc was detected in each of the storm water samples {{quote: "Barium, copper, and zinc were detected in each of the storm water samples at concentrations that do not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs."}}.

## Regulatory Status

Zinc is subject to Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for drinking water. In the January 2018 sampling event, none of the reported concentrations of zinc exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs {{quote: "None of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary maximum contaminant levels (MCLs) with the exception of manganese in sample BG2."}}. In the March 2017 sampling event, none of the reported concentrations of zinc exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs {{quote: "None of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs with the exception of manganese in samples PL2 and PL4."}}. In the March 10, 2023 sampling event, the reported zinc detections were below their Primary and Secondary MCLs {{quote: "The reported metal detections were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such exist."}}. In the February 4, 2025 sampling event, zinc was detected in each of the storm water samples at concentrations that do not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs {{quote: "Barium, copper, and zinc were detected in each of the storm water samples at concentrations that do not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs."}}.
