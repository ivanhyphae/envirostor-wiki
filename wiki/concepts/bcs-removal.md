BCS Removal refers to the excavation and relocation of barium-containing soil (BCS) from Stockpiles 2 and 3 within the SR 132 right-of-way, performed under the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP), with confirmation sampling used to verify that residual soil met RDIP thresholds before clean fill could be placed. The removal was carried out in discrete episodes tied to specific construction needs — bridge overcrossing footings, an MSE wall, and later utility/sign foundations — each generating its own verification or characterization dataset and, in two cases, formal [[dtsc]] sign-off before work could proceed.

## RDIP verification thresholds

Removal verification sampling at Stockpiles 2 and 3 was judged against two RDIP thresholds: [[barium]] at 1,000 mg/kg (groundwater protection) and lead at 80 mg/kg (residential screening level). These apply to samples collected from the base of excavations exposing native soil, analyzed by EPA Test Method 6010B.

## Stockpile 2 west and east end removals (Feb–Mar 2020)

BCS was removed from the west end of Stockpile 2 (adjacent to Emerald Avenue) and the east end (adjacent to SR 99) to allow construction of bridge overcrossing structures. Removed material was placed as embankment fill within the Stockpile 2 fill containment zones.

- **West end:** excavated to approximately 82 feet MSL; eight samples (S2W 1-0 through 8-0) collected February 28, 2020. Samples S2W 1-0 and S2W 3-0 exceeded the barium threshold, so the western half was deepened to approximately 81 feet MSL and resampled on March 6, 2020 (two additional samples).
- **East end:** the eastern half was excavated to approximately 83 feet MSL; six samples (S2E 1-0 through 8-0) collected February 21, 2020. Sample S2E 3-0 exceeded the barium threshold, so that area and the western half were excavated to approximately 82 feet MSL and resampled on February 28, 2020 (seven additional samples: S2E 3A-0 and S2E 7-0 through 12-0). Of these, only S2E 8-0 still exceeded, requiring further excavation to approximately 81 feet MSL and one additional sample on March 6, 2020.

Final results across both areas ranged from 54 to 760 mg/kg barium and 1.5 to 35 mg/kg lead — all below the 1,000 mg/kg and 80 mg/kg thresholds. This iterative deepen-and-resample pattern (each initial exceedance triggering roughly a 1-foot additional cut and a new sample) recurs at both ends of Stockpile 2 and again at Stockpile 3, indicating the native soil surface was not uniformly clean and required verification-driven adjustment rather than a single planned cut. [[dtsc]] (Dean Wright) reviewed the March 9, 2020 technical memorandum and, in a March 10, 2020 email, accepted it, agreeing the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 had met RDIP thresholds and were suitable for clean embankment fill placement.

## Stockpile 3 removal (Feb–Mar 2020)

Stockpile 3 BCS, located east of SR 99 between Kansas Avenue and the MID water canal, was excavated and trucked to the Stockpile 1 fill containment zone: 3,075 truck-loads, a preliminary estimate of approximately 38,440 cubic yards (at 12.5 cubic yards/truck). Excavation reached approximate elevations of 82.5 to 84 feet MSL. Twenty-four verification samples (S3 1-0 through S3 24-0) were collected between February 28 and March 12, 2020, plus two berm samples (S3 SE BERM, S3 SW BERM) at the southern end adjacent to the MID canal. Sample S3 16-0 was collected from storm drain backfill rather than native soil.

Samples S3 9-0 through S3 11-0 exceeded the barium threshold; those areas were deepened approximately one foot and resampled. Final results ranged from 46 to 970 mg/kg barium and 1.6 to 40 mg/kg lead, both below thresholds. [[dtsc]] (Dean Wright) accepted the March 13, 2020 memorandum on March 18, 2020, finding Stockpile 3 BCS removal complete with no further RDIP monitoring required, and the area suitable for clean embankment fill "and other highway improvements." The March 16, 2020 Caltrans transmittal (Richard Stewart) accompanying this approval request also sought agency sign-off to begin placing the minimum 12-inch clean soil cover and/or 4-inch aggregate base on the Stockpile 1 and 2 consolidation zones, and separately noted that BCS removal did not include the regulated ADL soil along northbound SR 99, which remained outside this action's scope.

## Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation (June 2020)

Following BCS removal along the Stockpile 2 southerly slope for MSE wall construction, characterization (not RDIP threshold verification) sampling was performed under a *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan* dated April 9, 2020. Seven trenches (STK2-1 through STK2-7) at approximately 200-foot intervals were sampled at 1-foot intervals to maximum footing depths of 3 and 5 feet (29 samples total). Barium ranged from 53 to 930 mg/kg — 14 of 29 samples exceeded the 120 mg/kg site-specific background level, 13 of those 14 from the surface to 3 feet deep. Lead ranged from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg, mostly near the 3.8 mg/kg background value. Excavated BCS material was transported for placement as fill within the Stockpile 2 containment zone.

Because this sampling was evaluated against background levels rather than the 1,000 mg/kg groundwater-protection threshold used for prior removals, the outcome was a placement restriction rather than a pass/fail verification: material from the upper 3 feet required placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones, while material from below 3 feet was suitable for placement within those zones, as general embankment fill, or as capping material pending regulatory approval.

## Stockpile 2 BCS foundation spoil (2021–2022)

Smaller-volume BCS spoils generated later from structure foundation work in the southern Stockpile 2 containment zone were managed under a February 28, 2022 workplan rather than RDIP verification excavation. The Weather Station Foundation (Station 1138+60) generated approximately 2 cubic yards from a 4.5-foot-deep excavation completed July/August 2021, spread on the adjacent ground surface. A planned Overhead Sign #1 foundation (Station 1139+30), a 5-foot-diameter by 25-foot-deep excavation, was estimated to generate approximately 18.2 cubic yards. Combined, a conservative 40 cubic yards was to be stockpiled under plastic sheeting pending disposal or reuse determination.

The workplan proposed compositing eight discrete grid samples into two four-part composites, analyzed for Title 22 metals (EPA Methods 6010B and 7471A for mercury), with WET/TCLP leaching tests reserved for samples with elevated totals. Results would be compared against site-specific background levels of 120 mg/kg barium and 3.8 mg/kg lead — the same background values used in the MSE wall characterization, not the 1,000/80 mg/kg RDIP removal thresholds. Onsite reuse was contingent on results not exceeding those background levels and on Caltrans/DTSC approval of the reuse location; absent that approval, material was to be profiled for disposal at Forward Landfill (Manteca) or another accepting facility. The sources do not report the analytical results of this sampling or whether reuse was approved.
