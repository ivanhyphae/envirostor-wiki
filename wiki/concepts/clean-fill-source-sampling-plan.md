## Clean Fill Source Sampling Plan

### Overview
The Clean Fill Source Sampling Plan was a deliverable developed for the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]] in [[modesto]], [[stanislaus-county]], California. Its purpose was to establish the procedures for sampling and analyzing potential sources of clean fill material intended for use in the remediation of the [[sr-132]] corridor, specifically within the [[carpenter-road-area]] and [[basin-5]]. The plan aimed to ensure that imported fill met project-specific criteria, thereby preventing the introduction of contaminants into the project site.

### Site Record
The "Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan" document details the methodology for evaluating potential clean fill sources. This plan specifies the sampling locations, the frequency of sampling, and the analytical parameters necessary for characterizing the fill material. It was developed to support the use of clean fill in remediation activities, particularly in areas such as [[basin-5]]. The plan contains specific information regarding the number of samples to be collected, the analytical methods to be employed, and the criteria for accepting the fill material.

### Regulatory Status
The use of clean fill material is subject to regulatory approval. The sampling plan serves as a foundational document to demonstrate compliance with these regulatory requirements, ensuring that imported materials do not pose a risk to human health or the environment.

### Open Items
Further details regarding the specific analytical results obtained under this sampling plan and their subsequent acceptance by regulatory agencies are expected to be found in subsequent project documentation.
