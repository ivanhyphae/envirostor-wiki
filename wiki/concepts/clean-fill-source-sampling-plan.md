## Clean Fill Source Sampling Plan

### Overview
The Clean Fill Source Sampling Plan was developed to guide the sampling and analysis of potential clean fill sources for the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]] in [[modesto]], [[stanislaus-county]], California. This plan is a component of the overall remediation strategy for the [[sr-132]] corridor, specifically addressing the [[carpenter-road-area]] and [[basin-5]]. The objective was to ensure that imported fill material met specified criteria to prevent the introduction of contaminants into the project area.

### Site Record
The "Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan" outlines the procedures for evaluating potential sources of clean fill. This plan details the sampling locations, frequency, and analytical parameters required to characterize the fill material. The plan was developed to support the use of clean fill in the remediation efforts, particularly in areas like [[basin-5]]. Specific details regarding the number of samples, analytical methods, and acceptance criteria are contained within the referenced plan.

### Regulatory Status
The use of clean fill is subject to regulatory approval, ensuring that imported materials do not pose a risk to human health or the environment. The sampling plan serves as a basis for demonstrating compliance with these requirements.

### Open Items
Further details on the specific analytical results obtained under this sampling plan and their acceptance by regulatory agencies would be found in subsequent project documentation.
