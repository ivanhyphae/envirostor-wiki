## Encapsulated Soil

### Overview

Encapsulated soil refers to soil that has been treated and contained to prevent the migration of contaminants. At the SR 132 project, this specifically applies to soil from [[stockpile-1]] that was determined to be [[barium-and-lead-impacted-soil]]. The encapsulation process was a component of the remediation strategy to manage this impacted soil.

### Site Record

Stockpile-1, identified by APN 005-020-016, contained soil impacted with barium and lead. Following a review of groundwater data, Caltrans determined that the soil in Stockpile-1 did not pose a threat to groundwater. This determination was documented in the "Approval of GW Statistical Evaluation" (Document 900259), issued by the DTSC on October 26, 2017. As a result of this approval, Caltrans was permitted to encapsulate the soil from Stockpile-1. The encapsulation was a condition for the [[discontinue-groundwater-monitoring]] at the site.

### Regulatory Status

The encapsulation of soil from Stockpile-1 was approved by the DTSC on October 26, 2017, as documented in the "Approval of GW Statistical Evaluation" (Document 900259). This approval was contingent on the finding of [[no-impact-to-groundwater]]. The encapsulation served as a management measure for the [[barium-and-lead-impacted-soil]] within Stockpile-1.
