## Pavement Conditions

The pavement conditions at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site are documented in the [[annual-operations-maintenance-site-inspection-form]]. These inspections assess the condition of paved surfaces related to the stockpiles, including highway surfaces and paved shoulders.

## Site Record

The "Annual Stockpile Inspection Form" includes photographic documentation and brief descriptions of pavement conditions. For Stockpile #1, the "Pave surface conditions Stock Pile #1 (looking east)" shows a concrete highway surface bordered by a metal guardrail. {{quote: "Pave surface conditions Stock Pile #1 (looking east)"}} The "North lane Stockpile #1" section also depicts a paved shoulder protected by a metal guardrail. {{quote: "North lane Stockpile #1"}}
