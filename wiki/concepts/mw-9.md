## Overview

MW-9 is a groundwater monitoring well installed at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It was installed in May 2012 by Geocon Consultants, Inc. to monitor groundwater quality.

## Site record

On May 29 and 30, 2012, two hollow-stem auger soil borings were advanced to facilitate the installation of groundwater monitoring wells MW-9 and MW-10. These wells were constructed to a depth of approximately 40 feet using 2-inch diameter Schedule 40 polyvinyl chloride (PVC) well casing and 10-foot-long, 0.010-inch-slotted screens. The screens were placed from approximately 29.5 to 39.5 feet. A #2/12 Monterey silica sand filter pack was placed from approximately 27.5 feet to the bottom of each boring. Following installation, each well was pre-developed by swabbing the saturated screen interval. Two-foot-thick bentonite well seals were placed above the filter packs, and the remaining annular space was grouted to the surface with a Portland cement slurry. A traffic-rated, flush-mounted well box set in concrete was installed at the surface.

On June 18, 2012, MW-9 and MW-10 were developed by hand-bailing and then purging with a submersible pump to remove silt and sediment and induce groundwater flow. Approximately ten well volumes of groundwater were extracted from each well.

On June 20, 2012, groundwater samples were collected from MW-9 and MW-10 after purging. Field measurements of pH, electrical conductivity, and temperature were recorded during purging. Dissolved oxygen (DO) and oxidation-reduction potential (ORP) were also measured.

Soil conditions encountered during the installation of MW-9 and MW-10, located north of the stockpiles adjacent to SR 99, consisted of interbedded layers of predominantly silt and sands to a depth of 40 feet. Some clay was observed at MW-10 at a depth of 30 feet. Groundwater was first encountered at a depth of 33.5 feet in MW-9.

General minerals analyses for groundwater samples from MW-9 collected in June 2012 indicated that the sample was calcium-dominant. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 13 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. Total dissolved solids (TDS) was reported at 510 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

In September 2012, MW-9 was sampled. The sample from MW-9 was calcium-dominant. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 22 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 630 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. Manganese was detected in the sample from MW-9.

In November 2012, MW-9 was sampled. The sample from MW-9 was calcium-dominant. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 24 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 640 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

In January 2013, MW-9 was sampled. The sample from MW-9 was calcium-dominant. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 30 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 680 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

In March 2013, MW-9 was sampled. The sample from MW-9 was calcium-dominant. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 30 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 700 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. Manganese was detected in the sample from MW-9.

In September 2013, MW-9 was sampled. The sample from MW-9 was calcium-dominant. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 36 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 750 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

In December 2013, MW-9 was sampled. The sample from MW-9 was sodium-dominant. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 25 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 630 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

In February 2014, MW-9 was sampled. The sample from MW-9 was calcium-dominant. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 34 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 750 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

In June 2014, MW-9 was sampled. The sample from MW-9 was calcium-dominant. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 29 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 680 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

In April 2019, MW-9 was dry.

## Regulatory status

Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-9 have exceeded the California primary MCL of 10 mg/l in multiple sampling events, including June 2012 (13 mg/l), September 2012 (22 mg/l), November 2012 (24 mg/l), January 2013 (30 mg/l), March 2013 (30 mg/l), September 2013 (36 mg/l), December 2013 (25 mg/l), February 2014 (34 mg/l), June 2014 (29 mg/l).

TDS concentrations in MW-9 have exceeded the California secondary MCL of 500 mg/l in multiple sampling events, including June 2012 (510 mg/l), September 2012 (630 mg/l), November 2012 (640 mg/l), January 2013 (680 mg/l), March 2013 (700 mg/l), September 2013 (750 mg/l), December 2013 (630 mg/l), February 2014 (750 mg/l), and June 2014 (680 mg/l).

Manganese was detected in the sample from MW-9 in September 2012 and March 2013.
