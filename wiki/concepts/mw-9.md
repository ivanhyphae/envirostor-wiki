MW-9 is a groundwater monitoring well installed at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It was installed in May 2012 to monitor groundwater quality in the vicinity of the soil stockpiles.

Manganese was detected in the groundwater sample from MW-9 in September 2012 and March 2013.

## Well Installation and Development

MW-9 was installed in May 2012 by Geocon Consultants, Inc. using hollow-stem auger borings to a depth of approximately 40 feet. It was constructed with 2-inch diameter Schedule 40 PVC well casing and a 10-foot-long, 0.010-inch-slotted screen placed from approximately 29.5 to 39.5 feet. A sand filter pack was installed from approximately 27.5 feet to the bottom of the boring, followed by a bentonite seal and Portland cement grout. A traffic-rated well box was installed at the surface.

On June 18, 2012, MW-9 was developed by hand-bailing and purging with a submersible pump to remove silt and sediment. Approximately ten well volumes of groundwater were extracted.

## Regulatory Status

Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-9 have consistently exceeded the California primary MCL of 10 mg/L in multiple sampling events from June 2012 through June 2014. Total dissolved solids (TDS) concentrations have also exceeded the California secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in these same sampling events.
