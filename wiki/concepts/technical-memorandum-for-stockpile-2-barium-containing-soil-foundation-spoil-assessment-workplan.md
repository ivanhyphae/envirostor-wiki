## Technical Memorandum For Stockpile 2 Barium Containing Soil Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan

### Overview
The Technical Memorandum For Stockpile 2 Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan, dated February 28, 2022, was submitted by Geocon Consultants Inc. on behalf of [[caltrans]] to the DTSC. This document outlines the planned sampling and management of BCS spoils generated during the installation of the Weather Station and Overhead Sign #1 at Stockpile-2 for the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California (Site Code: 900259).

### Site Record
The DTSC approved the stockpiling and sampling procedures detailed in the Technical Memorandum. The approval letter, dated March 4, 2022, states that if sampling results indicate concentrations of [[barium]] and lead are below established site-specific background levels, the material may be reused on-site at locations mutually agreed upon by both DTSC and [[caltrans]]. If sampling results show the spoils material is above background concentrations, the material is to be profiled for disposal at the Forward Landfill in Manteca or another acceptable landfill facility.

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC has concurred with the assessment workplan. The reuse or disposal of the BCS spoils is contingent upon sampling results relative to site-specific background concentrations for [[barium]] and lead.
