The Technical Memorandum For Stockpile 2 Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan, dated February 28, 2022, was submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] on behalf of [[caltrans]] to the DTSC. This document details the proposed sampling and management plan for BCS spoils generated during the installation of the Weather Station and Overhead Sign #1 at Stockpile-2 for the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California (Site Code: 900259).

### DTSC Approval and Conditions

The DTSC approved the stockpiling and sampling procedures outlined in the Technical Memorandum via an approval letter dated March 4, 2022. The approval stipulated that if sampling results for [[barium]] and lead concentrations are below established site-specific background levels, the BCS spoils may be reused on-site at locations mutually agreed upon by both DTSC and [[caltrans]]. Conversely, if sampling results indicate concentrations above background levels, the material is to be profiled for disposal at the Forward Landfill in Manteca or another acceptable landfill facility. The DTSC has concurred with the assessment workplan, making the reuse or disposal of the BCS spoils contingent upon these sampling results relative to site-specific background concentrations for [[barium]] and lead.
