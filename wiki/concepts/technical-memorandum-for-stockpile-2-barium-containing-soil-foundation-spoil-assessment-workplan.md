The Technical Memorandum For Stockpile 2 Barium Containing Soil Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan outlines the proposed sampling and analysis plan for soil within Stockpile 2, specifically addressing potential barium contamination related to the [[weather-station-foundation-and-overhead-sign-1-installation]].

## Site record

The [[weather-station-foundation-and-overhead-sign-1-installation]] project involved the placement of foundation spoil material within Stockpile 2. The Technical Memorandum For Stockpile 2 Barium Containing Soil Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan was developed to assess the barium content of this spoil material. The workplan was approved by the Department of Transportation (Caltrans) on January 26, 2023.

## Regulatory status

The assessment of Stockpile 2 spoil material is being conducted in relation to [[site-specific-background-levels]] for barium. Sampling results from the [[weather-station-foundation-and-overhead-sign-1-installation]] foundation spoil indicated [[barium-and-lead-sampling-results-below-site-specific-background-concentrations]].
