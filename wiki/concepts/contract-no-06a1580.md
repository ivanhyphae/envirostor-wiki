## Contract No 06a1580

### Overview
Contract No 06a1580 is associated with the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project. This contract is referenced in relation to groundwater monitoring activities and the assessment of specific analytes.

### Site Record
Groundwater monitoring data associated with Contract No 06a1580 includes measurements for barium and lead. In the March 2013 Groundwater Monitoring Report, barium was detected at MW-10 at a concentration of 1.3 mg/L. Lead was detected at MW-10 at a concentration of 0.013 mg/L in the same report. The June 2013 Groundwater Monitoring Report also references Contract No 06a1580, noting lead at MW-10 at 0.013 mg/L and barium at MW-10 at 1.3 mg/L.
