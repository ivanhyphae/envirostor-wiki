## DTSC Response March 1, 2012

### Overview

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) issued a response on March 1, 2012, regarding Caltrans's submittal of the "Response to DTSC Comments on the Draft Remedial Action Plan for Soil Stockpiles" dated February 2012. This response addressed DTSC's comments and outlined the path forward for the remediation of soil stockpiles at the SR 132 site in Stanislaus County.

### Site Record

On March 1, 2012, DTSC provided comments on Caltrans's February 2012 response to DTSC's comments on the Draft Remedial Action Plan (RAP). DTSC indicated that Caltrans's February 2012 submittal was responsive to DTSC's comments. DTSC requested that Caltrans address specific comments related to the RAP, including those concerning the need for additional soil sampling and analysis, and the proposed remediation strategy.

### Regulatory Status

DTSC is the regulatory agency overseeing the remediation of the soil stockpiles. The response dated March 1, 2012, is part of the ongoing regulatory review process for the Draft Remedial Action Plan.

### Open Items

As of the March 1, 2012 response, DTSC had outstanding comments that Caltrans needed to address before the Draft RAP could be finalized and approved. These comments pertained to specific technical aspects of the proposed remediation.
