# Douglas S. Krause

## Overview
Douglas S. Krause, CIH, is a Certified Industrial Hygienist at Geocon Consultants, Inc., who contributed technical expertise to the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project, specifically in the preparation of the Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan for the State Route 99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off-Ramp in Modesto, California. Krause's role is documented in the June 13, 2012 monitoring plan submitted under Project No. S9650-06-03 and Contract No. 06A1634 (EA No. 10-0A671), where he is identified as providing the certified industrial hygienist credentials alongside Project Manager [[john-e-juhrend]].

## Site record
Krause is listed as a co-signatory on the transmittal letter dated June 13, 2012, addressed to Mr. Richard Stewart, PG of Caltrans District 6. The letter was issued by geocon-consultants, which prepared and submitted the [[stockpile-3-excavation]] Monitoring Plan for the [[sr-99]] [[kansas-avenue-off-ramp]] improvements. Krause's specific professional certification — Certified Industrial Hygienist (CIH) — is noted in the signature block, indicating his role in providing industrial hygiene oversight for the plans described in the document, including air monitoring and verification procedures for excavations within Stockpile 3. No other project documents or site-specific actions directly attributed to Krause individually are available in the current source material.
