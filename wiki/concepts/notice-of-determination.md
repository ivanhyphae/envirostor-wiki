## Notice of Determination

A Notice of Determination (NOD) is a formal document filed by a public agency to announce its decision on a project subject to the [[california-environmental-quality-act]] (CEQA). It signifies whether the project is determined to have a significant effect on the environment and outlines the actions taken by the agency. For the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]], the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) filed a NOD following its approval of the project's [[remedial-action-plan]].

## Overview

The Notice of Determination (NOD) serves as the final CEQA documentation for the DTSC's involvement in the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. It is filed after the DTSC has approved the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and is contingent upon Caltrans certifying the Final Environmental Impact Report (EIR). The NOD indicates the DTSC's determination regarding the project's environmental impact and its compliance with CEQA requirements.

## Site Record

On March 19, 2018, the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) filed a Notice of Determination for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project. {{quote: "On March 19, 2018, the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) filed a Notice of Determination for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles Project."}} This filing followed the DTSC's approval of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the project on March 15, 2018. {{quote: "The DTSC approved the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the project on March 15, 2018."}} The NOD stated that the project would not have a significant effect on the environment. {{quote: "The Notice of Determination stated that the project will not have a significant effect on the environment."}}

The project described in the NOD involves the containment of approximately 160,000 cubic yards of soil containing metals, primarily barium, strontium, and lead, during the construction of the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project. {{quote: "The project consists of containment of three stockpiles totaling approximately 160,000 cubic yards of soil containing metals (primarily barium, strontium, and lead) during construction of the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project."}} The DTSC, as a CEQA Responsible Agency, utilized the EIR/EA prepared by Caltrans, the Lead Agency, for its CEQA analysis. {{quote: "Caltrans, acting as the Lead Agency under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA), prepared an Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment(EIR/EA) and Remedial Action Plan RAP). Caltrans certified the EIR/EA on March 2, 2018. DTSC, as a CEQA Responsible Agency, is utilizing the EIR/EA for the RAP CEQA analysis."}}

The DTSC made the following determinations as a Responsible Agency under CEQA:
*   The project will not have a significant effect on the environment. {{quote: "- 1. The project will not have a significant effect on the environment."}}
*   An Environmental Impact Report that included this project was prepared and certified by the California Department of Transportation pursuant to the provisions of CEQA. {{quote: "- 2. An Environmental Impact Report that included this project was prepared and certified by the California Department of Transportation pursuant to the provisions of CEQA."}}
*   Mitigation measures were made a condition of project approval. {{quote: "- 3. Mitigation measures were made a condition of project approval."}}
*   Findings were made pursuant to the provisions of CEQA. {{quote: "- 5. Findings were made pursuant to the provisions of CEQA."}}

The NOD also noted that a Statement of Overriding Considerations was not adopted for this project by the DTSC. {{quote: "- A *Statement of Overriding Considerations was not adopted for this project."}}

The final environmental document, comments and responses, and the record of project approval are available to the public at the Caltrans District 10 Office, the DTSC File Room, and online via Caltrans and the DTSC EnviroStor website. {{quote: "This is to certify that the final environmental document, comments and responses, and the record of project approval are available to the public at the following locations: Caltrans District 10 Office 1976 Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr. Blvd. Stockton, California 95205 (209) 948-7543 DTSC - File Room 8800 Cal Center Drive Sacramento, California 95826 (916) 255-3758 Caltrans - Online at: http://www.dot.ca.gov/d10/x-project-sr132west.html DTSC - EnviroStor website: http://www.envirostor.dtsc.ca.gov/public/profile report?global id=60001626 http://www.envirostor.dtsc.ca.gov/public/profile report?global id=50280024"}}

## Regulatory Status

The filing of the Notice of Determination by the DTSC signifies the completion of its CEQA review process for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. It confirms that the project has been reviewed and approved, with the determination that it will not have a significant effect on the environment. The NOD is filed pursuant to the [[public-resources-code]]. {{quote: "The Notice of Determination is filed pursuant to the Public Resources Code."}}
