## Notice of Determination

A Notice of Determination (NOD) is a document filed by a public agency that has made a decision on a project subject to the [[california-environmental-quality-act]] (CEQA). It indicates whether the project will have a significant effect on the environment and what actions the agency has taken. For the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]], a NOD was filed following the approval of the project.

## Site record

On March 19, 2018, the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) filed a Notice of Determination for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles Project. This filing was in response to the DTSC's approval of the project's [[remedial-action-plan]]. The NOD stated that the project would not have a significant effect on the environment. {{quote: "On March 19, 2018, the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) filed a Notice of Determination for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles Project. The DTSC approved the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the project on March 15, 2018. The Notice of Determination stated that the project will not have a significant effect on the environment."}}

## Regulatory status

The filing of the Notice of Determination signifies a regulatory milestone in the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]], indicating that the project has met CEQA requirements and has been deemed not to have a significant environmental impact. This action is a procedural step following the approval of the [[remedial-action-plan]] by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]]. The NOD is filed pursuant to the [[public-resources-code]]. {{quote: "The Notice of Determination is filed pursuant to the Public Resources Code."}}
