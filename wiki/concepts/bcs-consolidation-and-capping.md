## Bcs Consolidation and Capping

### Overview

Bcs Consolidation and Capping refers to a remedial action implemented at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This action involved consolidating soil containing [[barium]] and then covering it with a cap. The purpose was to manage soil impacted by [[aerially-deposited-lead]] and barium, particularly in areas designated for future construction or where excavation had occurred.

### Site Record

The [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project involved the management of soil stockpiles, some of which contained elevated levels of barium. As part of the remedial strategy, soil identified as "consolidated barium-containing soil" was subjected to consolidation and capping. The Interim Removal Action Completion Report, dated December 2022, details the completion of these actions. Specific volumes or concentrations of barium directly associated with the "Bcs Consolidation and Capping" action are not explicitly detailed in the provided source material beyond its identification as a remedial measure for barium-containing soil.

### Regulatory Status

The management of soil containing barium at the site was overseen by regulatory agencies including the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). Remedial actions, including consolidation and capping, were undertaken to address contaminant levels relative to applicable screening levels, such as the DTSC-Residential Screening Level. The specific regulatory approvals and compliance status for the Bcs Consolidation and Capping action are detailed within the project's remedial action plans and completion reports.
