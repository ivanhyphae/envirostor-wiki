The [[clean-fill-source-testing-exhibit]] details the proposed testing methodology for clean cover fill material intended for use in the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project. This exhibit is a component of the environmental management strategy for the project, specifically addressing the characterization of imported fill material.

## Site record

A proposed testing plan for clean cover fill sources was outlined for the Route 132 right-of-way (ROW) at and east of N. Carpenter Road. The plan specified a maximum excavation depth of 5 feet {{quote: "5' max cut depth"}}. It required the advancement of 12 borings to a depth of 5 feet {{quote: "12 borings to 5'"}}. From these borings, 3 composite samples were to be created, each representing a 4-point composite {{quote: "3 4-point composites"}}. The testing protocol included analysis for pesticides and metals in the 0-2 foot interval and metals in the 2-5 foot interval {{quote: "0-2' pesticides/metals"}}, {{quote: "2-5' metals"}}. This testing was associated with the SR 132 West - Freeway/Expressway Project, Alternative 1 {{quote: "SR 132 West - Freeway/Expressway Project, Alternative 1"}}.
