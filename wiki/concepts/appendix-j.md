## Appendix J Comments and Responses

Appendix J of the Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) for the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project contains the compiled public comments received during the comment period for the Draft EIR/EA and Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP). This appendix also includes the responses to these comments, prepared by Caltrans as the Lead Agency for CEQA, and by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) for comments pertaining to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles.

### Overview

Appendix J serves as the official record of public engagement and agency responses concerning the environmental review and proposed remedial actions for the SR 132 West Project, including the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. It documents the process by which public input was considered and integrated into the final project documentation. DTSC's responses within Appendix J are considered its formal response to comments on the Draft Final RAP.

### Site Record

The public comment period for the Draft EIR/EA and Draft Final RAP was held from January 18, 2017, to March 17, 2017. A joint public hearing was conducted on February 22, 2017. A total of 305 comments were received, with 62 specifically addressing the Draft Final RAP. Comments were submitted via letter, email, comment cards, and orally at the public hearing.

Appendix J includes responses to comments from one State agency, four Local and County agencies, 181 individual letters and emails, 66 public hearing comments, and 48 public hearing transcript comments. Eleven master responses were prepared to address similar comments submitted by multiple parties.

One commenter, Bruce R. Frohman, a former Modesto City Council Member, submitted comments on February 7, 2017, inquiring about changes to the project design since 2014 and specifically asking if the toxic waste soil stockpiles in the freeway right-of-way would be removed.

The appendix also contains information regarding the composition of the stockpiles, citing a DTSC document from August 2006 that describes the soil as "contaminated with barium, arsenic, and polynuclear aromatic hydrocarbons and soil containing petroleum hydrocarbons." Further details provided in the source material describe the hazards associated with barium, arsenic, and polynuclear aromatic hydrocarbons, including toxicity, carcinogenicity, and potential health effects. A Bureau of Mines report from 1961 is referenced, noting that FMC Corp. began producing barium hydroxide monohydrate at its Modesto plant.

### Regulatory Status

DTSC, in consultation with the RWQCB, responded to comments pertaining to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. Caltrans, as the Lead Agency for CEQA, concurred with DTSC's responses and incorporated them into Appendix J.

### Open Items

The provided source material does not explicitly detail any outstanding open items related to Appendix J itself, beyond the fact that it contains the responses to all comments received.
