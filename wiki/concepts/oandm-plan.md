The Operations and Maintenance (O&M) Plan is a critical component for the long-term management of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project, located along State Route 132 (SR 132) in Stanislaus County, California. This plan outlines the procedures for ongoing monitoring, maintenance, and reporting to ensure the effectiveness of the implemented remedy and to protect human health and the environment. The O&M Plan is a requirement stemming from the Remedial Action Workplan approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for soil stockpiles generated during the SR 132 widening project. These stockpiles contain soil with elevated levels of lead and barium, originating from historical vehicle emissions and regional agricultural practices. The selected remedy involves consolidation and capping of these impacted soils, with the O&M Plan governing the post-remediation oversight.

## Site record

The O&M Plan specifically addresses barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles that were consolidated, capped with clean fill material, and utilized as highway embankment fill during the construction of Interim Phase 1 of the SR 132 West Project. {{quote: "This O&M Plan has been prepared for barium-containing soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 through 3 that were consolidated, capped with clean fill material, and utilized as highway embankment fill during Interim Phase 1 construction of the SR 132 West Project in accordance with the 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) and Caltrans-approved project design plans and specifications."}} The stockpiles are located within designated Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones, adjacent to and extending west of SR 99. {{quote: "BCS Stockpiles 1 through 3, portions of which contain elevated levels of barium and lead, have been contained during Interim Phase 1 of the project by utilizing them as clean capped embankment material within the overall limits of BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2 located adjacent and extending westerly of SR 99 to beyond Emerald Avenue (Preconstruction Site Plan, Figure 2)."}}

The O&M Plan details procedures for managing BCS material within the Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones should it be disturbed. {{quote: "This section describes procedures to be implemented should BCS material within the Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones (Restricted Areas) be disturbed."}} Caltrans is responsible for providing a minimum of ten working days advance notification to DTSC before any planned activities that may disturb BCS material or associated clean cover fill. {{quote: "Caltrans is responsible for minimum ten working day advance notification to DTSC of any planned activities that may disturb BCS material and/or associated clean cover fill materials."}} No disturbance is permitted without prior written authorization from DTSC. {{quote: "No BCS disturbance is permitted without prior written authorization from DTSC."}}

Annual inspections are a key component of the O&M Plan. The O&M Site Inspection Form is used by field crews to document the condition of the cap/SR 132 Freeway. {{quote: "Included with the Draft O&M Plan was a blank copy of the O&M Site Inspection Form that will be used by field crews conducting inspections to document the condition of the cap/SR 132 Freeway."}} DTSC recommends that inspections also occur after events such as fires, substantial rainfall (1 inch in 24 hours), significant seismic events, or traffic accidents that could damage the cap. {{quote: "DTSC recommends this section be revised to indicate that in addition to the annual inspections, inspections will also occur after events such as fires, substantial rainfall (1 inch in 24 hours), significant seismic events, traffic accidents that may cause damage to the cap, etc."}} Periodic patrols should also note any unauthorized access, such as homeless encampments, that could impact the cap. {{quote: "The periodic patrols referenced in this section should also note if unauthorized access such has a homeless encampment has resulted in any impact to the cap."}}

The O&M Plan was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of Caltrans and submitted in December 2022. {{quote: "GEOCON PROJECT NO. S1908-01-01 DECEMBER 2022"}} {{quote: "OPERATIONS AND MAINTENANCE PLAN CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES SR 132 WEST PROJECT - INTERIM PHASE 1"}} Comments from DTSC dated August 1, 2022, on the Draft O&M Plan dated March 2022, were addressed in the final version. {{quote: "Comments provided by the DTSC in writing dated August 1, 2022 on the Draft O&M Plan dated March 2022 have been addressed in this O&M Plan."}}

Annual inspection reports are submitted to DTSC for approval. For example, the Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report dated January 26, 2026, which documented an inspection at State Route 132 West Expressway at the State Route 132/99 Interchange, was approved by DTSC. {{quote: "Thank you for your submission of the Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report (Report) dated January 26, 2026, which outlines the outcome of an inspection conducted at State Route 132 West Expressway at the State Route 132/99 Interchange."}} {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)'s Project Manager and Engineering and Special Projects Office (ESPO) completed their review and based upon DTSC's review of the report, it is hereby approved and accepted as final."}} The report noted that vegetation was still sparse along the road, and that leftover erosion control materials were limiting erosion. {{quote: "The vegetation is still sparse along the road, which was noted during last year's report. It appears that leftover erosion control materials are limiting erosion currently."}}

## Regulatory status

The O&M Plan is prepared in accordance with the requirements of the DTSC, which is the lead regulatory agency overseeing the project. {{quote: "The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in cooperation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB), is the lead regulatory agency providing site mitigation oversight under a Caltrans Interagency Agreement."}} The plan is a deliverable for the Remedial Action Completion Report. {{quote: "The Draft O&M Plan was submitted by Geocon Consultants Inc. on behalf of the Department of Transportation and outlines the activities associated with the inspection, maintenance, and repair of the portion of State Route 132 that is utilized as the clean cap cover over two barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles."}} DTSC retains the authority to modify O&M requirements based on monitoring results and site conditions. {{quote: "DTSC retains authority to modify requirements based on monitoring results and site conditions over time."}}

The Land Use Covenant (LUC) also mandates annual inspections and reporting. The owner is required to conduct an annual inspection verifying compliance with the Covenant and submit a report to the Department by January 18th of each year. {{quote: "In accordance with the June 20, 2023 O&M Plan, Exhibit C, or its amendments or revisions that have been approved by the Department, the Owner shall conduct an annual inspection of the Property verifying compliance with this Covenant and shall submit an annual inspection report to the Department for its approval by January 18th of each year."}}

## Open items

The duration and specific monitoring frequencies for the O&M Plan were not fully finalized in the initial documentation. {{quote: "The plan duration and specific monitoring frequencies have not yet been finalized in the available record."}} While annual inspections are standard, DTSC recommends additional inspections after specific events like fires, significant rainfall, seismic events, or traffic accidents. {{quote: "DTSC recommends this section be revised to indicate that in addition to the annual inspections, inspections will also occur after events such as fires, substantial rainfall (1 inch in 24 hours), significant seismic events, traffic accidents that may cause damage to the cap, etc."}}
