Caltrans SR 132 99 Task Order 4 is a specific work authorization under a larger contract for the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. This task order specifically addresses the remediation of soil stockpiles associated with the project.

The record establishes that the soil stockpiles were generated during the widening of SR 132. These stockpiles were located at the former Modesto Reservoir Pump Station site. The remediation plan for these stockpiles was developed by G. Scott McGowen, Inc. and submitted to the [[dtsc]] for review.

The remediation plan proposed by G. Scott McGowen, Inc. involved the excavation and off-site disposal of contaminated soil. The plan identified specific contaminants of concern within the stockpiles, including lead and arsenic. The proposed excavation depth was to be determined based on site-specific risk assessments and regulatory requirements.

The [[dtsc]] reviewed the remediation plan and provided comments. Richard Stewart, PG, of the [[dtsc]], communicated these comments to Caltrans. The remediation plan was subject to approval by the [[dtsc]] before implementation.
