## Water Quality Objectives

### Overview
Water Quality Objectives (WQOs) are benchmarks used to assess the quality of water and determine if it meets specific standards for various uses. In the context of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, WQOs are relevant for evaluating the potential impact of soil stockpiles on groundwater.

### Site Record
The 132Stockpile Fact Sheet Modesto DTSC 117 (2-2-17) document, dated February 2, 2017, indicates that the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) has established WQOs for the site. These objectives are used to assess the potential for constituents in the soil stockpiles to migrate to groundwater. Specific WQOs are not detailed in this fact sheet, but the document implies their use in the evaluation process.

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC has established WQOs for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. These objectives are part of the regulatory framework governing the remediation of the stockpiles. The fact sheet does not specify whether current conditions meet these WQOs, but it indicates that the DTSC is overseeing the process.
