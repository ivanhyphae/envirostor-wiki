## Rick Demi

Rick Demi is identified as the Construction Manager for WSP/City of Modesto in relation to the State Route 132 Project in Modesto, California. He received a Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021, from John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon, concerning the characterization testing of clean fill material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly. This memorandum detailed the suitability of excavated soil for use as clean capping material over barium-containing soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2, pending approval from the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).

### Site Record

On April 12, 2021, Rick Demi received a Technical Memorandum summarizing the results of clean fill characterization testing performed on approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil from the Carpenter Road Shoofly. This soil was intended for use as clean capping material for BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2. Twenty-four discrete soil samples were collected from the shoofly and composited into eight 3-part samples for analysis. Barium was detected in all analyzed samples at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 analyzed samples at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg.

### Regulatory Status

The Technical Memorandum of April 12, 2021, concluded that the soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly met the BCS clean cap criteria, as approved by the DTSC. This criterion was based on an upper-bound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg. The lead concentrations were also reported as less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg.
