## Overview
Rick Demi is identified as the Construction Manager for WSP/City of Modesto on the State Route 132 Project in Modesto, California. He received several Technical Memoranda related to the characterization and remediation of barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles and borrow areas.

## Site record
Rick Demi received a Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021, regarding Carpenter Road Shoofly clean fill characterization testing. This memo detailed the contractor's plan to use approximately 5,000 cubic yards of excavated native soil from the Carpenter Road Shoofly as clean capping material for portions of BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2, pending approval from the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).

He also received Technical Memoranda concerning clean cap borrow area characterization testing for Basin 5, dated July 2, 2020, and for the general BCS Stockpiles area adjacent and easterly of Carpenter Road, dated February 27, 2020. Additionally, Demi received memoranda regarding BCS removal verification testing for Stockpile 2, dated March 9, 2020, and for Stockpile 3, dated March 13, 2020.

The soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly excavation, analyzed for barium and lead, yielded barium concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) and lead concentrations from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg. These concentrations were below the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg and less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg, respectively. Based on these results, the soil meets the BCS clean cap criteria.

The proposed Basin 5 clean cap borrow area, sampled on June 16, 2020, yielded barium concentrations ranging from 66 to 94 mg/kg, consistent with the site-specific background concentration for barium of 72.8 mg/kg. The proposed clean cap borrow area adjacent and easterly of Carpenter Road, sampled on December 21, 2020, yielded barium concentrations ranging from 60 to 110 mg/kg, also consistent with the site-specific background concentration for barium of 72.8 mg/kg.

## Regulatory status
The soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly excavation meets the BCS clean cap criteria, defined by an upper-bound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg. This criteria was established in a variance document approved by the DTSC.
