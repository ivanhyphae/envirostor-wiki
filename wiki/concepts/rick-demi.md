Rick Demi is identified as the Construction Manager for WSP/City of Modesto on the State Route 132 Project in Modesto, California. {{quote: "To: Rick Demi, Construction Manager WSP/City of Modesto"}} He received a Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021, regarding Carpenter Road Shoofly clean fill characterization testing. {{quote: "Date: April 12, 2021 To: Rick Demi, Construction Manager WSP/City of Modesto"}} This memo detailed the contractor's plan to use approximately 5,000 cubic yards of excavated native soil from the Carpenter Road Shoofly as clean capping material for portions of barium-containing soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2, pending California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) approval. {{quote: "Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil will be excavated from the Carpenter Road Shoofly once the overcrossing structure under construction is completed. The contractor plans to use the excavated soil material as clean capping material to be placed over portions of barium containing soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2 pending California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) approval."}}

Demi also received Technical Memoranda regarding clean cap borrow area characterization testing for Basin 5 and the general BCS Stockpiles area, both dated in 2020. {{quote: "Date: Revised July 2, 2020 To: Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager WSP/City of Modesto"}} {{quote: "Date: February 27, 2020 To: Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager WSP/City of Modesto"}} He also received memoranda concerning BCS removal verification testing for Stockpile 2 and Stockpile 3, both dated in March 2020. {{quote: "Date: March 9, 2020 To: Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager WSP/City of Modesto"}} {{quote: "Date: March 13, 2020 To: Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager WSP/City of Modesto"}}

## Site record

Twenty-four discrete soil samples were collected from the Carpenter Road Shoofly along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6). Samples were taken at depths of 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet from the top of the slope. {{quote: "We collected 24 discrete soil samples from the Carpenter Road Shoofly along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6) as depicted on the attached exhibit. Soil samples were collected from the surface of the soil material exposed on the slope face at 2, 5, 10 and 15 feet from the top of the slope."}} These were composited into eight 3-part samples and analyzed for barium and lead. {{quote: "The laboratory was instructed to composite three discrete soil samples from the same elevation along each slope face of the shoofly resulting in eight 3-part composite samples. The composite soil samples were analyzed for barium and lead..."}}

Barium was detected in all 10 analyzed soil samples at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). {{quote: "Barium was detected in each of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg)"}} These concentrations were below the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "less than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg."}} Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 samples at concentrations from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, which were less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg."}}

Based on these analytical results, the soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly excavation meets the BCS clean cap criteria, as established in a referenced variance document approved by DTSC. {{quote: "Based on the barium and lead data presented herein, soil material generated from excavation of the Carpenter Road Shoofly meet the BCS clean cap criteria as established in the referenced variance document as approved by DTSC (upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg)."}} This information is to be included in the Removal Action Completion Report following the capping activities. {{quote: "The information presented herein will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted following completion of the Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities."}}

The proposed Basin 5 clean cap borrow area, sampled on June 16, 2020, yielded barium concentrations ranging from 66 to 94 mg/kg. {{quote: "Barium 66 to 94 (72.8 mg/kg)"}} These concentrations are consistent with site-specific background concentrations for barium of 72.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "Barium 66 to 94 (72.8 mg/kg)"}}

The proposed clean cap borrow area adjacent and easterly of Carpenter Road, sampled on December 21, 2020, yielded barium concentrations ranging from 60 to 110 mg/kg. {{quote: "Barium 60 to 110 (72.8 mg/kg)"}} These concentrations are consistent with site-specific background concentrations for barium of 72.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "Barium 60 to 110 (72.8 mg/kg)"}}

## Regulatory status

The soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly excavation meets the BCS clean cap criteria, defined by an upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "(upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg)"}} This criteria was established in a variance document approved by the DTSC. {{quote: "as established in the referenced variance document as approved by DTSC"}}
