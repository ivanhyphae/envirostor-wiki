The Stockpile 2 Spoils Workplan governs the sampling and disposition of excavation spoils generated when a Weather Station and Overhead Sign #1 were installed on [[stockpile-2]] at the SR 132 West project. [[geocon-consultants]] submitted the underlying Technical Memorandum, "Stockpile 2 Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan," dated February 28, 2022, on behalf of [[caltrans]], and [[dtsc]] approved it by letter dated March 4, 2022 (Site Code: 900259). The workplan matters because it sets a bright-line, results-driven test for whether the spoils can stay on-site or must leave as waste — the outcome turns entirely on how the excavated material compares to site-specific background for barium and lead.

## Approval and its conditions

DTSC's March 4, 2022 letter, signed by Dean Wright, PG, of the Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, concurred with the stockpiling and sampling procedures described in the Tech Memo and established a two-branch disposition path for the spoils:

- If sampling results show [[barium]] and lead concentrations **below** established site-specific background, the material may be reused on-site, at locations mutually agreed upon by DTSC and Caltrans.
- If sampling results show concentrations **above** background, the material must be profiled for disposal at the Forward Landfill in Manteca or another acceptable landfill facility.

The letter does not state the numeric background values for barium or lead, nor does it report the actual sampling results for the Stockpile 2 spoils. The approval is therefore conditional in form: it authorizes the sampling procedure and predetermines the disposition rule, but leaves the operative question — reuse or landfill disposal — to be answered by results not contained in this record.

## What is not established here

This letter documents the approval of the sampling plan, not its execution. It does not record the sample results, which disposition path was triggered, whether spoils were reused on-site or profiled for disposal at Forward Landfill, or any closure confirming the spoils were handled consistent with the approved workplan. Any determination that the Stockpile 2 excavation spoils were fully and correctly resolved would depend on a subsequent record not present in this file.
