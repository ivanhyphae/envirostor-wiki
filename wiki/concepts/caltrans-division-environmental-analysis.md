# Caltrans Division of Environmental Analysis

## Overview

The Caltrans Division of Environmental Analysis (DEA) served as the lead agency responsible for environmental compliance, regulatory coordination, and oversight of investigation and remediation activities at the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles site in Stanislaus County, California. DEA directed the site characterization efforts, managed the stockpile sampling and analysis program, and coordinated with regulatory agencies including the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) regarding disposal characterization and off-site waste management decisions.

## Site record

DEA oversaw the investigation of soil stockpiles generated during construction activities on State Route 132. The stockpiles, characterized through sampling and laboratory analysis, contained constituents of concern including aerially deposited lead (ADL) from historical exhaust emissions.

DEA managed the sampling program that determined lead concentrations in the stockpiled material. Analytical results from composite sampling conducted under DEA direction were compared against regulatory thresholds to determine appropriate disposal classification. The characterization effort included evaluation of total lead concentrations and soluble threshold limit concentration (STLC) testing where applicable under Title 22 of the California Code of Regulations.

DEA coordinated the technical documentation including the sampling and analysis plan, the stockpile characterization report, and waste profile documentation required for off-site disposal. The agency communicated analytical results and disposal recommendations to DTSC for concurrence on waste classification determinations.

## Regulatory status

DEA operated under the regulatory framework of the California Health and Safety Code and Title 22 California Code of Regulations for hazardous waste characterization. The primary thresholds applicable to the stockpile material included:

- **Total lead**: Compared against the federal Toxicity Characteristic Leaching Procedure (TCLP) threshold and California total threshold limit concentration (TTLC) of 1,000 mg/kg for lead
- **Soluble lead**: Evaluated against the California STLC of 5 mg/L

DEA communicated with DTSC regarding the disposal classification of the ADL-impacted soil based on analytical results from the stockpile characterization program.
