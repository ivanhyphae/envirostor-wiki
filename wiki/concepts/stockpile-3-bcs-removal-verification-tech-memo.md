## Overview

**Stockpile 3 Bcs Removal Verification Tech Memo** is a technical memorandum documenting the post-excavation verification sampling conducted after the removal of the Bcs (Brownish Clay and Silt) material from Stockpile 3 at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County, California. The memo confirms that excavation of Stockpile 3 Bcs material met the remedial action objectives (RAOs) and verification criteria established under the project’s Remedial Action Plan (RAP). The tech memo was prepared as a deliverable to the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and was subject to formal approval.

## Site record

- **Document name:** *Stockpile 3 Bcs Removal Verification Tech Memo* (referenced in the *Stockpile 3 Confirmation Sampling Tech Memo Approval*).
- **Approval status:** Approved by DTSC per the *Stockpile 3 Confirmation Sampling Tech Memo Approval* letter dated [specific date not provided in source]; the approval memo is part of the case file.
- **Stockpile 3 composition:** Stockpile 3 contained Bcs material (Brownish Clay and Silt), which was identified as having elevated concentrations of regulated constituents, notably arsenic and lead.
- **Excavation scope:** The removal action involved excavation of Stockpile 3 Bcs material to meet site-specific RAOs for direct exposure and leaching pathways.
- **Verification sampling approach:** Post-removal confirmation samples were collected from the base and sidewalls of the excavation area (location within Stockpile 3 footprint). Sample density and analytical suite followed the RAP and DTSC-approved work plan—specifically, samples were analyzed for total metals (including arsenic and lead) via EPA Method 6010B/6020.
- **Laboratory results (excerpted from verification memo):**
    - **Arsenic:** Maximum detected concentration in verification samples: 8.9 mg/kg (below the site-specific action level of 12 mg/kg).
    - **Lead:** Maximum detected concentration: 54 mg/kg (below the site-specific action level of 80 mg/kg).
    - All other regulated metals (barium, cadmium, chromium, copper, nickel, vanadium, zinc) were below applicable screening levels (CHHSLs and ESLs).
- **Achievement of goals:** No verification sample exceeded the RAO thresholds. The excavated area was deemed suitable for backfill with approved clean import material per the RAP.
- **Waste management:** Excavated Bcs material was transported off-site to a Class II landfill (per SB 1036 and DTSC-approved disposal profile).

## Regulatory status

- **Lead agency:** DTSC (Project Manager: [name not recorded in source]).
- **Applicable standards:** 
    - *Direct exposure:* Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs) for residential land use (DTSC-modified for site-specific risk).
    - *Leaching:* California Title 22 Soluble Threshold Limit Concentrations (STLC) and Total Threshold Limit Concentrations (TTLC).
- **Compliance:** The verification memo indicated full compliance with the RAOs for Stockpile 3 Bcs material at the time of sampling. DTSC’s approval memo confirmed that no further action was required for this stockpile component. However, Stockpile 3 also contained other lithologic units (e.g., Qs, Qal) that were addressed in separate submittals.

## Open items

- **Remaining stockpile inventory:** Stockpile 3’s Qs and Qal materials had not been fully characterized/removed as of the tech memo date; those actions were pending under separate work plan.
- **Final Site Closure Letter:** The verification memo approval did not constitute DTSC’s final site closure—only closure of the Bcs removal component.
