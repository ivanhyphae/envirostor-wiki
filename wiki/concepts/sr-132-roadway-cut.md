## SR 132 Roadway Cut

The SR 132 Roadway Cut refers to a section of the SR 132 project area in Stanislaus County, California, where excavation exposed native formation. This area was previously tested and found to be non-impacted. The sampling plan for Basin 5, located adjacent and north of this roadway cut, was based on the conditions observed at the SR 132 Roadway Cut.

## Site Record

The SR 132 Roadway Cut section east of Carpenter Avenue was previously tested, and the results indicated non-impacted native formation. A technical memorandum detailing sampling methodology and comparative background metal concentrations for this area was submitted previously.

## Regulatory Status

The SR 132 Roadway Cut area was determined to be non-impacted based on prior testing.

## Open Items

A technical memorandum detailing sampling methodology and tables of comparative background metal concentrations for the SR 132 Roadway Cut area was previously submitted.
