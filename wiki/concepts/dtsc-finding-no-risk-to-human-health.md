## DTSC Finding No Risk To Human Health

### Overview
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) has determined that the soil stockpiles associated with the [[state-route-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]] do not pose a risk to human health under current management conditions. This determination was made after reviewing soil sampling results provided by the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans).

### Site Record
The DTSC reviewed soil sampling results for the soil stockpiles located on Caltrans property in the vicinity of Kansas Avenue and State Route (SR) 99 in Modesto, Stanislaus County. The DTSC's finding, communicated in correspondence dated March 1, 2012, stated: "The Department has reviewed the soil sampling results and determined that the soil stockpiles do not pose a risk to human health." This finding specifically addressed the risk to:
*   Caltrans workers who access the fenced site to conduct mowing operations, conduct fence repairs, or other routine activities.
*   Trespassers.
*   Residents adjacent to the stockpiles.

The DTSC's review of these soil stockpiles began in January 2005, conducted via Interagency Agreements and Task Orders with Caltrans.

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC's finding of no risk to human health was predicated on specific conditions. Correspondence dated December 17, 2009, stated: "DTSC finds soil stockpiles, as currently managed by Caltrans on Caltrans property, do not pose a risk to human health for: 1) Caltrans workers who access the fenced site to conduct mowing operations, conduct fence repairs, or other routine activities; 2) trespassers; and 3) residents adjacent to the stockpiles." However, these findings were explicitly stated to be in effect "Until such time that the SR 132/99 Interchange project is constructed."
