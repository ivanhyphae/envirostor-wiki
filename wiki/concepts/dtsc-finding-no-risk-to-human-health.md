## DTSC Finding No Risk To Human Health

### Overview
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) has determined that the soil stockpiles associated with the [[state-route-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]] do not pose a risk to human health under current management conditions. This determination was made after reviewing soil sampling results provided by the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans).

### Site Record
The DTSC reviewed soil sampling results for the [[sr-132-west-expressway-freeway-soil-stockpiles]] located on Caltrans property near Kansas Avenue and State Route (SR) 99 in Modesto, Stanislaus County. {{quote: "The Department has reviewed the soil sampling results and determined that the soil stockpiles do not pose a risk to human health."}} This finding was communicated in DTSC correspondence dated March 1, 2012. {{quote: "03.01.2012 SR 132 west Expressway Followup5_srb.RA"}}

The DTSC's finding specifically addressed the risk to:
*   Caltrans workers accessing the fenced site for routine activities such as mowing or fence repairs. {{quote: "Caltrans workers who access the fenced site to conduct mowing operations, conduct fence repairs, or other routine activities"}}
*   Trespassers. {{quote: "trespassers"}}
*   Residents adjacent to the stockpiles. {{quote: "residents adjacent to the stockpiles"}}

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC, acting under Interagency Agreements and Task Orders with Caltrans, reviewed reports related to the characterization of these soil stockpiles. {{quote: "Beginning in January 2005 the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), via Interagency Agreements and Task Orders with the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans), reviewed reports related to the characterization of soil stockpiles on Caltrans "right-of-way" property in the vicinity of Kansas Avenue and State Route (SR) 99 in Modesto, Stanislaus County."}} The DTSC's determination of no risk to human health is a key component of the regulatory oversight for these stockpiles. {{quote: "The Department has reviewed the soil sampling results and determined that the soil stockpiles do not pose a risk to human health."}}

This finding was predicated on specific conditions, as stated in DTSC correspondence dated December 17, 2009: {{quote: "DTSC finds soil stockpiles, as currently managed by Caltrans on Caltrans property, do not pose a risk to human health for: 1) Caltrans workers who access the fenced site to conduct mowing operations, conduct fence repairs, or other routine activities; 2) trespassers; and 3) residents adjacent to the stockpiles". However, in this same communication the above DTSC findings are predicated upon the following conditions: "Until such time that the SR 132/99 Interchange project is constructed"}}
