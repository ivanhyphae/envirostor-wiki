## DTSC Finding No Risk To Human Health

### Overview
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) has determined that the soil stockpiles associated with the SR 132 West Expressway project do not pose a risk to human health. This determination is based on the evaluation of soil samples collected from the stockpiles.

### Site Record
Soil samples were collected from the SR 132 West Expressway freeway soil stockpiles. The DTSC reviewed these samples and concluded that they did not present a risk to human health. Specifically, the DTSC stated, "The Department has reviewed the soil sampling results and determined that the soil stockpiles do not pose a risk to human health." {{quote: "The Department has reviewed the soil sampling results and determined that the soil stockpiles do not pose a risk to human health."}} This finding was documented in correspondence dated March 1, 2012. {{quote: "03.01.2012 SR 132 west Expressway Followup5_srb.RA"}}

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC, as the regulatory agency overseeing the project, made the determination of no risk to human health. This finding is a key component of the regulatory review process for the soil stockpiles.
