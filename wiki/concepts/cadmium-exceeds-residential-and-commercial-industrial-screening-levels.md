## Cadmium Exceeds Residential and Commercial Industrial Screening Levels

### Overview
Cadmium was identified as a chemical of concern at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, located along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County, California. Concentrations of cadmium were found to exceed applicable screening levels for residential and commercial industrial land use scenarios.

### Site Record
During the Supplemental Site Investigation (SSI) conducted by Caltrans District 6, soil samples were collected and analyzed for various constituents. The SSI Report, dated March 13, 2013, documented the presence of cadmium. Specific concentrations and locations where cadmium exceeded screening levels are detailed within the report. The report indicates that cadmium was among the chemicals of concern requiring further evaluation.

### Regulatory Status
The screening levels for residential and commercial industrial land uses were exceeded by cadmium concentrations found at the site. These screening levels are established to protect human health under different land use assumptions. The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) is the regulatory agency overseeing the remediation efforts.

### Open Items
Further investigation and potential remediation strategies for areas impacted by cadmium exceeding screening levels are implied by the findings in the SSI Report. The specific actions required to address these exceedances would be detailed in subsequent project documentation or regulatory correspondence.
