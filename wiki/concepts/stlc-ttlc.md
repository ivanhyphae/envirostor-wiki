The STLC (Soluble Threshold Limit Concentration) and TTLC (Total Threshold Limit Concentration) are regulatory standards used to classify soil as hazardous waste in California. These thresholds are critical for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, as they dictate how excavated soil must be managed and disposed of. Soil exceeding either the STLC or TTLC for specific contaminants requires special handling and disposal procedures.

## Surface Water Sampling Reports

The geocon-consultants-inc. firm has conducted surface water sampling at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles on multiple occasions, as documented in their reports. These reports detail sampling activities performed in accordance with approved protocols.

*   **October 28, 2016:** Surface water sampling was conducted at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, located south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California. The sampling followed protocols established in the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)* and an addendum thereto.
*   **March 24, 2017:** Surface water sampling was performed at the same site, adhering to the previously approved sampling and analysis plans.
*   **January 8, 2018:** Further surface water sampling was carried out at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, following the established protocols.

These sampling events were conducted under Caltrans Contract No. 06A2184, Task Order No. 1, EA No. 10-0X2700.

## Regulatory Framework

The STLC and TTLC standards are derived from California regulations, specifically [[title-22-mcl]]. These regulations are applied to soil samples collected during site investigations and remediation activities for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. Documents such as the *Final Feasibility Study Report* and the *Draft Final Remedial Action Plan* for the project would utilize these thresholds to evaluate excavated soil for off-site disposal or reuse. The specific STLC and TTLC values for contaminants of concern at the site are detailed within these project documents.
