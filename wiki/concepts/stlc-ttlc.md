The terms STLC and TTLC are abbreviations used in the context of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. They refer to regulatory thresholds for the leachability and total content of hazardous substances in soil, respectively. These are key metrics used in assessing the potential environmental impact and determining appropriate remediation strategies for contaminated soil.

## Site record

The STLC and TTLC thresholds are derived from California regulations, specifically [[title-22-mcl]]. These regulations are applied to soil samples collected during site investigations and remediation activities. For example, the S9800-01-17 Modesto Soil Stockpiles Final FS Report.0614 document lists "STLC" and "TTLC" as abbreviations, indicating their relevance to the project's environmental assessments. The S1200-01-01 Final Caltrans Modesto Stockpile RAP_03.18 and S9800-01-17 Modesto Stockpiles Draft Final Remedial Action Plan.Revised_10.14 documents, which outline the Remedial Action Plan for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles, would utilize these thresholds to evaluate the suitability of excavated soil for off-site disposal or reuse.

## Regulatory status

STLC and TTLC are regulatory standards established by the state of California to classify soil as hazardous waste. Soil exceeding these thresholds requires specific management and disposal procedures. The [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, managed by [[caltrans]] and often involving consultants like Geocon-Consultants-Inc., must adhere to these standards as part of the [[ceqa]] (CEQA) and other relevant environmental regulations. The specific values for STLC and TTLC for various contaminants of concern at the site would be detailed in the project's Feasibility Study and Remedial Action Plan documents.
