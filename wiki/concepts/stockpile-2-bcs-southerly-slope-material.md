## Stockpile 2 Bcs Southerly Slope Material

### Overview
This entry pertains to soil material located on the southerly slope of Stockpile 2 BCS, a soil stockpile associated with the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. This material was subject to sampling and analysis as part of the [[mse-wall-footing-sampling-plan]].

### Site Record
Sampling of the southerly slope material of Stockpile 2 BCS was conducted. The [[mse-wall-footing-sampling-plan]] indicated that once the Stockpile 2 BCS southerly slope material was removed, the wall footing excavation sampling in that area would be completed. The results of this sampling are documented within the project's technical memorandums and acceptance emails.

### Regulatory Status
The regulatory status of the Stockpile 2 Bcs Southerly Slope Material is tied to the overall remediation efforts overseen by regulatory agencies such as the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]]. Specific compliance or exceedance data for this particular material is detailed within the referenced sampling plans and subsequent reports.
