## Site Specific Background Concentrations

### Overview

Site-specific background concentrations are established thresholds used to evaluate contaminant levels at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, specifically for [[barium]] and [[lead]]. These values are critical for determining whether detected concentrations of these metals in soil samples exceed naturally occurring levels at the site, thereby informing remediation decisions.

### Site Record

The Technical Memorandum for Stockpile 2 Barium-Containing Soil Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan (undated) established site-specific background concentrations for barium and lead. The approved workplan for Stockpile 2 Spoils Workplan (900259), dated January 26, 2023, references these established site-specific background concentrations. Specifically, the site-specific background concentration for barium was determined to be 1,000 mg/kg, and for lead, it was 200 mg/kg.

Sampling results for Stockpile 2, as documented in the Approval Letter Stockpile 2 Spoils Workplan (900259) dated January 26, 2023, indicated that barium concentrations ranged from 10 mg/kg to 1,100 mg/kg, and lead concentrations ranged from 10 mg/kg to 200 mg/kg. The approval letter further states that barium and lead sampling results were below site-specific background concentrations, with the exception of one barium result at 1,100 mg/kg, which was noted as being slightly above the site-specific background concentration of 1,000 mg/kg.

### Regulatory Status

The site-specific background concentrations for barium (1,000 mg/kg) and lead (200 mg/kg) serve as the benchmark for evaluating soil quality at the site. The Approval Letter Stockpile 2 Spoils Workplan (900259) indicates that most sampling results were below these thresholds, signifying compliance with the established background levels. However, the single exceedance of barium at 1,100 mg/kg required specific consideration within the project's remediation strategy.
