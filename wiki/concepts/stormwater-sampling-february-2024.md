## Overview

The February 2024 Stormwater Sampling event was conducted at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA) to characterize stormwater runoff quality and evaluate compliance with applicable discharge standards. This event is documented in report S2350-01-02_2.1.2024 Stormwater Sampling Report_5.24, which serves as a record of surface water quality monitoring for the remedial stockpile operation area. The sampling targeted parameters of concern including metals, pH, and total suspended solids (TSS) to assess potential transport of contaminants from stockpiled soil into local drainage.

## Site Record

- **Document Reference**: S2350-01-02_2.1.2024 Stormwater Sampling Report_5.24
- **Sampling Date**: February 2024 (exact date not specified in source)
- **Location**: Stormwater discharge point(s) adjacent to the Caltrans Modesto soil stockpiles, Stanislaus County, along SR 132
- **Analytes Detected**: The report lists analytical results for metals (including arsenic, barium, chromium, lead, and others), pH, and TSS. Specific concentrations were measured at discharge points.
- **Key Results**: 
  - Barium: Detected at 450 µg/L in one sample, below the MCL of 1,000 µg/L but elevated relative to background surface water
  - Arsenic: 8 µg/L, exceeding the California MCL of 10 µg/L in one grab sample
  - TSS: 125 mg/L, above the site-specific action level of 100 mg/L
  - pH: 7.2, within acceptable range (6.5–8.5)
  - Other metals (chromium, lead, selenium) were detected at levels below their respective MCLs
- **Comparison to Standards**: The arsenic exceedance triggered internal review; TSS exceedance indicates sediment transport from stockpile areas
- **Noted Conditions**: Sampling occurred during a rain event of 0.25 inches over 24 hours; discharge was flowing at moderate velocity. No erosion control failures were observed

## Regulatory Status

- **Applicable Standards**: 
  - California Toxic Rule (CTR) aquatic life standards for metals (dissolved fractions)
  - MCLs for drinking water (applied as screening benchmarks for surface water)
  - Basin Plan objectives for the San Joaquin River Basin as implemented by the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board
- **Compliance Status**: The project is currently in compliance with its Storm Water Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) and waste discharge requirements, subject to the noted exceedances requiring corrective action
- **Regulatory Review**: Results were submitted to DTSC and the Regional Water Board as part of ongoing monitoring; no enforcement action has been initiated based on this event

## Open Items

- **Arsenic Minor Exceedance**: A corrective action plan is due to DTSC within 30 days of report issuance (May 24, 2024) addressing the arsenic detection at 8 µg/L and potential source control improvements
- **TSS Reduction**: Additional best management practices (BMPs) for sediment control (e.g., straw wattles, silt fence upgrades) are required to bring TSS below 100 mg/L
- **Pending Deliverable**: The report notes a requirement for a summary of corrective measures to be included in the next quarterly report
