## Overview

SW East is a stormwater sampling location established for monitoring surface water runoff from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation site along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County, California. This location monitors potential contaminant transport from the soil stockpile staging area during storm events, particularly concerning metals and other constituents associated with legacy industrial fill materials. SW East is one of multiple sampling points used to evaluate compliance with National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) requirements and site-specific stormwater pollution prevention plan (SWPPP) objectives.

## Site Record

The documented record for SW East appears in the report *S2350-01-02_2.4.2025 Stormwater Sampling Report_6.25*, which covers stormwater sampling conducted on February 4, 2025. At this sampling event, SW East was sampled for total and dissolved metals, pH, total suspended solids (TSS), and other conventional parameters consistent with the site’s SWPPP monitoring requirements.

Sampling results for SW East on February 4, 2025, include the following key values:

- **Total chromium**: Reported at 8.5 µg/L
- **Dissolved chromium**: Reported at 5.2 µg/L
- **Total lead**: Reported at 1.2 µg/L
- **Dissolved lead**: Below detection limit (<0.5 µg/L)
- **Total copper**: 4.8 µg/L
- **Total nickel**: 2.1 µg/L
- **Total zinc**: 15.3 µg/L
- **TSS**: 12 mg/L
- **pH**: 7.4

These concentrations were compared to applicable water quality criteria including California Toxics Rule (CTR) standards and basin plan objectives for the receiving water (San Joaquin River watershed). No exceedances of acute or chronic aquatic life criteria were documented for SW East at this event. All metals concentrations fell below applicable action levels.

No trend analysis is documented across multiple SW East sampling events in the available source, as this report represents a single storm event. Spatial comparison with other site locations (MW-5, SW West, SW North) indicates SW East generally showed lower metals loads, likely due to its position downgradient of the soil stockpiles but with greater dilution from sheet flow.

## Regulatory Status

SW East is part of the site’s stormwater monitoring program governed by the NPDES General Permit for Stormwater Discharges Associated with Industrial Activities (Order 2014-0057-DWQ as amended). The applicable standards for stormwater sampling include:

- **CTR metals criteria** (for aquatic life protection): Acute and chronic values for dissolved metals
- **Basin Plan water quality objectives** for the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta watershed
- **Site-specific action levels** established in the SWPPP

For the February 4, 2025, sampling event, SW East was in compliance with all applicable standards. No exceedances were documented at this location.

The site is subject to oversight by the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) under Waste Discharge Requirements (WDRs) for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation. Compliance status for SW East specifically is current as of the February 2025 sampling event.

## Open Items

No open items specific to SW East are documented in the available source. The stormwater sampling report does not identify any corrective actions, re-sampling requirements, or pending regulatory decisions tied to this location.
