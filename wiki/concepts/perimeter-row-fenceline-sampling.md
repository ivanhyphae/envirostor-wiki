## Perimeter Row Fenceline Sampling

### Overview
Perimeter Row Fenceline Sampling refers to a specific sampling strategy employed at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This method involves collecting soil samples along the perimeter of stockpiles to assess potential contaminant migration and ensure compliance with regulatory requirements. The sampling was conducted as part of the overall site investigation and remediation efforts under [[project-no-s9525-06-44]].

### Site Record
The [[sampling-and-analysis-plan]] for [[project-no-s9525-06-44]] outlines the procedures for Perimeter Row Fenceline Sampling. This sampling was performed by [[geocon-consultants]] as part of their work for Caltrans. The objective was to collect samples from the perimeter of the soil stockpiles.

The workplan details the collection of samples from the perimeter of the stockpiles. Specifically, it states: {{quote: "Perimeter Row Fenceline Sampling will be conducted on the perimeter of the stockpiles."}} The workplan also notes that the sampling was to be conducted in accordance with the [[sampling-and-analysis-plan]].

### Regulatory Status
The Perimeter Row Fenceline Sampling is a component of the broader investigation and remediation efforts at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site, which is overseen by regulatory agencies. The results of this sampling inform compliance with applicable standards and requirements set forth in documents such as the [[ea-no-10-403500]].
