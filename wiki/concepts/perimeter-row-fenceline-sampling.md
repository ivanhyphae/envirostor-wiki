## Perimeter Row Fenceline Sampling

### Overview
Perimeter Row Fenceline Sampling is a specific sampling methodology employed at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This technique involves collecting soil samples along the outer boundary of soil stockpiles. The primary purpose of this sampling is to assess potential contaminant migration from the stockpiles and to ensure adherence to regulatory requirements. This sampling effort is a component of the broader investigation and remediation activities conducted under [[project-no-s9525-06-44]].

### Site Record
The [[sampling-and-analysis-plan]] for [[project-no-s9525-06-44]] details the procedures for Perimeter Row Fenceline Sampling. This sampling was executed by [[geocon-consultants]] as part of their contracted work for Caltrans. The workplan explicitly states that "Perimeter Row Fenceline Sampling will be conducted on the perimeter of the stockpiles." The sampling was to be performed in accordance with the procedures outlined in the [[sampling-and-analysis-plan]].

### Regulatory Status
Perimeter Row Fenceline Sampling is integrated into the overall investigation and remediation framework for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This site is under the purview of regulatory agencies, and the data generated from this sampling informs compliance with applicable standards and requirements, as referenced in documents such as the [[ea-no-10-403500]].
