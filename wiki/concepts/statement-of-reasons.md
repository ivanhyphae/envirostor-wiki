## Overview

The **Statement Of Reasons** in the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles case is a formal document included within the Revised Administrative Record, as transmitted by Caltrans to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) on May 18, 2015. Its purpose is to set forth the factual and legal basis for the remedial or removal action decisions that Caltrans and DTSC determined were necessary to address contaminated soil stockpiles along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County. The Statement of Reasons serves as the regulatory justification for the selected response action, linking site conditions to applicable thresholds and agency authorities.  

## Site record

The existence and transmittal of the Statement of Reasons are established solely by the source document:  

- **Document**: *Final Caltrans Cover Letter Revised Administrative Record Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles*, dated May 18, 2015.  
- The cover letter confirms that the Revised Administrative Record (including the Statement of Reasons) was submitted to DTSC for review and concurrence.  
- The cover letter does not reproduce the Statement of Reasons itself, and no additional project‑specific values (concentrations, dates, volumes) are available from this single source document.  

Based on the record as transmitted, the Statement of Reasons is presumed to reference the contamination profile of the stockpiles (e.g., barium, arsenic, lead), the exposure pathways, and the statutory basis (Health and Safety Code, CEQA, etc.) for the removal action. However, those details are not explicitly stated in the cover letter.  

## Regulatory status

- The Statement of Reasons was prepared in support of Caltrans’ request for DTSC’s concurrence on the Revised Administrative Record.  
- The cover letter indicates that DTSC approval was pending as of May 18, 2015.  
- Applicable standards and thresholds (e.g., MCLs, DTSC screening levels) that the Statement of Reasons would rely upon are not enumerated in the cover letter.  

## Open items

- The actual contents of the Statement of Reasons have not been disclosed in the sole source provided; a full review of the Revised Administrative Record would be required.  
- DTSC’s formal response to the May 18, 2015 submittal is not documented in the cover letter and remains an unresolved deliverable.
