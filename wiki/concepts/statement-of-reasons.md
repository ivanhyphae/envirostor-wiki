The Statement of Reasons (SOR) is a document that provides the agency's justification for its regulatory decisions. For the [[state-route-132-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, the SOR would explain the rationale behind the [[dtsc]]'s actions concerning the soil stockpiles.

The Administrative Record for the [[state-route-132-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project includes a "Final Caltrans Cover Letter Revised Administrative Record Caltrans Modesto SoilStockpiles" dated May 18, 2015. This cover letter references the Revised Administrative Record, indicating that it contains the necessary documentation, including any Statements of Reasons that may have been issued. However, the cover letter itself does not present the content of a Statement of Reasons.
