## Overview
Basin 5 is one of five former stormwater detention basins at the [[caltrans]] [[sr-132]] project site in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. These basins were repurposed as soil stockpile areas during right-of-way acquisition and construction. Basin 5's soil was characterized as a potential source of clean fill material for capping [[bcs-stockpiles-1-and-2]].

## Site record
A [[basin-5-clean-fill-sampling-plan]], dated June 11, 2020, outlined the sampling and analysis for soil intended for capping the State Route 132 stockpiles. On June 16, 2020, six exploratory trenches (BASIN5 1 through 6) were excavated by the highway contractor to a maximum depth of 10 feet within the borrow area. Soil samples were collected at depth intervals of 0 to 2 feet, 2 to 5 feet, and 5 to 10 feet. These samples were field composited into 3-part composites, yielding two 3-part composite samples for each depth interval.

Analysis of these composite samples reported the following concentration ranges (in milligrams per kilogram [mg/kg]) for Title 22 metals, with site-specific background concentrations in parentheses:

*   Arsenic: <1.0 to 1.8 (1.2 mg/kg)
*   [[barium]]: 66 to 94 (72.8 mg/kg)
*   Chromium: 8.5 to 11 (8.6 mg/kg)
*   Cobalt: 3.3 to 3.8 (4.4 mg/kg)
*   Copper: 7.5 to 9.3 (7.5 mg/kg)
*   [[lead]]: 5.8 to 11 (2.0 mg/kg)
*   Nickel: 6.2 to 7.9 (5.3 mg/kg)
*   Vanadium: 33 to 37 (31.3 mg/kg)
*   Zinc: 29 to 36 (26.3 mg/kg)

Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, molybdenum, selenium, silver, thallium, mercury, and organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) were not detected above laboratory reporting limits in any composite soil sample. The reported metal concentrations were found to be consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. Based on this data, the Basin 5 clean cap borrow area was deemed suitable for generating clean cover fill materials for capping [[bcs-stockpiles-1-and-2]]. This information was intended for inclusion in the [[interim-removal-action-completion-report]].

## Regulatory status
The [[basin-5-clean-fill-sampling-plan]], dated June 11, 2020, was approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the Waterboard via email on June 12, 2020.

A Revised Technical Memorandum (Revised Memo) dated July 2, 2020, detailing the Basin 5 clean cap borrow area soil characterization, was reviewed by DTSC and the Water Board. Concerns regarding chromium concentrations were addressed through additional information provided by Caltrans and [[geocon-consultants]]. On July 9, 2020, DTSC communicated via email their acceptance of the Revised Memo, agreeing that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and suitable as clean cover material for capping [[stockpile-1]] and [[bcs-stockpiles-1-and-2]].
