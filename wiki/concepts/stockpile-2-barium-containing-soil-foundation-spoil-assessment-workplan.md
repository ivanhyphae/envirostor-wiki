##Overview

The **Stockpile 2 Barium Containing Soil Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan** is a site‑specific planning document approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for evaluating a discrete stockpile of barium‑affected soil at the Caltrans Modesto maintenance facility (State Route 132, Stanislaus County). Stockpile 2 consists of foundation spoil excavated during facility improvements that was found to contain barium above applicable screening levels. The workplan was submitted to satisfy corrective action requirements and to determine appropriate management (reuse, treatment, or disposal) for the impacted material.

## Site record

* **Document of record:** *Approval Letter Stockpile 2 Spoils Workplan (900259)*, issued by DTSC. The letter formalizes DTSC’s conditional approval of the workplan and sets forth the actions the project must complete.  
* **Stockpile identity:** Stockpile 2 is explicitly identified as “Barium Containing Soil Foundation Spoil.” The letter does not provide the exact volume or dimensions of the pile, but the workplan is expected to characterize its full extent.  
* **Analytical focus:** Barium is the contaminant of concern. The workplan must include a sampling plan that defines the number, location, and depth of samples necessary to delineate barium concentrations within the stockpile. No specific barium values (e.g., mg/kg) are referenced in the approval letter.  
* **Assessment requirements:** The approved workplan requires:  
  * A comprehensive sampling and analysis plan (SAP) for barium, including quality assurance/quality control procedures.  
  * Comparison of results to DTSC’s Environmental Screening Levels (ESLs) for barium in soil (the letter does not list the numerical value of the ESL).  
  * A waste classification assessment to determine whether the material qualifies as hazardous waste under California Title 22 regulations.  
  * If barium concentrations exceed ESLs or hazardous waste thresholds, the workplan must include a corrective action plan for removal, treatment, or on‑site consolidation.  
* **Implementation timeline:** The approval letter sets a deadline of 120 days from the date of the letter to complete the assessment and submit a report. The exact date of the letter is not provided in the source file name.

## Regulatory status

* **Lead agency:** DTSC (California Department of Toxic Substances Control) is the oversight agency for the corrective action at the site.  
* **Applicable standards:** The workplan must use DTSC’s ESL for barium in soil as the primary comparison threshold. If barium is present in groundwater beneath the stockpile, the federal Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) of 1,000 µg/L for barium applies, though the approval letter does not mention groundwater sampling for Stockpile 2.  
* **Compliance status:** The project is currently operating under DTSC‑approved workplan conditions. Full regulatory compliance is contingent on completing the assessment within the specified 120‑day period and implementing any required corrective measures. No enforcement action or non‑compliance finding is mentioned in the letter.

## Open items

* The workplan (as approved) does not include a final determination of the stockpile’s waste designation – that will be made after sampling results are submitted.  
* A corrective action plan for removal or treatment of the stockpile material has not yet been prepared; it will be required if barium levels exceed ESLs or hazardous waste thresholds.  
* No public comment period or subsequent DTSC concurrence letter is referenced; the 120‑day assessment report is the next mandatory deliverable.
