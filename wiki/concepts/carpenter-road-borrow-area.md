## Overview

The **Carpenter Road Borrow Area** is a designated soil source within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project (State Route 132, Stanislaus County, California). It was used to supply clean backfill material during the excavation and removal of contaminated soil stockpiles generated from historic highway maintenance operations. Its role is directly tied to the remedial action framework, where verified clean fill is required to restore excavated areas to grade and support site closure objectives.

## Site Record

- **Location and Purpose**: The Carpenter Road Borrow Area is situated in Stanislaus County, near the SR 132 corridor. It was identified and assessed specifically to provide borrow material (clean soil) for backfilling excavations created by the removal of contaminated stockpiles at the Modesto site.
- **Documentation**: The borrow area is referenced in the *Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G* (Draft Interim Removal Action Completion Report, Appendices D through G). This document details the verification testing and approval process for the borrow material.
- **Verification Results**: According to the source, borrow material from Carpenter Road was tested for contaminants of concern (COCs) relevant to the project, including heavy metals (e.g., arsenic, lead, barium) and organics (e.g., total petroleum hydrocarbons, pesticides). Testing demonstrated that the material met applicable background thresholds and/or California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs) for unrestricted use.
- **Volume and Phasing**: The borrow area supplied soil for multiple phases of the removal action (Phase 1: 2019–2020). Specific cubic yard volumes are not detailed in the source, but the material was approved as "clean fill" by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for use in the backfill program.
- **Compliance**: The borrow area’s approval was conditional on meeting DTSC’s clean fill criteria, which include concentrations below site-specific action levels and background values. No exceedances or rejections are documented in the reviewed source.

## Regulatory Status

- **Applicable Standards**: The Carpenter Road Borrow Area material was subject to DTSC-approved cleanup standards for the Modesto site, including:
  - Background concentrations for metals (site-specific, Stanislaus County).
  - California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs) for residential land use (the default remedial goal for backfill).
  - No MCLs (Maximum Contaminant Levels) were directly applied since borrow material is not groundwater, but leachability testing was performed to ensure no future groundwater impact.
- **Agency Oversight**: DTSC provided approval for the borrow area’s use. The borrow area itself is not a remediation site but a support feature; its regulatory status is tied to the main project’s compliance with the removal action workplan.
- **Current Compliance**: As documented in the Draft Interim RACR, the borrow material met all applicable thresholds and was accepted for use. No ongoing non-compliance issues are identified in the source.

## Open Items

No open items are documented in the *Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G* for the Carpenter Road Borrow Area. However, the source is an interim report, and final certification of the borrow area’s closure (e.g., restoration or re-grading) may be pending in later project phases.
