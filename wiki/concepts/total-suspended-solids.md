## Total Suspended Solids (TSS)

### Overview
Total Suspended Solids (TSS) is a parameter analyzed in surface water samples collected as part of the Modesto Stockpiles Stormwater monitoring program. The analysis is conducted to quantify the amount of solid material suspended in the water.

### Site Record
Surface water samples were analyzed for Total Suspended Solids (TSS) by Standard Method (SM) 2540D.

In the January 2018 sampling event, TSS was among the parameters analyzed. {{quote: "Total suspended solids (TSS) by SM 2540D."}} The laboratory report and chain-of-custody documentation for this event are located in Appendix A. {{quote: "Surface water analytical results for this monitoring event are summarized on Tables 2 and 3. The laboratory report and chain-of-custody documentation are in Appendix A."}}

In the March 2017 sampling event, TSS was also analyzed using SM 2540D. {{quote: "Total suspended solids (TSS) by SM 2540D."}} The laboratory report and chain-of-custody documentation for this event are in Appendix A. {{quote: "Surface water analytical results for this monitoring event are summarized on Tables 2 and 3. The laboratory report and chain-of-custody documentation are in Appendix A."}}

Data from the March 2017 sampling event indicates TSS results for sample ID BG2 on 3/24/2017 were 10 mg/L. {{quote: "BG2 3/24/2017 10 250\*\* --- --- 250\*\* --- --- --- 500\*\*"}}
