## Total Suspended Solids (TSS)

### Overview
Total Suspended Solids (TSS) is a parameter measured in surface water samples collected as part of the Modesto Stockpiles Stormwater monitoring program. The analysis quantifies the amount of solid material suspended in the water.

### Site Record
Surface water samples were analyzed for Total Suspended Solids (TSS) using Standard Method (SM) 2540D.

In the January 2018 sampling event, TSS was among the parameters analyzed. The laboratory report and chain-of-custody documentation for this event are located in Appendix A of the January 2018 Stormwater Sampling report.

In the March 2017 sampling event, TSS was also analyzed using SM 2540D. The laboratory report and chain-of-custody documentation for this event are located in Appendix A of the March 2017 Stormwater Sampling report.

Data from the March 2017 sampling event indicates TSS results for sample ID BG2 on 3/24/2017 were 10 mg/L.

### Regulatory Status
The provided sources do not specify applicable regulatory standards or thresholds for Total Suspended Solids in the context of this project.
