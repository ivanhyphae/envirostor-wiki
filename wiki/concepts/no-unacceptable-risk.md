The concept of "No Unacceptable Risk" in the context of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project signifies that the implemented remediation and management strategies are deemed sufficient to protect human health and the environment. This determination is based on the evaluation of contaminant levels and the effectiveness of the chosen [[containment-strategy]].

## Site record

The Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, dated March 18, 2019, outlines the findings and proposed actions. The RAP addresses soil stockpiles located in Stanislaus County, California, associated with the State Route 132 (SR 132) West Freeway/Expressway Project. The primary contaminant of concern evaluated in relation to risk is [[barium]].

Specific concentrations of barium in the soil stockpiles were documented.
