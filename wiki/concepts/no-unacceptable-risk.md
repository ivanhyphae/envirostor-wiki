## Overview
The concept of "No Unacceptable Risk" for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project signifies that the implemented remediation and management strategies are considered sufficient to protect human health and the environment. This determination is based on the evaluation of contaminant levels and the effectiveness of the chosen [[containment-strategy]].

## Site record
The Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, dated March 18, 2019, addresses soil stockpiles located in [[stanislaus-county]], California, associated with the State Route 132 (SR 132) West Freeway/Expressway Project. The primary contaminant of concern evaluated in relation to risk is [[barium]].

## Regulatory status
The [[draft-final-remedial-action-plan]] prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] for [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] was submitted to the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). The plan outlines the strategies to achieve "no unacceptable risk to human health or groundwater" for the stockpiled soils.
