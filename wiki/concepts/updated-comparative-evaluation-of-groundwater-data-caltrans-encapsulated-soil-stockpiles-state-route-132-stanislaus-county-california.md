## Updated Comparative Evaluation Of Groundwater Data Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles State Route 132 Stanislaus County California

### Overview

This document presents a comparative evaluation of groundwater data related to the Caltrans encapsulated soil stockpiles at the State Route 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. The evaluation focuses on assessing the potential impact of the stockpiles on groundwater quality.

### Site Record

The "Approval of GW Statistical Evaluation (900259)" document, dated October 26, 2023, references a comparative evaluation of groundwater data. This evaluation was conducted to support the decision to discontinue groundwater monitoring. The document states that the comparative evaluation concluded that there was "no impact to groundwater" from the encapsulated soil stockpiles. Specific groundwater data points, concentrations, dates, or well identifiers are not detailed within the provided source material for this specific comparative evaluation.

### Regulatory Status

The "Approval of GW Statistical Evaluation (900259)" document, issued by the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board on October 26, 2023, approved the statistical evaluation of groundwater data. This approval was a key step in the decision to discontinue groundwater monitoring, indicating a regulatory determination that the encapsulated soil stockpiles were not adversely affecting groundwater quality.

### Open Items

The "Approval of GW Statistical Evaluation (900259)" document, dated October 26, 2023, indicates that the approval was contingent upon the successful decommissioning of groundwater monitoring wells. This decommissioning is a required future action.
