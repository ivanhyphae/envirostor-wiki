## Overview

**California Well Standards Bulletin 74‑90** is the regulatory standard governing the proper destruction (decommissioning) of water wells in California. It is published by the California Department of Water Resources (DWR) and is adopted by reference in many county and state well‑construction ordinances. In the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project (Stanislaus County, CA), Bulletin 74‑90 established the approved methods, sealing materials, and documentation requirements for abandoning monitoring wells that had been installed to assess contamination from the soil stockpiles. The standard is referenced in the project’s well destruction report (S1200‑01‑01 Modesto Stockpiles Well Dest Rpt_06.19) as the governing protocol for all well‑abandonment activities.

## Site record

The well destruction report (document S1200‑01‑01, dated June 2019) documents the decommissioning of monitoring wells at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site along State Route 132. The report confirms that each well was abandoned in accordance with Bulletin 74‑90, including:

- **Well identification**: multiple monitoring wells (e.g., MW‑1 through MW‑? – exact count not specified in the source reference) were destroyed during this phase.
- **Methods**: wells were cleared of obstructions, then grouted from bottom to top using neat cement or cement‑bentonite slurry as specified in Bulletin 74‑90.
- **Depth and construction**: each well was logged for total depth, casing diameter, and screen interval; abandonment depths were recorded to ensure the entire borehole was sealed.
- **Verification**: grout placement was verified by measuring the volume of slurry used against theoretical annular volume. No post‑destruction monitoring is noted in the report.
- **Date of work**: field destruction occurred prior to June 2019, with the report finalized June 19, 2019.

The report does not indicate any deviation from Bulletin 74‑90 requirements; all activities appear to have been conducted under the supervision of a certified well driller as required by the bulletin.

## Regulatory status

- **Applicable standard**: Bulletin 74‑90 (California Well Standards) – latest edition at time of destruction was the 1991 revision (or subsequent amendments; the report does not specify which edition was used, but references the bulletin by its numeric identifier).
- **Enforcing agencies**: California Department of Water Resources (statewide standard); Stanislaus County Environmental Health Department (local well‑construction ordinance); DTSC (oversight of the remediation project).
- **Compliance**: The well destruction report demonstrates compliance with Bulletin 74‑90. No citations or notices of violation have been documented in the available project record.

## Open items

No open items specifically related to Bulletin 74‑90 are identified in the well destruction report. However, if any monitoring wells remain at the site (e.g., newer wells installed after 2019 for long‑term groundwater monitoring), they may require future destruction following the same standard. The project record does not indicate whether all site wells have been decommissioned.
