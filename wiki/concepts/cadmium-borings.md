## Overview

Cadmium Borings refers to the subset of soil borings collected during the Site-Specific Investigation (SSI) at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA) for which cadmium was a target analyte. These borings were advanced within the stockpile materials and adjacent native soil to characterize the lateral and vertical distribution of cadmium, a metal of concern due to its potential mobility and toxicity. The investigation is documented in the *Modesto Stockpiles SSI Report Rev. 0313* (document number S9525-06-44), which serves as the primary record for cadmium data at this site.

## Site record

The SSI Report Rev. 0313 presents cadmium results from multiple boring locations across the stockpile area. Borings were typically sampled at discrete depth intervals. The report provides concentration data in milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) for soil and, where applicable, micrograms per liter (µg/L) for laboratory leachate or groundwater. Site-specific action levels (e.g., DTSC-modified PRGs or Caltrans project-specific screening levels) are used as benchmarks. The report identifies intervals where cadmium concentrations exceed these thresholds.

The spatial distribution of elevated cadmium is concentrated in the southern portion of the stockpile footprint (borings designated CB-01 through CB-06 in the SSI). Maximum reported concentrations and exceedance summaries are tabulated in the report's data tables (e.g., Table 4-2, Table 5-3). Temporal trends are not applicable because the SSI represents a single comprehensive sampling event.

Verification results after any interim removal actions are not documented in the SSI Report Rev. 0313; the report focuses on characterization. Phase designations (e.g., Phase 1 2019–2020) are used in the report to describe the investigation sequence, but cadmium borings were included in all phases.

## Regulatory status

The applicable regulatory framework for cadmium in soil at this site includes:

- **DTSC California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs)** for residential and industrial land use.
- **Caltrans project-specific risk-based screening levels** established in the Work Plan and approved by DTSC.
- **Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs)** for groundwater (5 µg/L) if cadmium leaching is a concern.
- **Background thresholds** from off-site native soil sampling referenced in the SSI report.

The SSI report indicates that cadmium concentrations in several borings exceed the residential CHHSL but are below or near industrial screening levels. The site is currently in the investigation phase; no final determination of compliance has been made. A Remedial Action Plan (RAP) is pending further data evaluation from the SSI.

## Open items

- Completion of a human health risk assessment using the SSI cadmium data (including bioavailability adjustment, if any) is listed as a required deliverable in the DTSC-approved Work Plan.
- A decision on whether cadmium mass will be addressed via removal, capping, or institutional controls has not been finalized in the SSI Report Rev. 0313.
- Additional confirmatory borings around the high-cadmium zone (borings CB-04 and CB-05 per SSI figures) may be required to delineate the hotspot boundary.
- The final SSI report is subject to DTSC review and approval; any comments on cadmium data gaps will be addressed in a revision or addendum.
