The Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Agreement is a formal contract between the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and [[caltrans]] that establishes the procedures for maintaining the integrity of the installed remedy at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. This agreement is a crucial component of the post-remediation phase, ensuring the long-term safety and effectiveness of the cleanup actions.

## Site Record

The O&M Agreement specifically addresses the "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Phase 1 Interim State Route 132 West Project, Modesto, Stanislaus County, California (Site Code: 900259)". The agreement covers Stockpile 1, located adjacent and west of Emerald Avenue between approximate State Route (SR) 132 Stations 1125+50, and Stockpile 2, located between Emerald Avenue and SR 99 between approximate SR 132 Stations 1135+35 and 1148+30.

The installed remedy, as described in the O&M Agreement, consists of the consolidation and clean fill capping of [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) as highway embankment fill for the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]]. The O&M Agreement mandates specific procedures to ensure the integrity of the clean capped Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones. These procedures are to be completed pursuant to the O&M Agreement.

The O&M Agreement is referenced within the [[land-use-covenant]] as Exhibit C. The Land Use Covenant also requires the owner to conduct an annual inspection of the property verifying compliance with the covenant and submit an annual inspection report to the Department by January 18th of each year, in accordance with the O&M Plan, Exhibit C, or its amendments.

The final O&M Agreement was sent via electronic mail on June 20, 2023.

## Regulatory Status

The O&M Agreement is entered into by DTSC and Caltrans under the authority of California Health and Safety Code section 25355.5(a)(1)(C) and/or section 25187. DTSC oversees the operation and maintenance of the installed remedy.

## Open Items

The O&M Agreement is a prerequisite for the recording of the [[land-use-covenant]] on the remediated area. DTSC indicated they would send a draft of the LUC for Caltrans' review and signature within several weeks following the O&M Agreement.
