The Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Agreement is a formal contract between the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) that outlines the procedures for maintaining the integrity of the installed remedy at the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. This agreement is a critical component of the post-remediation phase, ensuring the long-term safety and effectiveness of the cleanup actions. The O&M Agreement is entered into pursuant to California Health and Safety Code sections 25355.5(a)(1)(C) and/or 25187.

## Site Record

The O&M Agreement specifically addresses the "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Phase 1 Interim State Route 132 West Project, Modesto, Stanislaus County, California (Site Code: 900259)". {{quote: "FINAL OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE AGREEMENT FOR THE CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES, PHASE 1 INTERIM STATE ROUTE 132 WEST PROJECT, MODESTO, STANISLAUS COUNTY, CALIFORNIA (SITE CODE: 900259)"}} The agreement covers Stockpile 1, located adjacent and west of Emerald Avenue between approximate State Route (SR) 132 Stations 1125+50, and Stockpile 2, located between Emerald Avenue and SR 99 between approximate SR 132 Stations 1135+35 and 1148+30. {{quote: "The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the California Department of Transportation (Proponent) enter into this Operation and Maintenance Agreement (Agreement) for the site located adjacent and west of Emerald Avenue between approximate State Route (SR) 132 Stations 1125+50 (Stockpile 1) and between Emerald Avenue and SR 99 between approximate SR 132 Stations 1135+35 and 1148+30 (Stockpile 2) in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. Stockpiles 1 and 2 collectively constitute the Site."}}

The installed remedy, as described in the O&M Agreement, consists of the consolidation and clean fill capping of barium-containing soil (BCS) as highway embankment fill for the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]]. {{quote: "A DTSC-approved remedy has been installed at the Site for the remediation of soil. The remedy consists of consolidation and clean fill capping of barium-containing soil (BCS) as highway embankment fill for the SR 132 West Expressway."}}

The O&M Agreement mandates specific procedures to ensure the integrity of the clean capped Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones. {{quote: "This section summarizes the O&M procedures that will be implemented by Caltrans to ensure the integrity of the clean capped Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones."}} These procedures are to be completed pursuant to the O&M Agreement. {{quote: "The O&M activities will be completed pursuant to the O&M Agreement entered into between DTSC and Caltrans."}}

The O&M Agreement is referenced within the [[land-use-covenant]] as Exhibit C. {{quote: "ARTICLE IV RESTRICTIONS AND REQUIREMENTS ... 4.2. <u>Soil Management</u>. Soil management activities at the Property are subject to the following requirements in addition to any other applicable Environmental Restrictions: ... (a) No activities that will disturb the soil (e.g., excavation, grading, removal, trenching, filling, earth movement, mining, or drilling) shall be allowed at the Property without a Soil Management Plan pre-approved by the Department in writing, as established in the June 20, 2023 Final Operation and Maintenance Agreement for the Property (Exhibit C)."}} The Land Use Covenant also requires the owner to conduct an annual inspection of the property verifying compliance with the covenant and submit an annual inspection report to the Department by January 18th of each year, in accordance with the O&M Plan, Exhibit C, or its amendments. {{quote: "4.6. Inspection and Reporting Requirements. In accordance with the June 20, 2023 O&M Plan, Exhibit C, or its amendments or revisions that have been approved by the Department, the Owner shall conduct an annual inspection of the Property verifying compliance with this Covenant and shall submit an annual inspection report to the Department for its approval by January 18th of each year."}}

The final O&M Agreement was sent via electronic mail on June 20, 2023. {{quote: "Sent Via Electronic Mail June 20, 2023"}}

## Regulatory Status

The O&M Agreement is entered into by DTSC and Caltrans under the authority of California Health and Safety Code section 25355.5(a)(1)(C) and/or section 25187. {{quote: "1.0 Jurisdiction. This Agreement is entered into by DTSC and Proponent pursuant to Health and Safety Code section 25355.5(a)(1)(C) and/or section 25187 which authorizes DTSC to enter into an enforceable agreement to oversee the investigation and/or remediation of a release or threatened release of any hazardous substance or hazardous waste at or from the Site, and to oversee the operation and maintenance of any remedy installed at the Site."}} DTSC oversees the operation and maintenance of the installed remedy. {{quote: "and to oversee the operation and maintenance of any remedy installed at the Site."}}

## Open Items

The O&M Agreement is a prerequisite for the recording of the Land Use Covenant (LUC) on the remediated area. {{quote: "The next project task will be recording the Land Use Covenant (LUC) on the remediated area to restrict its use."}} DTSC indicated they would send a draft of the LUC for Caltrans' review and signature within several weeks following the O&M Agreement. {{quote: "DTSC will send a draft of the LUC for your review and signature within the next several weeks."}}
