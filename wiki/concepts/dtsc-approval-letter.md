## DTSC Approval Letter

### Overview
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) issued an approval letter related to the remediation case file for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, located on SR 132 in Stanislaus County, California. This approval signifies regulatory acceptance of specific project documents and findings.

### Site Record
The DTSC approved the [[supplemental-site-investigation-report]] and [[human-health-risk-assessment]] on April 4, 2013.{{quote: "On April 4, 2013, DTSC approved the Final Supplemental Site Investigation Report (SSI) and Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA)."}}

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC's approval on April 4, 2013, indicates regulatory concurrence with the findings and conclusions presented in the approved documents.
