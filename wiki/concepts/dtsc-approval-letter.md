## DTSC Approval Letter

### Overview
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) has issued approval letters related to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, specifically concerning sampling plans for clean fill material. These approvals signify regulatory acceptance of proposed sampling methodologies and analytical approaches.

### Site Record
On April 7, 2021, DTSC, along with the Waterboard, reviewed and approved the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan.{{quote: "DTSC and the Waterboard have reviewed the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan (Plan) received on April 5, 2021 that outlines the proposed sampling and analysis for soil at this location that will be used as clean fill."}} The approval stated that the plan was consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and that DTSC had no comments.{{quote: "The Plan is consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and there are no comments. This e-mail serves as DTSCs approval of the Plan."}}

The DTSC had previously approved the Final Supplemental Site Investigation Report (SSI) and Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA) on April 4, 2013.{{quote: "On April 4, 2013, DTSC approved the Final Supplemental Site Investigation Report (SSI) and Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA)."}}

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC's approval of the Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan on April 7, 2021, indicates regulatory concurrence with the proposed sampling and analysis for soil intended for use as clean fill. The earlier approval of the SSI and HHRA on April 4, 2013, signifies regulatory acceptance of the site investigation findings and risk assessment.
