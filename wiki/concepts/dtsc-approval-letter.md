## DTSC Approval Letter

### Overview
The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) has issued approval letters for key documents related to the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, signifying regulatory acceptance of investigation findings, risk assessments, and sampling plans for clean fill material.

### Site Record
On April 4, 2013, DTSC approved the Final Supplemental Site Investigation Report (SSI) and Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA) for the project.

On April 7, 2021, DTSC, in conjunction with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], approved the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan. This plan, received on April 5, 2021, outlines the proposed sampling and analysis for soil to be used as clean fill at the [[carpenter-road-area]]. DTSC noted that the plan was consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and stated, "there are no comments. This e-mail serves as DTSCs approval of the Plan."

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC's approval of the Final SSI and HHRA on April 4, 2013, indicates regulatory acceptance of the site's environmental conditions and associated risks. The subsequent approval of the Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan on April 7, 2021, signifies regulatory concurrence with the proposed methods for characterizing material designated as clean fill.
