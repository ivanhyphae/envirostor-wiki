# Title 22 MCL

## Overview

Title 22 Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) are the regulatory benchmarks against which groundwater analytical results were compared in the [[june-2014-groundwater-monitoring-report]] for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. The MCLs applied in this project are those established under Title 22 of the California Code of Regulations and were used by geocon-consultants to evaluate whether detected concentrations of metals in monitoring wells exceeded regulatory thresholds.

## Site record

The [[june-2014-groundwater-monitoring-report]] (dated June 2014, for monitoring conducted in June 2014) documented comparison of groundwater analytical results to Title 22 MCLs. The report specifically references Title 22 MCLs in the context of metals analysis — particularly for [[arsenic]], [[barium]], and [[chromium]] — across multiple monitoring wells at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). The data were submitted to [[geotracker]].

For [[barium]], the report explicitly documents a finding of [[barium-below-mcl]] — a determination made by comparing the detected barium concentration against the applicable Title 22 MCL for that analyte.

## Regulatory status

Title 22 MCLs serve as the applicable regulatory thresholds for groundwater quality assessment at this site. Exceedance or compliance determinations for individual analytes are made by comparing detected concentrations against the MCL values established under Title 22. The [[june-2014-groundwater-monitoring-report]] uses these MCLs as the primary compliance benchmark for evaluating groundwater monitoring results.
