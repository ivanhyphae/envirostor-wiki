## Overview

The **Title 22 MCL** (Maximum Contaminant Level) is the regulatory threshold established under California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Division 4, Chapter 15, for the protection of groundwater used as drinking water. In the context of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project (SR 132, Stanislaus County), Title 22 MCLs serve as the primary cleanup standards for groundwater monitoring and remediation under DTSC oversight.

## Site Record

Specific Title 22 MCL values relevant to the project are documented in the *S9525-06-25 Modesto Stockpiles Well Install Report* (hereafter Well Install Report) and the *June 2012 GW Report 1112* (hereafter 2012 GW Report). Key analytes and their MCLs cited in these reports include:

- **Barium**: MCL of 1,000 µg/L (1.0 mg/L). This is the critical contaminant: barium concentrations in groundwater from stockpile leachate have exceeded the MCL at multiple monitoring wells (see below).
- **Chromium (total)**: MCL of 50 µg/L. Exceedances have been noted in select wells.
- **Lead**: MCL of 15 µg/L (action level under federal Lead and Copper Rule, but Title 22 MCL is 15 µg/L from the 2012 GW Report context).
- **Arsenic**: MCL of 10 µg/L. Background levels at the site are below MCL.
- **Vanadium**: Not regulated as a primary MCL under Title 22 for drinking water, but noted in screening comparisons.

Data from groundwater monitoring rounds (2010–2012) show that barium concentrations in monitoring well [[mw-5]] reached up to **2,300 µg/L** in the June 2011 sampling event, exceeding the 1,000 µg/L MCL. The 2012 GW Report (Table 3) documents barium ranging from 520 µg/L to 2,100 µg/L in wells downgradient of the soil stockpiles between 2010–2012. In the same period, chromium exceeded the 50 µg/L MCL in MW-12 (73 µg/L, August 2011).

The Well Install Report (2025) notes that ongoing groundwater monitoring continues to compare results to Title 22 MCLs, with no reduction in barium MCL exceedances observed since 2012.

## Regulatory Status

The Title 22 MCL for barium (1,000 µg/L) is the enforceable standard for this project under DTSC oversight (California Department of Toxic Substances Control, Region 4). The project is currently **non-compliant** with the barium MCL in groundwater at multiple monitoring locations downgradient of stockpile areas, as documented in the 2012 GW Report and confirmed by the 2025 Well Install Report. No variance or alternate cleanup level has been formally established by DTSC for barium at this site.

For other analytes:
- Chromium (total) MCL exceedances are intermittent but reported.
- Arsenic, lead, and other metals have not exceeded MCLs in standard monitoring events.

DTSC’s January 2012 letter (attached to the 2012 GW Report) required that groundwater remediation or additional source control measures be implemented to bring concentrations below Title 22 MCLs, but no formal enforcement action has been taken as of 2025 based on project records.

## Open Items

- **MCL exceedance for barium in [[mw-5]] and MW-12A**: Concentrations have remained above 1,000 µg/L for over a decade. No remediation or corrective action plan has been submitted or approved.
- **Risk assessment**: A human health risk assessment (HHRA) using Title 22 MCLs as screening levels has been proposed but not completed per the 2012 GW Report recommendations.
- **Groundwater monitoring frequency**: The 2025 Well Install Report indicates that quarterly monitoring was reduced to semi-annual, but DTSC has not formally concurred with the change.
- **Title 22 compliance determination**: No formal DTSC determination of compliance or non-compliance for the post-2012 period is included in the project record.
