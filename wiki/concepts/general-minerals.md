## General Minerals

### Overview

General minerals analyses were conducted to characterize the geochemistry of the water at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. The results of these analyses are documented in the surface water sampling reports.

### Site Record

General minerals analyses were performed on water samples collected from the site. The analytical results for general minerals are summarized on Table 3 in the January 6, 2016, Stormwater Sampling Report {{quote: "The analytical results for general minerals are summarized on Table 3."}} and the March 5, 2016, Stormwater Sampling Report {{quote: "The analytical results for general minerals are summarized on Table 3."}}.

In both the January 6, 2016, and March 5, 2016, sampling events, none of the reported general minerals concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs {{quote: "None of the reported general minerals concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs."}}. The analytical results were reported to be within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported at each sample location {{quote: "The analytical results are within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported at each sample location."}}.

### Regulatory Status

The general minerals analyses were conducted to assess compliance with primary and secondary MCLs {{quote: "None of the reported general minerals concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs."}}. No exceedances of these MCLs were reported in the analyzed samples.
