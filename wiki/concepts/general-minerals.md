## Overview

General minerals analyses were conducted to characterize the geochemistry of surface water and groundwater at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. These analyses help in understanding the water's composition and consistency over time.

## Site Record

General minerals analyses have been performed on water samples collected from the site. The analytical results for general minerals are summarized in Table 3 of various stormwater sampling reports.

In the January 6, 2016, and March 5, 2016, sampling events, none of the reported general minerals concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs {{quote: "None of the reported general minerals concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs."}}. The analytical results from these events were reported to be within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported at each sample location {{quote: "The analytical results are within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported at each sample location."}}.

For the March 24, 2017, samples, the analytical results were consistent with previous events {{quote: "Analytical results for the March 24, 2017, samples are consistent with the results from previous events which began in 2013."}}. Similarly, for the January 8, 2018, samples, the analytical results were also consistent with previous events {{quote: "Analytical results for the January 8, 2018, samples are consistent with the results from previous events which began in 2013."}}.

Groundwater monitoring in February 2014 indicated that nitrate as nitrogen concentrations ranged from 2.8 mg/l (MW-3) to 34 mg/l (MW-5), and total dissolved solids (TDS) concentrations ranged from 340 mg/l (MW-3 and MW-7) to 750 mg/l (MW-5) {{quote: "Nitrate as nitrogen and TDS were both reported for each of the groundwater samples, with nitrate as nitrogen concentrations ranging from 2.8 (MW-3) to 34 mg/l (MW-5) and TDS concentrations ranging from 340 (MW-3 and MW-7) to 750 mg/l (MW-5)."}}.

The general minerals analyses conducted for groundwater characterization included dissolved calcium, dissolved magnesium, chloride, nitrate as nitrogen, sulfate, dissolved potassium, dissolved sodium, sulfide, total alkalinity, and TDS {{quote: "To further characterize the geochemistry of the groundwater, general minerals analyses were conducted and included the following constituents: dissolved calcium, dissolved magnesium, chloride, nitrate as nitrogen, sulfate, dissolved potassium, dissolved sodium, sulfide, total alkalinity, TDS"}}.

Stiff diagrams constructed using site groundwater data for February 2014 showed that groundwater sampled in each monitoring well was bicarbonate (HCO3) dominant, with variations in sodium and potassium (Na+K) and calcium composition {{quote: "The diagrams show that groundwater sampled in each monitoring well is bicarbonate (HCO3) dominant. However, variations in the sodium and potassium (Na+K) and calcium composition are readily apparent."}}. Samples from wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-7, MW-9, and MW-10 had a calcium-dominant composition, while samples from wells MW-3, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-8 were sodium-dominant {{quote: "In February 2014, the samples from wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-7, MW-9, and MW-10 had a calcium-dominant composition while the samples from wells MW-3, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-8 were sodium-dominant."}}. Water samples from well MW-5 switched from calcium-dominant to sodium-dominant in 2013 {{quote: "Water samples from well MW-5 switched from calcium-dominant to sodium-dominant in 2013."}}.

## Regulatory Status

The general minerals analyses were conducted to assess compliance with primary and secondary MCLs {{quote: "None of the reported general minerals concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs."}}. No exceedances of these MCLs were reported in the analyzed samples from the January 6, 2016, and March 5, 2016, sampling events {{quote: "None of the reported general minerals concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs."}}.

In February 2014, the reported nitrate concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equaled or exceeded the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l {{quote: "The reported nitrate concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equal or exceed the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l"}}. The reported TDS concentrations for samples from MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equaled or exceeded the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l {{quote: "and the reported TDS concentrations for samples from MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equal or exceed the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l."}}.

With the exception of nitrate in the samples collected from wells MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10, none of the reported general minerals for the groundwater samples collected in February 2014 equaled or exceeded their respective California primary MCLs {{quote: "With the exception of nitrate in the samples collected from wells MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10, none of the reported general minerals for the groundwater samples collected in February 2014 equaled or exceeded their respective California primary MCLs."}}.
