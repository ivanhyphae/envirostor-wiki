## General Minerals

### Overview

General minerals analyses were conducted to characterize the geochemistry of the water at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. These analyses are documented in the surface water sampling reports.

### Site Record

General minerals analyses were performed on water samples collected from the site. The analytical results for general minerals are summarized on Table 3 in the January 6, 2016, Stormwater Sampling Report {{quote: "The analytical results for general minerals are summarized on Table 3."}}, the March 5, 2016, Stormwater Sampling Report {{quote: "The analytical results for general minerals are summarized on Table 3."}}, the January 8, 2018, Stormwater Sampling Report {{quote: "The analytical results for general minerals are summarized on Table 3."}}, and the March 24, 2017, Stormwater Sampling Report {{quote: "The analytical results for general minerals are summarized on Table 3."}}.

In the January 6, 2016, and March 5, 2016, sampling events, none of the reported general minerals concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs {{quote: "None of the reported general minerals concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs."}}. The analytical results were reported to be within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported at each sample location {{quote: "The analytical results are within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported at each sample location."}}. Similarly, for the March 24, 2017, samples, the analytical results were consistent with previous events {{quote: "Analytical results for the March 24, 2017, samples are consistent with the results from previous events which began in 2013."}}. For the January 8, 2018, samples, the analytical results were also consistent with previous events {{quote: "Analytical results for the January 8, 2018, samples are consistent with the results from previous events which began in 2013."}}.

### Regulatory Status

The general minerals analyses were conducted to assess compliance with primary and secondary MCLs {{quote: "None of the reported general minerals concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs."}}. No exceedances of these MCLs were reported in the analyzed samples from the January 6, 2016, and March 5, 2016, sampling events {{quote: "None of the reported general minerals concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs."}}.
