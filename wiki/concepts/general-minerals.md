## Overview

General minerals analyses were performed to characterize the geochemistry of surface water and groundwater at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. These analyses provide insight into the water's composition and consistency over time.

## Site Record

General minerals analyses have been conducted on water samples collected from the site. The results are summarized in Table 3 of various stormwater sampling reports.

During the January 6, 2016, and March 5, 2016, sampling events, none of the reported general minerals concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs. The analytical results from these events were within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported at each sample location.

For the March 24, 2017, samples, the analytical results were consistent with previous events that began in 2013. Similarly, for the January 8, 2018, samples, the analytical results were also consistent with previous events.

Groundwater monitoring in February 2014 indicated that nitrate as nitrogen concentrations ranged from 2.8 mg/l (MW-3) to 34 mg/l (MW-5), and total dissolved solids (TDS) concentrations ranged from 340 mg/l (MW-3 and MW-7) to 750 mg/l (MW-5). The general minerals analyses conducted for groundwater characterization included dissolved calcium, dissolved magnesium, chloride, nitrate as nitrogen, sulfate, dissolved potassium, dissolved sodium, sulfide, total alkalinity, and TDS.

Stiff diagrams constructed using site groundwater data for February 2014 showed that groundwater sampled in each monitoring well was bicarbonate (HCO3) dominant. Samples from wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-7, MW-9, and MW-10 had a calcium-dominant composition, while samples from wells MW-3, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-8 were sodium-dominant. Water samples from well MW-5 switched from calcium-dominant to sodium-dominant in 2013.

Observations made during the March 5, 2016, sampling event and from site reconnaissance did not identify any area or location where stormwater runoff appeared to be migrating offsite. Stormwater falling on the stockpile appeared to be retained within the boundary of the Site.

## Regulatory Status

The general minerals analyses were conducted to assess compliance with primary and secondary MCLs. No exceedances of these MCLs were reported in the analyzed samples from the January 6, 2016, and March 5, 2016, sampling events.

In February 2014, the reported nitrate concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equaled or exceeded the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l. The reported TDS concentrations for samples from MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equaled or exceeded the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l. With the exception of nitrate in the samples collected from wells MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10, none of the reported general minerals for the groundwater samples collected in February 2014 equaled or exceeded their respective [[california-mcl]]s.
