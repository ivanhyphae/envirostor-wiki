## Overview
The MSE Wall Footing Sampling Plan outlines the strategy for characterizing soil within the Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) wall footing excavations at [[stockpile-1]] and stockpile-2. The objective is to determine the suitability of this soil for on-site reuse, as evaluated by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). This plan was developed by [[geocon-consultants]] and submitted by [[john-e-juhrend]].

## Site Record
The sampling plan initially designated ten locations for soil sampling within the MSE wall footing excavations, spaced approximately 200 feet apart. Excavations were planned to a depth of 4 to 5 feet, with discrete soil samples collected at one-foot intervals.

For Stockpile 1, a Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020, submitted by [[john-e-juhrend]] of [[geocon-consultants]] to [[richard-stewart]] at [[caltrans]], detailed the sampling and laboratory analysis of MSE wall footing excavation soil. This memo sought approval for the use of this soil as embankment fill and/or clean cover material within Stockpiles 1 and 2.

For Stockpile 2, a revised Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020, was submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] to [[caltrans]]. This memo addressed sampling along the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation area and its laboratory results. The memo concluded that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones, while soil below that depth is suitable as embankment fill or capping material. This material below 3 feet bgs was also considered part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan.

## Regulatory Status
The MSE Wall Footing Sampling Plan requires approval from the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] for the characterization and classification of soils intended for reuse.

On April 7, 2020, [[dean-wright]] of DTSC officially accepted the Technical Memorandum for Stockpile 1, with the condition that MSE wall footing soil could not be used as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2. This decision was based on the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil being above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg for barium, and the MSE wall footing falling outside the designated borrow area in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan. The accepted use for this material was placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or as general bridge embankment fill.

On June 22, 2020, [[dean-wright]] of DTSC officially accepted the revised Technical Memorandum for Stockpile 2. DTSC and the Water Board agreed that soil down to 3 feet bgs must be placed in designated containment zones, but soil below that depth is suitable as embankment fill or capping material.

The analytical data generated from sampling is compared against Real-time Data Interpretation and Planning (RDIP) and regulatory screening levels, including DTSC residential screening levels for lead and site-specific background levels for barium and lead.

## Open Items
As of March 31, 2020, DTSC approval of the proposed sampling plan for Stockpile 1 was pending. This approval was necessary to permit sampling to proceed once the Biologically Coverable Soil (BCS) slope zone material was removed.
