The Updated Statistical Evaluation Report, dated February 26, 2024, is [[geocon-consultants-inc]]'s update to its 2014 comparative evaluation of groundwater data at the Caltrans Modesto Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles site, located south of the intersection of SR 99 and Kansas Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County. Prepared for [[caltrans]] District 6 under Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2, EA No. 10-1E7003, and addressed to Adam Inman, PG, of the Office of Environmental Engineering, the report incorporates groundwater data collected since 2014 and concludes with a recommendation that the remaining four Caltrans monitoring wells be decommissioned and that groundwater monitoring at the site be discontinued.

## Purpose and recommendation

The report's stated purpose is narrow: to extend the 2014 statistical comparative evaluation of groundwater quality with the data gathered in the intervening years, and to determine whether continued monitoring is warranted. The transmittal letter states the conclusion directly — that the four remaining Caltrans monitoring wells should be decommissioned and groundwater monitoring related to the soil stockpiles should be discontinued. This is a recommendation to end the site's groundwater monitoring obligation, contingent on whatever review [[caltrans]] and the applicable regulatory agencies give it.

The transmittal was signed by Rebecca L. Silva, Project Manager, and Dr. Andrew Kopania, PG, CHG, Senior Hydrogeologist, both of Geocon.

## Scope and limits of the available record

The source material provided is the cover transmittal letter for the report rather than its technical body: it establishes the report's title, date, project numbering, addressee, and headline recommendation, but does not itself contain the underlying statistical methods, well-by-well data, or comparison to [[california-primary-mcl]] or [[california-secondary-mcl]] values that would have supported the decommissioning recommendation. The document also does not identify which four wells remain (see [[mw-10]] for a well-specific record) or state whether [[central-valley-rwqcb]] or [[dtsc]] had acted on the recommendation as of the report date. The letter explicitly disclaims that its contents reflect the views of the authors alone and do not constitute a standard, specification, or regulation of the State of California or the Federal Highway Administration.
