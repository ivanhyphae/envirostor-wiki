## Overview
A [[groundwater-monitoring-round]] is a periodic sampling and analysis event conducted to assess groundwater quality. For the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, these rounds are specifically designed to monitor the presence and concentration of Chemicals of Concern (COC) in the groundwater near three soil stockpiles. The primary COCs identified at this site are [[barium]], lead, and [[strontium]].

## Site record
Groundwater monitoring reports have been generated for various dates, including March 2012, May 2012, September 2012, November 2012, and January 2013. The monitoring wells were installed in 2006 to monitor groundwater quality in the vicinity of three soil stockpiles. Caltrans is required to prepare, at a minimum, quarterly groundwater monitoring reports for groundwater monitoring wells associated with the Site. These reports shall discuss depth to groundwater measurements; well purging and sampling; chain of custody reports; laboratory analysis, including analytes, methods, and results; field and laboratory quality assurance/quality control; and comparison of groundwater monitoring results from previous sampling events.

## Regulatory status
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB), reviewed a groundwater monitoring report prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] and dated May 2012. The DTSC concurred with this report.

## Open items
The DTSC requested that future groundwater monitoring reports include a discussion comparing results and concentration trends from previous sampling events, along with graphs and maps depicting concentration trends of primary COCs over time. All graphics, including existing "stiff" diagrams, must include a legend defining symbols and colors used. Groundwater monitoring should continue on a quarterly schedule.
