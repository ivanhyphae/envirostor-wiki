## Overview
A [[groundwater-monitoring-round]] is a periodic sampling and analysis event conducted to assess groundwater quality. For the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, these rounds are specifically designed to monitor the presence and concentration of Chemicals of Concern (COC) in the groundwater near three soil stockpiles. The primary COCs identified at this site are [[barium]], lead, and [[strontium]].

## Site record
Groundwater monitoring reports have been generated for various dates, including March 2013, June 2013, September 2013, January 2013, November 2012, September 2012, May 2012, and June 2012. {{quote: "GROUNDWATER MONITORING REPORT – MARCH 2013"}} {{quote: "GROUNDWATER MONITORING REPORT – JUNE 2013"}} {{quote: "GROUNDWATER MONITORING REPORT – SEPTEMBER 2013"}} {{quote: "GROUNDWATER MONITORING REPORT – JANUARY 2013"}} {{quote: "GROUNDWATER MONITORING REPORT – NOVEMBER 2012"}} {{quote: "GROUNDWATER MONITORING REPORT – SEPTEMBER 2012"}} {{quote: "GROUNDWATER MONITORING REPORT – MAY 2012"}} {{quote: "ADDITIONAL WELL INSTALLATION AND GROUNDWATER MONITORING REPORT – JUNE 2012"}} {{quote: "GROUNDWATER MONITORING REPORT – MARCH 2012"}}

The monitoring wells were installed in 2006 to monitor groundwater quality in the vicinity of three soil stockpiles. {{quote: "The monitoring wells were installed in 2006 to monitor groundwater quality in the vicinity of three soil stockpiles."}} The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) reviewed a report detailing groundwater monitoring conducted on March 12, 2012, October 5, 2006, and June 14, 2006. {{quote: "The subject report presents analytical results of groundwater monitoring well sampling conducted on March 12, 2012, October 5, 2006, and June 14, 2006."}} The Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB) collected split samples during the March 12, 2012 sampling event. {{quote: "The RWQCB collected "split" water samples during the March 12, 2012 sampling event."}}

Caltrans is required to prepare, at a minimum, quarterly groundwater monitoring reports for groundwater monitoring wells associated with the Site. {{quote: "Caltrans shall prepare, at a minimum, quarterly groundwater monitoring reports for groundwater monitoring wells associated with the Site."}} These reports shall discuss depth to groundwater measurements; well purging and sampling; chain of custody reports; laboratory analysis, including analytes, methods, and results; field and laboratory quality assurance/quality control; and comparison of groundwater monitoring results from previous sampling events. {{quote: "The reports shall discuss: depth to groundwater measurements; well purging and sampling; chain of custody reports; laboratory analysis, including analytes, methods, and results; field and laboratory quality assurance/quality control; comparison of groundwater monitoring results from previous sampling events; and c"}}

## Regulatory status
The DTSC, in consultation with the RWQCB, reviewed a groundwater monitoring report prepared by Geocon Consultants and dated May 2012. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB) has reviewed "Groundwater Monitoring Report, California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) Modesto Soil Stockpiles - State Route (SR) 99 and 132, Modesto, California", prepared by Geocon Consultants and dated May 2012."}} The DTSC concurred with this report. {{quote: "DTSC concurs with the Groundwater Monitoring Report, dated May 2012"}}

## Open items
The DTSC requested that future groundwater monitoring reports include a discussion comparing results and concentration trends from previous sampling events, along with graphs and maps depicting concentration trends of primary COCs over time. {{quote: "DTSC concurs with the Groundwater Monitoring Report, dated May 2012 and requests that future groundwater monitoring reports include a discussion comparing groundwater monitoring results/concentration trends from previous sampling events as more data becomes available. Graphs and maps should be prepared depicting concentration trends of primary COC over time."}} All graphics, including existing "stiff" diagrams, must include a legend defining symbols and colors used. {{quote: "All graphics, including existing "stiff" diagrams should include a legend defining symbols/colors used to depict information."}} Groundwater monitoring should continue on a quarterly schedule. {{quote: "At a minimum, groundwater monitoring should continue on a quartérly schedule."}}
