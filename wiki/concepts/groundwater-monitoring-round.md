A [[groundwater-monitoring-round]] is a periodic sampling and analysis event conducted to assess groundwater quality. For the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, these rounds are specifically designed to monitor the presence and concentration of Chemicals of Concern (COC) in the groundwater near three soil stockpiles. The primary COCs identified at this site are [[barium]], lead, and [[strontium]].

## Site record
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) reviewed a report detailing groundwater monitoring conducted on March 12, 2012, October 5, 2006, and June 14, 2006. {{quote: "The subject report presents analytical results of groundwater monitoring well sampling conducted on March 12, 2012, October 5, 2006, and June 14, 2006."}} These monitoring wells were installed in 2006. {{quote: "The monitoring wells were installed in 2006 to monitor groundwater quality in the vicinity of three soil stockpiles."}} The Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB) collected split samples during the March 12, 2012 sampling event. {{quote: "The RWQCB collected "split" water samples during the March 12, 2012 sampling event."}}

The DTSC requested that future groundwater monitoring reports include a discussion comparing results and concentration trends from previous sampling events, along with graphs and maps depicting concentration trends of primary COCs over time. {{quote: "DTSC concurs with the Groundwater Monitoring Report, dated May 2012 and requests that future groundwater monitoring reports include a discussion comparing groundwater monitoring results/concentration trends from previous sampling events as more data becomes available. Graphs and maps should be prepared depicting concentration trends of primary COC over time."}}

## Regulatory status
The DTSC, in consultation with the RWQCB, reviewed the groundwater monitoring report. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB) has reviewed "Groundwater Monitoring Report, California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) Modesto Soil Stockpiles - State Route (SR) 99 and 132, Modesto, California", prepared by Geocon Consultants and dated May 2012."}} The DTSC concurred with the report. {{quote: "DTSC concurs with the Groundwater Monitoring Report, dated May 2012"}}

## Open items
Future groundwater monitoring reports are required to include a discussion comparing results and concentration trends from previous sampling events. {{quote: "DTSC concurs with the Groundwater Monitoring Report, dated May 2012 and requests that future groundwater monitoring reports include a discussion comparing groundwater monitoring results/concentration trends from previous sampling events as more data becomes available."}} Graphs and maps depicting concentration trends of primary COCs over time are also required. {{quote: "Graphs and maps should be prepared depicting concentration trends of primary COC over time."}} All graphics, including existing "stiff" diagrams, must include a legend defining symbols and colors used. {{quote: "All graphics, including existing "stiff" diagrams should include a legend defining symbols/colors used to depict information."}} Groundwater monitoring should continue on a quarterly schedule. {{quote: "At a minimum, groundwater monitoring should continue on a quartérly schedule."}}
