## 2004 Preliminary Site Investigation (PSI)

### Overview
The 2004 Preliminary Site Investigation (PSI) was conducted by Shaw Environmental, Inc. to characterize soil stockpiles within the SR99/132 Right-of-Way (ROW) for Caltrans. The investigation aimed to identify potential residual chemicals from former FMC impoundments. The PSI involved collecting soil samples from stockpiles, underlying native soil, and background soil for analysis.

### Site Record
The 2004 PSI involved drilling 51 borings. Soil samples were collected and analyzed for heavy metals, PAHs, nitrate, and pH. Analytical results indicated elevated barium concentrations in stockpile samples above the commercial/industrial California Human Health Screening Level (CHHSL). Elevated cadmium above the commercial/industrial CHHSL was detected in soil samples from 8 of 25 borings at Stockpile 2 and in 2 of 10 borings at Stockpile 3. Appendix A of the Fenceline Workplan contains the 2004 PSI boring locations and soil analytical data.

### Regulatory Status
The 2004 PSI identified elevated concentrations of barium and cadmium above commercial/industrial CHHSLs. These findings informed subsequent investigations and regulatory reviews.

### Open Items
The 2004 PSI identified elevated cadmium in specific borings within Stockpiles 2 and 3, which later became a focus for additional sampling as noted in the Fenceline Workplan's "PURPOSE AND PROJECT SCOPE" section.
