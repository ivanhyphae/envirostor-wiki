## 2004 Preliminary Site Investigation (PSI)

### Overview
The 2004 Preliminary Site Investigation (PSI) was conducted by Shaw Environmental, Inc. to characterize soil stockpiles located within the SR99/132 Right-of-Way (ROW) for Caltrans. The investigation aimed to identify potential residual chemicals from former FMC impoundments. The PSI involved collecting soil samples from stockpiles, underlying native soil, and background soil for analysis.

### Site Record
The 2004 PSI consisted of drilling 51 borings. Soil samples were collected and analyzed for heavy metals, PAHs, nitrate, and pH. The analytical results indicated elevated barium concentrations in stockpile samples above the commercial/industrial California Human Health Screening Level (CHHSL). Elevated cadmium above the commercial/industrial CHHSL was detected in soil samples from 8 of 25 borings at Stockpile 2 and in 2 of 10 borings at Stockpile 3 {{quote: "The PSI consisted of drilling 51 borings from which soil samples were collected from stockpile, underlying native soil and background soil and analyzed for heavy metals, PAHs, nitrate and pH. The analytical results indicated elevated barium concentrations in the stockpile samples above the commercial/industrial California Human Health Screening Level (CHHSL). Elevated cadmium above the commercial/industrial CHHSL was further detected in soil samples obtained from 8 of 25 borings at Stockpile 2 and in 2 of 10 borings at Stockpile 3 (see attached Appendix A for 2004 PSI boring locations and soil analytical data)."}}.

### Regulatory Status
The 2004 PSI identified elevated concentrations of barium and cadmium above commercial/industrial CHHSLs. These findings informed subsequent investigations and regulatory reviews.

### Open Items
The 2004 PSI identified elevated cadmium in specific borings within Stockpiles 2 and 3, which later became a focus for additional sampling {{quote: "Additional stockpile soil sampling in areas of elevated cadmium soil impacts identified in Stockpiles 2 and 3 during the 2004 PSI."}}.
