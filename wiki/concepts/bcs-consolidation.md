## Bcs Consolidation

### Overview

Bcs Consolidation refers to the process of consolidating soil within the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site, specifically in the [[carpenter-road-area]] and [[kansas-avenue]] areas, as part of the remediation strategy. This consolidation is a precursor to the implementation of a [[consolidation-and-capping]] remedy, aiming to manage soil with elevated [[barium]] concentrations. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) has been involved in the review and approval of this approach.

### Site Record

The remediation plan for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] involves the consolidation of soil containing barium. This consolidation is primarily focused on the areas identified as the [[carpenter-road-area]] and the [[kansas-avenue]] area. The goal is to bring these soils together to facilitate the subsequent capping process.

Specific details regarding the volume of soil consolidated, the exact dates of consolidation activities, and the precise barium concentrations within the consolidated mass are documented in project reports. For instance, the "RDIP Variance" document, dated October 26, 2021, discusses the consolidation of soil in the Carpenter Road area and the Kansas Avenue area. This variance was submitted by Caltrans to the DTSC.

The consolidation process is a key component of the proposed remedy, which aims to meet [[clean-cap-criteria]]. The effectiveness of the consolidation and subsequent capping will be verified through post-remediation sampling and analysis.

### Regulatory Status

The approach of soil consolidation followed by capping has been reviewed by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). A "RDIP Variance" was submitted by Caltrans on October 26, 2021, to address the consolidation of soil in the Carpenter Road and Kansas Avenue areas. This variance outlines the proposed remediation strategy, which includes consolidation as a necessary step before capping. The DTSC's approval or conditional approval of this variance is critical for the progression of the remediation.

### Open Items

Further details regarding the specific barium concentrations that necessitated consolidation in the Carpenter Road and Kansas Avenue areas, as well as the exact volumes of soil consolidated, are expected to be detailed in subsequent project documentation. The DTSC's final decision on the RDIP Variance and the subsequent implementation and verification of the consolidation and capping remedy remain pending or subject to specific conditions outlined in their review.
