## Overview

The Borrow Area Sampling Plan is the DTSC-approved sampling and analysis protocol for characterizing native soil proposed for use as [[clean-fill-suitable-as-cover]] on the [[barium-containing-soil]] stockpiles at the Caltrans Modesto Highway 132 (SR 132) remediation site. The plan was developed by geocon-consultants|Geocon Consultants at the request of caltrans|Caltrans and covers soil to be excavated from a borrow area located along the SR 132 alignment between carpenter-road-area|Carpenter Road and points east, within State right-of-way held since the 1960s. dtsc|DTSC reviewed and conditionally approved the plan on February 13, 2020, through dean-wright|Dean Wright.

## Site record

### Borrow area location and history

The clean fill source area is situated within the Highway 132 right-of-way, extending eastward from Carpenter Road. Prior to Caltrans ownership in the 1960s, the property was in agricultural use. According to Geocon's February 4, 2020 submittal, previous environmental investigations — the Blackburn 2014 ISA and the Rincon 2018 Phase II ESA — did not identify any high-risk documented contamination releases in the immediate vicinity. Residential backyard testing conducted by Rincon adjacent to and west of Carpenter Road detected pesticides and metals at concentrations below applicable regulatory screening levels.

Geocon noted that test borings drilled at Carpenter Road in 2017 (geotechnical logs appended to the sampling plan) confirmed the material consists of native soil.

### Proposed scope of work

As submitted by Geocon on February 4, 2020:

- **Number of borings:** 12 borings to a depth of 5 feet each
- **Sampling method:** Depth-discrete 4-point composite samples
- **Analytes and depth intervals:**
  - 0–2 feet: organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) and heavy metals (3 composite samples)
  - 3–5 feet: heavy metals only (3 composite samples)
- **Total samples:** 6 composite samples
- **Planned start date:** Tentatively February 11, 2020, pending contract authorization, utility clearance (USA notice), and DTSC approval
- **Laboratory turnaround:** 48-hour analytical turnaround planned if necessary

### DTSC approval and modifications

DTSC responded on February 13, 2020, through Dean Wright, who communicated the agency's sole modification request. DTSC reviewer Perry Myers had no additional comments beyond the one change Wright relayed: **composite 3 samples instead of 4.** No other plan elements were modified.

The approval was transmitted via email from Dean Wright to Richard C. Stewart (Caltrans) and Jennifer Lugo (Caltrans), copied to Juergen Vespermann, Kai Pavel, and Perry Myers.

## Regulatory status

The sampling plan was approved by DTSC subject to one condition: reducing the composite scheme from 4-point to 3-point composites. No other conditions, rejection grounds, or further review steps were imposed. The plan was developed to demonstrate that the borrow area soil meets standards for use as clean fill cover over the BCS (barium-containing soil) stockpiles. Applicable regulatory screening levels referenced in the supporting information include those evaluated in the 2014 ISA and 2018 Phase II ESA, though the approval emails do not specify numeric thresholds.

## Open items

- The field work required contract authorization (Dokken or City), utility mark-out (Bay Cities or Geocon), and a 72-hour USA notice before commencement. Whether these were completed and whether drilling proceeded on the tentative February 11, 2020 date is not documented in the available approval correspondence.
- Analytical results were to be provided to DTSC following laboratory analysis; availability of those results is not addressed in the approval emails.
