## Overview

The **Borrow Area Sampling Plan** (BASP) is the document governing soil characterization and acceptance criteria for off-site fill material imported to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). The BASP was prepared to address the regulatory requirement that any soil brought onto the project site—primarily for backfill after removal of chromium-impacted stockpiles—must meet standards protective of human health and the environment, consistent with the site's Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and DTSC oversight. The plan defines sampling frequency, analytical parameters, and threshold concentrations for borrow sources, ensuring imported material does not introduce new contaminants or exceed site-specific cleanup goals.

## Site record

The BASP was developed as part of the overall site investigation and remediation effort documented in the *Borrow Area Sampling Plan* (document dated June 2022, prepared by Caltrans). The plan applies to borrow sites (off-site sources) that would supply soil for backfill after the removal of approximately 12,000 cubic yards of chromium-contaminated soil from the Modesto Soil Stockpiles (Stockpile Areas 1–4) located at the SR 132 and Oakdale Road interchange.

Key specifications in the BASP include:

- **Sampling frequency**: One composite sample per 500 cubic yards of borrow material, with a minimum of one sample per borrow source. Each composite consists of a minimum of 5 discrete sub-samples collected from the stockpile or excavation face.
- **Analytical parameters**: Total chromium (by EPA 6010/6020), hexavalent chromium (EPA 3060A/7196A), arsenic (EPA 6010/6020), lead (EPA 6010/6020), and pH (EPA 9045). For borrow sources near known agricultural or industrial areas, additional parameters (e.g., pesticides, VOCs) may be required pending DTSC approval.
- **Threshold concentrations**: Borrow soil must not exceed the following (based on site-specific risk-based screening levels and California Human Health Screening Levels, CHHSLs):
  - Total chromium: 100 mg/kg
  - Hexavalent chromium: 0.6 mg/kg
  - Arsenic: 0.9 mg/kg (CHHSL residential)
  - Lead: 80 mg/kg (CHHSL residential)
- **Acceptance protocol**: If any composite sample exceeds a threshold, that increment (500 cubic yards) is rejected. The entire borrow source must be resampled (one per 100 cubic yards) before any additional material can be brought onsite. All analytical results must be submitted to DTSC for review within 14 days of collection.

The BASP explicitly references the site's Cleanup Goal for total hexavalent chromium in soil of 1.0 mg/kg (established in the 2021 RAP), and states that borrow material must not cause a cumulative exceedance of this goal when mixed with final site soils.

## Regulatory status

The BASP was approved by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) on July 15, 2022, as part of the overall RAP implementation. The plan operates under the following regulatory framework:

- **Oversight agency**: DTSC (Project Manager: S. Gonzalez)
- **Governing standard**: The threshold concentrations are based on CHHSLs for residential soil (USEPA RSLs/Prager 2022 for hexavalent chromium) and are consistent with the site RAP-approved cleanup goals. The BASP states these thresholds are "not to be exceeded in any single composite sample."
- **Compliance status**: As of the last documented DTSC status update (September 2023), two borrow sources have been approved (Source A and Source B, both from local aggregate suppliers). All sampling results from those sources (8 composites total) met the BASP thresholds. No importation has occurred yet; the site remains in the stockpile removal phase.

## Open items

- **Pending implementation**: The BASP has not yet been used for active import. Two borrow sources are pre-qualified, but material procurement and delivery are scheduled for Q2 2024 (per the project construction schedule in the 2023 Progress Report).
- **Performance criteria**: DTSC has requested a post-importation verification report summarizing all borrow material analytical results (to be submitted within 30 days of completion of backfill operations). This deliverable is not yet due.
- **Source C consideration**: A third borrow source (near Modesto Airport) has been proposed but has not yet been sampled. The June 2023 DTSC meeting notes indicate this source requires additional metals testing beyond the BASP because of historical agricultural applications; formal acceptance is pending.
