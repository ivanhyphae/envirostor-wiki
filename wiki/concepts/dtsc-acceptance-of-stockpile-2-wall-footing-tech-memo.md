## DTSC Acceptance of Stockpile 2 Wall Footing Tech Memo

### Overview
This entry documents the acceptance by the [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] of a technical memorandum (Memo) regarding soil sampling results for the Stockpile 2 mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area. The acceptance, conveyed via email on June 22, 2020, by [[dean-wright]] of the [[dtsc]], confirms the suitability of certain soil materials for reuse on-site.

### Site Record
On June 18, 2020, [[caltrans]] submitted a revised technical memorandum prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] (authored by [[john-e-juhrend]]) to [[dean-wright]] of the [[dtsc]] and [[perry-myers]] of the [[dtsc]], as well as Steven Meeks of the Water Board. This memo detailed sampling and laboratory analysis of soil along the Stockpile 2 southerly slope MSE wall footing excavation area.

The Memo determined that soil from Stockpile 2 down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones. Soil excavated from depths greater than 3 feet bgs was deemed suitable for use as embankment fill or capping material. This determination was based on laboratory results, which indicated that a specific value of 120 mg/kg, previously referenced, pertained to barium, not lead.

The email from [[dean-wright]] on June 22, 2020, officially accepted the Memo. It stated that both the [[dtsc]] and the Water Board agreed with the Memo's findings. The soil below 3 feet bgs was also considered part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, meaning a new VR was not required for its utilization.

### Regulatory Status
The acceptance of the Stockpile 2 Wall Footing Tech Memo by the [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] signifies regulatory agreement on the management and reuse of excavated soil from this specific area. The decision allows for the reuse of soil below 3 feet bgs as embankment fill or capping material, aligning with the approved Variance Request from April 9, 2020.
