This entry documents the regulatory acceptance of a technical memorandum concerning soil sampling results for the Stockpile 2 mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area. The acceptance, conveyed by the [[dtsc]] on June 22, 2020, confirmed the suitability of certain excavated soil materials for on-site reuse, thereby facilitating project progress.

### Stockpile 2 Wall Footing Technical Memorandum

On June 18, 2020, [[caltrans]] submitted a revised technical memorandum, prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]], to the [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. This memorandum detailed the sampling and laboratory analysis of soil from the Stockpile 2 southerly slope MSE wall footing excavation area. The analysis determined that soil excavated from Stockpile 2 down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) required placement in designated containment zones. However, soil excavated from depths greater than 3 feet bgs was deemed suitable for reuse as embankment fill or capping material. The memorandum clarified that a previously referenced 120 mg/kg value pertained to barium, not [[lead]].

### Regulatory Acceptance

[[dean-wright]] of the [[dtsc]], in an email dated June 22, 2020, officially accepted the Stockpile 2 Wall Footing Technical Memorandum. This acceptance was made in concurrence with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. The email confirmed that both agencies agreed with the memorandum's findings regarding the management of excavated soil. Furthermore, the soil from depths greater than 3 feet bgs was considered part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, eliminating the need for a new VR for its utilization.
