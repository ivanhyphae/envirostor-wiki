## Overview
The Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP) for the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site outlines the procedures for evaluating surface water runoff. The primary objective is to determine if this runoff migrates beyond Caltrans' right-of-way and if it has been impacted by chemicals of potential concern present in the stockpiles. The sampling activities are conducted in accordance with protocols approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC).

## Site record
The "S9525-06-44A Stormwater SAP Addendum 0213," prepared by [[geocon-consultants]], details proposed surface water sampling activities for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. {{quote: "ADDENDUM TO SURFACE WATER SAMPLING AND ANALYSIS PLAN CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES STANISLAUS COUNTY, CALIFORNIA"}} The purpose of this sampling is to assess surface water runoff migration and potential impacts from chemicals of concern within the stockpiles. {{quote: "The purpose of the sampling described in this addendum is to evaluate whether or not surface water runoff from the Site is migrating beyond Caltrans' right-of-way, and if so, whether the storm water has been impacted by chemicals of potential concern in the stockpiles."}}

The scope of services includes surface water sampling, laboratory analysis by a California-certified laboratory, and the preparation of a summary report. {{quote: "The proposed scope of services includes surface water sampling, analysis of the water samples by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of a summary report detailing the sampling activities."}}

The stockpiles, identified as 1 through 3, originated from soil excavated during the construction of SR 99 around 1961. This soil was sourced from property previously owned by Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC) and contained an evaporation pond. {{quote: "Stockpiles 1 through 3 were generated during construction of SR 99 through Modesto around 1961 when Caltrans excavated soil from property purchased from Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC) that contained an evaporation pond."}} These stockpiles were strategically placed in anticipation of the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway project. {{quote: "The stockpiles were placed in their present location in anticipation of construction of the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway project."}}

The "Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan" specifies vertical transect sampling depths at 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet. {{quote: "vertical transect sampling depths at 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet."}} This plan is associated with the SR 132 Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Source Testing Sampling Plan. {{quote: "SR 132 Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Source Testing Sampling Plan."}}

## Regulatory status
The surface water sampling activities are to be conducted in accordance with protocols approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). {{quote: "The surface water sampling will be performed in accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) as established in the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan,* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. and dated January 25, 2006."}} The *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* was prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. and is dated January 25, 2006. {{quote: "*Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan,* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. and dated January 25, 2006."}}
