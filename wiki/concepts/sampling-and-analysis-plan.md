The Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP) outlines the procedures for evaluating surface water runoff from the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site to determine if it migrates beyond Caltrans' right-of-way and if it has been impacted by chemicals of potential concern. The sampling activities are to be conducted in accordance with protocols approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC).

## Site record

The "Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan" details soil sampling depths for testing. Vertical transect sampling depths are specified at 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet. {{quote: "vertical transect sampling depths at 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet."}} This plan is associated with the SR 132 Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Source Testing Sampling Plan. {{quote: "SR 132 Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Source Testing Sampling Plan."}}

The "S9525-06-44A Stormwater SAP Addendum 0213" by [[geocon-consultants]] outlines proposed surface water sampling activities for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. {{quote: "ADDENDUM TO SURFACE WATER SAMPLING AND ANALYSIS PLAN CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES STANISLAUS COUNTY, CALIFORNIA"}} The purpose of this sampling is to evaluate surface water runoff migration and potential impact from chemicals of concern in the stockpiles. {{quote: "The purpose of the sampling described in this addendum is to evaluate whether or not surface water runoff from the Site is migrating beyond Caltrans' right-of-way, and if so, whether the storm water has been impacted by chemicals of potential concern in the stockpiles."}} The surface water sampling will be performed according to protocols approved by the DTSC, as established in the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan,* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. and dated January 25, 2006. {{quote: "The surface water sampling will be performed in accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) as established in the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan,* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. and dated January 25, 2006."}} The scope includes surface water sampling, laboratory analysis by a California-certified laboratory, and a summary report. {{quote: "The proposed scope of services includes surface water sampling, analysis of the water samples by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of a summary report detailing the sampling activities."}}

The stockpiles (1 through 3) were generated during the construction of SR 99 around 1961 from soil excavated from property that contained an evaporation pond. {{quote: "Stockpiles 1 through 3 were generated during construction of SR 99 through Modesto around 1961 when Caltrans excavated soil from property purchased from Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC) that contained an evaporation pond."}} These stockpiles were placed in anticipation of the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway project. {{quote: "The stockpiles were placed in their present location in anticipation of construction of the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway project."}}

## Regulatory status

The surface water sampling activities are to be performed in accordance with protocols approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). {{quote: "The surface water sampling will be performed in accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) as established in the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan,* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. and dated January 25, 2006."}}
