## DTSC Approval

The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) is a regulatory agency involved in the remediation of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. DTSC's approval is a key component in the decision-making process for the project's remedial actions.

### Site Record

The project record indicates that DTSC has reviewed and provided input on various project documents. For instance, the "DRAFT FINAL REMEDIAL ACTION PLAN" dated October 2014 was prepared for Caltrans regarding the Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, West Freeway/Expressway Project in Stanislaus County, California. This plan outlines proposed remedial actions, which would be subject to DTSC's review and approval.

### Regulatory Status

DTSC's role in the project involves overseeing the remediation process to ensure compliance with environmental regulations. Specific approvals from DTSC are required for the implementation of the chosen remedial strategies. The project documents suggest a collaborative process between Caltrans, its consultants (such as [[geocon-consultants-inc]]), and regulatory agencies like DTSC and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]].

### Open Items

Further DTSC approval would be required for the finalization and implementation of the Remedial Action Plan. The specific conditions or requirements for DTSC's approval are detailed within the project's regulatory correspondence and planning documents.
