DTSC approval refers to review and concurrence by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control on the technical documents [[geocon-consultants-inc]] prepared for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site, including the Supplemental Site Investigation (SSI) Report, the Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA) Update, the Final Feasibility Study (FS) Report, and the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP). DTSC's role in the record is as the lead agency for the human health risk and remedy-selection track of the project, running in parallel with [[central-valley-rwqcb-approval]] on the water quality side.

## Sequence of deliverables submitted for DTSC review

The document chain reflects an iterative regulatory process: the SSI Report characterized soil conditions at the stockpiles, the HHRA Update (dated December 17, 2012, revised March 1, 2013) evaluated human health risk from the constituents detected, the Final FS Report (dated June 2014) evaluated remedial alternatives, and the Draft Final RAP (revised October 2014) proposed the selected remedial action. Each later document builds on findings carried forward from the one before it — the FS Report's alternatives analysis and the RAP's proposed remedy both rely on the exposure and risk conclusions reached in the HHRA Update.

## Basis for the risk and remedy findings

The HHRA Update's conclusions, which the RAP's remedy selection depends on, rest on comparison of site soil concentrations to the California Human Health Screening Levels ([[chhsl]]). [[barium]] is among the constituents identified at the stockpiles and addressed in this risk and remedy chain.

## What the record does not show

The available material does not contain the text of a DTSC approval letter, a decision date, or any conditions of approval attached to the SSI Report, HHRA Update, FS Report, or RAP. The documents in the file are titled as drafts or revisions (the RAP is captioned "Draft Final," the HHRA Update is captioned "Revised March 1, 2013"), consistent with a document still moving through agency review, but no approval, conditional approval, or rejection by DTSC is recorded in the source material reviewed here.
