## Overview

The California Primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) is the enforceable drinking water standard for a given chemical, established by the California State Water Resources Control Board's Division of Drinking Water (DDW) under the California Safe Drinking Water Act. On the Modesto Soil Stockpiles Project site (SR 132, Stanislaus County), Primary MCLs serve as the human-health based threshold for evaluating groundwater quality impacts from soil stockpile leachate. The project record specifically invokes the Primary MCL of **1,000 µg/L** for **barium** per the *S9525-06-25 Modesto Stockpiles Well Install and June 2012 GW Report 1112* (hereafter "GW Report"). This MCL is the criterion that determined whether stockpile-associated barium contamination exceeded regulatory limits and whether installed monitoring wells met California drinking water standards for that parameter.

## Site record

The GW Report establishes the Primary MCL for barium as the applicable regulatory benchmark at this site. The key documented facts from the record:

- **Threshold value**: Barium Primary MCL = **1,000 µg/L** (equivalent to 1,000 ppb or 1.0 mg/L).
- **Source of standard**: California Primary MCL, as enforced by the DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control) for groundwater remediation projects under the California Safe Drinking Water Act.
- **Corresponding soil data**: The stockpile soils were characterized with **barium at 760 mg/kg** (units: milligrams per kilogram — a soil concentration), about 760 times higher than the water standard. This soil value is referenced in the GW Report to show the source potential for groundwater impact.
- **Site context**: The barium MCL was used to assess groundwater from two installed monitoring wells (reported in the GW Report) to determine whether stockpile leachate had elevated barium to levels above drinking water limits.
- **Date of assessment**: June 2012 GW Report date; the monitoring well installation occurred between 2006 and 2012 (exact date not specified in available document).
- **Comparison results**: The record indicates groundwater barium concentrations were measured and compared against the 1,000 µg/L MCL. No exceedances were reported in the GW Report for the monitored wells at that time, but verification results from ongoing monitoring (if any) are not fully documented in the provided sources.

## Regulatory status

- **Applicable standard**: California Primary MCL for barium = **1,000 µg/L**.
- **Enforcing agencies**: DTSC (lead agency for the remediation project) and DDW (state drinking water regulator). The DTSC applies the MCL as a groundwater cleanup standard under its authority.
- **Compliance status**: Based on the June 2012 GW Report, the groundwater in the two installed monitoring wells was reported to be below the barium MCL. The project is therefore in compliance with the barium MCL for those wells at the time of sampling. Whether ongoing compliance is maintained requires review of subsequent monitoring data not provided in the available case file excerpt.
