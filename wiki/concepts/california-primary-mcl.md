## California Primary MCL

### Overview
The California Primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) is a regulatory standard used to assess groundwater quality. In the context of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, these MCLs are applied to general mineral constituents detected in groundwater samples.

### Site Record
During the January 2013 groundwater sampling event, with the exception of nitrate, none of the reported general minerals exceeded their respective California primary MCLs.

### Regulatory Status
California Primary MCLs are the applicable standards for assessing general mineral constituents in groundwater at the site.
