# MW-6

## Overview

MW-6 is a groundwater monitoring well located south of Kansas Avenue, in the vicinity of Stockpile 2 at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). It is part of a network of ten monitoring wells (MW-1 through MW-10) installed to assess groundwater conditions adjacent to three soil stockpiles generated during SR 99 construction. MW-6 was installed in 2006 as part of the original well network (MW-1 through MW-8). The well monitors shallow groundwater downgradient of the stockpiles.

## Site record

### Well condition and field observations

During a pre-sampling inspection on September 4, 2013, the concrete and well box for MW-6 were observed to be damaged, likely from a lawnmower. The well casing itself did not appear damaged. The well box was replaced on September 23, 2013, and the well locks were also replaced during the September 2013 sampling event.

### Groundwater geochemistry

Groundwater sampled from MW-6 in September 2013 was classified as sodium-dominant based on Stiff diagram analysis. This is consistent with historical patterns, as samples from MW-6, along with MW-3 and MW-8, have consistently been reported as sodium-dominant across sampling events from 2012 through September 2013. This contrasts with calcium-dominant wells such as [[mw-1]], [[mw-2]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-7]], [[mw-9]], and [[mw-10]].

### Nitrate as nitrogen

Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-6 equaled or exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/L during the September 2013 sampling event. This exceedance was also reported for [[mw-1]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-5]], and [[mw-10]].

### Total dissolved solids (TDS)

TDS concentrations in MW-6 exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in September 2013. This exceedance was also reported for [[mw-1]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-5]], and [[mw-10]].

### Sulfide

Sulfide was reported in the MW-6 sample collected in September 2013 at a concentration of 0.062 mg/L, analyzed by EPA method.

### Dissolved metals

In the September 2013 sampling event, no dissolved metals concentrations in the MW-6 groundwater sample exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium were reported at concentrations similar to historical levels and remained significantly below numeric water quality thresholds. Lead was not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than the practical quantitation limit of 1.0 µg/L. Dissolved metals analysis was performed by ICP-MS (EPA 6020) and dissolved mercury by cold vapor AA (EPA 7470A).

### Cation-anion balance

A cation-anion balance analysis was performed on the MW-6 sample collected in September 2013 as part of the laboratory reporting.

## Regulatory status

During the September 2013 sampling event, analytical results were compared to Primary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) and Secondary MCLs promulgated by the California Department of Public Health (CDPH). For MW-6, nitrate as nitrogen concentrations equaled or exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/L, and TDS concentrations exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L. These exceedances were documented but not attributed to the stockpiles, as the upgradient well [[mw-1]] also showed similar exceedances, suggesting they may represent natural background concentrations for shallow groundwater in the site vicinity. Dissolved metals remained below all applicable numeric water quality threshold values.
