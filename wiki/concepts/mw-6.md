## Overview
MW-6 is a groundwater monitoring well at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It has been sampled and analyzed as part of multiple groundwater monitoring events.

## Site Record
During the March 12, 2012, sampling event, MW-6 went dry after purging 1 gallon. It was allowed to recover, purged of an additional half gallon, allowed to recover again, and then sampled. Purged groundwater from MW-6 was placed into a 55-gallon drum.

In March 2012, dissolved metals analyses were conducted for samples from MW-6. Antimony, arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, copper, lead, selenium, silver, thallium, and zinc were not reported above their respective PQLs. Barium, vanadium, and strontium were reported, with concentrations not exceeding drinking water thresholds. Total chromium and mercury were reported. Nickel and manganese were reported for MW-4, and molybdenum was reported for MW-6. The dissolved metals chromium, mercury, nickel, and manganese concentrations were reported for MW-6, with none exceeding drinking water thresholds, except for manganese in MW-4. Mercury results were considered estimated due to its presence in the equipment blank.

General minerals analyses for MW-6 in March 2012 indicated bicarbonate (HCO3) dominant water. Stiff diagrams for March 2012 showed MW-6 as sodium-dominant, a characteristic also observed in October 2006. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 18 mg/l in MW-6, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. Total dissolved solids (TDS) were also reported for MW-6.

In November 2012, MW-6 went dry after purging 1 gallon. It was allowed to recover, purged of an additional 1.5 gallons, allowed to recover a second time, and then sampled. In November 2012, MW-6 had a calcium-dominant composition according to Stiff diagrams. Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-6 were 24 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL. TDS concentrations in MW-6 were 640 mg/l, meeting or exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

In September 2013, MW-6 went dry after purging 1.5 gallons. It was allowed to recover, purged of an additional gallon, allowed to recover a second time, and then sampled. In September 2013, MW-6 had a sodium-dominant composition. Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-6 were 22 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL. TDS concentrations in MW-6 were 630 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL.

In December 2013, MW-6 was reported to have manganese detected at a concentration exceeding its numeric water quality threshold. In December 2013, MW-6 was sodium-dominant according to Stiff diagrams. Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-6 were 25 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL. TDS concentrations in MW-6 were 630 mg/l, meeting or exceeding the secondary MCL.

In February 2014, MW-6 was sodium-dominant according to Stiff diagrams. Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-6 were 34 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL. TDS concentrations in MW-6 were 750 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL.

In April 2019, MW-6 had a sodium-dominant composition. Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-6 were 40 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL. TDS concentrations in MW-6 were 760 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL.

## Regulatory Status
Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-6 have consistently exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/l in multiple sampling events, including March 2012 (18 mg/l), November 2012 (24 mg/l), September 2013 (22 mg/l), December 2013 (25 mg/l), February 2014 (34 mg/l), and April 2019 (40 mg/l).

TDS concentrations in MW-6 have also exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l in multiple sampling events, including November 2012 (640 mg/l), September 2013 (630 mg/l), December 2013 (630 mg/l), February 2014 (750 mg/l), and April 2019 (760 mg/l).

Manganese was detected in the groundwater sample from MW-6 in April 2019, exceeding its numeric water quality threshold.
