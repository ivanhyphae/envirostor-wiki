MW-6 is a groundwater monitoring well located at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, established to track potential impacts to groundwater from the soil stockpiles. It has been a consistent sampling point in the site's groundwater monitoring program.

## Groundwater Monitoring Results

### Nitrate as Nitrogen and Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)

Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-6 have consistently exceeded the primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) of 10 mg/l across multiple sampling events:

| Date        | Nitrate as Nitrogen (mg/l) | Exceeds Primary MCL (10 mg/l) |
| :---------- | :------------------------- | :---------------------------- |
| March 2012  | 18                         | Yes                           |
| November 2012 | 24                         | Yes                           |
| September 2013 | 22                         | Yes                           |
| December 2013 | 25                         | Yes                           |
| February 2014 | 34                         | Yes                           |
| April 2019  | 40                         | Yes                           |

Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) concentrations in MW-6 have also frequently met or exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l:

| Date        | TDS (mg/l) | Meets or Exceeds Secondary MCL (500 mg/l) |
| :---------- | :--------- | :---------------------------------------- |
| November 2012 | 640        | Yes                                       |

### Dissolved Metals

In March 2012, dissolved metals including antimony, arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, copper, lead, selenium, silver, thallium, and zinc were not reported above their respective PQLs in MW-6. Barium, vanadium, and strontium were reported at concentrations below drinking water thresholds. Total chromium and mercury were reported. Dissolved chromium and mercury concentrations were reported for MW-6, with mercury results considered estimated due to its presence in the equipment blank. In April 2019, manganese was detected in the groundwater sample from MW-6, exceeding its numeric water quality threshold.

### General Minerals and Water Chemistry

In March 2012, general minerals analyses indicated bicarbonate (HCO3) dominant water in MW-6. Stiff diagrams for March 2012 showed MW-6 as sodium-dominant, a characteristic also observed in October 2006. In November 2012, MW-6 had a calcium-dominant composition according to Stiff diagrams. By September 2013, MW-6 exhibited a sodium-dominant composition. In December 2013 and February 2014, Stiff diagrams indicated MW-6 was sodium-dominant. In April 2019, MW-6 also had a sodium-dominant composition.

## Well Purging and Sampling Notes

During several sampling events, MW-6 went dry after initial purging, requiring recovery periods before sampling could be completed.

*   **March 12, 2012:** Went dry after purging 1 gallon. Recovered, purged an additional 0.5 gallons, recovered again, then sampled.
*   **November 2012:** Went dry after purging 1 gallon. Recovered, purged an additional 1.5 gallons, recovered again, then sampled.
*   **March 18, 2013:** Went dry after purging 1.5 gallons. Recovered, purged an additional 1.7 gallons, recovered again, then sampled.
*   **June 5-6, 2013:** Went dry after purging 1.75 gallons. Recovered, purged an additional 2.5 gallons, recovered again, then sampled.
*   **April 12, 2017:** Insufficient water to purge and sample.
