# MW-6

## Overview

MW-6 is a groundwater monitoring well located south of Kansas Avenue, in the vicinity of Stockpile 2 at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). It is part of a network of ten monitoring wells (MW-1 through MW-10) installed to assess groundwater conditions adjacent to three soil stockpiles generated during SR 99 construction. MW-6 was installed in 2006 as part of the original well network (MW-1 through MW-8). The well monitors shallow groundwater downgradient of the stockpiles.

## Site record

### Well condition and field observations

During a pre-sampling inspection on September 4, 2013, the concrete and well box for MW-6 were observed to be damaged, likely from a lawnmower. The well casing itself did not appear damaged. The well box was replaced on September 23, 2013, and the well locks were also replaced during the September 2013 sampling event. {{quote: "On September 4, 2013, prior to opening the wells, Geocon observed each of the ten well boxes for signs of potential tampering. No signs of tampering were observed; however, the concrete and well box for MW-6 appeared damaged. We believe that the damage occurred when the well box was run over with a lawnmower. The well casing did not appear damaged. Upon receiving authorization, we replaced the well box on September 23, 2013. In addition, we replaced locks on each of the wells."}} {{quote: "wiki/sources/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013.md"}}

### Groundwater geochemistry

Groundwater sampled from MW-6 in September 2013 was classified as sodium-dominant based on Stiff diagram analysis. This is consistent with historical patterns, as samples from MW-6, along with MW-3 and MW-8, have consistently been reported as sodium-dominant across sampling events from 2012 through September 2013. This contrasts with calcium-dominant wells such as [[mw-1]], [[mw-2]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-7]], [[mw-9]], and [[mw-10]]. {{quote: "In September 2013, the samples from wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-7, MW-9 and MW-10 had a calcium-dominant composition while the samples from wells MW-3, MW-5, MW-6 and MW-8 were sodium-dominant."}} {{quote: "wiki/sources/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013.md"}}

### Nitrate as nitrogen

Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-6 equaled or exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/L during the September 2013 sampling event. This exceedance was also reported for [[mw-1]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-5]], and [[mw-10]]. {{quote: "The reported nitrate concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equal or exceed the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l"}} {{quote: "wiki/sources/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013.md"}}

### Total dissolved solids (TDS)

TDS concentrations in MW-6 exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L in September 2013. This exceedance was also reported for [[mw-1]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-5]], and [[mw-10]]. {{quote: "and the reported TDS concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6 and MW-10 exceed the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l."}} {{quote: "wiki/sources/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013.md"}}

### Sulfide

Sulfide was reported in the MW-6 sample collected in September 2013 at a concentration of 0.062 mg/L, analyzed by EPA method. {{quote: "Sulfide was reported for six of the ten samples with concentrations ranging from 0.011 (MW-3) to 0.28 mg/l (MW-1)."}} {{quote: "wiki/sources/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013.md"}}

### Dissolved metals

In the September 2013 sampling event, no dissolved metals concentrations in the MW-6 groundwater sample exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium were reported at concentrations similar to historical levels and remained significantly below numeric water quality thresholds. Lead was not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than the practical quantitation limit of 1.0 µg/L. Dissolved metals analysis was performed by ICP-MS (EPA 6020) and dissolved mercury by cold vapor AA (EPA 7470A). {{quote: "In the September 2013 sampling event, no dissolved metals concentrations in the MW-6 groundwater sample exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium were reported at concentrations similar to historical levels and remained significantly below numeric water quality thresholds. Lead was not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than the practical quantitation limit of 1.0 µg/l."}} {{quote: "wiki/sources/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013.md"}}

### Cation-anion balance

A cation-anion balance analysis was performed on the MW-6 sample collected in September 2013 as part of the laboratory reporting. {{quote: "A cation-anion balance analysis was performed on the MW-6 sample collected in September 2013 as part of the laboratory reporting."}} {{quote: "wiki/sources/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013/S9525-01-44C Modesto Stockpiles GW September 2013 1013.md"}}

## Regulatory status

During the September 2013 sampling event, analytical results were compared to Primary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) and Secondary MCLs promulgated by the California Department of Public Health (CDPH). For MW-6, nitrate as nitrogen concentrations equaled or exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/L, and TDS concentrations exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L. These exceedances were documented but not attributed to the stockpiles, as the upgradient well [[mw-1]] also showed similar exceedances, suggesting they may represent natural background concentrations for shallow groundwater in the site vicinity. Dissolved metals remained below all applicable numeric water quality threshold values. {{quote: "During the September 2013 sampling event, analytical results were compared to Primary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) and Secondary MCLs promulgated by the California Department of Public Health (CDPH). ## **Project Description and History**

Stockpiles 1 through 3 were generated during construction of SR 99 through Modesto around 1961 when Caltrans excavated soil from property purchased from Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC) that contained an evaporation pond. The stockpiles were placed in their present location in anticipation of construction of the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway project.

During the 1930s, Barium Products Ltd. occupied property at 1200 Barium Road (now Graphics Drive) in Modesto just east of SR 99 between Woodland and Kansas Avenues. Barium Products Ltd. was a chemical manufacturing company processing a variety of ores and minerals including barite (barium sulfate) and celestite (strontium sulfate). Materials produced included barium and strontium compounds; these were used in greases, lubricating oil and pigment blanks. Sodium sulfide generated as a by-product of barite processing was sold as a caustic and used as a reagent in the mining industry.

In 1943, Barium Products Ltd. was purchased by Westvaco Chlorine Products Corporation which subsequently merged with FMC in 1948. From the 1950s to the 1970s, a liquid residue from the processing operations was discharged to unlined evaporation ponds along the western portion of the FMC Site. The approximate boundaries of the former evaporation/disposal ponds are shown on Figure 2.

In 1961, a 4.3–acre parcel at the southwestern corner of the FMC site was purchased by the State of California for highway right-of-way needed to construct SR 99. An aerial photograph from 1957 shows that a portion of the southernmost pond on the FMC property was within the area purchased for right-of-way.

Soil in and around the pond was excavated during construction of SR 99 and stockpiled within the current Caltrans right-of-way at the location of the future State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway project. Three distinct stockpiles are present at the Site:

- Stockpile 1, located south of Kansas Avenue and west of North Emerald Avenue,
- Stockpile 2, located south of Kansas Avenue, between North Emerald Avenue and SR 99, and
- Stockpile 3, located south of Kansas Avenue and east of SR 99.
