## Stockpile Cover Integrity

### Overview
Stockpile cover integrity refers to the condition and effectiveness of the materials used to cover soil stockpiles at the State Route 132 Modesto site, ensuring containment and preventing erosion. This is assessed through regular site inspections.

### Site Record
The "Annual Stockpile Inspection Form" dated March 15, 2018, provides descriptions and photographic evidence of stockpile cover conditions for Stockpile #1 and Stockpile #2.

For Stockpile #2, the north slope is described as grass-covered. An associated image depicts a sloped, grass-covered embankment. Drainage structures on the north slope of Stockpile #2 include a corrugated metal drainage pipe discharging near riprap. A textured concrete retaining wall is present along a gravel path for Stockpile #2.

For Stockpile #1, the north slope is also described as grass-covered. Images show a grassy, sloping embankment. A plain light-gray concrete block wall is observed alongside the north lane of Stockpile #1. Pavement surface conditions for Stockpile #1 are also documented, with an image showing the paved surface conditions looking east.
