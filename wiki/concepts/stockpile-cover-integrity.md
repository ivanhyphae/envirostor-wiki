## Stockpile Cover Integrity

### Overview
Stockpile cover integrity refers to the condition and effectiveness of the materials used to cover soil stockpiles at the State Route 132 Modesto site, ensuring containment and preventing erosion. This is assessed through regular site inspections documented in the "Annual Stockpile Inspection Form."

### Site Record
The "Annual Stockpile Inspection Form" provides descriptions and photographic evidence of stockpile cover conditions.

For Stockpile #2, the north slope is described as grass-covered {{quote: "North shore of Stockpile #2"}}. An associated image depicts a sloped, grass-covered embankment {{quote: "North shore of Stockpile #2"}}. Drainage structures on the north slope of Stockpile #2 include a corrugated metal drainage pipe discharging near riprap {{quote: "Drainage structures, north slope of Stockpile #2"}}. A textured concrete retaining wall is present along a gravel path for Stockpile #2 {{quote: "Retaining wall Stockpile #2"}}.

For Stockpile #1, the north slope is also described as grass-covered {{quote: "North slope Stockpile #1"}}. Images show a grassy, sloping embankment {{quote: "North slope Stockpile #1"}}. A plain light-gray concrete block wall is observed alongside the north lane of Stockpile #1 {{quote: "North lane Stockpile #1"}}. Pavement surface conditions for Stockpile #1 are also documented, with an image showing the pave surface conditions looking east {{quote: "Pave surface conditions Stock Pile #1 (looking east)"}}.
