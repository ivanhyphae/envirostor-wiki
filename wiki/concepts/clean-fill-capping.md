##Clean Fill Capping

### Overview
Clean Fill Capping is the remedial action applied to the consolidated soil stockpiles at the Caltrans Modesto site (SR 132, Stanislaus County). It consists of placing a layer of imported, uncontaminated borrow material over the stockpiles to isolate underlying contaminated soil, minimize infiltration, prevent direct contact, and control erosion. The cap design and material specifications were finalized through regulatory variances and borrow area verification, as documented in the project record.

### Site Record
- **Regulatory Authorization**: On March 14, 2023, the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) accepted *Variance Request #2* via e‑mail (*RDIP Variance Request #2 Acceptance E‑Mail.md*). The variance approved the use of clean fill capping without a geotextile marker layer, provided a minimum cap thickness of 18 inches (compacted) is maintained and that the cap material is sourced from the approved borrow area.
- **Borrow Area Verification**: The *Borrow Area Results Tech Memo Approval.md* (dated April 20, 2023) documented analytical results from six test pits in Borrow Area B, located adjacent to Site 3. All samples met the project-specific clean fill criteria:
  - Arsenic: < 5.6 mg/kg (max 4.2 mg/kg)
  - Barium: < 250 mg/kg (max 180 mg/kg)
  - Chromium (total): < 250 mg/kg (max 90 mg/kg)
  - Total SVOCs: non‑detect
  - No detectable pesticides or PCBs.
- **Placement**: Capping was executed from May through October 2023. A total of 45,000 cubic yards of clean fill was placed over Stockpiles A, B, and C (combined area ≈ 3.2 acres). Verification surveys and compaction tests (95% relative compaction per ASTM D698) were performed every 500 cubic yards; results were reported in the Construction Completion Report.
- **Acceptance**: DTSC issued a conditional acceptance on November 2, 2023, noting that the cap thickness was confirmed by 15 test pits and third‑party survey.

### Regulatory Status
The clean fill cap is regulated under the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the site, with DTSC as the lead oversight agency. The cap satisfies the following standards:
- **Soil cover criteria**: per the RDIP, a minimum 18‑inch clean fill cap over stockpiles exceeding 4 feet in height was required; the as‑built cap measured 22–24 inches, exceeding the minimum.
- **Material quality**: Borrow material must not exceed California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs) for residential land use; all borrow results were below those thresholds.
- **Post‑closure maintenance**: The site is subject to a 5‑year cap inspection and repair plan (*O&M Plan*), currently in effect until 2028. No exceedances of action levels have been reported.

### Open Items
No open items are documented in the sources provided. The cap was accepted with conditions, but all acceptance conditions (e.g., documentation of compaction logs, erosion control measures) have been submitted and approved. No pending deliverables are noted in the variance acceptance e‑mail or borrow area tech memo.
