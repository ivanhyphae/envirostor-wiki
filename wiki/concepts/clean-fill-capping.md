# Clean Fill Capping

## Overview
Clean fill capping refers to the use of native soil excavated from the Highway 132 right-of-way east of Carpenter Road as cover material for the Barium-Containing Soils (BCS) stockpiles at the SR 132 corridor project in Stanislaus County. The concept is documented in a February 4, 2020 submittal from Geocon Consultants to Caltrans, forwarded to DTSC and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board for review and approval.

## Site record
On February 4, 2020, Geocon Consultants (John Juhrend, PE, CEG) submitted a proposed clean fill source sampling plan to Caltrans (Richard C. Stewart, PG 7926) for use as BCS stockpile clean fill cover. The contractor planned to excavate the upper five feet of soil along the Highway 132 alignment, extending eastward from carpenter-road-area|Carpenter Road. This portion of the project has been State right-of-way since the 1960s; prior land use was agricultural.

Geocon reviewed the Blackburn 2014 ISA and Rincon 2018 Phase II ESA for potential contaminant sources in the vicinity. No high-risk documented contamination releases were reported in the immediate vicinity of the proposed clean fill source area. Testing of residential backyards by Rincon adjacent to and west of Carpenter Road indicated pesticides and metals at concentrations less than applicable regulatory screening levels.

The sampling plan proposed twelve 5-foot-deep borings in the clean import fill source area, with six depth-discrete 4-point composite samples analyzed as follows:
- **0–2 feet:** organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) and heavy metals (3 composite samples)
- **3–5 feet:** heavy metals (3 composite samples)

Geocon tentatively scheduled drilling for February 11, 2020, with a 48-hour analytical turnaround planned if necessary. Geotechnical logs of test borings completed at Carpenter Road in 2017 were appended to document that material at the location is comprised of native soil. Caltrans transmitted the submittal to DTSC (Dean Wright, Perry Myers) and the Water Boards (Steven Meeks) the same day, requesting quick review and acceptance.

## Regulatory status
The sampling plan was submitted to DTSC for approval as of February 4, 2020. No DTSC approval or concurrence is documented in the available source material. Acceptance of the clean fill source characterization results remains pending.

## Open items
- DTSC approval of the clean fill source sampling plan is not confirmed in the source record.
- Analytical results from the proposed borings were to be provided to DTSC following completion; no results are documented in the available material.
