## RDIP Errata Acceptance

### Overview
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) formally accepted the Errata sheet and transmittal letter for the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the SR 132 West Expressway Project Modesto Stockpiles. This acceptance signifies DTSC's approval of revisions or corrections made to the original RDIP.

### Site Record
On February 5, 2020, DTSC Project Manager Dean Wright communicated DTSC's acceptance of the RDIP Errata sheet and transmittal letter. This communication was sent to Juergen Vespermann of [[caltrans]] and CC'd Perry Myers of DTSC and Richard C. Stewart of Caltrans. The transmittal letter and Errata sheet were provided by Juergen Vespermann on the same date.

### Regulatory Status
DTSC's acceptance of the RDIP Errata indicates that the agency has reviewed and approved the documented changes to the remedial design and implementation plan for the Modesto Stockpiles.
