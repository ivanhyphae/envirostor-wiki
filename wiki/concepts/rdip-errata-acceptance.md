The acceptance of the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) Errata by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) signifies approval of revisions to the plan for the SR 132 West Expressway Project Modesto Stockpiles. This regulatory decision is a critical step in the remediation process, confirming that proposed changes meet the necessary standards.

### RDIP Errata Acceptance by DTSC

On February 5, 2020, DTSC Project Manager Dean Wright formally communicated DTSC's acceptance of the RDIP Errata sheet and its accompanying transmittal letter. This acceptance was directed to Juergen Vespermann of [[caltrans]], with Perry Myers of DTSC and Richard C. Stewart of Caltrans copied on the correspondence. The transmittal letter and Errata sheet themselves were provided by Juergen Vespermann on the same date. This action confirms that DTSC has reviewed and approved the documented changes to the remedial design and implementation plan for the Modesto Stockpiles.
