## Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling Tech Memo

The Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling Tech Memo details the sampling and analysis conducted to confirm the effectiveness of remediation efforts for Stockpile 2 at the SR 132 site in Stanislaus County, California. This memo was submitted to and approved by the [[dtsc]] (DTSC).

## Site record

The Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling Tech Memo documented the results of confirmation sampling performed on Stockpile 2. The memo indicated that the sampling was conducted to verify that the remediation of Stockpile 2 met the requirements outlined in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]]. The memo was approved by the DTSC. {{quote: "The Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling Tech Memo was submitted to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for approval. The DTSC approved the Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling Tech Memo."}}

## Regulatory status

The remediation of Stockpile 2, as detailed in the Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling Tech Memo, was subject to the approval of the [[dtsc]] (DTSC). The DTSC's approval signifies that the remediation activities met the established regulatory requirements for this stockpile.
