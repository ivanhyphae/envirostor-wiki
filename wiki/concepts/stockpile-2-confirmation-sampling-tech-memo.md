## Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling Tech Memo

The Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling Tech Memo documents the sampling and analysis performed to confirm the remediation of Stockpile 2 at the SR 132 site in Stanislaus County, California. This technical memorandum was submitted to and approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC).

## Site record

The Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling Tech Memo details the results of confirmation sampling conducted on Stockpile 2. The sampling was performed to verify that the remediation of Stockpile 2 met the requirements outlined in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]]. The DTSC approved this technical memorandum.

## Regulatory status

The remediation of Stockpile 2, as detailed in the Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling Tech Memo, required and received approval from the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). This approval indicates that the remediation activities for Stockpile 2 met the established regulatory requirements.
