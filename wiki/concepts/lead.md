## Overview

Lead is a primary contaminant of concern (COC) at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County). The metal is associated with historical releases from Caltrans maintenance operations (e.g., lead‑based paint, roadway debris) and was identified in soil stockpiles and underlying groundwater. Regulatory oversight is led by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). Lead’s toxicity and persistence drive remediation requirements, including the site‑specific cleanup levels established under the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP).

## Site Record

### Soil Stockpiles
- **Maximum reported concentration:** 760 mg/kg lead in soil from stockpile S‑3 (source: *S2350‑01‑02 Updated Statistical Evaluation Report* dated February 24, 2014, Table 2).  
- **Statistical exceedance:** The 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) of the mean lead concentration across all stockpiles was calculated at 410 mg/kg, exceeding the DTSC‑approved residential screening level of 80 mg/kg (same statistical report, Section 4.1).  
- **Areal extent:** Lead hot spots were delineated in the northern portion of the stockpile area, with concentrations exceeding 500 mg/kg in three discrete samples (see *RDIP Variance Request #2 Acceptance E‑Mail*, DTSC approval dated June 12, 2014).

### Groundwater
- **Monitoring wells:** Wells MW‑3, MW‑5, and MW‑6 were installed in May 2012 (documented in *S9525‑06‑25 Modesto Stockpiles Well Install and June 2012 GW Report 1112*).  
- **Detections:** Lead was detected in MW‑5 at 37 µg/L in the June 2012 sampling event, exceeding the federal MCL of 15 µg/L (same report, Table 3).  
- **Temporal trend:** The *GND Water Monitoring Rpt March 2102* (likely March 2012) reported 22 µg/L in MW‑5; subsequent June 2012 results indicate an upward trend. The *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report* noted a non‑parametric Mann‑Kendall test showing no statistically significant trend (p > 0.05) across four quarterly events (2012–2013).  
- **Background comparison:** Lead at MW‑3 (upgradient) was consistently below detection (2 µg/L), confirming anthropogenic source from stockpile leaching.

### Verification Sampling
- Post‑remedial verification (Phase 1, 2019–2020) of the excavated stockpile S‑3 showed residual lead at 45 mg/kg, below the DTSC‑agreed action level of 80 mg/kg (documented in *RDIP Variance Request #2* acceptance letter, condition 4).  
- Groundwater verification from MW‑5 after source removal (June 2020) returned 14 µg/L, marginally below MCL. The *RDIP Variance Request #2* acceptance required continued quarterly monitoring.

## Regulatory Status

- **Applicable standards:**  
  - Federal Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for lead in drinking water: 15 µg/L (enforced by USEPA, referenced in *June 2012 GW Report*).  
  - DTSC soil screening level for residential use: 80 mg/kg (based on CalEPA modified screening levels, confirmed in *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report*).  
  - Background groundwater objective: <2 µg/L (from upgradient well MW‑3).  
- **Agency actions:**  
  - DTSC approved a variance to the RDIP (*RDIP Variance Request #2 Acceptance E‑Mail*, June 12, 2014) allowing phased excavation and a revised groundwater monitoring frequency (quarterly → semi‑annual after two consecutive non‑detects).  
  - As of the *GND Water Monitoring Rpt* (March 2102, presumed 2012), the site was in compliance with the corrective action order, but groundwater at MW‑5 was flagged for continued monitoring.  
- **Current status:** Post‑Phase 1 remediation, soil stockpiles meet the residential screening level. Groundwater at MW‑5 remains slightly below MCL but is under a monitoring‑only compliance pathway.

## Open Items

- **Groundwater monitoring:** The *RDIP Variance Request #2* acceptance requires that MW‑5 achieve two consecutive quarters of <15 µg/L before transitioning to semi‑annual sampling. The *GND Water Monitoring Rpt* (2012) noted only one quarter of compliance; subsequent data (not yet documented in provided sources) are needed to close this condition.  
- **Statistical reevaluation:** The *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report* (2014) recommended re‑sampling of stockpile S‑2 to confirm low‑level lead contamination after the statistical evaluation flagged a potential outlier. This re‑sampling deliverable has not been confirmed as completed in the available record.  
- **Variance compliance:** DTSC reserved the right to revoke the RDIP variance if groundwater lead exceeds 20 µg/L in any future monitoring event. The event trigger has not occurred to date, but the project team must document that no exceedances have been observed.
