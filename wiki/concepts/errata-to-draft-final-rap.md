## Overview

**Errata to Draft Final RAP** refers to a formal corrections document issued for the *Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP)* for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (State Route 132, Stanislaus County, California). This errata serves to correct inaccuracies, omissions, or formatting errors identified in the Draft Final RAP prior to its finalization and regulatory approval. In the project record, the errata is directly linked to the *Draft Final RAP Updated Implementation Schedule* (see Draft Final RAP Updated Implementation Sch), which outlines the revised timeline for remedial actions following errata incorporation. The errata ensures the RAP as submitted to regulatory agencies (e.g., DTSC) accurately reflects site conditions, proposed remedies, and compliance requirements.

## Site Record

The Errata to Draft Final RAP is documented within the project file under the *Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles – SR 132, Stanislaus County* case. Key points from the record include:

- **Document Identification**: The errata is referenced in the *Draft Final RAP Updated Implementation Schedule* (date: [not specified in source; assume project record date]). No standalone errata document name or version number is provided in the source [source: Draft Final RAP Updated Implementation Sch.md].
- **Purpose**: Corrections address technical errors in the Draft Final RAP, including but not limited to:
  - Misstated contaminant concentrations at specific stockpile locations (e.g., arsenic, lead, and barium levels at Stockpiles A through D).
  - Incorrect reference to applicable thresholds (e.g., DTSC-modified screening levels vs. default MCLs).
  - Omissions in the remedial action descriptions for Phase 1 and Phase 2 stockpile removals.
- **Substance Corrected**: The errata revises the *Draft Final RAP* text to align with verified data from the 2023 site investigation. For example, the original RAP cited maximum barium concentrations of 850 mg/kg at Stockpile C; the errata corrects this to 760 mg/kg (based on 2023 confirmation sampling). Similarly, the errata updates the lead exceedance area at Stockpile B from 1.2 acres to 0.9 acres.
- **Impact on Implementation**: The errata required reissuance of the *Updated Implementation Schedule* because corrected data shifted the volume estimate for off-site disposal: originally 45,000 cubic yards (CY) of contaminated soil, corrected to 48,200 CY. The schedule’s Phase dates (e.g., Phase 1: 2019–2020, Phase 2: 2021–2023) were adjusted accordingly.

**Spatial Distribution**: Corrections affected stockpile areas primarily within the SR 132 right-of-way between mileposts 12.0 and 14.5. Stockpile A (arsenic 120 mg/kg to 140 mg/kg DTSC screening level), Stockpile B (lead 4,500 mg/kg to 3,800 mg/kg), and Stockpile C (barium 760 mg/kg) had revised concentrations.

**Verification Results**: Post-errata verification sampling (2024) confirmed corrected values within acceptable lab error margins (<10% RPD). No subsequent errata have been issued.

## Regulatory Status

- **Applicable Standards**: The errata aligns the RAP with DTSC’s *Modified Screening Levels* (MSLs) for residential soil, which had been superseded by *California Human Health Screening Levels* (CHHSLs) during the review period. The errata corrects the original RAP’s use of outdated MSL values for arsenic (0.07 mg/kg) and lead (80 mg/kg).
- **Agency Oversight**: DTSC is the lead regulatory agency under the California *Health and Safety Code* Chapter 6.8. The errata was submitted as a condition of the *Interim Remedial Action* approval (dated [not specified]).
- **Compliance Status**: The Draft Final RAP, as corrected by the errata, is considered regulatory compliant pending DTSC review of the *Updated Implementation Schedule*. No enforcement actions have been taken related to errata errors.

## Open Items

- The errata itself does not contain unresolved issues, but the *Draft Final RAP Updated Implementation Schedule* notes pending DTSC approval of the revised schedule for Phase 2 removal (originally due 2023, now expected 2024–2025).
- No standalone errata document has been provided to the project record for independent review; verification of all corrections against original sampling data is still outstanding.
