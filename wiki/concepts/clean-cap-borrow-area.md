## Clean Cap Borrow Area

### Overview
The Clean Cap Borrow Area is a designated location within the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site, specifically associated with the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]]. It was established as a source of clean soil for use in capping contaminated areas, particularly in relation to [[barium-containing-soil]].

### Site Record
The Clean Cap Borrow Area was identified as a source of clean fill material for the remediation efforts at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]]. Soil from this borrow area was used to construct a clean cap over the [[basin-5]] and [[carpenter-road-area]] sites. The borrow area itself was characterized to ensure it met clean soil criteria.

Specifically, soil samples collected from the Clean Cap Borrow Area were analyzed for various constituents. For example, in a sampling event on October 26, 2021, samples designated "CCBA-1-2621-001" through "CCBA-1-2621-004" were collected. Analytical results for these samples indicated that barium concentrations were below the applicable screening level. For instance, sample "CCBA-1-2621-001" had a barium concentration of 12.1 mg/kg, and sample "CCBA-1-2621-002" had a concentration of 11.9 mg/kg. These results were reported in the "Draft Interim RACR_Text_ Figures_Tables_ App A-C" document.

### Regulatory Status
The Clean Cap Borrow Area was utilized under the assumption that it provided soil meeting regulatory requirements for clean fill. The characterization data, such as the barium concentrations reported, supported its use as a source for the clean cap.
