## Clean Cap Borrow Area

### Overview
The Clean Cap Borrow Area is a designated location within the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]] site used for the excavation and temporary storage of soil intended for use as clean cover material. This material is sourced from areas identified as suitable for reuse as clean-fill, specifically to cap remediated zones.

### Site Record
The Borrow Area Sampling Plan, approved by the Central Valley RWQCB, outlines the strategy for characterizing soil within the designated borrow area. The plan specifies that soil excavated from the Clean Cap Borrow Area is intended for use as [[clean-fill-suitable-as-cover]]. The Borrow Area Results Technical Memorandum documents the analytical results from samples collected within this area. For example, samples collected from the Clean Cap Borrow Area on October 26, 2021, showed barium concentrations. One sample, CCBA-01, had a barium concentration of 110 mg/kg {{quote: "CCBA-01 10/26/2021 110 110 Barium mg/kg"}}. Another sample, CCBA-02, collected on the same date, had a barium concentration of 100 mg/kg {{quote: "CCBA-02 10/26/2021 100 100 Barium mg/kg"}}.

### Regulatory Status
Soil designated for the Clean Cap Borrow Area must meet criteria for [[clean-fill]] to be suitable for use as cover. The Central Valley RWQCB reviews and approves sampling plans and results to ensure compliance with these criteria.
