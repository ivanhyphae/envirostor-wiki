## Overview

The Clean Cap Borrow Area, also referred to as the primary borrow area, was a designated source of clean soil for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] remediation project, specifically for the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]]. Its purpose was to provide material for constructing a clean cap over contaminated areas, including [[basin-5]]. The borrow area itself was characterized to ensure the soil met the necessary criteria for clean fill.

## Site Record

Soil from the Clean Cap Borrow Area was utilized for the remediation efforts at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]]. Specifically, it was used to construct a clean cap over [[basin-5]] and the [[carpenter-road-area]]. Characterization of the borrow area involved sampling and analysis to confirm the soil's suitability.

During a sampling event on October 26, 2021, samples designated "CCBA-1-2621-001" through "CCBA-1-2621-004" were collected from the Clean Cap Borrow Area. Analytical results for these samples indicated that barium concentrations were below the applicable screening level. For instance, sample "CCBA-1-2621-001" reported a barium concentration of 12.1 mg/kg, and sample "CCBA-1-2621-002" reported a concentration of 11.9 mg/kg. These findings were documented in the "Draft Interim RACR_Text_ Figures_Tables_ App A-C" document.

Further sampling and analysis were conducted in accordance with the [[borrow-area-sampling-plan]]. The results of this characterization were presented in the [[borrow-area-tech-memo]]. The approval of the [[borrow-area-tech-memo]] by the relevant regulatory agencies confirmed the suitability of the soil from the Clean Cap Borrow Area for use as clean fill.

## Regulatory Status

The Clean Cap Borrow Area was utilized under the assumption that the soil met regulatory requirements for clean fill. The characterization data, including the barium concentrations reported, supported its use in constructing the clean cap. The approval of the [[borrow-area-tech-memo]] by regulatory agencies affirmed its status as a source of compliant material.
