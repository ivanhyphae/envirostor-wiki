The [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project involved surface water sampling conducted by [[geocon-consultants]] under Contract No. 06A1895, Task Order No. 17, and EA No. 10-0X2700. Sampling activities were performed on December 11, 2015, and January 6, 2016, at the site located southerly of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California. The sampling followed protocols established in the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. in January 2006, and an addendum dated February 20, 2013.

## Site Record

Surface water sampling was conducted on December 11, 2015, and January 6, 2016. The sampling locations were identified on a Site Plan, Figure 2, which also depicted the approximate site boundaries and Stockpiles 1 through 3.

## Regulatory Status

The sampling was performed in accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
