## Bent 2 Overcrossing Structure

The Bent 2 Overcrossing Structure is a component of the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. It is associated with the [[bent-2-stockpiles]] and the remediation of soil stockpiles at the site. Specifically, the structure is relevant to the management and placement of [[clean-cap-material]] as part of the remediation strategy.

## Site record

Soil samples collected from beneath the Bent 2 Overcrossing Structure were analyzed as part of the [[bent-2-excavation-soil-stockpiles-characterization-testing]]. These samples were tested for various constituents, including [[barium]]. The results of this testing informed decisions regarding the remediation of the [[bent-2-stockpiles]].

## Regulatory status

The remediation of the Bent 2 Overcrossing Structure area is subject to regulatory oversight. The [[clean-cap-criteria]] were established to ensure that the remediation met regulatory requirements. A [[variance-to-remedial-design-implementation-plan]] was issued by the DTSC on October 26, 2021, which addressed the placement of clean cap material over the Bent 2 Overcrossing Structure.
