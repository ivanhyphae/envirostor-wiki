## Perimeter Fences

### Overview
Perimeter fences were installed at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site to restrict access and prevent unauthorized entry. This action was part of the remediation efforts related to soil stockpiles at the SR 132 location in Stanislaus County, California.

### Site Record
During the site investigation and remediation activities, perimeter fences were erected around specific areas. For instance, a fence was installed around [[stockpile-1]] and [[stockpile-1]] as part of the [[stockpile-3-fence-installation]] effort. The installation of these fences was a measure to control site access.

### Regulatory Status
The installation of perimeter fences is a physical control measure intended to support the overall remediation strategy and regulatory compliance by limiting public exposure to the stockpiled materials.
