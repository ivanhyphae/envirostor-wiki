The [[dtsc-acceptance-of-bent-2-soils-tech-memo]] represents the formal approval by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) for the use of excavated soil from the [[bent-2-area]] as clean cap material. This acceptance is a critical step in the remediation process for the SR 132 project, allowing for the efficient management of excavated materials while ensuring the integrity of the site remedy.

### Approval of Bent 2 Soils for Clean Cap Material

On November 6, 2020, Dean Wright, PG, DTSC Project Manager, formally accepted the Technical Memorandum (Tech Memo) dated October 30, 2020, and the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020. This acceptance was communicated via email to Richard C. Stewart of [[caltrans]], Perry Myers of DTSC, and Steven Meeks of the CVRWQCB.

The Tech Memo, submitted by [[caltrans]] on November 2, 2020, proposed using excavated soil from the Bent 2 column excavation as clean cap cover material. While the soil sample results presented in the Tech Memo did not meet the clean cap criteria established in the April 9, 2020 Variance Request approved by DTSC, both DTSC and the CVRWQCB agreed to a conditional use. The approved condition allows for the placement of this soil on top of Stockpile 2 at a maximum thickness of 6 inches. This layer must then be covered with clean base material and the asphalt or concrete used for roadway construction.

### Conditions for Use and Stormwater Management

DTSC and the CVRWQCB acknowledged that, due to project scheduling, the Bent 2 soils might be temporarily exposed to the elements during the winter months. Consequently, strict adherence to the rainy season surface water sampling effort and the erosion control measures outlined in the Revised SWPPP Amendment dated October 22, 2020, is deemed very important. These measures are intended to prevent contaminants from migrating off-site and impacting surrounding areas.
