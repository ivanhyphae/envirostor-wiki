## Carpenter Road Shoofly Area

### Overview
The Carpenter Road Shoofly Area is a location within the State Route 132 Project in Modesto, California, from which approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil were excavated. This excavated soil was characterized for its suitability as clean capping material for Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2. The characterization testing was performed to obtain approval from the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for its use as clean fill.

### Site Record
Characterization testing of soil from the Carpenter Road Shoofly Area involved collecting 24 discrete samples along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6). Samples were taken at depths of 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet from the top of the slope. These discrete samples were composited into eight 3-part composite samples.

Analysis for barium and lead was conducted using EPA Test Method 6010B. Barium was detected in all 10 analyzed samples at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 analyzed samples at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg.

### Regulatory Status
The soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly Area was evaluated against the clean cap criteria established in a *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan* dated April 9, 2020, and approved by the DTSC. The upper-bound background threshold for barium was 120 mg/kg. The detected barium concentrations (28-96 mg/kg) were less than this threshold. The detected lead concentrations (1.4-3.8 mg/kg) were less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg.

Based on these analytical results, the soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly Area met the criteria to be considered clean fill suitable as cover for the BCS stockpiles, pending DTSC approval.

### Open Items
The information from the Carpenter Road Shoofly clean fill characterization testing was to be included in the Removal Action Completion Report following the completion of capping activities for Stockpile 1 and 2.
