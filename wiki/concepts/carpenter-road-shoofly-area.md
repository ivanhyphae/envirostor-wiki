The Carpenter Road Shoofly Area is a location within the State Route 132 Project in Modesto, California, from which approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil were excavated. This excavated soil was characterized for its suitability as clean capping material for Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2.

### Soil Characterization and Regulatory Review

Characterization testing of soil from the Carpenter Road Shoofly Area involved collecting 24 discrete samples along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6). Samples were taken at depths of 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet from the top of the slope. These discrete samples were composited into eight 3-part composite samples. Analysis for barium and lead was conducted using EPA Test Method 6010B. Barium was detected in all 10 analyzed samples at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 analyzed samples at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg.

The soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly Area was evaluated against the clean cap criteria established in a *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan* dated April 9, 2020, and approved by the DTSC. The upper-bound background threshold for barium was 120 mg/kg. The detected barium concentrations (28-96 mg/kg) were less than this threshold. The detected lead concentrations (1.4-3.8 mg/kg) were less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg.

### DTSC Approval

On April 7, 2021, DTSC, through Dean Wright, P.G., approved the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan. Subsequently, on April 22, 2021, DTSC, also via Dean Wright, P.G., accepted the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021. This memorandum detailed the sampling and analysis performed, concluding that the results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria. Kyle Cockerham of the Water Board was copied on these communications.

The information from the Carpenter Road Shoofly clean fill characterization testing was to be included in the Removal Action Completion Report following the completion of capping activities for Stockpile 1 and 2.
