## Overview

The Interim Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) documents the completion of consolidation and clean fill capping of barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles as highway embankment fill during Phase I construction of the State Route (SR) 132 West Project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. This interim report covers activities performed in accordance with the *Remedial Design Implementation Plan* (RDIP) dated January 30, 2019. A Final RACR is anticipated following the completion of Ultimate Phase 2 construction in 2028. The lead regulatory agency overseeing this project is the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in cooperation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB).

## Site Record

The Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles are located along the eastern portion of the SR 132 West Project alignment. Interim Phase 1 construction involved the development of two of four planned traffic lanes from east of SR 99 to North Dakota Avenue. During this phase, BCS Stockpiles 1 through 3, which contain elevated levels of barium and lead, were consolidated and contained as highway embankment material within the limits of BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2, located west of SR 99. These capped areas were then covered with clean fill and a structural pavement section. The SR 132 was opened to traffic on November 3, 2022.

Geocon Consultants, Inc. prepared the Interim RACR on behalf of the City of Modesto and Caltrans. The report details completed RDIP activities and BCS containment implementation, environmental and health and safety monitoring, and post-construction monitoring and maintenance.

Key technical memorandums and documentation included in the appendices of the Interim RACR are:
*   Variances to RDIP (April and September 2020)
*   Stockpiles 2 and 3 BCS Removal Verification Testing Technical Memorandums
*   Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Footing Characterization Testing Technical Memorandums
*   Clean Cap Characterization Testing Technical Memorandums
*   Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zone Legal Description and Boundary Survey
*   Draft Interim RACR Transmittal and DTSC Acceptance
*   Air Monitoring Technical Memorandum
*   Operations and Maintenance Plan

A Draft Interim RACR was submitted to DTSC on December 23, 2021. DTSC provided acceptance of this draft report via a letter dated February 24, 2022. A site survey was conducted on June 28, 2022, by DTSC, Caltrans, City, and other project personnel to confirm the completed clean capping of the Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones and compliance with the RDIP.

## Regulatory Status

The DTSC is the lead regulatory agency for site mitigation oversight under a Caltrans Interagency Agreement. The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) is also cooperating in oversight. The remedial action selected in the Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP, Geocon, 2018) was consolidation and containment by incorporating the stockpiles as highway embankment fill and covering them with clean fill and a structural pavement section. This approach was implemented during Interim Phase 1 and will be continued during Ultimate Phase 2 construction.

## Open Items

A Final RACR will be submitted following the completion of Ultimate Phase 2 construction of the SR 132 West Project, which is scheduled for completion in 2028.
