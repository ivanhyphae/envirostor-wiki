# Interim Removal Action Completion Report

## Overview

The **Interim Removal Action Completion Report** (Interim RACR) documents the completion of consolidation and clean fill capping of [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) Stockpiles 1 through 3 during [[interim-phase-1]] construction of the State Route (SR) 132 West Project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. Prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] for [[caltrans]] and the City of Modesto, the report was submitted to the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) under the Caltrans Interagency Agreement. It confirms that containment activities were performed in accordance with the 2019 [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) and Caltrans-approved design plans. This report represents an interim milestone; a Final RACR is anticipated after the completion of Ultimate Phase 2 construction, projected for 2028.

The Interim RACR serves to document the physical completion of BCS containment as highway embankment fill, summarize verification testing to confirm achievement of removal thresholds, and outline post-construction monitoring and maintenance requirements. DTSC accepted the document on February 24, 2022, as a sufficient technical status report, deferring certification of the overarching Remedial Action Plan (RAP) until after Ultimate Phase 2 build-out.

## Site record

### Submission and acceptance history

Geocon submitted a Draft Interim RACR to DTSC on December 23, 2021. The draft detailed the status of BCS grading and clean fill capping, noting that remaining work included structural pavement section placement, erosion control, drainage, median barrier, and signage.

DTSC, in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], reviewed the draft and issued an acceptance letter on February 24, 2022, signed by Dean Wright, PG. The letter confirmed that work completed to date adhered to the approved RDIP and two variance requests approved on April 17, 2020, and September 21, 2020. DTSC accepted the document "as a technical status report with no comments."

A site survey conducted on June 28, 2022, by DTSC, Caltrans, the City, and other project personnel confirmed the completed clean capping of the Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones. SR 132 opened to traffic on November 3, 2022.

### Scope of activities documented

The Interim RACR documents the following completed activities:

*   **Stockpile 3 removal and relocation**: BCS material was removed from the east side of SR 99 and placed within the Stockpile 1 Containment Zone. Verification sampling at Stockpile 3 involved 24 soil samples (S3 1-0 through S3 24-0) collected between February 28 and March 12, 2020. Excavation depths reached elevations between 82.5 and 84 feet above mean sea level (MSL). Three sample locations (S3 9-0 through S3 11-0) initially exceeded the barium verification threshold; these areas were deepened by approximately one foot and resampled. Final barium concentrations in the Stockpile 3 excavation ranged from 46 to 970 mg/kg, and lead from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg. These results were below the RDIP verification thresholds of 1,000 mg/kg for barium (groundwater protection) and 80 mg/kg for lead (residential screening level).

*   **Stockpile 2 removal verification**: The west end of Stockpile 2 was excavated to approximately 82 feet MSL, with 8 initial samples (S2W 1-0 through 8-0). Two locations (S2W 1-0, S2W 3-0) exceeded the barium threshold, leading to deepening to 81 feet MSL and two additional samples. The east end was initially excavated to approximately 83 feet MSL, then deepened in stages to 82 and 81 feet MSL in areas where S2E 3-0 and S2E 8-0 exceeded the threshold. Final barium concentrations ranged from 54 to 760 mg/kg, and lead from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg.

*   **Stockpile 1 and 2 MSE wall footing characterization**: Testing confirmed that Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material (trenches STK1-1 through STK1-3) contained barium at 42–420 mg/kg and lead at 1.1–6.7 mg/kg. This barium concentration was above the site-specific background of 120 mg/kg but below the 1,000 mg/kg removal threshold. Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing characterization (trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7) found barium at 53–930 mg/kg, with 14 of 29 samples exceeding the 120 mg/kg background threshold, primarily in the upper 3 feet.

*   **Clean cap borrow area characterization**: Testing at the Carpenter Road borrow area (borings B1 through B12, February 21, 2020) and [[basin-5]] borrow area (trenches BASIN5 1 through 6, June 16, 2020) confirmed that fill from these sources met clean cap criteria, with metal concentrations consistent with naturally occurring background.

*   **Additional fill source testing**: The Carpenter Road Shoofly (24 discrete samples, composited into 8 samples, April 2021) yielded barium at 28–96 mg/kg and lead at 1.4–3.8 mg/kg, meeting the DTSC-approved clean cap threshold of 120 mg/kg for upper-bound background barium. In contrast, Bent 2 excavation soil stockpiles (October 30, 2020) did not meet clean cap criteria; 4 of 10 samples exceeded the 120 mg/kg barium background threshold.

*   **Air monitoring**: Air monitoring conducted during BCS grading activities showed total dust, barium, and lead levels below designated project action and risk levels, indicating effective dust control.

### Report structure and appendices

The Interim RACR is organized into six sections: Introduction, Project Organization, RDIP Activities and BCS Containment Implementation, RDIP Monitoring, Post Construction Monitoring/Maintenance, and Project Websites and References.

Eight appendices support the report: Appendix A (Variances to RDIP), Appendix B (Stockpiles 2 and 3 BCS Removal Verification Testing Technical Memorandums), Appendix C (Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Footing Characterization Testing Technical Memorandums), Appendix D (Clean Cap Characterization Testing Technical Memorandums), Appendix E (Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zone Legal Description and Boundary Survey), Appendix F (Draft Interim RACR Transmittal and DTSC Acceptance), Appendix G (Air Monitoring Technical Memorandum), and Appendix H (operations-and-maintenance-plan|Operations and Maintenance Plan).

## Regulatory status

DTSC accepted the Interim RACR on February 24, 2022, as a technical status report, confirming that Phase 1 containment activities complied with the RDIP and associated variances. However, DTSC's acceptance letter specified that **certification of the RAP would not occur until after Ultimate Phase 2** construction. Ultimate Phase 2 will necessitate a supplemental RDIP for DTSC review and approval, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and potential revisions to the Operations and Maintenance (O&M) Plan and O&M Agreement.

Key conditions associated with the Interim RACR acceptance include:

*   Submittal of a final operations-and-maintenance-plan|Operations and Maintenance Plan (O&M Plan) for DTSC review, to be incorporated into the Final Phase 1 RACR (anticipated for submittal in July or August 2022).
*   The O&M Plan must include a soil management plan, annual pavement and containment feature inspections, and groundwater monitoring.
*   Following approval of the Final Phase 1 RACR, DTSC will enter into an O&M Agreement with Caltrans and complete a fmc-corporation|land use covenant for the containment areas.
*   Ultimate Phase 2 will be conducted under a separate agreement between DTSC and Caltrans.

The verification thresholds for soil removal in effect at the time of the Interim RACR were:
*   Barium: 1,000 mg/kg (groundwater protection basis)
*   Lead: 80 mg/kg (residential screening level)

All final excavation samples from Stockpiles 2 and 3 were below these thresholds.

## Open items

*   **Final Phase 1 RACR**: Requires as-built plans, cross-section figures, final survey data, and the DTSC-approved O&M Plan.
*   **O&M Plan submittal and approval**: DTSC requires the plan for review and incorporation into the Final Phase 1 RACR.
*   **O&M Agreement and Land Use Covenant**: To be executed between DTSC and Caltrans after approval of the Final Phase 1 RACR.
*   **Ultimate Phase 2 RACR**: Will necessitate a supplemental RDIP, new field oversight, and potential revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement.
*   **RAP certification**: Deferred until the completion of Ultimate Phase 2 build-out, projected for 2028.
