## No Discharge Runoff From Caltrans Right Of Way Observed

This article documents observations regarding storm water runoff from the [[caltrans]] right of way at the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]] site in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California.

## Site Record

Storm water sampling was conducted on February 1, 2024, and February 4, 2025, at the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles site. These sampling events were performed in accordance with the *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)*, prepared by [[geocon-consultants]], Inc., dated December 13, 2023. The sampling was conducted in general accordance with protocols approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. The reports detailing these sampling activities do not contain specific findings or data points that directly address or quantify "No Discharge Runoff From Caltrans Right Of Way Observed." The reports focus on the collection and analysis of storm water samples from designated locations at the site.

## Regulatory Status

The storm water sampling activities are conducted under Caltrans Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2, EA No. 10-1E7003. The protocols for sampling and analysis were approved by the DTSC and the Central Valley RWQCB.
