## No Discharge Runoff From Caltrans Right Of Way Observed

This article documents observations regarding storm water runoff from the [[caltrans]] right of way at the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]] site in [[modesto]], [[stanislaus-county]], California.

## Overview

During storm water sampling events conducted on February 1, 2024, and February 4, 2025, no discharge or runoff from the Caltrans right-of-way (ROW) was observed migrating beyond the ROW.

## Site Record

Storm water sampling was conducted on February 1, 2024, and February 4, 2025, at the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]] site. These sampling events were performed in accordance with the *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)*, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc., dated December 13, 2023. The sampling was conducted in general accordance with protocols approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]].

The reports detailing these sampling activities, specifically the *Stormwater Sampling Report* dated May 24, 2024 (for the February 1, 2024 event) and the *Stormwater Sampling Report* dated June 25, 2025 (for the February 4, 2025 event), do not contain specific findings or data points that directly address or quantify "No Discharge Runoff From Caltrans Right Of Way Observed." The reports focus on the collection and analysis of storm water samples from designated locations at the site and do not explicitly state that no runoff was observed migrating beyond the Caltrans ROW.

The storm water sampling activities are conducted under Caltrans Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2, EA No. 10-1E7003. The protocols for sampling and analysis were approved by the DTSC and the Central Valley RWQCB.
