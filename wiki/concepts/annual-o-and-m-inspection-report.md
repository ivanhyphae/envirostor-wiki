The Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report is a deliverable submitted to the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) that details the findings of an inspection conducted at the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] site.

## Site record

The Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report dated January 26, 2026, was submitted by Caltrans. This report outlined the outcome of an inspection conducted at State Route 132 West Expressway at the State Route 132/99 Interchange. The inspection noted that vegetation along the roadway remained sparse, a condition also observed in the previous year's report. Erosion control materials were observed to be limiting erosion at the time of the inspection. Caltrans maintenance crews were observed removing debris from the north slope of stockpile #2, where the area was freshly raked with debris piles at the bottom of the slope.

## Regulatory status

The DTSC reviewed and approved the Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report dated January 26, 2026. DTSC encouraged continued efforts to promote vegetation along the roadway to prevent erosion.

## Open items

The next required submittal for this project is an Annual Land Use Covenant Inspection Report, which is due to DTSC no later than January 18, 2027.
