The [[groundwater-monitoring-report]] is a recurring deliverable prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] for [[caltrans]] concerning the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. These reports document the findings of groundwater sampling and analysis conducted at the site, which is located south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California. The primary purpose of these reports is to present analytical results of groundwater monitoring well sampling, track depth to groundwater measurements, and compare current results with historical data. The reports are prepared in accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).

## Site record

Groundwater monitoring reports have been submitted for various periods, including:

*   **May, June, and July 2012:** DTSC reviewed these reports, prepared by Geocon Consultants, which presented analytical results from groundwater monitoring well sampling. The June 2012 report also documented the installation of monitoring wells [[mw-9]] and [[mw-10]]. {{quote: "The subject reports present analytical results of groundwater monitoring well sampling implemented in May, June, and July 2012 respectively. The June 2012 report also discusses the installation of monitoring wells identified as MW-9, located south of Kansas Avenue and west of SR 99; and MW-10, located south of Kansas Avenue and east of SR 99."}}
*   **December 2013:** Geocon Consultants prepared this report, presenting results from the December 2013 sampling event. The scope included depth to groundwater measurements, groundwater sample collection from ten groundwater monitoring wells, and laboratory analysis. {{quote: "This report presents the results of the December 2013 sampling event. ... The scope of services reported herein included depth to groundwater measurements, groundwater sample collection from ten groundwater monitoring wells, analysis of the water samples by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of this report."}}
*   **February 2014:** Geocon Consultants prepared this report, presenting results from the February 2014 sampling event. The scope included depth to groundwater measurements, groundwater sample collection from ten groundwater monitoring wells, and laboratory analysis. {{quote: "This report presents the results of the February 2014 sampling event. ... The scope of services reported herein included depth to groundwater measurements, groundwater sample collection from ten groundwater monitoring wells, analysis of the water samples by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of this report."}}
*   **June 2014:** Geocon Consultants prepared this report, presenting results from the June 2014 sampling event. The scope included depth to groundwater measurements, groundwater sample collection from ten groundwater monitoring wells, and laboratory analysis. {{quote: "This report presents the results of the June 2014 sampling event. ... The scope of services reported herein included depth to groundwater measurements, groundwater sample collection from ten groundwater monitoring wells, analysis of the water samples by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of this report."}}
*   **October 2016 and April 2017:** Geocon performed groundwater monitoring activities, and this report presents the results of these events. The scope included depth to groundwater measurements and groundwater sample collection from five of ten monitoring wells that contained sufficient groundwater in April 2017. {{quote: "This report presents the results of the October 2016 and April 2017 monitoring events. ... The scope of services reported herein included depth to groundwater measurements in October 2016 and April 2017, groundwater sample collection from five of ten monitoring wells that contained sufficient groundwater in April 2017, analysis of the water samples by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of this report."}}
*   **October 2018 and April 2019:** Geocon performed groundwater monitoring activities, and this report presents the results of these events. The scope included depth to groundwater measurements and groundwater sample collection from eight monitoring wells in April 2019. {{quote: "This report presents the results of the October 2018 and April 2019 monitoring events. ... The scope of services reported herein includes depth to groundwater measurements in October 2018 and April 2019, groundwater sample collection from eight monitoring wells in April 2019, analysis of the water samples by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of this report."}}

The reports are prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] under contract with [[caltrans]]. The primary chemicals of concern (COC) identified at the site are barium, lead, and strontium, which originated from excess native soils and pond tailings generated during the construction of SR 99 in the early 1960s. {{quote: "The primary chemicals of concern (COC) are barium, lead, and strontium."}}

## Regulatory status

The DTSC, in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB), reviews and concurs with the submitted groundwater monitoring reports. {{quote: "DTSC concurs with the subject groundwater monitoring reports and requests that future groundwater monitoring reports be completed as groundwater data becomes available."}} Task 9 of the Caltrans - DTSC Agreement mandates that Caltrans prepare, at a minimum, quarterly groundwater monitoring reports for wells associated with the site. These reports must discuss depth to groundwater measurements, well purging and sampling, chain of custody, laboratory analysis, quality assurance/quality control, and comparisons to previous sampling events. {{quote: "Caltrans shall prepare, at a minimum, quarterly groundwater monitoring reports for groundwater monitoring wells associated with the Site. The reports shall discuss: depth to groundwater measurements; well purging and sampling; chain of custody reports; laboratory analysis, including analytes, methods, and results; field and laboratory quality assurance/quality control; comparison of groundwater monitoring results from previous sampling events; and c"}}
