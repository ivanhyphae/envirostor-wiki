The [[groundwater-monitoring-report]] is a recurring deliverable prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] for [[caltrans]] concerning the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. These reports document the findings of groundwater sampling and analysis conducted at the site, which is located south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California. The primary purpose of these reports is to present analytical results of groundwater monitoring well sampling, track depth to groundwater measurements, and compare current results with historical data. The reports are prepared in accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).

## Site record

Groundwater monitoring reports have been submitted for various periods, including:

*   **March 2012:** This report presented the results of the March 2012 sampling event. The scope of services included depth to groundwater measurements, the sampling of eight groundwater monitoring wells, submittal of the water samples to a California-certified laboratory for analytical testing, and preparation of this report.
*   **March 2013:** This report presented the results of the March 2013 sampling event. The scope of services included depth to groundwater measurements, groundwater sample collection from ten groundwater monitoring wells, analysis of the water samples by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of this report.
*   **June 2013:** This report presented the results of the June 2013 sampling event. The scope of services included depth to groundwater measurements, groundwater sample collection from ten groundwater monitoring wells, analysis of the water samples by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of this report.
*   **September 2013:** This report presented the results of the September 2013 sampling event. The scope of services included depth to groundwater measurements, groundwater sample collection from ten groundwater monitoring wells, analysis of the water samples by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of this report.
*   **December 2013:** This report presented the results of the December 2013 sampling event. The scope of services included depth to groundwater measurements, groundwater sample collection from ten groundwater monitoring wells, analysis of the water samples by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of this report.
*   **June 2012:** This report documented the installation of monitoring wells [[mw-9]] and [[mw-10]] and presented analytical results from groundwater monitoring well sampling implemented in May, June, and July 2012.

The reports are prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] under contract with [[caltrans]]. The primary chemicals of concern (COC) identified at the site are barium, lead, and strontium, which originated from excess native soils and pond tailings generated during the construction of SR 99 in the early 1960s.

## Regulatory status

The DTSC, in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB), reviews and concurs with the submitted groundwater monitoring reports. Task 9 of the Caltrans - DTSC Agreement mandates that Caltrans prepare, at a minimum, quarterly groundwater monitoring reports for wells associated with the site. These reports must discuss depth to groundwater measurements, well purging and sampling, chain of custody, laboratory analysis, quality assurance/quality control, and comparisons to previous sampling events.

## Open items

Future groundwater monitoring reports are requested to be completed as groundwater data becomes available.
