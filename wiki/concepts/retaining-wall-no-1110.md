## Retaining Wall No. 1110

### Overview
Retaining Wall No. 1110 is a structural element within the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project. Its significance in the project record is tied to the development and approval of a sampling plan for soil designated as clean fill.

### Site Record
A revised "Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan" was submitted on April 5, 2021. This plan detailed the proposed sampling and analysis procedures for soil intended for use as clean fill at Retaining Wall No. 1110. The plan was reviewed by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. On April 7, 2021, Dean Wright of DTSC communicated that the plan was consistent with prior borrow area sampling efforts and that DTSC had no comments. This communication served as DTSC's approval of the plan. {{quote: "DTSC and the Waterboard have reviewed the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan (Plan) received on April 5, 2021 that outlines the proposed sampling and analysis for soil at this location that will be used as clean fill. The Plan is consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and there are no comments. This e-mail serves as DTSCs approval of the Plan."}}

### Regulatory Status
The sampling plan for Retaining Wall No. 1110 was developed under the oversight of the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] to ensure that soil used as clean fill met project requirements.
