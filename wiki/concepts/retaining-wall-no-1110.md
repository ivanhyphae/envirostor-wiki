## Retaining Wall No. 1110

### Overview
Retaining Wall No. 1110 is a structural component within the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. Its primary relevance in the project record pertains to the sampling and approval of soil designated for use as clean fill at its location.

### Site Record
On April 5, 2021, a revised "Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan" was submitted. This plan detailed the proposed sampling and analysis procedures for soil intended for use as clean fill at Retaining Wall No. 1110. The plan was reviewed by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. On April 7, 2021, DTSC, through Dean Wright, communicated that the plan was consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and that DTSC had no comments. This communication served as DTSC's approval of the plan. The DTSC email stated: "The Plan is consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and there are no comments. This e-mail serves as DTSCs approval of the Plan."

### Regulatory Status
The sampling plan for soil to be used as clean fill at Retaining Wall No. 1110 was developed under the oversight of the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. DTSC provided approval for the plan on April 7, 2021.
