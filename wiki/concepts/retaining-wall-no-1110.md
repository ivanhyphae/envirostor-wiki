## Retaining Wall No. 1110

### Overview
Retaining Wall No. 1110 is a structural element relevant to the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. Its primary connection to the project record is its designation as a location for which a clean fill sampling plan was developed and approved.

### Site Record
The "Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan" was revised and submitted on April 5, 2021. This plan outlined the proposed sampling and analysis for soil intended for use as clean fill. The plan was reviewed by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[rwqcb]]. On April 7, 2021, Dean Wright of DTSC communicated that the plan was consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and that there were no comments. This email served as DTSC's approval of the plan.

### Regulatory Status
The sampling plan for Retaining Wall No. 1110 was developed to ensure that soil used as clean fill met project requirements, aligning with the oversight of the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] and the [[rwqcb]].
