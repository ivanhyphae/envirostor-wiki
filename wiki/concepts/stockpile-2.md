## Stockpile 2

Stockpile 2 was one of three Barium-Containing Soil (BCS) stockpiles located within the [[caltrans]] State Route 132 (SR 132) right-of-way (ROW) in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. It was the largest of the three stockpiles, with an estimated volume of 102,000 cubic yards (yd³). Stockpile 2 was situated between Emerald Avenue and SR 99.

### Site Record

Prior to construction of the SR 132 West Project, Stockpile 2 was approximately 1,650 feet long in the east-west direction and 160 feet wide. Its average maximum fill thickness was approximately 20 feet. The stockpile was primarily comprised of layered, poorly graded sand and silty sand, similar to the underlying native alluvial deposits of the Modesto Formation.

Between February and March 2020, the western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2 were excavated to facilitate the construction of SR 132 bridge overcrossing abutments. During this BCS removal, ten verification soil samples were collected from eight locations (S2W-1 through S2W-8) at the western end and fourteen verification soil samples from twelve locations at the eastern end. These samples were collected from native soil at approximate elevations ranging from 81 to 83 feet MSL.

Initial analytical data for barium from four sample locations exceeded the verification threshold of 1,000 mg/kg. These areas were subsequently deepened by approximately one foot and resampled. The final verification soil samples from these deepened areas contained barium concentrations ranging from 54 to 760 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg. These results met the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) verification thresholds for barium (1,000 mg/kg) and lead (80 mg/kg), indicating suitability for placement of clean embankment fill and bridge abutment improvements.

Excavation for Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) wall footing construction along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2 occurred between May and June 2020. Soil samples were collected from trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7 at one-foot intervals to depths of 3 and 5 feet. Barium was detected in 29 samples at concentrations ranging from 53 to 930 mg/kg. Fourteen samples exceeded the site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg, with 13 of these collected from depths between the surface and 3 feet. Lead was detected in all samples at concentrations ranging from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg. Based on these results, soil excavated from the upper three feet of the MSE wall footing required placement within the designated Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones. Soil excavated from depths greater than 3 feet was deemed suitable for placement within the containment zones, as general embankment fill, or as capping material, pending regulatory approval.

The Stockpile 2 BCS Containment Zone is located between Emerald Avenue and SR 99, between approximate SR 132 Stations 1135+35 and 1148+30.

### Regulatory Status

The RDIP established verification thresholds for BCS removal areas:
*   **Barium:** 1,000 mg/kg (groundwater protection)
*   **Lead:** 80 mg/kg (residential screening level)

A DTSC-approved Variance to the RDIP dated April 9, 2020, identified site-specific upperbound background concentrations for comparison with proposed clean fill source area characterization soil samples:
*   **Barium:** 120 mg/kg
*   **Lead:** 3.8 mg/kg

The DTSC accepted the Stockpile 2 BCS Removal Verification Testing Technical Memorandum dated March 9, 2020, via email on March 10, 2020, confirming that the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 met the RDIP verification thresholds and were suitable for clean embankment fill.

The DTSC also accepted the Stockpile 2 MSE Wall Footing Excavation Characterization Testing Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020, via email on June 22, 2020. This acceptance confirmed that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) required placement in designated containment zones, while soil below 3 feet bgs was suitable as embankment fill or capping material and considered part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request.

### Open Items

The "Bent 2 Excavation Soil Stockpiles Characterization Testing" Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020, noted that the Bent 2 soil stockpiles (placed on Stockpile 2) did not meet the clean cap criteria established in the April 9, 2020 Variance (barium > 120 mg/kg). However, it was suggested that the material might be suitable for partial clean cap (6 inches or less) on the upper surface of Stockpile 2, pending DTSC review and approval, as additional clean cap would be placed over the structural pavement section.
