# Stockpile 2

Stockpile 2 was the largest of three barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, located between Emerald Avenue and SR-99 within the SR 132 right-of-way. Roughly 1,650 feet long, 160 feet wide, and containing an estimated 102,000 cubic yards of fill, it was consolidated and capped in place as embankment fill during Interim Phase 1 of the SR 132 West Project rather than removed offsite. The stockpile's western and eastern ends and southern slope required excavation to make way for bridge abutments and a mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) retaining wall, and the material generated by those excavations, together with all of Stockpile 3, was consolidated into the Stockpile 2 BCS Containment Zone under a [[dtsc]]-approved remedial design.

## Origin and characterization

Stockpile 2 was formed in the early 1960s from pond tailings and native soil excavated from former fmc-area evaporation ponds and placed as fill within Caltrans right-of-way. It was the most heavily impacted of the three stockpiles: the 2004 Shaw PSI reported a maximum barium concentration of 60,700 mg/kg, later investigations (Shaw 2006 SI) reported a maximum of 64,000 mg/kg, and elevated cadmium exceeding commercial/industrial CHHSLs was detected in samples from 8 of 25 borings within the stockpile. Surface soil samples from five fenceline borings along the north side of Stockpile 2, adjacent to commercial/industrial development, contained the highest barium concentrations recorded at any fenceline location, exceeding 1,000 mg/kg. Cadmium was not detected above the 1.0 mg/kg reporting limit in any of the 2006 or 2012 confirmatory sampling, leading Geocon to conclude the 2004 cadmium detections were not reproducible and that cadmium is not a chemical of concern for the site.

## Remedial approach: containment rather than removal

The 2018 Final RAP selected containment (Alternative 4) over offsite removal, incorporating Stockpile 2 into SR 132/SR-99 interchange embankment fill. Complete offsite removal and replacement with clean fill was priced at roughly $21.5 million and rejected as unfunded. Under the 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP), Stockpile 2's western and eastern ends (at the Emerald Avenue and SR-99 bridge abutments) were excavated and the BCS relocated into a designated containment zone spanning the stockpile's upper surface and southern slope; all of Stockpile 3 (roughly 24,000–29,100 cy) was also consolidated into this zone. The completed Stockpile 2 Containment Zone runs between approximate SR 132 Stations 1135+35 and 1148+30.

## BCS removal and verification (west and east ends)

Excavation at the western end (Emerald Avenue abutment, to Station 1135+35) and eastern end (SR-99 abutment, to Station 1148+30) occurred February–March 2020, with BCS moved by scraper directly into the containment zone. Verification sampling followed RDIP thresholds of 1,000 mg/kg barium (groundwater protection) and 80 mg/kg lead (residential screening level).

| Location | Initial samples | Result | Action | Final result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| West end (S2W-1 through S2W-8) | 8 samples, Feb 28, 2020, at ~82 ft MSL | Barium exceeded threshold at S2W 1-0 and S2W 3-0 | Deepened ~1 ft to ~81 ft MSL; resampled Mar 6, 2020 | Barium 54–760 mg/kg, lead 1.5–35 mg/kg |
| East end (S2E-1 through S2E-12) | 6 samples, Feb 21, 2020, at ~83 ft MSL | Barium exceeded at S2E 3-0 | Excavated to ~82 ft MSL; 7 more samples Feb 28, 2020 | S2E 8-0 still elevated |
| East end (deepened) | 1 sample, S2E 8-0 area | — | Excavated to ~81 ft MSL; resampled Mar 6, 2020 | Below thresholds |

Across the four sample locations initially exceeding the threshold, barium concentrations before deepening ranged from 1,100 to 4,100 mg/kg. After deepening, all final samples met both thresholds (barium 54–760 mg/kg, lead 1.5–35 mg/kg), and DTSC accepted the March 9, 2020 Stockpile 2 BCS Removal Verification Testing Technical Memorandum, closing out the west and east end removals without further RDIP monitoring.

## MSE wall footing excavation and the clean-fill boundary problem

Constructing the southern-boundary MSE retaining wall required excavating Stockpile 2's southerly slope to a maximum depth of 3 to 5 feet. This excavation became the central regulatory issue for Stockpile 2, because the material was a candidate source of clean cap fill but tested inconsistently against the site's [[site-specific-background-levels]] (barium 120 mg/kg, lead 3.8 mg/kg), established in the April 9, 2020 RDIP Variance prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] and approved by DTSC.

Geocon sampled trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7 at approximately 200-foot intervals, in one-foot depth increments to 3 and 5 feet, reported in the June 18, 2020 Technical Memorandum:

- Barium was detected in all 29 samples, ranging from 53 to 930 mg/kg.
- 14 of 29 samples exceeded the 120 mg/kg background value; 13 of those 14 were within the upper 3 feet.
- One deeper sample (STK2-7-4, below the planned 3-foot footing depth) also exceeded background at 140 mg/kg.
- Lead ranged from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg, mostly near the 3.8 mg/kg background value.

Because barium exceeded background concentrations in the upper 3 feet, that material could not be used as clean cap and instead had to be placed within the Stockpile 1/2 containment zones as embankment fill. Material excavated deeper than 3 feet was found suitable for placement in the containment zones, as general embankment fill, or — pending DTSC approval — as capping material. This same 120/3.8 mg/kg background standard, applied to comparable Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing results (barium 42–420 mg/kg, all above background but below the 1,000 mg/kg removal threshold), meant Stockpile 1 and 2 footing spoils were managed under an identical depth-based sorting rule.

## Bent 2 excavation spoils and partial clean-cap use

In October 2020, roughly 4,000 cubic yards of native soil excavated from Bent 2 of the SR-99 overcrossing structure (west of SR-99) was placed in end-dump rows atop Stockpile 2, partly over a fill berm from the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation previously approved by DTSC as clean cap. Ten samples (STK1–STK10) showed barium from 31 to 230 mg/kg, with 4 of 10 at or above the 120 mg/kg background threshold, and lead from 2.6 to 13 mg/kg (6 of 10 detects), near background. Because the material did not meet the clean-cap background criterion, Geocon proposed to DTSC that it nonetheless be used as partial clean cap (6 inches or less) on Stockpile 2's upper surface, reasoning that the planned structural pavement section would provide additional cover over the entire surface; fill from previously approved clean sources would still be required on the northern slope.

## Weather station and sign foundation spoils (2022)

A separate, smaller spoils issue arose from installation of a weather station and Overhead Sign #1 on Stockpile 2. DTSC approved Geocon's February 28, 2022 Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan Technical Memorandum on March 4, 2022, agreeing that spoils testing below site-specific background could be reused on site at locations mutually agreed by DTSC and Caltrans, and that spoils above background would be profiled for disposal at Forward Landfill in Manteca or another acceptable facility.

## Clean cap sourcing

Under the RDIP, the completed Stockpile 2 consolidation zone required a minimum 12-inch clean fill cap (or 4-inch aggregate base as an approved alternative). The Carpenter Road underpass excavation area was evaluated and approved by DTSC as a clean fill source per Geocon's February 27, 2020 memorandum, supplementing the MSE wall footing spoils sorted by the background criterion above. A second RDIP Variance (September 2, 2020) clarified that a 4-inch Class 3 aggregate base layer could substitute for the specified 4-inch aggregate base atop the completed containment zones.

## Post-construction status

As-built survey data for the completed Stockpile 2 Containment Zone was prepared by Dokken Engineering and used to support a DTSC-required land use covenant/deed restriction to be recorded with the Stanislaus County Clerk-Recorder. Air monitoring during all Stockpile 2 grading activities (February–June 2020) measured total dust, barium, and lead against RDIP action levels (4.0 mg/m³ total dust, 25 µg/m³ barium, 1.5 µg/m³ lead) and found levels below thresholds throughout. A supplemental RDIP for Ultimate Phase 2 construction, which will complete containment of Stockpile 2 behind a northern retaining wall, is anticipated but not yet documented in the available record.
