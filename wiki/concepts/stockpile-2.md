Stockpile 2 was one of three Barium-Containing Soil (BCS) stockpiles located within the [[caltrans]] State Route 132 (SR 132) right-of-way (ROW) in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. It was the largest of the three stockpiles, with an estimated volume of 102,000 cubic yards (yd³), and was situated between Emerald Avenue and SR 99.

### Site Description and History

Prior to construction of the SR 132 West Project, Stockpile 2 was approximately 1,650 feet long in the east-west direction and 160 feet wide, with an average maximum fill thickness of approximately 20 feet. The stockpile primarily consisted of layered, poorly graded sand and silty sand, similar to the underlying native alluvial deposits of the Modesto Formation. The stockpile was created in the 1960s using native soils excavated during the construction of SR 99 and SR 132, as well as residue from the former FMC facility.

### Excavation and Verification

Between February and March 2020, the western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2 were excavated to facilitate the construction of SR 132 bridge overcrossing abutments. Ten verification soil samples were collected from eight locations at the western end, and fourteen from twelve locations at the eastern end, from native soil at approximate elevations ranging from 81 to 83 feet MSL. Initial analytical data for barium from four sample locations exceeded the verification threshold of 1,000 mg/kg. These areas were deepened by approximately one foot and resampled. The final verification soil samples from these deepened areas contained barium concentrations ranging from 54 to 760 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg, meeting the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) verification thresholds for barium (1,000 mg/kg) and lead (80 mg/kg).

Excavation for Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) wall footing construction along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2 occurred between May and June 2020. Soil samples were collected from trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7 at one-foot intervals to depths of 3 and 5 feet. Barium was detected in 29 samples at concentrations ranging from 53 to 930 mg/kg, with 14 samples exceeding the site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg. Lead was detected in all samples at concentrations ranging from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg. Based on these results, soil excavated from the upper three feet required placement within the designated Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones. Soil excavated from depths greater than 3 feet was deemed suitable for placement within the containment zones, as general embankment fill, or as capping material.

### Regulatory Status and Acceptance

The RDIP established verification thresholds for BCS removal areas: Barium at 1,000 mg/kg (groundwater protection) and Lead at 80 mg/kg (residential screening level). A DTSC-approved Variance to the RDIP dated April 9, 2020, identified site-specific upper-bound background concentrations for comparison: Barium at 120 mg/kg and Lead at 3.8 mg/kg.

The DTSC accepted the Stockpile 2 BCS Removal Verification Testing Technical Memorandum dated March 9, 2020, via email on March 10, 2020, confirming that the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 met the RDIP verification thresholds. The DTSC also accepted the Stockpile 2 MSE Wall Footing Excavation Characterization Testing Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020, via email on June 22, 2020, confirming that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) required placement in designated containment zones, while soil below 3 feet bgs was suitable as embankment fill or capping material.

### Open Items

The "Bent 2 Excavation Soil Stockpiles Characterization Testing" Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020, noted that the Bent 2 soil stockpiles (placed on Stockpile 2) did not meet the clean cap criteria (barium > 120 mg/kg). However, it was suggested that the material might be suitable for partial clean cap (6 inches or less) on the upper surface of Stockpile 2, pending DTSC review and approval.

### Containment Zone

The Stockpile 2 BCS Containment Zone is located between Emerald Avenue and SR 99, between approximate SR 132 Stations 1135+35 and 1148+30.
