## Overview

Task Order No. 4, also identified as DTSC #12-T0106, was an agreement executed under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310 between [[caltrans]] and the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). This task order was part of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, specifically addressing the SR 132/99 corridor in Stanislaus County. The agreement was executed on June 6, 2013, with DTSC's execution date of June 5, 2012.

## Project Personnel

The Caltrans Task Manager for Task Order No. 4 was Richard Stewart, P.G. Stewart certified that the task order and its attachments were within the scope of the project and necessary for its successful completion.

The DTSC Task Order Manager was Steven Becker, P.G.

Scott Nelson served as the Caltrans Contract Manager and certified that Task Order No. 4 complied with the provisions of Agreement No. 43A0310 and was necessary for satisfactory project completion, with sufficient funding encumbered.

G. Scott McGowen, P.E., Chief Environmental Engineer for Caltrans' Division of Environmental Analysis, and Paul Ruffin, P.E., DTSC Contract Manager for the Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program, also signed the agreement, signifying its execution.

## Cost Estimate

Task Order No. 43A0310-04 was associated with cost estimates for DTSC oversight of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles for Fiscal Years 2013/14, 2014/15, and 2015/16. The total task order amount was \$ 172,000.00. This included allocations for FY 13/14 of \$ 98,312.00, FY 14/15 of \$ 39,871.00, and FY15/16 of \$ 33,774.00.

## Term of Task Order

The term of Task Order No. 4 was from June 1, 2013, through May 31, 2016. The execution date was June 1, 2013.
