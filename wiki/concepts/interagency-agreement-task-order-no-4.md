## Interagency Agreement Task Order No 4

Task Order No. 4, including its amendments, was established under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310 between the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). This task order provided for DTSC's oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, and processing of requests related to the mitigation of impacts from the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] located near [[state-route-132-99]] in Stanislaus County, California.

### Site Record

Task Order No. 4, Amendment 1, was executed on May 24, 2016. The original term of Task Order No. 4 was June 7, 2013, through May 31, 2016. Amendment 1 extended the termination date to May 31, 2018. The total amount payable by Caltrans under Task Order No. 4, Amendment 1, was \$158,183.00. Funding was allocated as \$64,183.00 for prior fiscal years and \$94,000.00 for FY 16/17.

Task Order No. 4, Amendment 2, was executed on February 9, 2018. This amendment extended the term of Task Order No. 4, Amendment 1, from June 7, 2013, through May 31, 2016, to June 7, 2013, through May 31, 2018. The total amount payable by Caltrans under Task Order No. 4, Amendment 2, was \$261,957.00. Funding was allocated across fiscal years: \$98,312.00 in FY 13/14, \$39,871.00 in FY 14/15, \$20,000.00 in FY 15/16, and \$70,000.00 in FY 17/18.

The DTSC Task Order Manager for Task Order No. 4 was Steven Becker. The Caltrans Task Manager was [[richard-stewart]], and [[richard-bailey]] served as the Caltrans Contract Manager.

### Regulatory Status

The purpose of Task Order No. 4, and its amendments, was to provide for the reimbursement of costs incurred by DTSC for oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, and/or processing applications or requests made by Caltrans to mitigate impacts from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. Amendments to Task Order No. 4 did not alter the scope of services, invoices, meetings, or electronic submittal of documents provisions of the original Task Order.
