## Interagency Agreement Task Order No 4

This article details Task Order No. 4, Amendment 2, under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310 between the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). This task order, and its amendments, provided for DTSC's oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, and processing of requests related to the mitigation of impacts from the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] located near [[state-route-132-99]] in Stanislaus County, California.

### Site Record

Task Order No. 4, Amendment 2, was executed on February 9, 2018, extending the original term of June 7, 2013, through May 31, 2016, to May 31, 2018. {{quote: "Original Term of Task Order: June 7, 2013 through May 31, 2016 Amended Term: June 7, 2013 through May 31, 2018 Amendment Execution Date: FEBRUART 9 2018"}} The total amount payable by Caltrans under this Task Order was \$261,957.00. {{quote: "Total Task Order Amount: \$ 261,957.00"}} Funding was allocated across fiscal years: \$98,312.00 in FY 13/14, \$39,871.00 in FY 14/15, \$20,000.00 in FY 15/16, and \$70,000.00 in FY 17/18. {{quote: "FY 13/14: \$ 98,312.00 FY 14/15: \$ 39,871.00 FY 15/16: \$ 20,000.00 FY 17/18: \$ 70,000.00"}} The DTSC Task Order Manager was Steven Becker, and the Caltrans Task Manager was [[richard-stewart]]. [[richard-bailey]] served as the Caltrans Contract Manager. {{quote: "DTSC Task Order Manager: Steven Becker Caltrans Task Manager: Richard Stewart Caltrans Contract Manager: Richard Bailey"}}

### Regulatory Status

The purpose of Task Order No. 4, Amendment 1, was to provide for the reimbursement of costs incurred by DTSC for oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, and/or processing applications or requests made by Caltrans to mitigate impacts from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. {{quote: "The original Task Order and this amendment provides for the reimbursement of costs incurred by the DTSC for oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, and/or processing applications(s) or request(s) made by Caltrans to mitigate impacts from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles."}} This amendment did not alter the scope of services, invoices, meetings, or electronic submittal of documents provisions of the original Task Order. {{quote: "This amendment does not change the scope of services provision of the original Task Order. This amendment does not change the invoices, meetings, electronic submittal of document provision of the original Task Order."}}
