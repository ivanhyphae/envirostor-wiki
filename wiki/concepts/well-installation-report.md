## Overview

The [[well-installation-report]] for the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site documents the construction and placement of groundwater monitoring wells. This report is a key component of the site's environmental investigation, providing the foundational data for subsequent groundwater quality assessments. The report was prepared by [[geocon-consultants]].

## Site record

The [[well-installation-report]] details the installation of multiple groundwater monitoring wells. Specifically, well MW-10 was installed on January 26, 2012. The report also lists the installation of wells MW-1 through MW-9 as part of the project.
