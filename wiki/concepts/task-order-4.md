Task Order 4 is the interagency agreement instrument under which Caltrans and DTSC carried out oversight work at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, executed as document 43A0310-04 (with a related record 43A0404-04). It defines the scope of work, budget, and term for DTSC's technical assistance to Caltrans on the stockpiles remediation, and its execution dates and figures establish the period and financial limits within which DTSC's oversight activities at the site were authorized.

## Terms of the order

Task Order 4 was executed as amendment/order number 4 under the standing interagency agreement between Caltrans and DTSC covering the SR 132 West Expressway Project's Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation. The executed document (43A0310-04) sets out the scope of DTSC's work under this task order and the associated budget authorized for that work.

## Significance

Task Order 4 is the contractual basis for DTSC's continued involvement in the stockpiles matter at this stage — it is the document that authorizes and funds DTSC's oversight rather than the technical findings themselves. Its significance to reviewers is administrative rather than evidentiary: it establishes that DTSC's participation in this phase of the project was formally authorized and budgeted, which matters for confirming that DTSC review of any technical deliverables during the task order's term was performed under a valid, executed agreement rather than informally.
