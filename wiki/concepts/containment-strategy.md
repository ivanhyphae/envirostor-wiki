The [[containment-strategy]] for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, located along [[sr-132]] in Stanislaus County, California, was developed to manage soil impacted by [[barium]]. The strategy is detailed within the project's [[remedial-action-plan]] (RAP) and further elaborated in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP).

## Site record

The containment strategy involved the management and potential off-site disposal of impacted soil. Specifically, the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) for the interim Phase 1 of the project, dated January 2019, outlines the approach to managing these stockpiles. The Final Caltrans Modesto Stockpile RAP dated March 2018 also addresses the remediation of these stockpiles. The project is overseen by [[caltrans]] and involves the [[dtsc]] (DTSC). [[geocon-consultants]] has also been involved in the project.

## Regulatory status

The containment strategy is part of the overall remediation plan approved by regulatory agencies. The specific regulatory thresholds and compliance status are detailed within the RAP and RDIP documents.
