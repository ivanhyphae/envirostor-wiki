The [[stockpile-2-bcs-foundation-spoil-assessment-workplan]], submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] on behalf of [[caltrans]], details the sampling and management plan for Barium Containing Soil (BCS) generated during the installation of a weather station and Overhead Sign #1 at [[stockpile-2]] on the State Route 132 West project. The DTSC approved this workplan on March 4, 2022.

## Approval and Management Conditions

The DTSC approved the Technical Memorandum for Stockpile 2 Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan, dated February 28, 2022. This approval allows for the reuse of BCS material on-site if sampling results indicate that concentrations of [[barium]] and lead are below established site-specific background levels. Any on-site reuse locations must be mutually agreed upon by both the DTSC and [[caltrans]].

If sampling results reveal that the spoils material exceeds background concentrations for barium and lead, the DTSC has agreed that the material should be profiled for disposal at the Forward Landfill in Manteca or another acceptable landfill facility.

## Regulatory Status

The DTSC reviewed and approved the Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan on March 4, 2022. This approval is conditional upon the sampling results meeting the established site-specific background concentrations for barium and lead.
