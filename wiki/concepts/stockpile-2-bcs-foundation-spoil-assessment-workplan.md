## Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan

### Overview
The Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan, submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of [[caltrans]], outlines the planned sampling and management of Barium Containing Soil (BCS) spoils generated during the installation of a weather station and Overhead Sign #1 at [[stockpile-2]] on the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California (Site Code: 900259). The [[dtsc]] (DTSC) approved this workplan on March 4, 2022.

### Site Record
The DTSC approved the sampling and management procedures detailed in the Technical Memorandum for Stockpile 2 Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan, dated February 28, 2022.

The DTSC agreed that if sampling results indicate concentrations of [[barium]] and lead are below established site-specific background levels, the BCS material may be reused on-site at locations mutually agreed upon by both DTSC and Caltrans.

If sampling results show the spoils material is above background concentrations, the DTSC agreed that the material should be profiled for disposal at the Forward Landfill in Manteca or another acceptable landfill facility.

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC reviewed and approved the Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan on March 4, 2022. The approval is contingent on the sampling results relative to site-specific background concentrations for barium and lead.
