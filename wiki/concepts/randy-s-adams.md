Randy S Adams served as the [[dtsc]] Project Manager for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County), identified in the February 2, 2017 fact sheet as the named point of contact for public comment on the project, based at DTSC's office at 8800 Cal Center Dr., Sacramento CA 95826.

The fact sheet lists Adams alongside Nathan Schumacher, DTSC Public Participation Specialist, as the two contacts for questions about the site, and separately lists Russ Edmondson, DTSC Public Information Officer, as the contact for news media inquiries — distinguishing Adams's role as the technical/project point of contact from the department's public-outreach and media functions. The fact sheet directs commenters to reach Adams by mail, by email (Randy.Adams@dtsc.ca.gov), or by phone ((916) 255-3591), and notes that key technical reports and fact sheets for the site are posted on DTSC's EnviroStor database under two global ID profiles for the project.

The available material does not otherwise document specific actions, decisions, or approvals made by Adams in the record; his role as documented here is limited to that of the designated DTSC contact for the site as of the February 2017 fact sheet.
