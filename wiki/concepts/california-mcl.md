California MCLs are drinking water standards that are relevant to the assessment of groundwater quality at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site.

## Site record

Groundwater monitoring wells were installed adjacent to the stockpiles in May and June 2006. {{quote: "The results of analysis of groundwater samples collected from the eight monitoring wells in June and October 2006 show that the concentrations of COPCs that were analyzed for did not exceed drinking water standards (MCLs)."}} {{quote: "The COPCs are at concentrations less than California MCLs."}}

Stormwater monitoring was conducted in March 2006. {{quote: "With the sole exception of an elevated barium concentration reported for one stormwater sample collected from the northwestern side of Stockpile 3 (sample SW03), the stormwater samples did not contain target analytes exceeding MCLs or determined site background levels."}} {{quote: "Barium was reported at a concentration of 2,000 micrograms per liter (µg/l) in sample SW03 exceeding the MCL of 1,000 µg/l."}}

## Regulatory status

California MCLs are used to evaluate groundwater quality. {{quote: "The results of analysis of groundwater samples collected from the eight monitoring wells in June and October 2006 show that the concentrations of COPCs that were analyzed for did not exceed drinking water standards (MCLs)."}} {{quote: "The COPCs are at concentrations less than California MCLs."}}
