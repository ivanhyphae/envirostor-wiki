The California Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) is a regulatory standard for drinking water quality set by the state. In the context of the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, California MCLs are used as benchmarks to evaluate the suitability of soil for reuse or the need for remediation.

## Site record
The Hearing Summary Report dated June 21, 2017, references California MCLs in relation to several analytes found at the SR 132 site. Specifically, it notes that the California MCL for manganese is 50 µg/L. This value was used as a screening level for soil reuse.

## Regulatory status
California MCLs serve as regulatory thresholds for contaminants in drinking water. For the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, these standards are applied to assess the potential risks associated with soil reuse, particularly concerning groundwater impacts.
