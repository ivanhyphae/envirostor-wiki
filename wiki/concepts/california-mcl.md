The California Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) is a regulatory standard for drinking water quality set by the state. In the context of the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, California MCLs are used as benchmarks to evaluate the suitability of soil for reuse or the need for remediation.

## DTSC Concurrence with Feasibility Study

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), reviewed the "Final Feasibility Study Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County" dated June 24, 2014, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. DTSC concurred with the Final Feasibility Study on June 30, 2014.
