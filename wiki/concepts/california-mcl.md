California MCLs are drinking water standards that are relevant to the assessment of groundwater quality at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site.

## Site record

Groundwater monitoring wells were installed adjacent to the stockpiles in May and June 2006. Analysis of groundwater samples collected in June and October 2006 indicated that concentrations of COPCs did not exceed drinking water standards (MCLs). Specifically, COPCs were found at concentrations less than California MCLs.

Stormwater monitoring conducted in March 2006 revealed that, with the exception of an elevated barium concentration in one sample (SW03) from the northwestern side of Stockpile 3, stormwater samples did not contain target analytes exceeding MCLs or determined site background levels. The barium concentration in sample SW03 was 2,000 micrograms per liter (µg/l), exceeding the MCL of 1,000 µg/l.

In January 2013, groundwater samples from wells MW-1 through MW-10 were analyzed. With the exception of manganese detected in the sample from MW-6, none of the reported dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Regarding general minerals, with the exception of nitrate, none of the reported general minerals exceeded their respective California primary MCLs. Total dissolved solids (TDS) exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l for samples from wells MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10. Barium and strontium concentrations were reported at levels significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds.

In November 2012, groundwater samples from wells MW-1 through MW-10 were analyzed. With the exception of manganese detected in the sample from MW-8, none of the reported dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. For general minerals, with the exception of nitrate, none of the reported general minerals exceeded their respective California primary MCLs. TDS concentrations exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l for samples from wells MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10. Barium and strontium concentrations were reported at levels significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds.

In May 2012, groundwater samples from wells MW-1 through MW-8 were analyzed. With the exception of manganese, which was reported at greater than the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l for the sample from MW-4, none of the reported dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Regarding general minerals, with the exception of nitrate, none of the reported general minerals exceeded their respective California primary MCLs. TDS concentrations were reported greater than the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l for samples from wells MW-4, MW-5, and MW-6. Barium, lead, and strontium concentrations were reported at levels significantly below their water quality thresholds.

Stormwater samples PL1 through PL4 collected on December 11, 2015, showed that none of the reported dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective primary MCLs. However, manganese concentrations for samples PL2, PL3, and PL4 exceeded the secondary MCLs. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective practical quantitation limits (PQLs).

## Regulatory status

California MCLs are used to evaluate groundwater quality. The results of analysis of groundwater samples collected from the eight monitoring wells in June and October 2006 showed that the concentrations of COPCs did not exceed drinking water standards (MCLs). The COPCs were at concentrations less than California MCLs.

In January 2013, with the exception of nitrate, none of the reported general minerals for the groundwater samples collected exceeded their respective California primary MCLs. TDS exceeded the secondary MCL for samples from wells MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10.

In November 2012, with the exception of nitrate, none of the reported general minerals for the groundwater samples collected exceeded their respective California primary MCLs. TDS exceeded the secondary MCL for samples from wells MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10.

In May 2012, with the exception of nitrate, none of the reported general minerals for the groundwater samples collected exceeded their respective California primary MCLs. TDS exceeded the secondary MCL for samples from wells MW-4, MW-5, and MW-6.

Stormwater samples collected on December 11, 2015, showed that none of the reported dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective primary MCLs.
