## Stockpile 2 Removal

### Overview
Stockpile 2 was a location where [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) was managed as part of the [[caltrans]] SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. The removal and relocation of BCS material from Stockpile 2, along with Stockpile 1, was contingent on the successful verification testing and approval of Stockpile 3's removal. Following the approval of Stockpile 3's remediation, [[caltrans]] sought authorization to begin placing a [[clean-soil-cover]] and/or aggregate base material on the surfaces of the BCS consolidation zones for Stockpiles 1 and 2.

### Site Record
The removal and relocation of all [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) material from Stockpile 2 was completed. This action was pending approval after the verification testing of Stockpile 3 was deemed acceptable.

### Regulatory Status
The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) approved the remediation of Stockpile 3 on March 18, 2020, which was a prerequisite for proceeding with the placement of clean cover materials on Stockpile 2.

### Open Items
Following the approval of Stockpile 3's remediation, [[caltrans]] requested approval to commence the placement of a minimum 12-inch [[clean-soil-cover]] and/or a minimum 4-inch thick layer of aggregate base material on the surfaces of the BCS consolidation zones of Stockpile 2.
