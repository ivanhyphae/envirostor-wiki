Kyle Cockerham is an individual associated with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board) who was included in email correspondence regarding the remediation case file for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, specifically concerning the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California.

## Site record

Kyle Cockerham was CC'd on an email dated Wednesday, April 7, 2021, from Dean Wright of the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) to Richard Stewart of [[caltrans]]. The email conveyed DTSC's approval of the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan, which was received on April 5, 2021. This plan outlined the proposed sampling and analysis for soil intended for use as clean fill at the location. The email stated that the plan was consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and that DTSC had no comments. {{quote: "DTSC and the Waterboard have reviewed the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan (Plan) received on April 5, 2021 that outlines the proposed sampling and analysis for soil at this location that will be used as clean fill. The Plan is consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and there are no comments. This e-mail serves as DTSCs approval of the Plan."}}

Cockerham was also CC'd on the preceding email sent by Richard Stewart to Dean Wright on Monday, April 5, 2021, which was a reply concerning "Modesto 132/99 Clean Cap Test Locations." {{quote: "RE: Modesto 132/99 Clean Cap Test Locations"}}

## Regulatory status

As an employee of the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], Kyle Cockerham's involvement indicates the Water Board's regulatory oversight and participation in the review and approval process for the soil sampling plan related to the Modesto soil stockpiles.
