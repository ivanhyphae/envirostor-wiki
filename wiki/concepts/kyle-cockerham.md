Kyle Cockerham is identified in project correspondence as a point of contact for the [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. He is involved in the review and approval process for sampling plans and technical memorandums related to the [[carpenter-road-shoofly-area]] project.

### DTSC Approval of Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Sampling Plan

On April 7, 2021, Dean Wright of the [[dtsc]] emailed Richard Stewart of [[caltrans]] to confirm that the [[dtsc]] and the Waterboard had reviewed the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan. Kyle Cockerham was copied on this email, indicating his involvement in the review process. Wright stated that the plan was consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and that there were no comments, serving as the [[dtsc]]'s approval of the plan.

### DTSC Approval of Carpenter Shoofly Technical Memorandum

On April 22, 2021, Dean Wright of the [[dtsc]] emailed Richard Stewart of [[caltrans]] regarding the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021. Kyle Cockerham was cc'd on this email, signifying his role in the review. Wright confirmed that the [[dtsc]] and the Water Board agreed that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria. This email served as the [[dtsc]]'s acceptance of the memorandum.
