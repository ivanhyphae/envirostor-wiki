## Kyle Cockerham

Kyle Cockerham is an individual associated with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board) who was involved in email correspondence regarding the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] remediation case file, specifically concerning the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California.

## Site record

Kyle Cockerham was CC'd on an email dated Wednesday, April 7, 2021, from Dean Wright of the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) to Richard Stewart of [[caltrans]]. This email conveyed DTSC's approval of the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan, which had been received on April 5, 2021. The plan detailed the proposed sampling and analysis for soil designated for use as clean fill at the site. The email stated that the plan was consistent with prior borrow area sampling efforts and that DTSC had no comments. {{quote: "DTSC and the Waterboard have reviewed the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan (Plan) received on April 5, 2021 that outlines the proposed sampling and analysis for soil at this location that will be used as clean fill. The Plan is consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and there are no comments. This e-mail serves as DTSCs approval of the Plan."}}

Cockerham was also CC'd on a preceding email sent by Richard Stewart to Dean Wright on Monday, April 5, 2021, which was a reply concerning "Modesto 132/99 Clean Cap Test Locations." {{quote: "RE: Modesto 132/99 Clean Cap Test Locations"}}

Additionally, Kyle Cockerham provided comments on the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Surface Water Report dated December 17, 2020. In an email sent on Thursday, March 25, 2021, to Richard Stewart of [[caltrans]], Cockerham stated, "I don't have any comment on the Modesto Soil Stockpile Surface Water Sampling report." {{quote: "I don't have any comment on the Modesto Soil Stockpile Surface Water Sampling report."}} This email was in response to a forwarded surface water report by Dean Wright of DTSC, who had requested that any Water Board comments be directed to Richard Stewart and CC'd to him. {{quote: "Just forwarding the attached surface water report on the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project. If the Waterboard has any comments they should be directed to Richard Stewart and please just cc me. Thanks,"}}

## Regulatory status

As an employee of the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], Kyle Cockerham's involvement signifies the Water Board's regulatory oversight and participation in the review and approval processes for both the soil sampling plan and the surface water report related to the Modesto soil stockpiles. His lack of comments on the surface water report indicates acceptance of that document by the Water Board. {{quote: "I don't have any comment on the Modesto Soil Stockpile Surface Water Sampling report."}}
