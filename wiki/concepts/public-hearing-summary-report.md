## Public Hearing Summary Report

The hearing was conducted by Caltrans, in conjunction with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB). The Stanislaus Council of Governments (StanCOG) was also involved. The report summarizes public comments and agency responses regarding the project's environmental considerations, including potential impacts and proposed mitigation measures.
