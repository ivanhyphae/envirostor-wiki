## Public Hearing Summary Report

The Public Hearing Summary Report documents the proceedings of a public hearing held in June 2017 concerning the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. The hearing addressed the 10-STA-132 (PM 11.0/15.0) and 10-STA-99 (PM 15.7/17.5) projects, identified by Project ID 10-0000-0424 {{quote: "City of Modesto, Stanislaus County 10-STA-132(PM 11.0/15.0) 10-STA-99 (PM 15.7/17.5) Project ID 10-0000-0424"}}. The hearing was conducted by [[caltrans]], in conjunction with the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. The stanislaus-council-of-governments (StanCOG) was also involved {{quote: "June 2017"}}. The report summarizes public comments and agency responses regarding the project's environmental considerations, including potential impacts and proposed mitigation measures, which would have been informed by the [[human-health-risk-assessment]].
