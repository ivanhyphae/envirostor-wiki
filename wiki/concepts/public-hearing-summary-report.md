## Overview

The Public Hearing Summary Report documents the proceedings of a public hearing held in June 2017 concerning the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. The hearing addressed the 10-STA-132 (PM 11.0/15.0) and 10-STA-99 (PM 15.7/17.5) projects, identified by Project ID 10-0000-0424. The hearing was conducted by [[caltrans]], in conjunction with the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. The stanislaus-council-of-governments (StanCOG) was also involved. The report summarizes public comments and agency responses regarding the project's environmental considerations.

## Site Record

The Public Hearing Summary Report was issued in June 2017. The report pertains to the City of Modesto, Stanislaus County, specifically addressing the 10-STA-132 (PM 11.0/15.0) and 10-STA-99 (PM 15.7/17.5) projects, identified by Project ID 10-0000-0424. The hearing addressed concerns related to the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, which involved the remediation of soil stockpiles. The report serves as a record of public input and agency responses to the proposed actions.

## Regulatory Status
