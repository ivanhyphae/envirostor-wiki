The BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2 refer to consolidated [[barium-containing-soil]] located easterly of the proposed clean cap borrow area within the State Route 132 project in Modesto, California. These stockpiles are the subject of remediation efforts, specifically capping with clean cover fill material. The characterization of the borrow area was performed by [[geocon-consultants]] to ensure the suitability of the extracted soil for this purpose.

## Site record

The proposed clean cap borrow area, located adjacent and easterly of Carpenter Road, was characterized through 12 direct-push borings (B1 through B12) completed on December 21, 2020. Borings extended to a maximum depth of 5 feet, with continuous core soil samples collected at 0 to 2 feet and 2 to 5 feet intervals. These samples were composited into three-part composites, yielding four samples per depth interval. Analysis for Title 22 metals and mercury was conducted by Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL).

Reported concentration ranges for Title 22 metals in the borrow area soil, with site-specific background concentrations in parentheses, were:
*   Arsenic: 1.0 to 1.5 mg/kg (1.2 mg/kg)
*   Barium: 60 to 110 mg/kg (72.8 mg/kg)
*   Beryllium: 1.3 to 1.6 mg/kg (0.2 mg/kg)
*   Chromium: 7.5 to 11 mg/kg (7.1 mg/kg)
*   Cobalt: 3.2 to 4.9 mg/kg (4.4 mg/kg)
*   Copper: 5.3 to 9.9 mg/kg (7.5 mg/kg)
*   Lead: 1.7 to 7.0 mg/kg (2.0 mg/kg)
*   Nickel: 5.6 to 8.2 mg/kg (5.3 mg/kg)
*   Silver: 1.6 to 2.5 mg/kg (0.2 mg/kg)
*   Vanadium: 16 to 25 mg/kg (31.3 mg/kg)
*   Zinc: 21 to 41 mg/kg (26.3 mg/kg)

Antimony, cadmium, selenium, thallium, mercury, and organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) were not detected above laboratory reporting limits. The reported metal concentrations were deemed consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. Based on these findings, the proposed borrow area was determined to be suitable for generating [[clean-cover-fill-material]] for capping BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "Based on the data presented herein, the proposed clean cap borrow area appears to be suitable to generate clean cover fill materials for capping BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2."}} This information was intended for inclusion in the Removal Action Completion Report. {{quote: "This information will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted following completion of the stockpile capping activities."}}

## Regulatory status

The soil sampling strategy for the borrow area was developed in general accordance with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and was approved by Mr. Dean Wright with DTSC. {{quote: "The soil sampling strategy utilized was developed in general accordance with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and was approved Mr. Dean Wright with DTSC."}}
