## Overview
The BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2 are consolidated areas of [[barium-containing-soil]] located easterly of the proposed clean cap borrow area within the State Route 132 project in Modesto, California. These stockpiles are subject to remediation efforts, specifically capping with clean cover fill material. Geocon-consultants performed characterization of a borrow area to ensure the suitability of extracted soil for this purpose.

## Site record

The proposed clean cap borrow area, situated adjacent and easterly of Carpenter Road, was characterized through 12 direct-push borings (B1 through B12) completed on December 21, 2020. Borings extended to a maximum depth of 5 feet, with continuous core soil samples collected at 0 to 2 feet and 2 to 5 feet intervals. These samples were composited into three-part composites, yielding four samples per depth interval. Analysis for Title 22 metals and mercury was conducted by Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL).

Reported concentration ranges for Title 22 metals in the borrow area soil, with site-specific background concentrations in parentheses, were:
*   Arsenic: 1.0 to 1.5 mg/kg (1.2 mg/kg)
*   Barium: 60 to 110 mg/kg (72.8 mg/kg)
*   Beryllium: 1.3 to 1.6 mg/kg (0.2 mg/kg)
*   Chromium: 7.5 to 11 mg/kg (7.1 mg/kg)
*   Cobalt: 3.2 to 4.9 mg/kg (4.4 mg/kg)
*   Copper: 5.3 to 9.9 mg/kg (7.5 mg/kg)
*   Lead: 1.7 to 7.0 mg/kg (2.0 mg/kg)
*   Nickel: 5.6 to 8.2 mg/kg (5.3 mg/kg)
*   Silver: 1.6 to 2.5 mg/kg (0.2 mg/kg)
*   Vanadium: 16 to 25 mg/kg (31.3 mg/kg)
*   Zinc: 21 to 41 mg/kg (26.3 mg/kg)

Antimony, cadmium, selenium, thallium, mercury, and organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) were not detected above laboratory reporting limits. The reported metal concentrations were deemed consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. Based on these findings, the proposed borrow area was determined to be suitable for generating [[clean-cover-fill-material]] for capping BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "Based on the data presented herein, the proposed clean cap borrow area appears to be suitable to generate clean cover fill materials for capping BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2."}} This information was intended for inclusion in the Removal Action Completion Report. {{quote: "This information will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted following completion of the stockpile capping activities."}}

The Remedial-Design-Implementation-Plan (RDIP) for the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase I State Route 132 West Project, dated January 30, 2019, outlines the capping strategy. Section 4.7 of the RDIP states that the surface of completed Stockpile-1 and BCS-Stockpile 2 barium-containing soil will be capped with a minimum 12-inch-thick clean fill material. {{quote: "The surface of the completed Stockpile 1 and 2 barium-containing soil and ADL consolidation zones (including east- and west-facing temporary slopes) will be capped with minimum 12-inch-thick clean fill material consisting of approved imported fill and/or minimum 4-inch-thick AB materials (pending placement of structural pavement section, hardscape and/or additional clean embankment fill)."}}

The initial proposed source of clean imported fill soil was vacant and agricultural land within planned excavation areas along the SR-132 ROW between Carpenter Road and Rosemore Avenue. {{quote: "The proposed source of clean imported fill soil consists of currently vacant and agricultural land within planned excavation areas along the SR-132 ROW between the Carpenter Road and Rosemore Avenue overcrossings as depicted on Figure 1."}} The highway contractor, Bay Cities Paving and Grading, identified an area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road within the SR 132 right-of-way as the clean fill material source. Geocon-consultants evaluated this area and determined that the planned highway underpass excavation area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road would generate soil material suitable for use as clean cover fill material, as documented in a February 27, 2020, Technical Memorandum. {{quote: "Geocon, in coordination with Caltrans and DTSC, evaluated this area and determined the planned highway underpass excavation area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road would generate soil material suitable for use as clean cover fill material (Geocon February 27, 2020 Technical Memorandum)."}} DTSC subsequently approved this planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area. {{quote: "DTSC subsequently provided approval of the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area."}}

An additional source of clean fill material was identified by the highway contractor at proposed [[mechanically-stabilized-earth-wall]] footing excavations (approximately five-foot-deep) along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2. Geocon-consultants evaluated the MSE wall footing material along the southern boundary of Stockpile-1 following excavation of the southern slope BCS material. {{quote: "An additional source of clean fill material was identified by the highway contractor at the proposed mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations (approximately five-foot-deep) located along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2. Geocon evaluated the MSE wall footing material along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1 following excavation of the southern slope BCS material (Geocon April 6, 2020 Technical Memorandum)."}} In coordination with [[caltrans]] and DTSC, it was determined that the upper foot of planned wall footing excavation soil contained elevated [[barium]] concentrations that would not be suitable for use as clean cover fill. {{quote: "In coordination with Caltrans and DTSC, it was determined that the upper foot of planned wall footing excavation soil contained elevated barium concentrations that would not be suit"}}

## Regulatory status

The soil sampling strategy for the borrow area was developed in general accordance with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and was approved by Mr. Dean Wright with DTSC. {{quote: "The soil sampling strategy utilized was developed in general accordance with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and was approved Mr. Dean Wright with DTSC."}} DTSC also provided approval of the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area. {{quote: "DTSC subsequently provided approval of the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area."}}
