The Caltrans Ramp Letter Dated 5 29 12 is a correspondence from Caltrans to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) regarding the remediation of soil stockpiles at the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County. This letter addresses the proposed excavation and off-site disposal of Stockpile 3, located in the Carpenter Road Area.

The letter confirms Caltrans' intent to excavate and dispose of Stockpile 3, which was identified as a source of contamination. Caltrans proposed to manage the excavated soil as hazardous waste, with disposal at an approved facility. The letter also indicates that Caltrans would be submitting a Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for DTSC review and approval, detailing the proposed remediation activities.

## Excavation and Disposal of Stockpile 3

Caltrans, through the Ramp Letter Dated 5 29 12, communicated its decision to proceed with the excavation and off-site disposal of Stockpile 3. This stockpile was situated within the Carpenter Road Area of the SR 132 project. The proposed management of the excavated material was as hazardous waste, with the intention of transporting it to a licensed disposal facility. This action was a key step in the remediation process for the site.
