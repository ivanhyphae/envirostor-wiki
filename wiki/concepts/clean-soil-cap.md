The clean soil cap is the barrier of imported or verified low-concentration soil that Caltrans and DTSC required over Stockpiles 1 and 2 on the SR 132 project, with an upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg governing whether excavated material could serve that function. The Bent 2 excavation soil, tested against this threshold in October 2020, failed it — a result that did not stop the material's reuse but limited how it could be used, illustrating that "clean cap" was a quantitative, threshold-driven designation rather than a general description of soil quality.

## Origin of the criteria

The clean cap criteria originate in the Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan (April 9, 2020), approved by DTSC, which set the upperbound site-specific background value for barium at 120 mg/kg as the threshold a soil must meet to qualify as clean cap. A separate fill berm, generated from the lower portion of the Stockpile 2 mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation, had already been reviewed and approved for use as clean cap by DTSC; the Bent 2 end dumps were partly placed on top of that pre-approved berm.

## Bent 2 stockpile testing against the criteria

Geocon Consultants collected ten discrete samples (STK1–STK10) from the Bent 2 end dump piles at approximately 80-foot intervals, analyzed for barium and lead by EPA Method 6010B. Barium was detected in all ten samples, ranging from 31 to 230 mg/kg; four of the ten equaled or exceeded the 120 mg/kg background threshold. Lead was detected in six of ten samples, from 2.6 to 13 mg/kg, in a range similar to the site-specific background of 3.8 mg/kg and not treated as a basis for exceeding the cap criteria.

Because four of ten samples met or exceeded the barium threshold, Geocon concluded the Bent 2 stockpile soil as a whole did not meet the clean cap criteria established in the April 9, 2020 variance.

## Consequence: partial cap use, not disqualification

The barium exceedance did not remove the Bent 2 soil from use as cap material outright. Geocon's October 30, 2020 memorandum proposed, pending DTSC review and approval, that the material could still serve as a partial clean cap — limited to 6 inches or less — on the upper surface of Stockpile 2, on the reasoning that the planned structural pavement section (base material and asphalt/concrete) would be placed over the entire upper surface afterward, providing an additional barrier over the soil. This made the pavement section itself part of the containment strategy for that surface, not merely a running surface.

The same relief did not extend to the northerly slope of Stockpile 2, which would not receive the pavement section. For that slope, the memorandum states fill material from previously approved clean fill sources would be required — meaning the Bent 2 soil, having failed the barium threshold, was excluded from use as cap on that slope.

## Status

The October 30, 2020 memorandum frames the 6-inch partial-cap use as pending DTSC review and approval; the record here does not show DTSC's determination. The memorandum states this information would be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted following completion of the Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities.
