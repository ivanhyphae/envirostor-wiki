# Supplemental Site Investigation

The Supplemental Site Investigation (SSI) was the follow-on characterization effort conducted at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles following an earlier phase of site characterization, undertaken to fill data gaps and support the site's human health risk assessment. It was governed by a workplan submitted in September 2012 and resulted in a final report, prepared with an accompanying Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA), dated April 4, 2013. The SSI's findings and the HHRA built on it are the basis for evaluating whether the stockpiled soil poses a risk to human health and how the material can be managed or disposed of.

## Workplan and scope

The Supplemental Site Characterization Workplan, finalized September 20, 2012, laid out the additional sampling and analysis Caltrans proposed to perform beyond the initial site characterization. The workplan was developed for the SR 132 West Expressway Project stockpile locations and set the scope that the subsequent SSI report addressed.

## Final SSI and HHRA report

The Final SSI and HHRA, dated April 4, 2013, presents the results of the supplemental sampling called for in the 2012 workplan and integrates those results into a human health risk assessment for the stockpiled soils. The document combines the site investigation and the risk assessment into a single deliverable rather than treating them as separate work products, reflecting that the supplemental sampling was undertaken specifically to support the risk evaluation of the stockpiles.
