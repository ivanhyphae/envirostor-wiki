## Supplemental Site Investigation

The Supplemental Site Investigation (SSI) was conducted as part of the remediation efforts for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, located along SR 132 in Stanislaus County, California. The SSI aimed to gather additional data to support the Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA) and inform remediation decisions.
