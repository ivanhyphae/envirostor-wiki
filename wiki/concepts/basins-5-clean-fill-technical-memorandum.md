## Basins 5 Clean Fill Technical Memorandum

### Overview

The Basins 5 Clean Fill Technical Memorandum documents the soil characterization for the borrow area intended for use as clean cap material for the State Route 132 stockpiles. This memorandum was reviewed and approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB).

### Site Record

On July 9, 2020, DTSC issued its acceptance of the Revised Technical Memorandum (dated July 2, 2020) concerning the Basin 5 clean cap borrow area soil characterization. This acceptance followed a review of additional information provided by Caltrans on June 29, 2020, which addressed concerns regarding chromium concentrations. DTSC and the RWQCB agreed that the composite soil sample results from Basin 5 were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and deemed them suitable for use as clean cover material for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2.

The rationale for suitability was detailed in a footnote within the Revised Memo, explaining that while some chromium values exceeded the 95% Upper Confidence Limit (UCL), these concentrations were considered consistent with background levels. Specifically, John Juhrend of Geocon Consultants, Inc. noted that the reported chromium values (ranging from 3.5 to 11 mg/kg) were "very low, orders of magnitude below land use screening levels, consistently representative of native soil background, and not associated with any contamination impacts." He further referenced a UC Davis background soil report indicating a low background concentration for chromium of 23 mg/kg.

The initial submittal of analytical data from the Basin 5 borrow area for review occurred on June 23, 2020. Concerns were raised by DTSC on June 25, 2020, regarding chromium results exceeding the 95% UCL, prompting a request for revision of the technical memorandum to include a rationale for the suitability of these concentrations as capping material.

### Regulatory Status

The Revised Technical Memorandum for Basin 5 was accepted by DTSC on July 9, 2020. This acceptance signifies that the soil from the Basin 5 borrow area meets the criteria for use as clean cover material, consistent with the approved [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]]. The [[central-valley-rwqcb]] also concurred with this determination.
