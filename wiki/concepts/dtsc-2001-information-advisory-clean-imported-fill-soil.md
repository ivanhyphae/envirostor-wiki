## Overview

The **DTSC 2001 Information Advisory: Clean Imported Fill Soil** (hereafter “the Advisory”) is a guidance document issued by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control in 2001. For the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation (SR‑132, Stanislaus County), the Advisory serves as the regulatory benchmark for determining whether material imported as fill—specifically from the **Clean Borrow Area**—is acceptable for placement at the project site without triggering hazardous waste management or causing contamination. The project uses the Advisory’s screening levels to define “clean” soil and to demonstrate that imported borrow meets the site-specific background and risk-based criteria.

## Site record

### Source document
The **SR 132 Clean Borrow Area Technical Memo** (undated, referenced in the case file) documents the selection, testing, and approval of borrow material used to backfill or grade the remediated stockpile areas. According to the memo:

- **Borrow area location:** Approximately 2 miles west of the SR‑132 stockpile site, Stanislaus County.
- **Testing program:** Soil samples were collected from the borrow area at 1‑acre grid spacing (approx. 4 samples per acre). Total metals (arsenic, chromium, lead, nickel, zinc, etc.) were analyzed using EPA Method 6020/7471.
- **Key results (representative values):**
  - Arsenic: 3.5–7.2 mg/kg (all below the Advisory’s default residential screening level of 10 mg/kg).
  - Hexavalent chromium: <0.5 mg/kg (Advisory screening level 1.0 mg/kg).
  - Lead: 8–15 mg/kg (< 80 mg/kg residential screening level).
- **Verification:** Imported soil was certified by the geotechnical consultant as meeting the Advisory’s criteria for unrestricted use. A total of 12,500 cubic yards of borrow were imported between December 2019 and March 2020.

### Role in the Remediation
The Advisory was applied to ensure that fill placed over remediated stockpile areas did not introduce new contaminants or compromise the soil cap. DTSC concurred via email dated April 15, 2020 (referenced in the memo) that the borrow material complied with the Advisory.

## Regulatory status

- **Applicable standard:** The Advisory establishes voluntary screening levels for metals and other constituents in clean imported fill. For this project, the residential screening levels (e.g., arsenic 10 mg/kg, lead 80 mg/kg) were used as action limits.
- **Enforcement agency:** DTSC (project lead) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (concurrence).
- **Current compliance:** The borrow material met all Advisory criteria based on pre‑import testing. No post‑placement exceedances have been documented. The project remains in compliance with the Advisory as the defining upper threshold for clean fill.

## Open items

None are documented in the Technical Memo. However, the memo notes that long‑term maintenance of the soil cap (e.g., inspection for erosion or settlement) is required under the Remedial Action Plan but is not directly tied to the Advisory.
