The Statement of Findings, dated March 15, 2018, is [[caltrans]]'s determination that the soil comprising [[stockpile-1]] and the other Modesto soil stockpiles generated during the [[state-route-132-west-expressway-project]] is clean fill suitable for use as cover material, based on comparison of stockpile sampling results against [[site-specific-background-levels]]. It is the document that closes the characterization question for the stockpiles by concluding the material does not require handling as contaminated soil.

The Statement of Findings sets out that constituent concentrations detected in the stockpiled soil fall within the range of naturally occurring background levels established for the site, rather than reflecting contamination requiring remediation or special disposal. On that basis it concludes the soil is [[clean-fill-suitable-as-cover]] and can be used as [[clean-soil-cover]] rather than being managed or disposed of as a waste.

## Basis and role in the project record

The finding rests on the comparison between stockpile sample results and site-specific background concentrations, rather than on a generic regulatory cleanup threshold. This distinguishes the Statement of Findings from a determination that the soil is free of all detectable constituents: it is a determination that the levels present are consistent with what would be expected in native, undisturbed soil in the area, and therefore do not signify contamination attributable to the site's prior use or the highway project.

The Statement of Findings functions as the disposition document for the stockpiled soil generated by the SR 132 West Expressway Project — it is the record that allowed the material to be characterized and used rather than treated as a waste stream requiring further investigation, sampling escalation, or offsite disposal as contaminated soil.
