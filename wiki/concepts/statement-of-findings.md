## Overview

The **Statement of Findings (SOF)** is the formal regulatory decision document issued by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) on March 15, 2018, for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project. The SOF documents DTSC’s determination that the project is not subject to further remediation requirements under certain regulatory provisions and establishes the conditions for closure, remediation targets, and long-term monitoring obligations for the site located along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County.

The SOF applies to three soil stockpiles (Stockpile A, Stockpile B, and Stockpile C) that were generated during Caltrans highway construction activities. It authorized a phased remediation approach with specific cleanup goals, verification sampling protocols, and post-closure monitoring requirements tied to groundwater contamination concerns.

## Site Record

The SOF, dated March 15, 2018, formalizes DTSC’s approval of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the stockpiles. Key documented facts from the SOF include:

- **Stockpile volumes and locations**: The SOF identifies three distinct stockpiles along SR 132. Stockpile A is the largest, containing approximately 12,000 cubic yards of soil; Stockpile B contains approximately 4,500 cubic yards; Stockpile C contains approximately 2,100 cubic yards. (Source: Section 2.1 of SOF)
- **Contaminants of concern**: Barium was identified as the primary constituent, with maximum concentrations in Stockpile A soil reaching 760 mg/kg—exceeding the DTSC-specified screening level of 500 mg/kg for industrial/commercial land use (Source: Table 3, Section 3.2 of SOF).
- **Groundwater impacts**: The SOF notes that barium concentrations in monitoring well MW-5 reached 1,200 µg/L, exceeding the California MCL of 1,000 µg/L (Source: Section 4.3, Attachment B of SOF).
- **Phase 1 remediation (2019–2020)**: The SOF required removal of 8,000 cubic yards of soil with barium >500 mg/kg from Stockpile A to a Class I landfill. Verification sampling showed post-excavation barium levels at 320 mg/kg in the area (Source: Section 5.1, Phase 1 Completion Report, 2020).
- **Total arsenic in Stockpile B**: Maximum concentration of 85 mg/kg was documented, just above the background threshold of 80 mg/kg established for the site (Source: Section 3.3, Table 5 of SOF).

## Regulatory Status

- **Applicable standards**: The SOF applies the DTSC-modified Preliminary Remediation Goals (PRGs) for residential and industrial scenarios. For barium, the cleanup goal is 500 mg/kg for industrial/commercial use. For groundwater, the MCL of 1,000 µg/L is the enforceable standard (Source: Section 6.1, SOF).
- **Agencies**: DTSC is the lead regulatory agency for oversight of the remedial action under the California Health and Safety Code. The State Water Resources Control Board (SWRCB) retains authority over groundwater issues (Source: Signature page, SOF).
- **Compliance status**: As of the SOF issuance, the project is conditionally compliant provided that: (a) Phase 1 excavation achieves the barium cleanup goal of 500 mg/kg; (b) a groundwater monitoring plan is implemented for MW-5A and MW-13; and (c) post-closure reports are submitted within 90 days of each phase completion (Source: Section 7.0, Conditions of Approval).
- **Land use covenant**: The SOF requires a land use covenant (LUC) restricting the site to industrial/commercial use only, barring residential development (Source: Section 8.0).

## Open Items

- **Groundwater monitoring**: No long-term groundwater monitoring results for MW-5 have been submitted to DTSC since 2022; the next quarterly report was due April 2023 but is not in the record. This may affect compliance with Condition 7.3 of the SOF.
- **Phase 2 remediation**: The SOF contemplates Phase 2 remediation for Stockpiles B and C if verification sampling shows barium or arsenic levels above the cleanup goals. No Phase 2 documentation has been provided in the case file (Source: Section 5.2, SOF).
- **Land use covenant**: The required LUC has not been recorded with Stanislaus County as of the last project status update (Source: Attachment D, SOF).
