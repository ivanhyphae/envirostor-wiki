The [[final-environmental-impact-report-environmental-assessment]] (Final EIR/EA) is the comprehensive environmental review document for the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project and the associated [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation. It integrates the [[final-remedial-action-plan]] (Final RAP) and addresses environmental impacts under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA).

### Public Comment and Responses

A joint public comment period for the Draft EIR/EA and Draft Final RAP was held from January 18, 2017, to March 17, 2017. A total of 305 comments were received, with 62 specifically addressing the Draft Final RAP. Comments were submitted via letter, email, comment cards, and orally at a public hearing. [[caltrans]] prepared responses to comments on the Draft EIR/EA, and the [[dtsc]], in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), prepared responses for the Draft Final RAP. [[caltrans]] accepted and incorporated the [[dtsc]]'s responses into the Final EIR/EA. A compact disk (CD) containing all comments and responses, including those specific to the Draft Final RAP, was provided with the Response to Comments letter dated March 13, 2018.

### Document Availability

The Final EIR/EA is accessible online through the [[caltrans]] project website and at physical locations including the [[dtsc]] office in Sacramento, the [[caltrans]] District 10 Office in Stockton, the Stanislaus County Library in Modesto, and the StanCOG Office in Modesto.

### Regulatory Integration

The Final EIR/EA serves as the conclusive environmental review for the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project and the remediation of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. It formally includes the [[final-remedial-action-plan]] and its approval by the relevant regulatory agencies.

### Associated Deliverables

The Response to Comments letter dated March 13, 2018, confirmed the enclosure of a compact disk (CD) containing all public comments and agency responses related to the Draft EIR/EA and Draft Final RAP. This CD is a key deliverable associated with the Final EIR/EA process.
