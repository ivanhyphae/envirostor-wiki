## Final Environmental Impact Report Environmental Assessment

### Overview
The Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (Final EIR/EA) is a comprehensive document that evaluates the environmental impacts of the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project and includes the Final Remedial Action Plan (Final RAP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. It was prepared by [[caltrans]] as the lead agency under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA), with the DTSC acting as a responsible agency for the RAP. The Final EIR/EA incorporates responses to public comments received on the Draft EIR/EA and Draft Final RAP.

### Site Record
A joint public comment period for the Draft EIR/EA and Draft Final RAP was held from January 18, 2017, to March 17, 2017. A total of 305 comments were received, with 62 specifically addressing the Draft Final RAP. Comments were received via letter, email, comment cards, and orally at a public hearing.

Responses to comments were prepared by [[caltrans]] for the Draft EIR/EA and by DTSC in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) for the Draft Final RAP. [[caltrans]] agreed with and incorporated DTSC's responses into the Final EIR/EA. The comments and responses are organized by agency type and format of submission. A compact disk (CD) containing all comments and responses, including those specific to the Draft Final RAP, was included with the Response to Comments letter dated March 13, 2018.

The Final EIR/EA is available online via [[caltrans]]'s project website and at physical locations including the DTSC office in Sacramento, the [[caltrans]] District 10 Office in Stockton, the Stanislaus County Library in Modesto, and the StanCOG Office in Modesto.

### Regulatory Status
The Final EIR/EA serves as the final environmental review document for the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project and the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation. It incorporates the [[final-remedial-action-plan]] and its approval by the relevant agencies.

### Open Items
The Response to Comments letter dated March 13, 2018, indicates that a compact disk (CD) containing all comments and responses, including those specific to the Draft Final RAP, was enclosed. This CD is a deliverable associated with the Final EIR/EA process.
