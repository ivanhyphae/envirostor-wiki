Scott Nelson served as the Caltrans Contract Manager for two interagency agreements with the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) related to the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project.

## Role in Project

Nelson's role as Caltrans Contract Manager involved certifying that Task Orders and their attachments complied with the provisions of the overarching interagency agreements and were necessary for the satisfactory completion of contracted work. He also certified that sufficient funding had been encumbered to pay for the work.

## Documented Actions

Nelson signed Task Order Number 6 under Agreement No. 43A0260, which had a term from June 22, 2012, through May 31, 2013, with a total amount of \$ 150,000.00. {{quote: "Scott Nelson, Caltrans Contract Manager"}} {{quote: "Date: 6/21/12"}} {{quote: "Caltrans Contract Manager: Scott Nelson"}} {{quote: "Term of Task Order: June 22, 2012 through May 31, 2013"}} {{quote: "Total Task Order Amount: \$ 150,000.00"}}

He also signed Task Order Number 4 under Agreement No. 43A0310, which had a term from June 1, 2013, through May 31, 2016, with a total amount of \$ 172,000.00. {{quote: "Scott Nelson, Caltrans Contract Manager"}} {{quote: "Scott Nelson, Caltrans Contract Manager"}} {{quote: "Term of Task Order: June 1, 2013 through May 31, 2016"}} {{quote: "Total Task Order Amount: \$ 172,000.00"}}

In both instances, Nelson certified that the Task Orders were within the scope of the project and necessary for its successful completion. {{quote: "I certify that this Task Order and any attachments are within the scope of the project and are necessary for the successful completion of the project."}} {{quote: "I certify that this Task Order No. 6/ DTSC #08-T3616 and attachments comply with the provisions of Agreement No. 43A0260 and are necessary for the satisfactory completion of the product(s) for which the Department has contracted, and that sufficient funding has been encumbered to pay for this work."}} {{quote: "I certify that this Task Order No. 4/ DTSC #12-T0106 and attachments comply with the provisions of Agreement No. 43A0310 and are necessary for the satisfactory completion of the product(s) for which the Department has contracted, and that sufficient funding has been encumbered to pay for this work."}}
