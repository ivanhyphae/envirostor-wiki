## Scott Nelson

Scott Nelson served as the Caltrans Contract Manager for two interagency agreements with the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) related to the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. In this capacity, he was responsible for certifying that task orders and their attachments complied with the overarching interagency agreements and were necessary for the satisfactory completion of contracted work. He also certified that sufficient funding had been encumbered to cover the costs.

## Documented Actions

Nelson signed Task Order Number 6 under Agreement No. 43A0260. This task order had a term from {{quote: "June 22, 2012 through May 31, 2013"}} {{quote: "June 22, 2012 through May 31, 2013"}} and a total amount of {{quote: "\$ 150,000.00"}} {{quote: "\$ 150,000.00"}}. He certified that this task order {{quote: "No. 6/ DTSC #08-T3616 and attachments comply with the provisions of Agreement No. 43A0260 and are necessary for the satisfactory completion of the product(s) for which the Department has contracted, and that sufficient funding has been encumbered to pay for this work."}} {{quote: "No. 6/ DTSC #08-T3616 and attachments comply with the provisions of Agreement No. 43A0260 and are necessary for the satisfactory completion of the product(s) for which the Department has contracted, and that sufficient funding has been encumbered to pay for this work."}}. The execution date for this task order was {{quote: "June 22, 2012"}} {{quote: "June 22, 2012"}}.

Nelson also signed Task Order Number 4 under Agreement No. 43A0310. This task order had a term from {{quote: "June 1, 2013 through May 31, 2016"}} {{quote: "June 1, 2013 through May 31, 2016"}} and a total amount of {{quote: "\$ 172,000.00"}} {{quote: "\$ 172,000.00"}}.
