## Rick Deml

Rick Deml is an individual referenced in project communications concerning the remediation of soil stockpiles at the SR 132, Stanislaus County, California site. His role appears to be within the approval process for sampling plans submitted to the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC).

### Site Record

On an unspecified date, John Juhrend of Geocon Consultants, Inc. sent an email to Richard Stewart that included an appended clean fill sampling plan for the Carpenter Road Shoofly. Juhrend requested that Rick Deml forward this sampling plan to the DTSC for approval. The email stated, "Please forward the sampling plan to DTSC for approval."

### Regulatory Status

The communication indicates that DTSC approval was a necessary step before sampling could commence. The email noted, "Once we receive the approval we will complete the sampling."
