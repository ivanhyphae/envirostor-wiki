## Rick Deml

Rick Deml is an individual referenced in project communications regarding the remediation of soil stockpiles at the SR 132, Stanislaus County, California site. His involvement appears to be related to the approval process for sampling plans.

### Site Record

Rick Deml received an email from John Juhrend of Geocon Consultants, Inc. on an unspecified date, which contained an appended clean fill sampling plan for the Carpenter Road Shoofly. {{quote: "Good afternoon Richard – I have appended the clean fill sampling plan for the Carpenter Road Shoofly per details provided by WSP below."}} Juhrend requested that Deml forward the sampling plan to the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) for approval. {{quote: "Please forward the sampling plan to DTSC for approval."}}

### Regulatory Status

The email indicates that DTSC approval was required for the clean fill sampling plan. {{quote: "Once we receive the approval we will complete the sampling."}}
