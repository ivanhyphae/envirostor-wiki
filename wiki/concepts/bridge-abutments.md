## Bridge Abutments

Bridge abutments are a component of the selected cleanup alternative for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]]. They will be used to provide containment for the soil stockpiles during the construction of the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]].

## Overview

Bridge abutments are part of the containment strategy for the soil stockpiles located south of [[kansas-avenue]]. This strategy, outlined in the [[remedial-action-plan]], involves encasing the stockpiles within the infrastructure of the new freeway.

## Site Record

The soil stockpiles will be contained behind [[retaining-walls]], bridge abutments, and beneath the roadway. {{quote: "The stockpiles will be contained behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath the roadway."}} Unpaved portions of the stockpiles will be covered with clean fill. {{quote: "Unpaved portions will have clean fill cover."}} This containment method is designed to prevent exposure and minimize surface water infiltration into groundwater. {{quote: "This containment remedy provides long-term protection of people and the environment by preventing exposure and minimizing the infiltration of surface water into groundwater under the stockpiles."}}

## Regulatory Status

The [[remedial-action-plan]] for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles was approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). {{quote: "DEPARTMENT OF TOXIC SUBSTANCES CONTROL APPROVES REMEDIAL ACTION PLAN FOR CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES, STATE ROUTE 132 WEST FREEWAY/EXPRESSWAY"}} The DTSC determined that the containment remedy, including the use of bridge abutments, will not have a significant environmental impact when implemented with project controls. {{quote: "DTSC finds that Caltrans' Final EIR/EA contains all the necessary mitigation measures to maintain a less than significant impact."}}
