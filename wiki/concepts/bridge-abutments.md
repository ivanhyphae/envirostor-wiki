## Overview
Bridge abutments are a component of the containment strategy for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. They are part of the infrastructure used to contain soil stockpiles containing metals, primarily [[barium]], [[strontium]], and [[lead]]. This containment is designed to prevent exposure and minimize surface water infiltration into groundwater.

## Site record
The [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] will be contained behind retaining walls, [[bridge-abutments]], and beneath the roadway. {{quote: "The stockpiles will be contained behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath the roadway."}} Unpaved portions will be covered with clean fill. {{quote: "Unpaved portions will have clean fill cover."}} This containment remedy is part of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]]. {{quote: "The selected cleanup alternative in the RAP includes containment of the stockpiles during construction of the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project."}}

## Regulatory status
The containment remedy, which includes the use of [[bridge-abutments]], provides long-term protection by preventing exposure and minimizing surface water infiltration. {{quote: "This containment remedy provides long-term protection of people and the environment by preventing exposure and minimizing the infiltration of surface water into groundwater under the stockpiles."}} Land use controls and annual inspections of the pavement and other containment features, along with groundwater monitoring, will be conducted to ensure the integrity of the remedy. {{quote: "Land use controls and inspection of the pavement and other features of the containment remedy and groundwater monitoring will be conducted annually to ensure the integrity of the remedy into the future."}} The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] determined that the selected containment remedy will not have a significant effect on the environment with the implementation of project controls. {{quote: "DTSC finds that Caltrans' Final EIR/EA contains all the necessary mitigation measures to maintain a less than significant impact."}}
