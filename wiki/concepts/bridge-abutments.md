## Overview
Bridge abutments are a component of the containment strategy for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. They are part of the infrastructure used to contain soil stockpiles containing metals, primarily [[barium]], [[strontium]], and [[lead]]. This containment is designed to prevent exposure and minimize surface water infiltration into groundwater.

## Site record
The [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] will be contained behind retaining walls, [[bridge-abutments]], and beneath the roadway. Unpaved portions will be covered with clean fill. This containment remedy is part of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]].

## Regulatory status
The containment remedy, which includes the use of [[bridge-abutments]], provides long-term protection by preventing exposure and minimizing surface water infiltration. Land use controls and annual inspections of the pavement and other containment features, along with groundwater monitoring, will be conducted to ensure the integrity of the remedy. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] determined that the selected containment remedy will not have a significant effect on the environment with the implementation of project controls.
