## Decommission Groundwater Monitoring Wells

### Overview

The decommissioning of groundwater monitoring wells at the SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA site is a necessary step in the remediation process, particularly following the determination that there is [[no-impact-to-groundwater]] from the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]]. This action signifies the conclusion of the groundwater monitoring phase for specific wells, allowing for the cessation of sampling and reporting requirements associated with those locations.

### Site Record

The decision to decommission specific groundwater monitoring wells was made as part of the overall project progression. The approval of the Groundwater Statistical Evaluation, documented in Case File 900259, provided the basis for discontinuing groundwater monitoring. This evaluation concluded that the groundwater beneath the site was not impacted by the [[barium-and-lead-impacted-soil]] or the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]]. Consequently, the need for ongoing monitoring at certain wells was eliminated. The specific wells designated for decommissioning are identified within the project's regulatory correspondence and work plans.

### Regulatory Status

The decommissioning of groundwater monitoring wells is contingent upon regulatory approval, typically following a demonstration of no significant impact to groundwater resources. The approval of the Groundwater Statistical Evaluation (Case File 900259) by the relevant regulatory agency served as the trigger for this action, indicating that the project had met the criteria for discontinuing monitoring at the affected wells.
