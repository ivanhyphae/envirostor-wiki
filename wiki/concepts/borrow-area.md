## Borrow Area

The term "Borrow Area" in the context of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation refers to locations identified as sources for clean cover fill material. This material is required to cap consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) within Stockpile-1 and Stockpile-2 as part of the State Route (SR) 132 West Project. The selection and approval of these borrow areas are governed by the Remedial-Design-Implementation-Plan-RDIP and require variance approvals from the DTSC. Geocon Consultants Inc. is responsible for evaluating and sampling these potential sources.

## Site Record

The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) initially identified currently vacant and agricultural land within planned excavation areas along the SR-132 Right-of-Way (ROW) between Carpenter Road and Rosemore Avenue as the proposed source of clean imported fill soil.

The highway contractor, Bay Cities Paving and Grading, initially identified an area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road within the SR 132 ROW as a clean fill source. Geocon Consultants Inc. evaluated this area and, in a February 27, 2020 Technical Memorandum, determined that the planned highway underpass excavation area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road would generate suitable soil material. The DTSC subsequently approved this planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area.

An additional source of clean fill material was identified by the highway contractor at proposed mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations, approximately five feet deep, located along the southern boundaries of Stockpile-1 and Stockpile-2. Geocon Consultants Inc. evaluated the MSE wall footing material along the southern boundary of Stockpile-1 following excavation of the southern slope BCS material. In a April 6, 2020 Technical Memorandum, Geocon Consultants Inc. coordinated with [[caltrans]] and the DTSC to determine that the upper foot of planned wall footing excavation soil contained elevated barium concentrations, rendering it unsuitable for use as clean cover fill.

## Regulatory Status

The use of borrow areas for clean cover fill material is subject to the requirements outlined in the Remedial-Design-Implementation-Plan-RDIP and requires RDIP-Variance-Approval from the DTSC.
