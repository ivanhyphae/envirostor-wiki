The "Borrow Area" refers to locations identified as sources for clean cover fill material required for the remediation of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. This clean fill is essential for capping consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) within Stockpile-1 and Stockpile-2 as part of the State Route (SR) 132 West Project. The identification, evaluation, and approval of these borrow areas are critical steps in the remediation process, involving entities such as [[caltrans]], [[dtsc]], and [[geocon-consultants]].

## Borrow Area Identification and Evaluation

Initially, the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) proposed using vacant and agricultural land within planned excavation areas along the SR 132 Right-of-Way (ROW) between Carpenter Road and Rosemore Avenue as the source for clean imported fill soil.

The highway contractor, Bay Cities Paving and Grading, identified an area adjacent to and east of Carpenter Road within the SR 132 ROW as a potential clean fill source. [[geocon-consultants]] evaluated this area and, in a February 27, 2020 Technical Memorandum, concluded that soil generated from the planned highway underpass excavation adjacent to and east of Carpenter Road would be suitable. The [[dtsc]] subsequently approved this area as a clean fill source.

A second potential source of clean fill material was identified by the contractor at proposed mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations, approximately five feet deep, located along the southern boundaries of Stockpile-1 and Stockpile-2. [[geocon-consultants]] evaluated the MSE wall footing material along the southern boundary of Stockpile-1. In an April 6, 2020 Technical Memorandum, [[geocon-consultants]] coordinated with [[caltrans]] and the [[dtsc]] to determine that the upper foot of planned wall footing excavation soil contained elevated barium concentrations, rendering it unsuitable for use as clean cover fill.

## Basin 5 Borrow Area Approval

The [[dtsc]] and the Water Board reviewed a Revised Technical Memorandum dated July 2, 2020, which detailed the soil characterization for the [[basin-5]] clean cap borrow area intended for capping the SR 132 stockpiles. Concerns regarding chromium concentrations in the Basin 5 area were addressed with additional information provided by [[caltrans]] on June 29, 2020, and summarized in the revised memo. The agencies agreed that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and suitable for use as clean cover material for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2. This email, dated July 9, 2020, served as the [[dtsc]]'s acceptance of the Revised Memo.

Prior to this, on June 23, 2020, [[caltrans]] had submitted analytical data from the Basin 5 borrow area to the [[dtsc]] and Water Board for review and approval per the RDIP. The [[dtsc]] had initially expressed concerns on June 23, 2020, regarding chromium results, requesting a revised technical memo with rationale for why these concentrations were acceptable, especially for capping material. They also suggested conducting DIWET (dewatering infiltration and wastewater extraction testing) analysis for total and hexavalent chromium leachability.

## Sampling Plan Approval

On February 13, 2020, the [[dtsc]] approved the borrow area sampling plan, with the only request being to composite 3 samples instead of 4. This followed an email from [[caltrans]] on February 4, 2020, submitting the proposed boring locations, sample preparation, and analyte list for characterization of the soil proposed for use as clean fill, as prepared by [[geocon-consultants]]. The contractor planned to excavate the upper five feet of soil along the SR 132 alignment from [[carpenter-road-area]] extending east for use as BCS stockpile clean fill cover. [[geocon-consultants]] planned to perform 12 five-foot-deep borings in the clean import fill source area and analyze six depth-discrete 4-point composite samples for organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) and heavy metals (0-2 feet) and heavy metals (3-5 feet).
