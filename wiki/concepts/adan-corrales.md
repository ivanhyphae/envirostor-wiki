## Adan Corrales

Adan Corrales is associated with Bay-Cities-Paving-and-Grading (BCPG) and has communicated with the [[caltrans]] project team regarding clean cap material testing and approval for the SR 132 project in Modesto.

## Site Record

On February 25, 2021, Adan Corrales, identified as a Project Intern for BAY CITIES PAVING & GRADING, sent an email to the WSP team, attaching plans that outlined an area for clean cap test locations for the Modesto 132/99 project.

On March 1, 2021, Scott Frenette of WSP responded to Adan Corrales. Frenette noted that material from Retaining Wall No. 1110 had already been approved for use as clean cap material. However, he stated that the remaining material under the Carpenter Shoo-fly would require testing and approval by the Department-of-Toxic-Substances-Control (DTSC) before it could be used. Frenette requested specific information from Corrales to assist in developing a sampling plan for the Carpenter Shoo-fly material, including:
*   Depth of excavation
*   Total fill volume
*   Verification of USA clearance or the need for USA marking/utility conflict identification
*   BCPG's planned excavation timeline

## Regulatory Status

The Department-of-Toxic-Substances-Control (DTSC) is the regulatory agency responsible for approving sampling plans for clean cap material intended for use from the Carpenter Shoo-fly.
