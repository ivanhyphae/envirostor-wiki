## Adan Corrales

Adan Corrales is associated with Bay-Cities-Paving-and-Grading (BCPG) and has been involved in communications regarding clean cap material testing and approval for the SR 132 project in Modesto.

## Site Record

Adan Corrales, as Project Intern for BAY CITIES PAVING & GRADING, communicated with the WSP team on February 25, 2021, regarding clean cap test locations for the Modesto 132/99 project. {{quote: "WSP Team, Please see attached plans for the outlined area for clean cap test locations. Thank you, Adan Corrales Project Intern BAY CITIES PAVING & GRADING"}}

On March 1, 2021, Scott Frenette of WSP responded to Adan Corrales, stating that material from Retaining Wall No. 1110 had already been approved for use as clean cap material. However, the remaining material under the Carpenter Shoo-fly would require testing and approval by the Department-of-Toxic-Substances-Control (DTSC) prior to use. Frenette requested specific information from Corrales to facilitate the development of a sampling plan for the Carpenter Shoo-fly material, including:
*   Depth of excavation
*   Total fill volume
*   Verification of USA clearance or need for USA marking/utility conflict identification
*   BCPG's planned excavation timeline {{quote: "Adan, The material from Retaining Wall No. 1110 has already been approved for use as clean cap material. However, the remaining material under the Carpenter Shoo-fly will need to be tested and approved prior to use as clan cap material. We need to provide a sample plan to DTSC for approval prior to testing the Carpenter Shoo-fly material. Please provide the following: - 1. Depth of excavation - 2. Total fill volume - 3. Verify USA cleared or need to mark USA/any known utility conflicts - 4. When BCPG plans on excavating the material"}}

## Regulatory Status

The Department-of-Toxic-Substances-Control (DTSC) is the agency responsible for approving sampling plans for clean cap material from the Carpenter Shoo-fly. {{quote: "We need to provide a sample plan to DTSC for approval prior to testing the Carpenter Shoo-fly material."}}
