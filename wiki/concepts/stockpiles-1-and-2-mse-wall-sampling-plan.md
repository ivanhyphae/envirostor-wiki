The Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan is a deliverable associated with the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) – Interim Phase 1, for the State Route 132 (SR 132) project in Stanislaus County, California. This plan outlines sampling requirements for a mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) retaining wall located near Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2, which contain barium-containing soil (BCS). The plan was developed by Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Project No. S1200-01-01).

## Site record

The sampling plan is depicted on a grading plan map titled "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles," dated January 2019 {{quote: "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, dated January 2019"}}. This map identifies Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 as large barium-containing soil stockpiles situated along the roadway corridor and bisected by Emerald Avenue {{quote: "Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2"}}. A proposed sampling location is marked on the plan near the retaining wall/fence line ("RW/Fence Line"), adjacent to the southern slope retaining wall {{quote: "Proposed Sampling Location"}}. The map also indicates the presence of groundwater monitoring wells MW1, MW2, and MW4 in the vicinity of the stockpiles and MSE wall area {{quote: "MW1, MW2, and MW4"}}.

The grading plan designates areas for "Imported Clean Embankment Fill/Structure Backfill Placement" and the "Emerald Ave. Eastern Bridge Abutment Stockpile Removal Area." Additionally, a "Barium-Containing Soil and ADL Consolidation Zone" is labeled on the plan {{quote: "Barium-Containing Soil and ADL Consolidation Zone"}}. The plan includes detailed topographic contours and a 100-foot scale bar {{quote: "100-foot scale bar"}}.

A separate plan sheet focuses on the western bridge abutment area near SR-99 and Kansas Avenue, depicting Stockpile 2 and Stockpile 3, along with monitoring wells MW3, MW5, MW6, MW9, and MW10 {{quote: "MW3, MW5, MW6, MW9, and MW10"}}. The sampling location associated with the MSE wall is shown on the Stockpile 1 and 2 plan sheet, with the proposed sampling point located at the interface between the stockpile area and the retaining wall alignment {{quote: "Proposed Sampling Location"}}.

The plan is documented in grading plan figures dated January 2019 {{quote: "dated January 2019"}}, submitted at the 65% design level and marked "Not for Construction" as of March 5, 2018 {{quote: "65% SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION 03-05-18"}}.
