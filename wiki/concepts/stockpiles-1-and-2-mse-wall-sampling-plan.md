The Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan is the Geocon Consultants, Inc. proposal (Project No. S1200-01-01) governing soil sampling in the footprint where a mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall was to be built through the areas formerly occupied by Barium-Containing Soil Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 along SR 132. Its purpose was to characterize soil remaining beneath and adjacent to the stockpile footprints before construction of the retaining wall, tying stockpile removal to the geotechnical work needed for the roadway embankment and southern slope retaining wall.

The grading plan sheet (65% Submittal, "Not for Construction," dated 03-05-18, with a later revision dated January 2019) shows a proposed sampling location near the retaining wall/fence line at the southern slope retaining wall associated with Stockpile 1, and identifies Stockpile 1 and 2 as areas requiring sampling in connection with the BCS Removal Sampling Plan. The Figure 3-2 grading plan (January 2019), part of the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, maps Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 as separate Barium-Containing Soil areas straddling Emerald Ave., with groundwater monitoring wells MW1, MW2, and MW4 in the vicinity, and designates a "Barium-Containing Soil and ADL Consolidation Zone" alongside zones for imported clean embankment fill and structure backfill placement and the Emerald Ave. eastern bridge abutment stockpile removal area. A separate Interim Phase 1 grading plan sheet, also dated January 2019, shows the same consolidation-zone and existing-stockpile designations extending to Stockpile 2 and Stockpile 3 near SR-99 and Kansas Ave., with monitoring wells MW3, MW5, MW6, MW9, and MW10 shown and the western bridge abutment stockpile removal area identified near SR-132 Post Mile 1148+30.

The available material consists of grading plan sheets rather than a narrative sampling report: it establishes the spatial relationship between the two stockpiles, the proposed sampling location at the southern slope retaining wall, the consolidation zone for barium-containing soil and ADL, and the clean fill/backfill placement areas needed for MSE wall construction, but it does not give sample results, analytes reported, or specific concentrations for this sampling plan.
