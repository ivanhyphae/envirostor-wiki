The Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan is a deliverable associated with the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) – Interim Phase 1. This plan details the sampling strategy for a mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) retaining wall near [[stockpile-1]] and [[stockpile-1]], which contain barium-containing soil (BCS). The plan was developed by [[geocon-consultants]].

## Overview

The Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan outlines the collection of soil samples from the footing excavations of an MSE wall. The purpose of this sampling is for characterization and classification by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) to determine the suitability of the excavated soils for reuse on-site. The sampling is specifically tied to the removal of BCS from the Stockpile 1 southern slope zone.

## Site record

The sampling plan targets ten locations spaced approximately 200 feet apart within the MSE wall footing excavations. Discrete soil samples will be collected from one-foot intervals to the planned footing excavation depth of approximately 4 to 5 feet.

Each discrete sample will be analyzed for barium and lead with a 48-hour turnaround time. The analytical data will be compared against specific screening levels to assess the suitability for reuse as clean capping material, abutment/embankment fill, or placement in BCS containment zones.

The screening levels for barium are:
*   Maximum site-specific background: 120 mg/kg
*   RDIP BCS removal threshold (groundwater protection): 1,000 mg/kg
*   Construction worker ESL: 3,000 mg/kg
*   Residential ESL: 15,000 mg/kg
*   Commercial ESL: 220,000 mg/kg

The screening levels for lead are:
*   Maximum site-specific background: 3.8 mg/kg
*   DTSC residential: 80 mg/kg

The plan was submitted for approval on March 31, 2020.

## Regulatory status

The Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan was reviewed by the DTSC and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. On April 2, 2020, [[dean-wright]] of DTSC communicated that both agencies had reviewed the plan and had no comments, serving as acceptance of the plan. The sampling plan is part of the Remedial Design Implementation Plan – Interim Phase 1.

## Open items

The initial submission of the sampling plan was at the 65% design level and marked "Not for Construction" as of March 5, 2018. While the plan was accepted by DTSC and the Water Board on April 2, 2020, the provided sources do not confirm if the sampling has been performed, if analytical results have been generated, or if the data has been used to make reuse decisions for the excavated soils.
