## FMC Operations

### Overview

FMC Operations refers to activities associated with the former [[fmc-chemical-processing-facility]] that are relevant to the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] remediation case. Specifically, this concept relates to the origin and characteristics of soil stockpiled by Caltrans, which may have been impacted by historical operations at the FMC site.

### Site Record

The [[draft-final-remedial-action-plan]] for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles (dated March 18, 2019) references FMC operations in the context of the source of the soil stockpiles. The plan indicates that the soil stockpiles were generated from the FMC Chemical Processing Facility. The document does not provide specific concentrations, dates, or volumes directly attributed to "FMC Operations" as a distinct entity within the site record, but rather uses it to contextualize the origin of the stockpiled material.

### Regulatory Status

The regulatory status of FMC Operations is not directly addressed in the provided source material. The focus is on the remediation of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, which may have originated from or been influenced by FMC's historical activities. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) is the lead regulatory agency overseeing the remediation of the soil stockpiles.

### Open Items

No specific open items are documented for "FMC Operations" within the provided source material. The open items section of the [[draft-final-remedial-action-plan]] pertains to the remediation of the soil stockpiles themselves.
