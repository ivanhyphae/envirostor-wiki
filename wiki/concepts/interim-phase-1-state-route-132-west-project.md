The [[interim-phase-1-state-route-132-west-project]] is a component of the larger [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project in Stanislaus County, California. This project involves the management of two barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles, which are covered by a clean cap that utilizes a portion of State Route 132. The operations and maintenance (O&M) of this cap are detailed in a draft O&M plan.

## Site record

The Draft Operation and Maintenance Plan (Draft O&M Plan), dated March 2022, was submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] on behalf of the Department of Transportation. This plan outlines the inspection, maintenance, and repair activities for the portion of State Route 132 serving as the clean cap cover over two barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles. A blank O&M Site Inspection Form was also included with the Draft O&M Plan.

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), provided comments on the Draft O&M Plan on August 1, 2022. Key comments included:

* A misspelling of "Maintenance" in the Subject line of the Draft O&M Plan.
* A recommendation to include a minimum number of days for advance notification to DTSC of any BCS disturbance, suggesting "10 working days advance notice to ensure prior written authorization from DTSC is provided before the planned disturbance begins."
* A recommendation to add text to Section 2.4 to indicate that Health and Safety Code (HSC) 25123.3(b)(4)(A) requirements will be met, noting that the minimum thickness of 6 millimeters provided in the section meets HSC requirements but that the HSC uses mils, not millimeters. The comment also noted a misspelling of "millimeter."
* A recommendation to revise Section 3.1 to indicate that inspections will occur annually and also after events such as fires, substantial rainfall (1 inch in 24 hours), significant seismic events, or traffic accidents that may cause damage to the cap.
* A recommendation that periodic patrols referenced in Section 2.7 should also note if unauthorized access, such as a homeless encampment, has resulted in any impact to the cap, particularly on the north vegetated slope.
* A recommendation to expand the O&M Site Inspection Form.

The site code for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles is 900259.

## Regulatory status

The Draft O&M Plan is being reviewed by the DTSC in consultation with the RWQCB. The plan will be included in the [[remedial-action-completion-report]] as Appendix G.

## Open items

The DTSC provided comments on the Draft O&M Plan, indicating areas that require revision before it can be finalized. These include addressing spelling errors, clarifying notification procedures for BCS disturbance, ensuring compliance with HSC requirements for cap thickness, expanding inspection triggers, and documenting impacts from unauthorized access.
