The Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Site is a hazardous waste remediation matter arising from soil stockpiles associated with the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway project in Stanislaus County, California. Caltrans, as the responsible party, and the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), as the oversight regulator, have managed the site through a series of consultant agreement task orders and a remedial action planning process that began by at least 2012 and remained active through at least the January 2017 update to the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP). The site's regulatory history is documented primarily through interagency task orders, a draft interim Remedial Action Completion Report (RACR), and a Draft Final RAP with an implementation schedule that was revised at least once.

## Oversight structure and task orders

DTSC's oversight of Caltrans' remedial work at the site has been funded and organized through task orders issued under a standing consultant agreement between Caltrans and DTSC. Task Order 6, executed June 22, 2012 under Agreement 43A0260 (DTSC #08-T3616), ran from June 22, 2012 through May 31, 2013, with a total value of $150,000, all in FY 11/12. Randy Adams served as DTSC Task Order Manager, Richard Stewart as Caltrans Task Manager, and Scott Nelson as Caltrans Contract Manager.

A follow-on task order (Task Order 4, unnumbered field in the source but distinct from Task Order 6) was executed June 1, 2013 under a new Agreement 43A0310 (DTSC #12-T0106), running June 1, 2013 through May 31, 2016, for a total of $172,000, split across FY 13/14 ($98,312.00), FY 14/15 ($39,871.00), and FY 15/16 ($33,774.00). Steven Becker replaced Randy Adams as DTSC Task Order Manager; Richard Stewart and Scott Nelson continued in their respective Caltrans roles. The multi-year term and phased funding indicate the site's remediation work was expected to extend well past the original 2012–2013 task order window, consistent with the RAP process not reaching a public review stage until 2017.

## Draft Final RAP and implementation schedule

The Draft Final Remedial Action Plan for the site, dated October 2014, included an Implementation Schedule at Section 8 (page 47). On January 10, 2017, Richard Stewart, then Engineering Geologist and Acting Branch Chief of Caltrans District 6's Central Region Hazardous Waste Branch, wrote to DTSC Senior Engineering Geologist Randy S. Adams to update that schedule. The revised schedule set public notice of the Draft Final RAP and the SR-132 Project EIR/EA for a 45-day public review beginning January 18, 2017; a public meeting on February 22, 2017; a DTSC responsiveness summary in spring 2017; and revision of the Draft Final RAP as needed with DTSC approval of the Final RAP in spring 2017. All other dates in the original October 2014 Implementation Schedule were stated to remain unchanged. The letter does not indicate whether the Final RAP was in fact approved on this revised schedule.

## Change in DTSC project manager

DTSC's project manager assignment for the site changed again after the RAP process described above. On December 11, 2024, Dean Wright, P.G., DTSC Project Manager for the Site Mitigation and Restoration Program in Sacramento, notified Caltrans (Adam Inman) that the DTSC Project Manager role for the site had passed to Arielle Mcleskey of DTSC's Sacramento office, Environmental Scientist in the Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program. The notice instructed that further correspondence be directed to Mcleskey. This administrative change, occurring roughly a decade after the 2014 Draft Final RAP and the 2017 schedule update, indicates the site's regulatory oversight remained open into late 2024, with multiple DTSC project managers (Randy Adams, Steven Becker, Dean Wright, and Arielle Mcleskey) having held responsibility for it over the life of the case.

## Significance for the case file

The record shows a remediation effort spanning at least 2012 through late 2024 under successive Caltrans-DTSC task orders and personnel, with a Draft Final RAP dated October 2014 whose public review and DTSC approval were rescheduled into 2017. The available material does not confirm that the Final RAP was approved or that remedial construction was completed; the most recent document in the file is an administrative point-of-contact change, not a substantive closure or approval record.
