# Rdip

## Overview
Rdip appears as a label in the legend and annotations on grading plan figures for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remedial Design Implementation Plan – Interim Phase 1, prepared by geocon-consultants. The figure legends identify "Rdip" among mapping symbols, though the specific feature or measurement it represents is not explicitly defined in the available source narrative. The designation is documented on civil engineering drawings dated January 2019, associated with the site layout along SR 132 and SR 99 in Stanislaus County, California.

## Site record
The source material consists of three grading plan figures (Figures 3-2 and associated sheets) from the remedial design implementation plan, dated January 2019 and marked as a 65% submittal ("NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION 03-05-18"). In these drawings, "Rdip" appears as a symbol entry in the figure legend. No measurement values, coordinate references, monitoring data, or descriptive text within the narrative portions of the sampling plan explicitly define or quantify Rdip.
