# SR 132 99 Interchange

The SR 132 99 Interchange is a portion of the larger [[state-route-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]] in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. This interchange project involves the remediation and containment of soil stockpiles containing elevated levels of [[barium]] and, to a lesser extent, lead. The remedial strategy involves incorporating these stockpiles as highway embankment fill, with containment achieved through structural pavement sections, imported clean-fill-capping|clean fill, and retaining walls. The project is being managed by [[caltrans]] with oversight from the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and in cooperation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (CVRWQCB).

## Site Record

The soil stockpiles, identified as containing elevated levels of barium and lead, are located south of the SR 99/Kansas Avenue interchange. The remedial action plan, approved by DTSC on March 14, 2018, designates containment of these stockpiles by incorporating them as highway embankment fill for the SR-132/SR-99 interchange.

The project is being constructed in two phases:
*   **Interim Phase 1:** Planned for completion by 2020. This phase includes the construction of the SR-132 West/6th Street and SR-132/East/5th Street extensions, two traffic lanes from east of SR-99 to North Dakota Avenue, the Carpenter Road interchange, and roadway structures across Emerald Avenue and SR-99. During Interim Phase 1, Stockpiles 1 and 2 will be incorporated as embankment material. The northern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2, which will not be contained by retaining walls or pavement during this phase, will be contour graded for drainage and covered with a minimum 12-inch-thick vegetated, clean-soil-cover|clean soil cap. A portion of Stockpile 3 was initially planned for encapsulation but due to design changes, the entire Stockpile 3 will be removed and placed as highway embankment fill over Stockpile 1 and within adjacent State ROW between Stockpile 1 and Emerald Avenue.
*   **Ultimate Phase 2:** Planned for completion by 2028. This phase includes widening the highway to four lanes, constructing structures for the widening, and the SR-99/SR-132 interchange. During Ultimate Phase 2, retaining walls will be constructed along the northern Right-of-Way boundary of Stockpiles 1 and 2, completing the containment of the barium-containing soil.

The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) - Interim Phase 1 was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. and submitted on January 30, 2019.

## Regulatory Status

The DTSC is the lead regulatory agency overseeing site mitigation. The remedial alternative selected for the barium-containing soil stockpiles is containment, as approved by DTSC on March 14, 2018.

## Open Items

An additional RDIP will be prepared for the Ultimate Phase 2 portion of the project, planned for completion in 2028.
