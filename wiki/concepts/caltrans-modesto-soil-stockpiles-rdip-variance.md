The Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles RDIP Variance is a document prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). It addresses the need for clean cover fill materials to cap consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) within Stockpiles 1 and 2 as part of the State Route (SR) 132 West Project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California.

### Clean Fill Material Sources

The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, dated January 30, 2019, stipulated that the surface of completed Stockpiles 1 and 2, including temporary slopes, would be capped with a minimum 12-inch-thick layer of clean fill material. This material could consist of approved imported fill and/or a minimum 4-inch-thick layer of aggregate base (AB) materials, pending the placement of structural pavement, hardscape, or additional clean embankment fill. The proposed initial source for this clean imported fill soil was identified as currently vacant and agricultural land within planned excavation areas along the SR-132 right-of-way between the Carpenter Road and Rosemore Avenue overcrossings. Specific clean fill source areas, once defined by the highway construction contractor, were to be sampled by Geocon for laboratory analysis in accordance with DTSC's *Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Material* and pre-approved by DTSC prior to placement.

The highway contractor, Bay Cities Paving and Grading, initially identified an area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road within the SR 132 right-of-way as the clean fill material source. Geocon, in coordination with Caltrans and DTSC, evaluated this area and determined that the planned highway underpass excavation area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road would generate soil material suitable for use as clean cover fill. DTSC subsequently approved this planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area.

An additional source of clean fill material was identified by the contractor at the proposed mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations, approximately five feet deep, located along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2. Geocon evaluated the MSE wall footing material along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1 after excavation of the southern slope BCS material. In coordination with Caltrans and DTSC, it was determined that the upper foot of the planned wall footing excavation soil contained elevated barium concentrations, rendering it unsuitable for use as clean cover fill.
