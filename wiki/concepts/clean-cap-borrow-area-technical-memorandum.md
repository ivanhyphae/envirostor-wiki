The [[clean-cap-borrow-area-technical-memorandum]], dated February 27, 2020, was prepared by Geocon-Consultants-Inc. for [[caltrans]] and submitted to the DTSC. This memorandum details the soil characterization results for the proposed clean cap borrow area, intended for use in the remediation of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project.

### Soil Characterization and Approval

The memorandum presented composite soil sample results from the borrow area. The DTSC reviewed these results and, by email on March 4, 2020, agreed that the data indicated the soil was suitable for use as clean cover material. The DTSC determined that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. This approval specifically pertained to the use of this characterized borrow area soil as clean cover for Stockpiles 1 and 2.
