## Clean Cap Borrow Area Technical Memorandum

### Overview
The Clean Cap Borrow Area Technical Memorandum, dated February 27, 2020, was prepared by Geocon-Consultants-Inc. and submitted by [[caltrans]] to the DTSC for review and approval. The memorandum details the characterization of soil intended for use as a clean cap over the State Route 132 stockpiles. The DTSC reviewed the memorandum and agreed that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations, deeming the material suitable for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2.

### Site Record
The Technical Memorandum dated February 27, 2020, presented composite soil sample results for the proposed clean cap borrow area. The DTSC accepted these results via email on March 4, 2020, confirming that the data indicated suitability for use as clean cover material.

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC has approved the use of the characterized borrow area soil as clean cover material for Stockpiles 1 and 2. This approval was communicated on March 4, 2020, based on the findings presented in the February 27, 2020, Technical Memorandum. The results were deemed consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations.
