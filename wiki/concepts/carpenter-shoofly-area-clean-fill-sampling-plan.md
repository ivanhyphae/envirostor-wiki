The Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan is a document that details the proposed sampling methodology for clean fill material within the Carpenter Shoofly Area of the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. This plan is part of the remediation efforts for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles case file.

The plan outlines a sampling strategy for clean fill material, specifying vertical transect sampling depths at 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet. The associated engineering site plan, titled 'Traffic Handling Plan Stage 2B', illustrates the project layout, including North Carpenter Road and Kansas Avenue, and details construction elements such as barricades and temporary railing. It also indicates excavation parameters of 20 feet depth and 5,000 cubic yards, with a scale of 1 inch equals 50 feet. The plan was developed by Geocon Consultants, Inc.
