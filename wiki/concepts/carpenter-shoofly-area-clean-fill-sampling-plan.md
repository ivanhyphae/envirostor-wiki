## Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan

### Overview
The Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan was developed to characterize the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill area. The sampling and analysis conducted under this plan confirmed that composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria. The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) accepted the technical memorandum detailing these findings.

### Site Record
On April 12, 2021, Geocon Consultants, Inc. submitted a Technical Memorandum to Caltrans detailing the sampling and analysis completed for the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill area. {{quote: "Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated April 12, 2021 describing the sampling and analysis that was completed to characterize the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill area."}} This memo was reviewed by the DTSC and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Water Board). {{quote: "DTSC and the Water Board have reviewed the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated April 12, 2021"}} The agencies agreed that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria. {{quote: "The agencies agree that the composite soil sample results are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and meet the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria."}}

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC provided acceptance of the Technical Memorandum on April 22, 2021. {{quote: "This e-mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the Memo."}} This acceptance signifies that the sampling results met the established clean cap criteria for Barium Containing Soil.
