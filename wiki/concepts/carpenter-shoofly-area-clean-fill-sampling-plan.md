## Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan

### Overview
The Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan was a deliverable developed to characterize the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill area. The sampling and analysis conducted under this plan confirmed that composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the Barium-containing-soil clean cap criteria. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) accepted the technical memorandum detailing these findings.

### Site Record
On April 12, 2021, Geocon Consultants, Inc. submitted a Technical Memorandum to [[caltrans]] detailing the sampling and analysis completed for the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill area. {{quote: "Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated April 12, 2021 describing the sampling and analysis that was completed to characterize the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill area."}} This memo was reviewed by the DTSC and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (Water Board). {{quote: "DTSC and the Water Board have reviewed the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated April 12, 2021"}} The agencies agreed that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria. {{quote: "The agencies agree that the composite soil sample results are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and meet the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria."}}

The sampling plan itself is associated with a site plan titled 'Traffic Handling Plan Stage 2B' for the 'SR 132 Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Source Testing Sampling Plan'. {{quote: "SR 132 Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Source Testing Sampling Plan"}} This plan details construction notes, signage placement, and geometric survey data, including excavation parameters of a 20' depth and 5,000 cubic yards. {{quote: "20' depth, 5,000 cubic yards"}} Technical specifications for soil sampling are listed, detailing vertical transect sampling depths at 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet. {{quote: "2, 5, 10, and 15 feet"}} The plan is associated with Caltrans branding and project identifiers, noting a scale of 1 inch equals 50 feet. {{quote: "1 inch equals 50 feet"}} The DGN file is noted as "...\Plans\a40350md016.dgn". {{quote: "...\Plans\a40350md016.dgn"}}

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC provided acceptance of the Technical Memorandum on April 22, 2021. {{quote: "This e-mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the Memo."}} This acceptance signifies that the sampling results met the established clean cap criteria for Barium Containing Soil.
