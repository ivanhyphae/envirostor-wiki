The [[water-board]] is a regulatory agency involved in the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] remediation case. Specifically, the [[rwqcb]] (a regional branch of the Water Board) reviewed the "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpile Surface Water Sampling report – December 17, 2020" and indicated no comments.

## Site Record

On March 25, 2021, Kyle Cockerham, PG, Site Cleanup Unit for the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region, stated, "I don't have any comment on the Modesto Soil Stockpile Surface Water Sampling report." {{quote: "I don't have any comment on the Modesto Soil Stockpile Surface Water Sampling report."}} This statement was in response to a forwarded surface water report concerning the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project. {{quote: "Just forwarding the attached surface water report on the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project."}}

## Regulatory Status

The [[water-board]], through its Central Valley Region office, reviewed the surface water sampling report dated December 17, 2020. {{quote: "Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region"}} The agency indicated no comments on the report. {{quote: "I don't have any comment on the Modesto Soil Stockpile Surface Water Sampling report."}}
