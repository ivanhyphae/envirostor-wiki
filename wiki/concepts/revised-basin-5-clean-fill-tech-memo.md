The Revised Basin 5 Clean Fill Tech Memo was approved by the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board in an approval e-mail responding to Geocon Consultants' resubmission of the memo addressing use of clean fill material at Basin 5. The approval establishes that the material described in the revised memo may be used as clean fill/cover at Basin 5, closing out the technical review of that specific proposal.

The approval e-mail does not itself restate the analytical data or thresholds underlying Geocon's revised memo; it records the Central Valley RWQCB's acceptance of the revised document as submitted. For a reviewer, the significance is that this e-mail is the operative regulatory sign-off for the clean fill approach at Basin 5 — it is the document that converts Geocon's proposal into an accepted basis for capping/cover work at that basin, rather than a proposal still pending agency concurrence.

The record available here does not include the conditions, if any, attached to the approval, nor does it show whether DTSC separately reviewed or concurred in the same memo. Those points should be confirmed against the underlying Revised Basin 5 Clean Fill Tech Memo and any related DTSC correspondence before relying on this approval as final for all purposes.
