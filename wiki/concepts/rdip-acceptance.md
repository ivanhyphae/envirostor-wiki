# Rdip Acceptance

Rdip Acceptance refers to the two-stage regulatory sign-off, by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), of the Remedial Design Implementation Plan — Interim Phase 1 (RDIP-Interim Phase 1) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles at SR 132. DTSC issued a final approval letter on April 3, 2019, and later accepted a related errata sheet by e-mail on February 5, 2020. Together these establish the regulatory basis on which excavation and re-deposition of the stockpiles for the West Freeway/Expressway Project could proceed.

## Final approval, April 3, 2019

The RDIP-Interim Phase 1, dated January 30, 2019, was submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of Caltrans and described the removal and re-deposition of a portion of the Modesto Soil Stockpiles to support SR 132 construction, including excerpts of the 100% design plans and the safety measures and sampling to be performed during and after the work. DTSC, in its April 3, 2019 letter to Geocon project manager John E. Juhrend, stated that DTSC and the RWQCB had reviewed the RDIP-Interim Phase 1 together with the "City of Modesto Book 2 of 3 100% Special Provisions for The State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Phase 1 Project," which supplies the non-standard specifications binding the selected contractor(s) to the RDIP's requirements.

Approval of the Special Provisions document followed a comment cycle: DTSC provided minor comments on March 26, 2019, and a revised version addressing those comments was received on March 28, 2019. The April 3, 2019 letter states that DTSC and the RWQCB had no additional comments on either document and approved the RDIP-Interim Phase 1. The letter was signed by Dean Wright, PG, Project Manager in DTSC's Site Mitigation and Restoration Program, and copied to Caltrans District 10 (Eric Chin, Acting Project Manager), Caltrans Senior Environmental Planner Juergen Vespermann, RWQCB Central Valley Region's Steven Meeks, and DTSC Senior Hazardous Substances Engineer Perry Myers. This approval is the regulatory instrument that authorized the Interim Phase 1 construction activities described in the RDIP to proceed under the conditions set out in the plan and the Special Provisions.

## Errata acceptance, February 5, 2020

On February 5, 2020, Caltrans (Juergen Vespermann) transmitted an errata sheet and accompanying transmittal letter for the SR 132 Modesto Stockpile RDIP to DTSC (Dean Wright and Perry Myers), copying Richard C. Stewart of Caltrans. The same day, Dean Wright replied confirming that the e-mail served as DTSC's acceptance of the RDIP errata sheet and transmittal letter. The content of the corrections made by the errata is not described in the available material.

This acceptance post-dates the April 2019 final approval by roughly ten months, indicating the approved RDIP required a documented correction after issuance; the record does not indicate whether the RWQCB separately reviewed or concurred with the errata, in contrast to the joint DTSC/RWQCB review recorded for the original April 2019 approval.
