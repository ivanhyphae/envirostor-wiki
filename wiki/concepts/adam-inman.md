Adam Inman is a project manager at [[caltrans]], specifically within the Hazardous Waste and Paleontology unit for District 6. He is the recipient of correspondence from the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) regarding reports submitted for the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles site, located on State Route 132 in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California.

## Site record

Inman, on behalf of Caltrans, submitted a [[well-destruction-completion-report]] and a [[storm-water-and-surface-water-monitoring-report-fy24-25]] dated June 18, 2025, prepared by [[geocon-consultants]], Inc. {{quote: "submission of the Well Destruction Report and of the Storm Water Sampling Report both dated June 18, 2025, and prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of California Department of Transportation (Caltrans)."}} The site, identified by Site Code 900259, consists of three stockpiles containing soil contaminated with barium since the 1960s. {{quote: "The subject property is located south of the intersection of State Route 99 and Kansas Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California and referred to as the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles (Site) also known as the State Route 132 West Expressway by the California Department of Toxic Substance Control (DTSC). The Site consisted of three stockpiles that contain soil contaminated with barium since the 1960s."}}

Ten groundwater monitoring wells were installed in 2012 to monitor for potential contamination. {{quote: "In 2012, ten groundwater monitoring wells were installed to monitor for potential contamination moving to groundwater while the plan for encapsulation of the stockpiles was being prepared."}} Six of these wells were decommissioned in 2019 to facilitate highway construction. {{quote: "The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board, in addition to DTSC, reviewed the groundwater monitoring reports and after years of data supporting no metals were leaching into the groundwater from the stockpiles, agreed to their decommissioning. Six of the ten wells were decommissioned in 2019. This was necessary to continue the construction of the Caltrans highway."}} The Well Destruction Report submitted by Inman outlines the procedures for decommissioning the remaining four wells. {{quote: "The Well Destruction Report outlines the procedures to destroy the remaining four wells."}}

The Storm Water Sampling Report describes results from sampling used to evaluate the integrity of the stockpile containment system during rain events. {{quote: "In addition to groundwater sampling, surface water run-off is also collected to ensure the metals in the soil are not displaced into the surface water. The Storm Water Sampling Report describes the results of storm water sampling used to evaluate the integrity of the stockpile containment system during qualifying rain events."}} The results from this report were within reporting limits. {{quote: "The results of the Storm Water Sampling Report were all within reporting limits."}}

## Regulatory status

On January 30, 2026, DTSC issued a conditional approval for the Well Destruction Report and an approval for the Storm Water and Surface Water Monitoring Report (FY24/25). {{quote: "CONDITIONAL APPROVAL OF THE WELL DESTRUCTION COMPLETION REPORT AND APPROVAL OF THE STORM WATER AND SURFACE WATER MONITORING REPORT (FY24/25), CALTRANS ENCAPSULATED SOIL STOCKPILES, STATE ROUTE 132, MODESTO, STANISLAUS COUNTY, CALIFORNIA (SITE CODE: 900259)"}} The conditional approval of the Well Destruction Report was contingent on the incorporation of a specific comment from DTSC's Geological Services Branch (GSB). {{quote: "DTSC conditionally approves the Well Destruction Report, provided the following comment is incorporated in the final version and provided to DTSC."}} The Storm Water Sampling Report was reviewed by DTSC's GSB and Human and Ecological Risk Office (HERO) and was approved. {{quote: "The Storm Water Sampling Report was reviewed by geologists from our Geological Services Branch (GSB) and toxicologists from our Human and Ecological Risk Office (HERO) and is hereby approved."}}
