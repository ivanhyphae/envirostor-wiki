Adam Inman is a project manager at [[caltrans]], serving as the Caltrans Service Request Manager for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project. This project is located on State Route 132 in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California, and falls under the purview of [[caltrans-district-6]]'s Hazardous Waste and Paleontology unit. Inman is the primary point of contact for correspondence from the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) concerning reports submitted for this site.

## Site record

Inman, acting on behalf of Caltrans, submitted a [[well-destruction-completion-report]] and a [[storm-water-and-surface-water-monitoring-report-fy24-25]], both dated June 18, 2025, and prepared by [[geocon-consultants]], Inc. These submissions relate to Site Code 900259, which encompasses three soil stockpiles contaminated with barium since the 1960s. The site is located south of the intersection of State Route 99 and Kansas Avenue in Modesto.

Ten groundwater monitoring wells were installed in 2012 to monitor for potential contamination. Six of these wells were decommissioned in 2019 to facilitate highway construction. The Well Destruction Report submitted by Inman details the procedures for decommissioning the remaining four wells. The Storm Water Sampling Report evaluates the integrity of the stockpile containment system during rain events by analyzing surface water runoff. The results from this report were within reporting limits.

Inman's role is further defined by Interagency Agreement Service Request (SR-04), executed on August 25, 2025, between Caltrans and DTSC. This agreement, with a term extending to June 30, 2030, covers DTSC's regulatory oversight for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, supporting Phase 2 of the State Route 132 West Expressway construction.

## Regulatory status

On January 30, 2026, DTSC issued a [[conditional-approval-letter]] for the Well Destruction Report and an approval for the Storm Water and Surface Water Monitoring Report (FY24/25). The approval for the Well Destruction Report was conditional upon the incorporation of a specific comment from DTSC's Geological Services Branch (GSB). The Storm Water Sampling Report was reviewed by DTSC's GSB and Human and Ecological Risk Office (HERO) and was approved.

## Open items

As of March 16, 2026, [[arielle-mcleskey]] of DTSC inquired if Inman had submitted the revised Well Destruction Completion Report. A single correction was noted in DTSC's conditional approval letter dated January 30, 2026.
