Adam Inman is a contact person for Caltrans, District 6, involved in the remediation of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. He is a recipient and provider of correspondence related to the site's environmental monitoring and well destruction activities.

## Site Record and Deliverables

Adam Inman received a conditional approval for the Well Destruction Completion Report and approval for the Storm Water and Surface Water Monitoring Report (FY24/25) from the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) on January 30, 2026. These reports, dated June 18, 2025, were prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of Caltrans. The site, located south of the intersection of State Route 99 and Kansas Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California, is also known as the State Route 132 West Expressway. The site historically contained soil contaminated with barium since the 1960s, with remediation efforts including the relocation of soil and the planned construction of a 4-lane highway.

Groundwater monitoring wells were installed in 2012 to assess potential contamination migration. After years of data indicated no metals leaching into the groundwater, the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board and DTSC agreed to the decommissioning of these wells. Six of the ten wells were decommissioned in 2019 to facilitate highway construction. The Well Destruction Report submitted by Caltrans outlined the procedures for decommissioning the remaining four wells.

In addition to groundwater sampling, surface water run-off was monitored to ensure metals were not displaced into surface water. The Storm Water Sampling Report indicated that all results were within reporting limits. DTSC's review of the Storm Water Sampling Report, which included input from their Geological Services Branch (GSB) and Human and Ecological Risk Office (HERO), resulted in its approval. The Well Destruction Report, reviewed by DTSC's GSB, received conditional approval, pending the incorporation of a specific comment into the final version.

Adam Inman was also the recipient of an email from Arielle McLeskey of DTSC on March 16, 2026, inquiring about the revised Well Destruction Completion Report. McLeskey referenced a conditional approval letter dated January 30, 2026, which required one correction, and stated that the revised report had not been received.
