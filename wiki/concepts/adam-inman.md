Adam Inman is the Caltrans District 10 Hazardous Waste Program contact for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles land use covenant, identified in the covenant document (Recorded Document No. 43A0487_04) as the recipient of DTSC's annual inspection reminder correspondence. He appears in the record as the person to whom DTSC directs required annual inspection reporting under the covenant, making him the named point of accountability for that recurring obligation.

The February 2025 email "RE: Reminder - Annual Inspection Report" is addressed to Inman regarding the site's annual inspection report obligation under the land use covenant. The message functions as a compliance reminder tied to the covenant's recurring reporting requirement rather than as a new finding or determination.

The land use covenant itself (43A0487_04, Modesto Stockpiles, signed) lists Inman by name and title as the Caltrans contact of record for the encumbered property, the role through which DTSC correspondence on covenant compliance — including annual inspection reminders — is routed.
