The Draft Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project documents the remediation activities undertaken for barium-containing soils (BCS) at the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. The report was submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of the Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and reviewed by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB). The DTSC accepted the draft report on February 24, 2022.{{quote: "ACCEPTANCE OF THE DRAFT REMOVAL ACTION COMPLETION REPORT – CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES, PHASE 1 INTERIM STATE ROUTE 132 WEST PROJECT, MODESTO, STANISLAUS COUNTY, CALIFORNIA (SITE CODE: 900259)"}}

## Site Description

Prior to remediation, the site contained BCS Stockpiles 1 through 3 within the Caltrans State Route 132 right-of-way.
*   **Stockpile 1:** Located approximately 150 feet west of Emerald Avenue, it was about 600 feet long (east-west), 160 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 34,000 cubic yards (yd3).{{quote: "Stockpile 1 was located approximately 150 feet westerly of Emerald Avenue and was roughly 600 feet long in the east-west direction, 160 feet wide, and had an estimated fill volume of 34,000 cubic yards (yd3)."}}
*   **Stockpile 2:** Situated between Emerald Avenue and State Route 99, it measured approximately 1,650 feet long (east-west) and 160 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 102,000 yd3.{{quote: "BCS Stockpile 2, located between Emerald Avenue and State Route 99, was roughly 1,650 feet long in the east-west direction, 160 feet wide, and had an estimated fill volume of 102,000 yd3."}}
*   **Stockpile 3:** Formerly on the east side of State Route 99, it was approximately 1,100 feet long and 120 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 24,000 yd3.{{quote: "BCS Stockpile 3, formerly located on the east side of State Route 99, was roughly 1,100 feet long, 120 feet wide, and had an estimated fill volume of 24,000 yd3."}}

## Site Remediation Activities – Soil Removal and Consolidation

Remediation activities, conducted in accordance with the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and Interim Phase 1 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP), involved excavating Stockpile 3, the western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2, and portions of the southern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 to facilitate the construction of the new State Route 132 Expressway.

The excavation of BCS Stockpile 3 yielded approximately 27,675 yd3 of soil.{{quote: "The removal of BCS Stockpile 3 included the excavation of approximately 27,675 yd3 of soil"}} This material was transported by truck and used as embankment fill within the BCS Stockpile 1 containment zone. Twenty-nine confirmation soil samples were collected from the base of the Stockpile 3 excavation area and analyzed for barium and lead. Three of these samples exhibited barium concentrations exceeding the cleanup goal of 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg).{{quote: "Twenty-nine confirmation soil samples were collected from the bottom of the BCS Stockpile 3 excavation area and analyzed for barium and lead. Three of the confirmation samples showed barium concentrations above the cleanup goal of 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) for this contaminant."}} These areas were subsequently excavated to an additional depth of approximately one foot and resampled. The final confirmation samples indicated that the remaining soil met the cleanup goals.{{quote: "These three areas were subsequently deepened by approximately one foot and resampled. ## Regulatory Status

The cleanup goal for barium in soil at this site is 1,000 mg/kg.{{quote: "Three of the confirmation samples showed barium concentrations above the cleanup goal of 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) for this contaminant."}} The DTSC, in consultation with the RWQCB, has accepted the Draft Removal Action Completion Report for the interim Phase 1 activities.{{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) has reviewed the Draft Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project (Draft Phase 1 RACR) submitted by Geocon Consultants Inc. (Geocon) on behalf of the Department of Transportation (Caltrans)."}}
