## Overview
The Draft Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project (Draft Phase 1 RACR) documents the remediation activities for barium-containing soils (BCS) at the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. The report was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of Caltrans and reviewed by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB). The DTSC accepted the draft report on February 24, 2022.

## Site Record

### Site Description
Prior to remediation, the site contained BCS Stockpiles 1 through 3 within the Caltrans State Route 132 right-of-way.
*   **Stockpile 1:** Located approximately 150 feet west of Emerald Avenue, it was about 600 feet long (east-west), 160 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 34,000 cubic yards (yd3).
*   **Stockpile 2:** Situated between Emerald Avenue and State Route 99, it measured approximately 1,650 feet long (east-west) and 160 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 102,000 yd3.
*   **Stockpile 3:** Formerly on the east side of State Route 99, it was approximately 1,100 feet long and 120 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 24,000 yd3.

### Site Remediation Activities – Soil Removal and Consolidation
Remediation activities involved excavating Stockpile 3, the western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2, and portions of the southern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 to facilitate the construction of the new State Route 132 Expressway. These activities were conducted in accordance with the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and Interim Phase 1 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP).

The excavation of BCS Stockpile 3 yielded approximately 27,675 yd3 of soil. This material was transported by truck and used as embankment fill within the BCS Stockpile 1 containment zone.

Twenty-nine confirmation soil samples were collected from the base of the Stockpile 3 excavation area and analyzed for barium and lead. Three of these samples exhibited barium concentrations exceeding the cleanup goal. These areas were subsequently excavated to an additional depth of approximately one foot and resampled. The final confirmation samples indicated that the remaining soil met the cleanup goals.

## Regulatory Status
The cleanup goal for barium in soil at this site is 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). The DTSC, in consultation with the RWQCB, has accepted the Draft Removal Action Completion Report for the interim Phase 1 activities. The acceptance date was February 24, 2022.
