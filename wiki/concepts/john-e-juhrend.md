# John E. Juhrend

John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, served as a Project Manager and Senior Engineer for [[geocon-consultants]] on various aspects of the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. His documented involvement spans multiple technical memorandums, sampling plans, and approval processes related to the remediation and management of soil stockpiles along State Route 132 (SR 132) in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California.

## Site Record

Juhrend's professional activities on the project are recorded in several key documents:

*   **Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan:** Juhrend was the Project Manager for Geocon Consultants, Inc. for the "Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan" submitted on June 13, 2012. This plan addressed the excavation of Stockpile 3 for the State Route 99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off-Ramp Project. He signed the transmittal letter to Mr. Richard Stewart, PG, at [[caltrans]] District 6, referencing Contract No. 06A1634 and Work Request EA No. 10-0A671. The plan summarized site investigations, health risks associated with [[barium]] in Stockpile 3, and procedures for air monitoring and excavation verification. {{quote: "STOCKPILE 3 EXCAVATION MONITORING PLAN MODESTO RAMP REHABILITATION PROJECT STATE ROUTE 99 KANSAS AVENUE NORTHBOUND OFF-RAMP MODESTO, CALIFORNIA CONTRACT NO. 06A1634, EA NO. 10-0A671"}} {{quote: "Sincerely, GEOCON CONSULTANTS, INC. John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG Project Manager"}}
*   **Carpenter Road Shoofly Clean Fill Characterization Testing:** Juhrend served as Project Manager for Geocon on a Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021, regarding "Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Characterization Testing" for the State Route 132 Project. This memo detailed the analysis of approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil excavated from the Carpenter Road Shoofly, intended for use as clean capping material over Stockpiles 1 and 2. The testing confirmed that barium concentrations ranged from 28 to 96 mg/kg, and lead concentrations ranged from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, meeting the clean cap criteria established by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). {{quote: "Based on the barium and lead data presented herein, soil material generated from excavation of the Carpenter Road Shoofly meet the BCS clean cap criteria as established in the referenced variance document as approved by DTSC (upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg)."}} {{quote: "John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon"}}
*   **Stockpile 1 Wall Footing Sampling Tech Memo:** Juhrend, as Senior Engineer for Geocon Consultants, Inc., submitted a technical memo on April 6, 2020, to Richard Stewart at Caltrans, requesting approval for the use of Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing excavation soil material within Stockpiles 1 and 2 as embankment fill and/or clean cover material. {{quote: "Good afternoon Richard – please submit the appended technical memo to Dean Wright at DTSC and Steve Meeks at the Water Board for approval of use of Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing excavation soil material within Stockpiles 1 and 2 as embankment fill and/or clean cover material."}} {{quote: "John Juhrend, PE, CEG, CEM | Senior Engineer Geocon Consultants, Inc."}}
*   **SR 132 Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan:** Juhrend, as Senior Engineer for Geocon Consultants, Inc., submitted a sampling plan on June 11, 2020, for Basin 5, requesting expedited approval from DTSC. He noted that the sampling frequency was based on the location of Basin 5 adjacent to a previously tested SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road. {{quote: "Good morning Richard – if acceptable to you please submit the appended sampling plan to DTSC for expedited approval."}} {{quote: "John Juhrend, PE, CEG, CEM | Senior Engineer Geocon Consultants, Inc."}}
*   **Final RDIP Approval:** Juhrend, as Project Manager for Geocon Consultants, Inc., received the final approval of the Remedial Design Implementation Plan — Interim Phase 1 from DTSC on April 3, 2019. The approval letter, signed by [[dean-wright]], PG, Project Manager for DTSC, acknowledged the plan's description of activities for the Interim Phase 1 construction project involving the removal and re-deposition of Modesto Soil Stockpiles to support SR 132 construction. {{quote: "Dear Mr. Juhrend: The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) has reviewed the Remedial Design Implementation Plan — Interim Phase 1 (RDIP-Interim Phase 1) submitted by Geocon Consultants Inc. (Geocon) on behalf of the Department of Transportation (Caltrans)."}} {{quote: "Mr. John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG April 3, 2019 Page 2"}}
*   **Final O and M Agreement:** Juhrend, as Project Manager for Geocon Consultants, Inc., was a signatory on the cover letter submitting the "Final Operations and Maintenance Plan (O&M Plan) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles" dated August 1, 2022. {{quote: "Sincerely, GEOCON CONSULTANTS, INC. John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG Project Manager"}}
*   **Stormwater Sampling Reports:** Juhrend is listed as a Senior Engineer and Project Manager for Geocon Consultants, Inc. on the "2.1.2024 Stormwater Sampling Report" (dated May 2024) and the "2.4.2025 Stormwater Sampling Report" (dated June 2025). These reports document stormwater sampling events conducted for Caltrans. {{quote: "Sincerely, GEOCON CONSULTANTS, INC. Rebecca L. Silva John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG Project Manager Senior Engineer"}}
*   **Modesto Stockpiles GW:** Juhrend is identified as "Project Manager" and "Principal" for Geocon Consultants, Inc. in the document "S9525-06-44 Modesto Stockpiles GW.0612". {{quote: "Project Manager John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG Principal"}}
