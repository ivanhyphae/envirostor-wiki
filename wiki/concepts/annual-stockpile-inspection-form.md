The Annual Stockpile Inspection Form is the recurring inspection instrument used to document the physical condition of the Modesto soil stockpiles on an annual basis. Its function in the case file is to provide a dated, repeatable record of stockpile integrity — cover condition, erosion, encroachment, and similar physical observations — separate from the analytical sampling programs (groundwater, stormwater, soil) that establish contaminant concentrations at the site.

Because the form is a recurring administrative record rather than a single analytical event, its significance in this case file is procedural: it is the mechanism by which Caltrans demonstrates ongoing physical control of the stockpiles between the analytical sampling rounds that drive the substantive findings elsewhere in the case file (soil, groundwater, and stormwater results). The available source material does not give the individual inspection dates, findings, or any exceedance or deficiency recorded on a specific form, so no trend or condition-by-year account can be given here.
