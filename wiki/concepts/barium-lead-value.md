The Barium Lead Value is a specific concentration threshold discussed in the context of soil sampling for the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file.

## Site record

A revised technical memorandum from [[geocon-consultants]] dated June 18, 2020, addressed a 120 mg/kg value, clarifying that this concentration was for [[barium]], not [[lead]]. This clarification did not alter the determination that the upper three feet of soil from Stockpile 2's southerly slope MSE wall footing required placement within designated fill containment zones. Soil from depths greater than three feet was deemed suitable for use in containment zones, embankment fill, and clean cover.

## Regulatory status

The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] reviewed the technical memorandum. On June 22, 2020, [[dean-wright]] of DTSC, in an email to Richard C. Stewart of [[caltrans]], confirmed DTSC's acceptance of the memorandum. This acceptance affirmed the decision that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones, while soil below that depth is suitable for use as embankment fill or capping material. This material below 3 feet bgs was also considered part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, meaning a new VR was not required for its utilization.
