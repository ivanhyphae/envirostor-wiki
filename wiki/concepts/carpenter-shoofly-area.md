## Overview

The area is the subject of a **Clean Fill Sampling Plan** (Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan), indicating that clean fill material was intended to be placed or verified in this area as part of the remedial action. The plan was reviewed and approved by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
