This deliverable details the characterization testing performed on approximately 4,000 cubic yards of native soil excavated from the [[bent-2-area]] for the State Route 132 Project. The testing was conducted by [[geocon-consultants]] to assess the suitability of this material for use as a clean cap on [[stockpile-2]].

### Site Record and Analytical Results

Ten discrete soil samples, designated STK1 through STK10, were collected from the Bent 2 end dump piles. These samples were spaced approximately 80 feet apart, alternating between the two end dump rows. The samples were analyzed for barium and lead using EPA Test Method 6010B.

*   **Barium:** Detected in all 10 samples at concentrations ranging from 31 to 230 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). Four samples exceeded the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg.
*   **Lead:** Detected in 6 of the 10 samples at concentrations ranging from 2.6 to 13 mg/kg. These concentrations were within the range of the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg.

A summary of the analytical data was presented in Table 1 of the technical memorandum, with a full laboratory report attached.

### Regulatory Status and Proposed Use

Based on the barium data, the Bent 2 soil stockpiles did not meet the clean cap criteria established in the *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan*, dated April 9, 2020, which was approved by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). This variance set an upper-bound background threshold for barium at 120 mg/kg.

However, pending DTSC review and approval, the material was considered potentially suitable for use as a partial clean cap (6 inches or less) on the upper surface of [[stockpile-2]]. This proposed use was contingent on additional clean cap, consisting of the planned structural pavement section, being placed over the entire upper surface. Fill material from previously approved clean fill sources would be required for placement on the northerly slope of [[stockpile-2]]. This information was intended for inclusion in the Removal Action Completion Report following the completion of clean fill capping activities for Stockpile 1 and 2.

### Open Items

As of the October 30, 2020, technical memorandum, the suitability of the Bent 2 soil stockpiles for use as a partial clean cap was pending DTSC review and approval consideration.
