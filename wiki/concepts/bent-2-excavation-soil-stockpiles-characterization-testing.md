Approximately 4,000 cubic yards of native soil excavated from Bent 2 of the SR 132 overcrossing structure (west of State Route 99) was placed in two rows of end dumps spanning roughly 800 feet along the southern portion of the top of Stockpile 2. Geocon Consultants tested this material to determine whether it met the clean cap criteria that DTSC had approved for the site, and found that it did not, on barium grounds. The finding did not halt the capping effort but redirected it: the Bent 2 soil was proposed for a narrower, secondary use instead of full clean cap.

## Sampling and results

Geocon collected ten discrete soil samples (STK1 through STK10) from the Bent 2 end dump piles at approximately 80-foot intervals running east to west, alternating between the two end dump rows. Samples were bagged and transported under chain-of-custody protocol to California Laboratory Services, which analyzed each for barium and lead by EPA Test Method 6010B on an expedited 24-hour turnaround. Results are summarized in the memorandum's Table 1 (not reproduced here) and in the attached CLS laboratory report.

Barium was detected in all ten samples, ranging from 31 to 230 mg/kg. Four of the ten samples were at or above the site-specific maximum background value of 120 mg/kg established under the referenced *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan* (April 9, 2020). Lead was detected in six of ten samples, ranging from 2.6 to 13 mg/kg — within the range of the site-specific maximum background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg, and therefore not a driver of the outcome.

## Consequence: failed clean cap criteria, proposed partial use

Because four of ten samples equaled or exceeded the 120 mg/kg barium background threshold, Geocon concluded the Bent 2 stockpiles do not meet the clean cap criteria set in the DTSC-approved variance. This is a direct, sample-based failure against a numeric standard, not a marginal or ambiguous result — it rules out using this material as the full-thickness clean cap originally intended for Stockpile 2's upper surface.

Geocon's memorandum instead proposed, pending DTSC review and approval, that the material be used only as a partial clean cap of 6 inches or less on the upper surface of Stockpile 2, on the reasoning that the planned structural pavement section (base material and asphalt/concrete) would be placed over the entire upper surface afterward, providing an additional layer above the Bent 2 material. This proposal is recorded as pending DTSC action at the time of the memorandum; no DTSC decision on it appears in this record. The memorandum separately states that fill from previously approved clean fill sources — not Bent 2 material — would be required for the northerly slope of Stockpile 2, since that slope location was not offered as a candidate for the exception.

A portion of the Bent 2 end dumps had already been placed atop a fill berm made from the lower portion of the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation, which DTSC had separately approved for use as clean cap; the Bent 2 characterization testing concerns only the end dump material above that berm, not the berm itself.

## Disposition

The memorandum states that this characterization information would be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted following completion of the Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities. No later document in this record confirms whether DTSC approved the partial (6-inch) use of Bent 2 material or whether the material was ultimately placed as proposed.
