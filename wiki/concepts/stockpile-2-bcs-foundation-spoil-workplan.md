## Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Workplan

This Technical Memorandum, dated February 28, 2022, prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] for WSP/City of Modesto, addresses the management and disposal/reuse of barium-containing soil (BCS) spoils generated from foundation excavations for the State Route 132 Project in Modesto, California. The BCS material originated from the southern portion of the [[stockpile-2-bcs-containment-zone]].

## Site Record

BCS material was generated from two foundation excavations:

*   **Weather Station Foundation Slab/Anchor** (approximate Station 1138+60): Excavations completed in July/August 2021, approximately 4.5 feet deep, generated about 2 cubic yards of BCS material. This material was spread on the adjacent ground surface.
*   **Overhead Sign #1** (approximate Station 1139+30): A planned 5-foot-diameter by 25-foot-deep excavation is expected to generate approximately 18.2 cubic yards of BCS material.

A conservative estimate of 40 cubic yards of BCS material from both foundations will be stockpiled prior to offsite landfill disposal or onsite reuse. The pre-moistened BCS material will be placed on and covered with minimum 6-millimeter-thick plastic sheeting, surrounded by erosion control measures. The stockpile cover will be secured with ropes and weights.

Eight discrete soil samples will be collected from the stockpile on a grid pattern at various depths. These samples will be composited into two 4-part composite samples and analyzed for Title 22 metals using EPA Test Methods 6010B and 7471A (mercury) with a 48-hour turn-around time. Soluble metals testing may be required if total metal concentrations are elevated to determine if the BCS material is a California or Federal RCRA hazardous waste.

The total [[barium]] and lead concentrations will be compared to site-specific background levels:
*   Barium: 120 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg)
*   Lead: 3.8 mg/kg

If total barium and lead concentrations do not exceed these background levels, the stockpiled BCS may be suitable for onsite reuse, subject to approval by Caltrans and DTSC. If onsite reuse is not approved, the BCS material will be profiled for acceptance at Forward Landfill in Manteca or another accepting landfill.

## Regulatory Status

The management and disposal of BCS material are subject to Caltrans and DTSC approval. Analytical results will be compared to site-specific background levels for barium (120 mg/kg) and lead (3.8 mg/kg) to determine suitability for onsite reuse.

## Open Items

The suitability of the BCS material for onsite reuse is pending analytical results and approval from Caltrans and DTSC.
