## Overview

Cesar E Chavez Park is a public park located in the City of Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. In the context of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project (State Route 132), the park is identified in the Draft Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (DEIR/EA) and its appendices (A–F) as a nearby community resource that could be affected by project‑related activities. The DEIR/EA documents the park’s proximity to the soil stockpile staging areas and its designation as a sensitive receptor for noise, air quality, and visual impacts during remediation phases. No further site‑specific data (e.g., soil or groundwater concentrations, monitoring locations) for the park are reported in the appendices provided.

## Site record

- **Document reference**: *SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remediation Project Draft Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment, Appendices A–F* (Caltrans, [year not specified in source]).  
- **Spatial relationship**: The park is described in the DEIR/EA as lying within 0.5 miles of the primary stockpile zones along SR 132 (appendix maps).  
- **Sensitive receptor designation**: The DEIR/EA includes the park in its list of sensitive receptors for noise (Section 4.2), air quality (Section 4.3), and traffic (Section 4.4). Specific threshold exceedance data for the park are not tabulated in the appendices; general project‑wide mitigation measures are applied (e.g., watering of stockpiles, construction barriers).  
- **No measured contaminant levels**: Appendices A–F do not contain soil gas, groundwater, or soil sampling results specifically for Cesar E Chavez Park. All monitoring wells and sample locations referenced (e.g., MW‑5, stockpile area samples) are on or adjacent to the Caltrans right‑of‑way, not within the park itself.

## Regulatory status

- **Applicable standards**: The DEIR/EA evaluates project impacts under CEQA and NEPA. For the park, the applicable criteria are noise standards from local municipal codes and Caltrans noise abatement policy (Ldn ≤ 67 dBA for residential/recreational land uses) and air quality thresholds set by the San Joaquin Valley Air Pollution Control District (SJVAPCD).  
- **Agency decisions**: No separate regulatory action specific to Cesar E Chavez Park is documented in the appendices. The DEIR/EA’s Mitigation Monitoring and Reporting Program (MMRP) includes conditions that apply to all sensitive receptors, including the park.  
- **Compliance status**: Based on the available appendices, project‑related activities are designed to comply with the established thresholds during all phases. No exceedances at the park have been reported.

## Open items

- The DEIR/EA appendices do not include long‑term monitoring plans for the park after remediation is complete.  
- A final determination of whether park‑specific noise monitoring or additional air quality modeling is required during construction is not resolved in the provided documents.
