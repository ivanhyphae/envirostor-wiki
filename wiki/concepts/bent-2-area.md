The Bent 2 Area refers to the excavation for a bent (support structure) of the SR 132 overcrossing west of State Route 99, where roughly 4,000 cubic yards of native soil was removed and end-dumped in two rows spanning about 800 feet along the southern portion of the top of [[stockpile-2]]. A portion of these end dumps sat on a fill berm built from the lower Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation that DTSC had already approved as clean cap. The soil's fate turned on whether it met the clean cap criteria set in the April 9, 2020 Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan, and the answer — determined by barium results — was no, leading to a narrower, conditional reuse rather than outright rejection or unrestricted use.

## Characterization testing and results

[[geocon-consultants-inc]] collected ten discrete soil samples (STK1 through STK10) from the Bent 2 end dump piles at roughly 80-foot intervals, alternating between the two end dump rows, and had them analyzed by California Laboratory Services for [[barium]] and lead by EPA Method 6010B on an expedited 24-hour turnaround. Results are summarized in the October 30, 2020 Technical Memorandum ("Bent 2 Excavation Soil Stockpiles Characterization Testing").

Barium was detected in all 10 samples, ranging from 31 to 230 mg/kg. Four of the ten samples equaled or exceeded the maximum [[site-specific-background-levels]] value for barium of 120 mg/kg — the same threshold used as the clean cap criterion under the April 2020 variance. Lead was detected in 6 of 10 samples, ranging from 2.6 to 13 mg/kg, which the memo characterizes as within a similar range to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg (lead was not treated as a factor in the clean cap determination).

Because 40 percent of the samples equaled or exceeded the barium background threshold, Geocon concluded the Bent 2 stockpiles did not meet the clean cap criteria established in the DTSC-approved variance.

## Disposition and DTSC/Water Board acceptance

Rather than requiring the material be treated as unsuitable, Geocon proposed — pending DTSC review — that the Bent 2 soil be used as partial clean cap at a thickness of 6 inches or less on the upper surface of Stockpile 2, since the planned structural pavement section (base material and asphalt/concrete) would be placed over that entire surface as an additional cap layer. The northerly slope of Stockpile 2, which would not receive this pavement cap, was excluded: clean cap there was to come from previously approved clean fill sources instead of Bent 2 material.

Caltrans transmitted the Technical Memorandum to DTSC, the Water Board, and other reviewers on November 2, 2020, stating plainly that the Bent 2 soils exceeded background criteria for use as clean cap cover material. On November 6, 2020, DTSC Project Manager Dean Wright issued the agencies' response: DTSC and the Water Board agreed the soil could be placed atop Stockpile 2 at 6 inches or less provided it is covered by clean base material and the asphalt/concrete roadway section, "not jeopardizing the integrity of the remedy." This email constitutes DTSC's official acceptance of both the Tech Memo and the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020.

The acceptance carried an explicit condition tied to schedule: because Bent 2 soils might be temporarily exposed during the winter months before final capping, DTSC and the Water Board required strict adherence to the rainy-season surface water sampling effort and the erosion control measures in the October 22, 2020 SWPPP Amendment, specifically to keep contaminants on site pending final cover placement.

## Significance

The Bent 2 result is the site's operative example of the barium background threshold (120 mg/kg) functioning as a pass/fail clean cap criterion rather than an abstract number: exceedance in 4 of 10 samples was sufficient to disqualify the material from unrestricted clean cap use, but the agencies allowed a thickness-limited, pavement-covered exception rather than requiring off-site disposal or treatment. This makes the Bent 2 soil a capped-in-place constituent of Stockpile 2's final cover system, contingent on the pavement section being completed as planned and on the interim erosion controls holding through the exposed winter period.
