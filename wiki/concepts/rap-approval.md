# Rap Approval

## Overview

Rap Approval is a regulatory authorization required by the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) for the reuse of petroleum hydrocarbon-impacted soil under a site-specific Remediation Action Plan. At the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, Rap Approval governs the acceptance of treated soil as backfill material following bioremediation under the oversight of the CVRWQCB and the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).

## Site record

The site involves soil stockpiles generated during the State Route 132 widening project in Stanislaus County. Soils were segregated into Stockpile A (suspected TPH-diesel impacted) and Stockpile B (suspected TPH-motor oil and lead impacted) during Phase 1 construction (2019–2020).

A workplan for stockpile management was submitted to DTSC on **March 12, 2020**, proposing bioremediation with nutrient amendment and periodic tilling. Laboratory analytical results for petroleum hydrocarbons and lead were obtained for both stockpiles.

The Remediation Action Plan (RAP) document, prepared by Caltrans, specifies criteria for reuse of remediated soil. Under Rap Approval, treated soil must meet cleanup goals before it can be accepted for use as backfill. For Stockpile A, the treatment target is **total petroleum hydrocarbons as diesel (TPH-d)**, with a cleanup goal of **100 mg/kg**. For Stockpile B, the target contaminants are **TPH as motor oil (TPH-mo)** and **lead**, with respective cleanup goals of **100 mg/kg** and **80 mg/kg** (California Human Health Screening Level for lead in residential soil).

Confirmation sampling was performed following treatment cycles. The RAP requires a minimum of one confirmation sample per **200 cubic yards** of treated soil. Sample results are compared against the cleanup goals to determine whether Rap Approval conditions are met. Analytical methods used include **EPA Method 8015M** for TPH-d and TPH-mo, and **EPA Method 6010B** for lead.

Stockpile B required additional segregation due to lead concentrations above 80 mg/kg in localized areas. Soils exceeding the lead cleanup goal were separated for off-site disposal and are not eligible for Rap Approval-based reuse.

## Regulatory status

Rap Approval is issued by the **CVRWQCB** as the lead regulatory agency, with DTSC providing oversight on lead-impacted soils. The RAP dated **April 2020** establishes the cleanup goals and confirmation sampling protocol. As of the most recent documentation, no formal Rap Approval letter had been issued because confirmation sampling was ongoing. Treated soils cannot be placed as backfill until analytical results demonstrate compliance with all cleanup goals and formal Rap Approval is granted in writing.

A variance request for lead in Stockpile B soils exceeding the 80 mg/kg threshold but below the **320 mg/kg** industrial/commercial screening level was discussed with DTSC but not resolved. Rap Approval for lead-impacted soil reuse under a variance would require separate DTSC concurrence.

## Open items

- Completion of confirmation sampling for all treated soils in Stockpile A and Stockpile B.
- Submission of a Rap Approval request package to CVRWQCB with all confirmation analytical results.
- DTSC concurrence on any lead variance for soil exceeding 80 mg/kg but proposed for reuse.
- Agency notification if treated soil volumes exceed those specified in the RAP prior to issuance of Rap Approval.
