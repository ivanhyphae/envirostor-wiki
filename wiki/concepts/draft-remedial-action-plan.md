The Draft Remedial Action Plan (DRAP) for the [[state-route-132-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site outlines proposed actions to address contamination. It is a key deliverable within the regulatory process overseen by the [[dtsc]].

### Implementation Schedule Updates

On January 10, 2017, [[richard-stewart]], Engineering Geologist and Acting Branch Chief for Caltrans District 6, provided an update to the Implementation Schedule (Section 8, page 47) of the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan, dated October 2014. The updates to the schedule were as follows:

*   **January 18, 2017:** Public notice of the availability of the Draft Final RAP and the SR-132 Project EIR/EA for a 45-day public review period.
*   **February 22, 2017:** A public meeting was scheduled.
*   **Spring 2017:** The [[dtsc]] was to provide a Responsiveness Summary, addressing public comments.
*   **Spring 2017:** The Draft Final RAP was to be revised as needed, followed by [[dtsc]] approval of the Final RAP.

All other dates within the original Implementation Schedule were to remain unchanged.
