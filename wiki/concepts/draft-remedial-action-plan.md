## Draft Remedial Action Plan

### Overview
The Draft Remedial Action Plan (DRAP) is a document outlining proposed actions to address contamination at the [[state-route-132-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. It is part of the regulatory process managed by the [[dtsc]].

### Site Record
The [[dtsc]] provided comments on the Draft Remedial Action Plan in a letter dated May 18, 2015. This letter was part of the Revised Administrative Record for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles.

### Regulatory Status
The DRAP is a step in the regulatory process leading to a final decision on remediation.

### Open Items
The [[dtsc]] provided comments on the DRAP on May 18, 2015, indicating that revisions were necessary before it could be considered final.
