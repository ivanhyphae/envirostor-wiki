Mercury was detected in surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023, as part of the remediation case file for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA).

## Site Record

On March 10, 2023, mercury was detected in surface water samples. The analytical results for dissolved metals, including mercury, were summarized in Table 2 of the referenced report. The specific analytical method used for mercury was AA (Cold Vapor) by EPA 7470A. The laboratory flagged some of the data with a "J" value, indicating an estimated concentration.

The following samples had mercury detections:
*   BG1: {{quote: "BG1"}}
*   BG3: {{quote: "BG3"}}
*   PL6: {{quote: "PL6"}}
*   PL7: {{quote: "PL7"}}

The reported metal detections, including mercury, were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such exist. {{quote: "The reported metal detections were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such exist."}}

The analytical results from the March 10, 2023 sampling event were the first surface water samples collected after road construction was completed and the stockpiles were encapsulated. {{quote: "The analytical results from the March 10, 2023 sampling event were the first surface water samples collected since road construction was completed and the stockpiles encapsulated."}}
