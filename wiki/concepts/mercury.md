## Overview
Mercury is a contaminant that has been detected in surface water samples collected as part of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] remediation case file (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). Its presence has been monitored through various sampling events.

## Site Record
Mercury was detected in surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023. The analytical method used for mercury was AA (Cold Vapor) by EPA 7470A. The laboratory flagged some of the data with a "J" value, indicating an estimated concentration. The following samples had mercury detections: BG1, BG3, PL6, and PL7. The reported metal detections, including mercury, were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such exist. These March 10, 2023, results were the first surface water samples collected after road construction was completed and the stockpiles were encapsulated.

In October 2016, mercury was not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than its respective method detection limits (MDLs) for samples PL1 through PL5, BG1, and BG2.

In March 2017, mercury was not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than its respective method detection limits for samples PL1 through PL5.

In January 2018, mercury was not detected in the samples at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective laboratory reporting limits.

## Regulatory Status
The reported metal detections, including mercury, were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such exist.
