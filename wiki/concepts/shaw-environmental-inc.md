## Shaw Environmental Inc.

Shaw Environmental Inc. was a consultant involved in the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. Their primary contribution documented in the provided sources is the preparation of the Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (FEIR/EA).

### Site Record

Shaw Environmental Inc. prepared the "Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment" for the SR 132 Project. This document served as a comprehensive review of the project's potential environmental impacts.

### Regulatory Status

The Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment prepared by Shaw Environmental Inc. was a key document in the project's compliance with the [[california-environmental-quality-act]]. Its completion was a prerequisite for project approval and subsequent actions.
