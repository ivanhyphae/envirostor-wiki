## Shaw Environmental Inc.

Shaw Environmental Inc. was involved in the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, specifically in relation to the [[environmental-impact-report-environmental-assessment]] and the development of the [[remedial-action-plan]].

### Site Record

Shaw Environmental Inc. prepared the "Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment" for the SR 132 Project. {{quote: "Shaw Environmental Inc. prepared the Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment for the SR 132 Project."}} This document was a key component of the project's regulatory process.

### Regulatory Status

The "Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment" prepared by Shaw Environmental Inc. was a prerequisite for project approval under the [[california-environmental-quality-act]].
