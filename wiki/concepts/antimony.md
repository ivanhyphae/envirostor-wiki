## Antimony

### Overview
Antimony was among the dissolved metals analyzed in surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023, following the completion of road construction and encapsulation of soil stockpiles. While not detected above the Method Detection Limit (MDL) in the samples analyzed, the MDL itself exceeded the established Primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL).

### Site record
On March 10, 2023, antimony was not reported above the MDLs in the background and surface water samples. {{quote: "Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, nickel, silver, and thallium were not reported above the MDLs in the background and surface water samples."}} The laboratory's MDL for antimony was 8.8 micrograms per liter (µg/L). {{quote: "In the case of antimony, the MDL (8.8 micrograms per liter [µg/L]) exceeded the Primary MCL of 6.0 µg/L."}}

### Regulatory status
The Primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for antimony is 6.0 µg/L. {{quote: "In the case of antimony, the MDL (8.8 micrograms per liter [µg/L]) exceeded the Primary MCL of 6.0 µg/L."}} The MDL for antimony in the March 10, 2023, surface water samples was 8.8 µg/L, which exceeded this MCL. {{quote: "In the case of antimony, the MDL (8.8 micrograms per liter [µg/L]) exceeded the Primary MCL of 6.0 µg/L."}}
