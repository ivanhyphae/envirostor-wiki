The **Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP)**, first issued by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on January 30, 2019, outlines the strategy for the interim containment of [[barium]]-containing soil (BCS) from Stockpiles 1, 2, and 3 during Phase 1 construction of the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project. The RDIP details the remedial design, management of BCS, construction monitoring, and reporting requirements for the selected remedy: consolidation and containment of BCS within highway embankment fills, followed by capping with clean fill and structural pavement. This plan specifically addresses Interim Phase 1, with a separate RDIP intended for Ultimate Phase 2. The RDIP was developed under the oversight of the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and in cooperation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (CVRWQCB).

## Site record

The RDIP, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for Caltrans, details the technical approach for the interim containment of BCS. The plan was conditionally approved by the DTSC on August 10, 2018, pending incorporation of administrative revisions and review of design plans {{quote: "CONDITIONAL APPROVAL OF THE DRAFT REMEDIAL DESIGN IMPLEMENTATION PLAN, CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES, STATE ROUTE 132, WEST FREEWAY/EXPRESSWAY PROJECT, STANISLAUS COUNTY, CALIFORNIA DATED JULY 18, 2018"}}. A subsequent conditional approval was issued on October 12, 2018, following review of the revised plan {{quote: "CONDITIONAL APPROVAL OF THE REMEDIAL DESIGN IMPLEMENTATION PLAN -INTERIM PHASE 1. CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES, STATE ROUTE 132. WEST FREEWAY/EXPRESSWAY PROJECT, STANISLAUS COUNTY, CALIFORNIA DATED SEPTEMBER 14, 2018"}}.

The RDIP specifies that the surface of completed Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS consolidation zones will be capped with a minimum 12-inch-thick clean fill material {{quote: "Section 4.7 of the RDIP contains the following text with respect to evaluation and approval of identified sources for clean cover fill materials: 'The surface of the completed Stockpile 1 and 2 barium-containing soil and ADL consolidation zones (including east- and west-facing temporary slopes) will be capped with minimum 12-inch-thick clean fill material consisting of approved imported fill and/or minimum 4-inch-thick AB materials (pending placement of structural pavement section, hardscape and/or additional clean embankment fill.'"}}.

Verification testing thresholds for BCS removal, as outlined in the RDIP, include:
*   Barium: 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg, groundwater protection) {{quote: "Barium - 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg, groundwater protection)"}}
*   Lead: 80 mg/kg (residential screening level) {{quote: "Lead - 80 mg/kg (residential screening level)"}}

Technical Memoranda submitted to Caltrans and the City of Modesto detail the BCS removal verification testing. For Stockpile 2, BCS removal at the west and east ends met the barium and lead verification thresholds, with barium concentrations ranging from 54 to 760 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg {{quote: "Each of the final excavation soil samples contained barium and lead below the RDIP verification thresholds. Barium concentrations ranged from 54 to 760 mg/kg, and lead concentrations from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg."}}. For Stockpile 3, BCS removal also met the verification thresholds, with barium concentrations ranging from 46 to 970 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg {{quote: "Each of the final excavation soil samples contained barium and lead below the RDIP verification thresholds. Barium concentrations ranged from 46 to 970 mg/kg, and lead concentrations from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg."}}.

A variance to the RDIP was issued on April 9, 2020, to address identified sources of clean cover fill material for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2 {{quote: "Subject: VARIANCE TO REMEDIAL DESIGN IMPLEMENTATION PLAN"}}. This variance addressed the use of soil from planned highway underpass excavation areas adjacent to Carpenter Road and from mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2.

The RDIP specifies a project schedule with key milestones:
*   RDIP Final Approval: Early 2019 {{quote: "RDIP Final Approval: Early 2019"}}
*   Start Barium-Containing Soil Containment: Mid 2019 {{quote: "Start Barium-Containing Soil Containment: Mid 2019"}}
*   Complete Barium-Containing Soil Containment: Late 2019 {{quote: "Complete Barium-Containing Soil Containment: Late 2019"}}

## Regulatory status

The RDIP was prepared under the oversight of the DTSC and in cooperation with the CVRWQCB {{quote: "The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in cooperation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB), is the lead regulatory agency providing site mitigation oversight..."}}. DTSC provided conditional approval of the Draft RDIP on August 10, 2018 {{quote: "CONDITIONAL APPROVAL OF THE DRAFT REMEDIAL DESIGN IMPLEMENTATION PLAN..."}} and a subsequent conditional approval of the RDIP – Interim Phase 1 on October 12, 2018 {{quote: "CONDITIONAL APPROVAL OF THE REMEDIAL DESIGN IMPLEMENTATION PLAN -INTERIM PHASE 1..."}}.

The DTSC accepted the technical memorandum detailing the Stockpile 3 BCS removal verification testing on March 18, 2020, stating that "no further BCS RDIP monitoring requirements are necessary and this areas is now suitable for clean embankment fill" {{quote: "DTSC has reviewed the Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated March 13, 2020 describing the removal of Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Stockpile 3 and the subsequent confirmation soil sampling that was completed to show this area has achieved the cleanup levels established in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). The sampling results indicate that the Stockpile 3 BCS has been removed and that the remaining soil meets the RDIP verification thresholds. DTSC agrees that no further BCS RDIP monitoring requirements are necessary and this areas is now suitable for clean embankment fill. This e‐mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the Memo."}}.

## Open items

The RDIP is specific to Interim Phase 1 construction. An additional RDIP will be prepared for the Ultimate Phase 2 portion of the project {{quote: "This RDIP is specific to that portion of the SR-132 West Project that contains the three bariumcontaining soil stockpiles (Stockpiles 1 through 3) during Interim Phase 1 construction. ... An additional RDIP will be prepared for the Ultimate Phase 2 portion of the project planned for completion in 2028."}}.
