## Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) - Interim Phase 1

The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) - Interim Phase 1 outlines the technical approach and detailed plans for the implementation of the Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. This plan specifically addresses the containment of barium- and lead-containing soil stockpiles by incorporating them as highway embankment fill during the construction of the SR-132 West Expressway Project. The RDIP was prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] for [[caltrans]] under the oversight of the DTSC and in cooperation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]].

### Site Record

The RDIP - Interim Phase 1 was submitted to the DTSC on January 30, 2019, by [[geocon-consultants]]. It was conditionally approved by the DTSC on October 12, 2018, pending review of the 100% design plans. The DTSC provided final approval of the RDIP - Interim Phase 1 on April 3, 2019.

The RDIP addresses the containment of three barium-containing soil stockpiles (Stockpiles 1 through 3) during Interim Phase 1 construction of the SR-132 West Project. The remedial alternative selected is containment by incorporating the stockpiles as highway embankment fill and covering them with a structural pavement section and imported clean fill.

The anticipated schedule for Interim Phase 1 construction, as outlined in the RDIP, included:
*   RDIP Final Approval: Early 2019
*   Project Design Approval: Early 2019
*   Contractor Bid Award: Early to Mid 2019
*   Start Barium-Containing Soil Containment: Mid 2019
*   Complete Barium-Containing Soil Containment: Late 2019
*   Complete Interim Phase 1 Construction: Late 2020
*   Remedial Completion Report Approval: 2021

The RDIP document organization includes sections on Project Organization, Barium-containing Soil Containment Design, Containment Design Implementation, Monitoring Plan, Regulatory Reporting, and Post Construction Monitoring/Maintenance. Appendices include DTSC Draft RDIP Conditional Approval Letters, ADL Soil Reuse Conditions and 2016 DTSC Agreement, Excerpts of 100% SR-132 West Project Design Plans, Technical Memorandum Post Excavation Soil Confirmation Levels, DTSC's *Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Material*, Health and Safety Plan, and Air Monitoring Worksheet.

The project organization identifies [[caltrans]] as the Site Owner/Responsible Party. [[geocon-consultants]] is Caltrans' Environmental Consultant. The DTSC and [[central-valley-rwqcb]] are the regulatory agencies. The City of Modesto (City)/Stanislaus Council of Governments is the SR-132 West Project Proponent, with Dokken Engineering as the Project Proponent's Civil Engineer.

[[caltrans]] is responsible for verifying that highway construction work associated with the soil stockpiles conforms to the RAP and RDIP. This includes supervising groundwater monitoring well abandonment, observing and documenting soil excavation and fill placement, performing soil verification sampling, conducting air and stormwater monitoring, and interacting with regulatory agencies.

The City's construction management firm and highway construction contractor are responsible for worker health and safety, site grading, construction of retaining walls/bridge structures, management of placed barium-containing fill material, dust control, stormwater best management practices, and placement of pavement and imported clean cover soil.

The RDIP was subject to errata and variances. An Errata to the January 2019 RDIP was issued post-construction contract award, updating roles and responsibilities to reflect Caltrans' direct oversight rather than Geocon as the state's agent. A Second Variance to the RDIP (RDIP Variance #2), dated September 2, 2020, addressed the use of Class 3 aggregate subbase material as clean cover. The DTSC and [[central-valley-rwqcb]] accepted the use of Class 3 subbase as an acceptable clean cap alternative, but stipulated a minimum cap thickness of 6 inches (instead of the proposed 4 inches) due to its fine-grained nature and requested a composite sample for barium and lead analysis.

A Variance Request for the use of Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Footing Soil as Clean Cover was reviewed by DTSC and the Water Board. They agreed that soil from 1 to 5 feet below grade in this area, with a 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) for barium of 113.1 mg/kg, could be used as clean cap soil, as this concentration was within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background.

### Regulatory Status

The RDIP - Interim Phase 1 was conditionally approved by the DTSC on August 10, 2018, and October 12, 2018, with final approval granted on April 3, 2019. The DTSC and [[central-valley-rwqcb]] are the lead regulatory agencies overseeing the project.

### Open Items

As of the approval dates of the RDIP and its variances, the project was proceeding with implementation. Specific open items would be detailed in subsequent progress reports and the Remedial Action Completion Report.
