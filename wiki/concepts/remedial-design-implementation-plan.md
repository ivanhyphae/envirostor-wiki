The **Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP)**, first issued by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on January 30, 2019, outlines the strategy for the interim containment of [[barium]]-containing soil (BCS) from Stockpiles 1, 2, and 3 during Phase 1 construction of the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project. The RDIP details the remedial design, management of BCS, construction monitoring, and reporting requirements for the selected remedy: consolidation and containment of BCS within highway embankment fills, followed by capping with clean fill and structural pavement. This plan specifically addresses Interim Phase 1, with a separate RDIP intended for Ultimate Phase 2. The RDIP was developed under the oversight of the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and in cooperation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (CVRWQCB).

## Site record

The RDIP, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for [[caltrans]], details the technical approach for the interim containment of BCS. The plan was conditionally approved by the DTSC on August 10, 2018, pending incorporation of administrative revisions and review of design plans. A subsequent conditional approval was issued on October 12, 2018, following review of the revised plan.

The RDIP specifies that the surface of completed Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS consolidation zones will be capped with a minimum 12-inch-thick clean fill material.

Verification testing thresholds for BCS removal, as outlined in the RDIP, include:
*   Barium: 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg, groundwater protection)
*   Lead: 80 mg/kg (residential screening level)

Technical Memoranda submitted to Caltrans and the City of Modesto detail the BCS removal verification testing. For Stockpile 2, BCS removal at the west and east ends met the barium and lead verification thresholds, with barium concentrations ranging from 54 to 760 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg. For Stockpile 3, BCS removal also met the verification thresholds, with barium concentrations ranging from 46 to 970 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg.

A variance to the RDIP was issued on April 9, 2020, to address identified sources of clean cover fill material for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2. This variance addressed the use of soil from planned highway underpass excavation areas adjacent to Carpenter Road and from mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2. A second variance, dated September 2, 2020, clarified the alternative use of a 4-inch-thick layer of Class 3 aggregate base (AB) over the top of the completed BCS Containment Zones.

The RDIP specifies a project schedule with key milestones:
*   RDIP Final Approval: Early 2019
*   Start Barium-Containing Soil Containment: Mid 2019
*   Complete Barium-Containing Soil Containment: Late 2019

## Regulatory status

The RDIP was prepared under the oversight of the DTSC and in cooperation with the CVRWQCB. DTSC provided conditional approval of the Draft RDIP on August 10, 2018, and a subsequent conditional approval of the RDIP – Interim Phase 1 on October 12, 2018.

The DTSC accepted the technical memorandum detailing the Stockpile 3 BCS removal verification testing on March 18, 2020, stating that "no further BCS RDIP monitoring requirements are necessary and this areas is now suitable for clean embankment fill."

## Open items

The RDIP is specific to Interim Phase 1 construction. An additional RDIP will be prepared for the Ultimate Phase 2 portion of the project.
