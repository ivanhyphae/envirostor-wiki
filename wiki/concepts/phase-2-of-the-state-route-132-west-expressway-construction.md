## Phase 2 Of The State Route 132 West Expressway Construction

This section details the role and activities related to Phase 2 of the State Route 132 West Expressway Construction project, specifically concerning the Modesto Soil Stockpiles. The California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) have an interagency agreement to manage the environmental oversight for this project.

## Site Record

The DTSC's involvement is governed by Caltrans Agreement Number 43A0487, which includes DTSC Agreement Number 25-T5466. Service Request 43A0487-04, titled "Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132," specifically supports the construction of Phase 2 of the State Route 132 West Expressway. The term for this Service Request is from August 4, 2025, to June 30, 2030, with a total order amount of \$113,000.00. The charge district and EA/EFIS are 10-1E700/10-1500-0115.

The DTSC's scope of services includes regulatory oversight for Caltrans' environmental work associated with the Modesto Soil Stockpiles. Specific tasks undertaken by DTSC include:
*   Review and comment on routine monitoring and/or inspection reports.
*   Review and comment on Remedial Design Implementation Plans and associated documents.
*   Review and comment on well destruction reports.
*   Participation in meetings and phone calls regarding the Site and construction activities.
*   Providing field oversight of monitoring and field activities conducted by Caltrans, as needed.
*   Preparation and modification of the Service Request.

Work under this Service Request commenced on August 4, 2025, and is scheduled to terminate on June 30, 2030. The schedule for work items will be implemented as needed and negotiated by the Caltrans and DTSC Service Request Managers. A minimum of a 30-day review period is anticipated for DTSC's review of submitted documents.

While no formal progress reports are required, DTSC will provide written comments and recommendations on submitted data, reports, and other information. Each billing invoice from DTSC will include a summary of activities for indirect and direct charges, along with daily logs for the invoice period. Caltrans may request copies of these daily logs and timesheets for DTSC staff listed on quarterly invoices.

## Regulatory Status

The DTSC provides regulatory oversight for the environmental work associated with the Modesto Soil Stockpiles to support the construction of Phase 2 of the State Route 132 West Expressway.

## Open Items

This Service Request may be amended by Caltrans to address any mutually agreed upon modifications or expansions to the Scope of Work.
