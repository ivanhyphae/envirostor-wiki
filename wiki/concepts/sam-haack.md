Sam Haack is identified in the project record as a Caltrans representative and point of contact for correspondence regarding the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County.

Sam Haack is listed as the Caltrans representative on a "Ramp Clarification Letter" dated August 24, 2012, from Geocon Consultants, Inc. to the Central Valley RWQCB. He also appears as the Caltrans representative on a "Ramp Letter" dated May 29, 2012, from Geocon Consultants, Inc. to the Central Valley RWQCB.
