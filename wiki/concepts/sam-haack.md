# Sam Haack

## Overview
Sam Haack is a named recipient, together with Steven Becker and Steven W. Meeks, of the [[ramp-clarification-letter]] dated August 24, 2012, issued by Caltrans District 10 concerning the disposition of excavated material from [[stockpile-3-excavation]] at the SR 99 Kansas Avenue northbound off-ramp project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. The letter was addressed to the Department of Toxic Substances Control ([[dtsc]]) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. Haack's full name, title, and specific role are not detailed in the source document; the letter's attention line identifies him as "Mr. Randy Adams, CEG," suggesting the correspondence was routed through Haack to Adams rather than Haack being a primary addressee. No further direct references to Haack's involvement, decisions, or responsibilities in the remedial process appear in the project record available.

## Site record
The August 24, 2012 letter from Caltrans to Steven Becker and Steven Meeks carries the attention line: "Attn: Mr. Randy Adams, CEG." The name "Sam Haack" appears in relation to this routing. The letter provides clarification regarding the management of an estimated 6,000 cubic yards of material to be excavated, of which approximately 2,800 cubic yards would originate from the northwestern end of Stockpile No. 3. The document references the "Transmittal of Site Investigation Data, Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project SR 99 - Kansas Avenue Northbound Off-Ramp, Modesto California" (Geocon, April 24, 2012) and Caltrans's prior letter dated May 29, 2012. No sampling data, analytical results, or operational instructions in the source are explicitly attributed to Sam Haack. Haack is not mentioned as an author, signatory, or investigator.

## Regulatory status
The source document does not attribute any regulatory authority, decision-making role, or enforcement capacity to Sam Haack. The letter was addressed to DTSC and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board; Haack's name appears only in the attention routing.
