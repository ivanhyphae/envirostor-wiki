The [[100-percent-design-plan-set]] is a set of construction plans developed by [[dokken-engineering]] for the Route 99-132 Separation and Widening project, specifically for [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]]. These plans, dated December 10, 2018, and marked "SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION," outline the construction limits and design details for the project.

## Site record

The [[100-percent-design-plan-set]] includes detailed drawings of typical cross-sections for Route 132. These cross-sections specify various roadway components, including traveled ways, median widths, shoulder widths, drainage ditches, sound walls, and HMA dikes. For instance, typical cross-sections indicate the use of "12 inches of clean soil" {{quote: "12 inches of clean soil"}} in certain areas. Slopes are detailed with percentages ranging from 2% to 10% {{quote: "2% to 10%"}}. Specific station ranges are provided for these cross-sections, such as Sta 1135+21.07 to Sta 1141+71.71 and Sta 1122+69.67 to Sta 1133+71.07 {{quote: "Sta 1135+21.07 to Sta 1141+71.71"}}, {{quote: "Sta 1122+69.67 to Sta 1133+71.07"}}.

Layout plans within the [[100-percent-design-plan-set]] illustrate horizontal alignments, road dimensions, curve data, right-of-way lines, and sound walls. For example, plan L-15 shows Route 132 with dimensions such as 24', 5', and 18' {{quote: "24', 5', 18'"}} and includes curve data for curve 12 {{quote: "curve 12"}}. These plans also depict topographical features and residential streets near the project area {{quote: "Arrowhead Ct, Comstock Ct, Custer Ct"}}. The project identification numbers associated with these plans are 10-403504 and 1000000424 {{quote: "10-403504, 1000000424"}}. The Project Engineer listed is Kathryn Wickam {{quote: "Kathryn Wickam"}}.
