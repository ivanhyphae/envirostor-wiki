The [[100-percent-design-plan-set]] comprises detailed construction plans developed by [[dokken-engineering]] for the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project, a component of the larger [[route-99-132-separation-and-widening]] initiative. These plans, dated December 10, 2018, and marked "SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION," delineate the project's construction limits and design specifications.

## Site record

The [[100-percent-design-plan-set]] includes numerous drawings illustrating typical cross-sections for Route 132. These diagrams detail various roadway elements such as traveled ways, median widths, shoulder widths, drainage ditches, sound walls, and HMA dikes. For example, typical cross-sections specify the use of "12 inches of clean soil" in designated areas. Slopes are indicated with percentages ranging from 2% to 10%. Specific station ranges are provided for these cross-sections, including Sta 1135+21.07 to Sta 1141+71.71 and Sta 1122+69.67 to Sta 1133+71.07.

Layout plans within the set, such as plan L-15, illustrate horizontal alignments, road dimensions (e.g., 24', 5', 18'), curve data (e.g., curve 12 with Radius 8000', Delta 03°45'24", Tangent 262.36', Length 524.53'), right-of-way lines, and sound walls. These plans also depict topographical features and adjacent residential streets, including Arrowhead Ct, Comstock Ct, and Custer Ct. The project identification numbers associated with these plans are 10-403504 and 1000000424. The Project Engineer listed is Kathryn Wickam.
