## MW-1

### Overview

MW-1 is an upgradient groundwater monitoring well at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site in Stanislaus County, California. It is one of ten wells ([[mw-1]] through [[mw-10]]) completed in the shallow zone of the upper aquifer, within the unconsolidated sand, silty sand, and silt layers of the Modesto Formation. These wells are situated approximately 35 feet below ground surface, under unconfined to semi-confined conditions. MW-1 was installed in 2006 and serves as a key upgradient point to distinguish site-related groundwater impacts from natural background conditions, particularly for nitrate and total dissolved solids (TDS).

### Site Record

#### September 2013 Sampling Event

On September 4, 2013, the depth to groundwater at MW-1 was measured at 33.74 feet below the top of casing (TOC). {{quote: "On September 4, 2013, prior to opening the wells, Geocon observed each of the ten well boxes for signs of potential tampering. No signs of tampering were observed; however, the concrete and well box for MW-6 appeared damaged. We believe that the damage occurred when the well box was run over with a lawnmower. The well casing did not appear damaged. Upon receiving authorization, we replaced the well box on September 23, 2013. In addition, we replaced locks on each of the wells. We measured the depth to groundwater and the dissolved oxygen (DO) levels and oxygen-reduction potential (ORP) in monitoring wells MW-1 through MW-10 using a battery-operated water level meter, a Hanna Model No. 9143 DO meter, and an Oakton ORP meter. Depth to water measurements were obtained from a surveyed reference point at the top of the well casings (TOC). In September 2013, depth to groundwater at the Site ranged from 33.74 (MW-1) to 42.05 (MW-5) feet below TOC."}} This was the shallowest depth recorded among the ten wells sampled.

General Minerals:
*   Nitrate as nitrogen: 12 mg/L, exceeding the California primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) of 10 mg/L. {{quote: "Nitrate as nitrogen and TDS were both reported for each of the groundwater samples, with nitrate as nitrogen concentrations ranging from 2.5 (MW-3) to 36 mg/l (MW-5) and TDS concentrations ranging from 300 (MW-3) to 750 mg/l (MW-5). The reported nitrate concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equal or exceed the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l, and the reported TDS concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6 and MW-10 exceed the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l."}}
*   Total dissolved solids (TDS): 590 mg/L, exceeding the California secondary MCL of 500 mg/L. {{quote: "Nitrate as nitrogen and TDS were both reported for each of the groundwater samples, with nitrate as nitrogen concentrations ranging from 2.5 (MW-3) to 36 mg/l (MW-5) and TDS concentrations ranging from 300 (MW-3) to 750 mg/l (MW-5). The reported nitrate concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equal or exceed the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l, and the reported TDS concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6 and MW-10 exceed the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l."}}
*   Sulfide: 0.28 mg/L, which was the highest concentration detected among the six wells where sulfide was present. {{quote: "Sulfide was reported for six of the ten samples with concentrations ranging from 0.011 (MW-3) to 0.28 mg/l (MW-1)."}}

Stiff diagram analysis classified the September 2013 groundwater sample from MW-1 as calcium-dominant, consistent with the water chemistry observed at [[mw-2]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-7]], [[mw-9]], and [[mw-10]]. {{quote: "In September 2013, the samples from wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-7, MW-9 and MW-10 had a calcium-dominant composition while the samples from wells MW-3, MW-5, MW-6 and MW-8 were sodium-dominant."}} This calcium-dominant signature has been consistently observed at MW-1 since groundwater monitoring resumed in March 2012.

Dissolved Metals:
No dissolved metals concentrations in the September 2013 sampling event exceeded applicable numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium were reported at concentrations consistent with historical levels and remained significantly below their respective numeric water quality thresholds. {{quote: "Although concentrations of arsenic, barium, chromium, strontium and vanadium were reported for the samples collected from each well, none of the reported concentrations exceed their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water."}} Lead was not detected at or above the practical quantitation limit (PQL) of 1.0 µg/L. {{quote: "Lead was not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than the respective practical quantitation limit (PQL) in samples from each well."}}

#### June 2014 Sampling Event

On June 4, 2014, the depth to groundwater at MW-1 was measured at 35.09 feet below TOC. {{quote: "In June 2014, depth to groundwater at the Site ranged from 35.09 (MW-1) to 42.86 (MW-5) feet below TOC. Based on the groundwater elevation data, the groundwater flow is toward the south-southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008, which is generally consistent with historical flow."}} This measurement contributed to the determination of groundwater flow toward the south-southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008.

General Minerals:
*   Nitrate as nitrogen: 15 mg/L, exceeding the California primary MCL of 10 mg/L. The report notes that as MW-1 is an upgradient well, this concentration "may be indicative of natural background nitrate concentrations for the shallow groundwater in the vicinity of the Site." {{quote: "Noteworthy is that MW-1 is an upgradient monitoring well; thus, the reported nitrate of 15 mg/l may be indicative of natural background nitrate concentrations for the shallow groundwater in the vicinity of the Site."}}
*   Total dissolved solids (TDS): The report indicates that TDS concentrations for samples from MW-1, [[mw-4]], [[mw-5]], [[mw-6]], and [[mw-10]] exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L. The specific value for MW-1 is not explicitly stated in this section but is implied to be above the threshold. {{quote: "TDS was reported at concentrations exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l for the samples collected from wells MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10."}}
*   Sulfide: 0.12 mg/L. {{quote: "Sulfide was reported for samples collected from wells MW-1, MW-8, and MW-10 at respective concentrations of 0.12, 0.017, and 0.023 mg/l."}}

Stiff diagrams for June 2014 indicated that MW-1 had a calcium-dominant composition, consistent with [[mw-2]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-7]], [[mw-9]], and [[mw-10]]. {{quote: "In June 2014, the samples from wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-7, MW-9, and MW-10 had a calcium-dominant composition while the samples from wells MW-3, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-8 were sodium-dominant."}}

Dissolved Metals:
No dissolved metals concentrations reported for MW-1 in June 2014 exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium concentrations were similar to historical levels and remained significantly below their numeric water quality thresholds. {{quote: "None of the reported dissolved metals concentrations for the groundwater samples collected in June 2014 exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values."}}

#### April 2017 Sampling Event

On April 12, 2017, the depth to groundwater at MW-1 was measured at 35.52 feet below TOC. {{quote: "On April 12, 2017, prior to opening the wells, Geocon observed each of the ten well boxes for signs of potential tampering or required maintenance. We observed no signs of tampering or damage to the well boxes. We measured the depth to groundwater in each well and oxygen-reduction potential (ORP) in monitoring wells MW-1 through MW-4 and MW-7 using a battery-operated water level meter and an Oakton ORP meter. Depth to water measurements were obtained from a surveyed reference point at the top of the well casings (TOC). Monitoring wells MW-5, MW-6, and MW-8 through MW-10 were dry or had insufficient water to purge and sample. In April 2017, depth to groundwater measured in wells MW-1 through MW-4 and MW-7 ranged from 35.52 to 40.73 feet below TOC."}}

General Minerals:
Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 24 mg/L, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/L. {{quote: "Nitrate as nitrogen was reported for the groundwater samples collected from MW-1 through MW-4 and MW-7 at concentrations ranging from 2.6 to 24 mg/l, three of which are greater than the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l."}} TDS was reported at 550 mg/L, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L. {{quote: "TDS was reported for the samples collected from MW-1 through MW-4 and MW-7 at concentrations ranging from 230 to 550, three of which exceed the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l."}} Sulfide was reported at 0.18 mg/L. {{quote: "Nitrate as nitrogen was reported for the groundwater samples collected from MW-1 through MW-4 and MW-7 at concentrations ranging from 2.6 to 24 mg/l, three of which are greater than the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported for the samples collected from MW-1 through MW-4 and MW-7 at concentrations ranging from 230 to 550, three of which exceed the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. Sulfide was reported for the samples collected from MW-1 through MW-4 and MW-7 at concentrations ranging from 0.016 to 0.18 mg/l."}}

The Stiff diagram for April 2017 indicated a calcium-dominant composition. {{quote: "In April 2017, the samples from wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, and MW-7 had a calcium-dominant composition while the sample from well MW-3 had a sodiumdominant composition."}}

Dissolved Metals:
No dissolved metals concentrations exceeded applicable numeric water quality thresholds. {{quote: "With the exception of manganese in the groundwater sample collected from well MW-1, none of the reported dissolved metals concentrations for the groundwater samples collected in April 2017 exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values."}}
