MW-1 is a groundwater monitoring well located at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, installed in 2006 as part of the initial monitoring network. It is considered an upgradient well, providing baseline data for groundwater quality in the vicinity of the three soil stockpiles.

## Groundwater Monitoring Results
