## MW-1

### Overview

MW-1 is an upgradient groundwater monitoring well at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site in Stanislaus County, California. It is one of ten wells ([[mw-1]] through [[mw-10]]) completed in the shallow zone of the upper aquifer, within the unconsolidated sand, silty sand, and silt layers of the Modesto Formation. These wells are situated approximately 35 feet below ground surface, under unconfined to semi-confined conditions. MW-1 was installed in 2006 and serves as a key upgradient point to distinguish site-related groundwater impacts from natural background conditions, particularly for nitrate and total dissolved solids (TDS).

### Site Record

#### September 2013 Sampling Event

On September 4, 2013, the depth to groundwater at MW-1 was measured at 33.74 feet below the top of casing (TOC). This was the shallowest depth recorded among the ten wells sampled.

General Minerals:
*   Nitrate as nitrogen: 12 mg/L, exceeding the California primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) of 10 mg/L.
*   Total dissolved solids (TDS): 590 mg/L, exceeding the California secondary MCL of 500 mg/L.
*   Sulfide: 0.28 mg/L, which was the highest concentration detected among the six wells where sulfide was present.

Stiff diagram analysis classified the September 2013 groundwater sample from MW-1 as calcium-dominant, consistent with the water chemistry observed at [[mw-2]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-7]], [[mw-9]], and [[mw-10]]. This calcium-dominant signature has been consistently observed at MW-1 since groundwater monitoring resumed in March 2012.

Dissolved Metals:
No dissolved metals concentrations in the September 2013 sampling event exceeded applicable numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium were reported at concentrations consistent with historical levels and remained significantly below their respective numeric water quality thresholds. Lead was not detected at or above the practical quantitation limit (PQL) of 1.0 µg/L.

#### June 2014 Sampling Event

On June 4, 2014, the depth to groundwater at MW-1 was measured at 35.09 feet below TOC. This measurement contributed to the determination of groundwater flow toward the south-southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008.

General Minerals:
*   Nitrate as nitrogen: 15 mg/L, exceeding the California primary MCL of 10 mg/L. The report notes that as MW-1 is an upgradient well, this concentration "may be indicative of natural background nitrate concentrations for the shallow groundwater in the vicinity of the Site."
*   Total dissolved solids (TDS): The report indicates that TDS concentrations for samples from MW-1, [[mw-4]], [[mw-5]], [[mw-6]], and [[mw-10]] exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L. The specific value for MW-1 is not explicitly stated in this section but is implied to be above the threshold.
*   Sulfide: 0.12 mg/L.

Stiff diagrams for June 2014 indicated that MW-1 had a calcium-dominant composition, consistent with [[mw-2]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-7]], [[mw-9]], and [[mw-10]].

Dissolved Metals:
No dissolved metals concentrations reported for MW-1 in June 2014 exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium concentrations were similar to historical levels and remained significantly below their numeric water quality thresholds.

#### April 2017 Sampling Event

On April 12, 2017, the depth to groundwater at MW-1 was measured at 35.52 feet below TOC.

General Minerals:
Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 24 mg/L, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/L. TDS was reported at 550 mg/L, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L. Sulfide was reported at 0.18 mg/L.

The Stiff diagram for April 2017 indicated a calcium-dominant composition.

Dissolved Metals:
No dissolved metals concentrations exceeded applicable numeric water quality thresholds, with the exception of manganese in the groundwater sample collected from well MW-1.

#### April 2019 Sampling Event

In April 2019, the depth to groundwater at MW-1 was measured at 34.22 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was determined to be toward the south at an average gradient of 0.01.

General Minerals:
Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 24 mg/L, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/L. TDS was reported at 550 mg/L, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L.

### Regulatory Status

MW-1 is subject to the California primary MCL for nitrate as nitrogen (10 mg/L) and the secondary MCL for TDS (500 mg/L). Concentrations of nitrate as nitrogen have exceeded the primary MCL in multiple sampling events, and TDS has exceeded the secondary MCL in multiple sampling events. As an upgradient well, these exceedances are noted as potentially indicative of natural background conditions.
