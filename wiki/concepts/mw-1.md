## Overview

MW-1 is a groundwater monitoring well located at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It was installed in 2006 as part of the initial monitoring network (MW-1 through MW-8) adjacent to the three soil stockpiles. MW-1 is considered an upgradient monitoring well.

## Site record

Groundwater monitoring at MW-1 has been conducted periodically since its installation.

*   **March 2012:** Depth to groundwater was measured at 30.12 feet below the top of the well casing (TOC). Groundwater flow was toward the southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008.
*   **May 2012:** Depth to groundwater was 29.74 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the southeast at an average gradient of 0.0006. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 12 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) was reported at 460 mg/l, below the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   **September 2012:** Depth to groundwater was 32.73 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 12 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 460 mg/l, below the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   **November 2012:** Depth to groundwater was 32.28 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 12 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 420 mg/l, below the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   **March 2013:** Depth to groundwater was 31.15 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the east-southeast at an average gradient of 0.0006. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 13 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 500 mg/l, meeting the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. Manganese was detected at concentrations exceeding the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l.
*   **September 2013:** Depth to groundwater was 33.74 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the southeast at an average gradient of 0.0007. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 12 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 590 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   **December 2013:** Depth to groundwater was 33.46 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the southeast at an average gradient of 0.001. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 13 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 500 mg/l, meeting the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   **February 2014:** Depth to groundwater was 33.40 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 10 mg/l, meeting the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 440 mg/l, below the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   **June 2014:** Depth to groundwater was 35.09 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the south-southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 15 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 530 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   **April 2017:** Depth to groundwater was 35.52 feet below TOC. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 9.2 mg/l, below the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 420 mg/l, below the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. Stiff diagrams indicate calcium-dominant water.
*   **April 2019:** Depth to groundwater was 36.36 feet below TOC. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 2.3 mg/l, below the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 300 mg/l, below the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. Stiff diagrams indicate calcium-dominant water.

## Regulatory status

*   **Nitrate as nitrogen:** Exceeded the California primary MCL of 10 mg/l in multiple sampling events between May 2012 and June 2014.
*   **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS):** Exceeded the California secondary MCL of 500 mg/l in multiple sampling events between September 2013 and June 2014.
