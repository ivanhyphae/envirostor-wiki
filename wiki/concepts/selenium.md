## Selenium

### Overview
Selenium is a contaminant that has been detected in surface water samples collected at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. Its presence has been noted in samples taken during various sampling events, including those conducted after the completion of road construction and encapsulation of the stockpiles.

### Site Record
Selenium was detected in surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023. {{quote: "The following metals were detected in one or more of the surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023: arsenic, barium, chromium, copper, lead, molybdenum, selenium, vanadium, zinc, strontium, and mercury."}} The laboratory flagged some of these data with a "J" value, indicating an estimated concentration. {{quote: "The laboratory flagged some of the data with a 'J' value, meaning that the value is estimated."}} Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, nickel, silver, and thallium were not detected above the Method Detection Limits (MDLs) in the background and surface water samples collected on this date. {{quote: "Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, nickel, silver, and thallium were not reported above the MDLs in the background and surface water samples."}}

In a surface water sampling event on October 28, 2016, selenium was reported for sample PL5. {{quote: "Selenium was reported for sample PL5."}} The report notes that none of the reported concentrations for dissolved metals, including selenium, exceeded their respective primary or secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs).

In a surface water sampling event on March 24, 2017, selenium was reported for sample PL2. {{quote: "Selenium was reported for sample PL2."}} Similar to the October 2016 results, none of the reported dissolved metal concentrations, including selenium, exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs.

In a surface water sampling event on January 8, 2018, selenium was not detected in the samples at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective laboratory reporting limits. {{quote: "Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected in the samples at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective laboratory reporting limits."}}

### Regulatory Status
The reported dissolved metal detections, including selenium, from the March 10, 2023 sampling event were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such standards exist. {{quote: "The reported metal detections were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such exist."}}
