## Selenium

### Overview
Selenium was detected in surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023, as part of the remediation case file for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles located on SR 132 in Stanislaus County, California. The detection occurred after road construction was completed and the stockpiles were encapsulated.

### Site Record
On March 10, 2023, selenium was detected in surface water samples. {{quote: "The following metals were detected in one or more of the surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023: arsenic, barium, chromium, copper, lead, molybdenum, selenium, vanadium, zinc, strontium, and mercury."}} The laboratory flagged some of the data with a "J" value, indicating an estimated concentration. {{quote: "The laboratory flagged some of the data with a 'J' value, meaning that the value is estimated."}} Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, nickel, silver, and thallium were not detected above the Method Detection Limits (MDLs). {{quote: "Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, nickel, silver, and thallium were not reported above the MDLs in the background and surface water samples."}}

### Regulatory Status
The reported dissolved metal detections, including selenium, were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such standards exist. {{quote: "The reported metal detections were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such exist."}}
