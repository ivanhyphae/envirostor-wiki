## Interagency Agreement 43a0260

The Interagency Agreement 43a0260, specifically Task Order Number 6, established the contractual relationship between the State of California, Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, also identified as "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles - State Route 132/99".

## Site record

This Task Order was effective from June 22, 2012, through May 31, 2013, with an execution date of June 22, 2012. {{quote: "Term of Task Order: June 22, 2012 through May 31, 2013"}} {{quote: "Execution Date: June 22, 2012"}} The total allocated amount for this task order was \$150,000.00. {{quote: "FY 11/12: \$ 150,000.00"}} {{quote: "Total Task Order Amount: \$ 150,000.00"}}

The agreement incorporates by reference all terms, conditions, duties, and obligations of State Agreement 43A0260. In instances of conflict between Agreement 43A0260 and this Task Order, the Task Order takes precedence, except for administrative and financial matters, where Agreement 43A0260 prevails. {{quote: "The execution of this Task Order shall incorporate by this reference all terms, conditions, duties and obligations attendant upon the parties as set forth in State Agreement 43A0260. If any conflicts exist between that of Agreement 43A0260 and this Task Order, the Task Order will prevail except with respect to administrative and financial matters, in that case, Agreement 43A0260 will prevail."}}

The DTSC Task Order Manager for this agreement was Randy Adams, and the Caltrans Task Manager was Richard Stewart. Caltrans' Contract Manager was Scott Nelson. {{quote: "DTSC Task Order Manager: Randy Adams"}} {{quote: "Caltrans Task Manager: Richard Stewart"}} {{quote: "Caltrans Contract Manager: Scott Nelson"}}

## Regulatory status

This agreement served as the contractual basis for DTSC's involvement in the remediation of soil stockpiles at the State Route 132/99 location in Modesto, as managed by Caltrans. {{quote: "Consultant Agreement: 43A0260 Caltrans/Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC); DTSC #08-T3616"}} {{quote: "Task Order Title: Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles - State Route 132/99"}}
