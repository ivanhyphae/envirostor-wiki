The Interagency Agreement 43a0260 is a consultant agreement between the State of California, Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). This agreement, specifically Task Order Number 6, covers the "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles - State Route 132/99" project. The term of this task order was from June 22, 2012, through May 31, 2013, with an execution date of June 22, 2012. The total amount for this task order was \$150,000.00.

## Site record

The agreement incorporates by reference all terms, conditions, duties, and obligations of State Agreement 43A0260. In cases of conflict between Agreement 43A0260 and this Task Order, the Task Order prevails, except for administrative and financial matters, where Agreement 43A0260 takes precedence. {{quote: "Consultant Agreement: 43A0260 Caltrans/Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC); DTSC #08-T3616"}} {{quote: "Task Order Number: 6"}} {{quote: "Task Order Title: Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles - State Route 132/99"}} {{quote: "Term of Task Order: June 22, 2012 through May 31, 2013"}} {{quote: "Execution Date: June 22, 2012"}} {{quote: "FY 11/12: \$ 150,000.00"}} {{quote: "Total Task Order Amount: \$ 150,000.00"}} {{quote: "The execution of this Task Order shall incorporate by this reference all terms, conditions, duties and obligations attendant upon the parties as set forth in State Agreement 43A0260. ## Regulatory status

This agreement establishes the contractual framework between Caltrans and DTSC for the remediation of soil stockpiles at the State Route 132/99 location in Modesto.
