## Interagency Agreement Task Order No 97

Interagency Agreement Task Order No 97 (TO 97) was established to address the remediation of soil stockpiles at the SR 132 site in Stanislaus County, California. This task order was executed between the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and [[stantec-consulting-services-inc]]. The scope of TO 97 included groundwater and surface water investigations and reporting related to the soil stockpiles.

## Site Record

Under TO 97, groundwater samples were collected and analyzed. During the [[groundwater-monitoring-round]] conducted in the first quarter of 2023, groundwater samples from monitoring well [[mw-10]] showed a concentration of 1,2-dichloroethane at 0.0039 parts per billion (ppb). In the same monitoring round, monitoring well [[mw-4]] had a concentration of 1,2-dichloroethane at 0.0035 ppb. The [[depth-to-water]] in MW-10 during this period was 14.7 feet below ground surface.

Surface water samples were also collected and analyzed under TO 97. The final surface water letter report, dated March 9, 2023, documented these findings.
