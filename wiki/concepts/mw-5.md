# MW-5

## Overview
MW-5 is a groundwater monitoring well installed at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site in Stanislaus County, California. It is part of the initial network of monitoring wells installed in 2006. MW-5 is notable for reporting concentrations of nitrate as nitrogen and total dissolved solids (TDS) that have exceeded applicable water quality thresholds.

## Site record
During the [[september-2013-sampling-event]], the depth to groundwater at MW-5 was measured at 42.05 feet below the top of casing (TOC). Groundwater elevation data from this event indicated a flow direction towards the southeast with an average gradient of 0.0007.

In September 2013, nitrate as nitrogen was reported at a concentration of 36 mg/l in the sample from MW-5, exceeding the primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) of 10 mg/l. This was the highest nitrate concentration reported among the ten monitoring wells during that sampling event.

Total dissolved solids (TDS) were reported at a concentration of 750 mg/l in MW-5, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. This was also the highest TDS concentration among the ten wells in September 2013.

Sulfide was reported at a concentration of 0.012 mg/l in the MW-5 sample collected in September 2013, as documented in the laboratory report (Lab ID: 1302710-02).

The dissolved metals analytical results for MW-5 from September 2013, analyzed via EPA Method 6020 (ICP-MS) and EPA Method 7470A, did not show any concentrations exceeding their respective numeric water quality threshold values.

A review of nine recent groundwater sampling events (March, May, June, July, September, and November 2012; January, March, and June 2013) indicated that MW-5 has historically reported nitrate and TDS concentrations exceeding water quality thresholds. Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations have exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/l in MW-5 across multiple events, and TDS concentrations have exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l over the same period.

On the Stiff diagrams for the September 2013 event, MW-5 was classified as sodium-dominant. This classification differed from historical sampling events, where MW-5 was described as consistently calcium-dominant. The September 2013 report suggests this shift "may be indicative of a mixing of different water types at this location."

In the June 2014 sampling event, the depth to groundwater at MW-5 was measured at 42.86 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the south-southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008. In June 2014, nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 29 mg/l in MW-5, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 680 mg/l in MW-5, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. The Stiff diagram for MW-5 in June 2014 indicated a sodium-dominant composition.

## Regulatory status
The September 2013 monitoring results for MW-5 were evaluated against the following water quality threshold values:
*   **Primary MCLs** (California Department of Public Health): Nitrate as nitrogen (10 mg/l) was exceeded.
*   **Secondary MCLs** (California Department of Public Health): TDS (500 mg/l) was exceeded.

No exceedances of dissolved metals thresholds were reported for MW-5.

MW-5 was among the wells (along with MW-1, MW-4, MW-6, and MW-10) where both the primary MCL for nitrate and the secondary MCL for TDS were exceeded in September 2013.

## Open items
The shift in MW-5's ionic composition from calcium-dominant to sodium-dominant in 2013 was flagged for continued monitoring in the September 2013 report, as it may indicate mixing of different water types at this location. The September 2013 report does not document any formal regulatory action, enforcement, or remedial decision specifically tied to MW-5's exceedances. Groundwater monitoring was scheduled to continue quarterly, with the next event anticipated in December 2013.
