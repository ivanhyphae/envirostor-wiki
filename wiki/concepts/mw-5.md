## Site record
On March 12, 2012, the depth to groundwater at monitoring well MW-5 was measured at 38.74 feet below the top of the casing (TOC). On September 19, 2012, the depth to groundwater at MW-5 was 41.19 feet below TOC. On November 28, 2012, the depth to groundwater at MW-5 was 41.18 feet below TOC. On January 22, 2013, the depth to groundwater at MW-5 was 39.62 feet below TOC. On March 18, 2013, the depth to groundwater at MW-5 was 39.62 feet below TOC. On January 22, 2013, nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 30 mg/l in MW-5, and TDS was reported at 680 mg/l in MW-5. On February 25, 2014, nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 34 mg/l in MW-5, and TDS was reported at 750 mg/l in MW-5. On April 12, 2017, MW-5 was dry. On May 2, 2016, MW-5 was dry or had insufficient water to purge and sample.

In March 2012, MW-5 went dry after purging 1 gallon. In May 2012, MW-5 went dry after purging 1 gallon. In September 2012, MW-5 went dry after purging 0.65 gallon. In November 2012, MW-5 went dry after purging 0.75 gallon. In January 2013, MW-5 went dry after purging 1 gallon. In March 2013, MW-5 went dry after purging 1 gallon. In February 2014, MW-5 went dry after purging 1 gallon.

In March 2012, the groundwater sample from MW-5 was calcium-dominant. In May 2012, the sample from MW-5 changed from sodium-dominant to calcium-dominant water. In September 2013, the sample from MW-5 was sodium-dominant. In December 2013, the sample from MW-5 was sodium-dominant. In February 2014, the samples from MW-3, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-8 were sodium-dominant.

In March 2012, nitrate concentrations for MW-5 were 26 mg/l. In May 2012, nitrate concentrations for MW-5 were 26 mg/l. In September 2013, nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-5 were 36 mg/l. In December 2013, nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-5 were 25 mg/l. In February 2014, nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-5 were 34 mg/l.

In March 2012, TDS was reported at greater than the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l for the sample collected from MW-5. In May 2012, TDS was reported at concentrations greater than the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l for the samples collected from wells MW-4, MW-5 and MW-6. In September 2013, TDS concentrations for samples from MW-5 exceeded the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l. In December 2013, TDS concentrations for samples from MW-5 equal or exceed the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l. In February 2014, TDS concentrations for samples from MW-5 equal or exceed the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l.

In April 2019, manganese was reported for the sample collected from MW-5 at a concentration of 59 µg/l, which exceeds its secondary MCL.
