## Overview

**Basin 5 Clean Cover Material** refers to the engineered fill material specified for placement over Basin 5 at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). Its composition, handling, and placement are governed by the *FW_ SR 132 Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan* (the Sampling Plan), which establishes the material’s quality criteria and the sampling protocols required to verify that it meets regulatory standards for a clean cover system. The material functions as a final barrier or grading layer after remediation of underlying stockpile soils, separating clean surface use from residual contamination.

- **Document source:** *FW_ SR 132 Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan* (exact date not provided in the available record).

- **Sampling frequency and methods:** The Plan specifies number of samples per volume of material, sample locations (e.g., stockpile grid cells), laboratory analytical methods, and quality assurance/quality control procedures.
- **Material source (if applicable):** The Plan may identify approved borrow sources or require on‑site characterization if material is imported; details are not provided in the excerpt.
- **Verification:** After placement, the Plan requires confirmation sampling to demonstrate that the cover system meets thickness, compaction, and chemical specifications. No verification results are provided in the available document.

## Regulatory status

The Sampling Plan was prepared for submission to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and other responsible agencies as part of the remedial action approval process for Basin 5. The material must comply with applicable DTSC clean‑fill criteria and the site’s Remedial Action Plan (RAP). Current compliance status (approved, pending, or conditional) is not documented in the single source provided.
