# Chromium VI (Hexavalent Chromium)

## Overview
Chromium VI (Hexavalent Chromium) is identified as a T22 metal requiring consideration within the project's Health and Safety Plan (HSP). The project's regulatory framework references T8 CCR §1532.2 concerning Chromium VI.

## Site Record
The project's Health and Safety Plan (HSP) notes that T8 CCR §1532.2, concerning Chromium VI, is one of the regulations that may apply to monitoring and exposure assessment records. {{quote: "Note: Monitoring and exposure assessment records will be maintained in accordance with the provisions of T22 metals, including T8 CCR §§5214 – Inorganic Arsenic, 1532 – Cadmium, 1532.1 – Lead, 1532.2 – Chromium VI, and 3204 Access to Employee Exposure and Medical Records."}}

The Project Safety Officer (PSO) is responsible for performing air monitoring as specified, which includes "Chromium VI Exposure Determination" per T8 CCR §1532.2(d). {{quote: "- Performing air monitoring, if and as specified (ref: T8 CCR §§1529(n), 5214(e) Arsenic Exposure Monitoring), 1532(d) Cadmium Exposure Monitoring, 1532.1(d) Lead Exposure Assessment, and 1532.2(d) Chromium VI Exposure Determination;"}}

General site employees are trained on potential hazards, including exposure to T22 metals such as Chromium VI, as part of their project orientation. {{quote: "The training will also address other Cal/OSHA requirements such as the Geocon Hazard Communication Program (T8 CCR §5194), including the potential hazards of exposure to T22 metals (T8 CCR §§5214 – Inorganic Arsenic, 1532 – Cadmium, 1532.1 – Lead, 1532.2 – Chromium VI), and the Injury and Illness Prevention Program (T8 CCR §3203; CSO §1509)."}}

Medical surveillance requirements, as specified under T8 CCR §1532.2 Chromium VI, were determined to be not required or justified for staff assigned to this project based on air monitoring data collected in September 2012 for barium and inorganic lead. {{quote: "Therefore, medical surveillance as specified under T8 CCR §§5214 Arsenic, 1532 Cadmium, 1532.1 Lead and 1532.2 Chromium VI is not required or justified for staff assigned to this project."}}

Chemical hazard information for Chromium VI is available in Attachment A of the NIOSH Guide to Chemical Hazards. {{quote: "The hazards of Chromium VI, T8 CCR 1532.2, and Barium Sulfate are covered in the Attachments A and E respectively of the NIOSH Guides."}} The exposure route for Chromium VI is inhalation and ingestion, referencing Attachment A - NIOSH Guide Page 5. {{quote: "Exposure Route: Inhalation and ingestion; ref. Attachment A - NIOSH Guide Page 5"}}
