# Chromium VI (Hexavalent Chromium)

## Overview
Chromium VI (Hexavalent Chromium) is identified as a T22 metal requiring consideration within the project's Health and Safety Plan (HSP). The project's regulatory framework references T8 CCR §1532.2 concerning Chromium VI.

## Site Record
The project's Health and Safety Plan (HSP) notes that T8 CCR §1532.2, concerning Chromium VI, is one of the regulations that may apply to monitoring and exposure assessment records. The Project Safety Officer (PSO) is responsible for performing air monitoring as specified, which includes "Chromium VI Exposure Determination" per T8 CCR §1532.2(d). General site employees are trained on potential hazards, including exposure to T22 metals such as Chromium VI, as part of their project orientation. Medical surveillance requirements, as specified under T8 CCR §1532.2 Chromium VI, were determined to be not required or justified for staff assigned to this project based on air monitoring data collected in September 2012 for barium and inorganic lead. Chemical hazard information for Chromium VI is available in Attachment A of the NIOSH Guide to Chemical Hazards. The exposure route for Chromium VI is inhalation and ingestion.

## Regulatory Status
The project references T8 CCR §1532.2 concerning Chromium VI.
