## Overview
Stockpile consolidation is a remedial action undertaken as part of the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. This process involves managing and consolidating [[barium-containing-soil]] within designated stockpiles, specifically [[bcs-stockpiles-1-and-2]], as outlined in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]]. The primary objective is to prepare these stockpiles for final capping and encapsulation.

## Site record
The [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) for the Interim Phase 1 of the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project, prepared by [[geocon-consultants]], Inc. for [[caltrans]], identifies three soil stockpiles: STOCKPILE 1, STOCKPILE 2, and STOCKPILE 3. {{quote: "STOCKPILE 1, STOCKPILE 2, and STOCKPILE 3"}} These stockpiles are located near the Former FMC Facility and Former Disposal Pond areas. {{quote: "Former FMC Facility", "Former Disposal Pond"}}

Within the RDIP, specific removal and fill areas for barium-containing soil and associated waste (ADL) are detailed. Stockpile 1 has no planned removal, while Stockpile 2 has planned removals for the Emerald Avenue bridge abutment (2,200 yd3) and the SR-99 bridge abutment (1,650 yd3). Stockpile 3 is slated for complete removal (24,000 yd3). {{quote: "Stockpile 1 - no planned removal", "Stockpile 2 - Emerald Avenue bridge abutment (2,200 yd3 ) and SR-99 bridge abutment (1,650 yd3 )", "Stockpile 3 - complete removal (24,000 yd3 )"}} The total estimated barium-containing soil removal volume is 27,850 yd3. {{quote: "Total estimated barium-containing soil removal volume = 27,850 yd3"}}

Consolidation areas for barium-containing soil and ADL at Stockpiles 1 and 2 are designated for fill placement. These include the upper surface and southern slope area of Stockpile 1 (15,000 yd3), an area in the State ROW between Stockpile 1 and the Emerald Avenue bridge abutment (12,500 yd3), and the upper surface and southern slope area of Stockpile 2 (27,000 yd3). {{quote: "Stockpile 1 - upper surface and southern slope area (15,000 yd3 )", "State ROW - between Stockpile 1 and Emerald Avenue bridge abutment (12,500 yd3 )", "Stockpile 2 - upper surface and southern slope area (27,000 yd3 )"}} The total estimated barium/ADL consolidation area volume is 54,500 yd3. {{quote: "Total estimated barium/ADL consolidation area volume = 54,500 yd3"}} This leaves an estimated 26,650 yd3 of stockpile consolidation area available for ADL soil fill placement. {{quote: "approximately 26,650 yd3 of soil stockpile consolidation area is available for fill placement of ADL soil."}}

Typical cross-sections of completed Interim Phase 1 construction depict the placement of minimum 12-inch-thick clean cover fill material over the barium-containing soil and ADL consolidation zones. {{quote: "Placement of minimum 12-inch-thick clean cover fill material over the barium-containing soil and ADL consolidation zones."}} Alternatively, a minimum 4-inch-thick aggregate base (AB) may be placed on the upper stockpile surface pending subsequent placement of structural pavement and hardscape. {{quote: "minimum 4-inch-thick aggregate base (AB) materials may be placed on the upper stockpile surface pending subsequent placement of structural pavement and hardscape."}}

A Variance to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP Variance), prepared by [[geocon-consultants]], Inc. for [[caltrans]] and submitted to the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) on April 9, 2020, addresses the capping of consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) within Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "VARIANCE TO REMEDIAL DESIGN IMPLEMENTATION PLAN", "CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES", "INTERIM PHASE 1 STATE ROUTE 132 WEST PROJECT MODESTO, STANISLAUS COUNTY, CALIFORNIA", "April 9, 2020"}} This variance discusses the use of clean cover fill materials. The planned highway underpass excavation area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road was identified as a suitable source for clean fill material, with DTSC providing approval of this source. {{quote: "DTSC subsequently provided approval of the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area."}} An additional source of clean fill material was identified at proposed mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "An additional source of clean fill material was identified by the highway contractor at the proposed mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations (approximately five-foot-deep) located along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2."}}

The Ultimate Phase 2 buildout will involve the construction of an MSE retaining wall along the northerly SR-132 ROW adjacent to Stockpiles 1 and 2, with clean imported fill material placed over the capped stockpile slopes to complete final encapsulation of barium-containing soil. {{quote: "Construction of the Ultimate Phase 2 buildout will complete final encapsulation of barium containing soil within Stockpiles 1 and 2."}}

A draft Remedial Action Completion Report will be submitted to DTSC approximately 30 days after the completion of Interim Phase I soil stockpile monitoring activities. This report will include details on observed conditions during stockpile grading/containment, volumes of excavated and placed material, analytical results, imported clean cover fill material documentation, and as-built drawings of completed consolidation zones. {{quote: "A draft Remedial Action Completion Report will be prepared by a California Professional Engineer and submitted to DTSC approximately 30 days following the completion of the Interim Phase I soil stockpile monitoring activities.", "Source, quantity and laboratory documentation for imported clean cover fill materials", "Stockpile consolidation zone survey data and exhibits (i.e. signed and sealed as-built drawings)"}}

## Regulatory status
The management and consolidation of barium-containing soil stockpiles are governed by the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). A variance to the RDIP was issued, indicating specific approvals and conditions related to the sourcing and placement of clean cover fill materials for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "VARIANCE TO REMEDIAL DESIGN IMPLEMENTATION PLAN", "DTSC"}}

## Open items
The sources indicate that the Ultimate Phase 2 buildout will complete the final encapsulation of barium-containing soil within Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "Construction of the Ultimate Phase 2 buildout will complete final encapsulation of barium containing soil within Stockpiles 1 and 2."}}
