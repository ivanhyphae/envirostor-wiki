## Overview
Stockpile consolidation is a remedial action implemented as part of the [[sr-132-west-project]] in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. This process involves the management and consolidation of [[barium-containing-soil]] within designated stockpiles, specifically [[bcs-stockpiles-1-and-2]], as detailed in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]]. The primary objective of stockpile consolidation is to prepare these stockpiles for final capping and encapsulation.

## Site record
The [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) for the Interim Phase 1 of the [[sr-132-west-project]], prepared by [[geocon-consultants]], Inc. for [[caltrans]], identifies three soil stockpiles: STOCKPILE 1, STOCKPILE 2, and STOCKPILE 3. These stockpiles are situated near the Former FMC Facility and Former Disposal Pond areas.

Within the RDIP, specific removal and fill areas for barium-containing soil and associated waste (ADL) are delineated. Stockpile 1 has no planned removal. Stockpile 2 has planned removals for the Emerald Avenue bridge abutment (2,200 yd3) and the SR-99 bridge abutment (1,650 yd3). Stockpile 3 is designated for complete removal, estimated at 24,000 yd3. The total estimated volume of barium-containing soil removal is 27,850 yd3.

Consolidation areas for barium-containing soil and ADL at Stockpiles 1 and 2 are designated for fill placement. These include the upper surface and southern slope area of Stockpile 1 (15,000 yd3), an area in the State ROW between Stockpile 1 and the Emerald Avenue bridge abutment (12,500 yd3), and the upper surface and southern slope area of Stockpile 2 (27,000 yd3). The total estimated volume for barium/ADL consolidation areas is 54,500 yd3. This leaves an estimated 26,650 yd3 of stockpile consolidation area available for ADL soil fill placement.

Typical cross-sections of completed Interim Phase 1 construction illustrate the placement of a minimum 12-inch-thick clean cover fill material over the barium-containing soil and ADL consolidation zones. Alternatively, a minimum 4-inch-thick aggregate base (AB) may be placed on the upper stockpile surface, pending subsequent placement of structural pavement and hardscape.

A Variance to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP Variance), prepared by [[geocon-consultants]], Inc. for [[caltrans]] and submitted to the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) on April 9, 2020, addresses the capping of consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) within Stockpiles 1 and 2. This variance details the use of clean cover fill materials. The planned highway underpass excavation area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road was identified as a suitable source for clean fill material, with DTSC providing approval of this source. An additional source of clean fill material was identified at proposed mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2.

The Ultimate Phase 2 buildout will involve the construction of an MSE retaining wall along the northerly SR-132 ROW adjacent to Stockpiles 1 and 2. Clean imported fill material will be placed over the capped stockpile slopes to complete final encapsulation of barium-containing soil.

A draft Remedial Action Completion Report will be submitted to DTSC approximately 30 days after the completion of Interim Phase I soil stockpile monitoring activities. This report will include details on observed conditions during stockpile grading/containment, volumes of excavated and placed material, analytical results, imported clean cover fill material documentation, and as-built drawings of completed consolidation zones.

## Regulatory status
The management and consolidation of barium-containing soil stockpiles are governed by the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). A variance to the RDIP was issued, outlining specific approvals and conditions related to the sourcing and placement of clean cover fill materials for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2.

## Open items
The Ultimate Phase 2 buildout is planned to complete the final encapsulation of barium-containing soil within Stockpiles 1 and 2.
