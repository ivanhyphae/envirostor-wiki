## Overview
Stockpile consolidation refers to the process of combining soil stockpiles as part of the remediation efforts for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. This action is a component of the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] for the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project in Stanislaus County, California. The primary goal is to manage and potentially treat soil that has been excavated and stored on-site.

## Site record
The [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] for the interim Phase 1 of the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project, prepared by [[geocon-consultants]], Inc. for Caltrans, outlines the management of soil stockpiles. The project area includes identified barium-containing soil stockpiles. An aerial site map within the Remedial Design Implementation Plan depicts three designated stockpiles labeled STOCKPILE 1, STOCKPILE 2, and STOCKPILE 3. {{quote: "STOCKPILE 1, STOCKPILE 2, and STOCKPILE 3"}} These stockpiles are located in proximity to the Former FMC Facility and Former Disposal Pond areas. {{quote: "Former FMC Facility", "Former Disposal Pond"}} The plan addresses the management of these stockpiles as part of the overall remediation strategy.
