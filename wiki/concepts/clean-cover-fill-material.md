## Overview
Clean Cover Fill Material refers to soil excavated from designated borrow areas within the [[sr-132]] right-of-way, intended for use in capping consolidated [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) within [[bcs-stockpiles-1-and-2]]. The suitability of this material is determined by its chemical characteristics, specifically its compliance with criteria for clean imported fill soil, as approved by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).

## Site record
Two primary areas have been identified and characterized as sources for clean cover fill material: the Basin 5 Clean Cap Borrow Area and the BCS Stockpiles Clean Cap Borrow Area, both located adjacent and easterly of Carpenter Road within the [[sr-132]] right-of-way. These areas were historically used for agricultural purposes.

### Basin 5 Clean Cap Borrow Area
Characterization testing was performed at the Basin 5 clean cap borrow area, which is approximately 750 feet long, 100 to 200 feet wide, and excavated to an approximate depth of 10 feet. Soil sampling occurred on June 16, 2020, with 6 exploratory trenches (BASIN5 1 through 6) excavated to a maximum depth of 10 feet. Samples were collected at depth intervals of 0 to 2 feet, 2 to 5 feet, and 5 to 10 feet. These were composited into 3-part composites for each depth interval, yielding two 3-part composite samples per depth.

Analysis of these composite samples for Title 22 metals revealed the following concentration ranges (in milligrams per kilogram [mg/kg]), with site-specific background concentrations in parentheses:
*   Arsenic: <1.0 to 1.8 (1.2 mg/kg) {{quote: "Arsenic <1.0 to 1.8 (1.2 mg/kg)"}}
*   Barium: 66 to 94 (72.8 mg/kg) {{quote: "Barium 66 to 94 (72.8 mg/kg)"}}
*   Chromium: 8.5 to 11 (8.6 mg/kg) {{quote: "Chromium 8.5 to 11 (8.6 mg/kg1 )"}}
*   Cobalt: 3.3 to 3.8 (4.4 mg/kg) {{quote: "Cobalt 3.3 to 3.8 (4.4 mg/kg)"}}
*   Copper: 7.5 to 9.3 (7.5 mg/kg) {{quote: "Copper 7.5 to 9.3 (7.5 mg/kg)"}}
*   Lead: 5.8 to 11 (2.0 mg/kg) {{quote: "Lead 5.8 to 11 (2.0 mg/kg)"}}
*   Nickel: 6.2 to 7.9 (5.3 mg/kg) {{quote: "Nickel 6.2 to 7.9 (5.3 mg/kg)"}}
*   Vanadium: 33 to 37 (31.3 mg/kg) {{quote: "Vanadium 33 to 37 (31.3 mg/kg)"}}
*   Zinc: 29 to 36 (26.3 mg/kg) {{quote: "Zinc 29 to 36 (26.3 mg/kg)"}}

Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, molybdenum, selenium, silver, thallium, mercury, and organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) were not detected above laboratory reporting limits. {{quote: "Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, molybdenum, selenium, thallium, mercury and OCPs were not detected in each composite soil sample analyzed above the laboratory reporting limits."}} The reported metal concentrations were deemed consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. {{quote: "The reported metal concentrations in the composite samples are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations."}} Based on this data, the Basin 5 borrow area was determined suitable for generating clean cover fill material. {{quote: "Based on the data presented herein, the proposed Basin 5 clean cap borrow area appears to be suitable to generate clean cover fill materials for capping BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2."}} This determination was documented in a Technical Memorandum dated July 2, 2020. {{quote: "Date: Revised July 2, 2020"}}

### BCS Stockpiles Clean Cap Borrow Area
A separate characterization testing effort was conducted at a proposed clean cap borrow area, also located adjacent and easterly of Carpenter Road within the [[sr-132]] right-of-way. This area is approximately 1,250 feet long, 200 feet wide, and excavated to an approximate depth of 5 feet. Soil sampling was completed on December 21, 2020, using 12 direct-push borings (B1 through B12) to a maximum depth of 5 feet. Continuous core soil samples were collected at depth intervals of 0 to 2 feet and 2 to 5 feet. These were composited into 3-part composites, yielding four 3-part composite samples for each depth interval.

Analysis of these composite samples for Title 22 metals revealed the following concentration ranges (in milligrams per kilogram [mg/kg]), with site-specific background concentrations in parentheses:
*   Arsenic: 1.0 to 1.5 (1.2 mg/kg) {{quote: "Arsenic 1.0 to 1.5 (1.2 mg/kg)"}}
*   Barium: 60 to 110 (72.8 mg/kg) {{quote: "Barium 60 to 110 (72.8 mg/kg)"}}
*   Beryllium: 1.3 to 1.6 (0.2 mg/kg) {{quote: "Beryllium 1.3 to 1.6 (0.2 mg/kg)"}}
*   Chromium: 7.5 to 11 (7.1 mg/kg) {{quote: "Chromium 7.5 to 11 (7.1 mg/kg)"}}
*   Cobalt: 3.2 to 4.9 (4.4 mg/kg) {{quote: "Cobalt 3.2 to 4.9 (4.4 mg/kg)"}}
*   Copper: 5.3 to 9.9 (7.5 mg/kg) {{quote: "Copper 5.3 to 9.9 (7.5 mg/kg)"}}
*   Lead: 1.7 to 7.0 (2.0 mg/kg) {{quote: "Lead 1.7 to 7.0 (2.0 mg/kg)"}}
*   Nickel: 5.6 to 8.2 (5.3 mg/kg) {{quote: "Nickel 5.6 to 8.2 (5.3 mg/kg)"}}
*   Silver: 1.6 to 2.5 (0.2 mg/kg) {{quote: "Silver 1.6 to 2.5 (0.2 mg/kg)"}}

Antimony, cadmium, selenium, thallium, mercury, and OCPs were not detected above laboratory reporting limits. {{quote: "Antimony, cadmium, selenium, thallium, mercury and OCPs were not detected in each soil sample analyzed above the laboratory reporting limits."}} The reported metal concentrations were found to be consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. {{quote: "The reported metal concentrations in the composite samples are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations."}} This borrow area was also deemed suitable for generating clean cover fill material for capping [[bcs-stockpiles-1-and-2]]. {{quote: "Based on the data presented herein, the proposed clean cap borrow area appears to be suitable to generate clean cover fill materials for capping BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2."}} This determination was documented in a Technical Memorandum dated February 27, 2020. {{quote: "Date: February 27, 2020"}}

### Carpenter Road Shoofly
Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil will be excavated from the Carpenter Road Shoofly once the overcrossing structure under construction is completed. This excavated soil material is planned for use as clean capping material to be placed over portions of [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2, pending [[dtsc]] approval. Soil sampling was performed along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6). Soil samples were collected from the surface of the soil material exposed on the slope face at 2, 5, 10 and 15 feet from the top of the slope. Eight 3-part composite samples were created and analyzed for barium and lead.

Barium was detected at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg), less than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "Barium was detected in each of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg), less than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg."}} Lead was detected at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg."}} Based on this data, the soil material generated from excavation of the Carpenter Road Shoofly meets the BCS clean cap criteria as established in the referenced variance document as approved by DTSC. {{quote: "Based on the barium and lead data presented herein, soil material generated from excavation of the Carpenter Road Shoofly meet the BCS clean cap criteria as established in the referenced variance document as approved by DTSC (upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg)."}} This determination was documented in a Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021. {{quote: "Date: April 12, 2021"}}

## Regulatory status
The soil sampling strategy for the borrow areas was developed in general accordance with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and was approved by Mr. Dean Wright with DTSC. {{quote: "The soil sampling strategy utilized was developed in general accordance with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and was approved Mr. Dean Wright with DTSC."}}
