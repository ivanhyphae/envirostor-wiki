## Overview
Bg3 is a background surface water sampling location established as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. Its purpose is to provide a reference point for surface water quality in areas not directly impacted by the stockpiles.

## Site record
Background sample location BG3 was established on November 2, 2022, along the east side of North Carpenter Road, approximately 700 feet south of the southeast corner of North Carpenter Road and Kansas Avenue. {{quote: "Background sample BG2 was removed from the surface water sampling network and a new background sample BG3 was added. BG3 is located along the east side of North Carpenter Road approximately, 700 feet south of the southeast corner of North Carpenter Road and Kansas Avenue (Figure 2)."}}

During the surface water inspection on February 28, 2023, no visible water ponding was observed in the area around background surface water location BG3. {{quote: "There was no visible water ponding in the area around background surface water location BG3."}}

On March 10, 2023, Stantec collected a surface water sample from BG3. Although there was no visible water flowing into the catch basin at this location, there was ponding water within the catch basin. Stantec removed the catch basin grate and collected a sample of the water from within the catch basin. {{quote: "Background sample BG3 is typically collected from surface water entering the catch basin. There was no visible water flowing into the catch basin, however, there was ponding water within the catch basin. Per the direction of Caltrans, Stantec removed the catch basin grate and collected a sample of the water from within the catch basin (Appendix A, Photograph 13)."}}

The March 10, 2023, analytical results for sample BG3 indicated the presence of arsenic, barium, beryllium, and antimony, among other detected metals. {{quote: "The following metals were detected in one or more of the surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023: arsenic, barium, chromium, copper, lead, molybdenum, selenium, vanadium, zinc, strontium, and mercury. ... Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, nickel, silver, and thallium were not reported above the MDLs in the background and surface water samples."}} The specific concentration for Bg3 as a constituent is not detailed in the provided sources; rather, BG3 is the identifier for the background sample location.
