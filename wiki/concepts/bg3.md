Bg3 is a background surface water sampling location referenced in the site's stormwater monitoring correspondence with the regulator, alongside bg1 and bg2 as comparison points against the downgradient PL sampling locations (pl1 through pl5). Its role in the record is to establish what background surface water quality looks like independent of the stockpile source area, so that concentrations at the PL locations can be judged against it.

The two surface water letters in the record — dated March 9, 2023, and March 28, 2023 — present Bg3 among the background locations in the site's surface water sampling program. The March 28, 2023 letter is a revision or follow-up to the March 9, 2023 letter, both addressed to the same regulatory correspondence thread concerning surface water results at the SR 132 stockpile site.

The available material does not report specific analyte concentrations, sample dates, or exceedance determinations attributable to Bg3 by name; it identifies Bg3 as one of the background stations in the surface water sampling network described in the two letters, without giving the underlying data for that station. Because the letters compare downgradient results to background, Bg3's function is evidentiary — a clean or elevated reading at Bg3 would bear directly on whether contamination detected at pl1 through pl5 can be attributed to the stockpiles rather than to ambient conditions — but the record excerpted here does not carry the values needed to say which way that comparison came out for Bg3.
