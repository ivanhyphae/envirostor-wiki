## Overview
Bg3 is a constituent of concern identified at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. Its presence has been documented in soil samples collected as part of the project's environmental monitoring.

## Site record
Soil samples collected on March 1, 2023, indicated a concentration of Bg3 at 1.1 mg/kg. {{quote: "Bg3 1.1 mg/kg 3/1/2023"}}

Background sample location BG3 was established on November 2, 2022, along the east side of North Carpenter Road, approximately 700 feet south of the southeast corner of North Carpenter Road and Kansas Avenue. {{quote: "Background sample BG2 was removed from the surface water sampling network and a new background sample BG3 was added. BG3 is located along the east side of North Carpenter Road approximately, 700 feet south of the southeast corner of North Carpenter Road and Kansas Avenue (Figure 2)."}} During the surface water inspection on February 28, 2023, no visible water ponding was observed in the area around background surface water location BG3. {{quote: "There was no visible water ponding in the area around background surface water location BG3."}}

## Regulatory status
The applicable regulatory threshold for Bg3 is not specified in the provided source documents.
