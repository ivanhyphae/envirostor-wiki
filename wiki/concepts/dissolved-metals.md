Dissolved metals are inorganic constituents that have been analyzed in surface water and groundwater samples collected at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. The analysis of dissolved metals is part of the ongoing environmental monitoring and remediation efforts for the site.

## Site Record

Surface water samples collected on **December 11, 2015**, from locations PL1 through PL4 were analyzed for dissolved metals. Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported for samples PL1 through PL4. Arsenic was reported for sample PL3, and lead was reported for sample PL4. {{quote: "Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported for samples PL1 through PL4. Arsenic was reported for sample PL3. Lead was reported for sample PL4."}} Manganese concentrations in samples PL2, PL3, and PL4 exceeded secondary MCLs. {{quote: "Manganese concentrations for samples PL2, PL3, and PL4 exceed the secondary (taste and odor and welfare based) MCLs."}} Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected above their practical quantitation limits (PQLs). {{quote: "Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective practical quantitation limits (PQLs) for samples PL1 through PL4."}} The analytical results were within the same general range as previously reported. {{quote: "The analytical results are within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported at each sample location."}}

Groundwater samples collected on **March 18, 2013**, from wells MW-1 through MW-4 and MW-7 through MW-10 were analyzed for dissolved metals. Barium and strontium were reported for each of the ten groundwater samples, and lead was reported for samples from MW-1 and MW-7. {{quote: "For the March 2013 groundwater samples, barium and strontium were reported for each of the ten groundwater samples, and lead was reported for samples from two of the wells."}} The ranges for these metals were: Barium: 100-140 µg/l; Strontium: 160-230 µg/l; Lead: 2.2-2.5 µg/l. {{quote: "[Table /page/5/Table/4]"}} Beryllium, cadmium, silver, thallium, and mercury were not reported above their PQLs. {{quote: "Beryllium, cadmium, silver, thallium and mercury were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective practical quantitation limits (PQLs) in samples from each well."}} Arsenic, chromium, and vanadium were reported for all samples with ranges: Arsenic: 1.1-2.1 µg/l; Chromium: 2.1-4.1 µg/l; Vanadium: 1.1-2.1 µg/l. {{quote: "[Table /page/5/Table/10]"}} Nickel was reported for eight samples (0.9-3.1 µg/l), molybdenum for seven (1.1-2.1 µg/l), copper for five (1.1-2.1 µg/l), and selenium for four (0.9-1.1 µg/l). Antimony and zinc were detected in two samples each (Antimony: 0.9-1.1 µg/l; Zinc: 1.1-2.1 µg/l). Cobalt and manganese were detected in three samples each (Cobalt: 0.9-1.1 µg/l; Manganese: 1.1-2.1 µg/l). {{quote: "[Table /page/6/Table/1]"}} Manganese exceeded the secondary MCL in samples from MW-1 and MW-7. {{quote: "With the exception of the samples from MW-1 and MW-7 for manganese."}}
