## Overview
Dissolved metals are inorganic constituents present in surface water samples collected at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. These metals have been analyzed to assess their presence and concentration in stormwater runoff.

## Site Record
Surface water samples were collected and analyzed for dissolved metals on multiple occasions.

On December 11, 2015, samples PL1 through PL4 were collected. Dissolved metals reported for these samples included antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc. Arsenic was reported for sample PL3, and lead was reported for sample PL4. {{quote: "Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported for samples PL1 through PL4. Arsenic was reported for sample PL3. Lead was reported for sample PL4."}} Manganese concentrations in samples PL2, PL3, and PL4 exceeded secondary MCLs. {{quote: "Manganese concentrations for samples PL2, PL3, and PL4 exceed the secondary (taste and odor and welfare based) MCLs."}} Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected above their practical quantitation limits (PQLs). {{quote: "Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective practical quantitation limits (PQLs) for samples PL1 through PL4."}} The analytical results were within the same general range as previously reported. {{quote: "The analytical results are within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported at each sample location."}}

On January 6, 2016, samples PL1 through PL5 and BG1 and BG2 were collected. Dissolved metals reported for samples PL1 through PL5 included barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, potassium, sodium, and strontium. Nickel and zinc were reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5. Molybdenum was reported for samples PL1, PL2, PL4, and PL5. Antimony was reported for samples PL1, PL4, and PL5. Manganese was reported for samples PL3 through PL5. Vanadium was reported for samples PL2 and PL3. Lead was reported for sample PL3. {{quote: "Barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, potassium, sodium, and strontium were reported for samples PL1 through PL5. Nickel and zinc were reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5. Molybdenum was reported for samples PL1, PL2, PL4, and PL5. Antimony was report for samples Pl1, PL4, and PL5. Manganese was reported for samples PL3 through PL5. Vanadium was reported for samples PL2 and PL3. Lead was reported for sample PL3."}} None of these concentrations exceeded primary or secondary MCLs. {{quote: "None of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs)."}} Arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported above their PQLs. {{quote: "Arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, lead, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective practical quantitation limits (PQLs) for samples PL1 through PL5."}} For background samples BG1 and BG2, barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and zinc were reported. Antimony was reported for sample BG2, and vanadium for sample BG1. {{quote: "Barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and zinc were reported for samples BG1 and BG2. On March 5, 2016, samples PL1 through PL5 and BG1 were collected. Dissolved metals reported for samples PL1 through PL5 included antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium. ## Regulatory Status
Primary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) were not exceeded for any reported dissolved metals in the December 11, 2015, and January 6, 2016, sampling events. {{quote: "None of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs)."}} For the March 5, 2016, sampling event, primary MCLs were also not exceeded. {{quote: "None of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs."}}

Secondary MCLs were exceeded for manganese in sample PL2 on March 5, 2016, with a concentration of 60 µg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l. {{quote: "Manganese was detected in sample PL2 at a concentration of 60 micrograms per liter (µg/l) which exceeds its secondary MCL (taste and odor based) of 50 µg/l."}} On December 11, 2015, manganese concentrations in samples PL2, PL3, and PL4 also exceeded secondary MCLs. {{quote: "Manganese concentrations for samples PL2, PL3, and PL4 exceed the secondary (taste and odor and welfare based) MCLs."}}
