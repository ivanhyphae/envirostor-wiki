## Overview
Dissolved metals are inorganic constituents that have been analyzed in surface water and groundwater samples collected at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site as part of ongoing environmental monitoring and remediation efforts.

## Site Record
Surface water samples collected on **December 11, 2015**, from locations PL1 through PL4 were analyzed for dissolved metals. Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported for samples PL1 through PL4. Arsenic was reported for sample PL3, and lead was reported for sample PL4. Manganese concentrations in samples PL2, PL3, and PL4 exceeded secondary MCLs. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected above their practical quantitation limits (PQLs). The analytical results were within the same general range as previously reported.

On **January 6, 2016**, surface water samples PL1 through PL5 and background samples BG1 and BG2 were collected. Dissolved metals reported for samples PL1 through PL5 included barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, potassium, sodium, and strontium. Nickel and zinc were reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5. Molybdenum was reported for samples PL1, PL2, PL4, and PL5. Antimony was reported for samples PL1, PL4, and PL5. Manganese was reported for samples PL3 through PL5. Vanadium was reported for samples PL2 and PL3. Lead was reported for sample PL3. None of these concentrations exceeded primary or secondary MCLs. Arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported above their PQLs. For background samples BG1 and BG2, barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and zinc were reported. Antimony was reported for sample BG2, and vanadium for sample BG1. None of these concentrations exceeded primary or secondary MCLs. Arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, lead, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported above their PQLs. The analytical results were within the same general range as previously reported.

On **March 5, 2016**, surface water samples PL1 through PL5 and BG1 were collected. Dissolved metals reported for samples PL1 through PL5 included antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium. Zinc was reported for samples PL2, PL4, and PL5. Manganese was reported for samples PL2 and PL4. Arsenic was reported for sample PL2. With the exception of manganese in sample PL2, none of these concentrations exceeded primary or secondary MCLs. Manganese was detected in sample PL2 at 60 µg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l. Beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, lead, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported above their PQLs. For sample BG1, antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported. None of these concentrations exceeded primary or secondary MCLs. Arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, lead, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported above their PQLs. The analytical results were within the same general range as previously reported for BG1.

Groundwater samples collected on **March 18, 2013**, from wells MW-1 through MW-4 and MW-7 through MW-10 were analyzed for dissolved metals. Barium and strontium were reported for each of the ten groundwater samples, and lead was reported for samples from MW-1 and MW-7. The ranges for these metals were: Barium: 100-140 µg/l; Strontium: 160-230 µg/l; Lead: 2.2-2.5 µg/l. Beryllium, cadmium, silver, thallium, and mercury were not reported above their PQLs. Arsenic, chromium, and vanadium were reported for all samples with ranges: Arsenic: 1.1-2.1 µg/l; Chromium: 2.1-4.1 µg/l; Vanadium: 1.1-2.1 µg/l. Nickel was reported for eight samples (0.9-3.1 µg/l), molybdenum for seven (1.1-2.1 µg/l), copper for five (1.1-2.1 µg/l), and selenium for four (0.9-1.1 µg/l). Antimony and zinc were detected in two samples each (Antimony: 0.9-1.1 µg/l; Zinc: 1.1-2.1 µg/l). Cobalt and manganese were detected in three samples each (Cobalt: 0.9-1.1 µg/l; Manganese: 1.1-2.1 µg/l). Manganese exceeded the secondary MCL in samples from MW-1 and MW-7.

Groundwater samples collected on **June 5 and 6, 2013**, from wells MW-1 through MW-10 were analyzed for dissolved metals. Barium and strontium were reported for all ten samples, with ranges: Barium: 100-140 µg/l; Strontium: 160-230 µg/l. Lead was not detected above its PQL. Beryllium, cadmium, silver, thallium, zinc, and mercury were not reported above their PQLs. Arsenic, chromium, and vanadium were reported for all samples with ranges: Arsenic: 1.1-2.1 µg/l; Chromium: 2.1-4.1 µg/l; Vanadium: 1.1-2.1 µg/l. Nickel was reported for nine samples (0.9-3.1 µg/l), selenium for eight (0.9-1.1 µg/l), and molybdenum for seven (1.1-2.1 µg/l). Copper was reported for three samples (1.1-2.1 µg/l), antimony for two (0.9-1.1 µg/l), and cobalt and manganese for one sample each (Cobalt: 0.9 µg/l; Manganese: 1.1 µg/l). No dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values.

Groundwater samples collected on **March 12, 2012**, from wells MW-1 through MW-8 were analyzed for dissolved metals. Barium, vanadium, and strontium were reported for all samples with ranges: Barium: 100-140 µg/l; Vanadium: 1.1-2.1 µg/l; Strontium: 160-230 µg/l. Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, copper, lead, selenium, silver, thallium, and zinc were not reported above their PQLs. Chromium was reported for all samples except MW-7 (2.1-4.1 µg/l). Mercury was reported for all samples except MW-3, but these results were qualified as estimated due to its presence in the equipment blank. Nickel and manganese were reported for MW-4 (Nickel: 1.1-2.1 µg/l; Manganese: 1.1-2.1 µg/l), and molybdenum for MW-6 (1.1-2.1 µg/l). Manganese exceeded the secondary MCL in MW-4.

Groundwater samples collected on **April 30, 2015**, from wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, and MW-7 were analyzed for dissolved metals. Barium and strontium were reported for all four samples with ranges: Barium: 100-140 µg/l; Strontium: 160-230 µg/l. Lead was not reported above its PQL. Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, copper, selenium, silver, thallium, zinc, and mercury were not reported above their PQLs. Chromium and vanadium were reported for all samples with ranges: Chromium: 2.1-4.1 µg/l; Vanadium: 1.1-2.1 µg/l. Molybdenum and nickel were reported for three samples (Molybdenum: 1.1-2.1 µg/l; Nickel: 1.1-3.1 µg/l), and arsenic for two samples (1.1-2.1 µg/l). Manganese was reported for one sample (1.1-2.1 µg/l). None of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water.

Groundwater samples collected on **May 2, 2016**, from wells MW-1 and MW-7 were analyzed for dissolved metals. Barium and strontium were reported for both samples with ranges: Barium: 100-140 µg/l; Strontium: 160-230 µg/l. Lead was not reported above its PQL. Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, selenium, silver, thallium, zinc, and mercury were not reported above their PQLs. Arsenic, chromium, molybdenum, nickel, and vanadium were reported for both samples with ranges: Arsenic: 1.1-2.1 µg/l; Chromium: 2.1-4.1 µg/l; Molybdenum: 1.1-2.1 µg/l; Nickel: 1.1-3.1 µg/l; Vanadium: 1.1-2.1 µg/l. Cobalt, copper, and manganese were reported for MW-1 (Cobalt: 0.9 µg/l; Copper: 1.1-2.1 µg/l; Manganese: 1.1-2.1 µg/l). None of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values.

On **January 8, 2018**, surface water samples were collected from PL1 through PL4 and BG1 and BG2. Dissolved metals were analyzed. Antimony, arsenic, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported for some or all of the samples. None of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary maximum contaminant levels (MCLs) with the exception of manganese in sample BG2.

## Regulatory Status
The DTSC has identified barium, lead, and strontium as primary chemicals of concern in groundwater for the site. Groundwater monitoring has been conducted to compare analytical results to Primary MCLs and Secondary MCLs promulgated by the California Department of Public Health.
