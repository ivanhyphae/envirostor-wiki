Rincon Consultants, Inc. was involved in the preparation and revision of the Soil Management Plan (SMP) for the State Route 132 (SR 132) Modesto project. They submitted a revised SMP on February 8, 2021, which incorporated requests from the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). Rincon Consultants also conducted Phase II Environmental Site Assessments (ESA) as noted on an Initial Site Assessment Site Map dated October 2014 {{quote: "2018 Rincon Phase II ESA"}}.

## Site record

Rincon Consultants revised the Soil Management Plan (SMP) dated February 8, 2021 {{quote: "Revised Soil Management Plan Submittal"}}. This revised SMP included lowering the screening level for diesel range total petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH-d) to 500 mg/kg {{quote: "the screening level for diesel range total petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH‐d) has been lowered to 500 mg/kg to reflect the DTSC screening level for commercial/industrial soil"}}. Rincon Consultants indicated that the City of Modesto would like to proceed with excavation activities as described in the SMP, including targeted excavation of TPH-d impacted soils at borings RB-10 and RB-11 within Basin 6A {{quote: "Targeted excavation of TPH‐d impacted soils at borings RB‐10 and RB‐11 within Basin 6A and confirmation soil sampling"}}, and excavation of approximately 500 cubic yards from south of 611 Franklin within Basin 6B {{quote: "Excavation of 500 cubic yards +/‐ from just south of 611 Franklin within Basin 6B where there are no known TPH‐ d impacted soils"}}.

An Initial Site Assessment Site Map from October 2014 notes "2018 Rincon Phase II ESA" {{quote: "2018 Rincon Phase II ESA"}}. A plan for "Proposed clean cover fill source testing" associated with Route 132 ROW at and east of Carpenter Road outlines requirements for 12 borings to 5 feet, the creation of 3 4-point composites, and testing for "0-2' pesticides/metals" and "2-5' metals" {{quote: "Route 132 ROW @ and east of Carpenter Road', a '5' max cut depth', requirements for '12 borings to 5'', the creation of '3 4-point composites', and testing protocols for '0-2' pesticides/metals' and '2-5' metals'"}}.

## Regulatory status

The revised Soil Management Plan (SMP) dated February 8, 2021, was reviewed by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board (WB) {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board (WB) have reviewed the revised Soil Management Plan (SMP) dated February 8, 2021"}}. DTSC and the WB provided acceptance of the SMP via email on February 11, 2021, with two minor comments {{quote: "This e‐mail serves as the WB/DTSC official acceptance of the SMP"}}. One comment requested that soil with Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) above screening levels be labeled until transported offsite {{quote: "The SMP indicates that soil with Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) above screening levels will be segregated and covered. In addition, it is requested that this material be labeled until it is transported offsite."}}. The other comment requested consultation with the WB and DTSC if TPH impacted soil is present at the maximum depth of the basins {{quote: "The SMP does not discuss the actions that will be taken should TPH impacted soil still be present when the maximum depth of the basins is achieved. Should this scenario occur, Caltrans should be alerted and consultation with the WB and DTSC should take place to determine appropriate next steps."}}.

The Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) had not responded to requests for a meeting to discuss the appropriate screening level for protection of groundwater as of February 8, 2021 {{quote: "As you are aware, the Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) has not responded to requests for a meeting to discuss the appropriate screening level for protection of groundwater"}}.

## Open items

As of February 11, 2021, two minor comments were provided by DTSC and the Water Board regarding the Soil Management Plan (SMP):
*   Soil with Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) above screening levels should be labeled until transported offsite {{quote: "The SMP indicates that soil with Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) above screening levels will be segregated and covered. In addition, it is requested that this material be labeled until it is transported offsite."}}.
*   If TPH impacted soil is present at the maximum depth of the basins, Caltrans should be alerted, and consultation with the WB and DTSC should occur to determine next steps {{quote: "The SMP does not discuss the actions that will be taken should TPH impacted soil still be present when the maximum depth of the basins is achieved. Should this scenario occur, Caltrans should be alerted and consultation with the WB and DTSC should take place to determine appropriate next steps."}}.
