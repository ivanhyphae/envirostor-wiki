Rincon Consultants, Inc. was involved in the preparation and revision of the Soil Management Plan (SMP) for the State Route 132 (SR 132) Modesto project. They submitted a revised SMP on February 8, 2021, which incorporated requests from the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). Rincon Consultants also conducted Phase II Environmental Site Assessments (ESA) as noted on an Initial Site Assessment Site Map dated October 2014.

## Site record

Rincon Consultants revised the Soil Management Plan (SMP) dated February 8, 2021. This revised SMP included lowering the screening level for diesel range total petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH-d) to 500 mg/kg. Rincon Consultants indicated that the City of Modesto would like to proceed with excavation activities as described in the SMP, including targeted excavation of TPH-d impacted soils at borings RB-10 and RB-11 within Basin 6A, and excavation of approximately 500 cubic yards from south of 611 Franklin within Basin 6B.

An Initial Site Assessment Site Map from October 2014 notes "2018 Rincon Phase II ESA". A plan for "Proposed clean cover fill source testing" associated with Route 132 ROW at and east of Carpenter Road outlines requirements for 12 borings to 5 feet, the creation of 3 4-point composites, and testing for "0-2' pesticides/metals" and "2-5' metals".

## Regulatory status

The revised Soil Management Plan (SMP) dated February 8, 2021, was reviewed by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board (WB). DTSC and the WB provided acceptance of the SMP via email on February 11, 2021, with two minor comments. One comment requested that soil with Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) above screening levels be labeled until transported offsite. The other comment requested consultation with the WB and DTSC if TPH impacted soil is present at the maximum depth of the basins.

The Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) had not responded to requests for a meeting to discuss the appropriate screening level for protection of groundwater as of February 8, 2021.

## Open items

As of February 11, 2021, two minor comments were provided by DTSC and the Water Board regarding the Soil Management Plan (SMP):
* Soil with Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) above screening levels should be labeled until transported offsite.
* If TPH impacted soil is present at the maximum depth of the basins, Caltrans should be alerted, and consultation with the WB and DTSC should occur to determine next steps.
