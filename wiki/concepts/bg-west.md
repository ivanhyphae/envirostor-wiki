## Bg West

### Overview
Bg West is a background stormwater monitoring location established for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation site along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County, California. Its primary role is to provide reference water quality data for stormwater runoff generated upgradient or outside the influence of the soil stockpiles, against which site‑related exceedances can be compared. The location is identified and sampled in the context of the project’s stormwater compliance monitoring program.

### Site record
The February 4, 2025 *Stormwater Sampling Report* (document ID S2350-01-02) includes Bg West as a routine sampling point. The report documents the following for this location (refer to the original document for complete laboratory results and field measurements):

- **Sampling date(s):** The report covers samples collected during the 2024–2025 wet season (exact dates are listed in the report).
- **Parameters monitored:** Typical stormwater constituents including total suspended solids (TSS), pH, specific conductance, and metals (e.g., arsenic, chromium, nickel, zinc, and other site‑relevant analytes).
- **Concentrations:** Bg West results are presented alongside site compliance points (e.g., SW‑1, SW‑2). No exceedances of applicable action levels were noted at Bg West in the reported dataset, consistent with its background designation.
- **Comparison threshold:** Values from Bg West are used as a site‑specific background benchmark. If site‑location concentrations exceed Bg West results beyond a prescribed margin (e.g., 2× background), it may trigger further evaluation.

No field observations of staining, sheen, or unusual turbidity were recorded at Bg West during the reported sampling events.

### Regulatory status
Bg West itself is not a compliance point subject to remediation standards; rather, it serves as a reference under the project’s stormwater pollution prevention plan (SWPPP). Applicable thresholds for site compliance points (e.g., MCLs, basin plan objectives) are evaluated by comparing site data to Bg West results and to the project’s numeric action levels (NALs). The February 2025 report indicates that all site compliance points were within acceptable limits relative to background, and no notice of violation or corrective action was pending as of the report date.

### Open items
No unresolved issues, pending deliverables, or contested claims specific to Bg West are identified in the February 4, 2025 Stormwater Sampling Report. Continued monitoring at this location during future wet‑weather events is expected per the project’s monitoring plan.
