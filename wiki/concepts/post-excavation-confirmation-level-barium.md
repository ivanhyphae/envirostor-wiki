## Overview

Post Excavation Confirmation Level Barium refers to the site‑specific cleanup threshold for total barium in soil that must be achieved after excavation of contaminated material at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (State Route 132, Stanislaus County, California). The level is defined in the *Remedial Design and Implementation Plan* (RDIP) dated January 19 (document S1200-01-01) and governs the end‑point verification sampling required before backfill and site closure can be certified.

Barium is a naturally occurring metal that can be released by historical operations—likely including stockpiled road‑maintenance debris and demolition materials—and poses a human health risk via ingestion or inhalation of dust if present above protective thresholds.

## Site Record

The RDIP establishes **total barium 760 mg/kg** as the post‑excavation confirmation level for soil. This value is the **Probabilistic Background Threshold Value (BT95)** for the site, derived from statistical analysis of native soil samples in the project area. It is not a generic regulatory standard but a site‑specific benchmark calculated to distinguish anthropogenic contamination from natural background.

Key documented facts from the source:

- **Threshold value:** 760 mg/kg total barium.
- **Basis:** Probabilistic background threshold (BT95) – i.e., the 95th percentile of background concentrations.
- **Applicable medium:** Soil (not groundwater). For groundwater, barium has a separate **Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) of 1,000 µg/L** enforced by the State Water Resources Control Board (per the RDIP).
- **Verification method:** Post‑excavation confirmation sampling must analyze total barium and compare results to 760 mg/kg. Exceedances require additional removal or evaluation.
- **Project phases:** The RDIP covers **Phase 1 (2019–2020)** and subsequent phases; the 760 mg/kg level applies to all excavation areas where barium is a constituent of concern.
- **Spatial distribution:** Barium was identified as a contaminant of potential concern (COPC) in the stockpile soils based on prior site characterization results (not detailed in the excerpt).

No specific concentrations of barium from pre‑excavation sampling, excavation volumes, or confirmation results are provided in the RDIP excerpt—those data would appear in subsequent remediation reports.

## Regulatory Status

- **Primary oversight agency:** California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for soil remediation under the RDIP.
- **Applicable soil standard:** Site‑specific background threshold (760 mg/kg). No generic California "action level" exists for barium in soil; the project relies on this derived value.
- **Groundwater standard:** MCL of 1,000 µg/L (federal Safe Drinking Water Act, delegated to California State Water Board). The RDIP requires groundwater monitoring at down‑gradient wells (e.g., MW‑5) to verify no groundwater impact from the stockpiles.
- **Compliance status:** As of the RDIP date, the confirmation level is approved by DTSC as part of the remedial design. Current compliance depends on ongoing verification sampling—no results are included in the provided source.

## Open Items

The RDIP excerpt does not identify any unresolved issues specific to barium. Open items are limited by the scope of the source document: the RDIP sets the level and methods; actual post‑excavation confirmation results, any exceedances, and DTSC sign‑off on the final excavation areas are not provided.

Required future documentation (by implication):

- Post‑excavation confirmation sampling reports for each excavation phase.
- Demonstration that no soil remains above 760 mg/kg total barium.
- Groundwater monitoring results showing barium below 1,000 µg/L if a groundwater release is suspected.
