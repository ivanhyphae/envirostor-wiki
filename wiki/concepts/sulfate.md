## Overview
Sulfate is a chemical compound that has been monitored in stormwater runoff associated with the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]] project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. The monitoring is part of the project's stormwater management plan, overseen by agencies including the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]].

## Site record
Sulfate was among the analytes tested in surface water samples collected from constructed impoundments within the [[caltrans]] Right-of-Way (ROW) by Shaw in March 2006. Samples were analyzed for sulfate, along with dissolved metals, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), nitrate, and sulfide {{quote: "The samples were analyzed for dissolved metals, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), nitrate, sulfate, and sulfide."}}.

Later, [[geocon-consultants]] collected surface water samples between April 2013 and April 2019. Sulfate was included in the laboratory analyses performed by Asset, using EPA Test Method 9056/300.0 {{quote: "Sulfate by EPA Test Method 9056/300.0"}}.

In a stormwater sampling event on February 1, 2024, sulfate was reported for samples collected from two of the three sample locations (SW-West and BG-West) at concentrations of 3.6 and 6.5 mg/l {{quote: "sulfate was reported for the samples collected from two of the three sample locations (SW-West and BG-West) at concentrations of 3.6 and 6.5 mg/l."}}. These concentrations did not exceed the secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCL) for drinking water {{quote: "TDS and sulfate concentrations do not exceed the secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCL) for drinking water."}}.

In a subsequent sampling event on February 4, 2025, sulfate was reported for samples SW-East and SW-West at respective concentrations of 21 and 14 mg/l {{quote: "Sulfate was reported for samples SW-East and SW-West at respective concentrations of 21 and 14 mg/l."}}. These concentrations also did not exceed the Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCL) for drinking water {{quote: "TDS and sulfate concentrations do not exceed the Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCL) for drinking water."}}.

In the January 8, 2018 sampling event, sulfate was reported for sample BG2 at a concentration of 5.6 mg/l {{quote: "0.40 5.6 0.023 240 2.5 19 <5.0 19 38"}}. In the March 24, 2017 sampling event, sulfate was reported for sample BG2 at a concentration of 10 mg/l {{quote: "10 250\*\* --- --- 250\*\* --- --- --- 500\*\*"}}.

## Regulatory status
The secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCL) for drinking water are used as a benchmark for sulfate concentrations in stormwater samples. In both the February 1, 2024, and February 4, 2025, sampling events, the reported sulfate concentrations were below these MCLs {{quote: "TDS and sulfate concentrations do not exceed the secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCL) for drinking water."}} and {{quote: "TDS and sulfate concentrations do not exceed the Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCL) for drinking water."}}.
