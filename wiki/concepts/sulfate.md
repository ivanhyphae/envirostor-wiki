Sulfate is a chemical compound that has been monitored in stormwater runoff associated with the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]] project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. The monitoring is part of the project's stormwater management plan, overseen by agencies including the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]].

## Site record

Sulfate was among the analytes tested in surface water samples collected from constructed impoundments within the [[caltrans]] Right-of-Way (ROW) by Shaw in March 2006. Samples were analyzed for sulfate, along with dissolved metals, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), nitrate, and sulfide.

Geocon-Consultants collected surface water samples between April 2013 and April 2019. Sulfate was included in the laboratory analyses performed by Asset, using EPA Test Method 9056/300.0.

In a stormwater sampling event on January 8, 2018, sulfate was reported for sample BG2 at a concentration of 5.6 mg/l. In a March 24, 2017 sampling event, sulfate was reported for sample BG2 at a concentration of 10 mg/l.

In a stormwater sampling event on February 1, 2024, sulfate was reported for samples collected from two of the three sample locations (SW-West and BG-West) at concentrations of 3.6 and 6.5 mg/l.

In a subsequent sampling event on February 4, 2025, sulfate was reported for samples SW-East and SW-West at respective concentrations of 21 and 14 mg/l.

## Regulatory status

The secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCL) for drinking water are used as a benchmark for sulfate concentrations in stormwater samples. In the February 1, 2024, sampling event, the reported sulfate concentrations of 3.6 mg/l (SW-West) and 6.5 mg/l (BG-West) did not exceed the secondary MCL. In the February 4, 2025, sampling event, the reported sulfate concentrations of 21 mg/l (SW-East) and 14 mg/l (SW-West) also did not exceed the secondary MCL.
