## DTSC Conditional Approval of the Well Destruction Completion Report

DTSC conditionally approved the Well Destruction Completion Report for the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles site (Site Code: 900259) in a letter dated January 30, 2026, from Director Katherine M. Butler's office to Adam Inman of Caltrans District 6. The report, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. and submitted June 18, 2025, described the procedures for destroying the four groundwater monitoring wells remaining after six of the original ten wells were decommissioned in 2019. Approval was made conditional on incorporation of a single comment from DTSC's Geological Services Branch (GSB) into a final version of the report, which had not been received as of March 16, 2026.

The January 30, 2026 letter was a combined approval covering two separate submittals: the Well Destruction Report and the Storm Water Sampling Report (FY24/25), both dated June 18, 2025. The Storm Water Sampling Report — reviewed by GSB geologists and by toxicologists from the Human and Ecological Risk Office — was approved outright, with all results reported within reporting limits. The Well Destruction Report, reviewed only by GSB, received conditional rather than outright approval, pending incorporation of one GSB comment into a final version to be provided to DTSC. The text of that comment is not included in the available material.

By March 16, 2026 — roughly six and a half weeks after the conditional approval letter — DTSC Project Manager Arielle McLeskey emailed Adam Inman asking whether he had sent the revised Well Destruction Completion Report, noting she had not received it and asking him to forward it if it had already been sent. This email indicates that, as of that date, the condition attached to the approval had not yet been satisfied, or at least that satisfaction had not reached DTSC. The record does not show a subsequent response from Caltrans or a final closure of this condition.

## Status of the condition

The approval is not a final closure of the Well Destruction Report: it remains conditional until Caltrans incorporates the unspecified GSB comment and provides DTSC with the final version. As of the most recent correspondence in the file, that final version had not been confirmed received by DTSC, leaving the well destruction record for the four remaining wells formally open on this point.
