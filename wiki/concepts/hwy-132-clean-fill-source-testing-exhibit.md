## Hwy 132 Clean Fill Source Testing Exhibit

The Hwy 132 Clean Fill Source Testing Exhibit details the sampling and testing plan for identifying suitable clean fill material for the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project. This plan specifically targets areas within the Route 132 right-of-way (ROW) east of N. Carpenter Road in Modesto, California, to ensure fill material meets project requirements, particularly for areas with recognized environmental conditions (RECs).

### Site Record

The proposed clean fill source testing is outlined on sheet L-13 of the exhibit, focusing on the intersection of Route 132 and N. Carpenter Road. The designated testing area is described as the "Route 132 ROW @ and east of Carpenter Road." The protocol specifies a maximum cut depth of "5' max cut depth." A total of "12 borings to 5'" were planned. From these borings, "3 4-point composites" were to be created. The planned analyses included "0-2' pesticides/metals" and "2-5' metals." A red dashed polygon on the plan delineates this proposed clean fill source testing area, encompassing the central overpass area around the roadway intersection.

An "Initial Site Assessment Site Map" dated October 2014, for the SR 132 West - Freeway/Expressway Project, Alternative 1, also identifies areas designated as "Proposed Clean Fill Source." This map includes handwritten annotations and arrows pointing to specific locations. It also identifies parcels with RECs and risk classifications, referencing "2018 Rincon Phase II ESA" in handwritten annotations.

### Regulatory Status

This testing plan is a component of the environmental due diligence for the [[sr-132-west-project]]. The exhibit's purpose is to identify and characterize suitable clean fill material to comply with regulatory requirements for construction projects involving potentially contaminated sites. The specific regulatory thresholds for the tested analytes (pesticides/metals) are not detailed within this exhibit.

### Open Items

The exhibit itself represents a plan for future actions. The results of the proposed testing and the subsequent approval of identified sources as clean fill material are pending.
