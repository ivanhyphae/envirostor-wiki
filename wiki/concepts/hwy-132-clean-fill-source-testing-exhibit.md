## Hwy 132 Clean Fill Source Testing Exhibit

The Hwy 132 Clean Fill Source Testing Exhibit outlines the plan for sampling and testing potential sources of clean fill material for the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project. This exhibit specifically addresses the area within the Route 132 right-of-way (ROW) east of N. Carpenter Road in Modesto, California. The purpose of this testing is to ensure the suitability of fill material for use in construction, particularly in areas identified with recognized environmental conditions (RECs).

### Site Record

The plan for clean fill source testing is detailed on sheet L-13, focusing on the intersection of Route 132 and N. Carpenter Road. The proposed testing area is described as the {{quote: "Route 132 ROW @ and east of Carpenter Road"}}. The protocol specifies a maximum cut depth of {{quote: "5' max cut depth"}}. A total of {{quote: "12 borings to 5'"}} were to be advanced. From these borings, {{quote: "3 4-point composites"}}, were to be created. The testing was to include analysis for {{quote: "0-2' pesticides/metals"}} and {{quote: "2-5' metals"}}. A red dashed polygon on the plan encompasses the central overpass area around the roadway intersection, delineating the proposed clean fill source testing area {{quote: "red dashed polygon drawn over the central overpass area surrounding the roadway intersection"}}.

An "Initial Site Assessment Site Map" dated October 2014, for the SR 132 West - Freeway/Expressway Project, Alternative 1, also indicates areas designated as {{quote: "Proposed Clean Fill Source"}}, with handwritten annotations and arrows pointing to specific locations. This map also identifies parcels with RECs and risk classifications {{quote: "identified sites with recognized environmental conditions (RECs), and risk classifications (High, Medium, Low)"}}. The map references {{quote: "2018 Rincon Phase II ESA"}} in handwritten annotations.

### Regulatory Status

This testing is a component of the environmental due diligence for the [[sr-132-west-project]]. The exhibit aims to identify and characterize suitable clean fill material to meet regulatory requirements for construction projects involving potentially contaminated sites. The specific regulatory thresholds for the tested analytes (pesticides/metals) are not detailed within this exhibit.

### Open Items

The exhibit itself represents a plan for future action. The results of the proposed testing and the subsequent approval of identified sources as clean fill material are pending.
