## Hwy 132 Clean Fill Source Testing Exhibit

The Hwy 132 Clean Fill Source Testing Exhibit details the plan for sampling and testing potential sources of clean fill material for the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project, specifically within the Route 132 right-of-way east of N. Carpenter Road in Modesto, California. This exhibit outlines the methodology for ensuring the suitability of fill material to be used in construction, particularly in areas identified with recognized environmental conditions (RECs).

### Site Record

The plan for clean fill source testing was documented on a sheet labeled L-13, focusing on the intersection of Route 132 and N. Carpenter Road. The proposed testing area is within the "Route 132 ROW @ and east of Carpenter Road" {{quote: "Route 132 ROW @ and east of Carpenter Road"}}. The testing protocol specified a maximum cut depth of 5 feet {{quote: "5' max cut depth"}}. A total of 12 borings were to be advanced to a depth of 5 feet {{quote: "12 borings to 5'"}}. From these borings, 3 four-point composites were to be created {{quote: "3 4-point composites"}}. The testing was to include analysis for "0-2' pesticides/metals" and "2-5' metals" {{quote: "0-2' pesticides/metals" and "2-5' metals"}}. A red dashed polygon was drawn on the plan, encompassing the central overpass area around the roadway intersection, likely delineating the extent of the proposed clean fill source testing area {{quote: "red dashed polygon drawn over the central overpass area surrounding the roadway intersection"}}.

An Initial Site Assessment Site Map, dated October 2014, for the SR 132 West - Freeway/Expressway Project, Alternative 1, also shows areas designated as "Proposed Clean Fill Source" with handwritten annotations and arrows pointing to specific locations {{quote: "Proposed Clean Fill Source" with arrows pointing to specific areas}}. This map also identifies parcels with RECs and risk classifications {{quote: "identified sites with recognized environmental conditions (RECs), and risk classifications (High, Medium, Low)"}}.

### Regulatory Status

The testing is a component of the environmental due diligence for the [[sr-132-west-project]], aiming to identify and characterize suitable clean fill material, likely to meet regulatory requirements for construction projects involving potentially contaminated sites. The specific regulatory thresholds for the tested analytes (pesticides/metals) are not detailed within this exhibit.

### Open Items

The exhibit itself represents a plan for future action (testing). The results of this testing and subsequent approval of the identified sources as clean fill material would be the next steps.
