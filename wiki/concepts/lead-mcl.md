Lead was among the constituents evaluated against the California primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) framework at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, alongside other metals such as [[barium]] and [[chromium]]. The California MCL for lead governs whether groundwater and stormwater results are treated as impacted, and it is the standard against which the site's lead detections were checked in the DEIR/EA record.

The available source material does not report specific lead concentration values, sampling dates, or exceedances tied to the lead MCL at this site. Without those figures, it cannot be established from the record whether lead was detected at concentrations approaching, meeting, or exceeding the California primary MCL in any sampling round, or whether it appeared in groundwater, stormwater, or stockpile soil.

Because the record is silent on lead-specific results, no conclusion can be drawn here about lead's contribution to the site's overall contamination picture or to any groundwater- or stormwater-impact determination. This stands in contrast to other constituents discussed in the same DEIR/EA — such as [[barium]] against the [[barium-mcl]], or [[chromium]] — for which the source material does report measured values and threshold comparisons.
