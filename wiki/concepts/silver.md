## Silver

### Overview
Silver is a metal that has been analyzed in surface water samples collected as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. In the documented sampling events, silver was not detected above the method detection limits (MDLs) in the analyzed samples.

### Site record
During the surface water sampling event on **March 10, 2023**, silver was not reported above the Method Detection Limits (MDLs) in the background and surface water samples collected. {{quote: "Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, nickel, silver, and thallium were not reported above the MDLs in the background and surface water samples."}} (Stantec Consulting Services Inc., Surface Water Report – March 10, 2023).

In the surface water sampling event on **October 28, 2016**, silver was not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than its respective MDLs in samples collected from locations PL1 through PL5, BG1, and BG2. (Geocon Consultants, Inc., SURFACE WATER SAMPLING REPORT – OCTOBER 28, 2016).

In the surface water sampling event on **January 8, 2018**, silver was not detected in the analyzed samples at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective laboratory reporting limits. (Geocon Consultants, Inc., SURFACE WATER SAMPLING REPORT – JANUARY 8, 2018).

In the surface water sampling event on **March 24, 2017**, beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits for samples PL1 through PL5. (Geocon Consultants, Inc., SURFACE WATER SAMPLING REPORT – MARCH 24, 2017).

### Regulatory status
No specific regulatory status or applicable thresholds for silver in surface water are mentioned in the provided source material.
