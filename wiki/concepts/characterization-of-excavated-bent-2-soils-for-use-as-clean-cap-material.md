## Characterization of Excavated Bent 2 Soils for Use as Clean Cap Material

### Overview

This section details the characterization of soils excavated from the Bent 2 area for their potential use as [[clean-cap-material]] at the SR 132 site in Stanislaus County, California. The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board (WB) reviewed a Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020, concerning these soils. While the analytical results did not meet the established clean cap criteria, a conditional acceptance was granted for the soil's placement on top of Stockpile 2.

### Site Record

On November 2, 2020, Richard C. Stewart of Caltrans submitted a Technical Memorandum to DTSC and the Water Board, presenting analytical results for soils from the Bent 2 column excavation. These results indicated that the soils exceeded background criteria for use as [[clean-cap]] cover material.

On November 6, 2020, Dean Wright of DTSC responded, officially accepting the Technical Memorandum and a Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020. DTSC and the Water Board agreed that the Bent 2 soils could be placed on top of Stockpile 2 at a thickness of 6 inches or less. This placement is contingent upon the soil being covered with clean base material and the asphalt/concrete used for roadway construction.

Given the project schedule, the Bent 2 soils may be temporarily exposed to the elements during the winter months. Strict adherence to the rainy season surface water sampling effort and erosion control measures outlined in the Revised SWPPP Amendment is therefore critical to ensure contaminants remain on site and do not impact the surrounding area.

### Regulatory Status

The DTSC and the Water Board reviewed the characterization of the Bent 2 soils. The soil sample results did not meet the clean cap criteria as established in the April 9, 2020 Variance Request approved by DTSC. However, a conditional acceptance was granted by DTSC and the Water Board for the placement of these soils on Stockpile 2 under specific conditions.

### Open Items

The primary condition for the use of Bent 2 soils as clean cap material is that they must be covered with clean base material and the final roadway surface (asphalt/concrete). Additionally, strict adherence to the rainy season surface water sampling and erosion control measures outlined in the Revised SWPPP Amendment is required due to potential temporary exposure to the elements.
