## Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board

The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) is a regulatory agency that shares oversight responsibilities with the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. The CVRWQCB was involved in the review and approval of various project documents, including the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) and technical memorandums related to soil characterization and management.

## Site Record

The CVRWQCB was a recipient of correspondence and documentation related to the remediation and management of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles as part of the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project. Specifically, the CVRWQCB, along with DTSC, reviewed and approved:

*   The **Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) - Interim Phase 1**, dated January 30, 2019, which was approved by DTSC in consultation with the CVRWQCB on April 3, 2019. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) has reviewed the Remedial Design Implementation Plan — Interim Phase 1 (RDIP-Interim Phase 1) submitted by Geocon Consultants Inc. (Geocon) on behalf of the Department of Transportation (Caltrans). ... DTSC and the RWQCB have no additional comments on either document and the RDIP—Interim Phase 1 is approved."}}
*   The **Final Feasibility Study**, dated June 24, 2014, which DTSC reviewed in consultation with the CVRWQCB. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) has reviewed the report titled 'Final Feasibility Study Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County' dated June 24, 2014, prepared by the Department of Transportation's (Caltrans) contractor, Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Geocon). ... DTSC concurs with the Final Feasibility Study."}}
*   The **Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan** was reviewed by DTSC and the Waterboard (referring to the CVRWQCB). {{quote: "DTSC and the Waterboard have reviewed the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan (Plan) received on April 5, 2021 that outlines the proposed sampling and analysis for soil at this location that will be used as clean fill. The Plan is consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and there are no comments. This e-mail serves as DTSCs approval of the Plan."}}
*   The **Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum** was reviewed by DTSC and the Water Board. {{quote: "DTSC and the Water Board have reviewed the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated April 12, 2021 describing the sampling and analysis that was completed to characterize the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill area. The agencies agree that the composite soil sample results are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and meet the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria. This e-mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the Memo."}}
*   The **Revised Soil Management Plan (SMP)**, dated February 8, 2021, was reviewed by DTSC and the Water Board. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board (WB) have reviewed the revised Soil Management Plan (SMP) dated February 8, 2021 which describes the excavation, confirmation sampling, handling, and proper disposal of impacted soil for the two proposed retention basins associated with new construction along State Route 132 (SR 132). ... This e-mail serves as the WB/DTSC official acceptance of the SMP."}}
*   The **Modesto Soil Stockpile Surface Water Report – December 17, 2020** was reviewed by the CVRWQCB. {{quote: "I don't have any comment on the Modesto Soil Stockpile Surface Water Sampling report."}}

The CVRWQCB, along with DTSC, was also listed as an addressee on the **Interim Removal Action Completion Report (RACR)** for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, dated December 2021. {{quote: "Addressee Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board, Mr. Kyle Cockerham"}}

Groundwater and stormwater monitoring activities near the BCS soil stockpiles were completed by Caltrans' subconsultant, Stantec. Reports for these activities are accessible via DTSC's EnviroStor and GeoTracker websites. {{quote: "Four groundwater monitoring events were completed for the remaining wells (MW-4, MW-6, MW-9 and MW-10) between November 2019 and March 2021. ... Annual groundwater sampling events will continue until it is determined by the CVRWQCB that the clean-capped BCS material does not pose a material threat to groundwater quality."}} {{quote: "Additional stormwater sampling events will be performed in the vicinity of the BCS Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones at regulatory-approved modified locations during qualifying rain events until completed construction of Ultimate Phase 2 of the SR 132 West Project."}}

The CVRWQCB, in cooperation with DTSC, is responsible for providing site mitigation oversight under a Caltrans Interagency Agreement. {{quote: "The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in cooperation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB), is the lead regulatory agency providing site mitigation oversight under a Caltrans Interagency Agreement."}}

## Regulatory Status

The CVRWQCB, in conjunction with DTSC, provides regulatory oversight for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. Annual groundwater sampling events will continue until the CVRWQCB determines that the clean-capped BCS material does not pose a material threat to groundwater quality. {{quote: "Annual groundwater sampling events will continue until it is determined by the CVRWQCB that the clean-capped BCS material does not pose a material threat to groundwater quality."}}
