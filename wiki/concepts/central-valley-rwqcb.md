## Overview

The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Central Valley RWQCB) is the state agency with jurisdiction over water quality in Stanislaus County, where the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation site (SR 132) is located. Under the Porter-Cologne Water Quality Control Act, the RWQCB establishes basin plan objectives, sets waste discharge requirements, and oversees groundwater monitoring and cleanup actions that affect waters of the state. In this project, the RWQCB acts as a co‑regulator alongside DTSC, with specific authority over groundwater quality, discharge controls, and compliance with applicable water quality standards.

## Regulatory status

The Modesto Soil Stockpiles site is subject to the Central Valley RWQCB’s *Water Quality Control Plan for the Sacramento and San Joaquin River Basins* (Basin Plan). Applicable standards include:

- Maximum contaminant levels (MCLs) for constituents detected in groundwater (e.g., arsenic, barium, chromium, etc.) as set by Title 22 of the California Code of Regulations.
- Basin Plan objectives for groundwater quality, including narrative and numeric criteria for waste constituents.
- RWQCB‑issued waste discharge requirements or conditional waivers for site remediation activities.

Based on the documented acceptances (WB Acceptance E‑mail, RDIP Variance Request #2 Acceptance E‑mail), the project was in compliance with RWQCB‑approved submissions at the times of those actions. No notices of violation or enforcement orders are referenced in the provided source titles.
