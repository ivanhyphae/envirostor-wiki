## Overview

The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Central Valley RWQCB) is a regulatory agency with oversight responsibilities for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case. In this matter, the Central Valley RWQCB — alongside the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) — received formal notification and documentation from Caltrans regarding the disposition of excavated material from the ramp safety improvements at State Route 99, Kansas Avenue northbound off-ramp in Modesto, Stanislaus County.

## Site record

The Central Valley RWQCB was a direct addressee on the August 24, 2012, clarification letter from Caltrans District 10. The letter was sent to Mr. Steven W. Meeks, P.E., identified as Senior Water Resources Control Engineer at the Regional Water Quality Control Board, 11020 Sun Center Drive, Suite 200, Rancho Cordova, CA 95670. This correspondence clarified the disposition of excavated material and stated that completion and implementation of an Interim Removal Action Workplan was unnecessary for the ramp project.

The Central Valley RWQCB was also an addressee on Caltrans's prior letter dated May 29, 2012, which initially outlined the disposition of excavated material from the ramp safety improvements. The August 24, 2012 letter served to further clarify the information originally presented in that May 29 correspondence.

## Regulatory status

The project record does not document any specific regulatory action, approval condition, compliance determination, or enforcement action taken by the Central Valley RWQCB in connection with this matter. The agency's role in the record is as a recipient of information submitted by Caltrans. The Central Valley RWQCB shared regulatory oversight with DTSC, as both agencies were jointly notified of Caltrans's determination that an Interim Removal Action Workplan was not required based on additional site investigation data and proposed material management.
