## Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL)

Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL) is a laboratory contracted to perform analytical testing on groundwater samples collected as part of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. ATL conducted analyses under chain-of-custody protocols for various parameters, including dissolved metals, major ions, and other chemical constituents, utilizing specified U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) and Standard Methods. The laboratory reports and associated chain-of-custody documentation are included in the project's appendices.

### Site Record

ATL received groundwater samples for analysis on multiple occasions. For the December 2013 monitoring event, ATL received samples on December 12, 2013, under Work Order Number 1303937, with a client reference of "Modesto Stockpiles, S9525-01-44C". The laboratory reports were issued on December 19, 2013.

For a subsequent monitoring event, ATL received samples on December 13, 2013, under Work Order Number 1303962, also referencing "Modesto Stockpiles, S9525-01-44C". The laboratory reports for this event were issued on December 20, 2013.

In February 2014, ATL received samples on February 26, 2014, under Work Order Number 1400558, with a client reference of "Modesto Stockpiles, S9800-01-17". The laboratory reports were issued on March 5, 2014. ATL also received samples on February 27, 2014, under Work Order Number 1400577, with the same client reference "Modesto Stockpiles, S9800-01-17". The laboratory reports for this event were issued on March 7, 2014.

The analyses performed by ATL included:
*   Title 22 dissolved metals (including strontium) following United States Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Test Methods 6020/7470.
*   Dissolved calcium, magnesium, potassium and sodium by EPA Test Method 6020.
*   Chloride, nitrate as nitrogen and sulfate by EPA Test Method 300.0.
*   Sulfide by Standard Method (SM) 4500.
*   TDS by SM 2540C.
*   Total alkalinity, bicarbonate alkalinity, carbonate alkalinity by SM 2320B.

### Regulatory Status

ATL holds various laboratory certifications, including ELAP No.: 1838, NELAP No.: 02107CA, CSDLAC No.: 10196, ORELAP No.: CA300003, and TCEQ No.: T104704502. Test results contained within the data packages meet the requirements of the National Environmental Laboratory Accreditation Conference and/or applicable state-specific certification programs.
