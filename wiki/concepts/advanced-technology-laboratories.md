## Overview

**Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL)** is the third-party analytical laboratory contracted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. to perform all groundwater chemistry analyses for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). ATL holds California ELAP No. 1838, NELAP No. 02107CA, and certifications from CSDLAC, ORELAP, and TCEQ. For this project, ATL provides defensible analytical data under chain-of-custody protocol, supporting groundwater monitoring, regulatory compliance reporting, and potential liability assessment.

## Site Record

ATL received groundwater samples from the September 2012 monitoring event on September 20, 2012, under ATL Work Order Number 1203303. The laboratory director, Eddie Rodriguez, certified the results on October 4, 2012. Samples were delivered from the field to Geocon Consultants, Inc. at 3160 Gold Valley Drive, Suite 800, Rancho Cordova, CA 95742.

The analytical suite performed by ATL for this event included:

- **Dissolved metals (Title 22 CAM 17 plus strontium):** EPA Method 6020 (ICP-MS) for metals; EPA Method 7470 for mercury
- **Major cations:** Dissolved calcium, magnesium, potassium, sodium by EPA Method 6020
- **Anions:** Chloride, nitrate as nitrogen, sulfate by EPA Method 300.0 (ion chromatography)
- **Sulfide:** Standard Method 4500
- **Total dissolved solids (TDS):** SM 2540C
- **Alkalinity series:** Total alkalinity, bicarbonate alkalinity, carbonate alkalinity by SM 2320B
- **Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs):** EPA Method 8270-SIM

The laboratory report includes QA/QC qualifiers: "M2" for matrix spike recovery outside acceptance limits due to possible matrix interference (batch validated by LCS), "D1" for dilution due to possible matrix interference, "D6" for dilution due to high concentration of target analyte, and "B" for analyte detected in method blank. The chain-of-custody documentation shows samples were preserved appropriately (HNO₃ for metals, H₂SO₄ for anions, NaOH for sulfide) and field-filtered for dissolved metals.

Results are presented on Tables 2 and 3 of the September 2012 monitoring report, with full laboratory reports and chain-of-custody records in Appendix B.

## Regulatory Status

ATL operates under California Environmental Laboratory Accreditation Program (ELAP) certification No. 1838, ensuring analytical methods meet NELAC standards and California DTSC/SWRCB requirements for groundwater monitoring at the Modesto Stockpiles site. The laboratory's data package is intended to support compliance with Caltrans Contract 06A1580 and applicable California Title 22 drinking water standards (MCLs) for detected constituents.

## Open Items

- No issues with ATL's certification, method performance, or data defensibility have been documented in the September 2012 report. Any unresolved QA/QC exceedances (matrix spike recoveries, dilutions) are flagged with qualifiers but were validated by laboratory control samples.
- The project record does not indicate whether ATL performed analyses for subsequent monitoring events beyond September 2012.
