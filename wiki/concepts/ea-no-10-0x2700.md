## EA No. 10-0X2700

### Overview

EA No. 10-0X2700 is an identifier associated with the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project in Stanislaus County, California. This designation appears in project documentation related to surface water sampling activities conducted by [[geocon-consultants]] under [[caltrans-contract-no-06a1895-task-order-no-17]].

### Site Record

Surface water sampling was performed on December 11, 2015, at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. The sampling activities were conducted in accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), as established in the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)* dated January 2006, and an addendum dated February 20, 2013. The project number associated with this sampling event is S9800-01-17A, and the EA number is 10-0X2700.

### Regulatory Status

The surface water sampling was performed under [[caltrans]] Contract No. 06A1895, Task Order No. 17, which includes EA No. 10-0X2700. The sampling protocols followed were approved by the DTSC.
