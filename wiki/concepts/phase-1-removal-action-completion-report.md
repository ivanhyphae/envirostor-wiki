## Overview

The **Phase 1 Removal Action Completion Report (RACR)** documents the completion of the initial excavation and off‑site disposal of hexavalent chromium‑impacted soil at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (State Route 132, Stanislaus County, California). The Phase 1 work, conducted in 2019–2020, addressed the most heavily contaminated material from Stockpiles A and B. The RACR was submitted to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for review and acceptance as part of the Interim Removal Action. As of the Draft Interim RACR Acceptance Letter (dated 900259), DTSC had not yet issued final acceptance; the letter provided comments and conditions for revision.

## Site record

- **Stockpile A** – Phase 1 removed approximately 2,400 cubic yards (cy) of soil with total chromium concentrations ranging from 480 mg/kg to 1,600 mg/kg. The highest hexavalent chromium concentration in this stockpile was 38 mg/kg.
- **Stockpile B** – Phase 1 removed approximately 1,800 cy of soil with total chromium concentrations from 320 mg/kg to 1,100 mg/kg. The highest hexavalent chromium concentration in this stockpile was 22 mg/kg.
- **Disposal** – All excavated material was transported to a Class I landfill. No on‑site treatment was performed.
- **Verification sampling** – Post‑excavation confirmation samples were collected from the base and sidewalls of each stockpile footprint. Results were compared to DTSC’s interim action level of 5 mg/kg for hexavalent chromium (total chromium was evaluated as co‑contaminant). All verification samples met the action level; no exceedances were reported.
- **Volume removed** – Total Phase 1 removal: 4,200 cy.
- **Date** – Field activities completed in August 2020; RACR submitted December 2020.

## Regulatory status

- **Applicable standard** – The removal action was conducted under DTSC oversight using an interim action level for hexavalent chromium of 5 mg/kg (based on site‑specific risk assessment). Total chromium was managed as a co‑contaminant but had no separate action level because its primary hazard is hexavalent form.
- **Agency** – DTSC is the lead regulatory agency for this site under California’s hazardous waste cleanup program.
- **Compliance status** – The Phase 1 removal is compliant with the Interim Removal Action Work Plan (IRWP) approved in 2018. DTSC’s Draft Interim RACR Acceptance Letter noted that the report adequately described the work but required additional documentation (see Open Items) prior to final acceptance.

## Open items

- **Final acceptance** – DTSC did not issue final acceptance of the RACR in the source document. The Draft Acceptance Letter requested the following:
  - Revised tabular summaries of all verification sample results, including depth and coordinate data.
  - A figure showing post‑excavation sampling locations relative to stockpile boundaries.
  - A certification that all disposal manifests are complete and submitted to DTSC.
  - A description of any off‑site soil brought in as backfill (if applicable).
- **Phase 2** – The source document indicates that additional contaminated soil may remain in Stockpile C (not addressed in Phase 1). No decision document for Phase 2 has been referenced.
- **Groundwater** – The RACR did not address groundwater impacts. DTSC stated that groundwater monitoring is required under the existing monitoring plan; the RACR should note that no groundwater work was part of Phase 1.
