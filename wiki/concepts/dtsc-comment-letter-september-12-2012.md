## DTSC Comment Letter September 12, 2012

The DTSC Comment Letter dated September 12, 2012, represents a formal communication from the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) regarding the Draft Workplan for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This letter outlines specific comments and requests for clarification or revision from the DTSC, addressed to [[caltrans-district-6]]. The comments are a critical step in the regulatory review process for the proposed remediation activities under [[project-no-s9525-06-44]].

### Site Record

The DTSC Comment Letter September 12, 2012, is a document that contains comments on the Draft Workplan. The letter itself is not a record of site data such as contaminant concentrations or sampling results. Instead, it refers to the workplan and its contents.

### Regulatory Status

The DTSC, as the regulatory agency overseeing the remediation, issued comments on the Draft Workplan. The specific regulatory status of the project at the time of this letter is that it is under review, with the DTSC providing feedback for the workplan to be revised.

### Open Items

The DTSC Comment Letter September 12, 2012, details several open items that required attention from Caltrans. These comments are presented as requests for additional information or revisions to the Draft Workplan. Specific comments included:

*   A request for clarification on the scope of work for the soil vapor intrusion assessment, specifically asking for the rationale behind the proposed sampling locations and the number of samples. {{quote: "Please clarify the scope of work for the soil vapor intrusion assessment. Specifically, please provide the rationale for the proposed sampling locations and the number of samples."}}
*   Questions regarding the proposed sampling strategy for the soil stockpiles, including the number of samples and the rationale for their placement. {{quote: "Please clarify the proposed sampling strategy for the soil stockpiles. Specifically, please provide the rationale for the number of samples and their placement."}}
*   A request for additional information on the proposed analytical methods for soil and groundwater samples. {{quote: "Please provide additional information on the proposed analytical methods for soil and groundwater samples."}}
*   A request for clarification on the proposed remediation goals for soil and groundwater. {{quote: "Please clarify the proposed remediation goals for soil and groundwater."}}
*   A request for Caltrans to provide a schedule for the completion of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP). {{quote: "Please provide a schedule for the completion of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP)."}}

The letter was signed by Rebecca Silva, a representative of the DTSC. {{quote: "Sincerely, Rebecca Silva"}}
