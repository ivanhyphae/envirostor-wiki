## DTSC Comment Letter September 12, 2012

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) issued a comment letter on September 12, 2012, regarding the Draft Workplan for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This letter, addressed to [[caltrans-district-6]], outlined specific requests for clarification and additional information from Caltrans concerning the proposed site characterization and remediation activities under [[project-no-s9525-06-44]]. The DTSC's comments were a formal part of the regulatory review process for the workplan.

### Site Record

The DTSC Comment Letter September 12, 2012, is a correspondence document. It does not contain site-specific data such as contaminant concentrations, sampling results, or analytical findings. Its content is focused on the review of the Draft Workplan and the information provided within it.

### Regulatory Status

At the time of this letter, the Draft Workplan for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site was under review by the DTSC. The comment letter signifies that the DTSC required revisions and further details from Caltrans before the workplan could be approved.

### Open Items

The DTSC Comment Letter September 12, 2012, detailed several specific points requiring Caltrans' attention and response. These included:

*   A request for clarification on the scope of work for the soil vapor intrusion assessment, specifically asking for the rationale behind the proposed sampling locations and the number of samples.
*   Questions regarding the proposed sampling strategy for the soil stockpiles, including the number of samples and the rationale for their placement.
*   A request for additional information on the proposed analytical methods for soil and groundwater samples.
*   A request for clarification on the proposed remediation goals for soil and groundwater.
*   A request for Caltrans to provide a schedule for the completion of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP).

The letter was signed by Rebecca Silva, representing the DTSC.
