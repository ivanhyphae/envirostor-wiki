## Appendix F

Appendix F of the SR 132 DEIR/EA contains the "Soil Stockpile Management Plan" for the State Route 132 West Freeway Expressway Project. This plan outlines the procedures for managing soil stockpiles generated during construction to prevent potential environmental impacts.

## Site Record

The Soil Stockpile Management Plan (Appendix F) details the following:

*   **Stockpile Locations:** Soil stockpiles were to be located within the project limits, on Caltrans-owned property, or on other approved temporary locations.
*   **Stockpile Characterization:** Soil was to be characterized for potential contaminants prior to stockpiling. This characterization would inform management and disposal decisions.
*   **Stockpile Management:** Measures were to be implemented to prevent erosion and dust generation, including covering stockpiles, using berms, and watering.
*   **Disposal:** Stockpiled soil would be reused on-site for beneficial fill or exported to an approved off-site disposal facility, depending on its characterization.

## Regulatory Status

The Soil Stockpile Management Plan was developed to comply with the requirements of the [[california-environmental-quality-act]] (CEQA) and the [[national-environmental-policy-act]] (NEPA), as documented in the [[draft-environmental-impact-report-environmental-assessment]]. The plan itself serves as a mitigation measure to address potential impacts related to soil management during construction.
