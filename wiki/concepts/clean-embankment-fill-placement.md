## Overview

**Clean Embankment Fill Placement** refers to the approved placement of uncontaminated soil cover over areas of Stockpile 2 at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, California) after removal of barium-containing soil (BCS) and confirmation that residual contamination met Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) cleanup levels. This action was authorized by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) on March 10, 2020, following verification sampling at the east and west ends of the stockpile.

## Site Record

On March 9, 2020, Geocon Consultants, Inc. (via John Juhrend) submitted a Technical Memorandum (Memo) to Caltrans (Richard C. Stewart) documenting the removal of Barium Containing Soil (BCS) from the east and west ends of Stockpile 2. The memo described the removal activities and subsequent confirmation soil sampling designed to demonstrate that these areas achieved the cleanup levels established in the RDIP.

Confirmation sampling results showed that both the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 met the RDIP verification thresholds. Specifically:

- **Verification criteria**: RDIP-established thresholds for barium contamination in soil
- **Sampling area**: East and west ends of Stockpile 2 following BCS removal
- **Agency acceptance**: DTSC Project Manager Dean Wright (P.G.) accepted the memo via email on March 10, 2020, stating: *"The sampling results indicate that the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 have met the RDIP verification thresholds and DTSC agrees that these areas are now suitable for the placement of clean embankment fill."*

The email chain shows concurrent BCS removal at Stockpile 3, which was expected to be completed by March 10, 2020, pending final verification testing results.

## Regulatory Status

- **Lead regulatory agency**: DTSC (Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit), with Perry Myers also copied
- **Concurrent oversight**: Regional Water Quality Control Board (Steve Meeks)
- **Applicable guidance document**: Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP)
- **Regulatory status**: DTSC formally confirmed that these areas of Stockpile 2 were suitable for clean embankment fill placement. No conditions or follow-up actions were specified in the approval email.
