The California Secondary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) is a standard for drinking water quality set by the California Department of Public Health (CDPH) that addresses constituents affecting aesthetic qualities like taste, odor, and appearance, rather than direct health risks. Within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, the Secondary MCL for Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) is 500 mg/l, serving as a threshold for evaluating groundwater quality.
