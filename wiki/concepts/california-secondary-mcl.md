## California Secondary MCL

The California Secondary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) is a regulatory standard for drinking water quality set by the California Department of Public Health (CDPH). It addresses constituents that affect the aesthetic qualities of water, such as taste, odor, and appearance, rather than direct health risks.

### Site Record

Within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, the California Secondary MCL for Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) is 500 mg/l.

*   In May 2016, TDS was reported at 510 mg/l in well MW-7, exceeding the secondary MCL.
*   In January 2013, TDS concentrations ranged from 310 mg/l (MW-7) to 680 mg/l (MW-5). The reported TDS concentrations for MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   In November 2012, TDS concentrations ranged from 340 mg/l (MW-7) to 640 mg/l (MW-5 and MW-10). The reported TDS concentrations for MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 met or exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   In May 2012, TDS was reported at concentrations greater than the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l for samples collected from wells MW-4, MW-5, and MW-6.
*   In September 2012, TDS concentrations ranged from 280 mg/l (MW-8) to 630 mg/l (MW-10). The reported TDS concentrations for MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 exceeded the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l.
*   In December 2013, TDS concentrations ranged from 270 mg/l (MW-3) to 630 mg/l (MW-5). The reported TDS concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, and MW-10 equaled or exceeded the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l.
*   In June 2014, TDS concentrations ranged from 320 mg/l (MW-3) to 680 mg/l (MW-5). The reported TDS concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 exceeded the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l.
*   In April 2015, TDS concentrations ranged from 490 mg/l (MW-2 and MW-7) to 660 mg/l (MW-1). The reported TDS concentrations for samples from MW-1 and MW-4 exceeded the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l.

### Regulatory Status

The California Secondary MCL for TDS is a threshold used to evaluate groundwater quality at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. Exceedances of this standard have been noted in various monitoring wells across multiple sampling events, as detailed in the site record.
