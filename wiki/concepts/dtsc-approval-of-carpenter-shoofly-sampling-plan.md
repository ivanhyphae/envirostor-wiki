The [[dtsc-approval-of-carpenter-shoofly-sampling-plan]] is a regulatory decision by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) that authorized the sampling strategy for the [[carpenter-road-shoofly-area]] at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. This approval was a prerequisite for conducting necessary field activities to assess potential contamination.

### DTSC Approval of Sampling Plan

On March 29, 2021, DTSC, through Kyle Cockerham, formally approved the sampling plan for the Carpenter Road Shoofly Area. This approval was communicated via email to Perry Myers of [[caltrans]]. The approved plan details the procedures and locations for collecting soil samples within the Carpenter Road Shoofly Area to evaluate potential contamination.
