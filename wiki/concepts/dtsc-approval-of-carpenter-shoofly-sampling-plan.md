## DTSC Approval of Carpenter Shoofly Sampling Plan

### Overview
The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) approved the sampling plan for the Carpenter Road Shoofly Area on March 29, 2021. This approval was a necessary step for the remediation activities at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, specifically concerning the [[carpenter-road-shoofly-area]]. The sampling plan was developed to guide the collection of data related to potential contamination within this area.

### Site Record
The DTSC's approval of the sampling plan was communicated via email on March 29, 2021. The email was sent by Kyle Cockerham of DTSC to Perry Myers of Caltrans. The subject of the email was "Approval of Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Sampling Plan." This plan outlines the methodology for sampling within the Carpenter Road Shoofly Area.

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC, as the regulatory agency overseeing the remediation, provided the approval for the sampling plan. This approval signifies that the proposed sampling strategy meets the DTSC's requirements for data collection at the site.
