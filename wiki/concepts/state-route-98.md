## State Route 98

### Overview
State Route 98 (SR 98) is a highway project in Stanislaus County, California, that involved the remediation of soil stockpiles. Specifically, the project addressed issues related to [[barium]] contamination in soil, necessitating the use of [[clean-cover-fill-material]] and [[clean-soil-cover]] to manage these stockpiles.

### Site Record
The SR 132 project, which includes SR 98, involved the management of [[barium-containing-soil]] in stockpiles. Two specific stockpiles, identified as BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2, were located in the [[carpenter-road-area]] within the [[city-of-modesto]]. These stockpiles were generated from excavation activities associated with the SR 132 project. The remediation strategy involved the placement of a clean soil cover over these stockpiles.

### Regulatory Status
The management of the soil stockpiles, including those potentially impacting SR 98, was subject to regulatory oversight in [[california]]. The remediation efforts were designed to meet environmental standards for managing contaminated soil.
