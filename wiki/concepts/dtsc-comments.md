## Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) Comments

### Overview
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB), reviewed the "Final Supplemental Site Characterization Workplan, Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132 and 99, Stanislaus County, California" (Workplan). DTSC concurred with the Final Workplan, which was prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] to address DTSC's comments dated September 12, 2012. The Workplan aims to further characterize soil stockpiles at the [[state-route-132-99-west-expressway]] project site to evaluate and finalize a remedy for hazardous substances. This includes assessing potential lateral and vertical migration of contaminants and updating the Human Health Risk Assessment.
