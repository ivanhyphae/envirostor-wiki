## Overview
Stockpile 3 was one of three Barium-Containing Soil (BCS) stockpiles located within the Caltrans State Route 132 right-of-way in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. It was formerly located on the east side of SR 99. Stockpile 3 was completely excavated and used as embankment fill during the construction of Interim Phase 1 of the SR 132 West Project.

## Site record
Stockpile 3 was estimated to have a fill volume of 24,000 cubic yards (yd<sup>3</sup>) and was approximately 1,100 feet long and 120 feet wide. It was located east of SR 99, south of Kansas Avenue, and bordered by SR 99 to the south and west, and commercial/light industrial development to the north and east. The Modesto Irrigation District (MID) Lateral #4 canal concrete box culvert extended beneath its southeastern end.

In 2012, approximately 2,800 yd<sup>3</sup> of the northern portion of Stockpile 3 was excavated and disposed of in a Class II landfill as part of the rehabilitation of the SR 99 northbound off-ramp to Kansas Avenue. This work involved widening the off-ramp shoulder and constructing a retaining wall against the Stockpile 3 embankment. Air monitoring conducted during this excavation indicated that airborne levels of barium and lead were below regulatory thresholds.

Between February and March 2020, Stockpile 3 was completely removed. Approximately 27,675 yd<sup>3</sup> of BCS material was transported in 3,075 truckloads to be placed as embankment fill within the designated Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone. Twenty-nine verification soil samples were collected from the Stockpile 3 removal area. Initial results for three samples showed barium concentrations above the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) verification threshold of 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). These areas were deepened by approximately one foot and resampled. The final verification soil samples contained barium concentrations ranging from 46 to 970 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg, both below the RDIP verification thresholds.

Air monitoring was performed between February and June 2020 during BCS grading activities, including the relocation of Stockpile 3 material. Results indicated that airborne levels of total dust, barium, and lead were below designated project action and risk levels.

## Regulatory status
The RDIP established soil excavation verification thresholds for barium at 1,000 mg/kg (for groundwater protection) and for lead at 80 mg/kg (based on DTSC residential screening level). Site-specific upper-bound background concentrations for barium and lead were established at 120 mg/kg and 3.8 mg/kg, respectively.

The DTSC, in a letter dated December 17, 2009, stated that the soil stockpiles, as managed by Caltrans, did not pose a risk to human health for Caltrans workers, trespassers, or adjacent residents. Caltrans was directed to continue managing the stockpiles by limiting access, inspecting and maintaining fencing, prohibiting excavation or off-site removal, and maintaining the current grade and vegetative cover.

## Open items
The *Removal Action Completion Report* (RACR) was to be submitted following completion of Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities.
