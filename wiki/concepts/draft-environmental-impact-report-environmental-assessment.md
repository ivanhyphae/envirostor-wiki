## Draft Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment

The Draft Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (Draft EIR/EA) was a critical document prepared for the [[state-route-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]] and the associated [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] remediation. As the lead agency under the [[california-environmental-quality-act]] (CEQA) and the National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA), the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) developed this document. It was made available for public review and comment alongside the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP).

### Overview

The Draft EIR/EA examined the potential environmental impacts of the proposed State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project, which included plans for the containment of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. This document represented a collaborative effort between Caltrans, the Federal Highway Administration, and the Stanislaus Council of Governments (StanCOG). The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) reviewed the Draft Final RAP, which was integral to the environmental review process detailed in the EIR/EA.

### Site Record

The Draft EIR/EA specifically addressed the Caltrans Modesto Stockpiles, which are situated in three areas within state right-of-way south of Kansas Avenue: between Carpenter Avenue and Emerald Avenue, between Emerald Avenue and SR 99, and east of SR 99. These stockpiles collectively contain approximately 160,000 cubic yards of soil with elevated levels of metals, primarily [[barium]], [[lead]], and [[strontium]]. The preparation of the Draft EIR/EA was conducted under the oversight of the DTSC and RWQCB.

Caltrans certified the EIR/EA on March 2, 2018. The DTSC, acting as a CEQA Responsible Agency, utilized this EIR/EA for its CEQA analysis concerning the RAP.

### Regulatory Status

The Draft EIR/EA played a key role in the project's regulatory process. Caltrans, as the lead agency, certified the EIR/EA on March 2, 2018. Subsequently, on March 14, 2018, the DTSC, as a CEQA Responsible Agency, approved the project detailed in the EIR/EA, determining that it would not result in significant environmental effects. Mitigation measures were stipulated as conditions for project approval.

### Public Comment Period

A public comment period for the Draft EIR/EA and the Draft Final RAP was open from January 18, 2017, to March 17, 2017. A public hearing was also conducted on February 22, 2017.
