## Draft Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment

The Draft Environmental Impact Report (EIR)/Environmental Assessment (EA) was a key document prepared for the [[state-route-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]] and the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] remediation. It was developed by the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) as the lead agency under the [[california-environmental-quality-act]] (CEQA) and the National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA). The document, along with the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP), was made available for public review and comment.

### Overview

The Draft EIR/EA served to study the potential environmental effects of the proposed State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project, which included the containment of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. The document was a joint effort between Caltrans, the Federal Highway Administration, and the Stanislaus Council of Governments (StanCOG). The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) reviewed the Draft Final RAP, which was part of the overall environmental review process documented in the EIR/EA.

### Site Record

The Draft EIR/EA addressed the Caltrans Modesto Stockpiles, which occupy three areas within state right-of-way south of Kansas Avenue: between Carpenter Avenue and Emerald Avenue, Emerald Avenue and SR 99, and east of SR 99. These stockpiles total approximately 160,000 cubic yards of soil containing metals, primarily [[barium]], [[lead]], and [[strontium]]. The Draft EIR/EA was prepared under the oversight of the DTSC and RWQCB.

Caltrans certified the EIR/EA on March 2, 2018. {{quote: "Caltrans, acting as the Lead Agency under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA), prepared an Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment(EIR/EA) and Remedial Action Plan RAP). Caltrans certified the EIR/EA on March 2, 2018."}} The DTSC, as a CEQA Responsible Agency, utilized this EIR/EA for its CEQA analysis of the RAP. {{quote: "DTSC, as a CEQA Responsible Agency, is utilizing the EIR/EA for the RAP CEQA analysis."}}

### Regulatory Status

The Draft EIR/EA was a component of the regulatory process for the project. Caltrans, as the lead agency, certified the EIR/EA. {{quote: "Caltrans certified the EIR/EA on March 2, 2018."}} The DTSC, as a responsible agency, approved the project described in the EIR/EA on March 14, 2018, determining that the project would not have a significant effect on the environment. {{quote: "As Lead Agency a Responsible Agency under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA), DTSC approved the above-described project on March 14, 2018 and has made the following determinations: - 1. The project will not have a significant effect on the environment."}} Mitigation measures were made a condition of project approval. {{quote: "- 3. Mitigation measures were made a condition of project approval."}}

### Public Comment Period

A public comment period for the Draft EIR/EA and Draft Final RAP ran from January 18, 2017, to March 17, 2017. {{quote: "STARTS: January 18, 2017 ENDS: March 17, 2017"}} A public hearing was also held on February 22, 2017. {{quote: "DATE: February 22, 2017 TIME: 6:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. (Open House Format)"}}
