The Caltrans Dtsc Agreement is a regulatory document that governs the remediation of soil stockpiles at the SR 132 site in Stanislaus County, California. It outlines the responsibilities of [[caltrans]] and the DTSC in managing and addressing contamination at the site.

### DTSC Approval of Remedial Action Plan

The DTSC approved the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles on March 15, 2013. This approval was contingent upon [[caltrans]] fulfilling specific conditions. The RAP proposed excavation and off-site disposal of contaminated soil, with verification sampling to confirm the effectiveness of the remediation.

### Conditions of Approval

The DTSC's approval of the RAP required [[caltrans]] to implement several conditions. These included the proper management of excavated soil, adherence to waste characterization and disposal requirements, and the submission of post-remediation verification reports. The agreement also stipulated that [[caltrans]] would be responsible for all costs associated with the remediation and long-term monitoring.

### Personnel Involved

[[scott-nelson]], acting as the Project Manager for [[caltrans]], was responsible for overseeing the implementation of the RAP. [[paul-ruffin]] and [[richard-stewart]] were identified as key contacts within the DTSC for this project, with G-Scott-McGowen also noted in relation to the agreement.
