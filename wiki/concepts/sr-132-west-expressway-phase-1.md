# SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1

The SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1 project involved the remediation of the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] through containment and integration into the highway embankment. This project was overseen by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (RWQCB). The remediation strategy, as detailed in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP), involved consolidating barium-containing soil (BCS) and capping it with clean fill material.

## Overview

The SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1 project is the implementation of the remediation strategy for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. The chosen remedy, Alternative 4, involves the containment of barium-containing soil (BCS) by incorporating it as highway embankment fill for the SR 132 West Expressway. This approach balances contaminant isolation with the project's need for elevated fill material. The project is located adjacent to and west of Emerald Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California.

## Site Record

The remediation of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles was implemented as part of the SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1 project. The remedy consists of consolidating barium-containing soil (BCS) and capping it with clean fill material, utilizing it as highway embankment fill for the SR 132 West Expressway. The project site encompasses two main stockpile areas: Stockpile 1, located between SR 132 Stations 1125+50, and Stockpile 2, situated between Emerald Avenue and SR 99, from SR 132 Stations 1135+35 to 1148+30.

The initial construction phase of the project was planned to begin in 2018 and conclude in 2020. This phase included the construction of retaining walls and bridge abutments, paving the southern half of the site, and covering the northern half with a graded engineered soil layer.

A [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. and approved by the DTSC on April 3, 2019. An Errata to the January 30, 2019 RDIP was issued after the construction contract award to update roles and responsibilities, specifically noting that Geocon was no longer the consultant employed by the state.

Variances to the RDIP were requested and approved. On September 21, 2020, a variance was accepted allowing the use of Class 3 aggregate subbase material as a clean cap alternative, with a revised minimum thickness of 6 inches due to its fine-grained nature. Another variance, accepted on April 17, 2020, permitted the use of excavated MSE wall footing soil from Stockpile 1 as clean cap soil, provided its barium concentrations were within naturally occurring background levels.

## Regulatory Status

The remediation of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles was conducted under the oversight of the DTSC and the RWQCB. Alternative 4, containment by construction of the State Route 132 West project, was recommended by Caltrans and concurred with by DTSC and RWQCB. This alternative was selected to provide long-term protection of human health and the environment by isolating contaminants while meeting the project's need for fill material.

A Final Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Agreement was enclosed by DTSC on June 20, 2023. An Operations and Maintenance Plan (O&M Plan) was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. and submitted in December 2022. DTSC provided comments on the Draft O&M Plan on August 1, 2022.

## Open Items

As of June 20, 2023, the next project task was the recording of the Land Use Covenant (LUC) on the remediated area to restrict its use. DTSC indicated that a draft of the LUC would be sent for review and signature within the following weeks.
