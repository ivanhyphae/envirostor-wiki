# SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1

The SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1 project involved the remediation of the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] through containment and integration into the highway embankment. This project was overseen by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (RWQCB). The remediation strategy, as detailed in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP), involved consolidating barium-containing soil (BCS) and capping it with clean fill material.

## Overview

The SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1 project is the implementation of the remediation strategy for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. The chosen remedy, Alternative 4, involves the containment of barium-containing soil (BCS) by incorporating it as highway embankment fill for the SR 132 West Expressway. This approach balances contaminant isolation with the project's need for elevated fill material. The project is located adjacent to and west of Emerald Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California.

## Site Record

The remediation of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles was implemented as part of the SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1 project. The remedy consists of consolidating barium-containing soil (BCS) and capping it with clean fill material, utilizing it as highway embankment fill for the SR 132 West Expressway. {{quote: "The remedy consists of consolidation and clean fill capping of barium-containing soil (BCS) as highway embankment fill for the SR 132 West Expressway."}} The project site encompasses two main stockpile areas: Stockpile 1, located between SR 132 Stations 1125+50, and Stockpile 2, situated between Emerald Avenue and SR 99, from SR 132 Stations 1135+35 to 1148+30. {{quote: "The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the California Department of Transportation (Proponent) enter into this Operation and Maintenance Agreement (Agreement) for the site located adjacent and west of Emerald Avenue between approximate State Route (SR) 132 Stations 1125+50 (Stockpile 1) and between Emerald Avenue and SR 99 between approximate SR 132 Stations 1135+35 and 1148+30 (Stockpile 2) in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. Stockpiles 1 and 2 collectively constitute the Site."}}

The initial construction phase of the project was planned to begin in 2018 and conclude in 2020. {{quote: "The opening phase would begin in 2018 and end in 2020."}} This phase included the construction of retaining walls and bridge abutments, paving the southern half of the site, and covering the northern half with a graded engineered soil layer. {{quote: "The initial construction phase (see Phase 1: Typical Soil Stockpile Cross-Section) will build retaining walls and bridge abutments, and pave the southern half of the site. The northern half will be covered with a graded engineered soil layer."}}

A [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. and approved by the DTSC on April 3, 2019. {{quote: "The RDIP was submitted to the Department of Toxic Substances and subsequently approved on April 3, 2019."}} An Errata to the January 30, 2019 RDIP was issued after the construction contract award to update roles and responsibilities, specifically noting that Geocon was no longer the consultant employed by the state. {{quote: "This Post Construction Contract Award Errata updates the pertinent sections of the RDIP to reflect changes to previously identified roles and responsibilities, namely striking Geocon as the consultant employed by the state."}}

Variances to the RDIP were requested and approved. On September 21, 2020, a variance was accepted allowing the use of Class 3 aggregate subbase material as a clean cap alternative, with a revised minimum thickness of 6 inches due to its fine-grained nature. {{quote: "Both DTSC and the WB agree that Class 3 subbase can be used as an acceptable clean cap alternative however, a visual inspection of this stockpiled material conducted on September 15, 2020 revealed that it is primarily a fine sand with most of the grains likely small enough to pass through a No. 4 sieve. Given its abundant fine grained nature, both agencies agree that a minimum cap thickness of 6 inches rather than 4 inches is warranted and that increased monitoring may be necessary."}} Another variance, accepted on April 17, 2020, permitted the use of excavated MSE wall footing soil from Stockpile 1 as clean cap soil, provided its barium concentrations were within naturally occurring background levels. {{quote: "Both agencies agree that this material can therefore be used as clean cap soil on the project."}}

## Regulatory Status

The remediation of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles was conducted under the oversight of the DTSC and the RWQCB. Alternative 4, containment by construction of the State Route 132 West project, was recommended by Caltrans and concurred with by DTSC and RWQCB. {{quote: "Based on an evaluation of the remedial actions, Caltrans recommends Alternative 4, containment by construction of the State Route 132 West project. DTSC and RWQCB concur with this recommendation."}} This alternative was selected to provide long-term protection of human health and the environment by isolating contaminants while meeting the project's need for fill material. {{quote: "This alternative offers long-term protection of human health and the environment, while balancing contaminant isolation with removal/disposal costs and the need for elevated fill material necessary to construct the State Route 132 West project between Needham Street and Carpenter Avenue."}}

A Final Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Agreement was enclosed by DTSC on June 20, 2023. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) has enclosed a final copy of the Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Agreement for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Phase 1 Interim State Route 132 West Project, Modesto, Stanislaus County, California (Site)."}} An Operations and Maintenance Plan (O&M Plan) was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. and submitted in December 2022. {{quote: "This O&M Plan has been prepared for barium-containing soil stockpiles that were consolidated, capped with clean fill material within designated Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones, and utilized as highway embankment fill between State Route 99 and west of Emerald Avenue during construction of Interim Phase I of the SR 132 West Project."}} DTSC provided comments on the Draft O&M Plan on August 1, 2022. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), has reviewed the Draft Operation and Maintenance Plan (Draft O&M Plan) noted above which will be included in the Remedial Action Completion Report as Appendix G."}}

## Open Items

As of June 20, 2023, the next project task was the recording of the Land Use Covenant (LUC) on the remediated area to restrict its use. {{quote: "The next project task will be recording the Land Use Covenant (LUC) on the remediated area to restrict its use."}} DTSC indicated that a draft of the LUC would be sent for review and signature within the following weeks. {{quote: "DTSC will send a draft of the LUC for your review and signature within the next several weeks."}}
