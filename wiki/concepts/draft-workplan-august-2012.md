## Draft Workplan August 2012

### Overview
The Draft Workplan August 2012 represents a proposed plan for remediation activities at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This document was submitted for review by the [[dtsc-comment-letter-september-12-2012]].

### Site Record
The Draft Workplan August 2012 was submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] on behalf of Caltrans. It was prepared in response to comments from the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) regarding previous workplans. The workplan outlines proposed sampling and analysis activities.

### Regulatory Status
The Draft Workplan August 2012 was under review by the DTSC as of September 12, 2012. The DTSC provided comments on this draft workplan in a letter dated September 12, 2012.

### Open Items
The DTSC's comment letter dated September 12, 2012, details several open items and requests for clarification or revision within the Draft Workplan August 2012. These include comments on:
*   The proposed sampling locations and rationale.
*   The analytical methods to be employed.
*   The proposed remediation strategy.
*   The scope of the human health risk assessment.

The DTSC requested that Caltrans address these comments and resubmit a revised workplan.
