## Draft Workplan August 2012

### Overview
The Draft Workplan August 2012, submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] on behalf of Caltrans, outlines proposed sampling and analysis activities for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This document was prepared in response to comments from the [[dtsc-comment-letter-september-12-2012]].

### Site Record
The Draft Workplan August 2012 details proposed sampling and analysis activities. Specific details regarding proposed sampling locations, analytical methods, the remediation strategy, and the scope of the human health risk assessment are contained within this document.

### Regulatory Status
As of September 12, 2012, the Draft Workplan August 2012 was under review by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). The DTSC issued comments on this workplan in a letter dated September 12, 2012.

### Open Items
The DTSC's comment letter dated September 12, 2012, identified several open items requiring Caltrans' attention and revision of the Draft Workplan August 2012. These comments pertained to the proposed sampling locations and their rationale, the analytical methods to be employed, the proposed remediation strategy, and the scope of the human health risk assessment. The DTSC requested that Caltrans address these comments and resubmit a revised workplan.
