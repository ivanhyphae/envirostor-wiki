## Site Record


### Dissolved Metals

In the groundwater sample collected from MW-3 in September 2013, manganese was detected at a concentration that exceeded its numeric water quality threshold. The specific concentration and threshold are not detailed in the provided source material for MW-3. However, the general conclusion for dissolved metals in September 2013 was that, with the exception of manganese detected in samples from MW-3 and MW-9, none of the reported dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values.

### General Minerals

In the groundwater sample collected from MW-3 in September 2013, nitrate as nitrogen was reported at a concentration of 3.0 mg/l. This concentration meets or exceeds the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l. Total dissolved solids (TDS) were reported for MW-3 at 300 mg/l, which does not exceed the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l. Sulfide was reported for MW-3 at a concentration of 0.011 mg/l.

In the groundwater sample collected from MW-3 in September 2012, nitrate as nitrogen was reported at a concentration of 3.0 mg/l. In the groundwater sample collected from MW-3 in March 2013, nitrate as nitrogen was reported at a concentration of 2.7 mg/l. This concentration does not exceed the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l. In the groundwater sample collected from MW-3 in January 2013, nitrate as nitrogen was reported at a concentration of 2.9 mg/l. This concentration does not exceed the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l. In the groundwater sample collected from MW-3 in February 2014, nitrate as nitrogen was reported at a concentration of 2.8 mg/l. In the groundwater sample collected from MW-3 in June 2014, nitrate as nitrogen was reported at a concentration of 2.6 mg/l. This concentration does not exceed the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l.
