MW-3 is a groundwater monitoring well located at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It plays a role in tracking groundwater quality and potential contaminant migration.

## Site Record

### Dissolved Metals

In September 2013, manganese was detected in the groundwater sample from MW-3 at a concentration exceeding its numeric water quality threshold. Other dissolved metals concentrations in MW-3 samples did not exceed their respective numeric water quality thresholds.

The primary MCL for nitrate is 10 mg/L, and the secondary MCL for TDS is 500 mg/L.

### Stiff Diagrams

Stiff diagrams for MW-3 consistently indicate a sodium-dominant water composition from March 2012 through April 2019. This pattern is noted in comparison to other wells, such as MW-6 and MW-8, which also frequently exhibit sodium dominance, while wells like MW-1, MW-2, and MW-4 are often calcium-dominant.

### Depth to Groundwater Measurements

Monitoring well MW-3 was reported as dry during the following groundwater monitoring events:
*   October 2016
*   April 2015
*   October 2015
