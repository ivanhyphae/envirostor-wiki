The [[brownfields-and-environmental-restoration-program]] is a program that addresses contaminated sites, often referred to as brownfields, with the goal of environmental restoration. In the context of the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]], this program is relevant to the remediation efforts undertaken.

## Site record

The [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] involved the management and remediation of soil stockpiles. The [[brownfields-and-environmental-restoration-program]] is associated with the overall environmental restoration goals for the site. Specific details regarding the program's direct involvement with analytes, wells, or specific remediation actions at the Modesto site are not explicitly detailed in the provided source material beyond its general purpose.

## Regulatory status

The [[brownfields-and-environmental-restoration-program]] operates within the framework of environmental regulations. The [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] underwent review processes, including the preparation of an [[environmental-impact-report-environmental-assessment]]. A Notice of Determination was issued, indicating a decision regarding the project's environmental impact. The project's compliance and status are subject to the oversight of regulatory agencies such as the [[dtsc]] (DTSC) and adherence to the [[public-resources-code]].
