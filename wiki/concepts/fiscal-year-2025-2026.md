The Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026 refers to the period from July 1, 2025, to June 30, 2026. During this time, the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) is responsible for providing regulatory oversight for the State Route 132 West Expressway project in Modesto, California. This oversight is mandated by.

## Site Record

The DTSC has provided an estimate of costs for regulatory oversight activities for FY 2025-2026. The schedule and cost estimate are subject to updates as necessary to reflect changes to the project or rates.

The DTSC's estimated oversight costs for FY 2025-2026 are detailed in a table that breaks down tasks, anticipated timelines, and estimated costs.

## Regulatory Status

As part of the cleanup at the site, a [[land-use-covenant]] was implemented to restrict certain uses or activities. Pursuant to, DTSC has the authority to require Caltrans to cover all costs associated with the administration of the LUC.

This document is an estimate of projected costs, not an invoice. DTSC will issue invoices based on actual costs incurred. Caltrans remains liable for all response costs incurred by DTSC under and. DTSC reserves all rights to pursue cost recovery under applicable state or federal law.
