## Overview

The Annual Cost Estimate Letter for Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026 details the Department of Toxic Substances Control's (DTSC) projected oversight costs for the State Route 132 West Expressway project in Modesto, California. This estimate is a requirement under [[california-health-and-safety-code-section-25269.5]]. The letter also clarifies DTSC's authority to recover costs associated with the administration of a [[land-use-covenant]] at the site, as per [[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]].

## Site Record

The DTSC issued an Annual Cost Estimate Letter on December 24, 2025, to Caltrans D-6 | Office of Environmental Engineering, addressed to Mr. Adam Inman, P.G. {{quote: "December 24, 2025"}} The letter pertains to the fiscal-year-2025-2026}}, which commenced on July 1, 2025, and concludes on June 30, 2026. {{quote: "for the Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026 which began July 1, 2025, and ends June 30, 2026."}} The project is identified by Site Code: 900259. {{quote: "STATE ROUTE 132 WEST EXPRESSWAY (AKA: STATE ROUTE 132/99 INTERCHANGE), INTERSECTION OF STATE ROUTE 132/99 IN MODESTO, CALIFORNIA (SITE CODE: 900259)"}} The cost estimate is derived from DTSC's contracted estimation rates established under an interagency agreement. {{quote: "The cost estimate is based on DTSC's contracted estimation rates under the interagency agreement."}}

## Regulatory Status

California Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5 mandates that DTSC provide a cost estimate for its regulatory oversight activities. {{quote: "Section 25269.5 of the California Health and Safety Code requires the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) to provide a cost estimate for regulatory oversight activities"}} Furthermore, California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Section 67391.1(h) empowers DTSC to require Caltrans to reimburse all costs associated with the administration of a Land Use Covenant. {{quote: "Pursuant to California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Section 67391.1 (h), DTSC has the authority to require Caltrans to pay all costs associated with the administration of the LUC."}}

Caltrans is responsible for all response costs incurred by DTSC, as outlined in Health and Safety Code Section 79650 et seq. and the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA) 42 U.S.C. Section 9601 et seq. {{quote: "Caltrans remains liable for all response costs incurred by DTSC pursuant to *Health and Safety Code Section 79650 et seq.*, and the *Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA) 42 U.S.C. Section 9601 et seq.*"}} DTSC reserves the right to seek cost recovery under any applicable state or federal law provisions. {{quote: "DTSC reserves all rights to pursue cost recovery under any, and all applicable provisions of state or federal law."}}
