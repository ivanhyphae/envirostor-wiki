The Annual Cost Estimate Letter for Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026 outlines the Department of Toxic Substances Control's (DTSC) projected oversight costs for the State Route 132 West Expressway project in Modesto, California. This estimate is a requirement under [[california-health-and-safety-code-section-25269.5]]. The letter also addresses DTSC's authority to recover costs associated with the administration of a [[land-use-covenant]] at the site, as per [[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]].

## Site Record

The DTSC issued an Annual Cost Estimate Letter on December 24, 2025, to Caltrans D-6 | Office of Environmental Engineering, specifically to Mr. Adam Inman, P.G. {{quote: "December 24, 2025"}} The letter covers the [[fiscal-year-2025-2026}}, which began July 1, 2025, and ends June 30, 2026. {{quote: "for the Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026 which began July 1, 2025, and ends June 30, 2026."}} The project is identified by Site Code: 900259. {{quote: "STATE ROUTE 132 WEST EXPRESSWAY (AKA: STATE ROUTE 132/99 INTERCHANGE), INTERSECTION OF STATE ROUTE 132/99 IN MODESTO, CALIFORNIA (SITE CODE: 900259)"}} The estimate is based on DTSC's contracted estimation rates under an interagency agreement. {{quote: "The cost estimate is based on DTSC's contracted estimation rates under the interagency agreement."}}

## Regulatory Status

California Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5 mandates that DTSC provide a cost estimate for regulatory oversight activities. {{quote: "Section 25269.5 of the California Health and Safety Code requires the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) to provide a cost estimate for regulatory oversight activities"}} Additionally, California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Section 67391.1(h) grants DTSC the authority to require Caltrans to cover all costs related to the administration of a Land Use Covenant. {{quote: "Pursuant to California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Section 67391.1 (h), DTSC has the authority to require Caltrans to pay all costs associated with the administration of the LUC."}}
