## Overview

The Annual Cost Estimate Letter for Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026 details the Department of Toxic Substances Control's (DTSC) projected oversight costs for the State Route 132 West Expressway project in Modesto, California. This estimate is a requirement under [[california-health-and-safety-code-section-25269.5]]. The letter also clarifies DTSC's authority to recover costs associated with the administration of a [[land-use-covenant]] at the site, as per [[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]].

## Site Record

The DTSC issued an Annual Cost Estimate Letter on December 24, 2025, to Caltrans D-6 | Office of Environmental Engineering, addressed to Mr. Adam Inman, P.G. The letter pertains to the [[fiscal-year-2025-2026]], which commenced on July 1, 2025, and concludes on June 30, 2026. The project is identified by Site Code: 900259. The cost estimate is derived from DTSC's contracted estimation rates established under an interagency agreement.

## Regulatory Status

California Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5 mandates that DTSC provide a cost estimate for its regulatory oversight activities. Furthermore, California Code of Regulations, Title 22, Section 67391.1(h) empowers DTSC to require Caltrans to reimburse all costs associated with the administration of a Land Use Covenant.

Caltrans is responsible for all response costs incurred by DTSC, as outlined in Health and Safety Code Section 79650 et seq. and the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA) 42 U.S.C. Section 9601 et seq. DTSC reserves the right to seek cost recovery under any applicable state or federal law provisions.
