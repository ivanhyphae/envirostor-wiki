The Fenceline Workplan, submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] to [[dtsc]] in September 2012 on behalf of [[caltrans]], proposed a program of [[fenceline-sampling]] around the Modesto Soil Stockpiles to characterize conditions at the site perimeter. DTSC reviewed the September 2012 submittal and returned comments, which Geocon addressed in a Response to DTSC Workplan Comments dated September 2012 (S9525-06-44). The two documents together constitute the supplemental site investigation workplan record for this phase of the project.

## What the workplan proposed

The Fenceline Workplan set out a sampling approach directed at the fenceline surrounding the stockpiles, intended to supplement the site characterization already developed for the stockpile soils themselves. It was prepared by Geocon Consultants under the S9525-06-44 project number for Caltrans.

## DTSC review and Geocon's response

DTSC provided comments on the workplan, and Geocon's Response to DTSC Workplan Comments (September 2012) addresses those comments point by point, revising or clarifying elements of the proposed fenceline sampling approach in response to DTSC's review. The response document is the vehicle through which the workplan was reconciled with DTSC's requirements before the sampling program went forward.

## Significance

The workplan and its response record establishes that the fenceline sampling program was not self-directed by Caltrans or Geocon but was shaped through DTSC's review-and-comment process, making the fenceline dataset a regulator-vetted characterization tool rather than a purely internal one. Beyond the existence of this review cycle, the source material available does not detail the specific comments DTSC raised or the specific changes Geocon made in response.
