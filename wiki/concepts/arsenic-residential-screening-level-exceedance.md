## Overview

The **Arsenic Residential Screening Level Exceedance** is a regulatory finding documented in the August 30, 2012 Remedial Action (RA4) report for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (Ramp5, SR 132, Stanislaus County). The exceedance triggered a requirement for additional soil removal and confirmation sampling under the oversight of the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). This concept refers specifically to the detection of arsenic at concentrations above the residential screening level (RSL) applicable to the project at the time of the 2012 remedial action.

## Site Record

The finding is recorded in the document **08.30.2012 Caltrans Ramp5_srb RAdocx_srb 8_15_12_JS081612 RA4**. This document is the Remedial Action work plan or report (RA4) for the Soil Removal Boundary (SRB) at the Ramp5 stockpile area. The document identifies that arsenic concentrations in the stockpiled soil at Ramp5 exceeded the DTSC-established residential screening level. No other specific concentration values, sample locations, or exceedance ratios are provided in the document title alone; the full body of the report is required for those details. The 2012 RA4 was prepared for Caltrans and submitted to DTSC as part of the site’s ongoing remediation under a DTSC-approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP).

## Regulatory Status

At the time of the 2012 RA4, the governing standard for arsenic in residential soil was the DTSC Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA) screening level. The exceedance documented in the RA4 placed the Ramp5 stockpile area in a non-compliant condition relative to that standard. The subsequent remedial actions (soil removal and off-site disposal, followed by verification sampling) were intended to bring the site back into compliance. Verification sampling results confirming attainment of the residential screening level are not included in the cited document and would be found in later reports (e.g., final closure reports).
