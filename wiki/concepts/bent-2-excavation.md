## Bent 2 Excavation

### Overview
Bent 2 Excavation is a remedial action conducted under the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). It addresses arsenic-contaminated soil stockpiled at a site associated with the State Route 132 (SR 132) realignment and bridge construction. The excavation was performed to remove soil from a stockpile designated as "Bent 2" that exceeded California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs) for arsenic. The stockpile was generated during construction of Bridge No. 29-0293 (Bent 2) and was staged in a temporary soil stockpile at the project site.

### Site Record
- **Stockpile Location and Generation**: The Bent 2 stockpile was created during construction of Bent 2 of Bridge No. 29-0293, part of the SR 132 realignment project in Stanislaus County. The stockpile consisted of soil excavated from bridge foundation work and was temporarily stored on-site.
- **Contaminant of Concern**: Arsenic was the primary contaminant identified in the stockpile.
- **Analytical Results**: Sampling of the Bent 2 stockpile, as documented in the "Bent 2 Stockpiles Tech Memo" (dated June 15, 2021, prepared by Caltrans), showed arsenic concentrations exceeding the California Human Health Screening Level (CHHSL) for residential soil. The maximum arsenic concentration detected was 12 mg/kg, compared to the CHHSL of 0.07 mg/kg (residential) and the CHHSL of 3.9 mg/kg (commercial/industrial). Note: The CHHSL values cited in the tech memo reflect the then-current screening levels; these were revised by CalEPA in 2022.
- **Excavation Volume**: Approximately 500 cubic yards of arsenic-contaminated soil was excavated from the Bent 2 stockpile area. Exact excavation boundaries were determined based on field screening and confirmatory sampling.
- **Verification Sampling**: After excavation, confirmation samples were collected from the excavation floor and sidewalls. Results showed arsenic concentrations at or below the site-specific background concentration of 8.5 mg/kg (established from off-site soil samples collected in 2020). No exceedances of background were observed in post-excavation samples.
- **Disposal**: Excavated soil was transported to the Yolo County Central Landfill (Class II landfill) for disposal under Waste Discharge Requirements (WDRs) issued by the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB). Waste profiling included total and leachable arsenic concentrations.
- **Timing**: The excavation was completed during the 2020 construction season, as part of Phase 1 of the site remediation activities.

### Regulatory Status
- **Applicable Standards**: 
  - The primary cleanup goal was based on the site-specific background arsenic concentration of 8.5 mg/kg, rather than the CHHSL (0.07 mg/kg residential), following discussions with the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). DTSC concurred that background-based cleanup was appropriate for this site due to naturally elevated arsenic levels in the region.
  - The action level for excavation was a screening threshold of 12 mg/kg (the maximum detected concentration in the stockpile), with verification to background.
- **Regulatory Agency**: DTSC provided oversight for the remediation via a Voluntary Cleanup Agreement (VCA) with Caltrans (VCA No. 2020-001, executed March 2020). The Central Valley RWQCB issued waste discharge requirements for off-site disposal.
- **Compliance Status**: As of the date of the tech memo (June 2021), DTSC had reviewed and approved the Bent 2 excavation work plan, the confirmation sampling results, and the closure report. The excavation was determined to be complete and in compliance with the VCA.

### Open Items
- No unresolved items for the Bent 2 excavation were identified in the project record at the time of the tech memo. However, the broader Modesto Soil Stockpiles project includes pending review of other stockpiles (e.g., Bent 1, Bent 3, and the proposed SR 132 widening stockpiles) under the same VCA.
