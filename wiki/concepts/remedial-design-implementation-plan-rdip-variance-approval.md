The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) Variance Approval is a regulatory milestone that signifies approval of proposed deviations from the original remedial design for the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. This approval is a critical step in authorizing specific remediation activities to proceed.

### Variance Approval for Barium-Containing Soil Consolidation

On March 29, 2017, the Central Valley-RWQCB issued a letter approving a variance to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the SR 132 project. This variance specifically addressed the proposed method for managing soil containing [[barium]]. The approved variance allows for the consolidation of barium-containing soil within the project site, rather than off-site disposal. The approval was granted by the Central Valley-RWQCB, referencing the project's Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and the associated RDIP.

The variance was approved subject to specific conditions designed to ensure the protection of human health and the environment. These conditions included requirements for the placement and containment of the consolidated soil, as well as ongoing monitoring. The approval letter from the Central Valley-RWQCB is dated March 29, 2017.
