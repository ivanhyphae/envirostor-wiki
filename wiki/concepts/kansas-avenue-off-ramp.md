# Kansas Avenue Off Ramp

## Overview
The Kansas Avenue Off Ramp is the northbound off-ramp from sr-99|State Route 99 to Kansas Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. It is the site of the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project, a Caltrans traffic safety and widening improvement project. Construction activities on the east side of the off-ramp, including shoulder widening and the installation of a retaining wall, require excavation into the northern portion of modesto-soil-stockpiles-project|Stockpile 3. This excavation involves the removal of an estimated 2,800 cubic yards of stockpile material and underlying native soils.

## Site record
The Kansas Avenue Off Ramp improvements are detailed in the Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. and dated June 13, 2012. The project area is depicted in Figures 1, 2, and 3 of this plan. Key construction elements within the off-ramp corridor include:

*   **Retaining wall**: Planned along the east side of the off-ramp, adjacent to Stockpile 3.
*   **Shoulder widening**: East-side widening necessitating the retaining wall and slope re-grading into Stockpile 3.
*   **Drainage features**: Associated drainage construction as part of the safety upgrade.

The excavation area for the off-ramp construction overlaps the northern extent of Stockpile 3. This stockpile, crescent-shaped and approximately 2.5 acres in size, is located on the east side of SR 99 south of Kansas Avenue. Groundwater beneath the site is documented at a depth of approximately 36 feet below natural grade.

In April 2012, Geocon conducted 19 soil borings within the Stockpile 3 off-ramp excavation area to characterize the material for construction. This area is identified on the Site Plan (Figure 3) as the "Proposed Stockpile 3 Excavation Area." The stockpile material originated from excavations in the early 1960s of a 4.3-acre parcel that included unlined evaporation ponds. These ponds contained liquid residues from the processing of barite and celestite ores, resulting in elevated concentrations of metals, notably [[barium]].

Groundwater monitoring wells MW6, MW7, and MW8 are located within Stockpile 3 and are relevant to groundwater monitoring for the stockpile material. Monitoring well MW-10, installed in June 2012, is located south of Kansas Avenue and east of SR 99, in the vicinity of the off-ramp and Stockpile 3.

## Regulatory status
The dtsc|Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board, oversees the stockpiles. DTSC's December 17, 2009 response letter to Caltrans' 2009 Final Preliminary Endangerment Assessment concluded that the stockpiles, as managed by Caltrans, do not pose a risk to human health for workers, trespassers, or adjacent residents. However, DTSC directed Caltrans to continue managing the stockpiles.

DTSC concurred with the May, June, and July 2012 groundwater monitoring reports for the broader Modesto Soil Stockpiles site in a November 29, 2012 letter from Randy S. Adams. These reports included data from monitoring wells MW-9 and MW-10, installed in June 2012.

## Open items
The Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan (June 13, 2012) states that its information is valid only as of that date and would require an update to reflect additional site activities. Specific real-time air monitoring action levels and post-excavation verification sampling criteria for the off-ramp work are detailed in Section 4.0 of the plan. Compliance with these monitoring requirements during actual construction remains an item for documentation in subsequent project deliverables.
