# Kansas Avenue Off Ramp

## Overview
The Kansas Avenue Off Ramp is the northbound off-ramp from sr-99|State Route 99 to Kansas Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. It is the locus of the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project, a traffic safety and widening improvement undertaken by the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) under Contract No. 06A1634, EA No. 10-0A671. The off-ramp corridor directly abuts Stockpile 3, one of three fill embankments placed in the early 1960s containing soil excavated from former evaporation ponds at an adjacent barium|barium-processing facility. Construction activities—shoulder widening on the east side of the off-ramp—require excavation into the northern portion of Stockpile 3 and the laying back of the embankment slope, as well as construction of a retaining wall. The work entails removal of an estimated 2,800 cubic yards of stockpile material plus underlying native soils.

## Site record
The off-ramp improvements are depicted on the Improvement Plan (Figure 2) of the Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan prepared by geocon-consultants|Geocon Consultants, Inc. and dated June 13, 2012. The project location is shown on Figure 1 (Vicinity Map) and Figure 3 (Site Plan) of the same document. Key elements of the off-ramp construction zone include:

- **Retaining wall**: Planned against a portion of the Stockpile 3 fill embankment on the east side of the off-ramp.
- **Shoulder widening**: East-side widening that necessitated the retaining wall and slope re-grading into Stockpile 3.
- **Drainage features**: Associated drainage construction as part of the overall safety upgrade.

The off-ramp construction excavation area overlaps the northern extent of crescent-shaped Stockpile 3, which occupies approximately 2.5 acres on the east side of SR 99 south of Kansas Avenue. A Modesto Irrigation District canal (Lateral No. 4) crosses beneath the southern portion of Stockpile 3. Three groundwater monitoring wells—MW6, MW7, and MW8—are located within Stockpile 3 and are relevant to groundwater monitoring related to the stockpile material. The stockpile fill at its northern end (the area of off-ramp excavation) reaches a maximum thickness of approximately 17 feet. Groundwater beneath the site is present at a depth of approximately 36 feet below natural grade.

During April 2012, Geocon performed 19 soil borings specifically within the Stockpile 3 off-ramp excavation area to provide supplemental, project-specific characterization data for the planned construction. The off-ramp excavation area is labeled on the Site Plan (Figure 3) as the "Proposed Stockpile 3 Excavation Area."

The stockpile material itself originated from excavation in the early 1960s of a 4.3-acre parcel at the southwest corner of the former Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC) facility, which included a portion of unlined evaporation ponds. Those ponds had received liquid residues containing elevated metals—notably barium|barium—and other chemicals of potential concern from the chemical processing of barite (barium sulfate) and celestite (strontium sulfate) ores.

## Regulatory status
The 2009 Final Preliminary Endangerment Assessment (PEA) prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc., and the dtsc|Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)'s December 17, 2009 response letter concluded that the stockpiles as managed by Caltrans do not pose a risk to human health for Caltrans workers, trespassers, or adjacent residents. However, DTSC directed Caltrans to continue managing the stockpiles. The Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan, submitted to Caltrans on June 13, 2012, under the signature of john-e-juhrend|John E. Juhrend (Project Manager) and douglas-s-krause|Douglas S. Krause (Certified Industrial Hygienist), details air monitoring and verification procedures to be implemented during the Kansas Avenue off-ramp excavation activities. The plan was prepared in accordance with Caltrans Contract No. 06A1634. The DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board, maintains oversight of the stockpiles, as evidenced by the November 29, 2012 letter from Randy S. Adams concurring with May, June, and July 2012 groundwater monitoring reports for the broader Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. Monitoring well MW-10, installed in June 2012, is located south of Kansas Avenue and east of SR 99, within the vicinity of the off-ramp and Stockpile 3 area.

## Open items
The Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan explicitly states that the information contained in the plan is valid only as of the date of the plan (June 13, 2012) and would require an update to reflect additional site activities. Specific real-time air monitoring action levels and post-excavation verification sampling criteria for the off-ramp work are detailed in Section 4.0 of the plan but not excerpted in full in the available record. Compliance with those monitoring requirements during actual construction remains an item for documentation in subsequent deliverables.
