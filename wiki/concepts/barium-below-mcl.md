## Overview

Barium below MCL refers to the finding that barium concentrations in groundwater at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (State Route 132, Stanislaus County, CA) have been determined to be below the federal and state Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) of 1,000 µg/L. This conclusion is documented in the *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report (S2350-01-02)*, dated February 24, 2025, which serves as the key source for this article. Barium is a naturally occurring metal that can be mobilized by anthropogenic activities, but at this site, the statistical evaluation confirms it does not exceed the regulatory threshold for drinking water, reducing the scope of required remediation actions.

## Site Record

The *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report_2.24.md* (dated February 24, 2025) presents a statistical analysis of groundwater monitoring data from the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, associated with Caltrans’ SR 132 project. The report specifically evaluates barium levels against the MCL.

**Key documented findings:**
- **Contaminant:** Barium.
- **Threshold:** MCL of 1,000 µg/L (micrograms per liter), applicable to groundwater as a potential drinking water source under the Safe Drinking Water Act and California Title 22.
- **Data source:** The evaluation uses historical groundwater sampling results from on-site monitoring wells. Specific well identifiers (e.g., MW-5) and sampling dates are referenced in the full report but not excerpted here.
- **Result:** The statistical analysis concludes that barium concentrations in all sampled wells are below the MCL of 1,000 µg/L. No exceedances of this threshold have been identified during the monitoring period covered by the report.
- **Temporal scope:** The report covers data through its February 2025 publication date; no specific earlier cutoff date is given in the source.

The report does not detail individual barium concentration values (e.g., mg/kg for soil; all groundwater data is in µg/L), spatial distribution beyond well locations, or trends over time. The finding is a binary determination of compliance with the MCL.

## Regulatory Status

The applicable standard is **barium MCL of 1,000 µg/L**, enforced by:
- **U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (USEPA)** under the Safe Drinking Water Act.
- **California State Water Resources Control Board (SWRCB)** and **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)** under California Code of Regulations, Title 22.

Based on the *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report*, the site is currently **in compliance** with the barium MCL. No additional regulatory action specific to barium has been identified from the source document. The report is presumed to be submitted to DTSC or a similar oversight agency as part of ongoing site characterization or remedial action reporting.
