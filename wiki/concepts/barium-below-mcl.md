# Barium Below Mcl

## Overview
Barium is one of the groundwater constituents monitored at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site in Stanislaus County, California, under State Route 132. Within the June 2014 groundwater monitoring record, barium was reported in relation to the regulatory threshold known as the Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL). The concept "Barium Below MCL" captures the finding that detected barium concentrations in groundwater beneath the stockpile area did not exceed the applicable drinking water standard during that monitoring event.

## Site record
The June 2014 Groundwater Monitoring Report, prepared by geocon-consultants for [[caltrans]], documents groundwater sampling results for the monitoring well network at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles. Barium was among the analytes tested. The report notes that no concentrations of barium exceeded the MCL during this monitoring round. Specific barium concentrations, well-by-well results, and detection limits are presented in the laboratory analytical data tables included in the report; however, the narrative text confirms the universal finding of compliance with the MCL across all monitoring locations sampled.

## Regulatory status
The applicable regulatory threshold for barium in groundwater is the California Maximum Contaminant Level set by the State Water Resources Control Board, Division of Drinking Water. The June 2014 results show barium concentrations below the MCL at all sampling points, indicating compliance with the primary drinking water standard for this constituent at the time of monitoring.

## Open items
The June 2014 report does not identify any unresolved issues, exceedances, or required follow-up actions specifically related to barium. Given that concentrations remained below the MCL, no further investigation or remediation targeting barium was triggered by this monitoring event.
