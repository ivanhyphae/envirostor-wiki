## Site Specific Background Levels

### Overview
Site-specific background levels are used as a benchmark for evaluating contaminant concentrations in soil and groundwater at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project. These levels represent naturally occurring concentrations of substances in the local environment, distinct from contamination introduced by project activities.

### Site Record
*   **Arsenic:** Concentrations of arsenic in Fenceline Boring soil samples were within the range of site-specific background levels.
*   **Barium:**
    *   In Fenceline Borings, barium concentrations ranged from 140 to 4,300 mg/kg in surface soil samples and 42 to 680 mg/kg in the deepest soil samples. Deeper soil samples generally contained barium within the background range of 47 to 110 mg/kg (for 5-foot-deep background soil samples).
    *   Surface soil samples from five borings along the north side of Stockpile 2, adjacent to commercial/industrial development, had barium concentrations greater than 1,000 mg/kg.
    *   In Cadmium Borings, barium concentrations ranged from 58 to 130,000 mg/kg.
    *   The maximum site-specific background value for barium was established as 120 mg/kg. Soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly had barium concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 mg/kg, which is less than this background value.
    *   Soil from the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing excavation had a 95% Upper Confidence Limit above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg.
*   **Lead:**
    *   In Fenceline Borings, lead concentrations were generally within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background levels, with the exception of some samples.
    *   Soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly had lead concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, which is less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg.
    *   Soil from the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation below 3 feet bgs was considered suitable as embankment fill or capping material.

### Regulatory Status
The site-specific background levels for barium (120 mg/kg) and lead (3.8 mg/kg) are used as thresholds for clean cap criteria and material classification.
