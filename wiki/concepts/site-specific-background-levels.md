Site-specific background levels are used as benchmarks to evaluate contaminant concentrations in soil and groundwater at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project. These levels represent naturally occurring concentrations of substances in the local environment, distinct from contamination introduced by project activities. The maximum site-specific background value for barium was established as 120 mg/kg, and for lead, it is 3.8 mg/kg.

### Barium

In Fenceline Borings, barium concentrations in surface soil samples ranged from 140 to 4,300 mg/kg, and in the deepest soil samples, they ranged from 42 to 680 mg/kg. While deeper soil samples generally fell within the background range of 47 to 110 mg/kg (for 5-foot-deep background soil samples), surface soil samples from five borings along the north side of Stockpile 2, adjacent to commercial/industrial development, exhibited barium concentrations exceeding 1,000 mg/kg. Soil from the Cadmium Borings showed barium concentrations ranging from 58 to 130,000 mg/kg. Soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly had barium concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 mg/kg, which is below the maximum site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg. However, soil from the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing excavation had a 95% Upper Confidence Limit for barium that exceeded the maximum background of 120 mg/kg.

### Lead

Lead concentrations in Fenceline Borings were generally within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background levels, with some exceptions. Soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly had lead concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, which is less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg. Soil from the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation below 3 feet was considered suitable as embankment fill or capping material.

### Regulatory Application

The site-specific background levels for barium (120 mg/kg) and lead (3.8 mg/kg) serve as thresholds for clean cap criteria and material classification. For instance, soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly met the clean cap criteria as established in the Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan, approved by DTSC, due to barium concentrations being less than 120 mg/kg and lead concentrations being less than or equal to 3.8 mg/kg. Conversely, Bent 2 soil stockpiles did not meet the clean cap criteria due to barium concentrations exceeding 120 mg/kg.
