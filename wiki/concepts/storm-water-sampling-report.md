## Storm Water Sampling Report

### Overview
The Storm Water Sampling Reports document the collection and analysis of storm water at the [[caltrans]] Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles site in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. These reports are prepared by Geocon Consultants Inc. in accordance with Caltrans Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2. The sampling protocols are established in the *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)*, approved by the DTSC and [[central-valley-rwqcb]].

### Site Record
Two Storm Water Sampling Reports have been documented:

*   **February 1, 2024:** Storm water samples were collected on this date. The sampling was conducted in general accordance with the *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)* dated December 13, 2023.
*   **February 4, 2025:** Storm water samples were collected on this date. The sampling followed the same protocols as the previous report, referencing the *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)* dated December 13, 2023.

The site is located south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue. Stockpiles 1 and 2, which were encapsulated beneath SR 132, and the storm water sampling locations are depicted on the Site Plan (Figure 2) within these reports.

### Regulatory Status
The sampling activities are conducted in accordance with protocols approved by the DTSC and [[central-valley-rwqcb]].
