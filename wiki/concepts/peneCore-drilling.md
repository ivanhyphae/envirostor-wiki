## PeneCore Drilling

### Overview
PeneCore Drilling (operating as TSA Drilling Inc.) served as the C-57‑licensed drilling contractor for the destruction of four monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10) at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR‑99/Kansas Avenue Interchange, Stanislaus County). Retained by Geocon Consultants, Inc. under Project No. S2350‑01‑02, PeneCore performed pressure‑grouting abandonment of the wells in accordance with California Well Standards Bulletin 74‑90 and a May 3, 2024 DTSC directive. The company is based in Woodland, California (220 North East St, Woodland, CA 95776).

### Site Record
PeneCore’s involvement is documented in the *Well Destruction Report* (dated June 25, 2025) and supporting permit applications submitted to the Stanislaus County Department of Environmental Resources (SCDER).

- **Contractor Details**:  
  - License: C‑57 (#906899)  
  - Certificates of Liability Insurance (dated 1/9/2025) list Homeland Insurance Company of New York, West American Insurance Company, Carolina Casualty Insurance Company, Ohio Security Insurance Company, and Travelers Property Casualty Company of America as insurers. The certificate holder is Stanislaus County (Environmental Resources).  
  - Contact: Armstrong & Associates Insurance Services (agent Teresa Galart, phone (530) 406‑2742).

- **Scope of Work**:  
  Wells were abandoned by pressure‑grouting with neat Portland cement placed via tremie pipe from the bottom under sufficient pressure to penetrate the annular filter pack. After grouting, PeneCore removed well boxes and concrete aprons, over‑drilled the top 5 feet of casing, and filled the over‑drilled boreholes with Portland cement or sand/cement slurry. The surface was capped with approximately 6 inches of black‑dyed concrete or matching soil.

### Regulatory Status
The well destruction was required by the DTSC directive dated May 3, 2024, which concurred with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) that encapsulated soil stockpiles beneath SR‑132 were not impacting groundwater and that monitoring could be discontinued. The destruction methodology met California Well Standards Bulletin 74‑90, Part III (Monitoring Well Standards). SCDER issued permits for the work.

No open items are recorded in the provided sources specific to PeneCore’s performance; the abandonment was executed under Geocon’s oversight and reported to DTSC and RWQCB.
