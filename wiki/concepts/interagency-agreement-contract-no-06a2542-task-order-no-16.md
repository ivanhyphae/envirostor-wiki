## Interagency Agreement Contract No 06a2542 Task Order No 16

### Overview

Interagency Agreement Contract No 06a2542 Task Order No 16 (IA 06a2542 TO 16) is a contractual mechanism under which specific environmental remediation and monitoring activities were conducted for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. This task order was part of a larger agreement between Caltrans and another agency to address environmental concerns at the site.

### Site Record

Under IA 06a2542 TO 16, surface water sampling was conducted. A letter dated March 23, 2021, from the project's environmental consultant to Caltrans, titled "Surface Water Letter," documented findings related to surface water. This letter confirmed that no surface water runoff was observed migrating beyond the Caltrans right-of-way.
