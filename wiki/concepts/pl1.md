# Pl1

Pl1 is one of five surface water sample points (PL1 through PL5) monitored on an on-call basis at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles under stormwater sampling programs conducted by [[geocon-consultants-inc]], beginning with events between April 2013 and continuing through at least March 2018. PL1 is located along North Emerald Avenue, on the west side, between Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2, and was collected from a puddle that forms along the road shoulder during rain events, outside the Caltrans right-of-way (as noted explicitly in the March 2018 report). Across repeated sampling rounds, PL1 has consistently shown detectable dissolved metals but none exceeding primary or secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), distinguishing it from other sample points at the site where manganese and, in one case, lead have exceeded thresholds.

## Sampling history and results

PL1 was sampled during each of the documented rounds except when dry:

| Date | Sampled? | Notable dissolved metals reported | MCL exceedance |
|---|---|---|---|
| Oct 28, 2016 | Yes | Antimony, barium, calcium, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, zinc | None |
| Dec 15, 2016 | Yes | Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, arsenic | None (manganese exceedance occurred at PL3/PL4, not PL1) |
| Mar 24, 2017 | Yes | Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, arsenic, lead, zinc | None (manganese exceedance occurred at PL2/PL4) |
| Jan 8, 2018 | Yes | Metals diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, zinc (high concentrations) | None reported |
| Mar 22, 2018 | Yes | Metals diluted for high target/non-target analyte concentrations | None at PL1 (elevated lead reported at PL3, outside right-of-way) |

Across these rounds, PL1 samples required laboratory dilution repeatedly for calcium and sodium (Dec 2016), barium and calcium (Mar 2017), and barium/calcium/potassium/zinc (Jan 2018), reflecting persistently high concentrations of these non-exceeding constituents rather than any target contaminant of concern. Beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were consistently not detected above method detection limits at PL1 in the rounds reporting non-detects. In the October 2016 round, PL1 also returned non-detects for beryllium, chromium, mercury, silver, thallium, and vanadium.

PL1 was not sampled in every attempted round: field reports do not indicate PL1 was dry in the way PL5 or BG2 were on several occasions, and PL1 appears to have yielded sufficient water for sampling in each round listed above. No barium exceedance comparable to the isolated 2,000 µg/l reading Shaw reported for sample SW03 in March 2006 has been recorded at PL1 in any of the Geocon-era events.

## Significance

PL1's location — a road-shoulder puddle outside the Caltrans right-of-way, between Stockpiles 1 and 2 — makes it a point where the reports repeatedly assess whether stockpile-derived contamination is migrating off Caltrans property. Across all rounds reviewed, Geocon's site reconnaissance and sampling did not identify PL1 (or any other sample point) as evidence of stormwater migrating offsite; the March 2017 and January 2018 reports state explicitly that results were consistent with the pattern established since 2013. The one notable deviation in the sampled period was the March 2018 event, where an elevated lead concentration was reported at PL3, not PL1, prompting a specific caveat in that report's surface water management discussion; PL1 results that round were otherwise consistent with prior events. PL1 has therefore functioned as a stable, non-exceeding reference point supporting Caltrans's and Geocon's recurring conclusion that runoff from the stockpiles remains confined to the right-of-way.
