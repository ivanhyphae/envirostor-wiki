## Overview
PL1 is a surface water sampling location within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project area. It is situated along North Emerald Avenue, between Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2. This location has been consistently sampled during rain events to monitor surface water quality.

## Site record
Surface water samples collected at PL1 have been analyzed for various dissolved metals.

In December 2015, sample PL1 was collected from a puddle of water on the west side of North Emerald Avenue, between Stockpiles 1 and 2. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals, including antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium. Arsenic was reported for sample PL3, and lead for sample PL4. Manganese concentrations in samples PL2, PL3, and PL4 exceeded the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l, but the source does not specify the manganese concentration at PL1. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected above their method detection limits. Laboratory notes indicated that samples PL1 through PL4 were diluted due to high concentrations of target analytes.

In October 2016, sample PL1 was again collected from a puddle of water on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. Barium, calcium, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, potassium, sodium, strontium, and zinc were reported for samples PL1 through PL5. Antimony was reported for samples PL1, PL2, and PL4. Arsenic and lead were reported for samples PL3 and PL5. Cobalt was reported for samples PL2, PL3, and PL5. Manganese was reported for samples PL2 through PL5, and its concentration in sample PL3 was equal to the secondary MCL. Beryllium, chromium, mercury, selenium, silver, thallium, and vanadium were not detected above their MDLs. Laboratory notes indicated that samples PL1 through PL5, BG1, and BG2 were diluted due to high concentrations of target analytes or failing internal standards.

In December 2016, sample PL1 was collected from a puddle of water on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. Barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium were reported. Antimony was reported for samples PL1, PL2, and PL4. Arsenic was reported for samples PL1 and PL3. Cobalt and lead were reported for sample PL3. Zinc was reported for samples PL2 through PL4. Manganese concentrations in samples PL3 and PL4 exceeded the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected above their MDLs. Laboratory notes indicated that samples PL1 through PL4, BG1, and BG2 were diluted due to high concentrations of target analytes.

In January 2018, sample PL1 was collected from a puddle of water on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. Antimony, arsenic, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported for some or all of the samples. No concentrations exceeded primary or secondary MCLs, with the exception of manganese in sample BG2. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected above their laboratory reporting limits. Laboratory notes stated that samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 and BG2 were diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc due to high concentrations of target analytes.

In March 2017, sample PL1 was collected from a puddle of water on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium were reported. Arsenic was reported for samples PL1 through PL4. Cadmium was reported for samples PL4 and PL5. Lead was reported for samples PL1 through PL3 and PL5. Manganese was reported for samples PL2 and PL4. Selenium was reported for sample PL2. Zinc was reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5. Manganese concentrations in samples PL2 and PL4 exceeded the secondary MCL, but the source does not specify the manganese concentration at PL1. Beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were not detected above their method detection limits. Laboratory notes indicated that samples PL1 through PL5 were diluted for barium and calcium due to high concentrations, and for magnesium, manganese, potassium, and sodium due to high concentrations of non-target analytes.

Surface water management efforts at PL1 involve maintaining vegetative/fiber cover and straw wattles along the western slope of Stockpile 2 to intercept and redirect surface water flow within the Caltrans right-of-way. Observations during sampling events have not identified runoff migrating offsite, with stormwater appearing to be retained within the Caltrans right-of-way.

In December 2020, construction of overpass abutments along the east and west sides of Emerald Avenue resulted in the former surface water sample locations for PL1 and PL2 no longer existing. Straw wattles were placed at the base of the slopes and abutments to minimize potential offsite discharges of storm water.

## Regulatory status
The analytical results for dissolved metals at PL1 have not exceeded primary or secondary MCLs, with the exception of manganese in other locations (PL2 and PL4 in March 2017).

## Open items
Caltrans plans to conduct additional surface water sampling during qualifying rain events in 2017 and 2018. Due to grading on the site, the ability to collect future samples at the currently established locations is unknown.
