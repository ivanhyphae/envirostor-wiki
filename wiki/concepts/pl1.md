PL1 is a surface water sampling location established by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for monitoring at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It is situated along North Emerald Avenue, between Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2, and plays a role in assessing potential offsite migration of contaminants in surface water runoff.

### Surface Water Sampling at PL1

Surface water samples have been collected from PL1 on multiple occasions to analyze for dissolved metals and other constituents.

*   **December 15, 2016:** Sample PL1 was collected from a puddle on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. Analysis for dissolved metals revealed concentrations of antimony, arsenic, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium. Zinc was also reported. The manganese concentration exceeded the secondary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) of 50 µg/l. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected above their method detection limits (MDLs). The laboratory noted dilutions for calcium, sodium, and magnesium due to high concentrations of target and non-target analytes.
*   **March 24, 2017:** Sample PL1 was collected from a puddle on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals, with reported concentrations for antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium. Arsenic and lead were also reported, as was zinc. No reported concentrations exceeded primary or secondary MCLs, although manganese in other samples (PL2 and PL4) did. Beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were not detected above their MDLs. Dilutions were noted for barium, calcium, and magnesium due to high concentrations of target and non-target analytes.
*   **October 28, 2016:** Sample PL1 was collected from a puddle on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. Analysis for dissolved metals included antimony, barium, calcium, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and zinc. Cadmium, manganese, cobalt, arsenic, and lead were also reported. Selenium was not reported. No reported concentrations exceeded primary or secondary MCLs, though manganese in sample PL3 was equal to the secondary MCL. Beryllium, chromium, mercury, silver, thallium, and vanadium were not detected above their MDLs. The laboratory noted dilutions for nitrate, sulfide, chloride, sulfate, and various dissolved metals due to failing internal standards and possible matrix interference.
*   **January 8, 2018:** Sample PL1 was collected from a puddle on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. The laboratory noted dilutions for barium, calcium, potassium, and zinc due to high concentrations of target analytes.
*   **March 22, 2018:** Sample PL1 was collected from a puddle on the west side of North Emerald Avenue, outside of the Caltrans right-of-way. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. The laboratory noted dilutions for metals due to high concentrations of target or non-target analytes, or failing internal standards. Samples PL1 through PL3 and BG1 were also diluted for sulfide due to possible matrix interference.

### Regulatory Observations

During the December 15, 2016 sampling event, the manganese concentration reported for PL1 exceeded the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l.
