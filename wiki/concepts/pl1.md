## Overview
PL1 is a surface water sampling location established by geocon-consultants-inc. for monitoring at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It is situated along North Emerald Avenue, between Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2.

## Site record
Surface water samples have been collected from PL1 on multiple occasions.

*   On December 15, 2016, sample PL1 was collected from a puddle of water on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. Barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium were reported. Antimony was also reported. Arsenic was reported, and cobalt and lead were reported. Zinc was reported. The manganese concentration reported for PL1 exceeded the secondary Maximum Containment Level (MCL) of 50 micrograms per liter (µg/l). Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits (MDLs). The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL4, BG1, and BG2 were diluted due to high concentrations of target analytes, specifically calcium and sodium. PL1 was also diluted for magnesium.
*   On March 24, 2017, sample PL1 was collected from a puddle of water on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium were reported. Arsenic was reported. Lead was reported. Zinc was reported. None of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs, with the exception of manganese in samples PL2 and PL4. Beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits for samples PL1 through PL5. The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL5 were diluted for barium and calcium due to high concentrations of target analytes, and for magnesium, manganese, potassium, and sodium due to high concentrations of non-target analytes.
*   On October 28, 2016, sample PL1 was collected from a puddle of water on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. Antimony, barium, calcium, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and zinc were reported. Cadmium and manganese were reported. Cobalt was reported. Arsenic and lead were reported. Selenium was not reported. None of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs). The manganese concentration reported for sample PL3 was equal to the secondary MCL. Beryllium, chromium, mercury, silver, thallium, and vanadium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits (MDLs) for samples PL1 through PL5. The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL5, BG1, and BG2 were diluted due to failing internal standard in the original run and required dilution due to possible matrix interference. PL1 was diluted for nitrate and sulfide. PL1 was diluted for chloride. PL1 was diluted for sulfate. PL1 was diluted for barium, beryllium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, magnesium, manganese, nickel, potassium, sodium, thallium, and vanadium.
*   On January 8, 2018, sample PL1 was collected from a puddle of water on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 and BG2 were diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc due to high concentrations of target analytes.
*   On March 22, 2018, sample PL1 was collected from a puddle of water on the west side of North Emerald Avenue. The sample was collected outside of the Caltrans right-of-way. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 were diluted for metals due to high concentrations of target analyte, failing internal standard in the original run, or high concentrations of non-target analyte. Samples PL1 through PL3 and BG1 were also diluted for sulfide due to possible matrix interference.

## Regulatory status
During the December 15, 2016 sampling event, the manganese concentration reported for PL1 exceeded the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l.
