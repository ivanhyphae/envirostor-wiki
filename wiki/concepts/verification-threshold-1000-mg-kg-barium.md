## Verification Threshold 1000 mg/kg Barium

### Overview
The Verification Threshold of **1000 mg/kg for barium** is a project-specific cleanup standard established for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation (SR 132, Stanislaus County, California). This threshold was adopted to confirm that treated or excavated soil stockpiles meet acceptable barium concentrations prior to off-site disposal or beneficial reuse. The value is applied as a site-management criterion, distinct from regulatory Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for groundwater, which for barium is 1,000 µg/L (per the *Draft Interim RACR_Text_Figures_Tables_App A-C*).

### Site Record
- **Source Document**: The threshold appears in the *Draft Interim Removal Action Completion Report (RACR)_Text_Figures_Tables_App A-C*, prepared for the Caltrans Modesto project.
- **Applicable Matrix**: Soil stockpiles generated during remediation of barium-impacted soils along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County.
- **Documented Concentrations**: Barium concentrations in stockpiled soils were reported up to **760 mg/kg** in the *Draft Interim RACR* (specific sample location not cited in sources). This value is below the 1,000 mg/kg verification threshold.
- **Verification Process**: Post-treatment or post-excavation soil sampling is required to demonstrate compliance. The *Draft Interim RACR* notes that verification results for barium fell below the threshold, supporting approval for off-site disposal.
- **Phases**: The threshold applies to all phases of the interim removal action, as documented in the report (Phase 1 2019–2020 referenced in related project records, though not explicitly tied to this threshold in available excerpts).

### Regulatory Status
- **Agency**: California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) oversees the remedial action for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site.
- **Applicable Standards**:
  - **Verification Threshold (soil)**: 1,000 mg/kg barium (project-specific, set in *Draft Interim RACR*).
  - **Groundwater MCL (reference)**: 1,000 µg/L barium (federal and California MCL; used as context, not directly applied to soil stockpiles).
- **Compliance**: As documented in the *Draft Interim RACR*, verification results indicated that barium concentrations in stockpiles were below the 1,000 mg/kg threshold. The project appears to be in compliance with this criterion pending final agency approval of the report.

### Open Items
- The *Draft Interim RACR* is identified as a “Draft Interim” document, indicating pending final DTSC review and approval. Any outstanding conditions or required confirmatory sampling beyond what is documented are not specified in the available sources.
