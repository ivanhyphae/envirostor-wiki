## Borrow Areas

### Overview
Borrow areas are locations from which soil was excavated for use in construction projects. In the context of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles case, borrow areas are relevant as the original source of the stockpiled soil that required remediation.

### Site Record
The Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, dated March 18, 2021, references borrow areas as the origin of the soil. Specific details regarding the exact locations, volumes of soil removed from each borrow area, or the specific contaminants identified within those original borrow areas are not detailed in the provided source material.

### Regulatory Status
The regulatory status of the borrow areas themselves is not addressed in the provided source material. The focus of the RAP is on the remediation of the soil stockpiles.

### Open Items
No open items related to borrow areas are documented in the provided source material.
