## PL3

### Overview
PL3 is a surface water sampling location situated along the southern edge of [[stockpile-2]]. Its purpose is to monitor surface water quality in the vicinity of the stockpiles.

### Site Record
On March 24, 2017, sample PL3 was collected from a puddle of water at the intersection of Bennett Street and the alley behind Loletta Street, located outside the Caltrans right-of-way and beyond the chainlink fence enclosing the south side of Stockpile 2. This sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium were reported. Arsenic was reported for samples PL1 through PL4, and lead was reported for samples PL1 through PL3 and PL5. Manganese was reported for samples PL2 and PL4. Selenium was reported for sample PL2. Zinc was reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5. None of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs, with the exception of manganese in samples PL2 and PL4. Beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits for samples PL1 through PL5.

On March 22, 2018, sample PL3 was collected from a puddle of water at the intersection of Bennett Street and the alley behind Loletta Street, outside the Caltrans right-of-way beyond the chainlink fence enclosing the south side of Stockpile 2. Lead was detected in sample PL3 at a concentration of 46 micrograms per liter (µg/l), which exceeds its primary maximum contaminant level (MCL) for drinking water of 15 µg/l. Manganese was detected in samples PL2 through PL4 and BG1 at concentrations ranging from 54 to 370 µg/l, which exceeds its secondary MCL for drinking water of 50 µg/l. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected in the samples at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective laboratory reporting limits.

On December 15, 2016, sample PL3 was collected from a puddle of water where Bennett Street intersects the alley behind Loletta Street, outside the Caltrans right-of-way beyond the chainlink fence that encloses the south side of Stockpile 2. Barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium were reported for samples PL1 through PL4. Antimony was reported for samples PL1, PL2, and PL4. Arsenic was reported for samples PL1 and PL3. Cobalt and lead were reported for sample PL3. Zinc was reported for samples PL2 through PL4. The manganese concentrations reported for samples PL3 and PL4 exceeded the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective MDLs for samples PL1 through PL4.

On October 28, 2016, sample PL3 was collected from a puddle of water where Bennett Street intersects the alley behind Loletta Street, outside the Caltrans right-of-way beyond the chainlink fence that encloses the south side of Stockpile 2. Antimony, barium, calcium, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and zinc were reported for samples PL1 through PL5. Cadmium and manganese were reported for samples PL2 through PL5. Cobalt was reported for samples PL2, PL3, and PL5. Arsenic and lead were reported for samples PL3 and PL5. Selenium was reported for sample PL5. None of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs. The manganese concentration reported for sample PL3 was equal to the secondary MCL. Beryllium, chromium, mercury, silver, thallium, and vanadium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective MDLs for samples PL1 through PL5.

On January 8, 2018, sample PL3 was collected from a puddle of water where Bennett Street intersects the alley behind Loletta Street, outside the Caltrans right-of-way beyond the chainlink fence that encloses the south side of Stockpile 2.

On March 24, 2017, sample PL3 was collected from a puddle of water where Bennett Street intersects the alley behind Loletta Street. The sample was collected outside the Caltrans right-of-way beyond the chainlink fence that encloses the south side of Stockpile 2.

### Regulatory Status
In March 2018, lead was detected in sample PL3 at a concentration of 46 µg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 15 µg/l. Manganese was also detected in PL3 at a concentration of 370 µg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l. In December 2016, manganese concentrations in PL3 exceeded the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l. In October 2016, the manganese concentration reported for sample PL3 was equal to the secondary MCL.
