# Mse Wall

## Overview

The term "MSE Wall" (Mechanically Stabilized Earth Wall) appears in project records associated with the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation along SR 132 in Stanislaus County, California. The referenced document — the Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan — establishes that sampling activities are planned in proximity to an MSE wall structure located along the southern boundary of the site, near the right-of-way (RW) and fence line. The sampling plan, prepared by Geocon Consultants (Project No. S1200-01-01), is part of the Barium-Containing Soil (BCS) removal effort under the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) – Interim Phase 1.

## Site record

The Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan (dated January 2019, 65% submittal) identifies a proposed sampling location in the vicinity of the southern slope retaining wall. The document's grading plan (Figure 3-2, 65% submittal dated 03-05-18, marked "NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION") delineates Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 as Barium-Containing Soil stockpiles, situated along the ROUTE 132 corridor and bisected by Emerald Avenue. The sampling location is designated near the "RW/Fence Line," adjacent to the retaining wall structure at the southern extent of the stockpile area.

Specific details regarding the number of sampling points, sampling depths, analytical parameters, or exact coordinates at the MSE wall location are not further elaborated in the provided source material.

## Regulatory status

No regulatory standards, agency determinations, or compliance status specific to the MSE wall are documented in the available source material.

## Open items

The sampling plan was at a 65% submittal stage as of March 2018, marked "NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION." No subsequent documentation of finalization, approval, field execution, or analytical results from the MSE wall sampling is provided in the current source record. The status of sampling activities and any regulatory acceptance of the plan remain open items.
