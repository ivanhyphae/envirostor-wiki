The mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations at Stockpiles 1 and 2, along with the Bent 2 column excavation, generated soil that fell outside the designated borrow area covered by the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, forcing [[caltrans]] to characterize this material separately and seek case-by-case agreement from [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] on whether it could be reused as embankment fill or clean cover, or had to be contained. The record shows three distinct determinations, each turning on comparison to background criteria and depth below ground surface, with divergent outcomes.

## Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing

[[geocon-consultants-inc]] submitted a technical memorandum dated April 6, 2020 characterizing soil from the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing excavation for possible use as capping material at Stockpiles 1 and 2. [[dean-wright]] of DTSC, reviewing jointly with the Water Board, accepted the memo's findings on April 7, 2020 but rejected the proposed reuse as capping material: the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil exceeded the maximum background value of 120 mg/kg (for [[barium]]), and the footing excavation area itself lay outside the borrow area defined in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan. As a condition of acceptance, DTSC required that this soil instead be placed within the Stockpile 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill — the two disposition paths available to soil that fails clean-reuse criteria.

## Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing

A separate, revised technical memo dated June 18, 2020 covered the Stockpile 2 southerly slope MSE wall footing excavation. The revision corrected an error in the prior draft: the 120 mg/kg comparison value cited in the memo's third paragraph was clarified as applying to [[barium]], not lead. On this basis, DTSC accepted the memo on June 22, 2020 with a depth-based split disposition: soil down to 3 feet below ground surface exceeded acceptable levels and had to go into the designated containment zones, while soil below 3 feet bgs was found suitable for use as embankment fill or capping material. DTSC also determined that the sub-3-foot material was already covered by the April 9, 2020 Variance Request to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, so no additional variance request was needed before that soil could be used.

## Bent 2 excavation soil

Soil from the Bent 2 column excavation was proposed as clean cap material, per a technical memo dated October 30, 2020. Unlike the wall footing soils, this batch failed outright against the clean cap criteria established in the April 9, 2020 Variance Request — Richard Stewart's cover e-mail of November 2, 2020 already flagged that analytical results exceeded background criteria for clean cap use. DTSC and the Water Board, in their November 6, 2020 acceptance, did not require containment as with the wall footing soils; instead they permitted a limited, conditional reuse: the Bent 2 soil could be placed atop Stockpile 2 at a thickness of 6 inches or less, provided it was subsequently covered with clean base material and the asphalt/concrete roadway section. Because project schedule meant this soil could be temporarily exposed through the winter months before final paving, DTSC conditioned its acceptance on strict adherence to the rainy-season surface water sampling effort and the erosion control measures in the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020, to keep contaminants from migrating off site during that exposure window.

## Pattern across the three determinations

| Excavation area | Tech memo date | DTSC/Water Board acceptance date | Disposition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing | April 6, 2020 | April 7, 2020 | All soil to containment zones or bridge embankment fill; not usable as cap |
| Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing | June 18, 2020 (revised) | June 22, 2020 | 0–3 ft bgs to containment zones; below 3 ft usable as embankment fill/cap, covered under existing April 9, 2020 Variance Request |
| Bent 2 column excavation | October 30, 2020 | November 6, 2020 | Not usable as clean cap outright; permitted as ≤6-inch layer atop Stockpile 2 under base material and pavement, conditioned on SWPPP erosion/sampling controls |

Across all three, the controlling comparison was the 120 mg/kg background value for barium, and in every case the excavated soil at or near surface failed that comparison, while deeper or thinly-applied material was given a conditional path to reuse rather than blanket containment. The MSE wall footing areas were treated more strictly than the Bent 2 soil because they fell outside the approved borrow area boundary, while the Bent 2 soil's reuse was allowed on the basis of encapsulation (base material and pavement) rather than depth.
