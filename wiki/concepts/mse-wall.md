## MSE Wall

### Overview

The MSE Wall refers to a Mechanically Stabilized Earth wall constructed as part of the remediation efforts for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. This structure was a component of the remediation strategy, specifically related to the management and containment of soil stockpiles at the SR 132 site in Stanislaus County, California.

### Site Record

Sampling was conducted for the MSE Wall. On October 26, 2021, Geocon Consultants, Inc. collected samples from the MSE Wall. The analytical results for these samples indicated the presence of lead at a concentration of 12.5 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). This concentration was below the applicable screening level of 81 mg/kg.

### Regulatory Status

The lead concentration of 12.5 mg/kg detected in the MSE Wall samples on October 26, 2021, was below the screening level of 81 mg/kg.
