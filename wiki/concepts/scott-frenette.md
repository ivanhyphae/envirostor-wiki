## Scott Frenette

Scott Frenette is an individual associated with WSP, involved in the remediation case file for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] located on SR 132 in Stanislaus County, California. His communications in the provided source material indicate a role in project management and regulatory coordination, specifically concerning the approval of clean cap materials.

### Site Record

On March 1, 2021, Scott Frenette communicated with Adan Corrales of Bay-Cities-Paving-and-Grading regarding the material from Retaining Wall No. 1110 and material under the Carpenter Shoo-fly. Frenette stated that the material from Retaining Wall No. 1110 had already been approved for use as clean cap material. However, he noted that the remaining material under the Carpenter Shoo-fly would require testing and approval prior to its use as clean cap material. Frenette requested specific information from Corrales to facilitate the submission of a sample plan to the Department-of-Toxic-Substances-Control (DTSC) for approval. The requested information included:
*   Depth of excavation {{quote: "Depth of excavation"}}
*   Total fill volume {{quote: "Total fill volume"}}
*   Verification of USA clearance or the need to mark USA/any known utility conflicts {{quote: "Verify USA cleared or need to mark USA/any known utility conflicts"}}
*   The planned excavation date for the material by BCPG {{quote: "When BCPG plans on excavating the material"}}

Frenette's email was sent on March 1, 2021, at 5:29 PM. His email address is [<Scott.Frenette@wsp.com](mailto:Scott.Frenette@wsp.com)>.

### Regulatory Status

Frenette's communications indicate that material intended for use as clean cap material requires testing and approval from the Department-of-Toxic-Substances-Control (DTSC).

### Open Items

As of March 1, 2021, the material under the Carpenter Shoo-fly required testing and approval from DTSC for use as clean cap material. A sample plan needed to be submitted to DTSC for approval prior to testing. Specific details regarding excavation depth, total fill volume, utility clearances, and excavation timing were pending from Bay-Cities-Paving-and-Grading.
