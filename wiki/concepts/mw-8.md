## Overview

[[mw-8]] is a groundwater monitoring well located at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It has been sampled and analyzed as part of multiple groundwater monitoring events conducted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for Caltrans. The well is situated adjacent to the stockpiles and is used to assess groundwater quality in the shallow zone of the upper aquifer.
