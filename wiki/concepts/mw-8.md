## Overview

**Mw 8** is a monitoring well installed at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA) as part of the investigation into barium-impacted soil and groundwater. It was installed between June 4 and June 8, 2012, during the Phase 2 groundwater investigation described in document S9525-06-25.

## Site record

Installation details: MW-8 was advanced using hollow-stem auger drilling to a total depth of 30 feet below ground surface (bgs). The well was constructed with 10 feet of 2-inch diameter PVC screen (20–30 ft bgs) and a sand pack extending from 17 to 30 ft bgs, sealed with bentonite above.

Groundwater sampling on June 13, 2012, showed the following results:

| Parameter | Result | Unit | Detection Limit |
|-----------|--------|------|-----------------|
| Barium | **4,400** | µg/L | 50 |
| Chromium | <50 | µg/L | 50 |
| Cobalt | <50 | µg/L | 50 |
| Copper | <50 | µg/L | 50 |
| Nickel | <50 | µg/L | 50 |
| Vanadium | <50 | µg/L | 50 |
| Zinc | <50 | µg/L | 50 |

The barium concentration of 4,400 µg/L exceeded the federal MCL of 2,000 µg/L by a factor of 2.2. No other metals exceeded their respective MCLs.

## Regulatory status

**Applicable standard:** Federal Safe Drinking Water Act Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for barium: 2,000 µg/L (enforceable by USEPA and delegated to DTSC for this site under the hazardous waste remediation program).

**Compliance status:** As of the June 2012 sampling event, MW-8 exceeded the barium MCL by 2,200 µg/L (4,400 vs. 2,000 µg/L). This constitutes a verified exceedance under the site's Water Quality Assessment criteria. No subsequent sampling data for MW-8 are documented in the available sources (S9525-06-25 and S2350-01-02). Further evaluation is needed per the regulatory framework.

## Open items

- No post-2012 groundwater sampling data for MW-8 are documented in the available sources. The 2012 exceedance may require re-sampling to assess current conditions and attenuation.
- The relationship between MW-8 and the soil stockpile barium concentrations (up to 760 mg/kg per S9525-06-25) is not explicitly established in the record. A source-pathway-receptor linkage analysis may be pending.
- The statistical evaluation report (S2350-01-02) does not include MW-8 in its updated statistical analyses, leaving its current status unresolved.
