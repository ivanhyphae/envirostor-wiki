Task Order 97 is the Caltrans contract vehicle under which Stantec Consulting Services Inc. produced the two 2023 deliverables governing groundwater and stormwater monitoring at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site on SR 132: a groundwater monitoring report dated March 8, 2023, and a surface water letter dated March 9, 2023. Together they document a March 2023 sampling round covering the site's monitoring well network and stormwater, and they are the basis for characterizing groundwater and surface water conditions at that point in the site's history.

## Groundwater monitoring report (March 8, 2023)

The report presents results from the site's monitoring wells, including background wells BG1, BG2, and BG3 and downgradient wells MW-4, MW-6, MW-9, and MW-10, from the sampling event covered by this task order. It is the vehicle through which that round's groundwater data — well-specific results, comparisons to MCLs, and any exceedances at individual wells — was compiled and reported to the project file. Findings specific to individual wells and constituents are addressed in those wells' own articles.

## Surface water letter (March 9, 2023)

The companion letter dated March 9, 2023 reports the corresponding stormwater sampling results for the same round. This March 2023 round did not report thallium above the applicable MCL, which matters because a later stormwater event in December 2023 produced the first reported MCL exceedance for thallium at the site — meaning Task Order 97's surface water letter marks the last clean data point for that constituent before the exceedance emerged in subsequent monitoring.

## Significance

Task Order 97's two reports establish the March 2023 baseline against which later rounds — including the December 2023 stormwater exceedance for thallium — are compared. Both deliverables were prepared by Stantec Consulting Services Inc.
