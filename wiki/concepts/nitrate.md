## Nitrate

### Overview
Nitrate is a general mineral constituent analyzed in groundwater samples collected at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It has been detected in groundwater at concentrations exceeding the [[california-primary-mcl]] for drinking water in several monitoring wells.

### Site Record
Nitrate, expressed as nitrogen, has been detected in groundwater samples from various monitoring wells.

*   In September 2013, nitrate concentrations ranged from 2.5 mg/l (MW-3) to 36 mg/l (MW-5). The reported concentrations for MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equaled or exceeded the [[california-primary-mcl]] of 10 mg/l.
*   In December 2013, nitrate concentrations ranged from 2.4 mg/l (MW-3) to 25 mg/l (MW-5). The reported concentrations for MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, and MW-10 exceeded the [[california-primary-mcl]] of 10 mg/l.
*   In February 2014, nitrate concentrations ranged from 2.8 mg/l (MW-3) to 34 mg/l (MW-5). The reported concentrations for MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equaled or exceeded the [[california-primary-mcl]] of 10 mg/l.
*   In June 2014, nitrate concentrations ranged from 2.6 mg/l (MW-3) to 29 mg/l (MW-5). The reported concentrations for MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, and MW-10 equaled or exceeded the [[california-primary-mcl]] of 10 mg/l.
*   In April 2015, nitrate concentrations ranged from 7.0 mg/l (MW-4) to 17 mg/l (MW-1). The reported concentrations for MW-1 and MW-2 exceeded the [[california-primary-mcl]] of 10 mg/l.
*   In May 2016, nitrate as nitrogen was reported for MW-1 at 9.2 mg/l and for MW-7 at 8.7 mg/l, both less than the [[california-primary-mcl]] of 10 mg/l.

Monitoring well MW-1, an upgradient well, has shown nitrate concentrations that may be indicative of natural background levels. For example, in September 2013, MW-1 had a nitrate concentration of 12 mg/l. In December 2013, MW-1 had a nitrate concentration of 13 mg/l. In February 2014, MW-1 had a nitrate concentration of 10 mg/l. In June 2014, MW-1 had a nitrate concentration of 15 mg/l. In April 2015, MW-1 had a nitrate concentration of 17 mg/l.

Nitrate analyses were conducted using EPA Test Method 300.0. In some instances, nitrate analyses were reported as being run outside of their holding time, but data qualification was deemed unnecessary due to similarity with rerun results.

### Regulatory Status
The [[california-primary-mcl]] for nitrate is 10 mg/l. Several groundwater samples from monitoring wells at the site have exceeded this threshold.
