Nitrate is a general mineral constituent analyzed in groundwater samples collected at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It has been detected in groundwater at concentrations exceeding the [[california-primary-mcl]] for drinking water in several monitoring wells.

### Groundwater Monitoring Results

Nitrate, expressed as nitrogen, has been detected in groundwater samples from various monitoring wells.

*   **September 2013:** Concentrations ranged from 2.5 mg/l (MW-3) to 36 mg/l (MW-5). MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equaled or exceeded the [[california-primary-mcl]] of 10 mg/l.
*   **December 2013:** Concentrations ranged from 2.4 mg/l (MW-3) to 25 mg/l (MW-5). MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, and MW-10 exceeded the [[california-primary-mcl]] of 10 mg/l.
*   **February 2014:** Concentrations ranged from 2.8 mg/l (MW-3) to 34 mg/l (MW-5). MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 equaled or exceeded the [[california-primary-mcl]] of 10 mg/l.
*   **June 2014:** Concentrations ranged from 2.6 mg/l (MW-3) to 29 mg/l (MW-5). MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, and MW-10 equaled or exceeded the [[california-primary-mcl]] of 10 mg/l.
*   **April 2015:** Concentrations ranged from 7.0 mg/l (MW-4) to 17 mg/l (MW-1). MW-1 and MW-2 exceeded the [[california-primary-mcl]] of 10 mg/l.
*   **May 2016:** Nitrate as nitrogen was reported for MW-1 at 9.2 mg/l and for MW-7 at 8.7 mg/l, both below the [[california-primary-mcl]] of 10 mg/l.

Monitoring well MW-1, an upgradient well, has shown nitrate concentrations that may be indicative of natural background levels. In September 2013, MW-1 had a nitrate concentration of 12 mg/l. In December 2013, MW-1 had a nitrate concentration of 13 mg/l. In February 2014, MW-1 had a nitrate concentration of 10 mg/l. In June 2014, MW-1 had a nitrate concentration of 15 mg/l. In April 2015, MW-1 had a nitrate concentration of 17 mg/l.

Nitrate analyses were conducted using EPA Test Method 300.0. In some instances, nitrate analyses were reported as being run outside of their holding time, but data qualification was deemed unnecessary due to similarity with rerun results.

### Soil and Stormwater

Nitrate was detected in stockpile soil samples at concentrations within the range of background during the 2006 Shaw SI. DI-WET soluble nitrate concentrations ranged from 0.2 to 2.6 mg/l in 28 of 33 soil samples analyzed. Stormwater samples were also analyzed for nitrate. In December 2016, stormwater samples collected adjacent to the stockpiles were analyzed for nitrate as nitrogen, with results consistent with background values.

### Regulatory Threshold

The [[california-primary-mcl]] for nitrate is 10 mg/l. Several groundwater samples from monitoring wells at the site have exceeded this threshold.
