The [[phase-1-bcs-consolidation-remedial-action-completion-report]] documents the completion of interim remedial actions for barium-containing soils (BCS) at the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. This report, submitted by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] on behalf of [[caltrans]], details the consolidation and capping of BCS as part of the Interim Phase 1 construction. The [[dtsc]], in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], reviewed and accepted this report, signifying the conclusion of the Interim Phase 1 remediation efforts.

## Remedial Action Completion

The [[phase-1-bcs-consolidation-remedial-action-completion-report]] establishes that soil removal, consolidation, and capping activities were completed in accordance with the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and Remedial Design and Implementation Plan (RDIP). Two variance requests to the RDIP, approved on April 17, 2020, and September 21, 2020, were also incorporated into the completed actions. The report includes As-Built Plans, cross-section figures, final survey data, and a final Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan.

## Regulatory Acceptance

The [[dtsc]] formally accepted the [[phase-1-bcs-consolidation-remedial-action-completion-report]] on January 18, 2023. This acceptance marks the official closure of the Interim Phase 1 remediation phase.

## Future Actions

Following the acceptance of the report, the [[dtsc]] will prepare a draft O&M Agreement for [[caltrans]] review within 30 days of the January 18, 2023, acceptance letter. A Land Use Covenant (LUC) for the remediated area will also be prepared for review, signature, and recording with the Stanislaus County Assessor's office. Final certification of the RAP for this project is contingent upon the completion of the Ultimate Phase 2 State Route 132 build-out, anticipated in 8 to 10 years. This future phase will require a supplemental RDIP for [[dtsc]] review and approval, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and potential revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement, all conducted under a separate agreement between [[dtsc]] and [[caltrans]].
