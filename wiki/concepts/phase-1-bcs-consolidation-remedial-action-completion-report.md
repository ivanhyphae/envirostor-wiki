## Phase 1 BCS Consolidation Remedial Action Completion Report

This document, the Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project (RACR), dated December 1, 2022, was submitted by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] on behalf of the Department of Transportation (Caltrans). It details the consolidation and capping activities for barium-containing soils (BCS) completed as part of the Interim Phase 1 Construction for the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. The [[dtsc]], in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], reviewed and accepted this report.

### Site Record

The RACR describes the completion of soil removal, consolidation, and capping activities. These actions were performed in accordance with the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP), Remedial Design and Implementation Plan (RDIP), and two variance requests to the RDIP approved on April 17, 2020, and September 21, 2020. The report also includes As-Built Plans, cross-section figures, final survey data, and a final Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan.

### Regulatory Status

The [[dtsc]] accepted the Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project (RACR) on January 18, 2023. This acceptance marks the conclusion of the Interim Phase 1 effort.

### Open Items

Following the acceptance of the RACR, the [[dtsc]] will prepare a draft O&M Agreement for Caltrans' review within 30 days of the January 18, 2023, acceptance letter. A Land Use Covenant (LUC) for the remediated area will also be prepared for review, signature, and recording with the Stanislaus County Assessor's office.

Final certification of the RAP for this project will not occur until after the completion of the Ultimate Phase 2 State Route 132 build-out effort. This future phase is anticipated to begin in 8 to 10 years and will involve adding additional lanes and completing the final build-out of State Route 132. This will require a supplemental RDIP for [[dtsc]] review and approval, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and potential revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement. The Ultimate Phase 2 project will be conducted under a separate agreement between [[dtsc]] and Caltrans.
