## Overview

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) is a state agency responsible for overseeing the investigation and remediation of hazardous substance releases in California. In the context of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, DTSC's role has been primarily one of oversight, review, and approval of Caltrans' work plans, reports, and remedial actions. This oversight is formalized through Interagency Agreements and Task Orders, where Caltrans reimburses DTSC for these services. DTSC's involvement ensures that Caltrans' activities related to the soil stockpiles meet regulatory requirements for protecting human health and the environment.

## Site Record

The DTSC has been involved in the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project since at least 2006, when Caltrans submitted a Final Preliminary Endangerment Assessment under DTSC direction. {{quote: "Under direction of DTSC through a preceding 2006 Interagency Agreement task order, Caltrans submitted a Final Preliminary Endangerment Assessment in 2009."}}

DTSC has reviewed and provided comments on various project documents, including:
*   Groundwater Monitoring Reports for May, June, and July 2012. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB) has reviewed: 1) "Groundwater Monitoring Report - May 2012...; 2) "Additional Well Installation and Groundwater Monitoring Report - June 2012...; and 3) "Groundwater Monitoring Report - July 2012..." {{quote: "DTSC concurs with the subject groundwater monitoring reports and requests that future groundwater monitoring reports be completed as groundwater data becomes available."}}}}
*   Site Investigation Data for the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project. {{quote: "Based on the current site investigation data presented in the document titled "Transmittal of Site Investigation Data, Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project SR 99 - Kansas Avenue Northbound Off-Ramp, Modesto California", (Geocon, 4/24/2012), none of the soil samples in the area to be excavated in Stockpile No. 3 contained total metal concentrations at or higher than the California hazardous waste Total Threshold Limit Concentration (TTLC)."}}}}
*   Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) and subsequent variances. {{quote: "In accordance with your request on behalf of the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), we have prepared this Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP Variance)..."}} {{quote: "DTSC subsequently provided approval of the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area."}}}} {{quote: "A second DTSC-approved Variance to the RDIP dated September 2, 2020 was prepared by Geocon to provide clarification of the alternative use of a 4-inch-thick layer of Class 3 aggregate base (AB) over the top of the completed BCS Containment Zones versus a 4-inch-thick layer of AB as specified in the RDIP."}}}}
*   Sampling and Analysis Workplans. {{quote: "DTSC and the Waterboard have reviewed the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan (Plan) received on April 5, 2021 that outlines the proposed sampling and analysis for soil at this location that will be used as clean fill. The Plan is consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and there are no comments. This e-mail serves as DTSCs approval of the Plan."}}}}
*   Technical Memoranda related to soil characterization and clean fill material. {{quote: "DTSC and the Water Board have reviewed the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated April 12, 2021 describing the sampling and analysis that was completed to characterize the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill area. This e-mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the Memo."}}}} {{quote: "DTSC has reviewed the Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated March 9, 2020 describing the removal of Barium Containing Soil from the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 and the subsequent confirmation soil sampling that was completed to show these areas have achieved the cleanup levels established in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). The sampling results indicate that the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 have met the RDIP verification thresholds and DTSC agrees that these areas are now suitable for the placement of clean embankment fill. This e-mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the Memo."}}}} {{quote: "DTSC has reviewed the Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated March 13, 2020 describing the removal of Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Stockpile 3 and the subsequent confirmation soil sampling that was completed to show this area has achieved the cleanup levels established in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). This e-mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the Memo."}}}} {{quote: "DTSC and the Water Board have reviewed the Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated April 6, 2020 describing the sampling along the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area and the laboratory results of their analysis.
