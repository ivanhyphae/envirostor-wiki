The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) is a state agency responsible for overseeing the investigation and remediation of hazardous substance releases in California. In the context of the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, DTSC's role has been primarily one of oversight, review, and approval of Caltrans' work plans, reports, and remedial actions. This oversight is formalized through Interagency Agreements and Task Orders, where Caltrans reimburses DTSC for these services. DTSC's involvement ensures that Caltrans' activities related to the soil stockpiles meet regulatory requirements for protecting human health and the environment.

## Site Record

The DTSC has been involved in the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project since at least 2006, when Caltrans submitted a Final Preliminary Endangerment Assessment under DTSC direction.

DTSC has reviewed and provided comments on various project documents, including:

* Groundwater Monitoring Reports for May, June, and July 2012. }}
* Site Investigation Data for the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project. }}
* Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) and subsequent variances. }} }}
* Sampling and Analysis Workplans. }}
* Technical Memoranda related to soil characterization and clean fill material. }} }} }} }} }}

DTSC has also been involved in the approval of Remedial Action Plans (RAPs) and Remedial Design Implementation Plans (RDIPs). }} }}

DTSC has provided oversight of field activities, including sampling and remedial activities, as appropriate. }}

DTSC has also been involved in the review and approval of groundwater monitoring reports. }} }}

DTSC has also been involved in the review and approval of well destruction reports. }}

DTSC has also been involved in the approval of stormwater and surface water monitoring reports. }} }}

DTSC has also been involved in the change of Project Managers for the site. }}

## Regulatory Status

DTSC, in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], has reviewed and concurred with groundwater monitoring reports. }} }}

DTSC has approved sampling plans and technical memorandums related to clean fill material. }} }}

DTSC has conditionally approved the Well Destruction Report. }}

DTSC has accepted the Storm Water Sampling Report. }}

DTSC has approved variances to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). }}

DTSC has accepted technical memorandums regarding the characterization of excavated soils. }} }} }} }}

DTSC has been notified of a Project Manager change for the site. }}

## Open Items

DTSC has requested that future groundwater monitoring reports be completed as groundwater data becomes available. }}
