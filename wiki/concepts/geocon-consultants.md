## Overview

Geocon Consultants, Inc. is an environmental consulting firm that has provided services for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. Their work has included preparing sampling plans, excavation monitoring plans, and groundwater monitoring reports for [[caltrans]]. Geocon's involvement spans various aspects of the project, from initial site investigations to ongoing monitoring and reporting.

## Site Record

Geocon Consultants prepared a sampling-plan for clean fill source testing in February 2020. This plan involved drilling 12 borings and analyzing six depth-discrete 4-point composite samples for organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) and heavy metals. The proposed clean fill source area was located along the Highway 132 alignment, extending east from Carpenter Road. Geocon noted that previous investigations (Blackburn 2014 ISA and Rincon 2018 Phase II ESA) did not report high-risk contamination releases in the vicinity, and residential backyard testing by Rincon indicated pesticides and metals at concentrations less than applicable regulatory screening levels. Geocon tentatively scheduled drilling for February 11, 2020, with a 48-hour analytical turnaround planned.

Geocon also prepared a Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan in June 2012 for the State Route 99 Kansas Avenue Off-ramp Project. This plan summarized previous site investigations and health risks associated with identified contaminants, notably [[barium]], in Stockpile 3. It also outlined procedures for air monitoring and verification of completed excavations. Geocon's project number for this plan was S9650-06-03.

In August 2014, Geocon Consultants prepared a Groundwater Monitoring Report for June 2014 for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. Geocon's project number for this report was S9800-01-17. The report indicated that none of the dissolved metals concentrations in the June 2014 groundwater samples exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. [[barium]] and [[strontium]] were reported at concentrations similar to historical levels and remained significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds.

Geocon Consultants also prepared a Sampling Plan for Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall in January 2019.

## Regulatory Status

Geocon's work has been conducted in coordination with regulatory agencies such as the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB). The Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan addressed health risks associated with contaminants, including [[barium]], in Stockpile 3. The June 2014 groundwater monitoring report noted that barium and strontium concentrations were significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds.
