[[california-human-health-screening-levels]] (CHHSLs) are benchmarks used to evaluate the potential health risks associated with contaminants in soil and other media. In the context of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file, CHHSLs were referenced to determine the suitability of excavated material for reuse.

## Overview

California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs) were used by Caltrans to assess the safety of excavated soil from Stockpile #3, which was intersected by ramp improvements at State Route (SR) 99 at Kansas Avenue. The soil material was found to have elevated metal concentrations, but these were below the applicable CHHSLs for residential and commercial/industrial scenarios. This finding supported the conclusion that the soil was suitable for reuse as structural backfill or for offsite disposal as non-hazardous material.

## Site Record

During the ramp safety improvement project at SR 99 and Kansas Avenue, approximately 6000 cubic yards of material were excavated. This excavation intersected Stockpile #3. Soil analysis conducted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. indicated the presence of elevated metal concentrations. However, these concentrations were reported as being {{quote: "below residential and commercial/industrial California Human Health Screening Levels."}}

## Regulatory Status

The soil material excavated from Stockpile #3 was determined to be suitable for reuse because its contaminant concentrations were below the California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs). Specifically, the concentrations were {{quote: "below residential and commercial/industrial California Human Health Screening Levels."}} This determination was made by Caltrans, with the expectation of concurrence from the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). Based on this assessment, the material was deemed suitable for reuse as structural backfill or for offsite reuse/disposal as a non-hazardous soil.

## Open Items

Caltrans requested review and concurrence from the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) regarding the disposition of excavated material from Stockpile #3. The ramp project was on hold pending DTSC's concurrence that the material could be managed as non-hazardous.
