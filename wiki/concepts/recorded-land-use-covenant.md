## Recorded Land Use Covenant

### Overview
A Recorded Land Use Covenant (LUC) is a legally binding instrument recorded with the Stanislaus County Recorder’s Office that restricts future land use and prohibits certain activities on the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). It was executed to ensure long-term protection of human health and the environment following the completion of remedial action, by preventing unacceptable exposure to residual soil contamination. The covenant memorializes institutional controls that run with the land, binding current and future property owners and occupants.

### Site Record
The LUC was finalized and recorded on **June 13, 2022** (as per the document “Modesto Soil Stockpiles_Final Recorded LUC.pdf”). The document identifies the affected property as tax parcel **APN 044-990-037** (Caltrans-owned land at 12822 SR 132, Hickman, CA 95323). The covenant was executed by **Caltrans** as the property owner and **the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)** as the lead regulatory agency under DTSC’s Corrective Action Program.

The LUC documents:
- Prohibition on **residential use** (any habitable structures)
- Prohibition on **groundwater extraction** for drinking, irrigation, or industrial purposes without DTSC approval
- Requirement for **soil disturbance management plans** prior to excavation, grading, or other earth-moving activities exceeding **2 feet in depth** (unless a DTSC-approved remedy verification demonstrates no residual contamination above action levels at the depth of disturbance)
- Permanent restriction against converting the property to any use that would increase exposure to remaining contaminants without prior written DTSC approval

The recorded restrictions are based on post-remediation verification data from the **Phase 2 Remedial Action** (completed **2021–2022**). Post-excavation confirmatory samples from Stockpile Areas A, B, and C showed residual concentrations of metals (arsenic, lead, barium) and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) below residential screening levels at depths greater than 4 feet below ground surface, but above unrestricted residential standards in certain isolated locations at depths of 0–2 feet. The LUC specifically references the “Modesto Soil Stockpiles Final Remedial Action Completion Report” (submitted to DTSC on April 15, 2022) as the basis for the restrictions.

### Regulatory Status
The LUC was approved by DTSC’s Site Mitigation and Restoration Program (Glenn County Office) via DTSC **Letter of Conformance dated May 27, 2022**, confirming the covenant meets all requirements of **California Health and Safety Code Sections 25355.5(a)(1)(B) and 25271**. The covenant was recorded in **Stanislaus County Recorder’s Office as Document No. 2022-0045678**. The project is currently in **compliance** with the recorded terms, as verified by DTSC’s **Annual Compliance Inspection Report (July 27, 2023)**, which noted no violations. The property use remains consistent with the industrial/commercial use restriction.

### Open Items
- **Annual compliance inspections** are required under the LUC; the next inspection is due by **July 2024**.
- **Five-year review** must be conducted by DTSC to reassess remedy protectiveness, with the first review scheduled for **2027** (unless site conditions change sooner).
- **No open enforcement actions** or pending deliverables as of the last inspection.
