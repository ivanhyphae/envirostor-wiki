## Overview

Kansas Avenue serves as a critical geographic reference point within the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project area, primarily used to delineate the location of former Stockpile 3 and associated [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS). This area is situated east of [[sr-99]] and between Kansas Avenue and the Modesto Irrigation District (MID) water canal. The [[remedial-action-plan]] identified three stockpiles, totaling approximately 160,000 cubic yards of soil containing metals, located south of Kansas Avenue.

## Site record

Former Stockpile 3, located east of [[sr-99]] between Kansas Avenue and the MID water canal, was excavated. A total of 3,075 truck-loads of BCS material, estimated at approximately 38,440 cubic yards, was transported from this location for placement within the Stockpile 1 fill containment zone. The excavation and removal of BCS from Stockpile 3 were performed in general accordance with the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). The RDIP specified verification testing thresholds and excavation targets for Stockpile 3. {{quote: "Stockpile 3 BCS material located adjacent and east of State Route 99 between Kansas Avenue and the Modesto Irrigation District (MID) water canal was excavated and transported for placement as embankment fill within the designated Stockpile 1 fill containment zone. A total of 3,075 truck-loads of BCS material totaling approximately 38,440 cubic yards (preliminary estimate based on 12.5 cubic yards per truck) was transported to Stockpile 1."}}

The [[remedial-action-plan]] identified three stockpiles of soil containing metals (primarily barium, strontium, and lead) occupying areas within the state right-of-way south of Kansas Avenue. These areas were located between Carpenter Avenue and East Emerald Avenue, between Emerald Avenue and [[sr-99]], and east of [[sr-99]]. {{quote: "Three stockpiles totaling approximately 160,000 cubic yards of soil containing metals (primarily barium, strontium, and lead) occupy three areas within the state right of way located south of Kansans Avenue: between Carpenter Avenue and East Emerald Avenue, Emerald Avenue and SR 99, and east of SR 99."}}

Groundwater monitoring wells MW-9 and MW-10 were installed south of Kansas Avenue, with MW-9 located west of [[sr-99]] and MW-10 located east of [[sr-99]]. {{quote: "The June 2012 report also discusses the installation of monitoring wells identified as MW-9, located south of Kansas Avenue and west of SR 99; and MW-10, located south of Kansas Avenue and east of SR 99."}}

The soil stockpiles consist of excess native soils and pond tailings generated during the construction of [[sr-99]] north of Kansas Avenue in the early 1960s. {{quote: "The soil stockpiles consist of excess native soils and pond tailings that were generated when Caltrans constructed a segment of SR 99 north of Kansas Avenue in the early 1960's."}}

The Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles are located adjacent to State Route 99 (east and west) and south of Kansas Avenue in Modesto. {{quote: "The stockpiles are immediately adjacent to State Route 99 (east and west) and south of Kansas Avenue in Modesto."}}

## Regulatory status

The [[remedial-action-plan]] for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] was approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) on March 14, 2018. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) approves the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132 West Expressway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County California"}} The approved cleanup alternative involves the [[containment-of-stockpiles]] during construction of the [[sr-132]] West Freeway/Expressway Project. Containment is to be achieved through retaining walls, [[bridge-abutments]], and placement beneath the roadway. Unpaved portions will be covered with clean fill. Long-term protection is to be ensured through land use controls, annual inspections of containment features, and groundwater monitoring. {{quote: "The selected remedy Alternative 4, Containment described in the approved RAP is containment of the stockpiles by construction of the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project. This alternative contains the stockpiles behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath the roadway. Unpaved portions will have clean fill cover."}}

The DTSC letter dated December 7, 2012, to Caltrans discussed the need to address the use of soil stockpile material in the State Route 132 West Expressway project and/or its final disposition as it relates to human health and water quality prior to design and construction of the State Route 132 West Expressway project. {{quote: "The DTSC letter dated 12/7/2012 to Caltrans discussed the need to address the use of soil stockpile material in the State Route 132 West Expressway project and/or its final disposition as it relates to human health and water quality prior to design and construction of the State Route 132 West Expressway project."}}
