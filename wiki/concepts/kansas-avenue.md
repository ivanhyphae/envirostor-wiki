## Overview

Kansas Avenue is a geographic reference point within the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project area in Modesto, California. It is used to delineate the location of [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) and the former Stockpile 3. Specifically, Stockpile 3 BCS material was located adjacent and east of State Route 99, between Kansas Avenue and the Modesto Irrigation District (MID) water canal. The [[remedial-action-plan]] for the project identified three stockpiles, totaling approximately 160,000 cubic yards of soil containing metals, located south of Kansas Avenue.

## Site record

Former Stockpile 3, located east of State Route 99 between Kansas Avenue and the MID water canal, was excavated. A total of 3,075 truck-loads of BCS material, estimated at approximately 38,440 cubic yards, was transported from this location for placement within the Stockpile 1 fill containment zone. The excavation and removal of BCS from Stockpile 3 were performed in general accordance with the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). The RDIP specified verification testing thresholds and excavation targets for Stockpile 3.

The [[remedial-action-plan]] identified three stockpiles of soil containing metals (primarily barium, strontium, and lead) occupying areas within the state right-of-way south of Kansas Avenue. These areas were located between Carpenter Avenue and East Emerald Avenue, between Emerald Avenue and SR 99, and east of SR 99.

## Regulatory status

The [[remedial-action-plan]] for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] was approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). The approved cleanup alternative involves the [[containment-of-stockpiles]] during construction of the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project. Containment is to be achieved through retaining walls, [[bridge-abutments]], and placement beneath the roadway. Unpaved portions will be covered with clean fill. Long-term protection is to be ensured through land use controls, annual inspections of containment features, and groundwater monitoring.
