## Alternative 2

Alternative 2 is one of two build alternatives considered for the [[sr-132]] West project. [[caltrans]] identified Alternative 2 as its preferred alternative, balancing environmental impacts, project feasibility, right-of-way acquisition, cost, and the ability to meet the project's purpose and need. This preference was noted and included in the public record.

### Site Record

Alternative 2, along with Alternative 1, involves constructing a new alignment for SR 132 utilizing existing Caltrans right-of-way acquired in 1958, between Dakota Avenue and SR 99.

The project is planned in two phases. Phase 1, common to both Alternative 1 and Alternative 2, includes the construction of a two-lane expressway on the southern half of the proposed alignment from North Dakota Avenue to the Needham Street Bridge Overcrossing. Phase 1 is estimated to cost approximately $82 million. Phase 2 would add the two northernmost lanes within the new alignment from North Dakota Avenue to the Needham Street Bridge Overcrossing. The total estimated project cost for both phases is up to $214 million.

A key distinction between Alternative 1 and Alternative 2 lies in the interchange at SR 99. Alternative 2 involves the reconstruction of the southbound SR 99 Kansas Avenue off-ramp, whereas Alternative 1 involves the construction of a southbound SR 99 Needham Street off-ramp. Under Alternative 2, the southbound SR 99 off-ramp to Kansas Avenue would remain open, but the northbound SR 99 on- and off-ramps would be closed.

Noise abatement measures, such as soundwalls, were evaluated for both build alternatives. Noise barriers were found to be feasible and reasonable in Area 3 (north of the alignment, between North Dakota Avenue and North Carpenter Road), Area 4 (south of the alignment, east of North Carpenter Road to west of SR 99, north of L Street), and Area 7 (east of SR 99, between North Washington Street and North Franklin Street). Noise barriers were found not to be feasible in several areas, including Area 1 (SR 132 from the project western terminus to Garrison Avenue and areas west of North Dakota Avenue, including SR 132 (Maze Boulevard)) and Area 2 (south of the proposed SR 132 expressway alignment, between North Dakota Avenue and North Carpenter Road).

### Regulatory Status

Alternative 2 has been identified as the preferred alternative by Caltrans. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) has reviewed and concurred with Caltrans' approach to managing the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] under the [[remedial-action-plan]]. Alternative 4 (Containment) of the [[draft-final-remedial-action-plan]], which involves placing stockpiles beneath the pavement of the SR 132 West project, is the recommended alternative by DTSC. This containment strategy is compatible with the construction of Alternative 2, as the stockpiles would be located beneath the roadway pavement.
