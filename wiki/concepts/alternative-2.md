## Alternative 2

Alternative 2 is one of two build alternatives considered for the [[sr-132]] West project, identified as the preferred alternative by Caltrans. It involves the construction of a new alignment for SR 132, distinct from improvements to the existing SR 132 (Maze Boulevard). This alternative was selected for its balance in minimizing environmental impacts, project feasibility, right-of-way acquisition, overall cost, and its ability to meet the project's purpose and need.

### Site Record

Alternative 2, along with Alternative 1, would utilize the adopted route for SR 132 between Dakota Avenue and SR 99, using existing Caltrans right-of-way acquired in 1958. {{quote: "Both Alternative 1 and Alternative 2 would use the adopted route to realign the segment of existing SR 132 (Maze Boulevard) between Dakota Avenue and SR 99 (see Appendix F of the EIR/EA)."}}

The project is planned in phases. Phase 1, common to both Alternative 1 and Alternative 2, includes the construction of a two-lane expressway on the southern half of the proposed alignment from North Dakota Avenue to the Needham Street Bridge Overcrossing. {{quote: "Both build alternatives (Alternative 1 and Alternative 2) would be the same under Phase 1 and would include construction of a two-lane expressway on the southern half of the proposed alignment from North Dakota Avenue on the west end of the project to the Needham Street Bridge Overcrossing on the east end of the project."}} Phase 1 is estimated to cost approximately $82 million. {{quote: "Phase 1 is estimated to cost approximately \$82 million."}}

Phase 2 would involve adding the two northernmost lanes within the new alignment from North Dakota Avenue to the Needham Street Bridge Overcrossing. {{quote: "Phase 2 would involve the construction of the two northernmost lanes within the new alignment from North Dakota Avenue on the west end of the project to the Needham Street Bridge Overcrossing on the east end of the project."}} Phase 2 is estimated to cost up to $132 million, bringing the total project cost to an estimated $214 million. {{quote: "The total project is estimated to cost up to \$214 million."}}

A key difference between Alternative 1 and Alternative 2 lies in the interchange at SR 99. Alternative 2 involves the reconstruction of the southbound SR 99 Kansas Avenue off-ramp, whereas Alternative 1 involves the construction of a southbound SR 99 Needham Street off-ramp. {{quote: "The alignments of the two alternatives are similar between North Dakota Avenue and SR 99; however, the major difference involves the construction of a southbound SR 99 Needham Street off-ramp under Alternative 1, compared to the reconstruction of the southbound SR 99 Kansas Avenue off-ramp under Alternative 2."}}

Under Alternative 2, the southbound SR 99 off-ramp to Kansas Avenue would remain open, but the northbound SR 99 on- and off-ramps would be closed. {{quote: "Under Alternative 2, the southbound SR 99 off-ramp to Kansas Avenue would remain open, but the northbound SR 99 on- and off-ramps would be closed."}}

Noise abatement measures, such as soundwalls, were evaluated for both build alternatives. Noise barriers were found to be feasible and reasonable in Area 3 (north of the alignment, between North Dakota Avenue and North Carpenter Road), Area 4 (south of the alignment, east of North Carpenter Road to west of SR 99, north of L Street), and Area 7 (east of SR 99, between North Washington Street and North Franklin Street). {{quote: "Area 3 North of the proposed SR 132 expressway alignment, between North Dakota Avenue and North Carpenter Road, under both of the build alternatives"}}, {{quote: "Area 4 South of the proposed SR 132 expressway alignment, east of North Carpenter Road, to areas west of SR 99, north of L Street, under both of the build alternatives"}}, {{quote: "Area 7 East of SR 99, between North Washington Street and North Franklin Street, under both of the build alternatives"}} However, noise barriers were found not to be feasible in several areas, including Area 1 (SR 132 from the project western terminus to Garrison Avenue and areas west of North Dakota Avenue, including SR 132 (Maze Boulevard)) and Area 2 (south of the proposed SR 132 expressway alignment, between North Dakota Avenue and North Carpenter Road). {{quote: "Area 1 SR 132 (Maze Boulevard) from the project western terminus to Garrison Avenue and areas west of North Dakota Avenue, including SR 132 (Maze Boulevard), under both of the build alternatives"}}, {{quote: "Area 2 South of the proposed SR 132 expressway alignment, between North Dakota Avenue and North Carpenter Road, under both of the build alternatives"}}

### Regulatory Status

Alternative 2 has been identified as the preferred alternative by Caltrans. {{quote: "Alternative 2 has been identified as the preferred alternative because it provides the best balance among avoiding and/or minimizing environmental impacts, project feasibility, right-of-way acquisition, overall cost, and ability to meet the project's purpose and need."}} This preference is noted in multiple public comment responses. {{quote: "Your preference for Alternative 2 has been included in the public record. Alternative 2 has been identified as the preferred alternative because it provides the best balance among avoiding and/or minimizing environmental impacts, project feasibility, right-of-way acquisition, overall cost, and ability to meet the project's purpose and need."}} {{quote: "Your preference for Alternative 2 is noted and has been included in the public record. Alternative 2 has been identified as the preferred alternative because it provides the best balance among avoiding and/or minimizing environmental impacts, project feasibility, right-of-way acquisition, overall cost, and ability to meet the project's purpose and need."}}

The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) has reviewed and concurred with Caltrans' approach to managing the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] under the [[remedial-action-plan]]. Alternative 4 (Containment) of the RAP, which involves placing stockpiles beneath the pavement of the SR 132 West project, is the recommended alternative by DTSC. {{quote: "DTSC concurs with Draft Final RAP Alternative 4, Containment, which is the recommended alternative in the Draft Final RAP."}} This containment strategy is compatible with the construction of Alternative 2, as the stockpiles would be located beneath the roadway pavement. {{quote: "This alternative contains the stockpiles behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath the pavement of the SR 132 West project."}}
