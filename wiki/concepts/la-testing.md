# LA Testing

LA Testing was the analytical laboratory contracted to perform expedited analysis of air monitoring samples collected during BCS stockpile grading activities at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). The firm's Huntington Beach, CA facility {{quote: "5431 Industrial Drive, Huntington Beach, CA 92649"}} analyzed 37-mm mixed cellulose ester (0.8µ MCE) filter cassettes submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. {{quote: "between February and June 2020"}} under Geocon Project No. {{quote: "S1908-01-01"}}, with samples identified under the project name {{quote: "Modesto Soil"}} {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}.

## Site Record

LA Testing served as a subcontractor to Geocon Consultants, Inc. {{quote: "6671 Brisa Street, Livermore, CA 94550"}}, who was performing air monitoring services per the referenced RDIP protocols. Geocon's field personnel collected personal, upwind, and downwind air samples during BCS grading operations using personal sampling pumps calibrated at {{quote: "2.0 to 3.0 liters per minute"}}, and submitted the filter media to LA Testing under expedited turnaround times {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}.

The laboratory analyzed samples for three parameters:

*   **Total dust** by NIOSH Method 0500 (reported in mg/m³)
*   **Barium** by NIOSH Method 7300 Modified (reported in µg/m³)
*   **Lead** by NIOSH Method 7300 Modified (reported in µg/m³)

All analyses were performed at the Huntington Beach facility, which held {{quote: "AIHA-LAP, LLC-IHLAP accreditation #101650"}} during the project period {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}.

LA Testing processed samples on a near-daily basis between {{quote: "February 18 and June 16, 2020"}}, consistent with the field sampling schedule. Laboratory reports were typically issued the same day as analysis or the following day {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}. For example, samples collected {{quote: "February 18, 2020 (LA Testing Order 332003316)"}} were received {{quote: "February 19, 2020 at 9:30 AM"}} {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}. One report for Order {{quote: "332003693"}} was amended on {{quote: "February 24, 2020 at 12:13:31"}} with the reason {{quote: "Data Entry-Change to Location"}} {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}.

Quality assurance documentation included standard QC elements, reporting conventions with data qualifiers (MDL, RL, J, ND, D), and chain of custody forms {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}. The laboratory stated that {{quote: "Results are not field blank corrected."}} for all samples {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}. Each report also included the disclaimer: {{quote: "The laboratory is not responsible for data reported in mg/m³, which is dependent on volume collected by non-laboratory personnel."}} {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}.

The sample identification system used by LA Testing corresponded to Geocon's field monitoring locations, with prefixes such as PNL (Personal), PRM (Perimeter), and FB (Field Blank) {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}. The project's EMSL quotation number was {{quote: "342020395810"}} {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}.

## Regulatory Status

No direct regulatory findings, violations, or compliance determinations specific to LA Testing are documented in the project record. The laboratory's accreditation and use of promulgated NIOSH methods indicate conformance with standard industrial hygiene analytical requirements. The laboratory's role was operational, providing analytical support for data that Geocon and the project team would evaluate against applicable action levels {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}.

## Open Items

No unresolved issues, pending deliverables, or corrective actions specific to LA Testing are documented in the project record. The analytical work appears to have been completed for the February–June 2020 monitoring period {{quote: "Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G/Draft Interim RACR_ App D-G.md"}}.
