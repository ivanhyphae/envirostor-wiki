LA Testing was the analytical laboratory contracted to perform expedited analysis of air monitoring samples collected during BCS stockpile grading activities at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). The firm's Huntington Beach, CA facility analyzed 37-mm mixed cellulose ester (0.8µ MCE) filter cassettes submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] between February and June 2020.

## Site Record

LA Testing served as a subcontractor to Geocon Consultants, Inc., who was performing air monitoring services per the referenced RDIP protocols. Geocon's field personnel collected personal, upwind, and downwind air samples during BCS grading operations using personal sampling pumps calibrated at 2.0 to 3.0 liters per minute, and submitted the filter media to LA Testing under expedited turnaround times.

The laboratory analyzed samples for three parameters:

*   **Total dust** by NIOSH Method 0500 (reported in mg/m³)
*   **Barium** by NIOSH Method 7300 Modified (reported in µg/m³)
*   **Lead** by NIOSH Method 7300 Modified (reported in µg/m³)

All analyses were performed at the Huntington Beach facility, which held AIHA-LAP, LLC-IHLAP accreditation #101650 during the project period.

LA Testing processed samples on a near-daily basis between February 18 and June 16, 2020, consistent with the field sampling schedule. Laboratory reports were typically issued the same day as analysis or the following day. For example, samples collected February 18, 2020 (LA Testing Order 332003316) were received February 19, 2020 at 9:30 AM. One report for Order 332003693 was amended on February 24, 2020 at 12:13:31 with the reason "Data Entry-Change to Location".

Quality assurance documentation included standard QC elements, reporting conventions with data qualifiers (MDL, RL, J, ND, D), and chain of custody forms. The laboratory stated that "Results are not field blank corrected" for all samples. Each report also included the disclaimer: "The laboratory is not responsible for data reported in mg/m³, which is dependent on volume collected by non-laboratory personnel."

The sample identification system used by LA Testing corresponded to Geocon's field monitoring locations, with prefixes such as PNL (Personal), PRM (Perimeter), and FB (Field Blank). The project's EMSL quotation number was 342020395810.

## Regulatory Status

No direct regulatory findings, violations, or compliance determinations specific to LA Testing are documented in the project record. The laboratory's accreditation and use of promulgated NIOSH methods indicate conformance with standard industrial hygiene analytical requirements. The laboratory's role was operational, providing analytical support for data that Geocon and the project team would evaluate against applicable action levels.

## Open Items

No unresolved issues, pending deliverables, or corrective actions specific to LA Testing are documented in the project record. The analytical work appears to have been completed for the February–June 2020 monitoring period.
