# La Testing

## Overview

LA Testing was the analytical laboratory contracted to perform expedited analysis of air monitoring samples collected during BCS stockpile grading activities at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). The firm's Huntington Beach, CA facility (5431 Industrial Drive, Huntington Beach, CA 92649) analyzed 37-mm mixed cellulose ester (0.8µ MCE) filter cassettes submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. between February and June 2020. All work was performed under Geocon Project No. S1908-01-01, with samples identified under the project name "Modesto Soil."

## Site record

### Scope and contractual role

LA Testing served as a subcontractor to Geocon Consultants, Inc. (6671 Brisa Street, Livermore, CA 94550), who was the environmental consultant performing air monitoring services per the referenced RDIP (Remedial Design and Implementation Plan) protocols. Geocon's field personnel collected personal, upwind, and downwind air samples during BCS grading operations using personal sampling pumps calibrated at 2.0 to 3.0 liters per minute, and submitted the filter media to LA Testing under expedited turnaround times.

### Analytical methods performed

The laboratory analyzed samples for three parameters:

- **Total dust** by NIOSH Method 0500 (reported in mg/m³)
- **Barium** by NIOSH Method 7300 Modified (reported in µg/m³)
- **Lead** by NIOSH Method 7300 Modified (reported in µg/m³)

All analyses were performed at the Huntington Beach facility, which held AIHA-LAP, LLC-IHLAP accreditation #101650 during the project period.

### Sample processing timeline

LA Testing processed samples on a near-daily basis between February 18 and June 16, 2020, matching the field sampling schedule. Laboratory reports were typically issued the same day as analysis or the following day, consistent with the stated "expedited turn-around-times." Sample receipt dates correspond closely with collection dates, and analysis dates generally follow within one calendar day of receipt. For example:

- Samples collected February 18, 2020 (LA Testing Order 332003316) were received February 19, 2020 at 9:30 AM
- Samples collected February 19, 2020 (Order 332003450) were received February 20, 2020 at 9:00 AM, with analytical results issued February 21, 2020
- Samples collected February 20, 2020 (Order 332003559) were received February 21, 2020 at 8:50 AM, with results issued February 21, 2020
- Samples collected February 21, 2020 (Orders 332003691 and 332003693) were received February 24, 2020 at 8:30 AM, with results issued February 24, 2020
- One report for Order 332003693 was amended on February 24, 2020 at 12:13:31, replacing the initial report with the reason "Data Entry-Change to Location"

Subsequent sampling dates follow the same pattern through June 2020, with the final documented sampling events occurring June 15–16, 2020 (Orders 332010802 and 332010853).

### Quality assurance documentation

Each analytical report included standard quality control elements:

**Reporting conventions:** The laboratory applied consistent data qualifiers as documented in the "Definitions" sections of each report:

- **MDL** — method detection limit
- **RL** — Reporting Limit (Analytical)
- **J** — Result was below the reporting limit, but at or above the MDL
- **ND** — indicates that the analyte was not detected at the reporting limit
- **D** — Dilution; sample required a dilution which was used to calculate final results

**Field blank procedures:** Most analytical reports noted whether discernable field blanks were submitted with samples, and all stated that "Results are not field blank corrected." For samples without volume measurements (such as field blanks), the reporting limit was stated as 0.05 mg.

**Laboratory disclaimer:** Each report carried the standard statement that "The laboratory is not responsible for data reported in mg/m³, which is dependent on volume collected by non-laboratory personnel."

**Analyst signatures:** Analytical reports were signed by the performing analyst(s) and countersigned by the Laboratory Manager or other approved signatory. Analysts identified in the reports include:

- Cara Blount (appears on reports from February–March 2020)
- Candy Campos (appears on reports from February–March 2020)
- Christine Do (appears on reports from March–May 2020)
- Andres Arestegui (appears on reports from March–June 2020)
- Minh Hong (appears on reports from March 2020)

Michael Chapman served as the Laboratory Manager or approved signatory throughout the project period.

**Chain of custody:** Industrial Hygiene Chain of Custody forms (identified as "COC-20 Industrial Hygiene - R0.2" or "R3.2" controlled documents) were maintained for each sample shipment, documenting sample IDs, collection dates/times, media type (37-mm MCE filters, manufacturer Zefon, part #7m83m or 7m93m), flow rates, sample volumes, analytes requested, and Geocon's client ID (GECN21). The project's EMSL quotation number was 342020395810.

### Sample identification system

LA Testing reports document a systematic sample identification scheme that corresponded to Geocon's field monitoring locations:

- **PNL** prefix — Personal samples (equipment operator monitoring)
- **PRM** prefix — Perimeter samples, with numeric suffixes (PRM-1 through PRM-5) and alphabetical series (AC, AN, AP, AR, AS, AT, AU, AW, AY, AZ, BC, BF, BG, etc.) indicating sampling events. These were further described as upwind (U) or downwind (D) of specific stockpiles (SP1, SP2, SP3).
- **FB** prefix — Field blanks

Each LA Testing report assigned its own laboratory ID number (e.g., 332003556-0001 through -0005) cross-referenced to Geocon's client sample descriptions.

## Regulatory status

No direct regulatory findings, violations, or compliance determinations specific to LA Testing are documented in the project record. The laboratory's accreditation (AIHA-LAP, LLC-IHLAP #101650) and use of promulgated NIOSH methods (0500, 7300 Modified) indicate conformance with standard industrial hygiene analytical requirements. The laboratory's role was operational — providing analytical support for data that Geocon and the project team would then evaluate against applicable action levels.

## Open items

No unresolved issues, pending deliverables, or corrective actions specific to LA Testing are documented in the project record. The analytical work appears to have been completed for the February–June 2020 monitoring period, with the note in the air monitoring narrative that "BCS grading activities have been completed for the Project" with the exception of BCS material temporarily placed for use as retaining wall backfill.
