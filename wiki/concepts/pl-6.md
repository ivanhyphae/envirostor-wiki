## Overview

Pl 6 is a surface water monitoring location established for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project (SR 132, Stanislaus County). It is referenced in Caltrans’ cover letter to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) transmitting surface water sampling results (Doc. 06A2542ct_to97_SurfaceWaterLetter_final.20230309). The location is used to assess potential off‑site migration of contaminants from the stockpile areas via stormwater runoff or ephemeral drainage. Its specific coordinates and physical description are not included in the available source excerpt.
