G. Scott McGowen, P.E., is identified in the project records as Chief Environmental Engineer, Division of Environmental Analysis, for the State of California, Department of Transportation. He appears in the signature pages of two Caltrans/DTSC interagency task orders governing remediation work at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles-site]], where he signed on behalf of Caltrans alongside [[paul-ruffin]], DTSC Contract Manager, who signed for the Department of Toxic Substances Control.

McGowen's signature appears on Task Order No. 6 under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0260 (DTSC #08-T3616), dated 6/21/12, and on Task Order No. 4 under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310 (DTSC #12-T0106), dated 6/7/13. In both instances his certification is the Caltrans-side counterpart to Paul Ruffin's signature for DTSC, executing the task order under the governing interagency agreement. These signatures are distinct from those of [[richard-stewart]], Caltrans Task Manager, and Scott Nelson, Caltrans Contract Manager, who separately certified on the same documents that the task order was within project scope and that funding had been encumbered; McGowen's certification, by contrast, is the final execution signature binding Caltrans to the task order's terms and conditions jointly with DTSC.
