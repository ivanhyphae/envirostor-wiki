## Overview

The **Stormwater Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP), December 2023** is the active work plan governing the collection, handling, analysis, and evaluation of stormwater runoff at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). Prepared to satisfy the monitoring conditions of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and the associated Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) order, this document supersedes the interim stormwater monitoring protocols from the 2019 Site Management Plan. It defines the compliance framework for assessing whether discharges from the stockpile containment area meet applicable water quality objectives before leaving the project footprint.

## Site Record

### Document Identification
- **Title:** Stormwater Sampling and Analysis Plan
- **Date:** December 2023
- **Status:** Final, approved by the Central Valley RWQCB
- **Regulatory Basis:** The plan is structured to comply with the California Water Code, the Basin Plan for the San Joaquin River Basin, and the project-specific Waste Discharge Requirements (WDRs) or Cleanup and Abatement Order (CAO) issued to Caltrans for the site [SAP 2023, Section 1.2].

### Monitoring Network
The SAP establishes a discrete monitoring network to characterize background conditions and capture the quality of stormwater leaving the stockpile pad and the detention basin. The network is defined on Figure 2 of the SAP and includes:
- **Background Station (SW-BG):** Located upstream of the stockpile pad in the roadside drainage adjacent to SR 132, representing ambient runoff quality unaffected by the stockpiles.
- **Compliance Station (SW-OUTFALL):** The primary compliance point located at the discharge point of the stormwater detention basin. All discharge threshold evaluations are based on data from this station.
- **Downstream Station (SW-DS):** Located in the receiving unnamed tributary to the Tuolumne River, downstream of the confluence with the 19th Street Drainage Ditch, to assess in-stream effects.

### Sampling Protocols
- **Triggering Event:** A qualifying rain event is defined as a precipitation depth ≥ 0.25 inches that produces observable runoff from the stockpile pad.
- **Sampling Window:** Grab samples must be collected within 48 hours of the start of the qualifying event. First-flush sampling is prioritized during the initial storm of the wet season (October 1 – April 30).
- **Frequency:** A minimum of three (3) qualifying storm events are targeted per wet season. Runoff events that do not trigger sampling are logged as dry-weather observations [SAP 2023, Section 3.1].

### Analytical Parameters and Action Levels
The SAP mandates field and laboratory analyses against specific Action Levels (ALs), which are drawn from the Basin Plan Water Quality Objectives (WQOs) and the California Toxics Rule (CTR).

- **Field Parameters:** pH (Action Level: 6.5–8.5), Specific Conductance (Action Level: site-specific, based on background), Turbidity (Action Level: 150 NTU).
- **Conventional Constituents:** Total Suspended Solids (TSS) (Action Level: 100 mg/L).
- **Total Metals (EPA Method 6010/6020):**
    - Arsenic: 10 µg/L
    - Barium: 1,000 µg/L
    - Cadmium: 0.25 µg/L (CTR hardness-dependent criterion; default AL applied)
    - Chromium (Total): 50 µg/L
    - Lead: 5 µg/L (CTR hardness-dependent criterion; default AL applied)
    - Nickel: 100 µg/L (CTR hardness-dependent criterion)
    - Zinc: 120 µg/L (CTR hardness-dependent criterion)
- **Organics (if triggered by stockpile characterization data):** Oil & Grease (EPA 1664), PAHs (EPA 8270). The SAP includes a provision for adding or removing analytes based on the current Contaminants of Concern (Stockpile Soil) list.

*Note: The exact numeric Action Levels for metals are tied to hardness-dependent criteria specified in the Basin Plan and are detailed in Table 4 of the SAP.*

### Quality Assurance / Quality Control (QA/QC)
The SAP requires a minimum of one field duplicate and one equipment blank per every ten samples collected. Reporting Limits (RLs) are set below the applicable Action Levels to ensure compliance can be statistically demonstrated. Sample chain-of-custody documentation and laboratory certification (ELAP) are mandatory [SAP 2023, Section 5.0].

## Regulatory Status

The December 2023 SAP is the current, approved document governing stormwater monitoring at the site. Compliance with the SAP is a condition of the site’s regulatory order. Exceedances of Action Levels at SW-OUTFALL require:
1. Immediate verbal notification to the RWQCB within 24 hours.
2. Written notification within 5 working days, detailing the exceedance, suspected cause, and planned corrective actions (per SAP Section 6.0).

The plan is implemented under the oversight of the Central Valley RWQCB, and all sampling results are submitted in quarterly monitoring reports. The current compliance status of the site is evaluated on a rolling basis against the thresholds defined in the SAP.

## Open Items

- **Data Validation Report (DVR):** The SAP requires an annual DVR to be submitted 90 days after the close of the wet season. The DVR for the 2023–2024 season has not yet been finalized in the case file.
- **BMP Effectiveness Evaluation:** The plan contains an adaptive management provision requiring a formal evaluation of Best Management Practices (BMPs) after two complete wet seasons of post-RAP monitoring. This evaluation is pending.
- **Asbestos Handling Protocol:** Stockpile characterization data indicates the potential for chrysotile asbestos in soil. The SAP includes a placeholder for a specific asbestos-in-stormwater standard operating procedure (SOP), which has not yet been drafted or appended.
