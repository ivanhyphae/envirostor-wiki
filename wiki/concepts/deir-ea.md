The Draft EIR/EA (Draft Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment) is the CEQA/NEPA environmental document prepared for the State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project, circulated jointly with the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) because the stockpile capping is proposed as part of the freeway/expressway construction. Its significance is procedural and evidentiary at once: it is the vehicle by which [[caltrans]], as CEQA lead agency and NEPA-assigned agency, disclosed the project's environmental effects and made the RAP — reviewed and approved for public noticing by [[dtsc]] and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board — available for public comment before any remedy could proceed.

## What was circulated and when

Caltrans, working with the Stanislaus Council of Governments (StanCOG), released the Draft EIR/EA together with the Draft Final RAP for public review and comment from January 18, 2017 through March 17, 2017. A public hearing in open-house format was held February 22, 2017, from 6:00 p.m. to 8:00 p.m. at Mark Twain Junior High School, 707 South Emerald Avenue, Modesto. The notice identified the project as construction of a four-lane freeway/expressway along the adopted SR 132 route south of Kansas Avenue, from Dakota Avenue to east of SR 99 at the Needham Street Bridge Overcrossing.

The document described the three Caltrans Modesto Stockpiles by location within state right-of-way south of Kansas Avenue — between Carpenter Avenue and Emerald Avenue, between Emerald Avenue and SR 99, and east of SR 99 — and quantified them at approximately 160,000 cubic yards of soil containing metals, primarily [[barium]], [[lead]], and [[strontium]]. The RAP attached to the Draft EIR/EA proposed, if approved, to allow placement of the impacted soil beneath roadway pavement, within bridge abutments, and behind retaining walls, framed as avoiding potential future impacts to human health and the environment.

## Finalization

A Notice of Availability dated March 12, 2018 (State Clearinghouse #2010012010), signed by Haesun Lim, Acting Senior Environmental Planner for Caltrans District 10, announced that the Final EIR/EA — issued with a Finding of No Significant Impact — together with the attached Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Draft Final [[remedial-action-plan]], had become available. The notice confirms that the Draft EIR/EA and Draft Final RAP had been circulated for public review from January 18, 2017 through March 17, 2017, and that Appendix J of the final document contains responses to comments, including responses to comments on the Draft Final RAP prepared by DTSC in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board. The final materials were made available at the Caltrans District 10 office in Stockton, the StanCOG office, the Stanislaus County Library, the DTSC file room in Sacramento, and online, including on the DTSC EnviroStor site under two site identifiers referenced in the notice.

## Why this matters to the record

The Draft EIR/EA is the document that ties the SR 132 West roadway construction schedule to the disposition of the stockpiled soil: the RAP's proposed remedy — capping and beneath-pavement/abutment/wall placement rather than offsite removal — was reviewed by DTSC and the Regional Water Quality Control Board and put out for public comment as part of the same environmental document, rather than as a standalone hazardous-waste remediation action. The RAP remained styled as a "Draft Final" RAP even at the point of the Final EIR/EA's release in March 2018, with public comments and agency responses addressed through the EIR/EA's Appendix J rather than a separately noticed RAP finalization.
