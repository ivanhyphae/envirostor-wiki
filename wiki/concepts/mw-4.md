MW-4 is a groundwater monitoring well located at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It has been consistently sampled and analyzed as part of the ongoing groundwater monitoring program to assess potential impacts from the soil stockpiles.

## Groundwater Monitoring Results

Groundwater samples from MW-4 have been analyzed for various constituents, including dissolved metals and general minerals, across multiple sampling events.

### Dissolved Metals

*   **March 2012:** Antimony, arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, copper, lead, selenium, silver, thallium, and zinc were not reported above their respective PQLs. Barium, vanadium, and strontium were reported at concentrations below drinking water thresholds. Total chromium and mercury were reported. Manganese was reported at 88 µg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL for taste and odor. Mercury results were considered estimated due to equipment blank presence.
*   **May 2012:** Barium and strontium were reported below drinking water thresholds. Lead was not reported above its PQL. Arsenic, chromium, and vanadium were within ranges not exceeding drinking water thresholds. Molybdenum and nickel were reported. Antimony, copper, and selenium were detected in some samples. Cobalt and manganese were reported, with all concentrations below their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water.
*   **November 2012:** Barium and strontium were reported within historical ranges and below water quality thresholds. Lead was not reported above its PQL. Arsenic, chromium, and vanadium were within ranges not exceeding drinking water thresholds. Nickel was reported. Molybdenum was not reported. Selenium was detected in seven of ten samples. Copper and manganese were detected in three of ten samples. Cobalt was detected in MW-8. All reported concentrations for these metals were below their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water.
*   **January 2013:** No exceedances of numeric water quality threshold values were reported, with the exception of manganese.
*   **March 2013:** No exceedances of numeric water quality threshold values were reported, with the exception of manganese detected in samples from MW-1 and MW-7.
*   **September 2013:** No exceedances of numeric water quality threshold values were reported.
*   **April 2017:** With the exception of manganese in MW-1, no reported dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium were reported at concentrations similar to historical levels and significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds.
*   **April 2019:** With the exception of manganese in MW-5, no reported dissolved metals concentrations exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium were reported at concentrations similar to historical levels and significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds.

### General Minerals and Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)

*   **March 2012:** Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 10 mg/l, meeting the primary MCL. TDS concentrations exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   **May 2012:** Nitrate as nitrogen met the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   **November 2012:** TDS exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations ranged from 2.8 to 24 mg/l.
*   **January 2013:** TDS exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations ranged from 2.9 to 30 mg/l.
*   **March 2013:** Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations ranged from 2.7 to 30 mg/l. TDS concentrations ranged from 290 to 700 mg/l, with TDS meeting or exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   **September 2013:** Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations ranged from 2.5 to 36 mg/l. TDS concentrations ranged from 300 to 750 mg/l, with TDS exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   **April 2017:** Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations ranged from 2.6 to 24 mg/l, with three samples exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS concentrations ranged from 230 to 550 mg/l, with three samples exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   **April 2019:** Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations ranged from 2.3 to 40 mg/l, with four samples exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS concentrations ranged from 300 to 760 mg/l, with five samples equal to or greater than the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

### Groundwater Depth and Flow

*   **October 2016:** MW-4 was dry.
*   **April 2017:** Depth to groundwater ranged from 35.52 to 40.73 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the east-southeast at an average gradient of 0.0007.
*   **April 2019:** Depth to water measurements were collected semi-annually, with the next event scheduled for October 2019.

### Water Chemistry Characterization

Stiff diagrams consistently indicate that groundwater sampled in MW-4 has a calcium-dominant composition from March 2012 through April 2019.

## Regulatory Status

*   **March 2012:** Manganese exceeded the secondary MCL for taste and odor. Nitrate as nitrogen met the primary MCL. TDS exceeded the secondary MCL.
*   **May 2012:** Nitrate as nitrogen met the primary MCL. TDS exceeded the secondary MCL.
*   **November 2012:** TDS exceeded the secondary MCL.
*   **January 2013:** TDS exceeded the secondary MCL.
*   **March 2013:** TDS met or exceeded the secondary MCL.
*   **September 2013:** TDS exceeded the secondary MCL.
*   **February 2014:** TDS exceeded the secondary MCL.
*   **June 2014:** TDS exceeded the secondary MCL.
*   **April 2019:** TDS was equal to or greater than the secondary MCL.
