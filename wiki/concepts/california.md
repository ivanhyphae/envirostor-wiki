# Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remediation Case File (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA)

## Overview

This case file pertains to the remediation of barium-containing soil (BCS) within stockpiles adjacent to State Route 132 in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. The primary focus is on the excavation and verification testing of Stockpile 3, and the characterization of a borrow area intended for clean cap fill. The project involved excavation of BCS, transportation to Stockpile 1, and subsequent verification sampling to ensure compliance with remediation goals.

## Site Record

### Stockpile 3 Excavation and Verification

On March 13, 2020, Geocon Consultants, through Project Manager John E. Juhrend, submitted a Technical Memorandum detailing the BCS removal verification testing for Stockpile 3. This stockpile was located east of State Route 99, between Kansas Avenue and the Modesto Irrigation District (MID) water canal. A total of 3,075 truckloads, approximately 38,440 cubic yards of BCS, were excavated and transported to Stockpile 1 for placement as embankment fill.

The excavation and verification testing were conducted in general accordance with the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). The RDIP established verification testing thresholds for barium at 1,000 mg/kg (for groundwater protection) and lead at 80 mg/kg (residential screening level). Stockpile 3 was excavated to elevations between 82.5 and 84 feet above MSL.

A total of 24 verification soil samples (S3 1-0 through S3 24-0) were collected from the base of the excavation between February 28 and March 12, 2020. Following analytical results indicating elevated barium concentrations above the verification threshold at sample locations S3 9-0 through S3 11-0, these areas were deepened by approximately one foot and resampled. Samples S3 SE BERM and S3 SW BERM were collected from the top of the berm at the southern end of the Stockpile 3 excavation.

With the exception of sample S3 16-0, which was collected from storm drain backfill adjacent to State Route 99, all verification samples were collected from native soil at the base of the excavation. Samples were analyzed for barium and lead using EPA Test Method 6010B by Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL).

All final excavation soil samples met the RDIP verification thresholds. Barium concentrations ranged from 46 to 970 mg/kg, and lead concentrations ranged from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg. Based on this data, the Stockpile 3 BCS removal was deemed complete and suitable for placement of clean embankment fill without further RDIP monitoring. This information was to be included in the Removal Action Completion Report.

### Clean Cap Borrow Area Characterization

On February 27, 2020, Geocon Consultants, also through Project Manager John E. Juhrend, submitted a Technical Memorandum regarding the characterization of a proposed clean cap borrow area. This area is located easterly of Carpenter Road within the State Route 132 right-of-way and was historically used for agriculture. The borrow area is approximately 1,250 feet long, 200 feet wide, and intended for excavation to a depth of 5 feet. The extracted material was to be used to cover consolidated BCS within Stockpiles 1 and 2.

The soil sampling strategy was developed in accordance with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and was approved by Mr. Dean Wright with DTSC. On December 21, 2020, 12 direct-push borings (B1 through B12) were completed on a grid pattern within the borrow area. Continuous core soil samples were collected from each boring at depth intervals of 0 to 2 feet and 2 to 5 feet. Samples were composited into 3-part composites, yielding four composite samples per depth interval. These were analyzed for Title 22 metals using EPA Test Methods 6010B and 6461A (mercury). Organochlorine pesticides (OCP) were also analyzed in the 0 to 2 feet interval samples using EPA Test Method 8081A.

Soil encountered generally consisted of brown to tan, damp, clayey to fine sandy silt. No field indicators of contamination were observed. Reported concentration ranges for Title 22 metals, with site-specific background concentrations in parentheses, included:

*   Arsenic: 1.0 to 1.5 mg/kg (1.2 mg/kg)
*   Barium: 60 to 110 mg/kg (72.8 mg/kg)
*   Beryllium: 1.3 to 1.6 mg/kg (0.2 mg/kg)
*   Chromium: 7.5 to 11 mg/kg (7.1 mg/kg)
*   Cobalt: 3.2 to 4.9 mg/kg (4.4 mg/kg)
*   Copper: 5.3 to 9.9 mg/kg (7.5 mg/kg)
*   Lead: 1.7 to 7.0 mg/kg (2.0 mg/kg)
*   Nickel: 5.6 to 8.2 mg/kg (5.3 mg/kg)
*   Silver: 1.6 to 2.5 mg/kg (0.2 mg/kg)
*   Vanadium: 16 to 25 mg/kg (31.3 mg/kg)
*   Zinc: 21 to 41 mg/kg (26.3 mg/kg)

Antimony, cadmium, selenium, thallium, mercury, and OCPs were not detected above laboratory reporting limits. The reported metal concentrations were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. The proposed clean cap borrow area was deemed suitable for generating clean cover fill materials.

## Regulatory Status

The remediation activities for Stockpile 3 were conducted in general accordance with the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). Verification testing thresholds were established for barium (1,000 mg/kg for groundwater protection) and lead (80 mg/kg residential screening level). The sampling strategy for the clean cap borrow area was developed in accordance with the DTSC *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and approved by DTSC.
