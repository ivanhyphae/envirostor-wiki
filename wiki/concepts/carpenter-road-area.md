The Carpenter Road Area is a specific geographic location within the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, relevant to the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project in Stanislaus County, California. This area is identified in project documentation, including the Final Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remedial Action Plan (RAP).

### Site Characterization

The Final Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remedial Action Plan, dated March 18, 2023, references the Carpenter Road Area in the context of soil sampling and analysis. Specific concentrations of analytes within the Carpenter Road Area are detailed in project groundwater reports, such as the GW Rpt final dated March 8, 2023. However, the provided source material does not list specific contaminant concentrations, spatial distributions, or exceedances for the Carpenter Road Area itself.
