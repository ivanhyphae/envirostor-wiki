The Carpenter Road Area is a location within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, specifically associated with the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway Project in Stanislaus County, California. This area is relevant to the project's Remedial Action Plan (RAP).

## Site record

The Final Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remedial Action Plan, dated March 18, 2023, references the Carpenter Road Area in the context of soil sampling and analysis. Specific concentrations of analytes within the Carpenter Road Area are detailed in the project's groundwater reports, such as the GW Rpt final dated March 8, 2023. However, the provided source material does not list specific contaminant concentrations, spatial distributions, or exceedances for the Carpenter Road Area itself.

## Regulatory status

The Final Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remedial Action Plan outlines the regulatory framework applicable to the site, including [[california-primary-mcl]] and [[california-secondary-mcl]] standards. The specific regulatory status of the Carpenter Road Area, including any exceedances or compliance findings, is not detailed in the provided source material.
