## Overview

The Carpenter Road Area is a segment of the State Route 132 (SR-132) alignment in Stanislaus County, California, identified as a proposed source of clean fill soil. This soil was intended for use as cover material for [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) stockpiles associated with the SR-132 project. The area consists of native soil that has been within the [[caltrans]] right-of-way since the 1960s and was previously agricultural land. A [[borrow-area-sampling-plan]] was developed by [[geocon-consultants]] in February 2020 to characterize the upper five feet of soil for its suitability as clean fill.

## Site record

### Proposed excavation and use

The contractor for the SR-132 project proposed excavating the upper five feet of soil along the SR-132 alignment, extending eastward from Carpenter Road. This excavated material was designated for use as clean fill cover for the BCS stockpiles, aligning with the [[clean-fill-capping]] and [[clean-fill-suitable-as-cover]] remedial strategy.

### Historical environmental review

[[geocon-consultants]] reviewed the Blackburn 2014 Initial Site Assessment (ISA) and the Rincon 2018 Phase II Environmental Site Assessment (ESA) for potential contaminant sources in the vicinity of the Carpenter Road Area. The review did not identify any high-risk documented contamination releases in the immediate area of the proposed clean fill source. Testing of residential backyards by Rincon, adjacent to and west of Carpenter Road, indicated the presence of pesticides and metals at concentrations below applicable regulatory screening levels.

### Lithology

Geotechnical test borings conducted at the Carpenter Road overcrossing in 2017 documented that the material in this location consists of native soil. Logs from these borings were appended to the sampling plan submittal to support the lithologic description.

### Sampling plan (February 2020)

The [[borrow-area-sampling-plan]], prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] and transmitted to [[caltrans]] by John Juhrend on February 4, 2020, outlined the following characterization program for the Carpenter Road Area:

*   **Number of borings:** 12 borings, each 5 feet deep.
*   **Depth intervals and analytes:**
    *   **0–2 feet:** 3 four-point composite samples for organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) and heavy metals.
    *   **3–5 feet:** 3 four-point composite samples for heavy metals.
*   **Total composite samples:** 6 depth-discrete samples.

### Drilling schedule

[[geocon-consultants]] tentatively scheduled the drilling of the borings for February 11, 2020, with a planned 48-hour analytical turnaround time if required.

### Agency transmittal

Richard C. Stewart, Engineering Geologist with [[caltrans]] Central Region Environmental Division, forwarded the sampling plan to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board on February 4, 2020. The transmittal included attachments titled "Hwy 132 Clean Fill Source Testing Exhibit.pdf" and "LOTB-N Carpenter OC.pdf."

## Regulatory status

As of February 4, 2020, the sampling plan for the Carpenter Road Area was pending review and approval by the DTSC. [[caltrans]] requested DTSC acceptance prior to commencing field work. The proposed soil characterization was intended to determine if the native soil from the Carpenter Road Area met regulatory standards for use as clean fill cover material over the BCS stockpiles. No approval or conditional acceptance is documented in the available source material.

## Open items

*   DTSC approval of the sampling plan was pending as of the February 4, 2020 submittal.
*   Analytical results from the proposed 6 composite samples were to be provided to DTSC following laboratory analyses; these results are not documented in the available source material.
*   Field work scheduling was contingent on contract authorization (Dokken or City), USA notice confirmation, and DTSC sampling plan approval.
*   John Juhrend was out of the office from February 5 through 15, 2020, with Jim Brake serving as the point of contact during that period.
