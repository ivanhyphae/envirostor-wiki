# Carpenter Road Area

The Carpenter Road Area is the segment of the SR 132 right-of-way east of the Carpenter Road overcrossing that served as the primary borrow source of clean fill for capping barium-containing soil (BCS) consolidated in [[stockpile-1]] and [[stockpile-2]]. Highway contractor Bay Cities Paving and Grading identified this area, and its characterization by Geocon and approval by [[dtsc]] established the sampling approach later extended to other borrow locations (Basin 5) on the project.

## Origin as clean fill source

Section 4.7 of the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) called for capping the completed Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS and ADL consolidation zones with a minimum 12-inch-thick clean fill cap, drawing on vacant and agricultural land within planned excavation areas along the SR 132 right-of-way between the Carpenter Road and Rosemore Avenue overcrossings. The RDIP required that specific source areas, once defined by the highway construction contractor, be sampled by Geocon in general accordance with DTSC's *Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Material* for pre-approval before placement. Bay Cities Paving and Grading identified an area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road within the SR 132 right-of-way as the first such source.

## Variance and initial approval

Geocon evaluated the planned highway underpass excavation area east of Carpenter Road and documented, in a February 27, 2020 Technical Memorandum, that it would generate soil suitable for use as clean cover fill. This evaluation was incorporated into the *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan* (Geocon, April 9, 2020), submitted to DTSC's Dean Wright to formalize approved clean-fill sources for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2. DTSC subsequently approved the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area. This April 9, 2020 Variance later became the reference document for the Carpenter Shoofly characterization work.

## Carpenter Shoofly characterization (2021)

A further clean-fill source within the Carpenter Road Area was the "Carpenter Shoofly," a temporary traffic detour excavation (Traffic Handling Plan Stage 2B) associated with the overcrossing structure then under construction. Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil were to be excavated once the overcrossing was completed, for use as capping material over Stockpiles 1 and 2, pending DTSC approval.

A sampling plan for the Carpenter Shoofly Area was submitted and revised; DTSC and the Central Valley Water Board reviewed the revised plan received April 5, 2021 and approved it by e-mail on April 7, 2021, noting it was consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and drew no comments.

Geocon then collected 24 discrete soil samples along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6), taken from the exposed slope face at 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet from the top of slope, at an excavation depth of 20 feet. The laboratory composited three discrete samples from the same elevation on each slope face, producing eight 3-part composite samples, analyzed for barium and lead by EPA Method 6010B under expedited 48-hour turnaround. Results were reported in Geocon's April 12, 2021 Technical Memorandum, *Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Characterization Testing*.

| Analyte | Detections | Range (mg/kg) | Site-specific background threshold |
|---|---|---|---|
| Barium | 10 of 10 samples | 28–96 | 120 mg/kg |
| Lead | 9 of 10 samples | 1.4–3.8 | 3.8 mg/kg |

All barium results fell below the 120 mg/kg maximum site-specific background value, and lead results were at or below the 3.8 mg/kg maximum site-specific background concentration. On this basis Geocon concluded the Carpenter Shoofly soil met the BCS clean cap criteria established in the DTSC-approved April 9, 2020 Variance. DTSC's Dean Wright, with the Water Board (Kyle Cockerham), reviewed the memorandum and confirmed by e-mail on April 22, 2021 that the composite results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the clean cap criteria, serving as DTSC's acceptance of the memorandum. Geocon's cover memo states this information was to be included in the Removal Action Completion Report following completion of Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping.

## Reuse as a sampling precedent (Basin 5)

The Carpenter Road Area characterization work was subsequently treated as a template rather than a one-off study. In June 2020, when Geocon proposed a clean fill sampling plan for a separate borrow location, Basin 5 (adjacent and north of the previously tested SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road), Caltrans's Richard Stewart forwarded the plan to DTSC noting that Geocon had not submitted a separate technical memorandum with background comparison tables for Basin 5, and was "assuming that the Carpenter technical memo that was previously submitted is applicable." The Basin 5 sampling frequency was based on the expectation of similar non-impacted native formation conditions as found in the Carpenter Road cut section.

## MSE wall footing exclusion

Not all soil adjacent to the Carpenter Road Area BCS stockpiles qualified as clean fill. Geocon separately evaluated material from mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations (approximately five feet deep) along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2, documented in an April 6, 2020 Technical Memorandum. Following excavation of the southern slope BCS material, Caltrans and DTSC determined that the upper foot of the planned wall footing excavation soil contained elevated barium concentrations unsuitable for use as clean cap material, distinguishing this footing soil from the acceptable Carpenter Road and Carpenter Shoofly source material.
