## Overview

The Central Valley RWQCB is a regulatory agency involved in the remediation case file for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, specifically concerning the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. Their role, as indicated by the provided source material, pertains to water quality control and the management of excavated materials. The Caltrans Ramp Clarification Letter dated August 24, 2012, addresses both the [[dtsc]] and the Central Valley RWQCB regarding the disposition of excavated material from the SR 99 at Kansas Avenue ramp project.

## Site Record

The Caltrans Ramp Clarification Letter dated August 24, 2012, states that the Central Valley RWQCB was among the recipients of clarification regarding excavated material. Specifically, it notes that the native soil material proposed for excavation outside the footprint of Stockpile-3-excavation is suitable for reuse as structural backfill or for offsite reuse/disposal as a non-hazardous soil to an accepting facility. The letter also indicates that the Central Valley RWQCB was informed that the results of additional investigations and proposed management of the material demonstrated that completion and implementation of an Interim-Removal-Action-Workplan was not needed for the ramp project.

## Regulatory Status

The Central Valley RWQCB is a recipient of information regarding the regulatory status of excavated materials. The Caltrans Ramp Clarification Letter dated August 24, 2012, indicates that the material excavated from Stockpile No. 3, after analysis, was determined not to be classified as a hazardous waste because solubility levels fell below the STLC. This information was communicated to the Central Valley RWQCB as part of the clarification process.
