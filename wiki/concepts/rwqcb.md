## Overview

The [[rwqcb]] is a regulatory agency involved in the remediation case file for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, specifically concerning the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. Its role pertains to water quality control and the management of excavated materials. The Caltrans Ramp Clarification Letter dated 8.24.12 addresses the Central Valley RWQCB regarding the disposition of excavated material from the SR 99 at Kansas Avenue ramp project. The DTSC Approval E-mail for Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Sampling Plan also indicates the RWQCB's involvement in reviewing and approving sampling plans for clean fill materials.

## Site Record

The Caltrans Ramp Clarification Letter dated 8.24.12 states that the Central Valley RWQCB was informed that native soil material proposed for excavation outside the footprint of Stockpile-3-excavation is suitable for reuse as structural backfill or for offsite reuse/disposal as a non-hazardous soil to an accepting facility. ## Regulatory Status

The Central Valley RWQCB is a recipient of information regarding the regulatory status of excavated materials. The Caltrans Ramp Clarification Letter dated 8.24.12 indicates that material excavated from Stockpile No. 3, after analysis, was determined not to be classified as a hazardous waste because solubility levels fell below the STLC. This information was communicated to the Central Valley RWQCB as part of the clarification process. {{quote: "The results of these samples fell below the STLC and thus the material is not classified as a hazardous waste."}}

The DTSC Approval E-mail for Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Sampling Plan states that the DTSC and the Waterboard have reviewed and approved the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan. {{quote: "The Plan is consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and there are no comments. This e-mail serves as DTSCs approval of the Plan."}}
