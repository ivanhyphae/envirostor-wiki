MW-2 is a groundwater monitoring well at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. It has been sampled and analyzed as part of multiple groundwater monitoring events to assess general minerals and dissolved metals.

## Groundwater Chemistry

Groundwater samples collected from MW-2 have been analyzed for general minerals and dissolved metals. Stiff diagrams indicate that groundwater from MW-2 has consistently exhibited a calcium-dominant composition from March 2012 through April 2019.

## Regulatory Status

The California Department of Public Health Primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for drinking water for nitrate as nitrogen is 10 mg/l. Concentrations in MW-2 have met or exceeded this MCL in multiple sampling events, including March 2012 (12 mg/l), November 2012 (12 mg/l), January 2013 (13 mg/l), March 2013 (13 mg/l), December 2013 (13 mg/l), February 2014 (10 mg/l), June 2014 (15 mg/l), April 2017 (17 mg/l), and April 2019 (17 mg/l).

The California Department of Public Health Secondary Maximum Contaminant Level (SMCL) for TDS is 500 mg/l. Concentrations in MW-2 have met or exceeded this SMCL in April 2017 (550 mg/l) and April 2019 (550 mg/l).
