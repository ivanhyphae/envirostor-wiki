## Overview

MW-2 is a groundwater monitoring well located at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. It has been sampled and analyzed as part of multiple groundwater monitoring events to assess general minerals and dissolved metals.

## Site record

Groundwater samples collected from MW-2 have been analyzed for general minerals and dissolved metals.

In March 2012, MW-2 exhibited a calcium-dominant composition. The Stiff diagram for MW-2 in March 2012 showed a broader concentration profile than in 2006, indicating a shift towards calcium dominance. The sample from MW-2 in March 2012 was characterized as having a similar balance of calcium and sodium in 2006, suggesting a mix of water types at that location. By March 2012, the mixed waters at MW-2 became calcium-dominant.

In May 2012, MW-2 had a calcium-dominant composition.

In November 2012, MW-2 had a calcium-dominant composition.

In January 2013, MW-2 had a calcium-dominant composition.

In March 2013, MW-2 had a calcium-dominant composition.

In September 2013, MW-2 had a calcium-dominant composition.

In December 2013, MW-2 had a calcium-dominant composition.

In February 2014, MW-2 had a calcium-dominant composition.

In June 2014, MW-2 had a calcium-dominant composition.

In April 2017, MW-2 had a calcium-dominant composition.

In April 2019, MW-2 had a calcium-dominant composition.

Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-2 have been reported as:
*   12 mg/l in March 2012.
*   10 mg/l in May 2012.
*   12 mg/l in November 2012.
*   13 mg/l in January 2013.
*   13 mg/l in March 2013.

Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) concentrations in MW-2 have been reported as:
*   490 mg/l in April 2015.

Sulfide was reported in MW-2 at concentrations of:
*   0.07 mg/l in May 2012.

*   0.020 mg/l in June 2014.
*   0.016 mg/l in April 2017.

## Regulatory status

The California Department of Public Health Primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for drinking water for nitrate as nitrogen is 10 mg/l. Concentrations in MW-2 have met or exceeded this MCL in multiple sampling events, including March 2012 (12 mg/l), November 2012 (12 mg/l), January 2013 (13 mg/l), March 2013 (13 mg/l), December 2013 (13 mg/l), February 2014 (10 mg/l), June 2014 (15 mg/l), April 2017 (17 mg/l), and April 2019 (17 mg/l).

The California Department of Public Health Secondary Maximum Contaminant Level (SMCL) for TDS is 500 mg/l. Concentrations in MW-2 have met or exceeded this SMCL in April 2017 (550 mg/l) and April 2019 (550 mg/l).
