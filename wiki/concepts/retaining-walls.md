## Overview
Retaining walls are a structural component of the containment system for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] as outlined in the [[remedial-action-plan]]. They function in conjunction with [[bridge-abutments]] and the roadway structure to enclose the stockpiled soil, thereby preventing direct exposure and limiting surface water infiltration.

## Site Record
The [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] are to be contained behind retaining walls, [[bridge-abutments]], and beneath the roadway. {{quote: "The stockpiles will be contained behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath the roadway."}} Unpaved portions of the contained stockpiles are to be covered with clean fill. {{quote: "Unpaved portions will have clean fill cover."}}

Photographic documentation from the Annual Stockpile Inspection Form includes an image labeled "Retaining wall Stockpile #2". {{quote: "Retaining wall Stockpile #2"}}. Another image, depicting the "North lane Stockpile #1", shows a "long plain light-gray concrete block wall". {{quote: "North lane Stockpile #1"}}

## Regulatory Status
The [[remedial-action-plan]] (RAP) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] received approval from the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). The DTSC concluded that the chosen containment remedy, which incorporates retaining walls, would not result in significant environmental effects, provided that project controls are implemented. {{quote: "DTSC has completed an independent analysis and determined that the selected containment remedy will not have a significant effect to the environment with implementation of the project controls (mitigations measures)."}} The RAP was approved on March 14, 2018. {{quote: "March 14, 2018"}}
