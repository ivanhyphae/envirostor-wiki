## Overview
Retaining walls are a key component of the containment strategy for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] as part of the [[remedial-action-plan]] for the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]]. They are used in conjunction with [[bridge-abutments]] and beneath the roadway to enclose the stockpiles, preventing exposure and minimizing surface water infiltration into groundwater.

## Site Record
The [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] are to be contained behind retaining walls, [[bridge-abutments]], and beneath the roadway. {{quote: "The stockpiles will be contained behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath the roadway."}} Unpaved portions of the contained stockpiles will be covered with clean fill. {{quote: "Unpaved portions will have clean fill cover."}}

Photographic documentation from the Annual Stockpile Inspection Form shows a "Retaining wall Stockpile #2" {{quote: "Retaining wall Stockpile #2"}}. Another image depicts the "North lane Stockpile #1" with a "long plain light-gray concrete block wall" {{quote: "North lane Stockpile #1"}}.

## Regulatory Status
The [[remedial-action-plan]] (RAP) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] was approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). The DTSC determined that the selected containment remedy, which includes retaining walls, will not have a significant effect on the environment with the implementation of project controls. {{quote: "DTSC has completed an independent analysis and determined that the selected containment remedy will not have a significant effect to the environment with implementation of the project controls (mitigations measures)."}} The RAP was approved on March 14, 2018. {{quote: "March 14, 2018"}}
