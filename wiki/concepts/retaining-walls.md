## Retaining Walls

Retaining walls are a component of the selected cleanup alternative for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]]. They are used as part of the [[containment-of-stockpiles]] strategy for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] associated with the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]].

## Site Record

The [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] will be contained behind retaining walls, [[bridge-abutments]], and beneath the roadway as part of the [[remedial-action-plan]]. Unpaved portions will have clean fill cover. This containment method is designed to prevent exposure and minimize surface water infiltration into groundwater under the stockpiles. Land use controls and annual inspections of the pavement and other containment features, along with groundwater monitoring, will ensure the long-term integrity of the remedy.

## Regulatory Status

The [[remedial-action-plan]] for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] was approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). The DTSC determined that the selected containment remedy, which includes retaining walls, will not have a significant effect on the environment with the implementation of project controls.
