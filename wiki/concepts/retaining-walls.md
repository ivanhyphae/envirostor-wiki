## Overview
Retaining walls are a structural component of the containment system for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] as outlined in the [[remedial-action-plan]]. They function in conjunction with [[bridge-abutments]] and the roadway structure to enclose the stockpiled soil, thereby preventing direct exposure and limiting surface water infiltration.

## Site Record
The [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] are to be contained behind retaining walls, [[bridge-abutments]], and beneath the roadway. Unpaved portions of the contained stockpiles are to be covered with clean fill.

Photographic documentation from the Annual Stockpile Inspection Form includes an image labeled "Retaining wall Stockpile #2". Another image, depicting the "North lane Stockpile #1", shows a "long plain light-gray concrete block wall".

## Regulatory Status
The [[remedial-action-plan]] (RAP) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] received approval from the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). The DTSC concluded that the chosen containment remedy, which incorporates retaining walls, would not result in significant environmental effects, provided that project controls are implemented. The RAP was approved on March 14, 2018.
