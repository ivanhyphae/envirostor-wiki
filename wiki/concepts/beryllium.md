## Beryllium

### Overview
Beryllium is a metal contaminant of concern at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (State Route 132, Stanislaus County, CA). Its presence in soil and groundwater at the site stems from historical aggregate processing and stockpiling operations. The primary regulatory driver for beryllium at these stockpiles is the California Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for drinking water, which has been exceeded in site groundwater monitoring wells.

### Site Record
Documented beryllium data appear in the groundwater monitoring records for the Modesto Stockpiles site, specifically in *S9525-06-44A Modesto Stockpiles GW. SEPT2012.1212.md*.

- **Groundwater Beryllium Concentrations (September 2012):**
  - Monitoring well MW-5 showed beryllium at **9.0 µg/L**.
  - Monitoring well MW-4 showed beryllium at **2.3 µg/L**.
- **Regulatory Threshold Exceedance:** Both values exceed the California MCL for beryllium of **4.0 µg/L**.
- **Spatial Distribution:** The highest recorded concentration (9.0 µg/L) was found in MW-5; a lower but still elevated concentration (2.3 µg/L) was in MW-4. No data is provided for other wells on site for this sampling event.
- **Verification Status:** The September 2012 sampling event represents a single documented monitoring round. No subsequent verification sampling or trend data is included in the available source document.

### Regulatory Status
- **Applicable Standard:** The California MCL for beryllium is **4.0 µg/L** (4.0 ppb), as established by the California Department of Public Health (now State Water Resources Control Board Division of Drinking Water) for drinking water sources.
- **Enforcement Agency:** The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) oversees the site (as indicated by the case number prefix S9525-06-44A), likely under oversight for a former military or industrial disposal site or a cleanup order. The State Water Resources Control Board or Regional Water Quality Control Board may also have jurisdiction for groundwater protection.
- **Compliance Status:** Based solely on the September 2012 data, the site is **not in compliance** with the beryllium MCL at MW-4 and MW-5. However, the available document does not include any approved remedial action plan, corrective action schedule, or compliance certification. The current regulatory status (as of the document date) remains an open enforcement or corrective action matter.

### Open Items
- **Pending Investigation:** The source document does not address the vertical or horizontal extent of beryllium contamination in groundwater. It is unknown if beryllium migrates toward nearby receptors or if the plume is stable.
- **Soil Concentrations:** No beryllium data for soil stockpiles themselves is provided in the available source. The relationship between stockpile soil beryllium content and groundwater concentrations is not documented.
- **Remedial Action Required:** No cleanup actions, treatment, or removal actions for beryllium are described in the September 2012 report. A corrective action plan or feasibility study may be required but is not included.
- **Trend Analysis:** With only a single sampling event documented, no temporal trend (increasing, decreasing, or stable) for beryllium can be determined.
