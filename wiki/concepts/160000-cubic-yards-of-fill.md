## Overview

The Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project involves approximately {{quote: "160,000 cubic yards of fill material"}} {{quote: "that was excavated from the SR 132 project site"}} {{quote: "and stockpiled at the Modesto site"}} {{quote: "for potential reuse."}} These stockpiles, generated from the SR 132 project, were placed at the Modesto site. The Remedial Action Plan (RAP) addresses the management of these stockpiles, which contain metals, primarily barium, lead, and strontium.

## Site record

The stockpiles were generated from excavations related to the SR 132 project and were placed at the Modesto site. The total volume of this fill material is approximately {{quote: "160,000 cubic yards of fill material"}} {{quote: "that was excavated from the SR 132 project site"}} {{quote: "and stockpiled at the Modesto site"}} {{quote: "for potential reuse."}} The stockpiles contain metals, including {{quote: "primarily barium, lead, and strontium."}}

The stockpiles are located within Caltrans right-of-way south of Kansas Avenue and are divided into three areas:
*   Stockpile 1: South of Kansas Avenue and west of Emerald Avenue.
*   Stockpile 2: South of Kansas Avenue, between Emerald Avenue and SR 99.
*   Stockpile 3: South of Kansas Avenue and east of SR 99.

These stockpiles were partially generated from excavations of soil from evaporation ponds that contained elevated heavy metals, notably barium, and polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs). The property beneath and northeast of the SR 99 and Kansas Avenue Interchange was previously occupied by chemical processing facilities. Pond tailings and native soil were removed from a parcel purchased by the State for SR 99 construction and placed in lifts to form bridge abutments and embankment fills for the future SR 99/132 Interchange.

The stockpiles are enclosed within security fencing and bordered by adjacent property boundary fencing/walls or structures. Residential areas are to the south of Stockpiles 1 and 2. Commercial/industrial development, Caltrans ROW, and city streets are in the remaining adjacent areas. The Modesto Irrigation District Lateral #4 canal crosses beneath the southern end of Stockpile 3.

Caltrans proposed to cap the soil stockpiles as part of the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway construction project. The Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) was prepared under the oversight of the DTSC and RWQCB. If approved, the RAP would allow for the placement of impacted soil beneath roadway pavement, within bridge abutments, and behind retaining walls.

## Regulatory status

The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) have reviewed and approved the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the stockpiles for public noticing. The RAP addresses the management of approximately 160,000 cubic yards of soil containing metals.
