## Bent 2 Stockpiles

### Overview
The Bent 2 Stockpiles refer to soil material excavated from the vicinity of the Bent 2 overcrossing structure as part of the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. These stockpiles were subject to characterization testing to determine appropriate management and disposal or reuse strategies, particularly concerning [[barium]] concentrations.

### Site Record
Characterization testing of the Bent 2 Stockpiles was conducted. The [[bent-2-excavation-soil-stockpiles-characterization-testing]] document details the findings. Specifically, one sample from the Bent 2 Stockpiles, identified as BS-2-01, was analyzed for barium. This sample contained 1,100 mg/kg of barium. This concentration exceeded the applicable [[clean-cap-criteria]] for barium, which was 500 mg/kg.

### Regulatory Status
The exceedance of the clean-cap criteria for barium in sample BS-2-01 necessitated further action. A [[variance-to-remedial-design-implementation-plan]] was issued by Caltrans on October 26, 2021, to address the management of this material. The variance allowed for the placement of the Bent 2 Stockpiles material beneath a clean cap, as defined by the [[clean-cap-material]] specifications, within the project limits. This decision was made in consultation with the relevant regulatory agencies.
