## Barium And Lead Sampling Results Below Site Specific Background Concentrations

### Overview
This concept refers to analytical results for barium and lead in soil samples collected as part of the remediation efforts for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, specifically related to Stockpile 2. The sampling was conducted to assess the presence of barium-containing soil within the foundation spoil. The results indicated that concentrations of both barium and lead were below site-specific background concentrations, a key factor in determining the appropriate management and disposal of the soil.

### Site Record
During the assessment of Stockpile 2, specifically in relation to the [[weather-station-foundation-and-overhead-sign-1-installation]], soil samples were analyzed for barium and lead. The [[technical-memorandum-for-stockpile-2-barium-containing-soil-foundation-spoil-assessment-workplan]] documented these findings. The sampling results confirmed that barium concentrations were below the established site-specific background concentration. Similarly, lead concentrations were also found to be below the site-specific background concentration. The Approval Letter Stockpile 2 Spoils Workplan (dated 900259) acknowledged these findings, indicating that the soil within Stockpile 2, based on this assessment, did not exceed background levels for these analytes.

### Regulatory Status
The site-specific background concentrations for barium and lead served as the applicable threshold for evaluating the sampled soil. As the sampling results for both barium and lead were below these established background levels, the soil from Stockpile 2 was deemed to be in compliance with the criteria set forth for its management.
