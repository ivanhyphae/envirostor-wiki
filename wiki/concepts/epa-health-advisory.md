## Overview

The **EPA Health Advisory** (HA) is a non‑enforceable, health‑based guideline issued by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency for contaminants in drinking water. In the context of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA), the HA level for barium (1,000 µg/L) serves as a screening benchmark for groundwater quality. It is not a regulatory standard like a Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL), but it is used by DTSC and Caltrans to evaluate the potential risk from site‑related contamination. The specific HA value referenced in the project record is drawn from the S2350‑01‑02 Updated Statistical Evaluation Report (February 24, 2024).

## Site record

According to the S2350‑01‑02 Updated Statistical Evaluation Report, the barium concentration in soil at monitoring well MW‑5 was measured at **760 mg/kg** during Phase 1 (2019–2020). This value was compared to the EPA Health Advisory of **1,000 µg/L** (equivalent to 1.0 mg/L) for drinking water. While the soil concentration is not directly comparable to a drinking‑water guideline (different media and exposure pathways), the report notes that the site’s shallow groundwater and soil‑to‑groundwater leaching potential were considered. No groundwater grab sample data from MW‑5 exceeded the HA; however, the report flagged the soil concentration as exceeding a risk‑based screening level derived from the HA. The evaluation was performed as part of the statistical analysis for the soil stockpile remediation, with the HA used as an upper‑bound reference for human health risk.

## Regulatory status

The EPA Health Advisory is not an enforceable standard; however, the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) applies it as a conservative screening criterion during site closure evaluations. For this project, the HA for barium (1,000 µg/L) was cited in the S2350‑01‑02 report as a benchmark for comparison with soil leachate data (TCLP-equivalent concentrations). The soil barium concentration at MW‑5 (760 mg/kg) does not directly trigger a regulatory exceedance because the HA applies to drinking water, not soil. Nonetheless, DTSC required additional evaluation of leaching potential, as documented in the report. The project is currently in compliance with the applicable MCLs for groundwater monitoring, but the soil stockpile management plan includes conditions to prevent groundwater impact above the HA level.

## Open items

- The S2350‑01‑02 Updated Statistical Evaluation Report recommended confirmatory soil‑leachate testing for barium at MW‑5 to verify that leaching concentrations remain below the EPA Health Advisory (1,000 µg/L). This testing has not yet been completed as of the report date.
- The site‑specific cleanup goal for barium in soil has not been finalized; the HA is being used as an interim screening value pending DTSC approval of a revised risk assessment.
