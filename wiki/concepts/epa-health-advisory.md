## Overview
The EPA Health Advisory is a guideline referenced for evaluating groundwater quality. In the context of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, these advisories serve as numeric water quality thresholds for specific dissolved metals.

## Site record
During the January 2013 groundwater monitoring round, the EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory was used as a numeric water quality threshold for arsenic, chromium, and vanadium. Analytical results for these dissolved metals were reported for each of the samples collected. The concentrations of arsenic, chromium, and vanadium reported for the samples from each well did not exceed their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water.

Similarly, during the June 2013 groundwater monitoring round, the EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory was referenced as a numeric water quality threshold for arsenic, chromium, and vanadium. Dissolved metals arsenic, chromium, and vanadium were reported for each sample collected. None of the reported concentrations for these metals exceeded their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water.

## Regulatory status
The EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory is applied as a numeric water quality threshold for certain dissolved metals, including arsenic, chromium, and vanadium, in groundwater samples collected at the site.
