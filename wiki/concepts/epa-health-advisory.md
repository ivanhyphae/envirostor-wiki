## Overview
The EPA Health Advisory is a guideline used for evaluating groundwater quality. In the context of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, these advisories are referenced when assessing contaminant levels in groundwater samples.

## Site record
During the June 2013 groundwater monitoring round, the EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory was referenced as a numeric water quality threshold for arsenic, chromium, and vanadium. {{quote: "(2) = EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory"}} The dissolved metals arsenic, chromium, and vanadium were reported for each of the samples collected with the following ranges: {{quote: "As shown on the following table, the dissolved metals arsenic, chromium and vanadium were reported for each of the samples collected with the following ranges:"}} Although concentrations of arsenic, chromium, and vanadium were reported for the samples collected from each well, none of the reported concentrations exceed their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water. {{quote: "Although concentrations of arsenic, barium, chromium, strontium and vanadium were reported for the samples collected from each well, none of the reported concentrations exceed their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water."}}

During the January 2013 groundwater monitoring round, the EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory was also referenced as a numeric water quality threshold for arsenic, chromium, and vanadium. {{quote: "(2) = EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory"}} The dissolved metals arsenic, chromium, and vanadium were reported for each of the samples collected with the following ranges: {{quote: "As shown on the following table, the dissolved metals arsenic, chromium and vanadium were reported for each of the samples collected with the following ranges:"}} Although concentrations of arsenic, chromium, and vanadium were reported for the samples collected from each well, none of the reported concentrations exceed their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water. {{quote: "Although concentrations of arsenic, barium, chromium, strontium and vanadium were reported for the samples collected from each well, none of the reported concentrations exceed their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water."}}

## Regulatory status
The EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory is used as a numeric water quality threshold for certain dissolved metals, including arsenic, chromium, and vanadium, in groundwater samples. {{quote: "(2) = EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory"}}
