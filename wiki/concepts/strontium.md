Strontium is a chemical element identified as a contaminant of potential concern (COPC) in the soil stockpiles and groundwater at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. The stockpiled materials originated from property formerly owned by FMC, which processed minerals including celestite (strontium sulfate) during the 1930s.

### Site Record

Strontium has been detected in soil samples from the stockpiles. In the Shaw 2004 Preliminary Site Investigation (PSI), the maximum strontium concentration reported was 231 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). This concentration was noted as being more than two orders of magnitude less than the United States Environmental Protection Agency's (USEPA) residential Regional Screening Level (RSL) of 47,000 mg/kg. Consequently, strontium was not identified as a COPC in the 2007 Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA).

In stormwater samples collected on February 28, 2014, strontium was found at concentrations higher than those reported for background samples.

Groundwater monitoring has consistently detected strontium. In January 2013, strontium was reported in all ten groundwater samples, with concentrations ranging from 690 to 1,400 micrograms per liter (µg/l). These concentrations were noted as being similar to historical levels and significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds. In June 2014, strontium was reported in each of the ten groundwater samples, with concentrations ranging from 770 to 1,400 µg/l. These levels were also described as similar to historical levels and significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds. In April 2015, strontium was reported in each of the two groundwater samples analyzed, with concentrations of 740 and 770 µg/l, which were similar to historical levels and significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds.

During a May 24, 2012, matrix spike analysis, strontium recoveries were reported as less than 70% and also exceeded 130%. However, since the dissolved metals concentrations were not close to exceeding water quality thresholds, no additional qualification of the data was deemed necessary.

### Regulatory Status

There is no California Human Health Screening Level (CHHSL) for strontium. The USEPA residential RSL for strontium is 47,000 mg/kg.
