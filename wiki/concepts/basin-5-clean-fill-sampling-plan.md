## Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan

### Overview
The Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan details the strategy for collecting soil samples at the proposed Basin 5 location. This area is situated adjacent to and north of the SR 132 roadway cut section east of [[carpenter-road-area]]. The plan was developed by [[geocon-consultants]] and submitted to [[caltrans]] for review, with subsequent approval by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). The sampling frequency and methodology are based on the assumption of geological conditions similar to those previously encountered in the area east of Carpenter Road.

### Site Record
The Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan was submitted on June 11, 2020. Geocon's submittal included a map illustrating sample locations but did not include a separate technical memorandum detailing sampling methodology and comparative background metal concentrations. Geocon assumed that the technical memorandum previously submitted for the area east of Carpenter Avenue would be applicable to Basin 5. The sampling frequency was determined by Basin 5's proximity to the SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road, which had previously exposed non-impacted native formation, and similar conditions were anticipated at Basin 5.

### Regulatory Status
The sampling plan was submitted for review and approval by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. On June 12, 2020, DTSC and the Waterboard reviewed the plan, dated June 11, 2020. They found the plan to be consistent with previous borrow area sampling and issued their approval via email, with no comments. [[dean-wright]] of DTSC communicated this approval.

### Open Items
Geocon's assumption that the technical memorandum previously submitted for the area east of Carpenter Avenue is applicable to Basin 5 requires confirmation.
