## Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan

### Overview
The Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan outlines the strategy for collecting soil samples at the proposed Basin 5 location, situated adjacent to and north of the SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road. This plan was submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. to Caltrans for review and subsequent approval by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). The sampling frequency and methodology are intended to be consistent with previous testing conducted in the area east of Carpenter Avenue, assuming similar geological conditions.

### Site Record
The sampling plan for Basin 5 was submitted on June 11, 2020. {{quote: "Date: Thursday, June 11, 2020 11:27:05 AM"}} The plan includes a map detailing sample locations. {{quote: "Geocon's submittal only included this map with sample locations."}} A technical memorandum detailing sampling methodology and comparative background metal concentrations was not included, as Geocon assumed the previously submitted technical memorandum for the area east of Carpenter Avenue would be applicable. {{quote: "Just an -fyi - a technical memorandum with sampling methodology and tables of comparative background metal concentrations was not included as was previous for the area east of Carpenter Avenue. Geocon is assuming that the Carpenter technical memo that was previously submitted is applicable."}} The sampling frequency is based on the proximity of Basin 5 to the previously tested SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road, which exposed non-impacted native formation. {{quote: "The sampling frequency is based on the location of Basin 5 adjacent and north of the previously tested SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road that exposed non-impacted native formation."}} Similar conditions are anticipated at Basin 5. {{quote: "Similar conditions are anticipated at Basin 5."}}
