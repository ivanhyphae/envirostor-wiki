## Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan

### Overview
The Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan outlines the strategy for collecting soil samples at the proposed Basin 5 location, situated adjacent to and north of the SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road. The plan was developed by [[geocon-consultants]] and submitted to [[caltrans]] for review and subsequent approval by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). The sampling frequency and methodology are based on the assumption of similar geological conditions to those previously encountered in the area east of Carpenter Avenue.

### Site Record
The sampling plan for Basin 5 was submitted on June 11, 2020. {{quote: "Date: Thursday, June 11, 2020 11:27:05 AM"}} Geocon's submittal included a map illustrating sample locations. {{quote: "Geocon's submittal only included this map with sample locations."}} A technical memorandum detailing sampling methodology and comparative background metal concentrations was not included, as Geocon assumed the previously submitted technical memorandum for the area east of Carpenter Avenue would be applicable. {{quote: "Just an -fyi - a technical memorandum with sampling methodology and tables of comparative background metal concentrations was not included as was previous for the area east of Carpenter Avenue. Geocon is assuming that the Carpenter technical memo that was previously submitted is applicable."}} The sampling frequency is based on the proximity of Basin 5 to the previously tested SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road, which exposed non-impacted native formation. {{quote: "The sampling frequency is based on the location of Basin 5 adjacent and north of the previously tested SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road that exposed non-impacted native formation."}} Similar conditions are anticipated at Basin 5. {{quote: "Similar conditions are anticipated at Basin 5."}}

### Regulatory Status
The sampling plan was submitted for review and approval by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). {{quote: "Dean, Perry, Steve, Clean cover mining location at proposed Basin 5 for your review and approval."}} On June 12, 2020, DTSC and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (referred to as the Waterboard in the source) reviewed the Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan dated June 11, 2020. {{quote: "DTSC and the Waterboard have reviewed the Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan (Plan) dated June 11, 2020 that outlines the proposed sampling and analysis for the soil that will be used to cap the State Route 132 stockpiles."}} The plan was deemed consistent with previous borrow area sampling, and DTSC issued its approval. {{quote: "The Plan is consistent with the previous borrow area sampling and there are no comments. This e-mail serves as DTSCs approval of the Plan."}}

### Open Items
The applicability of the previously submitted technical memorandum for the area east of Carpenter Avenue to Basin 5 is an assumption by Geocon and requires confirmation. {{quote: "Geocon is assuming that the Carpenter technical memo that was previously submitted is applicable."}}
