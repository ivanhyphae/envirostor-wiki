# Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan

The Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan is the document Geocon Consultants prepared to establish that imported fill material could be placed and used as clean cover at Basin 5, part of the Caltrans SR 132 soil stockpile remediation site in Stanislaus County. The plan was submitted for regulatory review and was approved by e-mail, with the approval subject to stated conditions rather than an unconditional sign-off.

## What the plan proposed

The plan set out a sampling approach for characterizing fill material proposed for use as clean cover at Basin 5, intended to demonstrate that the material met the criteria needed to be placed at the site without spreading contamination beyond what the remedy already addresses.

## Approval and its conditions

The approval was communicated by e-mail rather than a formal regulatory letter. The e-mail approving the SR 132 Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan confirms that the plan was accepted, but the approval was conditional: it did not simply clear the plan as submitted, but attached conditions that had to be met for the fill sampling and placement to proceed as proposed. The available material does not show a subsequent document confirming that those conditions were satisfied, so the record leaves the compliance loop open on that point.

## Significance

Because the plan governs whether imported fill can be certified as clean and used as cover at Basin 5, its approval status controls whether that fill placement work can proceed under the terms Caltrans and its consultant proposed. The conditional nature of the approval means the plan's acceptance is not, on this record, a closed matter — a reviewer checking the status of Basin 5 clean fill work should confirm whether the stated conditions were later documented as met.
