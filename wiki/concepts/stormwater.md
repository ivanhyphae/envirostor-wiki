Stormwater is precipitation that runs off the ground surface. In the context of the SR 132 project, stormwater management is a consideration for potential contaminant transport.

### Thallium in Stormwater

Thallium was detected in stormwater samples collected at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. A sample collected on March 15, 2013, from the stockpile surface runoff at Station 10+00 contained 0.0033 mg/L of thallium. This concentration exceeded the [[california-primary-mcl]] for thallium, which is 0.002 mg/L.
