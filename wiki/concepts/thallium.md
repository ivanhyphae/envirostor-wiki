Thallium was detected at low concentrations in stockpile soil at the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site, first reported in the June 2014 Final FS Report, and had not been found in groundwater or in surface water at the site before 2023. That changed with stormwater sampling in December 2023, which produced the first reported exceedance of the California primary MCL for thallium at the site. The March 2023 stormwater round, by contrast, did not report thallium above the MCL, making the December 2023 result an isolated, not a repeated, exceedance as far as the record shows.

## Soil

The June 2014 Final FS Report identified thallium in stockpile soil at low concentrations. Specific values are not given in the available material.

## Stormwater

| Date | Result relative to MCL |
|---|---|
| March 2023 | Not reported above MCL |
| December 2023 | Exceeded MCL (first reported exceedance) |

The California primary MCL is the threshold against which both rounds were evaluated (see [[california-primary-mcl]]). Concentrations for the December 2023 exceedance are not given in the available material. The reports covering this result are dated February 2024 and February 2025.

## Groundwater

Thallium has not been detected in groundwater at the site. The stormwater exceedance is accordingly a surface-pathway finding only, consistent with the broader determination that [[groundwater-not-impacted]] has not been undermined by thallium results.

## Significance

The December 2023 result matters because it is a new finding, not a continuation of a known problem: thallium had a clean history in groundwater and in surface water sampling through March 2023, and the stockpile soil detections from 2014 had not previously translated into a stormwater exceedance. The record does not show what, if anything, was required or done in response to the December 2023 exceedance; no remediation plan, agency directive, or follow-up sampling result tied to this specific exceedance appears in the available material.
