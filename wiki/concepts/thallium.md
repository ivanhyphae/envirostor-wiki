## Thallium

### Overview
Thallium was among the metals analyzed for in surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023, as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. It was not detected above the laboratory's Method Detection Limits (MDLs) in the background and surface water samples.

### Site Record
On March 10, 2023, thallium was not reported above the MDLs in the background and surface water samples collected. {{quote: "Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, nickel, silver, and thallium were not reported above the MDLs in the background and surface water samples."}}

### Regulatory Status
No specific regulatory status or applicable thresholds for thallium are mentioned in the provided source material.
