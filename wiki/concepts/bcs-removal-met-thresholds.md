## Barium Containing Soil (BCS) Removal Met Thresholds

### Overview
This concept refers to the successful remediation of Barium Containing Soil (BCS) from specific areas of [[stockpile-2]] at the SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA project site. Following removal activities, confirmation sampling demonstrated that the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 achieved the cleanup levels established in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). This allowed for the subsequent placement of [[clean-soil-cover]].

### Site Record
On March 10, 2020, the [[dtsc]] (DTSC), through Dean Wright, P.G., Project Manager, accepted a Technical Memorandum dated March 9, 2020. This memo documented the removal of BCS from the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 and the results of post-removal confirmation soil sampling. The sampling results indicated that these areas met the RDIP verification thresholds. DTSC's acceptance confirmed that these areas were suitable for the placement of clean embankment fill. The removal and verification activities were conducted by [[geocon-consultants]] and reported by [[caltrans]] Engineering Geologist, Richard C. Stewart.

### Regulatory Status
The cleanup levels for BCS removal were established in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). The confirmation sampling results, as accepted by DTSC on March 10, 2020, indicated that the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 met these RDIP verification thresholds.
