## Overview
Vanadium (V) is a dissolved metal analyte that has been monitored in groundwater at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. Its presence and concentration in groundwater samples have been evaluated against applicable drinking water thresholds as part of the site's groundwater monitoring program.

## Site record
During the April 2015 groundwater sampling event, vanadium was detected in samples collected from four site monitoring wells (MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, and MW-7). The analytical results were documented in the "Groundwater Monitoring Report – April 2015" prepared by [[geocon-consultants]]. The dissolved metals analysis, including vanadium, was performed using ICP-MS EPA Method 6020. The report presents vanadium concentrations alongside those of other dissolved metals such as arsenic and chromium. Vanadium was detected at concentrations ranging from 1.0 to 9.7 µg/l in samples from MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, and MW-7.

## Regulatory status
No specific regulatory status or comparison to MCLs for vanadium in groundwater was noted in the April 2015 groundwater monitoring report. The report indicated that for the metals analyzed, "none of the reported concentrations exceed their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water."

## Open items
No open items specifically related to vanadium were identified in the April 2015 groundwater monitoring report.
