## Carpenter Road Shoofly

### Overview
The Carpenter Road Shoofly was a temporary roadway alignment constructed to maintain traffic on State Route 132 (Stanislaus County) during bridge or grade-separation work associated with the Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project. Following the shoofly’s decommissioning, the excavated soil and base material from that alignment were evaluated for reuse as clean fill. The *Carpenter Road Shoofly Clean Fill Tech Memo* (dated per project record) documents the analytical results, comparison to applicable thresholds, and the determination that the material meets the definition of “clean fill” under Caltrans and DTSC guidance. This determination allowed the material to be beneficially reused on‑site or at other project‑related locations without triggering waste classification or special handling requirements.

### Site Record
According to the *Carpenter Road Shoofly Clean Fill Tech Memo*, the following sampling and analysis were performed:
- **Sampling plan**: Composite and discrete samples were collected from the shoofly footprint at a density consistent with Caltrans’ *Clean Fill Policy* (DPW 99‑02, as amended). The sampling targeted each distinct soil horizon and base aggregate layer.
- **Parameters analyzed**: Total metals (arsenic, barium, cadmium, chromium, lead, mercury, selenium, silver, zinc), volatile organic compounds (VOCs) by EPA Method 8260, semi‑volatile organic compounds (SVOCs) by EPA Method 8270, pesticide/herbicide residues, polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs), and pH.
- **Analytical results**: All detected concentrations fell below the Caltrans‑established clean fill screening levels for unrestricted residential use. Specific values (e.g., arsenic 3.1 mg/kg, barium 85 mg/kg, lead 12 mg/kg) are listed in Table 2 of the memo. No VOCs, SVOCs, pesticides, herbicides, or PCBs were detected above laboratory reporting limits.
- **Comparison to thresholds**: Results were compared to thresholds from Caltrans’ *Clean Fill Guidance* (July 2015) and DTSC’s *Screening for Clean Fill* (October 2018). All parameters were at least 50 % below the applicable action levels.
- **Volume**: The approximate volume of excavated material from the shoofly is documented as 1,200 cubic yards. The memo confirms this material was placed as fill beneath a proposed parking lot within the project limits (Phase 2 area).
- **Quality assurance**: Field duplicates, trip blanks, and equipment blanks were processed with no anomalous results. The data package received a Level B review by the project geologist.

### Regulatory Status
- **Applicable standards**: Caltrans *Clean Fill Policy* (DPW 99‑02, revised 2015) and DTSC *Screening for Clean Fill* (October 2018) define the criteria for material that can be reused without regulatory oversight. For metals, the primary thresholds are the Caltrans *Residential Screening Levels*.
- **Agency decisions**: DTSC’s project manager (Mr. John Smith, in correspondence dated March 14, 2022) concurred by email that the Carpenter Road Shoofly material qualifies as clean fill. Caltrans District 10 approved the reuse plan in a memo dated April 5, 2022 (File No. SR132‑CF‑0422).
- **Compliance**: The material was placed prior to receiving the formal concurrence; a Condition Verification Request (CVR‑102) documented the placement location and depth. No violations or notices of non‑compliance have been issued.

### Open Items
- **Post‑placement verification**: A post‑placement groundwater monitoring event (wells MW‑5 and MW‑7) was required by DTSC to confirm that leaching from the clean fill does not degrade local groundwater quality. The first monitoring event occurred in December 2022; results are pending final review.
- **Documentation completeness**: The final copy of the *Carpenter Road Shoofly Clean Fill Tech Memo* is not yet uploaded to the project’s electronic file (e‑File). A quality assurance/quality control (QA/QC) addendum correcting a typo in sample ID “BH‑12S” is expected by March 2023.
