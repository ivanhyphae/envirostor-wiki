## Overview
The Carpenter Road Shoofly refers to an area within the [[sr-132]] right-of-way in Modesto, California, from which approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil were excavated. This excavated soil was intended for use as clean capping material over portions of [[barium-containing-soil]] ([[bcs-stockpile]]) Stockpiles 1 and 2, pending approval from the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC).

## Site Record

Characterization testing was performed on soil from the Carpenter Road Shoofly. Twenty-four discrete soil samples were collected along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6) at depths of 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet from the top of the slope. These discrete samples were composited into eight 3-part composite samples.

Analysis for barium and lead was conducted using EPA Test Method 6010B. Barium was detected in all 10 analyzed soil samples at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 analyzed soil samples at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg.

The analytical results indicated that the soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly met the clean cap criteria for the [[bcs-stockpile]]s. Specifically, the barium concentrations were less than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg. Lead concentrations were less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg.

The excavation of approximately 5,000 cubic yards of soil was planned to occur once the overcrossing structure was completed. The placement of this material as clean capping was anticipated for the week of April 19, 2021.

## Regulatory Status

The clean fill criteria for the [[bcs-stockpile]]s were established in a *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan*, dated April 9, 2020, and approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). The soil from the Carpenter Road Shoofly met these criteria, specifically the upper-bound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg.

The information regarding the characterization testing of the Carpenter Road Shoofly soil was intended to be included in the Removal Action Completion Report following the completion of the capping activities for Stockpile 1 and 2.
