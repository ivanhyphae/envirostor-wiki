## Food and Machinery Corporation (FMC)

The Food and Machinery Corporation (FMC) is identified as the former operator of a barite (BaSO<sub>4</sub>) and celestite (SrSO<sub>4</sub>) processing disposal pond. Excavation activities near [[kansas-avenue]] uncovered tailings from this pond, which contributed to the contaminated soil stockpiles at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site.

### Site record

The Site consists of three stockpiles totaling over 120,000 cubic yards (yd<sup>3</sup>) of contaminated soil. These stockpiles originated from sub-grade construction for [[state-route-99]] in Modesto, Stanislaus County. Specifically, excavation near Kansas Avenue involved the removal of tailings from a portion of an FMC barite and celestite processing disposal pond situated on a parcel previously acquired by the state. The Site is impacted by hazardous substances, including metals such as [[barium]], lead, and strontium.
