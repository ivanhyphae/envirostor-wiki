## Food and Machinery Corporation (FMC)

The Food and Machinery Corporation (FMC) is identified as the former operator of a barite (BaSO<sub>4</sub>) and celestite (SrSO<sub>4</sub>) processing disposal pond. Excavation activities near Kansas Avenue uncovered tailings from this pond, which contributed to the contaminated soil stockpiles at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site.

### Site record

The Site consists of three stockpiles totaling over 120,000 cubic yards (yd<sup>3</sup>) of contaminated soil. {{quote: "The Site consists of three stockpiles in excess of 120,000 yd<sup>3</sup> of contaminated soil."}} These stockpiles originated from sub-grade construction for State Route 99 in Modesto, Stanislaus County. {{quote: "The stockpiles were generated during sub-grade construction of State Route 99 through Modesto, Stanislaus County."}} Specifically, excavation near Kansas Avenue involved the removal of tailings from a portion of an FMC barite and celestite processing disposal pond situated on a parcel previously acquired by the state. {{quote: "Excavation near Kansas Avenue removed tailings from a portion of a Food and Machinery Corporation (FMC) barite (BaSO<sub>4</sub>) and celestite (SrSO<sub>4</sub>) processing disposal pond that operated on a parcel purchased by the state."}} The Site is impacted by hazardous substances, including metals such as barium, lead, and strontium. {{quote: "The Site is impacted by hazardous substances (metals), including barium, lead, and strontium."}}
