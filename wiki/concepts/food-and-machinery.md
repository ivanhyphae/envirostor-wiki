## Food and Machinery Corporation (FMC)

The Food and Machinery Corporation (FMC) is referenced in the context of the origin of contaminated soil stockpiles at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. Specifically, excavation near Kansas Avenue uncovered tailings from a barite (BaSO<sub>4</sub>) and celestite (SrSO<sub>4</sub>) processing disposal pond that was operated on a parcel acquired by the state. This material contributed to the contamination of the soil stockpiles.

### Site record

The Site consists of three stockpiles in excess of 120,000 yd<sup>3</sup> of contaminated soil. {{quote: "The Site consists of three stockpiles in excess of 120,000 yd<sup>3</sup> of contaminated soil."}} These stockpiles were generated during sub-grade construction of State Route 99 through Modesto, Stanislaus County. {{quote: "The stockpiles were generated during sub-grade construction of State Route 99 through Modesto, Stanislaus County."}} Excavation near Kansas Avenue removed tailings from a portion of a Food and Machinery Corporation (FMC) barite (BaSO<sub>4</sub>) and celestite (SrSO<sub>4</sub>) processing disposal pond that operated on a parcel purchased by the state. {{quote: "Excavation near Kansas Avenue removed tailings from a portion of a Food and Machinery Corporation (FMC) barite (BaSO<sub>4</sub>) and celestite (SrSO<sub>4</sub>) processing disposal pond that operated on a parcel purchased by the state."}} The Site is impacted by hazardous substances (metals), including barium, lead, and strontium. {{quote: "The Site is impacted by hazardous substances (metals), including barium, lead, and strontium."}}
