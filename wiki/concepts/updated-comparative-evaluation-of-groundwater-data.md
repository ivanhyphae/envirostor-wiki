## Overview

The **Updated Comparative Evaluation of Groundwater Data** is a technical report submitted by Geocon Consultants Inc. on behalf of the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans), dated February 26, 2024. It assessed whether barium- and lead-impacted soil stockpiles encapsulated beneath the newly constructed State Route (SR) 132 Expressway in Stanislaus County, California, pose a present or future risk to groundwater. The report was reviewed and approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), via a letter signed May 3, 2024 (Site Code: 900259).

The evaluation concluded that the encapsulated soil is **not impacting groundwater** and **will not impact groundwater in the future**, and that groundwater monitoring may be discontinued.

## Site Record

- **Report Title:** *Updated Comparative Evaluation of Groundwater Data, Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, Stanislaus County, California*
- **Date of Submission:** February 26, 2024
- **Prepared by:** Geocon Consultants Inc., for Caltrans
- **Key Regulatory Reviewers:**
  - DTSC – Dean Wright, PG, Project Manager, Site Mitigation and Restoration Program
  - Central Valley RWQCB – Kyle Cockerham, PG, Site Cleanup Unit

- **Data Consistency:** The report noted consistency with the earlier 2014 evaluation, strengthening confidence that encapsulation remains effective.
- **Additional Supporting Factor:** The recent encapsulation of the soil beneath the Expressway was cited as further support for the conclusion of no groundwater impact.

- **Monitoring Wells:** The evaluation supported discontinuation of groundwater monitoring and decommissioning of the four remaining monitoring wells:
  - MW4
  - MW6
  - MW9
  - MW10

- **Decommissioning Reference:** The approval letter specifies that decommissioning is to be performed as per the approved **Remedial Design and Implementation Plan**.

## Regulatory Status

- **Lead Agencies:** DTSC (Site Mitigation and Restoration Program) and Central Valley RWQCB (Site Cleanup Unit).
- **Applicable Standards:** The report references MCLs (Maximum Contaminant Levels) in its analysis — specifically MCL 1,000 µg/L for barium — though the source material does not provide full details on all thresholds used.
- **Project Compliance Status:** Based on the approved evaluation, the encapsulated soil stockpiles are in compliance with applicable standards; no current groundwater impact is found.

## Open Items

- **Decommissioning Pending:** The four monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, MW10) are to be decommissioned per the approved Remedial Design and Implementation Plan. The timing and completion of decommissioning is a required future action not addressed in the approval letter.
