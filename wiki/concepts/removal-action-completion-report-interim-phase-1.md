## Removal Action Completion Report Interim Phase 1

### Overview
The Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project, dated December 1, 2022, documents the consolidation and capping activities for barium-containing soils (BCS) as part of the Interim Phase 1 construction at the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), accepted this report as final on January 18, 2023. This acceptance marks the conclusion of the Interim Phase 1 effort.

### Site Record
The Interim Phase 1 activities described in the RACR included soil removal, consolidation, and capping. These actions were completed in accordance with the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP), Remedial Design and Implementation Plan (RDIP), and two variance requests to the RDIP approved on April 17, 2020, and September 21, 2020. The RACR also included As-Built Plans, cross-section figures, final survey data, and a final Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan.

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC accepted the Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project on January 18, 2023. This acceptance signifies the completion of the Interim Phase 1 remediation efforts.

### Open Items
The DTSC will prepare a draft O&M Agreement for Caltrans review within 30 days of the acceptance letter. A Land Use Covenant (LUC) for the remediated area will also be completed and sent for review, signature, and recording with the Stanislaus County Assessor's office.

Final certification of the RAP for this project will not occur until after the Ultimate Phase 2 State Route 132 build-out effort is completed. This future effort, anticipated to begin in 8 to 10 years, will involve adding additional lanes and completing the final build-out of State Route 132. It will require a supplemental RDIP for DTSC review and approval, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and potential revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement. The Ultimate Phase 2 project will be conducted under a separate agreement between DTSC and Caltrans.
