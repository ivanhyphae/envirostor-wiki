# Modesto Soil Stockpiles (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA)

## Overview
The Modesto Soil Stockpiles refer to material excavated from the [[sr-132]] corridor, specifically within the [[carpenter-road-area]], during the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]]. These stockpiles were managed by [[caltrans]] and characterized for potential contaminants, including [[barium]]. The management and remediation of these stockpiles were overseen by [[geocon-consultants]]. The stockpiles were later encapsulated beneath the SR 132 structure. Stormwater sampling has been conducted at the site to monitor potential impacts.

## Site Record
The Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles are located south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. The site contains Stockpiles 1 and 2, which were encapsulated beneath SR 132.

Historically, the site area was occupied by Barium Products Ltd. in the 1930s, a chemical manufacturing company that processed barite and celestite. Liquid residue from processing operations was discharged to unlined evaporation/disposal ponds on the FMC facility from the 1950s to the 1970s. In 1961, a parcel within this area was purchased by Caltrans for SR 99 right-of-way. Soil from a pond within this area was excavated during SR 99 construction and stockpiled at the current SR 132 project location. Three stockpiles were constructed: Stockpile 1 (south of Kansas Ave and west of N Emerald Ave), Stockpile 2 (south of Kansas Ave, between N Emerald Ave and SR 99), and Stockpile 3 (south of Kansas Ave and east of SR 99). Earthwork for Phase I of the SR 99/132 expressway began in January 2020. Soil from Stockpile 3 was placed on Stockpiles 1 and 2. In October 2021, Stockpiles 1 and 2 were encapsulated beneath the SR 132 structural section. SR 132 opened to traffic in November 2022.

Stormwater sampling has been conducted at the site. On February 1, 2024, storm water samples were collected at the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles. No discharge/runoff from the Caltrans ROW was observed during this event. Caltrans planned to conduct additional stormwater sampling during qualifying rain events in 2024.

On February 4, 2025, stormwater samples were again collected. Discharge/runoff was not observed migrating away from the Caltrans ROW during this event. Caltrans planned to conduct additional stormwater sampling during qualifying rain events in 2025.

## Regulatory Status
Stormwater sampling protocols were established in the *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP),* prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc., dated December 13, 2023, and approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB).
