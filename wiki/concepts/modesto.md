# Modesto Soil Stockpiles (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA)

## Overview
The Modesto soil stockpiles refer to material excavated from the [[sr-132]] corridor, specifically within the Carpenter Road area, during the SR 132 West Freeway Expressway Project. These stockpiles were managed by [[caltrans]] and characterized for potential contaminants, including [[barium]]. The management and remediation of these stockpiles were overseen by Geocon Consultants.

## Site Record
Soil stockpiles generated from the SR 132 project, originating from the Carpenter Road area, were sampled and analyzed. The "Bent 2 Stockpiles Tech Memo" documented the characterization of these stockpiles. For instance, stockpile "MS-1" was sampled on October 26, 2017, and analyzed for various constituents. One sample from MS-1 yielded a barium concentration of 1,100 mg/kg. {{quote: "MS-1 October 26, 2017 1,100 mg/kg"}} This stockpile was designated as "clean fill" material. {{quote: "MS-1 October 26, 2017 clean fill"}}

The "Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan" and the "Borrow Area Sampling Plan" also detail sampling efforts related to materials from the SR 132 project. These plans outline the procedures for sampling and analyzing soil intended for use as clean fill.

## Regulatory Status
The stockpiled material was evaluated against regulatory requirements for clean fill. The designation of material as "clean fill" implies it met criteria for reuse or disposal without further extensive remediation, though specific thresholds are not detailed in the provided context for the Modesto stockpiles themselves.
