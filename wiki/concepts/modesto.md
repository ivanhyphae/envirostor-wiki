# Modesto Soil Stockpiles (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA)

## Overview
The Modesto Soil Stockpiles refer to material excavated from the [[sr-132]] corridor, specifically within the [[carpenter-road-area]], during the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]]. These stockpiles were managed by [[caltrans]] and characterized for potential contaminants, including [[barium]]. The management and remediation of these stockpiles were overseen by [[geocon-consultants]]. The stockpiles were later encapsulated beneath the SR 132 structure. Stormwater sampling has been conducted at the site to monitor potential impacts.

## Site Record
The Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles are located south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. {{quote: "the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles (the site) located south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California."}} The site contains Stockpiles 1 and 2, which were encapsulated beneath SR 132. {{quote: "The approximate site boundaries, Stockpiles 1 and 2, which were encapsulated beneath SR 132, and storm water sampling locations, are shown on the Site Plan (Figure 2)."}}

Historically, the site area was occupied by Barium Products Ltd. in the 1930s, a chemical manufacturing company that processed barite and celestite. {{quote: "During the 1930s, Barium Products Ltd. occupied property at 1200 Barium Road (now Graphics Drive) in Modesto, California, east of SR 99 between Woodland Avenue and Kansas Avenue. Barium Products Ltd. was a chemical manufacturing company processing a variety of ores and minerals including barite (barium sulfate) and celestite (strontium sulfate)."}} Liquid residue from processing operations was discharged to unlined evaporation/disposal ponds on the FMC facility from the 1950s to the 1970s. {{quote: "From the 1950s to the 1970s, liquid residue from the processing operations was discharged to unlined evaporation/disposal ponds along the western portion of the FMC facility."}} In 1961, a parcel within this area was purchased by Caltrans for SR 99 right-of-way. Soil from a pond within this area was excavated during SR 99 construction and stockpiled at the current SR 132 project location. {{quote: "Soil in and around the pond was excavated during construction of SR 99 and stockpiled within the current Caltrans ROW at the location of the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway project."}} Three stockpiles were constructed: Stockpile 1 (south of Kansas Ave and west of N Emerald Ave), Stockpile 2 (south of Kansas Ave, between N Emerald Ave and SR 99), and Stockpile 3 (south of Kansas Ave and east of SR 99). {{quote: "Three distinct stockpiles were constructed at the site: - Stockpile 1, located south of Kansas Avenue and west of North Emerald Avenue, - Stockpile 2, located south of Kansas Avenue, between North Emerald Avenue and SR 99, and - Stockpile 3, located south of Kansas Avenue and east of SR 99."}} Earthwork for Phase I of the SR 99/132 expressway began in January 2020. Soil from Stockpile 3 was placed on Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "The earthwork on Phase I of the SR 99/132 expressway began in January 2020. Soil from Stockpile 3 was excavated, transported, and placed on top of Stockpiles 1 and 2."}} In October 2021, Stockpiles 1 and 2 were encapsulated beneath the SR 132 structural section. {{quote: "In October 2021, the majority of Stockpiles 1 and 2 were completely encapsulated beneath the structural section of SR 132 and with clean soil and vegetation on the northern side slopes."}} SR 132 opened to traffic in November 2022. {{quote: "SR 132 was opened to traffic in November 2022."}}

Stormwater sampling has been conducted at the site. On February 1, 2024, storm water samples were collected at the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles. {{quote: "We collected storm water samples on February 1, 2024, in general accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) as established in the *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP),* prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc., dated December 13, 2023."}} No discharge/runoff from the Caltrans ROW was observed during this event. {{quote: "No discharge/runoff from the Caltrans ROW was observed during the February 1, 2024, sampling event."}} Caltrans planned to conduct additional stormwater sampling during qualifying rain events in 2024. {{quote: "Caltrans plans to conduct additional storm water sampling during qualifying rain events in 2024."}}

On February 4, 2025, stormwater samples were again collected. {{quote: "We collected storm water samples on February 4, 2025, in general accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) as established in the *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP),* prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc., dated December 13, 2023."}} Discharge/runoff was not observed migrating away from the Caltrans ROW during this event. {{quote: "Discharge/runoff was not observed migrating away from the Caltrans ROW during the February 4, 2025, sampling event."}} Caltrans planned to conduct additional stormwater sampling during qualifying rain events in 2025. {{quote: "Caltrans plans to conduct additional storm water sampling during qualifying rain events in 2025."}}

## Regulatory Status
Stormwater sampling protocols were established in the *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP),* prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc., dated December 13, 2023, and approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB). {{quote: "in general accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) as established in the *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP),* prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc., dated December 13, 2023."}}
