## Removal Action Completion Report (RACR)

### Overview

The Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) documents the status of removal, consolidation, and clean fill capping of barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles as highway embankment fill during Phase I construction of the State Route (SR) 132 West Project in Modesto, California. The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) provided oversight for this remediation effort under a Caltrans Interagency Agreement. An Interim RACR has been prepared to summarize completed activities, with a Final RACR to be submitted following the completion of Ultimate Phase 2 construction.

### Site Record

The Interim RACR covers the consolidation and clean fill capping of BCS Stockpiles 1 through 3. These stockpiles, along with aerially deposited lead (ADL) impacted soil removed from the unpaved shoulder of northbound SR 99, have been consolidated and capped with DTSC-approved clean fill materials. Remaining work includes the placement of structural pavement, erosion control, drainage, and median barriers.

Specific removal verification testing was conducted for BCS Stockpiles 2 and 3. For Stockpile 3, excavation reached approximate elevations between 82.5 and 84 feet above mean sea level (MSL). A total of 24 verification soil samples were collected between February 28 and March 12, 2020. Following initial results, areas with elevated barium concentrations were deepened and resampled. Final excavation soil samples from Stockpile 3 contained barium concentrations ranging from 46 to 970 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg, meeting the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) verification thresholds.

For Stockpile 2, the west end was excavated to an approximate elevation of 82 feet MSL, with subsequent deepening to 81 feet MSL. The east end was excavated to an approximate elevation of 83 feet MSL, with deepening to 82 feet MSL and then 81 feet MSL in areas with elevated barium. Verification soil samples from Stockpile 2 contained barium concentrations ranging from 54 to 760 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg, meeting the RDIP verification thresholds.

Characterization testing was also performed for potential clean cap borrow areas. The Carpenter Road Shoofly borrow area, sampled on February 21, 2020, yielded soil with barium concentrations ranging from 60 to 110 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.7 to 7.0 mg/kg, consistent with background concentrations and suitable for clean cover fill. The Basin 5 borrow area, sampled on June 16, 2020, showed barium concentrations from 66 to 94 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 5.8 to 11 mg/kg, also consistent with background.

Testing of Bent 2 excavation soil stockpiles, conducted on October 30, 2020, revealed barium concentrations ranging from 31 to 230 mg/kg. Four of these samples exceeded the upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg. Lead concentrations ranged from 2.6 to 13 mg/kg. This material was deemed potentially suitable for partial clean cap (6 inches or less) pending DTSC review.

Carpenter Shoofly clean fill characterization testing, performed on April 12, 2021, showed barium concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, meeting the clean cap criteria.

Air monitoring performed during BCS grading activities indicated airborne levels of total dust, barium, and lead below designated project action and risk levels, confirming effective dust control.

### Regulatory Status

The RDIP verification thresholds for BCS removal are:
*   Barium: 1,000 mg/kg (groundwater protection)
*   Lead: 80 mg/kg (residential screening level)

The upperbound background threshold for barium, as approved by DTSC for clean cap material, is 120 mg/kg.

### Open Items

A Final RACR will be submitted following the completion of Ultimate Phase 2 construction of the SR 132 West Project, scheduled for completion in 2028. A supplemental RDIP will be submitted prior to Ultimate Phase 2 construction, detailing monitoring and sampling requirements for any planned BCS disturbance and groundwater monitoring well abandonment.
