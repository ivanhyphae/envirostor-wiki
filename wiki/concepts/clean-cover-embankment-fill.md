## Clean Cover Embankment Fill

### Overview
Clean Cover Embankment Fill refers to soil material approved for use as backfill or cover within the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] that meets specific criteria for contaminant concentrations. This designation allows for the reuse of excavated soil within the project footprint, rather than requiring off-site disposal or the import of new fill material.

### Site Record
On April 17, 2020, the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) officially accepted a Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) concerning soil excavated for the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing along the southern boundary of [[stockpile-1-mse-wall-footing]]. Specifically, the soil extending from 1 to 5 feet from existing grade in this area contained barium concentrations with a calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) of 113.1 mg/kg. This concentration was deemed to be within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background levels. Consequently, this material was approved for use as clean cap soil on the project.

The upper foot of this same excavation was deemed unsuitable as clean fill due to elevated barium concentrations and was designated for placement in Stockpile 1 and 2 permanent fill areas.

The DTSC also indicated that soil from the MSE wall footing excavation along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2 may be used as clean cap soil, provided its UCL for barium is also found to be under naturally occurring background concentrations. The approval for this material was pending submission and review of the relevant data.

The expectation, as stated in the Variance Request, was that if approved by the DTSC and the Regional Board, the soil excavated for the Stockpile 1 MSE footing would be used solely as a source of clean cover or embankment fill within the footprints of the stockpiles and would not be used at other areas of the project or hauled off-site.

### Regulatory Status
The use of soil as clean cover embankment fill is governed by the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) and requires approval from regulatory agencies, including the DTSC and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. The approval for the specified material from Stockpile 1 was granted on April 17, 2020.

### Open Items
The official approval or rejection of soil from the MSE wall footing excavation along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2 is pending.
