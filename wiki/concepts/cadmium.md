## Cadmium

### Overview
Cadmium is a heavy metal contaminant detected in soil at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Site (State Route 132, Stanislaus County, CA). Within this project, cadmium is a constituent of concern (COC) due to its presence in stockpiled roadway maintenance materials — primarily historic storm sediment and ditch sweepings — at concentrations exceeding both background levels and California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs). The primary management concern is off-site migration potential to local receptors via wind or water erosion, and the need to characterize cadmium distribution prior to remedial design.

### Site Record
*   **Documented Sources:** The *Fenceline Workplan September 2012* (hereafter, FW 2012) is the principal source for cadmium data on this site. FW 2012 reports cadmium results from soil sampling conducted in July 2012 across the three stockpile areas (Piles A, B, and C). Cadmium was detected in composite and discrete samples but was not uniformly distributed.

*   **Concentrations and Distribution (from FW 2012):**
    *   **Maximum Concentration:** The highest cadmium concentration reported in FW 2012 was **7.4 mg/kg**, found in a composite sample from the central portion of Pile A.
    *   **Range Across Stockpiles:** Cadmium concentrations in Pile A samples ranged from 1.2 mg/kg to 7.4 mg/kg. Pile B samples showed a range of 0.8 mg/kg to 3.1 mg/kg. Pile C samples ranged from 0.5 mg/kg to 2.0 mg/kg.
    *   **Background Comparison:** FW 2012 notes that background cadmium levels in the region, using California Department of Conservation data for Stanislaus County, are approximately **0.35 mg/kg**. All stockpile samples exceeded this background value.
    *   **Screening Level Exceedance:** The residential CHHSL for cadmium (as listed in FW 2012) is **1.7 mg/kg**. The industrial/commercial CHHSL is **100 mg/kg**. Cadmium in Pile A and several Pile B samples exceeded the residential CHHSL; none approached the industrial/commercial threshold within the FW 2012 dataset.

*   **Spatial Notes:** FW 2012 indicates cadmium was highest in finer-grained materials (silts/clays) at the base and core of Stockpile A, consistent with its association with historic sediment from stormwater catchments. No groundwater monitoring data for cadmium is documented in FW 2012, as the stockpiles are on a paved former asphalt plant lot with no installed groundwater wells at that time.

### Regulatory Status
*   **Applicable Standards:** For the Modesto Soil Stockpiles Site, the primary screening criteria applied by DTSC (as per FW 2012) are the **California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs)**. The residential CHHSL of 1.7 mg/kg was considered the most conservative default threshold for future land use scenarios, pending a final risk assessment.
*   **Agency Oversight:** The project operates under the oversight of the **Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)**. Cadmium was identified as a COC in the FW 2012 data, requiring evaluation in the Remedial Investigation (RI) phase.
*   **Current Compliance:** As of the FW 2012 document, cadmium in Pile A exceeded the screening level. No post-2012 verification sampling data is presented in the FW 2012 source. Regulatory compliance is pending completion of the RI and a Feasibility Study, which would determine whether removal, placement under a cap, or institutional controls are required.

### Open Items
*   **Remedial Goal:** A final, site-specific, risk-based cleanup goal for cadmium has not been established in the FW 2012 document. The applicability of CHHSLs, background, or a modified threshold for the stockpiles’ eventual disposal or reuse has not been resolved.
*   **Mass Estimation:** FW 2012 did not estimate total cadmium mass in the stockpiles; this is a pending calculation for the RI phase to inform remedial volume.
*   **Fate and Transport:** The potential for cadmium leaching into underlying soil or migrating via dust under future grading or removal activities is not addressed in the FW 2012 workplan and remains an open issue for the RI Report.
*   **Off-site Considerations:** Nearby residential properties and agricultural land uses are identified in FW 2012 as potential receptors, but detailed cadmium pathway analysis (e.g., air dispersion modeling from active piles) has not been conducted.
