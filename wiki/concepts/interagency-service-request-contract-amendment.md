## Overview

The [[interagency-service-request-contract-amendment]] for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, identified as Service Request No. 43A0487-04, formalizes the reimbursement of costs incurred by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for regulatory oversight of environmental work. This oversight supports the construction of [[phase-2-of-the-state-route-132-west-expressway-construction]]. The amendment is part of Caltrans Agreement Number 43A0487 and DTSC Agreement Number 25-T5466.

## Site Record

Service Request No. 43A0487-04 has a term from August 4, 2025, to June 30, 2030, with an execution date of August 25, 2025. The total order amount is \$113,000.00. The charge district and EA/EFIS are 10-1E700/10-1500-0115. The source district/unit is 10/4692, with a Fed/State split of 0%/100%.

DTSC's responsibilities under this Service Request include:
*   Review and comment on routine monitoring and/or inspection reports.
*   Review and comment on Remedial Design Implementation Plans and associated documents.
*   Review and comment on well destruction reports.
*   Participation in meetings and phone calls regarding the Site and construction activities.
*   Providing field oversight, as needed by DTSC, of monitoring and field activities conducted by Caltrans.
*   Preparation and future modification of this Service Request.

The Service Request may be amended by Caltrans to address mutually agreed-upon modifications or expansions to the Scope of Work.

## Regulatory Status

This Service Request operates under Caltrans Agreement #43A0487 and DTSC Agreement Number 25-T5466. Service Requests cannot modify, amend, or exceed the terms of these primary agreements.

## Open Items

No formal progress reports are required under this Service Request. However, Caltrans may request copies of daily logs and timesheets for DTSC staff listed on quarterly invoices. Each billing invoice submitted by DTSC must include a summary of activities for both indirect and direct charges, along with daily logs for the invoice period.
