The [[interagency-service-request-contract-amendment]] for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project is an amendment to Caltrans Agreement Number 43A0487, also known as DTSC Agreement Number 25-T5466. This specific amendment, Service Request No. 43A0487-04, formalizes the reimbursement of costs incurred by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for regulatory oversight of environmental work related to the Modesto Soil Stockpiles. This work supports the construction of [[phase-2-of-the-state-route-132-west-expressway-construction]].

## Overview

This Service Request amendment establishes the terms and conditions under which DTSC will provide regulatory oversight for Caltrans' environmental activities concerning the Modesto Soil Stockpiles. DTSC's role includes reviewing reports, plans, and other documents, participating in meetings, and providing field oversight as necessary. The amendment ensures that DTSC's costs for these services are reimbursed by Caltrans.

## Site Record

The Service Request is identified as No. 43A0487-04, with a term from August 4, 2025, to June 30, 2030. The execution date was August 25, 2025, and the total order amount is \$113,000.00. The charge district and EA/EFIS are 10-1E700/10-1500-0115. The source district/unit is 10/4692, with a Fed/State split of 0%/100%.

DTSC's specific tasks under this Service Request include:
*   Review and comment on routine monitoring and/or inspection reports. {{quote: "Review and comment on routine monitoring and/or inspection reports."}}
*   Review and comment on Remedial Design Implementation Plans and associated documents. {{quote: "Review and comment on Remedial Design Implementation Plans and associated documents."}}
*   Review and comment on well destruction reports. {{quote: "Review and comment on well destruction reports"}}
*   Participation in meetings and phone calls regarding the Site and construction activities. {{quote: "Participation in meetings and phone calls regarding the Site and construction activities"}}
*   Provide field oversight, as needed by DTSC, of monitoring and field activities conducted by Caltrans. {{quote: "Provide field oversight, as needed by DTSC, of monitoring and field activities conducted by Caltrans."}}
*   Preparation and future modification of this Service Request. {{quote: "Preparation and future modification of this Service Request"}}

The Service Request may be amended by Caltrans to address mutually agreed-upon modifications or expansions to the Scope of Work. {{quote: "This Service Request may be amended by Caltrans to address any mutually agreed upon modifications to, or expansions of, this Scope of Work."}}

## Regulatory Status

This Service Request operates under the framework of Caltrans Agreement #43A0487 and DTSC Agreement Number 25-T5466. {{quote: "The execution of this Service Request shall incorporate by this reference all terms, conditions, duties, and obligations attendant upon the parties as set forth in Caltrans Agreement #43A0487. Service Requests may not be used to modify, amend, or exceed the terms of Agreement Number #43A0487, DTSC Agreement Number 25-T5466."}}

## Open Items

No formal progress reports are required under this Service Request. {{quote: "There will be no formal progress reports, or other reports, required under this Service Request."}} However, Caltrans may request copies of daily logs and timesheets for DTSC staff listed on quarterly invoices. {{quote: "Caltrans may request a copy of the daily logs and timesheets of staff who are listed in the quarterly invoice from DTSC."}} Each billing invoice from DTSC must include a summary of activities for both indirect and direct charges, along with daily logs for the invoice period. {{quote: "Each billing invoice submitted by DTSC shall include summary of activities of indirect and direct charges and daily logs provided by DTSC to Caltrans for the invoice period."}}
