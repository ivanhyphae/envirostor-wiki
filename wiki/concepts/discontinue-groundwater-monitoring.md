## Discontinue Groundwater Monitoring

### Overview

The decision to discontinue groundwater monitoring at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site was based on statistical evaluations demonstrating that groundwater quality was not being impacted by the [[barium-and-lead-impacted-soil]] within the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]]. This action is a component of the overall site remediation strategy, which includes the management and eventual off-site disposal of the stockpiled materials.

### Site Record

Statistical evaluations of groundwater data were conducted to assess potential impacts from the stockpiles. These evaluations concluded that there was [[no-impact-to-groundwater]]. Specifically, a statistical evaluation submitted on January 26, 2023, and approved by the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) on February 15, 2023, supported the discontinuation of groundwater monitoring. This approval was contingent on the continued encapsulation of the soil stockpiles and their eventual removal from the site.

### Regulatory Status

The discontinuation of groundwater monitoring was approved by the CVRWQCB. This decision was based on the project's demonstration, through statistical analysis, that the stockpiled materials were not adversely affecting groundwater quality. The approval was granted under the condition that the soil remains encapsulated and is ultimately removed from the site.

### Open Items

The primary open item related to this decision is the eventual off-site disposal of the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]], which will permanently eliminate the source of potential concern for groundwater impact.
