The North Carpenter Road Overcrossing is a component of the SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA project, specifically related to the remediation of soil stockpiles. The primary documentation for this overcrossing is the [[log-of-test-borings]] which includes a plan view and a cross-sectional profile detailing soil conditions encountered during geotechnical investigations.

## Site Record

The [[log-of-test-borings]] for the North Carpenter Road Overcrossing project, dated February 13, 2019, provides soil classification data from three boreholes: A-17-086, A-17-085, and A-17-084. These boreholes were drilled by Geocon Consultants in 2017. The soil profile extends from approximately -50 feet to 80 feet elevation. The document includes specific soil descriptions, depth markers, and N-values for each borehole.
