## Overview

The North Carpenter Road Overcrossing is a structural component of the [[sr-132]] project in Stanislaus County, California. Its significance within the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] remediation case file stems from its location where geotechnical investigations, including [[log-of-test-borings]], were conducted. These investigations provide subsurface soil data relevant to the remediation efforts. The overcrossing is also referenced in the project's grading profiles, indicating its position relative to the proposed roadway alignment.

## Site Record

Geotechnical investigations for the North Carpenter Road Overcrossing are documented in a [[log-of-test-borings]] dated February 13, 2019. This log details soil classifications from three boreholes: [[a-17-086]], A-17-085, and A-17-084, drilled by Geocon Consultants in 2017. The soil profiles encountered in these borings range from approximately -50 feet to 80 feet elevation.

Grading profiles for Phase 1 of the SR 132 project indicate that the roadway transitions to a below-grade (depressed) profile west of the North Rosemore Avenue Overcrossing and continues in this state past the North Carpenter Road Overcrossing. East of the North Carpenter Road Overcrossing, the profile rises to an elevated position to cross over the North Emerald Avenue Undercrossing.

The Caltrans - DTSC Agreement identifies the "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles - State Route 132/99" as a project under Task Order Number 6 of Consultant Agreement 43A0260 between Caltrans and the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). The term of this Task Order was June 22, 2012, through May 31, 2013.
