## No Trespassing Signs

### Overview
No Trespassing signs were installed at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site as part of site security measures.

### Site Record
The installation of "No Trespassing" signs was noted in the context of perimeter fencing for [[stockpile-1]] and [[stockpile-1]]. Specifically, Caltrans's response dated March 24, 2012, indicated that "No Trespassing" signs were to be installed along with the perimeter fencing.

### Regulatory Status
The record does not specify any regulatory standards or requirements related to the "No Trespassing" signs themselves. Their installation appears to be a site management and security practice.

### Open Items
The record does not indicate any open items related to the "No Trespassing" signs.
