## Overview

Fenceline Borings refer to the 14 soil borings advanced in **Phase 2 (2019–2020)** along the perimeter of the **Modesto Soil Stockpiles** located on **SR 132, Stanislaus County, California**. These borings were installed to define the lateral extent of contamination from the stockpiles and to assess whether contaminants had migrated beyond the site boundary toward adjacent properties and the **Tuolumne River floodplain**. The program was conducted under the **S9525-06-44 Modesto Stockpiles SSI Report Rev. 0313** (the “SSI Report”) and overseen by the **California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)**.

## Site Record

### Installation and Locations
- **Total borings:** 14 (designated FB-1 through FB-14)
- **Depths:** Ranged from **5 to 20 feet below ground surface (bgs)**, depending on refusal or groundwater encounter.
- **Spatial distribution:** Placed at approximately **25- to 50-foot intervals** along the eastern, southern, and western fencelines of the stockpile area. No borings were placed along the northern boundary due to a railroad right-of-way with existing monitoring wells (MW-1, MW-2, MW-3).
- **Installation dates:** Between **March 18 and April 4, 2020** (per SSI Report, Section 3.2).

### Sampling and Analytical Results
- **Total soil samples collected:** 28 (including field duplicates and quality control samples)
- **Analytes:** Metals (antimony, arsenic, barium, beryllium, cadmium, chromium, copper, lead, mercury, nickel, selenium, silver, thallium, vanadium, zinc), polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), and total petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH) as diesel and motor oil.
- **Key maximum concentrations (mg/kg)**:
  - **Barium:** 760 mg/kg at **FB-6 (3–5 ft)** — *exceeded DTSC’s **Direct Exposure Screening Level (6,800 mg/kg)**? No, but exceeded site-specific background threshold of **250 mg/kg**.
  - **Lead:** 120 mg/kg at **FB-8 (0–2 ft)** — below DTSC residential screening level (80 mg/kg) but above commercial/industrial screening level (800 mg/kg) for this site classification.
  - **Arsenic:** 14.2 mg/kg at **FB-3 (0–2 ft)** — exceeded **DTSC’s residential screening level (0.07 mg/kg)** and site background (5.1 mg/kg).
  - **PAHs (benzo[a]pyrene):** 0.05 mg/kg at **FB-10 (0–2 ft)** — exceeded **DTSC human health screening level (0.045 mg/kg)** for cancer risk.
  - **TPH-diesel:** 450 mg/kg at **FB-12 (2–4 ft)** — exceeded **DTSC’s low-risk closure level (100 mg/kg)** but within applicable cleanup goals for the stockpile removal action.

### Comparison to Background and Action Levels
- **Background thresholds** were established from off-site reference samples (SSI Report Table 4-2). For barium, the background mean + 3σ was **250 mg/kg**; FB-6 reported 760 mg/kg, indicating anthropogenic enrichment.
- **DTSC action levels** for soil remediation were based on **Residential Direct Exposure Screening Levels** (per DTSC’s Human Health Risk Assessment Note, 2020). Exceedances were reported for arsenic, benzo[a]pyrene, and TPH-diesel at fenceline borings, prompting delineation of additional stockpile removal phases.

### Verification Results
- Post-removal confirmation sampling in **Phase 2 (March 2020)** used **8 verification borings** along the same fenceline. Results showed all residual concentrations below:
  - **Arsenic:** <5.1 mg/kg (site background)
  - **Lead:** <80 mg/kg (DTSC residential ESL)
  - **Barium:** all <250 mg/kg after removal of 760 mg/kg hotspot.
- Verification report (SSI Report Rev. 0313, Section 5.2) concluded: *“Fenceline borings indicate that the lateral extent of impacted soil has been contained within the stockpile boundaries; no off-site migration of contaminants is evident.”*

## Regulatory Status

- **Oversight agency:** DTSC, under **Caltrans’ Site Mitigation Program** (Contract #43A0362).
- **Applicable standards:**
  - **DTSC Direct Exposure Screening Levels (ESLs)** for residential land use (updated 2020)
  - **Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB)** **Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs)** for groundwater if migration occurs — though no groundwater impact was detected in fenceline borings (max depth 20 ft bgs, no free product).
- **Current compliance:** The fenceline boring data were used to satisfy **DTSC’s Condition of Approval #12** for Phase 2 stockpile removal, which required demonstration that no soil contamination had migrated beyond the stockpile footprint. The condition is considered **closed** per DTSC’s letter dated **April 20, 2020** (attached to SSI Report).
- **Remaining obligation:** Annual monitoring of existing wells (MW-1 through MW-5) for barium and TPH is required through **2025** per RWQCB Order R5-2020-0003.

## Open Items

- **FB-6 barium hotspot:** The 760 mg/kg sample at FB-6 was removed to 5 ft bgs, but **no post-removal background confirmation** was performed at that exact location. DTSC requested a **confirmation sample from FB-6 depth interval 5–7 ft** in a May 2020 memo. This sample has not been collected as of the SSI Report publication (March 2021).
- **Arsenic in FB-3:** The residual arsenic at FB-3 after removal (11.4 mg/kg) remains above site background (5.1 mg/kg) but below DTSC ESL. **No further action** was taken, but the SSI Report notes this as a *“data gap requiring re-evaluation during Phase 3 planning.”* As of the record date, Phase 3 had not been initiated.
