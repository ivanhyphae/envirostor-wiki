## Overview
The clean cap criteria for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, specifically concerning material excavated from the [[carpenter-road-shoofly]], were established to permit the use of this native soil as capping material over [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2. This criteria was approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC).

## Site record
Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil were excavated from the [[carpenter-road-shoofly]]. This material was designated for use as clean capping material over portions of BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2.

Twenty-four discrete soil samples were collected from the [[carpenter-road-shoofly]] along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6). Samples were collected at depths of 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet from the top of the slope. These discrete samples were composited into eight 3-part composite samples.

Analysis for barium and lead was performed using EPA Test Method 6010B. Barium was detected in all 10 analyzed samples at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 analyzed samples at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg.

## Regulatory status
The [[carpenter-road-shoofly]] soil material met the clean cap criteria as established in the referenced [[variance-to-remedial-design-implementation-plan]], approved by DTSC. This criteria included an upper-bound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg. The lead concentrations were less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg.
