The clean cap criteria for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, specifically concerning material excavated from the [[carpenter-road-shoofly]], were established to permit the use of this native soil as capping material over [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2. This criteria was approved by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).

## Site record

Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil were excavated from the [[carpenter-road-shoofly]]. {{quote: "Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil will be excavated from the Carpenter Road Shoofly once the overcrossing structure under construction is completed."}} This material was designated for use as clean capping material over portions of BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "The contractor plans to use the excavated soil material as clean capping material to be placed over portions of barium containing soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2 pending California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) approval."}}

Twenty-four discrete soil samples were collected from the [[carpenter-road-shoofly]] along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6). Samples were collected at depths of 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet from the top of the slope. {{quote: "We collected 24 discrete soil samples from the Carpenter Road Shoofly along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6) as depicted on the attached exhibit. Soil samples were collected from the surface of the soil material exposed on the slope face at 2, 5, 10 and 15 feet from the top of the slope."}} These discrete samples were composited into eight 3-part composite samples. {{quote: "The laboratory was instructed to composite three discrete soil samples from the same elevation along each slope face of the shoofly resulting in eight 3-part composite samples."}}

Analysis for barium and lead was performed using EPA Test Method 6010B. Barium was detected in all 10 analyzed samples at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). {{quote: "Barium was detected in each of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg)"}} Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 analyzed samples at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg"}}

## Regulatory status

The [[carpenter-road-shoofly]] soil material met the clean cap criteria as established in the referenced variance document, approved by DTSC. {{quote: "Based on the barium and lead data presented herein, soil material generated from excavation of the Carpenter Road Shoofly meet the BCS clean cap criteria as established in the referenced variance document as approved by DTSC (upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg)."}} This criteria included an upper-bound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "(upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg)"}} The lead concentrations were less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg."}}
