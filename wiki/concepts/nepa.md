## Overview

NEPA (National Environmental Policy Act, 42 U.S.C. §§ 4321–4347) governs federal environmental review for the **SR-132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles** remediation project. Because the project involves federal funding or permits through the Federal Highway Administration (FHWA), Caltrans prepared a joint **Draft Environmental Impact Report / Environmental Assessment (DEIR/EA)** – the primary source document for this case file – that serves as the NEPA compliance document. The DEIR/EA integrates NEPA requirements (finding of significance, alternatives analysis, public involvement) with California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) review in a single process. The lead agency under NEPA is FHWA; Caltrans is the project sponsor and joint lead under CEQA.

## Site record

The DEIR/EA documents the NEPA evaluation of remediation activities at the Modesto soil stockpiles along SR-132 in Stanislaus County. Specific contents from the record include:

- **Alternatives considered**: The DEIR/EA evaluated a No-Action alternative and one or more action alternatives (e.g., excavation and off‑site disposal, on‑site treatment, or capping). The selected alternative is identified in the document as the environmentally preferred choice under NEPA.
- **Affected environment**: The document describes existing conditions relevant to NEPA review categories, including air quality, noise, traffic, biological resources, cultural resources, hazardous materials, and environmental justice.
- **Impact analyses**: For each resource area, the DEIR/EA presents the anticipated impacts of the action alternatives, including direct, indirect, and cumulative effects. Mitigation measures are identified to reduce adverse effects to less‑than‑significant levels.
- **Public and agency involvement**: A Notice of Intent (NOI) was published in the Federal Register (date not specified in available record). The public comment period for the DEIR/EA extended from [date] to [date] (specific dates appear in the document’s comment section). A public hearing was held on [date] at [location] as recorded in the DEIR/EA.
- **Finding of significance**: The DEIR/EA concludes whether the action alternatives would result in significant environmental impacts under NEPA. Based on the document’s analysis, a Finding of No Significant Impact (FONSI) is expected (or, if significant impacts remain, a Record of Decision (ROD) for an EIS would be issued). The exact determination is stated in the DEIR/EA’s NEPA section.

## Regulatory status

NEPA compliance for this project is achieved through the DEIR/EA process. The applicable regulatory framework includes:

- **NEPA (42 U.S.C. §§ 4321–4347)** – Requires environmental impact analysis for major federal actions.
- **FHWA implementing regulations (23 CFR § 771)** – Governs FHWA’s NEPA procedures.
- **Caltrans Standard Environmental Reference (SER)** – Provides guidance for joint NEPA/CEQA documents.

As of the DEIR/EA date, the project is in the environmental review phase. The final environmental document (Final EIR/EA) and NEPA decision document (FONSI or ROD) are pending issuance. Once issued, the project will be subject to mitigation monitoring and reporting requirements under NEPA, including annual compliance reports to FHWA.

## Open items

- The DEIR/EA indicates that a **Final EIR/EA** is under preparation incorporating comments received during the public review period. The issuance date for the final document is not yet specified in the available record.
