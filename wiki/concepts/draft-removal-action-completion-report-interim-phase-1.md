The Draft Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project (Draft Phase 1 RACR) documents the remediation activities undertaken by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of Caltrans. This report was submitted to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and reviewed in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB). The report details the consolidation and capping of barium-containing soils (BCS) as part of the Interim Phase 1 Construction for the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California.

## Site Description

Prior to remediation, the site contained BCS Stockpiles 1 through 3 within the Caltrans State Route 132 right-of-way.
*   **Stockpile 1:** Located approximately 150 feet west of Emerald Avenue, it measured roughly 600 feet long (east-west), 160 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 34,000 cubic yards (yd³).
*   **Stockpile 2:** Situated between Emerald Avenue and State Route 99, it was approximately 1,650 feet long (east-west) and 160 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 102,000 yd³.
*   **Stockpile 3:** Formerly on the east side of State Route 99, it was approximately 1,100 feet long and 120 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 24,000 yd³.

## Site Remediation Activities – Soil Removal and Consolidation

In accordance with the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and the Interim Phase 1 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP), excavation and consolidation activities were performed.
*   **Stockpile 3 Excavation:** Approximately 27,675 yd³ of soil were excavated from Stockpile 3 and transported by truck to be used as embankment fill within the BCS Stockpile 1 containment zone.
*   **Confirmation Sampling:** Twenty-nine confirmation soil samples were collected from the bottom of the BCS Stockpile 3 excavation area and analyzed for barium and lead.
*   **Exceedance Management:** Three confirmation samples indicated barium concentrations above the cleanup goal of 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). These areas were excavated an additional foot and resampled. Subsequent confirmation samples met the cleanup goals.
*   **Stockpile 2 and 1 Modifications:** Soil was removed from the western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2 and from the southern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 to accommodate the construction of the new State Route 132 Expressway.

## Regulatory Status

On February 24, 2022, the DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley RWQCB, issued an Acceptance Letter for the Draft Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Phase 1 Interim State Route 132 West Project (Site Code: 900259). The acceptance was addressed to Mr. John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager at Geocon Consultants, Inc.
