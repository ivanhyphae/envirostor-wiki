The Draft Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project (Draft Interim RACR) documents the remediation activities undertaken by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] on behalf of [[caltrans]] for the State Route 132 West project in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. This report was submitted to the [[dtsc]] and reviewed in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. The report details the consolidation and capping of [[barium]]-containing soils (BCS) as part of the Interim Phase 1 construction.

## Site Description and Remediation Activities

Prior to remediation, the site contained BCS Stockpiles 1 through 3 within the Caltrans State Route 132 right-of-way. Stockpile 1 was located approximately 150 feet west of Emerald Avenue, measuring roughly 600 feet long (east-west), 160 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 34,000 cubic yards (yd³). Stockpile 2 was situated between Emerald Avenue and State Route 99, approximately 1,650 feet long (east-west) and 160 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 102,000 yd³. Stockpile 3 was formerly on the east side of State Route 99, approximately 1,100 feet long and 120 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 24,000 yd³.

In accordance with the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and the Interim Phase 1 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP), excavation and consolidation activities were performed. Approximately 27,675 yd³ of soil were excavated from Stockpile 3 and transported by truck to be used as embankment fill within the BCS Stockpile 1 containment zone. Twenty-nine confirmation soil samples were collected from the bottom of the BCS Stockpile 3 excavation area and analyzed for barium and lead. Three confirmation samples indicated barium concentrations above the cleanup goal of 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). These areas were excavated an additional foot and resampled, with subsequent confirmation samples meeting the cleanup goals. Soil was also removed from the western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2 and from the southern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 to accommodate the construction of the new State Route 132 Expressway.

## Regulatory Acceptance

On December 23, 2021, Geocon Consultants, Inc. submitted the Draft Interim Removal Action Completion Report to the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). The report summarized the progress of barium-containing soil grading and clean fill capping operations completed to date for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles within Interim Phase I of the State Route 132 West Project. The report noted that remaining work included additional placement of structural pavement section on the upper surface of the capped BCS Stockpiles, erosion control, drainage, and median barrier and signage. A Final Interim RACR was to be submitted following completed construction of Interim Phase I, scheduled for opening to traffic in September 2022.

On February 24, 2022, the DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley RWQCB, issued an Acceptance Letter for the Draft Removal Action Completion Report – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Phase 1 Interim State Route 132 West Project (Site Code: 900259).
