## Technical Memos and Agency Acceptance

This section details the review and acceptance of technical memorandums (Tech Memos) by regulatory agencies regarding soil characterization and its proposed use within the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. The primary agencies involved in these decisions are the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (CVRWQCB), represented by individuals such as Dean Wright (DTSC) and Steven Meeks (CVRWQCB). [[caltrans]] is the project proponent.

### Site Record

Several Tech Memos were submitted by Caltrans for review concerning the use of excavated soils:

*   **Stockpile 1 Wall Footing Sampling Tech Memo (April 6, 2020):** This memo addressed the characterization of soil from the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area for Stockpile 1. DTSC and the CVRWQCB agreed with the memo's findings, with the exception of using this soil as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2. The rationale provided was that the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil exceeded the maximum background of 120 mg/kg, and the excavation area was outside the designated borrow area. Consequently, this material was required to be placed within the fill containment zones of Stockpiles 1 and 2 or used as general bridge embankment fill. DTSC officially accepted the memo on April 7, 2020, with this condition.

*   **Stockpile 2 Wall Footing Tech Memo - Revised Version (June 18, 2020):** This revised memo concerned sampling of Stockpile 2 along the MSE wall footing excavation area. DTSC and the CVRWQCB agreed that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones. Soil below this depth was deemed suitable as embankment fill or capping material and was considered part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request (VR). Therefore, an additional VR was not required for this material. DTSC officially accepted this memo on June 22, 2020. The memo clarified that the 120 mg/kg threshold pertained to [[barium]], not lead.

*   **Characterization of Excavated Bent 2 Soils for use as Clean Cap Material (October 30, 2020):** This Tech Memo addressed the use of Bent 2 excavation soil as clean cap material. The soil sample results did not meet the clean cap criteria established in the April 9, 2020 Variance Request approved by DTSC. However, DTSC and the CVRWQCB agreed that this soil could be placed on top of [[stockpile-1]] at a thickness of 6 inches or less, provided it was covered with clean base material and the roadway asphalt/concrete. This decision aimed to allow efficient use of the Bent 2 soil without jeopardizing the remedy's integrity. DTSC officially accepted this Tech Memo and the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020, on November 6, 2020. This acceptance was contingent on strict adherence to the rainy season surface water sampling effort and erosion control measures outlined in the Revised SWPPP Amendment, to ensure contaminants remained on-site.

### Regulatory Status

The acceptance of these technical memorandums by DTSC and the CVRWQCB signifies regulatory approval for the proposed management and use of specific soil materials within the project, subject to the conditions outlined in the acceptance emails. These conditions often relate to placement within containment zones, specific thickness limitations, and adherence to stormwater management plans. The April 9, 2020 Variance Request established clean cap criteria, which were referenced in subsequent reviews.
