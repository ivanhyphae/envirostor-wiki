# Primary MCL

## Overview
Primary MCL (Maximum Contaminant Level) is a California Department of Public Health (CDPH) drinking water standard applied to groundwater analytical results at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. In the June 2014 groundwater monitoring report prepared by geocon-consultants, Primary MCLs served as the numeric water quality thresholds against which dissolved metals and general minerals results were evaluated. The report assessed whether reported concentrations from ten site monitoring wells exceeded their respective Primary MCLs.

## Site record
The June 2014 groundwater monitoring event used CDPH Primary MCLs as the regulatory benchmark for several analytes. Key findings, organized by constituent, are as follows:

**Nitrate (as nitrogen)** — The Primary MCL for nitrate is 10 mg/l. Reported nitrate concentrations for samples from five wells equaled or exceeded this threshold:

| Well ID | Nitrate as Nitrogen (mg/l) |
|---------|----------------------------|
| MW-1    | 15                         |
| MW-5    | 29                         |
| MW-6    | Not explicitly stated but reported as equal to or exceeding 10 mg/l |
| MW-9    | Not explicitly stated but reported as equal to or exceeding 10 mg/l |
| MW-10   | Not explicitly stated but reported as equal to or exceeding 10 mg/l |

The report notes that MW-1 is an upgradient monitoring well; thus, the reported nitrate of 15 mg/l may be indicative of natural background nitrate concentrations for shallow groundwater in the vicinity of the site.

**Dissolved metals (barium, lead, strontium)** — DTSC identified barium, lead, and strontium as the primary chemicals of concern in groundwater for the site. However, for the June 2014 sampling event, none of the reported concentrations of these metals exceeded their respective Primary MCLs.

**Other dissolved metals** — Arsenic, chromium, and vanadium were reported for each of the ten samples collected. The report explicitly states that although concentrations of arsenic, barium, chromium, strontium, and vanadium were reported, none exceeded their respective numeric water quality thresholds for drinking water (i.e., Primary MCLs, where applicable). Copper, molybdenum, nickel, selenium, and zinc were reported in a subset of samples; none exceeded their respective Primary MCLs. Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, manganese, silver, thallium, and mercury were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective practical quantitation limits (PQLs).

**TDS** — Total dissolved solids exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l in samples from wells MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10. Secondary MCLs address taste and odor, not primary health-based standards.

## Regulatory status
The Primary MCLs referenced in the June 2014 report are those established by the California Department of Public Health for drinking water. With the sole exception of nitrate exceedances at five wells (MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, and MW-10), none of the reported dissolved metals or general minerals concentrations equaled or exceeded their respective California Primary MCLs. The report concludes that for dissolved metals specifically, no exceedances of Primary MCLs were identified during the June 2014 monitoring event.
