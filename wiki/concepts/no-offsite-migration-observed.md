Two rounds of stormwater sampling at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site — collected January 6, 2016 and March 5, 2016 by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] — form the basis for the finding that stockpile-related constituents were not migrating off the site by way of stormwater runoff. This finding matters because it supports treating the stockpiles as manageable in place through stormwater controls rather than as an active offsite contamination pathway requiring further response.

The two sampling events were conducted at the site under wet-weather conditions to evaluate whether runoff leaving the stockpile area was carrying contaminants above applicable levels. Both rounds are documented in reports titled "Modesto Stockpiles Jan 6 2016 Stormwater Sampling" and "Modesto Stockpiles Mar 5 2016 Stormwater Sampling," the latter dated April 2016.

The available material does not include the specific analytical results or concentrations from either sampling event. Without those values in the record, the conclusion of no offsite migration rests on the existence and timing of the two sampling rounds rather than on stated data that can be compared to a threshold here. Whether the sampling covered the same constituents in both rounds, or whether it was required as a condition of some other approval, is not stated in the material available for this article.
