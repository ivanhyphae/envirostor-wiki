The claim of "No Offsite Migration Observed" at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site signifies that monitoring data has not shown evidence of contaminants moving beyond the designated project boundaries. This is a critical finding for assessing the effectiveness of containment and remediation strategies.

### Stormwater Monitoring Results

Stormwater sampling conducted on March 5, 2016, did not indicate offsite migration of contaminants. The sampling was performed as part of ongoing monitoring efforts for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project.

### Thallium Exceedances in Stormwater

While the March 5, 2016, stormwater sampling did not show offsite migration, other stormwater sampling events have identified exceedances of the California Primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for thallium. For example, a stormwater sample collected on an unspecified date, as noted in the "Thallium Exceeds MCL in Stormwater" capture, showed thallium concentrations above the MCL. This highlights the importance of continued stormwater monitoring to ensure containment.

### Groundwater Monitoring

Groundwater monitoring reports, such as the "Modesto Stockpiles July 2012 GW Report," have also been reviewed for evidence of offsite migration. These reports contribute to the overall assessment of contaminant containment.
