## Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Footing Soil

### Overview
Soil excavated from the upper portion of the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing along the southern boundary of modesto-soil-stockpiles-project|Stockpile 1 was evaluated for its suitability as [[clean-cover-embankment-fill]]. Due to elevated [[barium]] concentrations, the uppermost foot of this soil was deemed unsuitable as clean fill and was designated for placement within Stockpile 1 and 2 permanent fill areas. Soil from 1 to 5 feet below existing grade in this area, exhibiting barium concentrations within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background, was approved for use as [[clean-cap]] soil.

### Site Record
The remedial-design-implementation-plan|RDIP Variance Request Acceptance E-Mail, dated April 17, 2020, indicates that the upper foot of planned MSE wall footing excavation soil along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1 would not be suitable as clean fill due to elevated barium concentrations. This material was to be placed in Stockpile 1 and 2 permanent fill areas.

The soil extending from 1 to 5 feet from existing grade in this area contained barium concentrations with a calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) of 113.1 mg/kg. This concentration was determined to be within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background.

The RDIP Variance Request itself was dated April 9, 2020. [[caltrans]]' expectation, as stated in a letter dated April 13, 2020, was that if approved, the soil excavated for the Stockpile 1 MSE footing would only be used as a source of clean cover or embankment fill within the footprints of the stockpiles and would not be used at other project areas or hauled off-site.

A Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020, from Geocon-Consultants described sampling along the MSE wall footing excavation area and laboratory results. The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board reviewed this memo. The 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil was noted as being above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg.

### Regulatory Status
The RDIP Variance Request Acceptance E-Mail indicates that both the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board agreed that the soil from 1 to 5 feet below existing grade along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1 could be used as clean cap soil on the project. This decision was based on the barium concentrations being within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background. The RDIP Variance Request Acceptance E-Mail serves as the DTSC's official acceptance of the variance request for the soil along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1. The acceptance was communicated via an email dated April 17, 2020.

However, the DTSC and Water Board also agreed with the last paragraph of the Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020, with the exception of using the MSE wall footing soil as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material. Given that the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil is above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg and the fact that the MSE wall footing falls outside of the designated borrow area described in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, this material must be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill.

### Open Items
The official approval or rejection of soil from the MSE wall footing excavation along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2 was pending review of submitted data.
