## Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Footing Soil

### Overview
Soil excavated from the upper portion of the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing along the southern boundary of modesto-soil-stockpiles-project|Stockpile 1 was evaluated for its suitability as [[clean-cover-embankment-fill]]. Due to elevated barium concentrations, the uppermost foot of this soil was deemed unsuitable as clean fill and was designated for placement within Stockpile 1 and 2 permanent fill areas. However, soil from 1 to 5 feet below existing grade in this area, exhibiting barium concentrations within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background, was approved for use as clean cap soil.

### Site Record
The soil extending from 1 to 5 feet from existing grade along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1 contained barium concentrations with a calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) of 113.1 mg/kg {{quote: "the soil extending from 1 to 5 feet from existing grade in this area contained barium concentrations with a calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) of 113.1 mg/kg"}}. This concentration was determined to be within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background {{quote: "which is within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background"}}.

The RDIP Variance Request Acceptance E-Mail dated April 17, 2020, indicates that the upper foot of planned MSE wall footing excavation soil along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1 would not be suitable as clean fill due to elevated barium concentrations {{quote: "the VR indicates that the upper foot of planned mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation soil along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1 would not be suitable as clean fill due to elevated barium concentrations"}}. This material was to be placed in Stockpile 1 and 2 permanent fill areas {{quote: "This material will thus be placed in Stockpile 1 and 2 permanent fill areas"}}.

The RDIP Variance Request Acceptance E-Mail serves as the Department of Toxic Substances Control's (DTSC) official acceptance of the variance request for the soil along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1 {{quote: "This e-mail serves as DTSCs official acceptance of the VR for the soil along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1"}}. The acceptance was communicated via an email dated April 17, 2020 {{quote: "Friday, April 17, 2020 3:18:00 PM"}}.

The RDIP Variance Request itself was dated April 9, 2020 {{quote: "RDIP Variance Request to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (VR) dated April 9, 2020"}}. Caltrans' expectation, as stated in a letter dated April 13, 2020, was that if approved, the soil excavated for the Stockpile 1 MSE footing would only be used as a source of clean cover or embankment fill within the footprints of the stockpiles and would not be used at other project areas or hauled off-site {{quote: "It is our expectation that if approved by the DTSC and the Regional Board, Bay Cities will only use soil excavated for construction of the Stockpile 1 MSE footing as a source of clean cover or embankment fill within the footprints of the stockpiles and that the soil will not be used at other areas of the project or hauled off-site."}}.

### Regulatory Status
The RDIP Variance Request Acceptance E-Mail indicates that both the DTSC and the Water Board agreed that the soil from 1 to 5 feet below existing grade along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1 could be used as clean cap soil on the project {{quote: "Both agencies agree that this material can therefore be used as clean cap soil on the project"}}. This decision was based on the barium concentrations being within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background {{quote: "which is within the range of site-specific naturally occurring background"}}.

### Open Items
The approval or rejection of soil from the MSE wall footing excavation along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2 was pending review of submitted data {{quote: "The official approval or rejection of the soil along the southern boundary of Stockpile 2 will be provided once that data has been submitted and reviewed."}}.
