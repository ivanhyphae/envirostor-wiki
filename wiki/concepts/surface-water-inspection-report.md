## Surface Water Inspection Report

### Overview
The Surface Water Inspection Report is a deliverable documenting findings from inspections of surface water bodies relevant to the SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA project. These inspections are part of the ongoing monitoring and assessment of the site's environmental conditions, specifically related to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file (06A2542ct_to97).

### Site Record
A Surface Water Inspection Report was issued on March 9, 2023. This report is associated with the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file, identified as 06A2542ct_to97, under Interagency Agreement Task Order No. 97. The report was prepared by Stantec Consulting Services Inc. The findings within this report contribute to the understanding of site conditions, particularly in relation to the observation that no offsite migration was observed.
