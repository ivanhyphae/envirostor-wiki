## Chromium Results Exceed 95 Percent UCL

### Overview
This section details chromium concentrations found in soil samples from the Basin 5 borrow area, which were evaluated for their suitability as clean cover material for the State Route 132 stockpiles. The regulatory agencies, DTSC and the Water Board, reviewed these results to determine if they were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations.

### Site Record
The **Revised Technical Memorandum** dated July 2, 2020, addressed concerns regarding chromium concentrations in the Basin 5 area. Additional information provided via email and attachments on June 29, 2020, and summarized in a footnote within the Revised Memo, was used to evaluate these chromium results. The composite soil sample results from the Basin 5 area were deemed consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations.

### Regulatory Status
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board reviewed the chromium concentrations. DTSC, through an email dated July 9, 2020, accepted the Revised Technical Memorandum, stating that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and suitable for use as clean cover material for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2.
