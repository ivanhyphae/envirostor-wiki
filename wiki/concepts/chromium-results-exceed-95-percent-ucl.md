## Chromium Results Exceed 95 Percent UCL

### Overview
This section addresses concerns regarding chromium concentrations in soil samples from the [[basin-5]] borrow area. These results were evaluated to determine if the soil was suitable for use as clean cover material for the State Route 132 stockpiles, specifically in relation to naturally occurring background concentrations.

### Site Record
Concerns about chromium concentrations in the Basin 5 area were addressed in the **Revised Technical Memorandum** dated July 2, 2020. Additional information, provided via email and attachments on June 29, 2020, and summarized in a footnote within the Revised Memo, was used in this evaluation. The composite soil sample results from the Basin 5 area were determined to be consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations.

### Regulatory Status
The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] reviewed the chromium concentrations. DTSC, in an email dated July 9, 2020, accepted the Revised Technical Memorandum. The agencies agreed that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and therefore suitable for use as clean cover material for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2.
