## Stockpile 2 MSE Wall Footing Excavation Characterization Testing

### Overview
This section details the soil characterization testing performed in the excavation for the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing along the southerly slope of [[stockpile-2]]. This testing was conducted after the removal of barium-containing soil (BCS) to support the construction of the MSE wall. The excavated BCS material was relocated to the designated [[stockpile-2]] fill containment zone.

### Site Record
Soil samples were collected from trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7, spaced approximately 200 feet apart, at the midpoint of the planned MSE wall footing excavation. Samples were taken at one-foot intervals to a maximum depth of 3 and 5 feet. A total of 29 soil samples were analyzed for barium and lead using EPA Test Method 6010B.

Barium was detected in all 29 samples, with concentrations ranging from 53 to 930 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). Fourteen samples exceeded the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg. Of these, thirteen were collected from depths between the surface and 3 feet. One sample, STK2-7-4, had a barium concentration of 140 mg/kg but was collected deeper than the planned 3-foot footing excavation depth at that location.

Lead was detected in all samples, with concentrations ranging from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg. Most lead concentrations were within the same order of magnitude as the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg.

Based on these results, soil excavated from the upper three feet of the Stockpile 2 southerly slope MSE wall footing requires placement within the designated Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones. Material excavated from depths greater than 3 feet is suitable for placement as general embankment fill or as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2, pending regulatory approval. This data will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report.

The testing was performed following a *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan* dated April 9, 2020. The Technical Memorandum summarizing these findings is dated June 18, 2020, and was prepared by Geocon Consultants Inc. for WSP/City of Modesto.

### Regulatory Status
The site-specific background value for barium is 120 mg/kg. The site-specific background concentration for lead is 3.8 mg/kg.

### Open Items
This information will be incorporated into the Removal Action Completion Report following the completion of capping activities for Stockpile 1 and 2.
