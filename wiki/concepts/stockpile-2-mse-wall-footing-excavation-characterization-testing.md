The Stockpile 2 MSE Wall Footing Excavation Characterization Testing involved the analysis of soil samples from the excavation area for the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing along the southern slope of [[stockpile-2]]. This testing was performed to guide the management of excavated soil, particularly material previously identified as barium-containing soil (BCS).

### Excavation and Characterization

Soil samples were collected from trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7, positioned approximately 200 feet apart at the midpoint of the planned MSE wall footing excavation. Sampling occurred at one-foot intervals to a maximum depth of 3 and 5 feet. A total of 29 soil samples were analyzed for barium and lead using EPA Test Method 6010B.

Barium was detected in all 29 samples, with concentrations ranging from 53 to 930 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). Fourteen samples exceeded the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg. Of these, thirteen were collected from depths between the surface and 3 feet. One sample, STK2-7-4, exhibited a barium concentration of 140 mg/kg but was collected deeper than the planned 3-foot footing excavation depth at that location.

Lead was detected in all samples, with concentrations ranging from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg. Most lead concentrations were within the same order of magnitude as the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg.

### Regulatory Determination and Acceptance

Based on these analytical results, soil excavated from the upper three feet of the Stockpile 2 southerly slope MSE wall footing requires placement within the designated fill containment zones of Stockpiles 1 and 2. Material excavated from depths greater than 3 feet is considered suitable for placement as general embankment fill or as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2, pending regulatory approval.

The DTSC and the Waterboards reviewed the Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020, prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] for [[wsp]]/City of Modesto, which detailed these sampling results. By email dated June 22, 2020, DTSC provided official acceptance of the memorandum. DTSC confirmed that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones, while soil below that depth is suitable as embankment fill or capping material. This determination was considered part of the April 9, 2020, Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, meaning a separate VR was not required for the utilization of soil below 3 feet bgs.

### Site Background Values

*   Maximum site-specific background value for barium: 120 mg/kg
*   Maximum site-specific background concentration for lead: 3.8 mg/kg

### Open Items

The findings from this characterization testing will be incorporated into the Removal Action Completion Report following the completion of capping activities for Stockpile 1 and 2.
