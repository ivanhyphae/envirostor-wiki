## North Carpenter Road

### Overview
North Carpenter Road is a geographic reference point within the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project, specifically identified in the context of the "Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan." It is depicted on an engineering site plan related to traffic handling and soil sampling activities for the project.

### Site Record
North Carpenter Road is shown on the engineering site plan titled 'Traffic Handling Plan Stage 2B' for the 'SR 132 Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Source Testing Sampling Plan'. This plan details construction notes, signage placement, and geometric survey data, including excavation parameters of "20' depth, 5,000 cubic yards." The plan specifies technical details for soil sampling, including vertical transect sampling depths at 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet. The document is identified by the DGN file name "...\\Plans\\a40350md016.dgn" and the username "ndonnelly".
