## North Carpenter Road

### Overview
North Carpenter Road is a geographic reference point within the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project, specifically identified in the context of the "Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan." It is depicted on an engineering site plan related to traffic handling and soil sampling activities for the project.

### Site Record
North Carpenter Road is shown on the engineering site plan titled 'Traffic Handling Plan Stage 2B' for the 'SR 132 Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Source Testing Sampling Plan'. {{quote: "The image is an engineering site plan titled 'Traffic Handling Plan Stage 2B' for the 'SR 132 Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Source Testing Sampling Plan'. The document includes a detailed map layout indicating North Carpenter Road, Kansas Avenue, and surrounding sections."}} This plan details construction notes, signage placement, and geometric survey data, including excavation parameters of "20' depth, 5,000 cubic yards." {{quote: "It features construction notes, signage placement details like 'Bus Stop Sign', 'Barricade (Type III)', and 'Temp Railing', along with geometric survey data such as station points and excavation parameters (20' depth, 5,000 cubic yards)."}} The plan specifies technical details for soil sampling, including vertical transect sampling depths at 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet. {{quote: "Technical specifications for soil sampling are listed on the left, detailing vertical transect sampling depths at 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet."}} The document is identified by the DGN file name "...\\Plans\\a40350md016.dgn" and the username "ndonnelly". {{quote: "USERNAME ndonnelly DGN FILE ...\\Plans\\a40350md016.dgn"}}
