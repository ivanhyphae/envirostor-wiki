## Overview
The Errata to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] was issued by [[caltrans]] to update the January 30, 2019 RDIP. This errata specifically addresses changes in project roles and responsibilities following the award of the construction contract for the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]]. The primary change involves the removal of Geocon Consultants, Inc. as a designated consultant and clarifies that [[caltrans]] retains ultimate responsibility for ensuring project work aligns with the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and RDIP. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) accepted this errata.

## Site Record
The "Errata to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan – Interim Phase 1 January 30th, 2019" was prepared by [[caltrans]] and modifies Sections 2.0, "Project Organization," and 2.1, "Project Team Responsibilities," of the original RDIP.

Key entities and their roles as identified in the errata include:
*   **Site Owner/Responsible Party:** State of California/Caltrans
*   **Regulatory Agencies:** DTSC and [[central-valley-rwqcb]]
*   **SR-132 West Project Proponent:** City of Modesto (City)/Stanislaus Council of Governments
*   **Project Proponent's Civil Engineer:** Dokken Engineering
*   **Project Proponent's Construction Manager:** WSP, USA
*   **Contractor:** Bay Cities Paving and Grading, Inc.

[[caltrans]] is designated with the responsibility to verify that highway construction activities impacting the soil stockpiles conform to the RAP and RDIP requirements. This includes:
*   Supervising the abandonment of site-related groundwater monitoring wells affected by construction.
*   Observing and documenting excavation of barium-containing soil, fill placement, and capping activities.
*   Conducting verification soil sampling and analysis for the removal of barium-containing soil.
*   Performing sampling and analysis of proposed clean soil import sources.
*   Conducting worker zone and perimeter air monitoring.
*   Performing periodic stormwater monitoring, including observations and sampling.
*   Reviewing RDIP requirements with the construction manager and contractor.
*   Communicating with and updating the Caltrans Environmental Manager and the DTSC and [[central-valley-rwqcb]] Project Managers.

## Regulatory Status
The original RDIP was approved by the DTSC on April 3, 2019. The Errata to the RDIP was accepted by the DTSC on February 5, 2020. This acceptance was communicated via email by Dean Wright, P.G., Project Manager for the Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit at DTSC, to Juergen Vespermann of Caltrans and Perry Myers of DTSC. {{quote: "This e‐mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the RDIP Errata sheet and transmittal letter."}}
