The Errata to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] addresses changes to roles and responsibilities following the construction contract award for the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]].

## Overview
This errata document updates the January 30, 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. The primary purpose of the errata is to reflect changes in identified roles and responsibilities, specifically removing Geocon Consultants, Inc. as the designated consultant and clarifying Caltrans' ultimate responsibility for ensuring the work aligns with the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and RDIP. The errata was accepted by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).

## Site Record
The "Errata to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan – Interim Phase 1 January 30th, 2019" was prepared by Caltrans. It updates Section 2.0, "Project Organization," and Section 2.1, "Project Team Responsibilities."

Key project organizations and representatives identified in the errata include:
*   **Site Owner/Responsible Party:** State of California/Caltrans
*   **Regulatory Agencies:** DTSC and CVRWQCB
*   **SR-132 West Project Proponent:** City of Modesto (City)/Stanislaus Council of Governments
*   **Project Proponent's Civil Engineer:** Dokken Engineering
*   **Project Proponent's Construction Manager:** WSP, USA
*   **Contractor:** Bay Cities Paving and Grading, Inc.

Caltrans is responsible for verifying that highway construction work associated with the soil stockpiles conforms to the RAP and RDIP requirements. This includes:
*   Supervising the abandonment of site-related groundwater monitoring wells impacted by construction.
*   Observing and documenting barium-containing soil excavation, fill placement, and capping activities.
*   Performing verification soil sampling and analysis for barium-containing soil removal.
*   Performing sampling and analysis of proposed clean soil import sources.
*   Performing worker zone and perimeter air monitoring.
*   Performing periodic stormwater monitoring, including observations and sampling.
*   Reviewing RDIP requirements with the construction manager and contractor.
*   Interacting with and updating the Caltrans Environmental Manager and DTSC and CVRWQCB Project Managers.

## Regulatory Status
The original RDIP was approved by the DTSC on April 3, 2019. The Errata to the RDIP was accepted by the DTSC on February 5, 2020. Dean Wright, P.G., Project Manager for the Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit at DTSC, communicated this acceptance via email to Juergen Vespermann of Caltrans and Perry Myers of DTSC. {{quote: "This e‐mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the RDIP Errata sheet and transmittal letter."}}
