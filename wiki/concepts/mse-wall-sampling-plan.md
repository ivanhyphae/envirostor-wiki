The MSE Wall Sampling Plan governs the sampling of soil in Stockpiles 1 and 2 to determine whether that material could be reused as backfill for MSE (mechanically stabilized earth) wall construction on SR 132. WSP prepared and submitted the plan, and DTSC's acceptance of it, communicated by e-mail, is the record on which this article is based.

## Acceptance and scope

DTSC's e-mail accepted the Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan submitted by WSP. The acceptance covers sampling of soil from both stockpiles for the purpose of characterizing it in connection with its potential use in MSE wall construction. The e-mail itself is the operative document establishing that the plan, as submitted, was acceptable to DTSC; the available material does not include the sampling plan's own text, so the specific sampling density, analyte list, or acceptance criteria proposed by WSP are not part of this record.

## What the record does not show

The acceptance e-mail records DTSC's sign-off on the plan but not the results of any sampling conducted under it, and not any subsequent determination of whether Stockpile 1 or Stockpile 2 material was in fact approved for use as MSE wall backfill. Whether the plan was carried out as accepted, and what it found, is not established by this document.
