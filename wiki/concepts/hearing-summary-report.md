The Hearing Summary Report for the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file documents the proceedings of a public hearing held on June 21, 2017, concerning the proposed remediation of soil stockpiles at the SR 132 site in Stanislaus County, California. This report serves as a record of the testimony and evidence presented, informing subsequent regulatory decisions by the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and [[dtsc]].

### Public Hearing Proceedings

The public hearing on June 21, 2017, addressed the proposed remediation of soil stockpiles at the SR 132 site. Testimony was provided by representatives of [[caltrans]], the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], and the [[dtsc]]. The hearing focused on the proposed [[remedial-action-plan]] and its implications for environmental protection and public health.

### Key Testimony and Concerns

During the hearing, [[caltrans]] presented its proposed remediation strategy, emphasizing its commitment to addressing the identified soil contamination. Representatives from the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and [[dtsc]] provided their perspectives on the proposed plan, including technical review and regulatory oversight. Public comments and concerns were also heard, focusing on the long-term effectiveness of the proposed actions and potential environmental impacts.

### Regulatory Review and Next Steps

The Hearing Summary Report captures the discussions and feedback received during the public hearing. This information was intended to inform the final decision-making process by the regulatory agencies regarding the approval and implementation of the [[remedial-action-plan]]. The report details the various viewpoints expressed and the issues raised by stakeholders.
