The DTSC Service Request Manager is an administrative point of contact within the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. This role is primarily associated with the administrative processing and tracking of project-related requests and documentation.

### DTSC Service Request Manager Actions

The DTSC Service Request Manager is identified in correspondence regarding the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. Specifically, the manager is listed as the recipient of a "Request for Approval of Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Addendum" dated February 13, 2015, submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] on behalf of [[caltrans]]. The SWPPP Addendum was prepared in response to DTSC comments on the original SWPPP.
