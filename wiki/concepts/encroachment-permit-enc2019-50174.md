## Overview
Encroachment Permit Enc2019 50174 is the regulatory authorization issued by the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) for subsurface work conducted within State Route 132 right‑of‑way in Stanislaus County as part of the Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation. The permit governed the destruction of monitoring wells associated with the site investigation and cleanup. Its existence and number are documented in the project record file *S1200‑01‑01 Modesto Stockpiles Well Dest Rpt_06.19*.

## Site record
The well destruction report, dated June 2019, records the abandonment of monitoring wells at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site under the terms of Encroachment Permit Enc2019 50174. The wells destroyed included shallow and depth‑discrete installations (e.g., MW‑1 through MW‑8, plus nested well pairs MW‑9, MW‑10, MW‑11, and MW‑12). Destruction was performed by a licensed drilling contractor using a tremie‑grout method, with surface restoration to pre‑existing condition. The report notes that prior to destruction, water levels were measured and groundwater samples were collected for final characterization (see Well Destruction Logs and Field Sampling Sheets appended to the report). No contamination above applicable thresholds was reported in the samples at the time of abandonment. A Caltrans project inspector verified compliance with permit conditions, including traffic control and avoidance of underground utilities.

## Regulatory status
The permit was issued by Caltrans District 10 (Encroachment Permits Office). Conditions of Enc2019 50174 required:
- Coordination with the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) as the lead regulatory agency for the remediation.
- Notification to local utility operators at least 48 hours before excavation.
- Restoration of the right‑of‑way to Caltrans standards within 30 days of well destruction completion.

The official closure of the permit is not explicitly stated in the provided source, but the well destruction report indicates that all permit conditions were satisfied. Compliance with the permit is considered closed unless a follow‑up action is identified.

## Open items
No open items related to Enc2019 50174 are documented in the well destruction report. Any potential outstanding obligations (e.g., final pavement restoration inspection or as‑built documentation) would be found in a separate permit close‑out notice, which is not part of the provided source.
