[[dtsc]] approved the Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan on April 7, 2021, in an e-mail from [[dean-wright]] to Caltrans' Richard Stewart, with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board copied. The Plan governed sampling and analysis of soil at the Carpenter Shoofly location proposed for use as clean fill on the SR 132 project, and its approval cleared that soil for use as cover material without further conditions noted in the record.

## Approval

Caltrans submitted the revised Plan to DTSC on April 5, 2021. [[dean-wright]], DTSC's project manager, responded two days later stating that DTSC and the Regional Water Board had reviewed the revised Plan and found it "consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts," with no comments. The e-mail itself, sent April 7, 2021, constitutes DTSC's approval — no separate approval letter or attached conditions are referenced in the record.

The approval was addressed jointly by DTSC and [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (via reviewer Kyle Cockerham, copied on both messages), meaning the Plan cleared both agencies with responsibility for soil reuse and water quality on the project without requiring revision.

## Significance for clean fill use

Because the Plan was found consistent with prior borrow area sampling protocols rather than requiring a new or heightened level of scrutiny, its approval supports treating Carpenter Shoofly soil the same as other previously approved borrow sources for use as [[clean-fill-suitable-as-cover]] in the project's [[clean-fill-capping]] work. The record does not include the underlying Plan document, sample results, or analyte list, so the specific constituents tested (including whether [[barium]] was among them) and their concentrations are not established by this source.
