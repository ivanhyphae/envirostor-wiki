The DTSC Approval Carpenter Shoofly Sampling Plan signifies the regulatory approval of the sampling and analysis plan for soil intended for use as clean fill at the [[carpenter-road-shoofly-area]]. This approval is a critical step in the remediation process for the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles case file.

### DTSC Approval of Sampling Plan

On April 7, 2021, Dean Wright, PG, DTSC Project Manager, communicated DTSC's approval of the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan. This plan, received by DTSC on April 5, 2021, details the proposed sampling and analysis for soil at the location that will be utilized as clean fill. Wright stated that the Plan is consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and that DTSC had no comments. The email from Wright to Richard Stewart of [[caltrans]] served as DTSC's official approval of the Plan. Perry Myers of DTSC and Kyle Cockerham of the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] were copied on the approval email.
