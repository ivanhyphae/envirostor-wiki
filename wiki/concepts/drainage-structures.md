## Drainage Structures

### Overview
Drainage structures at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site are integral to managing surface water runoff and preventing erosion. These structures are documented in the [[annual-operations-maintenance-site-inspection-form]] and include components like pipes, ditches, and riprap. Their proper functioning is essential for maintaining the integrity of the stockpiles and surrounding areas.
