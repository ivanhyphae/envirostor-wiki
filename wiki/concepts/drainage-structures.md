## Drainage Structures

### Overview
Drainage structures at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site are critical for managing surface water runoff and mitigating erosion. These structures are documented in the [[annual-operations-maintenance-site-inspection-form]] and include components such as pipes, ditches, and riprap. Their functionality is essential for maintaining the integrity of the stockpiles and surrounding areas.

### Site Record
On the north slope of Stockpile #2, a corrugated metal drainage pipe extends down the slope and discharges near riprap at the base.

Also on the north slope of Stockpile #2, a narrow drainage ditch or small stream flows below the embankment.

On the north slope of Stockpile #1, a drainage ditch runs parallel to a concrete block wall.

### Regulatory Status
The maintenance and functionality of drainage structures are implicitly addressed within the overall site management and operations as documented in the [[annual-operations-maintenance-site-inspection-form]]. The provided source material does not detail specific regulatory standards for the drainage structures themselves.
