William Martinez is identified as a contact person for the "2025 GW Monitoring Report" in correspondence related to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file.

## Site Record

In a letter dated January 30, 2026, from the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) to Caltrans, William Martinez is listed as a contact for the "2025 GW Monitoring Report." This report, along with the Well Destruction Report, was submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of Caltrans. The site, located south of the intersection of State Route 99 and Kansas Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California, is referred to as the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles and also as the State Route 132 West Expressway. The stockpiles contain soil contaminated with barium since the 1960s. Groundwater monitoring wells were installed in 2012 to assess potential contamination migration. DTSC and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board reviewed groundwater monitoring reports and, based on data indicating no metals leaching into the groundwater, agreed to the decommissioning of the wells. Six wells were decommissioned in 2019 to facilitate highway construction. The Well Destruction Report details the procedures for destroying the remaining four wells. Additionally, surface water runoff is sampled to evaluate the containment system's integrity during rain events, with the Storm Water Sampling Report indicating results within reporting limits.

## Regulatory Status

The DTSC, through its Geological Services Branch (GSB), conditionally approved the Well Destruction Report. This approval is contingent upon the incorporation of a specific comment into the final version of the report. The Storm Water Sampling Report was reviewed by geologists from the GSB and toxicologists from the Human and Ecological Risk Office (HERO) and has been approved.
