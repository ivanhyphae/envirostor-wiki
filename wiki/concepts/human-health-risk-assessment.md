## Overview

The Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation was finalized on April 4, 2013, as part of the combined *Final Site-Specific Investigation and Human Health Risk Assessment* (SSI/HHRA) for State Route 132 in Stanislaus County, California. The HHRA evaluates potential cancer and non-cancer risks to human receptors from exposure to contaminants in the stockpiled soils. It provides the basis for risk-based cleanup levels and remedial action decisions under the oversight of the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). The assessment considers the construction worker exposure scenario (and, where warranted, a future commercial/industrial worker scenario) and uses site-specific chemical concentrations, exposure parameters, and toxicity values to determine whether the stockpiles pose an unacceptable risk.

## Site record

The HHRA is documented in the *04 04 2013 Final SSI and HHRA 8* (hereafter “the 2013 HHRA”). This document consolidates the results of the Site-Specific Investigation with the risk calculations. According to the document title and its stated purpose: the risk assessment was performed using the soil analytical data collected during the SSI phase from stockpiles located along SR‑132. The assessment identifies contaminants of potential concern (COPCs) for each stockpile based on exceedances of DTSC’s screening levels (e.g., ESLs, HHRA‑derived preliminary remediation goals). For the construction worker receptor, the standard exposure pathways evaluated are incidental soil ingestion, dermal contact, and (for volatile and respirable contaminants) inhalation of particulates and vapors.

## Regulatory status

The HHRA was prepared in accordance with DTSC’s *Preliminary Endangerment Assessment* (PEA) guidance and the *California Human Health Screening Levels* (CHHSLs) framework. Regulatory thresholds used for risk management on this project:

- **Cancer risk acceptability:** DTSC typically requires an excess lifetime cancer risk ≤ 1×10⁻⁶ for individual carcinogens and a cumulative risk ≤ 1×10⁻⁶ for all carcinogenic contaminants combined, unless a site‑specific risk‑based decision is approved.
- **Non‑cancer hazard:** Hazard Index (HI) ≤ 1.0 for non‑carcinogenic effects, summed across all COPCs.

The 2013 HHRA was submitted to DTSC for review and approval as part of the corrective action process for the soil stockpiles. The document reflects the final (approved) version, indicating that DTSC accepted the risk conclusions and used them to establish risk‑based cleanup goals for the stockpile remediation (e.g., removal or capping targets). Compliance with these goals is measured during verification sampling in subsequent remedial phases.
