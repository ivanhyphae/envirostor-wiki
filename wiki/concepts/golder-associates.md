## Overview

Golder Associates is the environmental consulting firm that prepared the **Carpenter Road Shoofly Clean Fill Tech Memo**, a project record document for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). In this project, Golder’s role was to evaluate the suitability of soil stockpiles for use as clean fill during the construction of the Carpenter Road shoofly (temporary bypass road). The memo documents the analytical data, comparison against regulatory thresholds, and recommendations regarding the stockpile material.
