## Weather Station Foundation And Overhead Sign 1 Installation

### Overview
The Weather Station Foundation and Overhead Sign 1 Installation refers to a specific location within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). This location is relevant to the assessment and management of soil stockpiles, particularly concerning the presence of barium and lead.

### Site Record
The "Approval Letter Stockpile 2 Spoils Workplan" (dated 900259) addresses the Weather Station Foundation and Overhead Sign 1 Installation in relation to barium-containing soil. Specifically, the document indicates that sampling results for barium and lead at this installation were below site-specific background concentrations. This finding is detailed in the "Technical Memorandum for Stockpile 2 Barium Containing Soil Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan."

### Regulatory Status
Sampling results for barium and lead associated with the Weather Station Foundation and Overhead Sign 1 Installation were found to be below site-specific background concentrations. This indicates compliance with established thresholds for these analytes at this specific location.
