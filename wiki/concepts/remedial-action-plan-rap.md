## Remedial Action Plan Rap

The Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles outlines the strategy for mitigating exposure to barium-containing soil (BCS). The primary remedial alternative selected was consolidation and containment of the BCS by incorporating it into highway embankment fill, followed by covering with clean fill and structural pavement. This approach was detailed in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan-rdip]] (RDIP) and implemented during Phase 1 construction of the [[caltrans]] State Route (SR) 132 West Project. A Final Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) will be submitted after Phase 2 construction is complete in 2028.

### Site Record

The RAP's implementation involved the consolidation of BCS Stockpiles 1 through 3 into designated BCS Containment Zones within the SR 132 highway embankment. This process occurred during Interim Phase 1 construction of the SR 132 West Project. Excavated BCS was sourced from Stockpile 3, bridge abutment excavations at Stockpile 2, and Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) retaining wall foundation excavations along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2. Additionally, shallow soil with hazardous waste concentrations of aerially deposited lead (ADL) was placed within these containment zones.

Verification soil sampling was conducted to ensure compliance with specified thresholds. For BCS removal areas, the thresholds were:
*   Barium: 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) for groundwater protection.
*   Lead: 80 mg/kg, based on the [[dtsc]] residential screening level.

During the excavation of the western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2 (February-March 2020), initial verification samples showed elevated barium concentrations. After deepening the excavation areas, final verification samples from these locations contained barium ranging from 54 to 760 mg/kg and lead from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg, meeting the RDIP verification thresholds.

Excavation of MSE wall footing areas along the southern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 (March-April 2020) also involved soil sampling. Barium concentrations in these samples ranged from 42 to 420 mg/kg, exceeding the site-specific background concentration of 120 mg/kg but remaining below the RDIP removal threshold of 1,000 mg/kg. Lead concentrations were generally within the range of the site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg and below the RDIP removal threshold of 80 mg/kg. Consequently, all excavated MSE wall footing material was placed in the BCS Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones.

Air monitoring was conducted between February 2020 and June 2020 during BCS grading activities. Results indicated that airborne levels of total dust, barium, and lead were below designated project action and risk levels, confirming the effectiveness of dust control measures.

### Regulatory Status

The RAP was developed under the oversight of the [[dtsc]], in cooperation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. The remedial alternative selected was consolidation and containment of BCS within highway embankment fills. Specific thresholds for barium (1,000 mg/kg for groundwater protection) and lead (80 mg/kg for residential screening) were established in the RDIP for verification sampling. Site-specific upper-bound background concentrations for barium (120 mg/kg) and lead (3.8 mg/kg) were also established for proposed clean fill source areas.

### Open Items

A Final RACR will be submitted following the completion of Ultimate Phase 2 construction of the SR 132 West Project, which is scheduled for completion in 2028.
