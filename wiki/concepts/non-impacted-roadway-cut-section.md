## Overview
The [[non-impacted-roadway-cut-section]] refers to a previously sampled area of the State Route 132 roadway cut east of Carpenter Road. This section was identified as exposing non-impacted native formation. The sampling and analysis conducted in this area served as a basis for anticipating similar conditions at the proposed [[basin-5]] clean fill mining location.

## Site record
The [[non-impacted-roadway-cut-section]] was sampled prior to the development of the [[basin-5-clean-fill-sampling-plan]]. The sampling frequency for Basin 5 was based on its location adjacent and north of this previously tested roadway cut section. Similar conditions were anticipated at Basin 5.

## Regulatory status
The [[basin-5-clean-fill-sampling-plan]], which references the [[non-impacted-roadway-cut-section]], was reviewed and approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Waterboard on June 12, 2020.
