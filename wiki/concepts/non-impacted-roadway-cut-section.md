The [[non-impacted-roadway-cut-section]] refers to a previously sampled area of the State Route 132 roadway cut east of Carpenter Road. This section was identified as exposing non-impacted native formation. The sampling and analysis conducted in this area served as a basis for anticipating similar conditions at the proposed [[basin-5]] clean fill mining location.

## Site record
The [[non-impacted-roadway-cut-section]] was sampled prior to the development of the [[basin-5-clean-fill-sampling-plan]]. The sampling frequency for Basin 5 was based on its location adjacent and north of this previously tested roadway cut section.{{quote: "The sampling frequency is based on the location of Basin 5 adjacent and north of the previously tested SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road that exposed non-impacted native formation."}} Similar conditions were anticipated at Basin 5.{{quote: "Similar conditions are anticipated at Basin 5."}}

## Regulatory status
The [[basin-5-clean-fill-sampling-plan]], which references the [[non-impacted-roadway-cut-section]], was reviewed and approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Waterboard on June 12, 2020.{{quote: "DTSC and the Waterboard have reviewed the Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan (Plan) dated June 11, 2020 that outlines the proposed sampling and analysis for the soil that will be used to cap the State Route 132 stockpiles. The Plan is consistent with the previous borrow area sampling and there are no comments. This e-mail serves as DTSCs approval of the Plan."}}
