## No Offsite Residents, Trespassers, or Future Construction Workers

This section addresses the measures and considerations within the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project to prevent exposure to contaminants by offsite residents, trespassers, and future construction workers. The primary goal is to ensure that the remediation and management of the stockpiled soil do not pose a risk to these populations.

### Site Record

The Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] outlines strategies to manage the soil and prevent offsite migration of contaminants. The project involves the management of approximately {{quote: "160,000 cubic yards of soil"}} {{quote: "from the FMC Corporation site"}} {{quote: "at the SR 132 and Highway 99 interchange"}} {{quote: "in Stanislaus County, California"}}. The soil was generated from the remediation of the [[fmc-chemical-processing-facility]].

The RAP specifies that the soil stockpiles are to be managed to prevent exposure. This includes measures to deter trespassers and to inform future construction workers about the nature of the soil and any necessary precautions. The containment strategy is designed to isolate the soil and its constituents, such as [[barium]], from the surrounding environment and potential receptors.

### Regulatory Status

The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) is the regulatory agency overseeing the remediation of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]]. The RAP was developed to meet the requirements set forth by the DTSC to ensure the protection of public health and the environment. The plan details how the soil will be managed to prevent offsite migration and exposure.

### Open Items

The long-term management and ultimate disposition of the soil stockpiles are critical to ensuring no future exposure risks. The RAP details the proposed containment strategy, but the final implementation and verification of these measures will be key to confirming the long-term effectiveness in preventing exposure to offsite residents, trespassers, and future construction workers.
