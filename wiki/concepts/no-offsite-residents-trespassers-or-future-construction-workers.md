## No Offsite Residents, Trespassers, or Future Construction Workers

This section addresses the project's strategy and requirements to prevent exposure of offsite residents, trespassers, and future construction workers to site contaminants. The primary mechanism for achieving this is through the implementation of a [[containment-strategy]] that isolates the stockpiled soil and prevents its migration or disturbance.

## Site Record

The Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] outlines specific measures to prevent offsite exposure. The containment strategy, as detailed in the RAP, is designed to ensure that soil stockpiles do not pose a risk to surrounding communities or individuals who may access the site in the future.

The RAP specifies that the containment strategy is intended to "prevent offsite residents, trespassers, or future construction workers from coming into contact with the soil stockpiles." {{quote: "prevent offsite residents, trespassers, or future construction workers from coming into contact with the soil stockpiles."}} This is a core objective of the remedial actions undertaken for the site.

## Regulatory Status

The requirement to prevent exposure to offsite residents, trespassers, and future construction workers is a fundamental aspect of the regulatory framework governing the remediation of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]]. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) oversees the remediation process and ensures that the chosen containment strategy meets these protective objectives. The approved Remedial Action Plan serves as the governing document for these requirements.
