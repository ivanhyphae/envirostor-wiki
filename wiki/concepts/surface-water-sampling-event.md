## Overview
Surface water sampling events at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site were conducted to assess the potential for contaminants to migrate offsite via stormwater runoff. These events were typically triggered by rainfall and involved collecting samples from various locations within and adjacent to the Caltrans right-of-way. The primary focus was on dissolved metals.

## Site record
Shaw completed a surface water sampling event at the soil stockpiles in March 2006, collecting seven surface water samples (SW01 through SW06 and SW08) during a qualifying rain event. {{quote: "Shaw completed a surface water sampling event at the soil stockpiles in March 2006 in general accordance with their January 2006 SAP. In total, seven surface water samples (SW01 through SW06 and SW08) were collected during a qualifying rain event (visible runoff and 72 hours of prior dry weather)."}} Runoff was confined to the Caltrans right-of-way, and shallow depressions were constructed to collect precipitation. {{quote: "Since there was no surface water migration beyond the Caltrans right-of-way, Shaw constructed shallow depressions within the right-of-way in order to collect precipitation falling on the stockpiles."}} Samples were analyzed for dissolved metals, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), nitrate, sulfate, and sulfide. {{quote: "The samples were analyzed for dissolved metals, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), nitrate, sulfate and sulfide."}} The March 2006 event reported an elevated barium concentration of 2,000 micrograms per liter (µg/l) in sample SW03, collected from the northwestern side of Stockpile 3. {{quote: "With the sole exception of an elevated barium concentration reported for the sample collected from the northwestern side of Stockpile 3 (sample SW03), the surface water samples did not contain elevated metals concentrations. Barium was reported at a concentration of 2,000 micrograms per liter (µg/l) in sample SW03."}} Barium in the other six samples ranged from 16 to 190 µg/l. {{quote: "Barium in the remaining six samples ranged from 16 to 190 µg/l."}} Shaw concluded this barium concentration was isolated and runoff was confined to the right-of-way. {{quote: "Shaw concluded that the elevated barium reported for sample SW03 was isolated and that runoff in the area was confined to Caltrans right-of-way."}}

Ten surface water sampling events were conducted between April 2013 and October 2016. {{quote: "We previously performed ten surface water sampling events at the soil stockpiles between April 2013 and October 2016."}} Sample points included PL1 and PL2 along North Emerald Avenue, PL3 along the southern edge of Stockpile 2, PL4 and PL5 at the drainage basin next to Stockpile 3, and background locations BG1 and BG2 next to storm water inlets south (Loletta Avenue) and north (North Emerald Avenue) of Stockpile 2. {{quote: "Sample points PL1 and PL2 are along North Emerald Avenue. Sample point PL3 is along the southern edge of Stockpile 2. Sample points PL4 and PL5 are at the drainage basin next to Stockpile 3. PL4 is where storm water enters the drainage basin next to Stockpile 3 and PL5 is within three feet of the gate valve that would release storm water to the SR 99 collection system conveying storm water to the Tuolumne River, if opened. ... Sample points BG1 and BG2 are next to storm water inlets both south (Loletta Avenue) and north (North Emerald Avenue) of Stockpile 2."}} Sample locations were approved by the DTSC and Central Valley Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB). {{quote: "The sample locations were approved by the DTSC and Central Valley Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB)."}} During these events, runoff was not observed migrating away from the Caltrans right-of-way. {{quote: "During the previous storm events we did not observe runoff migrating away from the Caltrans right-of-way."}}

A surface water sampling event occurred on December 15, 2016, during rainfall totaling approximately 0.45 inch. {{quote: "This section describes the field activities performed for the December 15, 2016, surface water sampling event. It was raining at the time of sample collection. The rainfall total for this event, which began and ended on December 15, 2016, was approximately 0.45 inch."}}

Surface water samples were collected on an on-call basis between April 2013 and March 2017. {{quote: "We previously collected surface water samples from five designated surface runoff locations (PL1 through PL5) and two background locations (BG1 and BG2) on an on-call basis between April 2013 and March 2017."}}

A surface water sampling event took place on March 24, 2017, during rainfall totaling approximately 1.21 inches. {{quote: "This section describes the field activities for the March 24, 2017, surface water sampling event. It was raining at the time of sample collection. The rainfall total for this event which began on March 20, 2017, and ended on March 26, 2017, was approximately 1.21 inches."}}

A surface water sampling event occurred on January 8, 2018, during a rainfall event that began on January 4, 2018, and ended on January 10, 2018, with a total of approximately 2.2 inches. {{quote: "This section describes the field activities for the January 8, 2018, surface water sampling event. It was raining at the time of sample collection. The rainfall total for this event which began on January 4, 2018, and ended on January 10, 2018, was approximately 2.2 inches."}}

## Regulatory status
Sample locations PL1 through PL5 and BG1 and BG2 were approved by the DTSC and Central Valley Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB). {{quote: "The sample locations were approved by the DTSC and Central Valley Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB)."}}
