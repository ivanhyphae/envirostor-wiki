## Overview
Surface water sampling events at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site were conducted to assess the potential for contaminants to migrate offsite via stormwater runoff. These events were typically triggered by rainfall and involved collecting samples from various locations within and adjacent to the Caltrans right-of-way. The primary focus was on dissolved metals.

## Site record
Shaw completed a surface water sampling event at the soil stockpiles in March 2006, collecting seven surface water samples (SW01 through SW06 and SW08) during a qualifying rain event. Runoff was confined to the Caltrans right-of-way, and shallow depressions were constructed to collect precipitation. Samples were analyzed for dissolved metals, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), nitrate, sulfate, and sulfide. The March 2006 event reported an elevated barium concentration of 2,000 micrograms per liter (µg/l) in sample SW03, collected from the northwestern side of Stockpile 3. Barium in the other six samples ranged from 16 to 190 µg/l. Shaw concluded this barium concentration was isolated and runoff was confined to the right-of-way.

Ten surface water sampling events were conducted between April 2013 and October 2016. Sample points included PL1 and PL2 along North Emerald Avenue, PL3 along the southern edge of Stockpile 2, PL4 and PL5 at the drainage basin next to Stockpile 3, and background locations BG1 and BG2 next to storm water inlets south (Loletta Avenue) and north (North Emerald Avenue) of Stockpile 2. Sample locations were approved by the DTSC and Central Valley Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB). During these events, runoff was not observed migrating away from the Caltrans right-of-way.

A surface water sampling event occurred on December 15, 2016, during rainfall totaling approximately 0.45 inch.

Surface water samples were collected on an on-call basis between April 2013 and March 2017.

A surface water sampling event took place on March 24, 2017, during rainfall totaling approximately 1.21 inches.

A surface water sampling event occurred on January 8, 2018, during a rainfall event that began on January 4, 2018, and ended on January 10, 2018, with a total of approximately 2.2 inches.

## Regulatory status
Sample locations PL1 through PL5 and BG1 and BG2 were approved by the DTSC and Central Valley Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB).
