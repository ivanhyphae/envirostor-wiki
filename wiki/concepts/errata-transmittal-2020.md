## Errata Transmittal 2020

### Overview

The Errata Transmittal 2020 is a formal corrective document issued by Caltrans District 10 to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) on February 6, 2020, updating the January 30, 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) – Interim Phase 1 for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). The transmittal amends roles and responsibilities within the RDIP to reflect the termination of Geocon, Inc.'s contract with the state.

### Site Record

The transmittal, signed by Caltrans Senior Environmental Planner Craig Ruhmann, was sent to DTSC Project Manager Dean Wright, P.G. It states that following DTSC concurrence with the June 2014 Feasibility Study and June 2014 Remedial Action Plan (RAP), the RDIP was prepared and accepted by DTSC in January 2019. These decision documents established the prescribed remedy for the stockpiles under the "containment alternative" during construction of the SR 132 West Expressway project.

At the time of the RDIP's preparation, Geocon, Inc. was under contract to perform environmental services and was identified as Caltrans' sole agent to ensure containment in accordance with the RAP and RDIP. The transmittal documents that, subsequent to DTSC's acceptance of the RDIP, Geocon is no longer under contract with the state.

The attached "Post Construction Contract Award Errata" updates the pertinent sections of the RDIP to reflect these changes, striking Geocon as the state's agent. The transmittal explicitly states: "Regardless of the consultant who will perform the work necessary to ensure that the stockpiles are remedied in accordance with the 'containment alternative', Caltrans is the responsible party that is required to demonstrate the work was performed as described in the RDIP."

### Regulatory Status

The Errata Transmittal 2020 does not change the substantive remedy or regulatory standards established by the 2014 RAP and 2019 RDIP. It only modifies implementation roles. Caltrans remains the responsible party under DTSC oversight for demonstrating compliance with the containment alternative. No new regulatory standards are introduced by this document.

### Open Items

The Errata Transmittal 2020 does not identify new unresolved issues but confirms the transfer of responsibility from Geocon to an unnamed future consultant, with Caltrans retaining ultimate accountability. The record does not indicate whether the errata itself was formally concurred with by DTSC or if a replacement consultant was subsequently retained.
