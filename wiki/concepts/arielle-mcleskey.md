Arielle Mcleskey is the DTSC Environmental Scientist in the Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program, who took over as Project Manager for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles-site]] from [[dean-wright]] in December 2024. In that role she corresponded with Caltrans's [[adam-inman]] on the status of the Well Destruction Completion Report, following up on a conditional approval her office had issued.

## Assumption of the DTSC Project Manager role

On December 11, 2024, Dean Wright, P.G., notified Adam Inman by email that the DTSC Project Manager role for the SR 132 – Modesto Soil Stockpiles project had changed and that Mcleskey, based in DTSC's Sacramento office, would serve as PM going forward. Wright instructed that all further documents or correspondence pertaining to the site be submitted to Mcleskey, and provided her title (Environmental Scientist, Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program), address (8800 Cal Center Drive, Sacramento, California 95826), email, and phone number (916-255-3631). John Juhrend of GeoCon Inc. and DTSC's Lora Jameson were copied on the notice.

## Follow-up on the Well Destruction Completion Report

On March 16, 2026, Mcleskey emailed Adam Inman asking whether he had sent the revised Well Destruction Completion Report, noting that she had issued a conditional approval letter dated January 30, 2026 with one correction needed, and that she did not see the revised report in her inbox. She asked Inman to resend it or forward the email if it had already been sent. The record does not show Inman's response or confirm when, or whether, the corrected report was subsequently submitted; the status of the [[dtsc-conditional-approval-well-destruction-report]] condition as of this email remains open in the file.
