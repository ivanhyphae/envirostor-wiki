{{overview}}
Arielle Mcleskey is an Environmental Scientist and Project Manager with the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) Site Mitigation and Restoration Program. She serves as the DTSC Service Request Manager for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] located on [[sr-132]] in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. In this capacity, she is responsible for overseeing project deliverables and acting as the primary point of contact for [[caltrans]].
