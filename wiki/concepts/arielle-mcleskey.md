## Arielle Mcleskey

Arielle Mcleskey is an Environmental Scientist and Project Manager with the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) Site Mitigation and Restoration Program. She is responsible for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] located on [[sr-132]] in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. Her role includes overseeing project deliverables and acting as the primary point of contact for [[caltrans]].

### Site Record

On March 16, 2026, Mcleskey contacted Adam Inman of Caltrans to inquire about the revised [[well-destruction-completion-report]]. She had previously issued a conditional approval for this report on January 30, 2026, noting a single correction that needed to be addressed.{{quote: "I sent a conditional approval letter dated 1/30/2026 with just one correction needed. I don't see it in my inbox."}}

### Regulatory Status

Mcleskey is the designated Project Manager for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] by the DTSC. She communicates directly with Caltrans regarding project documentation and regulatory compliance.

### Open Items

As of March 16, 2026, Mcleskey was awaiting the revised [[well-destruction-completion-report]] from Caltrans to finalize her review.{{quote: "I am reaching out to ask if you had sent me the revised Well Destruction Completion Report?"}}
