Arielle Mcleskey is an Environmental Scientist and Project Manager with the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) Site Mitigation and Restoration Program. She serves as the DTSC Service Request Manager for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, located on [[sr-132]] in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. In this role, she oversees project deliverables and acts as the primary point of contact for [[caltrans]].

## Site Record
Mcleskey is identified as the DTSC Service Request Manager for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, with the phone number 916-255-3631. She serves as the counterpart to Caltrans Service Request Manager, Adam Inman. The Service Request for this project, identified as Service Request No. 43A0487-04, has a term from August 4, 2025, to June 30, 2030. The execution date for this Service Request was August 25, 2025, with a total order amount of $113,000.00.

The scope of services for DTSC under this Service Request includes reviewing and commenting on various reports, such as well destruction reports, participating in meetings and phone calls regarding the site and construction activities, and providing field oversight as needed for monitoring and field activities conducted by Caltrans.

## Regulatory Status
Mcleskey is the designated Project Manager for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project by the DTSC. She communicates directly with Caltrans regarding project documentation and regulatory compliance.
