## Finding Of No Significant Impact

### Overview
A Finding of No Significant Impact (FONSI) is a determination made as part of the environmental review process under the National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA). For the [[state-route-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]], a FONSI was issued in conjunction with the Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA). This FONSI signifies that the project, as evaluated, is not expected to result in significant adverse environmental effects.

### Site Record
The Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) which included the Finding of No Significant Impact was made available on March 12, 2018 {{quote: "March 12, 2018"}}. This document was released concurrently with the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]]. {{quote: "FINAL ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT REPORT/ENVIRONMENTAL ASSESSMENT WITH FINDING OF NO SIGNIFICANT IMPACT - STATE ROUTE 132 WEST FREEWAY/EXPRESSWAY PROJECT"}} {{quote: "DRAFT FINAL REMEDIAL ACTION PLAN - CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES"}}

### Regulatory Status
The FONSI is an integral component of the Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) for the [[state-route-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]]. The Final EIR/EA, inclusive of the FONSI, was circulated for public review. {{quote: "The Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) with Finding of No Significant Impact along with the attached Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) are now available online and at the following locations:"}}

### Open Items
The Draft EIR/EA, accompanied by the Draft Final RAP, was circulated for public review between January 18, 2017, and March 17, 2017. {{quote: "The Draft EIR/EA with attached Draft Final RAP was circulated for public review from January 18, 2017 through March 17, 2017."}} Responses to comments received during this public comment period are documented in [[appendix-j]] – Comments and Responses. {{quote: "Responses to comments received are included in Appendix J – Comments and Responses."}} The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board, provided responses to comments specifically concerning the Draft Final RAP. {{quote: "Responses to comments for the Draft Final RAP include comments responded to by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board."}}
