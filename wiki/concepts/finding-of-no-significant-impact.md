## Finding Of No Significant Impact

### Overview
A Finding of No Significant Impact (FONSI) is a determination made as part of the environmental review process. For the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]], a FONSI was issued in conjunction with the Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA). This FONSI indicates that the project, as evaluated, is not expected to have significant adverse environmental effects.

### Site Record
The Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) with Finding of No Significant Impact was made available on March 12, 2018 {{quote: "March 12, 2018"}}. This document was released alongside the Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]]. {{quote: "FINAL ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT REPORT/ENVIRONMENTAL ASSESSMENT WITH FINDING OF NO SIGNIFICANT IMPACT - STATE ROUTE 132 WEST FREEWAY/EXPRESSWAY PROJECT"}} {{quote: "DRAFT FINAL REMEDIAL ACTION PLAN - CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES"}}

### Regulatory Status
The FONSI is a component of the Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) for the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]]. The Final EIR/EA, including the FONSI, was circulated for public review. {{quote: "The Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (EIR/EA) with Finding of No Significant Impact along with the attached Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Draft Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP) are now available online and at the following locations:"}}

### Open Items
The Draft EIR/EA with attached Draft Final RAP was circulated for public review from January 18, 2017 through March 17, 2017. {{quote: "The Draft EIR/EA with attached Draft Final RAP was circulated for public review from January 18, 2017 through March 17, 2017."}} Responses to comments received during this period are included in Appendix J – Comments and Responses. {{quote: "Responses to comments received are included in Appendix J – Comments and Responses."}} Comments on the Draft Final RAP were responded to by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board. {{quote: "Responses to comments for the Draft Final RAP include comments responded to by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board."}}
