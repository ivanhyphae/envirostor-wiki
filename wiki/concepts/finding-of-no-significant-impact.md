## Finding Of No Significant Impact

### Overview
A **Finding of No Significant Impact (FONSI)** is a regulatory determination under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) and the National Environmental Policy Act (NEPA) that a proposed project will not result in significant adverse environmental effects. For the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project on State Route 132 in Stanislaus County, the FONSI was documented in the *SR 132 West Signed NOA Public* record. This determination allowed the remediation to proceed without preparation of a full Environmental Impact Report (EIR) or Environmental Impact Statement (EIS), subject to adherence to identified mitigation measures.

### Site record
The FONSI for the SR 132 West soil stockpiles (Stanislaus County) is formally recorded in the *SR 132 West Signed NOA Public* document. This document was signed by the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) Acting District Director following a public review period. The record indicates that the environmental review considered:
- Potential impacts from excavation, stockpiling, and off-site disposal of contaminated soils.
- Contaminants of concern include arsenic, lead, and petroleum hydrocarbons at concentrations documented in prior site assessments (see Modesto Soil Stockpiles – Contaminant Summary).
- Mitigation measures incorporated into the project design to avoid or reduce impacts to air quality, biological resources, cultural resources, water quality, and traffic. Specific measures are listed in the Mitigation Monitoring and Reporting Program (MMRP) appended to the FONSI.
- The public was provided with a Notice of Availability (NOA) for the draft FONSI and supporting Initial Study/Mitigated Negative Declaration (IS/MND) in [month/year; not specified in source], with a 30-day comment period. No substantive comments were received that would require preparation of an EIR.

The FONSI concludes that with implementation of the prescribed mitigation measures, the project will not cause a significant impact on the environment.

### Regulatory status
The FONSI establishes compliance with:
- **CEQA** (California Public Resources Code §21000 et seq.) – the project qualifies for a Mitigated Negative Declaration.
- **NEPA** (42 U.S.C. §4321 et seq.) – the project is categorically excluded or receives a FONSI under 23 CFR 771.117.

The responsible lead agency is Caltrans, with concurrence from the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) regarding cleanup levels and remedial actions. The project must implement the MMRP referenced in the FONSI as a condition of approval. No further environmental documentation is required unless project scoping changes substantially.

### Open items
No unresolved issues are documented in the *SR 132 West Signed NOA Public* record. The FONSI has been signed and the public notice period has closed. Should any unanticipated impacts arise during construction, a reevaluation may be initiated.
