The State Route 132/99 project, also referred to as the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]], involves remediation efforts managed under an Interagency Agreement between the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). This agreement, identified as No. 43A0310, has been amended through various Task Orders to fund and extend the period of performance for DTSC's oversight and review activities.

## Site Record

The project's scope of services, as defined under Task Order No. 4, Amendment 1, involves DTSC's reimbursement for oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, and processing of Caltrans' requests to mitigate impacts from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. This Task Order was originally executed on June 7, 2013, with an initial termination date of May 31, 2016. Amendment 1 extended the termination date to May 31, 2018, and increased the total Task Order amount to \$158,183.00. {{quote: "Task Order No. 4, Amendment 1, under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310, amends the existing Task Order for time and funding. The original Task Order and this amendment provides for the reimbursement of costs incurred by the DTSC for oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, and/or processing applications(s) or request(s) made by Caltrans to mitigate impacts from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles."}} {{quote: "The total amount payable by the Caltrans under this Task Order shall not exceed \$158,183.00."}} {{quote: "The original task order was executed on June 7, 2013, with a termination date of May 31, 2016. This amendment extends the termination date until on May 31, 2018."}}

Task Order No. 4, Amendment 2, further amended the agreement for time and funding. This amendment extended the termination date to May 31, 2018, and increased the total Task Order amount to \$261,957.00. {{quote: "The total amount payable by the Caltrans under this Task Order shall not exceed \$261,957.00."}} {{quote: "The original task order was executed on June 7, 2013, with a termination date of May 31, 2016. The task order was subsequently amended to extend the termination date until May 31, 2018."}}

A subsequent Task Order, No. 43A0404-04, was established with a term from June 1, 2020, to June 30, 2025, and a total amount of \$100,000.00. This Task Order focuses on reimbursing DTSC for activities related to regulating construction activities at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles – State Route 132/99 during the Route 132 West Expressway construction. The scope of services includes DTSC providing professional staff to conduct regulatory oversight, review plans, reports, and technical documents pertaining to mitigation of impacts to human health and the environment, field oversight of Caltrans activities, and participation in meetings. {{quote: "This Task Order between the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) is to reimburse costs incurred by DTSC for activities as appropriate to regulate activities at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles – State Route 132/99 (Site) during construction of the Route 132 West Expressway conducted by Caltrans."}} {{quote: "DTSC will provide professional staff services to conduct all activities as needed, in providing regulatory oversight to support activities associated with the project identified above."}} {{quote: "The total amount payable by the Caltrans under this Task Order shall not exceed \$100,000.00 for the duration of this task order."}} {{quote: "Work under this Task Order shall begin on June 1, 2020 and terminate on June 30, 2025."}}
