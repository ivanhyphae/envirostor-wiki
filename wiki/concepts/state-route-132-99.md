The State Route 132/99 project, also referred to as the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]], involves remediation efforts managed under an Interagency Agreement between the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). This agreement, identified as No. 43A0310, has been amended through various Task Orders to fund and extend the period of performance for DTSC's oversight and review activities.

## Site Record

The project's scope of services, as defined under Task Order No. 4, Amendment 1, involves DTSC's reimbursement for oversight, review, consultation, decision document approval, and processing of Caltrans' requests to mitigate impacts from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. This Task Order was originally executed on June 7, 2013, with an initial termination date of May 31, 2016. Amendment 1 extended the termination date to May 31, 2018, and increased the total Task Order amount to \$158,183.00.

Task Order No. 4, Amendment 2, further amended the agreement for time and funding. This amendment extended the termination date to May 31, 2018, and increased the total Task Order amount to \$261,957.00.

A subsequent Task Order, No. 43A0404-04, was established with a term from June 1, 2020, to June 30, 2025, and a total amount of \$100,000.00. This Task Order focuses on reimbursing DTSC for activities related to regulating construction activities at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles – State Route 132/99 during the Route 132 West Expressway construction. The scope of services includes DTSC providing professional staff to conduct regulatory oversight, review plans, reports, and technical documents pertaining to mitigation of impacts to human health and the environment, field oversight of Caltrans activities, and participation in meetings.

The project operates under an Interagency Agreement (No. 43A0310) between Caltrans and DTSC, with DTSC providing regulatory oversight for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. Specific regulatory standards or thresholds for contaminants at the site are not detailed in the provided excerpts.
