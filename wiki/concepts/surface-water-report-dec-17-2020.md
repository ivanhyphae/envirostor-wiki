The Surface Water Report, dated December 17, 2020 and prepared by Stantec Consulting under Caltrans Task Order 16, documents surface water sampling at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. The report itself was transmitted to [[dtsc]] on March 23, 2021 by Ralph Carson of Stantec, forwarded by DTSC's Dean Wright to the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] on March 24, 2021, and reviewed by Kyle Cockerham of the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board, who responded on March 25, 2021 that he had no comment on the report.

The available record consists of this transmittal and review correspondence rather than the report itself, so the sampling results, locations, and analytes covered are not available in the material reviewed here. What the correspondence establishes is procedural: the report was routed to both oversight agencies with responsibility for the site, and the Water Board's Site Cleanup Unit reviewed it and raised no objection. Because Cockerham's response contains no comments, conditions, or requests for additional information, the record reflects that the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board's review of the December 17, 2020 surface water sampling concluded without any identified issue.

The three-month gap between the report's stated date and its March 2021 transmittal to DTSC and the Water Board is noted in the correspondence chain but is not explained in the available material.
