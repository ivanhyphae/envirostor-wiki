## Overview
The [[city-of-modesto]] is identified as a client of [[wsp]], which is involved in the [[sr-132]] project in Modesto, California. WSP, in conjunction with the [[city-of-modesto]], received technical memorandums detailing soil characterization testing for proposed borrow areas intended to generate [[clean-cover-fill-material]]. This material is slated for use in capping [[barium-containing-soil]] within existing stockpiles.

## Site record
The [[city-of-modesto]] is associated with the characterization testing of two borrow areas: the Basin 5 Clean Cap Borrow Area and the [[sr-132]] Clean Borrow Area. Both are situated adjacent and easterly of the [[carpenter-road-area]] within the [[sr-132]] right-of-way.

Testing at the Basin 5 Clean Cap Borrow Area, conducted by [[geocon-consultants]], involved sampling exploratory trenches on June 16, 2020. Soil samples were collected from depth intervals of 0 to 2 feet, 2 to 5 feet, and 5 to 10 feet. The soil was described as brown to tan, damp, clayey to fine sandy silt, with no observed field indicators of contamination. Metal concentrations in composite samples were reported within ranges consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. Barium concentrations ranged from 66 to 94 mg/kg, compared to a site-specific background of 72.8 mg/kg. Other metals, including arsenic, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, nickel, vanadium, and zinc, were also reported within background ranges. Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, molybdenum, selenium, silver, thallium, mercury, and organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) were not detected above laboratory reporting limits.

Testing at the [[sr-132]] Clean Borrow Area, also performed by [[geocon-consultants]], was completed on December 21, 2020. Direct-push borings reached a maximum depth of 5 feet, with continuous core samples collected at 0 to 2 feet and 2 to 5 feet. The soil composition was similar: brown to tan, damp, clayey to fine sandy silt, with no field indicators of contamination observed. Reported metal concentrations were consistent with background levels. Barium concentrations ranged from 60 to 110 mg/kg, with a background of 72.8 mg/kg. Other reported metals included arsenic, beryllium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, nickel, silver, vanadium, and zinc, with ranges provided. Antimony, cadmium, selenium, thallium, mercury, and OCPs were not detected above laboratory reporting limits.

Additionally, a Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021, from [[geocon-consultants]] to Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager for WSP/[[city-of-modesto]], details characterization testing for the Carpenter Road Shoofly clean fill. Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil were excavated from this area. Soil samples were collected from six vertical transects at depths of 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet from the top of the slope. Composite samples were analyzed for barium and lead. Barium was detected at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 mg/kg, which is less than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg. Lead was detected in 9 of 10 samples at concentrations from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg. Based on this data, the soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly met the clean cap criteria for [[barium-containing-soil]] stockpiles.

A Technical Memorandum dated March 13, 2020, from [[geocon-consultants]] to Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager for WSP/[[city-of-modesto]], summarizes barium containing soil (BCS) removal verification testing completed at former Stockpile 3. A total of 3,075 truck-loads of BCS material, totaling approximately 38,440 cubic yards, was transported to Stockpile 1. Verification soil samples collected from the base of the excavation between February 28 and March 12, 2020, contained barium concentrations ranging from 46 to 970 mg/kg, and lead concentrations from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg. These results were below the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) verification thresholds of 1,000 mg/kg for barium and 80 mg/kg for lead.

## Regulatory status
The soil sampling strategies for the borrow areas were developed in general accordance with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and were approved by Mr. Dean Wright with DTSC.
