The [[city-of-modesto]] is identified as a recipient of technical memorandums regarding soil characterization testing for the [[sr-132]] project in Modesto, California. Specifically, the [[city-of-modesto]] is listed as a client of WSP, which is involved in the project.

## Site record

The [[city-of-modesto]] is associated with the characterization testing of the proposed [[basin-5-clean-cap-borrow-area]] and the [[sr-132]] Clean Borrow Area. These areas are located adjacent and easterly of [[carpenter-road-area]] within the [[sr-132]] right-of-way. The purpose of these borrow areas is to generate [[clean-cover-fill-material]] for capping consolidated [[barium-containing-soil]] within existing Stockpile-1 and Stockpile-2.

Testing at the Basin 5 Clean Cap Borrow Area, performed by [[geocon-consultants]], involved sampling exploratory trenches on June 16, 2020. Samples were collected at depth intervals of 0 to 2 feet, 2 to 5 feet, and 5 to 10 feet. The soil generally consisted of brown to tan, damp, clayey to fine sandy silt, with no field indicators of contamination observed. Metal concentrations reported for composite samples were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. For example, barium concentrations ranged from 66 to 94 mg/kg, compared to a site-specific background of 72.8 mg/kg {{quote: "Barium 66 to 94 (72.8 mg/kg)"}}. Other metals like arsenic, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, nickel, vanadium, and zinc were also reported within ranges consistent with background levels {{quote: "Arsenic <1.0 to 1.8 (1.2 mg/kg) Barium 66 to 94 (72.8 mg/kg) Chromium 8.5 to 11 (8.6 mg/kg1 ) Cobalt 3.3 to 3.8 (4.4 mg/kg) Copper 7.5 to 9.3 (7.5 mg/kg) Lead 5.8 to 11 (2.0 mg/kg) Nickel 6.2 to 7.9 (5.3 mg/kg) Vanadium 33 to 37 (31.3 mg/kg) Zinc 29 to 36 (26.3 mg/kg)"}}. Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, molybdenum, selenium, silver, thallium, mercury, and organochlorine pesticides (OCPs) were not detected above laboratory reporting limits {{quote: "Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, molybdenum, selenium, silver, thallium, mercury and OCPs were not detected in each composite soil sample analyzed above the laboratory reporting limits."}}.

Testing at the [[sr-132]] Clean Borrow Area, also performed by [[geocon-consultants]], was completed on December 21, 2020. Direct-push borings were advanced to a maximum depth of 5 feet, with continuous core samples collected at depth intervals of 0 to 2 feet and 2 to 5 feet. Soil composition was similar, described as brown to tan, damp, clayey to fine sandy silt, with no field indicators of contamination observed. Reported metal concentrations were also consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. Barium concentrations ranged from 60 to 110 mg/kg, with a background of 72.8 mg/kg {{quote: "Barium 60 to 110 (72.8 mg/kg)"}}. Other reported metals included arsenic, beryllium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, nickel, silver, vanadium, and zinc, with ranges provided and compared to background data {{quote: "Arsenic 1.0 to 1.5 (1.2 mg/kg) Barium 60 to 110 (72.8 mg/kg) Beryllium 1.3 to 1.6 (0.2 mg/kg) Chromium 7.5 to 11 (7.1 mg/kg) Cobalt 3.2 to 4.9 (4.4 mg/kg) Copper 5.3 to 9.9 (7.5 mg/kg) Lead 1.7 to 7.0 (2.0 mg/kg) Nickel 5.6 to 8.2 (5.3 mg/kg) Silver 1.6 to 2.5 (0.2 mg/kg) Vanadium 16 to 25 (31.3 mg/kg) Zinc 21 to 41 (26.3 mg/kg)"}}. Antimony, cadmium, selenium, thallium, mercury, and OCPs were not detected above laboratory reporting limits {{quote: "Antimony, cadmium, selenium, thallium, mercury and OCPs were not detected in each soil sample analyzed above the laboratory reporting limits."}}.

The technical memorandums presenting this data were addressed to Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager for WSP/[[city-of-modesto]]. The soil sampling strategies were developed in general accordance with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and were approved by Mr. Dean Wright with DTSC.
