## Overview
Richard Bailey served as the Caltrans Contract Manager for multiple task orders related to the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project on [[state-route-132-99]]. His role involved overseeing agreements between Caltrans and the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for the remediation and management of soil stockpiles.

## Site Record
Richard Bailey's signature appears on Task Order No. 4, Amendment 1, under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310, executed on May 24, 2016. This amendment extended the task order term from May 31, 2016, to May 31, 2018, and increased the total task order amount to \$158,183.00.

He also signed Task Order No. 4, Amendment 2, under the same Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310, executed on February 9, 2018. This amendment further extended the task order term to May 31, 2018, and increased the total task order amount to \$261,957.00.

Additionally, Richard Bailey is listed as the Caltrans Contract Manager for Task Order No. 04 under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0404. This task order had an execution date of March 18, 2021, with a term from June 1, 2020, to June 30, 2025, and a total order amount of \$100,000.00.

## Regulatory Status
In his capacity as Caltrans Contract Manager, Richard Bailey certified that the work described in the task orders complied with the provisions of the respective agreements and was necessary for the satisfactory completion of contracted products. This certification was made in accordance with Deputy Directive DD-09-R3 and Government Code 19990.
