Richard Bailey served as the Caltrans Contract Manager for Task Order No. 4, Amendment 1 and Amendment 2 under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310, related to the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project on [[state-route-132-99]]. He also held the position of Caltrans Contract Manager for Task Order No. 04 under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0404, which also addressed the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles.

## Site Record

Richard Bailey's signature appears on Task Order No. 4, Amendment 1, executed on May 24, 2016. This amendment extended the term of the task order from May 31, 2016, to May 31, 2018, and increased the total task order amount to \$158,183.00. {{quote: "Caltrans Contract Manager: Richard Bailey"}} {{quote: "Amended Term: June 7, 2013 through May 31, 2018"}} {{quote: "Amendment Execution Date: LT 2015"}} {{quote: "Total Task Order Amount: \$158,183.00"}}

He also signed Task Order No. 4, Amendment 2, executed on February 9, 2018. This amendment further extended the task order term to May 31, 2018, and increased the total task order amount to \$261,957.00. {{quote: "Caltrans Contract Manager: Richard Bailey"}} {{quote: "Amended Term: June 7, 2013 through May 31, 2018"}} {{quote: "Amendment Execution Date: FEBRUART 9 2018"}} {{quote: "Total Task Order Amount: \$ 261,957.00"}}

Richard Bailey is also listed as the Caltrans Contract Manager for Task Order No. 04 under Agreement No. 43A0404, which had an execution date of March 18, 2021. The term for this task order was from June 1, 2020, to June 30, 2025, with a total order amount of \$100,000.00. {{quote: "Caltrans Contract Manager: Mr. Richard Bailey"}} {{quote: "Term of Task Order: June 1, 2020, to June 30, 2025"}} {{quote: "Execution Date: March 18, 2021"}} {{quote: "Total Order Amount: \$ 100,000.00"}}

## Regulatory Status

Richard Bailey, in his capacity as Caltrans Contract Manager, certified that the work described in the task orders complied with the provisions of the respective agreements and was necessary for satisfactory completion of contracted products. {{quote: "I certify that this Task Order and attachments comply with the provisions of Agreement No. 43A0310, are necessary for the satisfactory completion of the products contracted for, and that sufficient funding has been encumbered to pay for this work."}}
