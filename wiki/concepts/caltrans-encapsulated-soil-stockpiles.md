The Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles are a project site in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California, south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue. The project involves encapsulated soil stockpiles, identified as Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2, which were located beneath SR 132. Stormwater sampling has been conducted at this site to assess potential impacts.

## Site record
Stormwater sampling was conducted on February 1, 2024, and February 4, 2025, at the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles. These sampling events were performed in accordance with the *Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)*, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc., dated December 13, 2023.

During the February 1, 2024, sampling event, no discharge or runoff from the Caltrans Right of Way (ROW) was observed.
Similarly, during the February 4, 2025, sampling event, discharge or runoff was not observed migrating away from the Caltrans ROW.

The site plan indicates the locations of Stockpile #1 (Encapsulated) and Stockpile #2 (Encapsulated), as well as storm water sampling locations designated as SW-West, SW-East, and BG-West.

Caltrans plans to conduct additional stormwater sampling during qualifying rain events in 2024 and 2025.

Four groundwater monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, and MW10) associated with the Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles were destroyed on June 18, 2025. This action followed an updated comparative evaluation of groundwater data, which concluded that the encapsulated soil stockpiles do not have the potential to impact groundwater.

## Regulatory status
The stormwater sampling protocols were approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB).

On May 3, 2024, the DTSC and the Central Valley RWQCB approved the discontinuation of groundwater monitoring beneath the soil stockpiles and the decommissioning of the four remaining monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, and MW10). This approval was based on an Updated Comparative Evaluation of Groundwater Data, dated February 26, 2024, which indicated that the encapsulated soil is not impacting groundwater and will not do so in the future.

## Open items
Caltrans plans to conduct additional stormwater sampling during qualifying rain events in 2024 and 2025.
