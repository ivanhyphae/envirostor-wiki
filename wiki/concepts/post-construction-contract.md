## Post Construction Contract

### Overview

The Post Construction Contract Award Errata addresses changes in roles and responsibilities following the acceptance of the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]]. Specifically, it notes that Geocon, Inc., previously identified as [[caltrans]]' agent for ensuring stockpile containment, is no longer under contract with the state. Despite this change in consultants, [[caltrans]] remains the responsible party for demonstrating that the remediation work, as described in the RDIP and the associated Remedial Action Plan (RAP), has been performed in accordance with the "containment alternative."

### Site Record

The "Post Construction Contract Award Errata to the January 30th, 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan – Interim Phase 1" was issued on February 6, 2020, by [[caltrans]] District 6. This errata document updates the RDIP to reflect that Geocon, Inc. is no longer the state's employed consultant for ensuring stockpile containment. The errata was sent to Mr. Dean Wright, Project Manager for the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). The RDIP itself was accepted by the DTSC in January 2019, following the DTSC's concurrence with the June 2014 Feasibility Study and June 2014 Remedial Action Plan. The RDIP established the "containment alternative" as the prescribed remedy for the stockpiles during the construction of the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]].

The "Errata to January 2019 RDIP - SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1" document, dated February 6, 2020, clarifies that while Geocon Consultants, Inc. prepared the RDIP under an on-call contract with Caltrans, they are no longer the consultant employed by the state. The document emphasizes that regardless of the consultant performing the work, [[caltrans]] is the responsible party for ensuring the work is performed according to the RDIP. The RDIP was approved by the DTSC on April 3, 2019.

Section 2.0 of the "Errata to January 2019 RDIP - SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1" lists the project organization for the implementation of the RAP and RDIP during Interim Phase 1 construction of the [[state-route-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]]. Key entities and their roles include:
*   **Site Owner/Responsible Party:** State of California/Caltrans
*   **Regulatory Agencies:** DTSC and [[central-valley-rwqcb]]
*   **SR-132 West Project Proponent:** City of Modesto (City)/Stanislaus Council of Governments
*   **Project Proponent's Civil Engineer:** Dokken Engineering
*   **Project Proponent's Construction Manager:** WSP, USA
*   **Contractor:** Bay Cities Paving and Grading, Inc.

Section 2.1 details project team responsibilities. [[caltrans]] is responsible for implementing the RAP and RDIP under DTSC oversight, in cooperation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. [[caltrans]] is specifically responsible for:
*   Supervising the proper abandonment of site-related groundwater monitoring wells impacted by Interim Phase 1 highway construction.
*   Observing and photodocumenting barium-containing soil excavation, fill placement, and capping activities.
*   Performing barium-containing soil removal verification soil sampling and analysis.
*   Performing proposed clean soil import source sampling and analysis.
*   Performing worker zone and perimeter air monitoring.
*   Performing periodic stormwater monitoring, including observations and sampling.
*   Reviewing RDIP requirements with the construction manager and highway construction contractor.
*   Interacting with and periodically updating the [[caltrans]] Environmental Manager and DTSC and [[central-valley-rwqcb]] Project Managers.

### Regulatory Status

The "Post Construction Contract Award Errata" and the "Errata to January 2019 RDIP - SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1" documents operate within the framework established by the DTSC's approval of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). The RDIP, accepted by the DTSC in January 2019 and approved on April 3, 2019, outlines the "containment alternative" for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles.

### Open Items

The primary open item addressed by the Post Construction Contract Award Errata is the change in the designated consultant responsible for ensuring stockpile containment. While Geocon, Inc. was initially identified, they are no longer under contract. The errata clarifies that [[caltrans]] remains the responsible party to ensure the work is performed according to the RDIP, irrespective of which consultant ultimately performs the tasks.
