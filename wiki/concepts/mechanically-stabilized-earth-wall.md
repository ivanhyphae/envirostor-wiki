A Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) wall is a structural component utilized in the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. MSE walls were constructed along the southern boundaries of [[stockpile-1]] and stockpile-2 as part of the remedial design and implementation. Soil excavated from the footing excavations of these MSE walls was characterized to determine its suitability for reuse.

## Site record
Excavation for MSE walls along the southern boundaries of Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 generated soil material that required characterization.

For Stockpile 1, soil samples were collected from trenches STK1-1, STK1-2, and STK1-3 within the MSE wall footing excavation, to a maximum depth of 5 feet. {{quote: "For Stockpile 1, soil samples were collected from trenches STK1-1, STK1-2, and STK1-3 within the MSE wall footing excavation, to a maximum depth of 5 feet."}} Barium was detected in 15 soil samples at concentrations ranging from 42 to 420 mg/kg. {{quote: "Barium was detected in 15 soil samples at concentrations ranging from 42 to 420 mg/kg."}} The maximum site-specific background value for barium was 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "The maximum site-specific background value for barium was 120 mg/kg."}} The calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) for barium in this material was 171.9 mg/kg, and the mean was 128.3 mg/kg. {{quote: "The calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) for barium in this material was 171.9 mg/kg, and the mean was 128.3 mg/kg."}} Lead was detected in 14 of 15 samples at concentrations ranging from 1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg, with a maximum site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "Lead was detected in 14 of 15 samples at concentrations ranging from 1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg, with a maximum site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg."}} Based on this data, the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material was deemed suitable for placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill, or as capping material, pending regulatory approval. {{quote: "Based on the data presented herein, the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material excavated along the southerly boundary of Stockpile 1 should be suitable for placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill at the east end of Stockpile 1 and/or ends of Stockpile 2, or as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material pending regulatory agency approval."}} This was documented in a Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020. {{quote: "Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Footing Excavation Characterization Testing"}}

For Stockpile 2, soil samples were collected from trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7 within the MSE wall footing excavation, to maximum depths of 3 and 5 feet. {{quote: "We collected soil samples within the mid-point of the planned wall footing excavation from trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7 spaced at approximate 200-foot intervals. Discrete soil samples were collected from one-foot intervals to the maximum planned wall footing excavation depth of 3 and 5 feet."}} Barium was detected in 29 soil samples at concentrations ranging from 53 to 930 mg/kg. {{quote: "Barium was detected in each of the 29 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 53 to 930 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg)."}} Fourteen samples contained barium concentrations greater than the maximum site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "Fourteen of the 29 soil samples contained barium at concentrations greater than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg."}} Thirteen of these elevated samples were collected at depths between the surface and 3 feet. {{quote: "Thirteen of the 14 soil samples with elevated barium concentrations above the maximum background value were collected at depths between the surface and 3 feet."}} One sample, STK2-7-4, collected deeper than the planned footing excavation depth of 3 feet at that location, contained 140 mg/kg of barium. {{quote: "Sample STK2-7-4 contained a barium concentration of 140 mg/kg, though this sample was collected deeper than the planned footing excavation depth of 3 feet at this location."}} Lead was detected in all samples at concentrations ranging from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg, with the majority within the order of magnitude of the maximum site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "Lead was detected in each of the soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg. The majority of the soil samples contained reported lead concentrations within the same order of magnitude as the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg."}}

A Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020, summarized the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation characterization testing. {{quote: "Stockpile 2 MSE Wall Footing Excavation Characterization Testing"}}

## Regulatory status
The suitability of MSE wall footing excavation soil for reuse was subject to review and approval by regulatory agencies, including the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the Central Valley RWQCB.

For Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material, DTSC and the Water Board agreed with the Technical Memorandum's findings, with the exception of using the soil as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2. Given that the 95% UCL for barium exceeded the maximum background of 120 mg/kg and the footing excavation fell outside the designated borrow area in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]], this material was required to be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill. This decision was communicated via email on April 7, 2020. {{quote: "DTSC and the Water Board have reviewed the Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated April 6, 2020 describing the sampling along the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area and the laboratory results of their analysis. Both agencies agree with the last paragraph of the Memo with the exception of using the MSE wall footing soil as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material. Given that the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil is above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg and the fact that the MSE wall footing falls outside of the designated borrow area described in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, this material must be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill."}}

For Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing material, DTSC and the Water Board agreed with the Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020. Soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) was required to be placed in designated containment zones. Soil below 3 feet bgs was deemed suitable as embankment fill or capping material and was considered part of the April 9, 2020 [[variance-to-remedial-design-implementation-plan]] to the RDIP. This email serves as DTSC's official acceptance of the Memo. {{quote: "DTSC and the Water Board have reviewed the Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated June 18, 2020 describing the Stockpile 2 sampling along the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area and the laboratory results of their analysis. Both agencies agree with the Memo that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones but soil below that depth is suitable as embankment fill or capping material. The material below 3 feet bgs along the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing is also considered a part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan. Thus, another VR is not necessary before utilizing this soil. This e-mail serves as DTSCs official acceptance of the Memo."}}
