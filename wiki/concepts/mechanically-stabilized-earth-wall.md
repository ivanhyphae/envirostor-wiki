## Overview
Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) walls were a structural component constructed along the southern boundaries of [[stockpile-1]] and stockpile-2 as part of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. Soil excavated from the footing excavations of these MSE walls was characterized to determine its suitability for reuse.

## Site record
Excavation for MSE walls along the southern boundaries of Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 generated soil material that required characterization.

For Stockpile 1, soil samples were collected from trenches STK1-1, STK1-2, and STK1-3 within the MSE wall footing excavation, to a maximum depth of 5 feet. Barium was detected in 15 soil samples at concentrations ranging from 42 to 420 mg/kg. The maximum site-specific background value for barium was 120 mg/kg. The calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) for barium in this material was 171.9 mg/kg, and the mean was 128.3 mg/kg. Lead was detected in 14 of 15 samples at concentrations ranging from 1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg, with a maximum site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg. Based on this data, the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material was deemed suitable for placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill, or as capping material, pending regulatory approval. This was documented in a Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020.

For Stockpile 2, soil samples were collected from trenches STK2-1 through STK2-7 within the MSE wall footing excavation, to maximum depths of 3 and 5 feet. Barium was detected in 29 soil samples at concentrations ranging from 53 to 930 mg/kg. Fourteen samples contained barium concentrations greater than the maximum site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg. Thirteen of these elevated samples were collected at depths between the surface and 3 feet. One sample, STK2-7-4, collected deeper than the planned footing excavation depth of 3 feet at that location, contained 140 mg/kg of barium. Lead was detected in all samples at concentrations ranging from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg, with the majority within the order of magnitude of the maximum site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg.

A Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020, summarized the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation characterization testing.

## Regulatory status
The suitability of MSE wall footing excavation soil for reuse was subject to review and approval by regulatory agencies, including the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the Central Valley RWQCB.

For Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material, DTSC and the Water Board agreed with the Technical Memorandum's findings, with the exception of using the soil as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2. Given that the 95% UCL for barium exceeded the maximum background of 120 mg/kg and the footing excavation fell outside the designated borrow area in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]], this material was required to be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill. This decision was communicated via email on April 7, 2020.

For Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing material, DTSC and the Water Board agreed with the Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020. Soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) was required to be placed in designated containment zones. Soil below 3 feet bgs was deemed suitable as embankment fill or capping material and was considered part of the April 9, 2020 [[variance-to-remedial-design-implementation-plan]] to the RDIP. This email serves as DTSC's official acceptance of the Memo.
