## Mechanically Stabilized Earth Wall

A Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) wall is a component of the remedial design and implementation plan for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project. Specifically, an MSE wall is referenced in the context of the "Stockpiles 1 & 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan." This plan is associated with the remediation of [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) at the site.

## Site Record

The "Stockpiles 1 & 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan" is a document within the project's remedial design implementation. It is dated January 2019. This plan is related to the management and sampling of [[barium-containing-soil]] stockpiles, identified as [[stockpile-1]] and stockpile-2. The plan also references a "Southern Slope Retaining Wall," which is likely the [[mse-wall]] in question. The grading plan associated with this sampling plan indicates proposed sampling locations near the "RW/Fence Line" for the [[bcs-stockpile]].

## Regulatory Status

The regulatory status of the MSE wall itself is not explicitly detailed in the provided sources. However, its construction and sampling plan are part of the overall remediation effort for [[barium-containing-soil]], which is subject to regulatory oversight.
