The [[fiscal-year-cost-estimate-2022-2023]] for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] outlines the anticipated oversight costs by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) for activities scheduled between July 1, 2022, and June 30, 2023. This estimate is provided to the [[caltrans]] (Caltrans) in accordance with Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5.

## Site Record

The DTSC's estimated oversight costs for the period of July 1, 2022, through June 30, 2023, are detailed in a table within the provided document. The total estimated cost for this fiscal year is $100,000.00. This estimate is based on DTSC's 2021-2022 contract estimation rates, as new rates had not yet been issued. The estimate covers projected costs for investigation and cleanup activities anticipated at the site during this period.

The tasks scheduled for this fiscal year include:
*   Review of the Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan.
*   Preparation of the Fiscal Year Cost Estimate.
*   Review and approval of the Interim Phase 1 Remedial Action Completion Report (RACR).
*   Completion of the O&M Agreement.
*   Completion of the Land Use Covenant (LUC).
*   Project Management.

The DTSC emphasizes that Responsible Parties/Project Proponents remain liable for all costs incurred by DTSC as required by law.

## Regulatory Status

The DTSC is responsible for providing oversight and cost estimates for remediation activities at the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. The estimate provided is in accordance with Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5.

## Open Items

The DTSC's cost estimate is based on projected activities and is subject to change. Responsible Parties/Project Proponents are liable for all actual costs incurred by the DTSC.
