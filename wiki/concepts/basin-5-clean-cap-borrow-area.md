## Basin 5 Clean Cap Borrow Area

### Overview

The Basin 5 Clean Cap Borrow Area is a site designated for the excavation of soil to be used as [[clean-cover-fill-material]] for the remediation of [[barium-containing-soil]] within Stockpiles 1 and 2. This borrow area is located adjacent and easterly of Carpenter Road within the State Route 132 right-of-way in Modesto, California. The characterization testing for this area was performed by [[geocon-consultants]].

### Site record

The proposed borrow area is approximately 750 feet long, 100 to 200 feet wide, and planned for excavation to a depth of approximately 10 feet. {{quote: "The proposed borrow area is approximately 750 feet long, 100 to 200 feet wide, and will extend to an approximate excavation depth of 10 feet."}}

On June 16, 2020, six exploratory trenches (BASIN5 1 through 6) were excavated within the borrow area on a grid pattern. {{quote: "On June 16, 2020 we sampled 6 exploratory trenches (BASIN5 1 through 6) on a grid pattern within the borrow area as depicted on the attached plan sheets."}} Soil samples were collected from each trench at depth intervals of 0 to 2 feet, 2 to 5 feet, and 5 to 10 feet. {{quote: "Soil samples were collected from each trench at depth intervals of 0 to 2 feet, 2 to 5 feet, and 5 to 10 feet."}} These samples were field composited into depth-discrete 3-part composites, yielding two 3-part composite samples per depth interval. {{quote: "We field composited the samples into depth discrete 3-part composites yielding two 3-part composite samples for each sampling depth interval."}}

Each composite sample was analyzed for Title 22 metals using EPA Test Methods 6010B and 7471A (mercury). {{quote: "Each composite sample was analyzed for Title 22 metals following Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) Test Methods 6010B and 7471A (mercury)."}} The two composite samples from the 0 to 2 feet depth interval were also analyzed for organochlorine pesticides (OCP) following EPA Test Method 8081A. {{quote: "The two composite samples collected from a depth interval of 0 to 2 feet were further assigned for organochlorine pesticide (OCP) analysis following EPA Test Method 8081A."}}

Native soil encountered in the trenches generally consisted of brown to tan, damp, clayey to fine sandy silt. {{quote: "Native soil encountered in the trenches generally consisted of brown to tan, damp, clayey to fine sandy silt."}} No field indicators of potential contamination impacts were observed. {{quote: "No field indicators of potential contamination impacts (i.e. staining, odors, debris, etc.) were observed during the field sampling activities."}}

Reported concentration ranges (in milligrams per kilogram [mg/kg]) for Title 22 metals, with site-specific background concentrations in parentheses, are as follows:
*   Arsenic: <1.0 to 1.8 (1.2 mg/kg) {{quote: "Arsenic <1.0 to 1.8 (1.2 mg/kg)"}}
*   Barium: 66 to 94 (72.8 mg/kg) {{quote: "Barium 66 to 94 (72.8 mg/kg)"}}
*   Chromium: 8.5 to 11 (8.6 mg/kg) {{quote: "Chromium 8.5 to 11 (8.6 mg/kg1 )"}}
*   Cobalt: 3.3 to 3.8 (4.4 mg/kg) {{quote: "Cobalt 3.3 to 3.8 (4.4 mg/kg)"}}
*   Copper: 7.5 to 9.3 (7.5 mg/kg) {{quote: "Copper 7.5 to 9.3 (7.5 mg/kg)"}}
*   Lead: 5.8 to 11 (2.0 mg/kg) {{quote: "Lead 5.8 to 11 (2.0 mg/kg)"}}
*   Nickel: 6.2 to 7.9 (5.3 mg/kg) {{quote: "Nickel 6.2 to 7.9 (5.3 mg/kg)"}}
*   Vanadium: 33 to 37 (31.3 mg/kg) {{quote: "Vanadium 33 to 37 (31.3 mg/kg)"}}
*   Zinc: 29 to 36 (26.3 mg/kg) {{quote: "Zinc 29 to 36 (26.3 mg/kg)"}}

Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, molybdenum, selenium, silver, thallium, mercury, and OCPs were not detected above laboratory reporting limits in any composite soil sample analyzed. {{quote: "Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, molybdenum, selenium, silver, thallium, mercury and OCPs were not detected in each composite soil sample analyzed above the laboratory reporting limits."}}

The reported metal concentrations are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. {{quote: "The reported metal concentrations in the composite samples are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations."}} Based on this data, the Basin 5 clean cap borrow area is considered suitable for generating clean cover fill material. {{quote: "Based on the data presented herein, the proposed Basin 5 clean cap borrow area appears to be suitable to generate clean cover fill materials for capping BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2."}} This information is intended for inclusion in the Removal Action Completion Report. {{quote: "This information will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted following completion of the stockpile capping activities."}}

### Regulatory status

The soil sampling strategy was developed in general accordance with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and was approved by Mr. Dean Wright with DTSC. {{quote: "The soil sampling strategy utilized was developed in general accordance with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and was approved Mr. Dean Wright with DTSC."}}
