The Basin 5 Clean Cap Borrow Area is a site designated for the excavation of soil to be used as [[clean-cover-fill-material]] for the remediation of [[barium-containing-soil]] within Stockpiles 1 and 2. This borrow area is located adjacent and easterly of Carpenter Road within the State Route 132 right-of-way in Modesto, California. The characterization testing for this area was performed by [[geocon-consultants]].

### Site record

The proposed borrow area is approximately 750 feet long, 100 to 200 feet wide, and planned for excavation to a depth of approximately 10 feet. On June 16, 2020, six exploratory trenches (BASIN5 1 through 6) were excavated within the borrow area on a grid pattern. Soil samples were collected from each trench at depth intervals of 0 to 2 feet, 2 to 5 feet, and 5 to 10 feet. These samples were field composited into depth-discrete 3-part composites, yielding two 3-part composite samples per depth interval.

Each composite sample was analyzed for Title 22 metals using EPA Test Methods 6010B and 7471A (mercury). The two composite samples from the 0 to 2 feet depth interval were also analyzed for organochlorine pesticides (OCP) following EPA Test Method 8081A.

Native soil encountered in the trenches generally consisted of brown to tan, damp, clayey to fine sandy silt. No field indicators of potential contamination impacts were observed.

Reported concentration ranges (in milligrams per kilogram [mg/kg]) for Title 22 metals, with site-specific background concentrations in parentheses, are as follows:
*   Arsenic: <1.0 to 1.8 (1.2 mg/kg)
*   Barium: 66 to 94 (72.8 mg/kg)
*   Chromium: 8.5 to 11 (8.6 mg/kg)
*   Cobalt: 3.3 to 3.8 (4.4 mg/kg)
*   Copper: 7.5 to 9.3 (7.5 mg/kg)
*   Lead: 5.8 to 11 (2.0 mg/kg)
*   Nickel: 6.2 to 7.9 (5.3 mg/kg)
*   Vanadium: 33 to 37 (31.3 mg/kg)
*   Zinc: 29 to 36 (26.3 mg/kg)

Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, molybdenum, selenium, silver, thallium, mercury, and OCPs were not detected above laboratory reporting limits in any composite soil sample analyzed. The reported metal concentrations are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations. Based on this data, the Basin 5 clean cap borrow area is considered suitable for generating clean cover fill material. This information is intended for inclusion in the Removal Action Completion Report.

### Regulatory status

The soil sampling strategy was developed in general accordance with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *2001 Information Advisory Clean Imported Fill Soil* and was approved by Mr. [[dean-wright]] with DTSC.
