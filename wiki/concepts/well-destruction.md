The Modesto Soil Stockpiles project involved the decommissioning of groundwater monitoring wells. This process, known as well destruction, is a standard practice to prevent wells from acting as conduits for contaminant migration or becoming conduits for surface water infiltration into the aquifer. The specific wells destroyed and the dates of destruction are detailed in the project's well destruction reports.

## Site record

Several groundwater monitoring wells were destroyed as part of the project. For instance, a Well Destruction Report dated June 19, 2019, documented the destruction of wells including MW-10, MW-4, and MW-6. Another Well Destruction Report, dated June 25, 2019, also details well destruction activities. These reports, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc., outline the methods and materials used for well decommissioning.

## Regulatory status

Well destruction activities are typically conducted under the oversight of regulatory agencies such as the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). The DTSC reviews and approves well destruction reports to ensure that the decommissioning was performed in accordance with established protocols and to confirm that the wells no longer pose a risk to groundwater resources. Approval of the final version of the Well Destruction Report signifies regulatory acceptance of these actions.
