## City of Modesto Encroachment Permit

### Overview
The City of Modesto Encroachment Permit was a required authorization for the well destruction activities conducted by [[geocon-consultants]] on behalf of [[caltrans]] at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. This permit ensured that the abandonment of groundwater monitoring wells complied with local municipal requirements.

### Site Record
Geocon Consultants, Inc. obtained well destruction permit number ENC2019-50174 from the City of Modesto. The requisite fees were paid for this permit. Geocon notified the City of Modesto at least 48 hours prior to commencing the well destruction activities, which took place on April 29 and April 30, 2019.

### Regulatory Status
The well destruction activities were performed in general accordance with the requirements of California Well Standards Bulletin 74-90, Monitoring Well Standards, Part III, and in general accordance with City of Modesto permit requirements.
