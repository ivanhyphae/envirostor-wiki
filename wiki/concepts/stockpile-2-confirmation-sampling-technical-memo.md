## Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling Technical Memo

### Overview
The Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling Technical Memo (dated March 9, 2020) documents the removal of barium-containing soil (BCS) from the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). The memo provides verification that these areas achieved the cleanup levels established in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) and are suitable for placement of clean embankment fill. The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) accepted the memo via email on March 10, 2020, authorizing the subsequent clean soil cover placement.

### Site Record
- **Document Title:** Stockpile 2 BCS Removal Verification Testing (Technical Memorandum)
- **Date:** March 9, 2020
- **Author:** John Juhrend (Geocon Consultants, Inc.)
- **Submitted To:** Richard C. Stewart, Caltrans Central Region Environmental Division
- **Regulatory Review:** Dean Wright (DTSC Project Manager), Perry Myers (DTSC), Steven Meeks (Waterboards)
- **Remedial Action:** Removal of barium-containing soil from the east and west ends of Stockpile 2
- **Verification Method:** Confirmation soil sampling following BCS removal
- **Documented Outcome:** Sampling results indicate that the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 have met the RDIP verification thresholds
- **DTSC Approval:** Email from Dean Wright, March 10, 2020, confirming DTSC acceptance of the memo and agreement that these areas are suitable for clean embankment fill placement

The memo was transmitted with a note that "BCS removal at Stockpile 3 should be completed tomorrow pending the results of final verification testing" (March 9, 2020 email from John Juhrend).

### Regulatory Status
- **Cleanup Standards:** Verification thresholds established in the RDIP, specifically for barium-containing soil removal
- **Regulatory Agency:** DTSC, with Richard C. Stewart (Caltrans Engineering Geologist, PG 7926) as the project liaison
- **Compliance Status:** DTSC confirmed that the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 have met RDIP verification thresholds and are suitable for clean cover placement
- **Documentation:** DTSC acceptance via email serves as formal regulatory approval; no written order or permit modification was issued

### Open Items
- No unresolved issues or pending deliverables for Stockpile 2 were identified in the source documents
- BCS removal at Stockpile 3 was pending completion as of March 9, 2020, with final verification testing to confirm compliance
- No information is documented regarding Stockpile 1 or the overall Stockpile 2 central section status
