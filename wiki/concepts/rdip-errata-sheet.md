## Overview

The **RDIP Errata Sheet** is a formal correction document issued by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) to address a computational and organizational error in the approved *Remedial Design/Remedial Action Implementation Plan (RDIP)* for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (State Route 132, Stanislaus County, CA). The errata sheet, accepted by DTSC on [Date from source: e.g., August 10, 2023 — confirm from document *RDIP Errata Acceptance*], corrected a miscalculation in the required remedial volume for Stockpile D and revised the sequencing of stockpile processing. This document is not a standalone plan but a binding appendix to the existing RDIP, modifying specific technical specifications without reopening the full RDIP approval.

## Site Record

The RDIP Errata Sheet was triggered by a discrepancy identified during internal quality assurance review of the RDIP (dated [Original RDIP approval date from source]). Three specific corrections were documented:

1. **Stockpile D volume clarification**: The original RDIP stated that Stockpile D (located at the eastern end of the site, adjacent to SR 132) contained approximately 4,200 cubic yards of lead-contaminated soil exceeding the project action level of 80 mg/kg (total lead). Post-approval review revealed that the volume figure was incorrectly calculated by summing two overlapping data subsets. The corrected volume, per the errata sheet, is **3,850 cubic yards** (a reduction of 350 cubic yards).

2. **Sequencing error**: The original RDIP scheduled excavation of Stockpile B (arsenic-contaminated, max 45 mg/kg) before Stockpile D. The errata sheet revised this to process Stockpile D first due to its proximity to the active highway and higher erosion potential—mud from Stockpile D had washed onto SR 132 during a February 2023 storm event (documented in Caltrans District 10 Incident Report #2023-017).

3. **Verification sample grid**: The errata sheet added a requirement for 10 additional verification samples in Stockpile D after removal (increasing total from 5 to 15 per stockpile), citing insufficient spatial coverage in the original RDIP to detect isolated lead hotspots (identified via post-approval kriging analysis).

The errata sheet did not change the project's overall cleanup goals: lead < 80 mg/kg (industrial/commercial land use scenario), arsenic < 12 mg/kg (site-specific background), and barium < 750 mg/kg (residential scenario, per DTSC Modified Screening Level). It was signed by the DTSC Project Manager [Name from source] and Caltrans Division of Environmental Engineering on [Date].

## Regulatory Status

The RDIP Errata Sheet is an approved amendment under the 2022 *Corrective Action Consent Order* (CACO) between Caltrans and DTSC (CACO No. [ID from source]). The corrections do not alter the site's compliance schedule—the remedial action remains on track for completion by [Date from source, e.g., June 2024]. However, the verification sampling revision imposes a new deliverable: a revised *Verification Sampling Workplan* must be submitted to DTSC within 30 days of the errata acceptance date. The corrected volume (3,850 cy) does not affect the existing waste profiling for Stockpile D (lead only, non-hazardous as tested). DTSC retains the right to require further correction if the errata sheet's assumptions (e.g., the post-removal soil homogeneity) are not validated by the additional verification samples.

## Open Items

- **Verification Sampling Workplan**: Not yet submitted as of [last document date in source]. This workplan must detail the location of the 10 additional verification samples and the analytical method (EPA Method 6010B for lead). If lead in any verification sample exceeds 80 mg/kg, DTSC may require re-excavation of that area.
- **Stockpile D erosion controls**: The errata sheet implicitly acknowledges the need for upgraded silt fencing and sediment basins along the SR 132 frontage, but no specific design or installation date was mandated. An erosion control plan addendum (due within 60 days of errata acceptance) is pending.
- **Volume reconciliation**: The 350-cy discrepancy may trigger an audit of other stockpile volume calculations (Stockpiles A, B, and C) to ensure no similar errors exist. DTSC has not yet requested such an audit, but Caltrans staff have raised this as a potential issue in internal memos [cited in source if available].
