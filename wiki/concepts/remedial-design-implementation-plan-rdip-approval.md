The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) Approval signifies the formal acceptance by the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] of the proposed plan for addressing [[barium]] contamination at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. This approval is a critical step, authorizing the implementation of the selected remedial actions.

## RDIP Approval

The [[central-valley-rwqcb]] approved the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the SR 132 Soil Stockpiles site on March 29, 2016. This approval was contingent upon the satisfactory resolution of comments provided by the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] in their letter dated March 1, 2016. The RDIP details the proposed remedial actions for the site, including the excavation and off-site disposal of [[barium]]-containing soil.

## Barium Concentrations and Remedial Goals

The RDIP addresses [[barium]] concentrations in soil that exceeded the California-MCL for drinking water. Specifically, the RDIP targets soil with [[barium]] concentrations above 1,000 mg/kg, which is the California-Primary-MCL for [[barium]]. The remedial design aims to consolidate and dispose of these soils to mitigate potential risks.
