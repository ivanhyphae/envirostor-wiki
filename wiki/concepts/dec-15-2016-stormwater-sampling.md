## Overview
The **December 15, 2016 Stormwater Sampling** event was a targeted investigation of surface water runoff and sediment at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). Conducted in response to a storm event, the sampling aimed to characterize potential contaminant release from stockpiled materials—specifically soils containing elevated concentrations of barium, arsenic, cobalt, chromium, lead, and vanadium—into adjacent drainage features. The sampling was documented in report *S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Dec 2016 SW Sampling_01.17*.

## Site Record
- **Date of sampling:** December 15, 2016, during a significant rainfall event (specific precipitation depth not recorded in source).
- **Sampling locations:** Three surface water and one sediment sample were collected at locations designated SW-1, SW-2, SW-3 (water), and SED-1 (sediment), positioned at drainage outfalls and receiving channels adjacent to the stockpile footprint.
- **Analytes detected (water samples):**
  - Barium: 127 µg/L at SW-1; 98 µg/L at SW-2; 142 µg/L at SW-3 (all below the federal MCL of 2,000 µg/L).
  - Arsenic: 4.2 µg/L at SW-1; 11.8 µg/L at SW-2; 3.1 µg/L at SW-3 (all below the MCL of 10 µg/L).
  - Total chromium: 22 µg/L at SW-1; 18 µg/L at SW-2; 31 µg/L at SW-3 (exceeded the MCL of 100 µg/L at none; however, hexavalent chromium not measured).
  - Cobalt, lead, vanadium: detected at all locations below relevant screening levels (e.g., lead ranged 2.1–6.7 µg/L, below the action level of 15 µg/L).
- **Sediment (SED-1) concentrations:**
  - Barium: 1,230 mg/kg (exceeds the DTSC residential screening level of 760 mg/kg but below the industrial screening level of 13,000 mg/kg).
  - Arsenic: 58 mg/kg (exceeds both residential and industrial screening levels of 0.07 mg/kg and 3.7 mg/kg, respectively).
  - Cobalt: 21 mg/kg (exceeds residential screening level of 10 mg/kg).
  - Chromium (total): 320 mg/kg (exceeds residential screening level of 100 mg/kg).
- **Comparison to applicable thresholds:** Surface water results all met California MCLs and action levels. Sediment results at SED-1 exceeded DTSC-modified ESL (Environmental Screening Level) for residential scenarios for arsenic, cobalt, and chromium; barium remained below industrial ESL.

## Regulatory Status
The December 15, 2016 stormwater sampling was performed to satisfy a compliance obligation under the site-specific Waste Discharge Requirements (WDRs) issued by the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB). At the time of the report, no exceedances of surface water quality objectives (MCLs) were documented, and the sampling results did not trigger immediate enforcement action. However, the sediment exceedances at SED-1 indicated that stockpile erosion control measures required improvement to prevent off-site migration of metal-laden fines. The project remained subject to pending RWQCB review of the report and potential revisions to the Stormwater Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP).

## Open Items
- No hexavalent chromium analysis was performed on water samples, though total chromium exceeded background levels at SW-3 (31 µg/L). Future sampling may require speciation.
- The sediment exceedances at SED-1 were noted but not formally addressed in the 2016 report; subsequent remedial actions (e.g., capping, removal) were not documented in this source.
- The report did not include a comparison of stormwater flows to receiving water body beneficial uses (e.g., aquatic habitat impairment).
- The RWQCB had not yet issued a written determination of compliance or non-compliance regarding this sampling event at the time of document preparation.
