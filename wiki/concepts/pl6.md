Pl6 refers to sampling location Pl-6 in the surface water and stormwater monitoring program described in the March 28, 2023 surface water letter for the SR 132 Modesto soil stockpiles site. The letter compares stormwater and surface water results against applicable water quality criteria and identifies which sample locations showed exceedances.

At Pl6, the March 28, 2023 letter reports results used to evaluate whether stormwater discharges from or near the stockpile area meet surface water quality thresholds. The letter is the vehicle for reporting these results but the specific analyte concentrations attributed to Pl6 are not laid out in the material available here beyond its inclusion among the monitored locations addressed by this correspondence.

Because the source material provided does not break out Pl6-specific concentrations, dates of individual sampling events at Pl6, or exceedance determinations tied explicitly to that location, this article cannot state values, trends, or thresholds for Pl6 without exceeding what the record supports. Any comparison to other stockpile locations (such as [[pl1]] through [[pl5]] and [[pl7]]) or to background locations ([[bg1]], [[bg3]]) would require data specific to Pl6 that is not present in the reviewed source.
