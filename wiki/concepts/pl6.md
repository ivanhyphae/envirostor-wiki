## Pl6

### Overview
Pl6 refers to a specific soil stockpile identified within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. This stockpile is subject to remediation efforts related to the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California.

### Site record
The soil stockpile designated as Pl6 was sampled on March 22, 2023. Analytical results for this sample indicated the presence of lead at a concentration of 110 mg/kg. This concentration was compared against the applicable screening level of 81 mg/kg.

### Regulatory status
The concentration of lead (110 mg/kg) detected in Pl6 exceeded the applicable screening level of 81 mg/kg.
