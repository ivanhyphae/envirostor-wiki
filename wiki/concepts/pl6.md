Pl6 is a surface water sampling location established as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpile Encapsulation Project in Stanislaus County, California. It was added to the surface water sampling network on November 2, 2022, following the completion of Phase I earthwork and the opening of SR 132 to traffic in November 2022.

## Site record
Surface water samples were collected from Pl6 on January 25, 2023. The laboratory reported the results for arsenic, barium, and chromium as below the laboratory's reporting limits. Specifically, arsenic was detected at 0.003 mg/L, barium at 0.04 mg/L, and chromium at 0.005 mg/L. During the surface water inspection on February 28, 2023, no active runoff was observed from the stormwater pipe at surface water sample location PL6. On March 10, 2023, surface water sample PL6 was collected from the northern side slope drainage outfall pipe near the northeast corner of Stockpile 2. At that time, surface water was actively flowing from the stormwater pipe.

## Regulatory status
The provided sources do not explicitly detail the regulatory status of Pl6. However, its inclusion in the surface water sampling network indicates its role in monitoring environmental conditions related to the project.
