Pl6 is a designation for a surface water sampling location within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpile Encapsulation Project in Stanislaus County, California. It was added to the surface water sampling network on November 2, 2022.

## Site record
Surface water samples were collected from Pl6 on January 25, 2023. The results for arsenic, barium, and chromium in these samples were reported as below the laboratory's reporting limits. Specifically, arsenic was detected at 0.003 mg/L {{quote: "Arsenic 0.003 mg/L"}}, barium at 0.04 mg/L {{quote: "Barium 0.04 mg/L"}}, and chromium at 0.005 mg/L {{quote: "Chromium 0.005 mg/L"}}. During the surface water inspection on February 28, 2023, there was no active runoff observed from the stormwater pipe at surface water sample location PL6 {{quote: "Surface water sample location PL6, there was no active runoff from the stormwater pipe."}}.

## Regulatory status
The regulatory status of Pl6 is not explicitly detailed in the provided sources. However, its inclusion in the surface water sampling network indicates it is subject to environmental monitoring requirements.
