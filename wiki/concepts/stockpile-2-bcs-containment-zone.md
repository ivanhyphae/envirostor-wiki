## Stockpile 2 BCS Containment Zone

### Overview
The Stockpile 2 BCS Containment Zone is an area within the State Route 132 Project in Modesto, California, designated for the management and disposal/reuse of barium-containing soil (BCS) spoils. These spoils are generated from foundation excavations for structures such as the Weather Station Foundation Slab/Anchor and Overhead Sign #1.

### Site Record
Excavations for the Weather Station Foundation Slab/Anchor, located at approximately Station 1138+60, were completed in July/August 2021. These excavations, reaching a depth of 4.5 feet, generated approximately 2 cubic yards of BCS material, which was spread on the adjacent ground surface.

A planned 5-foot-diameter by 25-foot-deep foundation excavation for Overhead Sign #1, located at approximately Station 1139+30, is expected to generate approximately 18.2 cubic yards of BCS material.

A conservative estimate for the total BCS material generated from both the weather station and overhead sign foundations is 40 cubic yards. This material is to be stockpiled prior to offsite landfill disposal or onsite reuse. The BCS material will be pre-moistened and placed on, then covered with, minimum 6-millimeter-thick plastic sheeting. This cover will be surrounded by erosion control measures and secured with ropes and weights.

Eight discrete soil samples will be collected from the stockpile on a grid pattern at various depths. These samples will be transported to California Laboratory Services for analysis. The laboratory will composite the eight discrete samples into two 4-part composite samples. These composites will be analyzed for Title 22 metals using EPA Test Methods 6010B and 7471A (mercury) with a 48-hour turn-around time. Soluble metals testing may be required if total metal concentrations are elevated, to determine if the BCS material is a California or Federal RCRA hazardous waste.

The total barium and lead concentrations will be compared to site-specific background levels:
*   Barium: 120 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg)
*   Lead: 3.8 mg/kg

If the total barium and lead analytical data do not exceed these background concentrations, the stockpiled BCS may be suitable for onsite reuse, subject to approval by Caltrans and DTSC. If onsite reuse is not approved, the BCS material will be profiled for acceptance at Forward Landfill in Manteca or another accepting landfill.

### Regulatory Status
The management and disposal/reuse of BCS material are subject to Caltrans and DTSC approval. Site-specific background levels for barium (120 mg/kg) and lead (3.8 mg/kg) are used as thresholds for determining suitability for onsite reuse.

### Open Items
The suitability of the BCS material for onsite reuse is pending analytical results and subsequent approval from Caltrans and DTSC.
