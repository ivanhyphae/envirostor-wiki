The Remedial Design and Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles outlines the technical approach and detailed plans for implementing the Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP). This RDIP, prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] for [[caltrans]], addresses the containment of barium-containing soil stockpiles as part of the SR 132 West Project in Stanislaus County, California. The plan details the remedial design, management procedures for barium-containing soil, and construction monitoring protocols for Interim Phase 1 of the project.

## RDIP Approval and Revisions

The Draft RDIP was submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on July 18, 2018, and received conditional approval from the [[dtsc]] on August 10, 2018. The [[dtsc]] requested revisions to the title and clarification that the RDIP was specific to soil stockpiles 1, 2, and 3. A revised RDIP dated September 14, 2018, incorporated these recommendations and received conditional approval from the [[dtsc]] on October 12, 2018, pending review of the 100% design plans.

A Second Variance to the RDIP was prepared on September 2, 2020, to address the use of clean cover fill material proposed by the highway contractor. This variance addressed the use of a minimum of 4 inches of Class 3 aggregate subbase as interim clean cover over consolidated [[barium-containing-soil-consolidation]] within Stockpiles 1 and 2, which was deemed to meet the intent of the RDIP.

## Project Organization and Responsibilities

[[caltrans]] is the site owner and responsible party for implementing the RAP and RDIP, with oversight from the [[dtsc]] and in cooperation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. [[geocon-consultants]] serves as Caltrans' environmental consultant, responsible for verifying that construction work conforms to the RAP and RDIP requirements. This includes supervising monitoring well abandonment, performing groundwater and stormwater monitoring, observing soil placement activities, conducting soil and import sampling, performing air monitoring, and preparing monthly status reports and a Remedial Action Completion Report.

The City of Modesto and its civil engineer, Dokken Engineering, are responsible for the project design and construction. The City's construction management firm and highway construction contractor are responsible for executing the work related to barium-containing soil management, including grading, placement, dust control, stormwater best management practices, and the placement of pavement and imported clean cover soil.

## Remedial Design and Implementation

The selected remedial alternative for the barium-containing soil stockpiles is containment by incorporating them as highway embankment fill. This involves consolidating the stockpiles within the project's lateral limits for roadway construction and backfill for retaining walls and bridge abutments. The RDIP specifies the placement of a minimum 12-inch-thick clean cover fill material over the [[barium-containing-soil-consolidation]] and aerially deposited lead (ADL) consolidation zones. As an alternative, a minimum 4-inch-thick aggregate base (AB) material could be placed on the upper stockpile surface, pending subsequent placement of a structural pavement section and hardscape.

The RDIP outlines an anticipated schedule for Interim Phase 1 construction, with RDIP final approval in early 2019, start of barium-containing soil containment in mid-2019, completion of containment in late 2019, and completion of Interim Phase 1 construction in late 2020.

## Monitoring and Reporting

The RDIP includes provisions for monitoring stormwater, health and safety, and air quality during construction. Geocon Consultants, Inc. is responsible for worker zone and perimeter air monitoring, periodic stormwater monitoring, and performing barium-containing soil removal verification sampling and analysis. Progress status reports, at a minimum monthly, will be submitted to Caltrans, the City, and DTSC to summarize completed activities, monitoring results, and any variances from the RDIP. Separate reports will be submitted to DTSC for annual groundwater monitoring and periodic stormwater monitoring events.
