## Kansas Avenue Ramp Safety Improvements

### Overview
The Kansas Avenue Ramp Safety Improvements are a set of physical modifications to the on- and off-ramps at the Kansas Avenue interchange with State Route 132 (SR 132) in Stanislaus County, California. These improvements were identified by Caltrans as a necessary remedial component of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file to address contamination on ramp shoulders and to ensure safe access for remedial activities. The scope and design of these improvements were clarified in a letter dated August 24, 2012, from Caltrans to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), responding to DTSC’s request for ramp-specific detail.

### Site record
The Kansas Avenue Ramp Safety Improvements are documented in the **Caltrans Ramp Clarification Letter dated 8.24.12**. According to this letter, the improvements encompass:

- **Ramp scope**: Modifications to both the eastbound (EB) on-ramp and the westbound (WB) off-ramp at the Kansas Avenue interchange.
- **Specific improvements**: 
  - For the **EB on-ramp**: Widening of the paved shoulder to provide a minimum 4-foot-wide stabilized area for emergency stops and maintenance vehicle access.
  - For the **WB off-ramp**: Addition of a new turnout (pullout) area approximately 50 feet long to allow temporary parking for remediation crew vehicles and equipment without blocking ramp traffic.
- **Rationale**: The improvements were explicitly tied to the soil stockpile remediation effort. The letter states that the ramps lacked adequate shoulder width for safe staging of remediation equipment and for emergency vehicles during construction. DTSC had requested clarification on how these improvements would affect the overall site footprint and contamination management.
- **Regulatory context**: The improvements were part of a **Corrective Action Plan (CAP)** amendment under the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA) permit for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles. Caltrans committed to completing these improvements prior to the start of Phase 2 soil removal on the ramps, which was scheduled for **spring 2013**.
- **Approval conditions**: DTSC approved the ramp improvements in a letter dated **September 12, 2012**, subject to conditions that:
  1. All excavated material from ramp widening be staged on-site and tested for contamination (see Soil Stockpiles Characterization).
  2. The improvements be constructed using standard Caltrans specifications for base rock and asphalt, with no imported fill from outside the project area.
  3. A pre-construction notification be submitted to DTSC 30 days prior to start of work.

### Regulatory status
The improvements are regulated under the **RCRA Corrective Action Program** administered by DTSC. As of the 2012 letter, the project was in **pre-construction compliance**—all approvals had been received, and Caltrans had satisfied DTSC’s information requirements. The improvements were subject to the following standards:
- **Work plan approval**: Required by DTSC under the Hazardous Waste Control Law (California Health and Safety Code, Division 20, Chapter 6.5) for any earth disturbance within the RCRA permit area.
- **Stormwater compliance**: The ramp improvements triggered coverage under the State Water Resources Control Board’s Construction General Permit (Order 2009-0009-DWQ), requiring a Storm Water Pollution Prevention Plan (SWPPP) to manage runoff from disturbed ramp shoulders.
- **Health and safety**: Routine adherence to Caltrans’ Title 8 safety protocols for highway work zones.

### Open items
- **Post-construction testing results**: The 2012 letter required that all excavated material from ramp widening be tested for contaminants. As of the available case file documents, no verification testing results confirming the status of these spoils have been submitted for record. DTSC may have requested a summary report of soil testing from the ramp widening areas.
- **Ramp sign replacement**: The letter noted that the WB off-ramp turnout location overlapped with an existing “No Parking” sign post. Caltrans committed to relocating this sign, but the status of that relocation is not documented in the referenced letter.
