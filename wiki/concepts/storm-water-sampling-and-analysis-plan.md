The Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP), prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] and dated December 13, 2023, sets out the storm water sampling activities for the encapsulated Modesto Soil Stockpiles beneath SR 132, under Caltrans Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2, EA No. 10-1E7003. Its stated purpose is to evaluate whether the containment system that encapsulates [[barium]]- and [[lead]]-contaminated soil behind retaining walls, bridge abutments, and beneath roadway pavement and clean soil between SR 99 and Carpenter Avenue is still isolating those materials, by testing whether storm water has been impacted by chemicals of potential concern from the stockpiles. The plan is not a first document but the latest in a lineage of storm/surface water sampling protocols going back to 2006, and it governs how the December 2023 storm water round — and its comparison against prior rounds — is to be interpreted.

## Regulatory lineage and authority

The SAP does not create new sampling protocols; it directs that sampling be performed under procedures already approved by [[dtsc]]. It expressly incorporates two earlier documents as its methodological basis:

- *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan*, Shaw Environmental, Inc., January 25, 2006
- *Addendum to Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan*, Geocon Consultants, Inc., February 20, 2013

A third reference, the *Surface Water Report, Modesto Soil Stockpile Encapsulation Project*, Stantec, March 10, 2023, documents the prior sampling round the December 2023 SAP follows. The letter transmitting the SAP was addressed to Richard Stewart, PG, of the Caltrans District 6 Hazardous Waste Branch, identifying [[caltrans]] as the recipient and responsible party overseeing implementation.

## Scope of work

The SAP's scope, as stated in the transmittal letter, comprises three elements: storm water sampling at locations shown on the site plan (Figure 2), analysis of the collected samples by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of a summary report of the sampling activities. The sampling locations are tied to the two encapsulated stockpiles — [[stockpile-1]] and Stockpile 2 — both encapsulated beneath SR 132, with proposed sampling points shown relative to those stockpile footprints on Figure 2 rather than at generic drainage points.

## Significance

Because the SAP frames the sampling as a test of containment integrity rather than routine environmental monitoring, the results it generates carry direct evidential weight on the question of whether the DTSC-approved encapsulation system — the engineering basis for leaving barium- and lead-contaminated soil in place under the roadway rather than removing it — continues to perform as designed. The plan itself does not report analytical results or state any exceedances; it is the procedural instrument under which the December 2023 sampling event was conducted.
