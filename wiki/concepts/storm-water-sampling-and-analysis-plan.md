## Storm Water Sampling And Analysis Plan

### Overview
The Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP) outlines proposed storm water sampling activities for the former [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site in Stanislaus County, California. The purpose of the sampling is to evaluate the integrity of the stockpile containment system, which was designed to isolate [[barium]] and [[lead]]-contaminated soil. The containment system was approved by the DTSC and incorporated into the SR 132 West Expressway project. Analysis of storm water samples will assess the effectiveness of this containment system and determine if storm water has been impacted by chemicals of potential concern from the encapsulated stockpiles. The sampling protocols are based on those approved by the DTSC in previous plans prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. and Geocon Consultants Inc..

### Site Record
The Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan was prepared by Geocon Consultants Inc. in December 2023, under [[caltrans]] Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2. The plan addresses storm water sampling associated with the former Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, located south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue. Specifically, it pertains to Stockpile-1 and Stockpile-2, which were encapsulated beneath SR 132. The plan references the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* by Shaw Environmental, Inc. (January 25, 2006) and the *Addendum to Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* by Geocon Consultants Inc. (February 20, 2013), as well as the *Surface Water Report, Modesto Soil Stockpile Encapsulation Project* by Stantec (March 10, 2023). The scope of services includes storm water sampling, laboratory analysis by a California-certified laboratory, and a summary report.

### Regulatory Status
The containment system for the stockpiles was approved by the DTSC. The storm water sampling activities are being performed in accordance with protocols established in plans previously approved by the DTSC.

### Open Items
The proposed scope of services includes the preparation of a summary report detailing the sampling activities.
