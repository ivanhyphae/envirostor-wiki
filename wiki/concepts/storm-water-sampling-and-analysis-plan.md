The Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP) outlines proposed storm water sampling activities for the former [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site in Stanislaus County, California. This plan is designed to evaluate the integrity of the containment system that isolates [[barium]] and [[lead]]-contaminated soil, which was incorporated into the SR 132 West Expressway project and approved by the DTSC. Analysis of storm water samples will assess the effectiveness of this containment system and determine if storm water has been impacted by chemicals of potential concern from the encapsulated stockpiles.

### Plan Development and Scope

The Storm Water Sampling and Analysis Plan was prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] in December 2023, under [[caltrans]] Contract No. 06A2767, Task Order No. 2. The plan addresses storm water sampling associated with the former Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, specifically Stockpile-1 and Stockpile-2, which were encapsulated beneath SR 132. The sampling protocols are based on those approved by the DTSC in previous plans, including the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* by Shaw Environmental, Inc. (January 25, 2006) and the *Addendum to Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* by Geocon Consultants Inc. (February 20, 2013). The scope of services includes storm water sampling, laboratory analysis by a California-certified laboratory, and the preparation of a summary report detailing the sampling activities.

### Regulatory Context

The containment system for the stockpiles, which isolates barium and lead-contaminated soil behind retaining walls and bridge abutments and beneath roadway pavements, was approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). The storm water sampling activities detailed in this SAP are being performed in accordance with protocols previously approved by the DTSC.
