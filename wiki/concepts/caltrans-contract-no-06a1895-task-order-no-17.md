## Caltrans Contract No 06a1895 Task Order No 17

This contract, specifically Task Order (TO) No. 17 under Contract No. 06A1895, involved surface water sampling activities conducted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] in Stanislaus County, California. The work was performed under Environmental Assistance (EA) No. 10-0X2700.

## Site Record

Surface water sampling was conducted on December 11, 2015. These activities were performed in accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), as established in the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. (January 2006) and an *Addendum to Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. (February 20, 2013). The scope included sampling, laboratory analysis by a California-certified laboratory, and reporting. The sampling locations were identified on a Site Plan, Figure 2, which also depicted Stockpiles 1 through 3.

## Regulatory Status

The sampling was conducted under protocols approved by the DTSC. Specific regulatory thresholds or compliance status related to the December 11, 2015 sampling event are not detailed in the provided source material.
