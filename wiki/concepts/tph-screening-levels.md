## TPH Screening Levels

### Overview
Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) screening levels are used to evaluate the impact of petroleum-contaminated soil at the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. Soil exceeding these levels requires specific management and disposal procedures.

### Site Record
The screening level for diesel range Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH-d) was lowered to 500 mg/kg to align with the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) screening level for commercial/industrial soil. {{quote: "Among other revisions, the screening level for diesel range total petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH‐d) has been lowered to 500 mg/kg to reflect the DTSC screening level for commercial/industrial soil."}} This adjustment was part of a revised [[soil-management-plan]] submitted on February 8, 2021. {{quote: "Attached is the latest revised Soil Management Plan from the City of Modesto."}}

Soil with TPH above screening levels is to be segregated and covered, and labeled until transported offsite. {{quote: "The SMP indicates that soil with Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) above screening levels will be segregated and covered. In addition, it is requested that this material be labeled until it is transported offsite."}}

Excavation of TPH-d impacted soils was planned at borings RB-10 and RB-11 within Basin 6A. {{quote: "Targeted excavation of TPH‐d impacted soils at borings RB‐10 and RB‐11 within Basin 6A and confirmation soil sampling"}}

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] reviewed and accepted a revised [[soil-management-plan]] on February 11, 2021. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board (WB) have reviewed the revised Soil Management Plan (SMP) dated February 8, 2021..."}} {{quote: "This e‐mail serves as the WB/DTSC official acceptance of the SMP."}}

A minor comment from DTSC requested that soil with TPH above screening levels be labeled until transported offsite. {{quote: "The SMP indicates that soil with Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) above screening levels will be segregated and covered. In addition, it is requested that this material be labeled until it is transported offsite."}}

Additionally, the SMP was revised to address the scenario where TPH-impacted soil remains at the maximum excavation depth. In such cases, [[caltrans]] should be alerted, and consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and DTSC is required to determine next steps. {{quote: "The SMP does not discuss the actions that will be taken should TPH impacted soil still be present when the maximum depth of the basins is achieved. Should this scenario occur, Caltrans should be alerted and consultation with the WB and DTSC should take place to determine appropriate next steps."}}

The [[central-valley-rwqcb]] had not yet responded to requests for a meeting to discuss the appropriate screening level for the protection of groundwater as of February 8, 2021. {{quote: "As you are aware, the Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) has not responded to requests for a meeting to discuss the appropriate screening level for protection of groundwater, however, the outcome of that discussion will apply to soils left onsite or reused onsite at finished grade."}}
