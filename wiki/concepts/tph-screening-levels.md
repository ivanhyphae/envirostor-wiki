## TPH Screening Levels

### Overview
Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) screening levels are benchmarks used to evaluate the presence and extent of petroleum contamination in soil at the [[sr-132-modesto]] project. Exceedances of these levels trigger specific management and disposal protocols for affected soil.

### Site Record
The screening level for diesel range Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH-d) was revised to 500 mg/kg, aligning with the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) screening level for commercial/industrial soil. This revision was incorporated into a [[soil-management-plan]] submitted on February 8, 2021. Soil identified with TPH concentrations above these screening levels is to be segregated, covered, and labeled prior to offsite transport. Targeted excavation of TPH-d impacted soils was planned at borings RB-10 and RB-11 within Basin 6A.

### Regulatory Status
A revised [[soil-management-plan]] dated February 8, 2021, was reviewed and officially accepted by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] on February 11, 2021. A minor comment from DTSC requested that soil exceeding TPH screening levels be labeled until transported offsite. The [[central-valley-rwqcb]] had not yet responded to requests for a meeting to discuss the appropriate screening level for the protection of groundwater as of February 8, 2021.

### Open Items
The accepted [[soil-management-plan]] did not initially detail actions for scenarios where TPH-impacted soil remains at the maximum excavation depth. It was stipulated that in such cases, [[caltrans]] should be alerted, and consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and DTSC would be required to determine the appropriate next steps.
