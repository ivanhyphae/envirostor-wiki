## Remedial Action Plan Approval

### Overview
Remedial Action Plan (RAP) approval signifies the formal acceptance by regulatory agencies of the proposed strategy for addressing contamination at a site. For the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, this involved reviewing and approving the plan detailing how the stockpiled soil would be managed and remediated.

### Site Record
The Remedial Action Plan for the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles was approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) on March 29, 2017. This approval was a key step in the remediation process for the soil stockpiles located at the SR 132 project site in Stanislaus County.

### Regulatory Status
The approval of the Remedial Action Plan by the DTSC indicates that the proposed remediation strategy met the necessary regulatory requirements at the time of approval.
