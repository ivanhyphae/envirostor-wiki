Remedial Action Plan (RAP) approval is the formal regulatory acceptance of the proposed strategy for addressing contamination at a site. For the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles]], this approval by the [[dtsc]] was a critical step in authorizing the management and remediation of the stockpiled soil.

### Approval of the Remedial Action Plan

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) formally approved the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles]] on March 29, 2017. This approval, documented in a letter from DTSC, signifies that the proposed actions for managing the soil met regulatory requirements. The RAP outlined the strategy for addressing contamination concerns associated with the stockpiled soil generated during the [[sr-132-west-expressway-project]] in Stanislaus County.

The approval letter from DTSC, dated March 29, 2017, confirmed the acceptance of the RAP. This decision was based on the review of the proposed remedial measures, which were designed to manage the soil stockpiles in accordance with applicable regulations.
