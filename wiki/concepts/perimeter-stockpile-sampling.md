## Overview
Perimeter stockpile sampling is a sampling strategy implemented at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This method focuses on collecting samples from the boundaries of soil stockpiles. The objective of this sampling approach is to characterize the soil composition at the stockpile perimeters, which informs decisions regarding stockpile management and potential off-site migration of contaminants.

### Site Record
The methodology for perimeter stockpile sampling is detailed in the "Modesto Soil Stockpiles Fenceline Workplan," dated September 18, 2012. This workplan was prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] under [[project-no-s9525-06-44]] and is associated with [[ea-no-10-403500]]. The sampling activities were intended to be conducted in accordance with the approved [[sampling-and-analysis-plan]].

### Regulatory Status
The provided source material does not detail the specific regulatory context or applicable standards for perimeter stockpile sampling. However, this sampling strategy was developed as part of the broader remediation efforts for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles.
