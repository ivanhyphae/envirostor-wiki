## Perimeter Stockpile Sampling

Perimeter stockpile sampling refers to a specific sampling strategy employed at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site, focusing on the boundaries of soil stockpiles. This method was detailed in the "Modesto Soil Stockpiles Fenceline Workplan" dated September 18, 2012, prepared by [[geocon-consultants]]. The purpose of this sampling was to assess the composition of soil at the perimeter of stockpiles, likely to inform decisions regarding their management and potential off-site migration of constituents.

### Site Record

The "Modesto Soil Stockpiles Fenceline Workplan" outlines the methodology for perimeter stockpile sampling. This workplan was developed under [[project-no-s9525-06-44]] and is associated with [[ea-no-10-403500]]. The sampling was intended to be conducted in accordance with the approved [[sampling-and-analysis-plan]]. The workplan itself is dated September 18, 2012.

### Regulatory Status

The specific regulatory context and applicable standards for perimeter stockpile sampling are not detailed within the provided source material. However, the sampling strategy was developed as part of a larger remediation effort for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles.
