## Overview

The "No Action Alternative" is a baseline remedial alternative evaluated as part of the Feasibility Study (FS) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] remediation case file. It represents the scenario where no active remediation or management measures are implemented beyond the existing, minimal site controls. This alternative is required by the National Oil and Hazardous Substances Pollution Contingency Plan (NCP) for comparison against other potential remedial actions.

## Site Record

Under a no-action scenario, the stockpiles would remain in place without any access restrictions, fencing, or monitoring and maintenance. {{quote: "Under a no-action scenario the stockpiles would remain in place. There would be no access restrictions, no fencing, and no monitoring and maintenance."}} However, the sources note that as long as [[caltrans]] owns and controls the property as State right-of-way, they would maintain the perimeter fence and continue restricting access to Caltrans-authorized personnel. {{quote: "However, as long as Caltrans continues to own and control the property as State right-of-way they would maintain the perimeter fence and continue restricting access to Caltrans-authorized personnel."}} In this context, the most likely site occupant would be a trespasser. {{quote: "Therefore, the most likely site occupant would be a trespasser."}}

The 2007 HHRA and its recent update concluded that the concentrations of COPCs in the stockpiles do not pose an unacceptable level of health risk to an onsite trespasser. {{quote: "The 2007 HHRA and recent update to the HHRA concluded that the concentrations of COPCs in the stockpiles do not pose an unacceptable level of health risk to an onsite trespasser."}} Therefore, the no-action alternative can be considered protective of human health as long as land use remains the same and access is restricted. {{quote: "The no action alternative can therefore be considered protective of human health as long as land use remains the same and access is restricted."}}

This alternative would be the least protective of the environment, as it would not reduce the contaminant mass or the potential for COPCs to impact surface or groundwater quality. {{quote: "The no action alternative would be the least protective of the environment in that it would not reduce the contaminant mass or the potential of the COPCs to impact surface or groundwater quality."}} It would not reduce the toxicity, mobility, and/or volume of COPCs in the stockpiles. {{quote: "This alternative will not reduce the toxicity, mobility, and/or volume of COPCs in the stockpiles."}}

Mobility via erosion from wind or stormwater infiltration is limited by the vegetative cover. {{quote: "With respect to mobility of the COPCs in the stockpiles, mobility via erosion from wind or stormwater infiltration is limited by the vegetative cover."}} COPC concentrations in groundwater samples collected from monitoring wells adjacent to and downgradient of, and native soil samples collected from beneath, the stockpiles are inconclusive with respect to COPC migration from the stockpiles. {{quote: "Further, COPC concentrations in groundwater samples collected from monitoring wells adjacent to and downgradient of, and native soil samples collected from beneath, the stockpiles are inconclusive with respect to COPC migration from the stockpiles."}}

The no-action alternative is readily implementable because it requires no labor, materials, or equipment. {{quote: "No action is readily implementable because it requires no labor, materials, or equipment."}} There is no cost associated with this alternative. {{quote: "There is no cost associated with this alternative."}}

## Regulatory Status

This alternative would not meet State or Federal regulations with respect to hazardous waste levels of COPCs in soil on the Site because of the lack of site control and public notification. {{quote: "This alternative would not meet State or Federal regulations with respect to hazardous waste levels of COPCs in soil on the Site because of the lack of site control and public notification."}} It would have the lowest level of regulatory acceptance because of the lack of site controls and monitoring and maintenance. {{quote: "This alternative would have the lowest level of regulatory acceptance because of the lack of site controls and monitoring and maintenance."}}

## Community Acceptance

This alternative would likely have the lowest level of community acceptance due to the perceived threat to human health and the environment. {{quote: "It also would likely have the lowest level of community acceptance due to the perceived threat to human health and the environment."}} Although the presence of the stockpiles has been generally acceptable to the community for five decades, this alternative would likely not remain acceptable to the public due to an increased perception of risk to human health and the environment associated with the stockpiles. {{quote: "Although the presence of the stockpiles has been generally acceptable to the community for five decades, this alternative would likely not remain acceptable to the public due to an increased perception of risk to human health and the environment associated with the stockpiles."}}
