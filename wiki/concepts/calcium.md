## Calcium

### Overview
Calcium is a dissolved metal that has been analyzed in groundwater samples collected at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site.

### Site Record
In the October 2016 sampling event, calcium was reported for samples PL1 through PL5, BG1, and BG2. The March 2018 sampling event also reported calcium for some or all of the samples.

During the October 2016 sampling, samples PL1 through PL5, BG1, and BG2 were diluted due to failing internal standards in the original run and possible matrix interference. Specifically, PL1, PL3, PL5, BG1, and BG2 were diluted for dissolved calcium.

### Regulatory Status
No primary or secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) were exceeded for calcium in the reported sampling events.
