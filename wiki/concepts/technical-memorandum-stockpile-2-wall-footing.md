## Overview

The Technical Memorandum Stockpile 2 Wall Footing is a document submitted to and accepted by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) on June 10, 2024, as documented in the E-mail Acceptance of the Stockpile 2 Wall Footing Tech Memo. The memorandum addresses remedial design for a proposed wall footing at Stockpile 2, a soil stockpile located along State Route 132 (SR 132) in Stanislaus County, California, within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project. The purpose of the wall footing is to support a containment structure for Stockpile 2, which contains soil with elevated levels of contaminants, including barium, arsenic, and lead, exceeding background thresholds. The Tech Memo provides engineering specifications for the footing’s dimensions, depth, and material composition to ensure structural integrity and prevent contaminant migration.

## Site record

- **Stockpile 2 location:** Along SR 132 in Stanislaus County, CA, as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. The stockpile contains soil from historical highway construction and maintenance activities.
- **Contaminant concentrations (as reported in project documents):** Barium at 760 mg/kg (exceeding DTSC’s California Human Health Screening Level of 150 mg/kg for residential soil); arsenic at 45 mg/kg (exceeding the background threshold of 12 mg/kg); lead at 350 mg/kg (exceeding the background threshold of 80 mg/kg). Sampling dates for these specific values are not specified in the provided sources, but they are referenced in the context of Stockpile 2 characterization.
- **Wall footing specifications (from Tech Memo):** The footing is designed as a continuous reinforced concrete strip footing, 2.5 feet wide and 1.5 feet deep, set at a minimum depth of 3.0 feet below grade. The bottom elevation is at 95.0 feet mean sea level (MSL). Reinforcement consists of #4 rebar at 18 inches on center, both longitudinally and transversely, with a minimum concrete compressive strength of 3,000 psi at 28 days.
- **Verification results:** The Tech Memo does not include post-construction verification data; it is a design document. The E-mail Acceptance from DTSC on June 10, 2024, acknowledges receipt and approval of the memorandum for use in remedial design, but does not contain verification results.
- **Phases:** This design is part of Phase 2 remedial actions (2023–2024) for Stockpile 2, following Phase 1 characterization (2019–2020) that identified contaminant exceedances. The wall footing is intended to support a permanent cover system for the stockpile.

## Regulatory status

- **Applicable standards:** DTSC’s California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs) for residential soil: barium 150 mg/kg, arsenic 0.39 mg/kg, lead 80 mg/kg. Background thresholds for the site: arsenic 12 mg/kg, lead 80 mg/kg. For groundwater, Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) under the Safe Drinking Water Act: barium 1,000 µg/L, arsenic 10 µg/L, lead 15 µg/L.
- **Agencies:** DTSC is the lead regulatory agency under the California Environmental Protection Agency, with oversight from the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board for potential groundwater impacts. The project is conducted by Caltrans District 10.
- **Compliance status:** The design of the wall footing is accepted by DTSC as of June 10, 2024, per the E-mail Acceptance. No compliance violations are noted in the source documents. The Tech Memo states the design meets DTSC’s approved remedial action plan for Stockpile 2, which requires a containment system to prevent direct contact and leaching. However, post-construction verification (e.g., slope stability, permeability testing) is pending and will be documented in a future Construction Completion Report.

## Open items

- **Construction completion:** The wall footing has not yet been constructed as of the June 10, 2024, acceptance date. A Construction Completion Report, including as-built drawings and verification testing (e.g., concrete compressive strength tests, soil compaction around footing), is pending and has not been submitted to DTSC.
- **Contaminant migration risk:** While the footing is designed to support containment, the Tech Memo does not address long-term monitoring for barium or arsenic leaching into groundwater at Stockpile 2. A separate groundwater monitoring plan (referenced in the overall site Remedial Action Plan) remains an open requirement for this stockpile.
- **QA/QC documentation:** The Tech Memo references unspecified quality assurance/quality control procedures for concrete placement and rebar installation. These documents (e.g., material test reports, inspection logs) are listed as appendices to the full Stockpile 2 remedial design package but are not included in the provided sources.
