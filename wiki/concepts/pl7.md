## Overview
Pl7 is a constituent of concern at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, specifically related to surface water sampling. Its presence in surface water is being monitored in relation to potential impacts from the soil stockpiles.

## Site record
Surface water samples collected on March 7, 2023, showed Pl7 at a concentration of 0.000012 mg/L in sample SW-1. {{quote: "SW-1 0.000012 mg/L Pl7"}} This concentration was reported in a letter from Stantec Consulting Services Inc. to Caltrans dated March 9, 2023. {{quote: "SW-1 0.000012 mg/L Pl7"}}
