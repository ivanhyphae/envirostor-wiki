## BG2

BG2 is a background surface water sampling location situated near a storm water inlet north of Stockpile 2, along North Emerald Avenue. It is used to monitor surface water quality in the vicinity of the soil stockpiles.

## Site record

Surface water samples were collected from BG2 on multiple occasions.

On December 15, 2016, sample BG2 was collected from water flowing into a drop inlet north of Stockpile 2 on North Emerald Avenue. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, potassium, nickel, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported. Laboratory notes indicated that samples BG1 and BG2 were diluted due to high concentrations of calcium and sodium.

On January 8, 2018, sample BG2 was collected near a drop inlet north of Stockpile 2 on North Emerald Avenue. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. Antimony, arsenic, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported. Laboratory notes indicated that samples BG1 and BG2 were diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc due to high concentrations of target analytes. Samples BG1 and BG2 were also diluted for sulfide due to possible matrix interference.

Surface water samples could not be collected from BG1 and BG2 on March 24, 2017, due to insufficient water.

## Regulatory status

Manganese concentrations in sample BG2 exceeded its secondary MCL on December 15, 2016.
