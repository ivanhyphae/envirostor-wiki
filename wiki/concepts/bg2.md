Bg2 is one of two background surface water sample points established for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, sited at a storm drain inlet on North Emerald Avenue north of Stockpile 2. Paired with [[bg1]] on Loletta Avenue to the south, it was intended to characterize storm water quality unaffected by the stockpiles, against which the [[pl1]]–[[pl5]] perimeter and drainage-basin samples could be compared. Across the sampling rounds reported, Bg2 has not shown the isolated barium exceedance recorded historically at Stockpile 3, and manganese — the recurring exceedance at this site — has been reported at Bg2 without exceeding its secondary MCL, unlike its paired location Bg1.

## Location and purpose

Bg2 is collected from water flowing into a storm drain inlet on North Emerald Avenue, north of Stockpile 2, outside the influence of stockpile runoff. The location was approved by DTSC and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board as one of two background points bracketing Stockpile 2 (Bg1 to the south on Loletta Avenue, Bg2 to the north). Because it is upgradient/outside the encapsulated soil footprint, results at Bg2 serve as the comparison baseline for evaluating whether metals detected at the PL locations are attributable to the stockpiles rather than to ambient urban runoff.

## Sampling history and results

Bg2 was sampled during the October 2016 and December 2016 storm water events (and in earlier rounds referenced but not individually detailed in the available material, from April 2013 through October 2016).

**October 28, 2016.** Dissolved metals reported for Bg2 included antimony, barium, calcium, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, potassium, sodium, strontium, and zinc; cadmium, chromium, manganese, and nickel were also reported specifically for Bg2 (not Bg1). None of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs. Arsenic, beryllium, cobalt, lead, mercury, selenium, silver, thallium, and vanadium were not detected above method detection limits. The laboratory noted that Bg2 required dilution for several general minerals (nitrate, sulfide, chloride, sulfate) and for a suite of dissolved metals (barium, beryllium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, magnesium, manganese, nickel, potassium, sodium, thallium, vanadium) due to high analyte concentrations or possible matrix interference.

**December 15, 2016.** Dissolved metals reported for Bg2 (grouped with Bg1) included antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, potassium, nickel, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc. Unlike Bg1, Bg2's manganese concentration did not exceed the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l in this round — the exceedance was specific to Bg1. Arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected above method detection limits at Bg2. The report notes that, apart from the Bg1 manganese exceedance, results were within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported for Bg1 and Bg2, indicating a stable background signature across rounds.

## Significance for the site's compliance record

Because Bg2 is a background point rather than a stockpile-perimeter location, its clean results across both 2016 rounds — no primary or secondary MCL exceedances, and no repeat of the isolated 2006 barium spike seen at Stockpile 3 — support the consistent site conclusion that storm water runoff from the stockpiles was not migrating offsite and that ambient background metals concentrations in the surrounding drainage system were themselves unremarkable. The February 2026 DTSC letter approving the FY24/25 Storm Water and Surface Water Monitoring Report states that all storm water sampling results were within reporting limits, consistent with Bg2's individual round results showing no exceedances.
