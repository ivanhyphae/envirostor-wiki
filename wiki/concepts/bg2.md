## Overview
BG2 is a background surface water sampling location situated near a storm water inlet north of Stockpile 2, along North Emerald Avenue. It is used to monitor surface water quality in the vicinity of the soil stockpiles.

## Site record
Surface water samples were collected from BG2 on multiple occasions.

On December 15, 2016, sample BG2 was collected. {{quote: "We collected background sample BG2 from water flowing into a drop inlet north of Stockpile 2 on North Emerald Avenue (Photo 7)."}} The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. {{quote: "Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, potassium, nickel, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported for samples BG1 and BG2. Cobalt and lead were reported for sample BG1. The manganese concentration reported for sample BG1 exceeded its secondary MCL. With the exception of manganese, none of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs. Arsenic, beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective MDLs for samples BG1 and BG2. With the exception of manganese in BG1, the analytical results are within the same general range of concentrations as previously reported for BG1 and BG2."}} Laboratory notes indicated that samples BG1 and BG2 were diluted due to high concentrations of calcium and sodium. {{quote: "The laboratory notes state samples PL1 through PL4, BG1 and BG2 were diluted due to the high concentrations of the target analytes. PL1 through PL4, BG1 and BG2 were diluted for calcium and sodium."}}

On January 8, 2018, sample BG2 was collected. {{quote: "We collected sample BG2 near a drop inlet north of Stockpile 2 on North Emerald Avenue (Photo 7)."}} The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. {{quote: "Antimony, arsenic, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported for some or all of the samples. None of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary maximum contaminant levels (MCLs) with the exception of manganese in sample BG2. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected in the samples at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective laboratory reporting limits."}} Laboratory notes indicated that samples BG1 and BG2 were diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc due to high concentrations of target analytes. {{quote: "The laboratory notes state samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 and BG2 were diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc due to the high concentrations of the target analytes. Samples BG1 and BG2 were also diluted for sulfide due to possible matrix inference."}}

Surface water samples could not be collected from BG1 and BG2 on March 24, 2017, due to insufficient water. {{quote: "On March 24, 2017, we collected surface water samples from designated locations PL1 through PL5. We did not sample from locations BG1 and BG2 because there was insufficient water to sample."}}

## Regulatory status
Manganese concentrations in sample BG2 exceeded its secondary MCL. {{quote: "The manganese concentration reported for sample BG2 exceeded its secondary MCL."}}
