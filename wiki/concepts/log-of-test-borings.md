The Log of Test Borings (LOTB) for the [[north-carpenter-road-overcrossing]] project documents subsurface soil conditions encountered during geotechnical investigations. These logs provide critical data for understanding the stratigraphy and engineering properties of the soil at the project site, which is relevant to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file.

## Site record

The LOTB for the North Carpenter Road Overcrossing project, dated February 13, 2019, details three test borings: A-17-086, A-17-085, and A-17-084 {{quote: "Image /page/0/Figure/0 description: This image displays a Log of Test Borings for North Carpenter Road Overcrossing, containing a plan view and a cross-sectional profile view. The plan view shows the road alignment, proposed bridge, and locations for three boreholes labeled A-17-086, A-17-085, and A-17-084. Below the plan view, a detailed profile illustrates the depth and composition of the soil layers identified in these three boreholes, with elevations ranging from approximately -50 feet to 80 feet. Each borehole column provides specific soil classification data, including material descriptions, depth markers, and N-values. The document includes administrative details such as the district (10), route (99, 132), project date (2-13-19), and notes regarding drilling and sampling methods used by Geocon Consultants in 2017."}}. The borings were conducted by Geocon Consultants in 2017 {{quote: "The document includes administrative details such as the district (10), route (99, 132), project date (2-13-19), and notes regarding drilling and sampling methods used by Geocon Consultants in 2017."}}. The logs present soil classifications, depth intervals, and Standard Penetration Test (SPT) N-values for each borehole {{quote: "Each borehole column provides specific soil classification data, including material descriptions, depth markers, and N-values."}}. The elevations depicted in the cross-sectional profile range from approximately -50 feet to 80 feet {{quote: "Below the plan view, a detailed profile illustrates the depth and composition of the soil layers identified in these three boreholes, with elevations ranging from approximately -50 feet to 80 feet."}}.
