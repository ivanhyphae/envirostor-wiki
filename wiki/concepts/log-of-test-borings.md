## Overview
The Log of Test Borings (LOTB) for the [[north-carpenter-road-overcrossing]] project documents subsurface soil conditions encountered during geotechnical investigations. These logs provide critical data for understanding the stratigraphy and engineering properties of the soil at the project site, which is relevant to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file.

## Site record
The LOTB for the North Carpenter Road Overcrossing project, dated February 13, 2019, details three test borings: A-17-086, A-17-085, and A-17-084. The borings were conducted by Geocon Consultants in 2017. The logs present soil classifications, depth intervals, and Standard Penetration Test (SPT) N-values for each borehole. The elevations depicted in the cross-sectional profile range from approximately -50 feet to 80 feet.
