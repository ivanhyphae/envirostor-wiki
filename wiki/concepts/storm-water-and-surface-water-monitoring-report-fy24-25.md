The [[storm-water-and-surface-water-monitoring-report-fy24-25]] is a document prepared by Geocon-Consultants on behalf of [[caltrans]] for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file (Site Code: 900259). This report details the results of storm water sampling conducted to evaluate the integrity of the stockpile containment system during qualifying rain events. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) reviewed and approved this report.

## Site Record

The Storm Water Sampling Report describes the results of storm water sampling used to evaluate the integrity of the stockpile containment system during qualifying rain events. The results of the Storm Water Sampling Report were all within reporting limits. {{quote: "In addition to groundwater sampling, surface water run-off is also collected to ensure the metals in the soil are not displaced into the surface water. The Storm Water Sampling Report describes the results of storm water sampling used to evaluate the integrity of the stockpile containment system during qualifying rain events. The results of the Storm Water Sampling Report were all within reporting limits."}}

## Regulatory Status

The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) reviewed the Storm Water Sampling Report, with input from geologists from their Geological Services Branch (GSB) and toxicologists from their Human and Ecological Risk Office (HERO). The DTSC has approved the Storm Water Sampling Report. {{quote: "DTSC's review process is coordinated by the Project Manager and includes input from technical staff. The Storm Water Sampling Report was reviewed by geologists from our Geological Services Branch (GSB) and toxicologists from our Human and Ecological Risk Office (HERO) and is hereby approved."}}
