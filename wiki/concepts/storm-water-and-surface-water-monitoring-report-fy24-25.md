The [[storm-water-and-surface-water-monitoring-report-fy24-25]] is a deliverable prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] for [[caltrans]] concerning the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file (Site Code: 900259). This report documents the results of storm water sampling conducted to assess the effectiveness of the stockpile containment system during rain events. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) has reviewed and approved this report.

## Site Record

The Storm Water Sampling Report details the findings from storm water sampling, which was performed to evaluate the integrity of the stockpile containment system during qualifying rain events. The results obtained from the Storm Water Sampling Report indicated that all sampled parameters were within their respective reporting limits.

## Regulatory Status

The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) conducted a review of the Storm Water Sampling Report. This review incorporated input from geologists within the DTSC's Geological Services Branch (GSB) and toxicologists from their Human and Ecological Risk Office (HERO). The DTSC has formally approved the Storm Water Sampling Report.
