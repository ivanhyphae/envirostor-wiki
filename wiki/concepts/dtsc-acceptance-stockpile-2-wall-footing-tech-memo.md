The Stockpile 2 Wall Footing Tech Memo, prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] and dated June 18, 2020, addressed sampling along the [[mse-wall]] footing excavation on Stockpile 2's southerly slope. It concluded that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) required disposal in the project's fill containment zones, while soil below 3 feet bgs was suitable for reuse as embankment fill or clean cover. [[dean-wright]] of [[dtsc]] confirmed agency acceptance of the memo by email on June 22, 2020, with concurrence from the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], closing out the question of how this soil could be managed without a separate variance.

## Revision and the barium/lead correction

Richard Stewart of Caltrans transmitted a revised version of the memo on June 18, 2020, correcting an error in the third paragraph: a value of 120 mg/kg had been attributed to lead but was actually a [[barium]] result. The correction did not change the memo's underlying determination — the 3-foot bgs threshold and disposition of soil above and below it were unaffected. No lead or barium concentrations beyond the corrected attribution are given in the available material.

## DTSC and Water Board acceptance

Dean Wright's June 22, 2020 email stated that DTSC and the Water Board had reviewed the memo and agreed with its findings on the 3-foot bgs threshold. The email made two determinations with direct procedural effect:

- Soil below 3 feet bgs along the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing was deemed part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan.
- Because that soil was already covered by the existing VR, Caltrans did not need to submit another variance request before using it as embankment fill or capping material.

The email states it "serves as DTSC's official acceptance of the Memo," making this the operative approval document for how Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing soil could be classified and reused, distinct from the containment-zone disposal required for the upper 3 feet.
