## Overview
Copper is a metal detected in stormwater samples collected as part of the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. Its presence was evaluated against Primary or Secondary drinking water Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs).

## Site record
Copper was detected in each of the storm water samples {{quote: "Barium, copper, and zinc were detected in each of the storm water samples at concentrations that do not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs."}}.

Copper was also detected in one or more of the surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023 {{quote: "The following metals were detected in one or more of the surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023: arsenic, barium, chromium, copper, lead, molybdenum, selenium, vanadium, zinc, strontium, and mercury."}}. Some of these detections were flagged with a "J" value, indicating an estimated concentration {{quote: "The laboratory flagged some of the data with a \"J\" value, meaning that the value is estimated."}}. Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, nickel, silver, and thallium were not reported above the MDLs in the background and surface water samples {{quote: "Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, nickel, silver, and thallium were not reported above the MDLs in the background and surface water samples."}}.

The analytical results from the March 10, 2023 sampling event were the first surface water samples collected since road construction was completed and the stockpiles encapsulated {{quote: "The analytical results from the March 10, 2023 sampling event were the first surface water samples collected since road construction was completed and the stockpiles encapsulated."}}.

## Regulatory status
Copper concentrations in stormwater samples did not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs {{quote: "Barium, copper, and zinc were detected in each of the storm water samples at concentrations that do not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs."}}. The reported metal detections in surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023 were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such exist {{quote: "The reported metal detections were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such exist."}}.
