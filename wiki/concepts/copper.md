# Copper

## Overview
Copper is a metal that has been detected in surface water and stormwater samples collected at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. Its presence has been evaluated against state and federal drinking water Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs).

## Site record
Copper was detected in stormwater samples collected on October 28, 2016, with concentrations not exceeding their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs. {{quote: "Barium, copper, and zinc were detected in each of the storm water samples at concentrations that do not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs."}} (wiki/sources/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Oct 2016 SW Sampling_12.16/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Oct 2016 SW Sampling_12.16.md).

In surface water samples collected on March 24, 2017, copper was reported for samples PL1 through PL5. The concentrations did not exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs. (wiki/sources/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Mar 2017 SW Sampling_06.17/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Mar 2017 SW Sampling_06.17.md).

Copper was also detected in surface water samples collected on January 8, 2018, and March 22, 2018. In both instances, none of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs. (wiki/sources/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Jan 2018 SW Sampling_02.18/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Jan 2018 SW Sampling_02.18.md, wiki/sources/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles March 2018 SW Sampling_04.18/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles March 2018 SW Sampling_04.18.md).

On March 10, 2023, copper was detected in one or more of the surface water samples. {{quote: "The following metals were detected in one or more of the surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023: arsenic, barium, chromium, copper, lead, molybdenum, selenium, vanadium, zinc, strontium, and mercury."}} (wiki/sources/06A2542ct_to97_SurfaceWaterLetter_final.20230328/06A2542ct_to97_SurfaceWaterLetter_final.20230328.md). Some of these detections were flagged with a "J" value, indicating an estimated concentration. {{quote: "The laboratory flagged some of the data with a \"J\" value, meaning that the value is estimated."}} (wiki/sources/06A2542ct_to97_SurfaceWaterLetter_final.20230328/06A2542ct_to97_SurfaceWaterLetter_final.20230328.md).

## Regulatory status
Copper concentrations in stormwater samples collected on October 28, 2016, did not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs. {{quote: "Barium, copper, and zinc were detected in each of the storm water samples at concentrations that do not exceed their respective Primary or Secondary drinking water MCLs."}} (wiki/sources/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Oct 2016 SW Sampling_12.16/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Oct 2016 SW Sampling_12.16.md).

In surface water samples collected on March 24, 2017, March 22, 2018, and January 8, 2018, copper concentrations were reported to be below their respective primary or secondary MCLs. (wiki/sources/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Mar 2017 SW Sampling_06.17/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Mar 2017 SW Sampling_06.17.md, wiki/sources/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Jan 2018 SW Sampling_02.18/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles Jan 2018 SW Sampling_02.18.md, wiki/sources/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles March 2018 SW Sampling_04.18/S1200-01-01 Modesto Stockpiles March 2018 SW Sampling_04.18.md).

The reported metal detections in surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023, were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such exist. {{quote: "The reported metal detections were below their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs), where such exist."}} (wiki/sources/06A2542ct_to97_SurfaceWaterLetter_final.20230328/06A2542ct_to97_SurfaceWaterLetter_final.20230328.md).
