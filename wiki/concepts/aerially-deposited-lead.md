## Overview

Aerially Deposited Lead (ADL) refers to lead contamination originating from historical leaded gasoline emissions, which accumulated in surface soils along major transportation corridors, particularly in dry climates. Within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles case file (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA), ADL is the primary contaminant of concern (COC) driving the remediation strategy. It is characterized as shallow, laterally extensive lead contamination in soil, distinct from point-source industrial releases, and managed under a Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) Variance Request process due to unique handling and reuse conditions.
