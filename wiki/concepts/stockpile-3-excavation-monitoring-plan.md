# Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan

## Overview
The Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan was prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] for [[caltrans]] to guide planned highway safety improvement construction excavations adjacent to the State Route 99 (SR99) Kansas Avenue Off-ramp in Modesto, California. This plan addresses the presence of approximately 121,000 cubic yards of fill embankment, including Stockpile 3, which was partially generated from excavations in evaporation ponds containing elevated metals, notably [[barium]]. The plan outlines procedures for air monitoring and verification of completed excavations within Stockpile 3.

## Site Record
The Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan was submitted on June 13, 2012, by [[geocon-consultants]] to Mr. Richard Stewart of Caltrans District 6. The project number associated with this plan is S9650-06-03, and it pertains to Contract No. 06A1634 and EA No. 10-0A671 for the SR99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off-Ramp in Modesto, California. The plan summarizes previous and recent site investigations and the health risks associated with identified contaminants, particularly [[barium]], in Stockpile 3. It details procedures for air monitoring and excavation verification during construction of planned off-ramp traffic safety and widening improvements.

Caltrans, in cooperation with the [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], is addressing the soil stockpiles, which were placed in the early 1960s. These stockpiles, totaling approximately 121,000 cubic yards, are located within Caltrans right-of-way west and east of SR99 immediately south of the Kansas Avenue interchange. They were partially generated from excavations in evaporation ponds containing elevated metals (notably barium) and other chemicals of potential concern (COPCs).

The plan specifically outlines monitoring activities associated with the excavation of the northern portion of Stockpile 3 during construction of the SR99 Kansas Avenue off-ramp traffic safety improvements.

## Regulatory Status
The plan was prepared in accordance with Caltrans Contract No. 06A1634 and Work Request EA No. 10-0A671.
