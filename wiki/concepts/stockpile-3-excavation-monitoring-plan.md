# Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan

## Overview
The Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan was prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] for the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) to guide planned highway safety improvement construction excavations adjacent to the State Route 99 (SR99) Kansas Avenue Off-ramp in Modesto, California. This plan addresses the presence of approximately 121,000 cubic yards of fill embankment, including Stockpile 3, which was partially generated from excavations in evaporation ponds containing elevated metals, notably [[barium]]. The plan outlines procedures for air monitoring and verification of completed excavations within Stockpile 3.

## Site Record
The Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan was submitted on June 13, 2012, by [[geocon-consultants]] to Mr. Richard Stewart of Caltrans District 6. The project number associated with this plan is S9650-06-03, and it pertains to Contract No. 06A1634 and EA No. 10-0A671 for the SR99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off-Ramp in Modesto, California. The plan summarizes previous and recent site investigations and the health risks associated with identified contaminants, particularly [[barium]], in Stockpile 3. It details procedures for air monitoring and excavation verification during construction.

## Regulatory Status
The plan was prepared in accordance with Caltrans Contract No. 06A1634 and Work Request EA No. 10-0A671. Caltrans, in cooperation with the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), is addressing the soil stockpiles, which were placed in the early 1960s. The plan addresses contaminants of potential concern (COPCs) found in Stockpile 3, including [[barium]].
