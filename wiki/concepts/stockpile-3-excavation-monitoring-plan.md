# Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan

## Overview
The Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan was prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] for the [[caltrans]] to guide planned highway safety improvement construction excavations adjacent to the State Route 99 (SR99) Kansas Avenue Off-ramp in Modesto, California. This plan addresses the presence of approximately 121,000 cubic yards of fill embankment, including Stockpile 3, which was partially generated from excavations in evaporation ponds containing elevated metals, notably [[barium]]. The plan outlines procedures for air monitoring and verification of completed excavations within Stockpile 3.

## Site Record
The Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan was submitted on June 13, 2012, by [[geocon-consultants]] to Mr. Richard Stewart of Caltrans District 6. {{quote: "Project No. S9650-06-03 June 13, 2012 Mr. Richard Stewart, PG California Department of Transportation - District 6 855 M Street, Suite 200 Fresno, California 93721 Subject: STOCKPILE 3 EXCAVATION MONITORING PLAN MODESTO RAMP REHABILITATION PROJECT STATE ROUTE 99 KANSAS AVENUE NORTHBOUND OFF-RAMP MODESTO, CALIFORNIA CONTRACT NO. 06A1634, EA NO. 10-0A671"}} The project number associated with this plan is S9650-06-03, and it pertains to Contract No. 06A1634 and EA No. 10-0A671 for the SR99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off-Ramp in Modesto, California. {{quote: "CONTRACT NO. 06A1634, EA NO. 10-0A671"}} The plan summarizes previous and recent site investigations and the health risks associated with identified contaminants, particularly [[barium]], in Stockpile 3. {{quote: "The Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan summarizes the results of previous and recent site investigations, provides a summary of health risk associated with the identified contaminants (notably barium) in Stockpile 3, and describes the procedures to perform air monitoring and verification of completed excavations within Stockpile 3 during construction of planned off-ramp traffic safety and widening improvements."}} It details procedures for air monitoring and excavation verification during construction. {{quote: "describes the procedures to perform air monitoring and verification of completed excavations within Stockpile 3 during construction of planned off-ramp traffic safety and widening improvements."}}

Caltrans, in cooperation with the [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], is addressing the soil stockpiles, which were placed in the early 1960s. {{quote: "Caltrans and the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in cooperation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), have entered into an Interagency Agreement to address the presence of approximately 121,000 cubic yards of fill embankment (Stockpiles 1 through 3) located within Caltrans right-of-way (ROW) west and east of SR99 immediately south of the Kansas Avenue interchange. The soil stockpiles were placed in the early 1960s for a future highway alignment and were partially generated from excavations in evaporation ponds containing elevated metals (notably barium) and other chemicals of potential concern (COPCs)."}}
