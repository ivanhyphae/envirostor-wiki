##Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan

### Overview
The Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan (the Plan) is a project-specific environmental monitoring document prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) in support of the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project (State Route 99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off‑Ramp, Contract No. 06A1634, EA No. 10‑0A671). Dated June 13, 2012 (Project No. S9650‑06‑03), the Plan describes air monitoring and excavation verification procedures to be implemented during construction of traffic safety and widening improvements in the northern portion of Stockpile 3.

The Plan was developed under an Interagency Agreement between Caltrans, the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) to address approximately 121,000 cubic yards of fill embankment (Stockpiles 1 through 3) located within Caltrans right‑of‑way west and east of SR99 immediately south of the Kansas Avenue interchange. These stockpiles were placed in the early 1960s and originated from excavations of evaporation ponds containing elevated metals—notably barium—and other chemicals of potential concern (COPCs).

### Site record
- **Document title:** Stockpile 3 Excavation Monitoring Plan, Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project, State Route 99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off‑Ramp, Modesto, California.
- **Date:** June 13, 2012.
- **Prepared by:** Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Project Manager John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG; Certified Industrial Hygienist Douglas S. Krause, CIH).
- **Addressee:** Richard Stewart, PG, Caltrans District 6 (Fresno, California).
- **Purpose:** Summarize previous and recent site investigations; summarize human health risk associated with Stockpile 3 contaminants (notably barium); describe procedures for air monitoring and verification of completed excavations within Stockpile 3 during construction.
- **Stockpile volume context:** Plan references a total of ~121,000 cubic yards of fill embankment across Stockpiles 1‑3.
- **Contaminant of primary concern:** Barium (others are COPCs but not individually listed in the source).
- **Monitoring scope:** Excavation of the *northern portion* of Stockpile 3 only.
- **Key elements of the Plan (as described):**
  - Scoping meeting (§4.1)
  - Air monitoring procedures
  - Verification of completed excavations
- **Supporting sections:** Prior Geocon site investigation data (§2.0), summary of human health risk assessment (§3.0), report of completion (§5.0).

The Plan does not include appendices in the source file (notes “no appx” in the file name). No specific concentrations, thresholds, or verification analytical results are provided in this document; it is a monitoring plan, not a results report.

### Regulatory status
- **Agencies involved:** Caltrans (lead), DTSC, RWQCB.
- **Legal framework:** Interagency Agreement among the three agencies to address the three stockpiles.
- **Applicable standards:** The Plan references a “Summary of Human Health Risk Assessment” (§3.0) but does not cite specific regulatory action levels or screening values in the excerpts. The contaminants, particularly barium, were evaluated for health risk.
- **Compliance status:** The Plan was submitted as a deliverable under Contract No. 06A1634 and Work Request EA No. 10‑0A671. The source does not indicate whether Caltrans or DTSC formally approved the Plan. No statements of compliance or non‑compliance are included.

### Open items
The source document is a submitted plan; no unresolved issues, pending deliverables, or contested claims are described. The Plan anticipates a “Report of Completion” (§5.0) to be generated after excavation monitoring is finished, but that report is not part of this document.
