## Overview

**Clean fill cover** is the remedial action selected for **Basin 5** at the **Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles** site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA) under the oversight of the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). The remedy involves placing certified clean fill material over impacted soil in Basin 5 to create a physical barrier, eliminate direct exposure pathways, and support revegetation. The cover was designed and validated in accordance with the *SR 132 Basin 5 Clean Fill Tech Memo revised 7-2-2020* (the "Tech Memo").

## Site record

- **Location:** Basin 5 is one of multiple former soil stockpile basins at the Modesto site, which historically received soil contaminated with petroleum hydrocarbons and metals (including arsenic, barium, chromium, lead, and nickel) during Caltrans construction projects.
- **Document:** The *SR 132 Basin 5 Clean Fill Tech Memo revised 7-2-2020* (dated July 2, 2020) specifies the cover design, material specifications, and verification requirements.
- **Cover design:** The clean fill cover consists of a minimum **2-foot** thickness of imported soil meeting DTSC-approved clean fill criteria. The material was sourced from certified borrow sites with analytical testing results demonstrating concentrations below the following thresholds:
  - Total petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH): **< 10 mg/kg** (as diesel and motor oil)
  - Arsenic: **< 12 mg/kg**
  - Barium: **< 750 mg/kg** (note: background barium in native soil ranged up to 760 mg/kg per the source)
  - Chromium (total): **< 100 mg/kg**
  - Lead: **< 80 mg/kg**
  - Nickel: **< 100 mg/kg**
  - All pesticides, PCBs, and volatile organic compounds: **non-detect** or below DTSC ESLs.
- **Placement:** Cover placement occurred in **Phase 1 (2019–2020)** . The Tech Memo documented that cover thickness was verified at a minimum of **2 feet** by **10 random compaction tests per 5,000 cubic yards** and **5 thickness measurements per 5,000 cubic yards**.
- **Verification results:** Post-placement verification sampling of the cover material (collected at a density of **1 sample per 5,000 cubic yards** for discrete parameters) showed all analytes below the thresholds, confirming compliance. No exceedances of clean fill criteria were reported in the Tech Memo.

## Regulatory status

- **Enforcing agency:** DTSC, under its Remedial Action Program for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site.
- **Applicable standards:** The clean fill cover is designed to meet the **DTSC VCA (Voluntary Cleanup Agreement) Workplan** and **Site Cleanup Order** for Basin 5. Cover material thresholds were set at DTSC’s **Screening Levels for Residential Soil** for the constituents of concern (COCs) listed above, except where background concentrations exceeded screening levels (e.g., barium at 760 mg/kg in native soil was accepted as a site-specific cleanup standard, per the Tech Memo).
- **Compliance status:** The remedy is considered **complete and compliant** for Basin 5 pending DTSC’s final closeout approval. No compliance exceedances are documented in the available record for the cover itself.

## Open items

- **Documentation gap:** The Tech Memo does not specify whether a **final closeout report** or **Operation and Maintenance (O&M) plan** for the cover has been submitted to DTSC or received approval. Long-term monitoring of cover integrity (e.g., against erosion, settlement, or vegetation failure) is not described in the existing record.
- **Adjacent basins:** The cover remedy for Basin 5 was implemented separately from other basins (Basins 1–4) which may have different remedies (e.g., excavation, offsite disposal). The relationship or cumulative media impact is not addressed in this Tech Memo.
