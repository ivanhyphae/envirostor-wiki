The [[clean-fill-cover]] is a component of the remediation strategy for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, specifically related to the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. It is a material used to cap or cover soil stockpiles, intended to prevent the migration of contaminants and reduce direct contact. The implementation and verification of the Clean Fill Cover are detailed in project documents such as the [[borrow-area-sampling-plan]].

## Site record

The [[borrow-area-sampling-plan]] outlines the requirements for the Clean Fill Cover material. It specifies that the material must meet certain criteria to be considered suitable for use as a clean cover. For instance, the plan details sampling and analysis protocols for borrow areas to ensure the extracted material is appropriate for the Clean Fill Cover. Specific analytical results for materials intended for the Clean Fill Cover are documented within the sampling plan and subsequent reports. For example, the plan requires that the borrow area material be sampled for specific constituents, and the results must meet predefined criteria before the material can be used as Clean Fill Cover.

## Regulatory status

The use and acceptance of the Clean Fill Cover are subject to regulatory oversight. The criteria for the Clean Fill Cover material are established to meet the requirements of regulatory agencies overseeing the remediation project. Approval of the borrow area material for use as Clean Fill Cover is contingent upon meeting these established criteria, which are designed to ensure the effectiveness of the cover in isolating potential contaminants.
