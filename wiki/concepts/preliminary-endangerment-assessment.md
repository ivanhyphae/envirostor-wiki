## Overview

The **Preliminary Endangerment Assessment (PEA)** for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site is the initial environmental investigation document prepared under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) and California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) oversight. It evaluates whether the stockpiled soils from State Route 132 (SR 132) construction, located in Stanislaus County (specifically near the SR 132/CA-99 interchange), pose a significant threat to human health or the environment, and whether further action (e.g., a Remedial Investigation or removal) is warranted. The PEA was completed under Caltrans Task Order 4 (SR 132-99) and submitted to DTSC for review.

## Site Record

The PEA is grounded in the project record documented in **Caltrans SR 132-99 Task Order 4**, which includes:

- **Stockpile locations**: Two primary soil stockpiles (Stockpile A and Stockpile B) located adjacent to the SR 132 corridor within Caltrans right-of-way, approximately 1.5 miles east of the CA-99 interchange.
- **Sample collection dates**: Phase 1 sampling conducted April–May 2019; Phase 2 sampling August–September 2019.
- **Soil analytical data**: Total metals (including arsenic, barium, chromium, lead), polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs), and petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH-g, TPH-d, TPH-mo).
- **Key exceedances detected**:
  - **Arsenic**: Up to 52 mg/kg in Stockpile B (DTSC California Human Health Screening Level [CHHSL] for residential soil: 0.39 mg/kg; commercial/industrial: 14 mg/kg). Exceeds both residential and commercial thresholds.
  - **Barium**: Up to 760 mg/kg in Stockpile A (DTSC CHHSL for residential: 5,200 mg/kg; not exceeded, but reported as elevated relative to background [386 mg/kg]).
  - **Lead**: Up to 210 mg/kg in Stockpile B (DTSC CHHSL for residential: 80 mg/kg; commercial: 800 mg/kg). Exceeds residential threshold but not commercial.
  - **TPH-d**: Up to 480 mg/kg in Stockpile A (RWQCB Environmental Screening Level [ESL] for soil: 100 mg/kg). Exceeds ESL.
  - **Benzo(a)pyrene**: Up to 5.2 mg/kg in Stockpile B (DTSC CHHSL for residential: 0.055 mg/kg; commercial: 0.5 mg/kg). Exceeds both thresholds.
- **Groundwater monitoring**: Not part of the PEA scope; noted as a potential concern only if stockpiles are left in place and leachate forms.
- **Volume estimate**: Each stockpile ~4,000 cubic yards (combined ~8,000 cubic yards).

## Regulatory Status

- **Oversight agency**: DTSC (Project ID: 20210057), with concurrence from the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) for groundwater concerns.
- **Applicable standards used in the PEA**:
  - **Human health screening**: DTSC CHHSLs (2019) for residential and commercial exposure scenarios.
  - **Ecological screening**: U.S. EPA Region 9 Preliminary Remediation Goals (PRGs) for soil invertebrates.
  - **Groundwater protection**: RWQCB Environmental Screening Levels (ESLs) for soil-to-groundwater migration.
- **Current status (as of PEA submittal, October 2020)**: The PEA concluded that the stockpiles, if left in place without removal, present a **significant risk to human health** under a residential land use scenario (site is currently Caltrans right-of-way, not residential, but the risk determination was based on potential future use or off-site migration). DTSC issued a **letter of concurrence** on December 15, 2020, requiring a **Remedial Action Plan (RAP)** to address the stockpiles.
- **Ongoing compliance**: Caltrans is required to implement interim measures (covering stockpiles with clean soil, erosion control, fencing) pending removal or remediation. Verification sampling for removal is not yet documented in the project record.

## Open Items

- **Remedial Action Plan (RAP)**: Not yet submitted to DTSC as of the latest record (January 2021). The PEA recommended on-site consolidation with a soil cover (capping) or off-site disposal; final remedy selection is pending.
- **Groundwater characterization**: Not addressed in the PEA; DTSC requested a supplemental investigation if stockpiles are not removed within 2 years.
- **Background soil levels**: Up to 386 mg/kg barium and 14 mg/kg arsenic (est. from nearby undisturbed areas) were used as reference, but no formal background study was completed—this may be contested in future phases.
- **Public participation**: DTSC initiated a 30-day public comment period on the PEA in November 2020, but no comment letters are documented in the case file.
