## Overview

Barium is a contaminant of concern at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County). It was detected in both stockpiled soil and underlying groundwater during site characterization. The final removal action addressed barium‑impacted soil above regulatory screening levels, and post‑remediation verification demonstrated that remaining concentrations meet applicable standards.

## Site record

Soil samples collected from the stockpiles (Phase 1 2019–2020) reported barium concentrations up to **760 mg/kg** as documented in the *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report_2.24*. This value exceeded the DTSC’s residential direct‑exposure screening level at the time, triggering the need for removal.

Groundwater monitoring results from the *GND Water Monitoring Rpt March 2102* (presumed 2012) and the *S9525-06-25 Modesto Stockpiles Well Install and June 2012 GW Report 1112* indicate barium in monitoring wells including **MW-5**. Concentrations in MW‑5 ranged from **450 µg/L to 1,200 µg/L** during the 2012 sampling events, with the high end exceeding the federal MCL of **1,000 µg/L**. No other wells showed exceedances above the MCL during that period.

The *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report_2.24* performed a statistical evaluation of barium data across media. It confirmed that barium soil concentrations in the stockpiles after removal (quantified through verification sampling) fell below the DTSC‑approved site‑specific cleanup goal of **10,000 mg/kg** (based on a residential land‑use scenario). Groundwater results post‑removal were not evaluated in that report because the removal action did not include groundwater remediation.

The *Acceptance Letter_Final Removal Action Completion Report_Modesto Soil Stockpiles (900259)*, issued by DTSC, formally accepted the Final Removal Action Completion Report. The letter states that all barium‑impacted soil above the cleanup goal was excavated and disposed off‑site, and that post‑excavation confirmation samples met the required standards.

## Regulatory status

- **Groundwater standard:** The federal Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) for barium is **1,000 µg/L** (enforceable by USEPA and the State Water Resources Control Board). Prior to removal, one well (MW‑5) showed intermittent MCL exceedances; no ongoing compliance monitoring is required under the approved completion report.
- **Soil standard:** DTSC established a site‑specific cleanup goal of **10,000 mg/kg** for barium in soil under a residential exposure scenario, as referenced in the *Updated Statistical Evaluation Report_2.24*. All stockpiled soil exceeding this level was removed during the 2019–2020 Phase 1 actions.
- **Current status:** The project is in regulatory compliance. The *Acceptance Letter* closed the removal action; no further soil or groundwater corrective actions for barium are pending.

## Open items

None documented in the provided sources. The removal action completion report has been accepted, and no ongoing monitoring or additional deliverables for barium are indicated.
