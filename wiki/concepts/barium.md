## Barium

### Overview

Barium is a contaminant of concern at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, primarily found in soil stockpiles that originated from former evaporation ponds of the Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC). These stockpiles were used as highway embankment fill for the State Route (SR) 132 West Project. Remedial actions focused on containment of barium-containing soil (BCS) through consolidation and capping.

### Site Record

*   **Source of Contamination:** Barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles, originating from former FMC evaporation ponds and native soils excavated south of the SR 99/Kansas Avenue interchange.
*   **Concentrations in Soil Stockpiles:**
    *   Stockpile 3 removal verification samples ranged from 46 to 970 mg/kg.
    *   Stockpile 2 western and eastern end removal verification samples ranged from 54 to 760 mg/kg.
    *   Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing excavation samples ranged from 42 to 420 mg/kg.
    *   Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation samples ranged from 53 to 930 mg/kg.
    *   Carpenter Road Shoofly soil samples ranged from 28 to 96 mg/kg.
    *   Bent 2 excavation soil samples ranged from 31 to 230 mg/kg.
    *   The *Interim Removal Action Completion Report* (RACR) notes barium concentrations up to 7,000 mg/kg in Stockpile 3 during initial sampling, and up to 4,100 mg/kg in Stockpile 2.
    *   The *Final Remedial Action Plan* (RAP) notes barium concentrations up to 72,000 mg/kg in the stockpiles.
*   **Remedial Action Plan (RAP) Verification Thresholds:**
    *   Barium: 1,000 mg/kg (for groundwater protection).
*   **Site-Specific Background Concentrations (used for clean fill evaluation):**
    *   Barium: 120 mg/kg (per DTSC-approved Variance to RDIP, April 9, 2020).
*   **Remedial Actions:**
    *   Stockpile 3 was completely excavated and placed as embankment fill within the Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone between February and March 2020.
    *   Western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2 were excavated between February and March 2020 and placed as embankment fill within the Stockpile 2 BCS Containment Zone.
    *   Southerly slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 were excavated in March and April 2020, with excavated material placed within the Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones.
    *   Consolidation and clean capping of Stockpiles 1 through 3 within highway embankment fills (BCS Containment Zones) during Interim Phase 1 construction of the SR 132 West Project (2020-2022).
*   **Groundwater Monitoring:** Groundwater monitoring activities in the vicinity of the BCS stockpiles between November 2019 and March 2022 indicated reported concentrations of target analytes were below regulatory thresholds.

### Regulatory Status

*   **DTSC Cleanup Goal:** 1,000 mg/kg for barium in soil excavation verification areas.
*   **Site-Specific Background Concentration:** 120 mg/kg for barium, used for evaluating clean fill sources.
*   **California Title 22 MCL:** Not explicitly stated for barium in the provided sources.
*   **Environmental Screening Levels (ESLs):**
    *   San Francisco Regional Water Quality Control Board Tier 1 (unrestricted land use) ESL for barium is 3,000 mg/kg.
    *   DTSC residential screening level for barium is 3,000 mg/kg.
    *   Construction worker ESL for barium is 3,000 mg/kg.
    *   Commercial land use ESL for barium is 220,000 mg/kg.
*   **Air Monitoring Action Level:** 25 µg/m³ for barium, as presented in the highway contractor's *Health & Safety Plan*.

### Open Items

*   A Final Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) will be submitted following the completion of Ultimate Phase 2 construction of the SR 132 West Project, scheduled for 2028.
*   A supplemental Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) will be submitted prior to construction of Ultimate Phase 2.
*   Annual groundwater sampling events will continue until the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB) determines that the clean-capped BCS material does not pose a material threat to groundwater quality.
*   Additional stormwater sampling events will be performed by Caltrans in the vicinity of the Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones at regulatory-approved modified locations during qualifying rain events until completed construction of Ultimate Phase 2.
*   A Land Use Covenant (LUC) is required to restrict future land use and activities on the Site.
