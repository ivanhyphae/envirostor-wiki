## Cobalt

### Overview
Cobalt was among the metals analyzed for in surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023, as part of the remediation case file for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles located on SR 132 in Stanislaus County, California.

### Site Record
Cobalt was not reported above the Method Detection Limits (MDLs) in the background and surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023. {{quote: "Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, cobalt, nickel, silver, and thallium were not reported above the MDLs in the background and surface water samples."}}

### Regulatory Status
No specific regulatory status or applicable thresholds for cobalt in surface water are mentioned in the provided source material.
