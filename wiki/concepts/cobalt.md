## Cobalt

### Overview

Cobalt is a metal that has been analyzed for in surface water samples collected from the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site in Stanislaus County, California.

### Site Record

Cobalt was detected in surface water samples collected on October 28, 2016, at locations PL2, PL3, and PL5. The concentrations were not specified in the report. Cobalt was also reported in samples PL2, PL3, and PL5 from the October 28, 2016 sampling event. The report notes that these samples were diluted due to high concentrations of target analytes, failing internal standards, or high concentrations of non-target analytes.

In the March 22, 2018 surface water sampling event, cobalt was reported in samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1. The concentrations were not specified in the report.

In the January 8, 2018 surface water sampling event, cobalt was reported in samples PL1 through PL4, BG1, and BG2. The concentrations were not specified in the report.

Cobalt was not reported above the Method Detection Limits (MDLs) in the background and surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023.

### Regulatory Status

No specific regulatory status or applicable thresholds for cobalt in surface water are mentioned in the provided source material.
