The [[sr-132]] project in Modesto, California, involved the management and remediation of soil stockpiles, specifically those containing [[barium]]. A key component of this effort was the identification and utilization of clean fill material to cap these stockpiles. The Carpenter Road Shoofly was one such source of clean fill material, managed by [[wsp]] in conjunction with the [[city-of-modesto]].

## Site record

Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil were excavated from the Carpenter Road Shoofly. This material was intended for use as clean capping material over portions of barium-containing soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2.

Twenty-four discrete soil samples were collected from the Carpenter Road Shoofly along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6). Samples were taken from the surface at depths of 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet from the top of the slope. These discrete samples were composited into eight 3-part composite samples.

Analysis of these composite samples for barium and lead revealed:
*   Barium concentrations ranged from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg).
*   Lead concentrations ranged from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg.

The excavation depth was planned to be 20 feet, with an estimated 5,000 cubic yards of material.

## Regulatory status

The soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly was evaluated against the clean cap criteria established in a referenced variance document, as approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC).

The DTSC and the Water Board reviewed the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021. They agreed that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria. An email dated April 22, 2021, served as DTSC's acceptance of the Memo.

The maximum site-specific background value for barium was 120 mg/kg. The maximum site-specific background concentration for lead was 3.8 mg/kg.

## Open items

The information from the Carpenter Road Shoofly soil characterization testing will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted following completion of the [[stockpile-1]] and 2 clean fill capping activities.
