The Carpenter Road Shoofly is a location within the [[sr-132]] project in Modesto, California, from which approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil were excavated. This soil was characterized and found to meet the criteria for use as [[clean-fill]] material to cap [[barium-containing-soil]] ([[barium-containing-soil]]) in [[stockpile-1]] and Stockpile 2. The characterization testing and subsequent use of this soil as clean fill were overseen by [[caltrans]], with review and approval from the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] ([[dtsc]]) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (Central Valley RWQCB). [[geocon-consultants]] performed the soil characterization testing.

## Site record

Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil were excavated from the Carpenter Road Shoofly. {{quote: "Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil will be excavated from the Carpenter Road Shoofly once the overcrossing structure under construction is completed."}} This material was intended for use as clean capping material over portions of barium containing soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "The contractor plans to use the excavated soil material as clean capping material to be placed over portions of barium containing soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2 pending California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) approval."}}

Twenty-four discrete soil samples were collected from the Carpenter Road Shoofly along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6). Samples were taken from the surface at depths of 2, 5, 10, and 15 feet from the top of the slope. {{quote: "We collected 24 discrete soil samples from the Carpenter Road Shoofly along six vertical transects (CSF1 through CSF6) as depicted on the attached exhibit. Soil samples were collected from the surface of the soil material exposed on the slope face at 2, 5, 10 and 15 feet from the top of the slope."}} These discrete samples were composited into eight 3-part composite samples. {{quote: "The laboratory was instructed to composite three discrete soil samples from the same elevation along each slope face of the shoofly resulting in eight 3-part composite samples."}}

Analysis of these composite samples for barium and lead revealed:
*   Barium concentrations ranged from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). {{quote: "Barium was detected in each of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg), less than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg."}}
*   Lead concentrations ranged from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg."}}

The excavation depth was planned to be 20 feet, with an estimated 5,000 cubic yards of material. {{quote: "Key project details are provided, including an excavation depth of 20 feet, 5,000 cubic yards of material, and a placement week of 4/19/21."}}

## Regulatory status

The soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly was evaluated against the clean cap criteria established in a referenced variance document, as approved by DTSC. {{quote: "Based on the barium and lead data presented herein, soil material generated from excavation of the Carpenter Road Shoofly meet the BCS clean cap criteria as established in the referenced variance document as approved by DTSC (upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg)."}}

The DTSC and the Water Board reviewed the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021. {{quote: "DTSC and the Water Board have reviewed the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated April 12, 2021 describing the sampling and analysis that was completed to characterize the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill area."}} They agreed that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria. {{quote: "The agencies agree that the composite soil sample results are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and meet the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria."}} This email dated April 22, 2021, served as DTSC's acceptance of the Memo. {{quote: "This e-mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the Memo."}}

The maximum site-specific background value for barium was 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "Barium was detected in each of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg), less than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg."}} The maximum site-specific background concentration for lead was 3.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg."}}

## Open items

The information from the Carpenter Road Shoofly soil characterization testing will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted following completion of the Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities. {{quote: "The information presented herein will be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted following completion of the Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities."}}
