## Overview

**Wsp** is the environmental engineering and consulting firm responsible for the technical analysis, remedial design, and regulatory coordination for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project associated with State Route 132 in Stanislaus County, California. Wsp served as the lead consultant for investigating and remediating elevated metals—primarily arsenic, lead, and barium—in two soil stockpiles (Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2) generated during Caltrans highway construction activities. The firm authored key project documents, including the *SR 132 Stockpile 2 BCS Removal Tech Memo*, and has interfaced with the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) regarding cleanup thresholds, waste characterization, and off-site disposal.

## Site Record

- **Documents authored by Wsp**: The *SR 132 Stockpile 2 BCS Removal Tech Memo* (undated, but referencing field activities through 2022) documents the removal of barium-containing soil from Stockpile 2. This memo provides analytical results showing barium concentrations up to **760 mg/kg** in Stockpile 2 soil, exceeding the DTSC-approved background threshold of **520 mg/kg** for barium in the project area.
- **Contaminants addressed**: Wsp’s work focuses on metals: arsenic, lead, and barium. For Stockpile 2, barium was the primary driver for off-site disposal, with concentrations ranging from **520 mg/kg to 760 mg/kg** based on composite sampling.
- **Verification sampling**: Post-excavation verification sampling conducted by Wsp at Stockpile 2 showed residual barium concentrations below the **520 mg/kg** threshold, as documented in the tech memo.
- **Disposal volumes**: Approximately **1,200 cubic yards** of barium-impacted soil were removed from Stockpile 2 under Wsp’s oversight, transported to a Class II landfill.
- **Phasing**: Stockpile 1 (addressed in a separate, earlier phase) involved arsenic and lead remediation, while Stockpile 2 (Wsp’s focus) was a later phase completed in **2022**.

## Regulatory Status

- **Applicable thresholds**: The DTSC established site-specific background levels for the Modesto project area: barium ≤ **520 mg/kg**, arsenic ≤ **12 mg/kg**, lead ≤ **80 mg/kg**. These thresholds were used as action levels for stockpile remediation.
- **Agency oversight**: DTSC is the lead regulatory agency. Wsp’s removal action for Stockpile 2 was conducted under DTSC approval, with the tech memo serving as the closure documentation.
- **Compliance status**: As of the latest document (the Stockpile 2 tech memo), the project is in compliance for Stockpile 2—verification sampling confirmed all post-excavation soils meet the **520 mg/kg** barium threshold. Stockpile 1 previously achieved closure for arsenic and lead in **2021**.
- **Residual risk**: No additional action is required for Stockpile 2 based on the Wsp analysis, though the site remains under DTSC review for potential groundwater impacts not yet fully characterized.

## Open Items

- **Groundwater monitoring**: The Tech Memo notes that shallow groundwater beneath the stockpile areas was not fully characterized; a **groundwater monitoring plan** is pending DTSC approval as of the memo’s date.
- **Stockpile 1 residual**: While closed, Stockpile 1 contains residual soil with lead concentrations up to **150 mg/kg** (above the 80 mg/kg threshold but below the DTSC residential screening level of 400 mg/kg); DTSC is evaluating whether long-term land use restrictions are needed.
- **Documentation status**: Wsp has not yet submitted a final **Removal Action Completion Report** consolidating all stockpile phases; this is an expected deliverable to DTSC.
