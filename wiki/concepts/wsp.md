## WSP

WSP (formerly WSP/Parsons Brinckerhoff) is identified in project documentation as the Construction Manager for the State Route 132 Project in Modesto, California. They are the recipient of several technical memorandums from Geocon Consultants, Inc. detailing soil characterization testing and remediation efforts related to soil stockpiles.

## Site Record

WSP, in conjunction with the City of Modesto, managed the State Route 132 Project. They received technical memorandums from Geocon Consultants, Inc. regarding soil characterization testing for various areas, including:

*   **Bent 2 Excavation Soil Stockpiles:** On October 30, 2020, Geocon submitted a technical memorandum to Scott Frenette, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto, summarizing characterization testing of approximately 4,000 cubic yards of native soil excavated from Bent 2. This soil was placed on top of Stockpile 2. Ten discrete soil samples were analyzed for barium and lead. Barium ranged from 31 to 230 mg/kg, with four samples exceeding the site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg. Lead ranged from 2.6 to 13 mg/kg, with six samples exceeding the site-specific background value of 3.8 mg/kg.
*   **Carpenter Road Shoofly Clean Fill:** On April 12, 2021, Geocon submitted a technical memorandum to Rick Demi, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto, detailing characterization testing of approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil excavated from the Carpenter Road Shoofly. This material was intended for use as clean capping material over barium-containing soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2. Twenty-four discrete soil samples were collected and composited into eight 3-part composite samples, analyzed for barium and lead. Barium concentrations ranged from 28 to 96 mg/kg, all below the 120 mg/kg site-specific background value. Lead concentrations ranged from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, with nine samples at or below the 3.8 mg/kg site-specific background value.
*   **Basin 5 Clean Cap Borrow Area:** On July 2, 2020, Geocon submitted a revised technical memorandum to Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto, concerning characterization testing of the proposed Basin 5 clean cap borrow area. This area was intended to provide clean cap material for Stockpiles 1 and 2. Six exploratory trenches were sampled at various depths. Composite samples were analyzed for Title 22 metals. Barium concentrations ranged from 66 to 94 mg/kg, with a site-specific background of 72.8 mg/kg. Lead concentrations ranged from 5.8 to 11 mg/kg, with a site-specific background of 2.0 mg/kg.
*   **SR 132 Clean Borrow Area:** On February 27, 2020, Geocon submitted a technical memorandum to Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto, regarding characterization testing of a clean cap borrow area adjacent to Carpenter Road. Twelve borings were sampled to a depth of 5 feet. Composite samples were analyzed for Title 22 metals. Barium concentrations ranged from 60 to 110 mg/kg, with a site-specific background of 72.8 mg/kg. Lead concentrations ranged from 1.7 to 7.0 mg/kg, with a site-specific background of 2.0 mg/kg.
*   **Stockpile 2 MSE Wall Footing Excavation:** On June 18, 2020, Geocon submitted a technical memorandum to Michael Scott, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto, detailing characterization testing of soil excavated for the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing. Twenty-nine soil samples were analyzed for barium and lead. Barium concentrations ranged from 53 to 930 mg/kg, with fourteen samples exceeding the site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg. Lead concentrations ranged from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg, with the majority within the order of magnitude of the site-specific background value of 3.8 mg/kg.

## Regulatory Status

WSP, as the Construction Manager, would be involved in implementing decisions and approvals from regulatory agencies such as the DTSC regarding the use of soil materials, particularly concerning clean cap criteria and the management of barium-containing soil (BCS). The DTSC approved a variance to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan on April 9, 2020, which is referenced in multiple technical memorandums.
