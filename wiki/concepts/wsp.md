WSP (formerly WSP/Parsons Brinckerhoff) served as the Construction Manager for the State Route 132 Project in Modesto, California. In this capacity, WSP received and reviewed technical memorandums from Geocon Consultants, Inc. detailing soil characterization and remediation efforts related to various soil stockpiles and borrow areas.

## Soil Stockpile and Borrow Area Characterization

WSP, in conjunction with the City of Modesto, managed the State Route 132 Project. Geocon Consultants, Inc. provided technical memorandums to WSP personnel, including Rick Demi and Scott Frenette, detailing soil characterization testing for several areas critical to the project's remediation strategy.

*   **Bent 2 Excavation Soil Stockpiles:** On October 30, 2020, Geocon submitted a technical memorandum to Scott Frenette, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto, summarizing characterization testing of approximately 4,000 cubic yards of native soil excavated from Bent 2. This soil was placed on top of [[stockpile-2]]. Ten discrete soil samples were analyzed for barium and lead. Barium ranged from 31 to 230 mg/kg, with four samples exceeding the site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg. Lead ranged from 2.6 to 13 mg/kg, with six samples exceeding the site-specific background value of 3.8 mg/kg.
*   **Carpenter Road Shoofly Clean Fill:** On April 12, 2021, Geocon submitted a technical memorandum to Rick Demi, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto, detailing characterization testing of approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil excavated from the Carpenter Road Shoofly. This material was intended for use as [[clean-soil-cover]] over [[barium-containing-soil-consolidation]] (BCS) [[stockpile-1]] and [[stockpile-2]]. Twenty-four discrete soil samples were collected and composited into eight 3-part composite samples, analyzed for barium and lead. Barium concentrations ranged from 28 to 96 mg/kg, all below the 120 mg/kg site-specific background value. Lead concentrations ranged from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, with nine samples at or below the 3.8 mg/kg site-specific background value. The DTSC, via an email from Dean Wright on April 22, 2021, accepted this memorandum, agreeing that the results met the BCS clean cap criteria.
*   **Basin 5 Clean Cap Borrow Area:** On July 2, 2020, Geocon submitted a revised technical memorandum to Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto, concerning characterization testing of the proposed Basin 5 clean cap borrow area. This area was intended to provide clean cap material for [[stockpile-1]] and [[stockpile-2]]. Six exploratory trenches were sampled at various depths. Composite samples were analyzed for Title 22 metals. Barium concentrations ranged from 66 to 94 mg/kg, with a site-specific background of 72.8 mg/kg. Lead concentrations ranged from 5.8 to 11 mg/kg, with a site-specific background of 2.0 mg/kg.
*   **SR 132 Clean Borrow Area:** On February 27, 2020, Geocon submitted a technical memorandum to Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto, regarding characterization testing of a clean cap borrow area adjacent to Carpenter Road. Twelve borings were sampled to a depth of 5 feet. Composite samples were analyzed for Title 22 metals. Barium concentrations ranged from 60 to 110 mg/kg, with a site-specific background of 72.8 mg/kg. Lead concentrations ranged from 1.7 to 7.0 mg/kg, with a site-specific background of 2.0 mg/kg.
*   **Stockpile 2 MSE Wall Footing Excavation:** On June 18, 2020, Geocon submitted a technical memorandum to Michael Scott, Construction Manager, WSP/City of Modesto, detailing characterization testing of soil excavated for the [[stockpile-2]] MSE wall footing. Twenty-nine soil samples were analyzed for [[barium]] and lead. Barium concentrations ranged from 53 to 930 mg/kg, with fourteen samples exceeding the site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg. Lead concentrations ranged from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg, with the majority within the order of magnitude of the site-specific background value of 3.8 mg/kg.

## Regulatory Involvement

As Construction Manager, WSP was involved in the implementation of regulatory decisions. For instance, Scott Frenette of WSP communicated with Bay Cities Paving & Grading regarding the need for DTSC approval of a sampling plan for material from the Carpenter Shoofly prior to its use as clean cap material. This highlights WSP's role in ensuring compliance with regulatory requirements, such as those set forth by the DTSC.
