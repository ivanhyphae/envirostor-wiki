The RDIP Approval signifies the formal acceptance by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) of the plan detailing how Caltrans will manage and relocate soil stockpiles as part of the SR 132 West Expressway Project. This approval is a critical step, authorizing Caltrans to proceed with specific remedial actions for the stockpiles, ensuring they are handled in a manner protective of human health and the environment.

### RDIP-Interim Phase 1 Approval

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), provided final approval for the Remedial Design Implementation Plan—Interim Phase 1 (RDIP-Interim Phase 1) on April 3, 2019. This plan, submitted by Geocon Consultants on behalf of Caltrans, outlines the activities for the interim phase construction project, which includes the removal and re-deposition of a portion of the soil stockpiles to facilitate the construction of State Route 132. The RDIP-Interim Phase 1 incorporates excerpts from the 100% design plans and specifies safety measures and sampling protocols.

DTSC reviewed the RDIP-Interim Phase 1 and the associated "City of Modesto Book 2 of 3 100% Special Provisions for The State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Phase 1 Project." DTSC provided minor comments on the Special Provisions document on March 26, 2019, and a revised version addressing these comments was received on March 28, 2019. On April 3, 2019, DTSC and the RWQCB indicated they had no further comments, formally approving the RDIP-Interim Phase 1.
