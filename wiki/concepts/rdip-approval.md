## RDIP Approval

### Overview
The Remedial Design Implementation Plan — Interim Phase 1 (RDIP-Interim Phase 1) for the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles, located at the [[sr-132-west-expressway-project]] in Stanislaus County, California, received final approval from the [[dtsc]] on April 3, 2019. This plan details the activities for the Interim Phase 1 construction project, which involves the removal and re-deposition of a portion of the soil stockpiles to facilitate the construction of State Route 132. The RDIP-Interim Phase 1 also includes excerpts of the 100% design plans and outlines safety measures and sampling protocols to protect human health and the environment.

### Site Record
The [[dtsc]], in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), reviewed the RDIP-Interim Phase 1 submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] on behalf of [[caltrans]]. The agency also reviewed the "City of Modesto Book 2 of 3 100% Special Provisions for The State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Phase 1 Project," which ensures contractors adhere to the RDIP-Interim Phase 1 requirements. [[dtsc]] provided minor comments on the Special Provisions document on March 26, 2019, and a revised version addressing these comments was received on March 28, 2019.

### Regulatory Status
On April 3, 2019, the [[dtsc]] and the RWQCB indicated they had no further comments on the RDIP-Interim Phase 1 and the associated Special Provisions document. The RDIP-Interim Phase 1 was formally approved on this date.
