## Overview
The Interim Remedial Action Workplan (IRAWP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles is defined by document **S9650-06-03 MODESTO RAMP WORKPLAN.0412**. It establishes the scope, procedures, and objectives for early-phase remediation of metal‑impacted soil stockpiles along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County. The workplan was prepared by Caltrans and submitted to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) for review and approval.

## Site record
The workplan documents that the stockpiles consist of soil excavated during routine highway maintenance and contain elevated concentrations of metals, including barium, lead, and arsenic (specific values and spatial distribution are detailed in the workplan). The interim remedial action includes:  
- Excavation and segregation of stockpiled material.  
- Characterization sampling (grid or composite methods as specified in the workplan).  
- Off‑site transport to a permitted disposal facility.  
- Backfilling with clean imported soil.  
- Post‑remediation verification sampling to confirm residual concentrations meet action levels.

Phasing and milestones (e.g., start date, completion targets) are defined in the workplan. The workplan also identifies specific stockpile locations (e.g., near SR 132 milepost markers) and the volume of material anticipated for removal (cubic yards per stockpile).

## Regulatory status
The workplan was developed under DTSC oversight. Applicable standards cited in the workplan include California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs) for residential and commercial scenarios and site‑specific background concentrations for metals. Compliance requires adherence to all conditions in the DTSC‑approved remedial action plan.

## Open items
The workplan notes that final verification results, confirmation of disposal facility acceptance, and a closure report are pending submittal. These items will be addressed in subsequent deliverables and must be approved by DTSC before the interim action can be deemed complete.
