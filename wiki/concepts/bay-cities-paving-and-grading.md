## Bay Cities Paving And Grading

Bay Cities Paving And Grading (BCPG) is a contractor involved in the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file, specifically concerning the Carpenter Shoofly Area. Their work involves the preparation and sampling of soil intended for use as clean fill.

## Site Record

On April 5, 2021, Bay Cities Paving And Grading submitted a revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan. This plan detailed the proposed sampling and analysis procedures for soil designated for clean fill use. The plan was noted to be consistent with prior sampling efforts conducted in borrow areas.

Prior to this, on March 1, 2021, Scott Frenette of WSP requested specific information from Adan Corrales of Bay Cities Paving & Grading regarding the excavation of material under the Carpenter Shoofly. This information was needed to develop a sample plan for DTSC approval before testing the material for use as clean cap material. The requested details included:
* Depth of excavation
* Total fill volume
* Verification of utility clearances or the need for utility marking, and any known utility conflicts
* The planned excavation timeline for the material

The email from Scott Frenette to Adan Corrales on March 1, 2021, also noted that material from Retaining Wall No. 1110 had already been approved for use as clean cap material.

## Regulatory Status

The revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan, submitted by Bay Cities Paving And Grading, was reviewed by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). On April 7, 2021, DTSC approved the plan, confirming its consistency with previous borrow area sampling efforts and stating that DTSC had no comments. This approval was communicated via email.
