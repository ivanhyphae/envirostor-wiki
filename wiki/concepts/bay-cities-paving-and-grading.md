## Bay Cities Paving And Grading

Bay Cities Paving And Grading is a contractor involved in the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. Specifically, their work pertains to the Carpenter Shoofly Area, where soil is being prepared for use as clean fill.

## Site Record

Bay Cities Paving And Grading submitted a revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan on April 5, 2021. This plan outlined the proposed sampling and analysis for soil intended for use as clean fill. The plan was found to be consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts.

## Regulatory Status

The revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan was reviewed by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and the [[rwqcb]]. On April 7, 2021, DTSC approved the plan, stating that it was consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and that DTSC had no comments. This email serves as DTSC's approval of the plan.
