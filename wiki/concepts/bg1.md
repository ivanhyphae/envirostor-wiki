## Overview
BG1 is a background surface water sampling location used at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It is situated near a storm water inlet on Loletta Avenue, south of Stockpile 2. This location is used to collect background water samples for comparison with samples collected from runoff points.

## Site record
BG1 was sampled on multiple occasions as part of surface water monitoring efforts.

On December 15, 2016, sample BG1 was collected from water flowing into a drop inlet south of Stockpile 2 on Loletta Avenue. {{quote: "We collected background sample BG1 from water flowing into a drop inlet south of Stockpile 2 on Loletta Avenue (Photo 6)."}} The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. The laboratory noted that sample BG1 was diluted due to high concentrations of target analytes, specifically calcium, sodium, magnesium, and potassium. {{quote: "The laboratory notes state samples PL1 through PL4, BG1 and BG2 were diluted due to the high concentrations of the target analytes. PL1 through PL4, BG1 and BG2 were diluted for calcium and sodium. PL1, PL3, BG1 and BG2 were diluted for magnesium. PL3 was diluted for barium, and BG1 was diluted for potassium."}}

On March 24, 2017, there was insufficient water to collect a sample at BG1. {{quote: "On March 24, 2017, we collected surface water samples from designated locations PL1 through PL5. We did not sample from locations BG1 and BG2 because there was insufficient water to sample."}}

On January 8, 2018, sample BG1 was collected near a drop inlet south of Stockpile 2 on Loletta Avenue. {{quote: "We collected sample BG1 near a drop inlet south of Stockpile 2 on Loletta Avenue (Photo 6)."}} The laboratory noted that samples BG1 and BG2 were diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc due to high concentrations of target analytes. {{quote: "The laboratory notes state samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 and BG2 were diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc due to the high concentrations of the target analytes. Samples BG1 and BG2 were also diluted for sulfide due to possible matrix inference."}}

Manganese concentrations reported for sample BG1 in December 2016 exceeded the secondary Maximum Containment Level (MCL) of 50 micrograms per liter (µg/l). {{quote: "The manganese concentration reported for sample BG1 exceeded its secondary MCL."}}

## Regulatory status
Manganese concentrations in sample BG1 exceeded the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l. {{quote: "The manganese concentration reported for sample BG1 exceeded its secondary MCL."}}
