## Overview
BG1 is a background surface water sampling location utilized at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It is situated near a storm drain inlet on Loletta Avenue, south of Stockpile 2. This location is used to collect background water samples for comparison with samples collected from runoff points.

## Site record
BG1 has been sampled on multiple occasions as part of surface water monitoring efforts.

On December 15, 2016, sample BG1 was collected from water flowing into a drop inlet south of Stockpile 2 on Loletta Avenue. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. The laboratory noted that sample BG1 was diluted due to high concentrations of target analytes, specifically calcium, sodium, magnesium, and potassium. The manganese concentration reported for sample BG1 in December 2016 exceeded the secondary Maximum Containment Level (MCL) of 50 micrograms per liter (µg/l).

On March 24, 2017, there was insufficient water to collect a sample at BG1.

On January 8, 2018, sample BG1 was collected near a drop inlet south of Stockpile 2 on Loletta Avenue. The laboratory noted that samples BG1 and BG2 were diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc due to high concentrations of target analytes. Samples BG1 and BG2 were also diluted for sulfide due to possible matrix interference.

## Regulatory status
Manganese concentrations in sample BG1 exceeded the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l.
