The [[basin-5-borrow-area-analytical-data]] refers to the soil characterization results for the material intended for use as a clean cap borrow area for the State Route 132 stockpiles remediation. This data was reviewed by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board to determine its suitability for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2.

## Site record

The analytical data for the Basin 5 borrow area was summarized in a Revised Technical Memorandum dated July 2, 2020. This memo addressed concerns regarding chromium concentrations. The agencies reviewed additional information provided on June 29, 2020, which was summarized in a footnote within the Revised Memo. The composite soil sample results from Basin 5 were deemed consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations.

## Regulatory status

The DTSC and the Water Board reviewed the analytical data for the Basin 5 borrow area. On July 9, 2020, the DTSC issued acceptance of the Revised Technical Memorandum, stating that the composite soil sample results are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and suitable as clean cover material for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "The agencies agree that the composite soil sample results are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and suitable as clean cover material for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2. This e-mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the Revised Memo."}}
