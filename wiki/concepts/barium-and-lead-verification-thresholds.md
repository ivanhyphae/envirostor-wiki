## Barium And Lead Verification Thresholds

The Barium and Lead Verification Thresholds were established for the remediation of soil stockpiles at the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. These thresholds guided the excavation and verification sampling to ensure that residual concentrations of barium and lead in the soil were below acceptable levels before the placement of [[clean-cover-embankment-fill]].

## Site Record

During the excavation of Stockpile 2, verification soil samples were collected and analyzed for barium and lead. The established thresholds were:

*   **Barium:** 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) for groundwater protection.
*   **Lead:** 80 mg/kg, identified as the residential screening level.

On February 28, 2020, eight soil samples (S2W 1-0 through 8-0) were collected from the base of the western excavation of Stockpile 2. Initial analytical data indicated elevated barium concentrations above the verification threshold at sample locations S2W 1-0 and S2W 3-0. Consequently, the western excavation was deepened to an approximate elevation of 81 feet above MSL, and two additional verification soil samples were collected on March 6, 2020.

In the eastern half of Stockpile 2, six soil samples (S2E 1-0 through 8-0) were collected from the base of the excavation on February 21, 2020. Elevated barium concentrations above the verification threshold were found at sample location S2E 3-0. This area, along with the western half of the eastern excavation, was deepened to an approximate elevation of 82 feet above MSL. Seven additional verification soil samples (S2E 3A-0 and S2E 7-0 through 12-0) were collected on February 28, 2020. Sample location SE2 8-0 still contained elevated barium above the threshold, prompting further excavation to an approximate elevation of 81 feet above MSL. One final verification soil sample was collected from this deepened area on March 6, 2020.

All final excavation soil samples analyzed for barium and lead were below the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) verification thresholds. Barium concentrations ranged from 54 to 760 mg/kg, and lead concentrations ranged from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg. Based on this data, the excavation at both the west and east ends of Stockpile 2 met the barium and lead verification thresholds and was deemed suitable for the placement of clean embankment fill.

## Regulatory Status

The verification thresholds for barium and lead were established to meet regulatory requirements for the placement of clean embankment fill. The final verification sampling results indicated that the excavated areas met these thresholds, allowing for the completion of the remediation action at Stockpile 2.
