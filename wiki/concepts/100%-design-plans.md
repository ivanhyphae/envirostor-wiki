The 100% Design Plans are Caltrans' near-final construction drawings for the SR 132 / SR 99 interchange project in Modesto, dated 12-10-18 and marked "100% SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION." They cover the segment from SR 99 PM 14.6 (Begin Construction) to SR 132 station 1164+30 (End Construction), including the couplet configuration and adjacent basins, and their significance to this case file is narrow but concrete: several typical cross sections call out the use of 12 inches of clean soil in the roadway prism, tying the design directly to the disposition of the soil stockpiles addressed elsewhere in this record.

## What the plan set contains

The index (sheet IOP-1) identifies the project as Caltrans District 10, Stanislaus County, Route 99/132, project numbers 10-403504 and 1000000424, prepared by Dokken Engineering (110 Blue Ravine Road, Suite 200, Folsom, California) in coordination with the City of Modesto. The plans approval date recorded on the title sheet is 12/31/19, and the Project Engineer named is Kathryn Wickam; a separate layout sheet (L-15) is stamped by registered civil engineer Nathan J. Donnelly. The set runs to at least 880 sheets (sheet 9 of 880 is one of the typical cross-section sheets), covering the full corridor from SR 99 PM 14.6/17.6 to SR 132 PM 11.2/R14.4.

Typical cross-section sheets X-6 (Route 132, stations 1122+69.67 to 1141+71.71) and the following sheet for the Route 132 eastbound couplet (stations 1141+71.71 to 1149+43.70) show roadway sections with travel ways, medians, HMA dikes (Type F), concrete barriers, and retaining/sound wall 1142, along with cut/fill slopes ranging from 2% to 10%. Both sheets specify "12 inches of clean soil" in the roadway section. Horizontal layout sheets (L-15, L-16) show the SR 132 alignment through residential frontage on Arrowhead Court, Comstock Court, and Custer Court, including curve data for Curve 12 (radius 8000 feet, delta 03°45'24", tangent 262.36 feet, length 524.53 feet) and the location of adjacent basin areas and sound walls relative to the right-of-way line.

## Significance to the stockpile matter

The recurring "12 inches of clean soil" callout on the Route 132 cross sections is the plan set's direct point of contact with the soil stockpile remediation: it establishes that the roadway design assumes a defined clean-soil cover or layer be placed as part of construction. The plans are explicitly marked "not for construction" as of the 12-10-18 submittal, with a plans approval date of 12/31/19 — indicating this version was a pre-final design milestone rather than the issued-for-construction set. The source material does not indicate whether the clean soil specified in these sections was to be sourced from, or coordinated with, the disposition of the stockpiled soil addressed in the remediation case file, and it does not record any later revision, approval, or construction-phase confirmation of this cross-section detail.
