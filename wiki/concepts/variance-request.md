## Variance Request

A variance request is a formal request to deviate from a specific regulatory requirement or standard. In the context of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA), variance requests have been submitted and approved for certain aspects of the remediation process.

## Site record

On January 26, 2023, Caltrans submitted a variance request to the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] regarding the remediation of Stockpile 2. The request sought approval to manage soil from Stockpile 2, which contained elevated levels of [[lead]], without further treatment, based on a proposed wall footing design. This design was intended to encapsulate the soil within the footing. The variance was requested for the soil within Stockpile 2 that was to be incorporated into the wall footing. The rationale for the variance was that the wall footing would serve as a protective barrier, preventing direct contact and migration of contaminants.

The [[central-valley-rwqcb]] approved the variance request on February 16, 2023. This approval was contingent upon the successful implementation of the wall footing design and ongoing monitoring. The approval was communicated via an email from Perry Myers (Central Valley RWQCB) to Richard Stewart (Caltrans).

A technical memorandum and email acceptance dated January 26, 2023, and February 16, 2023, respectively, document the details of the variance request and its approval. The technical memo was prepared by Dean Wright. The email acceptance was from Perry Myers to Richard Stewart.

## Regulatory status

The variance request for Stockpile 2 was submitted to the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. The [[central-valley-rwqcb]] approved the variance request on February 16, 2023. The approval allows for the management of soil from Stockpile 2 within the wall footing without further treatment, provided the footing is constructed as designed.
