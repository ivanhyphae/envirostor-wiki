The [[modesto-irrigation-district]] (MID) is mentioned in relation to the location of Stockpile 3, which was situated adjacent to the MID water canal.

## Site record
Stockpile 3, containing barium-containing soil (BCS), was located adjacent and east of [[sr-99]] between [[kansas-avenue]] and the [[modesto-irrigation-district]] (MID) water canal. {{quote: "Stockpile 3 BCS material located adjacent and east of State Route 99 between Kansas Avenue and the Modesto Irrigation District (MID) water canal was excavated and transported for placement as embankment fill within the designated Stockpile 1 fill containment zone."}} The excavation of Stockpile 3 occurred between February 28 and March 12, 2020. {{quote: "A total of 24 verification soil samples (S3 1-0 through S3 24-0) were collected between February 28 and March 12, 2020 from the base of the excavation."}} Verification soil samples S3 SE BERM and S3 SW BERM were collected from the top of the berm at the southern end of the Stockpile 3 excavation adjacent to the MID water canal. {{quote: "Verification soil samples S3 SE BERM and S3 SW BERM were collected from the top of the berm at the southern end of the Stockpile 3 excavation adjacent to the MID water canal."}}
