The [[modesto-irrigation-district]] (MID) is referenced in the remediation of [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) at the State Route 132 Project in [[california]]. Specifically, Stockpile 3, a source of BCS, was located adjacent to an MID water canal.

## Site record
Stockpile 3, containing [[barium]]-containing soil (BCS), was situated adjacent and to the east of [[sr-99]], between [[kansas-avenue]] and the [[modesto-irrigation-district]] (MID) water canal. The BCS material from Stockpile 3 was excavated and transported for placement as embankment fill within the designated Stockpile 1 fill containment zone. The excavation of Stockpile 3 occurred between February 28 and March 12, 2020. A total of 24 verification soil samples (S3 1-0 through S3 24-0) were collected from the base of the excavation during this period. Verification soil samples designated S3 SE BERM and S3 SW BERM were collected from the top of the berm at the southern end of the Stockpile 3 excavation, adjacent to the MID water canal.

A total of 3,075 truck-loads of BCS material, totaling approximately 38,440 cubic yards, was transported from Stockpile 3 to Stockpile 1. This volume is a preliminary estimate based on 12.5 cubic yards per truck. The BCS removal verification testing was performed in general accordance with the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) prepared for the State Route 132 Project.
