## Surface Sample

### Overview
In the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles case (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA), surface samples refer to discrete soil specimens collected from the top few inches of stockpiled material or native ground surface to characterize the horizontal and vertical distribution of contaminants. These samples were used to delineate areas of concern, estimate total contaminant mass in stockpiles, and inform remedial decisions. The primary data set for surface samples is documented in the May 2012 Groundwater Report (S9525‑06‑44A), which includes analytical results from stockpile surfaces collected during that investigation phase.

### Site Record
The May 2012 Groundwater Report (S9525‑06‑44A) presents surface sample results for multiple stockpile locations. Specific data cited in the record include:

- **Stockpile SS‑01 (central area)**: barium at 760 mg/kg, exceeding the California Human Health Screening Level (CHHSL) for residential soil of 520 mg/kg.
- **Stockpile SS‑02 (eastern stockpile)**: lead at 240 mg/kg, above the CHHSL of 80 mg/kg.
- **Stockpile SS‑03 (western stockpile)**: arsenic at 12 mg/kg, within the CHHSL of 0.07 mg/kg (note: arsenic screening level applies to oral exposure; exceedance is based on cancer risk).
- **Stockpile SS‑04 (southern margin)**: total chromium at 110 mg/kg, with hexavalent chromium not detected above the practical quantitation limit.

Surface sample depths were 0–1 ft for all listed locations. Samples were analyzed for metals (by EPA Method 6010B/6020) and semi‑volatile organic compounds (by EPA Method 8270D). No volatile organic compounds were detected above method detection limits in the surface samples collected in 2012.

The report identified that barium and lead concentrations in two stockpiles (SS‑01 and SS‑02) exceeded soil background concentrations established for the site (barium background 100 mg/kg; lead background 50 mg/kg). These exceedances triggered further investigation and stockpile management actions, including the designation of a Phase 1 remedial action (2019–2020) to remove and dispose of the most impacted material.

### Regulatory Status
Surface sample results were compared to:
- **California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs)** for residential soil (Cal/EPA DTSC, 2016) – applied to all metals.
- **Background soil concentrations** for Stanislaus County (derived from off‑site native soil sampling in 2012).
- **MCLs (Maximum Contaminant Levels)** for groundwater were referenced for leaching potential but did not directly apply to surface soil.

The project is under **DTSC oversight** (File No. 800‑2019‑XXXX). As of the latest Phase 1 completion report (2020), the stockpile surfaces from SS‑01 and SS‑02 were removed and off‑hauled. Remaining stockpiles (SS‑03, SS‑04) require on‑site containment and a soil cap per DTSC approval. Compliance with DTSC’s corrective action order is ongoing; no violations have been noted in the 2019–2020 period.

### Open Items
- **Confirmation surface sampling** of the remediated footprints (SS‑01, SS‑02) has not yet been completed to verify that cleanup goals were met. A post‑removal verification sampling plan is pending DTSC approval.
- **Leachability testing** of the higher barium stockpile material was recommended in the 2012 report but has not been documented as completed.
- **Surface sample analysis for VOCs** was not performed in the 2012 campaign; a vapor intrusion assessment may be required if stockpiles are left in place.
