## Barium Containing Soil Consolidation

### Overview
Barium-containing soil consolidation was a remedial action undertaken at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This action involved the management and consolidation of soil impacted by barium.

### Site Record
The Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the site identified barium as a constituent of concern. The Remedial Design/Implementation Plan (RDIP) detailed the approach for managing barium-containing soils. Specifically, the RDIP outlined the consolidation of barium-impacted soil within the site boundaries. The Draft Interim Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) documents the completion of this remedial action.

### Regulatory Status
The [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and DTSC oversaw the remediation efforts. The RAP and RDIP, approved by these agencies, guided the consolidation process. The completion of the remedial action, including the consolidation of barium-containing soils, is documented in the Draft Interim RACR.
