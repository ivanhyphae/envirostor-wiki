Barium-containing soil consolidation was a remedial action undertaken at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site as part of the SR 132 West Project. This process involved managing and incorporating soils with elevated barium concentrations into highway embankment fills, thereby consolidating and containing the impacted material.

### Site Record and Remedial Actions

The [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site, originating from soil excavated from former FMC facility evaporation ponds and native soils, contained elevated levels of barium, with concentrations reaching up to 72,000 mg/kg in some samples. The Remedial Action Plan (RAP) identified barium as a primary contaminant of concern. The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) detailed the strategy for managing these barium-containing soils (BCS).

The selected remedial alternative was containment by incorporating the stockpiles as highway embankment fill and covering them with clean fill and structural pavement. This was implemented in phases during the SR 132 West Project construction.

*   **Stockpile 3:** This stockpile was entirely removed between February and March 2020. Approximately 27,675 yd³ of BCS material was transported and placed as embankment fill within the Stockpile 1 Containment Zone. Verification sampling confirmed that barium concentrations in the remaining native soil were below the RDIP threshold of 1,000 mg/kg.
*   **Stockpile 2:** The western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2 were excavated to facilitate bridge abutment construction. The excavated BCS material was placed as embankment fill within the Stockpile 2 Containment Zone. Verification sampling confirmed that barium and lead concentrations in the remaining native soil were below the RDIP thresholds of 1,000 mg/kg and 80 mg/kg, respectively.
*   **Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Excavations:** Soil from the southerly slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 was excavated to support the construction of Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) walls. While barium concentrations in the upper three feet of excavated material exceeded the site-specific background concentration of 120 mg/kg, they remained below the RDIP removal threshold of 1,000 mg/kg. All excavated MSE wall footing material was placed within the Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones.
*   **Aerially Deposited Lead (ADL) Soil:** Soil with elevated lead concentrations from the northbound SR 99 shoulder was also placed within the Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone, in accordance with the Soil Management Agreement for Aerially Deposited Lead-Contaminated Soils.

The consolidated BCS and ADL materials were then capped with a minimum of 12 inches of clean fill material, or a 4-inch layer of aggregate base pending placement of structural pavement and hardscape.

### Air Monitoring

Air monitoring was conducted between February and June 2020 during BCS grading activities, including excavation, relocation, and placement. Perimeter air monitoring documented total airborne particulate and metals (barium and lead) concentrations. Real-time particulate counter readings and laboratory analysis of air samples confirmed that dust control measures were effective, with airborne levels of total dust, barium, and lead remaining below designated project action and risk levels.

### Regulatory Oversight and Documentation

The remediation efforts were overseen by the [[dtsc]] in cooperation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP), approved by DTSC, guided the containment process. Technical Memoranda detailing excavation verification testing, borrow area characterization, and MSE wall footing testing were prepared by [[geocon-consultants]].

An Interim Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. to document the consolidation and clean fill capping of BCS Stockpiles 1 through 3 during Phase I construction. A Final RACR will be submitted following the completion of Ultimate Phase 2 construction, scheduled for 2028. An Operations and Maintenance (O&M) Plan was also prepared to address ongoing monitoring and maintenance of the containment measures.

A Land Use Covenant (LUC) is required for the contained soil stockpiles to restrict future land use to compatible activities, such as transportation, and to prevent unrestricted uses.

### Key Thresholds and Concentrations

*   **Barium Verification Threshold:** 1,000 mg/kg (for groundwater protection)
*   **Lead Verification Threshold:** 80 mg/kg (DTSC residential screening level)
*   **Site-Specific Background Barium:** 120 mg/kg
*   **Site-Specific Background Lead:** 3.8 mg/kg
*   **Fence Line Action Level for Total Dust:** 4.0 mg/m³
