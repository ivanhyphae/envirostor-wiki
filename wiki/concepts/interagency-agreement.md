The Interagency Agreement is the funding and authorization instrument between the California Department of Transportation and the Department of Toxic Substances Control that establishes DTSC's oversight role and cost recovery for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation, carried out under the State Route 132 West Expressway Project. It exists in the record as agreement 43A0310-04 and its subsequent amendments, together with a task order issued under it, and a related agreement 43A0404-04 and separate document 43A0487-04. The agreement is the mechanism by which DTSC's technical review and approval activities on this site are paid for and scoped, rather than a remedial decision in itself.

## Instruments in the record

Agreement 43A0310-04 was executed and appears in the file with two amendments, A01 and A02, both executed. Amendment A02 is specifically identified as the executed task order for "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles," tying the funding instrument directly to this site rather than to a broader Caltrans-DTSC relationship. A separate agreement, 43A0404-04, is also present in executed form. A further document, 43A0487-04, titled "Modesto Stockpiles - signed," is also part of the file.

The presence of two distinct agreement numbers (43A0310-04 and 43A0404-04) alongside a third stockpiles-specific document (43A0487-04) indicates that DTSC oversight of this site has been funded or authorized through more than one interagency instrument over time, rather than a single continuous agreement. The available material does not describe the relationship between these three numbers — whether one superseded another, whether they cover different scopes of work, or whether they run concurrently.

## What the record does not establish

The source material identifies these agreements and their execution status but does not give the dollar amounts, effective dates, terms of duration, or specific scopes of work assigned under each. Task Order A02 is named as covering the Modesto Soil Stockpiles, which confirms that this site's DTSC oversight work was charged against agreement 43A0310-04, but the content of the tasks authorized under it is not given in the available material.

## Significance

These agreements are the administrative basis for DTSC's involvement in reviewing and approving the site's remedial work — the funding vehicle rather than a technical or regulatory decision affecting the property. A reviewer checking whether DTSC's oversight of the Modesto Soil Stockpiles was properly authorized and funded would look to 43A0310-04 and its amendments, and to 43A0404-04 and 43A0487-04, as the governing instruments; a reviewer looking for the substance of DTSC's technical findings or approval conditions would need to look elsewhere in the file.
