The [[interagency-agreement]] (IA) is a foundational document governing the remediation of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. It establishes the roles and responsibilities of the involved agencies in addressing the environmental concerns at the site, particularly in relation to the [[human-health-risk-assessment]] (HHRA) and the development of a Draft-Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP). The agreement facilitates the cooperative efforts between [[caltrans]], the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC), and potentially the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] in managing the project.

## Site Record

The IA is referenced as the basis for the project's progression. Specifically, it underpins the work conducted under the [[brownfields-and-environmental-restoration-program]]. The 8/04 04 2013 Final SSI and HHRA document, which was prepared in accordance with the IA, details the findings of the Site Study Investigation (SSI) and the HHRA. The S9525-06-44 HHRA UPDATE Rev.0313 also operates within the framework established by the IA, providing an updated HHRA.

## Regulatory Status

The IA serves as the overarching regulatory framework for the remediation activities. It ensures that the project adheres to the requirements set forth by the involved state agencies, primarily DTSC. The subsequent approval of remedial actions, such as the [[dtsc-approval-letter]], signifies compliance with the directives and standards established or referenced within the IA.

## Open Items

No specific open items directly attributable to the Interagency Agreement itself are detailed in the provided source materials. The IA is presented as the established framework for addressing existing issues.
