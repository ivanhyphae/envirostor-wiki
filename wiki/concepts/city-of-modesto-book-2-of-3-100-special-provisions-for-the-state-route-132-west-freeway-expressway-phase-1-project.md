The "City of Modesto Book 2 of 3 100% Special Provisions For The State Route 132 West Freeway Expressway Phase 1 Project" is a document containing non-standard specifications for contractors working on the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project. These provisions ensure contractor adherence to the requirements outlined in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan — Interim Phase 1 (RDIP-Interim Phase 1) for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]].

## Site record

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) reviewed this document. DTSC provided minor comments on March 26, 2019, and a revised version addressing these comments was received on March 28, 2019. This document ensures that selected contractors will be subject to the requirements found in the RDIP-Interim Phase 1.

## Regulatory status

This document is integral to the regulatory framework for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. It implements the requirements of the RDIP-Interim Phase 1, which has been approved by the DTSC and RWQCB, through specific contractual specifications for contractors. The DTSC and RWQCB indicated they had no additional comments on this document and approved the RDIP-Interim Phase 1.
