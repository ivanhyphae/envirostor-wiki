The "City of Modesto Book 2 of 3 100% Special Provisions For The State Route 132 West Freeway Expressway Phase 1 Project" is a document that outlines non-standard specifications for contractors involved in the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project. These provisions are designed to ensure contractor compliance with the requirements stipulated in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan — Interim Phase 1 (RDIP-Interim Phase 1) for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]].

## Site record

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) reviewed this document. DTSC provided minor comments on March 26, 2019, and a revised version addressing these comments was received on March 28, 2019. {{quote: "In addition, DTSC and the RWQCB reviewed the document titled, "City of Modesto Book 2 of 3 100% Special Provisions for The State Route 132 West Freeway/Expressway Phase 1 Project". This document provides the non-standard specifications that ensure the contractor(s)-select will be subject to the requirements found in the RDIP-Interim Phase 1. DTSC provided minor comments on this document on March 26, 2019 and a revised version which addressed those comments was received on March 28, 2019."}}

## Regulatory status

This document is integral to the regulatory framework governing the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. It serves to implement the requirements of the RDIP-Interim Phase 1, which has received approval from the DTSC and RWQCB, through specific contractual specifications for contractors. {{quote: "The DTSC and the RWQCB have no additional comments on either document and the RDIP—Interim Phase 1 is approved."}}
