# Annual Inspection Report

## Overview

The Annual Inspection Report is a compliance deliverable for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. It documents the results of annual site inspections and maintenance activities conducted at the former soil stockpile area. The report's purpose is to demonstrate the ongoing effectiveness of the remedial action and compliance with regulatory requirements.

## Site record

The Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report dated January 26, 2026, outlines the outcome of an inspection conducted at State Route 132 West Expressway at the State Route 132/99 Interchange. {{quote: "Thank you for your submission of the Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report (Report) dated January 26, 2026, which outlines the outcome of an inspection conducted at State Route 132 West Expressway at the State Route 132/99 Interchange."}} The inspection was conducted on Monday, January 26, 2026. {{quote: "I was out on Monday and did the annual inspection at Modesto Stockpiles."}} No issues were observed during the inspection. {{quote: "I didn't observe any issues."}} Maintenance crews were observed removing debris from the north slope, specifically on stockpile #2, which was freshly raked with piles of debris at the bottom of the slope. {{quote: "You will notice in some of the photos that our maintenance crews were out there removing debris from the north slope. They had only done stockpile #2 when I was there but you can see that the area is freshly raked and there are piles of debris just past the bottom of the slope."}}

The vegetation along the roadway is still sparse, a condition also noted in the previous year's report. {{quote: "The vegetation is still sparse along the road, which was noted during last year's report."}} Leftover erosion control materials are currently limiting erosion, but vegetation along the roadway is encouraged to prevent erosion. {{quote: "It appears that leftover erosion control materials are limiting erosion currently. Vegetation along the roadway is still encouraged to prevent erosion."}}

## Regulatory status

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) reviewed and approved the Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report dated January 26, 2026. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)'s Project Manager and Engineering and Special Projects Office (ESPO) completed their review and based upon DTSC's review of the report, it is hereby approved and accepted as final."}} DTSC looks forward to reviewing the next submittal, an Annual Land Use Covenant Inspection Report, which is due to DTSC no later than January 18, 2027. {{quote: "We appreciate the submittal of this annual report and look forward to reviewing the next submittal for this project, an Annual Land Use Covenant Inspection Report, which is due to DTSC no later than January 18, 2027."}}
