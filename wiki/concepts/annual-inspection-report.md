# Annual Inspection Report

## Overview

The Annual Inspection Report is a compliance deliverable for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. It documents the results of annual site inspections and maintenance activities conducted at the former soil stockpile area. The report's purpose is to demonstrate the ongoing effectiveness of the remedial action and compliance with regulatory requirements.

## Site record

An Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report dated January 26, 2026, details an inspection conducted at the State Route 132 West Expressway at the State Route 132/99 Interchange. The inspection occurred on Monday, January 26, 2026. No issues were observed during this inspection. Maintenance crews were observed removing debris from the north slope, specifically on stockpile #2. At the time of the inspection, this area was freshly raked with piles of debris at the bottom of the slope.

The vegetation along the roadway was noted as sparse, a condition also documented in the previous year's report. Erosion control materials were present and appeared to be limiting erosion. However, the report encouraged vegetation along the roadway to further prevent erosion.

A reminder for the 2025 Annual Inspection Report was sent on October 27, 2025, indicating the report was due to the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) by January 18, 2026.

## Regulatory status

The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) reviewed and approved the Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report dated January 26, 2026. DTSC indicated that they looked forward to reviewing the next submittal, an Annual Land Use Covenant Inspection Report, which was due to DTSC no later than January 18, 2027.

## Open items

The 2025 Annual Inspection Report reminder noted a comment from the previous year's report regarding the checklist, specifically the first column labeled "surface conditions ok?".
