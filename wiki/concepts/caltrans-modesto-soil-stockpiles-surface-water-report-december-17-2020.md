## Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Surface Water Report December 17 2020

### Overview
The Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Surface Water Report – December 17, 2020, prepared by Stantec Consulting, documents surface water sampling activities related to the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project. This report was submitted to relevant agencies for review.

### Site Record
The report titled "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Surface Water Report – December 17, 2020" was prepared by Stantec Consulting. {{quote: "Attached for your files is a PDF of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Surface Water Report – December 17, 2020 prepared by Stantec Consulting."}} This report was forwarded by Dean Wright of the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) to Kyle Cockerham of the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (Waterboard) on March 24, 2021. {{quote: "Just forwarding the attached surface water report on the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project."}} Kyle Cockerham of the Waterboard indicated on March 25, 2021, that he had no comments on the report. {{quote: "I don't have any comment on the Modesto Soil Stockpile Surface Water Sampling report."}}

### Regulatory Status
The report was submitted to the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] for review. The [[central-valley-rwqcb]] has indicated no comments on the report.
