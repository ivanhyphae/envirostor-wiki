## Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Surface Water Report December 17 2020

### Overview
The Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Surface Water Report – December 17, 2020, is a deliverable that documents surface water sampling activities conducted for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project.

### Site Record
The report, titled "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Surface Water Report – December 17, 2020," was prepared by Stantec Consulting. Dean Wright of the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) forwarded this report to Kyle Cockerham of the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (Waterboard) on March 24, 2021, stating, "Just forwarding the attached surface water report on the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project." Ralph Carson of Stantec had previously sent the PDF of the report to Dean Wright on March 23, 2021.

### Regulatory Status
On March 25, 2021, Kyle Cockerham of the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] indicated that he had no comments on the report, stating, "I don't have any comment on the Modesto Soil Stockpile Surface Water Sampling report."
