## Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Surface Water Report December 17 2020

### Overview
The Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Surface Water Report – December 17, 2020, is a deliverable documenting surface water sampling activities conducted for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project.

### Site Record
The report, titled "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Surface Water Report – December 17, 2020," was prepared by Stantec Consulting. {{quote: "Attached for your files is a PDF of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Surface Water Report – December 17, 2020 prepared by Stantec Consulting."}} Dean Wright of the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) forwarded this report to Kyle Cockerham of the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (Waterboard) on March 24, 2021. {{quote: "Just forwarding the attached surface water report on the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project."}}

### Regulatory Status
On March 25, 2021, Kyle Cockerham of the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] stated that he had no comments on the report. {{quote: "I don't have any comment on the Modesto Soil Stockpile Surface Water Sampling report."}}
