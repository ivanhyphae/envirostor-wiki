The depth to water is a critical parameter measured at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site, providing insight into the groundwater table's position relative to the ground surface. These measurements are collected semi-annually by [[geocon-consultants]] as part of the ongoing [[groundwater-monitoring-report]] process. The data is essential for understanding groundwater flow dynamics and the potential for contaminant migration.

## Site record

Depth to groundwater measurements have been recorded at various monitoring wells across the site.

In October 2016, depth to groundwater measurements were obtained from monitoring wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-5, MW-6, MW-7, MW-9, and MW-10. The recorded depths ranged from 38.77 to 47.10 feet below the top of the well casing (TOC). Monitoring wells MW-3, MW-4, and MW-8 were reported as dry during this event. Based on the groundwater elevation data from this period, the groundwater flow was toward the south with an average gradient of 0.007.
{{quote: "In October 2016, depth to groundwater measured in wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-5, MW-6, MW-7, MW-9, and MW-10 ranged from 38.77 to 47.10 feet below TOC. Based on the groundwater elevation data, the groundwater flow was toward the south at an average gradient of 0.007, which is generally consistent with historical flow which has been toward the south to southeast."}}

In April 2017, depth to groundwater measurements were taken in monitoring wells MW-1 through MW-4 and MW-7. The depths ranged from 35.52 to 40.73 feet below TOC. Monitoring wells MW-5, MW-6, and MW-8 through MW-10 were reported as dry or having insufficient water for purging and sampling. The groundwater flow direction was determined to be toward the east-southeast with an average gradient of 0.0007.
{{quote: "In April 2017, depth to groundwater measured in wells MW-1 through MW-4 and MW-7 ranged from 35.52 to 40.73 feet below TOC. Based on the groundwater elevation data, the groundwater flow was toward the east-southeast at an average gradient of 0.0007, which is generally consistent with historical flow which has been toward the south to southeast."}}

In October 2018, depth to groundwater was measured in wells MW-1 through MW-7 and MW-9, with depths ranging from 36.36 to 46.99 feet below TOC. Well MW-8 was dry. The groundwater flow was toward the south at an average gradient of 0.008.
{{quote: "In October 2018, depth to groundwater measured in wells MW-1 through MW-7 and MW-9 ranged from 36.36 to 46.99 feet below TOC. Well MW-8 was dry. Based on the groundwater elevation data, the groundwater flow was toward the south at an average gradient of 0.008, which is generally consistent with historical flow which has been toward the south to southeast."}}

In April 2019, depth to groundwater measurements in wells MW-1 through MW-8 ranged from 34.22 to 45.65 feet below TOC. Wells MW-9 and MW-10 were dry. The groundwater flow was toward the south with an average gradient of 0.01.
{{quote: "In April 2019, depth to groundwater measured in wells MW-1 through MW-8 ranged from 34.22 to 45.65 feet below TOC. Wells MW-9 and MW-10 were dry. Based on the groundwater elevation data, the groundwater flow was toward the south at an average gradient of 0.01, which is generally consistent with historical flow which has been toward the south to southeast."}}

The shallow zone groundwater beneath the stockpiles was encountered at approximately 35 feet below ground surface (bgs) with an elevation of approximately 50 feet, under unconfined to semi-confined conditions.
{{quote: "The shallow zone groundwater beneath the stockpiles was encountered at approximately 35 feet (elevation approximately 50 feet) under unconfined to semi-confined conditions."}}
