## Overview
Depth to water measurements are a critical parameter for understanding groundwater conditions at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. These measurements, collected by [[geocon-consultants]], provide insight into the position of the groundwater table relative to the ground surface and are essential for assessing groundwater flow dynamics and the potential for contaminant migration.

## Site record
Depth to groundwater measurements have been recorded at various monitoring wells across the site.

In October 2016, depth to groundwater was measured in wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-5, MW-6, MW-7, MW-9, and MW-10. The recorded depths ranged from 38.77 to 47.10 feet below the top of the well casing (TOC). Monitoring wells MW-3, MW-4, and MW-8 were reported as dry. Based on the groundwater elevation data from this period, the groundwater flow was toward the south with an average gradient of 0.007.

In April 2017, depth to groundwater was measured in monitoring wells MW-1 through MW-4 and MW-7. The depths ranged from 35.52 to 40.73 feet below TOC. Monitoring wells MW-5, MW-6, and MW-8 through MW-10 were reported as dry or having insufficient water for purging and sampling. The groundwater flow direction was determined to be toward the east-southeast with an average gradient of 0.0007.

In October 2018, depth to groundwater was measured in wells MW-1 through MW-7 and MW-9, with depths ranging from 36.36 to 46.99 feet below TOC. Well MW-8 was dry. The groundwater flow was toward the south at an average gradient of 0.008.

In April 2019, depth to groundwater measurements in wells MW-1 through MW-8 ranged from 34.22 to 45.65 feet below TOC. Wells MW-9 and MW-10 were dry. The groundwater flow was toward the south with an average gradient of 0.01.

The shallow zone groundwater beneath the stockpiles was encountered at approximately 35 feet below ground surface (bgs) with an elevation of approximately 50 feet, under unconfined to semi-confined conditions.
