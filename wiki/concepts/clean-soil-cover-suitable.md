"Clean soil cover suitable" is the determination sought for the Bent 2 excavation soil stockpiles under the *Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan* (April 9, 2020), which set an upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg as the clean cap criterion. Geocon's October 30, 2020 testing found the Bent 2 material did not meet that criterion outright, but proposed a fallback use — as thin partial cap rather than full clean cap — pending DTSC's review.

## Testing and result

Geocon collected ten discrete soil samples (STK1 through STK10) from the Bent 2 end dump piles, spaced at approximately 80-foot intervals and alternating between the two end dump rows, and analyzed each for barium and lead by EPA Method 6010B. Barium was detected in all 10 samples, ranging from 31 to 230 mg/kg. Four of the 10 samples equaled or exceeded the 120 mg/kg background threshold — meaning the exceedance was not isolated to a single outlier but affected 40 percent of the stockpile samples, including at least one result (230 mg/kg) nearly double the threshold. Lead was detected in 6 of 10 samples, at 2.6 to 13 mg/kg, within a similar range to the site-specific background value of 3.8 mg/kg and not identified as a basis for disqualification.

## Consequence: disqualified as full clean cap, proposed as partial cap

Because barium exceeded the 120 mg/kg background threshold in a meaningful fraction of the samples, Geocon concluded the Bent 2 soil stockpiles do not meet the clean cap criteria established in the DTSC-approved variance. This directly disqualifies the material from serving as the sole or primary clean soil cover for [[bent-2-area]] stockpile surfaces under the variance's own terms.

Geocon's memo proposed an alternative, conditional use: the material "may be suitable" for use as partial clean cap — limited to 6 inches or less — on the upper surface of Stockpile 2, on the rationale that the planned structural pavement section (base material and asphalt/concrete) would be placed over the entire upper surface afterward, providing an additional barrier layer above the marginal soil. This proposal is explicitly conditioned on pending DTSC review and approval; the memo does not state that DTSC approved this use. For the northerly slope of Stockpile 2, which will not receive the structural pavement section, the memo states that fill material from previously approved clean fill sources will be required instead of the Bent 2 material — an outright exclusion of the Bent 2 stockpiles from that portion of the cap.

## Status and reporting

The memo states this information would be included in the Removal Action Completion Report to be submitted following completion of the Stockpile 1 and 2 clean fill capping activities. The record provided does not show whether DTSC granted the requested approval for the partial-cap use on Stockpile 2's upper surface.
