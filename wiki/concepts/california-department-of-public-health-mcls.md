### Overview

California Department of Public Health (CDPH) Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) are enforceable drinking‑water standards established under the California Safe Drinking Water Act. For the **Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles** site (SR‑132, Stanislaus County), CDPH MCLs serve as the primary human‑health benchmarks for evaluating groundwater quality during investigation and remediation.¹ The project record references these standards to determine whether groundwater contamination from soil stockpile operations requires further action or poses an unacceptable risk to potential receptors.
