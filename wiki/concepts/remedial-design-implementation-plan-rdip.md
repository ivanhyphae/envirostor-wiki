## Overview

The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project outlined the technical approach and detailed plans for the interim containment of barium-containing soil (BCS) and aerially deposited lead (ADL) impacted soil. The RDIP specified the consolidation of these materials as highway embankment fill and their subsequent capping with clean fill and structural pavement. The RDIP was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. in January 2019, with a supplemental RDIP planned for Ultimate Phase 2 construction. Variances to the RDIP were approved by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in April and September 2020.

## Site Record

The RDIP provided specific design details for the containment of three BCS Stockpiles within SR 132 highway embankment fills, referred to as BCS Containment Zones, located between SR 99 and the west end of Stockpile 1. Excavated BCS was generated from the removal of Stockpile 3, bridge abutment excavations at both ends of Stockpile 2, and mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) retaining wall foundation excavations along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2. The RDIP also included provisions for placing shallow soil from the northbound SR 99 shoulder containing ADL into the BCS Containment Zones.

The RDIP established the following soil excavation verification thresholds:
*   **Barium:** 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) for groundwater protection.
*   **Lead:** 80 mg/kg, based on the DTSC residential screening level.

A DTSC-approved Variance to the RDIP, dated April 9, 2020, identified planned clean fill source areas within the project boundaries, including MSE retaining wall foundation excavations adjacent to the southern boundaries of BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2. This Variance also presented site-specific upper-bound background concentrations for comparison with proposed clean fill source area characterization soil samples:
*   **Barium:** 120 mg/kg
*   **Lead:** 3.8 mg/kg

A second DTSC-approved Variance to the RDIP, dated September 2, 2020, clarified the alternative use of a 4-inch-thick layer of Class 3 aggregate base (AB) over the top of the completed BCS Containment Zones, as opposed to the 4-inch-thick layer of AB specified in the RDIP.

Stockpile 3 BCS was completely removed between February and March 2020, with approximately 27,675 yd³ of material transported to the Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone. Verification soil samples from Stockpile 3 removal areas, after initial deepening of some locations, showed barium concentrations ranging from 46 to 970 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg, meeting the RDIP verification thresholds.

The western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2 were removed between February and March 2020 for bridge overcrossing abutment construction. Final verification soil samples from these areas contained barium concentrations ranging from 54 to 760 mg/kg and lead concentrations from 1.5 to 35 mg/kg, meeting the RDIP verification thresholds.

Excavation of the southerly slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 for MSE wall construction in March and April 2020 yielded soil samples with barium concentrations greater than the site-specific background of 120 mg/kg but less than the RDIP removal threshold of 1,000 mg/kg. Lead concentrations were generally within the range of the site-specific background of 3.8 mg/kg and less than the RDIP removal threshold of 80 mg/kg. This material was placed within the BCS Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones.

Air monitoring was conducted between February 2020 and June 2020 during BCS grading activities. Results indicated that dust control measures were effective in reducing airborne particulates below regulatory health-based thresholds.

## Regulatory Status

The RDIP was prepared in general accordance with the criteria and requirements presented in the Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP). The DTSC, in cooperation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB), provides oversight for site mitigation. The RDIP established verification thresholds for barium (1,000 mg/kg) and lead (80 mg/kg). Site-specific background concentrations for clean fill material were established at 120 mg/kg for barium and 3.8 mg/kg for lead.

## Open Items

A Final Interim RACR will be submitted following the completed construction of Interim Phase 1 of the SR 132 West Project, scheduled for opening to traffic in September 2022. A Final RACR will be submitted following Ultimate Phase 2 construction, scheduled for completion in 2028. A supplemental RDIP will be submitted prior to construction of Ultimate Phase 2, providing monitoring and sampling requirements for any planned BCS disturbance, groundwater monitoring well abandonment/protective measures, and final grading/improvement design plans.
