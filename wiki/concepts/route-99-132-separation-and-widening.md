# Route 99 132 Separation And Widening

## Overview
The Route 99 132 Separation And Widening project, also referred to as SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1, involved construction activities in Stanislaus County, California, near Modesto. The project plans were developed by Dokken Engineering and Caltrans. The project scope included widening and separation of Route 99 and Route 132, with construction limits defined by specific post miles and stationing.

## Site Record
The project plans detail typical cross-sections for Route 132, indicating various roadway components and dimensions. For instance, typical cross-sections show traveled ways (ETW), median widths of 18', and shoulder widths ranging from 5' to 22'. These sections also specify the use of "12 inches of clean soil" {{quote: "12 inches of clean soil"}} in certain areas, with slopes ranging from 2% to 10%. Sound walls, such as "sound wall 1142" {{quote: "sound wall 1142"}} and "sound wall 1130" {{quote: "sound wall 1130"}}, are also indicated.

Layout plans for Route 132 show road dimensions, curve data, right-of-way lines, and sound walls. For example, curve 12 is described with a Radius of 8000', Delta of 03°45'24", Tangent of 262.36', and Length of 524.53'. {{quote: "Radius 8000', Delta 03°45'24", Tangent 262.36', Length 524.53'"}}

Construction limits for the project are marked by specific post miles and stationing. For SR99, construction begins at PM R14.6 {{quote: "Begin Construction for SR99 PM R14.6"}} and ends at SR99 PM 17.6 {{quote: "End Construction for SR99 PM 17.6"}}. For Route 132, construction limits are indicated by stationing, such as SR132 1164+30 {{quote: "SR132 1164+30"}}.

The project plans were dated December 10, 2018, and labeled "SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION" {{quote: "SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION 12-10-18"}} and "100%" {{quote: "100%"}}. Plan approval was dated December 31, 2019 {{quote: "12/31/19"}}. Project identification numbers include 10-403504 and 1000000424 {{quote: "10-403504, 1000000424"}}. The Project Engineer listed is Kathryn Wickam {{quote: "Kathryn Wickam"}}.

## Regulatory Status
The project plans were developed for Caltrans. The "100% Design Plans" document is dated December 10, 2018, and was marked "SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION" {{quote: "SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION 12-10-18"}}. Plan approval was granted on December 31, 2019 {{quote: "12/31/19"}}.
