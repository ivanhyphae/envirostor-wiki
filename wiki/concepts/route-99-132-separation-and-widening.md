# Route 99 132 Separation And Widening

## Overview
The Route 99 132 Separation And Widening project, also known as SR 132 West Expressway Phase 1, involved construction activities undertaken by Caltrans in Stanislaus County, California. The project's scope included the separation and widening of Route 99 and Route 132. The design plans were developed by Dokken Engineering.

## Site Record
The project plans detail various design elements for Route 132. Typical cross-sections indicate traveled ways (ETW), median widths of 18', and shoulder widths ranging from 5' to 22'. These sections also specify the use of "12 inches of clean soil" in certain areas, with slopes ranging from 2% to 10%. Sound walls, such as "sound wall 1142" and "sound wall 1130", are also indicated.

Layout plans for Route 132 include specific curve data, such as for "curve 12", which is described with a Radius of 8000', Delta of 03°45'24", Tangent of 262.36', and Length of 524.53'.

Construction limits for the project are defined by post miles and stationing. For SR99, construction begins at PM R14.6 and ends at SR99 PM 17.6. For Route 132, construction limits are indicated by stationing, such as SR132 1164+30.

The project plans were dated December 10, 2018, and marked "SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION" and "100%". Plan approval was dated December 31, 2019. Project identification numbers include 10-403504 and 1000000424. The Project Engineer listed is Kathryn Wickam.

## Regulatory Status
The project plans were developed for Caltrans. The "100% Design Plans" document is dated December 10, 2018, and was marked "SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION". Plan approval was granted on December 31, 2019.
