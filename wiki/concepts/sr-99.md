State Route 99 (SR 99) is a major north-south state highway in California that forms a significant geographical boundary for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project site. The soil stockpiles at this location originated from excavations during the construction of SR 99 in the early 1960s. The highway's alignment and associated ramps are critical for understanding the spatial distribution of the stockpiles and the placement of monitoring infrastructure.

## Site record
SR 99 runs diagonally through the project area near the intersection of Needham Street and Elm Avenue in Modesto. The "Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project" vicinity map (June 2012) by [[geocon-consultants]] identifies three numbered stockpile areas along the SR 99 corridor. Stockpile #3 is situated adjacent to the SR 99 / [[kansas-avenue]] northbound off-ramp. Geocon's improvement plan for the Kansas Avenue NB off-ramp (Figure 2, June 2012) shows the "Barium Pile" and the "Approximate Extent of Proposed Stockpile 3 Construction Excavation" in relation to SR 99's alignment. Monitoring wells MW-9 and MW-10 were installed south of Kansas Avenue, west and east of SR 99, respectively, as documented in the "Additional Well Installation and Groundwater Monitoring Report - June 2012." Wells MW-6, MW-7, and MW-8 are also located within the Stockpile 3 area adjacent to SR 99, as indicated on the site plan (Figure 3, June 2012).

The [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site, encompassing three stockpiles (SP-1, SP-2, and SP-3), is located at State Route 99 and State Route 132 in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California, 95354. The site occupies an approximate 21-acre area bordered by light industrial businesses to the east, commercial businesses to the north, a vacant State-owned parcel to the west, and residential homes to the south.

Stockpile 3 BCS material located adjacent and east of State Route 99 between Kansas Avenue and the Modesto Irrigation District (MID) water canal was excavated and transported for placement as embankment fill within the designated [[stockpile-1]] fill containment zone. A total of 3,075 truck-loads of BCS material totaling approximately 38,440 cubic yards was transported to Stockpile 1.

The BCS removal verification testing was performed in general accordance with the January 2019 [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) prepared for the State Route 132 Project. Each of the final excavation soil samples contained [[barium]] and [[lead]] below the RDIP verification thresholds. Barium concentrations ranged from 46 to 970 mg/kg, and lead concentrations from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg.

The stockpiles are immediately adjacent to State Route 99 (east and west) and south of Kansas Avenue in Modesto.

## Regulatory status
On November 29, 2012, the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC), in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region, issued a letter concurring with the May, June, and July 2012 groundwater monitoring reports for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 99 and 132). DTSC requested that future groundwater monitoring reports for the SR 99 stockpile site be submitted as groundwater data becomes available. The concurrence letter was distributed to [[caltrans]] District 10, the Stanislaus County Health Agency, and the Regional Water Quality Control Board.

## Open items
As per DTSC's directive dated November 29, 2012, future groundwater monitoring reports for the SR 99 Modesto Soil Stockpiles site are to be submitted as data becomes available. The project record does not specify whether subsequent reporting cycles have been completed.
