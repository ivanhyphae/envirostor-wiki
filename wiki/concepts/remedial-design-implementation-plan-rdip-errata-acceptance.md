The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) Errata Acceptance signifies the formal acknowledgment and approval of modifications or clarifications to the original RDIP for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] remediation case file. This acceptance is a critical step in the remediation process, confirming that proposed changes align with the overall remedial objectives and regulatory requirements.

### RDIP Errata Acceptance

The [[central-valley-rwqcb]] accepted the RDIP Errata on March 29, 2016. This acceptance was a condition for the subsequent approval of the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) itself. The RDIP Errata addressed specific aspects of the remediation plan, ensuring that the proposed actions were adequately detailed and compliant with regulatory standards.

### Barium Concentrations and Remediation

The RDIP Errata specifically addressed the management of [[barium]]-containing soil. The [[california-mcl]] for barium is 1 mg/L. The RDIP Errata confirmed that the proposed remediation strategy would manage soil with barium concentrations exceeding this threshold.
