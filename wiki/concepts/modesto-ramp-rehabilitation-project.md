## Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project

### Overview
The Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project involved the remediation of soil stockpiles associated with the SR 99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off-Ramp project in Stanislaus County, California. The remediation efforts focused on addressing concentrations of certain metals in the soil that exceeded applicable screening levels.

### Site Record
Soil samples collected from stockpiles associated with the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project indicated the presence of arsenic, barium, and lead.

*   **Arsenic:** Concentrations of arsenic were found to exceed residential and commercial/industrial screening levels.
*   **Barium:** Concentrations of barium were found to exceed residential and commercial/industrial screening levels.
*   **Lead:** Lead was also identified as a constituent of concern in the soil stockpiles.

The specific document referenced, "08.30.2012 Caltrans Ramp5_srb RAdocx_srb 8_15_12_JS081612 RA4", details these findings.

### Regulatory Status
The remediation of the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project soil stockpiles was subject to regulatory oversight. A DTSC-approval-letter was issued, indicating regulatory decisions regarding the remediation plan and its execution.
