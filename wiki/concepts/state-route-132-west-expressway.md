## State Route 132 West Expressway Soil Stockpiles

### Overview

The State Route 132 (SR 132) West Expressway project involves the management of soil stockpiles, specifically within [[basin-5]]. These stockpiles have been identified as containing barium and lead. Remediation efforts have focused on capping these stockpiles with clean fill, as detailed in the [[clean-fill-sampling-plan]] approved by the DTSC on June 12, 2020. Subsequent evaluations have determined that the encapsulated soil stockpiles are not impacting, and will not impact, groundwater.

### Site Record

On June 11, 2020, Caltrans submitted a [[clean-fill-sampling-plan]] to the DTSC for review and approval. This plan outlined proposed sampling and analysis for soil intended to cap the SR 132 stockpiles. The sampling frequency was based on the location of Basin 5 adjacent to and north of a previously tested SR 132 roadway cut section east of Carpenter Road, which exposed non-impacted native formation. Similar conditions were anticipated at Basin 5. The DTSC, in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], reviewed the plan and found it consistent with previous borrow area sampling, issuing approval via email on June 12, 2020.

An "Updated Comparative Evaluation of Groundwater Data, Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, Stanislaus County, California (Site Code: 900259)" dated February 26, 2024, was submitted by Geocon Consultants Inc. on behalf of Caltrans. This report evaluated the potential impact of barium and lead impacted soil stockpiles beneath the newly constructed SR 132 Expressway on groundwater. The evaluation was consistent with an earlier 2014 evaluation and the recent encapsulation of the soil.

On May 3, 2024, the DTSC, in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], approved this evaluation report. They concurred that the data indicates the encapsulated soil is not impacting groundwater and will not impact groundwater in the future.

### Regulatory Status

The DTSC and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] have reviewed and approved the sampling plan for clean fill material and the subsequent groundwater evaluation report for the SR 132 encapsulated soil stockpiles. Based on the "Updated Comparative Evaluation of Groundwater Data, Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, Stanislaus County, California (Site Code: 900259)" dated February 26, 2024, the DTSC and [[central-valley-rwqcb]] concur that groundwater is not impacted and will not be impacted by the stockpiles.

### Open Items

As per the approval of the groundwater evaluation report on May 3, 2024, the DTSC and [[central-valley-rwqcb]] concur with the recommendation to discontinue groundwater monitoring beneath the soil stockpiles. The four remaining monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, and MW10) are to be decommissioned in accordance with the approved Remedial Design and Implementation Plan.
