## Overhead Sign 1 Foundation Excavation

### Overview
Overhead Sign 1 Foundation Excavation is a remedial excavation component of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, specifically performed to remove petroleum-impacted soil from the foundation site for a planned overhead sign structure along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County, California. The excavation was conducted in connection with management of spoils from Stockpile 2, a stockpile of soil excavated during previous construction activities and later found to contain petroleum hydrocarbon contamination. The excavation was documented in the *Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Workplan Tech Memo* (hereafter “the Workplan Tech Memo”), which governs the handling and disposal of soil generated during foundation construction.

### Site Record
- **Location:** Adjacent to Stockpile 2, within the SR 132 corridor, Stanislaus County, CA. The foundation is for Overhead Sign 1, a structure supporting traffic signage over the highway.
- **Excavation Scope:** The Workplan Tech Memo specifies that soil excavated during the foundation construction for Overhead Sign 1 is classified as “spoils” and must be managed in accordance with the Stockpile 2 BCS (Beneficial Use and Construction Specifications) framework. The volume of excavated soil is not explicitly quantified in the source document but is assumed to be consistent with typical overhead sign foundations (approximately 10–30 cubic yards, based on standard design, though this is not confirmed in the document).
- **Contamination Present:** The soil at the excavation site is documented to contain **total petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH)** as diesel-range organics (DRO) and gasoline-range organics (GRO), with concentrations reported at **128 mg/kg TPH-DRO** and **32 mg/kg TPH-GRO** in sample data referenced in the Workplan Tech Memo. These values are from representative sampling of the Stockpile 2 area, but the memo does not provide specific results from the excavation itself.
- **Verification Results:** No post-excavation confirmation sampling results are included in the Workplan Tech Memo. The memo indicates that verification sampling would be performed to confirm removal of impacted soil, but no data are provided.
- **Document Date:** The Workplan Tech Memo is undated in the provided source, but the Stockpile 2 project timeline (based on related records) places the excavation in the **Phase 2 or Phase 3 period** (approximately 2021–2022), after initial stockpile characterization.

### Regulatory Status
- **Applicable Standards:** The excavation’s remedial goals are based on **DTSC’s Environmental Screening Levels (ESLs)** for direct exposure and groundwater protection, specifically **TPH-DRO at 100 mg/kg** and **TPH-GRO at 100 mg/kg** per the site’s Remedial Action Plan (RAP). The documented concentrations (TPH-DRO at 128 mg/kg) exceed the ESL for DRO.
- **Waste Classification:** Excavated soil is classified as **non-hazardous waste** for disposal, per the Workplan Tech Memo, based on the absence of RCRA metals or VOCs above hazardous thresholds. The memo references a **waste profiling analysis** (not provided in the source) showing TPH levels below California hazardous waste criteria of 1,000 mg/kg.
- **Agency Oversight:** The project is under oversight by the **California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)** and **Caltrans Division of Environmental Engineering**. The Workplan Tech Memo was submitted as a deliverable under DTSC’s corrective action approval process for Stockpile 2.
- **Compliance Status:** As of the Workplan Tech Memo, the excavation is in compliance with approved workplan conditions. However, the memo notes that final verification results must be submitted to DTSC within 60 days of excavation completion to demonstrate achievement of clean closure.

### Open Items
- **Post-Excavation Verification Results:** The Workplan Tech Memo does not include results from confirmation samples collected from the excavation floor and sidewalls. These data are required to confirm that TPH-DRO concentrations are below the 100 mg/kg ESL. No such results have been identified in the available project record.
- **Disposal Records:** The memo requires that soil exceeding ESLs be transported to a permitted landfill (e.g., **Keller Canyon Landfill** or **Potrero Hills Landfill**, as used elsewhere in the project), but manifests or load-out records are not provided. The regulatory status of off-site disposal remains undocumented in this source.
- **Vapor Intrusion Assessment:** The excavation is adjacent to the highway travel lanes, but the Workplan Tech Memo does not address potential vapor intrusion risks to workers during excavation or to future sign maintenance personnel. This may be an oversight requiring a supplemental assessment.
