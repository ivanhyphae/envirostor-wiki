## Overview
The Overhead Sign #1 foundation excavation is a planned excavation within the southern portion of the Stockpile 2 BCS Containment Zone for the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]]. This excavation is anticipated to generate approximately 18.2 cubic yards of [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS). The management and disposal/reuse of this BCS material are addressed in the "Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Assessment Workplan" prepared by [[geocon-consultants]].

## Site Record
The foundation excavation for Overhead Sign #1 is planned at approximate Station 1139+30 {{quote: "Overhead Sign #1 (approximate Station 1139+30): planned 5-foot-diameter by 25 foot-deep foundation excavation that will generate approximately 18.2 cubic yards of BCS material."}}. It is projected to be a 5-foot-diameter by 25-foot-deep excavation {{quote: "planned 5-foot-diameter by 25 foot-deep foundation excavation"}}. This excavation is expected to generate approximately 18.2 cubic yards of [[barium-containing-soil]] {{quote: "generate approximately 18.2 cubic yards of BCS material."}}. This material, along with spoils from the [[weather-station-foundation-slab-anchor]] excavation, is conservatively estimated to total 40 cubic yards {{quote: "The excavated BCS material generated from the weather station and overhead sign foundations (conservative estimate of 40 cubic yards)"}}.

The BCS material will be stockpiled prior to offsite landfill disposal or onsite reuse {{quote: "will be stockpiled prior to offsite landfill disposal or onsite reuse."}}. The stockpiled material will be pre-moistened and covered with minimum 6-millimeter-thick plastic sheeting, secured with ropes and weights, and surrounded by erosion control measures {{quote: "The pre-moistened BCS material will be placed on and covered with minimum 6-milimeter-thick plastic sheeting surrounded by erosion control measures (i.e. straw wattles, hay bales, etc.) as necessary. The stockpile cover will be secured with ropes and weights (i.e. sand bags, tires, etc.) as necessary."}}. Eight discrete soil samples will be collected from the stockpile {{quote: "Eight discrete soil samples will be collected from the stockpile on a grid pattern at various depths."}}. These samples will be composited into two 4-part composite samples {{quote: "The laboratory will be instructed to composite the eight discrete soil samples into two 4-part composite samples."}} and analyzed for Title 22 metals {{quote: "The composite soil samples will be analyzed for Title 22 metals following Environmental Protection Agency Test Methods 6010B and 7471A (mercury) under expedited 48-hour turn-around time."}}.

The total [[barium]] and [[lead]] concentrations will be compared to site-specific background levels of Barium - 120 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) and Lead - 3.8 mg/kg {{quote: "The total barium and lead concentrations will be compared to the following site-specific background levels:\n\n```\nBarium - 120 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg)\nLead - 3.8 mg/kg\n```"}}. If analytical data does not exceed these background concentrations, the BCS may be suitable for onsite reuse, subject to approval by Caltrans and DTSC {{quote: "In the event the total barium and lead analytical data does not exceed respective site-specific background concentrations, the stockpiled BCS may be suitable for onsite reuse provided Caltrans and DTCS approval of the proposed reuse location."}}. If onsite reuse is not approved, the BCS material will be profiled for acceptance at Forward Landfill in Manteca or another accepting landfill {{quote: "In the event Caltrans and/or DTSC do not approve of onsite reuse of the regulated BCS stockpile soil, the BCS material will be profiled for acceptance at Forward Landfill in Manteca or other accepting landfil"}}.

## Regulatory Status
The management of the BCS material from the Overhead Sign #1 foundation excavation is subject to Caltrans and [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) approval for onsite reuse {{quote: "provided Caltrans and DTCS approval of the proposed reuse location."}}. The material will be analyzed to determine if it is a California or Federal Resource Conservation and Recovery Act hazardous waste, based on soluble metals testing if total metal concentrations are elevated {{quote: "Soluble metals testing using the Waste Extraction Test and Toxicity Characteristic Leaching Procedure may be required for samples with elevated total metal concentrations to determine if the BCS material is a California or Federal Resource Conservation and Recovery Act hazardous waste."}}. The total barium and lead concentrations will be compared to site-specific background levels of Barium - 120 mg/kg and Lead - 3.8 mg/kg {{quote: "The total barium and lead concentrations will be compared to the following site-specific background levels:\n\n```\nBarium - 120 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg)\nLead - 3.8 mg/kg\n```"}}.
