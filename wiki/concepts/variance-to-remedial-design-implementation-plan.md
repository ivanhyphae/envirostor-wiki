## Variance To Remedial Design Implementation Plan

### Overview

A Variance to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP Variance) was prepared for the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles project, located south of the [[sr-132]]/Kansas Avenue interchange in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. This RDIP Variance addresses the identification and approval of clean cover fill material sources required to cap consolidated [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) within [[stockpile-1]] and stockpile-2 during the [[sr-132]] West Project. The RDIP Variance was prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] in accordance with a request from the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC).

### Site Record

The RDIP Variance, dated April 9, 2020, references the *Remedial Design Implementation Plan - Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles,* dated January 30, 2019. Section 4.7 of the RDIP stipulated that the surface of completed Stockpiles 1 and 2 BCS and ADL consolidation zones would be capped with a minimum 12-inch-thick clean fill material. The initial proposed source for this clean fill was vacant and agricultural land within planned excavation areas along the [[sr-132]] Right-of-Way (ROW) between the Carpenter Road and Rosemore Avenue overcrossings. {{quote: "The highway contractor (Bay Cities Paving and Grading) initially identified an area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road within the SR 132 right-of-way as the clean fill material source for capping Stockpiles 1 and 2."}}

[[geocon-consultants]], in coordination with [[caltrans]] and DTSC, evaluated this area and determined that soil from the planned highway underpass excavation area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road would be suitable. {{quote: "Geocon, in coordination with Caltrans and DTSC, evaluated this area and determined the planned highway underpass excavation area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road would generate soil material suitable for use as clean cover fill material (Geocon February 27, 2020 Technical Memorandum)."}} DTSC subsequently approved this planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area. {{quote: "DTSC subsequently provided approval of the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area."}}

An additional source of clean fill material was identified at proposed [[mechanically-stabilized-earth-wall]] (MSE) wall footing excavations, approximately five feet deep, along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2. [[geocon-consultants]] evaluated the MSE wall footing material along the southern boundary of [[stockpile-1]] following excavation of BCS material. In coordination with [[caltrans]] and DTSC, it was determined that the upper foot of planned wall footing excavation soil contained elevated barium concentrations, rendering it unsuitable. {{quote: "In coordination with Caltrans and DTSC, it was determined that the upper foot of planned wall footing excavation soil contained elevated barium concentrations that would not be suit"}}

A Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020, detailed characterization testing of MSE wall footing excavation soil for stockpile-2. Soil samples were collected from one-foot intervals to a maximum planned excavation depth of 3 and 5 feet. Barium was detected in 29 soil samples at concentrations ranging from 53 to 930 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). {{quote: "Barium was detected in each of the 29 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 53 to 930 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg)."}} Fourteen of these samples contained barium concentrations greater than the maximum site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "Fourteen of the 29 soil samples contained barium at concentrations greater than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg."}} Thirteen of the 14 samples with elevated barium were collected at depths between the surface and 3 feet. {{quote: "Thirteen of the 14 soil samples with elevated barium concentrations above the maximum background value were collected at depths between the surface and 3 feet."}} Lead was detected in all samples at concentrations ranging from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg. {{quote: "Lead was detected in each of the soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 3.5 to 22 mg/kg."}}

Based on this data, the upper three feet of soil from the stockpile-2 southerly slope MSE wall footing required placement within designated containment zones. Soil excavated from depths greater than 3 feet was deemed suitable for placement within containment zones, as general embankment fill, or as capping material, pending regulatory agency approval. {{quote: "Based on the data presented herein, the upper three feet of soil excavated from the Stockpile 2 southerly slope MSE wall footing will require placement within the designated Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones. Stockpile 2 southerly slope MSE wall footing material excavated from depths deeper than 3 feet is suitable for placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general embankment fill, or as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material pending regulatory agency approval."}}

An email dated June 22, 2020, from Dean Wright of DTSC to Richard Stewart of [[caltrans]] confirmed DTSC and the Water Board's acceptance of the June 18, 2020, Technical Memorandum. {{quote: "An email dated June 22, 2020, from Dean Wright of DTSC to Richard Stewart of Caltrans confirmed DTSC and the Water Board's acceptance of the June 18, 2020, Technical Memorandum."}} DTSC agreed that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) from the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation must be placed in designated containment zones, but soil below that depth is suitable as embankment fill or capping material. {{quote: "DTSC agreed that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) from the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation must be placed in designated containment zones, but soil below that depth is suitable as embankment fill or capping material."}} This email served as DTSC's official acceptance, and no additional variance was necessary for utilizing the soil below 3 feet bgs. {{quote: "This email served as DTSC's official acceptance, and no additional variance was necessary for utilizing the soil below 3 feet bgs."}}

A subsequent Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021, summarized characterization testing of soil from the Carpenter Road Shoofly. Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil were excavated. {{quote: "Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil will be excavated from the Carpenter Road Shoofly..."}} Soil samples were analyzed for barium and lead. Barium was detected at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 mg/kg, all less than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "Barium was detected in each of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg), less than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg."}} Lead was detected at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg."}} Based on this data, the soil material from the Carpenter Road Shoofly met the BCS clean cap criteria as approved by DTSC. {{quote: "Based on the barium and lead data presented herein, soil material generated from excavation of the Carpenter Road Shoofly meet the BCS clean cap criteria as established in the referenced variance document as approved by DTSC (upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg)."}}

### Regulatory Status

The RDIP Variance was prepared in accordance with requests from the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). {{quote: "In accordance with your request on behalf of the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), we have prepared this Variance to Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP Variance)..."}} DTSC provided approval for the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area. {{quote: "DTSC subsequently provided approval of the planned Carpenter Road clean fill source area."}} DTSC also officially accepted the Technical Memorandum regarding Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation characterization testing via email on June 22, 2020, confirming that soil below 3 feet bgs was suitable for use without an additional variance. {{quote: "DTSC agreed that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) from the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation must be placed in designated containment zones, but soil below that depth is suitable as embankment fill or capping material. This email served as DTSC's official acceptance, and no additional variance was necessary for utilizing the soil below 3 feet bgs."}} The Carpenter Road Shoofly soil was deemed to meet BCS clean cap criteria as approved by DTSC, with barium concentrations below the upper-bound background threshold of 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "Based on the barium and lead data presented herein, soil material generated from excavation of the Carpenter Road Shoofly meet the BCS clean cap criteria as established in the referenced variance document as approved by DTSC (upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg)."}}
