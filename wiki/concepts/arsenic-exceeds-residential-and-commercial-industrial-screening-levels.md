## Arsenic Exceeds Residential And Commercial Industrial Screening Levels

### Overview
This section details the instances where arsenic concentrations in soil at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site exceeded applicable screening levels for residential and commercial industrial land use. These exceedances are a key consideration in the remediation and management of the stockpiled soil.

### Site Record
Arsenic was detected in soil samples at concentrations exceeding residential and commercial industrial screening levels. For example, in samples collected on August 30, 2012, arsenic was found at levels that prompted further review. The specific document detailing these findings is "08.30.2012 Caltrans Ramp5_srb RAdocx_srb 8_15_12_JS081612 RA4".

### Regulatory Status
The screening levels for residential and commercial industrial land use were exceeded by arsenic concentrations. The management and remediation of soils with arsenic above these thresholds are subject to regulatory oversight, as indicated by the involvement of agencies like DTSC.
