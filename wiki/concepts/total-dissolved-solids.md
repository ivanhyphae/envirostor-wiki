## Total Dissolved Solids (TDS)

### Overview
Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) is a constituent analyzed in stormwater runoff samples collected from the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. The analysis of TDS is part of the monitoring program to assess the quality of stormwater runoff from the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]].

### Site Record
Surface water samples collected in January 2018 were analyzed for Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) using Standard Method (SM) 2540C.{{quote: "We delivered the surface water samples to ATL for the following analyses under chain-of-custody protocol: ... Total dissolved solids (TDS) by SM 2540C;"}} {{quote: "Total Dissolved Solids (Residue, Filterable) by SM 2540C - Quality Control"}}

In March 2017, surface water samples were also analyzed for TDS by SM 2540C.{{quote: "We delivered the surface water samples to ATL for the following analyses under chain-of-custody protocol: ... Total dissolved solids (TDS) by SM 2540C;"}} {{quote: "Total Dissolved Solids (Residue, Filterable) by SM 2540C Analyst: BL"}}

Stormwater samples collected in March 2023 and December 2023, and February 2024, were analyzed for Total Dissolved Solids (TDS).{{quote: "Storm water sampling events were conducted by Stantec in March 2023 and by Geocon in December 2023. Reported total dissolved solids (TDS), sulfate, and metal concentrations for the storm water samples during these two events were less than their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) except for thallium, which exceeded the primary MCL in storm water samples collected in December 2023."}} {{quote: "Storm water sampling events were conducted by Stantec in March 2023 and by Geocon in December 2023 and February 2024. Reported total dissolved solids (TDS), sulfate, and metal concentrations for the storm water samples during these events were less than their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) except for thallium, which exceeded the Primary MCL in storm water samples collected in December 2023."}}

### Regulatory Status
The project monitors TDS concentrations against Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs). The reported TDS concentrations in stormwater samples from March 2023, December 2023, and February 2024 were below these MCLs.{{quote: "Reported total dissolved solids (TDS), sulfate, and metal concentrations for the storm water samples during these two events were less than their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) except for thallium, which exceeded the primary MCL in storm water samples collected in December 2023."}} {{quote: "Reported total dissolved solids (TDS), sulfate, and metal concentrations for the storm water samples during these events were less than their Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) except for thallium, which exceeded the Primary MCL in storm water samples collected in December 2023."}}
