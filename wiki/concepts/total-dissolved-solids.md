## Overview
Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) is a constituent analyzed in stormwater runoff samples collected from the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. The analysis of TDS is part of the monitoring program to assess the quality of stormwater runoff from the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]].

## Site Record
Surface water samples collected in January 2018 were analyzed for Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) using Standard Method (SM) 2540C. In March 2017, surface water samples were also analyzed for TDS by SM 2540C. Stormwater samples collected in March 2023, December 2023, and February 2024 were analyzed for Total Dissolved Solids (TDS).

A table from March 2017 shows TDS results for sample ID BG2 on 3/24/2017 as 10 mg/L.

## Regulatory Status
The project monitors TDS concentrations against Primary and Secondary Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs). The reported TDS concentrations in stormwater samples from March 2023, December 2023, and February 2024 were below these MCLs. For the March 24, 2017 sampling event, the reported TDS concentration for BG2 was 10 mg/L, which was below the MCL of 500 mg/L.
