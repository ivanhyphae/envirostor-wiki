## Overview

The **Surface Water Sampling October 2016** event at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles (Site) was conducted on October 28, 2016, to characterize surface water quality in drainage features adjacent to Stockpiles 1 through 3. The sampling was performed by Geocon under Contract No. 06A2184, Task Order No. 1, EA No. 10-0X2700, in accordance with the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)* (Shaw Environmental, Inc., January 2006) and the *Addendum to Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* (Geocon, February 20, 2013). The results were documented in the *Surface Water Sampling Report – October 28, 2016*, submitted to Caltrans District 6 on December 22, 2016, for review by DTSC and other regulatory stakeholders.

## Site Record

- **Sampling Locations**: Shown on Figure 2 (Site Plan) of the report, situated in drainages around Stockpiles 1 through 3, southerly of the intersection of SR 99 and Kansas Avenue, Stanislaus County, California.
