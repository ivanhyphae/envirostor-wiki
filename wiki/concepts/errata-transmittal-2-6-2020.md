Errata Transmittal 2 6 2020 is a communication from [[caltrans]] to the DTSC regarding changes to the roles and responsibilities outlined in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] remediation. This transmittal addresses the period following the DTSC's concurrence with the June 2014 Feasibility Study and Remedial Action Plan, and the subsequent acceptance of the RDIP in January 2019.

The RDIP established the "containment alternative" as the prescribed remedy for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles during the construction of the SR 132 West Expressway project. At the time the RDIP was prepared, Geocon, Inc. was contracted by Caltrans to ensure the containment of the stockpiles in accordance with the Remedial Action Plan and RDIP. However, Geocon is no longer under contract with the state following the RDIP's acceptance by the DTSC.

This Errata Transmittal updates the RDIP to reflect these changes in roles and responsibilities, specifically removing Geocon as the state's designated agent. Caltrans retains responsibility for demonstrating that the remediation work is performed as described in the RDIP, regardless of the consultant ultimately tasked with ensuring stockpile remediation.
