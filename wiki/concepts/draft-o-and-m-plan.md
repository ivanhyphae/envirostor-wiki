The Draft Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] details the procedures for managing the clean cap cover over two barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles. This cap is an integral component of the [[interim-phase-1-state-route-132-west-project]] and serves as part of State Route 132. The plan was submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] on behalf of the Department of Transportation and includes a blank O&M Site Inspection Form. The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in coordination with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), has reviewed the draft plan and provided comments.

## Site Record

The Draft O&M Plan, dated March 2022, was submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Site Code: 900259. The document is titled "COMMENTS ON THE DRAFT OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE PLAN, CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES, INTERIM PHASE 1 STATE ROUTE 132 WEST PROJECT, STANISLAUS COUNTY, CALIFORNIA, DATED MARCH, 2022 (SITE CODE: 900259)".

## Regulatory Status

The DTSC and RWQCB have issued comments on the Draft O&M Plan. Key recommendations include:

*   **Advance Notification for BCS Disturbance:** The plan should specify a minimum of 10 working days advance notice to DTSC before any disturbance of the BCS. This ensures that prior written authorization from DTSC is obtained before the planned disturbance commences, as recommended for Section 2.1.
*   **Health and Safety Code Compliance:** Section 2.4 should explicitly state that Health and Safety Code (HSC) 25123.3(b)(4)(A) requirements will be met. While the stated minimum cap thickness of 6 millimeters meets HSC requirements, adding text to confirm compliance will eliminate ambiguity, as the HSC references mils, not millimeters. The DTSC also noted a misspelling of "millimeter" in this section.
*   **Post-Event Inspections:** Section 3.1 should be revised to include inspections following events that could potentially damage the cap. In addition to annual inspections, the plan should specify inspections after events such as fires, substantial rainfall (defined as 1 inch in 24 hours), significant seismic events, or traffic accidents.
*   **Unauthorized Access Reporting:** Section 2.7 should be updated to note if periodic patrols reveal unauthorized access, such as a homeless encampment, that has resulted in any impact to the cap, particularly on the north vegetated slope.

## Open Items

*   The DTSC identified a misspelling of "Maintenance" in the Subject line of the Draft O&M Plan.
*   The DTSC recommended expanding the O&M Site Inspection Form.

The Draft O&M Plan is intended to be included as Appendix G in the [[remedial-action-completion-report]].
