The Draft Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] outlines the procedures for inspecting, maintaining, and repairing the clean cap cover over two barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles. This cover is part of the [[interim-phase-1-state-route-132-west-project]] and is utilized as a portion of State Route 132. The plan was submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] on behalf of the Department of Transportation and includes a blank O&M Site Inspection Form for field crews. The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), reviewed the draft plan.

## Site Record

The Draft O&M Plan, dated March 2022, was submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Site Code: 900259. {{quote: "COMMENTS ON THE DRAFT OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE PLAN, CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES, INTERIM PHASE 1 STATE ROUTE 132 WEST PROJECT, STANISLAUS COUNTY, CALIFORNIA, DATED MARCH, 2022 (SITE CODE: 900259)"}}

## Regulatory Status

The DTSC and RWQCB have provided comments on the Draft O&M Plan. Key recommendations include:

*   Ensuring a minimum of {{quote: "10 working days advance notice to DTSC of any BCS disturbance"}} to allow for prior written authorization. {{quote: "Section 2.1 This section should include a minimum number of days for the advance notification to DTSC of any BCS disturbance. DTSC recommends 10 working days advance notice to ensure prior written authorization from DTSC is provided before the planned disturbance begins."}}
*   Including text in Section 2.4 to confirm that Health and Safety Code (HSC) 25123.3(b)(4)(A) requirements will be met, specifically regarding the minimum thickness of the cap. {{quote: "Section 2.4 DTSC recommends that this section include text to indicate that Health and Safety Code (HSC) 25123.3(b)(4)(A) requirements will be met. DTSC notes that the minimum thickness of 6 millimeters provided in this section will meet HSC requirements, but since the HSC uses mils (not millimeters), adding the recommended text above will eliminate any ambiguity. DTSC also notes that the word millimeter is currently misspelled and should be corrected."}}
*   Revising Section 3.1 to include inspections after events such as fires, substantial rainfall ({{quote: "1 inch in 24 hours"}}) , significant seismic events, or traffic accidents that could damage the cap, in addition to annual inspections. {{quote: "Section 3.1 DTSC recommends this section be revised to indicate that in addition to the annual inspections, inspections will also occur after events such as fires, substantial rainfall (1 inch in 24 hours), significant seismic events, traffic accidents that may cause damage to the cap, etc."}}
*   Noting in Section 2.7 if periodic patrols reveal unauthorized access, such as a homeless encampment, that has impacted the cap, particularly on the north vegetated slope. {{quote: "Section 2.7 The periodic patrols referenced in this section should also note if unauthorized access such has a homeless encampment has resulted in any impact to the cap. Especially on the north vegetated slope."}}
