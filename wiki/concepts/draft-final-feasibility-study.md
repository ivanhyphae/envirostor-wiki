The Final Feasibility Study for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA) was reviewed and concurred with by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (RWQCB).

## Overview
The Final Feasibility Study addresses comments made by DTSC on the Draft Final Feasibility Study. DTSC has concurred with the Final Feasibility Study.

## Site Record
The Final Feasibility Study report is titled "Final Feasibility Study Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County" and was dated June 24, 2014. It was prepared by Caltrans' contractor, Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Geocon). DTSC's comments on the Draft Final Feasibility Study were dated February 11, 2014. The Draft Final Feasibility Study was prepared by Geocon and dated December 18, 2013.

## Regulatory Status
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) has concurred with the Final Feasibility Study.
