The [[draft-final-feasibility-study]] for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project was prepared by Caltrans' contractor, Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Geocon). The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) reviewed and concurred with the Final Feasibility Study, following their review of the Draft Final Feasibility Study. The Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) was consulted during this process.

## Site Record

The Final Feasibility Study report is titled "Final Feasibility Study Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County" and is dated June 24, 2014 {{quote: "Final Feasibility Study Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County"}}. This report addressed comments made by DTSC on the Draft Final Feasibility Study, which was dated December 18, 2013 {{quote: "DTSC's comments on the Draft Final Feasibility Study were dated February 11, 2014. The Draft Final Feasibility Study was prepared by Geocon and dated December 18, 2013."}}.

## Regulatory Status

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) has concurred with the Final Feasibility Study {{quote: "DTSC has concurred with the Final Feasibility Study."}}.
