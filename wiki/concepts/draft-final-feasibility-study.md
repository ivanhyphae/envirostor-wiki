## Overview
The [[draft-final-feasibility-study]] for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Geocon) on behalf of [[caltrans]]. This study evaluated potential remedial actions for the site. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) reviewed the draft and provided comments, which were addressed in the Final Feasibility Study. The [[central-valley-rwqcb]] was consulted during the process.

## Site Record
The Draft Final Feasibility Study was prepared by Geocon and dated December 18, 2013 {{quote: "The Draft Final Feasibility Study was prepared by Geocon and dated December 18, 2013."}}. DTSC provided comments on this draft study on February 11, 2014 {{quote: "DTSC's comments on the Draft Final Feasibility Study were dated February 11, 2014."}}. The Final Feasibility Study, which addressed DTSC's comments, is titled "Final Feasibility Study Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County" and is dated June 24, 2014 {{quote: "Final Feasibility Study Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County"}}.

## Regulatory Status
The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] has concurred with the Final Feasibility Study {{quote: "DTSC has concurred with the Final Feasibility Study."}}.
