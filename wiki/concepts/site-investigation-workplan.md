The Site Investigation Workplan for the Modesto Ramp Rehabilitation Project outlines the procedures and methods to be employed for a site investigation at the State Route 99 (SR99) Kansas Avenue Off-ramp in Modesto, California. This workplan was submitted in accordance with Caltrans Contract No. 06A1634 and Work Request EA No. 10-0A671.

## Site Record

The Site Investigation Workplan was submitted by Geocon Consultants to Caltrans.{{quote: "In accordance with the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) Contract No. 06A1634 and Work Request EA No. 10-0A671, we are submitting this Workplan for a site investigation to be conducted at the State Route 99 (SR99) Kansas Avenue Off-ramp (the Project) in Modesto, California."}} The workplan details the scope of services requested by Caltrans and the methodologies to be used.{{quote: "This Workplan describes the scope of services requested by Caltrans and outlines the procedures and methods we will employ to complete the project."}}
