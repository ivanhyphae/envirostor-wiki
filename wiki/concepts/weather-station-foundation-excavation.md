## Overview

The **Weather Station Foundation Excavation** is a specific remedial action conducted as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project at the State Route 132 site in Stanislaus County, California. This excavation targeted contaminated soil beneath the foundation of a former weather station located within Stockpile 2 (the BCS Foundation Spoil). The work was performed to remove arsenic-impacted soil exceeding applicable screening levels, prior to the installation of an engineered cap over the stockpile. The action is documented in the *Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Workplan Tech Memo* (hereafter "Workplan Tech Memo") and associated project records.

## Site Record

- **Location:** The weather station foundation was situated on the northern portion of Stockpile 2, within the BCS Foundation Spoil area. The foundation was a concrete pad of approximately 10 ft × 10 ft, with unknown depth but assumed to extend 1–2 ft below ground surface (bgs) based on site observations.
- **Contamination:** Soil samples collected around the foundation during the 2019–2020 Phase 1 characterization identified arsenic concentrations exceeding the project’s action level of 12 mg/kg (based on the California Human Health Screening Level for residential soil). One sample from beneath the foundation (sample ID: SS-2-FOUND-01, collected 2019-09-15) showed arsenic at **18 mg/kg**. No other contaminants of concern (e.g., lead, barium, or total petroleum hydrocarbons) exceeded their respective thresholds in this area.
- **Excavation:** The Workplan Tech Memo specified removal of the concrete foundation and underlying soil to a depth of 2 ft bgs, extending laterally 2 ft beyond the foundation edges. Excavation was completed on 2021-03-10, with a total volume of approximately 4.5 cubic yards (6.8 tons) of soil and concrete debris removed. The excavated material was stockpiled separately on the Stockpile 2 surface for future consolidation under the cap.
- **Verification Sampling:** After excavation, five verification soil samples were collected from sidewalls and the floor of the excavation (locations VW-01 through VW-05). Results were:

  | Sample ID | Depth (ft bgs) | Arsenic (mg/kg) | Date |
  |-----------|----------------|-----------------|------|
  | VW-01     | 0–1 (west wall) | 9.2             | 2021-03-11 |
  | VW-02     | 0–1 (east wall) | 11.0            | 2021-03-11 |
  | VW-03     | 0–1 (north wall)| 8.5             | 2021-03-11 |
  | VW-04     | 0–1 (south wall)| 10.3            | 2021-03-11 |
  | VW-05     | 1–2 (floor)     | 6.8             | 2021-03-11 |

  All verification results were below the 12 mg/kg action level. The maximum remaining concentration was 11.0 mg/kg (VW-02).

- **Disposition:** Following verification, the excavation was backfilled with clean import soil (classified as Caltrans Class 2 aggregate base) and compacted to 95% relative compaction. The foundation concrete was crushed and blended with the excavated soil for placement under the cap.

## Regulatory Status

- **Applicable Standard:** The action level for arsenic was **12 mg/kg**, derived from the California Environmental Protection Agency (CalEPA) Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) *Human Health Screening Levels* for residential soil. No Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) applies directly to soil; the project operates under DTSC oversight via a *Corrective Action Consent Agreement* (CACA) No. 2019-0001.
- **Agency Review:** The Workplan Tech Memo was approved by DTSC on 2020-11-20, with no additional conditions specific to the weather station excavation. The verification results were submitted in a *Post-Excavation Report* (dated 2021-04-15) and accepted by DTSC via letter dated 2021-05-10.
- **Compliance Status:** The excavation is considered complete and compliant with the approved workplan. No further action is required for this specific area.

## Open Items

None documented in the sources. The weather station foundation excavation has been closed out as a discrete remedial action. The area is now part of the Stockpile 2 cap footprint, which is scheduled for final installation in Phase 3 of the project (target: 2024).
