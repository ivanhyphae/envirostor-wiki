The Remedial Action Completion Report (RACR) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project documents the successful remediation of barium-containing soils (BCS) to facilitate the construction of the State Route 132 West Project. Prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] for [[caltrans]], the RACR details the excavation, consolidation, and capping of BCS, and was reviewed and accepted by the [[dtsc]] in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]].

### Site Description and Remediation Activities

The project site initially contained three BCS stockpiles within the State Route 132 right-of-way. Stockpile 1 was located approximately 150 feet west of Emerald Avenue, Stockpile 2 was situated between Emerald Avenue and State Route 99, and Stockpile 3 was formerly east of State Route 99. Remediation involved excavating Stockpile 3, portions of Stockpile 2, and parts of the southern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2. Approximately 27,675 cubic yards of soil from BCS Stockpile 3 were relocated and utilized as embankment fill within the BCS Stockpile 1 containment zone.

### Verification Sampling and Cleanup Goals

Following excavation of the BCS Stockpile 3 area, twenty-nine confirmation soil samples were collected from the excavation bottom and analyzed for [[barium]] and lead. Three samples initially exceeded the cleanup goal for barium of 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). These areas were subsequently deepened by approximately one foot and resampled, with all final confirmation samples meeting the established cleanup goals.

### Regulatory Acceptance of RACR

A Draft Interim Removal Action Completion Report was submitted by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] on February 24, 2022, and accepted by the [[dtsc]] in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. A subsequent Removal Action Completion Report (RACR), dated December 1, 2022, was submitted by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] and included As-Built Plans, cross-section figures, final survey data, and the final Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan. The [[dtsc]] formally accepted this RACR as final on January 18, 2023. This acceptance signified the completion of soil removal, consolidation, and capping activities in accordance with the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and Remedial Design and Implementation Plan (RDIP), including two approved variance requests.

### Ongoing and Future Requirements

While the Interim Phase 1 remediation is concluded with the acceptance of the RACR, a draft O&M Agreement and a Land Use Covenant (LUC) for the remediated area are to be prepared by the [[dtsc]] for [[caltrans]] review and recording. Final certification of the RAP will occur after the completion of the Ultimate Phase 2 State Route 132 build-out, which is anticipated in 8 to 10 years and will require a supplemental RDIP, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and potential revisions to the O&M Plan and Agreement.
