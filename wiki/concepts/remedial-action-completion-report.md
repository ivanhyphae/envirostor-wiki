## Remedial Action Completion Report

The Remedial Action Completion Report (RACR) for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] will incorporate the [[operations-and-maintenance-plan]] as an appendix. This report serves to document the completion of remedial actions for barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles at the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] site, specifically addressing the portion of State Route 132 (SR 132) designated as a clean cap cover. The [[operations-and-maintenance-plan]] details the required activities for the inspection, maintenance, and repair of this cap.

## Site Record

The Draft Operation and Maintenance Plan, dated March 2022, was submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of the Department of Transportation for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1 State Route 132 West Project, Stanislaus County, California (Site Code: 900259) {{quote: "COMMENTS ON THE DRAFT OPERATION AND MAINTENANCE PLAN, CALTRANS MODESTO SOIL STOCKPILES, INTERIM PHASE 1 STATE ROUTE 132 WEST PROJECT, STANISLAUS COUNTY, CALIFORNIA, DATED MARCH, 2022 (SITE CODE: 900259)"}}.

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), reviewed the Draft O&M Plan {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), has reviewed the Draft Operation and Maintenance Plan (Draft O&M Plan) noted above which will be included in the Remedial Action Completion Report as Appendix G."}}.

Specific comments on the Draft O&M Plan included:
*   A misspelling of "Maintenance" in the Subject line {{quote: "The word "Maintenance" is misspelled in the Subject line of the Draft O&M Plan"}}.
*   A recommendation to include a minimum of 10 working days advance notification to DTSC for any BCS disturbance, to ensure prior written authorization {{quote: "Section 2.1 This section should include a minimum number of days for the advance notification to DTSC of any BCS disturbance. DTSC recommends - 10 working days advance notice to ensure prior written authorization from DTSC is provided before the planned disturbance begins."}}.
*   A recommendation to add text to Section 2.4 to explicitly state that Health and Safety Code (HSC) 25123.3(b)(4)(A) requirements will be met, noting that the minimum thickness of 6 millimeters provided meets HSC requirements, but clarification is needed due to the HSC using mils {{quote: "Section 2.4 DTSC recommends that this section include text to indicate that Health and Safety Code (HSC) 25123.3(b)(4)(A) requirements will be met. DTSC notes that the minimum thickness of 6 millimeters provided in this section will meet HSC requirements, but since the HSC uses mils (not millimeters), adding the recommended text above will eliminate any ambiguity."}}. A misspelling of "millimeter" was also noted {{quote: "DTSC also notes that the word millimeter is currently misspelled and should be corrected."}}.
*   A recommendation for Section 3.1 to include inspections after events such as fires, substantial rainfall (1 inch in 24 hours), significant seismic events, or traffic accidents that may damage the cap, in addition to annual inspections {{quote: "Section 3.1 DTSC recommends this section be revised to indicate that in addition to the annual inspections, inspections will also occur after events such as fires, substantial rainfall (1 inch in 24 hours), significant seismic events, traffic accidents that may cause damage to the cap, etc."}}. This is particularly important for the north side of the freeway, which will remain a vegetated slope until the final phase of the freeway is complete {{quote: "This will help ensure the integrity of the cap is maintained especially on the north side of the freeway which will remain a vegetated slope until the final phase of the freeway is complete."}}.
*   A recommendation that periodic patrols referenced in Section 2.7 should also note if unauthorized access, such as a homeless encampment, has impacted the cap, especially on the north vegetated slope {{quote: "Section 2.7 The periodic patrols referenced in this section should also note if unauthorized access such has a homeless encampment has resulted in any impact to the cap. Especially on the north vegetated slope."}}.
*   A recommendation to expand the O&M Site Inspection Form {{quote: "O&M Site Inspection Form DTSC recommends expanding the lin"}}.

The DTSC review was conducted in consultation with the RWQCB {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), has reviewed the Draft Operation and Maintenance Plan (Draft O&M Plan) noted above which will be included in the Remedial Action Completion Report as Appendix G."}}. The DTSC comments were sent via electronic mail on August 1, 2022, to Mr. John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager, Geocon Consultants, Inc. {{quote: "Sent Via Electronic Mail August 1, 2022 Mr. John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG Project Manager Geocon Consultants, Inc 3160 Gold Valley Drive, Suite 800 Rancho Cordova, California 95742 [Juhrend@geoconinc.com](mailto:juhrend@geoconinc.com)"}}.

## Regulatory Status

The DTSC, in consultation with the RWQCB, reviewed the Draft O&M Plan {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), has reviewed the Draft Operation and Maintenance Plan (Draft O&M Plan) noted above which will be included in the Remedial Action Completion Report as Appendix G."}}. DTSC provided comments and recommendations for revisions to the Draft O&M Plan {{quote: "After reviewing both documents, the following comments are provided:"}}.

## Open Items

The DTSC provided comments on the Draft O&M Plan that require revisions before it can be finalized for inclusion in the Remedial Action Completion Report. These include addressing misspellings, clarifying notification periods for BCS disturbance, confirming compliance with HSC requirements, expanding inspection triggers, and detailing reporting of unauthorized access impacts {{quote: "After reviewing both documents, the following comments are provided:"}}.
