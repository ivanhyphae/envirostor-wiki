# Modesto Soil Stockpiles Project

## Overview
The Modesto Soil Stockpiles Project is a Caltrans remediation site located southerly of the intersection of State Route 99 and Kansas Avenue in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. The soil stockpiles consist of excess native soils and pond tailings generated during construction of a segment of SR 99 north of Kansas Avenue in the early 1960s. Excavation traversed a portion of a 4.3-acre parcel purchased from Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC), where an evaporation pond was located in the southernmost corner. FMC and its predecessors processed barium sulfate and strontium sulfate ores and other minerals at the site. The primary chemicals of concern are [[barium]], lead, and strontium.

[[dtsc]], in consultation with the Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region, provides regulatory oversight. geocon-consultants has performed groundwater monitoring under Caltrans Contract No. 06A1895, Task Order No. 17. The site contains three stockpiles (Stockpile-1, Stockpile-2, Stockpile-3) and a network of ten groundwater monitoring wells ([[mws-1-10]]).

## Site record

### Site configuration and monitoring network
The site boundaries and stockpiles are depicted on the Site Plan (Figure 2) in the June 2014 Groundwater Monitoring Report. Ten monitoring wells were installed at the site: MW-1 through MW-8 were installed prior to 2012, and MW-9 and MW-10 were installed in June 2012. MW-9 is located south of Kansas Avenue and west of SR 99; MW-10 is located south of Kansas Avenue and east of SR 99. Wells were resurveyed by Morrow Surveying on June 18, 2012.

### Groundwater monitoring history
Groundwater monitoring resumed at the site in March 2012 under protocols approved by DTSC, as established in the *Final Work Plan, Groundwater Assessment* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc., dated January 2006. Monitoring was performed quarterly as of the June 2014 reporting period, with the next event scheduled for September 2014.

Prior data was documented in the *Site Investigation Report, Groundwater Assessment, Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles State Route 99/132 Project, Stanislaus County, California*, prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc., dated May 14, 2007.

DTSC reviewed three monitoring reports in a letter dated November 29, 2012, from Randy S. Adams, C.E.G., Senior Engineering Geologist, to Richard Stewart, P.G. of Caltrans: (1) "Groundwater Monitoring Report - May 2012," (2) "Additional Well Installation and Groundwater Monitoring Report - June 2012," and (3) "Groundwater Monitoring Report - July 2012," all prepared by Geocon Consultants and dated November 2012. DTSC concurred with the reports and requested that future groundwater monitoring reports be completed as groundwater data becomes available.

### June 2014 sampling results

**Metals:** None of the reported dissolved metals concentrations for groundwater samples collected in June 2014 exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium were reported at concentrations similar to historical levels and remained significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds. The remaining dissolved metals were also reported at concentrations similar to historical levels. Per Figure 4 (Barium Concentrations vs. Time), all data points from MW-1 through MW-10 across the monitoring period from May 2006 through June 2014 plotted well below the 1,000 µg/L water quality threshold. Per Figure 6 (Strontium Concentrations vs. Time), strontium concentrations through June 2014 remained below the 4,000 µg/L water quality threshold.

**General minerals:** Nitrate was reported in samples from wells MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, and MW-10 at concentrations equal to or exceeding California primary MCLs. Total dissolved solids (TDS) was reported at concentrations exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L for samples from wells MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10.

**Ionic composition:** Stiff diagrams for 2012 through June 2014 show very slight changes in ionic content since groundwater sampling resumed. Water samples from wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-7, MW-9, and MW-10 have consistently been calcium-dominant. Wells MW-3, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-8 have been sodium-dominant. Well MW-5 switched from calcium-dominant to sodium-dominant in 2013, which may indicate mixing of different water types at that location.

**PAHs:** Not analyzed or not applicable per project records.

## Regulatory status
The project operates under DTSC oversight in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board. Regulatory thresholds applied include California EPA Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) per May 2009 standards, including primary and secondary MCLs. The California Department of Public Health Notification Level and Regulatory Action Level, and EPA Drinking Water Health Advisory values are also referenced in the analytical results tables.

As of June 2014, dissolved metals including barium, lead, and strontium were below numeric water quality thresholds at all monitoring wells. Nitrate exceeded primary MCLs at five wells (MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, MW-10), and TDS exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L at five wells (MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, MW-10). DTSC concurred with the 2012 monitoring reports and directed future reporting to proceed as data becomes available.
