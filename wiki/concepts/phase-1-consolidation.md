Phase 1 Consolidation refers to the interim remedial measure described in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan – Interim Phase 1 (Geocon Consultants, Inc., January 2019), prepared for Caltrans District 6 Hazardous Waste Branch for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site along the [[sr-132-west-expressway-project]] corridor in Stanislaus County. The plan addressed three barium-containing soil stockpiles (Stockpile 1, Stockpile 2, and Stockpile 3) located near the Former FMC Facility and former disposal ponds, within an area also containing an Aerially Deposited Lead (ADL) removal area and ten groundwater monitoring wells (MW1–MW10).

## What the plan covered

The RDIP Interim Phase 1 figures identify the project area along SR-132 West, showing the SR-132 West Project Limits, the Barium-Containing Soil Stockpile area, and a Below Grade Cut Section, referenced against the Former FMC Facility boundary and former disposal ponds. Of the ten monitoring wells shown on the site map, the legend distinguishes wells to be protected in place from those to be abandoned, indicating that well disposition was addressed as part of the interim implementation.

The material available does not describe the specific consolidation methodology, volumes of soil involved, or the sequence of construction activities beyond identifying the plan's title, preparer, and the site features it addressed.

## Significance and open questions

Because this is an "Interim Phase 1" plan, it establishes that the [[barium]]-containing stockpiles were subject to a staged remedial approach rather than a single final remedy, and that groundwater monitoring wells were being triaged for retention or abandonment as part of that staging. The available source material does not include the plan's approval status, any [[dtsc]] or other agency response to it, or confirmation that the consolidation described was carried out as proposed. Any subsequent conditions, land-use restrictions, or completion documentation tied to this phase are not present in the material reviewed.
