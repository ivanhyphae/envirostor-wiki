## Phase 1 Consolidation

### Overview

Phase 1 Consolidation refers to the initial phase of remediation activities for the [[soil-stockpiles]] associated with the [[sr-132-west-expressway-project]] in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. This phase was implemented under the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) approved by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). The primary goal was the containment of barium-containing soils. [[caltrans]] is the responsible party for the implementation of the RAP and RDIP, with oversight from DTSC and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB).

### Site Record

The Remedial Design Implementation Plan – Interim Phase 1, dated January 30, 2019, was prepared for Caltrans by Geocon-Consultants, Inc. This plan detailed the containment alternative for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. The RDIP was approved by the DTSC on April 3, 2019.

Caltrans is responsible for verifying that highway construction work related to the soil stockpiles conforms to the RAP and RDIP. This includes:
*   Supervising the abandonment of impacted groundwater monitoring wells.
*   Observing and documenting excavation, fill placement, and capping of barium-containing soils.
*   Performing verification soil sampling and analysis for barium-containing soil removal.
*   Conducting sampling and analysis of proposed clean soil imports.
*   Performing worker zone and perimeter air monitoring.
*   Conducting periodic stormwater monitoring, including observations and sampling.
*   Reviewing RDIP requirements with the construction manager and contractor.
*   Coordinating with the Caltrans Environmental Manager and DTSC and CVRWQCB Project Managers.

The RDIP itself was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. under an on-call contract with Caltrans for hazardous waste services. An Errata to the January 2019 RDIP was issued post-construction contract award to update roles and responsibilities, specifically noting that Caltrans remains the responsible party regardless of the consultant performing the work.

### Regulatory Status

The Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for mitigation of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles was approved by the DTSC. The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) was submitted to the DTSC and received conditional approval on August 20, 2018, pending administrative revisions and review of design plans. A subsequent conditional approval was issued on October 12, 2018, after incorporation of revisions. The RDIP was formally approved on April 3, 2019. Caltrans is responsible for implementation under regulatory oversight and approval by DTSC, in cooperation with the CVRWQCB.

### Open Items

The Errata document indicates that a complete set of 100% design plans and final project specifications were to be submitted to DTSC and CVRWQCB in early 2019.
