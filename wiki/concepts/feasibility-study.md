## Overview
The Feasibility Study (FS) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. The purpose of the FS was to The study evaluated four alternatives: no action, institutional controls, removal (excavation and offsite disposal), and containment. The recommended alternative was containment, achieved by using the stockpiles as embankment material for the [[state-route-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]]. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC), in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], reviewed and concurred with the Final Feasibility Study.
