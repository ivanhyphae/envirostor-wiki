## Overview
The Feasibility Study (FS) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. The purpose of the FS was to {{quote: "present remedial action objectives (RAOs), general response actions (GRAs), and process options; develop and screen remedial alternatives; and present an individual and comparative analysis of each retained alternative for the stockpiles."}} The study evaluated four alternatives: no action, institutional controls, removal (excavation and offsite disposal), and containment. The recommended alternative was containment, achieved by using the stockpiles as embankment material for the [[state-route-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]]. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC), in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], reviewed and concurred with the Final Feasibility Study.

## Site record
The Final Feasibility Study (FS) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. {{quote: "Project No. S9800-01-17 June 24, 2014"}} The Final FS {{quote: "incorporates revisions to the Draft Final FS made in accordance with comments provided in DTSC's letter dated February 11, 2014."}} The Draft Final FS was dated December 18, 2013. The FS identified that {{quote: "the stockpiles, portions of which contain elevated levels of barium are planned to be contained within the project by utilizing them as embankment material for roadway construction, retaining wall backfill, and bridge abutments."}} It is anticipated that {{quote: "remedial and contour cut/fill grading will be necessary to achieve final finish grades and to properly consolidate and contain the existing soil stockpiles."}} The FS identified four alternatives for evaluation: Alternative 1: No action; Alternative 2: Institutional controls; Alternative 3: Removal (excavation and offsite disposal); and Alternative 4: Containment. The recommended alternative was Alternative 4 – Containment, {{quote: "achieved by use of the stockpiles in construction of the SR-132/SR-99 interchange portion of the planned SR 132 Project"}} {{quote: "which requires a significant amount of fill for the embankments of the SR-132/SR-99 interchange portion of the project"}} {{quote: "and is the reason the stockpiles were placed on the Site in the early 1960s."}}

## Regulatory status
The DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley RWQCB, has reviewed and concurred with the Final Feasibility Study. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the California Regional Water Quality Control Board, Central Valley Region (RWQCB) has reviewed the report titled 'Final Feasibility Study Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County' dated June 24, 2014, prepared by the Department of Transportation's (Caltrans) contractor, Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Geocon). The Final Feasibility Study addresses DTSC's comments for the Draft Final Feasibility Study. DTSC concurs with the Final Feasibility Study."}}

## Open items
The FS identified that {{quote: "Regulatory and community acceptance will be evaluated after the FS has been finalized and the preferred alternative is approved by the DTSC and CVRWQCB."}}
