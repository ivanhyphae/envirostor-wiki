The Feasibility Study (FS) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, located on State Route 132 in Stanislaus County, California, was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] (RWQCB), reviewed and concurred with the Final Feasibility Study.

## Site record

The Final FS Letter dated June 30, 2014, indicates that the Final Feasibility Study addresses comments made by DTSC on the [[draft-final-feasibility-study]] dated December 18, 2013. The DTSC's comments on the Draft Final Feasibility Study were dated February 11, 2014.

## Regulatory status

The DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley RWQCB, has reviewed and concurred with the Final Feasibility Study.
