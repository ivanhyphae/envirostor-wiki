Ms. Lora Jameson, P.G., serves as the Chief of the Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit within the Site Mitigation and Restoration Program at the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). In this capacity, she has issued annual cost estimates for DTSC's oversight of the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]], specifically for Phase 1 of the [[sr-132-west-project]] in Stanislaus County, California.

## Overview

Ms. Jameson's role, as documented in the provided sources, is to formally communicate DTSC's projected oversight costs to [[caltrans]] for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project. These communications are made in accordance with Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5 and are addressed to Caltrans representatives, including Richard Stewart, P.G., and Adam Inman, P.G. The cost estimates cover DTSC's anticipated activities and associated expenses for specific fiscal years.

## Site Record

Ms. Jameson has signed and issued the following annual cost estimates for DTSC oversight of the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project (Site Code: 900259-00):

*   **Fiscal Year 2022-2023:** The cost estimate for the period of July 1, 2022, through June 30, 2023, was issued on October 4, 2022. Tasks included review of the Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan, Fiscal Year Cost Estimate, review and approval of the Interim Phase 1 Remedial Action Completion Report (RACR), completion of the O&M Agreement and Land Use Covenant (LUC), and Project Management. The estimate was based on DTSC's 2021-2022 contract estimation rates.

*   **Fiscal Year 2023-2024:** The cost estimate for the period of July 1, 2023, through June 30, 2024, was issued on November 7, 2023. Tasks included Fiscal Year Cost Estimate, completion of the Land Use Covenant (LUC), review and approval of the Annual Inspection (due by January 31, 2024), and Project Management. The estimate was based on DTSC's 2022-2023 contract estimation rates.

*   **Fiscal Year 2024-2025:** The cost estimate for the period of July 1, 2024, through June 30, 2025, was issued on November 21, 2024. Tasks included Fiscal Year Cost Estimate, review and approval of the Annual Inspection (due by January 18, 2025), and amendment/review of the Interagency Agreement (set to expire on 6/30/2025), and Project Management.

In all issued cost estimates, Ms. Jameson emphasizes that these are projections and that Responsible Parties/Project Proponents remain liable for all costs incurred by DTSC as required by law. She also directs inquiries regarding the cost estimates to Dean Wright, P.G., the Project Manager.

## Regulatory Status

Ms. Jameson, in her capacity as Chief of the Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit, acts on behalf of the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] in overseeing the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]]. The DTSC's oversight is mandated by Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5.
