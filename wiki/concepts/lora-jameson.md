Ms. Lora Jameson, P.G., serves as the Chief of the Site Evaluation and Remediation Unit within the Site Mitigation and Restoration Program at the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). In this capacity, she has issued annual cost estimates for DTSC's oversight of the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]], specifically for Phase 1 of the [[sr-132-west-project]] in Stanislaus County, California.

## Overview

Ms. Jameson's role, as documented in the provided sources, is to formally communicate DTSC's projected oversight costs to [[caltrans]] for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project. These communications are made in accordance with Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5 and are addressed to Caltrans representatives, including Richard Stewart, P.G., and Adam Inman, P.G. The cost estimates cover DTSC's anticipated activities and associated expenses for specific fiscal years.

## Site Record

The estimate was based on DTSC's 2021-2022 contract estimation rates.
