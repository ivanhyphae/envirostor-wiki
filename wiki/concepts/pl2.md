# Pl2

Pl2 is a surface water sampling location on the east side of North Emerald Avenue, between Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2, one of five designated runoff-monitoring points (PL1–PL5) established as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles surface water program and sampled by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] during qualifying rain events between 2013 and 2018. Samples are collected from puddles that pool along the road shoulder during storms. Across the recorded events, Pl2 consistently showed manganese exceeding its secondary MCL, while other dissolved metals — including barium, the site's primary chemical of concern — remained below primary and secondary MCLs.

## Location and sampling context

Pl2 sits along North Emerald Avenue, paired with PL1 on the opposite (west) shoulder; both are described in later reports (2018) as located outside the Caltrans right-of-way, in contrast to PL3, PL4, and PL5, which lie within or at the edge of the right-of-way. Samples were collected using disposable bailers and a suction pump, field-filtered through 0.45-micron filters for dissolved metals analysis, and shipped to Advanced Technology Laboratories under chain-of-custody procedures. Field parameters (pH, conductivity, temperature, turbidity, ORP, and — when the meter functioned — dissolved oxygen) were recorded at each visit.

Pl2 was sampled in most events but not all: it was dry or had insufficient water at no point in the reviewed record, unlike PL5 and BG2, which were frequently dry (e.g., March 2017, January 2018, March 2018). Pl2 appears in the October 2016, December 2016, March 2017, January 2018, and March 2018 sampling rounds.

Barium — the site's principal contaminant of concern, tied to the former FMC facility's barium sulfate and strontium sulfate processing — was reported at PL2 in multiple rounds but never at a concentration exceeding an MCL. This distinguishes the ongoing PL2 record from the isolated March 2006 result at sample SW03 (2,000 µg/l, versus 16–190 µg/l at the other six 2006 locations), which predates the PL-series monitoring and is not linked to PL2.

Lead exceeded its primary MCL of 15 µg/l in March 2018, but at PL3 (46 µg/l), not at PL2. The March 2017 event report attributes lead detections to PL1 through PL3 and PL5, without specifying that PL2 exceeded the MCL. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were consistently not detected above method detection limits at PL2 across the reviewed events.

## Significance

The recurring manganese exceedance at PL2 (and at the other North Emerald Avenue and drainage-basin points) has not been treated in the record as evidence of stockpile-related contamination reaching surface water: each report's surface water management discussion concludes that stormwater runoff from the stockpiles is retained within the Caltrans right-of-way, and that results are "generally consistent" with prior rounds. No exceedance at PL2 is linked in the record to a required corrective action, regulatory directive, or change in the monitoring program. The January 2026 DTSC letter approving the FY24/25 Storm Water and Surface Water Monitoring Report states that results were "all within reporting limits," consistent with the absence of a sustained barium signal at PL2 despite repeated manganese detections above the secondary MCL.
