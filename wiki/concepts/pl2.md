PL2 is a surface water sampling location established by Geocon Consultants, Inc. as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. It is situated along North Emerald Avenue, between Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2, and plays a role in monitoring potential offsite migration of contaminants in surface water runoff.

### Surface Water Sampling Results

Surface water samples have been collected from PL2 on multiple occasions to assess dissolved metals concentrations.

*   **December 15, 2016:** A surface water sample was collected from a puddle on the east side of North Emerald Avenue, between Stockpiles 1 and 2. Analytical results indicated that manganese concentrations exceeded the secondary Maximum Containment Level (MCL) of 50 micrograms per liter (µg/l). Other analyzed dissolved metals, including antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and zinc, did not exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected above their method detection limits.
*   **March 22, 2018:** A surface water sample was collected from a puddle on the east side of North Emerald Avenue, outside the Caltrans right-of-way. Manganese concentrations ranged from 54 to 370 µg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l. Lead was detected at 46 µg/l, exceeding its primary MCL of 15 µg/l. Other reported metals did not exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected above their laboratory reporting limits.
*   **January 8, 2018:** A surface water sample was collected from a puddle on the east side of North Emerald Avenue, between Stockpiles 1 and 2. The sample was analyzed for dissolved mercury and other dissolved metals.

### Surface Water Management

Surface water management strategies at PL2 involve maintaining the stockpile's vegetative/fiber cover and straw wattles to intercept and redirect surface water flow within the Caltrans right-of-way. Observations during sampling events have not identified any instances of stormwater runoff migrating offsite from this area.
