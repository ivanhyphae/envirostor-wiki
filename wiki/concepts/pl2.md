PL2 is a surface water sampling location situated along North Emerald Avenue, between Stockpiles 1 and 2. This location was established to monitor surface water runoff originating from or influenced by the soil stockpiles.

## Site record
Surface water samples were collected from PL2 on multiple occasions. On December 15, 2016, sample PL2 was collected from a puddle of water on the east side of North Emerald Avenue. Barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium were reported for samples PL1 through PL4. Manganese concentrations in sample PL2 did not exceed the secondary Maximum Containment Level (MCL). The manganese concentrations reported for samples PL3 and PL4 exceeded the secondary Maximum Containment Level (MCL) of 50 micrograms per liter (µg/l). None of the other reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs.

On March 24, 2017, sample PL2 was again collected from a puddle of water on the east side of North Emerald Avenue. None of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs with the exception of manganese in samples PL2 and PL4.

On January 8, 2018, sample PL2 was collected from a puddle of water on the east side of North Emerald Avenue. None of the other reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs.

## Regulatory status
Manganese concentrations in sample PL2 exceeded the secondary MCL on March 24, 2017. None of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs with the exception of manganese in samples PL2 and PL4.
