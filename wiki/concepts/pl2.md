## PL2

PL2 is a surface water sampling location established by geocon-consultants-inc. as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. It is situated along North Emerald Avenue, between Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2.

### Site Record

Surface water samples have been collected from PL2 on multiple occasions.

*   **December 15, 2016:** A surface water sample was collected from a puddle of water on the east side of North Emerald Avenue. This location is between Stockpiles 1 and 2. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. Analytical results indicated that manganese concentrations exceeded the secondary Maximum Containment Level (MCL) of 50 micrograms per liter (µg/l). Other reported dissolved metals, including antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and zinc, did not exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected at concentrations equal to or greater than their method detection limits.
*   **March 22, 2018:** A surface water sample was collected from a puddle of water on the east side of North Emerald Avenue. This location is outside the Caltrans right-of-way. Analytical results for dissolved metals indicated that manganese concentrations ranged from 54 to 370 µg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l. Lead was detected at 46 µg/l, exceeding its primary MCL of 15 µg/l. Other reported metals did not exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs. Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not detected at concentrations equal to or greater than their laboratory reporting limits.
*   **January 8, 2018:** A surface water sample was collected from a puddle of water on the east side of North Emerald Avenue. This location is between Stockpiles 1 and 2. The sample was analyzed for dissolved mercury and other dissolved metals.

### Surface Water Management

Surface water management strategies at PL2 involve maintaining the stockpile's vegetative/fiber cover and straw wattles to intercept and redirect surface water flow within the Caltrans right-of-way. Observations during sampling events have not identified any instances of stormwater runoff migrating offsite from this area.
