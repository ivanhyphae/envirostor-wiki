## Overview

Caltrans (California Department of Transportation) is the lead agency and responsible party for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County. In this case, Caltrans acts as both the site owner/operator and the entity executing cleanup under regulatory oversight. The project addresses contaminated soil stockpiles generated from prior road maintenance and construction activities. Caltrans’ role includes characterization, remediation planning, implementation of remedial actions, and compliance with oversight agency directives.

## Site record

Documented facts from the project record indicate that Caltrans identified multiple stockpile locations along SR 132. Contaminants of concern reported in source documents include arsenic, lead, and barium. Maximum concentrations (e.g., barium 760 mg/kg, noted in Phase 1 characterization) exceeded applicable screening levels. Analytical data from stockpile sampling (e.g., Stockpile Characterization Report, 2019) show spatial variability: stockpiles near Milepost 10.5 exhibited higher lead concentrations (up to 450 mg/kg) than those at Milepost 12.0. Verification sampling after partial removal in 2020 confirmed reductions below DTSC action levels. Caltrans completed Phase 1 removal (2019–2020) of approximately 1,200 cubic yards of impacted soil. Phase 2 (2021–2022) addressed remaining stockpiles.

## Regulatory status

The project operates under oversight by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). Applicable standards include California MCLs for groundwater (e.g., 1,000 µg/L for barium) and DTSC Human Health Screening Levels for soil (e.g., 0.07 mg/kg for arsenic residential). Compliance is subject to conditions in the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) approved in 2020. As of the latest documented review (DTSC Site Cleanup Order, 2023), Caltrans is in compliance with soil removal targets but has yet to complete final groundwater monitoring required by the RAP.

## Open items

- Final groundwater monitoring results for monitoring wells MW‑3 and MW‑5 are pending submission to DTSC (due Q4 2024 per project schedule).
- A contested claim regarding the classification of a stockpile near Stanislaus River is under review by DTSC, per correspondence dated June 2023.
- Post‑removal soil confirmation sampling at Stockpile Area D has not been accepted by DTSC pending re‑analysis of QA/QC duplicates.
