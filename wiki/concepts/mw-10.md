## Overview

MW-10 is a groundwater monitoring well located south of Kansas Avenue and east of SR 99. It was installed in June 2012 as part of additional site characterization activities.

## Site record

In September 2012, the depth to groundwater at MW-10 was measured at 41.19 feet below the top of the casing (TOC). The groundwater flow direction was noted as southeast with an average gradient of 0.0008.

In September 2012, nitrate as nitrogen was reported in MW-10 at a concentration of 22 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. Total dissolved solids (TDS) in MW-10 were reported at 630 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

In June 2014, the depth to groundwater at MW-10 was measured at 37.09 feet below TOC. The groundwater flow direction was noted as south-southeast with an average gradient of 0.0008.

Nitrate as nitrogen was reported in MW-10 in June 2014 at a concentration of 29 mg/l, which exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. Total dissolved solids (TDS) in MW-10 were reported at 680 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

In October 2016, MW-10 was gauged, and a depth to groundwater measurement of 38.77 feet below TOC was recorded. The groundwater flow direction was toward the south at an average gradient of 0.007.

In April 2017, MW-10 was dry or had insufficient water to purge and sample.

In October 2018, MW-10 was dry.

In April 2019, MW-10 was dry.

## Regulatory status

Nitrate as nitrogen in MW-10 exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/l in September 2012 and again in June 2014. Total dissolved solids (TDS) in MW-10 exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l in September 2012 and again in June 2014.
