MW-10 is a groundwater monitoring well installed in May 2012 as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project. It is used to monitor groundwater quality at the site, specifically in relation to potential impacts from the soil stockpiles.

### Site Record

Monitoring well MW-10 was installed on May 29-30, 2012, to a depth of approximately 40 feet. It was constructed with a 2-inch diameter Schedule 40 PVC casing and a 10-foot-long, 0.010-inch-slotted screen set from approximately 29.5 to 39.5 feet. A #2/12 Monterey silica sand filter pack was installed from approximately 27.5 feet to the bottom of the boring, followed by a two-foot-thick bentonite seal and Portland cement grout. The well was completed with a 12-inch diameter, traffic-rated, flush-mounted well box.

Well development was performed on June 18, 2012, by bailing and pumping to remove silt and sediment, with approximately ten well volumes extracted.

Groundwater samples were collected from MW-10 on June 20, 2012. Analytical results indicated:
*   **Barium:** 1,100 µg/l
*   **Lead:** 2.2 µg/l
*   **Strontium:** 1,100 µg/l
*   **Manganese:** 1,100 µg/l (exceeding the secondary MCL for taste and odor)

Other dissolved metals, including antimony, beryllium, cadmium, silver, thallium, and mercury, were not detected above their respective PQLs. Arsenic, chromium, molybdenum, nickel, selenium, vanadium, and zinc were reported in the range of 1.1 - 1.3 µg/l.

General minerals analyses from the June 20, 2012 sample showed:
*   **Nitrate as nitrogen:** 9.2 mg/l
*   **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS):** 710 mg/l (exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l)

Stiff diagrams indicated that groundwater from MW-10 was sodium-dominant.

Depth to groundwater measurements and flow direction:
*   **November 28, 2012:** 38.78 feet below TOC; groundwater flow toward the southeast at a gradient of 0.0008.
*   **September 19, 2013:** 41.19 feet below TOC; groundwater flow toward the southeast at a gradient of 0.0008.
*   **December 11, 2013:** 42.46 feet below TOC; groundwater flow toward the southeast at a gradient of 0.001.
*   **February 25, 2014:** 42.04 feet below TOC; groundwater flow toward the southeast at a gradient of 0.0008.
*   **June 4, 2014:** 42.86 feet below TOC; groundwater flow toward the south-southeast at a gradient of 0.0008.
*   **October 18, 2018:** MW-10 could not be located.
*   **April 8, 2019:** MW-10 was dry.

*   **Nitrate as nitrogen:** Concentrations in MW-10 exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/l in March 2013 (30 mg/l), June 2013 (20 mg/l), September 2013 (22 mg/l), December 2013 (25 mg/l), February 2014 (34 mg/l), and June 2014 (29 mg/l).
