## Overview

MW-10 is a groundwater monitoring well located south of Kansas Avenue and east of SR 99. It was installed in June 2012 as part of additional site characterization activities. {{quote: "In June 2012, Geocon arranged for the installation of monitoring wells MW-9 and MW-10 at locations that are both upgradient and adjacent to the three stockpiles as shown on Figure 2."}}

## Site record

In September 2012, the depth to groundwater at MW-10 was measured at 41.19 feet below the top of the casing (TOC). {{quote: "In September 2012, depth to groundwater at the Site ranged from 32.73 (MW-1) to 41.19 (MW-5) feet below TOC."}} The groundwater flow direction was noted as southeast with an average gradient of 0.0008. {{quote: "Based on the groundwater elevation data, the groundwater flow is toward the southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008, which is consistent with historical flow."}}

In September 2012, nitrate as nitrogen was reported in MW-10 at a concentration of 22 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. {{quote: "The reported nitrate concentrations for MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9 and MW-10 exceed the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l..."}} Total dissolved solids (TDS) in MW-10 were reported at 630 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. {{quote: "...and the reported TDS concentrations for MW-5, MW-6 and MW-10 exceed the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l."}}

In June 2014, the depth to groundwater at MW-10 was measured at 37.09 feet below TOC. {{quote: "In June 2014, depth to groundwater at the Site ranged from 35.09 (MW-1) to 42.86 (MW-5) feet below TOC."}} The groundwater flow direction was noted as south-southeast with an average gradient of 0.0008. {{quote: "Based on the groundwater elevation data, the groundwater flow is toward the south-southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008, which is generally consistent with historical flow."}}

Nitrate as nitrogen was reported in MW-10 in June 2014 at a concentration of 29 mg/l, which exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. {{quote: "Nitrate as nitrogen and TDS were both reported for each of the groundwater samples, with nitrate as nitrogen concentrations ranging from 2.6 (MW-3) to 29 mg/l (MW-5) and TDS concentrations ranging from 320 (MW-3) to 680 mg/l (MW-5). The reported nitrate concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9 and MW-10 equal or exceed the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l..."}} Total dissolved solids (TDS) in MW-10 were reported at 680 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. {{quote: "...and the reported TDS concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 exceed the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l."}}

In October 2016, MW-10 was gauged, and a depth to groundwater measurement of 38.77 feet below TOC was recorded. {{quote: "In October 2016, depth to groundwater measured in wells MW-1, MW-2, MW-5, MW-6, MW-7, MW-9, and MW-10 ranged from 38.77 to 47.10 feet below TOC."}} The groundwater flow direction was toward the south at an average gradient of 0.007. {{quote: "Based on the groundwater elevation data, the groundwater flow was toward the south at an average gradient of 0.007, which is generally consistent with historical flow which has been toward the south to southeast."}}

In April 2017, MW-10 was dry or had insufficient water to purge and sample. {{quote: "Monitoring wells MW-5, MW-6, and MW-8 through MW-10 were dry or had insufficient water to purge and sample."}}

In October 2018, MW-10 was dry. {{quote: "In October 2018, depth to groundwater measured in wells MW-1 through MW-7 and MW-9 ranged from 36.36 to 46.99 feet below TOC. Well MW-8 was dry. Based on the groundwater elevation data, the groundwater flow was toward the south at an average gradient of 0.01, which is generally consistent with historical flow which has been toward the south to southeast."}}

In April 2019, MW-10 was dry. {{quote: "Wells MW-9 and MW-10 were dry."}}

## Regulatory status

Nitrate as nitrogen in MW-10 exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/l in September 2012 {{quote: "The reported nitrate concentrations for MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9 and MW-10 exceed the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l..."}} and again in June 2014 {{quote: "The reported nitrate concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9 and MW-10 equal or exceed the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/l..."}}. Total dissolved solids (TDS) in MW-10 exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l in September 2012 {{quote: "...and the reported TDS concentrations for MW-5, MW-6 and MW-10 exceed the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l."}} and again in June 2014 {{quote: "...and the reported TDS concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 exceed the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/l."}}.
