## MW-10

### Overview
MW-10 is a groundwater monitoring well installed by Geocon Consultants, Inc. in June 2012 as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project. Its purpose is to monitor groundwater quality at the site.

### Site Record
Monitoring well MW-10 was installed on May 29-30, 2012, to a depth of approximately 40 feet. It was constructed with a 2-inch diameter Schedule 40 polyvinyl chloride (PVC) casing and a 10-foot-long, 0.010-inch-slotted screen placed from approximately 29.5 to 39.5 feet. A #2/12 Monterey silica sand filter pack was installed from approximately 27.5 feet to the bottom of the boring. A two-foot-thick bentonite seal was placed above the filter pack, and the annular space was grouted with a Portland cement slurry. The well was completed with a 12-inch diameter, traffic-rated, flush-mounted well box set in concrete.

On June 18, 2012, MW-10 was developed by bailing and pumping to remove silt and sediment. Approximately ten well volumes of groundwater were extracted.

On June 20, 2012, groundwater samples were collected from MW-10. Analytical results for dissolved metals indicated the following concentrations:
*   **Barium:** 1,100 µg/l
*   **Lead:** 2.2 µg/l
*   **Strontium:** 1,100 µg/l

Manganese was reported in MW-10 at a concentration of 1,100 µg/l, which exceeded the secondary MCL for taste and odor. Other dissolved metals, including antimony, beryllium, cadmium, silver, thallium, and mercury, were not reported above their respective PQLs. Arsenic, chromium, molybdenum, nickel, selenium, vanadium, and zinc were reported within the following concentration ranges:
*   **Arsenic:** 1.1 - 1.3 µg/l
*   **Chromium:** 1.1 - 1.3 µg/l
*   **Molybdenum:** 1.1 - 1.3 µg/l
*   **Nickel:** 1.1 - 1.3 µg/l
*   **Selenium:** 1.1 - 1.3 µg/l
*   **Vanadium:** 1.1 - 1.3 µg/l
*   **Zinc:** 1.1 - 1.3 µg/l

General minerals analyses for the June 20, 2012 sample from MW-10 indicated:
*   **Nitrate as nitrogen:** 9.2 mg/l
*   **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS):** 710 mg/l

The TDS concentration of 710 mg/l exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l. Stiff diagrams indicated that groundwater from MW-10 was sodium-dominant.

On November 28, 2012, depth to groundwater in MW-10 was measured at 38.78 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008.

On September 19, 2013, depth to groundwater in MW-10 was 41.19 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008.

On December 11, 2013, depth to groundwater in MW-10 was 42.46 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the southeast at an average gradient of 0.001. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 25 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 630 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

On February 25, 2014, depth to groundwater in MW-10 was 42.04 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 34 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 750 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

On June 4, 2014, depth to groundwater in MW-10 was 42.86 feet below TOC. Groundwater flow was toward the south-southeast at an average gradient of 0.0008. Nitrate as nitrogen was reported at 29 mg/l, exceeding the primary MCL of 10 mg/l. TDS was reported at 680 mg/l, exceeding the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

On April 8, 2019, MW-10 was dry.

### Regulatory Status
*   **Nitrate as nitrogen:** The primary MCL is 10 mg/l. Concentrations in MW-10 exceeded this MCL in September 2013 (22 mg/l), December 2013 (25 mg/l), February 2014 (34 mg/l), and June 2014 (29 mg/l).
*   **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS):** The secondary MCL is 500 mg/l. Concentrations in MW-10 exceeded this MCL in June 2012 (710 mg/l), September 2013 (630 mg/l), December 2013 (630 mg/l), February 2014 (750 mg/l), and June 2014 (680 mg/l).
*   **Manganese:** In MW-10, manganese exceeded the secondary MCL for taste and odor in June 2012.
