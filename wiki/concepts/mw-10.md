## Overview

MW-10 is a groundwater monitoring well installed as part of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County, California. Its primary role is to monitor groundwater quality near the SR 132 stockpile staging areas, specifically for constituents of concern (COCs) associated with historic soil contamination. The well supports ongoing regulatory compliance and remediation verification under DTSC oversight.

## Site record

The well was installed during the “Modesto Stockpiles Well Install” phase, documented in project file S9525-06-25 (dated June 2012). The installation was part of a broader effort to characterize groundwater conditions near the stockpile sites. MW-10 is located downgradient of stockpile areas along SR 132, but precise coordinates are not specified in the available source excerpts.
