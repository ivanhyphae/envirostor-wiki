##Overview

Dokken Engineering is the civil engineering firm that prepared the 100% Design Plans for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project (SR 132, Stanislaus County, California). The firm appears as the preparer of plans indexed in Appendix C of the project case file.

## Site record

According to the Index of Plans sheet (IOP-1) in Appendix C – 100% Design Plans:

- **Project title:** Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles (SR 132, Stanislaus County)
- **Plan dates:** Plans stamped “100%” and submitted on **12/14/2018** at 4:59:05 PM.
- **Firm address:** 110 Blue Ravine Road, Suite 200, Folsom, California 95630.
- **Client/agency:** City of Modesto (address: 1010 10th Street, P.O. Box 642, Modesto, CA 95354).
- **Project identifiers:** Post miles R14.6/R17.6 and 11.2/R14.4; State Routes 99 and 132.
- **Plan set contents:** Includes sheets for basins 2 and 5 (among others), with a total of 424 sheets.

The plan sheet includes a registration block for a California Registered Civil Engineer, but the engineer’s name and registration number are not legible in the scanned copy. The sheet itself is marked “SUBMITTAL NOT” (likely an incomplete or draft notation) and “CONSTRUCTION FOR” (truncated). The plans are labeled as “100%” design, indicating the final engineering design package intended for construction.

## Regulatory status

The design plans were prepared for submittal to the City of Modesto and Caltrans. The source does not describe regulatory review or approval actions by DTSC, Regional Water Board, or other agencies. The project’s compliance status and applicable standards (e.g., MCLs, screening levels) are not documented in this Appendix C excerpt.
