Dokken Engineering served as the registered civil engineer for the Route 132 project in Stanislaus County. Their involvement is documented in the "100% Design Plans for Submittal" dated December 10, 2018. {{quote: "DOKKEN ENGINEERING 110 BLUE RAVINE ROAD, SUITE 200 FOLSOM, CALIFORNIA 95630"}} {{quote: "12-10-18 CONSTRUCTION FOR SUBMITTAL NOT"}}

## Site record

Dokken Engineering's role is confirmed by their inclusion on the "100% Design Plans" {{quote: "100% Design Plans for Submittal"}} dated December 10, 2018. {{quote: "12-10-18 CONSTRUCTION FOR SUBMITTAL NOT"}} These plans detail typical cross-sections for Route 132, outlining roadway elements such as traveled ways, median widths, shoulder widths, drainage ditches, sound walls, and HMA dikes. {{quote: "Image /page/2/Figure/0 description: This image is a technical engineering drawing titled Typical Cross Sections for Route 132, labeled as sheet X-6. It depicts two primary cross-sectional diagrams representing road designs for different station ranges: Sta 1135+21.07 to Sta 1141+71.71 and Sta 1122+69.67 to Sta 1133+71.07. The cross sections show roadway components such as traveled ways (ETW), median widths labeled as 18', various shoulder widths (ranging from 5' to 22'), drainage ditches, sound walls (1142 and 1130), and HMA dikes. Key annotations include the usage of 12 inches of clean soil, percentage slopes (ranging from 2% to 10%), right-of-way (R/W) indicators, and general roadway infrastructure measurements. The top right corner contains project administration information, including the district (10), county (STA), route (99, 132), and plan approval details by a Registered Civil Engineer."}} The plans also specify the use of "12 inches of clean soil" in certain areas. {{quote: "Key annotations include the usage of 12 inches of clean soil"}}

Registered Civil Engineer Nathan J. Donnelly, representing Dokken Engineering, is listed on various plan sheets, including layout plans for Route 132. {{quote: "Image /page/4/Figure/0 description: This image is a technical civil engineering layout plan, labeled L-15, for a highway construction project in Modesto, California, dated October 12, 2018. The plan shows the horizontal layout of Route 132, including road dimensions (24', 5', 18'), curve data for curve 12 (Radius 8000', Delta 03°45'24", Tangent 262.36', Length 524.53'), and various project details such as 'SR132' line stations, right of way lines, sound walls, and topographical features like residential streets (Arrowhead Ct, Comstock Ct, Custer Ct) and basin areas. The plan is scaled at 1"=50' and includes formal stamps from the Department of Transportation and the registered civil engineer, Nathan J. Donnelly."}}
