Dokken Engineering was the registered civil engineer for the Route 132 project in Stanislaus County. Their involvement is documented in the "100% Design Plans for Submittal" dated December 10, 2018.

## Site record

Dokken Engineering's role is confirmed by their inclusion on the "100% Design Plans for Submittal" dated December 10, 2018. These plans detail typical cross-sections for Route 132, outlining roadway elements such as traveled ways, median widths, shoulder widths, drainage ditches, sound walls, and HMA dikes. The plans specify the use of "12 inches of clean soil" in certain areas.

Registered Civil Engineer Nathan J. Donnelly, representing Dokken Engineering, is listed on various plan sheets, including layout plans for Route 132. For example, plan sheet L-15, dated October 12, 2018, shows the horizontal layout of Route 132 and includes Donnelly's stamp.
