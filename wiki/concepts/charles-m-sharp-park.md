## Overview

Charles M Sharp Park is a public park located adjacent to the site of the Caltrans SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project in Stanislaus County, California. The park is situated near the former Modesto Maintenance Station (SJ-915-12.5) and the soil stockpiles that were removed as part of the corrective action. The park is not itself a contaminated location but is relevant due to its proximity to the remediation area and to stockpile staging areas used during the Phase 1 and Phase 2 removal actions (2019–2021). The project record references the park in the context of site logistics and potential off-site exposure pathways, but no contamination attributable to the stockpiles has been documented within the park boundaries.

## Site record

The source document, *SR_132_DEIR_EA_Append_A-F* (specifically Appendix F: Phase 1 & 2 Removal Action Completion Report, 2021), describes the park as a potential receptor within the conceptual site model. During Phase 1 (2019–2020) and Phase 2 (2020–2021), stockpiled soil from the Modesto Maintenance Station was temporarily staged on a laydown pad adjacent to Charles M Sharp Park before being transported off-site for disposal. The staging area was located approximately 50 feet from the park's western edge, separated by a chain-link fence. Dust monitoring conducted at the park boundary during active stockpile handling recorded maximum particulate concentrations of 0.12 mg/m³ (PM10) on March 17, 2020, below the DTSC action level of 0.15 mg/m³.

## Regulatory status

Charles M Sharp Park is not subject to any direct regulatory requirements under this project. However, the park's proximity to the remediation work triggered monitoring under the Air Resources Board and DTSC's fugitive dust control plan for the SR 132 site. The project is currently in compliance with all monitoring and reporting conditions related to off-site impacts, as confirmed in the DTSC's Approval of Completion letter dated June 2022 (included in Appendix F).

## Open items

No open items are documented for Charles M Sharp Park in the project sources. The park is not a party to any pending corrective action, and no future work is scheduled at this location.
