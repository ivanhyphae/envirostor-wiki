## Remedial Design Implementation Plan – Interim Phase 1 (January 30, 2019)

### Overview

The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) – Interim Phase 1, dated January 30, 2019, outlines the prescribed remedy for the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles using the "containment alternative" during the construction of the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project. This plan was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. under an on-call contract with [[caltrans]] for hazardous waste services. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) approved the RDIP, which established the roles and responsibilities for implementing the remediation strategy.

### Site Record

The RDIP – Interim Phase 1 was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. and submitted to the DTSC. The DTSC reviewed a Draft RDIP prepared by Geocon on July 18, 2018, and issued a Conditional Approval Letter on August 20, 2018. A subsequent RDIP, prepared by Geocon on September 14, 2018, incorporated recommended administrative revisions and was conditionally approved by the DTSC on October 12, 2018. The RDIP – Interim Phase 1, dated January 30, 2019, includes applicable excerpts of the 100% design plans. A complete set of the 100% design plans and final project specifications were to be submitted to the DTSC and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] in early 2019.

The RDIP identifies the following project team organizations and representatives responsible for implementation during Interim Phase 1 construction:

*   **Site Owner/Responsible Party:** State of California/Caltrans
*   **Regulatory Agencies:** DTSC and CVRWQCB
*   **SR-132 West Project Proponent:** City of Modesto (City)/Stanislaus Council of Governments
*   **Project Proponent's Civil Engineer:** Dokken Engineering
*   **Project Proponent's Construction Manager:** WSP, USA
*   **Contractor:** Bay Cities Paving and Grading, Inc.

[[caltrans]] is responsible for verifying that the highway construction work associated with the soil stockpiles is performed in substantial conformance with the RAP and RDIP requirements. Specific responsibilities for [[caltrans]] include:

*   Supervising the proper abandonment of site-related groundwater monitoring wells impacted by planned Interim Phase 1 highway construction.
*   Observing and photodocumenting barium-containing soil excavation, fill placement, and capping activities.
*   Performing barium-containing soil removal verification soil sampling and analysis.
*   Performing proposed clean soil import source sampling and analysis.
*   Performing worker zone and perimeter air monitoring.
*   Performing periodic stormwater monitoring, including observations and sampling.
*   Reviewing RDIP requirements with the construction manager and highway construction contractor.
*   Interacting with and periodically updating the Caltrans Environmental Manager and DTSC and CVRWQCB Project Managers.

### Regulatory Status

The DTSC approved the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for mitigation of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles in June 2014. Subsequently, the DTSC accepted the RDIP in January 2019. The RDIP – Interim Phase 1, dated January 30, 2019, was approved by the DTSC on April 3, 2019.

### Open Items

An [[errata-to-remedial-design-implementation-plan]] was issued on February 6, 2020, to update the RDIP – Interim Phase 1. This errata addresses changes to previously identified roles and responsibilities, specifically striking Geocon, Inc. as the agent employed by the state, as Geocon was no longer under contract following the acceptance of the RDIP. The errata emphasizes that [[caltrans]] remains the responsible party for demonstrating that the work is performed in accordance with the RDIP, regardless of the consultant performing the work.
