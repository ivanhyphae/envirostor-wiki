This document is a notice from the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) to [[caltrans]] regarding a change in the Project Manager for the State Route 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case. It formally documents a personnel change within the DTSC oversight team for this specific project.

The DTSC notice, dated March 15, 2023, states that Arielle McKleskey is no longer the DTSC Project Manager for the State Route 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles. The notice designates [[dean-wright]] as the new DTSC Project Manager for this site. Wright's contact information, including his email address and phone number, is provided. The notice directs all future correspondence and inquiries regarding this project to Dean Wright.
