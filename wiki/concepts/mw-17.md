## Mw 17

### Overview
Mw 17 is a monitoring well area at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (State Route 132, Stanislaus County, California). It appears in the September 2013 groundwater monitoring report (document S9525‑01‑44C) as the focus of a conceptual site model and a hydraulic conductivity summary. The area includes a nearly removed main soil pile, remaining soil, a storm water pond, and several monitoring wells completed in the Holocene Terrace Deposit and the Merced Formation.

### Site record
A conceptual site model cross‑section (Figure 2 of the report) depicts the Mw 17 area with monitoring wells MW‑1 through MW‑7. Groundwater elevation contours are drawn across the section. The subsurface profile shows a “Main Pile (Nearly Removed)” and “Remaining Soil” above the Holocene Terrace Deposit, underlain by the Merced Formation. The screened intervals of the wells appear to target these units.

Table 1 of the same report provides hydraulic conductivity (K) values from slug tests performed in the Mw 17 area:
