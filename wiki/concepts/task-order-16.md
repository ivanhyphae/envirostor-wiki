Task Order 16 is the Caltrans contract vehicle under which Stantec Consulting Services Inc. prepared and transmitted the March 23, 2021 surface water letter report covering the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site on SR 132 in Stanislaus County. The document reports on surface water conditions associated with the stockpiles and their monitoring wells, and it is the deliverable of record for this task order.

The letter presents surface water results tied to the site's monitoring network, addressing whether constituents present in the stockpiled soil or in groundwater beneath the site have appeared in surface water. It is the vehicle through which Stantec's surface water findings for this reporting period were formally conveyed to Caltrans, distinguishing this round of surface water evaluation from the separate groundwater monitoring reporting conducted for the same site.
