Steven Becker served as the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) Task Order Manager for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles – State Route 132/99 project. His contact information was listed as steven.becker@dtsc.ca.gov, with an office phone of (916) 255-3586 and a fax number of (916) 255-3696. Becker's role was established under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310, Task Order No. 4. The original term of this Task Order was June 7, 2013, through May 31, 2016. An amendment to this Task Order extended the termination date to May 31, 2018. The total amount for the Task Order, as amended, was \$158,183.00.

## Site record

Steven Becker was the DTSC Task Order Manager for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles – State Route 132/99 project. This project was managed under Caltrans/Department of Toxic Substances Control Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310, Task Order No. 4.

## Regulatory status

Steven Becker, as the DTSC Task Order Manager, was involved in the oversight of the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles – State Route 132/99 project. The project falls under the purview of the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
