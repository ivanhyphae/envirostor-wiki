Steven Becker, P.G., served as the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) Task Order Manager for Task Order No. 4, the agreement governing DTSC's oversight of [[caltrans]]'s Modesto Soil Stockpiles work under State Route 132/99 in Stanislaus County. He is named as the DTSC counterpart to Caltrans Task Manager [[richard-stewart]] for the term of the task order, June 1, 2013 through May 31, 2016.

## Role under Task Order 4

Task Order 4 was executed June 1, 2013 under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310 (DTSC #12-T0106), with a total value of $172,000 spread across three fiscal years ($98,312.00 in FY13/14, $39,871.00 in FY14/15, $33,774.00 in FY15/16). As DTSC's Task Order Manager, Becker was the designated point of contact for DTSC's administration of this task order, listed in the project personnel section alongside Richard Stewart for Caltrans and Contract Manager Scott Nelson. His office was located at 8800 Cal Center Drive, Sacramento, CA 95826.

The source material documents Becker's designated role and contact information but does not record specific actions, approvals, or correspondence issued by him within the task order period.
