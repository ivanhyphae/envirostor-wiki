Steven Becker served as the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) Task Order Manager for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles – State Route 132/99 project {{quote: "DTSC Task Order Manager: Steven Becker"}}. His contact information was listed as steven.becker@dtsc.ca.gov, with an office phone of (916) 255-3586 and a fax number of (916) 255-3696 {{quote: "DTSC's Task Order Manager will be Steven Becker, P.G., (916) 255-3586 (office), (916) 255-3696 (fax), steven.becker@dtsc.ca.gov, 8800 Cal Center Drive, Sacramento, CA 95826."}}. Becker's role was established under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310, Task Order No. 4 {{quote: "Consultant Agreement: 43A0310 Caltrans/Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC); DTSC #12-T0106 Task Order Number: Task Order Title: Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles – State Route 132/99"}}. The original term of this Task Order was June 1, 2013, through May 31, 2016 {{quote: "Term of Task Order: June 1, 2013 through May 31, 2016"}}. An amendment to this Task Order extended the termination date to May 31, 2018 {{quote: "This amendment extends the termination date until on May 31, 2018."}}. The total amount for the original Task Order was \$ 172,000.00 {{quote: "Total Task Order Amount: \$ 172,000.00"}}. An amendment increased the total Task Order amount to \$158,183.00 {{quote: "The total amount payable by the Caltrans under this Task Order shall not exceed \$158,183.00."}}.
