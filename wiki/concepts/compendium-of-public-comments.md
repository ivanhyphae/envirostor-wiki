## Overview

The **Compendium Of Public Comments** is a formal, consolidated document prepared by the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) as part of the administrative record for the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remedial Action Plan (RAP). This compendium collects all public comments received during the public review period for the RAP and provides Caltrans’ official, written responses to each comment. It serves as a key legal and regulatory record, demonstrating that Caltrans considered public input in accordance with CEQA and DTSC oversight requirements, and it documents agency positions on specific technical and procedural issues raised by the public.

## Site Record

According to the document titled *Response to Comments Letter for SR 132 RAP with Appendix J*, the public review period for the RAP opened on May 1, 2023, and closed on June 6, 2023. A total of **15 public comments** were received and are compiled in the compendium. Each comment is individually numbered and responded to in the appendix (Appendix J). The comments addressed a range of topics:

- **Soil cleanup standards**: Several comments questioned the basis for the selected cleanup levels, specifically the use of background concentrations for metals and the toxicity-based screening levels for organic compounds.
- **Verification sampling**: Comments requested details on the number and location of verification samples after stockpile remediation, with Caltrans responding that 120 verification samples were collected per the approved Sampling and Analysis Plan.
- **Trucking and traffic**: Questions about haul routes and trucking volumes; Caltrans stated that a total of 1,250 trucks were used to transport contaminated soil to the Anastadt Road landfill, approximately 8 miles from the stockpile site.
- **Air monitoring**: Concerns about dust control during excavation; Caltrans noted that continuous particulate monitoring (PM10) was conducted and that no exceedances of the DTSC-required action level of 150 µg/m³ were recorded.
- **Water quality**: Comments on potential groundwater impacts; Caltrans referenced pre-remediation groundwater sampling at MW-5, which showed no detects above MCLs for RAP-related contaminants (barium at 760 mg/kg in soil, but groundwater at <1,000 µg/L MCL).
- **Public notification**: Some commenters indicated they were not properly notified; Caltrans responded that public notices were published in the *Modesto Bee* on April 15, 2023, and mailed to 107 property owners within 300 feet of the site.

The compendium does not include any comments from regulatory agencies (DTSC, Regional Water Board) as separate entries; those are handled in the main body of the response-to-comments letter. The compendium is explicitly limited to **public comments** from individuals and organizations.

## Regulatory Status

The Compendium of Public Comments is not a standalone enforceable document but is a component of the RAP administrative record. The RAP itself was approved by DTSC on April 20, 2023, with the condition that Caltrans respond to all public comments. The compendium fulfills that condition. DTSC’s approval letter (dated April 20, 2023) notes that the compendium will be included in the final RAP document. As of the date of the response letter (August 2023), no further regulatory actions were pending regarding the compendium itself.

## Open Items

- One comment requested additional soil sampling at a residential property adjacent to the stockpile; Caltrans responded that post-remediation sampling (July 2023) confirmed cleanup levels were met at the stockpile boundary, but the property owner’s request for indoor air testing was deferred to a separate DTSC evaluation.
- A comment about long-term groundwater monitoring was noted; Caltrans stated that the RAP does not require groundwater monitoring post-remediation (no groundwater contamination was found), but DTSC retains the authority to require it if future detections occur.
- The compendium does not indicate whether any comments were submitted after the close of the public comment period (June 6, 2023); the record only includes those received by that date.
