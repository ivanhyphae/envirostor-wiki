The *Addendum to Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* (SAP) was prepared by Geocon Consultants Inc. for [[caltrans]] on February 20, 2013. This addendum outlines proposed surface water sampling activities for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] in Stanislaus County, California, to assess potential off-site migration and chemical impact of surface water runoff from the stockpiles. The sampling protocols are to align with those established in the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. in January 2006, and approved by the [[dtsc]].

The *Addendum to Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* references the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. dated January 25, 2006. The project is identified by Caltrans Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44, and EA No. 10-403500. The stockpiles, designated as Stockpiles 1 through 3, were generated during the construction of State Route (SR) 99 around 1961. The site is located southerly of the intersection of SR 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California.
