# Sampling And Analysis Plan Addendum

The Sampling and Analysis Plan Addendum, dated February 20, 2013 and prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] under Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44 (EA No. 10-403500), supplements the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* that Shaw Environmental, Inc. prepared for [[caltrans]] on January 25, 2006. Rather than establishing a new sampling protocol, the addendum applies the DTSC-approved 2006 protocol to a defined round of surface water sampling at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site, addressed to Richard Stewart, PG, of Caltrans District 6's Hazardous Waste Branch. Its stated purpose is narrow and specific: to determine whether stormwater runoff from the site is migrating beyond the Caltrans right-of-way, and if so, whether that runoff has been affected by chemicals of potential concern present in Stockpiles 1 through 3.

## Scope defined by the addendum

The addendum sets out a scope of services consisting of surface water sampling, laboratory analysis by a California-certified laboratory, and preparation of a summary report on the sampling activities. It does not create new sampling methods or analyte lists independent of the 2006 Shaw Environmental plan; it invokes that plan's DTSC-approved protocols as the governing methodology, applying them to the stockpile site situated south of the SR 99/Kansas Avenue intersection, encompassing Stockpiles 1 through 3.

## Site history recited as basis for sampling

The addendum recites the origin of the stockpiles to justify why surface water at this location is being sampled: the material was excavated by Caltrans around 1961 during SR 99 construction through Modesto, from property purchased from Food Machinery and Chemical Corporation (FMC) that had contained an evaporation pond associated with prior chemical manufacturing (Barium Products Ltd., later Westvaco Chlorine Products Corporation, merged into FMC in 1948). The stockpiles were placed at their present location pending the SR 132 West Freeway/Expressway project. This history is presented in the addendum as the reason chemicals of potential concern are the sampling target, not as an independent finding of the addendum itself.

## What the record does not show

The available material does not report sampling results, dates of sample collection, analyte concentrations, or a disposition (approval, comment, or rejection) from Caltrans or DTSC on this addendum.
