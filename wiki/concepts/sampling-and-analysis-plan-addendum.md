The *Addendum to Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* (SAP) was prepared by Geocon Consultants Inc. for [[caltrans]] on February 20, 2013. It outlines proposed surface water sampling activities for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] located in Stanislaus County, California. The purpose of the sampling is to determine if surface water runoff from the site is migrating beyond Caltrans' right-of-way and if it has been impacted by chemicals of potential concern within the stockpiles. The sampling activities are to be conducted in accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), as established in the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. in January 2006.

## Site record

The *Addendum to Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* references the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan* prepared by Shaw Environmental, Inc. dated January 25, 2006. The project is identified by Caltrans Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44, and EA No. 10-403500. The stockpiles, designated as Stockpiles 1 through 3, were generated during the construction of State Route (SR) 99 around 1961. The site is located southerly of the intersection of SR 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California.
