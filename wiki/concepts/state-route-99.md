State Route 99 is a major transportation corridor in Modesto, California, that runs adjacent to and east of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project area. Specifically, it borders Stockpile 3 BCS material between [[kansas-avenue]] and the Modesto Irrigation District (MID) water canal. Excavation and removal activities for barium-containing soil (BCS) at Stockpile 3 involved material adjacent to State Route 99. Verification soil samples collected from the base of the Stockpile 3 excavation included sample location S3 16-0, which was collected from storm drain backfill materials adjacent to State Route 99.

## Site record

During the remediation of Stockpile 3, BCS material was excavated and transported for placement as embankment fill. Approximately 38,440 cubic yards of BCS material, totaling 3,075 truck-loads, was moved to Stockpile 1. Verification testing for BCS removal at Stockpile 3 was conducted in general accordance with the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP).

Excavation at Stockpile 3 reached approximate elevations between 82.5 and 84 feet above mean sea level (MSL). A total of 24 verification soil samples (S3 1-0 through S3 24-0) were collected from the base of the excavation between February 28 and March 12, 2020. Following the identification of elevated barium concentrations above the verification threshold at sample locations S3 9-0 through S3 11-0, these areas were deepened by approximately one foot and resampled.

Verification soil samples were analyzed for barium and lead using EPA Test Method 6010B. The final excavation soil samples from Stockpile 3 contained barium concentrations ranging from 46 to 970 mg/kg and lead concentrations ranging from 1.6 to 40 mg/kg. These results were below the RDIP verification thresholds for barium (1,000 mg/kg for groundwater protection) and lead (80 mg/kg, residential screening level).

At Stockpile 2, BCS was removed from the west end adjacent to Emerald Avenue and the east end adjacent to State Route 99 to facilitate the construction of bridge overcrossing structures. The removed BCS was placed as embankment fill within designated Stockpile 2 fill containment zones. Verification testing for BCS removal at Stockpile 2 was also performed in general accordance with the January 2019 RDIP.

## Regulatory status

The RDIP established verification testing thresholds for barium at 1,000 mg/kg for groundwater protection and for lead at 80 mg/kg as a residential screening level. Based on the verification soil sample analytical data, the BCS removal at Stockpile 3 met these thresholds.
