## Overview
The Weather Station Foundation Slab/Anchor is a structure associated with the [[sr-132-west-freeway-expressway-project]] in Modesto, California. Excavations for this foundation generated [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) that required management and assessment as part of the Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Workplan Tech Memo.

## Site Record
Excavations for the Weather Station Foundation Slab/Anchor, located at approximately Station 1138+60, were completed in July/August 2021. These excavations were 4.5 feet deep and generated approximately 2 cubic yards of BCS material that was spread on the adjacent ground surface.

The total estimated BCS material generated from both the weather station and overhead sign foundations is 40 cubic yards. This material was to be stockpiled prior to offsite landfill disposal or onsite reuse. The plan involved placing the pre-moistened BCS material on and covering it with minimum 6-millimeter-thick plastic sheeting, surrounded by erosion control measures. The stockpile cover was to be secured with ropes and weights.

Eight discrete soil samples were to be collected from the stockpile on a grid pattern at various depths. These samples would be composited into two 4-part composite samples for analysis. The analysis would include Title 22 metals, with specific methods for barium (EPA Test Method 6010B) and mercury (EPA Test Method 7471A).

Soluble metals testing, including the Waste Extraction Test and Toxicity Characteristic Leaching Procedure, might be required if total metal concentrations were elevated to determine if the BCS material was a California or Federal Resource Conservation and Recovery Act hazardous waste.

The total barium and lead concentrations would be compared to site-specific background levels: Barium - 120 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) and Lead - 3.8 mg/kg. If these concentrations did not exceed background levels, the BCS material could be suitable for onsite reuse with approval from Caltrans and [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]]. If onsite reuse was not approved, the BCS material would be profiled for acceptance at Forward Landfill in Manteca or another accepting landfill.

## Regulatory Status
The management and disposal/reuse of BCS material generated from the Weather Station Foundation Slab/Anchor excavations are subject to Caltrans and [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] approval.
