## California Health and Safety Code Section 25269.5

[[california-health-and-safety-code-section-25269.5]] mandates that the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) provide a cost estimate for its regulatory oversight activities at specific sites. For the State Route 132 West Expressway (also known as the State Route 132/99 Interchange) located in Modesto, California, DTSC provided such an estimate for Fiscal Year (FY) 2025-2026.

## Site Record

The DTSC's estimated oversight costs for FY 2025-2026 for the State Route 132 West Expressway (Site Code: 900259) were detailed in a letter dated December 24, 2025. This letter stated that Section 25269.5 of the California Health and Safety Code requires DTSC to provide a cost estimate for regulatory oversight activities at the site, which is located at the intersection of State Route 132/99 in Modesto, California. The cost estimate is based on DTSC's contracted estimation rates under an interagency agreement. The schedule and cost estimate are subject to updates as necessary to reflect changes to the project or the rates.

## Regulatory Status

As part of the cleanup at the Site, a [[land-use-covenant]] was implemented to restrict certain uses or activities. Pursuant to [[california-code-of-regulations-title-22-section-67391.1(h)]], DTSC has the authority to require Caltrans to pay all costs associated with the administration of the Land Use Covenant (LUC).

The document provided is an estimate of projected costs, not an invoice. DTSC will issue invoices based on actual costs incurred. Caltrans remains liable for all response costs incurred by DTSC pursuant to Health and Safety Code Section 79650 et seq. and the Comprehensive Environmental Response, Compensation, and Liability Act (CERCLA) 42 U.S.C. Section 9601 et seq. DTSC reserves all rights to pursue cost recovery under any and all applicable provisions of state or federal law.
