Stantec Consulting Services Inc. was retained by Caltrans to provide environmental consulting services for the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. Their work has included the investigation and reporting of surface water and groundwater conditions related to soil stockpiles at the site.

## Site Record

Stantec prepared a Groundwater Report dated March 8, 2023, titled "06A2542ct_TO97_GW Rpt_final.20230308.md". This report details groundwater sampling and analysis activities.

Stantec also issued several letters concerning surface water. These include:
*   A letter dated March 23, 2021, titled "06A2542ct_to16_SurfaceWaterLetter _final.20210323.md".
*   A letter dated March 9, 2023, titled "06A2542ct_to97_SurfaceWaterLetter_final.20230309.md".
*   A letter dated March 28, 2023, titled "06A2542ct_to97_SurfaceWaterLetter_final.20230328.md".

These surface water letters address conditions and findings related to the project's environmental monitoring.
