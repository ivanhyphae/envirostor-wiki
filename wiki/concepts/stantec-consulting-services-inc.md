## Stantec Consulting Services Inc

### Overview
Stantec Consulting Services Inc. has served as a consultant on the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. Their work has involved the preparation of reports related to surface water and groundwater at the site.

### Site record
Stantec prepared the "Surface Water Letter Report" dated March 23, 2021, and a subsequent "Surface Water Letter Report" dated March 9, 2023. They also contributed to the "Groundwater Report" dated March 8, 2023, which was identified as "06A2542ct_to97_GW Rpt_final-20230308". Additionally, Stantec was involved with the "Surface Water Letter Report" dated March 28, 2023.
