Stantec Consulting Services Inc is the environmental consulting firm that prepared and signed the groundwater and stormwater monitoring reports for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site on SR 132, submitting results and technical conclusions to the regulatory record on Caltrans's behalf. Its role in this case file is as author of record for the site's groundwater monitoring report and successive surface water (stormwater) sampling letters, including the interpretations of exceedances and the recommendations tied to them.

## Groundwater monitoring report

Stantec authored the groundwater monitoring report dated March 8, 2023 (06A2542ct_TO97_GW Rpt_final-20230308), which presents results for the site's monitoring wells (including bg1, bg2, bg3, and the pl-series wells) and Stantec's evaluation of those results against applicable groundwater standards. The report reflects Stantec's own sampling and laboratory data review rather than a third party's, so its stated conclusions about which wells showed exceedances and which did not are attributable directly to the firm.

## Surface water (stormwater) letters

Stantec authored three surface water letters in the file:

| Date | Document |
|---|---|
| March 23, 2021 | 06A2542ct_to16_SurfaceWaterLetter_final-20210323 |
| March 9, 2023 | 06A2542ct_to97_SurfaceWaterLetter_final-20230309 |
| March 28, 2023 | 06A2542ct_to97_SurfaceWaterLetter_final-20230328 |

These letters report stormwater sampling results and Stantec's findings for each event. The two 2023 letters, issued roughly three weeks apart, both address the same March 2023 stormwater sampling round; the March 28 letter follows the March 9 letter, indicating Stantec issued a revised or supplemental version of its findings for that event.

## Significance

Because Stantec is the sole author of both the groundwater and surface water technical findings in this file, the project's characterization of groundwater and stormwater conditions at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site — including any statements that particular constituents were or were not above thresholds in a given round — rests on Stantec's data collection and interpretation. Any reviewer assessing whether groundwater or surface water at the site is unimpacted is relying on Stantec's reported results as the evidentiary basis for that conclusion.
