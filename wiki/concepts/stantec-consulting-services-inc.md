Stantec Consulting Services Inc. has served as a consultant for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project, preparing key reports on surface water and groundwater conditions.

### Surface Water and Groundwater Reporting

Stantec prepared a "Surface Water Letter Report" dated March 23, 2021, and a subsequent "Surface Water Letter Report" dated March 9, 2023. They also authored a "Surface Water Letter Report" dated March 28, 2023. In addition, Stantec contributed to the "Groundwater Report" dated March 8, 2023, identified by the document code "06A2542ct_to97_GW Rpt_final-20230308".
