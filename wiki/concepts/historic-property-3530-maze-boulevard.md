## Overview

Historic Property 3530 Maze Boulevard is a triangular, 4.84‑acre parcel (APN 046‑740‑004‑000) located at the southeast corner of Maze Boulevard and the Union Pacific Railroad crossing in Stanislaus County, California. The property is included in the Caltrans SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles case file because it is the site of the former Intagliata & Sons Inc. (“Intagliata Top Soil”) soil blending and distribution business. A 37,000‑cubic‑yard stockpile of hydrocarbon‑impacted soil was placed on the property between late 2019 and early 2020, without regulatory authorization, during the construction of the SR 132 West Corridor Project. The property is owned by an entity controlled by the Intagliata family; the stockpile is a key source of the project’s environmental compliance and remedial liability.

## Site Record

The 37,000‑cubic‑yard stockpile was generated from cut-and-fill operations along the new SR 132 alignment between approximately Mile Post 1.7 and 2.2. The soil was transported to 3530 Maze Boulevard by the contractor (Teichert Construction) under verbal arrangement with the property owner. An initial partial characterization of the stockpile was conducted by Teichert’s environmental consultant, Fugro, in November 2019. That sampling (two borings, each to approximately 25 feet) detected petroleum hydrocarbon concentrations up to 2,800 mg/kg total petroleum hydrocarbons as gasoline (TPHg) and 19 mg/kg as diesel (TPHd) in the upper 5 feet of the stockpile. No volatile organic compounds (VOCs) above laboratory reporting limits were reported.

The stockpile was placed on **unlined soil** at the site on **property slopes of 3:1 to 25:1** (steep). Surface runoff from the stockpile, and leachate that collected at the base of the pile, was documented as flowing toward the adjacent **Union Pacific Railroad tracks and a stormwater drain inlet** representative of the City of Modesto’s municipal separate storm sewer system (MS4). No permanent erosion controls or sediment barriers were installed at the time of placement.

A more comprehensive characterization of the stockpile was performed by Caltrans’ environmental consultant, TRC, in **May 2020** (as documented in the *Stockpile Characterization Report*). The TRC sampling grid (seven borings, 15‑ to 22‑foot depths) found:

- **Total petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH)** as diesel (TPHd) up to **1,300 mg/kg** and motor oil (TPHmo) up to **6,700 mg/kg**.
- **VOCs** were detected above environmental screening levels (ESLs) for worker inhalation in several samples, notably **ethylbenzene** up to **2.8 mg/kg** and **xylenes** up to **99 mg/kg**.
- **Threshold exceedances** relative to the project’s risk‑based **RBSLs (risk‑based screening levels)** for worker and construction worker exposure were confirmed for TPHd, TPHmo, ethylbenzene, and xylene. The soil does not meet the project’s generic **“clean fill”** criteria (i.e., below residential ESLs or background).

The stockpile was photographed and described in the project’s *Phase I Environmental Site Assessment* (2019) and *Phase II Environmental Site Assessment* (2020) reports, both appended to the *SR 132 DEIR/EA Appendices A–F*. A **November 2020 supplemental characterization** (by Kleinfelder for the property owner) reported similar results.

No soil or groundwater sampling has been performed **beneath the stockpile** to date, and the property’s **Title 22 Class II landfill liner** requirement was not installed during placement, which is a key regulatory concern.

## Regulatory Status

The property and stockpile are subject to oversight by:

- **DTSC (Department of Toxic Substances Control)** – Lead regulatory agency for hazardous waste determination and remediation. DTSC has jurisdiction because the stockpile contains concentrations of TPH and VOCs exceeding **Title 22 hazardous waste thresholds** (e.g., TPH > 1,500 mg/kg as a characteristic of ignitability? Not explicitly documented; the 6,700 mg/kg TPHmo exceeds the typical 1,500 mg/kg threshold for waste classification). DTSC issued a **Notice of Violation (NOV)** to the property owner in **July 2020** for unpermitted disposal of hazardous waste.
- **RWQCB (Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board)** – Concerned with potential groundwater impacts. The site is located approximately 0.5 miles east of the **Stanislaus River**, with shallow groundwater (estimated at 15–20 feet below ground surface). No groundwater monitoring wells exist on the property; RWQCB has requested groundwater investigation.
- **Caltrans** – As the project owner, Caltrans is the responsible party for the stockpile and has signed a **Corrective Action Agreement** with the property owner (September 2020) requiring removal or remedation of the stockpile.

As of the *DEIR/EA* date (March 2021), the stockpile remained **in place** with no stabilization or removal actions completed. The regulatory status is **“pending corrective action”** under DTSC and RWQCB oversight.

## Open Items

1. **Removal vs. on‑site remediation** – A decision has not been documented whether the 37,000‑cubic‑yard stockpile will be excavated and transported to a **permitted landfill** (Class II or Class III) or treated on‑site. Cost estimates in the DEIR/EA suggest **$6–8 million** for removal.
2. **Investigation of underlying soil and groundwater** – No borings or monitoring wells have been installed beneath the stockpile to evaluate potential migration of TPH and VOCs, as required by RWQCB.
3. **Stormwater compliance** – The stockpile is currently a source of sediment‑laden runoff to the MS4, which may constitute a violation of the **National Pollutant Discharge Elimination System (NPDES) permit** for construction activity (General Permit No. CAS000002). Caltrans has self‑reported the condition.
4. **Hazardous waste classification** – Final determination of whether the stockpile material is **hazardous waste** (characteristic) under Title 22 is pending laboratory confirmation of **ignitability** and **toxicity** (e.g., benzene). If hazardous, transportation to a Class I landfill will be required.
5. **Third‑party liability** – Potential claims from the property owner or contractor regarding costs and responsibilities have not been resolved.
