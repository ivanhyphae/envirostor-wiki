## SR 99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off Ramp

### Overview
The SR 99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off Ramp is a location within the [[modesto-ramp-rehabilitation-project]] where soil samples were collected and analyzed for contaminants. Specifically, this ramp area was investigated for the presence of [[arsenic]], [[barium]], and [[lead]].

### Site Record
Soil samples collected at the SR 99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off Ramp showed concentrations of certain contaminants that exceeded screening levels.

*   **Arsenic:** A soil sample collected at the SR 99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off Ramp on August 30, 2012, had an arsenic concentration of 15.5 mg/kg. {{quote: "SR 99 Kansas Ave NB Off Ramp 8/30/2012 15.5 15.5"}} This concentration exceeds the residential and commercial/industrial screening levels for arsenic. {{quote: "arsenic-exceeds-residential-and-commercial-industrial-screening-levels"}}
*   **Barium:** A soil sample collected at the SR 99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off Ramp on August 30, 2012, had a barium concentration of 480 mg/kg. {{quote: "SR 99 Kansas Ave NB Off Ramp 8/30/2012 480 480"}} This concentration exceeds the residential and commercial/industrial screening levels for barium. {{quote: "barium-exceeds-residential-and-commercial-industrial-screening-levels"}}
*   **Lead:** Soil samples collected at the SR 99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off Ramp on August 30, 2012, showed lead concentrations of 11.1 mg/kg and 12.1 mg/kg. {{quote: "SR 99 Kansas Ave NB Off Ramp 8/30/2012 11.1 12.1"}}

### Regulatory Status
The concentrations of [[arsenic]] and [[barium]] found at the SR 99 Kansas Avenue Northbound Off Ramp exceed the applicable residential and commercial/industrial screening levels. The [[modesto-ramp-rehabilitation-project]] is part of the [[brownfields-and-environmental-restoration-program]], and remediation activities are subject to review and approval by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). A [[dtsc-approval-letter]] would outline specific requirements and decisions regarding the remediation of these exceedances.
