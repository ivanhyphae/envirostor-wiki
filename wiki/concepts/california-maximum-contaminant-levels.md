## California Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs)

### Overview

California Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) are drinking water standards used to assess groundwater quality at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site in [[stanislaus-county]], California. These standards are applied to ensure that groundwater meets regulatory requirements for safe drinking water.

### Site Record

Groundwater monitoring conducted in June and October 2006 by Shaw Environmental, Inc. indicated that concentrations of constituents analyzed did not exceed California MCLs. Subsequent groundwater monitoring by [[geocon-consultants]] in 2012 and 2013, including upgradient wells MW-9 and MW-10, yielded similar results, with contaminant of potential concern (COPC) concentrations remaining below California MCLs.

### Regulatory Status

The California MCLs are the applicable standards for assessing groundwater quality at the site. Project records indicate that groundwater has consistently met these standards during monitoring events conducted in 2006, 2012, and 2013.
