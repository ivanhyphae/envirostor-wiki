## DTSC Regulatory Correspondence

### Overview
This section details correspondence between the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and project stakeholders regarding the SR 132 West Expressway Freeway Soil Stockpiles project. This correspondence addresses findings, remediation progress, and regulatory determinations related to the soil stockpiles.

### Site Record
On March 1, 2012, a follow-up letter was sent by DTSC to the project team. This letter referenced previous correspondence and discussed the status of the soil stockpiles. The letter specifically addressed findings related to [[lead]] in the soil.

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC has issued findings regarding the environmental status of the soil stockpiles. A key determination was the DTSC's finding of "no risk to human health" associated with the stockpiles under specific conditions.

### Open Items
Further correspondence and review by the DTSC may be required to finalize regulatory closure for the soil stockpiles.
