The Operation and Maintenance (O&M) Plan for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles is a formal agreement between the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) that outlines procedures for managing barium-containing soil (BCS) stockpiles. These stockpiles, consolidated and capped with clean fill material, were incorporated as highway embankment fill for the State Route (SR) 132 West Expressway Project. The O&M Plan ensures the long-term protection of public health and the environment by detailing requirements for maintaining the integrity of the clean soil cover and addressing any potential disturbance of the BCS.

## Site Record

The O&M Plan specifically addresses BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2, which were consolidated and capped as highway embankment fill during Interim Phase 1 construction of the SR 132 West Project. The Site is located adjacent to and west of Emerald Avenue, between approximate SR 132 Stations 1125+50 (Stockpile 1) and between Emerald Avenue and SR 99 between approximate SR 132 Stations 1135+35 and 1148+30 (Stockpile 2).

Key requirements and procedures outlined in the O&M Plan include:

*   **Notification of BCS Disturbance:** Caltrans is required to provide a minimum of ten working days advance notification to DTSC before any planned activities that may disturb BCS material or associated clean cover fill. Disturbance of BCS is not permitted without prior written authorization from DTSC.
*   **Maintenance Procedures:** Standard Caltrans general highway maintenance procedures are referenced, but the O&M Plan's requirements take precedence in case of conflict.
*   **Inspections:** The O&M Plan mandates annual inspections of the containment zones. Additionally, inspections are required after events such as fires, substantial rainfall (defined as 1 inch in 24 hours), significant seismic events, or traffic accidents that may damage the cap. Periodic patrols are also to note any unauthorized access, such as homeless encampments, that could impact the cap, particularly on the north vegetated slope.
*   **Reporting:** Reporting requirements are established for both disturbance activities and O&M activities. Communication regarding implementation of the O&M Plan is to occur between Richard Stewart, PG, at Caltrans and Dean Wright, PG, at DTSC.
*   **Cost Recovery:** Caltrans is liable for all DTSC costs incurred in implementing the O&M Agreement, including oversight and response to contamination. DTSC will invoice Caltrans quarterly.

The Final Operation and Maintenance Agreement was sent via electronic mail on June 20, 2023. Comments on the Draft O&M Plan, dated March 2022, were provided by DTSC on August 1, 2022. An Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report dated January 26, 2026, was approved by DTSC on June 11, 2026. This report noted that vegetation was sparse along the road, and while erosion control materials were limiting erosion, continued vegetation along the roadway was encouraged.

## Regulatory Status

The O&M Agreement is entered into by DTSC and Caltrans pursuant to Health and Safety Code section 25355.5(a)(1)(C) and/or section 25187. The installed remedy consists of consolidation and clean fill capping of barium-containing soil (BCS). The O&M Plan is a component of the Remedial Action Completion Report.

## Open Items

The Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report dated January 26, 2026, noted that vegetation was still sparse along the road, and encouraged further vegetation to prevent erosion. DTSC also noted that an Annual Land Use Covenant Inspection Report was due to DTSC no later than January 18, 2027.
