## Revised Technical Memo (Basin 5 Clean Fill Borrow Area)

### Overview
The Revised Technical Memorandum (Revised Memo) dated July 2, 2020, addressed the soil characterization of the Basin 5 clean cap borrow area. This soil was intended for use as clean cover material to cap the State Route 132 stockpiles. The memo was revised to address concerns regarding chromium concentrations, incorporating additional information provided by Geocon Inc. and summarized in a footnote.

### Site Record
The Revised Memo, dated July 2, 2020, was submitted by Geocon Inc. The memo included a footnote addressing chromium concentrations. The composite soil sample results were deemed consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations.

### Regulatory Status
The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board reviewed the Revised Memo. DTSC, through Project Manager Dean Wright, provided acceptance of the Revised Memo via email on July 9, 2020. The Water Board, represented by Steven Meeks, indicated agreement with the revised memo, provided that the chromium values were footnoted with the explanation provided by John Juhrend. The agencies agreed that the soil characterized in the Revised Memo is suitable for use as clean cover material.
