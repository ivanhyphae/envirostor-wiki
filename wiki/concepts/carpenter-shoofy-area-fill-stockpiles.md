## Carpenter Shoofly Area Fill Stockpiles

### Overview
The Carpenter Shoofly Area Fill Stockpiles refer to material excavated and temporarily stored as part of the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. These stockpiles are relevant to the project's soil management and remediation efforts, particularly concerning potential [[barium-containing-soil]]. The [[carpenter-shoofly-area-clean-fill-sampling-plan]] outlines the strategy for sampling and characterizing these stockpiles to determine their suitability for reuse as [[clean-fill]] or for off-site disposal.

### Site Record
The "Traffic Handling Plan Stage 2B" for the "SR 132 Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Source Testing Sampling Plan" depicts the location of these stockpiles in relation to [[north-carpenter-road]] and [[kansas-avenue]]. The plan indicates excavation parameters of a "20' depth" and "5,000 cubic yards" for a specific area, suggesting the origin of some of the stockpiled material. The sampling plan details vertical transect sampling depths at "2, 5, 10, and 15 feet" within these stockpiles.

### Regulatory Status
The characterization of these stockpiles is crucial for determining their regulatory status, specifically whether they meet the criteria for clean-fill suitable as cover or require further management under the oversight of agencies like the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]].

### Open Items
The specific analytical results and subsequent regulatory determinations for the Carpenter Shoofly Area Fill Stockpiles are not detailed in the provided source material.
