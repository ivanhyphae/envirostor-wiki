# Operations and Maintenance Plan

## Overview

The Operations and Maintenance Plan (O&M Plan) is a document prepared for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132 West Project, Modesto, Stanislaus County, California). It governs the long-term management of barium-containing soil ([[barium-containing-soil]]) containment zones created during [[interim-phase-1]] construction. The O&M Plan was prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of Caltrans (Project No. S1908-01-01) and submitted to DTSC as Appendix H of the [[interim-removal-action-completion-report]].

The O&M Plan addresses stockpiles that were consolidated, capped with clean fill material within designated Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones, and utilized as highway embankment fill between State Route 99 and west of Emerald Avenue. DTSC, in cooperation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB), is the lead regulatory agency providing site mitigation oversight under a Caltrans Interagency Agreement.

## Site record

### Document development and submittal timeline

The O&M Plan was submitted under a cover letter dated December 1, 2022, from John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager at Geocon Consultants, Inc., to Dean Wright, PG, Project Manager at DTSC. A Draft O&M Plan dated March 2022 had been previously submitted, and DTSC provided written comments on August 1, 2022. Those comments were addressed in the final O&M Plan submitted December 2022.

DTSC's acceptance letter for the Draft Phase 1 RACR (referenced in the Interim RACR) indicated that the O&M Plan would be submitted for review such that an approved version could be incorporated into the Final Phase 1 Removal Action Completion Report. The Final Phase 1 RACR was anticipated for submittal in July or August of 2022, though the O&M Plan cover letter date indicates final submittal occurred in December 2022.

### Scope and regulatory basis

The O&M Plan implements requirements established in the 2019 [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) and the 2018 Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP). The selected remedial alternative to mitigate exposure to BCS was consolidation and containment — incorporating the stockpiles as highway embankment fill and covering with clean fill and structural pavement section during Interim Phase 1 and Ultimate Phase 2 construction.

According to the DTSC acceptance letter, the O&M Plan requires:
- A soil management plan
- An annual inspection of the pavement and other features of the containment remedy
- Groundwater monitoring

DTSC's March 14, 2018 letter approving the RAP established that following approval of the Final Phase 1 RACR, DTSC would enter into an O&M Agreement with Caltrans that would include the O&M Plan and complete a land use covenant for the containment areas.

### Notification requirements

Any disturbance of BCS material within the Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones must be performed in compliance with the O&M Plan. Caltrans is responsible for providing a minimum ten working day advance notification to DTSC of any planned activities that may disturb BCS material and/or associated clean cover fill materials. No BCS disturbance is permitted without prior written authorization from DTSC.

Where O&M Plan requirements conflict with standard Caltrans maintenance procedures (accessed via Caltrans' Maintenance Manual at dot.ca.gov/programs/maintenance/maintenance-manual), the O&M Plan requirements take precedence.

## Regulatory status

DTSC is the lead regulatory agency under a Caltrans Interagency Agreement, with cooperation from the CVRWQCB. The O&M Plan itself represents a required deliverable under the RAP approval framework, and its implementation will be governed by a future O&M Agreement between DTSC and Caltrans, accompanied by a land use covenant for the containment areas.

Certification of the RAP will not occur until after Ultimate Phase 2 build-out is completed (anticipated to begin in 8 to 10 years from the RACR acceptance date). Ultimate Phase 2 will include adding additional lanes and completing final build-out of the SR 132 project, and will require a supplemental RDIP for DTSC review and approval, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement if necessary. Ultimate Phase 2 will be conducted under a separate agreement between DTSC and Caltrans.

## Open items

- **Final Phase 1 RACR submittal:** The O&M Plan was to be incorporated into the Final Phase 1 RACR after DTSC approval. The anticipated submittal window (July–August 2022) passed, with the O&M Plan cover letter dated December 1, 2022 indicating finalization occurred later than originally projected.
- **O&M Agreement and land use covenant:** DTSC's March 14, 2018 RAP approval letter requires these instruments to be executed following Final Phase 1 RACR approval. The sources do not confirm whether these have been completed.
- **Ultimate Phase 2 O&M Plan revisions:** DTSC has indicated that revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement may be necessary as part of Ultimate Phase 2, with work anticipated to begin in approximately 8 to 10 years from the RACR acceptance date. This work will proceed under a separate agreement between DTSC and Caltrans.
- **As-Built Plan and final survey data:** Identified in the DTSC acceptance letter as remaining components to be completed and included in the Final Phase 1 RACR alongside the O&M Plan.
