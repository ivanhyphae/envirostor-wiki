# Operations and Maintenance Plan

## Overview

The Operations and Maintenance Plan (O&M Plan) is a key document for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]], specifically addressing the long-term management of [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) within containment zones established during [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] construction. Prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for [[caltrans]], the O&M Plan outlines procedures for maintaining the integrity of these containment areas, which were created by consolidating BCS, capping them with clean fill, and incorporating them into highway embankment fill. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC), in cooperation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB), oversees site mitigation under an interagency agreement.

## Site record

The O&M Plan was submitted to DTSC on December 1, 2022, by John E. Juhrend, PE, CEG, Project Manager at Geocon Consultants, Inc. {{quote: "Project No. S1908-01-01 December 1, 2022"}} This submittal addressed comments provided by DTSC on August 1, 2022, regarding a Draft O&M Plan dated March 2022. {{quote: "Comments provided by the DTSC in writing dated August 1, 2022 on the Draft O&M Plan dated March 2022 have been addressed in this O&M Plan."}}

The O&M Plan implements requirements from the 2019 [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) and the 2018 Final Remedial Action Plan (RAP). {{quote: "This O&M Plan has been prepared for barium-containing soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 through 3 that were consolidated, capped with clean fill material, and utilized as highway embankment fill during Interim Phase 1 construction of the SR 132 West Project in accordance with the 2019 *Remedial Design Implementation Plan* (RDIP) and Caltrans-approved project design plans and specifications."}} The remedial strategy involved consolidating BCS, capping it with clean fill, and using it as highway embankment fill during Interim Phase 1 and planned Ultimate Phase 2 construction. {{quote: "The selected remedial alternative to mitigate exposure to BCS was consolidation and containment by incorporating the stockpiles as highway embankment fill and covering with clean fill and structural pavement section during Interim Phase 1 and Ultimate Phase 2 construction of the SR 132 West Project."}}

Key procedures within the O&M Plan include:
*   **Soil Management:** Procedures for disturbing BCS material within Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones are detailed. {{quote: "This section describes procedures to be implemented should BCS material within the Stockpile 1 and 2 Containment Zones (Restricted Areas) be disturbed."}}
*   **Notification:** Caltrans is required to provide a minimum of ten working days' advance notification to DTSC before any planned activities that may disturb BCS material or associated clean cover fill. {{quote: "Caltrans is responsible for minimum ten working day advance notification to DTSC of any planned activities that may disturb BCS material and/or associated clean cover fill materials."}} No BCS disturbance is permitted without prior written authorization from DTSC. {{quote: "No BCS disturbance is permitted without prior written authorization from DTSC."}}
*   **Precedence of Requirements:** In cases where O&M Plan requirements conflict with standard Caltrans maintenance procedures, the O&M Plan requirements take precedence. {{quote: "Where requirements presented herein conflict with standard Caltrans maintenance procedures, the requirements presented within this O&M Plan shall take precedence."}}
*   **Reporting Contacts:** Designated contacts for Caltrans and DTSC are specified for notification and documentation of O&M activities. For Caltrans, this is Richard Stewart, PG, Engineering Geologist, Caltrans Central Region Environmental Division (559.383.5568; richard.c.stewart@dot.ca.gov). For DTSC, this is Dean Wright, PG, Project Manager (916.255.3591; Dean.Wright@dtsc.ca.gov). {{quote: "Notification and documentation regarding implementation of this O&M Plan shall be between the following Caltrans and DTSC contacts: Richard Stewart, PG, Engineering Geologist... Dean Wright, PG, Project Manager..."}} An addendum to the O&M Plan will be prepared if contact information changes. {{quote: "An addendum to this O&M Plan will be prepared in the event of a change in contact for Caltrans and/or DTSC."}}
*   **Inspections:** The O&M Plan requires an annual inspection of the pavement and other features of the containment remedy. {{quote: "The O&M Plan requires: ... An annual inspection of the pavement and other features of the containment remedy"}} Inspections are also recommended after events such as fires, substantial rainfall (1 inch in 24 hours), significant seismic events, or traffic accidents that could damage the cap. {{quote: "DTSC recommends this section be revised to indicate that in addition to the annual inspections, inspections will also occur after events such as fires, substantial rainfall (1 inch in 24 hours), significant seismic events, traffic accidents that may cause damage to the cap, etc."}} Periodic patrols should also note any unauthorized access, such as homeless encampments, that could impact the cap. {{quote: "The periodic patrols referenced in this section should also note if unauthorized access such has a homeless encampment has resulted in any impact to the cap."}}
*   **Groundwater Monitoring:** The O&M Plan also includes requirements for groundwater monitoring. {{quote: "The O&M Plan requires: ... Groundwater monitoring"}}

The O&M Plan references two figures: Figure 1 (Project Location Map) and Figure 2 (Preconstruction Site Plan). {{quote: "The O&M Plan references two site figures: - Figure 1: Project Location Map - Figure 2: Preconstruction Site Plan"}}

## Regulatory status

DTSC is the lead regulatory agency overseeing site mitigation, in cooperation with the CVRWQCB, under a Caltrans Interagency Agreement. {{quote: "The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in cooperation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (CVRWQCB), is the lead regulatory agency providing site mitigation oversight under a Caltrans Interagency Agreement."}} The O&M Plan is a required deliverable under the RAP approval framework. Its implementation will be governed by a future O&M Agreement between DTSC and Caltrans, along with a land use covenant for the containment areas. {{quote: "The O&M Plan itself represents a required deliverable under the RAP approval framework, and its implementation will be governed by a future O&M Agreement between DTSC and Caltrans, accompanied by a land use covenant for the containment areas."}}

Certification of the RAP will not occur until after the completion of Ultimate Phase 2 construction. {{quote: "Certification of the RAP will not occur until after Ultimate Phase 2 build-out is completed..."}} Ultimate Phase 2 will involve highway widening and will require a supplemental RDIP, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and potential revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement. {{quote: "...and will require a supplemental RDIP for DTSC review and approval, field oversight, an Ultimate Phase 2 RACR, and revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement if necessary."}}

## Open items

*   **O&M Agreement and Land Use Covenant:** DTSC's March 14, 2018 RAP approval letter stipulated that these instruments would be executed following the approval of the Final Phase 1 RACR. The current sources do not confirm their completion. {{quote: "DTSC's March 14, 2018 letter approving the RAP established that following approval of the Final Phase 1 RACR, DTSC would enter into an O&M Agreement with Caltrans that would include the O&M Plan and complete a land use covenant for the containment areas."}}
*   **Ultimate Phase 2 O&M Plan Revisions:** DTSC has indicated that revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement may be necessary for Ultimate Phase 2 construction, which is anticipated to begin in approximately 8 to 10 years from the RACR acceptance date. {{quote: "DTSC has indicated that revisions to the O&M Plan and O&M Agreement may be necessary as part of Ultimate Phase 2, with work anticipated to begin in approximately 8 to 10 years from the RACR acceptance date."}}
*   **As-Built Plan and Final Survey Data:** These components were identified in the DTSC acceptance letter as remaining items to be completed and included in the Final Phase 1 RACR alongside the O&M Plan. {{quote: "As-Built Plan and final survey data: Identified in the DTSC acceptance letter as remaining components to be completed and included in the Final Phase 1 RACR alongside the O&M Plan."}}
