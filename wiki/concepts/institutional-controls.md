## Overview
Institutional controls are administrative and legal measures used to restrict access to the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] and limit potential exposure to COPCs in the soil. These controls are part of the General Response Actions considered for the site. While they do not reduce the toxicity, mobility, or volume of contaminants, they can be implemented in conjunction with other remedial actions to achieve RAOs.

## Site Record
The stockpiles are currently managed under institutional controls, which include:
*   A maintained perimeter chain-link fence. {{quote: "The Site is enclosed within a maintained perimeter chain-link fence and has access restriction signage posted in visibly conspicuous locations."}}
*   Access restriction signage. {{quote: "The Site is enclosed within a maintained perimeter chain-link fence and has access restriction signage posted in visibly conspicuous locations."}}
*   Vegetative cover that is mowed twice yearly to reduce fire danger. {{quote: "The vegetative cover is mowed twice yearly to reduce fire danger."}}
*   Groundwater monitoring and reporting performed quarterly. {{quote: "Groundwater monitoring and reporting is performed on a quarterly basis..."}}
*   Stormwater monitoring performed on a storm-specific basis. {{quote: "...and stormwater monitoring is performed on a storm-specific basis."}}

These ongoing management activities and site conditions constitute institutional controls. {{quote: "Therefore, 'no action' would mean discontinuing the maintenance of the fence, signage, and the stockpile vegetative cover and no monitoring of groundwater, stormwater, or site conditions."}}

Alternative 2, Institutional Controls, involves:
*   Limiting access to only [[caltrans]]-authorized personnel. {{quote: "limiting access to only Caltrans-authorized personnel..."}}
*   Inspecting and maintaining the chain-link fence. {{quote: "inspecting and maintaining the chain-link fence..."}}
*   Prohibiting any activities involving excavation/grading, off-site removal of soil, or placement of other soil on the Site. {{quote: "...prohibiting any activities involving excavation/grading, off-site removal of soil, or placement of other soil on the Site..."}}
*   Maintaining the current vegetative cover. {{quote: "...and maintaining the current vegetative cover."}}
*   Continuing to maintain the groundwater monitoring system. {{quote: "They also stated that Caltrans should continue to maintain the groundwater monitoring system at the Site."}}
*   Monitoring and maintaining the site perimeter fence to restrict access. {{quote: "Under this alternative, the site perimeter fence would be monitored and maintained to restrict access..."}}
*   Continuing to maintain the vegetative cover to minimize erosion and potential offsite transport via wind or stormwater. {{quote: "...and the vegetative cover would continue to minimize erosion and potential offsite transport via wind or stormwater."}}
*   Potential use of informational technologies such as public notification via site signage, published notices, and public meetings. {{quote: "Informational technologies such as public notification via site signage, published notices, and public meetings, if warranted, could help to keep the public informed of the site conditions and status."}}
*   Potential use of governmental and administrative controls such as a deed restriction and land use covenant. {{quote: "Governmental and administrative controls such as a deed restriction and land use covenant would prevent the site from being developed for uses that may not be suitable under the current site conditions such as residential or other 'sensitive' land uses."}}
*   Continuing to monitor groundwater quality. {{quote: "...the CVRWQCB has indicated that the stockpiles would need to be maintained in order to protect groundwater quality if the SR-132 Project were not constructed."}}

The estimated annual cost for ongoing maintenance and monitoring (fence maintenance, mowing, quarterly groundwater monitoring, and weather-dependent stormwater monitoring) is approximately \$50,000. {{quote: "The costs associated with ongoing maintenance and monitoring, which includes as-necessary fence maintenance, annual mowing of the vegetative cover to reduce fire danger, and quarterly groundwater monitoring and weather-dependent stormwater monitoring is on the order of \$50,000 per year (Table 3)."}} This cost is considered low to moderate. {{quote: "This cost is considered to be low to moderate and is the second least costly of the four alternatives."}}

The planned completion of ultimate build-out is in 2028. {{quote: "1 = assumed to be necessary from present until planned completion of ultimate build-out in 2028."}} Public information dissemination activities could include public meetings, fact sheets, and public notices. {{quote: "2 = could include public meetings, fact sheets, public notices, and other forms of information dissemination to the public."}} Some monitoring activities are assumed to be discontinued after the interim progress phase is completed in 2018. {{quote: "3 = assumed that will be discontinued after interim progress phase is completed in 2018."}}

## Regulatory Status
The [[dtsc]] indicated in a memo dated December 17, 2009, that the stockpiles in their current condition do not pose an unacceptable risk to human health for [[caltrans]] workers, trespassers, or offsite residents, based on continued management. {{quote: "In their memo of December 17, 2009, the DTSC indicated that the stockpiles in their current condition do not pose an unacceptable risk to human health for: Caltrans workers, trespassers, or offsite residents adjacent to the stockpiles based on continued management of the stockpiles."}}

This alternative complies with State and/or Federal regulations under the site's current inactive (but maintained and monitored) use as long as the site remains fenced, its vegetative cover maintained, and groundwater quality monitoring continues. {{quote: "This alternative complies with State and/or Federal regulations under the Site's current inactive (but maintained and monitored) use as long as the Site remains fenced, its vegetative cover maintained, and groundwater quality monitoring continues."}}

The [[dtsc]] has accepted this alternative for the short-term, with the understanding that [[caltrans]] is proceeding with the planned construction of the SR-132 project, which will encapsulate the stockpiles (Alternative 4). {{quote: "This alternative currently has acceptance from the DTSC and CVRWQCB for the short-term with the understanding that Caltrans is moving forward with planned construction of the SR-132 project, which will encapsulate the stockpiles (Alternative 4)."}} The [[rwqcb]] has indicated that the stockpiles would need to be maintained to protect groundwater quality if the SR-132 Project were not constructed. {{quote: "...the CVRWQCB has indicated that the stockpiles would need to be maintained in order to protect groundwater quality if the SR-132 Project were not constructed."}}

## Open Items
A more proactive remedial action is likely preferred by the community due to the perception of some degree of health risk or threat to the environment. {{quote: "Due to the perception by the public of some degree of health risk or threat to the environment, a more proactive remedial action is likely preferred by the community."}}
