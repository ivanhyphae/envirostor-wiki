Paul Ruffin, P.E., served as the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) Contract Manager for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. In this capacity, he was responsible for executing interagency agreements and task orders between Caltrans and DTSC. His signature on these documents certified that the described work was within the scope of the agreement and necessary for project completion.

## Site record

He also signed Task Order No. 4/ DTSC #12-T0106 on June 5, 2012, under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310. The signature block for this document also lists his contact information as:

In both instances, he certified that the work described in the task orders was included in the required services.

## Regulatory status

Paul Ruffin, as DTSC Contract Manager, was a signatory to interagency agreements that governed the remediation activities for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. These agreements, such as No. 43A0260 and No. 43A0310, facilitated the oversight and execution of the project by DTSC.
