Paul Ruffin, P.E., served as the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) Contract Manager for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. In this role, he was responsible for executing interagency agreements and task orders between Caltrans and DTSC. His signature on these documents certified that the described work was within the scope of the agreement and necessary for project completion.

## Site record

Paul Ruffin signed Task Order No. 6/ DTSC #08-T3616 on June 20, 2012, under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0260. {{quote: "Paul Ruffin, P.E. DTSC Contract Manager Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program Dept. of Toxic Substances Control 8800 Cal Center Drive Sacramento, CA 95826 (916) 255-6677 pruffin@dtsc.ca.gov Date: 6/20/12"}} He also signed Task Order No. 4/ DTSC #12-T0106 on June 5, 2012, under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310. {{quote: "Paul Ruffin, P.E. DTSC Contract Manager Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program Dept. of Toxic Substances Control . 8800 Cal Center Drive Sacramento, CA 95826 (916) 255-6677 pruffin@dtsc.ca.gov Date: 6 7 13"}} In both instances, he certified that the work described in the task orders was included in the required services. {{quote: "I certify that I have read the "Description of Services" for this Agreement and in my expert opinion: The work described in this Task Order is included in the required services."}}

## Regulatory status

Paul Ruffin, as DTSC Contract Manager, was a signatory to interagency agreements that governed the remediation activities for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project. These agreements, such as No. 43A0260 and No. 43A0310, facilitated the oversight and execution of the project by DTSC.
