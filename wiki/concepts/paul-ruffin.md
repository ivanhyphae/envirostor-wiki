Paul Ruffin, P.E., served as DTSC Contract Manager, Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program, and executed Task Order 6 (DTSC #08-T3616, under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0260) and Task Order 4 (DTSC #12-T0106, under Interagency Agreement No. 43A0310) on behalf of the Department of Toxic Substances Control for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles-site]] project. His signature, alongside Caltrans' [[g-scott-mcgowen]], formalized DTSC's certification that the work described in each task order fell within the required scope of services under the respective interagency agreement.

Ruffin signed Task Order 6 on 6/20/2012, with Caltrans signatories [[richard-stewart]] and Scott Nelson signing 6/21/12. He signed Task Order 4 on 6/5/2012, with Stewart and Nelson signing on the Caltrans side on 6.6.13 and 6 7 13 respectively. His listed contact information places him at DTSC's Brownfields and Environmental Restoration Program, 8800 Cal Center Drive, Sacramento, CA 95826, telephone (916) 255-6677, email pruffin@dtsc.ca.gov.

## Role in the agreement structure

As DTSC Contract Manager, Ruffin's signature on both task orders constitutes DTSC's contractual certification that the described work is included in the required services under [[dtsc]]'s agreements with [[caltrans]] — a condition precedent to each task order taking effect alongside the Caltrans certifications from Stewart (Task Manager) and Nelson (Contract Manager). No further activity by Ruffin is documented in the available material beyond these two executed signature pages.
