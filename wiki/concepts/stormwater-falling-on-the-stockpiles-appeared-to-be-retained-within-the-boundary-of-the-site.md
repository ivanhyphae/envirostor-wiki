## Stormwater Falling On The Stockpiles Appeared To Be Retained Within The Boundary Of The Site

### Overview
This section details observations regarding stormwater management at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, specifically focusing on the apparent containment of precipitation within the site boundaries. This observation is relevant to understanding potential off-site migration of site constituents.

### Site Record
On March 5, 2016, surface water sampling was conducted at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, located south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California. The sampling was performed by [[geocon-consultants]] in accordance with approved protocols. During this sampling event, stormwater falling on the stockpiles appeared to be retained within the boundary of the site. This observation is noted in the context of assessing potential off-site migration.
