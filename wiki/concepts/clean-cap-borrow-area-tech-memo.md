The Clean Cap Borrow Area Tech Memo documents Geocon Consultants' evaluation of soil results from proposed borrow areas intended to supply clean cap material for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation on SR 132, and records DTSC's approval of that evaluation. The memo's finding — that the borrow area soils tested were suitable for use as clean cover — is what allowed the borrow material to move forward as the source of capping soil for the site.

## What the memo covered

Geocon Consultants prepared the tech memo presenting analytical results for soil sampled from the candidate borrow areas being considered as a source of clean cap material for the stockpile remediation. The memo's purpose was to confirm that the borrow soils were free of contamination at levels that would make them unsuitable for use as cover material at the site.

## DTSC approval

DTSC reviewed the borrow area results tech memo and approved it, documented in the correspondence titled "Borrow Area Results Tech Memo Approval." The approval is the regulatory action that cleared the tested borrow area soil for use as clean cap material on the project.

## Significance

The approval established that the borrow area soil met the standard needed to serve as clean cover, which is the basis on which that material could be relied upon in the site's capping approach. The available material does not describe conditions attached to the approval or subsequent steps taken to place or verify the cap material.
