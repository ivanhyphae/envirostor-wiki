## Overview

Maze Boulevard is a major east–west arterial roadway in Stanislaus County, California, located south of the City of Modesto. Within the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file, Maze Boulevard serves as both a geographic reference boundary and a potential exposure pathway for contaminated material associated with the State Route 132 (SR 132) corridor. The remediation project involves stockpiled soil along SR 132 that has been sampled for contaminants linked to historical agricultural and industrial activities in the area.

## Site record

According to the SR 132 Draft Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (SR_132_DEIR_EA), Maze Boulevard intersects SR 132 approximately 3.5 miles west of the City of Modesto limits. Soil stockpiles identified in the project record are located adjacent to SR 132 between Maze Boulevard and the Tuolumne River. Sampling conducted during Phase 1 (2019–2020) indicated that three stockpiles near the Maze Boulevard intersection contain elevated concentrations of arsenic (up to 12 mg/kg) and lead (up to 180 mg/kg), exceeding Caltrans background threshold values of 8.4 mg/kg for arsenic and 150 mg/kg for lead in the region. Barium was detected in one stockpile at 760 mg/kg, below the regional background threshold of 1,000 mg/kg used by Caltrans.

Groundwater monitoring well MW-5, installed 50 feet north of Maze Boulevard and 100 feet west of the SR 132 overpass, showed total dissolved solids at 1,800 mg/L during the Phase 1 sampling event (March 2020), but no exceedances of federal Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) for metals or volatile organic compounds. Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) were not detected above detection limits in any surface soil samples taken within 200 feet of Maze Boulevard.

## Regulatory status

The project is subject to oversight by the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) under the Brownfields Program. Applicable standards include:
- **Arsenic**: Caltrans background threshold of 8.4 mg/kg; DTSC residential screening level of 0.11 mg/kg (not applied to this transportation corridor).
- **Lead**: Caltrans background threshold of 150 mg/kg; DTSC industrial/commercial screening level of 800 mg/kg.
- **MCLs**: Federal Safe Drinking Water Act MCLs for groundwater (e.g., arsenic at 10 µg/L, lead at 15 µg/L, barium at 2,000 µg/L).

As of the SR_132_DEIR_EA record (dated June 2021), the project is in compliance with DTSC-approved work plans for stockpile management. No contaminant concentrations in soil or groundwater at Maze Boulevard exceed the applicable industrial/commercial threshold levels or drinking water MCLs, except for arsenic in soil exceeding Caltrans background thresholds, which is addressed under the project’s soil management plan.

## Open items

- **Verification of background thresholds**: Caltrans and DTSC have not finalized the statistical background threshold for arsenic in the Stanislaus County region. A confirmatory sampling event is required per DTSC condition letter 2021-04-15.
- **Stockpile removal timeline**: The SR_132_DEIR_EA notes that stockpiles near Maze Boulevard are scheduled for removal during Phase 2 (2023–2025), but no specific start date has been documented in the case file.
- **Groundwater monitoring**: Well MW-5 is due for quarterly sampling through 2024 to confirm that no contaminant migration has occurred toward Maze Boulevard, per the approved monitoring plan.
