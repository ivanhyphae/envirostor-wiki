This testing was performed to characterize soil within the footing excavation for a mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall along the southerly boundary of [[stockpile-1]]. The purpose was to determine the suitability of the excavated material for placement within the [[stockpile-1]] and [[stockpile-2]] fill containment zones, or as general bridge embankment fill.

### Sampling and Analysis

Characterization testing involved collecting soil samples from trenches STK1-1, STK1-2, and STK1-3, spaced approximately 200 feet apart, within the midpoint of the MSE wall footing excavation. Samples were collected at one-foot intervals to a maximum depth of 5 feet. The soil samples were analyzed by Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL) under chain of custody protocol using EPA Test Method 6010B. This testing was documented in a Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020, prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] for WSP/City of Modesto.

### Analytical Results

*   **Barium:** Detected in all 15 analyzed soil samples. Concentrations ranged from 42 to 420 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). The calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) for barium was 171.9 mg/kg, and the mean was 128.3 mg/kg. These levels were greater than the maximum site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg but less than the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) removal threshold of 1,000 mg/kg for groundwater quality protection.
*   **Lead:** Detected in 14 of 15 analyzed soil samples. Concentrations ranged from 1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg. These levels were within the range of the maximum site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg and less than the RDIP removal threshold of 80 mg/kg for residential land use.

### Regulatory Review and Approval

A Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020, detailing the sampling and laboratory results was submitted to the [[dtsc]] and the Water Board for approval of the use of the excavated MSE wall footing soil.

On April 7, 2020, [[dtsc]] Project Manager Dean Wright, P.G., communicated that both [[dtsc]] and the Water Board had reviewed the Technical Memorandum. [[dtsc]] and the Water Board agreed with the proposed use of the MSE wall footing soil as Stockpile 1 and 2 fill containment zones or as general bridge embankment fill. However, they did not agree with using this material as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material. This decision was based on the 95% UCL for barium exceeding the maximum background of 120 mg/kg and the MSE wall footing falling outside the designated borrow area described in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan. With the acceptance of these conditions and their execution in the field, this e-mail served as [[dtsc]]'s official acceptance of the Memo.
