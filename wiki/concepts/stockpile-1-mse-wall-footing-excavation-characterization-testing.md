## Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Footing Excavation Characterization Testing

### Overview
This testing was performed to characterize soil within the footing excavation for a mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall along the southerly boundary of [[stockpile-1]]. The purpose was to determine the suitability of the excavated material for placement within the [[stockpile-1]] and [[stockpile-2]] fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill, or as capping material. The testing focused on [[barium]] and lead concentrations.

### Site Record
Characterization testing involved collecting soil samples from trenches STK1-1, STK1-2, and STK1-3, spaced approximately 200 feet apart, within the midpoint of the MSE wall footing excavation. Samples were collected at one-foot intervals to a maximum depth of 5 feet.

*   **Barium:** Detected in all 15 analyzed soil samples. Concentrations ranged from 42 to 420 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg). These levels were greater than the maximum site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg but less than the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) removal threshold of 1,000 mg/kg for groundwater quality protection. The calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) for barium was 171.9 mg/kg, and the mean was 128.3 mg/kg.
*   **Lead:** Detected in 14 of 15 analyzed soil samples. Concentrations ranged from 1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg. These levels were within the range of the maximum site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg and less than the RDIP removal threshold of 80 mg/kg for residential land use.

The testing was conducted following the removal of barium-containing soil (BCS) from the Stockpile 1 southerly slope. The excavated BCS was transported for placement as embankment fill within the Stockpile 1 fill containment zone. The soil samples were analyzed by Advanced Technology Laboratories (ATL) under chain of custody protocol using EPA Test Method 6010B with a 24-hour turnaround time. This testing was documented in a Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020, prepared by Geocon Consultants Inc. for WSP/City of Modesto.

### Regulatory Status
*   **Barium:** Detected concentrations exceeded the site-specific background value of 120 mg/kg but remained below the RDIP removal threshold of 1,000 mg/kg. The San Francisco Bay Regional Water Quality Control Board's Environmental Screening Levels (ESLs) for barium are 3,000 mg/kg for construction workers, 15,000 mg/kg for residential land use, and 220,000 mg/kg for commercial land use.
*   **Lead:** Detected concentrations were within the range of the site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg and below the RDIP removal threshold of 80 mg/kg.

Based on these results, the excavated material was deemed suitable for placement within the Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill, or as capping material, pending regulatory agency approval.

### Open Items
The suitability of the excavated material for placement within the fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill, or as capping material was pending regulatory agency approval as of the April 6, 2020, Technical Memorandum. This information was to be included in the Removal Action Completion Report following completion of capping activities for Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2.
