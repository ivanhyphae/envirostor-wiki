## Overview

The **100 Percent Design Plans** represent the final, complete engineering design package for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project, encompassing the remedial construction of three contaminated basins (Basin 2, Basin 5, and Basin 6) along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County, California. These plans were prepared by Dokken Engineering, under contract to the City of Modesto, and submitted as Appendix C to the project record. The plans were stamped "FOR CONSTRUCTION 100% SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION" and dated December 10, 2018, with a received stamp indicating submission on December 14, 2018, at 4:59 PM. The plan set includes a total of 424 sheets covering post miles R14.6/R17.6 and 11.2/R14.4 for State Routes 99 and 132.

The 100% design plans serve as the authoritative construction document for the remedial action, detailing the engineering specifications, earthwork volumes, grading design, drainage infrastructure, and construction sequencing necessary to remediate the three basins. The plans were approved on December 31, 2019, by a Registered Civil Engineer (stamp on sheet IOP-1).
