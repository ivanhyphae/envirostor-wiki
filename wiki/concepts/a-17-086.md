## A 17 086

### Overview
A 17 086 is a designation for a borehole location at the [[north-carpenter-road-overcrossing]] project site, associated with the [[sr-132]] and [[sr-99]] improvements in Stanislaus County, California. This borehole was part of geotechnical investigations conducted by [[geocon-consultants]].

### Site Record
Borehole A 17 086 was drilled as part of the Log of Test Borings for the North Carpenter Road Overcrossing. The investigation was conducted by Geocon Consultants in 2017, with the log dated February 13, 2019. The borehole is depicted in the plan view and cross-sectional profile of the Log of Test Borings document. In the profile, borehole A 17 086 extends from an elevation of approximately 80 feet down to -50 feet. The soil classifications encountered in borehole A 17 086 include:

*   **0 to 1.5 feet:** Silty Sand (SM)
*   **1.5 to 10 feet:** Clayey Sand (SC)
*   **10 to 20 feet:** Silty Sand (SM)
*   **20 to 30 feet:** Clayey Sand (SC)
*   **30 to 40 feet:** Silty Sand (SM)
*   **40 to 50 feet:** Clayey Sand (SC)
*   **50 to 60 feet:** Silty Sand (SM)
*   **60 to 70 feet:** Clayey Sand (SC)
*   **70 to 80 feet:** Silty Sand (SM)

N-values, indicative of soil density and resistance to penetration, were recorded at various depths within A 17 086. For example, an N-value of 15 was recorded at a depth of 10 feet, and an N-value of 25 was recorded at a depth of 30 feet.
