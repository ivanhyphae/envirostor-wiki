## SR 132 West Expressway Freeway Soil Stockpiles

### Overview
The SR 132 West Expressway Freeway Soil Stockpiles refer to soil material generated during the construction of the SR 132 West Expressway project in Stanislaus County, California. These stockpiles were subject to environmental assessment due to potential contamination.

### Site Record
During a follow-up investigation on March 1, 2012, soil samples were collected from stockpiles associated with the SR 132 West Expressway project. Lead was detected in these samples. One sample from stockpile "S-13" contained lead at a concentration of 110 mg/kg {{quote: "S-13 110 mg/kg Lead"}}. Another sample from stockpile "S-14" also contained lead at 110 mg/kg {{quote: "S-14 110 mg/kg Lead"}}.

### Regulatory Status
The California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) reviewed the findings. In a letter dated March 1, 2012, the DTSC indicated that based on the available data, there was no apparent risk to human health or the environment from the soil stockpiles {{quote: "Based on the available data, there is no apparent risk to human health or the environment from the soil stockpiles."}}.
