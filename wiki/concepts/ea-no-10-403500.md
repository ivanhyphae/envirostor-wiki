## Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan for Modesto Soil Stockpiles

This article details the Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] located south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California. The workplan was submitted by [[geocon-consultants]] to the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) under Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44, and EA No. 10-403500. It addresses comments from the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) dated September 12, 2012.

## Site Record

The Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan outlines procedures and methods for the investigation. The workplan was submitted via electronic mail. {{quote: "In accordance with the California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) Contract No. 06A1580 and Task Order No. 44, we are submitting this Workplan for supplemental site investigation to be conducted at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles (Site) located south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California."}} The approximate site location is depicted on an attached Vicinity Map, Figure 1. {{quote: "The approximate site location is depicted on the attached Vicinity Map, Figure 1."}} Comments from the DTSC, in consultation with the RWQCB, dated September 12, 2012, were addressed in this workplan. {{quote: "Comments received from the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), in writing dated September 12, 2012, have been addressed in this Workplan."}}

## Regulatory Status

The workplan was developed in response to comments from the DTSC and the RWQCB. {{quote: "Comments received from the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB), in writing dated September 12, 2012, have been addressed in this Workplan."}}
