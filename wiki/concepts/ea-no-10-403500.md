## EA No. 10-403500

EA No. 10-403500 is an identifier associated with the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, specifically related to a Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan. This workplan was prepared by [[geocon-consultants]] for [[caltrans]] under Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order No. 44. The workplan addresses comments from the california-department-of-toxic-substances-control (DTSC) and the central-valley-regional-water-quality-control-board (RWQCB) dated September 12, 2012.

## Site Record

The Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles, identified by EA No. 10-403500, was submitted via electronic mail. The workplan details the scope of services and the procedures and methods to be employed for the supplemental site investigation. The site is located south of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California.

## Regulatory Status

The Supplemental Site Investigation Workplan was developed in response to written comments received from the DTSC, in consultation with the RWQCB, dated September 12, 2012.
