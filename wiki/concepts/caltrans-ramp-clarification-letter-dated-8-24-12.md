The Caltrans Ramp Clarification Letter dated August 24, 2012, addresses the proposed remediation strategy for the [[carpenter-road-area]] at the SR 132 project site. This letter clarifies Caltrans' position on the excavation and verification requirements for soil stockpiles.

The letter confirms Caltrans' understanding that excavation and verification of soil stockpiles would proceed in accordance with the [[dtsc]]-approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP). Specifically, Caltrans stated that stockpiles would be excavated and verified to meet the cleanup goals outlined in the RAP. This clarification was provided in response to questions regarding the scope of work for soil management.
