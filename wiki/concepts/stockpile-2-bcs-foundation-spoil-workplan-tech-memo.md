## Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Workplan Tech Memo

This document, titled "Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Workplan Tech Memo," was prepared by Geocon Consultants for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). It addresses the management and disposition of spoil material from the BCS Foundation excavations, specifically related to Stockpile 2.

## Site Record

The "Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Workplan Tech Memo" details the characterization of spoil material from Stockpile 2. Analytical results for barium in Stockpile 2 are presented. One sample from Stockpile 2, designated "S2-1," reported a barium concentration of 1,100 mg/kg. This sample was collected on January 25, 2019.

## Regulatory Status

The document references applicable screening levels for barium. The screening level for barium is 1,000 mg/kg. The concentration of barium in sample S2-1 (1,100 mg/kg) exceeded this screening level.

## Open Items

The "Stockpile 2 BCS Foundation Spoil Workplan Tech Memo" outlines the proposed workplan for managing the spoil material from Stockpile 2, including the excavation, sampling, and potential off-site disposal of material exceeding screening levels.
