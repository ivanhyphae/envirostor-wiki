The California Primary Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL) is a regulatory standard for the maximum permissible concentration of a contaminant in drinking water. In the context of the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles]], the MCL for [[thallium]] is a critical threshold used to evaluate potential impacts on stormwater and groundwater.

### Thallium Exceedances in Stormwater

Thallium was detected in stormwater samples collected from the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site at concentrations exceeding the California Primary MCL.
