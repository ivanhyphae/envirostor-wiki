The quantity of 38,440 cubic yards refers to the estimated volume of barium-containing soil (BCS) excavated from former Stockpile 3 and transported for placement as embankment fill within the designated Stockpile 1 fill containment zone. This material was located adjacent and east of State Route 99, between [[kansas-avenue]] and the [[modesto-irrigation-district]] (MID) water canal.

## Site record

A total of 3,075 truck-loads of BCS material, estimated at approximately 38,440 cubic yards, was transported from Stockpile 3 to Stockpile 1. {{quote: "A total of 3,075 truck-loads of BCS material totaling approximately 38,440 cubic yards (preliminary estimate based on 12.5 cubic yards per truck) was transported to Stockpile 1."}} This estimate was based on 12.5 cubic yards per truck. {{quote: "(preliminary estimate based on 12.5 cubic yards per truck)"}}

The excavation and transport of this material were part of the remediation efforts for the State Route 132 Project in Modesto, California. The BCS removal verification testing was performed in general accordance with the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) prepared for the project. {{quote: "This Technical Memorandum presents a summary of barium containing soil (BCS) removal verification testing completed at former Stockpile 3 within the State Route 132 right-of-way (see attached plan sheet). Stockpile 3 BCS material located adjacent and east of State Route 99 between Kansas Avenue and the Modesto Irrigation District (MID) water canal was excavated and transported for placement as embankment fill within the designated Stockpile 1 fill containment zone. A total of 3,075 truck-loads of BCS material totaling approximately 38,440 cubic yards (preliminary estimate based on 12.5 cubic yards per truck) was transported to Stockpile 1. The BCS removal verification testing was performed in general accordance with the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) prepared for the State Route 132 Project."}}
