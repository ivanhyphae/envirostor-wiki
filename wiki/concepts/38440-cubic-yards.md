The quantity of 38,440 cubic yards refers to the estimated volume of barium-containing soil (BCS) excavated from former Stockpile 3 and transported for placement as embankment fill within the designated Stockpile 1 fill containment zone. This material was located adjacent and east of State Route 99, between [[kansas-avenue]] and the [[modesto-irrigation-district]] (MID) water canal.

## Site record

A total of 3,075 truck-loads of BCS material, estimated at approximately 38,440 cubic yards, was transported from Stockpile 3 to Stockpile 1. This estimate was based on a preliminary calculation of 12.5 cubic yards per truck.

The excavation and transport of this material were part of the remediation efforts for the State Route 132 Project in [[california]]. The BCS removal verification testing was performed in general accordance with the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) prepared for the project. The Technical Memorandum summarizing this work was prepared by [[john-e-juhrend]] of [[geocon-consultants]] and addressed to Rick Demi, PE, Construction Manager for WSP/[[city-of-modesto]], on March 13, 2020.
