## Stockpile 2 BCS Removal Verification Testing

### Overview
This section details the verification testing conducted for the removal of barium-containing soil (BCS) from Stockpile 2, located within the State Route 132 right-of-way in Modesto, California. The BCS removal was performed at the west and east ends of Stockpile 2 to support the construction of bridge overcrossing structures. The removed BCS was subsequently placed within designated containment zones within Stockpile 2. The testing was performed in general accordance with the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP).

### Site Record
Verification testing for BCS removal at Stockpile 2 was conducted at the west end adjacent to Emerald Avenue and the east end adjacent to State Route 99. The testing followed the thresholds, excavation elevations, and minimum sample requirements outlined in the January 2019 RDIP.

### Regulatory Status
The verification testing thresholds were established in the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). Specific details regarding exceedances relative to applicable thresholds or compliance status are not detailed in the provided source material.
