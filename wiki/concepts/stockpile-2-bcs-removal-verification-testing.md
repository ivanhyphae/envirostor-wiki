This section details the verification testing conducted for the removal of barium-containing soil (BCS) from Stockpile 2, a component of the State Route 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. This testing was essential to confirm the successful excavation and management of BCS at the west and east ends of Stockpile 2, which was necessary for the construction of bridge overcrossing structures. The removed BCS was subsequently placed within designated containment zones within Stockpile 2. The verification testing was performed in general accordance with the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP).

### Excavation and Verification

Verification testing for BCS removal at Stockpile 2 was conducted at the west end adjacent to Emerald Avenue and the east end adjacent to State Route 99. The testing followed the thresholds, excavation elevations, and minimum sample requirements outlined in the January 2019 RDIP.

### Regulatory Thresholds

The verification testing thresholds were established in the January 2019 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP).
