## No Unacceptable Risk To Human Health Or Groundwater

This section addresses the determination that the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project does not pose an unacceptable risk to human health or groundwater. This conclusion is a key component of the regulatory review process for the site.

### Site Record

The assessment of risk is based on data collected from the site. For instance, barium concentrations have been evaluated. In the context of the [[draft-final-remedial-action-plan]], the project aims to ensure that contaminant levels do not pose an unacceptable risk.

### Regulatory Status

The determination of "no unacceptable risk" is a critical step in the regulatory process, influencing decisions regarding site closure and remediation. This determination is made in relation to applicable standards, such as Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs). The project's compliance with these standards is a prerequisite for demonstrating that human health and groundwater are protected.
