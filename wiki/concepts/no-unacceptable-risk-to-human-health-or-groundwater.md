## No Unacceptable Risk To Human Health Or Groundwater

This section details the determination that the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project does not present an unacceptable risk to human health or groundwater. This conclusion is a critical outcome of the site's remediation and regulatory review process.

### Site Record

The assessment of risk is based on site-specific data. The [[draft-final-remedial-action-plan]] for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, dated March 18, 2018, outlines the strategies to ensure that contaminant levels do not pose an unacceptable risk. The [[draft-final-feasibility-study]], dated June 2014, also contributes to this risk assessment.

### Regulatory Status

The determination of "no unacceptable risk" is a key regulatory milestone. This conclusion is made in relation to applicable standards, such as [[california-mcl]]s. The project's compliance with these standards is essential for demonstrating that human health and groundwater are adequately protected. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) is the agency overseeing this determination. A [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] Fact Sheet from February 17, 2017, indicates that the project aims to achieve this status. Caltrans submitted a Final Cover Letter on May 18, 2015, regarding the Revised Administrative Record for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles, which supports the project's progression towards this goal. An agreement between Caltrans and DTSC further solidifies the framework for achieving this status.
