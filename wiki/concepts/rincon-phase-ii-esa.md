The Rincon Phase II ESA refers to environmental site assessment activities conducted as part of the [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]] project in Modesto, California. These assessments were performed to identify and evaluate recognized environmental conditions (RECs) within the project area. Specifically, the "2018 Rincon Phase II ESA" is noted on an Initial Site Assessment Site Map, indicating its role in characterizing potential environmental impacts associated with the project's Alternative 1.

## Site record

The "Initial Site Assessment Site Map" for the SR 132 West - Freeway/Expressway Project, Alternative 1, dated October 2014, identifies various parcels within the project area and assigns risk classifications (High, Medium, Low) to parcels with RECs. Handwritten annotations on this map, in blue ink, specifically note "2018 Rincon Phase II ESA" and "Proposed Clean Fill Source" with arrows pointing to particular areas. {{quote: "2018 Rincon Phase II ESA"}} {{quote: "Proposed Clean Fill Source"}}

A detailed engineering layout plan, sheet L-13, for the intersection of Route 132 and N. Carpenter Road in Modesto, outlines a "Proposed clean cover fill source testing" plan. This plan specifies testing within the "Route 132 ROW @ and east of Carpenter Road" with a "5' max cut depth". The testing protocol includes "12 borings to 5'", the creation of "3 4-point composites", and analysis for "0-2' pesticides/metals" and "2-5' metals". A red dashed polygon is drawn over the central overpass area surrounding this intersection on this plan. {{quote: "Proposed clean cover fill source testing"}} {{quote: "Route 132 ROW @ and east of Carpenter Road"}} {{quote: "5' max cut depth"}} {{quote: "12 borings to 5'"}} {{quote: "3 4-point composites"}} {{quote: "0-2' pesticides/metals"}} {{quote: "2-5' metals"}}

Another civil engineering construction layout plan, sheet L-15, dated February 1, 2019, shows a section of Route 132 in Modesto. A large central area within a red-dashed boundary is marked with overlapping blue-circled 'X's, indicating areas of interest or activity related to the project, potentially including the areas identified for clean fill source testing. {{quote: "Route 132"}}
