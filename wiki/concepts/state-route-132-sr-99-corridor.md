## State Route 132 SR 99 Corridor

The State Route 132 SR 99 Corridor refers to the segment of highway construction and associated project area within Stanislaus County, California, involving the upgrade and modification of State Route 132 and its intersection with State Route 99. This corridor is the subject of a remediation case file related to soil stockpiles.

## Site record

The project plans for the State Route 132 SR 99 Corridor construction were dated December 10, 2018, and marked "Not for Construction." These plans illustrate construction limits for Route 132 and Route 99, including specific post mile markers such as SR99 PM R14.6 to R17.6 and SR99 124+20 to 1164+30 for SR132. The project identification numbers are 10-403504 and 1000000424.

Typical cross-sections for Route 132 are detailed in the plans. For instance, one cross-section covers station ranges from Sta 1135+21.07 to Sta 1141+71.71 and Sta 1122+69.67 to Sta 1133+71.07, showing traveled ways, median widths of 18 feet, shoulder widths ranging from 5 to 22 feet, drainage ditches, and sound walls. These sections specify the use of 12 inches of clean soil and indicate various percentage slopes from 2% to 10%. Another set of typical cross-sections for Route 132/Eastbound Couplet details roadway components including HMA Dike (Type F), 12-inch clean soil areas, concrete barriers, and retaining/sound wall 1142. These cover SR132 line stations 1145+72.62 to 1149+43.70 and East C line stations 1145+71.95 to 1148+92.31, as well as SR132 line stations 1141+71.71 to 1145+72.62 and East C line stations 1141+71.71 to 1145+71.95.

Layout plans for the corridor, such as drawing L-15 dated October 12, 2018, show horizontal road dimensions (24', 5', 18'), curve data for curve 12 (Radius 8000', Delta 03°45'24", Tangent 262.36', Length 524.53'), right-of-way lines, sound walls, and topographical features.

## Regulatory status

The project plans were approved by a Registered Civil Engineer, with plan approval dates noted as December 31, 2019.
