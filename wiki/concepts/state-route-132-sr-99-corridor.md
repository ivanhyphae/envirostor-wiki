The State Route 132 SR 99 Corridor is a segment of highway construction within Stanislaus County, California, involving upgrades to State Route 132 and its intersection with State Route 99. This corridor is the location of a remediation case file concerning soil stockpiles.

## Project Plans and Design Details

Caltrans project plans for the State Route 132 SR 99 Corridor construction were dated December 10, 2018, and marked "Not for Construction." These plans delineate construction limits for Route 132 and Route 99, including specific post mile markers: SR99 PM R14.6 to R17.6 and SR99 124+20 to 1164+30 for SR132. The project identification numbers are 10-403504 and 1000000424.

Typical cross-sections for Route 132 are detailed within these plans. One set of cross-sections covers station ranges from Sta 1135+21.07 to Sta 1141+71.71 and Sta 1122+69.67 to Sta 1133+71.07. These illustrate traveled ways, median widths of 18 feet, shoulder widths varying from 5 to 22 feet, drainage ditches, and sound walls, specifying the use of 12 inches of clean soil and slopes ranging from 2% to 10%. Another set of typical cross-sections for the Route 132/Eastbound Couplet details roadway components including HMA Dike (Type F), 12-inch clean soil areas, concrete barriers, and retaining/sound wall 1142. These sections span SR132 line stations 1145+72.62 to 1149+43.70 and East C line stations 1145+71.95 to 1148+92.31, as well as SR132 line stations 1141+71.71 to 1145+72.62 and East C line stations 1141+71.71 to 1145+71.95.

Layout plans for the corridor, such as drawing L-15 dated October 12, 2018, depict horizontal road dimensions (24', 5', 18'), curve data for curve 12 (Radius 8000', Delta 03°45'24", Tangent 262.36', Length 524.53'), right-of-way lines, sound walls, and topographical features.

## Regulatory Approval

The project plans received approval from a Registered Civil Engineer, with plan approval dates noted as December 31, 2019.
