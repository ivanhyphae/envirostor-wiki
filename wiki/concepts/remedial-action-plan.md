## Overview

The Remedial Action Plan (RAP) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles is documented in the *Final Removal Action Completion Report for Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles (SR 132), Stanislaus County, California, dated February 23, 2022*. The RAP was conducted as a removal action under DTSC oversight to address soil stockpiles contaminated with arsenic and lead. The plan involved excavation and off‑site disposal of impacted soil above site‑specific action levels. DTSC accepted the report by letter dated March 9, 2022, and concluded that no further remedial action is required.

## Site record

- **Contaminants of concern**: Arsenic and lead.
- **Action levels (residential)**: Arsenic 12 mg/kg; lead 380 mg/kg.
- **Volume removed**: Approximately 1,500 cubic yards of soil.
- **Removal method**: Excavation and off‑site disposal (disposal facility not specified in the acceptance letter).
- **Verification sampling results**:
  - Arsenic: maximum detected concentration 9.0 mg/kg.
  - Lead: maximum detected concentration 340 mg/kg.
- **Timeline**: Removal action completed prior to submission of the February 2022 report. The DTSC acceptance letter (March 9, 2022) confirms the action is finished.
- **Document reference**: *Final Removal Action Completion Report for Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles (SR 132), Stanislaus County, California* (February 23, 2022), accepted by DTSC in a letter dated March 9, 2022 (Project No. 900259).

## Regulatory status

- **Oversight agency**: California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC).
- **Applicable standards**: Site‑specific action levels (12 mg/kg arsenic, 380 mg/kg lead) based on residential California Human Health Screening Levels (CHHSLs) or equivalent—as determined by DTSC during plan approval.
- **Compliance status**: Removal action completed. Verification results all below action levels. DTSC accepted the report and determined **no further action required** at this time.
- **Future liability**: The acceptance letter notes that it does not relieve Caltrans of responsibility for any future discovery of contamination at the site.

## Open items

None documented in the acceptance letter. The action is closed subject to the standard reservation regarding future discovery.
