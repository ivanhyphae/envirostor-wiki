## Clean Cap Material

### Overview

Clean cap material refers to soil excavated from designated borrow areas that meets specific criteria for use as a protective cover over [[barium-containing-soil]] within stockpiles. This material is intended to prevent the migration of contaminants and protect the underlying [[barium-containing-soil]] in Stockpiles 1 and 2. The characterization of potential borrow areas, such as the BCS Stockpiles Clean Cap Borrow Area and the [[basin-5]] Clean Cap Borrow Area, was conducted to ensure the suitability of the excavated soil for this purpose.

### Site Record

Characterization testing was performed at the proposed clean cap borrow area adjacent to Carpenter Road, east of the State Route 132 right-of-way. This area was historically used for agricultural purposes.

*   **BCS Stockpiles Clean Cap Borrow Area:**
    *   Testing was conducted in February 2020, involving 12 direct-push borings (B1 through B12) to a maximum depth of 5 feet.
    *   Soil samples were analyzed for Title 22 metals.
    *   Reported concentration ranges for Title 22 metals included:
        *   Arsenic: 1.0 to 1.5 mg/kg (site-specific background: 1.2 mg/kg)
        *   Barium: 60 to 110 mg/kg (site-specific background: 72.8 mg/kg)
        *   Beryllium: 1.3 to 1.6 mg/kg (site-specific background: 0.2 mg/kg)
        *   Chromium: 7.5 to 11 mg/kg (site-specific background: 7.1 mg/kg)
        *   Cobalt: 3.2 to 4.9 mg/kg (site-specific background: 4.4 mg/kg)
        *   Copper: 5.3 to 9.9 mg/kg (site-specific background: 7.5 mg/kg)
        *   Lead: 1.7 to 7.0 mg/kg (site-specific background: 2.0 mg/kg)
        *   Nickel: 5.6 to 8.2 mg/kg (site-specific background: 5.3 mg/kg)
        *   Silver: 1.6 to 2.5 mg/kg (site-specific background: 0.2 mg/kg)
        *   Vanadium: 16 to 25 mg/kg (site-specific background: 31.3 mg/kg)
        *   Zinc: 21 to 41 mg/kg (site-specific background: 26.3 mg/kg)
    *   Antimony, cadmium, selenium, thallium, mercury, and OCPs were not detected above laboratory reporting limits.
    *   The reported metal concentrations were deemed consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations, indicating suitability for clean cover fill.

*   **Basin 5 Clean Cap Borrow Area:**
    *   Testing was conducted in June 2020, involving 6 exploratory trenches (BASIN5 1 through 6) to a maximum depth of 10 feet.
    *   Soil samples were analyzed for Title 22 metals.
    *   Reported concentration ranges for Title 22 metals included:
        *   Arsenic: <1.0 to 1.8 mg/kg (site-specific background: 1.2 mg/kg)
        *   Barium: 66 to 94 mg/kg (site-specific background: 72.8 mg/kg)
        *   Chromium: 8.5 to 11 mg/kg (site-specific background: 8.6 mg/kg)
        *   Cobalt: 3.3 to 3.8 mg/kg (site-specific background: 4.4 mg/kg)
        *   Copper: 7.5 to 9.3 mg/kg (site-specific background: 7.5 mg/kg)
        *   Lead: 5.8 to 11 mg/kg (site-specific background: 2.0 mg/kg)
        *   Nickel: 6.2 to 7.9 mg/kg (site-specific background: 5.3 mg/kg)
        *   Vanadium: 33 to 37 mg/kg (site-specific background: 31.3 mg/kg)
        *   Zinc: 29 to 36 mg/kg (site-specific background: 26.3 mg/kg)
    *   Antimony, beryllium, cadmium, molybdenum, selenium, silver, thallium, mercury, and OCPs were not detected above laboratory reporting limits.
    *   The reported metal concentrations were deemed consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations, indicating suitability for clean cover fill.

*   **Bent 2 Area Soils:**
    *   A Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020, addressed the characterization of excavated soils from the [[bent-2-area]] for use as clean cap material.
    *   The soil sample results from the [[bent-2-area]] did not meet the clean cap criteria as established in an April 9, 2020 Variance Request approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC).
    *   However, DTSC and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] agreed that this soil could be placed on top of Stockpile 2 at a thickness of 6 inches or less, provided it was covered with clean base material and the asphalt/concrete used for the roadway.
    *   This conditional acceptance was to allow efficient use of the soil while maintaining the integrity of the remedy.
    *   DTSC officially accepted the Technical Memorandum and the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020, via email on November 6, 2020.

### Regulatory Status

The suitability of soil for use as clean cap material is evaluated against criteria established by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC).

*   For the BCS Stockpiles Clean Cap Borrow Area and the [[basin-5]] Clean Cap Borrow Area, the characterization testing indicated that the reported metal concentrations were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations, suggesting suitability for clean cover fill.
*   Soils from the [[bent-2-area]] did not meet the established clean cap criteria as per the April 9, 2020 Variance Request.
*   Despite not meeting the primary criteria, DTSC and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] conditionally accepted the use of [[bent-2-area]] soil as a cap on Stockpile 2, with a maximum thickness of 6 inches, provided it was covered by other materials.

### Open Items

*   Strict adherence to the rainy season surface water sampling effort and erosion control measures outlined in the Revised SWPPP Amendment (October 22, 2020) is required to ensure contaminants remain on site and do not impact the surrounding area, particularly concerning the use of [[bent-2-area]] soils.
