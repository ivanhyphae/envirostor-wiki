PL5 is a surface water sampling location situated within the drainage basin adjacent to Stockpile 3. It is positioned within three feet of a gate valve that controls stormwater release to the SR 99 collection system, which conveys water to the Tuolumne River. This valve is reportedly only opened if the basin nears a capacity that could jeopardize the northbound lanes of SR 99, and Caltrans has stated it has not been opened in the recent past. PL5 is used to monitor surface water conditions in proximity to this potential discharge point.

## Surface Water Sampling at PL5

Surface water samples were collected from PL5 on multiple occasions between October 2016 and March 2018.

*   **October 28, 2016:** A surface water sample was collected from PL5 and analyzed for dissolved metals. The laboratory report indicated that samples PL1 through PL5, BG1, and BG2 were diluted due to a failing internal standard and possible matrix interference. Specific dilutions were noted for nitrate, sulfide, chloride, sulfate, and a suite of dissolved metals including barium, beryllium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, magnesium, manganese, nickel, potassium, sodium, thallium, and vanadium.
*   **December 15, 2016:** The PL5 location was dry, and a surface water sample could not be collected.
*   **March 24, 2017:** A surface water sample was collected from PL5. Analytical results for dissolved metals indicated the presence of antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium. Cadmium and lead were also reported. Manganese was reported for samples PL2 and PL4, and selenium for PL2. Zinc was reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5. None of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs, with the exception of manganese in samples PL2 and PL4. Beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were not detected above their method detection limits. The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL5 were diluted for barium and calcium due to high concentrations of target analytes, and for magnesium, manganese, potassium, and sodium due to high concentrations of non-target analytes. PL4 and PL5 were diluted for lead and thallium due to a failing internal standard.
*   **January 8, 2018:** Insufficient water was present at the PL5 location, preventing sample collection.
*   **March 22, 2018:** Insufficient water was present at the PL5 location, preventing sample collection.

## Surface Water Management

Observations made during sampling events and site reconnaissance have not identified any area or location where stormwater runoff appeared to be migrating offsite. Stormwater falling on the stockpiles appeared to be retained within the Caltrans right-of-way. Caltrans plans to conduct additional surface water sampling during qualifying rain events.
