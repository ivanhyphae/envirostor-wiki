## Overview
PL5 is a surface water sampling location situated within the onsite drainage basin adjacent to and south of Stockpile 3. Its purpose is to monitor storm water near a gate valve that could release water to the SR 99 collection system, which conveys storm water to the Tuolumne River. The gate valve is reportedly only opened if the basin nears a capacity that could jeopardize the northbound lanes of SR 99, and Caltrans has stated it has not been opened in the recent past.

## Site record
Surface water samples were not collected at location PL5 during the December 15, 2016 sampling event because it was dry. {{quote: "We did not sample from location PL5 because it was dry."}} The location of PL5 is described as being within 3 feet of a gate valve within the onsite drainage basin adjacent and south of Stockpile 3. {{quote: "Location of PL5 is within 3 feet of a gate valve within the onsite drainage basin adjacent and south of Stockpile 3 (Photo 5)."}}

During the March 24, 2017 sampling event, surface water samples were collected from PL5. {{quote: "On March 24, 2017, we collected surface water samples from designated locations PL1 through PL5."}} Analytical results for dissolved metals for sample PL5 included antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium. {{quote: "Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium were reported for samples PL1 through PL5."}} Cadmium was also reported for sample PL5. {{quote: "Cadmium was reported for samples PL4 and PL5."}} Lead was reported for sample PL5. {{quote: "Lead was reported for samples PL1 though PL3 and PL5."}} Zinc was reported for sample PL5. {{quote: "Zinc was reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5."}}

The laboratory noted that sample PL5 was diluted for barium and calcium due to high concentrations of the target analytes, and for magnesium, manganese, potassium, and sodium due to high concentrations of non-target analytes. {{quote: "The laboratory notes state samples PL1 through PL5 were diluted for: (1) barium and calcium due to the high concentrations of the target analytes, (2) magnesium, manganese, potassium, and sodium due to high concentration of non-target analyte..."}} Sample PL5 was also diluted for lead and thallium due to a failing internal standard in the original run. {{quote: "The laboratory notes also state that sample PL4 and PL5 were diluted for lead and thallium due to failing internal standard in the original run."}}

During the January 8, 2018 sampling event, a surface water sample could not be collected from PL5 due to insufficient water. {{quote: "We did not sample from location PL5 because there was insufficient water to sample."}} The gate valve at PL5 was not opened during this event. {{quote: "The gate valve was not opened during this event."}}

## Regulatory status
During the March 24, 2017 sampling event, none of the reported dissolved metal concentrations for samples PL1 through PL5 exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs, with the exception of manganese in samples PL2 and PL4. {{quote: "None of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs with the exception of manganese in samples PL2 and PL4."}} Beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits for samples PL1 through PL5. {{quote: "Beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits for samples PL1 through PL5."}}
