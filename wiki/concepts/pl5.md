PL5 is a surface water sampling location situated within the onsite drainage basin adjacent to and south of [[stockpile-1]]. Its purpose is to monitor storm water near a gate valve that could release water to the SR 99 collection system, which conveys storm water to the Tuolumne River. The gate valve is reportedly only opened if the basin nears a capacity that could jeopardize the northbound lanes of SR 99, and Caltrans has stated it has not been opened in the recent past.

## Site record
Surface water samples were not collected at location PL5 during the December 15, 2016 sampling event because it was dry.

During the March 24, 2017 sampling event, surface water samples were collected from PL5. Analytical results for dissolved metals for sample PL5 included antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium. Cadmium was also reported for sample PL5. Lead was reported for sample PL5. Zinc was reported for sample PL5.

The laboratory noted that sample PL5 was diluted for barium and calcium due to high concentrations of the target analytes, and for magnesium, manganese, potassium, and sodium due to high concentrations of non-target analytes. Sample PL5 was also diluted for lead and thallium due to a failing internal standard in the original run.

During the January 8, 2018 sampling event, a surface water sample could not be collected from PL5 due to insufficient water. The gate valve at PL5 was not opened during this event.

During the December 17, 2020 inspection, the storm water basin in the area of PL4 and PL5 has been enlarged and is identified as Basin 6B. The former gate valve that was located at the southern end of the former drainage basin has been removed.

## Regulatory status
During the March 24, 2017 sampling event, none of the reported dissolved metal concentrations for samples PL1 through PL5 exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs, with the exception of manganese in samples PL2 and PL4. Beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits for samples PL1 through PL5.
