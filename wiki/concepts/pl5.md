## Overview

PL5 is a surface water sampling location situated within the drainage basin adjacent to Stockpile 3. Its specific placement is within three feet of a gate valve that controls the release of stormwater to the SR 99 collection system, which conveys water to the Tuolumne River. This valve is reportedly only opened if the basin nears a capacity that could jeopardize the northbound lanes of SR 99, and Caltrans has stated it has not been opened in the recent past. PL5 is used to monitor surface water conditions in proximity to this potential discharge point.

## Site record

Surface water samples were collected from PL5 on multiple occasions between October 2016 and March 2018.

*   **October 28, 2016:** Surface water sample PL5 was collected. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. The laboratory report notes that samples PL1 through PL5, BG1, and BG2 were diluted due to a failing internal standard in the original run and possible matrix interference. Specifically, PL1 through PL5, BG1, and BG2 were diluted for nitrate and sulfide. PL1, PL3 through PL5, BG1, and BG2 were diluted for chloride. PL1 through PL4, BG1, and BG2 were diluted for sulfate. PL1, PL3, PL5, BG1, and BG2 were diluted for barium, beryllium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, magnesium, manganese, nickel, potassium, sodium, thallium, and vanadium.
*   **December 15, 2016:** Surface water sample PL5 could not be collected because the location was dry.
*   **March 24, 2017:** Surface water sample PL5 was collected. Analytical results for dissolved metals indicated the presence of antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium. Arsenic was reported for samples PL1 through PL4. Cadmium was reported for samples PL4 and PL5. Lead was reported for samples PL1 though PL3 and PL5. Manganese was reported for samples PL2 and PL4. Selenium was reported for sample PL2. Zinc was reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5. None of the reported concentrations exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs, with the exception of manganese in samples PL2 and PL4. Beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits for samples PL1 through PL5. The laboratory notes state samples PL1 through PL5 were diluted for barium and calcium due to high concentrations of the target analytes, and for magnesium, manganese, potassium, and sodium due to high concentrations of non-target analytes. PL4 and PL5 were diluted for lead and thallium due to a failing internal standard. PL3 was also diluted for chloride and sulfate due to possible matrix interference.
*   **January 8, 2018:** Surface water sample PL5 could not be collected due to insufficient water at this location.
*   **March 22, 2018:** Surface water sample PL5 could not be collected due to insufficient water at this location.

Observations made during sampling events and site reconnaissance have not identified any area or location where stormwater runoff appeared to be migrating offsite. Stormwater falling on the stockpiles appeared to be retained within the Caltrans right-of-way.

## Regulatory status

Surface water management at the site aims to retain stormwater within the Caltrans right-of-way. Analytical results from previous sampling events, including those at PL5, have generally been consistent with prior findings, with no observed offsite migration of stormwater.

## Open items

Caltrans plans to conduct additional surface water sampling during qualifying rain events in 2018.
