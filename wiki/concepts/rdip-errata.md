## RDIP Errata Acceptance

This section documents the acceptance of an errata sheet and transmittal letter for the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) related to the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] on [[sr-132-west-expressway-phase-1]].

## Site Record

On February 5, 2020, the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) formally accepted the RDIP Errata sheet and its accompanying transmittal letter for the SR 132 Modesto Stockpile project. This acceptance was communicated via email from Dean Wright, Project Manager at DTSC, to Juergen Vespermann of Caltrans and Perry Myers of DTSC. The errata and transmittal letter were provided by Juergen Vespermann on February 5, 2020.

## Regulatory Status

The acceptance of the RDIP Errata sheet by DTSC signifies a regulatory milestone in the remediation process for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles.
