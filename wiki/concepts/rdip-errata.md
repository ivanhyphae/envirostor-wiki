## RDIP Errata Acceptance

The acceptance of an errata sheet and transmittal letter for the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) for the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] is documented in the project record.

## Site Record

On February 5, 2020, the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) accepted the RDIP Errata sheet and its accompanying transmittal letter. This acceptance was communicated via email from Dean Wright, Project Manager at DTSC, to Juergen Vespermann of Caltrans and Perry Myers of DTSC. Juergen Vespermann provided the errata and transmittal letter on February 5, 2020.
