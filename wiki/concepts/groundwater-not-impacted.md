## Overview

The groundwater at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, located along State Route (SR) 132 in Stanislaus County, California, has been determined to be not impacted by the encapsulated soil stockpiles. This conclusion is supported by evaluations conducted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. on behalf of Caltrans, in consultation with the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB). The encapsulation of the soil beneath the SR 132 Expressway further supports this determination.

## Site Record

In a letter dated May 3, 2024, DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley RWQCB, approved the "Updated Comparative Evaluation of Groundwater Data, Caltrans Encapsulated Soil Stockpiles" report, dated February 26, 2024. This report, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc., evaluated the potential impact of barium and lead in the soil stockpiles on groundwater. DTSC and the RWQCB agreed that the data indicates the encapsulated soil is not impacting groundwater and will not impact it in the future. This conclusion is consistent with an earlier 2014 evaluation. As a result of this determination, DTSC and the RWQCB concurred with the recommendation to discontinue groundwater monitoring and decommission the four remaining monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, and MW10) as per the approved Remedial Design and Implementation Plan.

## Regulatory Status

The DTSC, in consultation with the Central Valley RWQCB, has approved the conclusion that groundwater is not impacted by the soil stockpiles. This approval, documented in a letter dated May 3, 2024, allows for the discontinuation of groundwater monitoring and the decommissioning of monitoring wells.

## Open Items

The decommissioning of the four remaining monitoring wells (MW4, MW6, MW9, and MW10) is to be conducted as per the approved Remedial Design and Implementation Plan.
