## Appendix A

Appendix A of the Draft Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (DEIR/EA) for the State Route 132 West Freeway Expressway Project contains the "Geotechnical Investigation Report" for the project. This report details the subsurface conditions encountered during the geotechnical investigation, including soil classifications, groundwater conditions, and recommendations for design and construction. It serves as a foundational document for understanding the physical environment of the project area.

## Site record

Appendix A documents the findings of the geotechnical investigation conducted for the SR 132 West Freeway Expressway Project. The investigation involved drilling borings and collecting soil samples to characterize subsurface materials. The report includes descriptions of soil strata, their engineering properties, and the presence and depth of groundwater. Specific details regarding soil types, their distribution, and engineering characteristics are presented within the report, which is referenced as a component of the DEIR/EA.

## Regulatory status

Appendix A itself does not establish regulatory status but provides technical data that informs regulatory decisions. The information contained within the geotechnical report supports the environmental review process under the [[california-environmental-quality-act]] and the [[national-environmental-policy-act]], as documented in the DEIR/EA.

## Open items

No open items are directly associated with Appendix A itself. It is a technical appendix providing data.
