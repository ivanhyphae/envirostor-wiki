Stockpile 1 was one of three Barium-Containing Soil (BCS) stockpiles generated from soils excavated during the SR 132 West Project construction. These stockpiles were consolidated and contained within highway embankment fills as part of the Interim Phase 1 construction.

### Site Description and History

Stockpile 1 was located approximately 150 feet west of Emerald Avenue, measuring roughly 600 feet long, 160 feet wide, with an estimated volume of 34,000 cubic yards. It was composed primarily of layered, poorly graded sand and silty sand, consistent with the underlying Modesto Formation. During Interim Phase 1 construction, Stockpile 3 material was placed as embankment fill on top of Stockpile 1. Excavated BCS from the southern slopes of Stockpile 1 for Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) wall construction was also placed within the Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone. Aerially Deposited Lead (ADL)-contaminated soil from the northbound SR 99 shoulder was also placed in the eastern end of the Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone.

Characterization testing of soil from MSE wall footing areas along the southern boundary of Stockpile 1, excavated to depths of 3 to 5 feet, revealed barium concentrations exceeding the site-specific background concentration of 120 mg/kg but below the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) removal threshold of 1,000 mg/kg. Lead concentrations were generally within the site-specific background range of 3.8 mg/kg and below the RDIP removal threshold of 80 mg/kg. Due to elevated barium, all excavated MSE wall footing material was placed in the Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones. The Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone is located adjacent to and west of Emerald Avenue between SR 132 Stations 1125+50 and 1133+50.

### Regulatory Status and Remediation

The RDIP established soil excavation verification thresholds of 1,000 mg/kg for barium and 80 mg/kg for lead. A DTSC-approved Variance to the RDIP, dated April 9, 2020, established site-specific upper-bound background concentrations for comparison: Barium at 120 mg/kg and Lead at 3.8 mg/kg. Clean fill source areas approved by DTSC for capping material over Stockpile 1 included the SR 132 ROW construction excavation area east of Carpenter Road and the Basin 5 construction excavation area east of Carpenter Road. The Bent 2 construction excavation stockpiles west of SR 99 were not approved for use as clean capping material.

The Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones are subject to a DTSC-required Land Use Covenant (LUC)/deed restriction. Caltrans is responsible for ongoing groundwater and stormwater monitoring activities related to the BCS stockpiles.

### Operations and Maintenance

Caltrans will perform minimum annual inspections of the Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones, focusing on the integrity of the cap, drainage structures, and security fencing. Supplemental inspections will occur after significant events such as fires, substantial rainfall, or seismic activity. Findings will be documented on an O&M Site Inspection Form and included in an annual report submitted to DTSC. Caltrans will also perform five-year reviews to assess the effectiveness of the containment measures.
