## Overview

Stockpile 1 is one of three Barium-Containing Soil (BCS) stockpiles identified within the State Route 132 (SR 132) right-of-way in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. It was designated for consolidation and containment as part of the Interim Phase 1 construction of the SR 132 West Project. The stockpile material was primarily comprised of layered, poorly graded sand and silty sand. Stockpile 1 was located approximately 150 feet westerly of Emerald Avenue, measuring roughly 600 feet long, 160 feet wide, with an estimated fill volume of 34,000 cubic yards (yd³). {{quote: "Prior to construction of Interim Phase 1 of the SR 132 West Project, the Site consisted of BCS Stockpiles 1 through 3 within Caltrans SR 132 right-of-way (ROW) as depicted on the Preconstruction Site Plan, Figure 2. The BCS Stockpiles are primarily comprised of layered, poorly graded sand and silty sand similar to underlying native alluvial deposits of the Modesto Formation. The average maximum stockpile fill thickness was approximately 20 feet. BCS Stockpile 1, located approximately 150 feet westerly of Emerald Avenue, was roughly 600 feet long in the east-west direction, 160 feet wide, and had an estimated fill volume of 34,000 cubic yards (yd³)."}} {{quote: "BCS Stockpile 1 through 3 site preparation operations performed by BCPG included installation of perimeter fencing where required. The surface of Stockpiles 1 through 3 were mowed using weed whackers and power mowers."}}

## Site record

Stockpile 1 was consolidated and contained within the SR 132 highway embankment fills, referred to as BCS Containment Zones, located between SR 99 and the west end of Stockpile 1. {{quote: "The RDIP provided specific design details regarding the containment of the three BCS Stockpiles within SR 132 highway embankment fills (BCS Containment Zones) located between SR 99 and the west end of Stockpile 1."}} Excavated BCS for placement within these zones was generated from the removal of Stockpile 3, bridge abutment excavations at either end of Stockpile 2, and Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) retaining wall foundation excavations along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "Excavated BCS for placement within the BCS Containment Zones was generated from removal of Stockpile 3, bridge abutment excavations at either end of Stockpile 2, and mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) retaining wall foundation excavations along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2."}} Aerially deposited lead (ADL) contaminated soil from the SR 99 northbound shoulder was also placed within the BCS Containment Zones. {{quote: "The RDIP provided for the placement of shallow soil excavated along the northbound SR 99 shoulder that contained hazardous waste concentrations of aerially deposited lead (ADL) to be placed within the BCS Containment Zones."}}

The southern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 were excavated in March and April 2020 to facilitate MSE wall construction. Soil from these MSE wall footing excavations was sampled. {{quote: "The southerly slopes of BCS Stockpiles 1 and 2 were excavated (steepened) in March and April 2020 to facilitate clean construction of the MSE walls and to facilitate placement of specified wall reinforcement backfill materials."}} {{quote: "We collected soil samples within the mid-point of the wall footing excavation in trenches STK1-1, STK1-2 and STK1-3 spaced at approximate 200-foot intervals. Discrete soil samples were collected from one-foot intervals to the maximum planned wall footing excavation depth of 5 feet."}} Barium was detected in these samples at concentrations ranging from 42 to 420 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg), which was {{quote: "greater than the maximum site specific background value of 120 mg/kg, but less than the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) removal threshold of 1,000 mg/kg based on groundwater quality protection."}} The 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) for barium in these samples was 171.9 mg/kg, and the mean was 128.3 mg/kg. {{quote: "The calculated 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) and mean of the entire barium data set are 171.9 and 128.3 mg/kg, respectively (statistical summary attached)."}} Lead was detected in 14 of 15 samples at concentrations ranging from 1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg, which was {{quote: "within the range of the maximum site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg, and less than the RDIP removal threshold of 80 mg/kg based on residential land use."}} Based on these results, the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material was determined suitable for placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill, or as capping material, pending regulatory approval. {{quote: "Based on the data presented herein, the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material excavated along the southerly boundary of Stockpile 1 should be suitable for placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill at the east end of Stockpile 1 and/or ends of Stockpile 2, or as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material pending regulatory agency approval."}}

The eastern end of Stockpile 1 was also a focus for clean fill cap material. The Carpenter Road shoofly excavation provided approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil for use as clean capping material. {{quote: "Approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil will be excavated from the Carpenter Road Shoofly once the overcrossing structure under construction is completed. The contractor plans to use the excavated soil material as clean capping material to be placed over portions of barium containing soil (BCS) Stockpiles 1 and 2 pending California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) approval."}} Soil samples from the Carpenter Road shoofly showed barium concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 mg/kg, and lead concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "Barium was detected in each of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg), less than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg. Lead was detected in 9 of the 10 soil samples analyzed at concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg, less than or equal to the maximum site-specific background concentration for lead of 3.8 mg/kg."}} This material met the BCS clean cap criteria. {{quote: "Based on the barium and lead data presented herein, soil material generated from excavation of the Carpenter Road Shoofly meet the BCS clean cap criteria as established in the referenced variance document as approved by DTSC (upperbound background threshold for barium of 120 mg/kg)."}}

The northern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 were capped with DTSC-approved clean fill material by the Fall of 2020. {{quote: "The majority of the upper surface and northern slopes of the completed Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones were capped with DTSC-approved clean fill material by the Fall 2020."}} In January 2021, erosion of the clean cap fill on the exposed northerly slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 occurred, requiring regrading and placement of clean fill from the Carpenter Road shoofly excavation in September 2021. {{quote: "In January 2021, a record rain event caused significant erosion of the clean cap fill materials placed on the exposed (unvegetated) northerly slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2. Plastic sheeting was placed along the slopes to minimize further erosion pending a revised plan to stabilize the slope face. The plastic sheeting was removed and the eroded areas regraded in September 2021 using clean fill deposited at the toe of slope and placement of approved clean fill material generated from excavation of the Carpenter Road shoofly."}} The vegetated slopes were then stabilized with a native plant mix, erosion control blanket, and fiber rolls. {{quote: "The cleaned capped northerly slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 were vegetated in October 2021 using a native plant mix hydroseed/hydromulch, and subsequent placement of an erosion control blanket (rolled erosion control product) and fiber rolls."}}

Groundwater monitoring wells MW1, MW2, and MW4 are mapped in proximity to Stockpile 1. {{quote: "Groundwater monitoring wells MW1, MW2, and MW4 are mapped in proximity to Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2."}}

## Regulatory status

The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) provided verification thresholds for BCS removal: Barium at 1,000 mg/kg for groundwater protection and Lead at 80 mg/kg for a residential screening level. {{quote: "The RDIP provided the following soil excavation verification thresholds for the planned BCS removal areas: Barium1 - 1,000 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg) based on groundwater protection Lead - 80 mg/kg based on DTSC residential screening level"}} For clean fill source areas, the RDIP Variance dated April 9, 2020, established site-specific upper bound background concentrations for barium at 120 mg/kg and lead at 3.8 mg/kg. {{quote: "The Variance further presented the following site-specific upperbound background concentrations for barium and lead for comparison with proposed clean fill source area characterization soil samples: Barium - 120 mg/kg Lead - 3.8 mg/kg"}}

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) approved the use of MSE wall footing excavation soil from Stockpile 1 as general bridge embankment fill or capping material, with the condition that it must be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones. {{quote: "Both agencies agree with the last paragraph of the Memo with the exception of using the MSE wall footing soil as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material. Given that the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil is above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg and the fact that the MSE wall footing falls outside of the designated borrow area described in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, this material must be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill."}}
