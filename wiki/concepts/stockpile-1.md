## Stockpile 1

### Overview

Stockpile 1 is one of three Barium-Containing Soil (BCS) stockpiles identified within the State Route 132 (SR 132) right-of-way in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. It was designated for consolidation and containment as part of the Interim Phase 1 construction of the SR 132 West Project. The stockpile material was primarily comprised of layered, poorly graded sand and silty sand. Stockpile 1 was located approximately 150 feet westerly of Emerald Avenue, measuring roughly 600 feet long, 160 feet wide, with an estimated fill volume of 34,000 cubic yards (yd³).

### Site record

Stockpile 1 was consolidated and contained within the SR 132 highway embankment fills, referred to as BCS Containment Zones, located between SR 99 and the west end of Stockpile 1. Excavated BCS for placement within these zones was generated from the removal of Stockpile 3, bridge abutment excavations at either end of Stockpile 2, and Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) retaining wall foundation excavations along the southern boundaries of Stockpiles 1 and 2. Aerially deposited lead (ADL) contaminated soil from the SR 99 northbound shoulder was also placed within the BCS Containment Zones.

The southern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 were excavated in March and April 2020 to facilitate MSE wall construction. Soil from these MSE wall footing excavations was sampled. Barium was detected in these samples at concentrations ranging from 42 to 420 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg), which was The 95% upper confidence limit (UCL) for barium in these samples was 171.9 mg/kg, and the mean was 128.3 mg/kg. Lead was detected in 14 of 15 samples at concentrations ranging from 1.1 to 6.7 mg/kg, which was Based on these results, the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing material was determined suitable for placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones, as general bridge embankment fill, or as capping material, pending regulatory approval.

The eastern end of Stockpile 1 was also a focus for clean fill cap material. The Carpenter Road shoofly excavation provided approximately 5,000 cubic yards of native soil for use as clean capping material. Soil samples from the Carpenter Road shoofly showed barium concentrations ranging from 28 to 96 mg/kg, and lead concentrations ranging from 1.4 to 3.8 mg/kg. This material met the BCS clean cap criteria.

The northern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 were capped with DTSC-approved clean fill material by the Fall of 2020. In January 2021, erosion of the clean cap fill on the exposed northerly slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 occurred, requiring regrading and placement of clean fill from the Carpenter Road shoofly excavation in September 2021. The vegetated slopes were then stabilized with a native plant mix, erosion control blanket, and fiber rolls.

Groundwater monitoring wells MW1, MW2, and MW4 are mapped in proximity to Stockpile 1.

Barium was detected in Stockpile 1 at a maximum concentration of 1,730 mg/kg in samples collected during the Shaw 2004 PSI. In the Shaw 2006 SI, barium was detected at a maximum concentration of 130 mg/kg in Stockpile 1.

The 2007 HHRA evaluated current offsite resident and trespasser exposure to COPCs in Stockpile 1 soil. The calculated cancer risk was 8E-8 and the non-cancer hazard index (HI) was 4E-2. The lead assessment for Stockpile 1 indicated that offsite residents or trespassers would not have a blood lead concentration greater than 10 micrograms per deciliter (μg/dL).

### Regulatory status

The Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) provided verification thresholds for BCS removal: Barium at 1,000 mg/kg for groundwater protection and Lead at 80 mg/kg for a residential screening level. For clean fill source areas, the RDIP Variance dated April 9, 2020, established site-specific upper bound background concentrations for barium at 120 mg/kg and lead at 3.8 mg/kg.

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) approved the use of MSE wall footing excavation soil from Stockpile 1 as general bridge embankment fill or capping material, with the condition that it must be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones.
