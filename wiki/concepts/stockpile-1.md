## Overview

Stockpile 1 is one of two primary Barium-Containing Soil (BCS) stockpiles addressed in the January 2019 *Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan*, prepared by Geocon Consultants, Inc. (Project No. S1200-01-01) for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) – Interim Phase 1. The stockpile is located along the State Route 132 corridor in Stanislaus County, California, within the area bisected by Emerald Avenue. It is designated for consolidation, sampling, and eventual removal as part of the remedial action for barium-impacted soil associated with the SR-132 project alignment. The MSE (Mechanically Stabilized Earth) wall sampling plan provides the sampling strategy for characterizing stockpile material prior to reuse in MSE wall construction.

## Site record

The *Grading Plan* (Figure 3-2, dated January 2019) depicts Stockpile 1 as a large area highlighted in yellow and labeled "Barium-Containing Soil Stockpile," situated north of Emerald Avenue along the SR-132 right-of-way. The figure shows Stockpile 1 directly across Emerald Avenue from Stockpile 2, with both stockpiles forming part of the larger "Barium-Containing Soil and ADL Consolidation Zone."

The plan identifies a "BCS Removal Sampling Plan" applicable to Stockpiles 1 and 2, with proposed sampling locations marked in the vicinity of Stockpile 1 near the right-of-way/fence line. The document is expressly marked as "65% SUBMITTAL NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION 03-05-18," indicating interim design status at the time of the sampling plan's preparation.

Groundwater monitoring wells MW1, MW2, and MW4 are mapped in proximity to Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2. An area designated for "Imported Clean Embankment Fill/Structure Backfill Placement" is shown adjacent to the stockpile zone, alongside the "Emerald Ave. Eastern Bridge Abutment Stockpile Removal Area."

The January 2019 *Grading Plan* cover sheet (page 1) identifies a yellow-highlighted feature labeled "STOCKPILE 61" as Barium-Containing Soil, with annotations referencing the BCS Removal Sampling Plan for Stockpiles 1 and 2 and the Southern Slope Retaining Wall. The relationship between "STOCKPILE 61" and Stockpile 1 is not further clarified in the available documentation.

## Regulatory status

The sampling plan was prepared under the RDIP – Interim Phase 1 framework. Specific regulatory standards, action levels, or agency approvals applicable to Stockpile 1 are not detailed in the available plan documents. The plan's "65% SUBMITTAL – NOT FOR CONSTRUCTION" designation as of March 5, 2018 indicates the document had not yet received final agency approval at that stage.
