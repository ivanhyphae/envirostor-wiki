## Stockpile 1

### Overview

Stockpile 1 was one of three Barium-Containing Soil (BCS) stockpiles located within the Caltrans State Route 132 right-of-way (ROW) in Modesto, Stanislaus County, California. These stockpiles were generated from soils excavated during the construction of the SR 132 West Project, including material from former FMC facility evaporation ponds known to contain elevated levels of barium and lead. Stockpile 1 was consolidated and contained within highway embankment fills as part of the Interim Phase 1 construction.

### Site Record

Stockpile 1 was located approximately 150 feet west of Emerald Avenue. It was roughly 600 feet long in the east-west direction, 160 feet wide, and had an estimated fill volume of 34,000 cubic yards (yd³). The stockpile was primarily composed of layered, poorly graded sand and silty sand, similar to underlying native alluvial deposits of the Modesto Formation. The average maximum stockpile fill thickness was approximately 20 feet.

During the construction of Interim Phase 1 of the SR 132 West Project, Stockpile 3 was completely excavated and its material was placed as embankment fill on top of Stockpile 1 and between Stockpile 1 and Emerald Avenue. Excavated BCS from the southern slopes of Stockpile 1 for Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) wall construction was also placed within the designated Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone. Aerially Deposited Lead (ADL)-contaminated soil excavated from the northbound SR 99 shoulder was also placed in the eastern end of the Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone.

Characterization testing of soil within the MSE wall footing areas along the southerly boundary of Stockpile 1, excavated to depths of 3 to 5 feet, revealed elevated barium concentrations greater than the maximum site-specific background concentration of 120 mg/kg, but less than the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) removal threshold of 1,000 mg/kg. Lead concentrations were generally within the range of the site-specific background concentration of 3.8 mg/kg and less than the RDIP removal threshold of 80 mg/kg. Due to the elevated barium, all excavated MSE wall footing material was placed in the Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones.

The Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone is located adjacent and west of Emerald Avenue between approximate SR 132 Stations 1125+50 and 1133+50. As-built plans and typical cross-sections of the completed Stockpile 1 BCS Containment Zone were prepared by Dokken Engineering based on survey data.

### Regulatory Status

The RDIP established soil excavation verification thresholds for planned BCS removal areas: Barium at 1,000 mg/kg (groundwater protection) and Lead at 80 mg/kg (DTSC residential screening level). A DTSC-approved Variance to the RDIP dated April 9, 2020, presented site-specific upper-bound background concentrations for comparison with proposed clean fill source area characterization soil samples: Barium at 120 mg/kg and Lead at 3.8 mg/kg.

The RDIP also provided for sampling and analytical testing of clean fill source areas in accordance with DTSC's clean fill material guidance. Clean fill source areas evaluated and approved by DTSC for use as capping material over Stockpile 1 included the SR 132 ROW construction excavation area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road, and the Basin 5 construction excavation area adjacent and east of Carpenter Road. The Bent 2 construction excavation stockpiles adjacent and west of SR 99 were not approved by DTSC for use as clean capping material.

The Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS Containment Zones are subject to a DTSC-required Land Use Covenant (LUC)/deed restriction to be recorded with the Stanislaus County Clerk-Recorder Office. Caltrans is responsible for ongoing groundwater and stormwater monitoring activities specific to the BCS Stockpiles.

### Open Items

Caltrans will perform five-year reviews to assess the effectiveness of the interim Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS containment measures. These reviews will focus on monitoring and maintenance of the implemented containment measures and the potential for exposure to contained and capped BCS/ADL soil materials.
