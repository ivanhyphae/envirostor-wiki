The Supplemental Site Investigation (SSI) Report for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project documents the findings of additional site investigations. This report, along with separate reports on contamination extent and human health risk assessment, was prepared to address requirements from the [[dtsc]] and [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. The finalized SSI Report was to be uploaded to the EnviroStor and [[caltrans]] project websites.

### Site Investigation Findings

A project meeting on November 16, 2012, involving representatives from [[caltrans]], [[dtsc]], [[central-valley-rwqcb]], and [[geocon-consultants-inc]], reviewed preliminary soil analytical data from Fenceline, Perimeter, and Cadmium Borings.

*   **Barium:** Concentrations of barium in all soil samples from the Fenceline and Perimeter Borings were reported below the residential CHHSL. Given these results and observed decreasing barium concentrations with depth, further assessment of the lateral and vertical extent of barium-impacted soil stockpiles was deemed unnecessary at that time.
*   **Cadmium:** Cadmium was determined not to be a COPC for the project site, as all reported concentrations from the Fenceline and Cadmium Borings were below the residential CHHSL of 1.7 mg/kg.
*   **Data Utilization:** Soil analytical data from the Fenceline, Perimeter, and Cadmium Borings were intended for use in updating the Shaw 2007 [[human-health-risk-assessment]]. geocon-consultants-inc. was responsible for preparing this update.
*   **Historical Review:** A review of historical information was to be conducted to ascertain the potential for barium-impacted pond tailings and soil from FMC evaporation ponds to have been deposited outside the [[caltrans]] ROW during the placement of soil stockpiles. This information is detailed in Section 2.2 and Figure 3 of the SSI Report.
*   **Sample Retention:** Unused soil samples from the Fenceline, Perimeter, and Cadmium Borings were to be retained by geocon-consultants-inc. and ATL under Chain of Custody (COC) protocol for potential additional laboratory analysis of metals within their six-month holding time.

### Report Title Consistency

The [[dtsc]] noted a discrepancy between the title of the SSI Report and the [[human-health-risk-assessment]] report. The SSI Report title is "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County," while the HHRA title is "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Routes 99 and 132, Stanislaus County, California." A revision to the HHRA title was requested to align with the SSI Report title.
