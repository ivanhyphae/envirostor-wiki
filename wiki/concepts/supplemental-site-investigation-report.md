## Supplemental Site Investigation Report

### Overview

The Supplemental Site Investigation Report (SSI Report) for the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] was prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] under [[caltrans]] Contract No. 06A1580, Task Order (TO) Number 44, and Expense Authorization (EA) 10-403500. This report documents findings from investigations conducted at the project site, located along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County, California.

### Site Record

A project meeting was held on November 16, 2012, with representatives from [[caltrans]], [[dtsc]], [[central-valley-rwqcb]], and [[geocon-consultants-inc]] to review preliminary soil analytical data from the Fenceline, Perimeter, and Cadmium Borings.

*   **Barium:** Based on reported barium concentrations below the residential CHHSL for all soil samples from the Fenceline and Perimeter Borings, and decreasing barium concentrations with depth, additional environmental assessment of the lateral and vertical extent of barium-impacted soil stockpiles was deemed not warranted at that time.
*   **Cadmium:** Cadmium was determined not to be a COPC for the project site, as reported concentrations were below the residential CHHSL of 1.7 mg/kg in all soil samples from the Fenceline and Cadmium Borings.
*   **Data Utilization:** Soil analytical data from the Fenceline, Perimeter, and Cadmium Borings were to be utilized to update the Shaw 2007 HHRA. [[geocon-consultants-inc]] was preparing this update.
*   **Historical Review:** A review of historical information was to be conducted to determine the potential for barium-impacted pond tailings and soil excavated from the FMC evaporation ponds to have been deposited outside of the Caltrans ROW during the placement of soil stockpiles. This information is presented in Section 2.2 and on Figure 3 of the SSI Report.
*   **Sample Retention:** Unused soil samples from the Fenceline, Perimeter, and Cadmium Borings were to be retained for additional laboratory analysis within the six-month holding time for metals, under Chain of Custody (COC) protocol by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] and ATL.

The title of the Human Health Risk Assessment (HHRA) report was noted by [[dtsc]] to be slightly different from the SSI report title. The SSI report title includes "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County," while the HHRA title includes "Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Routes 99 and 132, Stanislaus County, California." A revision to the HHRA title was requested to ensure consistency with the SSI report.

### Regulatory Status

The SSI Report was prepared in the context of investigations and assessments related to the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] project, which involves regulatory oversight from agencies including the [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]].
