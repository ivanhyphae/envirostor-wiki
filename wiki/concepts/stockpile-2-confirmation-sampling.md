Stockpile 2 Confirmation Sampling refers to the soil sampling conducted after removal of Barium Containing Soil (BCS) from the east and west ends of [[stockpile-2]], performed to verify that those areas had reached the cleanup levels set in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP) before clean embankment fill could be placed. [[geocon-consultants-inc]] documented the removal and sampling in a Technical Memorandum dated March 9, 2020, which [[caltrans]] forwarded to [[dtsc]] the same day for approval, and DTSC accepted the memo on March 10, 2020, one day later.

## Sequence of events

Geocon's John Juhrend notified Caltrans engineering geologist Richard Stewart on March 9, 2020, that BCS removal work was proceeding, referencing verification testing tied to the removal effort. That same evening, Stewart transmitted the completed Technical Memorandum to DTSC (Dean Wright and Perry Myers) and the Regional Water Quality Control Board (Steven Meeks), describing it as required per the RDIP and stating that Caltrans was awaiting agency approval before placing clean soil cover on Stockpile 2. DTSC's Dean Wright responded the next day, March 10, 2020, confirming that DTSC had reviewed the memo and that the confirmation sampling results showed the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 had met the RDIP verification thresholds.

## Approval and its effect

DTSC's March 10, 2020 email constitutes its acceptance of the memo and its determination that the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 were suitable for placement of clean embankment fill. The approval was limited to those two areas of the stockpile; the correspondence does not address the remainder of Stockpile 2. The specific analytical results and verification threshold values themselves are not given in the available correspondence — only the agencies' conclusion that the thresholds were met.

The same March 9, 2020 email chain also references BCS removal verification testing at Stockpile 3, noted as expected to be completed the following day pending final results, but no outcome for that separate effort is recorded in this correspondence.
