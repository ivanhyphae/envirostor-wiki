Steven Meeks is an individual associated with the Water Board (WB) who received communications regarding sampling plans and technical memorandums related to the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. He was included in email correspondence from [[caltrans]] to [[dean-wright]] and [[perry-myers]] at the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) and others, concerning the characterization and use of soils from various project areas, including [[stockpile-1]] and Stockpile 2, Bent 2, and Basin 5.

## Site record

Steven Meeks was copied on an email dated March 31, 2020, regarding the "Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan." This plan proposed sampling within the mid-point of the MSE wall footing excavation at Stockpiles 1 and 2. Discrete soil samples were to be collected from one-foot intervals to a depth of approximately 4 to 5 feet and analyzed for barium and lead. The analytical data was to be compared to site-specific background levels. The maximum site-specific background for barium was 120 mg/kg, and for lead was 3.8 mg/kg.

Meeks was also copied on emails concerning the characterization of excavated soils for use as clean cap material. On November 6, 2020, Dean-Wright of DTSC informed Richard-Stewart that DTSC and the Water Board had reviewed the Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020, regarding the use of Bent 2 excavation soil as clean cap material. While the soil did not meet clean cap criteria, it was agreed that the soil could be placed on top of Stockpile 2 at a thickness of 6 inches or less, provided it was covered with clean base material and roadway construction.

Additionally, Meeks was included in correspondence regarding Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing sampling. On April 7, 2020, Dean-Wright notified Richard-Stewart that DTSC and the Water Board had reviewed the Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020, describing the sampling along the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area and the laboratory results of their analysis. The MSE wall footing soil was not approved for use as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material because the 95% Upper Confidence Limit exceeded the maximum background of 120 mg/kg. The material was to be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill.

On June 22, 2020, Dean-Wright informed Richard-Stewart that DTSC and the Water Board had reviewed the revised Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020, concerning Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation. It was agreed that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones, but soil below that depth was suitable as embankment fill or capping material.

Meeks was also copied on an email dated June 11, 2020, regarding the "SR 132 Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan." This plan was for a clean cover mining location at proposed Basin 5.

## Regulatory status

Steven Meeks, representing the Water Board (WB), was involved in the review and approval process for the use of excavated soils on-site. His involvement indicates the Water Board's role in overseeing the environmental aspects of the project, particularly concerning soil reuse and potential contamination. The decisions made, such as the conditions for using Bent 2 soils or the placement requirements for MSE wall footing soils, reflect regulatory guidance and approvals from both DTSC and the WB.
