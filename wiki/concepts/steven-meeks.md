Steven Meeks is an individual associated with the Waterboards who was included in email communications regarding the characterization and use of soils from Stockpiles 1 and 2, specifically concerning Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) wall footing excavations.

## Soil Reuse Determinations

On March 31, 2020, Steven Meeks was included in an email from Richard Stewart of [[caltrans]] to [[dean-wright]] of DTSC and Perry Myers of DTSC, concerning a proposed sampling plan for MSE wall footing soils for Stockpiles 1 and 2. The plan, developed by Geocon Consultants Inc., proposed sampling at 1-foot intervals within the MSE wall footing excavations, with discrete soil samples analyzed for barium and lead. The analytical data was to be compared against site-specific background levels, RDIP thresholds, and various Environmental Screening Levels (ESLs) to determine suitability for reuse. For barium, the comparison levels included a maximum site-specific background of 120 mg/kg and an RDIP BCS removal threshold of 1,000 mg/kg for groundwater protection. For lead, the comparison levels included a maximum site-specific background of 3.8 mg/kg and a DTSC residential ESL of 80 mg/kg.

On April 7, 2020, Steven Meeks was copied on an email from Dean Wright of DTSC to Richard Stewart of [[caltrans]]. This email accepted a Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020, regarding Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing sampling. The acceptance was conditional on the MSE wall footing soil not being used as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material. Instead, the material was to be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill, as the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil exceeded the maximum background of 120 mg/kg and the footing fell outside the designated borrow area.

On June 18, 2020, Richard Stewart of [[caltrans]] sent a revised technical memo regarding Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing soil to Dean Wright of DTSC, Perry Myers of DTSC, and Steven Meeks of the Waterboards. The memo indicated that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones, but soil below that depth was suitable as embankment fill or capping material. This material below 3 feet bgs was also considered part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, meaning another VR was not necessary for its utilization. Dean Wright of DTSC officially accepted this memo on June 22, 2020.

On November 6, 2020, Steven Meeks was copied on an email from Dean Wright of DTSC to Richard Stewart of [[caltrans]]. This email accepted a Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020, concerning the use of Bent 2 excavation soil as clean cap material. The soil sample results did not meet the clean cap criteria. However, both DTSC and the Waterboards agreed that this soil could be placed on top of Stockpile 2 at a thickness of 6 inches or less, provided it was covered with clean base material and the roadway construction. Strict adherence to the rainy season surface water sampling effort and erosion control measures outlined in the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020, was emphasized.
