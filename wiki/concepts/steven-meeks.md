Steven Meeks is an individual associated with the Water Board (WB) who received communications regarding sampling plans and technical memorandums related to the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. He was included in email correspondence from Richard-Stewart at [[caltrans]] to Dean-Wright at the Department-of-Toxic-Substances-Control (DTSC) and Perry-Myers at DTSC, concerning the characterization and use of soils from various project areas, including Stockpiles 1 and 2, Bent 2, and Basin 5.

## Site record

Steven Meeks was copied on an email dated March 31, 2020, regarding the "Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan." {{quote: "To: [Wright, Dean@DTSC](mailto:Dean.Wright@dtsc.ca.gov); [Myers, Perry@DTSC](mailto:Perry.Myers@dtsc.ca.gov); [Meeks, Steven@Waterboards](mailto:Steven.Meeks@waterboards.ca.gov)"}} This plan proposed sampling within the mid-point of the MSE wall footing excavation at Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "As the BCS slope zone is removed we will sample borings or trenches within the mid-point of the wall footing excavation at each of the ten identified sampling locations spaced at approximate 200 foot intervals."}} Discrete soil samples were to be collected from one-foot intervals to a depth of approximately 4 to 5 feet and analyzed for barium and lead. {{quote: "Discrete soil samples will be collected from one-foot intervals to the depth of the planned wall footing excavation (~ 4 to 5 feet). Each discrete soil ample will be analyzed for barium and lead under expediated 48-hour turn-around time."}} The analytical data was to be compared to site-specific background levels and RDIP/regulatory screening levels. {{quote: "Barium: Maximum site-specific background 120 mg/kg ... Lead: Maximum site-specific background 3.8 mg/kg"}}

Meeks was also copied on emails concerning the characterization of excavated soils for use as clean cap material. On November 6, 2020, Dean-Wright of DTSC informed Richard-Stewart that DTSC and the Water Board had reviewed the Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020, regarding the use of Bent 2 excavation soil as clean cap material. {{quote: "The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) and the Water Board (WB) have reviewed the Technical Memorandum (Tech Memo) dated October 30, 2020 regarding the use of Bent 2 excavation soil as clean cap material."}} While the soil did not meet clean cap criteria, it was agreed that the soil could be placed on top of Stockpile 2 at a thickness of 6 inches or less, provided it was covered with clean base material and roadway construction. {{quote: "However, both DTSC and the WB agree that this soil may be placed on top of Stockpile 2 at a thickness of 6 inches or less as long as it is covered with clean base material and the asphalt/concrete used to construct the roadway."}}

Additionally, Meeks was included in correspondence regarding Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing sampling. On April 7, 2020, Dean-Wright notified Richard-Stewart that DTSC and the Water Board had reviewed the Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020. {{quote: "DTSC and the Water Board have reviewed the Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated April 6, 2020 describing the sampling along the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area and the laboratory results of their analysis."}} The MSE wall footing soil was not approved for use as Stockpile 1 and 2 capping material because the 95% Upper Confidence Limit exceeded the maximum background of 120 mg/kg. {{quote: "Given that the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil is above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg and the fact that the MSE wall footing falls outside of the designated borrow area described in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, this material must be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill."}}

On June 22, 2020, Dean-Wright informed Richard-Stewart that DTSC and the Water Board had reviewed the revised Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020, concerning Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation. {{quote: "DTSC and the Water Board have reviewed the Technical Memorandum (Memo) dated June 18, 2020 describing the Stockpile 2 sampling along the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area and the laboratory results of their analysis."}} It was agreed that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones, but soil below that depth was suitable as embankment fill or capping material. {{quote: "Both agencies agree with the Memo that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones but soil below that depth is suitable as embankment fill or capping material."}}

Meeks was also copied on an email dated June 11, 2020, regarding the "SR 132 Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan." {{quote: "To: [Wright, Dean@DTSC](mailto:Dean.Wright@dtsc.ca.gov); [Myers, Perry@DTSC](mailto:Perry.Myers@dtsc.ca.gov); [Meeks, Steven@Waterboards](mailto:Steven.Meeks@waterboards.ca.gov)"}} This plan was for a clean cover mining location at proposed Basin 5. {{quote: "Clean cover mining location at proposed Basin 5 for your review and approval."}}

## Regulatory status

Steven Meeks, representing the Water Board (WB), was involved in the review and approval process for the use of excavated soils on-site. His involvement indicates the Water Board's role in overseeing the environmental aspects of the project, particularly concerning soil reuse and potential contamination. The decisions made, such as the conditions for using Bent 2 soils or the placement requirements for MSE wall footing soils, reflect regulatory guidance and approvals from both DTSC and the WB.
