Thallium was detected at low concentrations in stockpile soil in the June 2014 Final FS Report and had not been reported in groundwater or in surface water at the site before 2023. The March 2023 stormwater sampling round did not report thallium above the California primary MCL. The December 2023 stormwater event is the first reported exceedance of the thallium MCL at the site, marking a departure from the constituent's prior absence from water-medium results.

The record does not give the reported thallium concentration for the December 2023 stormwater sample, nor does it restate the numeric MCL value in this context (the applicable California primary MCL for thallium is addressed separately in [[california-primary-mcl]]). The exceedance is discussed in reports dated February 2024 and February 2025.

No subsequent sampling round, response action, or regulatory correspondence tied specifically to this thallium exceedance is described in the available material. Whether the December 2023 result was isolated to that event or recurred in later stormwater rounds is not established in the sources reviewed.

## Significance

The exceedance matters because it changes thallium's status at the site: a constituent previously confined to low soil concentrations, with a clean record in groundwater and in surface water through at least March 2023, appeared above its drinking water standard in stormwater by December 2023. That shift bears on any claim that stormwater leaving the [[modesto-soil-stockpiles]] area is unimpacted, since thallium is the first constituent in this record to newly exceed an MCL in stormwater rather than continuing a pattern already seen in soil.
