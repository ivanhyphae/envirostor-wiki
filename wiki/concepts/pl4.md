## Overview

PL4 is a surface water sampling location situated at the drainage basin adjacent to Stockpile 3. This location is specifically where stormwater enters the drainage basin. The purpose of sampling at PL4 is to monitor surface water quality in relation to the soil stockpiles.

## Site record

Surface water samples were collected from PL4 on multiple occasions. On December 15, 2016, sample PL4 was collected from the drainage basin outfall adjacent to Stockpile 3, northeast of the SR 99 off-ramp to Kansas Avenue. {{quote: "We collected sample PL4 (Photo 4) at the drainage basin outfall adjacent to Stockpile 3, northeast of the SR 99 off-ramp to Kansas Avenue."}} Stormwater at this location originates from drainage inlets in the depressed section of SR 99 north and south of Kansas Avenue. {{quote: "From information provided by Caltrans, storm water in the basin originates exclusively from the drainage inlets located in the depressed section of SR 99 north and south of Kansas Avenue."}}

Analytical results for dissolved metals in sample PL4 collected on December 15, 2016, indicated the presence of barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium. Antimony was also reported. {{quote: "Barium, calcium, chromium, copper, magnesium, manganese, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium were reported for samples PL1 through PL4. Antimony was reported for samples PL1, PL2, and PL4."}} Zinc was reported for samples PL2 through PL4. {{quote: "Zinc was reported for samples PL2 through PL4."}} The manganese concentration reported for sample PL4 exceeded the secondary Maximum Containment Level (MCL) of 50 micrograms per liter (µg/l). {{quote: "The manganese concentrations reported for samples PL3 and PL4 exceeded the secondary Maximum Containment Level (MCL) of 50 micrograms per liter (µg/l)."}} Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits (MDLs) for sample PL4. {{quote: "Beryllium, cadmium, mercury, selenium, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits (MDLs) for samples PL1 through PL4."}}

During the December 15, 2016 sampling event, the laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL4 were diluted due to high concentrations of target analytes, specifically calcium and sodium. {{quote: "The laboratory notes state samples PL1 through PL4, BG1 and BG2 were diluted due to the high concentrations of the target analytes. PL1 through PL4, BG1 and BG2 were diluted for calcium and sodium."}}

On March 24, 2017, surface water samples were collected from PL4. {{quote: "On March 24, 2017, we collected surface water samples from designated locations PL1 through PL5."}} Analytical results for dissolved metals in sample PL4 collected on March 24, 2017, indicated the presence of antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium. {{quote: "Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium were reported for samples PL1 through PL5."}} Arsenic was reported for samples PL1 through PL4. {{quote: "Arsenic was reported for samples PL1 through PL4."}} Cadmium was reported for samples PL4 and PL5. {{quote: "Cadmium was reported for samples PL4 and PL5."}} Manganese was reported for samples PL2 and PL4. {{quote: "Manganese was reported for samples PL2 and PL4."}} Zinc was reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5. {{quote: "Zinc was reported for samples PL1 and PL3 through PL5."}} None of the reported concentrations for PL4 exceeded their respective primary or secondary MCLs, with the exception of manganese. {{quote: "None of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs with the exception of manganese in samples PL2 and PL4."}} Beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits for sample PL4. {{quote: "Beryllium, mercury, silver, and thallium were not reported at concentrations equal to or greater than their respective method detection limits for samples PL1 through PL5."}}

The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL5, including PL4, were diluted for barium and calcium due to high concentrations of target analytes, and for magnesium, manganese, potassium, and sodium due to high concentrations of non-target analytes. {{quote: "The laboratory notes state samples PL1 through PL5 were diluted for: (1) barium and calcium due to the high concentrations of the target analytes, (2) magnesium, manganese, potassium, and sodium due to high concentration of non-target analyte, and (3) nitrate as nitrogen due to possible matrix interference."}} Sample PL4 was also diluted for lead and thallium due to a failing internal standard in the original run. {{quote: "The laboratory notes also state that sample PL4 and PL5 were diluted for lead and thallium due to failing internal standard in the original run."}}

On January 8, 2018, surface water samples were collected from PL4. {{quote: "On January 8, 2018, we collected surface water samples from designated locations PL1 through PL4 and BG1 and BG2."}} The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 and BG2 were diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc due to high concentrations of target analytes. {{quote: "The laboratory notes state samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 and BG2 were diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc due to the high concentrations of the target analytes."}}

## Regulatory status

During the December 15, 2016 sampling event, the manganese concentration reported for sample PL4 exceeded the secondary Maximum Containment Level (MCL) of 50 micrograms per liter (µg/l). {{quote: "The manganese concentrations reported for samples PL3 and PL4 exceeded the secondary Maximum Containment Level (MCL) of 50 micrograms per liter (µg/l)."}} During the March 24, 2017 sampling event, manganese in sample PL4 exceeded its secondary MCL. {{quote: "None of the reported concentrations exceed their respective primary or secondary MCLs with the exception of manganese in samples PL2 and PL4."}}
