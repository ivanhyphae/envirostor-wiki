## PL4

PL4 is a surface water sampling location at the Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It is situated at the drainage basin next to Stockpile 3, specifically where stormwater enters this basin. This location is northeast of the SR 99 off-ramp to Kansas Avenue. Stormwater collected at PL4 originates from drainage inlets within the depressed section of SR 99 north and south of Kansas Avenue.

### Site Record

Surface water samples have been collected from PL4 on multiple occasions.

*   **December 15, 2016:** A surface water sample was collected from PL4. The sample was analyzed for dissolved metals. Manganese concentrations reported for PL4 exceeded the secondary Maximum Containment Level (MCL) of 50 micrograms per liter (µg/l). The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL4, BG1, and BG2 were diluted due to high concentrations of target analytes, specifically calcium and sodium. PL1, PL3, BG1, and BG2 were diluted for magnesium. PL3 was diluted for barium, and BG1 was diluted for potassium.
*   **March 22, 2018:** A surface water sample was collected from PL4. Dissolved metals analysis indicated that manganese was detected at concentrations ranging from 54 to 370 µg/l, exceeding its secondary MCL of 50 µg/l. The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 were diluted for metals due to high concentrations of target analytes, failing internal standards, or high concentrations of non-target analytes. Samples PL1 through PL3 and BG1 were also diluted for sulfide.
*   **January 8, 2018:** A surface water sample was collected from PL4. The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL4 and BG1 and BG2 were diluted for barium, calcium, potassium, and/or zinc due to high concentrations of target analytes. BG1 and BG2 were also diluted for sulfide.
*   **March 24, 2017:** A surface water sample was collected from PL4. Dissolved metals analysis indicated that manganese was reported for sample PL4, exceeding its secondary MCL. The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL5 were diluted for barium and calcium due to high concentrations of target analytes, and for magnesium, manganese, potassium, and sodium due to high concentrations of non-target analytes.
*   **October 28, 2016:** A surface water sample was collected from PL4. The laboratory noted that samples PL1 through PL5, BG1, and BG2 were diluted due to failing internal standards or possible matrix interference. PL1 through PL4, BG1, and BG2 were diluted for sulfate. PL1, PL3, PL5, BG1, and BG2 were diluted for several dissolved metals, including barium, beryllium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, magnesium, manganese, nickel, potassium, sodium, thallium, and vanadium.
*   **March 2006:** A surface water sample (SW03) was collected from the northwestern side of Stockpile 3, which is in the vicinity of PL4. This sample reported an elevated barium concentration of 2,000 µg/l. Other samples from this event ranged from 16 to 190 µg/l for barium.

Observations during sampling events consistently indicate that stormwater runoff is retained within the Caltrans right-of-way and does not migrate offsite.

### Regulatory Status

Manganese concentrations at PL4 have exceeded the secondary MCL of 50 µg/l in multiple sampling events, including March 2017 and March 2018.
