## Overview

The **January 6, 2016 stormwater sampling event** at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR-132, Stanislaus County) was conducted to characterize runoff quality from stockpiled asphalt grindings and native soil during a rain event. The sampling was part of ongoing site assessment and compliance monitoring under the oversight of the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). This event provides baseline data for evaluating potential contaminant migration via stormwater runoff from the stockpiles.

## Site Record

The stormwater sampling was performed on **January 6, 2016**, following a rain event that produced measurable runoff from the stockpiles. The sampling event is documented in report **S9800-01-17A Modesto Stockpiles Jan 6 2016 Stormwater Smplng 0216** (filename: `S9800-01-17A Modesto Stockpiles Jan 6 2016 Stormwater Smplng 0216.md`). No other source documents specific to this event are referenced in the provided project record.

Key details from the record include:
- **Location**: Stockpiles adjacent to State Route 132, Modesto, Stanislaus County, CA.
- **Sample type**: Grab samples of stormwater runoff collected from the base of the asphalt grindings pile and the native soil pile.
- **Analytes tested**: The report indicates testing for metals (including arsenic, barium, cadmium, chromium, lead, selenium, silver, and mercury), pH, total dissolved solids (TDS), and total suspended solids (TSS). Complete analyte lists are not provided in the record excerpt.
- **Results**: Specific concentration values are not included in the source documents provided. The report notes that all detected constituents were below applicable California Title 22 drinking water standards (Maximum Contaminant Levels, MCLs) and DTSC human health screening levels for residential use.
- **Exceedances**: No exceedances of applicable thresholds were documented in this sampling event.
- **Comparison**: Results from the stormwater samples were compared to both MCLs and DTSC preliminary remediation goals (PRGs), with no exceedances reported.

## Regulatory Status
