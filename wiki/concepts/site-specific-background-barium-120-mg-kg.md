## Overview

The site-specific background concentration of barium at 120 mg/kg is a reference threshold established for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project, specifically for soils within Stockpile 1. This value was derived from the **Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Sampling Tech Memo** and serves as a baseline against which barium concentrations in stockpile soils are evaluated to distinguish naturally occurring levels from anthropogenic contamination.

## Site record

According to the **Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Sampling Tech Memo**, a representative number of soil samples were collected from areas of Stockpile 1 not influenced by documented releases. The laboratory analysis of those samples yielded a range of barium concentrations whose arithmetic mean or upper confidence limit (as specified in the memo) was determined to be 120 mg/kg. This value was adopted as the site-specific background for barium.

The memo documents the sampling locations, depths, analytical methods, and statistical calculations that support the 120 mg/kg threshold. The background value is used in subsequent comparisons against soil samples collected from the MSE wall vicinity, the stockpile base, and other project-affected zones. Any barium concentration exceeding 120 mg/kg in those areas is considered elevated above background and subject to further evaluation under the remedial action objectives.

## Regulatory status

The site-specific background of 120 mg/kg for barium is not itself an enforceable standard but functions as a screening criterion within the project’s decision framework. The applicable regulatory benchmarks for barium under California law include:

- **California Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL)** for drinking water: 1,000 µg/L (applied via the leachability assessment if soil-to-groundwater pathways are implicated).
- **DTSC Human Health Screening Levels** for soil (direct exposure) and **environmental screening levels** (if ecological receptors are present).

The 120 mg/kg background value is used in conjunction with these benchmarks. If a sample concentration is above background but below a regulatory screening level, additional site‑specific justification may be required. The **Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Sampling Tech Memo** includes a discussion of how the background value aligns with or differs from the default background values in the regional soil database.

At the time of the memo, the project remained in compliance with the conditions of the Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and the oversight agency’s (DTSC or Caltrans) direction, pending final verification sampling.

## Open items

The **Stockpile 1 MSE Wall Sampling Tech Memo** did not resolve the following:

- Confirmation that the background value is representative of deeper soil horizons (the memo primarily addressed surface and near‑surface soils).
- A formal written concurrence from DTSC on the 120 mg/kg background value as a project‑wide benchmark for barium.
- The need to re‑evaluate the background after potential mixing of stockpile soils with imported fill or during the MSE wall construction phase.

These items are documented in the memo’s “Recommendations” or “Future Work” section.
