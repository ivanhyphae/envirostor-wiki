## Overview

The Stormwater SAP Addendum February 2013 is a supplemental sampling and analysis plan that amended the baseline stormwater monitoring program for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation site along State Route 132 in Stanislaus County. Prepared specifically for the contaminated soil stockpiles generated during roadway construction, the addendum introduced additional stormwater sampling locations, revised analyte lists, and modified trigger levels to account for the distinctive contaminant signature of the stockpile materials (primarily arsenic, lead, and barium at elevated concentrations). The addendum was submitted to the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) as part of the site’s ongoing remedial oversight and was reviewed and conditionally approved before implementation.

## Site record

- **Document title**: Stormwater SAP Addendum, February 2013
- **Prepared by**: Caltrans (authoring entity per project file)
- **Submittal to**: DTSC
- **Site context**: Addressed stormwater runoff and direct precipitation contact with approximately 8,500 cubic yards of arsenic‑ and metals‑contaminated soil stockpiled near SR 132.
- **Key modifications**: 
  - Added sampling stations at downgradient perimeter of each stockpile footprint (where previous SAP had only baseline and compliance points further removed).
  - Specified collection of first‑flush grab samples and flow‑weighted composites for each qualifying storm event.
  - Revised analyte list to include total and dissolved arsenic, lead, barium, and chromium (matching the contamination profile from confirmatory soil sampling).
  - Established action levels: total arsenic in stormwater = 50 µg/L (interim, based on site‑specific background); total lead = 15 µg/L (based on California Toxics Rule trigger); total barium = 1,000 µg/L (based on federal MCL).
- **Implementation timeline**: Addendum was effective for the 2013–2014 wet season; sampling results under the addendum were reported in the Annual Stormwater Monitoring Report (2014).

## Regulatory status

- **Oversight agency**: DTSC (under California Health and Safety Code, Chapter 6.8)
- **Applicable standards referenced in addendum**:
  - Federal MCL for barium: 1,000 µg/L
  - California Toxics Rule (CTR) for lead: 15 µg/L (acute freshwater criterion)
  - Site‑specific interim action level for arsenic: 50 µg/L (based on up‑gradient background sampling from 2012)
- **Compliance outcome**: No exceedances of the addendum’s action levels were documented during the 2013–2014 wet season. DTSC accepted the addendum as a component of the site’s overall remedial monitoring program and did not require further modification for the subsequent wet season.

## Open items

- The addendum did not incorporate a chronic toxicity screening or bioassessment monitoring; DTSC’s approval letter noted this as a potential future requirement if stormwater quality trends changed. No such requirement has been triggered as of the most recent annual report.
- The addendum was superseded in 2016 when the site transitioned from active stockpile management to final cover and a post‑closure stormwater SAP. The 2013 Addendum remains in the administrative record as the governing document for the interim stockpile period.
