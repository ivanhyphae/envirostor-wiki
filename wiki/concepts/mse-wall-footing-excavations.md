## MSE Wall Footing Excavations

### Overview

The mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavations were identified as a potential source of clean fill material for capping purposes at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. However, evaluation determined that the upper portion of the soil from these excavations contained elevated [[barium]] concentrations, rendering it unsuitable for use as clean cover fill.

### Site Record

The MSE wall footing excavations were approximately five feet deep and located along the southern boundaries of [[bcs-stockpiles-1-and-2]]. Geocon-consultants evaluated the material from these excavations along the southern boundary of [[stockpile-1]] following the excavation of the southern slope [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) material. It was determined that the upper foot of the planned wall footing excavation soil contained elevated barium concentrations that would not be suitable for use as clean cover fill material. This determination was documented in a Geocon April 6, 2020 Technical Memorandum.

### Regulatory Status

The elevated barium concentrations found in the upper foot of the MSE wall footing excavation soil meant this material was not suitable for use as [[clean-soil-cover]]. The Remedial-Design-Implementation-Plan (RDIP) Variance for the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles addresses the identified sources of clean cover fill materials needed to properly cap consolidated barium-containing soil (BCS) within Stockpiles 1 and 2. The surface of the completed Stockpile 1 and 2 BCS and [[stockpile-consolidation]] zones are to be capped with a minimum 12-inch-thick clean fill material, consisting of approved imported fill and/or minimum 4-inch-thick aggregate base (AB) materials, pending placement of structural pavement section, hardscape, and/or additional clean embankment fill.
