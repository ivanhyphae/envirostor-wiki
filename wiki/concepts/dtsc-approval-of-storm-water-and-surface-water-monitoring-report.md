## DTSC Approval of Storm Water and Surface Water Monitoring Report

### Overview
This section details the approval by the [[dtsc]] of the Storm Water and Surface Water Monitoring Report for the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site.

### Site Record
On January 24, 2025, the DTSC issued a letter responding to the 2025 Groundwater Monitoring Report. This letter also addressed the Storm Water and Surface Water Monitoring Report. The DTSC conditionally approved the Storm Water and Surface Water Monitoring Report.

### Regulatory Status
The DTSC conditionally approved the Storm Water and Surface Water Monitoring Report.
