## Overview

Perry Myers is an individual associated with the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) who has been involved in the review and approval of sampling plans and technical memorandums for the [[caltrans]] SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. His involvement is primarily documented through email correspondence where he is copied on communications between [[caltrans]] representatives, such as [[richard-stewart]], and DTSC representatives, such as [[dean-wright]]. Myers' role appears to be in providing DTSC's regulatory oversight and approval for the proposed sampling and characterization of soil materials intended for use as clean fill or embankment construction.

## Site Record

Perry Myers was copied on an email from Dean Wright of DTSC to Richard Stewart of Caltrans on April 7, 2021, which served as DTSC's approval of the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan.

On March 31, 2020, Myers was copied on an email from Richard Stewart of Caltrans to Dean Wright of DTSC, forwarding the "Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan" for approval.

Myers was also copied on an email from Dean Wright of DTSC to Richard Stewart of Caltrans on November 6, 2020, accepting a Technical Memorandum regarding the characterization of excavated Bent 2 soils for use as clean cap material.

Additionally, Myers was copied on emails from Dean Wright of DTSC to Richard Stewart of Caltrans on April 7, 2020, and June 22, 2020, approving Technical Memorandums related to Stockpile 1 and Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing sampling, respectively. and

On June 11, 2020, Myers was copied on an email from Richard Stewart of Caltrans to Dean Wright of DTSC, forwarding the "SR 132 Basin 5 Clean Fill Sampling Plan" for review and approval.

## Regulatory Status

Perry Myers, in his capacity at the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]], has been involved in the approval of sampling plans and technical memorandums that determine the suitability of various soil materials for use as [[clean-fill]], capping material, or embankment fill on the SR 132 project. These approvals are critical for the project's compliance with regulatory requirements set forth by DTSC and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]].
