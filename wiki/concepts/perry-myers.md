Perry Myers is an individual at the [[dtsc]] who reviewed and approved technical memorandums and sampling plans related to the remediation of soil stockpiles at the [[caltrans]] SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. He was copied on communications from [[richard-stewart]] of [[caltrans]] and [[dean-wright]] of [[dtsc]], indicating his involvement in the decision-making process for soil characterization and reuse.

## Soil Sampling Plan and Technical Memorandum Reviews

Perry Myers was copied on an email from [[richard-stewart]] on March 31, 2020, regarding the "Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan." This plan, submitted by [[geocon-consultants-inc]], proposed sampling MSE footing soils for characterization and [[dtsc]] classification for on-site reuse. The plan detailed collecting discrete soil samples from one-foot intervals within the MSE wall footing excavation, to a depth of approximately 4 to 5 feet, for analysis of barium and lead with a 48-hour turnaround time. The analytical data were to be compared against site-specific background levels, RDIP BCS removal thresholds, and construction worker, residential, and commercial ESLs.

He was also copied on an email from [[richard-stewart]] on April 6, 2020, concerning the "Stockpile 1 Wall Footing Sampling Tech Memo." This memo described sampling along the MSE wall footing excavation area for Stockpile 1 and its laboratory results.

Additionally, Perry Myers was copied on an email from [[richard-stewart]] on June 18, 2020, regarding a revised technical memo for "Stockpile 2 Wall Footing Tech Memo." This memo detailed sampling along the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation area and its laboratory results.

On April 5, 2021, Perry Myers was copied on an email from [[richard-stewart]] to [[dean-wright]] regarding the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan, which outlined proposed sampling and analysis for soil to be used as clean fill.

## Regulatory Acceptance of Deliverables

Perry Myers, in his capacity at the [[dtsc]], was involved in the review and approval of technical memorandums and sampling plans. Specifically, he was copied on emails from [[dean-wright]] of [[dtsc]] that conveyed official acceptance of these documents. On April 7, 2020, [[dean-wright]] communicated to [[richard-stewart]] that, with acceptance of a specific comment regarding the use of MSE wall footing soil, the email served as [[dtsc]]'s official acceptance of the "Stockpile 1 Wall Footing Sampling Tech Memo."

On June 22, 2020, [[dean-wright]] informed [[richard-stewart]] that the email served as [[dtsc]]'s official acceptance of the revised "Stockpile 2 Wall Footing Tech Memo," which detailed sampling along the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation area and its laboratory results.

On November 6, 2020, [[dean-wright]] also confirmed [[dtsc]]'s acceptance of the Technical Memorandum regarding the use of Bent 2 excavation soil as clean cap material, with Perry Myers copied on the communication.

On April 22, 2021, [[dean-wright]] confirmed [[dtsc]]'s acceptance of the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum, which described sampling and analysis to characterize the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill area, with Perry Myers copied on the email.
