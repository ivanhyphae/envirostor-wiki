# Caltrans Response Letter March 23 2012

This letter is Caltrans's written response, dated March 23, 2012, addressing site-specific background levels for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles along SR 132. It sits in the record as the Caltrans-side counterpart to prior DTSC correspondence on background concentrations, and marks a point in the back-and-forth between the two agencies over what threshold values should govern evaluation of the stockpiled soil.

## Content and context

The letter responds to an earlier DTSC communication concerning site-specific background levels — the values proposed as the baseline against which stockpile soil constituents would be compared, rather than generic screening levels. Its date, March 23, 2012, places it within the early background-level negotiation phase of the case, prior to the later feasibility study work.

The available source material does not set out the specific background values, constituents, or conditions discussed in the letter's text.

## Significance

This letter is the documented instance of Caltrans replying directly to DTSC on the background-levels question, establishing that the two agencies were in active correspondence on this point as of March 2012. Because the specifics of Caltrans's position are not present in the available material, this article cannot state whether the letter accepted, disputed, or proposed alternative values, nor whether DTSC's subsequent handling of site-specific background levels responded to anything raised here.
