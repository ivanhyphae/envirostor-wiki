The [[modesto-soil-stockpiles-project]] involved the preparation of an Environmental Impact Report (EIR) and an Environmental Assessment (EA) to evaluate the potential environmental impacts of the remediation activities. These documents are key components of the [[california-environmental-quality-act]] (CEQA) process, which requires state and local agencies to consider the environmental consequences of their actions. The EIR/EA for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project was prepared to inform decision-making and provide opportunities for public review and comment.

## Site record

The EIR/EA process for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project involved the preparation of specific documents. A Notice of Determination (NOD) was issued, indicating that an EIR had been completed and certified. The NOD was filed with the State Clearinghouse on March 19, 2018. {{quote: "Notice of Determination"}} The project was subject to the requirements of the [[public-resources-code]].

## Regulatory status

The EIR/EA process is a regulatory requirement under CEQA. The certification of the EIR and the subsequent filing of the NOD signify that the environmental review process for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles project has reached a specific stage. The project's compliance with CEQA, as documented by the EIR/EA, is a prerequisite for proceeding with the remediation activities. The outcome of the EIR/EA process, such as a finding of "no significant effect on the environment," would dictate subsequent regulatory steps.
