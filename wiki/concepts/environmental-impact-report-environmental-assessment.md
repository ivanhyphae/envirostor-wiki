The Final Environmental Impact Report/Environmental Assessment (FEIR/EA) for the SR 132 West Expressway Project, dated March 2, 2018, is the environmental compliance document under which Caltrans evaluated the project — including its handling of the soil stockpiles that are the subject of the broader remediation record — and cleared it for construction under both the California Environmental Quality Act and the National Environmental Policy Act. It is a joint document: Caltrans acted as lead agency under CEQA and, under its NEPA assignment authority, as the federal lead agency as well. The FEIR/EA and its appendices (Appendices A–G and H–K) constitute the principal record tying the project's design, right-of-way, and environmental findings together, and they are the source that the later Statement of Fact and remedial-action-plan record for the stockpiles builds upon.

## Structure of the document

The FEIR/EA was issued in three parts: a main document (Final Main Doc, dated March 2, 2018) and two appendix volumes — Appendices A–G and Appendices H–K — filed in reduced size alongside it. The main document carries the project description, environmental analysis, and agency findings; the appendices carry the supporting technical studies, including the hazardous waste and soil/groundwater material that underlies the stockpile remediation history addressed elsewhere in this case file.

## Relationship to the stockpile remediation record

The soil stockpiles at the center of this case file were generated by, and are addressed in connection with, the SR 132 West Expressway Project that the FEIR/EA cleared. The FEIR/EA is the environmental-review instrument for the roadway project itself; the remedial-action-plan and monitoring record for the stockpiles (developed with Geocon Consultants, Inc. and reviewed by DTSC) is a separate, later track of work that responds to conditions found in the stockpiled soil rather than to the FEIR/EA's own findings. The Statement of Fact dated March 15, 2018, filed close in time to the FEIR/EA's March 2, 2018 finalization, is part of the same project record but addresses the factual basis for the stockpile-related determinations rather than the CEQA/NEPA analysis itself.

## Significance

For reviewers, the FEIR/EA's importance is that it is the document of record establishing the project's environmental clearance and description — the baseline against which any later change in scope, design, or remedial handling of the stockpiled soil must be measured. It does not itself resolve the soil contamination questions (thallium, arsenic, and other constituents) that are tracked separately through the remedial-action-plan and monitoring reports; those are downstream technical matters governed by DTSC review and the clean-soil-cover determination, not by the FEIR/EA's CEQA/NEPA findings.
