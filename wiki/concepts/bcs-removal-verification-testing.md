## Overview
[[bcs-removal-verification-testing]] refers to the process of confirming that [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) has been removed from specific areas of a stockpile to meet cleanup levels established in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP). This testing is a critical step before the placement of [[clean-soil-cover]]. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) reviews and approves the technical memorandums documenting these verification results.

## Site Record

### Stockpile 2
On March 10, 2020, the DTSC approved the Technical Memorandum dated March 9, 2020, which documented the removal of BCS from the east and west ends of Stockpile 2. The confirmation soil sampling results indicated that these areas met the RDIP verification thresholds and were suitable for the placement of clean embankment fill. {{quote: "The sampling results indicate that the east and west ends of Stockpile 2 have met the RDIP verification thresholds and DTSC agrees that these areas are now suitable for the placement of clean embankment fill."}} The DTSC's acceptance of this memo was communicated via email on March 10, 2020. {{quote: "This e‐mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the Memo."}}

### Stockpile 3
On March 18, 2020, the DTSC reviewed and accepted the Technical Memorandum dated March 13, 2020, concerning the removal of BCS from Stockpile 3. The confirmation soil sampling results demonstrated that the BCS had been removed and the remaining soil met the RDIP verification thresholds. {{quote: "The sampling results indicate that the Stockpile 3 BCS has been removed and that the remaining soil meets the RDIP verification thresholds."}} This acceptance meant no further RDIP monitoring requirements were necessary for Stockpile 3, and the area was deemed suitable for clean embankment fill. {{quote: "DTSC agrees that no further BCS RDIP monitoring requirements are necessary and this areas is now suitable for clean embankment fill."}} The DTSC's acceptance was communicated via email on March 18, 2020. {{quote: "This e‐mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the Memo."}}

## Regulatory Status

The [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) established the cleanup levels and verification thresholds for BCS removal. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) is the regulatory agency responsible for reviewing and approving the verification testing results.

On March 10, 2020, DTSC approved the verification testing for Stockpile 2, confirming it met the RDIP thresholds. {{quote: "DTSC agrees that these areas are now suitable for the placement of clean embankment fill."}}

On March 18, 2020, DTSC approved the verification testing for Stockpile 3, confirming BCS removal and that remaining soil met RDIP thresholds. {{quote: "DTSC agrees that no further BCS RDIP monitoring requirements are necessary and this areas is now suitable for clean embankment fill."}}

## Open Items

Following the approval of Stockpile 3 BCS removal on March 18, 2020, [[caltrans]] requested approval to begin placing a minimum 12-inch clean soil cover and/or a minimum 4-inch thick layer of aggregate base material (AB) on the surfaces of the BCS consolidation zones of Stockpiles 1 and 2. {{quote: "Awaiting your approval to begin the placement of the minimum 12‐inch clean soil cover and/or a minimum 4‐inch thick layer of aggregate base material (AB) on the surfaces of the BCS consolidation zones (including temporary slopes) of Stockpiles 1 and 2."}}
