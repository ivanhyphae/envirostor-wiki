[[bcs-removal-verification-testing]] is the process used to confirm that [[barium-containing-soil]] (BCS) has been removed from designated areas within stockpiles to meet the cleanup levels specified in the [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP). This verification is a prerequisite for the placement of [[clean-soil-cover]]. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) is responsible for reviewing and approving the technical memorandums that document the results of this testing.

## Site Record

### Stockpile 2
On March 10, 2020, the DTSC approved the Technical Memorandum dated March 9, 2020, which documented the removal of BCS from the east and west ends of Stockpile 2. The confirmation soil sampling results presented in the memo indicated that these areas met the RDIP verification thresholds and were suitable for the placement of clean embankment fill. The DTSC communicated its acceptance of this memorandum via email on March 10, 2020.

### Stockpile 3
On March 18, 2020, the DTSC reviewed and accepted the Technical Memorandum dated March 13, 2020, concerning the removal of BCS from Stockpile 3. The confirmation soil sampling results demonstrated that the BCS had been removed and the remaining soil met the RDIP verification thresholds. This acceptance signified that no further RDIP monitoring requirements were necessary for Stockpile 3, and the area was deemed suitable for clean embankment fill. The DTSC's acceptance was conveyed via email on March 18, 2020.

## Regulatory Status

The [[remedial-design-implementation-plan]] (RDIP) established the cleanup levels and verification thresholds for BCS removal. The [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) is the regulatory agency responsible for reviewing and approving the verification testing results.

On March 10, 2020, DTSC approved the verification testing for Stockpile 2, confirming that the specified areas met the RDIP thresholds.

On March 18, 2020, DTSC approved the verification testing for Stockpile 3, confirming BCS removal and that the remaining soil met RDIP thresholds.

## Open Items

Following the approval of Stockpile 3 BCS removal on March 18, 2020, [[caltrans]] requested approval to begin placing a minimum 12-inch clean soil cover and/or a minimum 4-inch thick layer of aggregate base material (AB) on the surfaces of the BCS consolidation zones of Stockpiles 1 and 2.
