Dean Wright is a Project Manager with the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) who has been involved in the review and approval of technical memorandums and sampling plans related to the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation case file. He has communicated with [[richard-stewart]] of Caltrans and [[steven-meeks]] of the Water Board on these matters.

## Regulatory Actions and Approvals

Dean Wright, on behalf of DTSC, has officially accepted several technical memorandums and sampling plans, signifying regulatory approval for specific soil management and reuse strategies within the project. These decisions were often made in conjunction with the Water Board.

*   **April 7, 2020:** DTSC, through Dean Wright, accepted the Technical Memorandum dated April 6, 2020, regarding Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing sampling. This acceptance was contingent on the understanding that MSE wall footing soil, with a 95% Upper Confidence Limit above the maximum background of 120 mg/kg for barium, must be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill, rather than as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2.
*   **June 22, 2020:** DTSC, through Dean Wright, accepted the revised Technical Memorandum dated June 18, 2020, concerning Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing excavation. DTSC and the Water Board agreed that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones, while soil below that depth is suitable as embankment fill or capping material. This material below 3 feet bgs was considered part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan.
*   **November 6, 2020:** DTSC, through Dean Wright, accepted the Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020, regarding the use of Bent 2 excavation soil as clean cap material. While the soil did not meet clean cap criteria per the April 9, 2020 Variance Request, DTSC and the Water Board agreed it could be placed on top of Stockpile 2 at a thickness of 6 inches or less, provided it was covered with clean base material and roadway construction materials. This acceptance also stipulated the importance of following the rainy season surface water sampling effort and erosion control measures outlined in the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020.
*   **April 7, 2021:** DTSC, through Dean Wright, approved the Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan, received on April 5, 2021, which outlines proposed sampling and analysis for soil to be used as clean fill.
*   **April 22, 2021:** DTSC, through Dean Wright, accepted the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021. The agencies agreed that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria.

Dean Wright was also a recipient of an email on March 31, 2020, from [[richard-stewart]] requesting approval for the proposed sampling of MSE footing soils for Stockpiles 1 and 2.
