Dean Wright, PG, served as the [[dtsc]] Project Manager for the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles site through the bulk of the remediation record, corresponding with [[caltrans]]'s [[richard-stewart]] to review sampling plans and technical memoranda for [[stockpile-1]], [[stockpile-2]], and related borrow and excavation areas. He issued DTSC's written acceptance on each of the technical deliverables in the record, several of which imposed placement conditions rather than outright approving proposed reuse of soil as clean cap material. His role transferred to Arielle Mcleskey in December 2024.

## Decisions on stockpile and MSE wall footing soils

Wright reviewed and acted on a series of characterization memoranda for soil excavated during construction of the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) walls at Stockpiles 1 and 2, each turning on comparison to the site's maximum background barium concentration of 120 mg/kg (as established in the March 31, 2020 Stockpiles 1 and 2 MSE Wall Sampling Plan submitted by Geocon Consultants, Inc.'s John Juhrend).

- **Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing** (accepted April 7, 2020, responding to a April 6, 2020 tech memo): Wright found the sampled soil's 95% Upper Confidence Limit exceeded the 120 mg/kg background maximum, and noted the footing excavation fell outside the borrow area designated in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP). He required the material be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill — rejecting Caltrans/Geocon's proposal to use it as stockpile capping material.
- **Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing** (accepted June 22, 2020, responding to a June 18, 2020 revised tech memo): Wright accepted a split disposition by depth — soil above 3 feet bgs required containment zone placement, while soil below 3 feet bgs was acceptable as embankment fill or capping material. He also determined that this deeper material was already covered by the April 9, 2020 Variance Request to the RDIP, so no separate variance was needed.
- **Bent 2 excavation soil** (accepted November 6, 2020, responding to an October 30, 2020 tech memo): Wright and the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board ([[central-valley-rwqcb]]) found the sample results did not meet the clean cap criteria set by the April 9, 2020 Variance Request, but permitted a limited exception — the soil could be placed atop Stockpile 2 at 6 inches or less if covered by clean base material and the roadway asphalt/concrete. Because project schedule meant the Bent 2 soil could be temporarily exposed over the winter, Wright conditioned this acceptance on strict adherence to the rainy-season surface water sampling and erosion controls in the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020.

## Carpenter Shoofly borrow area approvals

Wright separately approved the Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan on April 7, 2021, after DTSC and the Water Board reviewed a revised version received April 5, 2021, finding it consistent with prior borrow area sampling and raising no comments. He then accepted the resulting Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum, dated April 12, 2021, on April 22, 2021, concluding that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria — clearing this borrow source for use as clean fill, in contrast to the MSE wall footing and Bent 2 soils, which did not qualify.

## Project manager transition

On December 11, 2024, Wright notified Caltrans's Adam Inman that the DTSC PM role for the site had changed, with Arielle Mcleskey of DTSC's Sacramento office assuming responsibility for the project and all further correspondence to be directed to her.
