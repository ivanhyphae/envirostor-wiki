## Dean Wright

Dean Wright is a Project Manager at the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). Within the context of the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] remediation project (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA), Wright served as a primary point of contact for DTSC, reviewing and approving various technical documents, sampling plans, and providing guidance on regulatory matters. He frequently communicated with [[richard-stewart]] of Caltrans and other project stakeholders.

## Site record

Wright's documented involvement spans several key stages of the project, including the approval of sampling plans and technical memorandums related to clean fill, stockpiles, and excavated soils.

On February 13, 2020, Wright communicated DTSC's approval condition for a backfill sampling plan: that composite samples be taken as 3 samples instead of 4. {{quote: "Perry didn't have any additional comments on the backfill sampling plan so our only request remains what I mentioned yesterday."}} He also participated in scheduling a conference call for February 13, 2020, with Caltrans and Bay Cities. {{quote: "The conference call details were: (877) 829-8910, Passcode 8536140."}} Wright also searched DTSC's records for a burial location report but was unable to locate it, requesting a copy from [[richard-stewart]].

On April 7, 2021, Wright, on behalf of DTSC and the Waterboard, approved the revised Carpenter Shoofly Area Clean Fill Sampling Plan. {{quote: "The Plan is consistent with previous borrow area sampling efforts and there are no comments. This e-mail serves as DTSCs approval of the Plan."}} He later accepted the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum dated April 12, 2021, stating that the composite soil sample results were consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and met the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria. {{quote: "This e-mail serves as DTSCs acceptance of the Memo."}}

Wright accepted the Stockpile 1 Wall Footing Sampling Tech Memo on April 7, 2020, with a condition: {{quote: "the MSE wall footing falls outside of the designated borrow area described in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, this material must be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill."}}

On June 22, 2020, Wright accepted the revised Stockpile 2 Wall Footing Tech Memo. He stated that soil down to 3 feet below ground surface (bgs) must be placed in designated containment zones, but soil below that depth is suitable as embankment fill or capping material. {{quote: "The material below 3 feet bgs along the Stockpile 2 MSE wall footing is also considered a part of the April 9, 2020 Variance Request (VR) to the Remedial Design Implementation Plan. Thus, another VR is not necessary before utilizing this soil."}}

Wright accepted the revised Soil Management Plan (SMP) for the two proposed retention basins on February 11, 2021, with two minor comments: {{quote: "The SMP indicates that soil with Total Petroleum Hydrocarbons (TPH) above screening levels will be segregated and covered. In addition, it is requested that this material be labeled until it is transported offsite."}} and {{quote: "The SMP does not discuss the actions that will be taken should TPH impacted soil still be present when the maximum depth of the basins is achieved. Should this scenario occur, Caltrans should be alerted and consultation with the WB and DTSC should take place to determine appropriate next steps."}}

Wright was also identified as a contact for DTSC's annual cost estimates for oversight. For the Fiscal Year 2022-2023, he was listed as the contact person for questions regarding the cost estimate. {{quote: "If you have any questions regarding the cost estimate, please contact Dean Wright at (916) 255-3591 or via email at Dean.Wright@dtsc.ca.gov."}} Similarly, for the Fiscal Year 2023-2024 cost estimate, he was listed as the contact. {{quote: "If you have any questions regarding the cost estimate, please contact Dean Wright at (916) 255-3591 or via electronic mail at Dean.Wright@dtsc.ca.gov."}}

On December 11, 2024, Wright notified [[adam-inman]] of Caltrans that [[arielle-mcleskey]] would be the new DTSC Project Manager for the SR 132 Modesto Soil Stockpiles project.

## Regulatory status

Wright's approvals signify DTSC's regulatory acceptance of various project plans and technical findings. For example, his acceptance of the Carpenter Shoofly Clean Fill Technical Memorandum indicated that the soil sample results met the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria. {{quote: "The agencies agree that the composite soil sample results are consistent with naturally occurring background concentrations and meet the Barium Containing Soil clean cap criteria."}} His acceptance of the revised SMP on February 11, 2021, indicated that the plan addressed DTSC and Water Board comments, with minor field-implementable revisions suggested.

## Open items

As of February 13, 2020, a burial location report referenced by [[richard-stewart]] remained unlocated in DTSC's records. Wright requested a copy from Caltrans, but its receipt was not documented in the provided source material.
