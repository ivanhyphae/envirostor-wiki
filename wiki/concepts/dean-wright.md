Dean Wright is a project manager at the California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) who served as the DTSC's primary point of contact for field decisions and sampling plan approvals related to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles remediation project (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). He coordinated with Caltrans staff, including [[richard-stewart]] and [[jennifer-lugo]].

## Site record

During February 2020, Wright's documented involvement focused on the [[borrow-area-sampling-plan]] for clean backfill material. On February 12, 2020, he forwarded a work notice mailing list from his PPS to [[richard-stewart]], initiating coordination on community notification procedures for the soil stockpile remediation.

On February 13, 2020, Wright communicated DTSC's single condition for approving the backfill sampling plan: that composite samples be taken as 3 samples instead of 4. This instruction was given directly to [[richard-stewart]] and [[jennifer-lugo]]. Perry Myers of DTSC had reviewed the plan and had no additional comments. Wright stated, "Perry didn't have any additional comments on the backfill sampling plan so our only request remains what I mentioned yesterday."

Wright also participated in scheduling a conference call for February 13, 2020, with Caltrans and Bay Cities (the contractor). The status of the sampling plan and work notification were expected agenda items. The conference call details were: (877) 829-8910, Passcode 8536140.

In an email chain dated February 12–13, 2020, Wright searched DTSC's EnviroStor database and electronic files from his predecessor, Randy, for a burial location report mentioned by Stewart. He was unable to locate it and requested a copy from Stewart for DTSC's records and to forward to Bay Cities as an example document.

## Regulatory status

Wright's February 13, 2020 approval of the backfill sampling plan, with the sole condition of compositing 3 samples instead of 4, represents DTSC's regulatory authorization for the sampling methodology. No other conditions, exceedances, or compliance issues were documented in the reviewed record.

## Open items

As of February 13, 2020, the burial location report referenced by [[richard-stewart]] remained unlocated in DTSC's records. Wright requested a copy from Caltrans, but whether it was received is not documented in the available source material.
