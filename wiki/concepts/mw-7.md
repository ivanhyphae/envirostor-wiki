# MW-7

## Overview
Monitoring well MW-7 is one of ten groundwater monitoring wells at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). It was sampled as part of the [[september-2013-sampling-event]] to characterize groundwater geochemistry and assess potential impacts from soil stockpiling activities. Groundwater from MW-7 exhibits a calcium-dominant bicarbonate water type, consistent with several other site wells including [[mw-1]], [[mw-2]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-9]], and MW-10.

## Site record
During the September 2013 sampling event, MW-7 was characterized through general minerals analysis and Stiff diagram construction. The Stiff diagrams, included in Appendix C of the September 2013 groundwater monitoring report (Geocon Consultants, Inc., Project No. S9525-01-44C, October 2013), show that groundwater from MW-7 is bicarbonate (HCO₃) dominant with a calcium-dominant cation composition. The well is classified as "Calcium Dominant" on the Stiff diagram legend, visually represented with an orange polygon. The MW-7 Stiff diagram appears on the same figure page as MW-8 (Figure, Appendix C, page 140), where MW-7 is specifically labeled as "CALCIUM DOMINANT" and MW-8 as "SODIUM DOMINANT."

The report documents that MW-7, along with MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-9, and MW-10, has consistently been reported as calcium-dominant during groundwater sampling from March 2012 through September 2013. This contrasts with wells MW-3, MW-6, and MW-8, which have been consistently sodium-dominant during the same period.

Notably, the September 2013 report does not list MW-7 among the wells where nitrate as nitrogen equaled or exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/L, nor among those where TDS exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L. The wells with nitrate exceedances were specifically identified as [[mw-1]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-5]], [[mw-6]], and MW-10. The wells with TDS exceedances were the same five wells. This places MW-7 in the group of wells where neither nitrate nor TDS concentrations triggered regulatory concern during this monitoring event.

Sulfide was reported at MW-3 (0.011 mg/L) and MW-1 (0.28 mg/L), but no sulfide result is specifically attributed to MW-7 in the report body. Laboratory analytical reports for MW-7 (Lab ID: 1302710-04) confirm the analyses performed, including dissolved metals by ICP-MS (EPA 6020), dissolved mercury by AA cold vapor (EPA 7470A), and cation-anion balance calculations. The laboratory reported sulfide as "ND" (not detected) for MW-7.

No dissolved metals concentrations in the MW-7 sample exceeded numeric water quality threshold values. Lead was not reported at or above the practical quantitation limit (PQL) of 1.0 µg/L in any of the September 2013 groundwater samples, including MW-7. Barium and strontium concentrations in MW-7, as in other site wells, remained significantly below numeric water quality thresholds and consistent with historical levels.

## Regulatory status
The September 2013 monitoring event did not identify any regulatory exceedances for dissolved metals in MW-7. General minerals parameters, including nitrate and TDS, did not exceed applicable California primary or secondary MCLs for this well. No specific regulatory actions or conditions are documented for MW-7.

## Open items
None documented specifically for MW-7. The September 2013 report concludes that groundwater monitoring should continue on a quarterly basis, with the next monitoring event scheduled for December 2013. No deviations, anomalies, or required follow-up actions are identified for this well.
