## MW-7

### Overview
Monitoring well MW-7 is one of ten groundwater monitoring wells at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). It was sampled as part of the [[september-2013-sampling-event]] and june-2014-sampling-event to characterize groundwater geochemistry and assess potential impacts from soil stockpiling activities. Groundwater from MW-7 exhibits a calcium-dominant bicarbonate water type, consistent with several other site wells.

### Site record
During the September 2013 sampling event, MW-7 was characterized through general minerals analysis and Stiff diagram construction. The Stiff diagrams, included in Appendix C of the September 2013 groundwater monitoring report (Geocon Consultants, Inc., Project No. S9525-01-44C, October 2013), show that groundwater from MW-7 is bicarbonate (HCO₃) dominant with a calcium-dominant cation composition. The well is classified as "Calcium Dominant" on the Stiff diagram legend, visually represented with an orange polygon. The MW-7 Stiff diagram appears on the same figure page as MW-8 (Figure, Appendix C, page 140 of the September 2013 report), where MW-7 is specifically labeled as "CALCIUM DOMINANT."

The report documents that MW-7, along with MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-9, and MW-10, has consistently been reported as calcium-dominant during groundwater sampling from March 2012 through September 2013. This contrasts with wells MW-3, MW-6, and MW-8, which have been consistently sodium-dominant during the same period.

Notably, the September 2013 report does not list MW-7 among the wells where nitrate as nitrogen equaled or exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/L, nor among those where TDS exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/L. The wells with nitrate exceedances were specifically identified as [[mw-1]], [[mw-4]], [[mw-5]], [[mw-6]], and MW-10. The wells with TDS exceedances were the same five wells. This places MW-7 in the group of wells where neither nitrate nor TDS concentrations triggered regulatory concern during this monitoring event.

Sulfide was reported at MW-3 (0.011 mg/L) and MW-1 (0.28 mg/L) in the September 2013 sampling event, but no sulfide result is specifically attributed to MW-7 in the report body. Laboratory analytical reports for MW-7 (Lab ID: 1302710-04) confirm the analyses performed, including dissolved metals by ICP-MS (EPA 6020), dissolved mercury by AA cold vapor (EPA 7470A), and cation-anion balance calculations. The laboratory reported sulfide as "ND" (not detected) for MW-7.

No dissolved metals concentrations in the MW-7 sample exceeded numeric water quality threshold values during the September 2013 sampling event. Lead was not reported at or above the practical quantitation limit (PQL) of 1.0 µg/L in any of the September 2013 groundwater samples, including MW-7.

During the June 2014 sampling event, Stiff diagrams constructed using site groundwater data also show that groundwater sampled in MW-7 is bicarbonate (HCO₃) dominant with a calcium-dominant composition. The MW-7 Stiff diagram is shown in Appendix C of the June 2014 groundwater monitoring report (Geocon Consultants, Inc., Project No. S9800-01-17, August 2014), appearing on the same figure page as MW-8 (Figure, Appendix C, page 162). The diagram for MW-7 is colored orange, indicating "CALCIUM DOMINANT."

The June 2014 report confirms that MW-7, along with MW-1, MW-2, MW-4, MW-9, and MW-10, has consistently been reported as calcium-dominant from March 2012 through June 2014. This contrasts with wells MW-3, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-8, which were reported as sodium-dominant in June 2014.

Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in June 2014 ranged from 2.6 mg/L (MW-3) to 29 mg/L (MW-5). TDS concentrations ranged from 320 mg/L (MW-3) to 680 mg/L (MW-5). The reported nitrate concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-5, MW-6, MW-9, and MW-10 equaled or exceeded the primary MCL for nitrate of 10 mg/L. The reported TDS concentrations for samples from MW-1, MW-4, MW-5, MW-6, and MW-10 exceeded the secondary MCL for TDS of 500 mg/L. MW-7 was not listed among the wells with nitrate or TDS exceedances in the June 2014 report.

Sulfide was reported for samples collected from wells MW-1, MW-8, and MW-10 at respective concentrations of 0.12, 0.017, and 0.023 mg/L in June 2014. The laboratory report for MW-7 (Lab ID: 1401676-03) indicates sulfide was "ND" (not detected).

None of the reported dissolved metals concentrations for the groundwater samples collected in June 2014 exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Barium and strontium concentrations in MW-7, as in other site wells, remained significantly below numeric water quality thresholds and consistent with historical levels.

### Regulatory status
The September 2013 and June 2014 monitoring events did not identify any regulatory exceedances for dissolved metals in MW-7. General minerals parameters, including nitrate and TDS, did not exceed applicable California primary or secondary MCLs for this well during these events. No specific regulatory actions or conditions are documented for MW-7.

### Open items
None documented specifically for MW-7. The June 2014 report concludes that groundwater monitoring should continue on a quarterly basis, with the next monitoring event scheduled for September 2014. No deviations, anomalies, or required follow-up actions are identified for this well.
