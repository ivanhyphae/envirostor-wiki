## Overview

MW-7 is a groundwater monitoring well located at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It has been sampled and analyzed as part of various groundwater monitoring events conducted by Geocon Consultants, Inc. for Caltrans.

## Site record

During the October 2016 groundwater monitoring event, MW-7 was found to be dry. In April 2017, depth to groundwater in MW-7 was measured at 40.73 feet below the top of the casing (TOC). Groundwater samples were collected from MW-7 in April 2017. In April 2019, depth to groundwater in MW-7 was measured at 46.99 feet below TOC.

In April 2017, Stiff diagrams indicated that groundwater sampled from MW-7 had a calcium-dominant composition. This calcium-dominant classification for MW-7 has been consistent across multiple sampling events, including May 2012, March 2013, January 2013, September 2013, November 2012, February 2014, June 2014, April 2015, and May 2016.

Nitrate as nitrogen concentrations in MW-7 have been reported as:
*   9.2 mg/l in May 2016.
*   7.0 mg/l in April 2015.
*   17 mg/l in April 2015, which exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/l.
*   13 mg/l in March 2013, which exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/l.
*   12 mg/l in May 2012, which met or exceeded the primary MCL of 10 mg/l.

Total Dissolved Solids (TDS) concentrations in MW-7 have been reported as:
*   510 mg/l in May 2016, which exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   490 mg/l in April 2015.
*   500 mg/l in March 2013, which met or exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.
*   340 mg/l in November 2012.
*   460 mg/l in January 2013.
*   590 mg/l in September 2013, which exceeded the secondary MCL of 500 mg/l.

Sulfide concentrations in MW-7 have been reported as:
*   0.036 mg/l in May 2016.
*   0.015 mg/l in April 2015.
*   0.18 mg/l in March 2013.
*   0.011 mg/l in September 2013.
*   0.52 mg/l in January 2013.

Dissolved metals concentrations in MW-7 have generally not exceeded their respective numeric water quality threshold values. Manganese was detected in MW-1 and MW-7 at concentrations exceeding water quality thresholds in March 2013. Lead was reported in MW-1 and MW-7 at 2.2 µg/l and 2.5 µg/l, respectively, in March 2013, both less than the numeric water quality threshold.

## Regulatory status

*   **Nitrate as nitrogen:** The primary MCL is 10 mg/l. Concentrations in MW-7 have exceeded this threshold in April 2015 (7.0 mg/l), March 2013 (13 mg/l), May 2012 (12 mg/l), September 2013 (12 mg/l), and January 2013 (12 mg/l).
*   **Total Dissolved Solids (TDS):** The secondary MCL is 500 mg/l. Concentrations in MW-7 have exceeded this threshold in May 2016 (510 mg/l) and March 2013 (500 mg/l).
