## Potential Groundwater Degradation

### Overview
The potential for groundwater degradation at the [[caltrans]] Modesto Soil Stockpiles site is a concern due to the presence of certain constituents within the soil stockpiles that could leach into underlying groundwater. The [[central-valley-rwqcb]] has established standards for groundwater quality that are relevant to this assessment.

### Site Record
The Final Feasibility Study Report for the Modesto Soil Stockpiles, dated June 2014, identified arsenic and chromium in the soil stockpiles at concentrations exceeding screening levels established for groundwater protection. The report states, "Arsenic and chromium were detected in soil stockpiles at concentrations exceeding the screening levels for groundwater protection."

### Regulatory Status
Groundwater quality at the site is regulated by the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]. The applicable standards for groundwater are the California Maximum Contaminant Levels (California MCLs). The Final Feasibility Study Report indicates that detected concentrations of arsenic and chromium in the soil stockpiles exceeded screening levels established for groundwater protection.
