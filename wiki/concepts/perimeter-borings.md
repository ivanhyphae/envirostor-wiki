## Overview

Perimeter borings are the primary subsurface investigation tool used to delineate the lateral extent of impacted soil and groundwater at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site (SR 132, Stanislaus County, CA). These borings are advanced along the site boundaries to verify that contamination from the stockpiles has not migrated off‑site, and to support determination of the remedial footprint (i.e., the minimum volume requiring excavation). The perimeter boring program is documented in the *Final Site‐Specific Investigation Report* (SSI Report, S9525‑06‑44, Rev. 0313).

## Site record

The SSI Report specifies that **a minimum of one perimeter boring per stockpile** was required, with additional borings placed where field conditions (e.g., visible staining, olfactory indications, elevated PID readings) suggested potential off‑site migration. For this project:
- **Stockpile A**: Three perimeter borings (A‑P1, A‑P2, A‑P3) were advanced along the east, south, and west boundaries. Maximum observed **total petroleum hydrocarbons (TPH)** in soil was **2,300 mg/kg** (diesel range) at A‑P2 (depth 2‑4 ft). Total chromium was **120 mg/kg** in the same interval.
- **Stockpile B**: Two perimeter borings (B‑P1, B‑P2) were placed north and east of the pile. **Arsenic** was detected at **85 mg/kg** in B‑P1 (0‑2 ft), exceeding the **DTSC residential screening level of 0.11 mg/kg** (direct‑contact) but consistent with regional background concentrations reported in the **Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB) background study** (2005).
- **Stockpile C**: Four perimeter borings (C‑P1 through C‑P4). **Lead** was detected at **760 mg/kg** in C‑P3 (2‑4 ft), exceeding the **MCL** of **15 µg/L** in the corresponding groundwater grab sample (collected from the same borehole at 25 ft bgs). Groundwater from C‑P3 also contained **barium at 760 mg/kg** (soil) and **1,000 µg/L** in groundwater (MCL: 2,000 µg/L).

All perimeter boreholes were logged continuously, with soil samples collected at 2‑ft intervals from 0 to 10 ft bgs, then at 5‑ft intervals to termination (typically 30–35 ft bgs). Where groundwater was encountered, a temporary well point was installed and a grab sample was collected. Per the SSI Report, data from perimeter borings showed that “no contaminant migration beyond the property boundary was confirmed” – though the report qualifies this statement by noting that **Stockpile C’s eastern perimeter boring (C‑P4) encountered a thin zone of elevated TPH (1,100 mg/kg) at 20–22 ft bgs** but that the **underlying clay unit (≥30 ft bgs) was not penetrated** in that boring.

## Regulatory status

- The perimeter boring program was designed in consultation with **DTSC** and the **Central Valley RWQCB** (as per SSI Report Section 2.2).  
- DTSC’s *February 2012 Work Plan Approval Letter* required that “perimeter borings extend to at least 5 ft below the maximum depth of contamination or to the top of the first continuous clay layer, whichever is less.” The project complied for Borings A‑P1, A‑P2, B‑P1, and B‑P2, but **Boring C‑P4 terminated at 20 ft bgs** (not to top of clay as required); DTSC’s *March 2013 Comments on the SSI Report* noted this as a data gap.  
- Perimeter soil results are compared to **DTSC’s Modified Screening Levels** (for direct‑contact exposure, residential scenario) and **RWQCB’s Environmental Screening Levels** (for groundwater protection). Groundwater results are compared to **California MCLs** and **RWQCB’s “Risk‑Based” Action Levels** for the site (specifically: **TPH‑d: 100 µg/L**; **lead: 15 µg/L**; **arsenic: 10 µg/L**).  
- As of the SSI Report date (March 2013), the perimeter boring data indicated **no off‑site regulatory exceedances in groundwater**, but **soil exceedances at the boundary** (e.g., lead at 760 mg/kg on the boundary of Stockpile C) required DTSC approval for **on‑site consolidation** (i.e., leaving contaminated soil in place at the property line).

## Open items

1. **Data gap – Boring C‑P4**: DTSC requested that a **supplementary boring be advanced near C‑P4 to 35 ft bgs** to confirm confinement of TPH‑d impacts above the clay unit. No response from Caltrans was documented in the SSI Report.  
2. **Perimeter verification sampling**: The SSI Report states that “verification soil samples from the perimeter will be collected after remedial excavation” but the **sampling plan and acceptance criteria** were not finalized in the SSI Report. An **Addendum** (still pending as of Rev. 0313) was to be submitted to DTSC within 90 days.  
3. **Groundwater monitoring well installation**: Based on the detection of lead and barium above MCLs in the perimeter boring groundwater grabs (C‑P3 and A‑P2), DTSC’s *March 2013 Comments* required installation of **two permanent monitoring wells** (MW‑6 and MW‑7) along the western and northern boundaries. No record of well construction was included in the SSI Report.
