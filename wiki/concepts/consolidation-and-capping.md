## Consolidation and Capping

### Overview
Consolidation and capping is a remedial action implemented at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site. This action involves consolidating soil and then covering it with a cap to mitigate potential risks associated with [[barium-containing-soil]]. The [[operation-and-maintenance-agreement]] outlines the requirements for this remedial action.

### Site Record
The [[operation-and-maintenance-agreement]] details the requirements for consolidation and capping. Specifically, it states that the "Consolidation and Capping" shall be performed in accordance with the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and the specifications outlined in the contract documents. {{quote: "Consolidation and Capping. The Consolidation and Capping shall be performed in accordance with the approved Remedial Action Plan and the specifications set forth in the contract documents."}} The agreement also references the "Final Report, Geotechnical Investigation, SR 132, Stanislaus County, California" by [[geocon-consultants]] dated January 2005, which likely provides the technical basis for the capping design. {{quote: "Final Report, Geotechnical Investigation, SR 132, Stanislaus County, California, dated January 2005, by Geocon Consultants."}}

### Regulatory Status
The [[operation-and-maintenance-agreement]] was entered into by Caltrans and the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). The agreement establishes the terms for the long-term operation and maintenance of the site, including the implemented consolidation and capping remedy. {{quote: "This Operation and Maintenance Agreement (Agreement) is entered into by and between the State of California Department of Transportation (Caltrans) and the State of California Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC)."}} The agreement itself serves as a regulatory mechanism to ensure the ongoing effectiveness of the remedy.

### Open Items
The [[operation-and-maintenance-agreement]] requires ongoing monitoring and maintenance of the consolidated and capped areas. Specific details regarding the frequency and scope of these activities are outlined within the agreement.
