## Consolidation and Capping

### Overview
Consolidation and capping is a remedial action implemented at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site to manage [[barium-containing-soil]]. This process involves consolidating contaminated soil and then covering it with a cap to mitigate potential risks. The [[operation-and-maintenance-agreement]] outlines the requirements for this remedial action.

### Site Record
The [[operation-and-maintenance-agreement]] specifies that the "Consolidation and Capping" shall be performed in accordance with the approved Remedial Action Plan (RAP) and the contract documents. This agreement references the "Final Report, Geotechnical Investigation, SR 132, Stanislaus County, California," dated January 2005, prepared by [[geocon-consultants]], as providing the technical basis for the capping design.

In a technical memorandum dated October 30, 2020, Caltrans proposed using excavated Bent 2 soil as clean cap material. While the analytical results for this soil did not meet the clean cap criteria established in a Variance Request approved by the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC) on April 9, 2020, both DTSC and the Water Board (WB) agreed to its use. This agreement, communicated via email on November 6, 2020, allowed for the placement of Bent 2 soil on top of [[stockpile-1]] at a thickness of 6 inches or less, provided it was covered with clean base material and the roadway construction. This approach aimed to efficiently utilize the Bent 2 soil without compromising the remedy's integrity. The email also emphasized the importance of following the rainy season surface water sampling effort and erosion control measures outlined in the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020, to ensure contaminants remained on-site.

### Regulatory Status
The [[operation-and-maintenance-agreement]] was executed by Caltrans and the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]] (DTSC). This agreement establishes the long-term operation and maintenance requirements for the site, including the implemented consolidation and capping remedy. The DTSC, through an email dated November 6, 2020, officially accepted the Technical Memorandum regarding the use of Bent 2 excavation soil as clean cap material, subject to specific conditions.

### Open Items
The [[operation-and-maintenance-agreement]] mandates ongoing monitoring and maintenance of the consolidated and capped areas. Specific details regarding the frequency and scope of these activities are outlined within the agreement.
