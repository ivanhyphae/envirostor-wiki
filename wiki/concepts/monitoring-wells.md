## Monitoring Wells

### Overview

Monitoring wells are installed at the [[caltrans-modesto-soil-stockpiles]] site to track groundwater quality. Their purpose is to detect the presence and movement of contaminants in the groundwater beneath and surrounding the soil stockpiles.

### Site Record

The 2025 Annual Inspection Report notes that monitoring wells were inspected. The report does not provide specific details regarding the condition or data collected from individual monitoring wells during this inspection.
