Phase 1 BCS Consolidation was the physical remedy implemented under the Interim Phase 1 program at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site: excavated barium-containing soil (BCS) from Stockpiles 2 and 3 was relocated and consolidated into the Stockpile 1 containment area rather than removed from the site, and the consolidated mass was capped. It matters because it converted three separate stockpiles with a combined estimated volume around 160,000 cubic yards into a single containment zone, and because the confirmation sampling performed to close out the excavated areas established the cleanup goal that governed whether soil could be left in place versus moved.

## Excavation and consolidation sequence

Under the approved [[remedial-action-plan]] and the approved Interim Phase 1 Remedial Design Implementation Plan (RDIP), work removed all of BCS Stockpile 3, the western and eastern ends of Stockpile 2, and soil from the southern slopes of Stockpiles 1 and 2 to make way for construction of the new State Route 132 Expressway. Stockpile 3 — originally on the east side of State Route 99, roughly 1,100 feet long by 120 feet wide, with an estimated fill volume of 24,000 cubic yards — was fully excavated: approximately 27,675 cubic yards of soil was trucked and placed as embankment fill within the BCS Stockpile 1 containment zone. This is the defining consolidation event of Phase 1: material from a separate, distant stockpile was physically moved into and became part of the Stockpile 1 mass, which was capped rather than the soil being taken off site.

## Confirmation sampling and the barium cleanup goal

Twenty-nine confirmation soil samples were collected from the base of the Stockpile 3 excavation and analyzed for [[barium]] and lead. Three of the twenty-nine exceeded the cleanup goal of 1,000 mg/kg for barium. Rather than being left as residual contamination, these three locations were deepened by approximately one foot and resampled; the final confirmation samples showed the remaining soil met the goal. This is the only excavation-verification result reported in the acceptance correspondence, and it establishes the operative threshold for the remedy: 1,000 mg/kg barium was the line determining whether soil could remain in the ground at the Stockpile 3 footprint or had to be treated as BCS requiring consolidation elsewhere.

## Regulatory disposition

The consolidation and capping work, together with the confirmation results, were documented in the Draft Interim Removal Action Completion Report (RACR) – Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, Interim Phase 1, submitted by [[geocon-consultants-inc]] on behalf of [[caltrans]]. [[dtsc]], in consultation with the [[central-valley-rwqcb]], reviewed the Draft Phase 1 RACR and issued an acceptance letter dated February 24, 2022 (Site Code 900259) covering the consolidation and capping activities completed to date as part of Interim Phase 1 construction. The acceptance is scoped to the work performed to date under Interim Phase 1; the record here does not describe conditions attached to that acceptance beyond describing the completed removal, consolidation, and confirmation-sampling activities.
