## Stockpile Management

The Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles project involved the management of soil stockpiles located southerly of the intersection of State Route (SR) 99 and Kansas Avenue in Stanislaus County, California. Surface water sampling was conducted on March 5, 2016, to assess conditions at the site, which included three identified stockpiles. The sampling activities were performed in accordance with approved protocols.

## Site Record

On March 5, 2016, surface water sampling was conducted at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles. The sampling was performed by [[geocon-consultants]] in general accordance with protocols approved by the California Environmental Protection Agency, Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC), as established in the *Final Surface Water Sampling and Analysis Plan (SAP)* dated January 2006, and an addendum dated February 20, 2013. The scope of services included surface water sampling, laboratory analysis, and reporting. The site plan, Figure 2, depicts the approximate site boundaries, Stockpiles 1 through 3, and surface water sampling locations.
