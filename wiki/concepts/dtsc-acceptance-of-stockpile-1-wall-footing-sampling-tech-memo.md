## DTSC Acceptance of Stockpile 1 Wall Footing Sampling Tech Memo

### Overview

This entry documents the acceptance by the [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] of a technical memorandum detailing soil sampling results from the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area for [[caltrans]]'s SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. The acceptance was contingent on specific placement of the sampled material.

### Site Record

On April 6, 2020, [[geocon-consultants-inc]] submitted a technical memorandum to [[caltrans]] regarding soil characterization for the Stockpile 1 MSE wall footing excavation. This memo was forwarded to [[dean-wright]] at [[dtsc]] and Steven Meeks at the Water Board for approval.

The memorandum described sampling along the MSE wall footing excavation area and presented laboratory analysis results. [[dean-wright]] of [[dtsc]], in an email dated April 7, 2020, communicated the acceptance of the memo by both [[dtsc]] and the Water Board. This acceptance was conditional on the understanding that the MSE wall footing soil would not be used as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2. The rationale provided was that the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil exceeded the maximum background of 120 mg/kg, and the excavation area fell outside the designated borrow area specified in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan. Consequently, this material was required to be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or used as general bridge embankment fill.

### Regulatory Status

The [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] reviewed the sampling results and the proposed use of the excavated soil. The maximum background concentration for the relevant analyte was identified as 120 mg/kg. The 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil exceeded this threshold.
