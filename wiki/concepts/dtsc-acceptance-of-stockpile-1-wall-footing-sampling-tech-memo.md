This entry documents the [[dtsc]]'s acceptance of a technical memorandum concerning soil sampling results from the mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) wall footing excavation area for [[caltrans]]'s SR 132 project. The acceptance was conditional on specific placement of the sampled material.

### DTSC and Water Board Acceptance

On April 7, 2020, [[dean-wright]] of the [[dtsc]], in an email to Richard Stewart at [[caltrans]], communicated the acceptance of a technical memorandum dated April 6, 2020. This memorandum, prepared by [[geocon-consultants-inc]], detailed soil sampling and laboratory analysis results from the MSE wall footing excavation area. The acceptance by both the [[dtsc]] and the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] was contingent on the understanding that the MSE wall footing soil would not be used as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2.

### Rationale for Placement Restriction

The acceptance was based on two key factors. First, the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil exceeded the maximum background concentration of 120 mg/kg for the relevant analyte. Second, the MSE wall footing excavation area was located outside the designated borrow area specified in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan. Consequently, this material was required to be placed within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or utilized as general bridge embankment fill.
