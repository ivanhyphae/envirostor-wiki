The [[om-inspection-report]] is a document submitted to the DTSC detailing the findings of an annual inspection of the [[soil-stockpiles]] located at the State Route 132 West Expressway at the State Route 132/99 Interchange in Stanislaus County, California.

## Site record

The Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report dated January 26, 2026, was submitted by [[caltrans]]. The inspection was conducted at the SR 132 West Expressway at the SR 132/99 Interchange. The report noted that vegetation along the roadway remained sparse, a condition also observed in the previous year's report. The report indicated that leftover erosion control materials were currently limiting erosion. Maintenance crews were observed removing debris from the north slope, specifically at stockpile #2, where the area was freshly raked with piles of debris at the bottom of the slope.

## Regulatory status

The Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) reviewed and approved the Annual Operation and Maintenance Inspection Report dated January 26, 2026. DTSC's Project Manager and Engineering and Special Projects Office (ESPO) completed their review, and the report was accepted as final. DTSC encouraged continued efforts to establish vegetation along the roadway to prevent erosion.

## Open items

The DTSC has requested an Annual Land Use Covenant Inspection Report, which is due to DTSC no later than January 18, 2027.
