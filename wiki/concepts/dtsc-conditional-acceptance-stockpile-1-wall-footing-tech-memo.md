# DTSC Conditional Acceptance, Stockpile 1 Wall Footing Tech Memo

On April 7, 2020, [[dtsc]] (with concurrence from the [[central-valley-rwqcb]]) conditionally accepted [[geocon-consultants-inc]]'s April 6, 2020 technical memorandum characterizing soil excavated from the [[mse-wall]] footing near Stockpile 1. DTSC agreed with the memo's findings but rejected its proposed use of the material as capping material for Stockpiles 1 and 2, instead restricting it to placement within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill containment zones or use as general bridge embankment fill. The restriction turned on two facts stated in the acceptance email: the 95% Upper Confidence Limit of the sampled soil exceeded the site's maximum background concentration of 120 mg/kg, and the MSE wall footing area lay outside the borrow area designated in the Remedial Design Implementation Plan.

## Sequence of the approval

[[john-juhrend]] of Geocon transmitted the technical memo to Caltrans' Richard Stewart on April 6, 2020, requesting that it be forwarded to [[dean-wright]] at DTSC and Steven Meeks at the Central Valley RWQCB for approval to use the MSE wall footing excavation soil as embankment fill and/or clean cover material within Stockpiles 1 and 2. Juhrend's email also noted that wall footing excavation sampling in the Stockpile 2 area could not yet be completed because BCS material on the southerly slope of Stockpile 2 had not yet been removed; that portion of the sampling remained outstanding at the time of this transmittal. Stewart forwarded the memo the same evening to Dean Wright, Perry Myers (DTSC), and Steven Meeks (Central Valley RWQCB) for review and approval. Wright responded the next day, April 7, 2020, on behalf of both DTSC and the Water Board, granting acceptance subject to the capping-material condition.

## Basis for the condition and its effect on soil disposition

The barium background threshold cited in the acceptance email — a maximum background of 120 mg/kg — is the specific figure that disqualified the wall footing soil from use as cap material: the memo's sampled soil produced a 95% UCL above that value. Because the excavation area also fell outside the borrow area authorized by the Remedial Design Implementation Plan, DTSC treated the material as ineligible for the more permissive capping use even though it accepted the memo's characterization and sampling work otherwise. The practical effect was to narrow the material's approved disposition to two options: containment within the Stockpiles 1 and 2 fill zones, or use as general bridge embankment fill — both of which keep the soil within an engineered or contained placement rather than as exposed surface cover. DTSC's email states that its acceptance of the memo is contingent on execution of this condition in the field, making the April 7, 2020 email a conditional rather than unqualified approval.

## Open item

The memo and acceptance correspondence do not address the Stockpile 2 southerly slope wall footing sampling that Juhrend identified as still pending as of April 6, 2020; no later document in this record confirms that sampling was completed or submitted for review.
