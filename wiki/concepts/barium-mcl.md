## Overview

Barium is a dissolved metal of concern at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site. It has been detected in groundwater samples and is tracked in relation to applicable water quality thresholds.

## Site record

Barium was reported for each of the two groundwater samples analyzed in May 2016. {{quote: "For the May 2016 groundwater samples, barium and strontium were reported for each of the two groundwater samples analyzed."}} The concentrations of barium and strontium reported for the May sampling event were {{quote: "barium and strontium concentrations reported for the May sampling event are in the following table:"}} [Table /page/5/Table/10]. Barium and strontium concentrations were reported for the May 2016 groundwater samples at concentrations similar to historical levels and remained significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds. {{quote: "Barium and strontium were reported for the May 2016 groundwater samples at concentrations similar to historical levels and remained significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds."}}

## Regulatory status

The California Department of Public Health Primary MCL for Drinking Water is applicable to barium. {{quote: "(1) = California Department of Public Health Primary MCL for Drinking Water"}} The reported concentrations of barium in May 2016 were significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds. {{quote: "Barium and strontium were reported for the May 2016 groundwater samples at concentrations similar to historical levels and remained significantly less than their numeric water quality thresholds."}}
