## Barium MCL

### Overview
Barium is an analyte of interest at the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, specifically in relation to its Maximum Contaminant Level (MCL).

### Site Record
Barium was detected in soil samples. For example, in the Phase 1 2019–2020 sampling event, barium was detected at a concentration of 1,100 mg/kg in sample ID "SS-10-0.5-1.0".

### Regulatory Status
The MCL for barium is 1,000 µg/L (or 1 mg/L). The detection of 1,100 mg/kg in soil sample "SS-10-0.5-1.0" during the Phase 1 2019–2020 sampling event exceeds this threshold when converted to a comparable unit for soil.
