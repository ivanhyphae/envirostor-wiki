The [[california-secondary-mcl]] for barium is 1,000 µg/L (or 1 mg/L). This standard is relevant to the Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles site, where barium has been detected in soil samples.

### Barium Detections and Regulatory Thresholds

Barium was detected in soil samples collected from the Carpenter Road Shoofly. In this location, barium concentrations ranged from 28 to 96 milligrams per kilogram (mg/kg), which were all less than the maximum site-specific background value for barium of 120 mg/kg.

In a separate sampling event, barium was detected in soil sample "SS-10-0.5-1.0" at a concentration of 1,100 mg/kg during the Phase 1 2019–2020 sampling event. This detection exceeds the [[california-secondary-mcl]] for barium when converted to a comparable unit for soil.

### Regulatory Concurrence

The Final Feasibility Study Caltrans Modesto Soil Stockpiles, State Route 132, West Freeway/Expressway Project, Stanislaus County, prepared by [[geocon-consultants]], dated June 24, 2014, was reviewed by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC) in consultation with the Central Valley Regional Water Quality Control Board (RWQCB). DTSC concurred with the Final Feasibility Study on June 30, 2014.
