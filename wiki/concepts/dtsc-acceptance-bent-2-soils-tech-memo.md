# Dtsc Acceptance Bent 2 Soils Tech Memo

On November 6, 2020, DTSC project manager [[dean-wright]] issued an email to Caltrans (Richard Stewart), copying [[central-valley-rwqcb]] staff, stating DTSC's and the Water Board's joint review findings on the Technical Memorandum dated October 30, 2020, which addressed characterization of soil excavated from [[bent-2-area]] for potential use as clean cap material. The email is DTSC's official acceptance of the Tech Memo, but the acceptance is conditional: the underlying sampling showed the soil did not qualify as clean cap material, and DTSC approved a narrower, restricted use instead of the one originally proposed.

## What the Tech Memo proposed and why it failed

Caltrans submitted the Tech Memo on November 2, 2020 (cover email from Richard Stewart) proposing to use soil excavated during the Bent 2 column excavation as clean cap cover material. Stewart's transmittal email states plainly that, based on the analytical results, the Bent 2 soils exceeded the background criteria for use as clean cap material. This background criteria standard traces to the April 9, 2020 Variance Request that DTSC had previously approved, which the November 6 email cites as the benchmark the soil failed to meet.

## DTSC's alternative approval

Rather than rejecting the material outright, DTSC and the Water Board agreed to a limited alternative use: the Bent 2 soil could be placed on top of Stockpile 2, but only under three conditions stated in the email:

- placed at a thickness of 6 inches or less;
- covered with clean base material; and
- covered with the asphalt/concrete used to construct the roadway.

DTSC's stated rationale was to allow efficient use of the excavated material without compromising the integrity of the remedy — i.e., the soil could be productively used on site as long as it was permanently isolated under clean material and pavement rather than left as an exposed surface.

## Interim exposure and the linked SWPPP condition

The acceptance email also addresses an interim condition: because of the project schedule at the time, the Bent 2 soils were expected to be temporarily exposed to the elements during the winter months before the base material and pavement cover could be completed. DTSC's acceptance is therefore expressly tied to a second document, the Revised Stormwater Prevention Plan (SWPPP) Amendment dated October 22, 2020, and its rainy-season surface water sampling and erosion control measures. The email states that following these measures "as strictly as possible" is important to ensuring contaminants remain on site and do not affect the surrounding area. The November 6, 2020 email serves as DTSC's official acceptance of both the Tech Memo and the Revised SWPPP Amendment as a single, linked approval — the placement approval for the Bent 2 soil is conditioned on stormwater and erosion controls being followed during the period the soil is exposed.

The available record does not show subsequent confirmation that the 6-inch cap, base material, and pavement were completed as specified, or that the rainy-season surface water sampling results were reported back to DTSC.
