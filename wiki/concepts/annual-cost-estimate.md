# Annual Cost Estimate

The Annual Cost Estimate Letter for Fiscal Year 2025-26, covering the State Route 132 site (identified as 900259), is Caltrans's recurring financial accounting to DTSC of the projected cost of maintaining the site's post-closure obligations under the land-use covenant. It documents what the operation and maintenance of the remediated stockpile site is expected to cost for the coming fiscal year, and stands as the current instrument by which the ongoing financial obligation tied to the site's institutional controls is tracked.

## Purpose and context

The letter is issued for the State Route 132 site under project number 900259, in Stanislaus County. It is produced annually, with the FY 25-26 letter being the version in the case file addressed by this article. Its function is to state the estimated cost of the activities required to maintain the site in compliance with its post-closure requirements for the coming year, rather than to report new sampling results, remedial actions, or regulatory decisions.

## Significance

Because the site remains subject to a land-use covenant, the annual cost estimate is the recurring mechanism by which Caltrans quantifies the financial obligation associated with that ongoing restriction. Its recurrence — a new letter each fiscal year — reflects that the covenant's obligations are not a one-time closeout cost but a continuing liability that must be re-estimated and accounted for annually for as long as the covenant remains in effect.
