Molybdenum is a metal detected in stormwater samples collected at the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]] site. Its presence in stormwater is noted in the context of [[caltrans]]'s remediation efforts, overseen by agencies such as the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]].

### Site Record
Molybdenum was detected in stormwater samples SW-East and SW-West. {{quote: "Molybdenum, vanadium, and strontium were detected in storm water samples SW-East and SW-West, however, MCLs have not been established for these analytes."}} {{quote: "The following metals were detected in one or more of the surface water samples collected on March 10, 2023: arsenic, barium, chromium, copper, lead, molybdenum, selenium, vanadium, zinc, strontium, and mercury."}}

During laboratory quality assurance/quality control (QA/QC) review, the Relative Percent Difference (RPD) for molybdenum in sample N071731-001A-DUP was found to be outside accepted recovery limits. {{quote: "The laboratory notes that the RPD in the MSD are outside accepted recovery limits for silver in samples N071731-001A-MSD and the RPD is outside accepted recovery limits for molybdenum in samples N071731-001A-DUP."}} Additionally, the analytical comments for EPA 6010B noted that the RPD for Sample Duplicate (DUP) is outside criteria for Molybdenum. {{quote: "RPD for Sample Duplicate (DUP) is outside criteria for Molybdenum; however, the Laboratory Control Sample (LCS) validated the analytical batch."}} Despite these RPD exceedances, the Laboratory Control Sample (LCS) validated the analytical batch for molybdenum. {{quote: "RPD for Sample Duplicate (DUP) is outside criteria for Molybdenum; however, the Laboratory Control Sample (LCS) validated the analytical batch."}}

Molybdenum was detected in surface water samples collected on March 24, 2017, at locations PL1 through PL5. {{quote: "Antimony, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and vanadium were reported for samples PL1 through PL5."}}

Molybdenum was also detected in surface water samples collected on October 28, 2016, at locations PL1 through PL5. {{quote: "Antimony, barium, calcium, copper, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, and zinc were reported for samples PL1 through PL5."}}

Molybdenum was detected in surface water samples collected on March 22, 2018, at locations PL1 through PL4 and BG1. {{quote: "Antimony, arsenic, barium, calcium, chromium, cobalt, copper, lead, manganese, magnesium, molybdenum, nickel, potassium, sodium, strontium, vanadium, and zinc were reported for some or all of the samples."}}

Molybdenum was detected in stormwater samples SW-East and SW-West collected on February 4, 2025. {{quote: "Molybdenum, vanadium, and strontium were detected in storm water samples SW-East and SW-West, however, MCLs have not been established for these analytes."}}

### Regulatory Status
Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) have not been established for molybdenum. {{quote: "Molybdenum, vanadium, and strontium were detected in storm water samples SW-East and SW-West, however, MCLs have not been established for these analytes."}}
