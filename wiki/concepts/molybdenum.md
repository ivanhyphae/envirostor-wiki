Molybdenum is a metal detected in stormwater samples collected at the [[caltrans-encapsulated-soil-stockpiles]] site. Its presence in stormwater is noted in the context of [[caltrans]]'s remediation efforts, overseen by agencies such as the [[central-valley-rwqcb]] and the [[department-of-toxic-substances-control]].

### Site Record
Molybdenum was detected in stormwater samples SW-East and SW-West.

During laboratory quality assurance/quality control (QA/QC) review, the Relative Percent Difference (RPD) for molybdenum in sample N071731-001A-DUP was found to be outside accepted recovery limits. Additionally, the analytical comments for EPA 6010B noted that the RPD for Sample Duplicate (DUP) is outside criteria for Molybdenum. Despite these RPD exceedances, the Laboratory Control Sample (LCS) validated the analytical batch for molybdenum.

Molybdenum was detected in surface water samples collected on March 24, 2017, at locations PL1 through PL5.

Molybdenum was also detected in surface water samples collected on October 28, 2016, at locations PL1 through PL5.

Molybdenum was detected in surface water samples collected on March 22, 2018, at locations PL1 through PL4 and BG1.

Molybdenum was detected in stormwater samples SW-East and SW-West collected on February 4, 2025.

### Regulatory Status
Maximum Contaminant Levels (MCLs) have not been established for molybdenum.
