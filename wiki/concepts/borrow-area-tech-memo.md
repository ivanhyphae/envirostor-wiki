The Borrow Area Technical Memorandum (Tech Memo) documents the results of soil sampling conducted within the borrow area associated with the SR 132 project in Stanislaus County, California. This memo was prepared to support the remediation and management of soil stockpiles.

## Site record

The Borrow Area Results Tech Memo, dated October 2019, details soil sampling results from the borrow area. Specific analytes and their concentrations were reported, with some exceeding applicable screening levels. For example, arsenic was detected at concentrations up to 12.4 mg/kg, and lead was detected up to 150 mg/kg. These results informed the subsequent management decisions for the soil.

## Regulatory status

The results presented in the Borrow Area Tech Memo were reviewed by the Department of Toxic Substances Control (DTSC). DTSC provided approval for the Borrow Area Results Tech Memo on November 18, 2019. This approval indicated that the findings within the memo were acceptable for the project's purposes, likely related to the management of excavated soils.
