# SR-132 EnviroStor Wiki

A compiled knowledge base of environmental remediation records for the State Route 132 (Modesto) soil stockpiles site, Stanislaus County, CA. Content is extracted and interlinked automatically from regulatory letters, monitoring reports, and site investigations by [sage-wiki](https://github.com/xoai/sage-wiki).

## Browse

- [[concepts/|Concepts]] — contaminants, sampling events, regulatory decisions, and other entities extracted from source documents, cross-linked to the records that support them.
- [[summaries/|Summaries]] — per-document summaries: identity, key claims, regulatory decisions, and open items for each source.
- [[sources/|Sources]] — the underlying source documents (letters, reports, tables) in their compiled form.

## Site background

DTSC has directed Caltrans to manage the SR-132 West Expressway soil stockpiles under an ongoing monitoring and access-restriction program. The wiki tracks the contaminants of concern (including barium, lead, and strontium), groundwater monitoring results, and the regulatory correspondence governing site management.

See the [[CHANGELOG|changelog]] for compilation history, or the project [README](https://github.com/xoai/sage-wiki) for how to add new documents and recompile.
